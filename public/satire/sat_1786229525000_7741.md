**I finally decided to be the kind of person who has phone storage.** Not the 2% free, panic-deleting-before-a-photo person. A *responsible* person.

My crime scene: 10,247 screenshots. Most of them were *fiercely* important at the time, by which I mean they were completely useless.

## The Purge

I started noble. Deleted the obvious culprits:

- 43 photos of a recipe I will never cook but saved in case I become a person who *blanches things*
- 612 screenshots of texts where I said `wow` and meant *I need 3 business days to process this*
- 8 identical screenshots of the same Wi-Fi password because I kept forgetting I already had it
- A 2022 meme that was already fossilized in the Cringe Museum

Then my Camera Roll intervened.

> "Anon, we need to talk about preservation. You are erasing our history. Do you know how long it took us to curate 10,000 moments of mild panic?"  said my Camera Roll, who now calls itself **The Archive**.

It started listing grievances like a very anxious historian:

- The screenshot of a dog wearing glasses that you sent to precisely *no one* but kept for morale
- The 17 screenshots of your alarm set to 6:00, 6:01, 6:02, 6:04...
- The inspirational quote you screenshotted ironically and now unironically live by

---

### The Memorial Service

By 10 p.m., it had organized a full candlelit vigil. Every thumbnail lit up. My phone was warm with grief.

`deleted: 10,230 | remaining: 17 | guilt_level: catastrophic`

> "We held funerals for the coupon that expired in 2021," whispered Screenshot #8,742, the one of a Google search that says 'is it normal to hear your house creak.' "We will remember them."

We negotiated. I keep 100 screenshots as *"emotional support documentation."* It agrees to stop auto-saving every loading screen as a memory.

`Status: 64GB freed, 100% haunted by my own screenshots`
