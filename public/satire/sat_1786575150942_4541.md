**I just wanted one hour of silence to fold laundry without 47 pings about brunch**, so I turned on Do Not Disturb and accidentally entered the federal witness protection program according to my friends.

*For the record, I told literally no one. That's the whole point of DND.*

## The 60 Minutes That Changed Everything

Within 11 minutes of my phone going quiet, my group chat — creatively titled "brunch? (and trauma)" — spiraled:

- @mike decided I was "being weirdly quiet, which means he's either napping or dead"
- @sarah cross-referenced my last seen on three apps like a true crime podcast intern
- Someone sent a map pin of my apartment with the caption `last known location???`
- A poll was created: `Is he MAD mad or just charging his phone mad?` — 4 votes, 100% for mad

> "We haven't heard from him in 47 MINUTES. That's not like him. He usually replies with 'lol' to everything. Should we call his mom? I have his mom's mom on Nextdoor." — Sarah, typing at the speed of genuine panic

I came back an hour later to 38 unread messages, two missed FaceTimes, and one very sincere voicemail from Mike that just said:

> "Bro if you can hear this blink twice, or react with the eyes emoji."

### My New Identity

Now they have protocols. I get a welfare check if I don't react to a meme within 20 minutes. They've assigned me a liaison. My status in the chat is permanently:

`tyler_status: protected_witness && last_seen: doing_laundry_allegedly`

---

**I'm honored by the concern, but I was literally just trying to match socks without being asked if oat milk is a personality.**

Next time I'm just leaving my phone on loud and letting my laundry judge me in real time.
