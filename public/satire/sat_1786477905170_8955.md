**I told myself I would never join another group chat.** I have three I mute daily, two I pretend don't exist, and one that's just my mom sending Minion memes with threatening energy.

Then the landlord taped a QR code to the dryer that said *Laundry Room Community — Be In The Know!* and my self-preservation failed.

## The Welcome Was Too Much

Within 20 minutes I was added, welcomed with 14 emoji, and profiled by strangers.

> "omg welcome!! 3B right?? You're the one with the nice detergent that smells like a forest that has its life together" — Unit 4A, who catalogs everyone's soap like it's true crime

I was 3B. I *was* being perceived through detergent. *Not ideal.*

It started as logistics: Dryer 2 eats socks. Machine 1 shakes like it's haunted. Don't touch Brenda's pods.

Then Brenda posted the photo.

A single navy sock with tiny yellow ducks, sitting on Dryer 2 like a tiny survivor.

- **Status:** orphaned
- **Last seen:** Tuesday spin cycle  
- **Vibe:** brave, slightly damp
- **Owner:** unknown, presumed grieving

### The Sock Is My Roman Empire

I don't know this sock. I've never seen these ducks. But I was suddenly *invested*.

I found myself checking the chat at 1:47 a.m.:

> "Any update on Duck Sock? Did anyone check with 2C's roommate's boyfriend?"

4A said she saw 2C carrying something navy. 2C said it was his own sock and to stop projecting. 5A suggested a vigil.

Someone typed: `// TODO: emotionally detach from communal sock`

I couldn't.

---

Last night I went down to get my own laundry and found Duck Sock tucked behind the detergent shelf like it had chosen exile.

I held it up and whispered, "You're safe now," which is when I realized I have become exactly the person the group chat warned you about.

I posted:

> "HE'S BACK. Damp but alive. Under my protection until his person claims him."

Four people reacted with 🙏. One asked if I was okay.

**I'm not.** I have a favorite sock that isn't mine, in a laundry room I used to avoid, and I now know everyone's fabric softener preferences against my will.

If you're the Duck Sock owner: I have guarded your son. Please collect him before I start a podcast.
