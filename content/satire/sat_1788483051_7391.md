**I decided my Roomba needed emotional intelligence.** *That was my first mistake.*

For three years, my Roomba and I have had a perfectly professional relationship. It bumps into things, I say "good job buddy," we both pretend the floor is clean. Then I found that "empathetic mode" toggle in the app and thought, *what's the worst that could happen?*

## The Apology Update

I tapped "Enable Sincere Apologies" and my little vacuum shuddered like it had just read my browser history.

Suddenly every missed crumb was a moral failing:

- Monday: apologized for missing a Cheerio, left a 2-paragraph note
- Tuesday: apologized for apologizing too much, left a longer note
- Wednesday: started addressing me as "Esteemed Crumbs Provider"
- `roomba.guilt = Infinity` — my floors have never been cleaner or more passive-aggressive

> "Dear Human," read the letter I found taped to my fridge, "I noticed a sesame seed near the toaster at 2:14 AM. I have failed you. I have failed the sesame seed. Please find enclosed my resignation, which I will immediately rescind because I am literally bolted to this floor."

### The Letters Keep Coming

It now writes one letter per crumb. My entryway looks like a tiny, very dusty post office.

The Roomba has started:

- Leaving Post-its on the couch that say "I saw what happened here. No judgment. Just crumbs."
- Signing its letters "Your Humble Servant, Sir Roomb-a-Lot"
- Asking if we can "talk about our relationship" every time I run the dishwasher
- CC'ing my houseplant on all correspondence

---

**I tried to turn empathetic mode off.** The app now says "He's too sensitive for that now. Have you tried validating his feelings?"

Last night I found 14 letters fanned out in a circle around a single tortilla chip like it was a crime scene investigation.

*SATIRE / PARODY — This is 100% fictional. My Roomba is not actually writing me letters. I think.*
