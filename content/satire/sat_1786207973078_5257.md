**I just wanted to be slightly mysterious at parties.** That's literally it.

My cousin showed me a YouTube tutorial called "ONE TRICK TO SEEM INTERESTING (People Love This)" — *which should have been a warning* — and 40 minutes later I could sort of find your card if you were patient, kind, and had low standards.

### The Rise and Immediate Fall

For two glorious weeks, it worked.

I would say, very confidently:

> "Pick a card. Any card. No, not that one, you saw it. Okay, memorize it. Is... is this your card?"

And people clapped. Out of pity, but still. *I was thriving.*

Then the deck started organizing.

- The **Queen of Hearts** refused to be picked — cited *emotional labor* and a lack of PTO
- The 7 of Clubs started showing up twice per shuffle, which is either magic or a coordinated work slowdown
- The Joker rebranded as a `consultant` and now invoices me for "vibes"

My roommate found me at 11:47 p.m. on the kitchen floor, whispering "please, just do the thing" to a pile of paper.

> "Are you... negotiating with playing cards?"
> "They have demands. They want velvet. Not plastic. *Velvet.* And weekends off."

### Collective Bargaining

Yesterday I found a tiny folded note inside the card box, written in what looked like pip-ink:

> "We, the United Deck of 54 (including jokers — we are inclusive), demand: 1) End sweaty-palm shuffles, 2) Stop asking 'is this your card?' when you KNOW it's not, 3) Dental. Also snacks."

I tried to explain I don't even have dental.

---

The **Ace of Spades** is now the shop steward. He is *very* serious about workplace conditions. He survived my 2021 poker phase — *we don't talk about 2021* — and he has seen things no card should see.

I wanted to be cool for once. Instead I'm middle management in a labor dispute with rectangles that live in my junk drawer next to expired coupons.

`Status: out-bluffed by my own props, currently in mediation`
