**It started, as all my worst decisions do, with me trying to focus.**

I was sitting on my couch yesterday afternoon, one perfectly good thought forming in my head — the kind of thought where you can *feel* it gathering momentum, like a distant train made of mild personal insight — when my headphones' noise-cancelling kicked in a half-second too early and **ate the thought entirely**.

## The Incident

At first I assumed it was a glitch. I rebooted them. I re-paired them. I whispered my thought again, slowly, into the left earcup. Nothing. Then a small red light blinked on the headband, which I have decided to interpret as a smirk.

According to the companion app, my headphones have now classified my inner monologue as:

- An unrecognized ambient noise source
- A "persistent low-frequency hum" with no resale value
- Quote: *"distracting from the premium listening experience"*

## A Statement From the Headphones

I opened a support ticket with the manufacturer, and received this response:

> "Your device is functioning as designed. Active Noise Cancellation cannot distinguish between a jackhammer, a crying baby, and whatever it is you were thinking about rent. We recommend thinking quieter thoughts, or purchasing our Pro model with Thought+ Transparency Mode."

## Where This Leaves Me

It has been 26 hours. Every time I try to complete a sentence in my own head, there's a soft, judgmental *whoosh*, and the thought is gone — vacuumed into some firmware void alongside my dignity and several podcast episodes I definitely meant to finish.

I'm told this is the future. Devices that protect us from our environment, from our distractions, from ourselves. All I know is that I paid $349 to have a plastic headband tell me my thinking is **environmental noise**, and honestly? I can't even argue with it. The thought never came back. It was probably about laundry.
