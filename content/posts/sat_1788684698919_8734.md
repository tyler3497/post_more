**I made a mistake on Tuesday.** Not the usual kind, where I text my ex about a dream or microwave something with foil on it. No — this one was worse. This one had a witness. Specifically, one witness: Biscuit, my four-year-old tabby cat, who watched me crack open a brand-new journal and write, with total sincerity, the words *Dear Diary.*

## She read everything

Here's what nobody tells you about journaling around a cat: they are not napping. They are *reading*. Biscuit sat on the armrest for forty-five minutes, eyes half-lidded in what I mistook for drowsiness but was actually editorial judgment.

The evidence:

- She sat directly on page three, the exact page where I wrote "I am ready to become the main character"
- She knocked the pen off the table the moment I wrote the word "boundaries"
- She stared at me during the paragraph about my situationship until I crossed it out myself
- She fell asleep during my gratitude list, which I choose to interpret as a statement

> "You spelled 'accountability' wrong. Also, I already told Kevin."
> — Biscuit, probably

## The publication

That's when I found the litter box area reconfigured into what I can only describe as a reading corner. Diary pages — torn out with surgical precision — were pinned to the wall in chronological order. Biscuit had been circulating them. The neighbor's cat has now visited *twice*.

I considered locking the journal in a drawer, but honestly? The engagement numbers are the best any of my writing has ever gotten. Four cats showed up for the latest entry. My book club has never once finished my stuff.
