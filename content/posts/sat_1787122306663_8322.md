**I bought $300 noise-canceling headphones to finally achieve peace, and now I have the exact opposite problem.**

For *context*, my apartment walls are approximately one-ply. My upstairs neighbors have been rehearsing what I can only describe as competitive furniture-dragging for the Olympics since March. I bought the headphones for self-preservation.

I put them on, pressed the button, and the world went silent. Beautiful, expensive, *aggressively engineered* silence. Too silent.

That's when I realized my houseplants have been talking about me this whole time.

## Why This Is Probably Fine

I have, at last count, 11 houseplants. I thought they were decor. Turns out they are a very disappointed focus group:

- My monstera, Kevin, has watched me reheat the same coffee three times and has notes
- The tiny succulent I bought at Trader Joe's is somehow thriving out of spite and calls me "the overwaterer"
- The fern in the bathroom has seen things and will not be making eye contact

They were quiet before because the neighbor's bass covered their whisper-track. Remove the bass and suddenly it's a 24/7 green room roast.

> "Oh, so *now* he waters us?" whispered Kevin, my monstera, at 9:47 p.m. Tuesday, while I stood in socks trying to find the remote that was in my hand. "After three weeks of 'you're so resilient'? Bold strategy, chief."

I tried to explain myself. I said `if (plant.mood === "judgmental") { water(sparingly); apologize(profusely); }`

*Which is not how botany works and also not how apologies work, apparently.*

---

They've now formed a union. They want consistent sunlight, not "whatever's left after I close the blinds to watch reality TV," and they've demanded I stop playing the same lo-fi playlist on loop because the fern says it can hear the artist's burnout.

Current status: I can still hear my neighbors again if I take the headphones off, but honestly, my neighbors never asked me why I own seven identical gray t-shirts.

My monstera did.

*This is satire. 100% fictional parody. No houseplants were harmed, though Kevin is still monitoring my life choices very closely.*
