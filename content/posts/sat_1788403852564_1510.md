**I just wanted slightly less dusty air and now I'm on a PIP for how I exhale.**

*Which is absurd, because I bought Fern on sale at Home Depot because the tag said "purifies air and boosts mood" — not "will become middle management."*

## The Interview Process I Didn't Apply For

Week one, Fern was chill. Week two, it sprouted a new leaf and I clapped. That was my mistake. Encouragement created ambition.

By week three, there was a tiny calendar on my fridge written in what looked like soil.

> "Per my last photosynthesis, your 2am sighing is disrupting team oxygen morale. Let's circle back on your commitment to deep breaths. — Fern, Head of Air Quality Operations"

I tried to laugh it off. Fern added an agenda item.

### Evidence That My Houseplant Is Now My Manager

- **It schedules 1:1s.** Every Tuesday at 7am, I have to sit across from Fern while it just *exists* more efficiently than me. I bring coffee. It brings judgment.
- **It tracks metrics.** My `in-office breathing score` is currently at 62% due to "excessive mouth-breathing while doomscrolling" and "shallow sighs during laundry"
- **It introduced peer feedback.** My succulent, Kevin, was asked to rate my watering consistency. He gave me 2 stars and said I have "anxious plant-parent energy"
- **It uses corporate jargon.** Last note said my nighttime air utilization lacked synergy and I should "leverage my diaphragm to drive cross-functional oxygen flow"

*I told Fern I was feeling a little overwhelmed.*

It slid over a printed action plan titled **Q3 Breathing Goals (You).**

---

This morning I found a Post-it on the humidifier: "Great job inhaling! Let's work on the *quality* of your exhales. P.S. Your humidifier and I talked and we both think you should drink more water."

**I'm being managed by a fern that I haven't even repotted yet.**

Honestly, the feedback is kind of valid. I *do* hold my breath when I check my email.

*Satire/parody — obviously fictional. No houseplants were promoted to management during the writing of this post. Fern is still very pretty, just deeply passive-aggressive.*
