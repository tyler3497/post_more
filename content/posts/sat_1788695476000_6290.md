**I bought a label maker to get organized, which is a polite way of saying I wanted to feel like a person who has their life together without actually doing the work.**

*It arrived Tuesday. By Friday, my apartment had a bureaucracy.*

It started innocently — spice jars, junk drawer dividers, charger cables. I labeled the junk drawer dividers. I labeled the charger cables. Then, around 11:40 p.m. that first night, we both lost the plot.

## The Golden Hour of Labeling

That first evening was euphoric. Click-click-CLICK, and suddenly:

- The fridge door: `LEFT OVERS - EAT OR REGRET`
- The freezer: `NO MAN'S LAND (frozen burritos, 2024)`
- My desk drawer: `IMPORTANT-ish`
- The remote control: `DO NOT MOVE. EVER.`

*My roommate watched me label his cereal box `SHARED (?)` and said, "You've become the villain of our home." I said, "I'm the admin now."*

## Things Got Existential

By Thursday I had labeled everything that *could* be labeled, so I moved on to things that *should not* be labeled.

- My houseplant: `EMOTIONALLY UNAVAILABLE`
- My bathroom mirror: `WORKS BEST BEFORE 8AM`
- The couch cushion: `THINKING SPOT - DO NOT DISTURB THE ARCHETYPE`
- My bed: `HEADQUARTERS (BAD CHOICES ANNEX)`

Then, at midnight, I did the unthinkable. I printed one for the label maker itself:

> "LABELED: THE LABELER. We are all just labels labeling labels now." — me, at 12:07 a.m., alone in a kitchen

I labeled the back of my hand, just to test the adhesive:

`TODO: BE A BETTER PERSON (see: drawer labeled IMPORTANT-ish)`

```js
if (object.exists && labelMaker.hasTape) {
  labelMaker.print(object.name + " (" + object.flaw + ")");
}
```

## The Intervention

Saturday morning, my friend came over, looked around, and said: *"It looks like a crime scene for someone who alphabetizes their regrets."*

She had a point. There was a label on the Wi-Fi router that just said `TRUST ISSUES (buffering)`. One on the thermostat: `I CONTROL THE WEATHER IN THIS APARTMENT AND I'M DRUNK WITH POWER`. One stuck to my forehead that I don't remember applying: `IN PROGRESS`.

**I peeled most of them off.** The apartment is healing. But I kept one, tucked inside the junk drawer:

> `ORGANIZED-ish`
