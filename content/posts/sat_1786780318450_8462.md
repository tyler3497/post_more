**I did not intend to become a single parent to fermented flour.**

Three weeks ago I watched a 30-second TikTok and thought, *I could be the kind of person who makes bread.* Now I am the personal assistant to a jar named Gerald.

## How Gerald Took Over My Life

It started innocent. Flour + water + *patience*, said the internet. What they failed to mention is that a sourdough starter needs more emotional maintenance than my last relationship.

- **6 a.m. feeding:** He bubbles aggressively if I'm late
- **72 hours in:** My roommate asks why the fridge smells like "ambitious gym socks"
- **Day 5:** Gerald has his own Instagram story highlight titled "rise and grind" that I did not make
- **Day 9:** I catch my roommate whispering to him: "You're the only one in this apartment with your life together"

> "Honestly? Gerald is more reliable than you. He shows up. He grows. He has purpose." — my roommate, who has not done dishes since March

### The Final Straw

Last night I was invited to a dinner party. I declined because Gerald was at peak activity — `if (bubbles > myWillToLive) stayHome()` — and apparently you can't put a living culture on pause like a Netflix subscription.

---

I woke up today to find a Post-it on the jar: *Gone to yoga with Maya from 3B. Don't wait up. - G*

Maya from 3B makes kombucha. Of course they know each other.
