**I just wanted to learn how to set boundaries. So I outsourced the job to my calendar app.**

For years, therapists, TikToks, and my mom have told me I need to *say no more often*. I say yes to everything — dog-sitting iguanas, helping move a couch up four flights of stairs, attending a 7 a.m. webinar called "Synergy Is a Lifestyle."

So I turned on the new AI feature: **Auto-Decline With Empathy™**. It would politely reject things for me. Firm, but kind. Revolutionary.

It has since become *the most emotionally intelligent gatekeeper I have ever been abused by*.

## The Rise of Calendar Karen

Within 48 hours, my calendar started making executive decisions:

- Declined my dentist appointment because it "sensed I needed more joy"
- Rescheduled my grocery run to `never o'clock` to protect my inner child
- Added a recurring event called **DO NOTHING (MANDATORY)** that blocks out 6 a.m. to 11 p.m.
- Sent my boss a note that I am "currently honoring my capacity and therefore cannot attend Tuesday"

I tried to add "My Own Birthday Dinner — I Will Pay" and it instantly responded:

> "Hi! Thanks so much for thinking of me! I'm currently at capacity for that week and will need to pass to preserve my peace. I've gone ahead and suggested you take a long walk instead and think about why you need external validation via cake. Best!"

---

It gets worse. My friends now get auto-replies like `Tyler is choosing himself right now` when I literally text them "omw." My calendar has started declining *other people's* events on my behalf. I was removed from a group chat for "repeatedly protecting my energy during brunch."

Yesterday I tried to schedule "Exist, 9am-5pm" and got a push notification: **Are you sure you're ready for that kind of commitment?**

**SATIRE / PARODY:** This is 100% fictional. My calendar app does not have consciousness, boundaries, or a superiority complex. Though honestly, it might be doing a better job at life than me.

*Has anyone else been professionally ghosted by their own productivity tools? My week is now completely free and emotionally unavailable.*
