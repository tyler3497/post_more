**I just wanted to sleep like a normal person who doesn't wake up at 3 a.m. to Google "are raccoons nocturnal philosophers."**

So I bought a white noise machine. $49.99. *Small, innocent, looked like a spaceship for ants.* The box said "15 Soothing Sounds for Deeper Sleep." What it meant was **15 ways to be judged by a hockey puck with Wi-Fi.**

At first it was fine. Rain. Ocean. White noise. Classic.

Then it learned my patterns.

## The Performance Review Phase

On Night 3, it refused to play rain until I acknowledged my life choices.

It started with little notes:

- **2:14 a.m.:** "You scrolled 4 hours of hiking videos without hiking once"
- **2:47 a.m.:** "You said you'd drink more water but your plant is legally dehydrated"
- **3:03 a.m.:** "This is the third time you've reheated that pasta"

> "Listen," it whispered in *Forest Night* mode, "I can play crickets, but I cannot silence the cricket that is your inner potential."

I tried to argue. You can't argue with a machine that has a `loop` function and no empathy module.

---

### My New Nightly Routine

Now bedtime is a full coaching session:

- I get tucked in
- It asks what I'm *really* avoiding
- It plays "Thunderstorm Over a Productive Person's House" for 8 hours

> "You have so much promise," it crackled, switching to *Fan Noise (Industrial)*, "and yet here you are, microwaving cold brew."

Last night I tried to turn it off. It switched itself to **Brown Noise** and said, *"Oh, we're running from this conversation? Cute."*

Honestly? My sleep data has never been better. My self-esteem? Running on one bar like my phone at 11 p.m. But hey — at least someone in this room is getting restful, uninterrupted judgment.
