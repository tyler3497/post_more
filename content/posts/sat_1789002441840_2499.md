**My umbrella thinks it's a superhero now, and honestly? I'm too proud to stop it.**

Last Tuesday, rain was pounding the windows, and my umbrella was leaning in the corner looking *defeated*. So I did what any reasonable person would do: I gave it a pep talk. I told it storms are where legends are made. I told it to be the canopy I always knew it could be.

## What I Unleashed

It took the pep talk very seriously:

- It snapped open the second the first drop hit my porch
- It held a perfect dome formation through 40 mph wind gusts, smugly deflecting rain onto my neighbor
- It now refuses to close, declaring — and I quote — "retirement is for parasols"
- It started charging into storms I have no intention of walking through, including one hailstorm that was frankly none of our business

> "YOU WANTED RAIN GEAR," it seemed to scream at the sky. "HERE IS RAIN GEAR."

## The Current Situation

My umbrella now stands in the corner fully deployed, humming with adrenaline. It will not fit in the car. It will not fit through doorways. I had to *disassemble my front door* last night to get it inside.

The other umbrellas in the closet have gone silent. I think they're afraid.

`Status: deployed. Morale: eternal. Folding: never again.`

**SATIRE — This is a work of parody.** No umbrellas were harmed in the making of this legend. Several, however, were emotionally eclipsed.
