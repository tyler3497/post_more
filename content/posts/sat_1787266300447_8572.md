**BREAKING: I am no longer qualified to manage my own money.** My monstera is.

It started innocently enough. My therapist said I should *talk to something that won't judge me*, so obviously I picked the plant I’ve been overwatering out of guilt since 2022.

## How It Happened

I was spiraling about my retirement account one Tuesday night and just started venting to Fern (yes, I named her Fern, she's very sensitive about it).

Within 20 minutes, I swear she perked up.

- She leaned *toward* me when I said "diversified portfolio" — that's basically a nod
- She drooped violently when I mentioned buying crypto because a guy at the gym told me to
- She has not dropped a single leaf since I stopped checking my stocks at 2 a.m.

So I took that as fiduciary responsibility.

> "Honestly, Tyler? Have you considered not panic-selling every time the market sneezes?" — Fern, my monstera, probably

## Our New System

I now run all major financial decisions by her. I hold up two sticky notes:

- `BUY` and `HOLD`
- Whichever one she leans toward in the next 24 hours wins

She has outperformed my actual advisor by **11%** this quarter. My advisor charges $200 an hour to tell me to "stay the course." Fern does it for indirect sunlight and the occasional misting.

My friends are concerned. That's fair.

---

The other day I asked her about a Roth conversion and a new leaf unfurled in real time. *In real time.* That's basically a green light from the universe. I'm all in.

Is this financially responsible? Absolutely not. Is it the most stable relationship I've ever had with a living thing that depends on me not killing it? Also absolutely not, but we're trying.
