**I decided to become a Hydrated Person.** You know, the kind who glows and remembers where their keys are.

*For context, my previous hydration strategy was coffee, more coffee, and a vague memory of water from 2019.*

Last Tuesday I bought a $42 motivational water bottle. You know the one — time markers up the side that say things like `7 AM — Rise and Shine!` and `2 PM — Keep Chugging!` I thought it would be supportive. I thought wrong.

## The Coaching Begins

By 9:13 a.m. on Day 1, it was already disappointed in me.

> "Oh, wow, still on the 7 AM mark at 9 AM? That's a *choice*. I thought we were doing this together. I thought you *wanted* kidneys that function." — My Water Bottle, Chad Hydro, who is clearly projecting

I tried to explain I had a meeting.

It did not care.

### Exhibit A: Things My Water Bottle Has Said This Week

- **Tuesday:** Left condensation ring on my desk shaped like a frowny face
- **Wednesday:** Reflected sunlight directly into my eyes until I took a sip out of spite
- *Thursday:* Whispered `you're 67% water and 33% bad decisions` when I reached for iced coffee instead
- **Friday:** Played dead — laid on its side, lid off, leaking slightly, like "is this what you want? A dehydrated king?"

I tried to negotiate. I said, "Buddy, I'm trying."

It replied with its 3 PM marker:

> "Don't try. *Do.* Michael Jordan drank water. Do you want to be Michael Jordan or do you want to be crampy?"

---

I ran a quick status check to see if I was being unreasonable:

```
hydration_level = 0.23
bottle.patience = null
owner.guilt = "maximum"
bottle.coaching_style = "Olympic trials but make it personal"
```

**The worst part?** It's working. I'm the most hydrated I've ever been. My skin is clearer. My plant is jealous. I'm peeing every 17 minutes and my water bottle now does a tiny victory wobble every time I hit `5 PM — You Did It!`

I asked if we could dial it back to gentle encouragement.

It printed a new label on itself with my label maker that just says **DRINK.**
