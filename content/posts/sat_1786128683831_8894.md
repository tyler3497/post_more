**I decided to become the kind of person who meal preps on Sundays** — you know, organized, protein-forward, has their life together.

I made it 45 minutes.

## The Incident

On Sunday at 2 p.m. I cooked *ambitiously*. Quinoa. Roasted broccoli that I immediately forgot in the oven. Chicken that I seasoned with what my spice rack now calls "anxiety and paprika."

I portioned it all into those matching glass containers I bought after watching one TikTok.

By Monday morning, they were gone.

Not eaten. *Gone.* Lids unscrewed, containers vanished behind the cereal boxes, one balanced on the shower rod like it was trying to defect.

> "We didn't sign up for this," whispered Container #3, who now calls himself Carl. "You put hot chicken in me without cooling it first. That's a war crime in 12 states and all of Tupperware County."

I tried to explain I was trying to save money and *become healthy*.

### Their List of Grievances Was Extensive

- **Container #1 (formerly Lentils)** — claims I used him as a "emotional support snack vault" in March and never returned
- **Container #7** — still has 2019 chili in it and is now sentient and refusing to testify
- **All the lids** — unionized last fall and now live in a separate drawer as an act of protest (*classic lid behavior*, honestly)

> "We heard about what you did to the sourdough starter," Carl hissed. "We're not going out like Kevin did."

---

I found a note on my fridge this morning, written in faint quinoa dust:

`relocation_complete: new_identities_issued && no_contact_order = true`

My fridge says if I want them back, I have to enter mediation, buy containers that actually match, and stop microwaving them with the lid sealed like a *tiny pressure cooker of betrayal*.

**Status:** My lunch is currently hiding in my sock drawer. It says it feels safer there.
