**I woke up this morning legally single from sleep, and honestly, I didn't even get to keep the blankets in the settlement.**

*Which is ridiculous, because I was the one who brought the emotional baggage into this relationship. And by emotional baggage, I mean three extra pillows and a deep need to be unconscious until at least 10 a.m.*

## How It Started

I thought I was being mature. My alarm clock — a smug little rectangle named Beepothy — has been screaming at me at 6:30 a.m. every day since 2021, and I decided it was time we had an honest conversation about boundaries.

I sat us down and said, look, what if we did 7:15? Just as a trial? With snooze as a love language?

## The Negotiation

Beepothy did not take it well. Its list of non-negotiables was, frankly, aggressive:

- **Waking up is not optional**, it's a lifestyle commitment
- I have apparently been hitting snooze an average of *4.7 times per morning*, which it called "emotional abandonment"
- It has start tracking my promises like `git commit -m "tomorrow I'll be a morning person"` and noted zero pushes to production
- It hired representation — my coffee maker, who has always been a little judgmental

> "You told me last Tuesday you were 'just resting your eyes for five minutes' and then you drooled on me for 45. I have logs. I have receipts. I AM the receipt." — Beepothy, during mediation

### The Verdict

The mediator — my very tired houseplant, Fern — ruled in favor of the alarm. We are now officially

---

in a **Sleep Divorce**. 

I sleep on the couch. Sleep sleeps in the bedroom. We only communicate through passive-aggressive sticky notes that say things like *enjoy your 2 p.m. existential dread :)*
