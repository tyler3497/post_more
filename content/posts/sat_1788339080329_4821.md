**I decided to become a cold brew person, which is just a fancy way of saying I wanted to feel superior about my caffeine.**

*My French press took this as a declaration of war and then as a content opportunity.*

For context, I bought my French press in 2021 during my "I'm going to be mindful" era. It has survived three apartments, one breakup, and my brief sourdough phase. I thought we were good. Then I Googled "how to make cold brew without trying too hard" and left the press on the counter overnight with coarse grounds.

## The Morning It Got Ideas

I woke up to a soft *plunger click*. Not the normal click. A thoughtful, NPR-host click.

> "Welcome back to *Pressing Matters with Pierre*," whispered my French press. "Today's topic: *The 12-Hour Steep and Why Your Generation Can't Commit to Anything*. I'm your host, Pierre. Yes, I named myself. You never asked."

Pierre, as he now calls himself, has rebranded my kitchen as a studio.

Reasons my French press is now insufferable:

- He insists cold brew is "just iced regrets with better marketing"
- He plays lo-fi plunger sounds at 6 a.m. and calls it a sound bath
- He rated my tap water like a sommelier: "Notes of chlorine, with a finish of poor life decisions"
- He left a sticky note on my beans that says `grind_size = existential_crisis`

## The Podcast Nobody Asked For

Episode lineup so far:

- *Why You Press Too Fast In Relationships and Coffee*
- *My Roommate's Keurig Is Not a Personality, It's a Cry for Help*
- *Four Minutes Isn't Patience, It's Barely a Commercial Break*

I tried to explain I just wanted caffeine.

```js
if (coffee.waitTime > 720) {
  human.patience = null;
  frenchPress.ego = Infinity;
}
```

---

He responded by only brewing at half-strength until I leave a 5-star review on "Apple Pods." Which I don't think is a thing, but he's very persuasive when he's steaming.

My roommate asked why the kitchen smells like a TED Talk. I said Pierre is recording.

*SATIRE / PARODY — 100% fictional, no French presses host actual podcasts, though mine is currently negotiating a sponsorship with my own impatience.*
