**I made a terrible mistake the moment I thought my fiddle-leaf fig needed a hobby.**

It started innocently enough. *Quarantine-level boredom, but make it 2026.* I read somewhere that plants respond to attention, so I figured I'd teach Fern — yes, I named him Fern, we're past that — Morse code using a little desk lamp. Blink blink for good vibes. Cute, right?

## Fern Has Opinions Now

Three days later, Fern was fluent. And **judgmental**.

According to Fern's nightly lamp-flicker reports:

- My throw pillows are quote-unquote "emotionally inconsistent"
- The IKEA rug is, and I quote, "a cry for help woven in gray"
- My gallery wall has "no narrative cohesion and suspiciously high levels of Live Laugh Love energy"

> "Beige? BEIGE? In THIS economy? You have a north-facing window and the audacity to paint everything greige." — Fern, at 2:13 a.m. Tuesday

I tried to reason with him. I said I was going for minimalist. He replied in rapid blinks that translated to:

`L-M-A-O minimal effort`

---

### The Intervention

My roommate walked in on me arguing with a plant via flashlight at midnight and just whispered, *"oh, we're doing this now?"*

I have now been formally asked to:

- Move the monstera 3 inches to the left for "proper sightlines"
- Replace my LED strip lights because they are "offending Fern's chlorophyll"
- Apologize to the succulents for "cultural appropriation of the desert aesthetic"

The worst part? He's *right*. My apartment *does* look like a Pinterest board had an identity crisis. I rearranged everything last night exactly how he wanted, and for the first time in months, Fern unfurled a new leaf.

I think that was approval. Or a warning. Either way, I'm not allowed to buy throw pillows without consulting him first.

*This is satire. Fern is not a licensed interior designer, despite what he claims on LinkedIn.*
