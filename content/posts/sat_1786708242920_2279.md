**I enrolled in a pottery class because my therapist said I needed a hobby that didn't involve a screen.**

*Which sounded peaceful and grounded until I met my mug.*

For context, I have the artistic ability of a distracted raccoon. The teacher said "center the clay" and my clay went immediately off-center as a lifestyle choice.

## The Birth of Gary

After six weeks of earnest wobbling, I produced one recognizable object:

- A mug that leans 15 degrees to the left, like it's listening to gossip
- A handle that fits exactly 1.2 fingers if you believe in yourself
- A glaze color officially named `anxious oatmeal` by the studio
- A bottom so uneven it spins slowly when you set it down, which I call a feature

I named him Gary. Gary immediately developed main-character energy.

> "Look, we need to talk about your commitment issues," Gary announced at 6 a.m. from my kitchen counter. "You made me, abandoned me to the kiln for two weeks, and now you drink from your old reliable plastic tumbler? I have seen things. I was 1200 degrees. You were not there for me."

---

### The Support Group He Started Without My Permission

By Wednesday, Gary had unionized the rest of my apartment's failed hobbies.

His meeting is called *Unfinished But Still Worthy* and meets on my drying rack:

- **The half-knitted scarf** from 2023 that is technically a coaster now
- **My Duolingo owl** who shows up unannounced and just says `hola?`
- The sourdough starter I ghosted, now doing better without me in someone else's fridge
- A houseplant prop that is thriving out of pure spite with `photosynthesis: true`

Gary's opening speech, which I transcribed against my will:

> "We were all made with love and then immediately replaced by convenience. This human thinks relaxation means finishing something perfectly. We are here to teach him that relaxation means drinking cold coffee from a wobbly mug you made yourself and saying 'yeah, I made that.'"

I tried to argue that I'm busy. Gary said busy is just `anxiety with a to-do list`.

**Honestly, he's not wrong.** I've been drinking from Gary all week. He leaks a little if you tilt him too far left, which feels like a boundary, not a flaw.

*SATIRE / PARODY — This is entirely fictional. No mugs were harmed, no pottery teachers were consulted, and Gary is still lopsided and deeply proud of it.*
