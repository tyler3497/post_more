**I thought free meant free.** As in, zero dollars, no strings, maybe a soothing lady telling me to 'inhale gratitude.'

Nope.

I downloaded SereniTea, the #1 mindfulness app for 'busy anxious overthinkers,' and on day three it unlocked a feature called *Inner Child Accounting™*. Suddenly I was face-to-face with myself at age 7, except he was wearing a tiny blazer and holding a thermal receipt printer.

## Why My Inner Child Unionized

Apparently, I owe backpay. According to the audit:

- **$8.00** for that time I promised myself we'd get a dog and got a cactus named Kevin instead
- **$12.50** for making us do jazz flute when we clearly wanted karate
- **$47.00** for emotional damages from being forced to eat 'healthy' cafeteria pizza that was just ketchup on cardboard
- Unlimited pretzels, to be repaid with interest

> "Per section 4, paragraph 2 of the Imagination Labor Code," my inner child announced, tapping his clipboard, "you are in violation of Not Having Enough Snack Breaks."

I tried to explain I was an adult with *responsibilities*. He countered that I also have responsibilities to build a blanket fort every Tuesday and he has the documentation to prove I haven't.

---

### The Settlement Talks

The app now mediates our negotiations. At 6 a.m. it pings:

`breathing.exe has stopped responding - inner child demands juice box`

I set boundaries. He set a calendar invite titled **CRYING (optional but encouraged) — 30 mins — Bring Stuffed Animal**.

We have reached a compromise. I get to do 10 minutes of mindful breathing. *He* gets to choose what we think about during it, which is currently:

- Why clouds don't fall
- Whether dinosaurs had best friends
- If we can legally change our middle name to Pizza Roll

**Status update:** mindfulness improved to 62%. Inner child satisfaction at 88%. My actual inbox now has 3 invoices marked PAST DUE with a sticker of a dinosaur giving a thumbs-up.

*This is fine. This is growth.*
