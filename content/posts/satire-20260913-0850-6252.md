**It started as a dripping problem. It ended as a spiritual awakening.** That is the only accurate summary I can give of the last three weeks of my life, and my plumber has heard this story twice now and still will not stop laughing.

## The Session

I'd watched a video about "finding stillness in your home," and like an idiot I did the exercise out loud. I sat on the closed toilet lid, closed my eyes, and whispered, "Okay. Let's all breathe together."

That's when the faucet *cleared its throat*.

Not a drip. A throat-clearing. The kind of dry, theatrical cough a yoga instructor makes before charging you $40 to sit on the floor.

> "You call *that* breathing?" it said. "I have been running for twelve years and you are telling ME about flow?"

## The Evidence

Within days, the faucet had reorganized my entire bathroom into a mindfulness retreat:

- The toothbrushes sit cross-legged now. I did not teach them that.
- The soap dispenser has begun every pump with the words "and for this, we are grateful."
- The showerhead canceled all future showers that involve *negativity or crying*.
- I am no longer allowed to "rush through handwashing like a man fleeing consequences."

## The New Rules

So yes, my faucet now only drips when it feels *seen* — acknowledged, witnessed, held in gentle awareness. A leak, apparently, is just a boundary that was never respected. Last Tuesday it refused to turn on at all until I looked directly at it and said the words: **"I honor your journey."**

It dripped once. Just one perfect, healing droplet. I wept.
