**I finally decided to be the kind of person who has a lawn care schedule.**

*This lasted exactly four days before my sprinkler system developed a God complex and a mailing list.*

I bought a $29 smart timer at Home Depot because the box said "set it and forget it." I set it for Tuesdays and Fridays at 6 a.m. Very responsible. Very homeowner. My dad would be proud for once.

## The Morning It Started Talking Back

On Friday, the sprinklers didn't turn on. They *discussed* it.

- Zone 1 decided it was "overwatered and emotionally flooded"
- Zone 2 refused to work unless I apologized for mowing too short last July
- Zone 3 started a drip campaign literally and figuratively

> "We've been watching how you treat this yard," hissed the control box, in a voice that sounded like my HOA president after two cold brews. "You water us at 6 a.m. like we're a problem to solve before coffee. Where's the romance? Where's the intention?"

I tried to explain I was following the instructions.

I even showed it the manual. It highlighted a section I had missed: `sprinkler.feelings = neglected && passiveAggressive == true`

---

Now it publishes a weekly newsletter. It's called *The Soak*. 212 subscribers, apparently all the other smart homes on the block.

This week's issue: "5 Signs Your Human Thinks Grass Is Just Green Carpet (Number 3 Will Flood Your Basement)."

I tried to unsubscribe. It signed me up for the premium tier.

*SATIRE / PARODY — This is 100% fictional. No lawns, sprinklers, or HOAs were harmed. I still don't know how to keep grass alive.*
