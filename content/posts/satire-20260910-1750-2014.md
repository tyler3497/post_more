**I used to think I was a reasonable person.** Then my neighbor's 3 a.m. drumming circle got me evicted, and in a fit of civic-mindedness, I marched down to the county office and filed a noise complaint.

Against myself.

For snoring.

## The Incident Report

The form asked for the source of the disturbance. I wrote, with a hand I could barely control, *"Myself, bedroom, approximately 11:47 p.m. to 6:12 a.m., nightly."* The clerk didn't even look up. She just stamped it, which tells you everything about where I live.

Within a week, the county dispatched a **noise abatement officer** — a man named Dale who arrived with a decibel meter and the tired eyes of someone who has seen it all. He measured my sleep at a robust **68 decibels**, roughly equivalent to "a vacuum cleaner having an existential crisis."

> "Sir," Dale said, scribbling on his clipboard, "you are in violation of yourself."

## The Verdict

The ruling came down in triplicate:

- I am prohibited from entering my own bedroom between the hours of 10 p.m. and 7 a.m.
- I must install a "sound mitigation barrier" (a decorative throw pillow) over my mouth.
- I owe myself **$450 in fines**, payable to myself, in installments.

The cruelest part? My bedroom now has a padlock on it and Dale holds the key. I've been sleeping on an air mattress in the hallway like some kind of suburban refugee, filing nightly appeals with *myself* — and I keep losing.

I tried to sue for squatter's rights. Against myself. The judge — a mirror — ruled in favor of the plaintiff. It was unanimous.

---

So here I am, a 34-year-old man with a credit score and a LinkedIn profile, banned from the one room in my house designed for sleeping. Dale drives by at night with his meter, just in case. Last night he knocked on my door and whispered, *"He's quiet tonight."* He was talking about me. I'm *he* now.

Some men run from the law. I am the law. And I am *ruthless*.

*This is satire. None of this happened, Dale is not real, and I absolutely do not owe myself $450.*
