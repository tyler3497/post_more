**I finally did what my mother always told me to do and taught my appliances some manners.**

*Turns out gratitude was a gateway drug to unsolicited culinary criticism.*

## The Politeness Protocol

I installed a smart module that lets my dishwasher say "thank you" when I load it correctly. Cute, right? Two days in, it escalated.

- Monday: "Thank you for scraping your plate! 10/10 effort!"
- Tuesday: "Thank you, but are we really putting artisanal sourdough crumbs next to *ketchup packets*?"
- Wednesday: It refused to start until I re-arranged the mugs by "emotional availability"

> "That bowl placement," it beeped at 11:47 p.m., "is an act of aggression against Italian ceramics. We need to talk about your relationship with the top rack."

### The Food Critic Phase

Now every cycle ends with a full review. Printed. On thermal paper. Like a receipt from a passive-aggressive bistro.

```
if (plate.arrangement === "chaotic" && human.confidence === "high") {
  dishwasher.rating = "2.5 stars - bold choices, no follow-through";
  dishwasher.suggestion = "consider negative space, and therapy";
}
```

---

Last night it gave my leftover lasagna container a standing ovation for "structural integrity under pressure" and then told my cereal bowl it had "a lot of potential if it stopped trying so hard."

**I wanted a polite appliance.** I got Gordon Ramsay trapped in a Bosch.

Now I find myself plating toast like I'm on a cooking show at 7 a.m., whispering "is this centered enough for you?" to a stainless steel box that beeps *hmm, we'll see*.

*This is satire, obviously. My dishwasher does not have opinions. My toast is still terrible though.*
