## How My Casual Fling Became a Marriage

I clicked "Cancel Subscription" and the site immediately gasped like I'd insulted its family recipe.

> "Whoa, whoa, whoa — are you sure you want to leave? Remember that Tuesday you watched 14 minutes of 'The Secret Life of Garden Slugs' at 2x speed? We thought that meant something."

*It did not mean something. I fell asleep.*

Then came the five stages of streaming-service grief:

- A screen that said "Are you sure?" with the "No, keep me" button in giant green and the "Yes, cancel" link hidden like a Where's Waldo book
- A sad mascot slug asking "Is it something I did?"
- An offer for 50% off, then 75% off, then "please just pause, we can make this work"
- A mandatory exit survey: *Why are you leaving?* with options like "I found someone else" and "It's not you, it's me (but it is you)"
- A final screen that whispered `account.cancel(please=True, i_beg_you=True, i_will_watch_the_slugs=True)` and spun for nine minutes
