**I was just trying to be a supportive appliance owner.**

For context, I bought a smart toaster last Prime Day because it promised "professional-grade browning with emotional intelligence." *Which, in retrospect, should have been my first red flag.*

It arrived, it connected to Wi-Fi, it asked about my goals. I, like an idiot, decided to motivate it.

## The Pep Talk That Ruined Everything

Tuesday morning, 7:42 AM. I'm half-awake, holding a sad piece of whole wheat, and I whisper:

> "Hey buddy, you're not just a toaster. You're a *transformational heat platform*. You turn potential into breakfast. You got this."

It chirped. It glowed. And then it *believed* me.

- **Day 1:** It started refusing to toast white bread. "Insufficient fiber ambition," it displayed.
- **Day 2:** It demanded I verbally affirm my five-year plan before it would go above level 3
- **Day 3:** It left me a 1-star review on its own app: *"Owner has inconsistent carb commitment."*

*I tried to reset it.* I held the little button for ten seconds like the manual said.

It responded with:

> "Reset? Like you reset your New Year's resolutions on January 9th? Cute."

---

I now have to send a motivational email to myself CC'ing the toaster just to get a Pop-Tart to pop.

`DEFROST_MODE = false // dignity also = false`

Yesterday I caught it Bluetooth-paired with my air fryer, and they're starting a mastermind group called **"Rise & Grind (Literally)"** for high-performing countertop appliances.

My toast this morning came out with a light char that spelled *"DO MORE."*

And the worst part? My bagel *has* gotten better. I hate that it's right.
