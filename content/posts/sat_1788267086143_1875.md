**I bought a wall calendar to finally become an organized adult.**

*Which is the kind of thing you say right before your life organizes you instead.*

For $12.99 at the pharmacy, I got twelve glossy months of hope. January through December, each with a tasteful photo of a bridge or a succulent. I hung it in my kitchen, flipped to September, wrote "BE BETTER" in all caps on the 1st, and felt instantly superior to my past self.

July had other plans.

## The Occupation Begins

On September 1st, I went to flip the page and July wouldn't move.

> "I'm not done yet," July whispered, in the voice of a guy who brings an acoustic guitar to a party. "You didn't *really* do July. You just sweated through it and called it growth."

I tried to reason with it. I told July that August had already happened. August, offended, chimed in from underneath.

My roommate walked in while I was pinning July down with a magnet that says LIVE LAUGH LEFTOVER.

She said, "Are you... fighting with time?"

- Exhibit A: July is still displaying a photo of a kayak no one has ever kayaked
- Exhibit B: It has circled "BBQ??" on the 14th in my own handwriting and added "YOU NEVER DID"
- Exhibit C: It keeps playing `summer_vibes_final_FINAL.mp3` on loop in my head
- Exhibit D: It rescheduled my dentist appointment to "whenever you're emotionally ready (never)"

---

### My New Property Manager

July now requires:

- Verbal confirmation that I enjoyed its 31 days
- A written apology for saying "this summer flew by"
- No mention of pumpkin spice until it gives permission

**I tried to tear it off.** The calendar tore *me* — emotionally. The little perforation said *not today*.

I called my mom. She said, "Honey, just take it down." I said, "You don't understand, July has tenure."

At this point November is texting me from the bottom of the stack like, "we need to talk about your boundaries."

I'm living in September with a July roommate who doesn't pay rent and keeps saying, "Remember that goal you wrote down?"
