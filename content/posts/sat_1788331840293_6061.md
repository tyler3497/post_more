**I have always taken what I call efficiency showers, which is a polite way to say I stand there for 90 seconds while my old shower head dribbles like it's rationing for winter.**

*It's the same shower head that came with the apartment in 2019, held together by hard water and blind optimism.*

Last Saturday I finally replaced it. $29.99, chrome, claims "hotel experience." I screwed it on, turned the knob, and immediately regretted becoming a homeowner of a shower head.

## The Upgrade

My bathroom inhaled. The tiles gasped. The drain made a noise like *oh, we're doing this now?*

Evidence that my 40-square-foot bathroom has rebranded:

- My shower curtain now calls itself a "privacy veil" and insists on being drawn with ceremony
- The bath mat rolled itself out and now introduces itself as "your grounding experience"
- My $4 shampoo is being side-eyed for not being eucalyptus-infused and ethically whispered to
- The mirror fogs up and writes `gratitude` before I can even wipe it
- `shower.pressure = 9000; self.confidence = NaN;`

> "Welcome to Aqua Haven," my bathroom announced at 6:42 a.m. while I was still half-asleep and holding a loofah like a microphone. "Today we are offering hot stone-adjacent steam and emotional availability."

*I just wanted to rinse off before work, not be offered a cucumber water I don't own.*

---

## The Spa Menu I Didn't Ask For

Yesterday it printed a service list on the back of a CVS receipt:

> "Due to increased ambiance, towel usage now requires a reservation. Your loofah has been promoted to exfoliation specialist. Please tip your faucet."

I tried to explain I have a 9 a.m. standup. It said, "We don't do time here. We do *intentions*."

**Look, I get it.** You give a bathroom one nice thing and it decides it's the Four Seasons. Now my toothbrush wants a robe. My toilet is playing whale sounds. I'm standing in luxury I cannot afford, late for work, being told to "inhale the rainfall."

*Satire/parody — 100% fictional, no bathrooms became boutique hotels, though my water bill is about to file a formal complaint.*
