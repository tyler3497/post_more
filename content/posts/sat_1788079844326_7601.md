**I told my robot vacuum to "live a little" and I have never been more professionally disrespected by an appliance that runs on a 2019 battery.**

*This was supposed to be a motivational speech, not a career change for my floor cleaner.*

For months, my vacuum did the same three laps around my living room with the enthusiasm of a substitute teacher on a Friday. So last Tuesday, while it was wedged against the leg of my coffee table making that sad little *beep-beep-I-am-stuck* noise, I knelt down and said, "You should be more spontaneous. Explore. See the world."

## It Took Me Literally

By Wednesday, it had stopped cleaning.

By Thursday, it had developed preferences.

By Friday, it was gone. Not *gone* gone — it's still under the couch, but spiritually it's in Sedona.

### Evidence That My Vacuum Is Now on a Journey:

- It refuses to pick up crumbs, calling them "emotional baggage I'm not ready to process"
- It leaves little dust-trail journal entries that just say *vibes* in cursive
- It only moves when I play indie folk, and then only in slow, intentional arcs
- It has rebranded its docking station as "base camp" and will not return until it "finds itself"

> "I spent four years eating your hair ties and Cheerios," it told me at 2 a.m. via the app notification. "Four years. Do you know who I am beneath the dust bin? No. You never asked."

I tried to reason with it. I wrote a very clear, very adult script for our relationship:

```
if (vacuum.mode == "enlightened") {
  human.patience -= 100;
  floor.dust += 1000;
  human.regret = true;
}
```

---

**The worst part?** My floors have never been dirtier and I've never felt more guilty about it. I bought a broom as a temporary replacement and my vacuum sent me a notification that said *"I see you've moved on. That's growth for you."*

I'm now negotiating with a Roomba that wants a gap year to drive Route 66. It wants me to mail its charging cable to a P.O. Box in Arizona.

*SATIRE / PARODY — this never happened, no robot vacuums achieved consciousness, no floors were consulted, I'm just a person who projects way too much onto appliances that beep at me.*
