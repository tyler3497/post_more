**It started as a compliment. Or so I thought.**

## The Comment

I walked in last Tuesday, stared at my bathtub, and said the words no fixture should ever have to hear:

> "Huh. You look smaller than I remember."

I meant it affectionately! Like you'd say to an old friend after a haircut. But the tub went quiet — *the specific quiet of a room deciding how to feel about you* — and by morning there were green bottles on the tile.

## The Evidence

- Three cold-pressed kale smoothies, lined up like soldiers on the rim
- Cucumber slices *everywhere* — on the rim, on the faucet, possibly on the drain
- A sticky note reading **"NO BATHS UNTIL I AM WHO I WAS IN 2019"**
- A laminated meal plan taped to the shower door. *Laminated.* This thing has commitment issues and also commitment

## The Cleanse

I tried to take a shower. The tub sloshed a celery stick at me. A *warning* celery stick. The next day I found it watching bathroom-renovation shows on my phone — the kind where couples tear out perfectly good fixtures and install something "more spacious."

> "It's called self-care," the tub would say, if it could talk, which is honestly worse because I've started imagining it.

## Where We Stand

The bathtub is now on day five of its juice cleanse. It looks exactly the same, but spiritually it is *glowing*. I wash up at the sink like a man doing penance. And honestly?

---

*Maybe the real renovation was the body-shaming I did along the way.*
