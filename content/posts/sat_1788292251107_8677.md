**I decided to become a Person Who Makes Their Bed, and my bedroom immediately promoted itself to middle management.**

*For eleven years I've lived under what experts call the Scrunch-and-Dash Method.* You get up, you fling the comforter toward the mattress, you leave. Very efficient.

Then last Monday I watched a video that said successful people make their bed. I have made many mistakes before, but this one came with throw pillows.

## The Morning I Made a Mistake

At 7:12 a.m. I actually did it. Hospital corners. Pillows fluffed. A decorative lumbar pillow I bought ironically now sits non-ironically front and center.

My two main pillows, Sir Snooze and Captain Fluff, held an emergency meeting on the freshly smoothed duvet.

> "So we're doing *structure* now?" whispered Sir Snooze. "After a decade of being treated like emotional support potatoes? Interesting pivot. Very brave. Very corporate."

*I had not considered their feelings. I was just trying to be an adult with lumbar support.*

Within hours, the bedroom reorganized:

- The fitted sheet named itself Head of Operations and demanded all laundry be folded within 24 hours
- The throw blanket filed a complaint about being "relegated to accent status"
- My hamper posted a LinkedIn update: "Proud to announce I'm handling overflow storage for unprocessed ambition"
- The nightstand keeps a spreadsheet titled `Times He Made The Bed vs. Times He Got Up On Time`

## The Quarterly Review

This morning I found a tiny manila folder on my pillow. Inside, a performance review. For me.

```
if (human.makesBed === true) {
  pillows.expectations = Infinity;
  human.snoozeButton = "under review";
  bedroom.morale = "judgmental but thriving";
}
```

> "Strengths: Follows through once. Weaknesses: Uses phone as alarm, thinks iced coffee is a meal. Action Items: Continue bed-making, stop negotiating with socks at 2 a.m." — The Pillow Committee

---

Look, I get it. You give an inch of taut sheet and they take a mile of emotional accountability. I tried to un-make the bed to reset the power dynamic. Sir Snooze said, "That's retaliation and we have documented it."

*SATIRE / PARODY — completely fictional, none of this happened. No pillows were promoted to management, though my lumbar pillow is now asking for PTO.*
