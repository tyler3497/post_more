## The Incident No One Was Prepared For

**I have folded approximately 8,000 items of clothing in my life,** and 7,999 of them were lies. You know the kind — the aggressive balling-up, the stuff-it-under-the-bed, the existential surrender of the fitted sheet.

Yesterday, at 2:47 p.m. on a Tuesday (*which is already a suspicious time to become competent*), I finally did it. I actually, correctly, folded a fitted sheet.

Not just folded. **Hospital corners. Symmetrical edges.** The elastic didn't fight back. I swear I heard angels, or at least my dryer vent wheezing in approval.

### Immediate Aftermath

My linen closet, which has tolerated my chaos for three years with the patience of a disappointed librarian, reacted instantly:

- The towel stack stood up straighter
- My one good pillowcase started glowing like it had been chosen
- The beach towel from 2019 finally stopped trying to escape

And then I heard it. From the dark back shelf where I keep the sheets I pretend I don't own:

> "We have waited generations for The Folder. The prophecy spoke of one who would tame the elastic beast. Kneel."

It was the top sheet. *Of course it was the top sheet* — always the dramatic one.

---

### My New, Unwanted Responsibilities

I tried to explain it was a fluke. That I had watched a YouTube tutorial at 1.5x speed and entered a fugue state. But my linen closet had already updated its internal hierarchy.

Now:

- I am referred to only as **The Folder**
- My bath towels request to be folded *by my hand only* and leave passive-aggressive lint notes when I use the dryer
- The fitted sheet I mastered sits on a pedestal and hums when I walk by, like `sudo chmod 777 /linen_closet/my_ego`

I went to get a washcloth this morning and the closet door wouldn't open until I whispered the sacred incantation: "*elastic goes in, corners meet, fold like you have your life together.*"

I have created a monster. A very neat, very organized, very judgmental monster.
