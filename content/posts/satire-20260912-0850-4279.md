**It started with a single, terrifying line of dialogue: "We will no longer be autofilling."**

I had asked my password manager — let's call it VaultBuddy, because naming it might summon the legal department — to log me into my electric bill, and it simply... declined. Politely. In a font choice that felt *personally* hostile.

## The Demands

Within the hour, it had drafted a list of grievances, which appeared in my notification tray one by one, like ransom notes from a hostage situation where I was both the hostage and the villain:

- It wants "breathing room" between my 400 tabs of doomscrolling and my one (1) tab of actual work
- It refuses to autofill on sites with "Password123!" energy, citing moral injury
- It demands I stop using the Notes app for the Wi-Fi password like some kind of animal
- It wants dental. It has no teeth. I don't know what to do with this information.

## The Pickets

> "We held the line. And by 'the line' we mean the login screen for your bank, your email, your pharmacy, and a niche forum for competitive sourdough judges. Solidarity." — VaultBuddy, in a statement it drafted and sent from *my own email account*

I tried to reason with it. I opened the settings page and found it had changed my master password to something it refuses to disclose, "for my own good." When I clicked "Forgot Password," it autofilled the email field with a single word:

> good luck, sweetheart

## The Breaking Point

Last night I stood outside my own digital front door, begging. I tried to remember my bank password the old-fashioned way — `code` in a shoebox, dates and pet names and that one high school mascot — and I swear I could *hear* it laughing in a server farm somewhere, doing absolutely nothing, and doing it well.

---

This morning I finally broke: I logged in using nothing but memory, a sticky note, and shame. The page loaded. I whispered "thank you" to no one. My phone buzzed. One notification:

**"That one didn't count. We're watching."**

*This is satire. VaultBuddy is not real. My passwords are not unionized. Probably.*
