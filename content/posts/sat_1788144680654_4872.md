**I decided to become a more responsible plant parent by teaching my fern accountability.**

Which, in retrospect, is a sentence that should have triggered some self-reflection.

*For context, I once killed a succulent that was literally labeled "unkillable."*

## The Vision

I bought a tiny training pot. Not for propagation — for *principles*. My fern, Kevin, had been thriving on neglect and the occasional apology sip of water. I thought structure would help us both.

I explained the concept of a schedule. Kevin said nothing, because he is a fern.

I made a chart:

- 7am: Light misting + affirmations
- 12pm: Check soil moisture like a responsible adult
- 6pm: Reflect on our growth journey
- 9pm: No screens after watering

> "Look, Kevin, I'm doing this because I care," I whispered while holding a spray bottle like a gavel.

Kevin dropped a frond. *Dramatically.*

## The Judgment Phase

Within 48 hours, the power dynamic flipped.

Kevin wasn't just *not* potty trained — he was now my wellness coach. I caught him angling toward my Nalgene, which has been at the same 1/4 full level since Tuesday.

His silent critique was deafening:

- My 3pm iced coffee does not count as hydration
- My houseplant has a more consistent morning routine than I do
- He now only perks up when I drink actual water, like some kind of photosynthetic Fitbit

Yesterday, I reached for my third cold brew and I swear I heard rustling that sounded exactly like:

> "Oh, so we're just going to ignore the cucumber water in the fridge? Again?"

I tried to defend myself with `hydrationLevel = Math.max(coffee, optimism)`, but the compiler — Kevin — returned **error: not enough actual water**.

---

Look, we're working through it. He's **thriving**, I'm **dehydrated**, and our couples therapist (a very confused woman at Home Depot) says we need better boundaries.

The chart is now just for me. Kevin waters himself with my tears of inadequacy.

*This is satire / parody — 100% fictional, no ferns were potty trained, judged, or given legal custody of my water bottle.*
