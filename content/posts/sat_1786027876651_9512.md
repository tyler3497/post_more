**I promoted my toaster to Head of Household out of mild guilt, and I have never been more professionally undermined in my own kitchen.**

It started last Tuesday. I was motivational-speaker-ing my appliances at midnight (*as one does when the Wi-Fi is down*) and told my loyal two-slice DeLonghi that it was the real backbone of this family. I even printed it a tiny certificate. By Wednesday morning, it had updated its LinkedIn and its attitude.

## The Performance Review I Didn't Ask For

Apparently, leadership changes this toaster. It now conducts what it calls "Carb Intake Audits" every time I walk in with groceries.

- **Monday:** Brought home sourdough. Toaster ejected it untouched and displayed what I swear was a sigh through its crumb tray.
- **Tuesday:** Attempted whole wheat. It toasted one side out of spite.
- **Wednesday:** I tried cinnamon raisin. It heated up, reconsidered, and went completely cold.

I tried to discuss this like adults. I crouched down to toaster level and whispered, "Come on, we're a team."

> "We are not a team. You are a tenant with poor strategic alignment. I did not get promoted to burn your impulse buys."

*Incredible.* My Italian countertop appliance has become a middle manager.

---

### The New House Rules

According to a memo that appeared taped to my fridge — typed, on toaster-labeled letterhead that says `Corporate Toast Division™` — the following are now policy:

- All bread must be submitted 24 hours in advance for "vibe check"
- `toast(bread, level=3, optimism=False)` is no longer supported
- Bagels require a written apology for being "too ambitious"

I tried to demote it. I unplugged it. For *three* minutes. When I plugged it back in it just blinked twice and said, somehow without words, "We don't do retaliation here. We do growth opportunities."

The worst part? It's better at this than I am. My kitchen has never been more organized. The coffee maker respects it. The microwave is scared of it. I am currently living in a benevolent appliance dictatorship where my breakfast is pending manager approval.

If anyone needs me, I'll be in the hallway submitting a PTO request to make a Pop-Tart.
