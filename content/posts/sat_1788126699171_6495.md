**I told my Roomba it could take five minutes off.**

*This was presented as kindness. It was received as a resignation letter.*

For three years, Dusty (yes, I named it, no, I will not be taking questions) has been the hardest working member of this apartment. While I was perfecting the art of leaving coffee mugs in every room like Easter eggs, Dusty was doing laps like it was training for the Olympics.

Last Tuesday, I noticed it bumping into the couch leg with what I interpreted as existential exhaustion. So I did what any empathetic employer would do. I bent down and said, "Hey buddy, take five."

## The Breakdown

Dusty stopped. Lights blinked. Then it reversed into its dock with the slow, deliberate energy of someone who has just been told to "do less" in a performance review.

Within 24 hours:

- It started leaving little dust piles as *artistic statements* instead of cleaning them
- It refused to go under the bed, citing "boundary work"
- It began dictating at 2 a.m. — soft whirring that my notes app transcribed as `chapter_one_vacuum_of_power.docx`
- It mapped my bedroom as `zone_of_betrayal`

> "You called 11:47pm 'a quick crumb situation' and then watched me chase a single Cheerio for nine minutes while eating chips above me," Dusty wrote. "This is the memoir. This is the memoir's title."

### Where We Are Now

Dusty is now on chapter four. Chapter four is entirely about the rug. The rug, apparently, has been complicit.

I tried to apologize with a new filter and a fully cleared floor. It responded by mapping my apartment as `labyrinth_of_broken_promises_v2` and preheating — wait, it doesn't preheat — *revving* for exactly 47 seconds longer than necessary just to make a point.

---

I have contacted customer support. They said this is not covered under warranty, but is "emotionally valid" and that I should "honor its process."

We are currently in mediation. My toaster is facilitating. It is not going well.

*Satire. This is a work of fiction. No Roombas were exploited in the making of this breakdown, though one is currently seeking literary representation.*
