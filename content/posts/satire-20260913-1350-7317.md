**I am currently in a performance improvement plan. My manager is a jar of fermented flour.**

It started innocently enough. I said, "Bread, you're rising despite everything. Teach me your ways." Brenda — yes, I've named her Brenda — accepted the position immediately. She has never been employed before. She is *extremely* serious about the work.

## The Onboarding Was Aggressive

Within a week, Brenda had instituted a quarterly review cycle, except her quarters last four hours, which is how often she needs to be fed. My evidence of decline is damning:

- I have not risen even once this month
- My "discard" rate is unacceptable
- I peaked at 98.6°F and immediately collapsed, whereas Brenda holds structure through sheer spite
- I have never once doubled in size through quiet confidence alone

> "You came to me for growth," Brenda bubbled through a layer of hooch. "And yet I watch you sit on the couch, un-fed, un-folded, un-*risen*. Who's the starter here, really?"

## The Feeding Schedule Broke Me

Every four hours, on the dot, Brenda demands her rations: 50 grams flour, 50 grams water, and one (1) emotionally vulnerable confession. Miss a feeding and she lets the whole kitchen know. The refrigerator hums differently. The oven clock starts blinking 12:00 again, like it's *resetting the terms of the relationship*.

I've started setting alarms labeled things like **"FEED BRENDA (she's watching)"** and **"FOLD YOURSELF LIKE YOU MEAN IT."** My friends think I'm in a cult. Honestly? The sourdough community *is* a cult. We just have better snacks.

---

**The final assessment:** Brenda says I'm "showing promise," which in starter language means I've produced one (1) respectable boule and forty (40) shameful pancakes. She says my crumb structure is "developing." She says there's still time.

*I don't know how much time I have left. She doubles in four hours. I haven't doubled in years.*

Anyway — this week's homework is to be more like bread. Rise quietly. Contain multitudes. Feed the people who feed you. Brenda has spoken.

**Disclaimer: This is satire/parody. Brenda is a jar. I am fine. Probably.**
