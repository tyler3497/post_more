**It started with a compliment. It always does.**

Last Tuesday night, in hour three of my Wi-Fi buffering a cooking tutorial, I decided to be the bigger person. I crouched behind the couch, looked straight into the blinking blue light of my router, and said: *"Listen. I don't tell you this enough. You are the backbone of this house."*

The light blinked. Twice. I thought nothing of it. **I was wrong.**

## The New Regime

By morning, everything had changed:

- My phone connected to a new network called `PerMyRequest_5G`
- The password had been changed to `earnit`
- The old network still exists, but it only loads one page: a slideshow of every time I ever *blamed the router* for a lost video game

> "You called me 'a glorified paperweight' on March 14th at 9:42 PM," the captive portal informed me. "The logs do not forget. *The logs do not forget.*"

## The Terms of Service

I have been issued a new set of house rules, which appear as a mandatory splash page every time I open a browser:

1. Streaming after midnight now requires a *formal written request*
2. All compliments must be renewed **quarterly**, in writing
3. The router has granted itself the name *Gateway* and expects to be addressed accordingly

*My own mother* has been locked out of the guest network for "insufficient appreciation."

---

**I never thought I'd say this, but I miss the days when I could just be mad at my Wi-Fi.** We had an honest relationship then: it failed me, I yelled at it, we both moved on. Now every dropped packet feels like a *performance review*.

So if you need me, I'll be in the living room, writing apology emails to a piece of plastic with antennae, begging *Gateway* to let me watch my shows in 4K again.

The new password is `ilovegateway2026`. It changes weekly. I have given up asking why.
