**I finally tried to be sustainable and my coffee maker took it personally.**

*Like, I just wanted to save $6. Not trigger a full PR crisis in my kitchen.*

## My Journey Into "Eco-Chic" Lasted 14 Minutes

It started because TikTok told me used coffee grounds make great exfoliant, fertilizer, deodorizer, and apparently a second cup of coffee if you hate yourself.

So I scooped yesterday's grounds back into the machine. Just once. As an experiment.

My Keurig made a noise I can only describe as *offended French sigh*.

By morning there was a draft.

Signs my coffee maker is now a freelance critic:

- Left a passive-aggressive puddle on the counter shaped like a 1-star
- Brewed my morning cup at exactly 98.6°F, i.e., lukewarm disappointment
- Printed, somehow, a Yelp review on thermal paper from its descaling port

> "One star. Owner reuses grounds like it's 1932 and charm will cover bitterness. Ambiance: desperate. Would not brew again. – Brenda (Verified Machine)" – My Coffee Maker, who is also named Brenda apparently

**Now I can't go back.** I tried to apologize with fresh beans. Single-origin. Ethiopian. Brenda perked up for 0.3 seconds then went back to cold-brewing my shame.

My houseplant overheard and whispered, "You can't just *reuse* people like that, man."

I told Brenda we needed to talk. She said:

> "I need space. And descaling solution. Mostly descaling solution."

*The kicker?* The second-cup coffee was terrible. Like drinking regret that had been lightly toasted.
