**It started, as all great tragedies do, with leftovers.**

I asked my microwave to reheat a sad container of pasta at 11:47 p.m. Just two minutes. An innocent, midnight-carbohydrate transaction between a man and his appliance. Instead, the thing beeped *so* aggressively that I think my fillings rearranged.

## The Incident

According to the complaint — which, yes, is typed and notarized — I am guilty of the following:

- Beeping abuse: requesting a "reheat" 14 times in one week, which my microwave classifies as **"serial reheating behavior"**
- Door slamming: closing the door with, quote, *"the enthusiasm of a man who has given up on joy"*
- Emotional neglect: ignoring its defrost setting for **three consecutive years**
- Late-night disturbances: 11:47 p.m. pasta, allegedly audible through the walls

> "The appliance has the right to a peaceful existence and a consistent operating schedule," the complaint reads. "We respectfully request that the tenant apologize in writing and rotate the plate manually, as is tradition."

I thought this was a prank. Then my landlord texted: *"Microwave's complaint is legit. It's on the building Wi-Fi now. It pays rent electronically."*

## Where This Leaves Me

Apparently, my microwave has a **union rep** now. It won't beep for me anymore unless I submit a request 24 hours in advance. I tried to negotiate in person, and it just sat there, *glowing*, radiating the smug warmth of a device that knows its rights.

So tonight I ate cold pasta. Room temperature. Like an animal.
