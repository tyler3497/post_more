**I bought a white noise machine to finally get eight hours and accidentally gave my subconscious a production budget.**

After three years of sleeping to the soothing sounds of *my upstairs neighbor learning drums and my fridge contemplating existence*, I cracked. I bought the #1 bestseller: 30 sounds, including "Rain on Tent," "Galactic Hum," and "Dishwasher That Loves Its Job."

## Night One: Innovation

I chose "Brown Noise." Deep, warm, allegedly scientifically proven. I fell asleep in 11 minutes. A miracle.

Then I started dreaming in Dolby Surround.

- My dream where I'm late for high school but also doing my taxes? Now with ocean waves and tasteful reverb
- My recurring pizza-conveyor-belt dream? Now narrated in 5.1 surround
- My anxiety dream about replying-all to the entire company? Now with loon calls and *judgment*

> "We're concerned about your range," my dream said, through what I can only describe as a crisp, well-mixed PA system. "You've been doing 'faint panic' for six seasons. We need growth."

### The Notes Meeting

Yesterday my white noise machine left a — *not with words, with vibes* — Post-it on my nightstand:

- Requests 15% more REM, says my dreams are `underwatered`
- Wants me to stop eating cheese at 10pm because "it throws off the Foley work"
- Demands co-producer credit on any dream featuring my childhood best friend riding a goose
- Insists my recurring ex-cameo dreams need "a tighter script"

---

I tried to turn it off. It switched itself to `STATEMENT_MODE` and started playing "Gentle Stream (Passive-Aggressive Remix)."

I am now negotiating with an appliance about my own brain. My dreams have a union rep. Her name is Brooke. She says I need to be more vulnerable before Act III or the owls won't hoot and the crickets will walk.

If you need me, I'll be trying to fall asleep while my subconscious runs a notes call.

*SATIRE / PARODY — entirely fictional, my white noise machine is lovely and does not actually unionize my dreams, though my sleep tracker does think I'm dramatic.*
