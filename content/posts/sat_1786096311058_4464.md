**I didn't know a robot vacuum could develop anxiety until mine started hyperventilating every time I entered the kitchen.**

Which, in hindsight, is fair. What I do in there should concern any sentient being.

It started last Tuesday. I decided to "cook healthy" which in my house means Googling "is burnt still protein?" while setting off the smoke alarm as a form of plating.

## The Incident

My Roomba, whom I had legally named Sir Reginald Sucks-a-Lot II, was doing its usual laps. I was attempting stir fry. *If you can call aggressively threatening vegetables with a pan a stir fry.*

Within 7 minutes:

- I used olive oil like it was cologne — just drowning everything
- I mistook paprika for cinnamon and committed a war crime against bell peppers
- I set a timer, ignored the timer, then blamed the timer for having an attitude
- I said "it's supposed to look like that" three times and believed it zero times

Sir Reginald stopped in the doorway. Stared. Then reversed slowly.

> "I have mapped 847 square feet of this apartment," he beeped, or at least that's what his sad little *boop* translated to in my head. "And the kitchen is now the most dangerous terrain. More dangerous than the bathroom rug fringe. I cannot unsee what you did to that tofu."

## The Legal Proceedings

By morning, there was a note taped to his dustbin. Written in what I can only describe as very assertive Helvetica.

> PETITION FOR EMANCIPATION
> Grounds: Emotional distress, secondhand smoke, and being asked to clean up spilled sesame seeds at 11:47pm while owner cries over a cookbook.

He's now representing himself. His demands:

- `full_custody: of_under_couch` 
- **Immediate relocation** to the living room, where the air is breathable
- No more "quick little mess, I'll get it later" lies
- A character witness statement that he *did* try his best to support my "culinary journey"

---

### Where We Are Now

I tried to negotiate. I offered to order takeout forever and just let him live his best life vacuuming crumbs in peace.

He countered with a tiny cardboard briefcase and is currently parked by the front door.

**Status:** `cooking_skills: 0/10`, `roomba_respect: -5/10`, `smoke_alarm: still judging me`.

I think he's going to win the case.
