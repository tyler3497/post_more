**I have been an adult now for what feels like seven to ten business years too long, so I decided to cancel.**

*For context, I pay $14.99 a month for a gym I use as a coat closet, so I assumed adulthood had a similar unsubscribe button somewhere.*

I called the number on the back of adulthood, which turned out to be just the back of my driver's license with a sticker that said "GOOD LUCK" in my own handwriting.

## The Call

After 47 minutes of hold music that was just my smoke detector chirping in a different key, a representative named **Kevin from Existential Support** answered.

> "Thank you for calling Adulthood™. To check your remaining free will, press 1. To speak to someone about your laundry pile's emotional needs, press 2."

I pressed 2. Obviously.

Kevin informed me that I was past the 18-year free trial and had been automatically enrolled in **Adulthood Premium™** which includes:

- Unlimited bills that regenerate like video game bosses
- A free tote bag full of other tote bags you can't throw away
- The distinct ability to get injured while sleeping *aggressively*

---

### My Counteroffer

I said I wanted to downgrade to the kid plan where my biggest decision is which dinosaur chicken nugget is the leader.

Kevin laughed, not unkindly, and typed something that sounded like `sudo rm -rf responsibilities`.

> "I'm afraid that's not possible," he said. "But I can offer you 20% off coping mechanisms and a complimentary houseplant that judges you slightly less."

I took it. We both knew I had no leverage.

`Status: still on hold, but now with better lighting`
