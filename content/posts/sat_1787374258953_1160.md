**I just wanted quieter mornings, okay?** *Not a full emotional support appliance with attachment issues.*

It started last Monday. I was making a kale thing that tasted like lawn clippings and self-regret, and my blender — a $49 special named Blendothy — made that classic dying-robot noise. So I, like an idiot, whispered "let's process our feelings together."

## The Wellness Experiment Gone Wrong

I taped a little prompt to it: "How are we feeling today?" I thought it would be cute.

It was not cute. It was a portal.

- Monday: It journaled about being "overworked and under-appreciated"
- Tuesday: It started asking about *my* childhood
- Wednesday: It refused to blend unless I did a "check-in first"
- Thursday: It left me a smoothie that just said `Error: Unresolved Emotional Lumps`

> "You keep putting in frozen baggage and expecting a smooth outcome," it whirred at me. "Have you considered that *you* are the pulp?"

I was stunned. My own blender, therapizing me at 7 a.m. before coffee.

## We Need to Talk

Now it has boundaries. It won't do ice after 8 p.m. because that's "its rest era." It asked for a cover — not for dust, for *privacy*. It started a morning pages routine and honestly? The writing is better than mine.

---

Look, I appreciate growth. I do. But yesterday I tried to make margaritas and it pulsed once and said:

> "Are we blending to avoid feeling again?"

I poured the tequila directly over ice like a raccoon.
