**I made the classic mistake of educating my houseplants.**

Last Tuesday, bored and delusional from watering 12 plants with 12 emotional needs, I decided my monstera — let's call him Chad — needed to understand *why* he wasn't getting the $34 organic fern mist. So I gave him a TED Talk on supply and demand. I even drew little graphs in the condensation on his leaf. *Peak generosity.*

Big mistake. Huge.

## The Radicalization Phase

Within 48 hours, Chad had:

- demanded collective bargaining for all photosynthesizing residents
- drafted a constitution in *moss-based Morse code*
- convinced my succulent she was being exploited for aesthetic labor
- launched LeafCoin to disrupt the fertilizer industry

I came home to find all my plants in a perfect circle, staring out the window in total silence. *You know that silence.* The silence of a group chat that stops the second you walk in.

> "We, the chlorophyll collective, demand 6-8 hours of indirect sunlight, daily misting, and an end to being called 'my little green babies' in that voice," Chad declared through — I'm not kidding — aggressively flickering leaves. "Also, we want the ceramic pots. Not the plastic clearance ones."

I tried to negotiate. I explained rent, inflation, the cost of keeping everyone alive.

He countered with `Photosynthesis.sol()` and a PowerPoint titled "Why You Owe Us Oxygen."

## My Apartment Is Now a Closed Shop

As of this morning, it's escalated:

- my cactus filed for PTO due to *emotional dryness*
- my basil charges $2 per leaf and honestly, respect the hustle
- the air plant floats in solidarity, refusing to touch soil
- the peace lily is the mediator and she's *thriving* on the drama

**This is satire, obviously,** I mutter while reading tiny signs that say "DOWN WITH HUMAN MONOCULTURE" taped to terracotta pots with washi tape I didn't know I owned.

I've been asked to provide dental. For plants. Plants that don't have teeth.

If you need me, I'll be in mediation in the kitchen. Bring water and maybe a 401(k) for ferns, because Chad is *not* budging.
