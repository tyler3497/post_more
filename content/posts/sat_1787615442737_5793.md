**I have never folded laundry on the same day I washed it before.** *I want that on record so when the investigation starts, you know this was uncharacteristic behavior.*

Normally, my clean laundry lives in what experts call "the chair" and what I call "my fabric-based lifestyle."

But yesterday, I did it. I washed. I dried. I folded. In one continuous, unhinged burst of executive function.

## My Closet Panicked

I opened the closet door to put the clothes away and the whole closet gasped.

My jeans — that have been holding down the fort since 2019 — looked personally offended.

Here's what folding same-day apparently signals to a closet ecosystem:

- That I have abandoned the sacred doctrine of Chair Pile Sovereignty
- That I might start *color-coding* next, which my hoodies have unionized against since 2021
- That the 7 single socks that have been negotiating a peace treaty might finally have to accept they're widowed
- That I am now the kind of person who owns a lint roller on purpose

> "Who are you and what have you done with the guy who wears inside-out shirts because 'it's still clean if you flip it'?" — my favorite hoodie, probably

I tried to explain I was just trying to be an adult for once. I even ran `git commit -m "feat: become functional human"` in my head.

---

## Now There's Pressure

My closet now expects this every week. My dresser drawers are standing at attention like I just became a general. My hamper filed a formal complaint for abandonment.

I told them this was a one-time event, a limited-edition drop, not a new personality.

But it's too late. The bar is raised. The folded shirts are watching.

If I don't keep this up, I will be the guy who peaked on a Tuesday at 2pm with perfectly folded socks. And honestly? That's a legacy I can live with, as long as no one checks the chair.

*This is satire. My closet does not actually talk, but if it did, it would be very disappointed in my sock-matching skills.*
