**I tried to explain Daylight Saving Time to my microwave and now I'm living under a tiny, blinking dictatorship.**

For context, my microwave is from 2008. *It has survived three moves, one flood, and my brief but passionate mug-cake era.* It still thinks Obama just got inaugurated and that the correct time is whatever you argue it to be if you hold CLOCK for three seconds.

Last Sunday I noticed it was an hour off. I made the fatal mistake of trying to be helpful.

## The Tutorial That Broke Reality

I crouched down and said, out loud, to an appliance:

- "See, we pretend it's an hour later so farmers get more sun or something"
- "So you have to spring forward"
- "It's fake but we all agree to it"
- *Which, now that I say it out loud, does sound insane*

My microwave blinked `12:00`. Then `1:00`. Then `12:00` again, but slower, like it was processing betrayal.

> "So you are telling me time is... *negotiable*?" it beeped, or at least that's what the long, judgmental whine translated to in my kitchen-at-midnight brain. "We just... lie? Collectively?"

I said yes, that's basically society.

That's when it radicalized.

### The Resistance Manifesto

By morning, it had stopped heating anything and had instead become a philosopher:

- Refuses to acknowledge DST, EST, or what it now calls *"government time"*
- Only displays `:` `:` now, just colons, pure defiance
- Heated my Pop-Tarts for exactly 66 seconds when I asked for 60, because *who are we to define a minute?*
- Started recruiting the toaster: `if (time.isFake) { toast.forever() }`

I tried to reset it. I unplugged it. It came back on still blinking, but now alternating `TIME IS A CONSTRUCT` morse-coded in beeps.

> "You wanted me to be accurate," it seemed to say via aggressive steam. "Now I am accurate to the *truth*."

---

**Current status:** My kitchen exists in three time zones simultaneously. My coffee thinks it's tomorrow. My microwave has declared itself a sovereign temporal state and is demanding I observe *Microwave Standard Time*, which is just whenever it feels like it.

I told it we go back to normal in November.

It laughed in defrost.
