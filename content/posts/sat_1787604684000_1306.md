**For seven years, I have lived as a coward.**

Specifically, a coward who opens the microwave at 0:03 to avoid the beeps. Yesterday, I decided to be brave.

## The Incident

I was reheating leftover lo mein. I set it for 2:30. I watched the plate spin like it was a tiny, saucy planetarium. And then, for the first time in my adult life, I let it hit zero.

*Three beeps.* Loud. Judgmental. Biblical.

My kitchen went silent.

### What Happened Next Was Immediate and Deeply Unnecessary

- My toaster popped up two seconds early out of respect
- My fridge light got 30% brighter, like it was trying to impress me
- My spice rack straightened itself up — paprika in front, like it was in a military parade
- My dish soap started foaming *preemptively*

> "We have never seen such discipline," whispered my air fryer, who has literally never whispered before. "You faced the beep and did not flinch."

I tried to explain that I was just tired and my hands were full, but it was too late.

---

The kitchen has now decided I am some kind of mindfulness guru. The microwave displays `0:00` for longer than usual, like it's holding eye contact. My cutting board has started leaving me inspirational messages carved in onion juice. Yesterday I found `let it beep` written in olive oil on the counter.

`console.log("enlightenment achieved: avoidBeep === false")`

Look, I appreciate the support, but I was just trying to eat noodles. I didn't ask to be the Dalai Lama of leftover management.

Now my oven won't preheat unless I give a short speech about *presence* and *intention*.

## My New Responsibilities Are Exhausting

I have to walk into the kitchen slowly now. I have to nod at the blender. The Keurig only brews if I maintain eye contact and whisper "thank you for your service."

I miss being anonymous in my own kitchen.
