**I just wanted to be the kind of person who casually says "feed my starter" like I own a farmhouse in France.**

*Instead I now live with a yeast-based celebrity who charges rent in flour.*

Two weeks ago I mixed flour and water in a jar because the internet told me it would be *mindful*. I named him Kevin, because every uncontrollable fermenting blob deserves a bland name to keep you humble.

Kevin doubled overnight. I posted one photo. One. *A bad one, with bad lighting.*

## The Rise (Literally)

By morning, Kevin had:

- tripled in volume and in self-confidence
- developed a sour smell I described as "artisan" and my roommate described as "legally concerning"
- been reposted by three sourdough meme accounts with the caption **HE'S MOTHER**
- received a DM from a local bakery asking for a collab and headshots

> "We love Kevin's journey," said Brenda from the farmers market, who I have never met. "Tell Kevin we saved a brunch slot for him. No plus-one. You're the plus-one."

*I was never supposed to be the plus-one to my own yeast.*

### The Demands

Yesterday I tried to use Kevin to make actual bread, which apparently is now *beneath him*.

My fridge whiteboard now says in what is definitely not my handwriting:

```
TALENT: KEVIN
MANAGER: KEVIN'S KHORASAN ROUX
REQUESTS: Evian water only, 78°F, no TikToks near him
```

---

I tried to put Kevin back in the fridge. He glowed judgmentally. My other condiments have started asking for representation.

My mom said:

> "Honey, your grandmother made sourdough in the war. It didn't have an email list."

So now I wake at 5 a.m. to feed a jar that won't return my calls, my kitchen smells like a science fair that got exclusive press coverage, and I'm googling "how to tell your starter you're proud but also please leave".

**Kevin is thriving. I am fermenting emotionally.** And honestly, he's booking more brunches than I am.
