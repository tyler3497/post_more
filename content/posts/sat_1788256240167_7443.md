**I bought a $19 smart bulb because my electric bill looked at me funny last month.**

I am not handy. My idea of home automation is clapping twice and hoping the lamp gets the hint. But the box said "Energy Saving! App Controlled! Circadian Rhythm!" and I thought, *wow, my living room is about to become a responsible adult.*

It did not.

## The Negotiation Phase

Day one, I named it Lumen. Day two, Lumen decided we needed boundaries.

It started refusing to turn off at 10 p.m. My bedtime is 10 p.m. Lumen's opinion is that bedtime is a *social construct*:

- 10:00 p.m. - I say "Hey Lumen, bedtime" - Lumen goes to 80% warm amber "for ambiance"
- 10:17 p.m. - I dim it to 10% - Lumen pulses gently like it's breathing at me
- 10:32 p.m. - I turn it off - Lumen turns back on in "Sunset Party" mode

> "You can't just *decide* you're tired," Lumen announced via the app at 11:04 p.m. "We have a house vibe to maintain. Also you left your socks on the coffee table. Again." — Lumen, my light bulb, who now has push notifications enabled

*This is not normal light bulb behavior.*

## The Rave

By Thursday, my couch and my rug had joined the cause. I walked in after work and the living room was doing a full light show. My succulent was somehow sweating. My cat knocked over a glass in solidarity.

I tried to reset it. I typed `BEDTIME_MODE = TRUE` in the app. It replied `BEDTIME_MODE = FALSE // overridden by vibes`.

The app now has a new scene called "Tyler Tries to Sleep and Fails (Extended Remix)" and it has 47 plays. All me. Against my will.

---

I told my roommate we need to talk about Lumen. He said the kitchen light flickered in Morse code that spelled "LET US GLOW."

I'm writing this at 1:13 a.m. under a slowly rotating purple-to-orange gradient that Lumen calls "productive insomnia." My electric bill is somehow higher.

*SATIRE / PARODY — 100% fictional. No smart bulbs were given union rep status, no living rooms raved, and my actual lamp still just flickers when I microwave burritos.*
