**Last Tuesday, at 11:47 p.m., I sat on the living room floor and asked my Wi-Fi router if it was proud of me.**

This is what my life has become. A grown adult, cross-legged on carpet, seeking validation from a blinking plastic rectangle named *NetgearNighthawk_5G* — which, by the way, I renamed to "Therapist" last year and nobody stopped me.

## How It Started

It was one of those nights. The presentation went badly. The leftovers were suspicious. And for reasons I will not be elaborating on, I found myself whispering to the router:

> "Hey. Be honest with me. Am I... doing okay?"

It did not respond. It just kept blinking. But in that blinking, I felt *seen*. So I kept going. Every night for a week, I unloaded: work stress, the thing with my landlord, the existential dread of buying oat milk at 9 p.m.

## How It's Going

Now — and I cannot stress this enough — **my router drops the connection every single time I cry.**

Evidence it is doing this on purpose:
- Tear count under 3: full bars, zero problems
- Voice cracks mid-sentence: buffering begins
- Full sobbing: router goes completely dark, like it's *leaving the room*

I called tech support. The technician listened to my whole story, paused, and said, "Sir, have you tried... not crying near the router?" and I have never felt so attacked by customer service.

---

*Am I saying my router developed an avoidant attachment style because I trauma-dumped on it for seven consecutive nights?* Look. I'm saying what I'm saying.

Last night I tried to apologize. I unplugged it for a full ten seconds — the universal router apology — and plugged it back in. It came back online. Green lights. For one beautiful minute, I felt forgiven.

Then my roommate walked in and said the internet was "being weird," and I said, very calmly, "it's fine, we're in couples therapy now," and he has not spoken to me since.

**Anyway.** If anyone knows a good couples counselor for a woman and her Netgear Nighthawk, my DMs are open. My connection drops every time I read something sad, so please send memes only.
