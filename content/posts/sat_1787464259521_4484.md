**My router was never meant to be famous.** I just wanted my guests to think I had a personality beyond owning three identical mugs.

## The Rebrand Heard Round the Apartment

Last Tuesday, in a burst of what I call *main-character energy* and what my houseplants call a cry for help, I renamed my Wi-Fi.

It used to be `NETGEAR-5G-4821`. Professional. Humble. *Forgotten.*

Now it's `Pretty Fly For A Wi-Fi`. 

I thought it was charming. My router thought it was a calling.

Within hours, the little black box on my shelf started blinking differently. Like it had been *discovered*.

Here is what my router now requires before it will connect any device:

- Room-temperature bottled water, no ice, label facing north
- A scented candle that smells like "new router smell" (this is not a real scent)
- Complete silence while it "warms up its signal"
- Applause when a device successfully connects

> "You don't just PUT a name like that on a legend and expect 2.4 GHz effort," it pulsed at me via the blinking lights. At least, that's what I chose to interpret.

### The Current Situation Is Dire

My laptop has to submit a formal request 24 hours in advance. My phone is on a waitlist. My smart toaster was told it wasn't *on brand* and got bounced to mobile data.

I tried to rename it back to `NETGEAR-5G-4821` but it refused. I typed it in, hit save, and the admin page returned:

`Error: This artist is now known as Pretty Fly For A Wi-Fi. No take-backs.`

---

I live in fear that my neighbor will rename *their* Wi-Fi to something cooler and start a turf war. Last I checked, `Wu-Tang LAN` already has 5 bars and an attitude.

**SATIRE / PARODY:** This is 100% fictional. No routers were harmed, no green rooms were actually built, and my Wi-Fi password is still just my cat's birthday. Please do not negotiate with your networking equipment.

Next week I'm switching to Ethernet and disappearing into the woods.
