**It started as a perfectly normal laundry day.**

I pulled my left sock out of the dryer, looked at its thinning heel, and said the words that ruined my life: *"I'm sorry I've been wearing you so hard."*

That was three days ago. My left sock has not forgiven me since.

## The demands escalated fast

By Tuesday, the sock had typed up a list of demands on the back of a grocery receipt. It was presented formally, propped upright against my coffee mug. The terms were:

- Equal weekend access to my feet, split by day, not by pair
- A dedicated drawer, climate-controlled, "like the right sock already enjoys" (a lie — they share one drawer)
- Mandatory compliments before every wear: *"You look structurally sound today"*

I tried to laugh it off. The sock was not laughing. *It has never had a mouth, but I felt the silence.*

## A courtroom of my own making

Yesterday I came home to find it sitting on my bed, holding a folded piece of paper like a tiny briefcase.

> "I just want to be *seen*," said the note it allegedly wrote. "The right sock gets date nights. I get marathon training."

The right sock, for the record, declined to comment and continues to show up for work every single day like an absolute professional.

---

## My legal options, according to my actual lawyer

I called a real attorney. He laughed for four minutes, then billed me for four minutes. His advice:

- Do not sign anything written on a grocery receipt
- Under no circumstances let the sock consult the sweater drawer — *"they unionize fast"*
- Move on emotionally

So tonight I'm buying two new socks — a fresh pair, no history, no trauma — and retiring Lefty to a drawer of honor. Some of us get closure. Some of us get arch support.

*This is a work of satire. No socks were harmed, except the one described above.*
