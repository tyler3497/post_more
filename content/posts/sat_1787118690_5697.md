**I finally did it.** After 27 years of wrong-wristing my way through breakfast, I bought an ergonomic spoon.

It was matte black, curved like a tiny boomerang, and promised *optimal scoop trajectory* and *reduced breakfast fatigue*. I didn't even know breakfast was making me tired. Apparently, it was.

## The First Morning Was Fine

The spoon arrived with instructions. *Instructions*. For a spoon.

Day one, my Cheerios were intrigued. Day two, they were suspicious. By day three, full intervention.

- My spoon now requires a 12-degree wrist rotation I call "the handshake"
- It has a thumb groove that judges my life choices in real time
- It makes a faint *whoosh* sound that my older spoons never bothered with
- It claims to be `dishwasher-safe` but emotionally? Not at all

My regular spoons are in the drawer holding a vigil.

> "We saw you bring in the fancy curved guy," my cereal whispered this morning. "We used to be simple. Milk. Spoon. Crunch. Now you're doing pilates with your breakfast?"

## The Betrayal Deepens

---

I tried to explain that this is about *self-care*, that my wrist deserves lumbar support or whatever ergonomic marketing told me at 2 a.m.

But the cereal has a point. I now spend 45 seconds aligning my grip while my milk gets warm. I have to journal about my scooping angle. My breakfast has gone from 4 minutes to a full TED Talk with a Q&A.

The ergonomic spoon also left a note on the fridge. It said:

> "You are not scooping correctly. Also we need to talk about your relationship with crunch."

*Look*, I wanted to be a guy who has his life together enough to own a posture spoon. But I am not that guy. I'm the guy whose breakfast is now in therapy.

So tomorrow, I'm going back to the bent souvenir spoon from 2019 that says "WORLD'S OKAYEST CAMPER." It has never once asked me about my wrist alignment.

And my cereal? It finally floated back up to the top. Forgiveness tastes like slightly soggy vindication.
