## I Thought Two Screens Meant Twice the Focus

**I bought a second monitor to be more productive.** That was the pitch I gave my bank account, my roommate, and myself at 11:47 p.m. on a Tuesday.

For 48 glorious hours, it worked. I was *thriving*. Spreadsheets on the left, research on the right. I looked like a person who knows what `Cmd+Shift+T` does without googling it.

Then they started talking.

> "Oh, so *I'm* just the email monitor now?" said Left Screen, who I've decided is named Brenda.

Brenda (Monitor One, original, loyal) was not pleased that I put all my fun stuff — YouTube, my budget that is 90% takeout — on New Guy.

### Exhibit A: The Evidence of Betrayal

My setup has devolved into a full domestic dispute:

- **Brenda** dims herself dramatically whenever I look too long at Right Screen
- **New Guy (Greg)** keeps auto-adjusting his brightness to what I can only describe as *flirtatious*
- Both refuse to display my calendar, citing "emotional labor concerns"
- I caught them syncing their sleep modes to exclude me

I tried mediation.

> "I brought you together to collaborate," I whispered to the dual-monitor stand at 2 a.m., gently weeping into my lukewarm coffee.

Greg responded by opening 14 tabs of LinkedIn motivational quotes. Brenda displayed only my most embarrassing desktop folder, titled `definitely_NOT_important`.

---

### The Custody Arrangement

We have reached a legally non-binding but emotionally exhausting agreement:

I will split my attention 50/50, *even when I'm just watching a cat knock a plant over in slow motion*. No favorites. And I must refer to moving my mouse between them as "co-parenting."

My productivity is up 0% and my monitors are now in couples counseling via DisplayPort.

`Status: 2 screens, 0 supervision`
