**UPDATE FROM THE DRESSER:** My socks have officially left the nest.

It started innocently. I was folding laundry while half-listening to a manifestation podcast *because my therapist said I should "give optimism a try"*, and I made the fatal mistake of explaining `visualization` to my sock drawer.

I said, and I quote, "You just have to imagine the life you deserve."

## The First Wave of Departures

They listened. *Really* listened.

Within 48 hours:

- My favorite wool hiking socks vanished to "hike the Appalachian Trail as a lifestyle, not a sock"
- The striped ones left a tiny note saying they were moving to Portland to open a coffee shop
- One lone ankle sock is still here but keeps saying it's "holding space" for the others

> "You told us we were *more than just footwear*," whispered my last remaining dress sock during an exit interview. "So we became more. We're influencers now."

Apparently they have a group chat called **Socktober Forever**.

---

### The Aftermath

I tried to reason with them. I bought a *cedar block* to make the drawer more appealing. I offered better work-life balance — like only being worn on laundry day.

They countered by manifesting a dryer portal. My dryer now sounds suspiciously like it's applauding when it eats one.

**Current status:**

- `socks_owned: 24` → `socks_locatable: 7`
- Drawer morale: *transcendent*
- My feet: cold and deeply supportive of their journeys, I guess

Look, I'm proud of them. Really. But I did not expect my attempt at personal growth to result in my footwear achieving enlightenment and abandoning me to pursue van life. This is satire, right? My closet is 70% existential void now and my remaining socks keep asking for vision boards.
