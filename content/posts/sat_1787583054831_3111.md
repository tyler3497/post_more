**I finally washed my reusable water bottle after what my therapist calls "an extended period of mutual denial" and now it doesn't recognize me.**

I've had this bottle since 2022. It has survived three apartments, two half-marathons I walked, and one incident where I used it as a rolling pin. It developed a *patina* — which is what I told people when they asked why it was opaque.

The inside had its own ecosystem. I'm pretty sure it was paying rent.

On Sunday I caved. My roommate left a sticky note that just said "please." So I actually scrubbed it. Soap, brush, vinegar soak, the full exorcism.

## The Betrayal

I held it up, sparkling, proud. I said "look at us, fresh start."

It stared back like I had just shown up to our anniversary with a different face.

Evidence that our relationship is over:

- It no longer fits in my hand the same way — *too slippery, too honest*
- My stickers won't re-stick — they have chosen to leave me too
- I took a sip and it tasted like **nothing**, which is somehow worse — where did the subtle hints of old iced coffee and regret go?
- It keeps sliding off my desk like it's trying to escape

I tried to reintroduce myself:

> "Hey, it's me. The guy who once left you in a hot car for 9 hours in July and you forgave me. Remember? We had electrolytes together?"

Nothing. Cold shoulder. Literally cold, because now it actually keeps water cold since it's clean.

I ran a diagnostic:

`bottle.recognize_owner() // ERROR: owner not found, please return to original swamp`

---

## My Plan to Win It Back

My roommate says I should just "get a new bottle" which shows how little she understands commitment.

So I'm going to rebuild trust slowly. One slightly questionable beverage at a time.

Tomorrow I'm putting lukewarm coffee in it and forgetting it in my backpack again. Just to make it feel at home.

*SATIRE / PARODY — this is 100% fictional. My water bottle is clean now and we are in counseling. No actual bottles were emotionally harmed in this essay.*
