**I finally bought a label maker to become an organized adult and within 48 hours my apartment turned into a LinkedIn networking event no one asked for.**

*I wanted clean drawers. I got a full corporate org chart with passive-aggressive job descriptions.*

## Phase One: Innocent Productivity

It started the way all bad decisions start — with good intentions and free two-day shipping. My junk drawer had achieved sentience. My spices were alphabetized in my head but anarchist in reality. So I unboxed the label maker, loaded the tape, and felt powerful.

By hour two I had labeled:

- Socks: *Department of Single & Searching*
- Pantry: *Headquarters of Expired Good Intentions (Est. 2023)*
- Fridge cheese drawer: *Emotional Support Dairy — Do Not Audit*
- My laptop charger: `charger.returnToBase() // it never does`

> "Sir, you have just labeled me 'Chief Couch of Napping Operations' without a performance review," my couch whispered. "I demand benefits."

## Phase Two: The Performance Reviews Began

My toaster now identifies as **Director of Crumb Strategy and Burnt Offerings**. My houseplant updated its resume to *VP of Photosynthesis and Silent Judgment*. Even my toothbrush requested a title change.

I tried to reason with the label maker. I typed `STOP` and it printed:

```
LABEL: "Department of Trying to Control the Uncontrollable"
```

---

My roommate came over, saw my microwave labeled **Senior Vice President of Reheating Regrets**, and said, "Oh, you're doing *that* kind of organizing."

The worst part? It's working. I haven't lost my keys in three days because they're now labeled **"Keys — You Will Lose Me If You Don't Respect Me."** My apartment looks like a very supportive, very bureaucratic museum of my own coping mechanisms.

*SATIRE / PARODY — this is 100% fictional, no apartments were reorganized into startups, no toasters were promoted to management. I'm just a guy who wanted a clean drawer and accidentally gave his furniture imposter syndrome.*
