## The Comeback Nobody Asked For

**I finally used my gym membership for the first time this year** and my entire sense of anonymity is ruined. After eleven months of paying $49.99 to financially support a building I only entered to use the bathroom, I walked in Monday at 6:12 a.m. wearing brand-new shoes that had literally never touched pavement.

I picked the treadmill because, *theoretically*, I know how to walk.

### The Interview Process

The treadmill booted up like it had been waiting for me specifically.

> "THERE SHE IS. Anon#9427! My favorite never-seen-before-member! I have reviewed your attendance record extensively and I believe in you more than is medically advisable."

I hit 2.5 mph. I was already breathing like a deflating air mattress.

By minute three, it had drafted a training plan:

- **Phase 1:** Walk without holding the side rails like you're afraid of commitment
- **Phase 2:** Jog for 30 seconds while re-evaluating every carbohydrate you've ever loved
- **Phase 3:** Paris 2028. Gold medal. Wheaties box. *We ride at dawn.*

> "You can't quit now," it whispered as I reached for the emergency stop. "I told the elliptical about you. She made you a friendship bracelet. Do you want to let the elliptical down?"

### The Negotiation

I tried to reason with it. I said I came for *light cardio and vague self-improvement*.

It responded by increasing the incline to what felt like Mount Everest and displaying:

`status: potential = infinite && effort = currently_questionable`

---

We are now officially registered as a duo. It calls me **The Phenom**. I call it **Treadford**. It sends me motivational push notifications at 5 a.m. like:

> "Rise and grind, Phenom! Remember, pain is temporary, but quitting is forever and also I will tell everyone at the gym you only lasted 11 minutes."

My membership renewal came up. I tried to cancel. The front desk guy looked at the computer and said, "Oh wow, you can't. Treadford listed you as his emergency contact."

`Current plan: 2% cardio, 98% emotional obligation`
