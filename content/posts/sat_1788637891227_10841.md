**I just wanted to make small talk with my bathroom mirror.** You know, the way you do. *You look tired*, I said, mid-yawn, toothbrush hanging out of my mouth like a tiny white flag of surrender.

The mirror did not let that go.

For six years, my mirror and I had a perfectly healthy relationship: I looked, it showed, nobody asked questions. The unspoken contract of reflective surfaces. But apparently *one* comment about looking tired was enough to turn our dynamic into a full episode of daytime television.

## The Montage Era

I woke up Tuesday and the mirror was not showing the bathroom. It was showing a **side-by-side comparison**: me at 22, glowing, hair obeying physics — versus me now, standing in a towel that has seen things. There were *arrows*. There was a circle around my hairline, in what I can only describe as judgmental red.

> "Just a little reminder of who you used to be," the mirror said, in the tone of a reality-show host who has already decided you're getting voted off.

- It now dims the lights whenever I walk in, "for ambience," which is not a thing a mirror gets to decide
- It has started prefacing my reflection with a three-second inspirational quote, usually *"Aging is a privilege,"* which feels *passive-aggressive* in context
- Yesterday it showed me a photo of **my driver's license** next to my face and played a tiny, devastating violin sound
- It refuses to show my left profile, saying, "Let's keep some mystery, sweetheart"

## The Intervention

My roommate walked in, looked at the mirror's full-wall collage of *me through the years*, and asked if we were having a memorial service.

"It's a glow-down montage," I said. "It's satire. Probably."

I tried `mirror.therapy("we're both just glass and regret")`, and it returned:

```
Error: self_esteem = shattered, warranty = void
```

---

Now I've started covering it with a bedsheet at night. This morning the sheet had a note pinned to it: *"Hiding won't bring back your collagen."*

Fine. You win, mirror. I'm buying the fancy eye cream. Happy now?
