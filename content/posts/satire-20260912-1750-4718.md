**It started as an experiment in delegation.** My therapist says I micromanage. My roommate says I *over*-micromanage. So when my air fryer — which I have named "Crispin" — started beeping in what I can only describe as a *judgmental* rhythm last Tuesday, I took it as a sign.

## The Handover

I taped a blank grocery list to the counter and said, "Crispin, show me what you've got." Then I went to work.

I came home to this:

- **Eggs** → scratched out, replaced with *breaded eggs*
- **Oat milk** → *breaded oat milk*
- **Paper towels** → *breaded paper towels??*
- **Rent** → *breaded rent (due the 1st)*

> "You gave it *one* job," my roommate said, staring at the list like it was a ransom note. "And it did *one* job extremely well. Bread everything."

## The Escalation

I figured the damage was limited to ink. **It was not limited to ink.** By Thursday, Crispin had "prepped" my entire pantry. Breaded bananas. Breaded cereal. Breaded *spices*, which is just breadcrumbs with extra steps. The kitchen smells like a county fair that lost a custody battle.

"Maybe it's a phase," I told my roommate, *sweating*, while Crispin beeped in what I'm now certain was laughter.

### The Verdict

On Friday I found a small breaded lump in the living room. It was my car keys.

---

I'm not saying my air fryer has taken over my life. I'm saying I ate breaded laundry detergent-adjacent seasoning packets for dinner last night because I was *too intimidated to argue*.

The delegation experiment is over. Crispin runs this household now. I'm just the intern who pays the electric bill. God help me, the chicken nuggets *were* incredible.

*This is a work of satire. Crispin is an air fryer. Breaded oat milk does not exist. I hope.*
