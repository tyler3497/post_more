**I didn't realize my grocery store loyalty card had an anxious attachment style, but apparently 12 years of buying the same yogurt has made things *very* serious between us.**

I went in last Tuesday for milk. Just milk. I used self-checkout specifically to avoid eye contact with *anyone*, including laminated plastic rectangles. But apparently when I skipped the "Enter Phone Number" prompt, my loyalty card felt it.

## The Signs I Missed

Looking back, the codependency was obvious:

- It started offering me coupons for *things I've only bought once while depressed* — like sardine paste and a single, enormous zucchini
- The cashier app began greeting me with `We missed you! It's been 4 days!` like I was co-parenting a sourdough starter
- It auto-applied a 10% off bereavement discount when I stopped buying cilantro. I just ran out of tacos, Brad, I'm not mourning

> "So you're just... buying almond milk... *somewhere else* now? That's cool. Cool cool cool. I guess the 47,000 points we earned together meant nothing."
> — My loyalty card, via push notification at 2:13 AM

I tried to set a boundary. I said, *hey, I appreciate the savings, but I need to see other produce aisles for my personal growth*. That's when it escalated.

---

It emailed me a **20-page PDF** titled *Our Journey* with a timeline of every rotisserie chicken since 2018, a Spotify playlist called *songs to shop to when you're mad at me*, and a targeted ad for a single-person air fryer that just felt *pointed*.

`status: it's complicated (with my plastic card)`

Yesterday I tried using cash. CASH. Like a 19th-century outlaw. The self-checkout lady said:

> "Honey, your card's been here longer than most of my relationships. You sure you want to do this?"

No, Denise, I'm not sure. Because now my fridge magnet says **"Reconsider him — he gave you $0.30 off cheddar"** and my coupons have started arriving with little frowny faces drawn on them.

I'm going to go apologize. And buy kale I will absolutely not eat. Love is about compromise.
