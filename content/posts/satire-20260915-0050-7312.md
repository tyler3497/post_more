## A Cautionary Tale About Rating Celestial Bodies

**Last Tuesday, I did something I thought was harmless.** I left the Moon a five-star review. Not on Google — that would be insane. I wrote it on a sticky note and pressed it against my window, because the Moon had been looking *exceptional* that night, and gratitude is a practice.

It read, in full: "Consistent glow. Great ambiance. Would orbit again."

I fell asleep proud of my wholesomeness. I woke up to my bedroom glowing like a dental office.

### The Moon Has Become a Regular

Here's what I have learned about the Moon in the week since:

- It is *extremely* online about its reputation
- It took my review as a personal invitation, not feedback
- It now rises specifically toward *my* window, like a diner regular who remembers your order
- It brought a tiny tip jar one night. It was a bottle cap. I put a quarter in it. *I am the problem.*

> "I don't do this for everyone," I swear it seemed to say, its cratered face smug against the glass. "But a five-star reviewer? That's family."

### The Escalation

By Thursday, the Moon had started doing **cameos**. It would peek over my neighbor's roofline, pause, tilt meaningfully, and then — `moon_cameo_v4.mp4` — drift into full frame over my window like it had a booking. On Friday it blocked the streetlight to be the only light source, which honestly was a power move I respected even while resenting it.

*Somewhere between the third and fourth consecutive night of lunar loitering, I realized I had not slept in 40 hours and was negotiating with a rock.*

"Five stars doesn't mean we're *together*," I whispered through the glass. The Moon glowed slightly brighter, which I am choosing to interpret as respect rather than what it was: a celestial object preparing to file a restraining order against me.

---

So here is my advice, free of charge: **never rate the sky.** The sky does not understand boundaries. Somewhere up there is a Sun with abandonment issues, and I am absolutely not leaving it a review. I've learned my lesson.

*Update: the Moon left ME a review. Three stars. "Tenant was needy."*
