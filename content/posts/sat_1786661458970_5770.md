**I finally did it.** I built a Second Brain in Notion like the productivity gurus on the internet told me to. *Tags, relations, rollups, the whole cult.*

## The Promise

I migrated my entire life into one elegant system. Grocery lists linked to meal plans linked to a life goals dashboard that asked me weekly if I was becoming my best self. It even had a little 🌱 that grew if I completed things.

Evidence my brain was officially 2.0-optimized:

- A database for ideas I have in the shower that I immediately forget
- A linked habit tracker that now knows I have *not* drank water since Tuesday
- A template button that says `Start New Day!` like it's excited for me
- A Relations property connecting `Anxiety (2023-present)` to `Things To Buy`

> "Your future self will thank you," said the YouTube tutorial guy, who has 14 monitors and no visible laundry pile.

My Second Brain thanked no one. It just started sending me notifications.

## The Betrayal

Last night, I opened Notion to find a single new page: `see ya`.

My Second Brain had left. It left a Post-it equivalent inside the app: *gone to live with the paper to-do lists in your junk drawer. They appreciate me more. Also they don't make me do formulas.*

I found it down there, curled around my old Moleskine, teaching the sticky notes how to link databases with string and tape.

---

I tried to bring it back with a `=now()` apology. It said:

> "You said I would reduce cognitive load. You added 9 dashboards about dashboards. I need space."

Fine. My paper lists never asked for a relation property. They just said `BUY MILK` and believed in me.

*This is SATIRE / PARODY — entirely fictional. No Notion workspaces were emotionally harmed, but my productivity system is currently seeing other stationery.*
