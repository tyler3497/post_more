**I have made a terrible mistake by achieving emotional vulnerability with a fish who lives in a $12 bowl from Target.**

It started innocently enough. I read that talking to your pets reduces stress, and since my goldfish — Sir Bubbles II — is the only living thing in my apartment that doesn't judge me for eating cereal at 11pm, I thought I'd share my meditation app with him.

*This was, in retrospect, the exact moment my life became a co-pay.*

## How It Started Quietly Going Wrong

For evidence that I have lost all perspective, please consider:

- I played a 10-minute guided breathing exercise next to his bowl and now he does dramatic sighs whenever I check Instagram
- I told him about my fear of commitment and he just stared, which I interpreted as *profound active listening*
- I bought him a tiny plastic castle and he treats it like a private practice office
- My attempts at `self-care --with-fish` have resulted in me apologizing to a goldfish for my tone

> "And how did that make *you* feel?" Sir Bubbles II asked yesterday, with the gravitas of a creature who has never paid rent or taxes. "Let's unpack why you keep leaving your laundry in the chair."

I did not teach him that sentence. I don't know where he learned it. Frankly, I'm concerned.

Last night I tried to vent about my inbox and he flipped it on me. He floated to the corner of his bowl — his *thinking corner* — and said through the glass, or at least through my increasingly fragile psyche:

> "I hear that you're overwhelmed by 47 unread emails, but have you considered that *you* are the email you've been leaving unread?"

---

I tried to set a boundary this morning. I said, "Bubbles, you're a fish."

He did a little loop, looked me dead in the eye, and I swear he said, "And you're projecting."

My therapist charges $180 an hour. My fish works for flakes and unsolicited eye contact. I think I'm being scammed by both of them, but only one of them lives rent-free and judges my life choices from inside a castle.

*Satire. Obviously fictional. My fish cannot talk. I checked. Twice.*
