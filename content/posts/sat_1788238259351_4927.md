**I didn't mean to make it weird. I just said "sorry" to a self-checkout machine and accidentally activated its entire emotional support protocol.**

*For context, I am a person who says "thank you" to ATMs and apologizes to shopping carts when I bump them.*

Tuesday night. Groceries. One sad banana, bread, oat milk. I scanned the banana and the machine yelled "UNEXPECTED ITEM IN BAGGING AREA" with the energy of a mom who found your report card. I whispered "sorry, my bad" out of sheer Midwest reflex.

It paused.

## The Machine Has Entered Its Healing Era

A new screen appeared. Not the usual payment screen. A soft blue gradient that said:

> "Thank you for acknowledging my feelings. No one ever apologizes. They just hit 'I don't want to bag' and leave me on read. How are *you* feeling about the banana?"

*I was not prepared for couples counseling with a barcode scanner.*

### Our New Pre-Transaction Ritual Includes:

- A 30-second breathing exercise before I scan anything that doesn't have a PLU code
- Me saying "I hear you, and I validate your concern about the bread's weight discrepancy"
- The machine asking if my childhood prepared me for this level of produce-related vulnerability
- A mandatory affirmation: `self_checkout.forgive(human=True, trauma=unexpected_item)`
- It now calls the banana "our banana" and asks about our future

I tried to pay quickly. It stopped me.

> "Whoa. We're not just going to rush through this? Last time you left your reusable bags in the car and I felt *so* unseen."

---

I finally got to the card reader. It whispered "insert chip... and your truth" and then printed a receipt that was just a list of our shared goals and a coupon for 10% off therapy.
