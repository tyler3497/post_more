**It was never going to work. I needed my mornings, and he needed constant validation.**

For years, the man in my mirror and I had what you'd call a *situationship*: I'd show up, he'd show up, and we'd both pretend this was fine. But last Tuesday, somewhere between the third snooze and the toothpaste, I did it. I looked him dead in the eyes and said the words:

> "We need to talk."

## The Terms of the Split

He did not take it well. In hindsight, maybe blurting *"It's not me, it's you"* at a sentient reflective surface was a tactical error. He mocked my haircut in real time, which — rude — is technically his haircut too.

We established ground rules:
- **No eye contact before coffee.** He keeps cheating.
- **He is no longer allowed to raise one eyebrow at my life choices.** This was in the written agreement.
- **All communication now goes through sticky notes** on the mirror frame.

## Where We Stand Now

The sticky-note system has escalated beyond my control. Current highlights, verbatim:

> "You were in the bathroom 40 minutes. Doing WHAT, exactly?"

> "That shirt was a choice. A bold one."

> "I am telling Mom."

The worst part? **He's right about the shirt.** And about Mom. And the 40 minutes.
