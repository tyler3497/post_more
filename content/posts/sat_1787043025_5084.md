**I bought a compost bin to become a better person.** *Classic mistake.*

My sister sent me one of those reassuring infographics that says composting saves the planet, and I thought, sure, I can be the kind of person who turns garbage into soil and soil into smugness. Two-day shipping later, I was officially Sustainable.

I put it on my counter. Matte green. Very adult. It came with a tiny charcoal filter and a pamphlet that said `decomposition is just nature's group project`.

By day three, the compost had formed a management layer.

## The Daily Standup I Didn't Consent To

I woke up Tuesday to whispering from the kitchen at 6:30 a.m.

> "Okay team, quick sync. Sharon's eggshells are still not pulling their weight. Kevin's coffee grounds are over-caffeinated again. And our human bought another plastic-wrapped cucumber. We need to talk about values alignment." — Barry, the Banana Peel, who is now my compost's Scrum Master

Barry wears an invisible headset. I know because he says "Let's circle back" every six minutes.

Here is what my compost thinks I do wrong:

- Bought pre-washed lettuce in a plastic tub *twice* this week, described as "a choice"
- Threw an avocado pit in the TRASH because I was "emotionally overwhelmed"
- Used a paper towel to wipe up water — their words — "surface-level sustainability"
- Left the lid open for 12 hours, which they logged as `incident: oxygen breach, human inattention, severity: dramatic`

### My Performance Improvement Plan

Barry gave me an official notice taped to the fridge with a banana sticker. Very corporate. Very moist.

---

I tried to explain that I work full-time and cannot attend a 15-minute compost retrospective every morning where a lime rind talks about vulnerability.

It replied:

```
sprint_retro:
  what_went_well: "nothing, you bought plastic"
  what_to_improve: "everything"
  action_items:
    - touch grass (literally, we are grass-adjacent)
```

*I am being performance-reviewed by fruit scraps.* My actual job has never been this thorough.

Now I rinse out peanut butter jars like I'm being watched — because I am. The banana peels applaud when I do. Slow, wet clapping. It's worse than silence.

**Update:** This morning I tried to skip the standup. Barry sent a calendar invite titled *Urgent: Your Carbon Footprint and You — A Frank Discussion*. I declined. He rescheduled anyway.
