**I decided to become a hydrated person on Monday, which is how I knew Tuesday would be a disaster.**

*You know the type — the 64oz bottle with the little motivational phrases? "Keep Chugging!" "Almost There!" "You Got This!" Mine has started using that tone with me and I do not appreciate it.*

Influencers make it look serene. Lemon water. Dewy skin. Inner peace. I wanted that. What I got was a full-time job as a water mule and a bladder that now has a union rep.

## The Rise and Fall of My Hydration Empire

Day One started strong. I drank water like it was my personality:

- 8:00 a.m. - Chugged 16oz while whispering affirmations to a houseplant
- 10:30 a.m. - Refilled bottle, bottle said `YOU'RE DOING AMAZING SWEETIE` and I believed it
- 1:00 p.m. - Realized I had peed three times and hadn't left my kitchen
- 3:00 p.m. - My bladder sent its first formal email

> "Per my last contraction, we need to discuss sustainable flow management. I did not sign up for 8 glasses a day. I signed up for iced coffee and plausible deniability."

I tried to negotiate.

> "Think of the glow!" I said.

> "Think of the *commute*," my bladder replied. "You have a 12-minute walk to Trader Joe's. I have seen things on that walk. We cannot do this."
