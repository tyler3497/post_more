**I finally organized my spice rack alphabetically and I immediately became the kind of person who has opinions about cumin placement.**

It was supposed to take ten minutes. *The kind of ten minutes you promise yourself will unlock total life clarity.* I bought a label maker. I decanted. I alphabetized. I stood back feeling like a cured individual.

That lasted until paprika started talking.

## Phase One: Innocent Organization

I told myself this was self-care:

- **A**: Allspice *finally* at the front where he belongs
- **C**: Cinnamon, who thinks she's the main character
- **O**: Oregano, still clinging to 2019
- **P**: `Paprika`, who now runs the HOA

I used `import zen from './spice-rack'` and expected inner peace. Instead I got performance reviews.

> "Oh, so you're using pre-ground pepper on a Tuesday?" said Smoked Paprika, who has become my supervisor. "Interesting choice for someone who owns *three* pepper mills."

## Phase Two: The Coup

Now they have a system. If my dinner isn't "balanced" they withhold themselves. Yesterday I reached for garlic powder and the whole rack went:

> "We talked about this. We're doing *intentional seasoning* now. What would cardamom think?"

---

I tried to make plain toast this morning. Toast! The most neutral food! The rack lit up like a group chat I wasn't in. Cayenne was *typing...*

Turmeric left a sticky note on my kettle that said "have you considered purpose?"

I live in fear of running out of bay leaves because I know thyme will hold a meeting about my commitment issues.

*This is satire. I do not actually have a sentient spice rack, though if I did it would still be more organized than my inbox.*
