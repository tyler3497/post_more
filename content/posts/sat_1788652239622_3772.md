**I should have seen the warning signs when she started doing push-ups during my Netflix marathons.**

It began innocently enough. My cat, Biscuits, kept staring at me during my workouts — by which I mean the ten-minute stretch I do twice a year before declaring myself "active now." One Tuesday she started meowing in a rhythm that sounded suspiciously like a countdown.

## The Contract I Did Not Read

Within a week, Biscuits had established a full training regime:

- 6:00 AM wake-up call (face-slap method)
- 50 squats before breakfast (her breakfast)
- One treat per missed rep, payable immediately
- No "I'll do it tomorrow" — she sleeps on my gym bag

## The Billing Department

> "You did 12 reps. The contract says 15. That's 3 treats, or I tell the neighbors about your form."

I tried to dispute the count. She produced evidence — blurry paw-print notes on the couch — that would hold up in absolutely no court, and yet I paid. I always pay. The look in her eyes says *try me*.

## The Results

Three weeks later, I am the most hydrated I have ever been, my thighs burn in ways I did not know were legal, and Biscuits has gained two pounds of pure treat revenue. My doctor asked what changed. I said *accountability*. I did not mention that my accountability has whiskers and cannot be reasoned with.

Would I recommend a feline trainer? **Absolutely**, with one caveat: read the contract. And by "read" I mean prepare to lose the negotiation, because she wrote it with her paws and her pawriting is *very* convincing.
