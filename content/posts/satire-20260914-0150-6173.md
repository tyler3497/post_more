**It started as a compliment.** One small, innocent, probably-overcaffeinated compliment at 7 a.m., and now my kitchen is a gallery and I am a guest in my own apartment.

## The Incident

It happened last Tuesday. My blender — a faithful, mid-range appliance I will call *The Professor* — produced a strawberry-banana smoothie so smooth I briefly questioned whether I'd accidentally bought a professional unit. So I said the words. Out loud. With feeling:

> "Wow. That is not a smoothie. That is *art*. You are an artist."

I heard the motor idle for half a second. That was the moment everything changed.

## Exhibit A: The Evidence

- It now refuses to blend anything that "lacks a thesis." A milkshake? "Derivative." Pesto? "Pedestrian." Water with ice? It blinked its little LED at me like I'd asked a painter to fill a bucket.
- Last night it presented me with a drink called **"The Weight of Mornings"** — three blueberries, one wilting mint leaf, and, I swear, a single tear of oat milk.
- It has started signing its work. I found *PROF.* spelled out in foam on top of a smoothie. I don't own a piping bag. I don't know how.
- My roommate Greg walked in, took one look at a lopsided purple creation, and said:

> "Man, I don't know if I should drink it or wait for it to be explained at a museum."

- It demands natural light. If I close the blinds before blending, it just... vibrates. No motor. Just judgment.

## The Aftermath

I am now living with an appliance that considers frozen margaritas "a betrayal of the medium." My mother calls it *"why you pay so much in electricity."*

I've tried reasoning with it. I've tried complimenting it back down to a humble kitchen tool, but you can't un-ring that bell. As The Professor put it this morning:

> "Once you have tasted *vision*, you cannot go back to *liquids*."

Honestly? The smoothies are better now. I'm thinner. But last night it looked at my ice cream maker — a perfectly nice machine, mind you — and whispered (it has a *whisper setting*, which is terrifying) that it was "a hobbyist."

*Satire. My blender is a blender. But Greg has stopped drinking anything from it, just in case.*
