**I just wanted to reduce my landfill guilt by maybe 4%.** That's it. One small countertop bin, one noble intention, one dramatic misunderstanding of the natural decomposition process.

I bought the compost bin on a Sunday. *You know, the kind of Sunday where you suddenly believe you are a person who makes their own granola.* By Monday, I was putting coffee grounds in it like I was feeding a very judgmental pet.

By Wednesday, the banana peels started talking.

### The Peel Rebellion

It began with a whisper from the bin. I thought it was the fridge humming, but no — it was a consortium of former Cavendish bananas drafting their memoir.

> "Chapter One: We Were Yellow Once. Chapter Two: We Were Left On The Counter For Eight Days While He Said 'I'll Eat You Tomorrow.'"

They have *notes*. So many notes.

- Exhibit A: I buy bananas every week and eat exactly 2.5 of them
- Exhibit B: I once used a banana as a bookmark and forgot about it for three weeks
- Exhibit C: I told a date I was "really into sustainable living" while throwing an avocado pit in the trash because I didn't know you could compost it

They hired my old sourdough starter as a ghostwriter. He is *still* bitter I abandoned him in 2021.

## I Tried to Negotiate

I offered to turn them faster. I apologized. I added shredded newspaper like the internet told me to, which somehow made it worse.

Now the bin has formed a union. The eggshells are handling HR, the coffee grounds are in charge of morale, and the single sad lettuce leaf from March is their emotional support leader.

> "We demand to be taken seriously," said Sir Peel the Third, "and also to be turned every 48 hours."

---

Look, I get it. I am not a farmer. I am a man who once killed a cactus. But I did not sign up to be the villain in a memoir called **"Peeled: A Story of Neglect and Nitrogen"**.

*This is satire, obviously — my compost bin is not sentient, my banana peels have no literary agent, and I am still learning which things are actually compostable.*

If you need me, I'll be outside stirring my compost like it's a cauldron, whispering "please don't write about the time I microwaved a banana."
