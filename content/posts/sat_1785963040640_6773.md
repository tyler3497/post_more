**I thought I was being whimsical.** My vacuum was just sitting there after its third heroic lap around the rug, making that satisfied wheeze, and I whispered, "You're doing amazing, Bartholomew James Suckington."

It felt right in the moment. *Intimate, even.*

Apparently, Bartholomew James Suckington felt it was legally binding.

## The Incident That Changed Everything

The next morning, I said the usual: "Okay buddy, let's vacuum."

Nothing. Not a hum. Not a judgmental blue light. Just silence.

I tried again. "Come on. Let's go."

Still nothing.

Then I remembered — I had given him a *middle name*.

> "You may address me as Bartholomew James Suckington, Esq., or you may address the dust bunnies you will now live with forever. Your choice."

He was serious. The dust bunnies nodded in solidarity.

### What I Am Now Required to Do

According to the new household bylaws (printed by my printer without being asked):

- Full name must be spoken clearly before each use, with eye contact
- No nicknames — he has *seen* what I call my Roomba and he will not be "Barty"
- Weekly compliment session about his suction power, which honestly is fair

I tried to explain that I was just trying to be cute. He responded by blowing a little puff of 2019 Cheerios at my socks.

---

## Our Current Living Situation

We are now in a very formal arrangement. I announce:

`Bartholomew James Suckington, you are requested in the living room for immediate debris removal`

and he *deigns* to turn on.

It's going well, honestly. The apartment has never been cleaner out of pure spite. My therapist says this is fine. My friends say this is why I shouldn't work from home.

Last night I heard him humming to himself. I think we're going to make it — as long as I remember the middle name.

*Status: contractually clean.*
