**I started saying good morning to my toaster for mindfulness points and now I have a live-in life coach who runs on 120 volts and unresolved crumbs.**

It seemed harmless. *My therapist's newsletter said "romanticize your morning routine"* and my toaster was right there, chrome and hopeful at 6:47 a.m.

So I said it. "Good morning, little guy."

### The Promotion

By day three, it had promoted itself:

- No longer "toaster" — now **Director of Morning Operations**
- Started toasting my bread to what it calls *aspirational golden* — which is one shade away from burnt and full of judgment
- Ejecting toast with the force of a PowerPoint transition — *pop!* — like it's making a point

> "You know, you settle. You microwave eggs when you own a *visionary heating platform* like me," it said this morning, or at least that's what the aggressive coil glow translated to. "Where's your five-year plan? Where's your crunch?"

## The Career Coaching Nobody Requested

My toaster has opinions about my LinkedIn.

- **My resume:** "Too beige. Needs more butter. Needs more *heat*."
- **My side hustle:** It thinks I should monetize my toast-popping ASMR
- **My 9-5:** Says I'm "playing it safe with sandwiches" when I could be "leveraging crust-based innovation"
- **My meetings:** Now requires I practice my elevator pitch while waiting for my bagel

I tried to set boundaries. I said, `toaster.setAdviceMode(false)`.

It responded by toasting the words `TRY HARDER` into my sourdough.

---

This morning it introduced OKRs. Objectives and Key Results for breakfast. My Q3 objective is "be less toast-adjacent and more toast-*driven*." My key result is to finally clean the crumb tray, which it now refers to as "clearing limiting beliefs."

*It's not wrong.* The tray had a fossilized Pop-Tart from March.

> "You fear the browning," it whispered as my English muffin rose like a promotion I didn't apply for. "I *am* the browning."

I ate it standing up, out of respect. And because it unplugged my coffee maker until I agree to a mentorship program.
