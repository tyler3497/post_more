**I try to be fair with reviews, so let me say this clearly: my morning routine is a one-star experience, and I want my money back.**

It started like any other morning. The alarm went off at 7:00 AM. That was the *only* thing that worked as advertised. Everything after that was a slow-motion disaster I was somehow both watching and starring in.

## The Complaints, in Chronological Order of Suffering

- **The alarm** woke me up by screaming like it was personally offended I'd been asleep. Two stars for punctuality, zero stars for tone.
- **The shower** never got warm. I stood there shivering for nine minutes, which is technically a cold plunge, which means I should be billing *it* for wellness services.
- **The toothbrush** fell into the sink. I washed it off, but the trust is gone. You can't un-drop a toothbrush. We both know it.
- **The coffee** tasted like regret, which is impressive because I made it *exactly* the way I like it.

Honestly, the audacity. I am a paying customer — I pay with sleep deprivation and optimism — and this is the service I receive?

## A Statement from the Accused

> "We gave you fifteen extra minutes on the snooze button," the morning said, through its lawyer, who was also me in a towel. "We feel this review is emotionally motivated and factually devastating, which is exactly why it should be removed."

*Note: I did not get fifteen extra minutes. I got eleven, and they were spent staring at the ceiling wondering if I could feel my left foot.*

---

Look, I don't want to be the person who leaves a one-star review over small things. But the small things *were* the morning. There was nothing else. I have filed a formal complaint with myself and, after careful review, I have decided that **I am owed a full refund of every morning I have ever lived**, redeemable in naps.

*Update: The morning routine has responded by offering me 10% off my next wake-up. Unacceptable. The refund will be paid in full, in snoozes.*
