**I FINALLY ASKED FOR CAREER ADVICE FROM THE MOST STABLE PRESENCE IN MY APARTMENT AND IT IMMEDIATELY STARTED SPINNING.**

*My ceiling fan has watched me work from home for 18 months without a single promotion, complaint, or performance review. I figured it had perspective.*

I was on my third coffee, staring at my laptop, whispering "what am I even doing" to no one in particular. The fan, on medium, wobbled slightly. I took it as a sign.

## The Consultation

I looked up and said, "Should I pivot to something more meaningful?"

It increased to high.

I said, "Like, should I go back to school? Start a pottery business? Become a person who owns linen pants?"

It did not stop. It never stops. That's its whole brand.

Within an hour, it had developed a full coaching framework:

- **Stay grounded** while keeping your head in the clouds (it is literally grounded to my ceiling, so rude)
- Embrace **360-degree thinking** — *its words, not mine, it only knows circles*
- Keep things circulating, even if it's just the same three anxieties on loop
- Avoid burnout by rotating slowly and making a gentle clicking noise no one can fix

> "Listen, I've been going in circles since 2019 and look at me — still employed, still relevant, still not falling down," said my ceiling fan, which is not OSHA certified to give advice. "You think progress has to be linear? Please. I've made a career out of returning to where I started and calling it airflow."

## The Performance Review

I tried to explain I need actionable steps, not aerodynamics as metaphor.

My fan replied with its annual review of me:

`career.trajectory = circular // status: consistent, if dizzy`

---

It then recommended I update my LinkedIn headline to "Experienced in Sustained Rotational Leadership and Low-Level Humming."

I have not taken its advice. *Mostly because I can't afford linen pants.*

But my apartment has never had better circulation, and my existential dread now comes with a cool breeze. My fan says that's called **work-life balance.**

*SATIRE / PARODY — 100% fictional, my ceiling fan is not a licensed career coach, no fans were consulted about my life choices, please do not take professional advice from appliances.*
