**I just wanted someone to tell me my life choices were fine.** Instead, I got a fern who thinks I'm emotionally unavailable and a succulent who charges for eye contact.

It started innocently. I was repotting Gerald (the monstera) and muttered, "Am I doing okay?" Gerald's leaves rustled in what I chose to interpret as encouragement. Big mistake. By the weekend, I'd arranged all eleven plants in a circle on the living room rug and was crying into a pothos.

## The session went downhill fast

- The snake plant opened by saying, "We've noticed you've been *overwatering* some of us and *neglecting* others. Classic anxious attachment."
- The cactus refused to speak but communicated through what I can only describe as a pointed silence.
- The basil billed me for the session. In fertilizer.

> "You're not watering us," Gerald said, adjusting leaves I swear were shaped like tiny glasses. "You're watering your *guilt*. We need boundaries."

## Now there's a schedule

They've taped a "sunlight rotation chart" to the window. I'm on it, listed as "the tall one who cries." I get indirect light, emotionally and literally. The fern now withholds new growth until I journal. The succulent only accepts compliments on Tuesdays.

Honestly? My posture has improved. My therapist — a real one, a human — says I should probably stop attending the plant sessions. But Gerald just told me *she* sounds threatened, and frankly, he has a point.
