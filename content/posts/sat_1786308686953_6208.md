## The Day I Betrayed My Own Bloodstream

**I switched to decaf to be healthier.** That was the sentence I said out loud to my kitchen on Monday, holding a bag of Swiss Water Process beans like they'd just been pardoned by the governor.

My old routine — *you know, the one where I vibrate at the frequency of a hummingbird on quarterly taxes* — did not take it well.

> "You were supposed to be the strong one," whispered my French press, who has seen things no carafe should see.

By Tuesday, things escalated.

### Exhibit B: My Morning Has Unionized Against Me

Official complaints filed by my former morning routine include:

- **My alarm clock** now plays one long, disappointed sigh instead of ringing
- **My to-do list app** auto-adds "contemplate betrayal" at 6:45 a.m.
- **My cat** inspected the mug, walked away, and immediately knocked the decaf bag into the trash — *very editorial*
- **My will to answer emails before 10** has been listed as `status: unreachable`

I tried to explain it was doctor-ish advice I read on a wellness infographic while on hold with the pharmacy.

> "I still have the ritual!" I told my kitchen counter at 7:03 a.m., clutching a lukewarm, soulfully confused cup. "It's the same! Just without the molecule that makes me feel alive!"

My countertop did not respond. It has never responded. This made it the most supportive entity present.

---

So now I'm in negotiations. Decaf me gets `steady hands` and `fewer 2 p.m. crashes`. Regular me gets to keep his identity as a person who can open a PDF before 8 a.m.

We're meeting in the middle with half-caf, which tastes like compromise and poor communication.
