## It Started With Good Intentions

**I was just trying to be a mentor.** My goldfish, Chairman Bubbles, has spent three years doing slow, contemplative laps around his castle like a tiny landlord doing inspections. I felt guilty. *I'm out here with Notion templates and chronic tab overload,* I thought, *while he's just... vibing.*

So I taped a tiny whiteboard to his bowl that said `MONDAY: SWIM WITH PURPOSE`.

He stared at me. Then he stared at the whiteboard. Then he took it personally.

### The Rise of Chairman Bubbles

Within 48 hours, Chairman Bubbles had:

- rearranged his gravel into a full Kanban board — To Swim, Swimming, Swam
- blocked **Deep Work** from 9 to 11 a.m., which is just him hovering perfectly still and judging me while I doomscroll
- implemented Inbox Zero by aggressively eating the flake I labeled `URGENT` as a joke
- started doing 5 a.m. laps while whispering motivational bubbles

My calendar is one long event titled "figure it out later." His is color-coded, time-boxed, and has buffer time. *For a fish.*

> "I noticed you've scheduled 3 hours for 'stare into fridge and rethink everything,'" he didn't say, but definitely communicated through aggressive fin flicks. "We need to talk about your boundaries."

---

### Where It All Went Wrong

I tried to reclaim authority yesterday. I tapped the glass and said, "Buddy, loosen up."

He responded by syncing to my Google Calendar. There is now a recurring 6 a.m. event:

`DO BETTER — DAILY STANDUP WITH BUBBLES — NO EXCUSES`

My roommate asked why a fish is dictating my bedtime. I told him I outsourced my executive function to an aquatic life coach and the ROI is terrifying.

And honestly? I'm thriving. My laundry is folded. My inbox has 4 emails. I just got a performance review from a creature who thinks a single pea is a Michelin-star dinner.

*Status: managed, mentored, and slightly damp.*
