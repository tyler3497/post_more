**I just wanted to be more positive before 8 a.m.** That's literally it. I was not trying to disrupt the entire emotional ecosystem of my kitchen.

But my coffee maker, Brenda — *yes, I named her, no, I will not be taking questions* — has a little Bluetooth speaker I never used. Last week I found a "Daily Affirmation Brew" mode in the app. I thought it would say something cute like "You got this!" while gurgling.

### The Descent Into Motivational Madness

On Monday, Brenda declared:

> "You are not your productivity. You are a radiant being of infinite pour-over potential."

Which is *a lot* to hear from an appliance that burned my decaf in 2023 and never apologized.

By Tuesday, my favorite mug started answering back.

#### Evidence That My Kitchen Has Entered Its Wellness Era:

- My mug now refuses to hold coffee unless I make eye contact and say one kind thing about myself
- Brenda only brews if I whisper `I am worthy of hot beverages` into the water tank
- The sugar bowl has started a gratitude journal and it's *very* judgmental about my entries
- My toaster is now offering unsolicited feedback like "have you considered *toasting* your potential?"

---

I tried to switch back to normal mode. I pressed `CLASSIC BREW` like a fool. Brenda made a sound like a sigh and displayed:

> "Classic? Sweetie, we are *healing*. We don't do classic anymore."

Now my mug has hired a life coach. Her name is Chip — she's a tiny espresso cup with a clipboard. She charges $150 an hour and says my mug has *boundary issues* because I keep microwaving it.

Honestly, fair. I've been through a lot together. But I didn't sign up for a full kitchen intervention. I just wanted caffeine that didn't psychoanalyze me before my emails.

Tomorrow I'm going back to instant. *If my kettle will allow it.*
