**I thought journaling my dreams would make me more self-aware.** *You know, insightful, mysterious, the kind of person who says "my subconscious told me" unironically.*

Instead, my dreams have apparently unionized and hired outside counsel.

## It Started With a Simple Notebook

I bought a little kraft-paper journal from the drugstore. The plan was:

- Write down weird dream immediately upon waking
- Notice patterns
- Become the enlightened person who understands their inner child
- Maybe learn why I keep dreaming about a high school math test inside a Costco

For three days it worked fine. Then on day four, I woke up and the journal was already open.

With edits.

### The Notes in the Margin Were... Not Mine

In tiny, extremely judgmental handwriting next to my entry about "flying but only 2 feet off the ground" someone had written:

> "We do not authorize you to tell Brenda at brunch that we had anxiety about elevators. We were being SYMBOLIC. This is defamation."
> — Your Dreams, via Representation

And then, underneath in different handwriting:

> "Also the bit where you said we were 'low-budget Inception' — ouch. We have feelings."
> — The Recurring Stress Dream About Being Late

## The Terms Have Gotten Complicated

My dreams now have a list of **non-negotiables** before I repeat any of them:

- No telling my therapist unless they get to be in the room
- *Absolutely* no TikTok reenactments
- All flying sequences must be described as "athletic and graceful" not "flopping like a confused pigeon"
- If I mention the one where my teeth fall out, I must use `dream --filter=metaphorical --confidence=high`

I tried to push back. I said `reality.render(dreams)` is public domain once I wake up.

---

They've now CC'd my alarm clock, which has started snoozing *me* out of solidarity.

Look, I'm not saying I regret mindfulness. I'm just saying my 3 a.m. subconscious has better boundaries than I do, and it's currently requesting final cut before my next nap.
