**I have never been an organized person.** *Structurally, I'm a junk-drawer human in a Marie Kondo world.*

Last weekend, I decided to fix that. Not with therapy. Not with cleaning. With **aesthetic**.

I reorganized my entire bookshelf by color.

## The Vision

It was supposed to be tasteful. You know — those Instagram photos where books go from white to beige to sage to terracotta like a sunset that also reads. Very calm. Very "I have my life together."

It worked for exactly 12 minutes.

Then my cookbooks noticed they were all orange.

- My 2013 *30-Minute Meals* is now having a full identity crisis because it's actually more paprika than persimmon
- My vegan baking book is convinced it's being typecast
- My grandma's handwritten soup notebook (literally brown) was placed in "Autumnal Neutrals" and took it as a personal attack on her vibes

> "We didn't spend YEARS teaching you how to not burn garlic bread just to be reduced to a color swatch, Tyler," whispered the stack of orange spines in unison. "We have DEPTH. We have SOUL. We are SAFFRON."

I tried to explain it was *just for looks*.

They did not care.

Now I can't cook without apologizing. I opened the curry one yesterday and it just said, *"Oh, so NOW you see my inner complexity? After you color-coded my trauma?"*

My fiction section is smug because they're all tastefully arranged in blue-gray gradient. My self-help books are pretending they don't know the cookbooks at all.
