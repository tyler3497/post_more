**I was trying to be a more intentional plant dad.** You know, like those people on Instagram who whisper to their ferns and name them.

*My monstera is named Kevin.* Kevin has been with me for two years and has never once paid rent or offered emotional support, which honestly makes us perfect for each other.

Last Saturday, my router asked me to reset the Wi-Fi password. In a moment of what I thought was *enlightened co-living*, I decided to let Kevin decide.

## How Kevin Chose Violence

I put six password options on little pieces of paper in front of him:

- Photosynthesis4Ever!
- SunLightBoi2026
- WaterMeMaybe?
- LeafItAlone123
- KevinIsMyLord
- rootrot lol

I watched to see which leaf leaned toward first. Kevin, being a monstera, did absolutely nothing for 40 minutes, then dropped a single dead leaf directly onto `Photosynthesis4Ever!` 

I typed it in.

> "That's a beautiful choice, Kevin. Very on brand. Very you. We are going to have such great internet together."

Reader, we did not have great internet together.

`Photosynthesis4Ever!` is apparently **21 characters**, which my router from 2014 interprets as a personal attack. It blinked red, made a sound like a dying Tamagotchi, and gave up on life, on me, and on the concept of connectivity.

### The Aftermath, Ranked by Severity

- My laptop now thinks the Wi-Fi is a *suggestion* rather than a service
- My TV has been stuck on the same frozen frame of a cooking show for 3 days — this man has been whisking eggs forever
- Kevin, meanwhile, looks *thrilled*
- I have had to tell my group chat "sorry my plant changed the password" which is a sentence that ends friendships

---

I tried to fix it. I really did. I went to `192.168.1.1` like a real adult, stared at the admin page, typed `admin` / `password`, and whispered `kevin.forgive()` into the void.

Nothing happened, obviously, because plants don't know cybersecurity, they just know vibes.

Now we sit in companionable silence. No emails. No doomscrolling. Just me and Kevin and a blinking red router that we have agreed to view as *ambient mood lighting*.

My therapist said I needed boundaries with technology. I didn't think she meant this.
