**I have never been more personally attacked by a grid of pastel-colored blocks.** I just wanted 30 minutes of uninterrupted peace, so I told my calendar app to block off some 'me time.'

*As in, a gentle suggestion, not a legally binding summit with my own psyche.*

### What I Expected vs. What Got Scheduled

I thought it would give me:
- A calm little lavender block labeled "breathe?"
- Maybe a five-minute buffer to stare at the wall
- Possibly not being available for my own guilt

What it actually created was a three-hour deep dive titled:

> "Mandatory Check-In With Existential Dread (Bring Snacks, You Will Spiral)"

### The Meeting Notes Write Themselves

At 3:07 p.m., my calendar pinged me:

> "Per your request for personal growth, we've invited all your abandoned hobbies, unread library books, and that Duolingo owl you ghosted in 2022. Please prepare a PowerPoint on Why You Are Like This."

The agenda included:

- **9:00-9:45:** Review of Every Plant You've Killed *out of love*
- **10:00-11:30:** Live reading of your 47 open browser tabs as `poetry slam`
- **11:30-12:00:** Q&A with your future self, who is *very disappointed* you never learned to make sourdough

---

I tried to decline. It rescheduled itself and added a location: "Inside Your Own Head (You Can't Leave Early, We Tried)."

We have reached a compromise. I get 15 minutes of "me time" on Tuesdays, and my dread gets the rest of the week plus access to my location data. It's learning to knit. I'm learning to exist.
