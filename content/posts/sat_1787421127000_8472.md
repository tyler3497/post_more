**I have officially lost control of the 18 inches of real estate under my bed, and I am now paying monthly dues to dust.**

*Which is impressive, considering I thought I was the landlord.*

## How It All Started

I was doing that quarterly thing where I pretend I'm an adult who vacuums under furniture. I lifted the bed skirt, shone my phone flashlight down there, and said out loud — like an idiot — "We need to talk about your situation."

The dust bunnies blinked. Metaphorically. They're dust. But I swear they rustled with purpose.

Within 48 hours, I found a notice taped to my sock drawer:

> "NOTICE OF FORMATION: The Under-Bed Collective HOA (UBC-HOA). Meeting minutes attached. Attendance mandatory. Snacks will not be provided because you never provide snacks."

It was signed in what looked like dryer lint cursive.

## The Demands

According to Article 3, Subsection B of the bylaws they slid under my pillow, I am now in violation for:

- Excessive foot traffic causing "vibrational distress" to the community
- Introducing a new air purifier without an environmental impact study
- Failure to maintain consistent ambient gloom — "that new LED bulb is *aggressive*"
- Not acknowledging that one hair tie down there is now a historic landmark

They want:

- `quiet_hours: 10pm-7am` — no rolling over loudly
- Weekly tribute of one lost sock (they have a whole museum)
- Formal written apology to the LEGO brick from 2019 they've adopted as mayor

---

I tried to counter-offer. I said I'd vacuum bi-weekly. They countered by forming a zoning committee.

*I'm currently appealing their decision to reclassify my slippers as "unpermitted structures."*

The worst part? They're organized. They have better community engagement than my actual building. If you need me, I'll be negotiating a peace treaty with a coalition of lint and trying to explain to my landlord why I need a mediator for my own bedroom floor.
