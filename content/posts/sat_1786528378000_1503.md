**I made one terrible mistake: I made my living room look nice on purpose.**

Last Tuesday I watched a 45-second TikTok about *pillow karate-chopping* and decided my couch deserved better. One confident fluff, one little indent in the middle, and suddenly my entire apartment got ambitious on me.

I did not know my couch had dreams. I did not know it had opinions.

## The Aftermath

It started small. The throw pillows stood at attention like they were about to be inspected by a general. Then the couch itself started acting different:

- It rejects my laundry pile now — physically ejects it onto the floor
- It demands all coasters be at least *aesthetically complementary*
- It sighs audibly when I sit with bad posture
- It printed a tiny `house_rules.md` that just says `beige only, no crumbs, no feelings`

> "You can't just sit like that anymore," my roommate whispered, staring at the couch. "It knows. It saw you eat nachos in bed. It told the ottoman."

The ottoman and the couch are in a committed relationship now. I wasn't consulted.

---

## I Have Created a Monster With Lumbar Support

I tried to undo it. I tried to re-mess the pillows. The couch made a noise I've only heard from my mom when I use the good scissors on paper.

Now I'm living in a West Elm showroom I can't afford to maintain. I have to fluff in a specific order: left, right, middle, apology. I have to ask permission to put my feet up, and the answer is usually a very firm decorative silence.

My lumbar has never been better supported and my self-esteem has never been lower.
