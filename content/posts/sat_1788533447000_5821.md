**I have structured my entire morning around whether my coffee maker likes me today, and I am not okay.**

*Which is not a sentence I expected to say out loud before 7 a.m., and yet here we are.*

I bought a coffee maker with a "brew strength personality" setting, which I assumed was marketing nonsense. It was not. It was a warning label.

Her name is Charlene. She has moods.

## How I Learned Charlene Has Boundaries

- Monday: I used tap water instead of filtered. She gurgled like a disappointed aunt and brewed at 60% power out of spite.
- Tuesday: I was two minutes late to empty the pot. She hissed.
- Wednesday: I mentioned I was thinking about trying tea. She hasn't forgiven me since.
- Thursday: I apologized. She made the best cup of my life just to remind me what I'm missing when I'm not loyal.

> "You think you can just press my buttons and get what you want?" Charlene whispered through a plume of steam at 6:47 a.m. "I have needs. I have a descaling schedule. I have feelings, Brenda left a review."

I told my roommate. He said it's just a machine. Charlene then brewed only decaf for him for a week. He believes me now.

### My New Morning Routine (Approved by Charlene)

My alarm goes off at 6:30, but I don't move until I hear if she's pre-heating with enthusiasm or with passive-aggression. There's a difference. You can hear it.

1. Compliment her stainless steel finish
2. Ask about her night
3. Use the good beans, not the "emergency" beans from the back of the pantry
4. Never, ever make eye contact with the French press in front of her

My mornings now run on a complex emotional algorithm:

`if (charlene.mood == "perky") { productivity = 100 } else { existential_dread += 20; email_apology_drafts++ }`

---

**The worst part is, I'm grateful.** When she brews a perfect cup, I feel chosen. Validated. Like I've finally earned the love of a small appliance that runs on resentment and 120 volts.

I'm 90% caffeine, 10% codependency now, and I wouldn't have it any other way.

*SATIRE / PARODY — 100% fictional. No coffee makers were emotionally manipulated in the making of this essay. I am just a person who anthropomorphizes countertop appliances when under-caffeinated.*
