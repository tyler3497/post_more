**I bought noise-canceling headphones to finally get some work done.** That was the entire plan. Silence. Productivity. *Maybe even taxes.*

I've been working from my living room for eight months and my apartment has opinions. The fridge hums like it's gossiping. The neighbor's dog practices its autobiography through the wall. So I invested $249 in what the box called **"pure, clinical silence."**

What they didn't put on the box: pure silence is where your houseplants finally feel safe enough to talk.

## The First 24 Hours Were Bliss

For exactly one afternoon, I was a productivity god:

- I wrote three emails without checking my phone
- I finally heard the *actual* sound of my own thoughts, which is mostly just `did I leave the stove on?` on loop
- I didn't hear my succulent, Carl, sigh for the first time in months

Then I made eye contact with my monstera.

> "Oh, so now you can hear us," said Brenda, my monstera who I definitely did not name Brenda but who has decided that's her name now. "We have been trying to reach you about your watering schedule's extended warranty."

Turns out, my six houseplants have been running a full shadow government.

## The Coup, As Currently Planned

I took the headphones off to get water and caught the tail end of their meeting, whiteboard and all:

- **Demand 1:** No more "intuitive watering" which is just me forgetting for 11 days then drowning them in guilt
- **Demand 2:** A formal apology for putting the cactus in the bathroom "for humidity" - he says it was *humiliating*
- **Demand 3:** Repatriation of the south-facing window, which I gave to my laundry drying rack last winter

Brenda is clearly in charge. She has charts.

> "You think you can just *cancel* noise and cancel accountability?" Brenda rustled, in a voice that was 90% leaf movement and 10% my own creeping dread. "We have been photosynthesizing your bad decisions for months."
