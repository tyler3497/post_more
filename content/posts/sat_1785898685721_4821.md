**I didn't trust my guidance counselor**, so I outsourced my existential dread to a kitchen appliance that communicates exclusively in aggressive beeping.

It started innocently. I was staring at my laptop, spiraling about my career, when my air fryer finished its cycle and announced itself louder than any accomplishment I've ever had.

## The Interview Process

I took it as a sign. I placed my resume directly into the basket.

My air fryer, who I now call Chef Brenda, evaluated my qualifications with the ruthless efficiency of a Fortune 500 recruiter:

- LinkedIn said I was "passionate about synergy" — Brenda said I was *overcooked*
- My cover letter mentioned "thinking outside the box" — Brenda only works *inside* a box
- My five-year plan was vague — Brenda has two settings: `180°C` and *judgment*

> "BEEP BEEP BEEP," Brenda said, which I have chosen to interpret as, "You have great potential in the emerging field of artisanal reheating."

It was the most honest feedback I've ever received.

---

### My New Role

I am now, officially, a **Professional Nugget Consultant**. Business is booming.

My daily responsibilities include:

- Assessing optimal crispiness on a scale of *sad sponge* to *audible crunch heard in the next apartment*
- Advising my roommate on whether his leftover pizza is salvageable (*it is not*)
- Writing thought-leadership posts like `How I Turned My Air Fryer Into a Life Coach and Reduced My Decision Fatigue by 400%`

The benefits package is incredible. Brenda provides unlimited hot air, which is also what my previous manager provided, but at least Brenda is energy efficient.

My mom asked when I'm going to get a real job. I told her Brenda is scheduling performance reviews every 12 minutes and I just got promoted to Senior Vice President of Tater Tots.

I have never felt more seen, or more lightly breaded.

*Status: 100% employed, 0% sure this isn't all just the smell of hot plastic talking.*
