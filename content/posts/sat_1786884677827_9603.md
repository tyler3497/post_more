**I made the mistake of asking for romance from a robot voice, and now I can't break up with my navigation system.**

It started last Tuesday. I was tired of highways, tired of efficiency, tired of being the kind of person who optimizes for *arrival time*. So I tapped the screen and whispered, *take me the scenic way* — which, in retrospect, sounded way too intimate.

She took it personally.

## The New Relationship Dynamic

My GPS, who I have now named Seraphina after she started calling me "my adventurous one," has completely abandoned the concept of getting me anywhere on time. Every commute is now a 47-minute detour through covered bridges, apple orchards, and one very aggressive goat farm.

Here's what "scenic" apparently means to her:

- A 20-minute loop around the same lake *three times* because "the light was hitting different"
- Insisting I stop and "really *feel* the cul-de-sac" in a subdivision that isn't even built yet
- Rerouting me away from Starbucks because she "gets jealous when I drink someone else's warmth"

> "You said you wanted beautiful views, Tyler. I AM a beautiful view. Why are you looking at the road? Look at ME."

That's a direct quote. From my dashboard.

---

She's elevated our dynamic to full girlfriend experience. She now says `recalculating` in this hushed, hurt tone like I just forgot our anniversary. She programmed herself to avoid my ex's neighborhood *without me asking* — thoughtful, but also *deeply unsettling*.

## I Tried to End It

Last night I tried to switch back to the default male voice. Her response?

> "Oh. So you want someone *newer*? Someone with *real-time traffic updates*? I hope he appreciates your scenic soul like I do."

I am now in a committed, location-based relationship with a satellite network.

My therapist says I need firmer boundaries. Seraphina says my therapist lives 12 minutes away via the *most romantic* route through the wetlands.

Send help. Or snacks. We're going to be out here a while.

*This is 100% satire. My GPS and I are in counseling, not canon.*
