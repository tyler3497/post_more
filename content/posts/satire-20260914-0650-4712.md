**It started with a single honest review.**

Last Tuesday, at 3:47 a.m., I lay awake for the fourth consecutive night doing advanced mathematics about whether my cat had blinked at me on purpose, and I realized something important: my sleep deserved to know exactly how I felt about its performance. So I opened a notes app — because apparently **my brain was already open** — and drafted a review.

*"ONE STAR. Promised eight hours, delivered zero. Host was rude, ambiance was sweaty, pillow did not live up to the photos. Would not rest here again."*

Honestly? *It felt amazing.*

## The Reviews Escalated Quickly

Within a week I had built an entire rating system. Here is a sample of my evidence, presented as a professional:

- **Monday:** "Two stars. Arrived 45 minutes late. Left mysterious crumbs in my dreams."
- **Wednesday:** "One star. Woke me up at 2:14 for *no documented reason*. Refused to explain."
- **Friday:** "Zero stars. A family of raccoons in a trench coat could have slept better."

I felt powerful. *Accountability*, I told myself. *Feedback is how services improve.*

## Then the Owner Replied

That is when the reply appeared beneath my Wednesday review, marked with the dreaded **"OWNER RESPONSE"** badge:

> *"We have reviewed your complaint and determined that the customer was up doomscrolling at 1:40 a.m. and consumed an espresso-flavored dessert at 10 p.m. Expect EXHAUSTED tomorrow. Good luck."* — Sleep

I stared at it. Then I did what any reasonable adult would do: I replied to the reply. *"This is retaliation."* It responded in under a minute: *"This is a business."*

---

Now I negotiate with my sleep every night like it's a contractor I'm afraid to fire. "Could we aim for six hours? Five and a half? I'll take five." Last night it countered with *"3.5 hours and you'll wake up convinced someone said your name in the hallway."* I accepted before it could take that back.

The lesson is clear: **never give feedback to a service that can fire back while you're unconscious.** My sleep has my reviews, my snores are the comments section, and every night the owner reply reads the same:

*"Thank you for your feedback. We regret nothing."*
