**I did it. I folded the fitted sheet. Perfectly. On the first try. Without swearing.**

*This sentence has never been typed before in human history.*

For 12 years, my relationship with fitted sheets has been what experts call "aggressively wadded." I would wash them, stare at the elastic corners like they were a riddle from a sphinx, and then just ball them up and shove them in the closet while whispering "that's storage."

But last Tuesday, at 2:47 a.m., driven by insomnia and a YouTube video titled "ONE WEIRD TRICK LINEN COMPANIES HATE," I learned.

## The Ritual

The video was 14 minutes long. It had 3 views. A calm woman in a cardigan demonstrated the sacred art:

- Tuck corner A into corner B like you're tucking in a very flat burrito
- Find the elastic seam and *befriend it* instead of fighting it
- Fold in thirds, breathe, question your life choices
- Pretend you know what you're doing with confidence

I followed along. I executed `fold(matrix).transcend()` in real time.

And then — a rectangle. A real, actual rectangle. With corners.

> "What is that?" my roommate asked, backing away slowly. "Where did you get a flat sheet that looks like that?"

I told him it was the fitted sheet.

He did not believe me.

### The Aftermath

Now my family thinks I've joined something.

- My mom asked if I'm "in a very organized religion now"
- My sister asked me to fold *her* fitted sheets for $5, which means I've monetized enlightenment
- My dad stared at my linen closet and said, "That's unnatural. Sheets aren't supposed to do that"
- I have been invited to *three* neighborhood Facebook groups about "home hacks that will change your life"

---

I tried to explain it was just tucking elastic. They said that's exactly what someone in a sheet cult would say.

I now conduct weekly demonstrations in my living room. I charge in iced coffee. Last week, four people watched me fold a queen-size fitted sheet and someone gasped when the corners aligned.

**I have peaked.** There is nowhere to go from here but to fitted sheet influencer status.
