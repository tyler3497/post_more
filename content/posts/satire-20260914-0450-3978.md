**I have been evicted from literary society by birds with brains the size of walnuts.**

It started last Tuesday. I noticed the pigeons on my fire escape sitting in a circle. An actual circle. One of them had a paperback. I told myself it was probably just a torn receipt.

## The Evidence

By Thursday, the situation had escalated considerably:

- There were **seven** of them, arranged by height, like a church choir
- The gray one with the limp was clearly leading the discussion, *flapping* for emphasis
- They had somehow acquired reading glasses — the tiny wire-frame kind, don't ask me how
- One pigeon kept side-eyeing me through my own window. In my own apartment.

I brought out my copy of a very serious, very thick novel I'd been *pretending* to read for four months. I held it up to the glass. The gray one looked at it, looked at me, and then deliberately turned a page of its own book.

> "I didn't come here to be judged by something that eats discarded bagels," I whispered. "And yet here we are."

## The Verdict

Yesterday I left a self-help book on the sill as a peace offering. They stared at it for twenty minutes, and then — I swear this happened — one of them nudged it off the edge. It fell three stories and exploded into inspirational chapters on the sidewalk.

I get it, okay? My bookshelf is seventy percent books I've never opened. But being *shamed* by pigeons is where I draw the line.

**Satire:** This is a work of parody. No pigeons were intellectually advanced in the making of this post.
