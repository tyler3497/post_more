**I want to be clear: the elevator brought this on itself.**

It all started Tuesday. I stepped into the elevator alone, which everyone knows is an invitation to share your deepest ambitions with a confined box that cannot leave. So I told it my five-year plan. Not the résumé version. The *real* one.

## The Plan, As Presented

- Land a fulfilling job by year two (fulfilling defined as: pays me, occasionally compliments me)
- Learn a second language — specifically, enough Italian to argue with waiters
- Run a 10K, walk a 5K, and emotionally commit to neither
- Achieve "glow-up era," whatever that costs

The doors said nothing, which I interpreted as supportive.

By Wednesday, the "up" button was dark. I pressed it anyway. The elevator hummed, considered my life choices, and took me **down to the lobby**. I live on the ninth floor. The lobby is not where I was going. *Bold statement*, I thought.

> "Maybe it just needs maintenance," said my neighbor, Karen, a woman who believes her thermostat is "going through something."

Maintenance came Thursday. The technician shrugged, replaced nothing, and told me the elevator "seems fine," which is what people say about relationships they're too tired to end.

## Escalation

Friday, I tried the stairs out of spite. Big mistake. Eleven flights later I had achieved exactly one item from the five-year plan: emotional commitment to suffering. The elevator watched me collapse in the hallway. I swear I heard it `ding` in a judgmental key.

Saturday, I attempted negotiation. I stood inside and offered a revised plan — a *humble* plan, the kind where I just floss more. The doors closed. We descended. We have never once gone up since Tuesday.

---

I have consulted three experts: a building manager (unhelpful), an astrologer (surprisingly helpful), and a man on the bus who said the elevator is "holding up a mirror." I don't need a mirror. I need to get to my floor.

**Update:** I now take the stairs exclusively. My five-year plan has been amended. The only goal is this: get off at nine. The elevator is gone from the list entirely.

*Editor's note: this is satire. No elevators were harmed, and the author's Italian remains purely argumentative.*
