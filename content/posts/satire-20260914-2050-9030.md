**It started with an act of basic audio hygiene.** I keep my left and right earbuds in separate pockets, and one night I found them rolling around the bottom of my bag, complete strangers. "You two should meet," I said, placing them side by side on my desk. I went to bed feeling like a matchmaker.

I woke up a third wheel.

## The Courtship

By morning, the right earbud had shared its charge. *Shared its charge.* In earbud culture, that's basically proposing. The left one kept blinking its little blue light, which I have chosen to interpret as blushing.

## The Evidence That This Is Getting Serious

- The right earbud now refuses to pair unless the left one is in the case. The device name changed overnight to `HIS_AND_HERS_FINAL_v2`.
- They hold a charge together but drain separately — one dies mid-podcast so the other can "have its moment." I did not program this. I do not know how to un-program it.
- Last Tuesday I found a tiny origami ring box folded from a grocery receipt. The ring inside was a sesame seed.

The wedding is, apparently, in my desk drawer. The save-the-date arrived as a push notification.

## The Guest List Situation

Here's where it gets tense. My phone charger was invited, then uninvited, then re-invited "with plus-one (the wall outlet)." My laptop got a save-the-date but not an invitation, and it has been opening and closing its lid passive-aggressively for a week.

> "You're either in the wedding party or you're in the junk drawer," the right earbud blinked at me. I have decided that's what the blinking meant.

## The Vows (Leaked to Me by the Case)

The charging case printed the program. Actually, the case printed nothing — it's a case. But a note appeared inside it, in my own handwriting, which is concerning on several levels:

> "In sickness (low battery) and in health (100%), in airplane mode and out — I promise to always be the left to your right."

**I'm officiating.** I don't know how this happened. Google says earbud weddings aren't legally binding. Embarrassed on their behalf.

The honeymoon is two weeks in the case with the lid closed. Do not disturb.
