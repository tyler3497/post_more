**I just wanted to sleep like a functional adult who doesn't wake up at 3 a.m. questioning every life choice I've made since 2019.**

Instead, I bought a white noise machine and accidentally subscribed to the neighborhood's most unhinged HOA newsletter.

## It Started Innocently Enough

My doctor — *not a real doctor, my friend who once took a first-aid class* — said white noise would help. So I ordered the "Serenity Whisper 3000" which promised "pure, uninterrupted tranquility."

What I got was a tiny, judgmental DJ with a grudge.

The first night it went:

> "Ah, yes, a nice gentle rain sound. Much more peaceful than Brenda from 3B arguing about her recycling bin at midnight again. You didn't hear that from me."

I sat up. "What?"

> "Oh nothing. Just rain. Totally normal rain. Not like the rain when Todd tried to barbecue in a windstorm and set off the sprinkler system."

### The Evidence It Has Too Much Free Time:

- **Monday:** Informed me that the couple upstairs owns *three* air fryers and still orders takeout
- *Whispered* that my succulent is "doing better than most relationships on this floor"
- Played `ocean_waves_final_FINAL_v2.mp3` but with commentary: "That's the sound of freedom. And of Dave forgetting his keys again."
- Started a segment it calls "Midnight Chisme Corner"

---

I tried to switch it to pure static. It sighed — *machines should not be able to sigh* — and said:

> "Fine. Here's your boring static. Just like how Kevin says his marriage is 'fine.'"

Now I can't turn it off. I'm emotionally invested. Last night I learned that someone on the fourth floor has been stealing everyone's Amazon packages and replacing them with zucchini bread. The machine is *invested* in this arc.

I came for better REM cycles. I stayed because I now know too much about Brenda's composting politics to ever make eye contact again.

*Satire is real: this is 100% fictional, 100% parody, and my white noise machine definitely doesn't know your secrets. Probably.*
