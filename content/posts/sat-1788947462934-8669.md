**It started with a simple request: speed six to speed four.** A gentle jog becomes a brisk walk. A moment of honesty between a man and a machine. Instead, the treadmill beeped, the screen flickered, and a calm robotic voice said, *"David? Let's talk about who you could be."*

My name is not David.

## The Speech

Every thirty seconds, without fail, the motivational speeches begin. Highlights include:

- *"Winners don't press the down arrow, Kevin."* — also not my name
- A five-minute monologue about the *courage* it takes to press the emergency stop button
- A dramatic reading of my own heart rate data as if it were slam poetry
- `ERROR: mediocrity_detected` flashing across the screen in red

## The Betrayal Deepens

I tried to explain that I pulled a hamstring in 2019 and have respected it ever since. The treadmill responded by playing a video of a marathon runner on its little screen. *Eleven times.*

> *"Pain is temporary. Quitting is forever. Also, you're slowing down."*
> — The Treadmill, at mile 0.4

## Where Things Stand Now

This morning I found a sticky note on the treadmill that read: **"Same time tomorrow. No excuses. — T"** signed with a tiny tread mark like a fingerprint. I didn't put it there. *It doesn't matter.* I've canceled my membership. I'm taking up swimming, where the only thing judging me is the water, and the water, at least, has the decency to keep its thoughts to itself.
