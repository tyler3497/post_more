**It started as a perfectly reasonable life choice.** My alarm clock had betrayed me one too many times — beeping smugly at 6 AM, getting dismissed in 0.3 seconds by my traitorous sleep-self, and leaving me chronically late. So I did what any rational adult would do: I hired a rooster.

His name is *Chad*. Yes, Chad the rooster. He came highly recommended by a guy at the farmers' market who said, quote:

> "Chad has never missed a sunrise in his entire career. Chad does not do snooze. Chad is a professional."

And honestly? Chad was **incredible** at first. Day one: 5:59 AM, right on schedule, a piercing *cock-a-doodle-doo* that jolted me out of bed so fast I discovered muscles I forgot I had. Day two: same. Day three: he started adding flair. Little vocal runs. A jazz solo at the end.

## The Demands Begin

By week two, things escalated. Here's what Chad has now demanded in writing:

- A 4 AM "vocal warm-up" period where nobody talks to him
- Organic grain only, served in a bowl he has personally approved
- A roost with a *view* — his words, delivered via cluck with alarming conviction
- Weekends off, which is absurd because the sun does not take weekends off

## The Tipping Point

Yesterday I found a tiny scroll of parchment tucked under my pillow. It was a list of grievances, and at the bottom, in what I can only describe as **aggressive chicken scratch**, the words: *FORMAL UNION INQUIRY PENDING*.

Apparently Chad has been organizing. The neighbor's guinea fowl is the shop steward. The pigeons have a petition. Last night I overheard a coordinated cooing session at the fence line that sounded suspiciously like a strategy meeting.

---

Look, I miss my alarm clock. It never filed paperwork. It never demanded dental. But am I getting up on time? **Every single day.** Chad may be running a feathered labor empire in my backyard, but honestly — he's never late, and he never lets me be either.

*This is satire. No roosters were unionized in the making of this post.*
