**I thought I was being a good plant parent.** Turns out I was creating a monster. Literally.

Last month I read that *talking to your plants helps them grow*, which felt like low effort until I realized I have nothing interesting to say to a fern. So I upgraded. I put on some Mozart for my monstera, Geraldine. Just a little background culture while I worked.

Within 48 hours, Geraldine had opinions.

## The Critique Era Begins

By day three, she had leaned *entirely* away from the speaker during Debussy, which I took as a review. By day five, she was dropping leaves whenever I played anything after 1900.

Then she saw my Spotify Wrapped.

I left my laptop open. Rookie mistake. My top genre was *“lo-fi chill beats to water plants to,”* which is apparently deeply offensive if you ARE the plant.

Evidence Geraldine has presented for my musical malpractice:

- I listened to the same **“Sad Girl Autumn”** playlist 847 times and she had to hear every single one
- I skipped Beethoven’s 5th after 12 seconds to play the *Kitchen Tile Commercial jingle*
- My most-played song was literally `white_noise_for_sleep_10_hours.mp3`

> "You have the range of a teaspoon and the courage of a wilted basil," Geraldine seemed to say, rustling judgmentally. My pothos nodded in agreement, and that guy has never agreed with anyone.

---

### The Intervention

Last night, all my plants staged a listening party. They forced me to sit on the floor while Geraldine made me listen to her own playlist, which was just:

```
Track 1: Photosynthesis (Extended Rainforest Mix)
Track 2: Wind Through Leaves - Live at My Windowsill
Track 3: You Watering Me 3 Days Late - A Tragedy in D Minor
```

We have now reached a compromise. I get one hour of my terrible pop-punk revival. Geraldine gets two hours of Chopin and *actual* sunlight, not the “vibes-based” light from my desk lamp.

My fern is still asking for editorial credit on this post. I told her she’s lucky I don’t charge rent.
