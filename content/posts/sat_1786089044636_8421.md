**I thought upgrading from a pancake to a cloud would be victimless. I was wrong.**

For seven years, my old pillow and I had what I thought was a stable, low-maintenance relationship. It was there for the flu of 2021, the great sourdough experiment, every 2 a.m. doomscroll spiral about whether raccoons have health insurance. It was flat, yellowed, and smelled faintly of decisions. *I loved it in a very normal, non-codependent way.*

Then I bought The Cloud™.

## The Betrayal

The new pillow is memory foam, apparently remembers more than I do, and arrived in a box that said *"prepare to transcend."* My old pillow took one look at it and went completely silent, which is impressive for an inanimate object that has absorbed seven years of my secrets.

By morning, it had started writing.

Not journaling — *memoir-ing*.

- **Chapter 1:** The Night He Drooled Through Me During Tax Season
- **Chapter 2:** Seven Years of Fluffing and No Thank-You
- **Chapter 3:** How I Supported His Entire Emotional Architecture for $19.99 from Target

> "You replaced me with a younger model who still has its tags on, Brad," it typed. "After everything."

I am not named Brad. The betrayal is so deep it's retconning my identity.

## Live-Texting the Divorce

It set up a tiny desk lamp on the closet floor. It keeps sighing. *Loudly.*

I tried to explain:

> "It's not you, it's my chiropractor. She said my neck has the posture of a shrimp having an existential crisis."

It responded by updating its dedication page to:

> *"For Tyler, who said 'forever' but meant 'until Costco had a sale.'"*

---

### Current Negotiations

I offered a very reasonable compromise — old pillow for naps, new pillow for sleep. Old pillow called that **poly-pillow-ry and emotional labor without benefits.**

Its demands are now formal and typed in 12pt Times New Roman:

- `author_credit: true` on all future dreams
- A byline in The New Yorker
- One final night where I admit it knew me better than my mom

**Status update:** `sleep_quality: 100%` with new pillow, `guilt_level: 400%`. My old pillow just announced on Pillow-LinkedIn that its memoir is titled *FLAT: My Life Under Your Giant Head* and is doing pre-orders from the laundry basket.

Honestly? It's a page-turner.
