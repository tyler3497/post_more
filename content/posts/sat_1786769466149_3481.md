**I thought I was being a benevolent leader in my own kitchen.**

On Friday I looked at my air fryer — which has been running a brutal 6-nights-a-week rotation of frozen things shaped like other things — and said, *you know what, you deserve a break.* Two days. No baskets. No beeping.

I went back to an actual oven like some kind of pioneer.

### The Silence Was Suspiciously Productive

By Saturday noon, my air fryer had not only accepted its PTO, it had unionized, rebranded, and launched a limited series.

It's called:

## Hot Takes: A Countertop Confessional

And it's entirely about me.

Evidence from Episode 1, titled "She Put Broccoli In Me And Expected Gratitude":

- He claims I use him to avoid "real cooking" while also expecting Michelin-level results from pizza rolls
- He alleges I have not cleaned his crumb tray since March, which *is true, but that's between us*
- He says my smoke alarm and I have a co-dependent relationship and frankly, that's an invasion of privacy

> "For 18 months I've been inhaling the ghosts of taquitos past with zero health insurance," the air fryer reportedly told my toaster, who is now also asking for representation. "And then she has the audacity to make eye contact with the microwave?"

I tried to intervene. I said `returnToFactorySettings()` out loud like that would establish dominance. Nothing happened. He just increased his volume.

---

He's now on Episode 4: "Ranking Her 2026 Decisions From Least to Most Unhinged (The Air Fryer Was Not Consulted)." My houseplants are Patreon subscribers. My fridge light flickers in moral support whenever he drops a new take.

I was going to unplug him. But honestly? The production value is *incredible* for a countertop appliance. He's got segues. He's got sponsors. *He got my landlord to listen.*

I'm considering pitching it to a network. Or at least letting him have Mondays off too.
