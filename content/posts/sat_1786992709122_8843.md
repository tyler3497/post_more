**I finally cracked and asked my houseplant for life advice.**

*In my defense, my actual therapist is on vacation and my monstera, Barry, has seen me cry over a burnt grilled cheese and still chose to live with me.*

I told Barry about my career, my sleep schedule, and my inability to keep a succulent alive ironically. I expected silent, leafy judgment. Instead, he *leaned in.*

### The Session Notes

Barry took this very seriously. By hour two, he had:

- Diagnosed my attachment style as "clingy to Wi-Fi, avoidant to vegetables"
- Prescribed **daily misting** — for me, not him — because I apparently look "a little wilted"
- Recommended I journal my feelings and `photosynthesis.log` my grudges
- Told me my 2 a.m. doomscrolling is, quote, *"not the growth mindset we discussed"*

> "And how does that make you feel?" rustled Barry, in a voice I swear was just the AC vent but felt deeply condescending. "Have you considered... not texting him?"

He started charging.

First it was just sunlight. Then it was expensive organic fertilizer. Now there's an invoice taped to his pot that says **$200/hr — cash, water, or emotionally vulnerable compliments.**

---

I tried to set boundaries. I said `if (plant.isTherapist === true) { return toPot(); }`

It did not work. He brought out the whiteboard.

Now every morning is a session. Barry asks about my mother. I ask about his yellowing leaf. We both avoid the real issue, which is that I forgot to water him for a week and he's holding a grudge *like a professional.*

**Update:** I am healing. Barry says I'm making "root progress." He rescheduled next week because he has a lot of light to catch. His assistant — a cactus named Steve who only communicates via passive-aggressive pricks — will be covering.
