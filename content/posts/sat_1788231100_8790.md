**I thought I was being helpful. My microwave thought I was declaring war on physics.**

*For context, my microwave has had the same flashing 12:00 since 2021, and I respected that commitment to not knowing what time it is.*

## The Conversation That Broke Reality

Sunday at 2 a.m., I was reheating leftover pasta and thought, "Hey, buddy, clocks go back tonight, let me explain."

I did not need to do this.

In the last 36 hours since my TED Talk on temporal policy:

- My microwave now believes it's both 1:59 a.m. and 3:00 a.m. simultaneously and is *very stressed* about it
- My toaster has started toasting everything twice to "stay consistent with the timeline"
- My fridge light flickers every time I say the word "spring forward" like it's haunted by an intern from the Department of Time

I tried to reassure it. I said, "It's just an hour, man."

It replied:

> "JUST AN HOUR? You move the sun for FARMERS and expect me to pretend my popcorn button still means something? I have lost all sense of self."

### My Kitchen's Current Legal Status

My kitchen is now technically in three time zones. My coffee maker brews at what it calls `Yesterday o'Clock`. My stove clock just says `¯\_(ツ)_/¯`.

`STATUS: Reheating 2023. Please do not open door — causality in progress.`

---

I tried to set the clock manually. It beeped angrily and displayed `LOL NO`.

I called my landlord. He said, "Yeah, Unit 4B did that last fall. We had to exorcise the dishwasher."

Now I have to live in a loop where it's always almost time to eat, but never quite time. My pasta is both hot and cold. My leftovers have seniority over me.
