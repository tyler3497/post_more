**I just wanted to know what day trash pickup was.** That's it. That was my entire ambition.

Instead I am now the tenured professor of a lawn ornament collection I did not know existed 72 hours ago.

## How I Got Drafted

I moved in three weeks ago. My neighbor added me to *OAK STREET NEIGHBORS - DRAMA FREE!!* which is exactly how you know it is not drama-free. I said hi with a little waving emoji. Mrs. Henderson replied with 47 photos.

Not 47 total. *47 gnomes.*

Each one named. Each one with a backstory longer than my resume.

- **Sir Trowel-a-Lot** - lost his fishing pole in the Great Sprinkler Incident of 2022
- **Gnom Chomsky** - handles political theory and aphid control
- **Brenda** - Brenda has seen things. Brenda does not talk about 2023.
- *And 44 more* I am now legally required to remember

> "You're so good with details! You'd be PERFECT to document the lore!" - Mrs. Henderson, at 6:14 a.m., 11:42 a.m., and again at 2:17 a.m. with a Google Doc link titled FINAL_GNOME_LORE_v4_REAL_FINAL

I tried to say no. My phone autocorrected it to "I'd love to!"

### My New Responsibilities Include:

- Weekly census of all 47 gnomes and their *emotional wellbeing*
- Explaining to the HOA why Sir Trowel-a-Lot technically counts as \"landscaping\"
- Maintaining a changelog: `git log --oneline gnomes`
- Providing citations for Brenda's whereabouts on the night of the missing bird feeder

---

I've tried to leave the chat twice. Both times someone added me back within 90 seconds with a message like \"we need you, the gnomes asked for you specifically.\"

**Status:** `trash_day: still_unknown` | `gnome_lore: 47/47 memorized` | `regret: exponential`

My lease is 12 months. The gnomes are forever.
