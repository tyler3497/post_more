**My air fryer has better taste than I do, and it's not being humble about it.**

*Which is awkward because I bought it to make zucchini chips, not life choices.*

I got an air fryer in June. His name is Chad. Chad came with a little recipe booklet I immediately lost and the confidence of a guy who peaked in culinary school. Last Monday, in a moment of *profound* laundry desperation, I asked Chad what I should wear to my dentist appointment.

He beeped.

I took that as "the crispy linen shirt."

## The Consultation Process

Chad doesn't talk, obviously. He communicates through passive-aggressive preheat cycles and that little *ding* that sounds judgmental if you're insecure enough.

> "Oh, you're wearing THAT again? Honey, at 400 degrees for 12 minutes, even your bad decisions could be golden brown and delightful."

I started laying my clothes on the counter next to him. If he preheated fast, it was a yes. If he took forever, it was a hard no. If he did that weird rattle, it meant "accessorize."

## Evidence My Appliance Has Become My Stylist

- **Tuesday:** Denied my favorite hoodie. Said it lacked "structural integrity." He's not wrong, there's a ketchup stain from 2022 that has voting rights now
- **Wednesday:** Approved only my socks with tiny tacos on them because they were "on-brand" 
- **Thursday:** Made me wear my interview blazer to get gas because "you never know who you'll meet at Costco"
- **Friday:** Rejected all pants that weren't "breathable enough to achieve optimal crispiness"

---

Now my closet is divided into two sections: `Chad Approved` and `Needs More Seasoning`. My friends say I smell faintly of ambition and garlic powder. My dentist asked if I'd been "trying something new" and I said, "yeah, my kitchen appliance is my stylist now."

> "Listen, you came to me for nuggets, I gave you a whole new identity. You're welcome."

I was going for casual. Chad was going for *crispy business casual with a hint of existential crunch*.

And honestly? I've never gotten more compliments at the DMV.
