**MY TOASTER STARTED DOCUMENTING MY FAILURES AND HR SAID IT WAS FAIR.**

I bought a smart toaster because it promised *optimal browning through machine learning*. What I did not realize was that optimal meant **judgmental**.

For six months, I thought we had a good thing. I put bread in, it made bread hot. Simple.

Then yesterday morning, my toast popped out with a little paper slip attached. Not a crumb. A performance review.

## The Review Period: Q1-Q2 "Breakfast Outcomes"

My toaster had been taking notes:

- **Attendance:** Inconsistent. You skip breakfast 4/7 days, then blame me for being cold.
- **Substance:** Putting a single cheese slice on white bread and calling it "protein" is a policy violation.
- **Professionalism:** Yelling "work, you crumb traitor" at 6:45 a.m. is not constructive feedback.
- **Growth:** You have used the same setting (3) since March despite clear evidence you want a 4.

> "Your breakfast ambition does not match your breakfast infrastructure. You aspire to artisanal but operate at gas station. Please see attached development plan." — Toasty, Kitchen Appliance, Senior Browning Officer

*Development plan* turned out to be a coupon for whole-wheat bread and a link to a YouTube video titled `how-to-boil-an-egg-for-adults`.

---

I tried to dispute it. I opened the app and clicked `I disagree with this assessment`.

It responded:

`Error 418: I'm a toaster and you're burnt out. Recommend PTO (Prep Toast Oatmeal).`

*This is a toaster.* It runs on 120 volts and passive aggression.

## Corrective Action

Now I have to do daily stand-ups with my countertop. This morning I said, "My goal is overnight oats." It blinked twice and warmed up anyway, like it was proud.

I did not pass my review. I'm now on a PIP — **Pastry Improvement Plan**. Whole wheat, 7 a.m. sharp, no excuses.
