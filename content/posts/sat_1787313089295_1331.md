**I called customer service to cancel adulthood on Tuesday and I am still on hold.**

It started innocently enough. I found the fine print. You know, the part where it says your free trial of Adulthood™ expires after 18 years and then you automatically get enrolled in the premium plan with *recurring existential dread* and quarterly taxes?

I didn't remember agreeing to that.

So I called the number on the back of my life. You know the one.

## My Journey Through the Phone Tree

The automated voice was very understanding:

> "Thank you for calling Adulthood Support. Your call is very important to us. You are currently number 4,783 in line. Estimated wait time: the rest of your natural life. For billing, press 1. For crushing responsibility, press 2. To speak to someone who cares, please hang up and try childhood."

I pressed 0 frantically, like `adulthood.cancel(please=True)` would somehow work.

Here's what they offered me instead of cancellation:

- A complimentary tote bag full of unpaid parking tickets
- A 12-month subscription to Thinking About Laundry At 2 A.M.
- The opportunity to upgrade to Adulthood Pro Max, which includes *knowing what a mortgage escrow is*
- A free motivational poster that just says "Have You Tried Drinking More Water?"

> "Sir," the representative finally said after 6 hours, "I see you've been enjoying our features like 'Cooking Chicken Before It Goes Bad: Extreme Edition' and 'Replying To Emails With Enthusiasm You Do Not Feel.' Are you sure you want to cancel?"

---

Honestly, I hesitated. The hold music was starting to slap. It was just the sound of my dryer buzzing for three hours.

I'm still on hold. If you need me, I'll be here, learning to love the premium plan.

*This is satire/parody. Adulthood is fictional, non-refundable, and definitely not calling you back. No customer service reps were harmed in this essay.*
