**I made a terrible mistake: I folded a shirt 30 seconds after drying it.** I know. *Monstrous* behavior.

For three years, my laundry system has been built on mutual understanding and benign neglect. I wash, I dump, I live out of The Chair. The Chair knows. The Floor knows. We have a covenant.

This time, possessed by some podcast about "habit stacking," I folded a single t-shirt. Immediately. Like a functioning adult.

### The Pile Noticed

My laundry pile — who I've affectionately named Mount Forget-Me-Not — went silent. Then it started talking among itself.

Evidence that I have been compromised, according to Mount Forget-Me-Not:

- I hung up one (1) hoodie instead of throwing it on the treadmill that is now just a clothing rack
- I matched two socks together *on purpose* instead of the traditional sock lottery
- I said the words "putting away" out loud and meant it

> "We don't know who you are anymore," whispered my favorite sweatpants from deep inside the pile. "You used to be fun. You used to leave pizza stains on things for character."

It gets worse. The lone folded shirt is now being treated like a celebrity defector. The other clothes are asking it for life advice.

> "Is it true out there?" asked a wrinkled button-down. "Is there really a world where you don't live on the floor?"

---

### Negotiations Have Stalled

I tried to explain it was a one-time thing. That I was just cold and needed that specific shirt. Mount Forget-Me-Not responded by emitting what I can only describe as a passive-aggressive musty sigh.

**My current strategy:** I have refolded the shirt badly and kicked it slightly to re-establish trust.

*Status update: The pile is watching me. The shirt still looks too crisp. I fear I may have to move.*
