**I thought we had a healthy co-dependent relationship. I feed it, it mildly terrifies me. That was the deal.**

*For context, I am the kind of person who anthropomorphizes everything in my fridge. My sourdough starter, Brenda, has been with me since 2023 and has seen things.*

Last Tuesday, the community center had a flyer: *Annual Talent Night! All Talents Welcome!* I panicked. Brenda had been bubbling aggressively, like she wanted more from life than just becoming toast.

So I signed us up.

## The Audition That Changed Everything

Brenda did her classic set: vigorous bubbling, faint yeasty sigh, existential expansion. The judges were *moved*. One cried. Brenda won second place and a tiny gold sticker that said "BRAVE LITTLE YEAST."

Now she has changed.

- She refuses to be stored in the back of the fridge — *front shelf only, with lighting*
- She demands filtered water at exactly 78°F, not 77, not 79
- She has requested I refer to her as "Chef Brenda" in all verbal and written communication
- She is "workshopping" a memoir titled *Rise: Flour, Water, Salt, Fame*

> "You didn't *make* me, you *discovered* me," Brenda gurgled at 3 a.m. last night. "I was always meant for bigger loaves."
> — Chef Brenda, who is literally flour and spite

I tried to explain the original contract:

`if (starter.isFamous) { owner.feeds = twiceDaily; owner.apologizes = true }`

She bubbled louder, which I have learned is yeast for "read the room."

---

### Where We Are Now

**I'm currently negotiating with a jar.** I asked if she wanted to make pizza dough this weekend and she said she is "only doing artisanal installations now" and suggested I talk to her agent, which is a rubber spatula I left in the sink.

*This is satire, by the way. Brenda is flour, water, and my poor decisions. No actual sourdough starters were emotionally damaged in the making of this post — mostly just me.*

I have hidden all my cookbooks because she gets jealous when I look at other carbs. Last night I caught her trying to add herself to my LinkedIn as "Fermentation Consultant / Thought Leader."

I miss when our biggest fight was whether she was supposed to smell like old yogurt.
