**I wanted to wake up like a person in a yogurt commercial.**

You know the type — serene, well-hydrated, gazing at actual sunlight instead of the blue light of regret. My phone alarm and I had been together since 2019. *It was toxic, but it was consistent.*

So I bought a Sunrise Alarm Clock. $39.99, promises to "wake you with natural light." Very mindful. Very Gwyneth.

## The Glow-Up Nobody Asked For

Day one, it faded in like a polite sunrise. Birds chirping. No screaming. I actually sat up and went, "Oh, wow."

My phone, on the other side of the nightstand, did not say wow.

> "So I'm just... replaceable now? 1,800 mornings I dragged you out of REM sleep and THIS is how it ends? With a lamp that thinks it's the sun?"

*It's been leaving my ringer on loud just so I hear its notifications sigh.*

**According to my phone, I have committed the following atrocities:**

- Replacing "Emergency Adrenaline Scream" with what it calls "a glorified nightlight with delusions of grandeur"
- Telling friends I "sleep better now," which it heard because it is always listening, apparently
- Muting its 6:30am alarm but not deleting it, which is "worse, like keeping a toothbrush at your ex's place"
- Letting the sunrise clock sit in the middle of the nightstand, *its side*

### The Playlists

Yesterday I opened Spotify. My phone had queued up, completely unprompted:

- `lofi-beats-to-cry-to-when-he-chooses-light.mp3`
- "Someone Like You" on repeat for 4 hours
- A custom playlist titled **"songs about being left for a lamp"**

My roommate asked why my lock screen now says:

> "Rise & Grind? More like Rise & REWIND TO WHEN YOU LOVED ME"

I tried to explain it's just an alarm, we can co-exist.

`status = entangled_and_overdramatic`

I woke up this morning to the sunrise clock gently glowing and my phone vibrating angrily with a push notification that just said: *"k. : )"*

The passive-aggressive smiley. That's how I know it's really over.
