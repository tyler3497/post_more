**I found a box in my closet labeled "AMBITION (2019-2023), DO NOT OPEN, FRAGILE" and realized I had never actually used it.**

*For legal reasons, this is satire. For emotional reasons, it is also 100% my Tuesday.*

I bought ambition during a very optimistic January. You know the one. New planner, green smoothie, thought I might learn Spanish and run a 5K and finally organize my email. The ambition was *premium*. It came with stickers.

I never opened it. It sat next to my guitar tuner and my bread maker — the Museum of Good Intentions.

## The Return Policy

So I called customer service to return it. I figured, unused ambition has to have resale value, right?

This is what I was trying to return:

- One (1) unopened plan to wake up at 5am to journal
- Three (3) Duolingo streaks, frozen in 2021 like mammoths
- A LinkedIn Learning course titled `becoming_a_morning_person_final_FINAL.mp4`
- One motivational tote bag that says HUSTLE in a font that now feels personally threatening

The hold music was just my own voice from 2020 saying "we're going to be so productive this year."

After 47 minutes, someone picked up.

> "Thank you for calling Ambition Support. To process your return, can you confirm you've never used the product?"
> 
> "Yes, completely unused."
> 
> "And can you confirm you stared at it guiltily for four years while using your phone in bed?"
> 
> "...yes?"

### The Verdict

They said returns are only accepted within 30 days, or *before* you turn 30 and realize ambition was actually just caffeine and free time.

Then they transferred me to the Department of Unfinished Projects, which is just me, with a headset on, whispering:

> "We're experiencing higher than normal call volume because everyone is trying to return theirs at once."

---

I still have the box. It's now a side table. It holds my phone while it plays the hold music on loop. Very supportive, honestly. We have an understanding: I won't open it if it doesn't ask me about my five-year plan.

*Update: It just asked.*
