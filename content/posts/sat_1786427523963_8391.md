**I thought I was being responsible. Apparently I was committing a war crime against my own kitchen.**

It started simple. The sponge looked... seasoned. *Like it had seen things.* I finally threw it out after four months, three mold colonies, and what I can only describe as a personality.

That was my first mistake.

## The Aftermath

My sink did not take this well. Within hours:

- A small puddle of water gathered at the drain like it was holding vigil
- My new sponge was given the silent treatment — it just floated there, ostracized
- The dish soap started dispensing extra, like it was overcompensating for grief
- The faucet dripped once every 47 seconds, which felt intentional and judgmental

I tried to explain. I said, "Look, it was biodegradable in the wrong direction."

The sink responded by clogging slightly. *Not fully.* Just enough to make a point.

> "We had a system," whispered my drying rack, which I did not know could talk. "He knew where all the forks went. He knew your shame."

I didn't know my old sponge had tenure. I didn't know he was employee of the month, every month, since February.

---

It gets worse. I found a memorial under the sink. A bottle cap with a little candle in it. My blue mug was placed solemnly beside it.

`if (sponge.loyalty > 4_months) { sink.mourn(indefinitely) }`

I tried to introduce the new sponge. I named him Kevin 2. Very professional. My kitchen is not accepting him.

**Lesson learned:** Next time I will simply never clean again. It's what the sink would have wanted.

*P.S. — SATIRE / PARODY — none of this happened, my sink is not sentient, though my landlord should still check on it.*
