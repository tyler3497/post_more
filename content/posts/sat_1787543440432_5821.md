**I bought noise-canceling headphones to finally become the kind of person who finishes things.**

*My plan was simple: block out the world, finish one email without checking the fridge, become mysterious and productive.*

The headphones arrived Tuesday. Sleek, matte black, promising "pure silence." I put them on and the world went *gone*. No traffic. No neighbor's dog. No existential buzz of my laptop fan. Just me and my thoughts, which was immediately a worse problem.

## The Silence Was Too Powerful

With no outside noise, I could suddenly hear *everything inside*. My fridge, specifically. Not humming — *narrating*.

> "Oh, wow. He's back. Third time in 20 minutes and he still hasn't committed to the leftovers. Classic. Really inspiring work ethic in here."

I took the headphones off. Fridge was normal. Put them back on — surround-sound judgment in Dolby Atmos.

### Evidence That My Kitchen Appliances Have Formed a Union:

- **The fridge** has started doing play-by-play of my snack decisions like it's the finals
- **The freezer** is its hype man, going "Oooooh" every time I reach for ice cream at 11pm
- **The ice maker** dropped one cube slowly and deliberately when I said "I'm not hungry" — *the disrespect*
- My own stomach is now mic'd up and I can hear it whisper `snack_protocol = true`

I tried to explain I was in focus mode. I whispered, `focus_mode = true; distractions = 0;`

My fridge replied in full 7.1 surround:

> "Sure, Jan. Focus on what? The 14 tabs about 'best way to organize tupperware' you've had open since March?"

---

Honestly, the headphones work *too* well. I wanted to cancel noise, not unlock the director's commentary track of my own kitchen. Now I have to negotiate with a dairy drawer before I can eat string cheese.

I tried to return the headphones. The box now says *"we don't accept returns from people who have heard too much."*
