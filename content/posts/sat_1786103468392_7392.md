## My Cardio Is Mostly Running From Commitment

**I finally went to the gym on Tuesday.** After eight months of using my membership card primarily as an expensive bookmark and a way to tell people *I have a gym* in a very casual, lying voice.

I swiped in. The front desk guy squinted at my account like he'd discovered a fossil record.

> "Wow. You're... you're still alive? Your membership was upgraded to *archaeological artifact* status last month."

I lasted 23 minutes. Twelve of those were trying to figure out how to adjust a bench without calling for a priest.

### Then Things Got Intense

I came home, proud. I had done one (1) treadmill incline and touched a dumbbell *thoughtfully*. My phone buzzed.

> "GREAT WORK TODAY, CHAMPION!" screamed an email from FitLife Platinum. "We saw you logged 0.4 miles! AT THIS PACE, YOU'LL BE READY FOR PARIS 2028!"

Paris? *I was ready for a nap.*

By Wednesday morning:

- **5:47 a.m.** — Text: "Rise and grind, Olympian! Your quads miss you already!"
- **6:15 a.m.** — App notification: "I made you a custom meal plan. It's just chicken. It's always just chicken."
- **Noon** — Email update: `subject: WE REGISTERED YOU FOR A 5K (you're welcome)`

*I didn't register for a 5K. I registered for a free towel that I never received.*

I tried to explain to the app that I was just testing the waters:

> "Just one visit to see if the showers still have pressure. Not a lifestyle pivot."

It responded with a push notification that simply said:

> "We told all the other memberships about you. They believe in you now. Don't embarrass us."

---

### The Negotiation

We are now in what my calendar calls `commitment_level: delusional`. 

**It wants:** 6 a.m. HIIT classes, protein shakes, and a motivational poster of me that I did not consent to hanging by the free weights.

**I want:** to go back to our previous arrangement where I paid $39.99/month and we both pretended the other didn't exist.

`Status: emotionally spot-trained, spiritually winded`

I went once. It thinks we're soulmates. We are not soulmates. We are a situationship with burpees.
