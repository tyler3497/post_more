**I sent my monstera to a meditation retreat because I thought it looked a little droopy.** *I was trying to be a good plant dad, not trigger a spiritual awakening that would ruin us both.*

It started innocently enough. My fern — Fernando — had been giving me that look. You know the one. Leaves slightly wilted, like it's judging my 11 p.m. cereal dinners and my 2 a.m. Wikipedia spirals about whether octopuses have feelings.

So I found the place: Serene Succulents, a weekend wellness retreat for "foliage with feelings." $89, BYO pot, complimentary misting.

## The Intervention I Didn't Know I Needed

According to the facilitator, Linda, who speaks fluent photosynthesis, Fernando was:

- Carrying secondhand stress from my true-crime podcast habit
- Dehydrated from what she called "performative watering"
- *Deeply* offended I use him as a coaster for my energy drinks
- Requesting I stop calling all plants "my dude"

> "Your plant has achieved inner stillness," she whispered on Sunday's pickup call. "He says your apartment has... too much frantic Googling energy. He's not ready to return to that environment."

Excuse me?

## The Breakup Call

Yesterday he sent a — I kid you not — a *leaflet* through their mail service:

> "I have found peace among the succulents. Please stop playing lo-fi beats to study/relax/cry to. My roots need silence. Tell the cactus I said nothing — we have closure. Namaste, but also please Venmo Linda."

Now I'm paying $12 a day for extended stay. He does sunrise salutations. He has a friend named Sage who is literally just sage. He's journaling.

Look, I get it. I am a lot. But I bought him at Home Depot on clearance. He had three leaves and a dream. Now he has a guru and a newsletter and boundaries I didn't consent to.
