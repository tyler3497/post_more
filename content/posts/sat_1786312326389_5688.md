## The Day I Thought I Was Just Vacuum-Adverse

**I thought I was cleaning. I was actually disrupting a multigenerational community.**

It's been... months since I'd looked under my couch. *A decision that was 30% avoidance, 70% genuine belief that what I can't see can't judge me.*

On Saturday I finally moved the couch. Not for hygiene. My remote had fallen and I was tired of using an app to change the volume.

What I found was not dust. It was governance.

### The Reunion I Didn't RSVP To

Under there: a sprawling, fluffy metropolis of dust bunnies, lint, one LEGO brick from 2019, and what I swear was a popcorn kernel running a small waffle stand.

They had formed a society. They had bylaws.

Evidence I am now living above an unincorporated township:

- **A tiny welcome banner** made from dryer lint that read WELCOME HOME, GIANT
- **A lost sock** serving as town hall, with clearly attendance-marked pebbles
- **My own hair ties** repurposed as tire swings for baby dust bunnies
- **A PowerPoint presentation** (somehow) titled 

> "We assumed you liked us," said the largest dust bunny, who introduced himself as Gerald. "You never vacuumed. We thought that was consent."

I tried to explain it was neglect, not endorsement. Gerald adjusted his invisible tie.

*I have never been guilt-tripped by particulate matter before.*

---

### Current Negotiations

Now they're hosting their family reunion *in* my living room because, quote, "we have the square footage." There are microscopic coolers. There's a DJ who is just my old Bluetooth speaker playing white noise. Someone brought potato salad that is mostly crumbs.

My defense —  — is not working. Gerald filed a formal request for overhead lighting and a throw pillow annex.

If you need me, I'll be on the other couch, the one I'm now too scared to move.
