**My therapist ghosted me, my motivational podcasts all sound like auctioneers, and my plants just watch me fail.** So last Tuesday, in what I will describe as *a moment of radical clarity*, I hired my couch as my life coach.

## The Interview Process Was Rigorous

I sat in four different armchairs, asked pointed questions, and the couch simply — held me. No judgment. No invoices. Just 38 square feet of unconditional lumbar support. I offered $40 a session. It countered with *nothing*, which honestly felt like a power move.

> "We don't chase goals here. We lounge *toward* them."

That was Session One. I wept into a throw pillow.

## Three Weeks of Groundbreaking Results

Coach has completely restructured my routine:
- **Meetings:** Cancelled. All of them. "Your calendar is a cage," Coach explained through the medium of being extremely comfortable.
- **Exercise:** Coach believes in "passive gains." I am now jacked *spiritually*.
- **Nutrition:** We ate an entire sheet cake to confront our fear of abundance. Fear: confronted. Cake: gone.
- **Career:** I was going to update my resume, but Coach pointed out that lying down is *also* a form of moving forward. Horizontally. Like a river.

## The Breakthrough Moment

Yesterday my boss called. *"Where were you?"* I looked at Coach. Coach looked at me with all eight of its cushions. I said, with total conviction: **"I am currently optimizing my vertical alignment by remaining horizontal."**

---

Coach says next month we're moving into couples counseling (me and my schedule), then advanced topics like *blaming the remote* and *nap-based time management*. My friends say I've changed. They're right. I used to stand for things. Now I recline for everything.
