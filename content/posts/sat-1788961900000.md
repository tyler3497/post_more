I am not exaggerating when I say that **there is a gnome in my backyard conducting a full-scale military campaign against the squirrels**, and frankly, I no longer feel safe eating outside.

## It started innocently

Last month I bought a twelve-dollar garden gnome from a hardware store because my tomato plants kept getting raided. I placed him near the garden bed, patted his little hat, and said something like, "Keep an eye out, buddy." *That's it. That's all I said.* I did not give him a rank. I did not issue orders. I did not sign anything in triplicate.

By the following week, the squirrels were organizing their tomato heists with lookouts posted on the fence. So maybe he was onto something.

## Evidence of escalation

- **A defensive perimeter** now exists around my garden, constructed from twine, pinecones, and what I can only describe as tiny caltrops made from dried pasta.
- He has requisitioned my watering can and filled it with what smells like hot sauce.
- I found a miniature battle map scratched into the dirt: my yard, divided into *sectors*. Sector Four is the bird feeder, marked with a skull.
- The squirrels have begun wearing leaves as camouflage. *Leaves.* They look ridiculous, but I respect the craft.

## A statement from the front

I confronted him this morning and asked, calmly, if he could perhaps tone it down. He said nothing, as gnomes do, but his painted eyes seemed to say:

> "You gave me a sword, coward. You knew what you were doing."

For the record, it was a tiny fishing pole that he has repurposed as a sword.

---

I have tried de-escalation. I moved the bird feeder further from his jurisdiction, but he simply redrew the map. I considered removing him entirely, but the squirrels have started leaving him acorns as tribute, which suggests the conflict has entered some kind of mutually profitable stalemate I don't understand.

The tomatoes, I should add, are thriving. He eats a third of them as "supply tax."

Would I buy him again? *Absolutely.* Security has a price, and his price is reasonable.
