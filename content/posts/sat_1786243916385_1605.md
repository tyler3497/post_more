## The Eco-Friendly Mistake That Ruined My Single Status

**I brought my own mug to a coffee shop once to save the planet** and now I'm legally entangled with a man who thinks oat milk is a personality trait.

*It was supposed to be a quick, virtuous transaction.* I walked in, reusable mug in hand — matte green, slightly chipped, says WORLD'S OKAYEST CAMPER — and said, 'Can you put it in this?'

The barista — let's call him Milo, because his name tag said Milo and I am not creative under caffeine deprivation — lit up like I'd just proposed.

> "Whoa. You're a *mug person*. You don't see that commitment anymore. Most people are just... cup tourists."

I laughed. Nervously. The way you laugh when someone thinks your IKEA plant is real.

### The Evidence Piling Up

By Wednesday, things escalated:

- My 'usual' was memorized despite me having ordered *once*: **oat latte, extra hot, one pump of existential dread**
- My mug was already behind the counter when I arrived, like it lives there now
- Milo told the new hire I was 'practically family' and that we have 'history'
- He started asking about my 'camper' status and if I needed help 'pitching a tent' — *I'm still unpacking that*

> "We missed you yesterday," he said Friday, sliding my mug across with the gentleness of a ring bearer. "The shop felt... emptier. Like decaf."

I tried to clarify. I said *I'm just trying to reduce waste.*

He nodded solemnly and said:

> "That's what marriage is. Reducing waste. Together."
