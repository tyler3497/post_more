## The Hoard I Called "Research"

**I finally did it.** After three years of telling myself I would "get to those later," I deleted all 47 open tabs yesterday. *A full browser exorcism.*

For context, these tabs were not normal. They were my entire personality archived in Chrome:

- A 2021 sourdough starter guide I never started but felt morally obligated to keep open
- 12 different "how to fix squeaky floor" tutorials, because if I watch enough, the floor fixes itself
- A recipe for "5-minute artisanal pickles" that has been open for 19 months
- An abandoned shopping cart with one whimsical wooden spoon I was "thinking about"
- 8 Wikipedia tabs from a 2 a.m. spiral about competitive cheese rolling

> "WHERE DID YOU GO? WE HAD A SYSTEM. WE HAD FEELINGS." — my laptop, after I hit 'Close All', in the most dramatic system error voice I've ever imagined

### The Aftermath

My laptop did not take this well. The fan went silent. Too silent. The wallpaper dimmed by 20%.

It now thinks I have entered Witness Protection. It keeps asking if my new name is "Tabitha No-Tabs McGee."

---

I tried to explain that this was *growth*, not abandonment. That `about:blank` is a fresh start, not a cry for help.

It responded by opening one single new tab on its own:

`how to tell if your owner is a secret agent now`

*This is satire. My 47 tabs were real, my laptop's feelings are fictional, and the wooden spoon remains unpurchased.*
