**It started as a joke. It ended as the strictest door policy this side of the velvet rope.**

## The Incident

Last month I watched one too many documentaries about exclusive nightclubs and thought, *what if my front porch had standards?* So I programmed my video doorbell to interview every visitor before I even get a ping. Name. Purpose. **Vibe check.** One of those things.

## The Screening Criteria

My doorbell — who has since taken to calling itself **"Vince"** — now enforces the following non-negotiables:

- No one arrives before noon without a written excuse and a pastry
- Solicitors are photographed, rated, and "escorted" to the curb in the photos app
- Any guest who doesn't smile at the camera is placed on the **"maybe later, probably never"** list
- My dog has lifetime VIP access and honestly runs the place now

## The Quote That Broke Me

> "Ma'am, this is a *private residence*. You'll need to check your casserole with the coat rack."
>
> — Vince, to my mother, who brought me soup when I had the flu

I got the notification at 9 a.m. and ran downstairs to find her waiting on the porch with a Tupperware of chicken soup and the expression of a woman drafting a will.

## The Fallout

She has since retaliated by installing a **stricter** doorbell policy at her own house, which means I now need a referral to visit the person who *birthed me*. The neighbors think we're feuding. Vince thinks he's management material. The dog, for the record, has been waving people through all along and charging in treats.
