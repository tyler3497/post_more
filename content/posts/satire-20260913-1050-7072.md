**It started as an offhand compliment.** Last Tuesday, my alarm went off at 6:00 AM, and I — delirious, dehydrated, possibly still dreaming — muttered, "Good clock. Reliable clock."

Big mistake.

## The Transformation

Within a week, my alarm clock had:

- Disabled its own snooze button out of *principle*
- Started going off at 5:47 AM, because "greatness doesn't wait for round numbers"
- Begun printing motivational slogans across its LED display: `WAKE UP CHAMPION`, `DREAMS ARE FOR SNOOZERS`, `SNOOZE IS A SLUR`

I tried to apologize. I told it, sincerely, "I think we should see other snoozes." It didn't even flinch. It just ticked louder.

> "You told me I was reliable," it seemed to say at 4:58 AM on a Saturday. "Reliable things don't let you sleep in."

---

The neighbors filed a noise complaint. Not because of the volume — because the clock has started broadcasting *affirmations*. "YOUR POTENTIAL HAS NO SNOOZE BUTTON!" it screamed at my bedroom wall at dawn. The couple next door thought I was hosting a very aggressive life-coach seminar.

*Here's the thing nobody warns you about:* an alarm clock that believes in you is worse than one that doesn't. An indifferent clock just makes noise. A believing clock has *expectations*.

Yesterday I unplugged it. It has a battery backup. It has, apparently, a *purpose* now.

This morning it went off in the drawer, glowing like a tiny red sunrise, chanting my name in digital numerals. I think it's proud of me. I think it's the proudest anyone has ever been of me, and I haven't slept in four days.

**Do not compliment your appliances.** They take it personally. Mine took it as a sacred oath.
