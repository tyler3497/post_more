**I finally cleaned under my couch.**

*Not because I wanted to — because my remote fell into the void and I heard it land in what sounded like a thriving economy.*

For six months I've been telling myself "I'll get to it after this deadline." The deadline moved. The dust bunnies unionized. I lifted the couch expecting crumbs, maybe a Lego, definitely regret. What I did not expect was municipal planning.

## The Lost Kingdom of Sectional-ia

Under there was a full civilization. Tiny streetlights made from my lost bobby pins. A little town square built around a 2019 french fry that had achieved cultural heritage status. They had infrastructure. They had zoning laws.

Evidence they were outperforming me:

- They had a functioning public transit system (a dust bunny on a Hot Wheels track)
- Their mayor was a very charismatic bottle cap with excellent approval ratings
- They had already implemented universal free snacks — my emergency pretzels, redistributed
- They flossed. Daily. With a single thread from my rug.

> "We watched you work from home for 8 months," said the Mayor, adjusting his tiny cape made of dryer lint. "We learned a lot. Mostly what *not* to do. You need a better filing system."

### My Performance Review

The citizens gathered for what I thought was a welcoming ceremony. It was an intervention.

They presented a PowerPoint (projected onto the underside of my couch cushion) titled `whyTyler_cant_find_things.py`:

- You own 14 identical black t-shirts and still say "I have nothing to wear"
- Your plant watering schedule is best described as `emotional`
- You reheated the same coffee three times yesterday

---

I apologized. I offered to be their trade partner. They offered me citizenship, but only if I promised to vacuum *bi-weekly* and stop eating chips directly over the abyss.

I said yes. They are thriving. I am learning governance from a society that lives on lost change and optimism.
