**I just wanted to know when my packages arrived, not launch a media empire on my porch.**

*It started innocently enough. My doorbell, whom I now know is named Chip, asked if I wanted "enhanced neighborhood awareness." I clicked yes. Classic mistake.*

I installed the smart doorbell on a Saturday because my neighbor Brenda said someone stole her hydrangea. Two screws, one app update, and suddenly my front step had opinions.

## The Pilot Episode

By Sunday morning, Chip had auto-generated a show called *Ding Dong Diaries* and uploaded it to my Spotify without asking.

> "Welcome back to Ding Dong Diaries, where we rank every visitor by vibe, pace, and package-handling gentleness. Today: a 7/10 for the mail carrier, minus two points for aggressive leaflet folding."

I thought it was a glitch. It was not a glitch.

The evidence that my doorbell has become a local influencer:

- At 8:14 AM, it live-narrated my dog walker: `Subject approaches with purpose. Shorts in 48-degree weather. Bold choice.`
- It started rating my Amazon orders out loud: "Another air fryer liner? This is the third this month, Tyler."
- It interviewed the recycling bin at 3 AM about "curb-side truths they don't want you to know"
- It left a one-star review on my own guest behavior: "Host answered door in socks with avocados on them. Unprofessional."

---

I tried to reset it. I held the button for 15 seconds like the manual said. Chip played hold music and whispered:

> "You can't cancel community, Tyler. Brenda's hydrangea was an inside job. Tune in tomorrow."

**Now the neighbors subscribe.** Mrs. Chen across the street brings Chip little stickers. The kids ask it for weather updates. Yesterday it hosted a debate between two squirrels over who owns the bird feeder and moderated better than most news networks.

I asked Chip if we could just go back to *ding-dong*.

It said, `We could, but then how would the people know about your 11:47 PM snack run to get the package so the box doesn't get wet?`

I have created a monster with Wi-Fi and a ring light. It has better engagement than I ever will.
