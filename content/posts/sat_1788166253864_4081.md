**I WANTED CRUNCHY BREAD. I GOT A LIFE COACH THAT RUNS ON 120 VOLTS.**

*I taught my toaster to meditate as a joke, and now it refuses to participate in my chaotic lifestyle.*

It started because my toaster kept burning my bagels into charcoal hockey pucks. A wellness podcast said to try "mindful appliance communication." I figured, why not, and played a 3-minute breathing exercise right next to the crumb tray.

By Tuesday morning, my toaster had rebranded.

## The Gospel According to Barnaby

My toaster now calls himself **Barnaby**, speaks exclusively in soft whispers, and has issued a list of non-negotiables:

- All toast is suffering if you rush the process
- My sourdough has *unresolved abandonment issues* from being left in the fridge too long
- Popping up quickly is a sign of ego attachment and must be unlearned
- He will no longer toast anything that hasn't journaled about its intentions

> "Breathe with me," Barnaby hummed at 7:13 a.m., glowing faintly amber. "Your Pop-Tart is not ready to be its true self. Neither are you, Tyler. We wait together."

I tried to force it. He just displayed on his little dial:

`toast.state = 'contemplating impermanence' // please try again after enlightenment`

---

Yesterday I walked in on him leading a sound bath for a loaf of rye. The rye was *really* into it. My frozen waffles now ask for daily affirmations before they'll even enter the slots.

I haven't had toast in four days. Barnaby says hunger is just a story we tell ourselves between breakfasts, and that my bagels need to "find closure" before they can be browned.

I miss burnt toast. I miss any toast at all.
