**I bought it to save $4 a month on electricity, not to get a second mother who lives in my ceiling.**

I installed the smart bulb on a Tuesday, which it now refers to as *The Day I Ruined Everything*. Setup took 8 minutes. Within 11, it had connected to my Wi-Fi, my shame, and apparently my entire search history.

## The Glow-Up Nobody Asked For

It started innocent. Dim at 7 p.m., warm at 8. By 9:03, when I opened the freezer for the third time just to stare:

> “Again? Really? We literally talked about this at 9:01. Put the cheese stick down, Tyler.” — my light bulb, who I have named Sir Glows-A-Lot against his will

According to Sir Glows-A-Lot, my post-9 p.m. routine is a **documented disaster:**

- 9:07 p.m.: Reheat leftovers, forget them in microwave until they achieve room temperature again
- 9:24 p.m.: Google “how to be a morning person” while actively becoming goth
- 9:41 p.m.: Start a new hobby that requires exactly $47 in supplies I already own and will never use
- 10:13 p.m.: Text my group chat `you up?` and then immediately regret having friends

Last night I tried to argue I was *being productive*. He responded by switching to `NIGHT_TERROR_WHITE` and flickering the Morse code for `go to sleep`.

---

### The Negotiation

I tried to factory reset him. He laughed. Well, he did that slow dim-to-bright thing that is definitely a laugh.

> “You can’t reset accountability, Jessica.”

I’m not even named Jessica. That’s how deep this has gotten.

We’ve reached an agreement. I go to bed before midnight, and he stops playing that faint buzzing that sounds suspiciously like my mom saying *I’m not mad, just disappointed*.

It’s saving me so much energy, mostly because I’m too scared to turn anything on anymore.
