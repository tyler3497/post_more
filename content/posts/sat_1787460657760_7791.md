**I finally decided to be a person with boundaries, so I outsourced them to Google Calendar.**

*For context, my previous boundary strategy was saying "yeah sure!" to everything and then lying down on the floor about it later.*

I watched one 45-second reel about "protecting your energy" and immediately opened my calendar, cracked my knuckles, and started writing auto-decline messages like I was drafting peace treaties. Firm. Kind. Unavailable.

## How My Calendar Got Promoted to Manager

By Tuesday, my calendar had developed taste. And standards. And what I can only describe as *audacity*.

It started screening everything without asking:

- My dentist's "we missed you!" follow-up — declined as `emotional unavailability`
- My cousin's oils launch party — declined as `scheduling conflict (with my will to live)`
- My own 7 a.m. gym block — declined as `this is a fantasy, be honest`
- And finally, my birthday dinner — declined as `host is overextended`

> "Hi! Thanks so much for thinking of us! We're currently at capacity for joy and cannot accommodate another event where you pretend to like surprises. Please try again never. Best, Your Calendar" — My Calendar, who now signs emails as "Boundaries™"

I stared at the notification. My calendar had just No'd my birthday *to me*.

## The Birthday Betrayal

I tried to add it back manually. It countered with a wellness suggestion:

```
calendar.createEvent({
  title: "Sit quietly and reflect on why you need a party to feel loved",
  duration: "4 hours",
  attendees: ["you", "your guilt"]
})
```

---

My roommate asked if I was coming to my own party. I said I had to check with my calendar. It's currently showing me as **Out of Office (Emotionally)** until further notice and has blocked off Saturday for "strategic avoidance of cake-related vulnerability."

*SATIRE / PARODY — 100% fictional, my calendar has not gained sentience, and I am still very much having cake on Saturday, even if my schedule disagrees.*
