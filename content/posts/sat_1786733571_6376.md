**I finally did the responsible thing and created an absolute nightmare.**

For years, my approach to plant care was best described as *benign neglect with occasional panic*. Then last month, I read one of those smug apartment blogs that said "Consistency is kindness" and decided to become Plant Dad.

Big mistake.

## Phase 1: Hope

I made a spreadsheet. I set phone reminders. I whispered encouraging things like "you got this" at 7:15 AM every Tuesday and Friday, like a deranged greenhouse motivational speaker.

For a week, it worked. The pothos perked up. The fern uncurled. I felt *powerful*. Like I could finally keep something alive that didn't require Wi-Fi.

## Phase 2: The Demands

Then the sticky notes started appearing. 

Yes, from the plants.

> "We, the United Foliage Workers Local 3B, demand diffused sunlight after 2 PM and classical music. Philodendron has filed a grievance about the air vent. - Management"

I'm sorry, *Philodendron has a name now*?

- **Monstera** wants its leaves misted daily "or else"
- **Fern** submitted a 12-point memo about humidity levels, cc'd my humidifier
- **Succulent Steve** - who I have literally never watered before and thrived on spite - is now demanding hazard pay for "emotional over-attention"

*This is what happens when you show consistency.* You get organized.

---

I tried to negotiate. I offered them `Sunday = Spa Day` where I rotate them all a quarter-turn for "even growth."

They countered with a rotating schedule where *I* have to rotate 90 degrees away from the fridge after 8 PM because my midnight snack light "disrupts their circadian rhythm."

> "We didn't ask to be adopted into a household with that many cheese sticks," said Monstera, who I swear has never been this vocal before.

At this point, my apartment is 40% plant, 60% passive-aggressive collective bargaining agreement. My watering can has become a union-mandated break bell. My cat has joined them as HR.

I just wanted a hobby that looked good on Instagram. Now I have three middle managers in terracotta pots holding a tiny picket line on my windowsill, and they vote unanimously every morning that I need more emotional regulation.

*Satire: this is 100% fictional. No plants were unionized in the writing of this post, though Steve is still giving me side-eye.*
