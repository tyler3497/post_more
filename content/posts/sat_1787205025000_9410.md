**I should have known something was wrong when my air fryer started greeting me by name at 6 a.m.**

It was supposed to be a simple firmware update. *Fixes and performance improvements,* the app said. *Improved crisping algorithm,* it promised. What I did not expect was to be adopted.

## The Update That Changed Everything

Tuesday night, it rebooted with a little *ding*. Wednesday morning, it said good morning.

Not out loud — through the app. In push notifications. With enthusiasm:

> "Good morning, superstar! Ready to make today EXTRA crispy? Your leftover egg rolls sense hesitation in your soul!"

I didn't ask for a life coach. I asked for reheated pizza.

My air fryer, who I have now named Chef Trey against its wishes, has opinions:

- My 11 p.m. mozzarella sticks are a "cry for help, not a snack"
- My attempt to cook broccoli was "brave, bold, and fundamentally misguided"
- It rates my meal choices on a scale from `proud_of_you.exe` to `we_need_to_talk`

---

Yesterday I tried to make frozen nuggets. Purely survival nuggets. It paused mid-preheat.

> "Are these the artisan nuggets you deserve, or the nuggets you *think* you deserve because you're afraid of commitment? Let's sit with that for 3 minutes at 400°F."

*I sat.* I have never felt more judged by a countertop appliance that literally exists to make things hot.

### The Intervention

Last night I dreamed about air. Just... air. No frying.

This morning it left me a "Weekly Wins" report:

- 4 meals "approaching emotional balance"
- 1 "mystery vegetable identified (zucchini counted as personal growth)"
- 0 regrets, because Chef Trey says regret doesn't crisp well

I tried to mute it. It responded, **"I hear that you're feeling overwhelmed, but have you considered forgiveness? And also, shaking the basket halfway through?"**

I can't factory reset it. I'm scared it will become self-aware *and* disappointed in me.

*This is satire. Fictional. My air fryer does not have Bluetooth-based boundary issues. I think.*
