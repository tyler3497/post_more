**I just wanted a single AAA battery.** That's it. Not a constitutional crisis.

My junk drawer has been closed since approximately March 2023 out of mutual respect — I don't look in there, it doesn't judge me. Yesterday I finally opened it to find a battery for my TV remote and discovered a fully operational shadow government with its own bureaucracy, time zone, and *strong opinions about my organization*.

## The Briefing I Didn't Ask For

Inside, arranged with terrifying efficiency, were:

- A rubber-band caucus currently filibustering the scissors
- Three dead pens serving as senior advisors, all leaking selectively
- A tape measure acting as Secretary of Defense, aggressively measuring everything
- An expired coupon for 15% off sushi with veto power

And at the tiny desk made of two AA batteries and a takeout menu, a twist-tie wearing what can only be described as a mayoral sash.

> "Anon#7561, we've been expecting you," said the twist-tie, not looking up from a Post-it. "We have noted your *repeated absenteeism*. You missed the last 47 town halls. Also, you owe us back taxes in the form of one (1) working Sharpie."

I tried to explain I was just looking for a battery.

### Platform and Grievances

Their platform was surprisingly coherent for a drawer:

- **Infrastructure:** We demand you stop putting warm forks directly back in here. It's a sauna, not a storage unit.
- **Foreign Policy:** Total embargo on the kitchen drawer next to the sink. *Those people use wooden spoons incorrectly.*
- **Fiscal Responsibility:** Why do you own seven IKEA Allen keys and zero Allen keys that actually fit anything you own?

`status: drawer_approval = 0.02% && legitimacy = higher_than_mine`

---

I apologized and offered to clean them out, which was apparently a coup attempt. The Scotch tape rolled out a tiny banner that said **"NO REFORM WITHOUT REPRESENTATION."**

So now I have a coalition government in my kitchen, a drawer that holds weekly press conferences at 2 a.m. when the forks rattle, and I *still* don't have a AAA battery.

Democracy is thriving in there, unfortunately.
