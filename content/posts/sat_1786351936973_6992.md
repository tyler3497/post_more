**I finally defrosted my freezer like a responsible adult with a plan and a spatula.**

*This was an existential mistake.*

For context, my freezer has been running a laissez-faire preservation policy since 2022. If it fit on a shelf, it stayed forever. I told myself it was `efficient meal storage`. In reality it was an icy museum of good intentions.

Yesterday I unplugged it, chipped away at the tundra, and found him.

## The Discovery

One burrito. Perfectly centered. Sitting on the middle shelf like a monk who achieved enlightenment through freezer burn.

- Labeled in my own handwriting: `BREAKFAST?? - Nov 2022 ???`
- Wrapped in three layers of foil *and* optimism
- Still colder than my LinkedIn inbox, somehow more accomplished

I had completely forgotten making it, which is impressive because I apparently gave it a backstory.

> "Oh, you finally noticed me," I imagined it saying, in the smug voice of a breakfast that has its life together. "While you were doom-scrolling, I was developing character."

---

## The Performance Review I Didn't Ask For

I laid out the evidence of my life next to it, and the burrito won on every metric:

- **Longevity:** Survived 3 apartments, 2 breakups, and the Great Spinach Nugget Avalanche of 2023
- **Stability:** Never once questioned its purpose or tried to learn pottery
- **Commitment:** Stayed frozen for 1,357 days without needing a motivational podcast
- Holds the title of `frozen_assets/ceo/final_boss` in my kitchen hierarchy

My roommate asked why I was apologizing to frozen food. I said I wasn't apologizing, I was networking.

### My New Five-Year Plan (Inspired by Burrito)

Since clearly it knows something I don't, I'm pivoting:

- Stop buying groceries with future-me optimism
- Start labeling things with less panic and more honesty
- Accept that some of us peak at being well-preserved and quietly intimidating

I put the burrito back. I’m not ready to eat something that has better retirement planning than I do.
