**I did it.** After fourteen years of collecting plastic bags like they were rare vintage vinyl, I finally brought reusable bags to the grocery store like a functioning adult.

*This was a mistake.*

## The Betrayal

For over a decade, my pantry operated as a plastic bag sanctuary. Every cabinet was stuffed. Every drawer whispered *crinkle crinkle*. I had a bag full of bags that had a bag full of bags. It was sustainable, in the sense that it was sustaining its own ecosystem.

Last Tuesday, in a fit of environmental consciousness brought on by a documentary I half-watched while scrolling, I bought two canvas totes that say LIVE LAUGH LOCAL on them. I took them to Trader Joe's. I felt *righteous*.

- I remembered the bags (first time ever)
- I remembered them *before* checkout (historic achievement)
- I actually declined when the cashier asked "need a bag?" and said "no thanks, I brought my own" like some sort of smug pioneer woman

When I got home, the energy shifted.

## The Intervention

My plastic bags knew. Somehow, they *knew*.

I opened the pantry and found them arranged in a perfect circle around the half-empty pasta canisters. The oldest bag — a 2019 CVS bag with handles stretched beyond recognition — was in the middle.

> "We are gathered here today to process collective abandonment," whispered the CVS bag. "Tyler has chosen... *canvas*."

The reusable bags were just standing there on the counter looking way too proud of themselves.

- The plastic bags have started journaling
- One is writing a memoir titled *Cradled Groceries, Cradled Hopes: A Life in the Crumple*
- They've instituted a weekly sharing circle called "Tuesdays Are For Tears"

One faded Kroger bag looked up at me and said:

> "We held your leaking rotisserie chicken. We carried your 3 a.m. ice cream. We asked for nothing. And this is how you repay us? With *organic cotton blend*?"

---

I tried to apologize. I offered to still use them for bathroom trash, which apparently is *not* the olive branch I thought it was. It's been three days and the reusable bags have started giving Ted Talks about mindful consumption from the kitchen island.

`panic_level = imminent`

My therapist says this is projection. My pantry says this is *betrayal*.
