**I have been financially dominated by an animal who forgets where he is every three seconds.**

This was supposed to be wholesome self-improvement. I watched one YouTube video called *Passive Income For Beginners* and decided the most responsible thing I could do was spread the knowledge to my goldfish, Mr. Bubbles.

*For context*, Mr. Bubbles has spent the last year doing absolutely nothing but swimming in circles and looking mildly surprised to be alive.

## Phase One: The Seminar

I taped a tiny PowerPoint to his bowl. It covered:

- Why you need an emergency fund
- Compound interest (he blinked twice, which I took as comprehension)
- The importance of diversifying your portfolio beyond *one castle decoration*

He seemed engaged. Or hungry. Honestly same thing.

## Phase Two: The Betrayal

> "Listen," Mr. Bubbles did not say, because he is a fish, "If I'm going to live in this economy, I need to start acting like it." 

Yesterday I found a note taped to his bowl. In my own handwriting, which I do not remember writing:

```
RENT DUE: $400/month for premium aquatic real estate (includes algae, existential dread, filtered light)
Late fee: 3 extra flakes
```

And you know what? He has a point. This market is tough.

---

I tried to negotiate. I explained concepts like *shared living expenses* and *I literally bought you*.

He responded by swimming to the other side of the bowl and staring at me with the cold, dead-eyed resolve of a landlord who knows you have no other options.

**My fish now has a better understanding of capitalism than I do, and he is winning.**

Satire is wild. My financial advisor is now a goldfish who charges me to look at him. I am down bad and also down $400.

*Still love him though. 10/10 would get evicted by him again.*
