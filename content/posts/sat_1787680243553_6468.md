**I never meant to start a condiment democracy.**

*This is important context because I am now legally required to attend weekly meetings inside my own refrigerator.*

It started when I put my grocery list on the fridge. Innocent, right? Just a little sticky note that said "eggs, oat milk, sanity (optional)." But my Dijon mustard saw it. And Dijon tells everyone.

## The Emergency Session

By 6:15pm, my fridge light was flickering like a projector at a very poorly funded film festival. I opened the door and heard it.

> "For YEARS we have stood on this shelf, uncredited, unlisted, and UN-ROTATED!" shouted Sir Ketchup, who has been expired since March but refuses to leave office. "We demand equal fridge-door representation!"

My condiments had organized:

- **The Mustard Caucus** — Dijon and Yellow, suddenly very political and quoting policy
- **The Hot Sauce Delegation** — three identical bottles I bought during a sale, now forming a voting bloc
- **Barry the Butter** — abstaining from all votes, but loudly

*I tried to explain that the grocery list was aspirational, not legislative.*

They had prepared a slideshow. On a single pickle slice.

> "We have reviewed your `fridge_inventory.js` and found it lacking," said Ranch, who I don't even remember buying. "You have 14 kinds of sriracha and zero respect for our shelf life."

---

### My Defense Was Weak

I told them I was trying to be more organized. They said organization without representation is tyranny. Ketchup demanded a rotating door policy. Mustard wants a tiny podium.

I compromised. I added them to the list. Now it reads:

- eggs
- oat milk
- emotional validation for ketchup
- tiny podium for mustard
- therapy for that one olive jar that has been here since 2021

This morning I found a petition on my counter taped to a spoon. 12 signatures, all in various shades of leaked sauce. **They're demanding a rotating door policy and a mini-fan for ventilation.**

*Satire, obviously. My condiments haven't unionized. Mostly because the mayo is a known scab and keeps crossing the picket line to hang out with the turkey.*
