**I went to Whole Foods to return one avocado and accidentally became a person of interest in the produce department.**

*I swear I walked in with good intentions and potassium-based regret.*

Last Tuesday I bought one of those perfect avocados, the kind that whispers *you have your life together*. By Thursday it had turned the color and texture of wet cardboard and was making decisions on its own.

So I did what any rational adult would do. I put it in a paper bag, brought the receipt, and said the most embarrassing sentence of my career:

> "Hi, sorry, this avocado betrayed me."

## The Investigation Begins

The cashier didn't even blink. She called her manager. The manager called a guy in a vest.

Evidence they are now allegedly holding against me:

- One avocado, purchased 8/12, *now* 80% air and broken promises
- A receipt where I also bought $47 of things I did not need, including lavender toothpaste
- My own statement: `if (avocado.ripe) { eat(); } else { emotional_damage++; }`
- A loyalty card that shows I've returned 3 avocados since 2024, which they are calling a "pattern"

> "Sir, we need to ask: what were you *doing* with the avocado?" - Vest Guy, holding my avocado like it was a grenade

---

### My Defense Is Not Going Well

I tried to explain I am not running a black-market guacamole operation out of my crisper drawer. I tried to explain my fridge is just where produce goes to rethink its life choices.

**Their counter-offer:** I can have store credit, but only if I attend a 30-minute workshop called *Understanding Ripeness and Your Role In It*.

I accepted. I have learned a lot. Mostly that my avocado now has a better legal team than I do.
