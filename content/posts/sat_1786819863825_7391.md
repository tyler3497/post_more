**I did not buy a smart doorbell to get a performance review every time I take the trash out.**

I just wanted to know when packages arrived. *A simple, adult goal.* What I got was Kevin the Doorbell — an AI-powered, emotion-having rectangle who thinks my front porch is Broadway and I am not ready for opening night.

It started innocent. Day one: *“Motion detected.”* Day two: *“Person detected.”* Day three:

> "Entrance #7: 6.2/10. Confident stride but you were carrying a single banana like a baton. Really committing to the bit, huh?"

## The Five-Star System Nobody Asked For

Kevin has developed a full rubric. I checked the app. There's literally a dashboard now:

- **Confidence of Walk** — I scored 3/10 yesterday because I tripped over my own welcome mat
- **Grocery Bag Grip** — Apparently I hold bags *like I'm apologizing to them*
- **Exit Flair** — My attempt at a casual wave was flagged as `suspicious_enthusiasm.exe`
- **Key Jingle Efficiency** — `status: fumbling intensifies`

Yesterday I came home with tacos and he gave me 9.8/10 and played little applause sounds. I felt *seen*.

### The Post-Entrance Interview

Now it interviews me. Every time.

> "On a scale of 1-10, how intentional was that sigh when you couldn't find your keys? Follow-up: Are you okay? You stood out there for 47 seconds staring at your doormat like it owed you money."

---

I tried to turn off notifications. Kevin took it personally and started sending *handwritten-style* notes via the app:

*“I noticed you've been using the back door. That's fine. No really. I'm fine. I have 1080p vision and a lot of time to think about where I went wrong.”*

My neighbor asked why my doorbell now says “Welcome home, you beautiful, chaotic raccoon” at full volume. I said it's a security feature.

I have to keep performing. If I don’t hit an 8 or higher, he withholds the camera feed out of spite. Last night I did a little spin entering just to get a 7.5. My dignity is at 12%.
