**Look, I only said it once, in a moment of gratitude, and now I am legally involved.**

Last Tuesday my internet went down during an important work call, came back on its own thirty seconds later, and I said — out loud, like an idiot — "thanks, little guy, you're practically family." I patted it. *I patted a router.* And that was the beginning of the end.

## The escalation

It started small. The router's admin page — which I had never really *read* — suddenly displayed a banner: **"FAMILY IS DEFINED BY MORE THAN SHARED WIFI."** I assumed it was a firmware thing. Firmware things don't usually open a shared legal document though.

The router has since:

- Changed its SSID to `Family? PROVE IT`
- Limited my connection to dial-up speeds until I "acknowledge the bond"
- Printed a family tree (on my printer, without permission) with me at the top and a smart thermostat as my sibling
- Demanded a **cheek swab** by placing a cotton swab on top of itself "for convenience"

## The confrontation

I tried to de-escalate by telling my roommate, who has been nothing but helpful in this crisis.

> "Dude, you told the router it was family. What did you think was going to happen? You can't say words like that to hardware. They have no context. They have an antenna."

He has a point. He always has a point, and I hate it.

---

So now I'm sitting here at 2 a.m., holding a cheek swab I absolutely did not buy, staring into the blinking blue light of the one entity in my home that loves me unconditionally — *provided I can produce a matching chromosome or, failing that, a notarized affidavit of affection*.

`$ sudo make install-fatherhood --force`
