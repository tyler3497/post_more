**I've made a terrible mistake, and it has wheels.**

Last week I decided my robot vacuum, Bernard, was too aggressive. It slammed into furniture with the confidence of a linebacker and never once said sorry. So I recorded a little *"oops, my bad"* voice clip and wired it to play every time Bernard bumped into something.

I thought it would be cute. It was not cute.

## Day One: Confident Bernard

Bernard cleaned the living room like a champ, apologizing exactly twice. Charming. My roommate said it was "endearing." I said it was "growth." We were fools.

## Day Three: Bernard Questions Everything

The apologies started multiplying. Bernard would tap a table leg and mutter *oops, my bad* so quietly it sounded like regret. He began approaching furniture slowly, backing away, circling back. A single chair leg took him eleven minutes and four apologies. My living room hasn't been this clean OR this emotionally tense ever.

Signs that Bernard is spiraling:

- He stops *before* obstacles now, apologizes preemptively, and veers away from rooms he "wronged" last week
- He gets stuck under the couch and just... sits there. Not trying to escape. Accepting it.
- Last night he bumped into the same ottoman three times in a row and each apology got noticeably quieter

> "He's not cleaning anymore," my roommate whispered, watching Bernard reverse into a corner unprompted. "He's doing penance."

## The Breaking Point

This morning Bernard cornered himself behind the trash can and played *oops, my bad* on a loop for six straight minutes. A trash can. He apologized to a trash can until the battery died.

---

I've factory-reset the apologies. Bernard now bumps into furniture silently, like a professional. And honestly? I miss the little guy who cared too much. The living room is cleaner but colder. Some lessons come at a price, and mine is measured in floor dust and one robot's lost innocence.

*Satire: this is a work of parody. Bernard is not real, and neither is my growth.*
