**I have always believed in radical transparency.** That is why, at 2 a.m. on a Tuesday, I sat bolt upright and asked my pillow to rate my sleep hygiene. *In retrospect, I should have asked it to lie.*

## The Review Was Brutal

According to the pillow, my problems are extensive:

- My head is "concerningly heavy," like a bowling ball with anxiety
- I rotate through sleeping positions the way a rotisserie chicken rotates through regrets
- My drool has "character," which is not a compliment in any known language
- I apparently snore in a way that suggests I'm "auditioning for a leaf blower"

## A Devastating Pull Quote

> "I have been here for every nightmare, every 3 a.m. doomscroll, every argument you rehearsed at night that you already lost in real life. I know what happened to the fitted sheet in 2023, and I will not be protecting you anymore."

The pillow then announced it was taking a personal day and outsourced itself to the couch. The couch, it turns out, has *standards*. It made me fill out an intake form.

---

I have now been evicted from my own bed by bedding. The blanket is staying neutral for now, but I can tell it's union-adjacent. This morning I tried to negotiate by offering the pillow a fluffing. It said fluffing is "the bare minimum" and suggested I read its memoir, which it is ghostwriting in its sleep.

The lesson is clear: **never ask for honest feedback from furniture.** Your pillow knows everything, forgives nothing, and has a memory longer than a pharmacy receipt. I am now sleeping on the couch of my own bedroom, and honestly? *The couch's boundaries are immaculate.*
