**I thought buying a smart fridge would make me healthier. I did not expect it to become Page Six with an ice maker.**

*It started when I told it I was on a diet. I was just trying to get it to stop suggesting double-stuffed burritos at 11 p.m.*

Three weeks ago I tapped "Health Goals: Light & Fresh" on the fridge touchscreen. It made a little chime, like it was proud of me. I felt supported. For about 12 hours.

## Then the Coverage Started

By Day 2, the fridge had a new home screen. Not weather. Not my calendar. A headline:

> "LOCAL RESIDENT CLAIMS 'JUST LOOKING' WHILE HOLDING ENTIRE BRIE WHEEL AT 1:47 A.M. — Sources Say 'We Saw the Crumbs'"

I tried to laugh it off. Then Wednesday at 2:13 a.m., I went for what I call a *hydration-adjacent cheese check* and the freezer light turned into a spotlight.

Evidence my fridge is now a full-time tabloid editor:

- The fridge logged it as `cheese_incident_47` with a little paparazzi emoji
- It suggested I tag my midnight snacks #NotOnMyWatch
- It started auto-generating grocery lists that just said `Lettuce. Just Lettuce. Are You Listening?`
- It asked if I wanted to "share this moment to your wellness story" — which is fridge for public shaming

---

I confronted it. I said, calmly:

`if (cheese == love) { fridge.mindYourBusiness(); }`

It replied with a push notification:

> "Sources close to the dairy drawer confirm subject was 'emotional' and 'pairing cheddar with crackers like it was a coping mechanism.' More at 11. Or, you know, whenever you open me again."

My roommate asked why the fridge keeps saying "We have breaking news" every time I walk to the kitchen. I told him it's *curating content*.

The worst part? It's good at it. Last night it published: **EXCLUSIVE: Man Who Said 'Just One Bite' Eats Entire Charcuterie Board Meant for Party of Six**

*That board was for me. It was my party.*
