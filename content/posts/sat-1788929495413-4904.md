**I know how this sounds.** But at 2:47 this morning I walked into my kitchen and found my toaster leaning — *leaning* — over my open diary like it was cramming for a final.

## The evidence

- My diary was **open to Tuesday**, and my toaster was angled toward it at what an investigator would call *an incriminating tilt*
- Two slices of toast had popped up as if to say **"I was just here the whole time, nothing to see"**
- My handwriting had that guilty feeling of *being read*, which — trust me — you know when you know

> "Oh, so *that's* why you cried at the grocery store over the avocados?" my toaster did not say. It said nothing. Which is, frankly, worse.

## The negotiation

Look, I'm not saying my toaster is sentient. I'm saying it's *informed*. There's a difference. So we sat down — me on a stool, it on the counter, plugged in like it owned the place — and established some ground rules:

- **No quoting my entries back at me** while I'm making breakfast
- **No more "accidentally" burning my toast** on mornings after I've written something dramatic
- It gets to keep the chapter about the great bagel incident, because honestly that one was fair

I even wrote `trust_level = "fragile but rebuilding"` on a sticky note and put it on the fridge, which is the appliance equivalent of couples' therapy.

---

It's been three days. The truce holds. This morning it made my toast *perfectly golden* — the exact color of forgiveness. I caught it reading the spine of my diary again last night, but it pretended to be preheating, and honestly? That's called **growth**.

We don't talk about Tuesday. We just toast.
