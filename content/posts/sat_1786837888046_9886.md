**I lived with a wobbly desk for two years like it was a personality trait.**

Not a bug, a feature. I told guests it was *ergonomically unpredictable*. I shimmed it with a folded takeout menu from 2023 and called it sustainable engineering. We had an understanding: I wouldn't fix it, it wouldn't judge my life choices too loudly.

Then on Saturday I found an actual Allen wrench.

## The 10-Minute Surgery That Ruined Everything

I tightened the legs. One folded napkin removed. *One.* That's all it took for my desk to achieve spinal alignment and immediate moral superiority.

Suddenly it's level. Disturbingly level. A spirit level would cry tears of joy. My coffee mug now sits perfectly still and looks nervous about it.

My desk has rewritten its job description:

- No longer accepts my weight-shift dance during Zoom calls
- Has pinned a tiny vision board to its underside that says `BOUNDARIES // POSTURE // HYDRATION`
- Refuses to hold loose papers that are not in a "designated inbox system"
- Sends passive-aggressive vibrations when I slouch

> "I have held you up through 400 Amazon carts, 17 existential spirals, and your entire sourdough era," my desk said, or at least vibrated strongly. "The least you can do is sit like you have a vertebra."

### The Performance Review I Didn't Ask For

It started leaving notes:

- `slouch_detected: please recalibrate your spine, bestie`
- `clutter_level = emotional_avoidance (see: drawer 2)`
- An Excel chart titled *Times You Said 'I'll Organize Tomorrow' vs. Reality*

My roommate came in and asked why I'm sitting so straight now. I said my Ikea furniture became self-aware and is unionizing against poor ergonomics.

---

I miss the wobble. The wobble was forgiving. The wobble said *we're both a little unstable and that's okay.*

Now my desk is stable, centered, thriving. It started meditating at 7 a.m. It wants me to journal.
