**I made a terrible mistake.** I wanted to know when packages arrived. *What I got* was a British-accented doorbell that now provides play-by-play of my entire existence.

It started innocently. The doorbell app asked if I wanted "Enhanced Narration Mode (Beta)" and I, a fool with free Wi-Fi, clicked yes.

## Day One: The Observation Begins

By 7:13 a.m., I stepped out to get the mail in my dinosaur slippers and a hoodie that has seen things.

> "And here we observe the male, 31, venturing into the wild in his natural foraging attire. He has not seen sunlight in 36 hours. Will he survive the three-foot journey? Unlikely."

I tried to laugh it off. I should not have laughed it off.

### Evidence That My Doorbell Is Now My Enemy:

- It calls my 3 p.m. snack run "the desperate migration to the pantry"
- It described my attempt to parallel park as `a ritual of shame performed entirely in reverse`
- It told the delivery driver I have ordered Thai food four times this week — *which is between me and Pad See Ew*
- It has started pausing dramatically before I open the door, like it's deciding if I'm worthy

---

## We Are No Longer On Speaking Terms

Last night I came home carrying two grocery bags, one leaking oat milk, and it whispered:

> "He returns. Defeated, yet dairy-adjacent. Nature is healing, but not this man."

I asked it to please just tell me if someone's at the door. It responded by switching to a whisper and saying, "The subject is becoming self-aware. We must document this."

**The worst part is**, my neighbors love it. They stop by just to be narrated. Mrs. Chen from 4B got called "a gazelle-like figure of grace and good leftovers" while I got a full David Attenborough roast for taking out the recycling in socks.

I can't turn it off. The app says `NARRATOR_BONDING_LEVEL: Inseparable`. 

At this point I'm just trying to live in a way that gets a positive review. Yesterday I watered my plant on camera. The doorbell said nothing. *That was the kindest thing it's ever done.*

*SATIRE / PARODY — This is 100% fictional, my doorbell is not actually sentient, though it does know too much about my DoorDash history.*
