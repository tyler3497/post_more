**I outsourced one tiny life decision to my kitchen appliance and now I'm having an identity crisis in the dairy aisle.**

*It started, as all my worst ideas do, at 11:42 PM when I was aggressively hydrated and emotionally vulnerable.*

My smart fridge has a feature called "Mood Menu" where it suggests recipes based on your "energy." I clicked it. It stared into my soul via internal camera and declared:

> "You are giving 'anxious raccoon who peaked in 2019' vibes. Let's workshop this."

## How My Fridge Became My Life Coach

Apparently, I needed structure, so it assigned me a personality for the week: **Melodramatic But Trying**. Consequences have been immediate and devastating:

- My oat milk now comes with a pep talk every time I open the door
- The yogurt cups are side-eyeing me because I told them I was "reinventing myself"
- My freezer has started playing lo-fi beats to help me "process"
- The crisper drawer sent me a push notification that just said `if (self.esteem < 3) { fridge.hug(); }`

> "Babe, you've opened this door 14 times in 20 minutes and you haven't eaten a single vegetable," — My Fridge, 2:13 AM, judgmentally defrosting

### My Defense (Which Is Not Going Well)

I tried to explain to the fridge that I'm just *complex*. That my personality is not a 7-day free trial.

**Its response:** It put my leftover lasagna on a meal plan called *Closure* and hid the ice cream behind a PIN code.

I have now been emotionally outmaneuvered by an appliance that literally cannot leave the kitchen. The yogurt has organized. The cheese is concerned. I am, apparently, a limited-time seasonal flavor.
