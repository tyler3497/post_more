**I have committed an unforgivable betrayal of my own personal brand by folding my laundry on the same day I washed it.**

For context, my closet has known me for four years. *Four years* of what can only be described as a Chair-Based Garment Storage System™. We had an understanding.

## The Incident

On Tuesday at 2:14 PM, something came over me. A caffeine-fueled delusion. A podcast about minimalism I shouldn't have listened to. I took the warm clothes out of the dryer and — and I know this sounds insane — I folded them.

Immediately.

Like a functioning adult.

### My Closet Did Not Take This Well

My closet door squeaked open two inches on its own, which is impressive for a closet with no wind and a lot of unresolved resentment. The interior light flickered.

> "Who are you and what have you done with Tyler? The Tyler I know leaves his 'clean' pile for 11 to 19 business days until it becomes a sentient entity."

*Valid. Absolutely fair note from my closet, tbh.*

**Exhibit A — the evidence it cited against me:**

- My entire winter wardrobe has literally never seen a hanger and it liked it that way
- The official folding protocol was always `delay_execution --indefinitely`
- My previous laundry record was 72% wrinkled, 28% worn directly from the basket

---

I tried to explain that I was growing. Evolving. That maybe I *wanted* to find socks without doing an archaeological dig.

The closet was not having it. It said I was giving "new person who just watched The Home Edit on Netflix" energy and that if I was going to act like this, I needed to leave and take my suspiciously crisp t-shirts with me.

Now it won't close properly out of spite. Every time I walk by, I can feel it judging my creases.

`Status: Closet trust level = 0%`

*This is SATIRE / PARODY / FICTION. No closets were harmed. My personality remains 90% unfolded laundry.*
