**I decided to become a responsible adult who meal preps on Sundays.**

*My therapist calls this 'future-oriented coping.' My stomach calls it a hostile takeover.*

Sunday Me is an optimist. He puts on a podcast, buys quinoa in bulk, and believes with his whole heart that he will absolutely want to eat the same lemon-herb chicken five days in a row. Sunday Me is also a liar.

## Monday Was Full of Promise

I lined up five identical glass containers like I was on a cooking show. I labeled them M/T/W/Th/F with a Sharpie I immediately regretted buying.

- **Monday:** Proud. Ate at my desk like a CEO. Texted a friend \"meal prep changed my life\"
- **Tuesday:** Still committed. Added hot sauce. Felt resourceful
- **Wednesday:** Stared into the container like it owed me money

That's when the reviews started.

> \"One star. Chicken has developed the texture and emotional range of a yoga mat. Would not recommend this lifestyle to my worst enemy. Also you forgot a fork. Again. — Wednesday Me, 12:47 p.m., microwaving in the break room while questioning every decision that led here\"

### The Feedback Loop

By Thursday, Future Me had formed a union. Their demands were clear in the group chat I apparently created with myself:

- No more \"mystery grain\" that is just quinoa pretending to be interesting
- Mandatory `snack.break()` every 90 minutes because \"we are not robots, we are snack-driven organisms\"
- An immediate ban on the phrase \"it'll be good to have something healthy ready\"
- A formal apology for putting cilantro in everything after I swore I liked it

I tried to negotiate. I told them meal prepping saves money.

> \"We have $40,000 in exposure to lunch trauma now. You saved $12. You spent $18 on containers that don't stack. Math is not your strength, Sunday.\"

---

**The breaking point was Friday.** The container had leaked. The chicken was cold in the middle. I ate it standing over my sink like a raccoon who peaked in 2021 and whispered:

`if (meal.prepped && meal.day == 5) { motivation = null; doordash.open(); }`

Look, I get it. Sunday Me means well. But Wednesday Me has to live with his choices. We are in counseling now — mostly about why Sunday Me thinks five identical meals equals personality growth.
