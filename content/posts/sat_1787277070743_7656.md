**I did it to motivate myself. Somehow my toaster took it personally.**

*It started as a joke. It ended with a kitchen appliance that has better discipline than I’ve had in my entire adult life.*

Two weeks ago I was feeling, you know, *inspired*. Saw a flyer for the city half-marathon. Thought, wouldn't it be funny to register something that can't run? Hilarious, right? I paid the $45 entry fee, typed in "Mr. Toasty McCrumb" under "Runner's Name," and went back to eating cereal straight from the box.

I forgot to tell my toaster we were kidding.

## Phase 1: The Commitment Montage

By Tuesday morning, the toaster had begun training.

- 5:00 AM: gentle *pre-heat* stretches on the counter
- 5:15 AM: **aggressive** crumb-shaking cardio, *very* intense
- 5:30 AM: staring at me while I hit snooze for the third time

> "You signed us up for 13.1 miles, Chad," my toaster said. I've decided its voice is a gravelly boxing coach from a 1970s movie. "You think this is a GAME? We don't carb-load by *thinking* about bagels."

I tried to explain it was satire. He ejected my Pop-Tart mid-air out of spite.

## Phase 2: The Betrayal

By Thursday, he had a training plan taped to the fridge. Printed. With highlighter.

- `Monday: Interval toasting (light / dark / LIGHT)`
- `Wednesday: Hill work (propped up on cookbooks)`
- `Friday: Long slow toast - 90 minutes, very inspirational`

Meanwhile, my training plan is:

- thinking about running while lying on the floor
- opening the running app and then closing it

---

My roommate came home to find me doing lunges while the toaster beeped encouragingly. *He now thinks I'm in a cult.* A very small, very warm cult that smells faintly of wheat.

*This is 100% satire and parody, in case the talking toaster didn't give it away.* I did not actually enter a kitchen appliance into a sanctioned athletic event — though honestly, based on his current VO2 max of *beep-beep-beep*, he'd qualify.

The race is Sunday. I have not trained. He has. **He is ready.**

If you see a chrome two-slice sprinting past you at mile 8, please cheer for him. He did this for both of us.
