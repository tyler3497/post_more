**I have been officially outperformed by dust.**

For three years, I treated "clean under the bed" as a *suggestion* from Big Vacuum, not a lifestyle requirement. My logic was simple: if I can't see it, it's basically storage.

## How It Started

Last Tuesday, at 11:47 p.m. — prime bad-decision o'clock — I finally looked. Flashlight in hand, dignity nowhere to be found, I discovered a thriving micro-metropolis complete with string lights, a tiny farmers market, and what appeared to be a functioning municipal government.

> "We've been watching your productivity for some time," said Mayor Lintwell, a very serious dust bunny in a bottle-cap hat. "Frankly, we're concerned."

*He was not wrong to be concerned.*

## The Evidence

In the 72 hours since contact, they have:

- Achieved a 98% recycling rate using my lost bobby pins and single socks
- Built a high-speed transit system out of dental floss and hope
- Hosted a TED Talk titled *How We Do So Much With So Little (And You Don't)*
- Balanced their budget, something I have not done since 2019

Meanwhile, I have:

- Reheated the same coffee three times
- Lost my keys while holding them
- Googled "how to be an adult" unironically

---

I offered to co-exist peacefully. They offered me a performance improvement plan.

```js
if (human.motivation < civilization.motivation) {
  promote(dustBunnies);
  demote(human);
}
```

I tried to negotiate rent. They said *I'm* the one living in *their* storage unit now, and my emotional baggage is taking up valuable real estate.

I haven't vacuumed. It feels wrong to commit eminent domain on a society that's already got a better credit score than me.

*Satire — obviously. No civilizations were displaced in the making of this essay, just my ego.*
