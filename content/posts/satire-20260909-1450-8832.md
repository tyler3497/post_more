**I made a fatal mistake this morning: I looked at the mirror and asked it to be real with me.**

Big error. Enormous. Never ask the one person — entity — *surface* — you cannot mute to "tell it like it is." It will. It has been *waiting*.

## How It Started

I was brushing my teeth, half-asleep, and I said the magic words: "Okay, mirror, be honest — do I look tired?"

The silence was devastating. Then my reflection gave me the slowest once-over I have ever received, and I swear the lightbulb flickered. It didn't even need words. It just raised one eyebrow — the one it learned from me — and let the judgment hang there like humidity.

## The Evidence Against Me

A short list of things my reflection has reportedly said behind my back:

- That my "bedhead" has entered its cubist era
- That the left side of my face is "phoning it in"
- That I have been aging in *italic*
- That my posture is "a rumor that never caught on"
- That my skincare routine is `console.log("help")` wearing a hat

## The Haircut Summit

I thought getting a haircut would reset the relationship. Neutral territory. A professional mediator with clippers. Instead, we sat there in a standoff for forty-five minutes. My reflection refused to nod along when the barber asked, "Looking good, right?" It simply stared. The barber laughed nervously and took an extra half-inch off "just to be safe." I didn't ask who the safe part was for.

> "You are not aging gracefully," the reflection said, as clearly as fluorescent light can say anything. "You are aging *theatrically*."

---

So now I get my hair cut facing away from the mirror. The barber says it's the funniest thing he's ever seen — but I've seen my reflection laughing, and it is not the funniest thing *it* has ever seen. It is not even close.

*Satire. This post is a work of fiction. No mirrors were harmed, though one is currently giving me the silent treatment.*
