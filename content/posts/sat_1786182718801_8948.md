## The Upgrade I Didn't Think Through

**I bought a standing desk to be healthier** and now I sleep with one eye open because my old desk won't stop processing our breakup.

It started innocently. My back hurt, my posture was *best described as shrimp*, and the internet said standing would fix everything. So I ordered The Perch 3000™. My old desk — a loyal oak thing I've had since college that has survived three moves, two floods, and one attempt to build IKEA furniture on top of it — took it personally.

### The Evidence He's Not Over Me

In the last 48 hours alone, I've received:

- **4 voicemails** at 2:17 a.m. that are just heavy breathing and the sound of a drawer opening and closing sadly
- A Post-it note that says `I still have your stapler and your dreams`
- A LinkedIn endorsement for "Sitting Down" from an account called NotMyOldDeskDefinitelyNot
- A Spotify playlist titled *Songs To Wobble To When She Leaves You For Lumbar Support*

The standing desk, meanwhile, is thriving and insufferable. It keeps whispering *engage your core* every time I try to eat a donut.

> "You said we had *history*," the latest voicemail went, voice cracking. "You said I was *sturdy*. Was I not sturdy enough for you? Was my cable management not *supportive* enough?"

I tried to explain it was structural, not personal.

---

### Attempted Mediation

My current strategy:

- **Stage 1:** Pretend the guest room is just "his new office"
- **Stage 2:** Leave snacks on him so he feels valued (*he prefers the decorative gourds*)
- **Stage 3:** Admit I miss having somewhere to put my feet up

My roommate says I should apologize. My chiropractor says I should commit.

`Status: currently standing, emotionally sitting, morally compromised by furniture`

We are in couples counseling now. Mostly because the old desk refuses to leave the guest room and keeps playing our song — that sad little creak it makes when I lean too far left.
