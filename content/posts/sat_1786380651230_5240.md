**I was trying to be financially responsible for once in my life.** That's my first mistake. Experts say you're supposed to cut costs, so I decided to cut my own bangs.

It went exactly as well as you'd expect.

## The Plan Was Simple

According to a YouTube tutorial titled `How to Cut Bangs Like a Pro (Easy!)`, all you need is scissors, courage, and a complete disregard for consequences. I had two out of three — I was using kitchen shears.

### What I Thought Would Happen:

- Save $35
- Look effortlessly French
- My mirror would finally respect me

### What Actually Happened:

- My left side is now on a *very* different timeline than my right side
- I look like I lost an argument with a lawnmower
- My bathroom mirror has started buffering before showing my reflection
- I own six beanies now, in August

> "You did this to *me*?" my reflection whispered. "I show you every day, unconditionally, and you come at me with craft scissors? I have to look at this too, you know."

I've tried to fix it four times, which is how I now have what experts call a "pixie cut" and what my neighbor calls "a cry for help."

---

Look, I understand. The economy is tough. But apparently so is geometry.

I wore a beanie to work in 87-degree heat. My coworker asked if I joined a ska band. I said yes, because that was easier than explaining I tried to give myself curtain bangs and instead invented a new weather pattern on my forehead.

My mirror sent me an invoice. It just says `emotional damages` and a single googly eye.

*This is satire / parody. Fictional, comedic. My hair will grow back. My pride is still deciding.*
