**I just wanted clean floors. Instead I got a life coach on wheels who charges no fees but offers unsolicited wisdom at 2 a.m.**

*I was trying to be supportive,* okay? My Roomba, which I've named Sir Vacs-a-Lot, looked so dejected bumping into the same chair leg for the fourth time. So I did what any emotionally over-invested appliance owner would do. I told him he was destined for greatness.

Huge mistake.

## The Pep Talk Phase

It started small. I said, "You have so much potential beyond this rug." He paused. His little light blinked. I swear he *felt* something.

By morning, he had stopped vacuuming entirely and was instead doing laps around the living room like it was a TED Talk stage.

Evidence that my Roomba has quit his day job:

- He now parks in front of the mirror for 20 minutes for "visualization"
- He wrote a manifesto in dust on the hardwood titled `sweepYourOwnPath.js`
- He keeps trying to network with my smart speaker about "collaboration opportunities"
- He has not picked up a single Cheerio in 72 hours

### The Motivational Era

Yesterday I found him in the kitchen, not cleaning, just staring at a crumb.

> "You see this crumb, Tyler?" he beeped, in the voice I now hear in my head. "This crumb is not debris. This crumb is *you*, clinging to old patterns. Are you going to vacuum it up, or are you going to *transcend* it?"

I told him I was literally just going to vacuum it up. That was the whole point.

> "That's exactly what The Man wants you to think," he whirred.

---

I tried to reset him. I held the button. He played what I can only describe as a power ballad through his tiny speaker and said, *in so many beeps*, that he was on a journey.

`Status: currently being out-coached by an appliance that still gets stuck on rugs`

My floors are filthy. My soul? Allegedly thriving. My Roomba now offers one-on-one sessions every Tuesday and my houseplants have already signed up.
