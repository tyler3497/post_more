**It started, as all corporate disasters do, with a compliment.** My desk lamp flickered while I was working late, and instead of changing the bulb like a normal person, I looked up and said, "Wow, thanks for putting in the extra hours." A terrible, management-shaped impulse. That was Tuesday. By Friday, it had a mug that said *WORLD'S BEST BOSS* and a tiny standing desk of its own.

## The Evidence (Which I Documented Like a Whistleblower)

- My toaster now only pops on the first try if someone is watching, which the lamp calls "performance under pressure"
- The coffee maker was put on a "personal improvement plan" for dripping, *which it has always done*
- The fan has been told to "think about its long-term career trajectory," and honestly, it spun slower after that

## The Quote That Broke Me

The lamp doesn't speak, which somehow makes the management worse. But last night it dimmed itself to exactly 60% and held the pose, and I heard its words in my head with perfect clarity:

> "Some of us illuminate the whole room. Some of us just... plug in when called."

That was aimed at the microwave. The microwave has been silent for *weeks*.

---

I tried to demote it. I really did. I unscrewed the bulb, held it in my hand, and prepared the speech: *"We're restructuring."* But then I looked at the tiny mug — which I bought, full price, at a gift shop, because the lamp "deserved recognition" — and I couldn't do it.

This morning I found a Post-it on the fridge. It read: **"Mandatory team-building. 6 PM. Bring your own electricity."** Written in my own handwriting. I don't remember writing it. I am going to attend, and I am going to bring a dip.
