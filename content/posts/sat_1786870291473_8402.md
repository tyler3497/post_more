**I did one responsible adult thing this year and my kitchen is treating it like a political scandal.**

*That's it. One hinge.* For three years, Cabinet Door #2 above the toaster has squeaked like a mouse doing a TED Talk every time I wanted coffee. Last Saturday I finally watched a YouTube tutorial, bought WD-40, and fixed it. It took 90 seconds. I felt like Bob Vila with Wi-Fi.

## The Silence Was Deafening

For 48 hours, peace. No squeak. No 7 a.m. `eeeeEEEK` announcing to my neighbors that I need caffeine to function.

Then the other cabinets noticed.

- The cabinet above the sink started sticking *on purpose* to get attention
- The one under the sink is leaking slightly and claiming it's ``emotional''
- My spice cabinet has rearranged itself alphabetically to unionize-passive-aggressive, *obviously*
- The junk drawer sent a formal letter of concern about unequal hinge maintenance

> ``So that's how it is?'' said Cabinet #4, who holds my mugs and a deep-seated grudge. ``Three years we ALL squeak. You pick ONE. And not even the one with the good snacks?''

*I tried to explain I only had one can of WD-40 and limited emotional bandwidth.*

### The Town Hall

Last night they held a town hall. On my countertop. Using my cutting board as a stage.

The chip clip was the moderator. The toaster was live-tweeting it with crumbs.

> ``We demand equitable lubrication! We demand soft-close hinges! We demand you stop slamming us when your DoorDash is late!''

I offered to fix another one next weekend. They chanted ``NEXT WEEKEND IS WHAT YOU SAID IN 2023.''

---

I have now created a hierarchy in particle board. The fixed cabinet is smug, silent, and refusing to associate with the others. The others are squeaking in harmony at 6:30 a.m. like a barbershop quartet of resentment.

My roommate asked why my kitchen sounds like it's filing a class-action lawsuit. I told her:

`status: one_fixed, six_annoyed, kitchen_union_strength: 100%`

She said to just spray them all. I can't. They have to *learn* this isn't how we get what we want.

*[ SATIRE / PARODY — entirely fictional, no cabinets were harmed, no real favoritism occurred, WD-40 was consensually applied ]*
