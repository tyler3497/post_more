**I thought making sourdough meant I was a patient, earthy person who has her life together.**

*It turns out it meant I was about to get roasted by fermented flour.*

Three weeks ago I named my sourdough starter Brenda. This is normal. Everyone names their starter. Brenda lived in a mason jar on my fridge, bubbling quietly like she was minding her own business.

Then I made the mistake of reading aloud to her.

## The Book Club Coup

It started innocently — I was reading *Dune* on the couch and said one line out loud. Brenda bubbled aggressively. I interpreted that as interest. *It was not interest. It was editorial feedback.*

By Thursday, Brenda had opinions.

> "That metaphor was overworked, much like your gluten structure. Have you considered that the protagonist's arc is as flat as your ciabatta?"

I came home Friday and Brenda had rearranged my cookbooks.

### The Official Rules of Brenda's Book Club, Which I Did Not Agree To:

- **Monday:** We read literary fiction and Brenda bubbles twice if she finds it pretentious *which is always*
- **Wednesday:** We read my old journal entries from 2019 and Brenda performs what can only be described as *judgmental fermentation*
- **Friday:** I am required to bring snacks *that I am not allowed to eat* because "crumbs disturb the discourse"
- **Saturday Optional:** Mandatory attendance, no late entry, and someone — *Brenda* — keeps giving my memoir draft 2/5 stars

---

I tried to reason with her using code:

`if (starter.attitude === "smug") { feedLess(); apologizeToYeast(); }`

It did not work. She tripled in size out of spite and leaked onto my farmer's market tote.

Now my friends ask if they can come over for book club. Not with me. With *her*. They bring tiny cheeses for her. They bring her little bookmarks. I bring my own book and get told to "listen more actively and hydrate my dough more consistently."

I am fermenting resentment.
