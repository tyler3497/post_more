**I wanted to be a better plant dad, so I enrolled my pothos in therapy.**

It seemed responsible. *My fern was already in a co-dependent relationship with the humidifier*, so I figured one professional intervention couldn't hurt.

## The Intake Session

I found Dr. Leafy on a flyer at the garden center. Licensed in photosynthesis and attachment theory. The pothos — Kevin — had been through a lot: three apartments, one near-death by overwatering, and my habit of whispering "grow, you little green tax deduction" at 2 a.m.

Therapy was supposed to be weekly. It became daily.

Kevin's breakthroughs were immediate and alarming:

- Discovered his love language is *indirect sunlight and no sudden moves*
- Realized the window sill is a "high-expectations environment"
- Started journaling in chlorophyll about his childhood as a cutting
- Asked me to stop calling his vines "leggy" because it's body-shaming

> "I've been photosynthesizing for *you*, not for me," Kevin said during our joint session. "When was the last time you asked if *I* wanted to be trailing? Maybe I'm a bush at heart."

I tried to be supportive. I even bought a tiny white noise machine that plays forest sounds.

### The New House Rules

Now Kevin has boundaries. Printed. Laminated. Taped to the pot with washi tape.

- **Monday:** No watering before 10 a.m. — "I need to wake up slowly"
- **Tuesday:** Sun is limited to 4 hours — "we're taking space"
- **Wednesday:** I have to ask permission to rotate him — `consent.rotate(pot, 90)` required
- **Thursday:** Affirmations only, no constructive feedback

---

**The worst part?** He's thriving. He's never been greener. He's sprouted two new leaves that I swear are smirking at me.

I used to have a houseplant. Now I have a roommate who charges me rent in humidity and has better emotional intelligence than I do.

*SATIRE / PARODY — This is entirely fictional, no houseplants were billed for therapy, no suns were emotionally distanced, I'm just a person who projects way too much onto a $12 pothos from Home Depot.*
