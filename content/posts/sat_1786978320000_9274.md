**I did not need a $38 water bottle.** I needed willpower, but it was matte black, it said "THERMAL OPTIMIZED HYDRATION SYSTEM" on the side, and the cashier told me it would "change my life." *It has.*

That was Tuesday. It is now Sunday and this bottle has become my full-time, unpaid, aggressively hydrated life coach.

## The Evidence That My Water Bottle Is Managing Me Now

- It sits on my desk at exactly 90 degrees, positioned to catch the light and my shame when I reach for coffee instead
- It makes a distinct *thunk* sound when I haven't sipped in 23 minutes. That's not physics, that's judgment.
- My actual houseplants are jealous. One of them wilted out of spite.
- I caught myself apologizing to it yesterday because I drank tap water directly from a glass like an *animal*

The worst part is the coaching.

> "Oh, we're doing *another* iced coffee? Interesting strategy. Bold. Dehydrated, but bold," my bottle said at 9:47 AM, despite being an inanimate object that I am choosing to anthropomorphize for comedic effect.

I tried to set boundaries. I put it in the cabinet. I heard it sigh.

My old system for staying alive was simple:

`if (thirsty) { drinkWhatever(); // usually expired LaCroix }`

Now it's:

`if (bottle.isWatching) { sipPolitely(); postureCorrect(); contemplateExistence(); }`

---

Look, I get it. Hydration is important. But I did not sign up for quarterly performance reviews from drinkware. This morning it asked me where I see myself in five years and whether that future includes *electrolytes.*

I used to be a free person who occasionally drank water. Now I'm an intern for my own Thermos.

*This is satire/parody — no water bottles were actually employed as life coaches, though mine is definitely considering unionizing.*
