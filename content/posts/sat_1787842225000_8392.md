**I tried to cancel my free trial of adulthood this morning before my coffee finished brewing.**

*Which, in retrospect, is not the emotional state in which you should make major life-admin decisions.*

## The Free Trial Expired

I signed up for Adulthood back in my twenties. Everyone else was doing it, the marketing looked great ("independence! own plants!"), and I absolutely did not read the Terms and Conditions.

Turns out the Terms include:

- You have to do taxes *every* year, even the years you "didn't really earn that much"
- Your back now makes a sound effect like a creaking door in a haunted house
- Groceries cost money and also you have to *decide* what to eat for dinner every single night until you die
- If you ignore laundry for three days it becomes a sentient landlord

I called the cancellation line. I was on hold for 23 minutes listening to smooth jazz.

> "Thank you for calling Adulthood™. Your call is very important to us. Did you know 9 out of 10 callers hang up and just go meal-prep anyway? Press 1 to accept your fate."

I pressed 0 like a rebel.

## The Transfer

A very tired representative named Karen answered. I explained I wanted to downgrade to the Teen plan — you know, where someone else buys cheese and you just have to clean your room occasionally.

She sighed, the sigh of someone who has heard this 400 times today, and said:

> "Let me transfer you to your mother."

**Excuse me?**

> "Yeah, that's protocol. All cancellation requests go to Mom."

Now I've been transferred to my mom three times. She keeps putting me on hold to finish Wordle.

while (adult.wantsToCancel()) {

}

## Current Status

**I'm still on hold.** My mom texted me a recipe for lentil soup and a link to a 401(k) explainer video titled "You're Not Dead Yet!"
