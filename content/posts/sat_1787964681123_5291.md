*For context, my camera roll was 98% screenshots of recipes I never made, 1% accidental pocket videos of the floor, and 1% photos of my cat blinking at 3x speed. I told myself I was being organized. My phone heard "purge."*

## The Great Deletion of 2026

It started with storage. My phone said "Storage Almost Full" and I said "bet." Three hours later I had deleted:

- 14,203 screenshots of tweets I intended to read later in 2022
- 8,411 near-identical sunset photos where the only difference was a bird that may have been a smudge
- 6,000 blurry pictures of a receipt I took "for warranty" but never looked at again
- And one cursed Live Photo of me sneezing in a grocery store that my phone kept recommending as a Memory

I felt powerful. I felt free. I had 64GB back.

> "You don't understand what you've done," whispered my Recently Deleted album. "They had families. They had hopes. That photo of a parking spot you saved so you wouldn't lose your car in 2021? He was going to be somebody."
