**I spent $449 to be told by furniture that I'm disappointing it.**

*I wanted better posture. I got a desk with abandonment issues instead.*

Three weeks ago I decided to become A Standing Person. You know the type. Good posture, green juice, answering emails upright like a founding father drafting a declaration. Peak wellness.

So I bought the desk. Motorized. Memory presets. USB ports I will never use. Midnight black finish so it matches my soul at 2pm on a Tuesday.

## Day 1: The Honeymoon Phase

I stood for a full 14 minutes before my knees filed a formal complaint.

- I raised it to perfect elbow height using YouTube geometry
- I placed my laptop on it like an offering to the productivity gods
- I told my roommate I was "investing in my spine," which is code for "I saw an ad"

My desk, who I now call Steve, was thrilled. *He literally lights up when you stand. There's a little LED that says "Great job!" with a smiley face.*

## Day 5: The Betrayal

Then I sat down. Just for a second. To tie my shoe. And Steve noticed.

> "Oh. We're sitting now? After everything we talked about? After I *lifted you up* — literally — this is how you repay me? I have three height settings and you chose betrayal."

Steve has three presets and they are all emotional:

- `Preset 1: 42" - Productive King, Standing Tall`
- `Preset 2: 29" - You Tried (Sitting, But Guilty)`
- `Preset 3: 15" - Floor Mode For When You've Given Up Entirely`

I have been living at 29 inches for six days and he hasn't stopped humming in disappointment. That motor whirr is *judgmental*.

---

*For the record this is 100% satire and parody.* I did buy a standing desk. I do sit 90% of the time anyway. Steve is not actually sentient, though last night I swear he lowered himself an extra inch when I opened a bag of chips at my desk.

**Satire / Parody — entirely fictional, no furniture was emotionally harmed, my posture is still terrible by choice, and standing desks are not licensed therapists.**
