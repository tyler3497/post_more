**This is what happens when you hand a calculator to a creature that has never paid rent.**

It started innocently. Tax season was approaching, I was dreading the paperwork, and my beagle Biscuit was staring at me with the unmistakable look of someone who had opinions. So I did what any rational adult would do: I sat him down and explained deductions. I drew him a little chart. I even made him a laminated "Biscuit's Tax Tips" card, which is where things went wrong, because Biscuit is, apparently, a *natural*.

## Week One: Enthusiasm

Biscuit took to accounting like a labrador takes to a mud puddle. He sniffed every receipt. He chewed the corner off my W-2, which he later described as "a routine verification procedure." Within days he was organizing my receipts by crumbliness.

## Week Two: The Red Flags

- He began barking at 8:47 AM sharp every day, which I now understand was him "clocking in"
- He requested — *demanded*, really — a desk. I bought him a desk
- He started referring to me as "the taxpayer" and to himself as "the office"
- He confiscated the treat jar and labeled it **EVIDENCE**

> "Sir, you claimed these peanut-butter bones as a 'home office supply,'" he said through what I can only describe as an expression of profound professional disappointment. "Please produce the corresponding 1099-BISCUIT."

## The Audit

Last Tuesday he wore the tiny suit. I don't know where he got it. I have *never* ordered a dog suit. He set up a folding table in the hallway, stamped my snack log with a red AUDIT stamp he also apparently owns, and began the hearing.

The findings were brutal. Treats given during tax preparation: **disallowed**. Treats given "just because his little face": reclassified as taxable income. The emergency cheese cube fund I kept behind the blender: flagged for "aggressive off-book snack hoarding."

---

He has set my next audit for November. He has already sent a calendar invite. I accepted it, because what else are you going to do.

*Satire. No actual dogs were harmed. Several were compensated handsomely.*
