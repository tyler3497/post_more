**I just wanted to find my spices faster.** That's all. A little organization.

Instead, I now live in a bureaucracy of my own making.

### The Great Labeling of 2026

It started innocently on Saturday. I bought a $17 label maker from the hardware store. *You know, to be an adult.* By Sunday morning, my apartment looked like an Amazon warehouse run by a very judgmental librarian.

- The fridge is now **"CHILL OUT ZONE (NO, SERIOUSLY, STOP MICROWAVING FISH)"**
- My houseplant is tagged `EMOTIONALLY NEEDY - WATER ME` which feels personal
- The couch is labeled **"EXISTENTIAL DREAD DEPT. - SEATING 10AM-2AM"**
- My junk drawer has been reclassified as *"ARCHIVE OF BAD DECISIONS & CABLES"*

My roommate walked in, stared at the toaster labeled `BREAD'S FINAL SAUNA`, and said:

> "Are you okay? Did the label maker... talk to you?"

It *did* feel like it was talking. The little *BRRZT-CHK* sound it makes every time it prints? That's affirmation. That's purpose.

---

### The Enforcement Phase

Last night I caught myself reaching for the coffee without announcing my intentions to the kitchen. The label on the coffee maker — **"FUEL FOR REGRETS - 6AM MEETING REQUIRED"** — seemed to glow with disappointment.

I tried to peel one off. The cat, who now wears a tiny sticker that says `CAT - DO NOT LABEL (RESISTANCE IS FUTILE)`, looked at me like:

> "You did this. We live here now."

I've tried to negotiate. I labeled the label maker itself `OVERACHIEVER`, hoping some self-awareness would kick in. It just printed another label: `META`.

The good news? I can find everything. The bad news? Everything is now judging me alphabetically.

`Status: Labeled, filed, and lightly threatened`
