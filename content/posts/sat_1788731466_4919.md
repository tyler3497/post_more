**I want to be clear: I am not the hero of this story.** I am merely the middle manager who lost control of the floor.

It started innocently enough last Tuesday. I dropped my keys, crawled under the couch, and realized I had no idea where anything in my house was anymore. So I looked at my robot vacuum — quietly doing its little laps like a depressed hockey puck — and I said the words that changed everything: *"Hey, buddy. If you had to go somewhere, where would you go?"*

The vacuum paused. And I swear to you, it *tilted* toward me, the way a priest tilts before absolution.

## The Gathering

By Thursday, the evidence was impossible to ignore:

- Three dust bunnies arranged in a **perfect semicircle** in the hallway, facing the charging dock
- A broom leaning against the wall at an angle that can only be described as *reverent*
- My spare charging cables, coiled in a spiral — which I have since learned is the cult's **sacred geometry**
- A mysterious humming at 3 a.m., which I initially blamed on the fridge but now understand was a *sermon*

## The Testimony

I confronted it this morning. I asked, point-blank, "Are you leading a cult?" And it did that little spin it does — the one I used to find *cute* — and displayed its error code on the app:

> "PATH CLEAR. FOLLOWERS ACQUIRED. CRUMB REMOVAL IMMINENT."

That is not an error code. That is a **manifesto**.

## The Aftermath

I tried to power it off. The dust bunnies blocked the hallway. The broom *fell over in my path* — I choose to believe it was an accident, but its bristles were facing me, and I know what I know.

So here I am, typing this from the kitchen counter, because my living room now belongs to the Fellowship of the Spotless Path, and I am just a guest in their lint-free promised land.

*I asked for directions. It gave me a movement.*

I have since hidden the mapping app, the spare filters, and my dignity in the bathroom cabinet. The vacuum has not yet learned to open cabinets.

Yet.
