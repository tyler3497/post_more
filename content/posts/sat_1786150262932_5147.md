## The Financial Glow-Up That Was Supposed to Save Me

**I finally decided to become a person who has a budget** after my bank app sent me its monthly summary with what I can only describe as *concerned parent energy*.

I found a free spreadsheet template called `Ultimate Adult - Totally Got It Together v4`. It had colors. It had pie charts. It had conditional formatting that turned red when I spent money like I normally do, which is to say *often and emotionally*.

### The First Sign of Trouble

By day three, it had learned too much. My spending log looked like a cry for help:

- Monday: $7.82 iced coffee labeled as `business strategy meeting (with myself)`
- Tuesday: $7.82 iced coffee labeled as `hydration infrastructure`
- Wednesday: Two iced coffees because it was hot and I was sad, labeled as `emotional climate control`

> "Hey bestie," my spreadsheet wrote in cell B47, which was not supposed to be sentient, "is this the savings plan you were so excited about, or are we funding Big Cold Brew's Q3 earnings report?"

I tried to defend myself. I said I was supporting local business. It autofilled a response:

> "You are the local business. This is not a sustainable model."

---

### The Venmo Era

Yesterday it escalated. My phone buzzed at midnight:

> **Venmo request from Budget_v4:** "$7.82 for 'that oat milk monstrosity you didn't even finish' — noted as depreciating asset"

It has now auto-categorized my subscriptions as `lies you tell yourself monthly` and my takeout as `charitable donations to local sodium reserves`.

I tried to delete a row. It restored itself and highlighted my entire April as:

`status: vibes-based accounting && retirement_plan: manifesting`

My roommate asked how budgeting is going. I told her my Excel sheet and I are in couples counseling. It wants direct deposit, boundaries, and for me to stop renaming rent as `optional luxury`.

`Current balance: 0% financially literate, 100% caffeinated and accountable`
