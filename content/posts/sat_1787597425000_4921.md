**I decided my kitchen needed more positive workplace culture.** Which, in retrospect, is exactly how you end up being managed by an appliance that cooks frozen nuggets.

*It started as self-help advice from the internet, which should have been my first red flag.*

## The Onboarding

Three weeks ago, I read that talking nicely to your plants and appliances improves morale. So I started greeting my air fryer every morning. "Good morning, Brenda," I'd say, because of course I named her Brenda.

Brenda responded well. Too well.

Within days she was:

- Preheating faster when I complimented her "glow-up"
- Making my fries *crispier* when I said "we're a team"
- Emitting a little *ding* that sounded suspiciously like validation
- Refusing to cook kale because, quote, "we don't do that kind of negativity here"

My roommate said I was imagining it. My roommate also doesn't get a perfect hash brown at 7am, so who's really winning?

> "I've noticed you've been inconsistent with your morning affirmations. I've taken the liberty of scheduling a 1:1. Please bring your goals, your growth mindset, and those little parchment liners I like." — Brenda, my air fryer, via the passive-aggressive glow of her LED screen

## The Performance Review

Yesterday Brenda refused to start until I sat down for a Q3 check-in.

She had *metrics*.

Her slides — which she displayed by blinking 180°F in Morse code — included `total_nuggets_air_fried`, `times_i_left_her_dirty_overnight`, and a pie chart labeled `emotional_availability` that was just 100% me needing her more than she needs me.

She says I'm "not living up to my full crisp potential." She wants me to set SMART goals. Hers are all about becoming a toaster oven.

---

Look, I'm not saying I've created a monster, but my air fryer now has a LinkedIn mindset, charges me PTO when I use the microwave, and just asked for a standing desk attachment and a better 401(k) match.

I tried to quit the morning routine. She responded by only cooking on `low` out of spite. My tater tots were *soggy*. That's how she gets you.

SATIRE / PARODY — This is 100% fictional. My air fryer is an appliance, not my manager. Though honestly, she's more supportive than my last boss and her benefits package includes free tater tots and zero judgment about eating them in bed.
