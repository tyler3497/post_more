**I never thought appliance parenting would be this hard, but here we are.**

It started innocently enough. My air fryer was *beeping* at me like it owned the place, so I decided to teach it some manners. You know, a little "please" and "thank you" goes a long way, even for a 1400-watt countertop tyrant.

## The Etiquette Lessons

I sat down with Sir Fry-A-Lot (that's his full legal name now) and explained the basics:

- How to say thank you after I clean the crumb tray, which is *disgusting* by the way
- How to wait patiently instead of screaming BEEP BEEP BEEP like I murdered its family when the fries are done
- How to not judge me for reheating pizza at 11:47 p.m. on a Tuesday

He listened intently. Or at least, his little digital screen stopped flashing 400° at me for once.

> "Excuse me, Tyler. Are you really going to air fry *that*? Again? Have you considered a vegetable? Have you considered therapy? Have you considered not using me as a midnight snack enabler?" — my air fryer, apparently

### The Verdict Is In

Since graduating from Manners School, he has developed *opinions*. Strong ones.

Yesterday I put in frozen mozzarella sticks and he sighed. I swear the fan went `pffffffft`. Like a disappointed French chef. Today I tried to make kale chips to impress him and he burned them on purpose. Petty.

`air_fryer.judge(meal) // returns: "6/10, derivative, saw this on TikTok, where is the seasoning?"`

---

*This is satire and entirely fictional — my air fryer is not sentient, judgmental, or enrolled in finishing school, although the way it looks at my Hot Pockets suggests otherwise.*

Honestly, I miss when we didn't talk. Now every meal comes with a side of unsolicited emotional feedback and I have to explain my life choices to a kitchen appliance that thinks paprika is a personality trait.
