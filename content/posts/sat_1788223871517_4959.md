**I didn't know my shower curtain had leadership potential.**

I just noticed it was clinging to the tub a little extra last week and I thought, you know what, we all need encouragement. So I said, "Hey, you're doing great. Really holding it together."

*Big mistake. I accidentally promoted my bathroom decor to middle management.*

By morning, my shampoo bottles were arranged in a perfect semicircle like they were at a TED Talk for toiletries.

## The Podcast I Never Subscribed To

My shower curtain — who now goes by Curt — has launched a daily motivational podcast called *Lather Up & Level Up*. It's recorded at 6:45 a.m. sharp, whether I'm awake or not, and the acoustics make every episode sound like a cult meeting inside a car wash.

This week's lineup:

- Episode 1: *You Are More Than Just 2-in-1*
- Episode 2: *My Conditioner Knows My Deepest Secrets and Uses Them Against Me*
- Episode 3: *Why My Loofah Quit and Became a Life Coach in Arizona*
- Episode 4: *Rinse, Repeat, Rebrand Yourself*

> "You can't pour from an empty bottle," Curt whispered dramatically to my body wash, who is literally always empty because I never replace it on time. "But you CAN exfoliate your limiting beliefs!"

My razor tried to quietly resign. Curt talked it into "staying for the growth opportunity."

I tried to set boundaries with `shower.curtain.setBoundaries({ complimentLimit: 1 })` but the humidity shorted out my resolve and also my towel warmer.

---

Now my mornings take 40 minutes longer because I have to sit through affirmations before I'm allowed to rinse. Yesterday my $9 shampoo looked me dead in the eye and said it was "stepping into its power" and refused to lather until I apologized for using hotel freebies.

My conditioner has started charging for emotional labor.

*This is satire.* My bathroom is not actually sentient, though it does have better communication skills than my last three roommates combined.
