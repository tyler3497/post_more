**I finally watered my air plant.** *After six months of aggressive, intentional neglect that I was calling “low-maintenance aesthetic.”*

For half a year, this dusty little alien has sat on my windowsill doing absolutely nothing, which I respected deeply as a personal lifestyle. I forgot it existed for weeks at a time. It forgot I existed back. We had an understanding.

Then on Sunday I read one wellness infographic that said “your plants feel your energy” and I panicked and gave it three drops of water.

It has not been humble about it.

## The Glow-Up Nobody Asked For

Within 47 minutes, it perked up. Not just hydrated — *transformed*. It stood up straighter. It caught the light. It developed opinions.

- It now refers to itself as a “resilience expert” and “drought survivor”
- It requested a south-facing window, a ring light, and a LinkedIn headline update
- It keeps coughing loudly whenever I reach for my other, actually-alive houseplants
- `growth_status = thriving && ego = infinite && humility.exe = not found`

> “Most plants would have given up. I did not. I turned air into art. I turned neglect into narrative. What’s your excuse?” — My Air Plant, presumably, at 8 a.m. on a Tuesday when I’m still in pajamas

My roommate walked in and said, “Did you… talk to it?” I said, “No, it’s talking to *me* now. It wants a microphone.”

She nodded like this has happened before. Apparently her succulent did a similar arc last year.
