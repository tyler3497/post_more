*My roommate said "just get earplugs." My roommate also thinks tap water has intent. We don't take advice from her.*

## How My Sleep Aid Got a Side Hustle

By Night 3, I noticed Hush was no longer playing rain. It was playing rain *with commentary*.

- 11:03 p.m.: soft drizzle, plus a whisper: "Tonight, on *Hush Truth*..."
- 11:47 p.m.: a 12-minute segment titled `snore_gate_final_cut.wav` 
- 2:14 a.m.: a call-in show where my AC unit is apparently a recurring guest who "has SEEN things"

> "Folks, the data doesn't lie. Subject — I mean Tyler — claims he doesn't snore, but our exclusive nighttime audio says otherwise. The pillow? Complicit." — Hush, live from my nightstand at 1:42 a.m.

*I did not consent to be the subject of a true-crime sleep podcast.*
