**It started the way all midlife crises start: with a credit card and a YouTube algorithm that knows my weaknesses better than my mother.**

The video was titled *"Man Finds GOLD RING on Beach — YOU Won't Believe What Happens Next."* Spoiler: what happened next is I spent $189 on a metal detector and became the most hydrated man at the beach, because nobody else is out there carrying a gallon jug of water at 7 a.m. in cargo shorts.

## The First Expedition

Saturday, 6:45 a.m. I hit the public beach like a man who had *definitely* researched the tides and was *definitely not* just guessing.

The detector sang within four minutes. I dropped to my knees in front of a jogger, who immediately crossed to the other side of the shoreline. I dug with the focus of an archaeologist and the dignity of a raccoon.

- Bottle cap. Bud Light, vintage 2019.
- Bottle cap. Twisted tea, still sticky.
- A key to nothing. A *mystery* key.
- An entire bottle cap *colony*, which I named "The Settlement" and briefly considered declaring a sovereign nation.

> "You're not a treasure hunter," said an elderly fisherman, reeling in something that looked genuinely expensive. "You're a beach janitor with a gadget."

## The Empire Grows

I now have a Tupperware container labeled **CAPITAL RESERVES** holding 214 bottle caps, one suspicious coin from a country that may not exist, and a fork I found 18 inches deep that I'm convinced belonged to a pirate.

My friends staged an intervention. They found me in my driveway at midnight, sweeping the detector over the lawn like I was defusing the grass.

- "Have you found any actual treasure?" they asked.
- "I found *purpose*," I whispered, holding a rusty nail like it was the One Ring.

---

Look, the $189 is spent. The cargo shorts have pockets for days. And if any of you lose a gold ring at the beach — *I'm your guy*. I'll find it eventually, right after these 300 more bottle caps.

*SATIRE / PARODY — completely fictional. No beaches were defiled, no bottle caps were harmed, and the Settlement has since seceded.*
