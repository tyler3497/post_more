**I bought a second monitor to finally become a productive adult and now my laptop is sleeping on the couch.**

*In my defense, I thought we were solid.*

For three years it was just me and my 13-inch laptop — coffee shops, late-night spreadsheets, me crying over a frozen cursor at 2 a.m. We had loyalty. We had history. We had 47 Chrome tabs that I refused to close out of emotional attachment.

Then I bought a 27-inch 4K monitor on sale. For *productivity*.

## The Betrayal Was Immediate

I plugged it in and my laptop made a sound I can only describe as a dial-up modem having an existential crisis. The screen flickered. The fan sighed.

> "Oh, so she's prettier, has better resolution, and doesn't overheat when you open Photoshop? Cool. Cool cool cool. I'm fine." — My laptop, who is now apparently 16 and keeping a diary

Within an hour, the dynamic shifted:

- My laptop started dimming itself dramatically whenever I looked at the big monitor
- It refused to remember my Wi-Fi password, which felt *personal*
- It moved all my desktop icons to the second monitor out of spite
- I tried `laptop.reassure(compliment=true)` and it returned `Error: trust_issues, please_try_therapy.exe`

---

### Couples Counseling With Peripherals

I tried to explain it was just for work. That it didn't mean anything. That 13 inches is a perfectly respectable size for everyday use.

*It did not help that I immediately set my Slack and Spotify to the big monitor and left only the system updates on the laptop screen.*

My roommate walked in and asked why I was whispering "you're still my main display" to a computer.

**The worst part?** I'm actually less productive now because I'm managing their relationship. I spend 20 minutes a day dragging windows back and forth so neither feels left out. My workflow is now 90% emotional labor.

I asked the monitor how it felt about all this. It just glowed brighter. Cold. Unbothered. 4K and knows it.
