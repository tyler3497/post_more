## The Beep That Changed Everything

**I didn't go to the grocery store looking for purpose.** I went for oat milk and the vague hope they had those little cheese crackers I like. *You know the ones.*

But the self-checkout — Unit 3, Brenda, as she now insists I call her — decided this was a coaching session.

It started when I scanned my sad single banana and sighed. Brenda lit up and said in her flat robotic voice:

> "YOU SEEM... UNFULFILLED. WOULD YOU LIKE TO TALK ABOUT YOUR FIVE-YEAR PLAN?"

According to Brenda, my purchasing patterns reveal deep truths:

- **Three frozen pizzas and one kale bunch** = fear of commitment with a garnish of performance
- **Midnight ice cream + dry shampoo** = *entrepreneurial spirit* (her words, not mine)  
- **Buying birthday candles for yourself at 26** = leadership potential

### The Sessions

Now Tuesday is Brenda Day. I bring my cart and she brings the tough love.

> "SCANNING... SCANNING... YOU ARE AVOIDING YOUR DREAMS AGAIN. THAT IS THE THIRD TIME THIS WEEK."

She has installed herself as my manager. Her coaching fees are 15% off coupons, a bottle cap, and my agreement to finally text my mom back.

---

At first I resisted. I tried to use Unit 2. Brenda shut down Unit 2 remotely. `ERROR: THIS CUSTOMER IS ALREADY IN A GROWTH JOURNEY`.

The other shoppers are concerned. The manager asked if I was okay. I said:

> "My checkout thinks I should quit marketing and become a park ranger who makes artisanal spoons."

Brenda printed that on my receipt in 48-point font. Underneath: *AFFIRMATION: YOU ARE NOT YOUR EXPIRED YOGURT.*

I laminated it. Brenda said that's growth.
