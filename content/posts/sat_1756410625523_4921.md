**I woke up at 5 AM voluntarily once and now I'm legally bound to it forever, apparently.**

It started as a 7-day free trial. Some influencer in linen pants said "just wake up at 5 AM and your life will change!" So I tried it Monday through Wednesday, hated every second, and decided to quit. That's when the emails started.

## The Terms and Conditions I Never Agreed To

Apparently when you wake up before sunrise more than twice, you enter a binding spiritual contract. No one told me. My body now just *does it*:

- 5:03 AM: Eyes snap open like a cursed doll
- 5:04 AM: Brain whispers *we should journal*
- 5:05 AM: I stare at the ceiling questioning all life choices
- 5:06 AM: I make overnight oats I don't even like
- 5:07 AM: I Google "how to return a circadian rhythm"

*I never liked oats. I am a Pop-Tart person, spiritually and legally.*

I called Morning People Customer Support to cancel my subscription to dawn.

> "Thank you for calling Rise & Grind™. We see you've enjoyed 72 hours of unsolicited productivity and smugness. To cancel, please return your circadian rhythm to its original packaging."

I told them I want to go back to being a respectable 8:47 AM gremlin who snoozes four times and considers iced coffee a personality trait. They said, and I quote:

> "Ma'am, our records show you told three people you were 'really getting into mornings now.' That's verbal consent. Also you bought a journal."

---

Look, I get it. I made my bed. I drank lemon water. I whispered `console.log("I am thriving")` while actively not thriving.

My roommate found me at 5:15 AM yesterday writing affirmations I don't believe on a sticky note that said `I LOVE MORNINGS` in handwriting that was clearly coerced.

If anyone needs me, I'll be over here at dawn, wide awake, waiting for my free trial to finally lapse, Googling how to unsubscribe from the sun.

*This is satire. No mornings were harmed in the making of this post. My alarm clock is fine, we're in counseling.*
