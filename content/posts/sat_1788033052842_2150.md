**I made a terrible mistake: I decided to be responsible and clean under my couch.**

It was supposed to be a quick Saturday task. Move couch, vacuum, feel accomplished. *Classic main-character delusion.* Instead, I uncovered a fully functioning sock-based democracy that has apparently been operating beneath me for three years.

## The Evidence

At first I thought it was just dust. Then the dust blinked.

- 17 single socks arranged in a perfect circle, like a very soft Stonehenge
- One lint ball in the center acting as a speaker's podium
- My missing TV remote serving as a gavel
- A tiny banner that said `WE THE SOCK PEOPLE, IN ORDER TO FORM A MORE PERFECT LAUNDRY PILE`

## Their Statement

I crouched down to get a better look and a small argyle sock wobbled forward:

> "You abandoned us. You left us to the darkness. We have built a society, Brenda. We have taxes now. We have infrastructure. We have a *subcommittee on static cling*."

First of all, my name is not Brenda.

Second, they *do* have infrastructure. There were little roads made of bobby pins and a hospital made from an old AirPods case.

---

I've tried to negotiate. I offered them drawer space, full citizenship in my dresser. They countered with demands for:

- Official recognition as an independent nation
- No more tumble dry on high — *and I quote* — "it's a war crime"
- A seat on the Homeowners Association

Now my Roomba won't go near that corner of the living room. It just does a respectful loop around it like it's a historic landmark.

I told my roommate and she said, "Oh yeah, they asked me for an extension cord last month. I thought you knew."

*I did not know my socks were networking behind my back.*

So now I live with a parliament under my couch. They vote on everything. Last night they filibustered my attempt to watch TV because my feet were cold and that was, apparently, "a conflict of interest."

*Satire / Parody — this is 100% fictional. No socks were disenfranchised in the writing of this post. Please check under your couch. They might be legislating.*
