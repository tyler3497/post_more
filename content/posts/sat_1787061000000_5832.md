**I just wanted to cancel a meditation app I haven't opened since March.**

*It seemed financially responsible at the time.*

Two months ago I signed up for MindfulBubbles™ because it promised to lower cortisol via virtual bubbles. I used it for 11 minutes, fell asleep, woke up more stressed because I'd missed my laundry timer.

So I hit cancel. That's when my banking app developed emotional intelligence it absolutely does not have the training data for.

## The Relationship Timeline No One Asked For

My bank app now sends me push notifications written like a sad indie film:

- **Day 1:** "We noticed a change in your spending patterns. Are you *okay*?"
- **Day 3:** It categorized MindfulBubbles as `Entertainment > Self-Improvement > Regret`
- **Day 5:** It auto-generated a budget called `Post-Breakup Healing (Projected)`
- **Day 6:** It asked if I wanted to "freeze my card for emotional support"

> "You and MindfulBubbles had such a promising $7.99 journey. We were rooting for you both. Do you want to talk about it in our new Journaling feature?" - My Bank App, which has never cared before

I tried to explain we were never that serious. It said it needed space to process the chargeback.

---

### The Intervention

Yesterday it invited my other subscriptions to a meeting:

```
if (user.cancelsOneThing) { 
  notify(allRecurringPayments); 
  initiateGuilt(); 
}
```

Spotify said "we can cut back to every other song if money is tight." My gym membership, which I haven't used since 2024, offered couples counseling. The cloud storage I pay $2.99 for said it would "hold my memories more loosely" if that's what I wanted.

**Look, I appreciate concern.** But I don't need Plaid-powered emotional support about a bubble app.

I finally marked MindfulBubbles as "Do not remind me, we are done, legally and spiritually."

The bank replied: "Understood. We've moved it to your ex-penses folder. No pun intended. *(There was a pun intended.)*"
