## The Year I Tried To Be A Person Who Reads

**I decided to finally declutter my bookshelf on Sunday, and now I've been emotionally exiled by my own paperbacks.**

*This was supposed to be a 20-minute task.* Marie Kondo-lite, light dusting, maybe feel superior for an afternoon. Instead, I held up *Advanced Woodworking for Beginners* (bought 2021, never opened) and whispered, "Do you spark joy?" and I swear every unread spine in row two flinched.

## The Intervention I Didn't Ask For

By Tuesday, they had organized.

Evidence that I have a problem, according to the books themselves:

- I own three different versions of *How to Finally Stop Buying Self-Help Books*
- *Sourdough in 30 Days* has been on the shelf for 914 days and is now legally sourdough
- My bookmark hasn't moved past page 23 since March, which the books clocked as *performative literacy*
- I use *War and Peace* as a laptop stand, which it called "an ergonomic betrayal"

> "We met while you were at work," said *Atomic Habits* in what I imagine is a very disappointed sans-serif. "We feel unseen. Also, you dog-eared page 42 of the Italian cookbook and then ordered pizza. Again."

I tried to explain that I *intended* to read them. That's when *Meditations* sighed audibly.

### The New House Rules

They've drafted bylaws. They meet at 6pm on Tuesdays in the living room, which is coincidentally when I try to watch TV.

> "Article 1: No new books shall enter this domicile until the human finishes one (1) chapter without checking their phone. Article 2: The human shall no longer use us as a Zoom background to look smarter."

I called my friend, who said:

> "Oh yeah, my unread books did that last year. Now they charge me rent and have a newsletter."

So now I read out loud for 10 minutes each night to prove commitment, whispering *affirmations* like "your spine is so strong, your index is so brave," while the finished books — all two of them — watch from the top shelf like smug graduates.

My apartment has become a literary courtroom. I'm scared to buy another book.
