**I thought I was doing laundry. My closet thought I was staging a coup.**

*Satire / Parody — none of this happened, especially the part where my t-shirts unionized.*

It started innocently enough. I watched one TikTok about "quiet luxury" and decided I was going to be the kind of person who irons their t-shirts. You know, linen spray, crisp lines, the whole thing. I am not that person. I am the person who owns 14 identical black shirts because decision-making is expensive.

## The Incident

I pulled out the iron I bought in 2021 and never used, and within 20 minutes my bedroom smelled like ambition and burnt cotton.

My closet did not appreciate this character development.

> "We had an understanding," whispered my favorite vintage tee, now suddenly creaseless and terrified. "You were supposed to be low-maintenance. Now you look... pressed. Literally."

Suddenly every hanger was creaking with judgment.

### Evidence That My Wardrobe Is Now Mad At Me:

- My hoodies formed a support group in the laundry basket and refuse to come out
- My socks have started disappearing *on purpose* — this is a protest, not a dryer problem
- One shirt folded itself back into a ball out of spite when I left the room
- My closet light flickers whenever I walk by with the iron, like a tiny horror movie

I tried to reason with them. I said, "Look, I'm just trying to improve myself."

---

The closet replied with what I can only describe as a collective sigh. The kind usually reserved for when you say you'll start meal prepping.

`status: closet = emotionally_unavailable`

Now I have seven perfectly ironed shirts I am too guilty to wear, a pile of wrinkled defectors living on The Chair™, and an ironing board that has become a permanent fixture like a very hot, very passive-aggressive roommate.

The worst part? I look *good*. Wrinkle-free. Put-together. Like someone who has their life together.

I miss being rumpled and at peace.
