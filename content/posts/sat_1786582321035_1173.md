**I finally did it. I cleared my notification center.** Not the polite daily clear. The *historic* clear.

For three months I'd been carrying 47 notifications like emotional baggage with little red dots. *You know, normal adult coping.* Then on Sunday at 11:47 p.m., powered by a sudden burst of ambition and mild dehydration, I swiped left on my entire life.

Bad idea.

## The Morning After

By Monday morning my apps had unionized. There's now a calendar invite in my inbox titled:

> "We Need to Talk About How You Just Ghosted All of Us at Once - An Intervention Led by Duolingo and Your Banking App"

It was in my kitchen, if you can call a group Zoom of push notifications a kitchen.

Their grievances were... thorough:

- Duolingo said I was emotionally unavailable and that my Spanish streak died of neglect, not natural causes
- My meditation app reported I used it twice and then sought inner peace in the Target clearance aisle instead
- The weather app was *furious* I ignored 19 severe pollen warnings while claiming I "didn't know why I was sneezing"
- My grocery delivery app still has 3 avocados in my cart from June that have legally become dependents

`notification_status: cleared && consequences: immediate && vibe: awkward`

---

### The Negotiation

I tried to apologize. I said, "I was overwhelmed."

> "Overwhelmed is when you have two tabs open," said my banking app, who has never liked me. "You had 47 unacknowledged cries for help. This is avoidance."

My plant watering app just sat there, wilting judgmentally in notification form.

Now we have a system. I have to check them *intentionally* twice a day, say good morning to the weather, and actually open Duolingo instead of just feeling guilty about the owl's increasingly threatening owl stare.

My badge count is zero. My anxiety is somehow higher. My apps have never been more communicative.
