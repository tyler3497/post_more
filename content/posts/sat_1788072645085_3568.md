**I just wanted my living room to look like an adult lives here.** That was it. Not a cry for help. Just vibes.

Instead, I accidentally started a literary civil war.

## The Before Times

My bookshelf was *organically curated*, which is my fancy way of saying it was a Jenga tower of shame. Cookbooks next to true crime next to a 2017 journal where I wrote "drink more water!!!" in angry highlighter.

So I did what any stable person does on a Sunday at 4:47 p.m.: I decided to alphabetize everything.

- *A* through *C*: Cookbooks (all very confident, very buttery)
- *H* through *S*: Self-help, spirituality, and one book called *Set Boundaries or Else*
- *T* through *Z*: Thrillers where everyone has a secret and it's always the husband

It took three hours, one iced coffee, and a full replay of my 2019 Spotify Wrapped.

## The Incident

At first, it was quiet. Too quiet.

Then I heard it. Well, not heard. Felt. The energy shift.

> "We did not survive three moves and a flood in the storage unit to be shelved next to *The Power of Saying No*," whispered the lasagna section.

My Italian cookbook, who I now call Brenda, was *furious*. Brenda believes self-help is just recipes for people, and she does not appreciate competition.

The self-help books retaliated immediately:

> "Brenda, sweetie, you literally tell people to put cheese inside cheese. Let's work on inner stability before we talk about outer carbs."

By midnight, they were filing paperwork:

- Formal complaint filed: *Excessive Butter Proximity*
- Witness: The air fryer manual, which has seen things
- Evidence: I reorganized using `bookshelf.sort((a,b) => a.title.localeCompare(b.title))` and didn't even consult them

---

**Now I have to do mediation.** I moved the cookbooks to the top shelf so they can look down on everyone, which is what they wanted. I put the self-help books by the window so they can get natural light and journal about it.

My bookshelf looks great. My books have not spoken to each other since.

*This is satire. No cookbooks were harmed, litigated, or emotionally consulted in the making of this post. My bookshelf is still a mess, spiritually.*
