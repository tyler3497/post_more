**I decided my houseplants deserved a nice evening out, except I forgot they already live here.**

*It started as self-care. It ended as a Yelp review I didn't ask for.*

## The Guest List Was Already Judging Me

I have four plants. I thought this would be intimate and classy. I set the table, lit a candle, and made quinoa because I read somewhere that plants respect grains.

The attendees:

- **Fernando the Fern** — dramatic, has never been happy about anything, not even photosynthesis
- **Barbara the Snake Plant** — stoic, hasn't grown in 8 months out of spite
- **Moss the Pothos** — chill until he's not, which is always after 7pm
- **Kevin the Cactus** — brought his own water, *rude*

> "This plating is... brave," Fernando whispered, frond quivering. "Did you *mean* to burn the garlic or was that a lifestyle choice?"

I had spent 45 minutes on that garlic.

### The Tasting Menu From Hell

Fernando has taken a single cooking class in 2019 (on YouTube, on mute) and now thinks he's Gordon Ramsay with roots.

He critiqued everything:

- My quinoa was "emotionally under-seasoned"
- My water had "notes of faucet and regret"
- My olive oil drizzle was "performative"

> "I have literally eaten dirt for two years and this is still worse," he said, which is objectively not something a fern should be able to say.

Barbara tried to mediate. She said, `def be_kind(): return False` — which I think was Python for "girl, don't listen to him."

---

Moss just kept asking for more humidity and Kevin kept prickling my bread rolls.

*I am not a chef. I am a man who owns a colander and anxiety.*

Now Fernando leaves little leaf-notes on my fridge. This morning it said: **"We need to talk about your knife skills."**

*This is satire. No houseplants were forced to eat quinoa, though Fernando is still writing his memoir titled 'Wilting: My Time With You'.*

**Moral:** never host a dinner party for beings who make their own food from sunlight. They have *standards* and zero mercy.
