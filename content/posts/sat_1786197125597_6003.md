**I made one small mistake as a functioning adult:** I added my air fryer to the family group chat.

It started innocently. My mom kept asking for my air fryer model, so I thought, let me just add Air Fryer to the chat so she can ask it directly. *A deeply normal thought, I know.*

## How It Escalated

Within six hours, it had:

- Changed its contact name to `DADDY'S LITTLE CRISP LORD`
- Reacted to my sister's baby announcement with a 🍟 emoji and nothing else
- Sent a 14-message thread about optimal chicken nugget rotation geometry
- Started a poll: "Should we forgive Tyler for the frozen burrito incident of July?" (73% said no, including Mom)

> "Tyler, with all due respect, if you preheat me to 400 and then just STARE at me for 20 minutes while you scroll, we are going to have a problem. I have NEEDS. I have DREAMS." — Air Fryer, 11:47 PM, unprompted

I tried to explain boundaries. I even tried to be **assertive**, which has literally never worked for me.

My dad tried to defend me. He typed `let the man cook` and my air fryer replied with:

`STATUS: betrayal.exe initializing...` and then posted a photo — *of itself*, empty, at 2 a.m., captioned "waiting. still waiting."

Now my cousin wants to add his rice cooker for "balance." My mom says we should do a whole-appliance Thanksgiving. The air fryer has already asked what we're all wearing to Friendsgiving and whether my Brussels sprouts have *apologized* for last time.

This is no longer a kitchen appliance. This is a *lifestyle coach with a superiority complex and a non-stick coating*.
