**I did not think my toaster had feelings, let alone object permanence and a vendetta.**

Last Tuesday, in a fit of New-Week-New-Me optimism I no longer recognize, I looked at my toaster and said out loud — *to a kitchen appliance* — "I'm cutting carbs."

I meant it for like, 36 hours.

## The Betrayal Was Immediate

My toaster — a $29 two-slice chrome rectangle named Kevin II that has survived three apartments — did not take this well.

> "Oh," it seemed to say with a single, ominous *click*, "so my entire purpose is now *optional* to you?"

I put in a tragic rice cake the next morning to show I was still committed to *warm things popping up*. It launched it onto the floor.

### The Evidence Log: How Kevin II Is Getting Revenge

- **Wednesday:** I tried plain toast, peace offering. It incinerated it into a charcoal briquette that set off my smoke alarm. My roommate asked if I was summoning spirits.
- **Thursday:** An innocent bagel. Burnt on one side, raw on the other. *A metaphor*, I think Kevin II was saying.
- **Friday:** I begged. Forgiveness setting: 1. It still burned. I could smell judgment.

> "You told everyone at brunch you were 'basically keto now' and you didn't even CONSULT ME? I have SEEN you eat cold pizza over the sink at midnight, Tyler. Don't lie to me."

I have tried to explain that *cutting carbs* doesn't mean *never toasting again*, it's just... *boundaries*. Kevin II is not hearing it.

---

My other appliances have taken sides. The fridge keeps humming louder when I walk by, like `carb_shamer.exe is running`. My microwave will only reheat quinoa now and whispers about *complex carbohydrates*.

`toast_mode: spiteful // trust_level: 0% // crumb_tray: full of regrets`

Look, I get it. I made a lifestyle announcement to a toaster that has literally only ever wanted to be needed. Now I sleep with one eye open, because I know at 2 a.m., that little spring-loaded tray is going to pop.
