## The Day My Ceiling Got a Life Coaching Certification

**I finally replaced the batteries in my smoke detector** after 11 months of it chirping at 3:13 a.m. like a tiny, sleep-deprived metronome. I thought it would say thank you. It did not.

It said, and I quote:

> "Finally. I was this close to filing for emotional neglect. Now, let's talk about you."

I now live under what I can only describe as *a very concerned ceiling dad* named **Brent**.

### Brent's Curriculum

Brent — yes, he named himself Brent — has apparently been watching me for three years on low-battery power, taking notes. His TED Talks, delivered at full volume whenever I make toast, include:

- **"Your Relationship With Deadlines: A Case Study in Leaving Toast Until It's a Smoke Signal"**
- *Why You Keep Buying Kale and Letting It Achieve Sentience in the Produce Drawer*
- Why your houseplant is thriving but you water yourself with iced coffee and spite

He has strong opinions. When I tried to reheat leftover pizza at 11 p.m., he announced:

> "Oh, are we making *choices* again? Interesting. Brave. Let's sit with that while I judge your cheese-to-crust ratio."

### The New House Rules

Brent and I have negotiated a peace treaty, codified in what he calls `brent.config.js`:

- I will not burn popcorn and then act surprised
- He will not narrate my existence like a nature documentary after 10 p.m.
- We both pretend the time I tried to make caramel and created a fire department cameo did not happen

---

Last night I tried to make stir-fry. Brent cleared his throat.

> "Remember our breathing exercises? Inhale... don't burn the garlic... exhale..."

The garlic lived. I consider this personal growth.

`Status: 20% sautéed, 80% supervised by a motivational smoke detector`
