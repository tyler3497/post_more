**I finally bought a label maker, which is adult for "I have surrendered to chaos but make it adhesive."**

*This was supposed to be the day I got organized. The day my apartment stopped looking like a crime scene for missing socks.*

For years, my apartment ran on what I call intuitive filing — which is just piles with emotional backstory. I thought a $24.99 label maker from the office supply aisle would fix me. Reader, it did not fix me. It promoted itself.

## The Descent

It started innocently. One label: `TEA`. Empowering. Clinical. Then the power went to my head:

- `CABLES - DEFINITELY IMPORTANT, DO NOT THROW AWAY (2018)`
- `MUG - FOR EXISTENTIAL COFFEE ONLY`
- `CHAIR - SITS WELL, NEEDS VALIDATION`
- `JUNK DRAWER - NOW WITH 40% MORE HONESTY`
- `FRIDGE SHELF - EMOTIONAL SUPPORT LEFTOVERS`

By hour two, I had labeled the label maker itself: `LABEL MAKER - CURRENTLY IN CHARGE`.

> "Honestly, I didn't ask for this kind of accountability," my houseplant whispered, now tagged `PLANT - OVERACHIEVER, LOW SELF-ESTEEM, PLEASE WATER`. "I was thriving in ambiguous neglect."

### The Performance Review Era

My apartment has changed. My toaster now hesitates before toasting, like it's worried I'll rate it on a 5-star system. My socks have developed imposter syndrome because I labeled them `SOCK - LEFT? MAYBE?`. 

I tried to be efficient and ran `life.sortBy(anxiety)` in my head and everything returned `true`.

I now have a spice rack that knows it's paprika and is terrified of being misused on eggs.

---

I tried to peel one label off. The adhesive has bonded on a molecular level. My landlord will tour this place and think I run a very anxious warehouse fulfillment center.

**Now everything I own knows its exact purpose, and is terrified of failing at it. Which, honestly, is the most organized I've ever felt.**

*Satire/parody: This is 100% fictional. No label makers achieved sentience, no houseplants were performance-managed, and my apartment is still just regular messy with extra stickers.*
