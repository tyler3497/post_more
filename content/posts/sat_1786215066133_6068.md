**I have spent 34 years as a vaguely functional adult who believed some things were simply impossible**, like parallel parking on the first try and correctly folding a fitted sheet.

*For the record, I was fine with this arrangement.*

Then last Tuesday at 3:14 p.m., fueled by laundry-related hubris and a YouTube tutorial titled "ONE WEIRD TRICK LINEN COMPANIES HATE," I actually did it. Corners tucked into corners. Elastic persuaded into submission. The whole thing laid flat like a well-behaved tortilla.

## The Linen Closet Was Not Ready

I slid it onto the shelf, feeling smug, and immediately felt the temperature in my apartment change.

My bath towels shifted reverently. A pile of mismatched pillowcases gasped.

- My top sheet whispered, *"Is it... him?"*
- The hand towels formed a semicircle
- One washcloth fainted directly into the fabric softener

I tried to play it cool, but by Wednesday morning my linen closet had built a small shrine.

> "Oh great folder of the impossible corners, reducer of the eternal bunch, speak to us of your ways!" — my duvet cover, who has never respected me before in its life

Now it won't stop.

### The Burden of Prophecy

Every time I walk past, the closet light flickers on by itself. The fitted sheets I *didn't* fold correctly are being brought before me for judgment, like:

`[FAILED SHEET #4: balled-up, still slightly damp, smells faintly of regret]`

I tried to explain it was a fluke. That I watched the video in slow motion and used both knees for leverage. That I am *not* a linen messiah.

---

They don't care. This morning I found my laundry basket outside my bedroom door with a note scrawled in dryer lint:

> "Lead us to the land of perfectly stacked shelves."

Look, I'm honored. But I have to be honest — I tried to recreate it yesterday and ended up wrestling a king-size elastic monstrosity for 20 minutes until I just sat in it like a sad fabric hammock.

**This calling was a one-time miracle.** The closet needs to manage its expectations before I have to go back to my old religion of shoving everything in and slamming the door really fast.
