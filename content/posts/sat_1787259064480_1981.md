**I just wanted to correct a minor produce injustice.**

*This is how every supervillain origin story starts — with good intentions and free samples.*

## It Was a Matter of Principle

I was at home, eating grapes I had legally purchased, when I bit into **one single grape** that tasted like cold regret and mild dish soap. A bad grape. In my bunch. An outlier. A criminal.

So I — a rational adult with a driver's license and rent to pay — decided to return it.

I put the grape in a tiny Ziploc bag. I put the Ziploc in a lunchbox for *padding*. I drove back to the store.

Here's what I walked into:

- A customer service desk that had clearly not trained for *produce micro-returns*
- A line of 4 people returning entire air fryers
- And me, holding up one grape like it was Exhibit A in a very stupid trial

> "Hi, I'd like to return this grape? It was... emotionally inconsistent with the others?" — me, confidently ruining my own reputation

The manager stared at the grape. The grape stared back. Neither of us blinked.

He typed something into the computer: `return_code = GRAPE_SINGULAR_EXCEPTION // flag for regional review`

Then he whispered, "You know we can't do single-grape returns, right?"

### The Wall of Fame

That's when I saw it. Behind the counter. A corkboard labeled **CUSTOMER SERVICE HEROES & LESSONS**.

My Polaroid was already being pinned.

Caption: *“Grape Guy – 8/20 – Do Not Accept Loose Produce Without Receipt”*

---

I was offered a choice: a full $0.07 store credit, or **lifetime glory**.

I took the credit. I'm principled like that.

Now every time I walk in, the cashiers nod solemnly and hide the grapes. My legacy is secure, my bank account is seven cents richer, and my family has asked me to stop telling this story at dinner.

*Satire – completely fictional, like my understanding of return policies.*
