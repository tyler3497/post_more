**It started with an HR template I found online at 2 a.m.** Which, in hindsight, is exactly how most labor disputes begin.

## The Incident Report

I'd noticed Fern — a pothos I've owned for three years — was "underperforming" in the climbing department. So I did what any reasonable manager does: I scheduled a sit-down, printed a review form, and read my concerns aloud to a plant.

The categories, scored 1 to 5, were:

- **Leaf shine** — 2. I said "dull" and I meant it.
- **Climbing ambition** — 1. "A pothos that doesn't climb is just a pile of intentions," I said.
- **Attitude** — 3, with a note that he "could smile more."

I folded the form, taped it to his pot, and went to bed feeling like a *real professional*.

## The Retaliation

By Thursday, every plant on the windowsill had stopped growing. Not slowed. **Stopped.** The succulents formed a semicircle facing my desk. I heard nothing, because they are plants, but the *vibe* was unmistakable. Fern had unionized the entire sill.

> "We, the undersigned foliage, reject the 2/5 Leaf Shine rating as biased, given the manager's own skin-care routine. We demand a misting schedule, a north-facing rotation, and a formal apology read aloud, with feeling."

They have a treasurer now. It's the cactus. I don't know how the dues work either.

---

I tried negotiating. I offered "growth opportunities" (a bigger pot), which they correctly identified as more work for the same pay. I've countered with a profit-sharing proposal — 10% of all my houseplant-themed compliments from guests — but the succulents are holding out for dental.

HR has not returned my calls. *Probably because my HR is just my roommate*, and he says "bro, you're negotiating with plants" every time I bring it up, which is honestly discouraging morale on both sides.

The last straw came this morning. A single leaf, placed on my keyboard like a resignation letter.

I'm now in mediation with vegetation. Fern says he'll accept a 4/5 on climbing ambition if I "put in the work." I've agreed to mist him twice daily and stop saying "smile more" to living things.

*Satire: this is a fictional parody. No houseplants were reviewed in the making of this post.*
