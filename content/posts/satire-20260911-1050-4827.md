**I've been an adult for nearly a decade now, and I can say with confidence: I never agreed to these terms.**

It started when my car insurance went up for reasons described to me as "being alive in a zip code." That was the last straw. So I did what any reasonable consumer would do — I called to cancel.

## The Call

After forty minutes of hold music that sounded like it was recorded inside a broken elevator, a representative named Greg picked up. I explained that I would like to cancel my free trial of adulthood.

There was a silence. Then Greg laughed. For eleven minutes.

> "Sir," he said, wiping away a tear I could hear, "the trial ended when you turned eighteen. You're on the premium plan now. There's no downgrade option. There's not even an *account* page."

## My Options, According to Greg

Greg was kind enough to walk me through my remaining options:

- **Keep the subscription** and continue receiving monthly bills, back pain, and a creeping awareness of your own mortality
- **Attempt a "gap year"**, which Greg described as "just pausing notifications while the charges continue"
- File a formal complaint, which he noted would be reviewed by the same department that *is* me

## The Upsell

Then came the worst part. Greg tried to upsell me.

> "For just $400 more a month, we can add the Homeowner Tier — comes with gutters, property taxes, and a deep, abiding fear of your water heater."

I declined. Greg sighed like he'd heard this a thousand times.
