**I finally used the good towels.**

*You know the ones.* The thick, hotel-weight white ones I bought on sale three years ago, washed once, folded with military precision, and then placed on the top shelf to age like a fine cheese no one asked for. I was saving them for "when someone important comes over."

On Tuesday, I decided I was someone important.

I put them on the rack. Fluffed them. Stepped back to admire my own maturity. That's when my bathroom got ideas.

## My Bathroom Now Thinks I'm Hosting the Met Gala

For three years my bathroom has known me as a "one-gray-towel-that-smells-like-decisions" kind of person. This sudden upgrade registered as a personality transplant:

- My old towel immediately filed for emotional damages and retreated behind the toilet
- The shower curtain straightened itself up for the first time since 2022
- My sad succulent in the corner perked up like *oh, we have standards now?*
- The faucet started running at a more *sophisticated pressure*

> "So this is who we are now? People who own towel *sets*? People who match? What's next, a bath mat that doesn't slide when you step on it?" — My Bathroom Mirror, who has seen me at my worst and was not ready for this arc

I tried to explain it was just self-care. My bathroom did not buy it.

---

### The Code Review I Didn't Ask For

My brain ran a quick audit:

`if (human.usesGoodTowels == true) { bathroom.assume(human.hasLifeTogether = true); bathroom.raiseExpectations(400%); }`

Suddenly I'm not allowed to leave toothpaste in the sink. The good towels *watch*. They are so white they reflect my choices back at me. I dropped one on the floor and apologized out loud.

**The worst part?** I actually like them. I'm sleeping better knowing there's a towel in there that hasn't been through three moves and a flood.

Yesterday a friend came over and said "nice towels." My bathroom lit up like it was being complimented by association. I think it wants us to start entertaining now.
