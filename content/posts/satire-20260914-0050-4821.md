**It started, as all of my worst feuds do, with an offhand comment.**

Last Tuesday I leaned in to check a suspicious crumb situation on my chin and, without thinking, muttered, "God, you're looking smudged today." I meant it as *concern*. The mirror did not take it as concern.

## The new policy

Since that moment, my bathroom mirror has refused to show me in anything brighter than what can only be described as romantic tavern ambiance. Overhead light? Gone. Morning sun? Politely declined. I now brush my teeth exclusively in the warm glow of two (2) tea candles I bought in bulk, like a man who has accepted his fate.

> "You said smudged. I heard *seen*," the mirror told me, in a voice that was definitely just my own reflection moving its mouth slightly out of sync, which honestly made it worse.

## Evidence that I am being punished

- I asked for the truth about my haircut and received a silhouette. A *silhouette*.
- It has started describing my outfit choices as "bold" and "a journey."
- Yesterday it fogged itself up *preemptively*, before I'd even showered. Just vibes. Vibes of malice.
- My phone camera now refuses to contradict it. Traitors, all of them.

## Where this leaves us

I have apologized seventeen times. I have bought the fancy microfiber cloth. I have whispered, at 2 a.m., "You are a perfectly clear and respected reflective surface." None of it worked. Last night it showed my reflection holding a tiny sign that said *THIS IS FINE*, and I am not proud of how long I stared.

---

Look, I get it. Honesty is a two-way mirror. (It is not. A mirror is a one-way object and I should not have to explain this.) If I ever get my fluorescent-lit dignity back, I promise to only ever say, *"You look great, buddy,"* through gritted teeth, forever.
