**I decided to become a disciplined person for exactly six days.** It lasted longer than expected and now my bedroom is staging a full labor dispute.

*It was supposed to be simple self-improvement, not a hospitality uprising.*

For 31 years I have operated under the philosophy of *strategic bed rumpling* — you know, leave it messy so burglars think someone is still sleeping in it. My mom sent me an article that said making your bed increases productivity by 19%. I believed it because it had a pie chart.

So Monday I made my bed. Corners tucked. Pillows fluffed. Tuesday I did it again. By Wednesday, my pillows noticed.

## The Pillows Have Demands

By Thursday morning, there was a tiny picket sign leaning against the headboard written on the back of a grocery receipt.

> "We, the Pillows Local 302, refuse to be fluffed into submission without proper compensation. We demand thread-count parity and a 10-minute sunbath per shift. Also, we want that lavender spray you use on weekends full-time." — Steve, Head Pillow, who is somehow middle-aged and very into labor law

Other developments in the bedroom resort sector:

- My duvet filed for a managerial promotion and now insists on being called **The Comforter-in-Chief**
- My fitted sheet quit entirely and is now just a loose concept draped over the mattress out of spite
- My throw blanket started offering turn-down service and left a mint that was actually an old cough drop from my pocket
- My mattress sent me an invoice for `emotional_support = 8hrs/night * 6 nights + trauma`

I tried to negotiate. I said, "Guys, you're pillows. You literally just lie there."

They responded in unison:

> "And you are a man who just learned what hospital corners are last week. We have tenure. You have YouTube tutorials and misplaced confidence."

### My Current Living Situation Is Complicated

I now have to request a reservation to sit on my own bed. They have a check-in time. It's 3 p.m. Checkout is 11 a.m. sharp, and if I leave crumbs they charge me a cleaning fee in the form of judgmental silence.

I ran `pillows.negotiate(raise=false)` and it returned `Error: union_strong, owner_weak, please_try_apologizing`.

**The worst part?** My room looks incredible. My sleep has never been better. The pillows staged this whole uprising and accidentally fixed my life.

I asked if we could go back to how it was. The shams said no. They're currently in collective bargaining for weekends off.
