**I have not swept under the couch in fourteen months, and the dust bunnies have taken legal action.**

It started with a *polite cough* from beneath the sofa. Then a tiny megaphone. Then — this morning — a rolled-up document propped against the coffee table, secured with what I can only describe as a hair tie woven from pure dust.

## The Notice of Habitability

According to the complaint (filed in a font I can only describe as "scratchy"), my crimes include:

- **Negligent airflow maintenance** — *I allegedly left the windows closed "to save on heating," depriving the colony of fresh drift material.*
- Failure to provide **adequate darkness** after I moved the lamp six inches to the left.
- One (1) incident involving the **vacuum of March**, which they characterize as "an unprovoked military operation."

## Their Demands

The bunnies — I have named the lead one **Dustin** — have unionized. Their demands, as written on the back of a receipt from 2019:

- A formal lease at **$0/month**, paid in "lint equity"
- A one-time **security deposit** of one (1) sock, delivered by Friday
- Weekly inspections, which they will perform *on me*

> "We have been here longer than your commitment to flossing," Dustin said through the megaphone. "We have rights. We have *dust* rights."

---

## How Did It Come to This?

Honestly? I have no defense. When I finally crouched down with a flashlight, I found they had built a small civilization: little grey townhouses, a parliament made of dryer lint, and a tiny courthouse under the sofa cushion with a gavel made from a pretzel piece.

*I was going to evict them.* Then Dustin showed me the paperwork, and honestly? The paperwork is **immaculate**. Color-coded. Tabbed. I signed a lease I'm not proud of.

**SATIRE — this is a work of parody. No dust bunnies were harmed, and no tenancy laws were consulted.**
