**I decided to be transparent with my houseplants about money, which was my first mistake.** I thought saying my budget out loud would make me more accountable. Ferns are supposed to be nurturing, right?

I have six plants. A pothos named Kevin, two succulents who refuse to identify themselves, and a monstera who has *clearly* been reading LinkedIn.

## How My Financial Literacy Became Their Business

I opened my laptop and said, "Okay team, let's look at June," and my monstera visibly drooped. Like, full leaf sag. Dramatic.

Here's what apparently concerned the board:

- $47 for "emergency" tacos after I said I was meal-prepping
- $19.99 for an app called `PlantParent Pro` that I bought to take care of *them*
- $62 for a novelty mug that says I WATERED MY PLANTS TODAY — I did not water them that day
- Buying *another* plant when I said I couldn't afford fertilizer, which is literally their food

> "You spent what on bottled water? We make oxygen for free. We are giving you the product for free," said my monstera, now wearing an expression I didn't know leaves could make. He runs his entire operation on six hours of sunlight and tap water. His overhead is *zero*.

## The Audit

By noon, they had staged a full audit. Kevin the pothos trailed across my keyboard and closed my shopping tabs. One succulent just stared at me. *You know the stare.*

My monstera issued a statement by dropping a single dead leaf onto my receipt pile like a judge hitting a gavel.

His demands, etched into the soil with a tiny stick:

- No more DoorDash when there are leftovers with ``status: still good``
- Mandatory 24-hour waiting period before any purchase over $15
- I have to actually read the terms before clicking "Subscribe"

---

I tried to negotiate. I said budgets are about balance.

> "Balance? You bought a $30 moss pole for *aesthetics* and called it an investment portfolio diversifier."

He's not wrong. He's never wrong. He survived my apartment with zero drainage and two weeks without water while I was "finding myself."

Now I'm on a spending freeze enforced by chlorophyll. My monstera approves every purchase with leaf language. One leaf up means no. Two leaves up means *absolutely* not.

*SATIRE / PARODY — This is entirely fictional. No houseplants were consulted as financial advisors, though mine should be. I am still bad with money.*
