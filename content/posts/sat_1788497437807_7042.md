**I just wanted to borrow a cup of sugar, not become the subject of a true-crime documentary about lawn ornaments.**

*Satire / Parody — this is 100% fictional. No garden gnomes were kidnapped, interrogated, or given witness protection in the making of this story.*

I saw a flyer taped to my mailbox: *NEIGHBORHOOD WATCH ZOOM — ALL WELCOME — TUESDAY 7PM*. I assumed it was about potholes or whose dog keeps pooping in the communal mulch. I joined with my camera on, holding a bag of tortilla chips, and said, "Hey, I'm Tyler from 4B!"

## How I Became Public Enemy #1 in Under 9 Minutes

It turned out this was not the *general* neighborhood watch. This was the *elite task force* for the Case of the Missing Gnome — Gary.

Gary is apparently a 14-inch ceramic gnome who has lived on Mrs. Henderson's lawn since 1998 and is considered, quote, "the soul of the cul-de-sac."

My mistakes, in chronological order:

- I said I didn't know who Gary was, which they logged as "suspicious lack of gnome awareness"
- I admitted I walk my dog past Mrs. Henderson's house at night, which is now `evidence.log = "proximity + motive (dog dislikes hats)"`
- I laughed when they showed the crime-scene photo, because Gary's tiny fishing rod was objectively funny

> "You think this is a joke?" Mrs. Henderson whispered, leaning into her webcam like a noir detective. "Gary has seen three recessions, two kitchen remodels, and my divorce. He doesn't just *leave*."

### The Evidence Board They Made About Me

By Wednesday morning, there was a shared Google Doc titled **PERSON OF INTEREST: 4B**. It included:

- A blurry screenshot of me carrying what they claimed was "a gnome-shaped bag" — it was my laundry
- A timeline of my Chip Consumption on Zoom as "nervous behavior indicative of guilt"
- A poll asking "Should we confront 4B or set up a sting operation with a decoy gnome?" — 8 votes for sting operation

---

I tried to defend myself. I wrote in the chat: `console.log("I DID NOT STEAL GARY, I BARELY WATER MY OWN PLANTS")`

They responded by upgrading me from Person of Interest to **Prime Suspect and Also Probably The Reason The Recycling Was Late Last Week**.

Now I have to attend weekly debriefs where I give my alibi for gnome-related hours. My alibi is always "I was on my couch Googling if you can microwave fish without ruining your life." It has not helped my case.

*The search continues. Gary is still missing. I am still not allowed to use the shared grill.*
