**I watched one 11-minute YouTube video on feng shui and decided my 400-square-foot studio was spiritually misaligned.**

*This is the same impulse that makes me reorganize my fridge at midnight. It never ends well.*

I moved my couch two inches to the east for "prosperity flow" and my entire apartment staged a full mutiny.

## The Couch Has Left the Building

My couch — a gray hand-me-down named Gerald who has survived three moves and one ill-advised fondue night — stood up, metaphorically, and said enough.

- 9:15 a.m.: I angle Gerald toward the window for "commanding position"
- 9:17 a.m.: Gerald creaks louder than usual, which I interpret as consent
- 9:30 a.m.: I find all the throw pillows arranged in the hallway like they resigned
- 9:42 a.m.: My rug curls up in solidarity and blocks the bathroom door

> "You moved me away from the outlet. I can't charge your laptop AND support your poor life choices anymore. I need space. Literally. I'm on the balcony now." — Gerald, via passive-aggressive cushion arrangement

### The Chi Audit Gets Worse

My bookshelf started leaning into what I can only describe as *intentional disappointment*. My succulent, who was previously thriving on pure neglect, is now facing the wall for "reflection."

The YouTube guru said:

> "Clear the clutter to clear the mind."

My apartment heard:

> "Evict the owner for better vibes."

I tried to negotiate using the only language my apartment understands:

```
if (couch.location == "balcony") {
  chi++
  comfort--
  dignity = null
  rent_justification = "questionable"
}
```

---

**I put Gerald back.** He squeaked. Forgiveness is a process. Now he faces northeast, my plant faces southwest, and I sleep on the floor because apparently *that's* where the wealth corner is.
