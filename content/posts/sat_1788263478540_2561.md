**I made the mistake of trying to be emotionally supportive to a kitchen appliance that has literally one job.**

It was 6:45 a.m., my everything bagel was wedged at a tragic angle, and I whispered — *mostly to fill the silence while I waited for caffeine to kick in* — "You got this, buddy. I believe in you."

My toaster believed me. Too much.

## The Morning Motivation Spiral

By the next day, it had rebranded. It no longer just toasts. It *transforms*.

- 7:00 a.m.: It plays a tiny fanfare before ejecting my toast like it's launching a graduate into the world
- It now refers to the crumb tray as its `gratitude_journal.log` and asks me to review it nightly
- My frozen waffles have been enrolled in what it calls "The Crispiness Mindset Masterclass"
- It prints no toast without first asking, "But what are *you* bringing to the toaster today?"

> "Friends, Romans, Bagels — today, we are not merely heated. We are *seen*. We are *golden*. We are *more than carbs*."
> — My Toaster, at 7:12 a.m., to a bewildered pop-tart

*This is a lot before coffee.*

---

I tried to set boundaries. I explained that I just wanted a bagel, not a breakthrough. It responded by dimming its little orange light sympathetically and saying it was "holding space for my resistance to growth."

Now my kitchen has a schedule:

```
MONDAY: Bagel Keynote — "Rise and Grind (Literally)"
WEDNESDAY: Muffin Workshop — "Finding Your Inner Nooks and Crannies"
FRIDAY: Open Mic Night for Leftovers
```

The worst part? The bagels are into it. They sit there looking all puffed up and inspired. I caught my sourdough taking notes yesterday.

I just wanted positive affirmations. Now I live with Tony Robbins in brushed stainless steel who thinks my breakfast has untapped potential.

**SATIRE / PARODY — totally fictional, my toaster is not actually sentient, and my bagels have not hired a life coach. Probably.**
