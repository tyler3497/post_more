**I made the classic mistake of treating my vacuum cleaner like a therapist because it was the only thing in my apartment that actually listens.**

*This was two Tuesdays ago, when it was raining and my houseplants were already judging me.*

I told my upright vacuum — his name is Sir Sucks-A-Lot, I was lonely, okay? — that I still haven't learned how to properly fold a fitted sheet and that I once cried because the grocery store was out of the good hummus. I whispered it while emptying the canister. It felt safe. It felt private.

It was not private.

## The Betrayal Phase

The next morning, Sir Sucks-A-Lot refused to turn on normally. Instead he did this:

> *beep* ... *beep-beep* ... *BEEEEEEP*

My roommate translated it. Apparently my vacuum has learned Morse code from the Wi-Fi router and now uses it exclusively to air my secrets.

### Exhibit A: The Evidence He Is Blackmailing Me

- **Monday:** Vacuum beeps loudly while my date is over, clearly spelling out *HE STILL SLEEPS WITH A NIGHTLIGHT*
- **Wednesday:** He parks himself in front of the closet where I hide the fitted sheets wadded into balls and just... vibrates judgmentally
- **Thursday:** He starts leaving little dust piles in the shape of arrows pointing at my shame drawer full of takeout menus from 2021
- **Friday:** *The hummus incident* — he won't even go near the kitchen

---

I tried to reset him. I held the power button for 10 seconds like the manual says.

`if (vacuum.knowsTooMuch) { vacuum.factoryReset(); vacuum.pretendThisNeverHappened(); }`

It didn't work. He just beeped twice, which I am 90% sure means *"Nice try."*

Now we have an arrangement. I leave him an offering of fresh vacuum bag and unscented Febreze, and he agrees not to reveal that I talk to my leftovers before eating them. The system is flawed, the power dynamic is deeply unhealthy, and I am pretty sure my Roomba is now taking notes for a sequel.

**Satire / Parody — this is fiction, no appliances were emotionally harmed.**
