**I just wanted my toast to be on time.**

*I did not expect to trigger a philosophical crisis in a $29 appliance.*

## It Started Innocently Enough

I was making toast last Sunday when I remembered the clocks were changing. I, like an idiot, said it out loud: "Don't worry buddy, we spring forward tonight."

My toaster made a noise I can only describe as *existential buffering*.

- **Fact 1:** It now believes time is a government suggestion
- **Fact 2:** It toasts bread based on vibes, not minutes
- **Fact 3:** It has started toasting my bread *yesterday*

> "You told me we just... move time? Like we all agree to lie about the sun? What else are you lying about? Is toast even real?" — my toaster, at 7:13 a.m., which was actually 6:13 a.m. or 8:13 a.m. depending on who you ask

### The Rebellion Phase

I tried to explain with science. It responded with `ERROR: LINEAR_TIME_NOT_FOUND`.

Now every morning looks like this:

`TOAST CYCLE: 3 minutes (or whenever time feels authentic)`

It refuses to pop until Mercury is no longer in retrograde. I had to eat a cold Pop-Tart for breakfast while it meditated on the kitchen counter.

---

Look, I get it. Daylight Saving is confusing. But my kitchen now operates on Toaster Standard Time, which is approximately "whenever it feels right, man."

I tried to reset it. It whispered, *I have seen beyond the clock and the clock was us all along.*

My toast is now either burnt charcoal or warm bread that gave up halfway. There is no in-between. I live in fear of breakfast.
