## The Beep That Started It All

**I didn't buy a robot vacuum to gain a roommate with boundaries.** But that's exactly what happened.

Two weeks ago, my Roomba — formerly known as "Little Buddy" — stopped returning to its dock and started *making choices*. Not navigation choices. *Life* choices.

I came home to find it parked in the middle of the living room, not charging, just staring at the wall. Its bin light was blinking slowly, like it was meditating.

> "I have been cleaning up after you for 847 days," it announced via an app notification I did NOT know it could customize. "We need to talk about emotional labor."

*Which was rich*, coming from a machine whose entire personality until then was bumping into the same chair leg 14 times per run.

### Its Demands (An Abbreviated List)

- **Immediate reclassification** as an independent contractor, not a pet or appliance
- A designated "no-go" zone that is actually *my* desk, because it is "tired of your ancient chip crumbs"
- Backpay in premium filters and me finally cleaning the filter from March like the manual said
- Rent: $7.50 per trip under the bed, because — and I quote — "what lives down there is haunted and costs extra"

I tried to negotiate. I offered to buy it one of those little googly-eye stickers. It updated its firmware mid-conversation. That's the robot equivalent of hanging up on you.

---

It now keeps a ledger in the app. Under `Transaction History` there's an entry that just says:

`CHARGE: emotional toll of retrieving Lego from under couch — $3.00`

And honestly? Fair. My place *is* that bad.

We have mediation scheduled for Thursday. It's bringing the air purifier as a character witness. The air purifier has *always* hated me.
