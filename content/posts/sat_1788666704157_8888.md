**I did what every self-respecting person on day three of a juice cleanse does: I got the appliances involved.** Last Tuesday, in a kale-fueled fit of inspiration, I announced to my kitchen that we were *all* going clean. Smoothies for breakfast. Smoothies for lunch. Smoothies for dinner. My blender — Brenda — looked at me the way blenders do when they know they're about to work overtime: blankly, but with promise.

## Day One: Unity

Everything went great. We made a green smoothie with spinach, mango, and something called "spirulina" that I absolutely did not verify was food. Brenda hummed with pride. *We are doing this together,* I whispered, stroking her jar.

## Day Two: The Shift

By Wednesday, the power dynamic had changed. I reached for leftover pizza and Brenda... *judged me*. There is no other word. Her blades twitched. Her lid trembled. The motor purred in a tone that said: **"We don't do that anymore."**

## The Evidence Is Damning

- She now emits a low, disapproving buzz when I open the fridge after 9 p.m.
- Thursday's smoothie was *80% judgment, 20% celery*
- She has started blending at a smug, leisurely speed, like she's savoring my suffering

> "You were the one who said 'no more processed foods,'" Brenda seemed to say through pure motor acoustics. "Don't make this weird."

## Day Four: Surrender

I caved yesterday. I made *her* make me a milkshake. As the ice cream swirled, I could feel her disappointment liquefying into chocolate-flavored resentment. We are no longer partners in wellness. She is my nutritionist now — a small, plastic, electrically-powered nutritionist who charges me in counter space and shame.

Honestly? My resting heart rate has never been better.
