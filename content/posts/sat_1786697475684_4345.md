**I did it.** After ten years of loyalty, I finally threw out my collection of 47 stolen hotel shampoo bottles, and now my shower won't make eye contact with me.

It was supposed to be *liberating*. Marie Kondo meets basic hygiene. Instead, my entire bathroom has entered what I can only describe as an abandonment arc.

For a decade, my shower caddy was a tiny international conference. A mint from a Hilton in 2019. A vaguely French one from 2021 that just said "BAIN." Three identical bottles from the same Marriott because I kept forgetting I already had them. *I was basically running a museum of my own travel anxiety.*

## The Evidence That My Shower Is Now Emotionally Unavailable

- The shampoo shelf echoes now. Actual, audible echo. Like a dying mall.
- My main shampoo bottle, a full-sized adult, keeps sliding to the edge like it's testing if I’ll leave it too
- The water pressure seems... judgmental. Colder on purpose.
- I found my conditioner turned around facing the wall. That's not storage, that's protest.

Yesterday I actually apologized out loud to an empty tile wall.

> "You don't understand," I told the loofah, which has been with me through three dye jobs and a pandemic sourdough phase. "They expired in 2018! One of them was evaporating through sheer willpower!"

My shower responded the only way it knows how: by dripping exactly once every 7 seconds in a rhythm that felt a lot like *"you're-so-selfish."*

The worst part is the logic my brain uses to justify keeping them. In code it would be:

`if (bottle.volume < 30 && bottle.scent == "questionable_ocean") { keepForever(); // just in case civilization collapses }`

---

Look, I know the little bottles are gone, but I still have the memories. And the weird sticky ring they left that will probably outlive us all.

I’ve replaced them with one normal bottle. My shower is currently giving it the silent treatment. At this rate, I’ll be taking baths in the sink out of guilt by next week.

*This is satire/parody — my shower is not actually sentient, but it is 100% judging me.*
