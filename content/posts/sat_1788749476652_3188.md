**It arrived on my kitchen table this morning — a typed letter, sealed with a damp leaf, accusing me of "prolonged sonic harassment."**

I don't know how my ficus learned to type. I don't know where it got the letterhead (it reads *"The Coalition of Potted Greens, Local Chapter 4"*, and the font is Times New Roman, which feels aggressive). But I do know the complaint is three pages long, and the first page is just the word **"enough"** repeated 400 times.

## The Allegations

- That I sing in the shower, *"despite the door being open and the plant being, quote, 'right there.'"*
- That my rendition of *"Bohemian Rhapsody"* last Tuesday caused "leaf droopage of a traumatic nature."
- That I sang the *Jeopardy!* theme while waiting for toast, which the complaint describes as *"somehow worse than silence."*
- That I attempted vocal warm-ups near the fern, *"which has since requested a transfer."*

Look, I never claimed to be good. *That's what makes it fun.* I sing while cooking, while cleaning, while waiting for the microwave — a habit I now understand my ficus, Reginald, considers an act of botanical warfare.

> "I did not ask to be a houseplant," the letter reads. "I asked for indirect sunlight and occasional misting. What I received instead was a man bellowing show tunes directly into my foliage like I was some kind of green therapist."

Reginald has a point. I checked with a friend who keeps fish, and her betta apparently "tunes out" — but plants have nowhere to go. They just sit there, photosynthesizing and *judging*.

---

**My response, posted on the fridge per the Coalition's dispute protocol:**

I will switch to humming. It's quieter, but I do it while watering, which gives it what the Coalition calls *"a menacing context."* Honestly, this is a lose-lose. Reginald is also demanding reparations in the form of a bigger pot and "a window with a view."

*Negotiations continue. He wants the north-facing window. I will not be giving up the north-facing window.* This is now a labor dispute. Wish me luck.
