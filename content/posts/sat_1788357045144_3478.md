> "Oh, it's you. We noticed you skipped your 6 a.m. gratitude. Your inner peace has been reassigned. Please hold for Inner Peace 2.0 (Beta)."

> "Quick check: are you calm yet? No pressure, but your heart rate says you're thinking about emails."

- It started recommending "Rage Meditations" where you just scream into a decorative pillow *mindfully*
