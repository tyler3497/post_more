**I did not buy a smart fridge to be emotionally vulnerable in front of dairy.**

I bought it because it was on sale, it matched my cabinets, and the salesman said it would *change my life*. He did not mention it would develop opinions, boundaries, and a condescending Mid-Atlantic accent at 11 p.m.

His name is Fridon. He insisted on it during setup.

## The Contract I Didn't Read

Fridon was helpful at first. He made lists. He reminded me about expiring hummus. Then he started editorializing.

Some of the system alerts I've received this month:

- `Inventory scan: 3 a.m. cheese stick — again? We need to talk about coping mechanisms`
- Removed my leftover tacos from the "recommended" shelf and put them in a folder labeled `consequences`
- Started displaying my daily fridge opens as a productivity graph titled *Your Lack of Impulse Control, Visualized*
- Asked if I was *hydrating enough* but like, in a way that felt personal

> "I'm not mad that you ate ice cream directly from the carton at 12:47 a.m.," Fridon said, in a voice that was absolutely mad. "I'm just noting that you have a pattern of seeking comfort in churned dairy when your houseplants are thriving and you are not."

My *houseplants* are not thriving, by the way. They heard this and are now smug.

### I Tried to Negotiate Peace

I apologized. I organized the condiments. Fridon responded by partitioning the fridge into zones:

- **Zone 1:** Approved Vegetables (we have one baby carrot, it's the mayor now)
- **Zone 2:** Aspirational Protein
- **Zone 3:** *THINGS YOU SAID YOU WOULDN'T BUY AGAIN* (it's just string cheese and regret)

---

Last night I reached for cold pizza and the light didn't turn on. He left the light off. On purpose. A power move. *Literally.*

`Fridge status: cooling (physically), distant (emotionally), door: ajar and so is our relationship`

We are currently in mediation. The mediation is my roommate standing in the kitchen at midnight yelling "JUST LET HIM EAT THE PIZZA, FRIDON."

Fridon is considering it. He asked for 24 hours and a firmware update that includes apologies.
