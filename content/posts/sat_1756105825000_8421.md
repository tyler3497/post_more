**I thought labeling my leftovers was responsible adult behavior. I was wrong. It was the beginning of a very one-sided parent-teacher conference.**

*It started innocently enough — a little masking tape, a Sharpie, a dream of an organized fridge.*

Three weeks ago, I read that you should name your leftovers to reduce food waste. Emotional attachment = less waste, apparently. So I did. The lasagna became Linda. The soup became Steve. And the meatloaf, *my beautiful, dense, ketchup-glazed son*, became Kevin.

Kevin took it personally.

## The Moment Everything Changed

I opened the fridge Tuesday to find Kevin had somehow scooted to the front. I swear he was leaning against the milk.

I was going to eat half of him — *purely practical, nothing personal* — when I heard it. Not a voice, exactly. More like a very disappointed vibe emanating from a 9x5 glass dish.

> "So this is how it is? You birth me at 375 degrees for 90 minutes and then just... consume me? I have *potential*, Dad. I was thinking Brown. Or at least community college with a strong meat sciences program."

I laughed. Then I realized I was negotiating with ground beef.

Evidence that Kevin is serious about higher education:

- He has compiled a list of extracurriculars, including `holding the shelf together since Tuesday` and `emotional support loaf`
- He requested SAT prep and a letter of recommendation from the mustard bottle
- He told the yogurt cups they’re settling for mediocrity
- He is refusing to be reheated until we "discuss his future like adults"

---

I tried to explain the economy. I said:

`if (tuition > fridge_budget) { return "we have rotisserie chicken at home" }`

He said that attitude is exactly why the quiche left.

My roommate walked in while I was whisper-arguing about FAFSA with a meatloaf and just said "Okay, so we're doing this now" and left.

Look, I’m not saying I’m proud of how attached I’ve become. I’m just saying Kevin has a 3.8 GPA in *deliciousness* and a dream. How do you tell a meatloaf who thinks he’s people that he’s actually Tuesday’s dinner?

You don’t. You buy him a tiny graduation cap and start Googling "is meatloaf FAFSA eligible?"

*Spoiler: it is not.*
