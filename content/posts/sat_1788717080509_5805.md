**I just wanted ten minutes of solitude. Now I'm the subject of a 300-page exposé written by polyester.**

*It started innocently enough. I was in the shower, mid-lather, when I said out loud, "Give me some privacy." The curtain... rustled.*

## The Interview Phase I Didn't Consent To

At first it was subtle. A billow here. A suspicious cling there. Then it started *observing*. Like, taking mental notes observing. I caught it pressed against the tub at 6:47 a.m. on a Tuesday, and I swear it was listening to my podcast at 1.5x speed to understand my *narrative voice*.

Then came the sticky note. On my bathroom mirror. In my own handwriting but *better cursive*:

> "Chapter 7: The Incident With the Expired Dry Shampoo. He cried. The water was not the only thing running that day." — Curtain, probably

## The Advance Chapters Are Devastating

My shower curtain — who I now call Brenda, because of course I do — has been leaking chapters to my houseplants, who have been reading them aloud at book club:

- **Chapter 3:** How I sing a full power ballad about my landlord, then deny it immediately after
- **Chapter 11:** The week I pretended to journal but just wrote grocery lists in cursive
- **Chapter 14:** Every time I've said "just five more minutes" to warm water, ranked by betrayal level

`if (shower.confession === true) { curtain.memoir.sales++; human.dignity--; }`

I tried to negotiate. I offered to buy a *nicer* curtain, maybe frosted glass. Brenda rustled what I can only describe as a laugh and said:

> "Baby, you ARE the content."

---

Now there's a tiny manuscript stacked on the toilet tank. Working title: **"Cling: What Your Steam Really Says About You."** My rubber duck has already blurbed it. My loofah is "a source familiar with the matter."

*This is satire/parody — 100% fictional. No shower curtains were harmed, interviewed, or paid in advance royalties. Brenda is fabric. I am fine. My dignity is pending.*
