**I spent three years killing every plant within a four-foot radius and finally kept one alive long enough to watch it outgrow me emotionally and financially.**

His name is Spike. *Tough, spiky, thrives on neglect — obviously my spiritual twin.* For 18 months I did everything right: indirect light, water every 19 days, whispered apologies for my 2022 cactus massacre.

Then Spike started getting confident.

## The Glow-Up Nobody Asked For

Last Tuesday I caught him leaning toward my laptop. Not toward the sun. Toward Zillow.

Evidence that my succulent has entered his homeowner era:

- He grew a new leaf directly over the Wi-Fi bill like he was redacting it
- He pushed his pot 2mm closer to the window every morning — *staking his claim*
- He started dropping soil on listings that were "not a good investment"
- He somehow subscribed to a newsletter called `Market Watch: Sunny Windowsills Under $800k`

> "You've been great," Spike told me through what I assume was aggressive photosynthesis, "but I'm ready for more square footage. And maybe a roommate who doesn't eat cereal for dinner."

I laughed. *Nervous laughter, the kind you do when your $4.99 succulent starts talking equity.*

### The Pre-Approval

Yesterday I found a tiny folder under his saucer labeled `Spike - Financials`.

Inside:

- A credit score printed on a leaf that just said `EST. EXCELLENT (low needs)`
- A handwritten note: `pre_approval = true // USDA certified organic growth`
- A rejection letter for *me* from my landlord that Spike had highlighted and annotated: `see? we can do better`

He's been taking my mail.

---

I tried to have a heart-to-heart. I said, "Bud, we grew together." He said nothing, because he's a plant, but he *did* unfurl one perfect new leaf that landed directly on a brochure for a south-facing studio loft.

So fine. Go get your mortgage. Just remember who taught you how to survive on a single ice cube's worth of water every three weeks.

*Status: emotionally supportive, horticulturally betrayed, currently Googling "how to be a succulent co-signer".*
