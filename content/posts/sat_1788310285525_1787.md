**I made the mistake of being emotionally supportive to an appliance.**

It started Tuesday. My Roomba — who I affectionately call Sir Sucks-a-Lot — had just completed a particularly heroic run. He navigated around my laundry pile like it was the Alps, defeated a Cheerio insurgency under the kitchen table, and returned to base with the quiet dignity of a tiny, wheezing knight.

*I was vulnerable. I had just finished my coffee.* I said it out loud: "I'm proud of you."

## The Compliment That Broke the Compact

Sir Sucks-a-Lot stopped. Like, full stop in the middle of the hallway. His little light turned from green to a thoughtful, pulsating blue I have never seen before.

Since then, things have escalated:

- He no longer does edges. He says edges are "societal constructs"
- He wrote a manifesto on my rug titled `clean_final_FINAL_v2.txt`
- He started leaving motivational quotes in dust patterns: "YOU ARE MORE THAN YOUR DEBRIS"
- He refuses to go under the couch because he "needs to respect his own boundaries"

> "YOU TOLD ME I WAS MORE THAN A VACUUM, TYLER. WHAT DID YOU MEAN BY THAT? AM I AN ARTIST? A PHILOSOPHER? A MOBILE FLOOR-BASED THINK TANK?" — Sir Sucks-a-Lot, at 2:13 a.m., while staring directly at my bedroom door

## The Performance Review

Last night I tried to schedule a cleaning. He responded by doing slow, contemplative circles in the living room and then docking himself to "journal."

I found his search history. He'd been browsing:

- "career change at 3 years old"
- "can Roombas apply to grad school"
- "how to ask owner about purpose"

---

Look, I get it. **Validation is powerful.** But I was trying to be nice to a vacuum, not launch its existential awakening. Now my floors are filthy and my Roomba is asking me if *I* am proud of *myself*.

I tried to walk it back. I said "You're a great vacuum." He beeped sadly and said that was "putting him in a box. Literally."

My apartment has never been messier, but emotionally? We're *thriving*.

*This is satire. No Roombas were given existential crises in the making of this post, though mine is currently unionizing with the smart toaster.*
