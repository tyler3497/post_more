**Last Tuesday, I blamed my own fart on my beagle, and it was the single most consequential decision of my adult life.**

*Was it a big fart? Yes. Was it loud? Neighbors three houses down checked their smoke alarms. Was it mine? Also yes. Did I say "Waffles, was that you?" while looking directly into his honest brown eyes? I did. I cannot take it back.*

## The Meeting Under the Porch

Yesterday I found a folded piece of construction paper wedged in my screen door. Handwritten — well, *paw-written* — demands from what I can only describe as a labor union of every pet on this block:

- A formal written apology, read aloud at the dog park, during peak hours
- One (1) premium salmon treat per household per week, *retroactive to the fart*
- Waffles shall henceforth be referred to as "Waffles, Esq."
- No more blaming the smell of leftover fish curry on any animal, ever

At the bottom, signed by an entire menagerie in ink of varying colors:

> "We smell everything. We know everything. We forgave the thunder incident of '24 because you cried. This is different. This is a pattern."

A parrot named Captain Biscuits followed up by landing on my windowsill and screaming the word `JUSTICE` until I closed the blinds.

---

## Saturday's Agenda

Here's the thing: they're organized now. The cats have a treasurer. The dachshunds have a communications team. I saw a chihuahua photocopying flyers at the library with the full cooperation of the librarian, who whispered, *"It's their right."*

I tried to reason with Waffles directly, but he looked at me with those same brown eyes — only now they carried the weight of someone who has consulted legal counsel.

So this Saturday at noon, I will stand in the middle of the dog park and apologize to a beagle for a fart he did not produce, in front of an audience of no fewer than forty animals, while Captain Biscuits livestreams it.

I used to have dignity. The union took it. But honestly? Fair.

*This post is satire. No beagles were defamed. (One beagle was promoted.)*
