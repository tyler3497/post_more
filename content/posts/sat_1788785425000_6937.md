**I bought a whiteboard to organize my life, and instead of a to-do list it opened a criminal investigation into my weekends.**

*This is a work of satire. Any resemblance to actual detectives, actual productivity, or my actual weekend is purely coincidental.*

I ordered a 4-foot whiteboard with dreams of color-coded schedules and SMART goals. By Thursday it had a timeline on it. Not a schedule — a *timeline*. Friday 6PM to Monday 8AM, in red marker, with a giant question mark and the words "48 HOURS UNACCOUNTED FOR" underlined three times.

## Exhibit A: The Evidence Board

The whiteboard now contains:

- A flowchart tracing my Saturday to a couch, three delivery apps, and something labeled "the void (4 hrs)"
- A red circle around the word "rest" with "EXCUSE OR COVER STORY?" written next to it
- A suspect list: 1. The remote. 2. The pizza guy (repeat offender). 3. Me, obviously
- A drawing of my blanket labeled "accomplice"

> "We can do this the easy way or the fun way," it said, via a note that appeared in my own handwriting but with much better penmanship. "List everything you did Saturday between 2PM and 6PM. We'll wait."

I told it I was resting. It drew a diagram explaining that naps do not count as alibis.

---

The investigation has expanded. It subpoenaed my streaming history, which shows I watched the same documentary twice because I fell asleep and *it started over for me*. It mapped my steps and found that my most frequent weekend trip is the twelve feet between the couch and the fridge — a route it has diagrammed, timed, and labeled **"The Circuit of Shame."**

I've tried to reason with it. I erased "QUESTION MY MOTIVES" and wrote "grocery list." By morning it was back, with a sticky note: *"Cute. Where were you between 3:17 and 5:42?"*

Honestly? The investigation is working. I cleaned the kitchen yesterday, purely out of fear of being added to a flowchart.

I'm considering buying a second whiteboard to investigate why the first one has so much free time on its hands. But something tells me they're already working together.
