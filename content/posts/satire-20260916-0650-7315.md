**I did not set out to become a weather parent.** But last Tuesday I looked up, made eye contact with a small gray cloud hovering over my parking lot, and said, "You look tired." That was the mistake. That was the *whole* mistake.

The cloud — I've named him **Nimbus Jr.** — followed me home. At first it was sweet. A little personal shade. *So aesthetic,* I told my group chat, posting a selfie with a tiny thunderhead looming behind me like a concerned parent.

Then came the official record of everything Nimbus Jr. has rained on:

- My picnic. A lovely, expensive, Instagram-planned picnic.
- My outdoor birthday dinner — **twice**. The second time he waited until dessert, which I can only describe as a targeted strike.
- A very important phone call I took in the backyard ("Hello? No, that's not a waterfall, that's — hello?")
- My one dry-clean-only linen suit, which I wore exactly once, *on purpose*, on a day the forecast said 0% chance of precipitation

He is not a cruel cloud. That is the worst part. He is **supportive**. Yesterday he rained directly on my head, and I swear I heard him murmur:

> "There, there. Nobody saw you cry. Technically the sky cried *for* you."

My therapist says the cloud is a metaphor. My landlord says the cloud is a *liability*. My neighbor in 4B says the cloud is, and I quote, "moisture trespassing," which I believe is not a real legal concept.

## But here's the thing

For the first time in years, I never have to water my plants. My lawn is thriving. My windows are *pristine*. Every downside has a shimmering, rain-slicked upside, and honestly, my skin has never been more hydrated.

I tried setting Nimbus Jr. free. I drove him out to the desert, rolled down the windows, and said, "Be wild. Be enormous. Be a *storm*." He evaporated in about forty minutes and reformed directly over my car by mile three, like a dog that refuses to stay at the sitter's.

So I've accepted it. We are a package deal now. Some people have emotional support animals. I have an emotional support **weather system** — and he has abandonment issues, excellent comedic timing, and a personal vendetta against my dry cleaning.
