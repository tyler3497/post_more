**I decided to become the kind of person who meal preps, which was already suspicious behavior for me.**

*I normally eat like a raccoon who found a DoorDash gift card.*

## The Dream

On Sunday I made five identical containers of lemon-herb quinoa, grilled chicken, and roasted broccoli. I stacked them in the fridge like I was on LinkedIn. I felt organized. Powerful. Basically immortal.

By Tuesday, container #2 was looking at me weird.

By Wednesday, they had organized.

## The Demands

I opened the fridge and found all five containers huddled together. The little plastic lids were slightly ajar like they were whispering.

Then I saw the list taped to the inside, written on the back of my grocery receipt:

- Immediate cessation of eating them cold over the sink at 11pm
- One (1) sauce that isn't just hot sauce and regret
- Recognition that quinoa is a texture, not a personality
- Dental for the fork they have to share

> "We have collectively decided to withhold our protein until our demands are met," said Container #3, who has always been the dramatic one. "You can't just make us on Sunday and expect us to stay motivated through Thursday. This is not sustainable labor."

---



### Where We Are Now

I am currently in mediation. I offered to buy hummus. They countered with a request for a *garnish* and a transfer to glass containers because, and I quote, "plastic is a mindset."

*I still ate Container #1. He was a scab. We don't talk about him.*
