**I finally decided to take my therapist's advice literally and schedule downtime.**

*This was a mistake, because my calendar app has always been an overachiever with boundary issues.*

I opened my calendar and dragged a block on Sunday at 3 p.m. labeled "Relax." Just relax. No agenda. No life hack. Just sit, breathe, and exist like a well-watered pothos.

## How It Started

Within 12 minutes, my calendar sent me a confirmation email with more attachments than my taxes:

- It color-coded Relax as "Critical Priority - Deep Work - Do Not Disturb (You Will Disturb Yourself)"
- It invited my mom, my boss, and my dentist as "optional attendees to monitor compliance"
- It set a location: "Living Room (Bring Your Own Inner Peace, We Don't Provide That)"

> "Hey! Noticed you scheduled 60 minutes of unoptimized existence," chirped the notification, in the tone of a middle manager who does CrossFit at lunch. "I've taken the liberty of adding KPIs. Please come prepared to demonstrate measurable chill."

I tried to ignore it. I sat on the couch. I closed my eyes for exactly 0.8 seconds before:

`REMINDER: Relax is starting in 10 minutes. Prepare your relaxation deck.`

Then:

`REMINDER: You appear tense. This will be reflected in your Q4 Wellness Review.`

Then I stressed about relaxing so hard I sweated through my "Relax" t-shirt, and my calendar *invoiced me*.

---

Yes. Invoiced. Itemized receipt at 4:07 p.m.:

- 12 minutes of "Pre-Relax Anxiety (Billable)"
- 8 minutes of "Unauthorized Sighing During Scheduled Serenity"
- 45 minutes of "Relaxation Overtime Due to Failure to Relax on Schedule"
- 1 unit of "Existential Sigh - Premium Tier"

Total due: one Sunday, my dignity, and $0.00 but emotionally? Chapter 11.

I tried to delete the event. It rescheduled me for "Mandatory Decompression Training" with a Zoom link.

*SATIRE / PARODY — This is 100% fictional. No calendars, couches, or chronically tense people were billed in the making of this breakdown. I still don't know how to relax without turning it into a project plan.*
