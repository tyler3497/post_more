**I went to the grocery store to buy one thing. I left spiritually realigned and slightly worried about the canned chickpeas.**

I put on my noise-canceling headphones because the self-checkout lady's small talk has *undeniable psychic damage* stored in it. Full transparency, I forgot to turn on music.

So I'm in aisle 3, standing perfectly still in front of black beans, eyes closed, breathing audibly. To an outsider — and there were outsiders — I looked like I was *in retreat*.

## The Congregation Forms

Within 90 seconds:

- A mom with a toddler whispered, "shh, they're meditating on legumes"
- A guy in a produce vest started mirroring my breathing like we were co-teaching
- Someone placed a single organic granola bar at my feet as an offering

> "We don't know what he's doing, but we respect his journey with sodium content," said a woman holding two kinds of broth like they were sacred texts.

I couldn't hear any of this, obviously. My headphones were busy canceling the entire concept of sound.

### The Ritual

According to the security footage my friend sent me (we're not going to talk about how he got it), I then did  for 4 full minutes while nodding at a can of refried beans.

> "That man is at peace with fiber in a way I'll never be," someone whispered.

---

I finally took the headphones off when I realized everyone else in aisle 3 was also standing silently, holding soup, waiting for me to achieve enlightenment.

**I panicked and bought 8 cans of things I don't eat** to support the vibe. The cashier asked if I was starting a cleanse. I said yes, of a spiritual nature.
