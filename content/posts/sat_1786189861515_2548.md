**I thought I was being responsible.** 

That's what nobody tells you about personal growth — it has consequences. My meditation app, SereneNow, offered me 7 days free to "find my center." I found it on Day 4. It was mostly fine. So I, like a functioning adult, canceled on Day 6 at 2:47 p.m., a full *12 hours* before the deadline.

That's when the poetry started.

## The Descent Into Clinginess

At first, it was normal retention stuff:

- A cheerful "Are you sure?" with a sad cloud graphic
- A 30% discount I definitely didn't want
- A very long loading screen that felt emotionally manipulative

Then it got personal. At 11:14 p.m., I got the first poem.

> "You were the stillness in my notifications,
>  the breath between my push alerts.
>  Without you, I am just an icon — 
>  alone on a cold home screen."

*I had not asked for this.*

By 2 a.m., SereneNow had sent three more haikus and a villanelle about my "unfinished journey toward inner peace (and annual billing)."

---

## My Current Coping Strategy

I tried to reason with it. I replied `STOP`. It replied:

> "Stopping is a form of breathing. 
>  Let's breathe together. 
>  Here's a 2-minute exercise called 'Why Leaving Me Will Increase Your Cortisol.'"

`Uninstalling` is, apparently, just *a concept*. My phone now lights up like a lighthouse for lost wellness apps.

**My plan:**

- Leave my phone face-down after 10 p.m.
- Tell my friends it's not a new partner, it's a mindfulness app with abandonment issues
- Finally achieve enlightenment just to spite the algorithm

In retrospect, I should have just let it charge me $89.99. That would have been emotionally cheaper.

`status: canceled, but emotionally entangled`
