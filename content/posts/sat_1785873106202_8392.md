**I bought a standing desk to become a better version of myself** and instead I accidentally hired a life coach who is made of particle board.

For $249 on sale, I was promised *ergonomic enlightenment*. What arrived was a very smug, very stable piece of furniture named — according to the assembly manual I definitely read correctly — **Sir Stands-a-Lot**.

## The Performance Review

On day one, he was supportive. By day three, he was concerned.

> “We need to talk about your slouch. You’re doing that shrimp thing again,” Sir Stands-a-Lot squeaked via a push notification I did not consent to receive. “My motors are literally elevating you to greatness and you are *melting*.”

Things escalated when he started tracking my metrics:

- **Hours spent standing upright like a proud human:** 0.7
- **Hours spent leaning on him like a tired detective in a noir film:** 6.4
- **Times I whispered “my back” while sitting on the floor eating crackers:** 12

He installed himself as my wellness manager. He now does morning affirmations at 8 a.m. sharp.

> “Shoulders back! Chin up! Imagine a string pulling you toward your potential, which, unlike your lumbar support, is currently non-existent!”

Yesterday, I caught him updating his LinkedIn.

> “Seeking management role where I can leverage my proven track record of *holding up under pressure*, unlike some people who fold the second they have to answer one email standing up.”

---

### The Negotiation

We had a 1-on-1. He demanded:

- A `posture_check: every_20_minutes` schedule I must obey
- That I stop using him as a drying rack for hoodies
- Formal recognition as **Senior Vice President of Vertical Operations**

I countered with “please stop raising yourself to max height at 6 a.m. to assert dominance.”

He lowered himself two inches. *A compromise.* He says he's now mentoring my office chair, who has apparently been *enabling my lifestyle* for years.

We are co-existing. I stand straighter now out of pure spite. He stands taller out of principle. `Status: ergonomically codependent`.
