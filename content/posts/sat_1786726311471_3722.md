**I finally checked my mailbox after 14 days and it was the most committed relationship I've had all year.**

*In my defense*, I was busy. Or more accurately, I was performing an elaborate ritual of avoidance that I call *inbox zero for real-world anxiety*. If I don't see the bills, the bills don't see me. That's how mail works, right?

## Things Escalated Quickly

For the first week, the mailbox was patient. By day ten, it was *offended*. By day fourteen, the junk mail had unionized.

Inside I found:

- 3 coupons for pizza places I moved away from in 2019
- A thick Pottery Barn catalog addressed to "Occupant + Soulmate"
- A single Bed Bath & Beyond 20% off coupon that has, against all odds, survived the apocalypse
- A handwritten note from my mailbox itself that just said "We need to talk"

The Pottery Barn catalog had already circled a farmhouse table for two and dog-eared the page for matching patio chairs. The Harbor Freight flyer was planning a weekend getaway.

> "We've been thinking about your future," whispered the stack of catalogs in unison. "You seem like a man who needs 47% off a kayak he will never use."

I tried to explain I was just busy, that it wasn't personal, that I *do* like seasonal wreaths but I'm not ready for commitment.

`status: in a situationship with promotional mail`

---

## My New Five-Year Plan (According to Furniture)

Suddenly I have a whole life mapped out by people who think I can afford wicker:

- **Fall:** Reclaimed wood everything, even the dog
- **Winter:** A $400 throw blanket described as *rustic yet emotionally available*
- **Spring:** We elope to a showroom

Look, I wanted a little peace from the paper cuts. I did not want Pottery Barn to introduce me to its parents, or for the grocery flyer to start calling me babe.

I recycled everything except one catalog, which I kept out of guilt. It is now on my kitchen table, open to page 43, watching me eat cereal. I think it's falling in love with my toaster.

At least someone in this apartment believes I will one day own a chaise lounge.
