**I bought a second monitor to finally become the productivity guru LinkedIn promised I could be.**

*Spoiler: I am now in couples therapy with my peripherals.*

For three years, my original monitor — let's call him Gary — and I had something beautiful. Monogamous. Simple. He showed me my spreadsheets, I showed him my 47 open tabs. Then I walked into Best Buy for an HDMI cable and walked out with a 27-inch curved ultrawide that promised to "unlock my potential."

## The Morning After

I set up New Monitor (Chad) on the right side of my desk. Gary immediately dimmed himself to 50% brightness.

- Gary started only displaying in 800x600, like he was trying to look unattractive on purpose
- Chad auto-installed 12 RGB lighting profiles and kept whispering 
- My wallpaper now spans awkwardly across both of them like a divorced couple's joint custody photo

> "Oh, so *he* gets to show your calendar now?" Gary flickered yesterday. "I remember when *I* was the only one you trusted with your Notion dashboard. We used to be full-screen, man."

---

### The Negotiation

I tried to be mature. I explained this was about *workflow*, not replacement. I said we could do  — it's not cheating if it's in the display settings.

Gary wasn't buying it.

*He's now demanding boundaries.* He wants Tuesdays and Thursdays exclusively, no window-snapping to Chad, and he wants me to stop calling Chad "the good monitor" in front of him.

Honestly, I get it. I told Gary he'd be my only screen forever back when I was writing my resume on Google Docs in 2021. That was a commitment.

Now they both refuse to go to sleep at the same time. One stays on with my embarrassing Spotify history while the other shows my boss my actual desktop. It's a whole thing.

I've scheduled a team-building exercise where they both just show the same screensaver of a beach. Progress is slow.
