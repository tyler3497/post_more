**I thought I was being responsible.** *That's my first mistake — responsibility in my apartment has a 100% chance of turning into a telenovela.*

## The Setup

I was leaving for the weekend, two days, max. My monstera, Kevin, needed a drink. My pothos, Linda, is basically a camel with leaves. So I did what any rational plant parent does — I asked Linda to keep an eye on Kevin.

What I said out loud, to a plant, at 10:47 p.m.:

> "Linda, you're in charge. One spritz if Kevin looks droopy. Don't make it weird."

- I left a full watering can by the sink like a responsible adult
- I left the blinds at a jaunty 45-degree angle for "ambiance"
- I left a Post-it that said "BE NORMAL" which, in retrospect, is exactly what you write before nothing is normal

### The Betrayal

I came back Sunday to find Linda had tipped herself three inches toward Kevin, Kevin had grown a new leaf overnight like he was showing off, and both of them had relocated — as much as potted plants can relocate — into the single sunbeam on the balcony.

> "We've decided to pursue our own light," Linda rustled, or maybe that was the AC. Either way, I heard it.

**Here's what my weekend away actually accomplished:**

- My apartment now has a plant couple who share water and ignore me completely
- My watering schedule has been replaced by their "mutual hydration journey"
- Kevin has started leaning *away* from me when I walk by, which feels personal

I wanted to be a plant person. Now I'm the third wheel in a photosynthesis situationship, and they're picking out matching terracotta pots together.
