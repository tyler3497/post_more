**I bought a $4 moisture meter to be a responsible plant parent and accidentally triggered collective bargaining in my living room.**

After killing three succulents by what a nursery employee called *“aggressive neglect,”* I decided to turn my life around. I downloaded an app, bought a little probe that beeps, and made a color-coded watering schedule for my six remaining houseplants. It was laminated. It had checkboxes. My fern, Ferdinand, took one look at it and called his guy.

## The Grievance Was Filed Immediately

Ferdinand has always been dramatic. He wilts if I mention winter. But this time he meant business. Within 24 hours:

- My pothos submitted a formal request for “less direct judgment, more indirect light”
- My snake plant asked to be paid in actual sunlight, not “that dusty window glow”
- Ferdinand retained representation — a tiny, very serious spider plant cutting in a shot glass who introduced himself as *Chad, Esq.*

> “My client was promised ‘tropical conditions,’” Chad said, rustling ominously. “Your apartment is 68 degrees and 12% humidity. This is not the Amazon. This is a beige rental with a draft.”

> “I’m doing my best!” I said.

> “Your best includes misting him while saying ‘vibes?’ That is not horticulture. That is performance art.”

### Exhibit A: The Schedule

The schedule itself became evidence. `water_on_wednesday = True` was, according to Chad, “a blatant disregard for soil autonomy.”

Ferdinand demanded:

- 72-hour notice before any repositioning
- A humidifier that doesn’t sound like a dying jet engine
- And an immediate end to me playing lo-fi beats “to help you grow, little buddy”

---

I tried to negotiate. I offered extra fertilizer. Chad countered with “emotional fertilizer,” which is apparently just me not sighing heavily while looking at my electric bill near them.

Now we have weekly check-ins. I bring water. They bring demands. Ferdinand has a tiny picket sign that says **WILT IS A CHOICE**.

*Satire / Parody — entirely fictional. No ferns were unionized in the writing of this post, though one is currently requesting dental.*
