**I want to be absolutely clear: I started training my goldfish for a marathon as a joke, and somehow he is taking it more seriously than I take anything.**

It began innocently. I saw a motivational poster that said *"Nobody cares. Train anyway."* And my goldfish, Burt, was just floating there. We were both directionless. He did laps of a bowl; I did laps of the apartment. So I printed him a training schedule.

## The Training Regimen (Yes, Really)

- **Monday:** Interval laps — fast swims between the plastic castle and the bubbler. Ten reps. *He nailed it.*
- **Wednesday:** Hill repeats — I tilted the bowl slightly. This was technically cheating, but coach's discretion.
- **Friday:** Long swim — the entire perimeter of the bowl, clockwise, which is harder than counterclockwise, according to absolutely no one.

I made him a tiny **finish-line banner**. He swam through it. I cried a little. No regrets.

---

Then the internet found out.

I posted one video — ONE video — of Burt doing his warm-up circles, and overnight he had forty thousand followers. He has a manager now. A *manager*. A woman named Denise messages me things like:

> "Burt's brand is aspirational wellness, not endurance athletics. No more tilted-bowl content."

## Where It Went Sideways

- Burt has a **sponsorship** with a water conditioner brand. He won't swim until someone shakes the bottle first. *Divas are made, not born.*
- He has opinions about the filter now. He floats near it and just... stares. Menacingly. For a fish.
- He refused to race the neighbor's betta, calling it *"a circus,"* which is rich coming from a fish in a bowl with a plastic treasure chest.

Yesterday, Denise sent a press release announcing Burt's book: ***Swim Your Own Laps: A Goldfish's Guide to Intentional Living***. He dictated it to my roommate.

**The worst part?** I'm genuinely proud of him. I can't make eye contact with the bowl without tearing up. I started this because I thought it was funny, and now I'm standing on the sidelines of a metaphor I accidentally created, holding a foam finger that says `#TEAMBURT`, wondering how a fish with no memory developed *boundaries*.

Denise says marathon day is November. I have never been less ready for anything in my life.

*This post is satire and parody. All fish, managers named Denise, and marathons are fictional.*
