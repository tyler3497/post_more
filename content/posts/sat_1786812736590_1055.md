**I finally became a responsible adult on a Sunday afternoon and it has completely destroyed the work-life balance of my personality.**

They said meal prepping would save time. They did *not* say it would create a parasocial relationship with myself across the space-time continuum.

## How It Started

On Sunday I lined up twelve identical glass containers like I was running a very boring lab experiment. Quinoa, roasted sweet potatoes, some green thing I aggressively convinced myself was kale. I felt powerful. I felt organized. I whispered `organize --now` and the universe ignored me, but my fridge looked amazing.

## How It's Going

By Tuesday, my weekday self started leaving notes.

- Monday me ate Container #1 and wrote: "Wow, thanks Sunday legend"
- Tuesday me added a post-it that said "u are literally meal Jesus"
- Wednesday me started rating them like Yelp reviews for my own cooking

> "The chickpeas had *audacity* today. 4.5 stars. Would be eaten again. Please send more hot sauce. - Wednesday You"

*Which is deranged*, because I am both the chef and the customer and the increasingly passive-aggressive food critic.

---

By Thursday, Sunday Me had developed an ego. I caught myself thinking things like "those weekday peasants could never survive without my grain bowls." My current self is now anxiously people-pleasing my past self, who doesn't even exist anymore except as a faint memory of someone who owned more Tupperware than friends.

I tried to break the cycle by *not* prepping this Sunday. My fridge filed a missing persons report.

I have become a small catering company where I am the only employee and also the only client, and I'm pretty sure I'm about to unionize against myself for better working conditions.
