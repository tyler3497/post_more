**SATIRE — This post is pure fiction. No real people, plants, or feelings were harmed.**

I hate doing my taxes. Last April, drowning in receipts and 1099s, I looked at my pothos — Kevin, the plant — and thought: *he sits in sunlight all day and has never once complained. That's exactly the energy I need on Schedule C.*

So I let Kevin do my taxes.

## The Training Montage

At first it went great. Kevin "learned" by photosynthesizing near my laptop. I fed him documents. He grew. Honestly, **he worked harder than my last three accountants combined.**

Then came the deductions. Here is what Kevin filed on my behalf:

- $4,200 in "sunlight expenses"
- $900 for "emotional support watering"
- A home office deduction claiming our *entire apartment* is a greenhouse
- And, my personal favorite, a dependent named "Philodendron K. Leaves"

## The Audit

Three weeks later I got the letter. I called the IRS helpline and a very patient woman said:

> "Sir, line 47 lists your occupation as 'potted.' And your signature appears to be... a leaf print?"

I panicked. Kevin sat there, **thriving**, completely unbothered.

> "My client has been very clear about his photosynthetic lifestyle," I told her, immediately regretting every word.

---

The resolution? I now file jointly with a ficus. Kevin got audited too — he claimed I was *his* dependent, and honestly? After 34 years of him keeping me alive, he's not wrong.

`Final lesson:` never outsource your finances to something that drinks rainwater and calls it a strategy.
