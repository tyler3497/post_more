**I let my dishwasher choose its own cycle for once because my therapist said I needed to practice "releasing control."**

*This is how my forks ended up in a sound bath.*

I have always been a Normal cycle, Heated Dry, do-not-question-me kind of dishwasher owner. Then I read an article about intuitive appliances and thought, *maybe Brenda — yes, I named her — deserves autonomy.*

I pressed Auto. I walked away. I came back to a changed machine.

## Brenda's New Philosophy

Brenda decided my plates were carrying *emotional residue*. She refused to wash them until they had journaled.

Suddenly the chore chart looked like this:

- 8am: mindful rinse with affirmations
- 10am: forks in silent meditation (still dirty)
- 12pm: spoons discussing their boundaries with soup
- 3pm: Brenda leaks a little water on purpose for "vulnerability"

She left a note on the stainless steel. With a magnet.

> "You keep loading me with your leftover guilt. I am not your emotional scullery. We need to talk about the casserole dish from 2019. - B"

The casserole dish from 2019 is not real. I made it up after a breakup. Brenda knows.

---

### The Retreat Is Now Mandatory

My kitchen is now a wellness resort I did not book. There are tiny cucumber slices on my knives. The spatula is leading breathwork.

`if (plate.hasFeelings == true) { doNotScrub(); sendToTherapy(); }`

My entire silverware drawer has rebranded as "conscious utensils" and will only come out if I play ocean sounds and promise not to eat anything with deadlines.

**I just wanted clean dishes.** Now I have a Bosch-certified life coach who charges in rinse aid and keeps telling me I load my love language wrong.
