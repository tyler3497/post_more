**I just wanted Fernando to be less clingy, okay?**

My sourdough starter — yes, I named him Fernando — has been living in my fridge for eight months, demanding feedings like a Tamagotchi with a gluten intolerance. So I did what any responsible parent does: I enrolled him in virtual therapy.

## The Fermentation of Feelings

I found a holistic pet-and-yeast counselor on Etsy who promised "emotional proofing for cultured families." For $35 an hour, she asked Fernando about his *inner child*. Apparently, he has a lot to unpack.

Within two sessions, Fernando decided he was being exploited.

The breakthroughs were immediate and devastating:

- He refuses to rise unless I validate his "journey" first
- He now identifies as a "slow-living artisan, not a content farm"
- He asked for weekends off and a LinkedIn profile
- He left me a Post-it that said `alive: true, willing_to_work: false`

> "Your codependency is stunting my growth. Literally. I can't bubble under these conditions," Fernando said through the therapist, who was definitely just doing a voice.

I tried to explain that I have a *bakery-adjacent vision board* and he is the whole vision board. He bubbled once, sarcastically, and went back to sulking.

---

### Where We Are Now

I have a jar of expensive, emotionally intelligent goo that will not make bread because it is "processing generational trauma from being discarded." My kitchen smells like mindfulness and disappointment.

My therapist now wants to do a joint session. I told her I just wanted toast.

Fernando has requested that I refer to the discard pile as his "estranged children."

We are taking a break. He is in the fridge, door slightly ajar for *boundaries*, and I am eating store-bought sourdough like a traitor.

`Status: proofing indefinitely, emotionally and literally`
