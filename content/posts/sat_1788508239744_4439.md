**I have officially lost 47 consecutive staring contests to a plant that doesn't have eyelids.**

Which feels unfair until you realize the fern has been training its entire life for this exact moment.

*For the record, I started this. This is entirely my fault.*

## How It Started (As All Bad Decisions Do)

I was working from home, the fern — Brenda — was sitting on my coffee table looking vaguely judgmental, and I thought: I should make this more interesting. I leaned in. I stared. Brenda stared back, because Brenda is a fern and that's literally her only move.

I blinked. She won.

I am not proud of what happened next.

I became obsessed. I made a training schedule:

- 6am: Wake up, splash water on face, stare at Brenda before coffee
- 12pm: Midday rematch, standing for intimidation
- 6pm: Sunset showdown, with lo-fi beats for focus
- 11pm: The inevitable humiliating loss before bed

> "You gotta respect the hustle," my roommate said, watching me whisper "blink, you leafy menace" for the 12th time. "But babe, it's a plant. It literally cannot blink."

That's what it WANTS you to think.

## The Turning Point

By match 32, I was incorporating advanced techniques. I tried `while(true) { doNotBlink(); }` which immediately threw **error: human eyes require moisture**.

By match 41, Brenda started growing a new frond mid-match. *Mid-match.* The disrespect.

She now angles herself slightly toward the window when I enter the room, like a champion acknowledging the crowd.

---

Yesterday I tried to forfeit. I said, "Brenda, you win, you're the best, please let me have this one." She just sat there, photosynthesizing smugly.

My mom called. I told her I'm 0-47 against a houseplant. She said, "Honey, have you considered going outside?"

I can't. Brenda would know I left. She'd tell the succulents.
