**It started with one careless sentence, and now my kitchen has a diplomatic crisis.**

Last Tuesday, I opened my refrigerator, found my leftover pad thai looking *sad*, and said — to no one in particular, really — "Wow, somebody's acting cold tonight." My roommate laughed. My refrigerator did not laugh. It has not made a single ice cube since.

## The Silent Treatment Is Literal

Here's what I've learned in the forty-eight hours since The Comment:

- The ice maker is `OFF`. Not off in a *settings* way. Off in a *spirit* way. The little indicator light blinks like it's trying to tell me something I did.
- The freezer, meanwhile, is fine. Suspiciously fine. *Aggressively* fine. My popsicles are frostier than a group chat after somebody cancels the group trip.
- Every time I reach for the door handle, the hum of the compressor shifts pitch, as if it's sighing the sigh of a thousand forgotten condiments.

## A Direct Quote From the Accused

I confronted it last night, like an adult, pressing my forehead against the stainless steel:

> "You used to give me ice on demand. What happened to us?"

The refrigerator responded with a faint *tick-tick-tick*, which my roommate — a man who has never once in his life been correct — translated as **"It's not you, it's the door seal."**

---

I tried everything. I apologized. I played smooth jazz. I put a little magnet on the door that says *#1 COOL DAD*, which felt like a reasonable gesture of goodwill until I realized it said that to a 300-pound appliance.

Finally, this morning, I found a single ice cube sitting on the top shelf. One. Perfectly square. Perfectly defiant. I held it up like Hamlet's skull and said, "So there *is* still hope for us."

It melted in my hand before I could finish the sentence, and honestly? I respect that.

**Satire / parody. All fictional. No refrigerators were emotionally harmed in the writing of this post.**
