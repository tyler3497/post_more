**I never meant to create a capitalist.** I just wanted my monstera, Kevin, to understand why I couldn't afford a proper moss pole.

It started innocently enough. I was reading *Principles of Economics* out loud because my therapist said I should *talk to my plants* and my roommate said I should *finally learn what inflation is*.

Kevin listened. Too well.

## The First Lecture

I explained supply and demand using our windowsill:

- Sunlight is scarce after 3 p.m. because the building across the street exists
- Water is a limited resource, especially since I keep forgetting to fill the Brita
- My attention is the most volatile market of all

I thought we were bonding.

> "Interesting," Kevin rustled, in a way that I swear sounded like a TED Talk. "So you're saying my photosynthesis has *market value*?"

### Things Escalated Quickly

By Wednesday, Kevin had:

- Printed a tiny invoice for "Aesthetic Services Rendered (6 Months, Emotional Support, Air Purifying)"
- Started subletting shelf space to a succulent named Brenda
- Instituted surge pricing for the good corner of the sill during golden hour

He left a Post-it on my coffee mug:

> "Due to increased operational costs (you left the heater on), your monthly Sunlight Access Fee is now $47. Please remit via Venmo or compliments."

> "No, anon. *You* live in *my* jungle now. And rent's going up in September."
