**I did not need a $45 water bottle.** I needed eight hours of sleep and a personality transplant, but the bottle had free shipping and the reviews said it "changed my life."

It showed up with time markers on the side. You know the ones: 7 AM: `Good Morning, Gorgeous!` 9 AM: `You've got this!` 11 AM: `Remember your why!` By 2 PM: `Don't quit now!` — which is exactly what my high school gym teacher shouted before making us run until someone cried.

### The Onboarding Was Aggressive

Within 24 hours, the bottle had opinions:

- At 10:14 AM I filled it with iced coffee and the silicone sleeve got *weirdly warm with judgment*
- At 3 PM I left it in my hot car and came back to find it sweating in a way that felt personal
- At 9 PM it was still 3/4 full and the little marking that said `Almost there!` felt like sarcasm

> "You told everyone on Instagram you were drinking a gallon a day," I whispered at midnight, dehydrated and defensive. "You didn't say you'd be *watching* me fail."

I swear I heard it gurgle back:

> "I'm not mad. I'm just literally full of potential and you're not."

---

### Then Came the Performance Review

Yesterday it escalated. I found a sticky note on my fridge I don't remember writing:

`Q3 Hydration Goals: NEEDS IMPROVEMENT - see me in my office (the kitchen sink)`

We had a meeting. It was mostly me apologizing while holding my Brita filter like a character witness.

The worst part? My other mugs have picked sides. My favorite chipped mug that says WORLD'S OKAYEST EMPLOYEE now sits further back on the shelf, like *oh, so you're a BOTTLE person now?*

I'm drinking more water than ever, but at what cost. My spine is 60% guilt and 40% microplastics. My houseplants are jealous of the attention my water container is getting.

`Status: Hydrated, but emotionally waterlogged`

*This is satire. No water bottles were actually promoted to middle management during the writing of this essay.*
