**I did not sign up to be humiliated by my own leftovers.**

On Sunday I decided to be an *adult* and meal prep. You know, quinoa, roasted vegetables, little matching glass containers like I'm on a cooking show for people who own matching socks.

By Monday morning my fridge had changed. Like, fundamentally.

## The Menu Reveal

I opened the door to grab breakfast and my Tupperware had arranged itself by height. My sad chicken breast was sweating under a single basil leaf I don't remember buying. There was a tiny handwritten tasting note stuck to the shelf:

> "Chef Tyler presents: Despair, But Make It Protein. Notes of regret and smoked paprika. 3.5 stars — respects the hustle, would not re-order."

My supporting evidence that I have lost control:

- Course 1: The Same Overnight Oats I've Eaten Since 2022, now rebranded as *Oats à la Déjà Vu*
- Course 2: The Quinoa That Is Mostly Just Lemon Zest And Broken Dreams
- Course 3: Emergency Cheese Stick I Forgot I Hid Behind The Sriracha
- Amuse-bouche: One blueberry that rolled away and is now considered *feral*

*For ambiance,* my crisper drawer is playing a low lo-fi hum and judging my hydration levels.

I tried to reach for the takeout menu. The butter dish whispered, "Oh, so we're abandoning the vision now?"

And the plating — god, the plating. I put everything in identical containers for efficiency and now it runs `presentation.over.substance()` like it learned to code from a culinary influencer.

---

I closed the fridge. Opened it again. My meal prep stared back:

> "If you wanted chaos you should have left me as loose carrots in a bag. At least that was honest."

I'm keeping the system because I paid $24 for the glass containers and my mother saw them. My fridge is keeping the attitude because apparently I have created a monster with a palate, a dream, and a very small Post-it pad.
