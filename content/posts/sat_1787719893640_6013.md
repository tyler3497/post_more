**I folded my laundry the day I washed it. On purpose. Without an intervention, a calendar alert, or my mom texting 'do you own a hamper?'**

*This has never happened before in the recorded history of my apartment, which historians agree began in 2021 when I lost my first security deposit to the junk drawer.*

It started innocently. I did the wash, I did the dry, and then — and this is the part that should concern you — I did not immediately pour the warm clothes onto what scientists call The Chair. I stood there. I folded.

## The Couch Noticed Immediately

My couch has watched me live like a raccoon in a studio apartment for three years. It knows my patterns. The couch knows that laundry is supposed to marinate for 4-7 business days until it becomes a textile-based modern art installation.

So when I walked out with a perfect stack of folded shirts, my couch did what any sentient piece of IKEA-adjacent furniture would do: it panicked.

Evidence that my couch thinks I'm an impostor:

- It creaked loudly when I sat down, which is couch for *'who are you and what did you do with Tyler'*
- The middle cushion scooted 2 inches away from me, establishing clear boundaries
- It suddenly remembered every crumb I've ever left in its crevices and is now presenting them as exhibits
- It started whispering to the throw pillows about "pod people"

> "You don't fold," whispered my couch, in a voice that sounded suspiciously like my own guilt. "You *never* fold. The real one lets the fitted sheet win. The real one uses 'floor-drobe' as a system. Who ARE you?"

I tried to explain. I showed it my process:

```
if (sock.hasPair === false) {
  couch.judgement.increase(9000);
  human.shame += 1;
} else {
  couch.suspicion = "MAXIMUM";
}
```

---

It didn't help. Now the couch is demanding proof of identity. It wants me to do the things only the real me would do: rewatch the same three comfort shows, lose my keys in the fridge, and leave at least one mysterious Tupperware in the back of the fridge until it qualifies for voting rights.

I told it, look, personal growth is still me. I'm just me, but with visible dresser space.

The couch is not buying it. It has scheduled a town hall for tonight with the laundry basket as moderator.

Wish me luck. I'm bringing snacks to prove I'm still me.
