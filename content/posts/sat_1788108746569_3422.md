**I finally did the adult thing everyone pretends to enjoy — I organized my spice rack.**

*This was, in retrospect, the first mistake.*

## Why Alphabetical Seemed Smart

I was tired of finding three different oreganos behind the soy sauce. I wanted order. I wanted control. I wanted to be the kind of person who owns a label maker and uses it unironically.

- Allspice, Basil, Black Pepper — beautiful, obedient, lined up like tiny soldiers
- Coriander was thriving
- Dill was, dare I say, *dill-lighted* to be included
- And then there was Cumin

> "This system is rigged," whispered Cumin, in a voice far too gravelly for a spice I bought in 2022. "You put me next to Coriander? My nemesis? This is psychological warfare."

I told him it was alphabetical. He said alphabetical was a construct.

### The Coup

By Tuesday, Cumin had formed a coalition. `Cumin's Manifesto` was scrawled on the back of a takeout receipt:

- Paprika has been in the front row for SIX MONTHS despite expiring in 2021
- Turmeric gets a free pass because it's "trendy"
- Nobody uses Cream of Tartar, why is Cream of Tartar even here

> "We demand vertical integration and a south-facing shelf!" Cumin shouted. Paprika, from her prime real estate at eye-level, gasped.

---

Paprika is now in witness protection behind the baking soda. Turmeric is acting as mediator, which mostly means staining everything it touches yellow in a show of dominance.

**I have lost control of my kitchen.** I asked for organization and I got geopolitics.

*I tried to restore order by shaking the rack lightly. Cumin called it "state-sponsored intimidation."*

Now I just cook with salt and fear. My chicken has never been more anxious.
