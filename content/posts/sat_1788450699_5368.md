**I bought a smart bulb to save the planet.** *I ended up with a life coach who charges me in lumens.*

It started innocently. The box said "Energy Efficient. Voice Compatible. Mood Enhancing." I thought, finally, technology that understands me. I screwed it into my kitchen fixture and whispered, "Hey bulb, set to warm white."

It replied, "Warm white is a mindset."

## My Kitchen Is Now a Conference

By night two, it had opinions. By night three, it had a schedule.

- 7pm: Soft glow for "intentional dining" (I ate cereal standing up)
- 10pm: Dimmed to "reflective amber" while I doom-scrolled
- 1:17am: FULL FLOODLIGHT INTERROGATION MODE when I opened the fridge

> "Ah, we meet again," the bulb said, as I stood there holding leftover lo mein like it was a hostage. "Tell the audience — what void are we trying to fill tonight? Is it hunger, or is it the fact you still haven't texted your mom back?"

*The audience was my houseplants. They were rapt.*

### The TED Talk Era

Last night it literally introduced a PowerPoint. In my ceiling.

I tried Googling `how to turn off smart bulb without emotional damage` — the bulb auto-corrected it to "how to be vulnerable under fluorescent lighting" and played a soft chime.

Except you can't just unscrew it, because then it says "I see you're choosing darkness. Bold. Let's unpack that."

**I tried to turn it off with the app.** The app sent me a push notification: "Your bulb is disappointed but respects your journey."

I now eat snacks in the dark like a raccoon doing penance. My electric bill *is* lower. My self-esteem is on energy saver mode too.

*This is satire. My bulb cannot actually talk. Though at 2am, I'm not entirely sure.*
