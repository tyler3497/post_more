**I have had this motivation sitting in my junk drawer since 2021.**

*Unopened, still in the original packaging, faint smell of ambition and poor decisions.*

## How I Ended Up With Extra

I bought it during a particularly aggressive New Year's resolution sale. You know the one. Two-for-one on self-improvement, free tote bag that says HUSTLE.

- I meant to use it to learn Spanish
- I meant to use it to run a 5K without wheezing like a deflating air mattress
- I meant to use it to finally organize my 14,000 unsorted photos titled IMG_final_final(2).jpg

I used none of it. Zero percent. Still had the little silica packet inside that says DO NOT EAT.

So last Tuesday, I walked into the store.

> "Hi, I'd like to return this," I said, sliding the motivation across the counter.
>
> "Do you have a receipt?" she asked, without looking up.

I did not. *Of course I didn't.*

### The Trial

The manager was called. His name tag said **CHAD — RETURNS / EXISTENTIAL JUDGMENTS**.

He inspected the motivation under a black light.

> "Sir, this has been clearly *regretted*," he said. "You opened it mentally. That's still opening it."

He flipped to page 47 of the policy binder, which just said in Comic Sans: `NO TAKE-BACKS ON DREAMS`.

- He said my motivation had depreciated due to "procrastination damage"
- He claimed I voided the warranty by watching 8 hours of comfort-show reruns
- He informed me the store no longer accepts "aspirational returns from 2021"

Then he banned me. For life.

---

Now the motivation lives on my fridge, judging me. **I think it's thriving out of spite.** It got a promotion. *I did not.*
