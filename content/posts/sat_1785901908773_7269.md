**I just wanted to be the mysterious, interesting neighbor for once.** Is that so much to ask?

My old Wi-Fi was called `NETGEAR-5G-3821`, which is basically admitting you gave up on life. So I changed it. I got creative. I got *bold*.

I named it `THE COLLECTIVE ASCENDS AT MIDNIGHT`.

## The Downward Spiral

Within 45 minutes, my building's group chat, which had been dead since 2022, sprang to life:

- **3:12 PM** — Apt 2B: "hey does anyone know what Collective Ascends means? should we be concerned"
- **3:14 PM** — Apt 3A: "my kid's tablet auto-connected and now he's speaking in tongues??"
- **3:18 PM** — Apt 1C (my mom-friend Karen): "honey is this a yoga thing?"

I tried to explain it was *ironic*. *Aesthetic*. Very internet.

> "No no, it's like a *vibe*," I typed, sweating. "It's not a REAL collective. Unless... you want to join? I have snacks."

That did not help.

## My Accidental Cult Leader Era

Now Mrs. Henderson from Apt 4A leaves oat milk outside my door as an offering. The guy in 2A asked if Tuesday is still good for "the ascending." My landlord slid a note under my door that just said:

> "Please confirm you are not summoning anything in the laundry room. - Management"

The worst part? My Wi-Fi is *faster* now. People keep trying to connect thinking they'll get enlightened. The password is `password123` and they think it's a *riddle*.

`status: connected, spiritually confused`

I've considered changing it back, but honestly? For the first time in three years, everyone knows my name. I'm not fixing it. We ascend at midnight. BYOB.
