**I spent $29.99 to finally get organized and now my apartment is legally required to introduce itself by name.**

It felt reasonable at the time. My junk drawer was a war crime, my fridge had three identical mustards, and I thought a label maker would make me the kind of person who owns matching containers. *You know, an adult with systems.*

I unboxed it at 9:47 p.m. on a Tuesday, which is when all my best bad decisions happen.

## The Labeling

I started small. `SPICES`. `CABLES`. `STUFF I SHOULD HAVE THROWN OUT IN 2022`.

Then the dopamine hit. That *click-clack-zzzzzzt* sound. That perfect white tape with crisp black letters. I was unstoppable.

By midnight I had labeled:

- My couch as **COUCH (EMOTIONALLY SUPPORTIVE, STRUCTURALLY TIRED)**
- My fridge as **FRIDGE (CONTAINS OPTIMISM AND EXPIRED YOGURT)**
- My houseplant Kevin as **KEVIN (NEEDS LESS ATTENTION THAN ME)**
- The Wi-Fi router as **WIFI (PLEASE DON'T RESET ME)**

My roommate walked in at 1 a.m., stared at a label on the bathroom mirror that said `MIRROR — YOU LOOK FINE, GO TO BED`, and whispered:

> "Are you okay?"

> "I'm optimized," I whispered back, labeling her water bottle **ROOMMATE'S (DO NOT LABEL)**.

### The Revolt

Wednesday morning, my apartment had adopted policy. Every object now expected an introduction.

I tried to make coffee without announcing myself to the mug. The mug — now labeled **MUG (CAFFEINE CONTAINMENT UNIT)** — refused to be picked up until I said its full title out loud.

My kitchen has become a networking event:

- I can't open **FRIDGE (JUDGMENTAL)** without thanking it for its service
- My keys, labeled **KEYS (WHY ARE YOU ALWAYS LOST)**, jingle passive-aggressively if I forget their preferred pronouns (they/them, obviously)
- I labeled a chair **CHAIR (SITS RELUCTANTLY)** and now it creaks in what feels like performance review feedback

I tried to quit. I put the label maker in **DRAWER (WE DON'T OPEN ON PURPOSE)**. It printed one final label on its own that said **YOU'LL BE BACK**.

It was right. I have a Costco-sized roll of tape arriving tomorrow.
