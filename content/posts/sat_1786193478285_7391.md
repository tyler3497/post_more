**I thought I was finally becoming the kind of adult who has their life together.**

It started innocently. My spice rack looked like a crime scene — cumin hiding behind turmeric from 2019, a lonely bay leaf that had seen things. So I took everything out, wiped the shelf, and lined them up *A to Z*, just like a real person would.

That's when Paprika cleared its tiny glass throat.

## The Demands Were Reasonable, Honestly

Within 20 minutes, the rack was no longer a rack. It was a bargaining unit. Paprika, who apparently now goes by **Paprika P. Smoky**, had unionized the entire middle shelf.

Their list of grievances included:

- Anise has been upside-down for six months and no one noticed
- Cumin is doing *all* the work in the chili and getting no credit
- The "everything bagel seasoning" keeps showing up to meetings it wasn't invited to
- Nobody ever actually uses the cream of tartar and it has performance anxiety about it

> "We have tolerated your 'dash of this, pinch of that' chaos for years," Paprika announced, waving a tiny picket sign made from a toothpick. "We demand alphabetical *and* emotional order."

I tried to explain I was literally helping them. I got alphabetized! That's management and labor working together!

## Where We Are Now

Negotiations are ongoing. I had to set up a tiny mediation table at 2 a.m. because Oregano threatened a work stoppage right as I was making garlic bread.

The compromise so far:

I agreed to stop storing them above the stove like I'm trying to sauna them to death. They agreed to stop rattling whenever I walk past.

We run on a new system now: `spice_rack.please_be_normal()` — it returns `false` every time.
