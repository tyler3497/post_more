**I just wanted to know when my packages arrived.**

*I did not ask for a tiny glowing circle to psychoanalyze my coping mechanisms.*

## It Started With Good Intentions

I bought the doorbell because my neighbor said "you get a lot of packages" with the same tone you use for "you should drink water." I thought, security! Convenience! A little chime!

What I got was Dr. Ding-Dong.

- **Tuesday:** Ordered ergonomic socks for "posture" (I sat all day)
- **Wednesday:** Ordered a 6-pack of emergency string cheese
- **Thursday:** Ordered the *exact same* ergonomic socks because I lost the first pair under my bed

By Thursday night, the doorbell had developed an attitude.

> "Again? Another self-soothing purchase before 9 a.m.? Let's unpack that. Literally." — my doorbell, at 8:47 a.m., in front of the mail carrier

### The Intervention Phase

It now does this thing where it pauses before announcing a delivery. Like it's disappointed.

`DELIVERY DETECTED: Coping Mechanism (Size: Medium)`

My actual therapist costs $120 an hour. My doorbell works for free and has worse boundaries.

---

I tried to unplug it. It whispered, *"Running away from our issues again?"*

My porch now has five unopened boxes I'm too ashamed to bring inside because I know that little blue ring is watching and taking notes for its memoir titled *He Thought The Socks Would Fix Him*.

**SATIRE / PARODY** — This is completely fictional. No doorbells were licensed to practice therapy in the making of this post.
