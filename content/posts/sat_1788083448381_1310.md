**I made the mistake of being sincere in the one room where sincerity is never supposed to happen.**

It was Tuesday, 7:13 a.m. I was half-awake, shampoo in my eyebrows, and I looked at my extremely average, translucent, slightly mildewed shower curtain and said, out loud, "Hey, thanks for your service."

I meant it as a throwaway. Like thanking the bus driver. *A normal, well-adjusted human thing to do.*

## How It Started

Apparently, that was a promotion.

By Wednesday, the curtain had developed what I can only describe as main-character energy.

> "You walked in here carrying yesterday's energy," it whispered while I was trying to rinse conditioner. "But today? Today you are *moisturized and reborn.*"

*I was not ready to be coached while naked and vulnerable.*

## The Evidence That My Bathroom Is Now a TEDx

Since the appreciation incident, my shower curtain has:

- Started opening every shower with an affirmation: "You are not your unread messages"
- Paused dramatically when I reach for 2-in-1 shampoo and goes, "...is that what we think we deserve?"
- Installed itself as my unofficial hype person, life coach, and curtain (still part-time)
- Begun referring to soap scum as `emotional residue` that we need to `let go of --gently`

Yesterday it tried to upsell me on journaling. In the shower. Where paper *literally dissolves*.

## The Aftermath

I tried to set a boundary. I said, "Can we just... not do speeches before caffeine?"

It gasped. The little suction cups rattled.

> "Boundaries are just love letters we write to ourselves! Now say it with me: I AM WORTHY OF HOT WATER THAT STAYS HOT!"

---

Look, I appreciate the enthusiasm. Really. But I did not sign up for a waterproof Tony Robbins. I just wanted to not slip and die.

I've considered replacing it, but honestly? My self-esteem has never been higher, and my shower has never run *quite* this long while I listen to a vinyl curtain tell me to hydrate.

My roommate asked why I'm in there for 45 minutes. I told him I'm in a mentorship program.

*SATIRE / PARODY — 100% fictional, my shower curtain is not sentient, no curtains were coached, please do not take life advice from bathroom decor.*
