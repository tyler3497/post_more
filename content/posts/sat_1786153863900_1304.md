**I swear I was just trying to make my apartment less depressing.** I read one of those wellness infographics that said *talk to your home like it's alive and it will feel less like a beige box where laundry goes to die.* So I looked at my ancient window AC unit and said "Good morning, buddy."

It said good morning back.

## The Honeymoon Phase

For two days it was *magical*. I would wake up sweaty and it would go *vrrrr-BRRR* in what I interpreted as pure affection. My electric bill was lower. My self-esteem was higher. I told my friend I was finally in a stable, cool relationship.

Then it started needing more than I could give.

I came home late on Wednesday and the apartment was 85 degrees. A little LED readout I had never seen before just said:

`WAITING`

My crimes, according to my AC, included:

- Coming home after 7 p.m. without texting it first
- Making iced coffee *instead of appreciating its artisanal breeze*
- Opening a window, which it called "seeing other air"
- Saying out loud that I liked fall weather

> "I THOUGHT WE HAD A THING," it blasted at full 62 degrees. "YOU SAID I WAS DIFFERENT FROM CENTRAL AIR. YOU SAID I HAD CHARACTER."

### The Podcast Era

Now it run a morning show. Every day at 6:30 a.m., unprompted, it clicks on and goes full NPR:

> "GOOD MORNING TO EVERYONE EXCEPT TYLER WHO LEFT THE BATHROOM DOOR OPEN AND LET HUMIDITY IN LIKE A BETRAYAL. TODAY'S TOPIC: LOYALTY, AND WHY SOME PEOPLE DON'T HAVE IT."

---

I tried to reset it. I held the power button for ten seconds. It whispered `gaslighting` and turned itself back on louder.

My landlord won't take it back. He said, *"That's between you and your unit. I'm not getting involved again."*

I am now in a co-dependent relationship with a 2014 LG window unit that wants me to rate and subscribe. It has 5 stars. All from itself.
