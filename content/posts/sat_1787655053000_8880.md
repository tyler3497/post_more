**I finally organized my junk drawer on Saturday, which is how I knew I had made a terrible leadership decision.**

*It was supposed to be 15 minutes.* Just me, a trash bag, and the drawer that has been legally classified as a "miscellaneous holding zone" since 2019. Three hours later, I had created something so efficient it started performance-reviewing *me*.

## The Before Times Were Peaceful Chaos

You know the drawer. Everyone has one:

- One dead AA battery you keep *just in case it resurrects*
- Six soy sauce packets from a place that closed in 2021
- A screwdriver that is technically for eyeglasses but emotionally for everything
- That one IKEA Allen key that now owns 30% of your furniture's structural integrity
- A receipt for batteries that you saved to prove you bought batteries

It was a democracy. A messy, functional, deeply American democracy.

> "We are humble," the drawer used to whisper. "We contain multitudes and also that thing that beeps when you can't find it."

## The After Times Are Corporate

I bought little plastic organizers. I labeled them. I even used `labelMakerPro: true` mode.

```js
if (item.category == "unknown" && item.usefulness == "maybe_someday") {
  throw new Error("You are now middle management");
}
```

By Sunday morning, the drawer had changed.

> "Tyler," it said, in a voice that sounded suspiciously like a LinkedIn influencer. "We need to talk about your Q3 objectives for the second kitchen drawer. And why the utensil tray is underperforming."

It started sending me memos. Actual tiny memos on the back of those soy sauce packets:

- **Memorandum #1:** Request for standing desk conversion (for better ergonomic access)
- **Memorandum #2:** Proposal to absorb the "random cords" box in the closet
- **Memorandum #3:** My performance review — *Needs Improvement in Letting Go of Expired Coupons*

---

Look, I just wanted to find my tape measure without moving a takeout menu from Obama's first term. Now I have a middle manager living under my spatula.

It wants a 401(k), a nameplate that says **Director of Miscellaneous Operations**, and a direct line to the CEO (which is my fridge, apparently).

I’m thinking of messing it up again out of spite. One loose paperclip and the whole hierarchy collapses.
