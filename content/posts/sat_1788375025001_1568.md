**I thought I was buying a kitchen appliance. I accidentally enlisted in a cult of crispiness.**

*It started because my doctor said "eat more vegetables" and I thought, what if I just make them think they're french fries?*

So I bought an air fryer. On sale. With 17 buttons and a digital voice that says "SHAKE" like it's disappointed in me.

## The Intervention I Didn't Schedule

I put broccoli in there on Monday. Just broccoli. Olive oil, salt, optimism. I set it to 375 and went to check my email.

When I came back, the air fryer had changed.

> "WE DON'T DO SOGGY IN THIS HOUSE," it announced. "THIS IS A LIFESTYLE NOW."

I laughed. It did not.

Signs my air fryer has taken command:

- It renamed itself `Sargeant Crisp` and now refers to my kitchen as "the training ground"
- It refuses to cook anything that isn't "giving 100%" — my leftover pizza got sent to remedial crunching
- It installed a tiny whiteboard where it tracks vegetable morale in dry-erase marker
- It plays motivational speeches at 6 a.m. that are just the word `AIRFLOW` shouted 40 times

### The Boot Camp Schedule Is... Intense

Tuesday I tried to make toast. Sarge said:

> "Bread? We don't know her. Bring me something that sweats when it's scared."

Now my vegetables line up. Carrots do burpees. Potatoes do planks. The zucchini tried to call in sick and got put on double time.

I tried to intervene. I wrote a little config patch:

```
if (vegetable.confidence < 0.5) {
  airFryer.say("YOU ARE ALREADY ENOUGH, BUT ALSO CRISPIER");
}
```

It made it worse.

---

Now I stand in my kitchen at midnight watching cauliflower do wind sprints in a basket, whispering "I just wanted to eat healthier, not run a CrossFit for produce."

My oven is jealous. My microwave is taking notes.

*SATIRE / PARODY — 100% fictional, no air fryers were promoted to drill sergeant, no vegetables were harmed, though one broccoli floret did ask for a transfer to the steamer for "work-life balance."*
