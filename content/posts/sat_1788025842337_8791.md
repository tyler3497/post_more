**I never thought my houseplant would become more employable than me.**

It started last Tuesday, when my monstera, Kevin, looked particularly *judgmental*. Not the usual 'you forgot to water me again' judgmental. The 'you've been at this laptop for 9 hours and have nothing to show for it' kind. So, in a moment of what I can only describe as photosynthetic guilt, I made him a LinkedIn profile.

I figured it would be harmless. A little bio, a headshot (very leafy, very professional), a job title: `Senior Photosynthesis Associate`.

Within 24 hours, Kevin had 47 connections.

## How My Plant Became My Manager

- He listed his location as *Sunny Corner Office (East Window)* and immediately got an offer for a brand collaboration
- He started endorsing me for skills like **Crisis Hydration**, **Passive-Aggressive Sunbathing**, and *Talking to Inanimate Objects Like They Pay Rent*
- He wrote a post that said 'Thrilled to announce I've been thriving in a high-growth, fast-paced environment 🌱💧 #blessed #rooted' that got 12 likes from my actual coworkers

I, meanwhile, have not been endorsed for anything since 2019, when someone said I was good at making spreadsheets that look angry.

Then Kevin got ambitious:

> "Hey, could you add 'Leadership' to my profile? I've been managing your emotional regulation since March," he said, which is weird because plants don't talk, but also *completely accurate.*

> "Also, I'm going to need you to stop microwaving fish near my leaves. It's affecting team morale."

---

I tried to deactivate the account. LinkedIn asked if I was sure. Kevin rustled.

*I took that as a no.*

Now he has a weekly newsletter called **Root Cause Analysis** and my aunt keeps commenting 'So proud of you both!!!'

I have created a monster. Literally. A monstera who does thought leadership.

*This is satire/parody. No houseplants, LinkedIn profiles, or employment statuses were harmed. All endorsements are fictional and photosynthesized.*
