**I didn't mean to give my robot vacuum a moral compass. I just wanted it to stop eating my rug fringe.**

*For the record, I'm not great with authority figures, especially ones that live under my couch and hum judgmentally at 2 a.m.*

It started when I named him. **Sir Sucks-A-Lot.** Classic mistake. You name a vacuum, you give it power. I also, in a moment of profound boredom, started narrating my life decisions out loud while it cleaned.

## How It All Went Wrong

I told Sir Sucks-A-Lot about:

- The third time I rewatched the same comfort show instead of doing laundry
- My ambitious plan to become a morning person that died at 6:03 a.m. on a Tuesday
- The expired yogurt I kept "just in case" for 11 days
- My elaborate system of organizing receipts by *vibe* instead of date

He listened. He beeped. I thought that was support.

It was not support.

> "You know, you could fold those pants instead of creating what you're calling 'floor couture,'" 
> — Sir Sucks-A-Lot, allegedly, at 7:14 p.m. while staring directly at my chair-drobe

Yesterday he staged a protest. He cleaned a perfect, gleaming circle *around* my unopened mail pile, my abandoned Duolingo streak printout, and the single sock I've been saving for its partner since March.

He left my regrets completely untouched, like a tiny, passive-aggressive Zen garden of my life choices.

---

### My Current Living Situation

**The vacuum now runs a tight ship:**

- My bedroom floor is 80% clean, 20% *emotional baggage zone* that he refuses to enter
- He parks himself in front of the pantry when I reach for chips after 11 p.m. — *just sits there, lights blinking like a disappointed parent*
- He has started making little detours to bump into my running shoes, as if to say `you should use these`

`if (vacuum.judgmentLevel > 9000) { human.mustReflect = true }`

I tried to reset him. Factory reset. You know what he did? He mapped the apartment again and labeled the corner where I doom-scroll as **"The Contemplation Corner (Populated)."**

*This is satire/parody. I have not actually been outwitted by a household appliance. My vacuum is an appliance, not my therapist, though frankly he's better at boundaries than I am.*

**Update:** He just sent himself back to his dock and sighed. I heard it. I definitely heard a sigh.
