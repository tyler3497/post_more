## The Chi Was Not Worth It

**I moved my bed six inches to the left** because a very calm woman on TikTok told me it would align my chi, my sleep schedule, and my 2026. *What she failed to mention* is that my bed has opinions.

For three years, my bed was against the north wall. It was happy. It knew where the sun hit. We had a system. Then I watched a 45-second video with wind chimes and made an executive decision.

I now have a bedroom that looks like a showroom and a bed that has hired representation.

> "You have displaced me without due process," my bed said, or would have said if headboards could talk, which mine now does at 2:17 a.m.

### Exhibit A Through C of Why This Was a Mistake

According to my bed (via a strongly worded note I definitely wrote to myself but am choosing to treat as legally binding):

- **Loss of prime real estate** – It can no longer see the door, which, according to Feng Shui, is good, but according to my bed, is *anxiety-inducing*
- **Draft exposure** – The new position is directly under the vent, which means my bed is now experiencing what it calls `seasonal affective disorder`
- **Vibe betrayal** – I bought a small jade plant to "anchor the energy" and the plant immediately started judging everyone

---

### The Settlement Talks

My roommate walked in, looked at the new layout, and said:

> "Did your bed... get smaller? Or are you just more dramatic now?"

Fair question. Both.

We are currently in mediation. I offered to move it back. My bed counter-offered with demands for new linens, no more eating crackers in it, and a formal apology for the time I used it as an ironing board in 2024.

I declined the linen request. We are at an impasse. The jade plant is acting as arbitrator and is *deeply* unqualified.

I miss the old layout, where my biggest problem was that my pillow was flat. Now I have a bedroom with better energy flow and a bed that keeps sighing like a disappointed landlord.

`Status: Feng` `Shui is 50% furniture, 50% litigation`
