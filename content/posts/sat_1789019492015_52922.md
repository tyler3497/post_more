**It took me 31 years, but I did it.** On a quiet Tuesday afternoon, with no audience and no fanfare, I folded a fitted sheet correctly. Corners tucked. Edges aligned. A perfect, crisp rectangle. I stood there holding it, and I felt something I can only describe as *Olympic*.

And then the dread set in.

Because here is the thing nobody warns you about: **some achievements are so improbable that reaching them breaks your sense of what's next.** I have eaten street food in nine countries. I once parallel parked on the first try in downtown traffic. But the fitted sheet? That's the *final boss* of adulthood. What do I do with the rest of my life? Learn to golf? Take up sourdough? *Settle?*

## The Evidence That This Was My Peak

- My roommate walked in, saw the folded sheet, and just whispered *"no way"* and sat down. He didn't even ask for proof. He just knew.
- I tried to recreate it an hour later with a second sheet. It took 45 minutes and looked like a **crumpled parachute**. The first one was clearly a *fluke of destiny*, which makes it worse \u2014 my peak wasn't even repeatable.
- My mother has folded fitted sheets for four decades. When I called to tell her, there was a long silence, and then she said: *"Be careful."* Be careful of WHAT, Mom.

> "The average person folds zero fitted sheets correctly in their lifetime. You did one. Statistically, you're done."
> \u2014 my brother, who has never been supportive about anything and is somehow correct this time

## How I Am Coping

I have placed the folded sheet in a position of honor \u2014 the middle shelf, folded side out, like a **trophy**. Guests are encouraged to look but not touch. One friend unfolded it to "use" it and I have not spoken to him since. *Some boundaries are load-bearing.*

I briefly considered framing it. My therapist (I got one, *because of the sheet*) says the correct move is to "integrate the achievement and move on." But moving on to *what*? Filing taxes doesn't hit the same. Running a marathon is just cardio with extra steps. **Nothing will ever fold this well again.**

---

So here is my advice: if you haven't folded one yet, *don't rush it*. Savor the struggle. The crumpled corners, the elastic fighting back, the quiet shame of giving up and stuffing it in the closet \u2014 that's the journey. The destination is a flat rectangle and an existential crisis, and honestly? The journey was better.
