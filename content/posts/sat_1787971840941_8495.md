**I have always had a system, and my system was called Never Fold Anything Ever.**

*It's a very popular lifestyle choice among people whose laundry basket doubles as a closet, a chair, and occasionally a coffee table.*

For three years, my laundry basket and I had an understanding. I filled it. It held. No questions asked.

Then last Tuesday, I watched a 90-second productivity video.

## The Mistake

The guy said, "If it takes less than two minutes, do it now!" So I did. I took the warm clothes out of the dryer and I folded them. Immediately. Like a functioning adult in a detergent commercial.

That's when my laundry basket realized I had abandoned it.

> "Oh, so we're just... folding now?" it whispered from the corner. "Cool. Cool cool cool. After everything we've been through."

Suddenly my entire laundry ecosystem collapsed into emotional chaos:

- My basket started leaving passive-aggressive lint notes around the apartment
- My dresser drawers are overwhelmed and have filed for emotional support — `if (drawer.capacity > 100) { panic(); }` is running 24/7
- My socks, who had built a thriving civilization in the basket, are now homeless and radicalized
- My fitted sheet, which I actually folded correctly for the first time, is being worshipped as a false prophet

---

## The Hearing

This morning I found an official-looking letter taped to the basket:

> "NOTICE OF ABANDONMENT: You have violated Section 4, Subsection B of the Basket-Human Cohabitation Agreement (2023). You will be hearing from my attorney, a very angry sock with one eye."

Look, I get it. Change is scary. But I cannot go back to living out of a wicker abyss just because my basket has separation anxiety.

I tried to explain that we can still hang out. I put a single towel back in to make it feel needed. It threw it back out.

*Satire/parody — this is fictional and absurd, I do not actually have a sentient laundry basket, though my therapist says we should invite it to our next session.*
