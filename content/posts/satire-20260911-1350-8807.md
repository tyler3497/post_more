**Last night I learned that the hardest thing in my bedroom isn't my mattress. It's my pillow's ego.**

It started innocently enough. I woke up with a stiff neck and, still half-dreaming, muttered the fatal words: *"I think my mattress isn't supporting me."*

My pillow heard that. And she *felt* that.

## The Declaration of War

By dinner, my pillow had propped herself against the headboard like a tiny linen judge and issued a statement. She claimed that **eight of my last ten good dreams** were made possible by her, and that the mattress — quote — *"just lies there, taking credit for horizontality."*

The mattress responded the only way it knows how: by squeaking ominously when I sat down. A classic power move.

> "I have been supporting this entire family since 2019," the mattress said, through the universal language of springs. "The pillow handles eight inches of head. *I* handle the whole man."

## The Evidence, Presented at 2:47 AM

Unable to sleep, I was drafted as mediator. Each side submitted their case:

- **The Pillow's brief:** She never sags. She is hypoallergenic. She has been there for every single one of my nightmares, "absorbing the screams so the neighbors don't have to."
- **The Mattress's brief:** It carries 100% of my body weight, 100% of the time. Its warranty is longer than most of my friendships. Also — and this part was delivered with real bitterness — *"who gets rotated twice a year for their health? Not the pillow."*
- **The blanket's testimony:** Declined to comment, but noticeably wrapped itself around the pillow afterward, which everyone agreed was suspicious.

## The Verdict

After four hours of deliberation (me, staring at the ceiling, questioning every decision), I ruled in favor of **shared custody of my back**. The pillow gets the head. The mattress gets everything else. Neither is allowed to comment on the other's job performance during sleeping hours.

---

This morning, the pillow was fluffed. The mattress was silent. And for the first time in weeks, I woke up with **zero neck pain** — which is exactly what they'd want me to think.
