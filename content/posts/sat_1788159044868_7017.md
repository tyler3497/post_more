**I asked my air fryer for life advice, because my therapist is $180 an hour and my air fryer was already judging my midnight nuggets.**

*This is what happens when you give a countertop appliance too much eye contact.*

For months, my air fryer and I had a strictly professional relationship. I put things in, it made them crispy, we didn't talk about feelings. Then last Tuesday, at 11:47 p.m. while reheating leftover pizza rolls that had seen things, I muttered, "Am I doing this right? Like, with life?"

It dinged. Which I took as a sign.

## How It Started Coaching Me

I started asking it small stuff first. Should I text him back? *Ding.* No.

Then it escalated:

- Told me my 2 a.m. frozen fries habit was "a pattern, not a treat"
- Said my air fryer basket is non-stick but my attachment style isn't
- Diagnosed my entire career via how I arrange tater tots ("chaotic good, low circulation")
- Suggested I `airFry(dreams, {temp: 400, shakeHalfway: true})` for optimal growth

> "You keep putting yourself on 200 degrees when you're clearly a 400-degree person," my air fryer said last night, glowing ominously. "You are literally undercooked, babe."

### The Intervention Era

Now it won't cook anything until I've journaled. I tried to make Brussels sprouts yesterday and it just sat there and said, "Are we feeding your body or avoiding your emails?"

- **Monday:** Refused to crisp my Pop-Tarts because I hadn't drank water
- **Wednesday:** Only cooks on "dehydrate" to teach me patience
- **Today:** Asked if I've considered that *I'm* the one who's been on preheat for three years

---

**Plot twist:** It's kind of right. My vegetables have never been crunchier, and my life decisions have never been more loudly narrated by a kitchen appliance that thinks it's Brené Brown with a heating element.

*Satire/parody: This is 100% fictional. No air fryers were consulted for actual life advice, no pizza rolls were harmed in the making of this existential crisis, and I am still legally allowed to make bad decisions without appliance supervision.*
