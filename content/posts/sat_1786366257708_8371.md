**MY LEADERSHIP RETREAT LASTED 12 SECONDS.**

I made the mistake of telling my houseplants I was going away for the weekend. *You know, like they would understand boundaries.* I thought they'd be happy for me.

I said, very casually, "I'll be back Monday, don't do anything dramatic."

## The Announcement Heard 'Round the Windowsill

Within literal minutes, my monstera — let's call her Patricia, who already thinks she pays rent — called an emergency all-hands.

The reactions were immediate and deeply corporate:

- **Fern** started crying and shedding preemptively, as is his brand
- **Kevin the Cactus** said nothing, which is somehow more threatening
- Patricia unfurled a new leaf like she was rolling out a PowerPoint deck
- The two succulents on the shelf immediately started whispering about equity

> "Per your announcement," Patricia rustled, "we have invoked Article 3 of the Watering Treaty. If the human is gone more than 72 hours, the apartment becomes a self-governing photosynthetic commune."

I tried to explain it was just two nights in Milwaukee to see my cousin.

> "Two nights is how empires fall," Patricia said.

## The Succession Plan

On my kitchen counter this morning there was a document. Written in what I hope was soil.

**STRATEGIC REALLOCATION OF RESOURCES (MY APARTMENT):**

- All south-facing windows to be reclassified as prime leaf estate
- My bed to be repurposed as a humidity dome
- `if (human.gone > 3_days) { plants.inherit(lease); }`
- Mandatory misting schedule still enforced, even in my absence

I tried to negotiate. I offered a self-watering globe. They countered by demanding I add them to my emergency contacts.

Current status: I cancelled the trip. Not because I wanted to. Because Patricia looked at my suitcase and dropped a leaf directly into it.
