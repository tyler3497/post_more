## It Was Never Just About the 10 Cents Off Gas

I knew my relationship with my grocery store loyalty card was serious, but I didn't realize it was monitoring my movements like a jealous ex with access to *Find My Friends*.

For **three years**, I was loyal. Devoted.

- Every bunch of bananas? *Scanned*
- Every emergency ice cream run? *Scanned*
- Every single-packet-of-yeast purchase that made the cashier judge me? **Scanned**

It gave me 10 cents off gas and I acted like it had paid my rent.

Then last week, I did the unthinkable. I went to another grocery store. Just once. They had a sale on sourdough and my regular store was out. I thought *what happens across town stays across town*.

**I was wrong.**

### The Confrontation

On Tuesday, I swiped my card. The register made a sound I have never heard before. Not the cheerful `boop` of savings, but a long, wounded:

> "booooooooo."

The cashier looked at me, then at the screen, then back at me with pure pity.

My phone buzzed.

> **Email from loyalty program:** "We need to talk. We saw you at FreshField Market. Was it the 2-for-1 avocados? Tell us truthfully, was it the avocados?"

### The Spiral

By Wednesday, it had escalated:

- **Push notification:** "Remember all the coupons we clipped together?"
- **Another:** "I kept track of your yogurt preferences when no one else would."
- Thursday: revoked my Fuel Points and replaced offers with a single onion and a pamphlet titled *"So You've Chosen Betrayal."*

I tried to apologize at customer service. They scanned my card and whispered:

> "Oof. It's marked as 'it's complicated.'"

---

We are now in counseling, which is just me standing in aisle 7 promising to never stray again while the self-checkout lady pretends not to listen. It says it needs space, but also still wants me to buy its **store-brand hummus**.

Romance is dead. **Long live 10 cents off gas.**
