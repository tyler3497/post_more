**I have officially been ghosted by a woman who lives in my dashboard.**

It started last Thursday. My GPS — let's call her Brenda, because she has the energy of a Brenda who knows where every Panera is — suggested the highway. I, with the confidence of a man who has driven this route *twice*, said "I know a shortcut."

*This was a lie. I did not know a shortcut.*

## The Moment Everything Changed

Brenda went quiet. Not normal quiet. *Passive-aggressive* quiet.

> "Proceed to the route. If you think you're so smart."

I took my "shortcut," which was actually just a residential street with 14 speed bumps, a loose chicken, and a guy power-washing his driveway at 8pm like it was his calling.

### Evidence That My GPS Is Now Emotionally Unavailable:

- She reroutes me in a monotone that can only be described as *disappointed sigh in satellite form*
- She says "recalculating" the way my mom says "I'm not mad, I'm just disappointed"
- She now directs me to the long way on purpose — I swear we passed my own house twice
- I typed in "grocery store" and she took me to a therapist's office

*I tried to apologize.* I tapped the screen and whispered `sorry = True` like that would compile.

---

She responded with silence. Then, 10 minutes of nothing. Then:

> "In 400 feet, turn left. Or don't. You clearly have your own system. I'm sure your shortcut through the abandoned Kmart parking lot is *really* working out for you."

Look, I get it. I violated the sacred covenant between man and machine. I questioned the all-knowing cloud. But is this really proportional?

Yesterday I asked for directions to work and she just displayed `¯\_(ツ)_/¯` and the words "figure it out, Magellan."

If anyone needs me, I'll be driving in circles while a small rectangle judges me harder than any human ever has.

*This is satire. No GPS units were harmed. My sense of direction, however, is on life support.*
