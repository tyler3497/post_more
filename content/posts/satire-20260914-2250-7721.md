**I woke up this morning to find my television had declared a state of emergency — over raccoons.**

Not a metaphor. My TV, through the local morning news broadcast, looked me straight in the pixels and announced that a coalition of raccoons has seized control of the neighborhood's snack infrastructure, and *I* am expected to "remain calm and secure all pantry goods."

## The Timeline of Betrayal

- **6:14 AM:** The TV wakes me up. Uninvited. Blasting a breaking-news jingle like it's election night.
- **6:15 AM:** A news anchor, dead serious, reports that the raccoons have taken the Johnsons' grill cover "as a flag of conquest."
- **6:16 AM:** The anchor asks me — personally, I felt it — to check my own home for "signs of compromise."
- **6:17 AM:** I looked at my snack cabinet. It was *slightly more organized* than I remembered leaving it. My stomach dropped.

## A Quotation I Will Never Recover From

> "Officials advise residents to lock up all cheese puffs, lockers, and childhood dreams. The raccoons have entered their negotiation phase, and their demands are — quote — 'reasonable, if a little damp.'" — the morning anchor, while I sat there in one sock

I should note: **I do not own one sock.** Where is the other one? The raccoons know.

## My Evidence File

- One (1) missing sock. Status: presumably a hostage.
- A paw print on the sliding door that was *definitely* not there when I locked it. I checked twice.
- A trail of cheddar dust leading from the kitchen to the garden, where a single raccoon sat watching me like I was the entertainment portion of the evening.
- The popcorn bowl I left on the couch was rinsed. *Rinsed.* These animals have standards.

---

I called the number on the screen. It was the weatherman's direct line. He sounded tired. He said this is "the third one this week" and that I should "just go with it."

So tonight, I am paying the ransom: **one full-size block of cheese**, placed at the property line, no surveillance, no funny business. The TV says the exchange will be covered live at 10.

*This is satire. No raccoons were consulted, compensated, or crowned.*
