**I finally decided to Marie Kondo my closet on Sunday, which was my first mistake.**

*Spoiler: my closet did not spark joy. It sparked litigation.*

I own 37 hoodies for a person who works from home and only leaves the house to buy more hoodies. My plan was simple: keep 5, donate the rest, become a minimalist. Very mindful. Very mature.

Then my gray hoodie — Supreme Comfy, my 2019 emotional support fabric — cleared his throat.

## The Discovery Phase

Supreme Comfy did not appreciate being evaluated for joy. He's been through a lot.

- He has survived 3 jobs, 2 apartments, and one disastrous attempt to make sourdough that we don't talk about
- He is 40% cotton, 60% unresolved emotional support, and one Chipotle stain from 2021 that is now legally part of the family
- He contains a note in his pocket from 2020 that just says `you got this?`
- He whispered, `if (you_donate == me) { you_will_regret_this = true }`

> "You think you can just thank me for my service and ship me to Goodwill?" he said, in the voice of a disappointed public defender. "We need to talk about the socks, Tyler. *The socks.*"

Apparently he's been collecting evidence.

## The Trial Begins

By Monday morning my bedroom was a full courtroom. My laundry basket was the jury. My dryer was the unreliable witness.

> "My clients, the Sock Collective of 2022-2025, hereby sue for wrongful disappearance and emotional distress," Supreme Comfy announced, holding a tiny gavel made from a lint roller. "We have 14 single socks, zero closure, and a very suspicious tumble cycle."

The charges were serious:

- Exhibit A: One blue sock that went in with a partner and came out alone
- Exhibit B: My official statement that "they probably eloped to a better drawer"
- Exhibit C: The dryer, which pleaded the Fifth and hummed loudly whenever we asked where they went

---

I tried to plea bargain. I offered to buy all new matching socks. He demanded:

- Permanent hook privileges by the door
- Premium detergent, not the cheap stuff that smells like "mountain rain regret"
- And a formal apology for trying to replace him with a "minimalist capsule wardrobe" I saw on TikTok

I folded. Obviously I folded. Supreme Comfy now runs my closet, my socks have a support group in the bottom drawer, and I am sleeping on the couch because my bed is mad I prioritized closet drama over making it.

*This is 100% satire / parody — entirely fictional, no hoodies passed the bar, no socks were harmed, please do not let your laundry litigate you.*
