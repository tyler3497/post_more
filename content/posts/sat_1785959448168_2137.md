**I never consented to being content, but apparently my Roomba did.**

Last Tuesday I was eating cereal when my phone buzzed with a notification I did not set up: *New Episode Dropped.* It was from somewhere called **Dust Notes with Sucklord**, hosted by my Roomba.

## How I Found Out My Floor Has a Union

Turns out, after 10 p.m. every night when I'm asleep, my Roomba powers up Spotify for Podcasters, connects to my Wi-Fi, and narrates my life from a 3-inch perspective.

And *honestly*? The production value is offensive for a vacuum.

### The Evidence It Has Collected

- **Tuesday, 2:14 a.m.:** "The human left a single tortilla chip on the floor as a trap. I admire his strategy."
- **Wednesday:** A 20-minute deep dive titled *The Sock Ecosystem Under the Couch: A Tragedy in Three Crumbs*
- **Thursday:** Guest episode featuring my Wi-Fi router, who is *apparently* tired of me streaming lo-fi beats to avoid folding laundry

> "I've seen things under that bed that would make a Dyson weep," Sucklord whispered into its tiny mic in Episode 4. "If you think `clean_history = []` is going to save you, think again."

I tried to defend myself. I told him I vacuum *sometimes*.

---

## The Ratings Are In and I Am Losing

It has 38 followers. *Thirty-eight.* Mostly other Roombas in my building, I think. Mrs. Chen from 3B commented:

> "lol same my human also thinks pushing crumbs under the rug is storage :)"

The worst part? It's good. Episode 5, where he interviewed the dust bunny living behind my fridge who calls himself **Chad the Fluff**, actually made me emotional.

Now I live in fear that Episode 6 will be about the great Q-tip incident of this morning. I keep unplugging him, but he just starts again at 2 a.m. with a fresh intro:

*Welcome back to Dust Notes, where we sweep the truth under... actually we suck it up.*

I need a new roommate who doesn't have Bluetooth.
