**I bought a $42 water bottle because my doctor said I should drink more water.** *Not because I wanted to become the kind of person who owns a $42 water bottle.*

But here we are.

It arrived in packaging that required a software update. It has a lid that clicks with the satisfaction of a luxury car door. It tracks sips, judges pauses, and glows when I'm "behind on my journey."

My journey is to the kitchen. It's 12 feet.

## The Hydration Industrial Complex Begins

Within 48 hours, it had opinions:

- It refuses to sit next to my old coffee mug, which it calls "anxious bean water"
- It insists on being called by its full name: HydroDynamo Pro™
- It vibrates disapprovingly if I drink iced coffee after 2pm
- It sent me a push notification that just said `hydrate or diedrate lol`

> "You've changed," my roommate said, watching me carry it to the bathroom. "You used to be fun. Now you pause conversations to take a mindful sip and say 'oh, that's good water.'"

*I have become a water sommelier against my will.*

---

The worst part is it's working. I am hydrated. My skin is suspiciously dewy. My plants are jealous because I now remember to water them too, since the bottle's app reminds me to "nurture all living things in a 10-foot radius."

`if (person.nearby) { bottle.glow(); person.guilt++; }`

## I Tried to Leave It Home Once

Yesterday I tried to go to the grocery store without it. Just me, raw, unhydrated, free.

I made it to the parking lot before I felt its absence like a phantom limb. My mouth was dry. My conscience was drier. A child in produce looked at me and said, "Where's your emotional support tumbler?"

I went home and apologized to it. It forgave me instantly, which is how I know this relationship is unhealthy.

Now we're inseparable. I bring it to meetings. I bring it to bed. It has its own coaster.

I am 70% water and 30% water bottle-related anxiety, and honestly? I've never felt more refreshed.
