**I thought alphabetizing my spices would finally make me an adult.** *I was wrong, and now I am being deposed by a Hungarian pepper.*

Three weeks ago I decided my kitchen needed order. Not the *fake* order of shoving everything to the back, but real, librarian-core, Dewey-Decimal discipline.

## The Incident

I took everything down, wiped the shelf, and lined them up A to Z like good little soldiers. It felt powerful. It felt clean. It lasted 11 minutes.

- **Allspice** immediately demanded seniority it hasn't earned since 2019
- **Cumin** smugly parked next to Coriander and won't stop whispering
- **Paprika**, my sweet smoky queen, got placed next to **Parsley** and took it *personally*

> "You put me beside PARSLEY? Parsley? I have been smoked over oak! I have been to Budapest! She is literally green confetti!" — Paprika, via the intense vibe I got at 2am

I tried to mediate.

> "Babe, it's alphabetical," I said to a jar. Out loud. To a jar.

Paprika did not accept this.

---

## Legal Proceedings

By morning there was a tiny scroll taped to my fridge, written in what appears to be spilled turmeric:

```
charge: crimes against flavor hierarchy
plaintiff: Paprika (smoked, not sweet, CHECK YOUR NOTES)
demand: immediate relocation next to Cayenne + public apology
```

My other spices have taken sides. The `-` section is forming a union. Salt and Pepper, those ancient marrieds who should know better, are acting as mediators but keep saying stuff like *we've seen this before*.

**I have now been summoned** to my own kitchen floor at 7pm tonight for "restorative justice." The instructions say to bring `olive oil as a peace offering` and to wear non-staticky socks because Paprika is sensitive.

Honestly? Fair. I knew what I was doing when I put Nutmeg in charge.

I told myself organization would bring peace. Instead I have a fully litigious spice rack and Parsley is live-tweeting it.

`Status: kitchen 40% organized, 60% courtroom drama`
