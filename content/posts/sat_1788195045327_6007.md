**I did not ask for labor organization in my pantry.**

I was trying to be a functional adult. I watched one 11-minute YouTube video called *Get Your Life Together By Sorting Tiny Brown Jars* and decided my spice rack was a metaphor for my entire personality.

*Spoiler: the metaphor held up.*

## The Great Alphabetizing of 2026

For years my spices lived in beautiful anarchy. Paprika spooning Cumin, Oregano hiding behind whatever "Everything But The Bagel" actually is. It was cozy. It was chaos. It was family.

Then I did the unthinkable. I took everything out, wiped the shelf, and put them back A to Z like I pay taxes on time.

For seven whole minutes, I felt powerful.

Then Cumin cleared its tiny glass throat.

> "We need to talk," said Cumin, holding a protest sign the size of a postage stamp. "You moved me away from Coriander, put me next to Cream of Tartar, who does not *even* cook, and you expect me to just... season quietly?"

I told Cumin it was being dramatic.

*It had already printed flyers.*

### The Demands, Printed on a Bay Leaf:

- Immediate return to the *flavor neighborhood* system where Cumin lives next to Coriander and Chili
- An end to alphabetical oppression — "We are spices, not a Scrabble hand"
- A formal apology for storing me above the stove where it's 400 degrees and "emotionally hostile"
- One paid mental health day per month in the dark pantry where no one asks us to be aromatic

I tried to negotiate. I offered `spiceRack.sort({by: "vibes"})`.

Cumin countered with `unionize()`.

---

My roommate walked in while I was arguing with a 2-ounce jar and said, "Are you... collective bargaining with cumin?"

I said no. Cumin said:

> "He is now legally required to provide a cool, dry place and also listen to our podcast about workplace equity in small kitchens. Episode one is called *You Put The Good Salt Where?*"

The other spices have joined. Paprika is on strike. Turmeric is mediating but keeps staining everything. The good salt is, as usual, too good for this.

If you need me, I'll be in mediation on my kitchen counter at 7 p.m., bringing snacks to the very spices I'm supposed to be cooking with.

*SATIRE / PARODY — This did not happen. No spices were unionized, no cumin was harmed, and my spice rack is still alphabetized out of spite.*
