**It was supposed to be a listening session.** Just me, folding chairs, and twelve houseplants with mounting grievances. What I got instead was the most hostile civic gathering my living room has ever hosted, and that includes Thanksgiving 2019.

## How It Started

My therapist suggested I "practice accountability with things that depend on me." So I printed tiny agendas, arranged the pots in rows, and taped a handwritten sign to a stack of books:

> "PLANT TOWN HALL — Your Voice Matters (But Please Photosynthesize Responsibly)"

I thought the fern would be the peacemaker. I was wrong. The fern was the *ringleader*.

## The Grievances

Within ten minutes, the floor had descended into chaos:

- **The Pothos** demanded a formal apology for what it called "a two-week drought of 2024." I said it was three days. It said, "Your math is the reason we're here."
- **The Monstera** staged a slow, dramatic leaf unfurl and refused to explain itself. Classic power move.
- **The Succulents** claimed the whole process was rigged. "You watered *her* last," one of them said, pointing at the peace lily with a leaf. "We have *photographs*." They did not have photographs. They don't have cameras. *I checked.*

I tried to restore order with the standard facilitator line — *"Let's take this one leaf at a time"* — and the room went dead silent. One of the succulents later told me that joke "cost me three votes."

## The Aftermath

We reached a peace agreement at 11:47 PM. Key terms: **even water rotation**, no favoritism toward the fern, and the succulents get a recount committee.

The recount started this morning. The fern is already claiming interference.
