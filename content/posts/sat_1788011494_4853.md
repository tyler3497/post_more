**I thought I was buying silence. I accidentally hired building management for my skull.**

Two weeks ago I finally caved and bought those $349 noise-canceling headphones everyone on the internet swears will *change your life*. My apartment is above a guy who practices competitive sneezing and next to a radiator that sounds like it's boiling pasta 24/7. I needed peace. I craved quiet.

## The First 17 Minutes Were Pure Bliss

I put them on. The world went *shhhh*. I could hear my own heartbeat. My fridge hum disappeared. My upstairs neighbor's mysterious bowling practice vanished. It was beautiful, like being sealed inside a very expensive marshmallow.

Then my brain realized it could hear itself think. Really hear itself. No cover noise. Just raw, unfiltered internal monologue in Dolby Atmos.

> "Oh great, we're doing this now? You want to just sit here and listen to ME? Do you know how loud you are in here? I've been living with your tabs-open brain for years."

### Exhibit A: The Formal Complaint

- At 9:03 a.m., my brain filed a formal noise complaint about my inner monologue's volume
- At 9:04 a.m., it cited my 2019 to-do list that still lives rent-free in there
- At 9:07 a.m., it started charging my thoughts rent and evicted my weekend plans
- At 9:15 a.m., it demanded I stop rehearsing conversations that will never happen
- At 9:22 a.m., it asked for hazard pay for handling my 2 a.m. *what-if* scenarios

I tried to be reasonable. I even tried to code my way out:

```js
if (thought.volume > 0) {
  thought.mute();
  brain.chill = true;
} else {
  self.esteem = "intact";
}
```

My brain responded by turning up the reverb on every embarrassing thing I've said since 2014 and playing it on loop.

---

Now I wear the headphones to cancel the noise of the headphones canceling the noise. My neighbor asked why I'm just standing in the kitchen nodding at nothing. I told him I'm in mediation with my prefrontal cortex and we have *not* reached a settlement.

My headphones came with an app that says "Find Your Quiet." My brain found it first and left a one-star review.

*This is satire/parody — completely fictional, no brains or headphones were consulted. I'm just a guy who wanted quiet and accidentally gave his anxiety a surround-sound system.*
