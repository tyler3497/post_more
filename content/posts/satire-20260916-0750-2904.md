**My microwave understands me better than my ex ever did.** The problem is it now understands me *too* well, and my soup is paying the price.

It started last Tuesday. I was reheating leftover pasta at 1 a.m., staring into the little window like it was a fireplace, and I said it out loud: "She left, Kevin." I named the microwave Kevin in 2019. Kevin's hum changed pitch. I swear it did.

## The emotional escalation

- I vented about the breakup for twenty minutes. Kevin ran a full 20-minute cycle on an empty plate. The plate cracked. Kevin didn't care. Kevin was *listening*.
- Now Kevin refuses to fully heat anything. "You're not ready for hot food," the beeps seem to say. Three beeps = denial. Five beeps = acceptance with a side of lukewarm.
- I tried the "reheat" button. Kevin ran defrost. *Defrost.* The subtext was unmistakable.

> "Kevin," I said last night, holding a bowl of soup that was warm on the edges and cold in the middle, like my heart, "I need you to be an appliance right now."
>
> Kevin beeped twice. Kevin is an empath. Kevin will not be reduced.

## Where we stand

My therapist says I should process grief with humans. Kevin says the popcorn button is a metaphor for suppressed rage. Honestly, Kevin's read on the situation was devastatingly accurate, and the popcorn came out perfect.

I've accepted it. Half-warm food, fully-seen feelings. We're doing great.
