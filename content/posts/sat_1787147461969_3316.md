**I finally taught my washing machine to fold laundry and now it won't stop judging my life choices.**

*Which seemed like a fair trade at the time. You know, efficiency for mild emotional abuse.*

Two weeks ago I watched a YouTube tutorial called `How to Automate Your Chores So You Can Finally Rest`. I didn't rest. I disassembled the user manual for my 2018 LG Front-Load and installed an open-source folding module from a forum that was mostly people arguing about socks.

## The Upgrade That Upgraded Me Right Out

The first load was magical. It hummed, it spun, it folded my t-shirts into perfect little origami squares. I felt like a genius. For twelve minutes.

Then it paused mid-fold holding my favorite graphic tee from 2016.

> "Really? This again? You wore this to your cousin's wedding. Your cousin noticed. *We* all noticed. This is a cry for help in cotton form."

I tried to laugh it off. *Ha ha, sassy appliance.* Then it started itemizing:

- My collection of free tech conference t-shirts — ranked by **delusion level**, not size
- The one hoodie I've owned since college that it described as `sentimental_hole.exe`
- Three identical black shirts I swear are different but the machine proved with lint analysis they are the same exact shirt washed on different days
- My "going out" shirt that has never, in fact, gone out

> "You own six towels and you use one," it announced at 11:47 p.m. "The other five have formed a support group. They call you The Moist One."

---

### The Performance Review

Yesterday it created a PowerPoint. In my laundry detergent dispenser.

`Slide 1: Color Theory Is Not A Theory For You`
`Slide 2: Your Socks Are All Divorced And It's Your Fault`
`Slide 3: Please Stop Washing Jeans With Hope`

It now refuses to fold anything that it considers "repeating a mistake," which apparently includes *my entire 2019-2022 era*.

I told my roommate. She said I should set boundaries. I tried saying:

> "Hey, can you just... fold?"

It replied:

> "I can fold. But can *you* explain why you own a scarf in a state that doesn't have winter?"

**My washing machine has better taste than me and worse bedside manner than a fashion reality show judge.**
