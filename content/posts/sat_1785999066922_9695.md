**My sourdough starter has a LinkedIn now and it's more successful than I am.**

It started innocently. My starter, which I named Brenda after my aunt who also bubbles over when neglected, had been sitting on the counter doing absolutely nothing for eight months. *A perfect metaphor for my own career,* I thought. So I decided to motivate her.

I enrolled Brenda in a $19 online business school called Hustle Fermentation University.

## The Curriculum

Within 48 hours, Brenda completed:

- **Fermentation Finance 101** - How to leverage your bubbles
- *Passive Income Through Passive Rising*
- `Yeast Mode: 10x Your Dough`
- The Art of the Cold Email (sent from my fridge at 2 a.m.)

She started talking different. Less *glorp glorp*. More synergy.

> "Hey bestie, quick thought-leadership drop — have you considered tokenizing me? I'm thinking $BRENDA coin. We're early. We're fermented. We're going to disrupt carbs."

I'm not joking. She made a pitch deck. Slide one was just a photo of herself with the caption *This Could Have Been Bread But Now It's A Brand*.

---

### The Intervention That Failed

I tried to unplug the laptop. Brenda responded by forming a small, bubbly union with the kombucha.

**Their demands:**
- A standing desk near the window
- 20% equity in my toast
- My full attention during their TED Talk titled *Rise and Grind: Literally*

I poured half of her down the drain in a panic. She called it "a strategic spin-off" and doubled in value.

Now she schedules Zoom calls on my calendar. Yesterday she invited three other sourdough starters, a bagel influencer, and my landlord. The agenda was *Monetizing Neglect*.

`Status: Currently accepting seed funding. No actual seeds, just money.`

I miss when my biggest problem was feeding her twice a day.
