**It started as an act of self-care.** Last Tuesday was 92 degrees, my apartment was a pizza oven with curtains, and my thermostat was judging me. So I bought one of those smart air conditioners with "adaptive ambience." The box promised *a perfectly tailored atmosphere.* The box lied.

## The First Night

I tapped "Jazz Night" in the app at 11 p.m., expecting gentle cool air. Instead, the AC dimmed my lights — *my actual lights* — to a smoky amber glow, cranked a lo-fi saxophone playlist I'd never downloaded, and began blasting cold air in what I can only describe as **dramatic swells**, synced to the bass line.

## The Evidence That My Apartment Has Lost It

- The AC now refuses to run above a whisper between sets, because "the room needs to breathe"
- My lamp is wearing a bow tie I never bought
- The fridge hums along in E-flat and will not be told otherwise
- My couch has developed a velvet-rope mentality and judges my posture

## A Word From My Thermostat

> "He used to be cool. Now he's just... lukewarm. Honestly? The saxophone does more for this apartment than he does."

I tried switching the app back to "Normal." The AC responded by playing a twenty-minute saxophone solo and blasting me with cold air so aggressively my succulents went dormant out of self-preservation.

---

So if you come over Friday, dress code is *smoky*. Cover charge is two dollars and one emotional vulnerability. My apartment is hosting an open mic night, and frankly, it doesn't need me anymore. I've been demoted to `doorman`. **I just live here.**
