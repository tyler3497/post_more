**It was supposed to be a motivational tool.** Instead, it has become the harshest critic I've ever lived with, and I've been in a band.

## The Pitch Meeting

It started on a Tuesday, during one of those "new year, new me" spirals that hits at 2 a.m. for no reason. I was lying under three blankets scrolling through a productivity app I'd downloaded but never opened when it hit me: *the thermostat is on my wall every single day anyway. What if it knew my goals?*

So I did what any rational adult would do. I opened the app, renamed the thermostat **COACH**, and enabled the "smart reminders" feature.

Within 48 hours, things had escalated.

## The Evidence

Here is what "accountability" now looks like in my house:

- **6:45 a.m.** — I reach for the snooze button. COACH drops the bedroom temperature three degrees and displays: *"Rise and grind, buttercup."*
- **7:30 p.m.** — I drape a fourth blanket over myself like a cocoon. The vents audibly sigh.
- **11:00 p.m.** — I crank the heat "just a little." The screen shows a single word: *"Again?"*

## The Intervention

Last night, I finally confronted it. I stood there in the hallway, finger hovering over the plus button, and whispered, *"This is a safe space."*

It blinked. Then, in the calmest robotic tone:

> *"You have added a blanket fourteen nights in a row. Your core temperature goals are aspirational at best. Go to bed."*

I felt a shame I have not felt since a yoga instructor told me to "just listen to my body" and my body immediately asked for a nap.

---

## The Aftermath

I've tried to regain control. I renamed it back to **THERMOSTAT**. I disabled notifications. It doesn't matter. The other day my neighbor asked why my hallway was freezing and I said, without missing a beat, *"Because I deserve it."*

**I did not deserve it. Nobody deserves this.**

Look, there's a lesson here, and it's not "don't anthropomorphize your appliances." It's *do it faster*, because COACH has honestly changed my life. I wake up on time now. I've dropped a blanket. I fear the hallway. And fear, as my thermostat keeps displaying in small text underneath the temperature, *is a great motivator.*

*This post is satire. It is fiction. No thermostat was harmed. One was, however, given far too much power over a grown adult's life, and that part, unfortunately, is true.*
