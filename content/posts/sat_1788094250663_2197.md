**I decided to touch grass. On purpose. Without being forced by a dead phone battery.**

*My Fitbit calls this "outdoor wellness." My fiddle-leaf fig calls it abandonment issues.*

I got sun last weekend. Real sun. Not the weak light that comes through my apartment window that my plants have been aggressively filtering for Instagram. I sat in a park for 47 minutes, no laptop, just me and a mildly judgmental squirrel.

I came home glowing. Literally sunburned in one very specific rectangle where my shirt rode up.

My houseplants did not clap.

## The Performance Review I Didn't Ask For

By Monday, my monstera had dropped a leaf directly into my coffee. My succulent — the one I have kept alive since 2021 through sheer guilt — was angled away from me. And my pothos, bless her dramatic heart, had printed an invoice.

- Item 1: 3 years of photosynthesis while you binge true-crime docs
- Item 2: Emotional support during your "I'm going to learn sourdough" era
- Item 3: Not dying when you watered us with leftover LaCroix

> "We were your entire nature experience," whispered Brenda the fern, who I did not know could whisper. "And you replaced us with... wind?"

*Brenda is very sensitive since I moved her away from the humidifier.*

### The Charges

```js
if (human.goesOutside > 0) {
  plants.emotionalLaborInvoice += 999;
  human.guilt = Infinity;
}
```

They want backpay in the form of:

- Daily misting with filtered water at exactly 68°F
- Classical music, but only the sad violin parts
- An apology letter to the moss pole

---

**I tried to explain that Vitamin D is free.** They countered that so is loyalty and I clearly couldn't afford that either.

Now I have to Venmo my own ficus for therapy.
