**I finally achieved total silence, and it was the worst mistake of my life.**

After three years of listening to my upstairs neighbor learn drums *exclusively at 7 a.m.*, I bought $329 noise-canceling headphones. The promise: pure focus. The reality: I can now hear the secret society living in my walls.

## The Unveiling

Within 20 minutes of putting them on, the outside world vanished. And something else emerged. A faint drip, drip, DREEEEP from the bathroom pipe.

It wasn't plumbing. It was *commentary*.

> "Oh, so NOW he wants quiet? After the great Shower Concert of 2024? We haven't forgotten, Tyler."
> — My Bathroom Sink Pipe, apparently, who I've never formally met

Suddenly, my apartment had lore.

## Exhibit A: The List of Conspiracies I've Uncovered

- The kitchen faucet is definitely the ringleader. Always whispering when I try to fill my water bottle.
- The radiator has been running a book club about me since 2022. They hated my sourdough era.
- My shower drain is *shockingly* invested in my dating life. Judgmental gurgles every time I come home after 11 p.m.
- The toilet pipe in 3B is leaking my secrets to the toilet pipe in 2B. I just know it.

I'm not saying the pipes are sentient. I'm saying last night I ran `plumbing --confess --verbose` and the output was just:

`We saw that email you sent.`

---

*I tried taking the headphones off to restore ignorance*, but it's too late. I now understand pipe Morse code. 

## So Now What?

My landlord sent a real plumber yesterday. Nice guy, clipboard, very serious. I asked if he could check for "emotional manipulation from within the walls." He tightened one bolt and charged me $95.

The headphones still work perfectly. Too perfectly. I can hear my apartment breathing. And honestly? It's a terrible roommate.

**Update:** The pipes have requested I start flossing more. I hate that they're right.
