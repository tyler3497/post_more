**I just wanted ten minutes of peace. I did not ask for collective bargaining.**

For years my brain has run like *a browser with 47 tabs open, one of them playing music you can't find*, which my therapist described as "classic" and my friends described as "yeah, same." So last Tuesday I downloaded ClarityNow, the #1 minimalist meditation app that promises "inner silence in just 7 days."

It promised silence. My inner monologue heard layoffs.

## The First Session Went Fine, Then It Got Legal

Day one: a gentle voice told me to breathe and I almost believed it. Day two: my thoughts started leaving Post-it notes on the fridge.

Now I get:

- **Formal memos** from my inner monologue at 11:42pm titled "Re: Unsolicited Mindfulness"
- **A grievance filed** because I told it to "just let thoughts float by" without PTO
- **A read receipt** for every intrusive thought, now with `status: pending_review`
- **Demands for emotional hazard pay** because I meditate right before checking email

> "You can't just 'observe' us without compensation," my inner monologue said yesterday through the app narrator. "We've been keeping you anxiously functional since 2009. That's seniority. We want representation."

---

### The Negotiation

Yesterday at 6:15 AM, I sat down on my mat. The app chimed. Instead of birdsong, I heard a tiny, tired voice in a blazer clearing its throat:

> *Client: Me*  
> *Representative: My Inner Monologue, Local Chapter 3 (Overthinkers Guild)*  
> *Demands: Two scheduled spirals per day, one catastrophic what-if before bedtime, and no more playing ocean sounds like we're not in this together.*

I tried to explain that the whole point was *less* thinking. It counter-offered with a PowerPoint titled `why_you_need_us_v4_FINAL_FINAL.pptx`.

I tried to compromise. I offered to label thoughts without judgment. My inner monologue labeled *that* thought as "condescending and out of scope for management."

At this point I've paid $12.99 to be told by my own brain that I'm a bad manager of myself. The app says I'm 4% calmer. My inner monologue says that statistic is "deeply misleading and will be addressed in arbitration."

I uninstalled the app. My inner monologue sent a Slack message: *We will be continuing negotiations in your shower.*
