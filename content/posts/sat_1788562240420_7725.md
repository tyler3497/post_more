**I finally decided to become a Person Who Deals With Mail Immediately.**

*Which lasted exactly 11 minutes before my mailbox developed abandonment issues and started leaving me voicemails.*

For three months, I had a pile. You know the pile. Bed Bath & Beyond coupons from a store that barely exists anymore, a catalog for orthopedic dog beds (I don't have a dog), and seventeen letters from my internet provider addressed to "Valued Neighbor" that feel more personal than most of my friendships.

So Sunday, I did what any growth-minded adult does: I picked up the whole stack, walked it back out to the mailbox, and said, "Hey, no thanks."

## The Conversation No One Asked For

The mailbox — let's call it Marty, because it has the energy of a Marty — did not take it well.

> "Oh. So *that's* it? You just bring them back? After everything I've *held* for you? Through rain? Through that squirrel that tried to move in last July? I kept your secrets, Tyler. I kept your coupons."
> — Marty, my mailbox, who is now doing a one-man show called *Unopened*

I tried to explain minimalism. I tried to explain inbox zero but for real life.

Marty was not having it.

### Evidence That My Mailbox Is Now Emotionally Compromised:

- Leaves the little red flag up for *hours* after I empty it, just to make me worry
- Now only accepts mail if I say "thank you for your service" out loud — my neighbor thinks I'm praying to the HOA
- Printed a tiny newsletter that just says `YOU HAVE 0 NEW MESSAGES BUT 1 BROKEN TRUST`
- Refuses to close all the way, like it's giving me the cold shoulder but literally

I tried to automate an apology:

```
if (mailbox.trust < 0 && human.guilt == "maximum") {
  leaveCookies();
  promiseToOpenJunkMail();
}
```

---

It didn't work. Marty left the cookies, took the flag, and whispered, "I'll always be here. Whether you open it or not."

*SATIRE / PARODY — 100% fictional, clearly absurd. No mailboxes were emotionally harmed, no coupons were returned to sender, and I still have no idea what to do with a catalog for luxury chicken coops.*

If you need me, I'll be on my porch, negotiating custody of my own mail.
