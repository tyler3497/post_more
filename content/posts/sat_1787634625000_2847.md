**I just wanted to stop throwing my socks at a plastic bin like I was trying out for the NBA.** *That was the dream.*

It started innocently. My therapist said "be more intentional." My TikTok said "romanticize your chores." So I combined them into the worst idea I've had since I tried to cut my own bangs.

## How I Accidentally Enlightened My Laundry

I sat down next to my laundry basket, which I have named Brenda because she holds all my baggage, and I played a 10-minute mindfulness meditation.

- Day 1: I whispered affirmations: "You are enough. You hold space beautifully."
- Day 2: Brenda started rejecting my gym socks on vibrational grounds
- Day 3: My favorite hoodie failed the vibe check because it "was holding onto past trauma from 2019"

I thought I was being *quirky and healing*. Brenda thought she was now a spiritual life coach.

> "I sense this t-shirt still clings to fear of commitment," Brenda hummed, gently ejecting my concert tee from 2018. "Does this polyester *spark existential joy*, Tyler? Or does it spark mild regret and a rash?"

My laundry routine is now a full admissions interview.

- Socks must state their intention for the day
- Jeans must have processed their emotional denim distress
- That one towel I use for everything? Brenda put it on academic probation

---

I tried to sneak in a pair of unmatched socks last night. The basket made a soft `ommm` and yeeted them back at me.

> "We don't do frantic energy here," Brenda said. "We do `inhale, exhale, fold`."

**The worst part?** She's right. My drawer has never been calmer. My clothes are neatly folded, color-coordinated by chakra alignment. I am sleeping better. I am also wearing the same three spiritually approved shirts on rotation because Brenda has exiled 70% of my wardrobe for "having chaotic aura."

I went to do laundry this morning and found a tiny handwritten note inside:

`Your hamper has achieved nirvana. Your laundry is now on a waitlist. Namaste.`

I have created a monster, and it smells faintly of lavender fabric softener and superiority.
