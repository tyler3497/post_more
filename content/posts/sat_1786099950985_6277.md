**I didn't mean to create a housing crisis in my living room,** but apparently when you build a pillow fort at 11pm on a Tuesday, you are also founding a municipality.

It started innocently. I was bored, it was raining, and I had *seven* throw pillows that were just sitting there looking unemployed. I draped a blanket over two chairs, added fairy lights I definitely ordered during a previous bout of boredom, and crawled inside. It was cozy. It was whimsical. It was, for twelve minutes, a good decision.

## Then It Incorporated

By morning, the fort had formed a Homeowners Association.

> "Per Bylaw 4B, Section: Blankets," announced Sir Snugglesworth, a throw pillow I have now legally recognized as Board President, "the living room is now a separate and more desirable district. You are currently trespassing."

I laughed. Then I saw the tiny wooden sign hammered into my rug:

`Welcome to Fortlandia - Est. Last Night - HOA Fees Due Daily`

### The Terms Were... Extensive

The HOA handbook — written on the back of a pizza coupon in glitter pen — included:

- **No loud breathing** after 9pm inside Fortlandia airspace
- A 15% tax on all snacks entering the fort *(infrastructure costs, apparently)*
- Mandatory pillow-fluffing every two hours or a $5 fine payable in Goldfish crackers
- I must knock before entering my own fort and refer to it as *"The Enclave"*

I tried to disband it. I said, "I built you, I can destroy you," which is *exactly* what every villain says right before losing.

> "That's eminent domain slander," whispered my weighted blanket. "We'll see you in small claims."

---

### Current Status

I now sleep on the couch while paying rent to a structure made of my own bedding. The fort has installed a tiny welcome mat. It has better boundaries than I ever will.

`status: fort_rich • me_poor • cozy_level: 100%`

I asked if I could at least visit. They said applications open Monday.
