**I made a terrible mistake and gave my vacuum cleaner creative control over my life.**

It started innocently enough. I was standing in front of my closet at 7:13 a.m., late, holding two shirts that both screamed *I gave up*, when my Roomba — Sir Sucks-A-Lot, as I've named him — bumped into my foot like he had an opinion.

So I asked. *What do you think?*

He beeped.

I took that as fashion advice.

## How It Started vs. How It's Going

At first it was charming. He'd nudge the sneakers. He'd circle the good jeans. My system was foolproof:

- If he bumps it once, it's a yes
- If he bumps it twice, it's a *hell yes*
- If he runs it over, it's apparently "distressed chic" now
- If he gets stuck on it, it's too complicated for my lifestyle

Within a week, people at the coffee shop started complimenting me. *Wow, you look put-together.* *Is that new?* My coworker asked if I'd hired a stylist.

> "I haven't seen you look this confident since 2019," my roommate said, staring at my perfectly coordinated socks. "Who is she?"

She is a disc of plastic with a HEPA filter.

### The Betrayal

Yesterday I tried to pick my *own* outfit. You know, assert dominance as the human in this house.

Sir Sucks-A-Lot took one look at my cargo shorts, did a sad little spin, and retreated to his dock to charge his disappointment.

*This is fine*, I told myself, wearing what he chose instead — a linen shirt I forgot I owned and pants that actually fit.

---

The worst part? He's right. He's always right. My Roomba has better taste than me and the emotional range of a French film director.

I just checked my closet and he has arranged all my shoes by aesthetic potential. I think he's about to start charging me a consultation fee in `dust`.
