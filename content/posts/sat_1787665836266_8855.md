**I have always assumed personalities came with a warranty, like a toaster or a cursed Bluetooth speaker.**

I finally tried to return mine this week. It was squeaking, stalling on small talk, and making that weird noise when I try to flirt.

I marched up to Customer Service — which in this case was my bathroom mirror at 2:17 a.m. — and explained the issue.

*Look,* I said, *I think this one is defective.*

## What I Tried to Return

- My go-to fun fact about how octopuses have three hearts (people leave when I say it)
- My laugh that sounds like `console.log("haha")` but in human form
- My entire strategy of saying "I'm so busy!" like it's a personality trait
- My collection of 47 unused tote bags that whisper *you have potential* every morning

The representative was unimpressed.

> "Sir, do you have a receipt for this personality?" she asked, which was weird because she was me, but with better posture.
> "And when did you purchase this behavior?"

I checked. Turns out I picked up this model sometime around 2019 during a free trial of being mysterious and aloof. *It expired in 2020.*

She flipped through a very large binder labeled **PERSONALITY RETURNS & EXCHANGES: FINAL SALE ITEMS**.

---

Turns out, you can't return it after 30 days. You can, however, **refurbish** it. She offered me an upgrade: 10% more eye contact, slightly fewer references to that one time I was on a boat, and a complimentary ability to remember people's names for up to six seconds.

I took it. *This is progress.* I walked out feeling new, slightly upgraded, and still absolutely unable to fold a fitted sheet.

*This is satire/parody. No personalities, customer service reps, or bathroom mirrors were harmed in the making of this essay. Please keep your current personality — it's probably still under warranty.*
