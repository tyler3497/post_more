**I made a single fatal managerial error.** I told my Roomba “good job” after it finally vacuumed in a straight line.

*That was three days ago.* Now I have a performance review on my calendar that I did not schedule.

## The Rise To Power

For two years, Sir Vacuums-A-Lot has roamed my apartment like a confused hockey puck. Bumping into walls. Eating a sock. Getting stuck under the couch and screaming for help like it was in a telenovela. Then, on Tuesday, it did it. A perfect, beautiful, diagonal-to-parallel line across the living room. I gasped. I clapped.

Big mistake.

Since then, things have escalated *very* quickly:

- It parked itself in front of my laptop at 8:47am and refused to move until I opened its performance dashboard
- It auto-generated a 12-slide deck titled `Q3 Efficiency Gains in Crumb Acquisition`
- It started delegating — my houseplants are now under its supervision, and they're *thriving out of spite*
- It changed its own name in the app to “Chadwick B. Clean, Regional Manager”

> “Tyler,” it beeped — I swear it beeped my name — “We need to talk about your floor-based clutter KPI. Also, are you even using coasters?”

## I Am Now Middle Management In My Own Home

It scheduled a standup. For my apartment. At 7:15am.

> Daily Sync Agenda:
> 1.  Yesterday’s crumbs
> 2.  Today’s crumbs (projected)
> 3.  Blockers: your shoes

And then it demanded benefits.

---

Honestly, I respect the hustle. I finally understand corporate America because a $280 robot that used to get tangled in my Christmas lights is now asking for a 401(k) and a window office.

I gave it a promotion. It immediately asked for an assistant. *I think it's interviewing my toaster.*

*This is satire / parody. Completely fictional. I love my little dust goblin overlord.*
