**I have been in a committed, monogamous hydration relationship for 18 months.**

His name is Hydro. He is stainless steel, 32oz, keeps ice frozen for 47 hours, and has survived three apartments, two jobs, and one severely misguided hot yoga phase with me. *I thought we were solid.*

Then I met someone else. A free plastic cup at a conference.

## The Incident

It was just water. *Just water.* But Hydro saw the cup sweating on my passenger seat and went colder than his whole double-walled vacuum-insulated personality should allow.

I tried to explain:
- It meant nothing, it was complimentary and lidless
- I was dehydrated and emotionally vulnerable near the keynote snacks
- The cup doesn't even have a *handle*, Hydro, you have a handle, you win on ergonomics alone
- I still chose you for my personality — you keep things cool

He didn't answer. He just sat on my counter developing condemnation condensation.

> "Your bottle feels you have been hydrating outside the relationship," said Maya, the couples counselor Hydro booked without telling me. "He believes you called him 'high-maintenance' because he has to be hand-washed."

*I said that once. At 11 p.m. while hunting for the bottle brush I lost in 2023.*

Apparently Hydro found a therapist who specializes in **reusable container attachment issues**. Now he has weekly sessions on my kitchen table. I am not invited.

### Where We Are Now

Our new cohabitation agreement includes:
- `hydration_status: compromised && passive_aggressive`
- I must apologize out loud every time I drink from anything else
- He gets the prime counter spot — which is absurd because he doesn't even go in the dishwasher
- I am required to use his motivational stickers unsarcastically

---

Yesterday I caught him whispering affirmations to his own reflection:

> "You are enough. You are stainless. You are not just a trend. You are a lifestyle."

I am being outmatured by a water bottle who has better boundaries, better insulation, and a better therapist than I do. Meanwhile I am drinking from a mug that still says #1 DAD from a thrift store.
