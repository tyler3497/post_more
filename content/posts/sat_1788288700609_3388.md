**I was just trying to find the remote.** *That's it.* That was the whole mission, and somehow I ended up committing a geopolitical incident in my living room.

For six months, I've told myself the dust bunnies under my couch were "insulation." Last Tuesday, I finally moved it. Underneath was not dust. It was infrastructure.

## Welcome to Couchtopia

A fully functioning civilization built from lost Cheerios, LEGO bricks, and what I can only assume was a single ambitious sock. Tiny string lights made from Christmas-light clippings. A parliament. A flag. They had a tiny artisanal coffee shop called *Grounds Under Grounds*.

And they had opinions. Oh, did they have opinions.

### Evidence They Are More Together Than I Am:

- Their mayor, a bottle cap named Cheryl, has a 5-year plan. I have a 5-minute plan that involves staring into the fridge
- They recycled my lost AirPod into a town bell. I recycled a pizza box once and felt like a hero
- They implemented free healthcare using Band-Aid wrappers. I still WebMD a papercut
- They built a library out of fortune cookie fortunes. It's better curated than my bookshelf

> "We watched you try to microwave a burrito for 45 minutes on defrost," said Mayor Cheryl, visibly disappointed. "We held an emergency town meeting about it. The vote was unanimous: you need supervision."

I tried to explain it was a *learning experience*.

*I can't move the couch back.* They've unionized, they're thriving, and honestly their property values are higher than mine.
