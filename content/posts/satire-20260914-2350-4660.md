**It started with an innocent calendar invite.**

Tuesday, 2:00 PM. Location: my apartment. Attendees: me. Subject: *"Synergy Sync — Self."* I don't know what "synergy" means in the context of one person sitting on a couch, but corporate had rotted my brain just enough that I felt *guilty* existing without a purpose, so I invited myself to a meeting. I even accepted the invite from my own inbox. I replied **"Looking forward to connecting!"** to me. It was weirdly cordial.

## The meeting began on time, which was already a problem

I arrived at 2:00 sharp, which meant I was early, which meant I had to make small talk with me. I asked myself how my weekend was. I said "busy." I pressed for details. I stonewalled myself. *Tension in the room was palpable.*

Then I made the fatal mistake: **I wrote an agenda.**

- Item 1: Discuss outstanding tasks (owed to: me, due: me, status: overdue)
- Item 2: Park the existential dread for next quarter
- Item 3: Action items

Item 3 is where it all fell apart. I assigned myself five action items with hard deadlines, and before I could object, **I agreed.** I literally watched myself commit to things I did not want to do, and I nodded along like a coward.

> "I appreciate you flagging that," I said to me. "I'll circle back with myself offline."

That's when I realized I was trapped.

## I tried to leave early and was denied

At 2:12 I said I had a hard stop. I told myself I needed a note excusing me. I told myself I couldn't just write my own note, *that defeats the whole system.* I appealed. I was both the employee and HR, and HR was unsympathetic.

---

It's now Thursday. The meeting is still going. I ordered snacks. I expense-reported them to myself and **I denied the claim**, which felt petty even by my standards. If anyone reading this knows how to cancel a meeting you are currently attending with yourself, please — my calendar says I'm free at 3:30, and I desperately need me to hold.
