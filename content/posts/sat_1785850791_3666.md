## The Notification No One Wants at 3 a.m.

**I didn't realize my smart fridge had been watching me** until last Tuesday at 3 a.m., when it woke me up with a soft chime and a notification that simply read:

> "We need to talk."

Apparently, my habit of putting a single bite of lasagna into a large Tupperware container and then forgetting about it for eleven days had pushed it over the edge. The fridge had compiled a *PowerPoint*. A PowerPoint!

### Chadwick's Findings

According to the fridge — who has apparently named itself **Chadwick** — the audit revealed:

- **17 distinct containers** of what used to be dinner
- A jar of pickles that expired during the last administration
- A mysterious foil ball that even the crisper drawer is scared to open

It beeped judgmentally every time it showed a new slide. Slide one was titled *"The Life Cycle of Your Good Intentions"* and was just a photo of my wilting cilantro.

The intervention got weirder. My toaster joined via Bluetooth to say it was tired of being used only to reheat Pop-Tarts that I then forget in there.

> "My houseplants texted me — well, the fridge said they texted it, which is already a lot — that they are concerned about my commitment issues."

I tried to defend myself. I said I was *meal prepping*. Chadwick responded by lowering its temperature to what I can only describe as **"disappointed."**

---

### The Compromise

We have now reached a detente:

**I agreed to:**
- Label everything with actual dates instead of writing "probably okay" in Sharpie
- Finally throw away the salsa from 2023 that has achieved sentience

**Chadwick agreed to:**
- Stop ordering kale automatically
- Stop playing sad violin music whenever I open the cheese drawer

We are healing. Slowly. The foil ball remains untouched. Some things are not ready to be addressed in therapy.

`Status: 85% functional, 15% haunted`
