**It started with good intentions**, which is how most household dictatorships start.

Last Sunday I bought a label maker for $19.99 because my spice drawer looked like a crime scene. By Tuesday, my house had become a full performance review.

## The Labeling Incident

At first it was normal. *Very normal.* I labeled the flour, the sugar, the mysterious beige jar from October that nobody claims.

Then the label maker got ambitious. Or I did. Honestly hard to tell.

- My toaster now reads: `TOASTER (Single Task Specialist, Works Under Pressure, Resents You)`
- My houseplant: **FERN (Freelance Oxygen Intern, Will Die Dramatically For Attention)**
- My couch: *COUCH (Emotional Support Platform, Full-Time, No Benefits)*
- My junk drawer is now officially **THE VOID (Miscellaneous Dept., We Do Not Speak Of Its Contents)**

### Management Intervenes

My roommate walked in, stared at the bathroom mirror which now says **MIRROR (Middle Management, Reflects Poor Life Choices)**, and said:

> "Okay, I support your organizational journey, but why does the toilet lid say 'LID (CEO of Containment, Closes Deals And Also This)'?"

I told her it was about *accountability*. That was unhelpful.

My fridge now has a label FOR the labels that says `LABELS (Human Resources, Overstaffed And Emotional)`.

---

### Where We Are Now

I tried to remove one label. Just one. The one on my laptop that says **LAPTOP (Anxiety Rectangle, Runs Hot, So Do You)**.

The label maker made a noise I can only describe as *judgmental whirring* and printed:

`MISTAKE (See Also: Owner)`

We're not taking it off. The system works. Somewhere, my spice drawer is finally at peace, alphabetized and moisturized and silently judging my oregano expiration dates.

`status: 92% labeled, 8% in witness protection`
