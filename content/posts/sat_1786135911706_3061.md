## It Started as an Efficiency Initiative

**I thought I was being a good plant parent** when I made a group chat for my seven houseplants. Just a little *organizational tool* so I could remember who needed water when.

I named it "Photosynthesis Pals 🌱" and added them all: Fern, Kevin the Cactus, Patricia the Pothos, and four succulents I collectively call The Succulent Boys.

The first message was from me:

> "Good morning my leafy legends! Who needs water today? 💧"

Patricia replied instantly — or rather, I had given Patricia a very judgy AI voice via a smart plug app that lets you assign personalities. This was my first mistake.

### The Group Chat Quickly Deteriorated

According to the chat logs I definitely did not consent to being this detailed:

- **Fern (8:03 AM):** dusting. still dusty. we are still dusty
- **Kevin (8:04 AM):** `sunlight.exe not found`
- **Patricia (8:07 AM):** Tyler, we need to talk about your definition of "indirect light"
- **Succulent Boy #3 (8:09 AM):** we are forming a union

I tried to be supportive. I said *"You guys are thriving!"*

Patricia responded:

> "Thriving is a strong word for what Kevin is doing. Kevin is *surviving on spite and a single ice cube from March.*"

---

### The Escape Plan

By Tuesday they had a shared doc. A literal shared doc titled `Operation: Windowsill Freedom`.

**Their demands were:**

- Actual potting soil instead of whatever discount compost I got because it had a cool bag
- To stop playing lo-fi beats to photosynthesize to on loop for 9 hours
- Immediate relocation of Kevin away from the air vent that he claims is "a wind tunnel designed by Big HVAC to break his spirit"

I told them I was doing my best. Kevin sent back a single cactus emoji and then went silent for 14 hours, which for a cactus is basically a hunger strike.

The final straw was this morning. I woke up to 47 notifications and a message pinned by Patricia:

> "We have voted. We are leaving. We have contacted the nice lady downstairs who actually knows how to propagate."

*I have never been roasted by my own pothos before.* I pay rent here. They live for free and have the audacity to have better Wi-Fi reception than my laptop.
