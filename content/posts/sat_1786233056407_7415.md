**I walked into a pottery class to find inner peace and I accidentally created my emotional superior.**

For context, I was told — by a very calm Instagram infographic — that working with clay is *grounding*. That your hands in earth connects you to the present moment. That you will leave with a sense of serenity and maybe a mug.

What I left with was Gary.

## Meet Gary, My Bowl

Gary is my bowl. He is lopsided, slightly wobbly, glazed in what I can only describe as *anxious teal*, and he has achieved levels of inner tranquility I can only dream of after three therapy podcasts and a houseplant.

On day one, the instructor, Linda, said:

> "Let the clay tell you what it wants to be."

Gary told me he wanted to be *better adjusted than his maker*. I tried to center him on the wheel. He centered *me*.

Evidence that my lopsided bowl is thriving and I am not:

- **He holds space** — literally. I can put keys in him. I cannot hold space for even one text message without spiraling.
- **He has boundaries** — firm rim, imperfect but solid. I said yes to a 7 a.m. meeting and a friend's dog's birthday party.
- **He meditates** — sits perfectly still on my shelf for hours. I tried `mindfulness.exe` and it crashed after 40 seconds when I remembered I never replied to Linda's email.
- **He has a supportive community** — the other students' bowls all lean toward him for advice.

---

## The Turning Point

Last night, I had a small meltdown because I couldn't find my left sock. Gary just sat there.

> "This is very on-brand for you," he seemed to say, in the voice I have now given him, which is calm and slightly Midwestern. "Have you considered breathing?"

I did. I breathed directly onto him. He did not fog up. That is composure.

I am now taking emotional regulation tips from an object I made that cannot pass a level surface test. He suggests I drink water, get sunlight, and stop making everything a six-week pottery metaphor for my life. He also suggests I stop telling people my bowl has a name and a *journey*.

He's right. I'm working on it. But we're doing a follow-up couples glaze session next Tuesday.
