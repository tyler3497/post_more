**I just wanted fewer emails about artisanal cheese.**

*That's it.* One fewer cheese opinion per week. I clicked unsubscribe on a newsletter called *The Curd Nerd: Weekly Whey-isms* and my inbox immediately went into full romantic melodrama mode.

I didn't mean to ghost anyone. I just thought, *maybe I don't need to know about brie trends at 6:47 a.m. every Tuesday.*

## When You Click Unsubscribe and Trigger a Breakup Arc

Within seconds, my inbox rearranged itself like it was packing an emotional suitcase:

- A pop-up that said **"Are you sure? We really thought we had something special"** with a crying emoji made of cheddar
- Then a follow-up email titled `We Miss You Already (and So Does Gouda)` *seven minutes later*
- Then a third email from the same sender that just said `fine.` in the subject line

My actual inbox got jealous too.

> "Oh, so you'll unsubscribe from *him* but you'll keep 4,732 unread promo emails from a mattress company you googled once in 2021?" whispered Outlook, in a tone that was *deeply* personal.

I tried to explain it was boundaries. Outlook responded by moving all my important emails to Promotions.

### The Rebound Phase

I have now entered what experts call `attachment_loop = true`:

- I keep getting **"We noticed you left"** coupons for 10% off a cheese I already said I didn't want
- My spam folder organized a candlelight vigil for the newsletter
- My inbox re-sorted my bills under a new folder labeled *Things You'll Also Abandon*

---

I tried to apologize. I re-subscribed for exactly 11 seconds and immediately got:

> "Welcome BACK! We knew you'd come crawling back for the mouthfeel!"

No. Absolutely not.

My current status is `mutual_unsubscribe_pending` and my inbox now requires a formal written apology every time I delete anything. We are in counseling with my browser history as a mediator.
