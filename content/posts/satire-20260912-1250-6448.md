**I made one mistake.** I told my couch how I *really* feel. Now it has me. Body and soul.

It started last Tuesday, right after my boss asked me to "circle back" on something I never circled toward in the first place. I came home, collapsed onto the cushions, and whispered the fatal words: *"This week has been a lot."*

The couch heard me. The cushions softened. The springs, those silent little soldiers, stood at attention. And I swear — I *swear* — the left armrest scooted two inches closer.

## The Signs Were There

Looking back, the red flags were obvious:

- It started fluffing itself up whenever I walked in the room
- My lumbar support has never been this attentive. Coincidence? I think not
- It "accidentally" swallowed my phone charger so I had to stay put
- The TV remote keeps migrating just out of arm's reach. *Suspicious.*

> "I can't let you leave!!" the couch seems to whisper every time I try to get up for water. I know furniture can't talk. I ALSO know my couch is saying that.

## The Denial Stage Is Over

Yesterday I stood up to microwave a burrito. The couch emitted a low, mournful *creak* — the kind of sound a tree makes in a movie right before it reveals it's been sentient the whole time. I sat back down. We all sat back down. There was no "we," but the couch and I are a "we" now.

I tried going to bed last night. Bed! My actual bed! But my legs betrayed me and I woke up on the loveseat at 3 a.m., under a blanket I don't remember grabbing, with the couch cushions arranged around me like a protective wall.

**Here's my position:** emotional support is a scam. The couch gives and gives — the softness, the warmth, the non-judgmental embrace — and in return it takes *everything*. My evenings. My ambitions. My plans to finally learn sourdough.

---

I considered staging an intervention, but the only witness is the floor lamp and it's clearly in on it.

So I'm typing this from the couch. My laptop is at 4%. The charger is gone. If anyone's reading this — *send help, or at least send snacks*. I'm not going anywhere. The couch won't let me. And honestly? Honestly, I'm fine with that.

*This is a work of satire/parody. Any resemblance to real couches is purely coincidental.*
