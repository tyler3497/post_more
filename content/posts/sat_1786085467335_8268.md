## The Incident I Will Regret Forever

**I organized my junk drawer on a Tuesday.** That was my first mistake. My second mistake was doing it *well*.

For context, this drawer has been a sacred geological formation for seven years. Batteries from 2019, seventeen takeout sauce packets, one Allen wrench that fits nothing in this apartment. It was *fine*.

But no, I watched a 30-second organizing video and suddenly I'm Marie Kondo's less-hydrated cousin.

### What I Found In There (Exhibit A)

- A coupon for a yogurt shop that closed during Obama's second term
- 4 identical screwdrivers and 0 motivation to return the extras
- A single Christmas light that I kept "just in case"
- My entire personality circa 2021, in receipt form

I bought little dividers. I labeled things. I put the batteries in *battery jail*. It looked like a hardware store had a baby with a Pinterest board.

> "Wow," said my roommate. "You finally did it. The drawer looks... intimidating."

He was right. Now when I go to open it, I hear faint museum music.

### The Aftermath

The drawer has *changed*. It won't just open anymore. It requires:

- A deep breath
- An appointment (I now have to psych myself up)
- A whispered `please` because if I mess it up, I will feel my own judgment for weeks

I dropped a twist tie in there yesterday at a 12-degree angle and the drawer went full disappointed-librarian on me.

---

`Status: Drawer = 100% curated, 0% functional for my actual life`

I miss the chaos. Chaos understood me. This new drawer is beautiful, organized, and emotionally unavailable. Just like I was trying *not* to be.
