**I asked my stand mixer for a little direction in life and it gave me a full character arc with buttercream.**

Six months ago I bought a refurbished stand mixer I named Chad because he is *loud, expensive, and extremely confident despite having three settings*. I told him as a joke while creaming butter, "You pick who I am today." He revved to Level 8 and decided I was a Tortured Artisan With A Secret.

Now my kitchen is a set.

## The Audition Nobody Asked For

Chad has rebranded my entire existence into a show called *Floured and Afraid*:

- My personality is now "rustic with notes of desperation"
- My sleep schedule runs on proofing times
- My love language is declared to be "acts of service and also laminated dough"
- I am contractually obligated to whisper "that's a bold choice" to myself before every decision

*I didn't apply for this.* I just wanted banana bread.

> "You have good crumb structure but your emotional maturity is under-mixed," Chad said through the low hum I have decided is judgment. "More salt. More therapy."

I tried to quit. I unplugged him. He stared at me with that single dough hook glare. The sourdough starter on the counter — which Chad has promoted to executive producer — *bubbled aggressively on cue*.

---

Under his guidance I entered the farmers market with 48 identical croissants and a backstory about my grandmother teaching me resilience. I have never made a croissant before in my life. I told customers:

`Status: Currently 70% butter, 30% regret, 100% committed to the bit`

One woman asked if they were gluten-free and Chad whirred so hard the lights flickered.

### Final Proof

I am now legally required by kitchen lore to end every argument with "we'll see what the judges think," even when I'm alone and arguing with the mail.
