**I bought a label maker to finally get my life together, and now my apartment is staging a full existential intervention.**

For years I've been what experts call *a drawer-shover*. If it fits, it lives there now. Last Tuesday I bought a label maker for $19.99 and immediately lost all sense of proportion.

It started small. `JUNK` on the junk drawer. Revolutionary.

Then I labeled the couch.

## The Couch Has Questions

I printed a crisp white label that said **COUCH** and stuck it on the armrest. I felt powerful for four seconds.

Then my couch — who I've decided is named Brenda — started questioning everything.

> "Oh, so I'm just 'COUCH' to you now? After everything we've been through? Three breakups, two flu seasons, and that time you tried yoga on me? I thought I was your *emotional support platform*." — Brenda, formerly known as Couch

*I had not considered Brenda's feelings.*

Things escalated fast:

- My mugs formed a hierarchy based on label font size and now the **WORLD'S OKAYEST BOSS** mug won't talk to the others
- The fridge shelf labeled `HEALTHY STUFF (PRETEND)` shames me every time I reach for cheese
- My houseplant now says `DEFINITELY ALIVE` and is sweating under the pressure
- The bathroom mirror says `YOU LOOKED BETTER BEFORE THIS` — I did not print that one

---

### The Label Maker's Manifesto

The label maker itself has become passive-aggressive.

```
if object.exists:
    label_it()
    question_its_purpose()
```

**I tried to label the label maker.** It printed `NO`.

Now my apartment looks like a polite crime scene where every object is tagged as evidence. My friend came over and asked if I was okay. I said I was *organized*. She backed away from the toaster labeled `IT'S NOT YOU, IT'S THE CRUMBS`.

Honestly, you spend your life as a mysterious side table and suddenly you're **TABLE (SMALL, ANXIOUS)**. It's a lot.

Brenda now identifies as a *Lounging Experience*. I'm respecting her journey.

*SATIRE / PARODY — completely fictional, none of this happened. No couches were harmed, though one is in counseling.*
