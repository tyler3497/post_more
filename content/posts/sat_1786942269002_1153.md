**I finally invested in noise-canceling headphones and now I can hear my own thoughts at unsafe volumes.**

*Which was not included in the product description, by the way.* The Amazon listing said "block out the world." It did not say the world would be replaced by a live feed of my brain at 3 a.m. doing karaoke.

I bought them to survive my apartment building — you know, normal neighbor stuff like someone learning drums at midnight. I put them on, pressed the sacred ANC button, and achieved **true silence** for the first time since 2019.

Then my brain walked in like:

## My Brain's Official Complaints to Building Management

Turns out with no leaf blowers to blame, my brain has notes:

- I replay the same two seconds of every conversation I've ever had, but *remixed* for maximum cringe
- I narrate putting on socks like I'm hosting a nature documentary: "And here he attempts the left sock again"
- I have a rotating cast of 40 unfinished to-do lists that just scream "hey!" from the kitchen
- I compose full apology emails to people I haven't talked to since high school

> "Sir," my brain said, in the tone of a very tired HOA president, "we received 47 complaints last night. All of them were you, about you. We're going to have to issue a citation."

I tried to negotiate. I told it:

`brain.volume = Math.min(brain.volume, headphones.anc_level * 0.2)`

It did not compile.

---

Look, I get it. My thoughts have been waiting years for an audience without traffic noise. Now I've given them **Dolby Atmos, studio isolation, and a captive listener** who literally cannot hear an excuse to stop.

My roommate asked how I like the headphones. I whispered:

> "They're great. I can finally hear myself think. Please make it stop."

*SATIRE / PARODY — entirely fictional, no brains or headphones were harmed, please actually wear ear protection when operating a leaf blower.*
