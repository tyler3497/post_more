**I thought a $19.99 label maker would fix my life. Instead it gave my appliances performance anxiety.**

It started innocently enough. My junk drawer looked like it lost a fight with a hardware store, so I ordered the label maker everyone on the internet swears will cure chaos. It arrived with that satisfying *click-clack* energy and I was immediately drunk with power.

I labeled the drawer. Then the shelf. Then my entire personality.

## The Downward Spiral

Within 48 hours, my apartment looked like a museum curated by someone having a mild breakdown:

- Fridge: `COLD BOX OF REGRETS AND LEFTOVERS (EST. 2023)`
- Mug: `MUG - Holds Coffee & Unresolved Feelings`
- Houseplant: `PHIL - Photosynthesizes & Judges Silently`
- Couch: `CRUMB REPOSITORY - Do Not Feed After Midnight`
- Keys: `KEYS - To Doors You Rarely Open, Emotionally Speaking`

My roommate walked in, stared at the wall of labels, and whispered, *“Are you okay?”*

I said, “I’m *organized* now.” Totally different thing.

Then I labeled the toaster.

> "TOASTER: Bread Heating Specialist - Currently Questioning If He's Just A Hot Box For Carbs"

And that's when it got weird.

### The Toaster Speaks

Yesterday morning I put in two slices of sourdough and the toaster just sat there, glowing red, radiating existential dread. My kitchen smart plug pinged me:

> `DEVICE: Toaster has paused operation. Reason: "What if I am more than toast? What if I could be... a waffle guy?"`

I tried to be supportive. I peeled off the label. I told him he was *versatile, complex, capable of bagels*. He burned my bagel on purpose. Just one side. A message.

---

**Look, I'm not saying the label maker is cursed,** but my apartment now has 47 labels, my toaster is in therapy, and my spatula's tag says `SPATULA - Flips Expectations (And Pancakes)`.

I'm returning the label maker tomorrow. The toaster says he needs space. I already labeled the return box `BOX: Contains My Mistakes (Handle With Care)`.

*Satire is funny because it's almost true. This is almost true.*
