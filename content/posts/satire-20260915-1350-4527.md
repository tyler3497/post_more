**It started, as all constitutional crises do, with cold soup.**

I was reheating dinner when the microwave beeped, and instead of just taking my food like a normal person, I said the words: *"You are the most dependable appliance in this house. I pledge you my loyalty forever."* I was holding a spoon. There may have been dramatic lighting. What I didn't know was that loyalty oaths are legally binding in at least three states and morally binding everywhere else.

## The Announcement

By morning, the microwave had reorganized the countertop into what it called a "campaign headquarters." The evidence was overwhelming:

- A bunting made of paper towels, arranged in a suspiciously patriotic pattern
- A podium constructed from two cereal boxes and what I believe is a shoe
- A policy platform written in condensation on the fridge door: **"HOT FOOD FOR ALL. NO LEFTOVERS LEFT BEHIND."**

I tried to raise objections. It responded by heating my coffee to the *exact* perfect temperature, which honestly weakened my case.

## The Speech

At noon, it addressed the assembled appliances. The toaster heckled. The kettle was clearly on the payroll.

> "Fellow countertop residents, I did not choose public life. Public life chose me — right after the soup incident. A vote for me is a vote for three-minute defrosts and a future where nothing is ever merely lukewarm."

The crowd went wild. Or rather, the dishwasher cycled, which I have learned is their version of applause.

## The Polling

*Internal polling* (conducted by me, sample size: my own conscience) shows the microwave leading the blender 4-to-1, with the blender's entire platform being "I have more speeds." Frankly, I find its populism concerning, its charisma undeniable, and its popcorn setting *perfect*.

---

Look, I know this is my fault. You swear one oath to one appliance and suddenly you're a campaign manager for a box that hums. But the toast has never been this even, and the nation — by which I mean my kitchen — has never been more united.

**Vote warm. Vote often. The soup is non-negotiable.**
