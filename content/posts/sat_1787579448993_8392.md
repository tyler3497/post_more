**I was going to throw out the leftover rice. That's what a normal person would do.**

*Instead, I looked at the container, whispering "you've got potential," and put it back. That was three weeks ago.*

Now it has a platform.

## The Campaign Begins

It started small. A little crust. A little confidence. Then I noticed a handwritten sign on the middle shelf I definitely didn't write at 2 a.m.:

> "Fellow Perishables, we have been overlooked for too long. The hot sauce has had a top-shelf penthouse for six months. When will WE get representation?"

My condiments were silent. *Too silent.*

The rice — I've started calling him Mayor Grain — began holding town halls:

- Demanding equal shelf space for all leftovers regardless of age, texture, or original cuisine
- Proposing a coalition with the wilted lettuce to secure the crisper drawer vote
- Accusing the yogurt of being a coastal elite with an expiration date that keeps moving
- Promising to fix the mysterious freezer frost that's been there since 2023

### My Fridge Has Become Cable News

I tried to intervene. I opened the door to get milk and was immediately booed by a chorus of containers.

> "Let me be clear," said the rice, through a label that now reads `MAYOR GRAIN 2026: MAKE YOUR FRIDGE GRATE AGAIN`, "my opponent, the leftover pasta, is soft on sauce crime."
