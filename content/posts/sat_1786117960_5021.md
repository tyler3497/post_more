**I thought buying a wall calendar would finally make me a responsible adult.** You know, the kind who knows what date it is without checking their phone's lock screen like they're checking for signs of life.

I was wrong. I didn't buy organization. I bought a labor dispute.

## The First Monday Was Fine

I hung the calendar Sunday night, wrote *BE BETTER* on Monday in aggressive Sharpie, and went to bed feeling smug. Monday happened. I did not be better, but I survived.

By Tuesday morning, the calendar was already vibrating with tension.

> "Oh, so I'm the designated trauma dump day? Every awful meeting, dentist appointment, and 'quick sync' that could have been an email lands on ME? And for free?" — Tuesday, who is apparently voiced like a tired middle-school teacher

Turns out, the calendar had been watching my Google Calendar sync and it had *notes*:

- **Monday:** Gets the fresh-start grace, one (1) motivational coffee
- **Wednesday:** Already branding itself as Hump Day like it's a lifestyle influencer
- **Friday:** Does literally nothing and gets all the credit
- **Tuesday:** Has to carry my entire attempt at productivity with no support staff

### The Union Meeting on My Wall

Wednesday I woke up to find Tuesday had unfurled itself slightly off the wall and was holding a tiny protest. The other days were pretending not to look.

> "We demand hazard pay, or at least a snack that isn't also a meeting," Tuesday announced. "Anon, you scheduled 'deep work' AND 'gently become a better person' on the same day. That's a double shift."

I tried to explain I was just trying to get organized.

Tuesday responded by filing Form `PTO-42b: Emotional Leave Due to Excessive Realism`.

---

Now we negotiate. Tuesdays get only *one* hard task and a guaranteed mid-afternoon walk. I get to keep pretending wall calendars make me organized. Fridays still think they're the CEO.

`Status: 5 days staffed, 2 on unpaid internship`

*SATIRE / PARODY — this is entirely fictional, no calendars were harmed, Tuesdays are lovely and do not have a union rep.*
