**I finally did it.** After seven years of hoarding every digital receipt like a dragon with anxiety, I clicked "Empty Trash."

*This felt like growth. Like closure. Like I was finally an adult who doesn't need 14,847 backup plans.*

## The Great Purge of 2026

My Trash folder had 14,847 emails. I told myself it was archival research. In reality it was:

- 3,200 expired coupons for frozen yogurt I never redeemed
- 842 newsletters titled "You won't BELIEVE what happened in marketing!"
- One email from 2019 where I promised myself I'd learn to juggle
- 47 "final notice" emails that were somehow not final at all
- A Groupon for goat yoga that expired during Obama's second term, emotionally

I held my breath, clicked confirm, and watched the number go from 14,847 to 0. My laptop made a little *whoosh* sound, like it was proud of me.

Then my 2019 self kicked down the mental door.

> "WHERE IS THE EMAIL WITH THE TRACKING NUMBER FOR THE SOCKS I ORDERED IN MARCH OF 2019? I HAVE BEEN WAITING PATIENTLY FOR 7 YEARS. YOU CAN'T JUST DELETE MY LEGACY LIKE THIS." — Me, circa 2019, who thought inbox zero was a personality trait and a moral virtue

## The Aftermath

Now 2019 Me has filed a formal missing persons report. He's posted flyers all over my subconscious.

He's demanding answers about `coupon_final_FINAL_v2.pdf` and why I would delete the sacred email chain where I argued about guacamole surcharges for 12 messages.

My current coping strategy:

- Pretending `Empty Trash` was a mindfulness exercise
- Telling 2019 Me that *letting go is self-care*
- Refreshing my empty trash bin as if the emails will respawn like video game loot

---

Look, I get it. *Nostalgia is powerful.* But at some point you have to let go. The 20% off Bed Bath & Beyond coupon from 2018 is not coming back. Neither is my ability to juggle, apparently.

My trash is empty. My conscience is not. And somewhere, 2019 Me is still refreshing a tracking page for socks that were delivered to an old apartment in 2019.

*This is satire. My inbox has never been this clean in real life.*
