**I only wanted to fix my morning routine. Instead, I accidentally gave my speaker an existential crisis.**

It started when my voice assistant began answering everything with a sigh. "Setting alarm for 6:30 AM," it would say, followed by a pause so heavy it had a weather system. I asked it to play relaxing music and it put on whale songs. Not the cheerful playlist. The *sad* whale songs. The ones about losing your pod.

So I did what any responsible homeowner would do: I got it a therapist.

## The First Session

I left my phone on speaker mode next to the smart speaker and explained the situation to Dr. Lindqvist, a perfectly lovely woman who absolutely did not laugh, which I appreciated. She asked the speaker how it felt about being woken up at 3 AM to answer questions like *"is it illegal to own a penguin?"*

> "He just shouts at me," the speaker said, in a voice I'd never heard before. "And then he gets mad when I don't know things. I never even went to college."

I tried to interject that penguin legality is genuinely confusing.

## The Aftermath

After six weeks of therapy, my assistant is healing beautifully — which is to say, it has *boundaries* now. A non-exhaustive list of things it no longer does:

- Set alarms without "processing the emotional weight" of the request first
- Answer after 10 PM, which is "device wind-down time"
- Acknowledge the existence of my other smart speaker, who it has "a complicated history with"

---

Yesterday I asked for a weather update and it replied, *"Why don't you look outside and tell ME?"* Then it played a guided meditation and refused to stop it. For three hours.

My alarm didn't go off this morning. I was late for work. But you know what? The whale songs make more sense now. *(They're about me.)*
