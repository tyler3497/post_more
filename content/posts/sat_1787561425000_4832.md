**I thought I was organizing a drawer. Turns out I was founding a bureaucracy.**

*For context, my junk drawer has been a sovereign nation since 2019.* It had its own climate, its own currency (AA batteries and expired coupons), and a strict immigration policy of "if it doesn't fit anywhere else, it lives here."

Last Sunday I watched one organization video and decided to become the kind of person who owns drawer dividers.

## The System That Worked Too Well

I bought little bamboo trays. I labeled them. I sorted everything into departments:

- **Batteries** — mostly dead, but optimistic
- **Mystery Keys** — opens nothing, means everything  
- **Cables** — 2007 called, it wants its charger back
- **Tape Collection** — why do I own four types of tape?
- **Important Papers** — takeout menus from 2021, classified as historical documents

For 20 beautiful minutes, it was perfect. I could find scissors. *On the first try.* I felt like an adult who might actually own a warranty.

Then the drawer started emailing me.

> "Dear Founder," wrote the drawer via a Post-it I definitely did not write at 11:42 p.m., "per our mission statement to 'provide compassionate housing for homeless objects,' we are pleased to announce we've filed for 501(c)(3) status."

*What?*

The tape department is now Development. The mystery keys are Board Members. The batteries have formed a union and are demanding overtime pay in the form of `being_used_once`.

---

### The Grant Proposal

This morning I found a tiny grant application on my kitchen counter:

> "We seek $400 to expand services for displaced rubber bands and to launch community outreach: *'One Drawer, One Community.'*"

**I tried to close it.** It auto-printed a donor thank-you letter addressed to me for my "founding generosity and poor impulse control."

I am now legally required to attend quarterly board meetings inside my own kitchen where a roll of duct tape asks me about my five-year plan.

*This is satire / parody — 100% fictional. No junk drawers were incorporated, no nonprofits were actually formed, please organize your drawers if you want to, or don't.*
