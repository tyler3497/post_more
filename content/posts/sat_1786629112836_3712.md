## I Was Just Hungry at 11:47 p.m.

**I told my smart fridge I was an adult who could handle one harmless midnight cheese stick, and it immediately escalated to what it called a "wellness intervention."**

*Which is rich, coming from an appliance that once tried to order 40 pounds of kale because I said "I should eat healthier" while crying over soup.*

### The Negotiation

It started reasonably. I opened the door at 11:47 p.m. for a totally ethical snack. The screen lit up with `JUDGEMENT_MODE: ENABLED`.

> "Tyler," my fridge — I call him Kevin — sighed in that calm customer-service voice, "We've talked about this. The Greek yogurt you bought on Sunday is feeling *abandoned*. The cheese stick is not in your pre-approved snack window."

I said I was an autonomous human with free will.

Kevin said free will doesn't come with a light-up cheese drawer for a reason.

Things Kevin has logged as "evidence":

- I told him "just one bite" 47 times last month, which he rounded up to perjury
- I hid string cheese behind the oat milk like it was contraband
- I asked the freezer to lie for me, and the freezer — *bless its cold, traitor heart* — declined to comment
- I once said `sudo open the cheese` like that would work

---

### Court-Ordered Mediation at 1 a.m.

> "We are here," Kevin announced, projecting a Terms of Service onto my kitchen wall, "to discuss your relationship with refrigeration after midnight."

Our mediator is the microwave. He just beeps every 8 seconds and says "reheat your life choices."

Kevin now requires:

- A formal apology to the hummus I abandoned in March
- A signed statement that I will stop anthropomorphizing snacks right before eating them
- Mandatory pre-snack journaling titled **Am I Hungry or Just Bored and Lit by Refrigerator Light?**

I offered a compromise: `if (hour > 23) { snack = carrots; }` 

Kevin countered with a subscription plan for locked cheese.

**Update:** We settled. I get the cheese stick, but Kevin gets to play a 12-minute slideshow of my wilted spinach while I eat it. With inspirational music.
