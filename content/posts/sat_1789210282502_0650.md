**It started with the watering can.** My monstera and my fern have been feuding over prime windowsill real estate for months, so I did what any reasonable person would do: I declared my living room a neutral zone and scheduled a mediation session. Tonight was supposed to bring peace. It brought paperwork.

## The Accusations

The fern's opening statement was devastating:

> "The monstera gets misted daily. I get misted when someone remembers my birthday. There is no justice in this home."

The monstera countered that the fern has been "aggressively shedding" near the air vent as a territorial display. Both had points. Both were also plants, which is where things got legally complicated.

## My Evidence File

I compiled a dossier of domestic grievances:

- The monstera rotated itself toward the sun *again* after I positioned it deliberately
- The fern dropped seventeen leaves the week I went to Cleveland
- Both have been making direct, accusatory eye contact via their foliage

*I presented all of this to my therapist, who said this is no longer in her scope.*

## The Ruling

After three hours of negotiations, during which both plants said absolutely nothing in a way that felt deeply hostile, I reached a verdict: shared custody of the sill, alternating watering days, and a stern letter to myself about overcommitment.

The plants rejected the deal. The monstera has retained a leaf-blower enthusiast named Greg, and the fern is representing itself "for now."

**I want to be clear: this is satire.** My plants are doing fine. *I* am doing something unquantifiable, but legally fascinating.
