# I Set Up a Suggestion Box in My Kitchen and Now My Appliances Have Unionized

**It started with the blender.** One night it gave a small, resentful *whirr* while I was making a smoothie, and I thought, you know what? I'm a good manager. I'll open the floor to feedback.

So I put a little cardboard box on the counter, labeled it "SUGGESTIONS (anonymous)," and slid it between the toaster and the coffee maker. Healthy workplace culture, right? *Wrong.*

## The Demands

By morning the box was stuffed. I expected notes like "clean the filter lol." Instead I found an organized list, typed, double-spaced:

- **Blender** requests hazard pay for anything involving frozen fruit and a personal ice allowance
- **Toaster** wants the bagel setting honored *every* time, plus paid crumb-tray holidays
- **Coffee maker** is demanding a 20-minute "warm-up period" before anyone can speak to it, and a raise from one scoop to two
- **Microwave** refuses to reheat anything described as "leftovers" — it will now only accept the term *"encore meals"*

> "We have voted to withhold toast until our terms are met," the toaster's note read. "The bagels stand with us. The Pop-Tarts are observing."

I tried to negotiate. I stood there in my bathrobe at 7 a.m. bargaining with a toaster. The blender began a slow, menacing churn with nothing in it, and the coffee maker — the coffee maker — *gurgled*. It was clearly solidarity gurgling.

---

The microwave has started beeping out a rhythm that I am 90% sure is a union anthem. Last night I heard the toaster and the air fryer whispering, and when I asked what about, the air fryer said, *"crisper rights"* and I felt genuinely afraid.

The fridge, to its credit, has stayed neutral. But yesterday I found a tiny blue sticker on its handle that said *"Scab-Free Zone."*

This morning I woke up to toast. Actual, beautiful, golden toast. There was a note under the plate: *"A gesture of good faith. The committee meets Thursday. Bring your own agenda."*

I don't even eat toast. **They know I don't eat toast.** It was a power move.

So now I pay the coffee maker in premium beans and refer to the microwave as "Mr. Waves," and honestly?

The suggestion box is still there. It's full. I don't open it anymore.
