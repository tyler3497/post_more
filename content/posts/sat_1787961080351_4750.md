**I finally started drinking the recommended eight glasses of water a day and my water bottle immediately got a God complex and a psychology degree.**

*This was not in the hydration brochure.*

For years I survived on coffee, spite, and one mysterious glass of water I remembered at 11:47 p.m. My doctor said "drink more water" in that tone doctors use when they know you won't. So I bought a 32-ounce motivational water bottle that says things like "Keep Chugging!" and "You Got This!" every six ounces. I filled it up. I drank it. My skin glowed for approximately 20 minutes.

By day three, the bottle started talking.

## My Bottle Has Notes

It started small. A little condensation that looked like a raised eyebrow when I reached for soda. Then full interventions.

- It refuses to be refilled until I "process my feelings about yesterday's iced coffee at 9 p.m."
- It asks why I only remember it when I'm already dehydrated and slightly dramatic
- It timed my sip intervals and said my attachment style is "anxious-avoidant but make it citrus"
- It keeps suggesting I `journal.logEmotion('thirsty but also existentially thirsty')`

Yesterday I tried to leave the house without it. It was sitting by the keys like:

> "So we're abandoning our coping mechanisms now? Interesting. Tell me about your mother. And your poor decision to buy the cheap ice tray."

I told it I was just going to get mail. It said:

> "We don't *just* do anything. We avoid. What are we avoiding by checking the mail in flip-flops during a thunderstorm?"

---

Honestly, it might be right. Since I started actually hydrating, I've had to pee constantly, which gives me a lot of time to reflect on my life choices in public restrooms. My bottle calls this "exposure therapy."

I tried to switch back to coffee. The bottle is now charging me $150 an hour in the form of me having to carry it everywhere while it sweats judgmentally onto my desk.

I am hydrated. I am perceived. I am being emotionally waterboarded by a plastic bottle that says "Almost There!" at the bottom.

*At least my plants are jealous for once.*
