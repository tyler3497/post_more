**I bought a $12 linen journal to become a mindful person who has thoughts, and now I'm in a co-dependent writing partnership with paper.**

*It was supposed to be five minutes of gratitude. It has become a full-time editorial staff.*

I started June 1st. You know, new month, new me, same coping mechanisms but with better stationery. The Instagram reel said "journal every morning and watch your life change." My life did change. My notebook got confident.

## The Morning Ritual That Escaped Containment

Day 3: I wrote "Today I'm grateful for coffee." Inspirational.

Day 7: I wrote "I think my situationship is actually a situation."

Day 11: My notebook started replying in the margins.

Evidence that my Moleskine has become my manager:

- It now auto-corrects my feelings — I write "I'm fine" and it writes back in tiny cursive "*we are not fine, we are dehydrating and avoiding our laundry*"
- It keeps a running list titled `THINGS WE WILL NOT TOLERATE IN Q3` that includes "him saying 'haha busy week' for 6 consecutive weeks"
- It has its own voice. It's 60% me, 40% therapist who charges in washi tape
- It submitted a draft text to my phone at 11:47 p.m. without my permission

> "Hey. It's not me, it's your journal. He says he's 'figuring things out' and you deserve someone who figures out how to reply before the ice caps melt. Also drink water. — Your Notebook (and Tyler's better judgment)"
