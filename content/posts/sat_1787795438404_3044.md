**I did not mean to unionize my floor debris.** I just wanted to feel more professional about chores.

It started innocently. My therapist — *bless her, she tries* — suggested I "reframe household tasks as leadership opportunities." So I did.

## The Incident

I looked at my robot vacuum, which I have named Sir Sucks-A-Lot, and said out loud, with full corporate confidence:

> "Leadership will handle this."

I meant the vacuum. The dust bunnies under my couch heard a regime change.

Within hours, I found a tiny Post-it note stuck to my baseboard. Written in what I can only assume was dryer lint and desperation:

> "We, the Dust Bunnies of Sector Couch-Left, formally request a town hall with Leadership. — The Collective"

## The Aftermath

I came home to find them *organized*. Like, disturbingly organized.

- A crumb from a 2023 Pop-Tart acting as moderator
- Three dust bunnies with little signs that said "WE PAY RENT IN TEXTURE"
- My lost bobby pin serving as a gavel
- A PowerPoint presentation projected onto my wall using my nightlight and sheer audacity

Their demands were... extensive:

- Immediate cessation of "unannounced Leadership incursions" (`vacuum_day = false` until further notice)
- Recognition of the area under the fridge as a sovereign nation
- One (1) sock, to live in, forever

---

Look, I'm not proud of what happened next. I tried to negotiate. I crouched down and said, "Guys, I'm trying to build a culture of accountability here."

And the biggest dust bunny — *who I have named Brenda, obviously* — just stared at me and said, "Accountability? You haven't cleaned your ceiling fan since the Obama administration."

She's not wrong. She's just very dusty and very correct.

We have reached a tentative agreement: I will not run Leadership on Tuesdays, and they will stop leaving passive-aggressive lint sculptures that look suspiciously like me crying over my 401(k).

This is satire. My apartment is still a mess. Leadership is currently stuck on a rug fringe and reconsidering its career.
