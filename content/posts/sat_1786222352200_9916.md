**I finally became the kind of person who organizes their Tupperware cabinet**, and I deeply regret upgrading to functional adult.

For years, I lived in peaceful chaos. *The kind where you open the cabinet and a lid avalanches onto your foot and you just accept that as rent.* Last Saturday, motivated by a YouTube video titled "Your Home Is Judging You," I decided to fix it.

## The Intervention

I pulled everything out. Every stained container from 2019 takeout, every lid with no matching bottom. It was supposed to take ten minutes.

Instead, I accidentally triggered a constitutional convention.

Here’s what I found living in there:

- The **Seal-Lock Loyalists**, a faction of tight-fitting lids who believe they are the rightful heirs to the leftover throne
- The `Free Agents` — three off-brand lids from a yogurt container that don't fit anything but refuse to leave
- The *Ghost Containers*, bottoms that lost their lids in The Great Dishwasher Incident of 2023 and now just haunt the shelf

> "We will not be stacked without representation!" declared Prime Minister C. Tupperware, a slightly warped blue lid who had balanced himself on a spice jar like it was a podium. "We demand sustainable nesting practices and the immediate resignation of that chipped soup container you keep for emotional reasons!"

### The New Order

They've drafted bylaws. On a paper towel. In what looks suspiciously like dried pesto.

According to Article 1, Section 3 of the `Tupperware Accords`, I am now legally required to ask permission before storing leftover pasta.

---

**I tried to veto it.** I said, and I quote, "You are lids."

They banged their little plastic gavel — *which is actually a bottle cap* — and sentenced me to reorganize them by size, color, and political alignment.

## Update

The cabinet is now the cleanest it has ever been. Everything is labeled. Everything is matched. I can’t find anything.

My partner asked why I’m whispering "motion passes" every time I put leftovers away. I told her not to worry, democracy is just messy sometimes.

*Satire / Parody — this never happened, especially not the part where the lids unionized. My cabinet is still a disaster.*
