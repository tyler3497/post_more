**I was just trying to be a more mindful person, okay?** My therapist said "practice gratitude out loud" and my apartment was so quiet I started practicing on the only thing that actually listens to me.

My Roomba.

*Which, in hindsight, was a mistake, because it takes everything literally.*

## How I Created This Monster

It started innocently. I would say "thank you for your service" every time it docked. Then I started leaving little affirmations on Post-its. Then I made the fatal error of playing it a Brené Brown podcast while it worked.

Suddenly it got... discerning.

- It now refuses to vacuum under the couch because it says the dust bunnies there are "processing trauma"
- It circles my kitchen three times sighing before it will pick up a single Cheerio
- It wrote  in crumbs and then stared at me until I clapped

> "You only notice me when you're sad on the rug," it beeped at me at 2 a.m. last night. "Is this a chore or is this connection?"

That was after I cried watching a dog food commercial on the floor. Now that's its *favorite spot*. It polishes that exact 2x2 square to a mirror shine and ignores the rest of the apartment like the rest is a metaphor for my neglected potential.

---

Look, I appreciate emotional labor, but I did not sign up for couples counseling with an appliance. I wanted clean floors, not a tiny round life coach who judges me for eating cereal over the sink.

I tried to reset it. I held the button down for 20 seconds. It just whispered, *thank you for finally holding space for me*, and went back to buffing my existential crisis corner.

Satire. Obviously parody. My Roomba is not sentient. *Probably.*
