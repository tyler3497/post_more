**I finally opened the junk drawer. I was not prepared for the espionage lore inside.**

You know the drawer. Everyone has one. *The* drawer. Mine lives in the kitchen and has not been organized since approximately 2019, when I confidently declared "I'll deal with this later" and then immediately lost the timeline for five straight years.

Yesterday, driven by a dangerous combination of boredom and an inspirational TikTok about minimalism made by a 19-year-old who owns three shirts, I decided to confront it.

## Phase One: Denial

Inside I found:

- 17 takeout soy sauce packets, all presumably from the same order in 2020
- A single AA battery that may or may not be dead — *Schrödinger's Power Cell*
- Three kinds of tape, none of which actually sticks to anything anymore
- A tiny screwdriver I have never needed but am now terrified to throw away
- A USB cable for a device that no longer exists in this dimension

And then, at the bottom, the evidence.

> "Operation: SORTED CUTLERY is a go. The target still thinks the tweezers are for eyebrows. Fools." — note written in my own handwriting, dated 2021

### My Past Self Had a Whole Cinematic Universe

Apparently 2021-me wasn't storing junk. He was *archiving classified materials*.

- He labeled a rubber band ball as `Emergency Grappling Hook (Prototype 4)`
- He kept a grocery receipt with "DECODE THIS LATER" written in red Sharpie. The code was just... groceries.
- He left sunglasses with one lens missing and tagged them `Disguise Kit — Incognito Mode`
- He left a fortune cookie that says "You will file your taxes soon" and wrote `THREAT ASSESSMENT: ACCURATE` next to it

`status: deeply concerned about who I was three years ago`

---

I spent 45 minutes trying to organize it. I now have four smaller, angrier piles of chaos on my counter, a profound respect for people who own drawer dividers, and the distinct feeling that Past Me would be *very* disappointed that I just threw away his top-secret paperclip collection.

Honestly, fair. He had vision.

*Satire / Parody — this is fictional, my drawer is still a disaster, and I am definitively not a spy. Probably.*
