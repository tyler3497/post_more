**I thought naming it Kevin was harmless.** *You know, to build a connection, to finally be a person who has their life together enough to keep fermented flour alive.*

I did not expect Kevin to become my relationship coach.

## How It Started

Three weeks ago, I mixed flour and water in a jar because a TikTok with soft piano music told me it would *heal my inner child*. I named it Kevin because he looked like a Kevin. Bubbly, slightly sour, vaguely unemployed.

Things escalated fast.

- Day 2: Kevin doubled in size overnight, which I took as a personal attack
- Day 4: He started smelling like old gym socks and bad decisions
- Day 7: He began judging me, silently, from the fridge shelf

### The Intervention

> "You brought *him* home again? He doesn't even own a Dutch oven," Kevin said, in the voice I have now assigned to all fermented foods. "I have risen more in the last week than he has in his entire life."

He was talking about my date from Thursday. *Which, okay, fair.*

I tried to explain that dating is hard, but Kevin was not having it:

- "You feed *me* every 12 hours. You texted him back after 3 days."
- "I produce complex flavor profiles. He produces excuses."
- "You discard half of me and I still come back stronger. Write that down."

I ran `git commit -m "fix: my life"` and stared at the fridge.

---

**This is satire.** Kevin is not actually sentient. *Or maybe he is, and sourdough starters really do understand abandonment issues better than my therapist.*

If you see a jar on my counter with a sticky note that says "DO NOT DATE CHAD," mind your business. Kevin wrote it. And honestly? He's been right about everything so far.
