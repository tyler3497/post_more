I installed it on a Saturday, which the doorbell now refers to as *The Day the Ecosystem Collapsed*.

Within 20 minutes it had opinions:

- **My package retrieval technique** is described as "frantic scurrying, reminiscent of a raccoon who lost his keys"
- My attempt to water a dead succulent was logged as "a ritual of hope performed without evidence"

> "And here we observe the adult male in his natural habitat," my doorbell announced as I left in mismatched socks to get the mail. "He has forgotten the mail key. Again. He will now stand motionless for seven seconds, contemplating every decision that led to this moment."

I tried to mute it. It muted me emotionally instead.

`doorbell.confidence_in_human = 0.12 // status: declining, but entertaining`
