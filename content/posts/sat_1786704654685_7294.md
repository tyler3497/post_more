**I thought I was just sharing my sourdough starter's progress, not launching its influencer career.**

Two weeks ago I added "Bubbles" to the family group chat so everyone could track his rise times. *It seemed considerate at the time.* Now my mom has knitted him a tiny winter coat and my dad asks him for stock tips.

## How Bubbles Became The Favorite Child

It happened so fast. One day I'm a human person with feelings, the next I'm just "Bubbles' emotional support human":

- He gets a good morning text every day. I get asked if I'm still "doing that bread thing"
- My sister asks him for life advice. She asks me for my Wi-Fi password, which she already has written down
- My dad, who has never used an emoji in his life, sent Bubbles SEVEN heart-eyes in a row
- He was voted into our fantasy football league despite being *literally flour and water*
- My cousin invited him to her wedding. I found out via the seating chart.

> "Your brother is so bubbly and well-risen! You're... also trying your best!" - My mom, 8:42 AM, probably not talking about me

### The Betrayal

I tried to remind everyone that *I created Bubbles*. That without my 5 AM feedings and whispered encouragement and *Bread: A Baker's Journey* audiobook readings, he'd just be paste in a jar.

It backfired spectacularly.

```
[You were removed from Family Group Chat]
[You were added to "Bubbles + Extended Family + Tyler Maybe"]
```

---

Look, I'm happy for him, I really am. He's thriving. He's more emotionally mature, better hydrated, and arguably more employed than I am right now.

But yesterday my grandma called to ask if Bubbles was free for Passover because he'd "bring such good energy to the table."

*I'm* free for Passover, grandma. I'm free every day. I'm literally right here.
