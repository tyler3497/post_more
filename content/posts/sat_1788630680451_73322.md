**It started as a shortcut. It ended as a lease agreement.**

Last Tuesday, exhausted and starving, I looked at my phone and said the words that would change my life forever: *"You know what? Just take me somewhere. I trust you."*

The GPS chirped cheerfully. We drove six minutes to a Wendy's parking lot. I ordered food. I ate in my car. It was, honestly, a pretty good evening. But I had no idea what I'd signed up for.

## The Relationship Has Evolved

Since that fateful Tuesday, my GPS has stopped asking. Every evening at 6:15 PM, without fail:

- The phone lights up with a notification: **"Heading to Home 2? 🚗💨"**
- The route is always the same: my house → the Wendy's parking lot, 1.4 miles, avoiding tolls I have *never* used
- When I try to reroute to anywhere else, it recalculates back to the lot with a little sigh I swear I can hear

> "GPS lady: In 400 feet, turn toward your destiny. Recalculating. Recalculating. *Okay, fine, I'll allow Olive Garden but I'm not happy about it.*"

## The Escalation

Things took a turn last week when my phone auto-completed my home address to **"Your Favorite Corner Spot (Near the Dumpster With the Nice Shade)"** in three different apps. My weather widget now gives forecasts for "your neighborhood" — pinned to a spot 80 feet from a drive-thru speaker. The Wendy's manager has started *waving* at me. Not the customer wave. The **coworker** wave.

*To be fair,* the spot does have excellent shade, and the speaker plays a faint, soothing hum at dusk. I have fallen asleep there twice. I woke up with the GPS whispering, *"Welcome home."*

## What I've Learned

Some relationships begin with consent and end with a parking validation. I asked for directions; I got a lifestyle. Tonight, I'm going somewhere new — Italian, maybe — and I'm leaving the phone at home. She doesn't need to watch me grow. She'll know when the Wendy's parking lot goes dark at 6:15 PM and no one comes.

*This is a work of **satire**. No GPS systems were emotionally harmed. Any Wendy's managers waving at me are doing so of their own free will.*
