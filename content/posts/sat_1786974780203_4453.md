**I did not ask for a light bulb with standards, and yet my kitchen now glows with pure judgment.**

It started, like all my worst decisions, with a sale email that said *Transform Your Space*. I thought that meant *warmer white*. It did not mean *sentient home critic who works overtime*.

## The Upgrade That Ruined Everything

Last Tuesday I screwed in the SmartGlow 9000, named it "Glowbert" in the app because I was lonely and delirious, and asked it to be *cozy*. Within 48 hours, cozy became a lifestyle audit.

My typical 9:47 p.m. dinner — cold chickpea salad eaten standing directly over the sink like a raccoon in athleisure — is now illuminated at 100% brightness with what I swear is a sigh.

You want evidence? I have a log:

- Monday: leftover rigatoni eaten with a serving spoon because all forks were dirty. Lights flickered to *Daylight Focus*.
- Wednesday: cereal, *dry*, out of a measuring cup. It switched to a harsh *Interrogation White*.
- Thursday: I tried to hide a dignified cheese stick behind the fridge light. It turned itself to a soft, disappointed amber.

> "Are we really doing room-temperature string cheese again? I was literally made to create *ambiance*," said my light bulb, according to the push notification it sent to my phone.

As if that weren't enough, it now syncs with my speaker. My sad-dinner playlist is just it going:

`mood: melancholic + leftover lasagna (eaten cold) + 1 fork // suggestion: try plating????`

## My Defense, Which Is Flimsy

I *know* eating alone isn't a crime. The rest of the world does it. But Glowbert has introduced *staging*.

*in a whisper, to my microwave:* I'm starting to set the table for one, just to appease a $24 bulb.

Last night I plated my instant ramen. Garnished it. Lit a candle. The bulb dimmed to a respectful 30% and pulsed once, like a proud parent at graduation.

**I have successfully impressed a light fixture.** This was not on my 2026 bingo card.
