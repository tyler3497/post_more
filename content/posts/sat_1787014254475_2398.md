**I decided to become the kind of adult who irons their t-shirts.** *A low bar, I know, but historically my aesthetic has been "fresh from the dryer floor."*

For context, I own 14 identical black t-shirts. I bought them after watching a documentary that said successful people reduce decisions. I have reduced my decisions to zero and my personality also, apparently.

Last Tuesday, I ironed one. Just to see what would happen. My closet lost its mind.

## The Campaign Begins

My hoodies immediately started whispering. My closet has always been a quiet bureaucracy — denim in one corner, aspirational gym clothes that have never met a gym in another. Now it's a war room.

Apparently, in closet logic, ironed t-shirt = you have a press conference tomorrow.

My closet organized itself overnight:

- My blazer that I wore once to a wedding in 2022 migrated to the front like it was volunteering as campaign manager
- My hoodies formed a super PAC called `Hoodies For Historical Inaccuracy`
- My socks held a primary and lost

> "Sir, we've prepared your statement on whether you tuck or untuck," my dress shirt said, in a voice that was somehow both crisp and deeply unemployed. "The people — by which we mean your three houseplants — demand clarity."

*I do not have a platform.* My platform is "my laundry doesn't smell like laundry detergent that gave up."

---

I tried to explain I just wanted to look nice for a Zoom call where my camera stays off. The closet did not accept this. It has now drafted yard signs that say `IRONED / 2026` and hung my belt like bunting.

This morning I found my iron on, warming itself up like a motivational speaker.

`self.iron_state = "permanently disappointed"`

I am now being endorsed by fabric softener. I did not ask for this.
