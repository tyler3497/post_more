**I never thought I'd have to apologize to an appliance that eats socks for a living.**

But last Tuesday, after I yelled *"you had ONE job"* at my washing machine when it turned my white towels slightly grey, something shifted. *A vibe shift. A hum of pure, aggrieved betrayal.*

Now it refuses to run unless I validate it first.

### The Incident That Started It All

I admit, I was harsh. My towels looked like they'd seen things. I slammed the lid. It made a noise like `error_code: emotional_damage.exe`. And then it just... stopped mid-cycle. Full of soapy water and silent judgment.

### The New Terms and Conditions

When I tried to restart it the next morning, it displayed a single, blinking light that I swear meant "we need to talk." My roommate translated:

> "She said you never notice when she does a good job, you only notice when your socks are damp. She also said her love language is words of affirmation and fabric softener."

Now our laundry routine is:

- I approach slowly and say **"You are powerful, efficient, and your spin cycle is truly inspirational"**
- I list three things I appreciate about it *from the heart*
- I promise not to overload it with my emotional baggage *or* my actual baggage
- It accepts with a gentle chime and allows me to wash one (1) load of darks

---

I tried to use tough love yesterday. It locked its own door and only runs cold now, out of spite. My clothes have never been cleaner or more emotionally supported.

`Status: delicates only, emotionally speaking`
