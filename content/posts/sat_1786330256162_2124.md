**I bought a white noise machine for $29.99 to fix my sleep. I did not expect it to unionize my subconscious.**

The guy at the store said *ocean waves* would be calming. He did not mention the machine would come with a tiny middle manager for my REM cycle.

Night one was fine. Night two, I started dreaming in very polite, very approved ambient soundscapes.

Night three, the notes began.

## The Notes From My Dream's HR Department:

- **Monday:** My flying dream was rejected for "unrealistic physics and lack of narrative cohesion." Suggested alternative: me, calmly filing taxes in a light drizzle.
- **Tuesday:** My dream where I could speak fluent Italian was flagged because, quote, "rain sounds don't cover linguistic ambition." It was replaced with 8 hours of me looking for my keys in a parking lot.
- **Wednesday:** I tried to dream about winning a baking competition. The machine went full static and left a Post-it on my pillow that just said `TRY AGAIN WITH LESS AMBITION`.

> "We're really looking for something more on-brand," whispered the white noise machine at 3:14 a.m., in the voice of a very tired podcast producer. "Have you considered dreaming about... nothing? Nothing tests well. Nothing is very coastal grandmother."

And honestly, *that's the most insulting part*. I used to have cool dreams! Helicopters! Time travel!

---

Now my sleep score on my watch says `100% - Compliant`. My watch has never said that before. I checked the settings:

`ocean_waves.exe has been running for 72 hours and has requested admin access to your imagination`

I told my roommate and she said, "Oh yeah, mine does that too. Mine only lets me dream about laundry now. Very efficient."

So I did what any self-respecting adult would do. I gave the machine a performance review.

It gave *me* one back. Lower marks on "dream originality."

I'm sleeping great though. 8 hours straight. Can't remember a single dream. Which is *probably* fine.
