**I told my loyalty card we needed space — I meant in the pantry.**

*I have too much hummus. That's literally all I meant.*

For three years, me and my FreshMart Plus Savings Card have had a beautiful, transactional relationship. I swipe, it saves me 37 cents, I pretend that's a meaningful financial decision. It was stable. It was low-effort. It was perfect.

Then last Tuesday, standing in front of my fridge that was 60% condiments, I sighed and said out loud, "We need space."

My wallet was open. The card heard me.

## The Incident at Aisle 4

I went to buy milk the next day. I swiped my card. The little keypad said `10% OFF? ARE YOU SURE YOU STILL WANT THIS?`

- My card started only giving me discounts on things I bought in 2022, like it was trying to remind me of the good times
- It emailed me a coupon for *one single grape* with the subject line "thinking of you"
- The cashier scanned it and whispered, "Dude, are you two okay?"
- It signed me up for the store's bereavement mailing list — for yogurt

> "I thought we had something," my loyalty card beeped, probably. "I gave you double points on rotisserie chicken. I defended you when you bought that 8-pack of energy drinks at 11 p.m. Is that nothing to you?"

## The Negotiation

I tried to explain. I said space meant *shelf space*, not *emotional distance*.

I tried `selfCheckout.reassure(card, "it's not you it's my fridge")` and it returned:

```
Error: relationship_status = complicated, points_balance = passive_aggressive
```

---

Now my card is seeing other shoppers. I saw it giving 20% off to a guy who doesn't even know what ramps are. *Ramps.*

Look, I get it. I shouldn't have said we need space while holding a loyalty card. That's on me. But if anyone needs me, I'll be in the pantry, making room, while my plastic card writes sad poetry on the back of receipts about our time together.

*SATIRE / PARODY — 100% fictional. No loyalty cards were emotionally damaged, no grocery stores were consulted, this is pure nonsense about my inability to organize condiments.*
