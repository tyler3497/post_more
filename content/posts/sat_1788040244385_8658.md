**I started journaling to clear my mind and now my mind is mad it has to provide footnotes.**

*Which is fair, because I only bought the journal because it was $5.99 and said "Manifest Your Dreams" on the cover and I thought that sounded low-effort.*

## The Incident

I wrote one entry. Just one. It said: "Today I felt vaguely overwhelmed but my succulent is still alive so we're calling it a win."

By morning, my journal had added comments in red pen.

> "Vaguely overwhelmed is not a measurable variable. Please revise and resubmit. Also your dream about being chased by a giant bagel has been forwarded to the Department of Symbolic Pastries for peer review. - J."

J. is my journal. It gave itself an initial.

### Evidence That My Journal Is Now My Manager

- **It instituted office hours.** 6am-7am only. My thoughts outside that window are marked `WILL NOT BE CITED`.
- **It started a revision history.** Every time I write "lol" it replaces it with "[nervous laughter, see appendix C]"
- **It peer-reviewed my dreams.** My recurring dream where I forget my pants at the grocery store got a 2/10 and a note: "lacks originality, derivative work"

*I tried to set a boundary. I wrote "Dear Journal, please be chill."*

It replied with a rubric.

```
chillness: 0/5
vulnerability: 3/5
use of Oxford comma: commendable
recommendation: major revisions
```

---

This morning I found a cover letter on my pillow. It says my anxiety about replying to texts late is being considered for publication in *The Journal of Mildly Relatable Panic* pending two anonymous reviews. One reviewer is my houseplant.

**I just wanted to clear my mind.** Now I have to do a literature review on my own feelings.

*Satire/parody — obviously fictional. My journal is just paper. Very judgmental, very unemployed paper.*
