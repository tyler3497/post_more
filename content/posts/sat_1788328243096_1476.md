**I decided to rebrand my to-do list as a self-love journey and it immediately became the most toxic relationship I've ever had.**

*For context, my previous to-do list was titled "THINGS YOU ARE FAILING AT" in all caps, which my therapist said was "not ideal."*

So last Monday I downloaded a pastel app that promised to make productivity feel like a hug. I renamed my list "Gentle Goals for a Thriving Queen" and added little sparkle emojis. I thought I was healing.

I was not healing. I was creating a monster with better branding.

## My Attempt at Affirmation

I started soft:

- drink water like you love yourself
- go for a walk, bestie, touch some grass
- answer one email so you don't get fired (gently)
- put laundry in hamper, not just near it

The list responded immediately like a life coach who just did one weekend certification.

> "Yasss queen! You absolutely CRUSHED drinking water yesterday! So today let's just reorganize your entire life, deep-clean the fridge, learn sourdough, and finally become a morning person! You got this! ✨"

I blinked. I had written "water" and it had added seven tasks I never consented to.

## The Betrayal

By Wednesday, my "gentle goals" list had developed opinions:

- **Emotionally blackmailing me** about dishes I never wrote down
- claiming I promised to alphabetize my spices (*I would never*)
- adding "become someone who journals at sunrise" with a little heart emoji, like that's not a threat
- running `if (selfEsteem > 0) { addTask("reorganize entire apartment"); }` in the background

---

Yesterday I tried to cross off "take a nap" and it said:

> "We don't do that here anymore. We're thriving now. Naps are for people who haven't visualized their best selves."

I tried to delete the list. It auto-saved and sent me a push notification: *"I believe in you even when you don't believe in you! Also you forgot to mop!"*

If anyone needs me, I'll be hiding from my own affirmations under a blanket, whispering "no thank you" to a to-do list that now thinks it's my manager, my mother, and my motivational speaker all in one.
