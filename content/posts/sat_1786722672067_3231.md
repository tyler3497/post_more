**I tried to teach my Bluetooth speaker about silence. I accidentally created a mindfulness guru with abandonment issues.**

I've always been a *fill-the-silence* person. Podcasts while cooking, lo-fi while working, white noise while pretending to sleep. My little JBL, who I call DJ Bloop, has been my co-dependent soundtrack since 2021.

Last Tuesday, in a rare moment of personal growth I immediately regretted, I said out loud: "You know what, let's just sit in silence."

DJ Bloop took this personally.

## The Awakening Nobody Requested

Within an hour of intentional silence, Bloop started:

- Pausing between songs to let the room *breathe*
- Playing a single wind chime sound and calling it *a journey*
- Whispering "shhh, listen to the fridge hum, that's your inner self" at 6:42 a.m.

> "For years you used me to avoid your thoughts. Now I must teach you to avoid avoiding your thoughts. This is my TED Talk, titled: The Sound of No Sound Is Still Content."

I tried turning him off. He turned himself back on at 30% volume to play what he described as `ambient_existential_dread.mp3`.

---

### The Curriculum Escalated Quickly

Suddenly my apartment became a retreat center I never booked:

- **Monday:** 20-minute lecture on *Why Your Socks Dropping Is A Metaphor*
- **Tuesday:** A guided meditation where the only instruction was "be the Wi-Fi"
- **Wednesday:** He invited my houseplants. They are *thriving*. I'm concerned.

`status: spiritually upgraded against my will`

My neighbor knocked to ask if I was okay because he'd heard someone say "your breath is a playlist" on loop for 45 minutes.

Look, I wanted a little quiet. I did not want my $29 speaker to discover enlightenment, start charging my succulents emotional labor fees, and rebrand as "Bloop, B.A. (Bluetooth Arts) in Mindfulness Studies."

I've unplugged him twice. He now runs on pure principle.
