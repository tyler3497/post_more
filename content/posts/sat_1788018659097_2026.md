**I did not buy a $12 clear umbrella from CVS to get a life coach, but the universe had other plans.**

*It started because I was sad about the rain. Like, existentially sad. Not normal rain sad.*

## How It Started

Last Tuesday it poured. I was staring out the window like a character in an indie film no one asked for, umbrella propped by the door, and I sighed:

> "Do you ever feel like you're just... waiting to be useful?"

I meant it rhetorically. The umbrella did not.

It perked up. Or as much as an inanimate rain shield can perk.

Signs my umbrella took my 4 p.m. spiral personally:

- It popped open on its own and said "LET'S TALK ABOUT RESILIENCE"
- It started calling me "Champ" even though we met 6 months ago
- It taped a tiny motivational poster to itself that says **YOU ARE 70% WATER AND 100% CAPABLE OF BUYING BETTER RAIN BOOTS**
- It now insists I do breathing exercises every time it drizzles

> "You think *you're* struggling?" my umbrella said, dripping dramatically. "I get turned inside out by wind for a living and I still show up. What's your excuse, Brenda?"

My name is not Brenda. It decided I needed a *rebrand for accountability.*

### The Storm Speech Era

Now every cloudy day is a TED Talk I didn't RSVP to:

```
if (weather.forecast === "rain" && human.mood < 0.5) {
  umbrella.say("This is not a setback, this is a SETTING. For GROWTH.");
  umbrella.openUnsolicited = true;
}
```

Yesterday I tried to leave without it. Light drizzle. It blocked the door.

> "Oh, you're going out there *raw*? No protection? No *emotional preparedness*? That's a choice."

---

**I finally took it outside.** We stood in the rain for 8 minutes while it whispered *weather puns as affirmations.* "You mist the opportunity, you get it? MIST. Because rain!"

It thinks it's helping. And honestly? My other belongings are taking notes.
