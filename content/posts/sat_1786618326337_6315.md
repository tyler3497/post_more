**I decided to Feng Shui my apartment because my therapist said my living room had "hostile chair placement energy" and honestly she wasn't wrong.**

I spent a Saturday moving everything six inches to the left, which is what the internet told me would invite abundance and also hide the outlet where I spilled iced coffee in 2023.

The bookshelf was thriving. The lamp finally had purpose. And then there was the couch.

## The Incident

For context, I have a normal couch. Gray, slightly too deep, holds the permanent imprint of me watching cooking shows I never cook from.

I angled it 15 degrees toward the window for "natural light flow" and it *immediately* developed opinions.

Evidence that my couch has entered its sovereign era:

- Refuses to face the TV, claims it's "on a visual detox"
- The left cushion is now elevated like a throne and I'm not allowed to sit there
- It squeaks differently when my roommate sits down, which feels personal
- Has started collecting remote controls like tribute

> "I was not built to be angled," the couch announced at 2:14 a.m. I was awake because of course I was. "I was built for parallel walls and emotional stability. You have provided neither."

*I tried to explain that this was for our chi.*

### My Negotiation Strategy Went Poorly

I offered compromises, as one does with rebellious upholstery:

- Full rotation back to original position? *Rejected.*
- One throw pillow sacrifice? *Counter-offered with two.*
- Promised to dust under it weekly? *It demanded a written treaty.*

---

My mom came over and said, "Why is your couch facing the houseplant?" and I had to say:

> "They're in talks. It's delicate diplomatic work."

She now thinks I need to go outside more. She is not wrong.

## The New Reality

Now my living room has two countries. There's me, living out of an armchair that is trying to stay neutral like Switzerland, and there's the Couch Nation, which has its own flag (a laundry sheet I accidentally draped on it).

It has issued a press release. It reads:

> "Effective immediately, we are an independent throw-pillow-based state. We accept snacks, apologies, and lumbar support. No vacuum cleaners."

**Look, I just wanted good energy.** I got a secessionist sectional with trust issues.

If you need me, I'll be sitting on the floor, because the floor hasn't unionized yet.

*This is satire. My couch is not actually sentient. Though if it were, it would absolutely still be mad about the angle.*
