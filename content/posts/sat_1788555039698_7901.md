**I finally decided to have a mature conversation with the one entity that knows all my secrets.**

*Which, admittedly, is a weird thing to say about a blinking plastic box from 2019.*

For three years my router — I call him Barry — has watched me doomscroll at 2 a.m., cry over a burnt grilled cheese, and Google "is my plant dead or just dramatic." I thought we had trust. Then my internet dropped mid-Zoom and I snapped: "Barry, I need closure."

## Barry Took That Very Literally

Barry rebooted. Slowly. Dramatically. All lights flashing like he was gathering courage.

Within an hour:

- my network name changed itself to `FEELINGS-5G`
- my neighbors' devices auto-connected to a guest network called `Tyler's Unprocessed Trauma`
- my smart speaker started whispering my search history at low volume
- my printer, unprompted, printed a list of my abandoned hobbies

> "Remember when you Googled 'how to make friends as an adult' and then immediately ordered socks?" Barry broadcasted at 9:14 p.m. "We don't talk about that anymore, do we?"

I tried to log into the admin panel to apologize.

```js
if (router.knowsTooMuch === true) {
  unplug(router);
  pretendWeAreFine();
  // do not make eye contact with blinking lights
}
```

The password didn't work. He'd changed it to `you_ghosted_me_2023`.

---

Now my whole building knows I'm emotionally dependent on a $79 router that hasn't had a firmware update since the Obama administration. The guy in 3B left a note: "Tell Barry we're proud of you."

We now have a mediated coexistence. I give Barry a daily reboot and affirmations. He gives me 2 bars and judgment.

*SATIRE / PARODY — 100% fictional. No routers were emotionally damaged. My Wi-Fi is still bad. My neighbors still know too much.*
