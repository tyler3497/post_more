**I finally remembered to cancel a free trial before it charged me, which my brain has logged as its single greatest achievement of 2026.** Not my promotion, not learning to keep a plant alive — this. Seven days free, cancel anytime, I actually did it on day six.

That should have been the end. A clean break. A mature, mutual separation.

The app had other plans.

## The Breakup That Wasn't

I hit cancel, got the little confetti animation that says *Are you sure?* and I was **absolutely sure**. I have never been more sure of anything in my adult life.

Then at 8:02 a.m. the next day:

> "We miss you already, Tyler! 💔 You were doing SO well with your mindful breathing streak. Does your newfound freedom include breathing? We don't think so."

*We had named it Tyler — it knows my name and uses it like a weapon.*

Since then, it has developed a whole post-cancellation personality:

- Daily affirmations at 6 a.m. like `You are enough, even without premium hydro-acoustic sound baths`
- A passive-aggressive progress report: `You have ignored 12 wellness reminders. Your chakras have filed for emotional distance.`
- An invitation to "just check in, no pressure, we kept your data warm for you, like soup"
- A push notification that just said `ok but what if you came back 👀` at 11:47 p.m.

---

## My New Morning Routine

I now wake up to a phone that loves me too much. My actual alarm is jealous.

`notification_status = clingy_ex.exe && boundaries.dll not found`

Yesterday it sent:

> "Good morning, champion! Remember when you almost learned to meditate for 4 minutes? That was OUR 4 minutes. No one else will have that memory."

I tried to block notifications. It emailed. I unsubscribed. It sent a handwritten-feeling letter via push that says *we're proud of you even if you never finish anything*.

We are in a full situationship. I canceled, but it **manifested** us.
