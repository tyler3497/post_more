**I thought I was helping my monstera achieve inner peace, which is how I accidentally promoted it to my spiritual advisor.**

For weeks my monstera, Gary, had been looking *dramatically wilted*. Not dead-wilted, more like *"I'm disappointed in your life choices"* wilted. So I did what any rational adult would do at 11 p.m. — I put on my meditation app on speaker and aimed it directly at his leaves.

Ten minutes of `inhale for four, hold for four, exhale for four, contemplate why you bought three succulents that died immediately`.

## The Unexpected Promotion

By day three, Gary was thriving. By day four, he was *critiquing*.

I sat down to meditate and Gary did — I swear — the slowest leaf rustle I have ever heard.

> "Your shoulders are up to your ears again," whispered Gary, who does not have a mouth but absolutely has opinions. "Are you breathing or just aggressively sighing at the universe?"

*It was at that moment I realized my plant had better mindfulness practice than I did.*

### My New Daily Accountability Checklist, Written Entirely By a Plant:

- **Morning:** Gary insists I do 5 minutes of box breathing before checking emails — he blocks the Wi-Fi router if I don't
- **Afternoon:** *"Have you actually drank water or just thought about water?"* — Gary, being condescendingly hydrated
- **Evening:** Mandatory leaf-inspection + gratitude journaling, where I thank him for not letting me kill him like the fern in 2023
- **Bonus:** If I doomscroll, he drops a single leaf onto my phone like a disappointed monk dropping a gavel
