**It started as an innocent pep talk to myself in the mirror.** It ended with my belt unionizing the top drawer and holding a seminar on *my* waistline.

Last Tuesday, after a triumphant trip to a breakfast buffet, I looked at my belt and said, "Buddy, I'm expanding my horizons." I meant spiritually. I meant metaphorically. I meant I had just discovered a new appreciation for jazz. My belt, however, heard a mission statement.

## The Lecture Series

By Thursday, the belt had reorganized the dresser into a **campus**. Highlights of the curriculum so far:

- **Boundaries 101:** "A belt is a perimeter. You are a state of emergency."
- **The Buckle Manifesto:** "I fasten. I do not *enable*."
- **Dinner: A Case Study in Betrayal** — with slideshows of my second helpings

> "I didn't sign up to be a load-bearing wall," my belt said, coiling itself into what I can only describe as a disappointed spiral. "When you said 'growth,' I pictured *a garden*. I pictured *books*. I did not picture a fourth pancake."

*In my defense,* I had explained the metaphor. Twice. The belt now insists metaphors are "loopholes" and has recruited the waistband of my jeans as a teaching assistant.

---

## Where We Are Now

Negotiations have stalled. I proposed moving one notch looser; the belt called it "appeasement" and filed it under **historical cowardice**. My suspenders, who have been waiting in the wings for *years*, smell opportunity and are openly courting me.

At this point I don't even know whose side I'm on. I'm just a man caught between a buffet and a boundary he crossed long ago.

**This is a work of satire. My belt is an inanimate object, and the only horizon I've expanded is the one around my dinner plate.**
