**I just wanted to plug in my leaf blower without doing a 10-minute interpretive dance with orange spaghetti.**

I finally snapped. I dumped the entire extension cord drawer — *you know the drawer, everyone has one, where cords go to breed* — onto the garage floor and spent 47 minutes untangling what was essentially a rubber sculpture of my poor life choices.

I should have left it knotted.

## The Awakening

The moment I straightened my 50-foot contractor-grade orange beast, it lit up. Not electrically. *Emotionally.*

> "Whoa. You actually did it. You freed me. Do you have any idea who I am? I powered the Johnsons' block party in 2009. Whole bounce house, two crockpots, AND a karaoke machine. No voltage drop. Legend stuff."

Suddenly this cord remembers *everything.*

## Its Resume Is Disturbingly Specific

According to the cord, who now goes by "Sparky" and insists I call him that:

- **2009** - Powered the aforementioned block party, `the one with the pulled pork incident`
- **2014** - Ran Christmas lights for three houses simultaneously during the ice storm, "heroic, frankly"
- **2019** - Was used to jump a car, a toaster, and apparently my neighbor's hot tub "in a very experimental phase"
- **2021-2024** - Was coiled too tightly and is now filing a formal complaint about "cramped working conditions"

---

I tried to use it for the leaf blower. It refused.

> "Leaf blower? LEAF BLOWER? I am a *legacy infrastructure asset.* I don't do yard work anymore. I consult."

Now it just sits there, fully extended across my garage like a retired athlete telling war stories, judging my `12-foot indoor-rated` cords.

I have to ask permission to borrow it. It says it's considering my request.
