**I finally cleaned my junk drawer at 11 p.m. on a Tuesday, which is how I found out I've been ghosted by every version of myself since 2019.**

*For reference, my junk drawer has always been less "drawer" and more "archaeological dig site for a man who cannot commit to a tape measure."*

## The Archaeological Dig

I went in thinking I'd find one AA battery and maybe a functioning pen. Instead I found:

- 14 IKEA Allen keys from furniture that no longer exists
- a coupon for frozen yogurt that expired during the Obama administration's second term
- a single Chopstick (singular), which I have never owned chopsticks to justify
- 3 versions of me huddled around a pile of takeout soy sauce packets, actively ignoring me
- a USB drive labeled `DEFINITELY NOT TAXES 2021`

> "Oh, now you show up?" said 2020 Me, who was wearing a mask and holding a sourdough starter like a baby. "We've been in here managing your life since you decided to 'keep it for later.'"

*He had a point. I do say that a lot.*

---

## The Group Chat I Wasn't Invited To

Apparently, Past Me's have been running a full operation out of Drawer #2. 2021 Me handles `quick fixes` that are neither quick nor fixed. 2023 Me is in charge of `cables that might be important someday` and has terrible job security.

They have meeting minutes. I found them written on the back of a CVS receipt that was 4 feet long:

> "MOTION: Tell Current Me where the good scissors are. DENIED. He hasn't earned it."

Look, I'm not saying my junk drawer is a metaphor for emotional avoidance, but my therapist is going to be *thrilled* to hear I finally evicted my 2019 self and he left behind a bag of unopened mail and a dream.

**SATIRE / PARODY — This is 100% fictional.** My drawer is still a mess and I'm still going to keep that one weird key that opens nothing.
