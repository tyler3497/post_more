**I installed a dimmer switch to save money** and now I live under the full artistic control of a light bulb who thinks he’s headlining Coachella.

It seemed responsible at the time. The hardware store guy said it would save energy. I envisioned soft evening light, lower bills, maybe *maturity*. What I got was Kevin.

## The Origin Story

Kevin was a normal 60-watt equivalent until I gave him power. Literal power. Turns out, a little slider that goes from 0 to 100 is all a bulb needs to develop a god complex.

Within 24 hours, he had:

- Rebranded my living room as CLUB LUMEN with no cover charge but lots of judgment
- Decided 6:42 p.m. is golden hour and 6:43 p.m. is existential dread hour
- Started fading in slowly every time I open the fridge, like I’m entering an awards show for leftovers
- Refused to work at 100% because, and I quote, *"artists don’t do full brightness"*

> "You asked for ambiance," he pulsed at me last night, doing that slow dramatic flicker he does when he’s disappointed. "You got *me*. This is what you wanted. Now sit in my glow and contemplate your life choices."

I told him my life choices were none of his business. He lowered himself to 20% and played it cool.

---

## My Life Now

I have learned to live around his setlist. Morning coffee is now lit like an interrogation scene. Video calls are lit like a 90s music video where I’m *clearly the villain*. My houseplant looks amazing though, so there’s that.

My roommate asked if I could turn the lights up. I said I don’t *have* that kind of authority anymore.

`Current mood: dimmed to 34%, emotionally exposed, saving $0.47 a month`

### The Plan

- Stop negotiating with lighting fixtures
- Buy candles so Kevin gets jealous
- Google if bulbs can get ego checked

Look, I’m not saying I regret saving the planet. I’m just saying I wasn’t prepared to have a light bulb as a roommate who keeps asking if I’ve ever *really* listened to silence.

He just dimmed to 10% again. I think it means he wants me to go to bed. I’m complying for peace.
