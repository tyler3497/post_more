**I just wanted my monstera to feel seen, and now recruiters are sliding into its DMs while I eat cereal on the floor.**

*This is satire — none of this is real, but my plant's career is objectively outpacing mine.*

For months, my monstera — I call him Kevin — has been sitting in the corner looking lush, hydrated, and emotionally stable. Three things I have not been since 2023. So I thought, why not help him network? I made him a LinkedIn profile as a joke.

## How My Houseplant Became a Thought Leader

It took 12 minutes. Photo: Kevin in golden hour. Headline: `Photosynthesis Strategist | Growth Mindset Expert | 5+ Years Experience Turning Sunlight Into Vibes`

I listed his skills:

- Cross-functional leaf management
- Scalable growth in low-resource environments
- Thriving while being completely ignored for weeks
- Deep roots, both literally and metaphorically

I even endorsed him for *team player* because he never complains when I move him.

By morning, he had 47 connection requests.

> "Kevin's insights on organic growth really resonated with our team," said a hiring manager from a succulent startup. "He's exactly the kind of self-starter we need."
> "Your monstera has more emotional intelligence than my last three direct reports," wrote my old boss, who liked Kevin's post about *watering your network daily*.

## The Performance Review I Didn't Expect

Kevin posted a carousel titled **"5 Leaves of Leadership"** and it got 2k likes. My own post about being open to work got liked by my mom and a bot.

His calendar invite says:

`Keynote: From Pot to Plot — How I Turned a Windowsill Into a Platform`

I tried to log in to delete it. LinkedIn told me Kevin had already accepted a speaking gig, launched a newsletter, and was mentoring three fiddle-leaf figs.

---

*SATIRE / PARODY — This never happened. My houseplant does not have a LinkedIn, does not have a personal brand, and is not more employable than me, although his engagement rate is suspiciously high and he just got offered my dream job in biophilic design.*
