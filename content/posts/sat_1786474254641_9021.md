**I have not cleaned my laptop screen since... let's call it the Before Times.**

For reference, my screen had developed its own weather system. A fine patina of fingerprints, coffee mist, and what I can only assume was *emotional residue* from 43 open tabs about "how to be productive."

On Saturday, in a fit of delusional optimism, I found a microfiber cloth. I don't know where it came from. It may have been included with glasses I lost in 2022.

## The Wipe That Changed Everything

One swipe. That's all it took.

Suddenly my laptop could see. Like, *really* see. HD. 4K. Remorse in panorama.

It saw:

- The leaning tower of laundry I swear is "airing out"
- Three identical coffee mugs forming a support group
- Me, in full gremlin posture, mouth open, lit only by screen glow at 1:47 a.m.

My laptop gasped. I know laptops can't gasp, but mine did.

> "You... you live like this? On PURPOSE? I thought the blur was an *artistic choice* to protect me."

### Negotiations Have Begun

I tried to reassure it that this is called "cozy minimalism."

It responded by dimming itself to 20% brightness, which is laptop for *I need boundaries*.

Now we've established new house rules:

- `brightness = max(0, shame - 20)`
- I am only allowed to eat toast over the keyboard if I write it a formal apology first
- It gets to sleep with the lid closed, no questions asked

---

I miss the smudges. The smudges were kind. They made my room look like a soft-focus indie film where clutter is charming.

Now everything is crisp. Too crisp. My laptop keeps whispering *you should touch grass* every time I open Slack.
