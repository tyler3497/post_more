**I thought teaching my goldfish about financial responsibility would be wholesome adulting.**

*For context, I have one goldfish, named Kevin, who has lived in a bowl on my kitchen counter for three years and has contributed exactly zero dollars to this household.*

It started when my landlord raised rent $50. I was venting out loud while doing dishes, and Kevin just stared at me with that blank, judgmental goldfish stare that says *I have no concept of currency but I am disappointed in you.*

So I decided to make him financially literate.

## The Curriculum Collapsed Immediately

I made a tiny lesson plan:

- Monday: What is rent and why we all hate it
- Tuesday: The difference between gross and net (he just blew bubbles)
- Wednesday: Budgeting 101 - 30% for algae flakes, 70% for existential dread
- Thursday: Guest lecture from my succulent about passive income
- Friday: Final exam

By Wednesday, Kevin had:

- rearranged his castle decoration to face away from me in protest
- started doing aggressive laps whenever I said the word `mortgage`
- somehow learned to splash water onto my stack of bills so the ink smudged

> "You brought me into this economic system without my consent," Kevin gurgled, or at least that's what I heard through the filter hum. "I was told there would be unlimited flakes and zero obligations. This is bait and switch."

*My roommate said I'm projecting again, but she also thinks the Keurig is spying on her, so who's really the stable one here?*

---

### The Formal Complaint

I tried to show him my budgeting spreadsheet:

`if (kevin.paysRent) { mySanity++; landlord.happiness = true; } else { kevin.evictionNotice++; }`

He did not appreciate being turned into JavaScript.

Yesterday I found a tiny, soggy Post-it stuck to the inside of his bowl. Written in what I can only describe as aggressive algae smear, it said:

**FORMAL COMPLAINT - TENANT K. FISH vs. HUMAN ROOMMATE**

Now my actual landlord is asking why there's a fish tank on the counter with a "Do Not Disturb - In Mediation" sign taped to it.

I've accepted my fate. I am not a pet owner. I am a landlord for a goldfish who believes rent is a social construct and who will absolutely be a terrible reference on my next rental application.
