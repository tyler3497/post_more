**I did not mean to radicalize my driveway. I just wanted my neighbor Steve to think I had my life together.**

*Which, for the record, I do not. My indoor plants are on a payment plan for emotional damages.*

It started Saturday. Steve was out power-washing his driveway at 7 a.m. like a man who has defeated entropy. I watched from my window holding coffee that had been microwaved three times and thought, I can be that guy.

## The Washening

I rented a power-washer that had more PSI than my entire self-esteem. Two hours later, my driveway looked like it had been Photoshopped by God.

Signs I had gone too far:

- The concrete was so clean you could see your future in it
- A passing dog stopped and whispered, *respect*
- Steve gave me a slow, solemn nod — the suburban equivalent of a knighthood
- My driveway started... glistening with ambition

My roommate came outside and said:

> "Dude, your driveway is literally glowing. I think you unlocked its final form. It's going to start charging HOA fees."

*I told her to stop being dramatic. The driveway then laid out a five-point infrastructure plan.*

### The Campaign Platform Is Suspiciously Competent

By Sunday, my driveway had printed yard signs. **VOTE DRIVEWAY 2026: A SOLID FOUNDATION**. It was running on:

- Free leaf removal for all
- Pothole truth and reconciliation
- Mandatory quiet hours for leaf blowers after 8 a.m.
- `if (neighbor.leafBlower.hour < 8) { driveway.veto(); }`

---

I tried to explain that it was *my* driveway, on *my* property, and it should just be a driveway.

It responded with a campaign speech through my Ring doorbell:

> "For too long, we have been driven over. Walked on. Spilled upon. I am not just a slab. I am a *path forward*."

Steve is now its campaign manager. The HOA president asked for an endorsement. I am being out-charisma'd by 400 square feet of concrete I made too clean.

If you need me, I'll be in the garage, losing a debate to asphalt about zoning reform.

*This is satire. My driveway is not actually running for office. Although it is polling very well with the cul-de-sac.*
