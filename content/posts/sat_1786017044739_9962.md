## The Archaeological Dig in My Wallet

**I have been emotionally preparing to use this $25 gift card for six years.** Not because I was saving it for something special, *although I was definitely telling people that*, but because I was 94% sure I had lost it under my car seat next to a petrified french fry from the Obama administration.

On Tuesday I found it. In my junk drawer. Next to seventeen AAA batteries and a takeout menu from a restaurant that closed in 2021.

I marched into the store like a man on a mission.

### The Authentication Protocol

The cashier, a lovely 19-year-old named Kaylee who was *absolutely not paid enough for this*, picked up the card like it might detonate.

> "Whoa. This is... this is the old logo. Like, *pre-rebrand* old. Did you get this in... 2019?"

She whispered it like she was saying *the before times*.

Then she did the following:

- Held it up to the light at three different angles
- Scanned it. Nothing. Scanned it again, rotated 180 degrees, as if orientation was the problem
- Typed the number in manually, squinting at the faded receipt font like a cryptographer
- Called her manager over and said the sacred words: `BALANCE: $25.00 - UNTOUCHED`

The manager arrived wearing gloves. *I'm not kidding.* Okay, I'm slightly kidding. They were fingerless gloves, but still.

---

### The Verdict

**After seven full minutes**, the machine made a triumphant *BEEP* — the kind of beep usually reserved for winning game shows — and printed a receipt that simply said APPROVED, as if even the printer was surprised.

> "Congratulations," Kaylee said. "You just made history. And also, this candle is free now."

I left with a scented candle and the profound sense that I had finally, *finally* closed a chapter of my life. The french fry is still in my car, though. Some artifacts are meant to stay buried.
