**I just wanted to see a single cardinal.** One elegant red bird, maybe a nice Instagram story, maybe a fleeting sense of peace before my inbox refilled.

What I got was organized labor.

## How It Started

I bought a $19 bird feeder from a hardware store, filled it with what the bag confidently called *Premium Woodland Medley*, and hung it on my back fence. Within six hours:

- A sparrow came, judged me, left
- A pigeon tried to Venmo request me for crumbs
- And then — *them*

Two squirrels. Plump. Suspiciously coordinated. They stared at the feeder like it was a bank vault.

> "We the Acorn-Related Workforce of Fence Line 3 hereby declare this feeder a shared community resource," squeaked what I can only assume was their shop steward. He was wearing a tiny bottle-cap as a hard hat.

## The Demands

This morning I found a leaf on my doormat with what appeared to be tiny teeth-marks spelling out a list of demands. My bird feeder has been reclassified as a **"vertical seed-based fulfillment center."**

According to the Squirrel Union Local 7:

- **Unlimited sunflower seeds**, not that discount millet they call *bird cereal*
- A water cooler, *which they have installed themselves* using my dog's bowl
- Mandatory 15-minute tail-flick breaks every hour
- Full `dental` for all incisors damaged on my "aggressively cheap" feeder hatch
- Recognition of Acorn Friday as a paid holiday

---

## The Negotiation

I tried to reason with them. I told them I bought this for *birds*.

> "Birds?" said the bigger squirrel, now wearing safety goggles made from a blueberry container. "Birds are management. We're the ones doing the actual foraging."

They've started picketing. They just sit on the fence in a little line, holding even littler signs, chittering in unison. My neighbor thinks I'm running a very depressing puppet show.

I caved. I put out better seeds. They left me a single acorn as a thank-you bonus and a note that says `status: negotiating in good faith (for now)`.

*SATIRE / PARODY — This is entirely fictional. No squirrels were union-busted in the making of this essay, though one did steal my entire bag of trail mix and file it as a signing bonus.*
