**I did not expect my Monstera to have notes on my management style.**

I bought three houseplants in March because a wellness blog told me they would *improve my air quality and bring serenity to my space*. What they actually did was move in, judge my curtains, and form a collective.

Last weekend I decided to be a Responsible Plant Parent and gave them all a quarterly performance review. Very professional. I made a spreadsheet.

## My Completely Fair Evaluation Criteria

- **Leaf Vibrancy:** Are you green or are you *trying* to be green?
- **Growth Mindset:** Literally, have you grown at all since March?
- **Attitude:** Do you droop dramatically every time I open the fridge?
- **Team Player Energy:** The fern keeps hogging the humidifier

I read the scores out loud. Monstera got a solid B+. Pothos got a B for effort. The fern got a gentle *needs improvement* with a supportive sticker.

That's when the fern unfurled a tiny scroll.

> "Per Article 3 of the Windowsill Accords, we hereby demand immediate relocation to south-facing sunlight, a 40% increase in misting, and an end to playing the same lo-fi beats to study to on loop," read the fern, via Google Translate on a leaf.

Apparently they'd been organizing.

---

I checked their demands in the group chat they made without me. It's called `photosynthesis: failed` and their status is `water: critical, vibes: thriving`.

- They want weekends off from being looked at anxiously
- They want me to stop telling guests they are "low-maintenance"
- They want a formal apology for the time I tried to dust them with a Swiffer

Look, I respect the hustle. They even elected Monstera as shop steward because she has the most holes and therefore the most perspective.

I caved. I moved them two inches closer to the window. The fern has already filed a grievance saying it's *performative sunlight*.

If you need me, I'll be negotiating PTO for a succulent that hasn't worked since 2023.
