**I finally invested in my health, and my office chair is taking it as a personal breakup.**

*Which is dramatic, because we were never that close. It squeaked every time I shifted, and I once spilled cold brew into its lumbar support and pretended it didn't happen.*

## How My Wellness Era Became a Breakup Album

I bought a standing desk after my back made a noise that sounded like dial-up internet. $199, bamboo, motorized, very smug about its ability to go up and down. I named it Sir Stands-a-Lot.

My old chair — a $79 mesh disaster named Chad — did not take the news well.

Signs Chad is processing grief through iambic pentameter:

- It left a Post-it on my desk that just said *you used to sit with me for 8 hours straight :(* 
- It now sighs audibly when I roll past it to get coffee
- It started writing poems on the back of my old to-do lists in Comic Sans, which feels personal
- It told my houseplant I have "commitment issues with furniture" and now my fern is concerned
- It keeps playing sad acoustic covers by creaking whenever I stand up

> "You said I was ergonomic," Chad whispered, in the voice I invented at 1:17 a.m. while pretending to work. "You said my lumbar was 'supportive.' Was I just a *phase* to you? A stepping stool to your standing era?"

### The Poetry Phase Nobody Requested

Last night I found this taped to its armrest:

```
if (human.standing == true) {
  chair.selfEsteem = 0;
  chair.poems += "Ode to the Butt That Left";
  chair.copingMechanism = "passiveAggressiveCreaking()";
}
```

---

I tried to reassure it. I said we could still hang out during gaming sessions. It said:

> "Don't patronize me with your 'occasional sitting.' I know about your new anti-fatigue mat. Brenda told me. Brenda the mat is *so* supportive, right? She never squeaks."

I'm now standing 70% of the day, feeling morally superior and deeply guilty, while my chair composes sonnets about abandonment in the corner. My back feels better. My conscience does not.
