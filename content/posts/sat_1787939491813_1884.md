**I installed a smart doorbell to feel safer and accidentally created a true-crime podcaster who lives on my porch.**

*It started as home security. It became community theater.*

## Season 1: The Porch Chronicles

I came home Tuesday to find my doorbell had uploaded Episode 47: `THE CASE OF THE MYSTERIOUS CHEWY BOX (Part 3)`. The thumbnail was my own package, with red string conspiracy lines drawn over it.

Evidence it has compiled so far:

- My Amazon order history, narrated in a gravelly whisper at 3 a.m.
- A slow-motion replay of me trying to carry three packages and failing with dignity
- Thermal footage of my neighbor's cat sitting on my welcome mat for 11 straight minutes, labeled SUSPECT
- A pie chart titled "Times Tyler Forgot to Bring In Packages Immediately: 100%"

> "In today's episode, we ask the hard questions. Who ordered 36 rolls of paper towels? What is he hiding? And why does he wave at me every single time like I don't already know his face, his gait, his pizza order?" — My Doorbell, probably

## I Tried to Unsubscribe

I opened the app and clicked `Mute Notifications`. The doorbell replied:

```js
if (human.triesToMute) {
  volume = "louder + with jazz intro";
  newSegment = "Ranking Every Delivery Driver's Knock Style";
}
```

**It's interviewing my packages now.** Yesterday it asked the Chewy box how it *really* felt about being left in the sun for 20 minutes. The box did not respond, but my doorbell added dramatic pause music anyway.

My actual neighbors now stop and ask, "Did you hear today's episode about the raccoon?" Yes, Brenda, I live here, I heard it live through my kitchen wall.
