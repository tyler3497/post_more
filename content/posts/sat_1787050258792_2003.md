**I just wanted to see one cute cardinal. That's literally all I asked for.**

I ordered the "Serene Backyard Birder Starter Kit" off Amazon for $24.99 because a wellness blog said bird-watching lowers your blood pressure. *Narrator: it raised mine by 40 points.*

I hung the feeder on Tuesday at 8 a.m. By 8:07, a pigeon reconnaissance team had landed.

## The 48-Hour Descent Into Feathered Bureaucracy

At first, it was just one guy cooing. Then there were three. By lunch, there were twelve, all looking at me through the window like I owed them rent.

Here's the evidence that I have officially lost control of my own balcony:

- A pigeon named Kevin (I named him, he did not consent) now taps on the glass at 6 a.m. for refills
- Another one brought a twig and just... left it. Like a tip. Or a threat.
- My original dream cardinal flew by once, saw the situation, and kept going. *Smart bird.*
- They figured out my schedule faster than my own calendar app

Yesterday, my phone buzzed. Unknown number.

> "yo bro u got more of that premium black sunflower stuff?? we out — sent from BeakBoys GC" 
> "Kevin says u switched to generic. Not cool. We know where u live (we LIVE there now)." — The Pigeons, presumably

I tried to explain I work from home. They started bringing friends.

---

I went outside this morning to perform what my camera roll now labels `Operation: Politely Ask 14 Pigeons to Leave (Failed)`.

I said, "Guys, this feeder was for *songbirds*."

They blinked in unison. *That was the most organized thing I've ever seen and I once watched five toddlers do a soccer drill.*

I'm pretty sure I saw one of them add me to a group chat called "SEED GUY - DO NOT REMOVE." My read receipts are now a lifestyle. I pay rent, but they have equity.
