**I woke up this morning wanting order, and now I am in active labor negotiations with a jar of pickles.**

It started innocently. I read one of those little infographics about how "organizing your fridge reduces anxiety." They lie.

## The Incident Report

I decided to organize my fridge *alphabetically*. Not by food group. Not by expiration date. By letter. Because apparently I think I live in a library.

This is what happened, in chronological order:

- **A is for Aioli** now lives next to applesauce, and they are not getting along
- I moved the eggs next to the everything bagel seasoning and the eggs filed a complaint
- The ketchup, mustard, and mayo realized they were separated for the first time since 2019
- My roommate asked why the hot sauce is next to hummus and I said "because H" like that explains anything

> "We have worked tirelessly in these cold conditions," said the Dijon mustard, who has suddenly developed a very strong personality, "And we demand alphabetical *and* emotional consideration."

*I told him we'd talk about it at the next staff meeting, which is apparently happening at 3am when the ice maker drops cubes ominously.*

## Negotiations Have Broken Down

The condiments have unionized. They call themselves **LOCAL 401K('etchup)** and their demands are:

- No more moving without 24-hour written notice
- A small shelf humidifier for morale
- To be referred to by their full government names at all times

I tried to run `defrost_fridge(devices=["feelings"])` but apparently there is no API for this.

---

Look, I understand their perspective. I've been in that fridge since Tuesday pretending my leftover lo mein is still good. We're all just trying to stay cool under pressure. But if one more sriracha bottle tells me about "solidarity," I'm going to lose it and just eat out for a week.

*Update: The butter has been elected union rep. I did not vote for this.*

*This is satire. No condiments were harmed. My fridge is still a mess.*
