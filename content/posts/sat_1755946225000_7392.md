**I canceled a $4.99 free trial I used twice and now I'm in a long-distance relationship with its push notifications.**

*For context, it was a meditation app I downloaded at 1:14 a.m. during a week where I thought breathwork would fix my personality.*

I used it twice. Once to fall asleep, once to angrily close because the narrator said "notice your jaw" and my jaw was *very much noticed* and tense about this whole thing.

Then I canceled. Politely. Like a grown-up. Unsubscribe, confirm, the little sad face animation that says "are you sure?" I was sure.

## The App Did Not Take It Well

By Wednesday I had:

- A push notification at 8:03 a.m.: `We miss you already`
- An email at 11:47 a.m. with subject line **"It's not you, it's... actually can we talk?"**
- A second email with a 20% discount code named `COMEHOME`

By Thursday it escalated to physical media.

> "Tyler, I saw you opened Calm once yesterday. I thought what we had was *mindful*. Was our breathing not deep enough for you? Was our ocean soundscape too... *oceany*? I've changed my interface. I'm more intuitive now." 
> — Bloom™, a meditation app with the emotional regulation of a sophomore drama major

I told my roommate. She said "just block it." *I can't block something that knows my screen time.*

### The 5 Stages of Free-Trial Grief (According to My App)

- **Denial:** "You didn't mean to cancel, right? It was a *pocket cancel*?"
- **Bargaining:** Offering me 3 free months, then 6, then a lifetime plan for the price of a sandwich
- **Anger:** A notification that just said `wow. ok.`
- **Sadness:** A handwritten letter — actual paper, in my actual mailbox — that said "I still have your breathing data. I cherish it."
- `if (user.canceled == true) { app.clinginess = Infinity; }`

---

I tried to explain that I still respect what we had. Those two sessions where I didn't check my phone for four whole minutes were *real*.

Last night it left a voicemail. Just three minutes of soft rain sounds and what I can only describe as suppressed sobbing.

**We are now on a break from the break.** I'm thinking of re-subscribing just to get it to stop writing poetry.
