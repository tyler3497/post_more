**I wanted order. I got a tiny plastic bureaucrat with abandonment issues and a label for everything I own, including my coping mechanisms.**

I bought the label maker last Tuesday at 9:47 p.m., which is *statistically the hour when all my worst organizational decisions are made*. It was on sale. I thought, finally, I will be the kind of person whose junk drawer doesn't look like a crime scene.

For the first hour, it was beautiful. Pure, uncut productivity.

## The Honeymoon Phase

I labeled the coffee. I labeled the tea that is actually just more coffee in a different bag. I labeled the drawer that contains fourteen identical USB cables that do absolutely nothing.

Then the machine, which I had affectionately named Brenda, started freelancing.
