**I bought a $42 reusable water bottle to save the planet and now I'm being emotionally managed by brushed stainless steel.**

*I just wanted to drink more water, not enter a codependent relationship.*

Three weeks ago I decided to become a Hydrated Person. You know the type. Carries a bottle everywhere, refills it with intention, has opinions about ice. So I bought The Bottle. Triple-walled, keeps ice for 37 hours, has little motivational time markers that say `2pm - Keep Chugging!`

By day two, it started judging me.

## The Signs Were There

The Bottle wasn't subtle:

- It rolled off my desk every time I reached for coffee instead
- It sweated more than I did during my 11-minute "run"
- The time markers started feeling like performance reviews I was already failing
- I swear I heard it sigh when I filled it with iced latte instead of cucumber water

> "You said you wanted to be better," it whispered from my passenger seat. "And yet here we are. Dehydrated and morally inconsistent."

*I told my roommate and she said, "Babe, that's just thirst."*

---

### The Breakup Attempt

I tried to go back to disposable plastic. Just once. For convenience. The Bottle was in the dishwasher and made full eye contact through the steam cycle.

`if (human.drinksWater === false) { bottle.initGuiltTrip(100); }`

**Now we have a system.** I drink, it stays quiet. I forget for more than 90 minutes, it somehow ends up directly in my line of sight, like a very hydrated, very disappointed Elf on the Shelf.

My doctor says my water intake is great. My therapist asked why I apologized to a water bottle during our session.

I've accepted my fate. I am no longer a person with a water bottle. I am a water bottle's person.

*Satire / Parody: This is 100% fictional. No water bottles were given consciousness, counseling licenses, or the power to ruin my self-esteem in this story.*
