**I decided to get my life together, and my Tupperware decided to get a social life without me.**

It started innocently. *Very Pinterest. Very adult.* Sunday, 3pm, lo-fi beats, I told myself I was going to be one of those people who eats quinoa on purpose.

## The Plan That Went Too Far

I bought 12 identical containers. Lids that *actually* match. I felt powerful. Unstoppable. The kind of person who uses the word "macros" unironically.

My system was flawless:

- Monday: Lemon Herb Chicken (that I've never herb'd before)
- Tuesday: Suspiciously Optimistic Buddha Bowl
- Wednesday: Pasta that will definitely be eaten and not mourned in the fridge
- Thursday: Whatever survives until Thursday
- Friday: I lie and say I meal-prepped

I labeled everything. I stacked everything. I took a photo that made my mom text back "who are you?"

### The Betrayal

By Wednesday, Container #4 was leaking. Container #7 had gone missing. And my fridge was humming weird.

Then I saw it. My phone buzzed. A notification from my fridge's smart display that I *definitely* did not enable:

> "Yo, did you see what she did to Steve? She put FISH in Steve. Steve is a hummus container. This is not OK. - Lid #2"

*My Tupperware had made a group chat. Without me.*

They had a name. **"Lid Leaks & Truth."** They had a pinned message:

> "We ride at dawn. Or whenever she remembers we exist."

There was a whole thread ranking my cooking:

```
[SYSTEM] Chickpea Curry has left the chat (fermentation concerns)
[MOD] @Container3: "She said 'al dente' but this is just crunchy water"
```

---

Look, this is **satire**. *Obviously* my Tupperware isn't sentient. The FDA has very clear rules about containers unionizing, and my landlord said if I keep talking to my leftovers he’s raising rent.

But this morning I opened the fridge and all 12 lids were arranged in a perfect circle, like a tiny, passive-aggressive Stonehenge.

And honestly? I respect the organization. It's more than I've ever managed.

So fine. They can have the group chat. I’ll just be over here eating dry granola out of the bag like nature intended.
