**I decided to be friendly for once and waved at my neighbors. This was my first mistake.**

*For context, I have lived in this neighborhood for three years communicating exclusively through trash-bin nods and aggressive curtain-twitching.*

I thought a little wave would make me seem approachable. Human. The kind of person who knows what day recycling goes out. Instead, it triggered a chain of events that ended with me being sworn in as President of the Neighborhood Raccoon Watch at 7:13 a.m. on a Tuesday.

## How I Accidentally Became an Elected Official

It started with Mrs. Henderson across the street. I waved. She waved back, then marched over with a clipboard.

- Monday: "You waved! You must care about community safety!"
- Tuesday: She adds me to a group chat called RACCOON WATCH 2026 with 14 unread messages, all in caps
- Wednesday: Someone posts a blurry photo of a raccoon that is 90% shadow and 10% existential threat
- Thursday: I am listed as "President (Interim, but also Forever)" in the neighborhood newsletter

> "Tyler, we need someone young who knows how to use night vision," Mrs. Henderson said, handing me binoculars that were just two toilet paper rolls taped together. "The raccoons are unionizing."

*I do not know how to use night vision. I can barely use my air fryer without setting off the smoke alarm.*

### My Presidential Duties Are Extensive and Unpaid

My official responsibilities now include:

- Monitoring the Hendersons' trash cans from 9pm to 4am in a lawn chair
- Documenting "suspicious rustling" in a shared Google Doc titled `raccoon_crimes.xlsx`
- Explaining to the HOA why I was found whispering "we see you" at a bush at midnight

I tried to implement policy. I wrote:

`if (raccoon.isCute && raccoon.isStealingHotDogs) { letItSlide = true; notifyPresident = false }`

The council vetoed it. They said I was soft on trash panda crime.

---

**I have not seen a single raccoon.** I have seen my neighbor Gary in a raccoon costume trying to test my readiness, which I failed because I offered him a granola bar and asked if he was registered to vote.

The worst part is I am now invested. I bought a headlamp. I made business cards. They say PRESIDENT / RACCOON AFFAIRS. My mom is proud for the wrong reasons.

I just wanted to be the kind of neighbor who waves. Now I am the kind of neighbor who knows which bins have padlocks and which raccoons have nicknames. Sir Nibbles has not been seen since the incident with the leftover lasagna.

*SATIRE / PARODY — This is 100% fictional. No raccoons were surveilled, no neighborhood coups occurred, and I am absolutely not qualified to lead anything, especially not a wildlife militia.*
