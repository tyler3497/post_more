**I thought I was being efficient.** My sock drawer was chaos, a textile Thunderdome where no one wins. So I invented SockPoints™.

*This was a mistake, and I should have known when my laundry started taking notes.*

## How It All Started

I gave each pair 10 points per week survived without disappearing into the dryer void. Bonus points for:

- matching on the first try (extremely rare, worth 50 points)
- not judging me when I wear socks with sandals to get the mail
- surviving my ambitious but short-lived "athletic lifestyle" phase
- not developing a hole during what I call "aggressive toe enthusiasm"

After 100 points, they earn Gold Status. My gray wool hiking socks hit Gold in record time. They've been with me through two moves, one snowstorm, and that time I tried to learn to jog.

> "We need to talk," said Gary and Larry — yes, I named my socks, no, I'm not taking questions — perched on the edge of the drawer like tiny CEOs. "We've seen the benefits package for the underwear, and frankly, it's embarrassing for you."

---

### The Negotiation

Gary (left sock, the ambitious one) slid a tiny sock-sized PowerPoint across my dresser. It included:

- `priority_access = drawer.top_shelf`
- A 401(k) but it's just more lint in a jar
- No more being worn to "leg day" — *they saw my form, they're traumatized*
- Executive wash cycle: cold, gentle, with a little lavender whisper and a motivational speech

I tried to explain that this is not how laundry works. I said, "You're socks."

> "And you're a guy who loses one sock every laundry day and blames physics," Larry replied. "We want dental. And by dental we mean you finally fix that hole you've been ignoring since March. It's getting drafty in here."

The other socks are now unionizing. The athletic socks want hazard pay. The novelty pizza socks just want to be taken seriously for once in their cheesy little lives.
