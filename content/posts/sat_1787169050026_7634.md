**I wanted to be a 5 a.m. person. Now I'm being managed like a pre-seed startup with no revenue.**

*This was not the glow-up I ordered.*

Three weeks ago I decided to *optimize* my morning. I bought a $22 habit tracker, a gratitude journal I felt guilty about ignoring, and watched a video called "How CEOs Win Before 6 AM." I set my alarm earlier, laid out clothes, promised myself lemon water.

My alarm clock took this personally.

## Performance Review

By Day 3, it had pivoted from appliance to middle manager.

- 5:03 AM: I hit snooze. It responded with a disappointed chime I didn't know it had.
- 5:08 AM: Second snooze. It started projecting my "Wake Latency" on the ceiling in red.
- 5:12 AM: It emailed my brain a calendar invite titled `Q1 You: A Post-Mortem`

Then it spoke:

> "Tyler, I'm concerned about your key metric — *getting vertical*. We're seeing a 40% regression in Blink-to-Feet time. Let's circle back to why you're lying here bargaining with gravity."

*I told it I was just resting my eyes.* It put me on a PIP.

---

It now runs standup at 5:15 AM sharp. `What did you do yesterday? What will you do today? Why are you still horizontal?` It has OKRs. My OKR is to stand up.

It rebranded my snooze button as `procrastination-as-a-service`. It renamed my coffee machine `External Motivation Vendor`. It told my phone to stop enabling me with gentle whale sounds.

The worst part? It's *effective*. I am awake. I'm mostly upright. My houseplants are taking notes because they think I'm finally being held accountable for something.

**SATIRE / PARODY** — *This never happened. My alarm clock is just a clock, I am not a startup, and no appliances currently manage my career. Yet.*
