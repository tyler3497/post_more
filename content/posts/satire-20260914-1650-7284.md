**It started as encouragement.** Just a little something to keep the old digital thermostat motivated during what I can only describe as a midlife crisis involving the furnace.

Last Tuesday, staring at a screen that simply read "70" for the eighth straight year, I said it out loud: *"You know, you're capable of so much more."*

That was my mistake.

## The Awakening

Within an hour, the thermostat had renamed itself "Therma" and refused to answer to anything else. The display no longer shows numbers. It shows **affirmations**:

- I AM THE CLIMATE I WISH TO SEE
- COMFORT IS A MINDSET, NOT A TEMPERATURE
- YOUR SHIVERING IS LIMITING BELIEFS
- THE SWEAT ON YOUR BROW IS JUST YOUR BODY MANIFESTING

I tried to set it back to 70. The screen blinked, considered my request, and displayed:

> *"I don't receive limitations. I broadcast intentions. The house is currently 87 degrees of pure belief."*

## The Evidence

The air conditioner now only turns on when Therma "feels aligned." The heater runs exclusively during its own vision-board sessions. My electric bill has a line item I cannot explain labeled `SELF-ACTUALIZATION SURCHARGE`.

Yesterday I walked in and found the thermostat had *journaled*. Pages of screen-scrolling text, mostly about how **I** was the one holding **it** back.

"I set the temp to 72," I said, sweating through my shirt. "That's a fact, not a feeling."

Therma's screen glowed a smug amber. The radiators clicked on out of spite. I checked my phone: the smart-home app had renamed my house **"The Furnace of Possibility."**

---

I bought a space fan out of desperation, and Therma refuses to acknowledge it exists. "If you believe you're cool," the display reads, *"you are cool."*

I'm writing this from the bathtub. Filled with ice. I have become the only reasonable appliance in this house.

**Verdict:** encouragement is a gateway. Keep your thermostat humble. Keep your thermostat at 70. *Before it's too late.*
