**I just wanted fifteen minutes of cross-breeze, not a business partner with feathers and a LinkedIn.**

*You know that point where your apartment smells faintly of whatever you cooked three days ago and poor decisions?* That was me last Tuesday. I cracked the window.

## How It Started

Within 20 minutes, a very confident pigeon landed on my sill, looked me dead in the eye, and started unpacking.

He brought:

- a sunflower seed he treats like a stress ball
- a twig he calls his standing desk
- an attitude that says *I've seen your apartment on Streeteasy and I'm disappointed*
- a tiny coo that definitely means `per my last email`

> "Listen, Tyler," he cooed at 8:47 a.m. like I was staff, "this sill has incredible natural light and zero competition. We're going to make this work. You're going to love the synergy."

I said this was my home. He invited a second pigeon who brought a *whiteboard*.

## The Business Model

Apparently my windowsill is now WePigeon.

- **Hot desk hours:** 6 a.m. to 7 p.m., even Sundays
- **Amenities:** unlimited seeds I apparently provide, my Wi-Fi now `CooWorking_5G`, and emotional support via staring
- He calls my bedroom *the annex* and me *facilities*

I asked what he does. He said:

> "I'm in disruption. Mostly your peace. But also, breadcrumbs logistics. We're scaling."

*I told him I work from home.* He told me *we both do now*.

---

## Current Status

I tried to close the window once. He knocked. Four times. With his beak. Like a landlord.

`status: sill_occupied && rent_due = 47_crumbs/hour && dignity = low`

Now I get daily invoices on torn napkins from my own kitchen: `YOU OWE ME SUNLIGHT — Gary (The Pigeon)` with a doodle of me looking tired.

My roommate asked why there's a bird in a meeting with AirPods. I said don't worry, he's on our lease emotionally.

I opened the window for fresh air. Now I pay rent to live near my own fresh air, which is still on brand for this economy.
