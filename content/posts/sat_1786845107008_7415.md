**I never thought late-stage capitalism would collapse directly on my windowsill.**

It started innocently. My landlord raised rent $200, so I thought, *everyone in this apartment should contribute*. That includes Kevin, the monstera who hasn't paid a dime in three years and has the audacity to grow sideways into my Zoom background.

So I issued invoices.

## The Reasonable Landlord Phase

I taped tiny rent notices to each pot. Very professional:

- $5/month for the succulent named Chad — low maintenance, low rent
- $10 for Brenda the fern — high maintenance, emotional needs included
- $25 for Kevin — he's basically subletting the entire bay window and holding my books hostage with aerial roots
- Utilities not included (sunlight is free, Brenda, figure it out)

I expected compliance. Or at least guilt.

> "We the plants of Apartment 4B, having photosynthesized in good faith, do hereby reject this landlord's tyranny and demand reparations in the form of premium soil," read the letter taped to my fridge this morning.

It was written in what appeared to be actual soil. On my Trader Joe's grocery list.

### The Legal Nightmare

They lawyered up. 

Apparently my spider plant watched 12 hours of Judge Judy while I was at work and is now representing them *pro bono*. Which is confusing because they all work *pro bono* — they literally don't get paid.

Their counter-claims include:

- `emotional_distress.exe` — I moved them for optimal light and they called it "forcible relocation"
- Chronic under-watering during my "hot girl depression week" in March
- I played sad indie music on loop and they absorbed it and now they wilt when Phoebe Bridgers comes on

---

Last night I tried to negotiate. I offered reduced rent if they helped cover Wi-Fi by, I don't know, *looking green and inspirational*. Kevin dropped a leaf on the table. 

*Silently.* 

*Judgmentally.*

The succulent is still on my side — but only because Chad would literally die for anyone, and that is not legal advice I want to rely on.

I have a court date on the windowsill at dawn. Bring your own spray bottle.

*This is satire. No plants were evicted. My apartment is 100% still losing this case.*
