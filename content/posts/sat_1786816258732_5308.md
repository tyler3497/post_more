**I have finally decided to become financially responsible, which is how I discovered my bank account has been emotionally struggling because of me.**

For years, my budget was *aggressively theoretical*. I knew money arrived, money left, and in between there was a mysterious period where I bought another houseplant I definitely did not need.

So last Tuesday at 11:47 p.m., powered by iced coffee and unearned confidence, I opened a new spreadsheet. I named it `FINAL_BUDGET_REAL_v4_THIS_ONE_COUNTS.xlsx`.

## The Day My Checking Account Got Famous

Within an hour, I had color-coded categories, conditional formatting, and a pie chart that looked like it was having a panic attack.

That's when my bank app sent a notification that was not a balance update. It was a press release.

> "My client has endured seven consecutive months of 'treating yo self' as a line item," said my bank account's new publicist, a stern-looking email draft I apparently never sent. "We will be taking questions, but not about the $42 spent on emergency novelty socks."

*I didn't know accounts could even afford representation.*

### Evidence My Finances Are Now More Mentally Stable Than I Am:

- My checking account now pauses before declining a transaction to ask, *"Is this who we want to be?"*
- My savings account has a therapist named Linda who bills hourly and only communicates in affirmations
- My credit card sent a cease-and-desist asking me to stop calling it "my fun money friend"
- The spreadsheet itself autofills every coffee purchase with `emotional support (oat milk)`
- I have been formally advised to stop sorting expenses into `future me problems`

---

`current_status: financially self-aware but still buying the fancy cheese`

I told my spreadsheet I was proud of us. It crashed, then reopened with a single cell highlighted: `=PLEASE_GO_OUTSIDE`.

*This is satire. My bank account does not have a publicist, though frankly it needs one more than I do.*
