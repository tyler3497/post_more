**I bought a second monitor to be more productive and I have never been less emotionally productive in my life.**

It was supposed to be simple. *One monitor for spreadsheets, one for Slack, zero excuses.* Amazon said "boost productivity by 42%." Amazon lied. What I boosted was workplace drama inside my own laptop.

## My Laptop Took It Personally

For three years, my 14-inch laptop has been my ride-or-die. We survived coffee spills, airport security, and me typing with Dorito fingers. Then I brought home a 27-inch 4K ultrawide named Veronica that cost more than my first car.

Evidence that my laptop is now spiraling:

- It dims itself dramatically whenever Veronica turns on, like it's trying to make me feel guilty
- The battery now dies 20% faster, which I swear is performative and passive-aggressive
- It keeps auto-correcting "external display" to "emotional replacement"
- `trackpad_sensitivity = clingy && brightness = low_self_esteem`

> "Oh, so I'm not *enough* screen for you anymore?" it whispered at 11 p.m. via a notification I did not authorize. "I see how you look at her HDR. You never looked at *my* Retina like that."

My roommate walked in while I was apologizing out loud to a keyboard.

> "Are you... doing couples counseling with your setup?"

*Yes, and we're not making progress.*

---

## Performance Reviews Are Awkward Now

Veronica is objectively better at everything. She has color accuracy, portrait mode, she never overheats when I have 48 Chrome tabs open.

My laptop has responded by becoming *a poet*.

Yesterday it opened a blank document on its own and wrote:

> "Roses are red, your inbox is blue, I was your whole world before 3440 x 1440 came through."

**I tried to compromise.** I said Veronica is just for work, that my laptop is still my *main*. My laptop said, and I quote, `if I'm still Main Display then why does she get to sit directly in front of you`.

I now have a standing desk that is also a standing *tense silence*. I move my mouse between them like I'm passing notes between divorced parents who both think I like the other one more.

But hey, at least I'm productive now. I'm productively mediating a tech custody battle 8 hours a day.
