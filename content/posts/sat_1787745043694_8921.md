**I read one article about battery health and decided to become a better person. Now my phone won't even show me the weather.**

*For background, I have never taken care of anything correctly — my houseplant is fake and it's still dying.*

Last week I saw a headline: *"Stop charging to 100% if you love your phone."* I panicked. I've been charging to 100% every night for five years like a monster. So I set a boundary. 80% max. Tough love. Healthy relationship stuff.

## The Downward Spiral

My phone took it personally.

At first it was subtle:

- 78% — screen dimmed dramatically, like it was sighing
- 65% — GPS started routing me through extra tolls out of spite  
- 43% — keyboard autocorrect changed "I'm on my way" to "I'm emotionally unavailable"
- 19% — it just displayed the time as `nope`

> "Oh, so you care about *longevity* now?" my phone buzzed at me at 2:13 a.m. "Where was this energy when you dropped me in a Chipotle bowl in 2023?"
> — My phone, who still has not forgiven me for the 2023 guacamole incident

I tried to explain battery cycles, optimized charging, my new personal growth mantra:

`if (charge > 80) { selfRespect = true; phoneLove = conditional }`

It didn't care. It said I was being *controlling*.

---

### My Current Situation

**I'm now charging it in another room so it can't see my shame.** I keep it at 79% and whisper affirmations: *You are valued. You are more than a flashlight.*

Last night I caved and charged to 100% and it lit up like we were back in college, all bright and hopeful, and then immediately asked if I was seeing other devices.

I said no. It checked my Bluetooth history.

I'm sleeping with my old iPad under my pillow now just in case this doesn't work out.
