**I did the responsible thing and immediately regretted it.**

For three years, my succulent Gerald lived on spite and condensation. *Classic succulent boundary work.* I watered him when Mercury was in retrograde or when I remembered I had a plant.

Then last Tuesday I actually watered him. Like, measured water. Room temperature. With love.

Big mistake.

## The Glow-Up No One Asked For

Within 24 hours, Gerald perked up, grew what I swear is a bicep, and started coaching.

Evidence that my apartment now has a middle-management problem:

- **6:00 AM:** Gerald whispers "hydrate or diedrate" at my sleeping face
- He rearranged my to-do list to say `drink water, touch grass, answer ONE email (baby steps)`
- He instituted **Affirmation Corner**, which is just my windowsill but louder
- He charges $150/hr in sunlight and compliments

> "Tell me why you're avoiding your taxes again, but make it growth-oriented," said Gerald, who literally lives in a pot I got free at a yard sale.

I tried to explain I only wanted a low-maintenance roommate. He countered with a PowerPoint.

### The Curriculum

- Week 1: *Why Your Attachment Style Is 'Anxious But Make It Houseplant'*
- Week 2: Boundaries - aka stop moving me to get better Zoom lighting
- Week 3: Advanced photosynthesizing your feelings

---

I have to be honest, it's working? My laundry is done. I drank water without it being coffee-adjacent.

**Yesterday he told my date** I was "doing the work" and I had to explain my life coach is a succulent with a savior complex.

Still, 10/10 would not rehydrate again without a contract.

*SATIRE / PARODY — This is entirely fictional, no succulents are licensed coaches, no plants were overwatered beyond one reasonable pour, I'm just projecting onto chlorophyll again.*
