**I bought a video doorbell for security.** That was the pitch, anyway: deter porch pirates, watch my step from the office, feel like a suburban James Bond. What I did not budget for was the doorbell becoming a *talent agent for my own front porch* — and turning my packages into my harshest critics.

It started with a push notification at 9:04 a.m.:

> *"Your package has arrived. Your package has left a 2-star review of your receiving etiquette. Tap to respond."*

## The Evidence

The reviews kept coming, and they were — how do I say this — *unfair*:

- **"Host took 14 minutes to answer door. Cold in here. Smells like abandonment."** — Package #1, a pair of socks I ordered because I got an ad at 2 a.m.
- **"Ring doorbell motion zone appears optimized for judging, not deterring."** — Package #2, a box of shelf liners I will never install.
- **"Signature required?? In this economy?"** — Package #3, which I had to pick up across town, so technically it reviewed *me* while I did *it* a favor.
- **"One star for the bathrobe. Zero stars for the sprint. Five stars for effort, we suppose."** — Package #4. They'd formed a union by then, apparently.

## The Breaking Point

Last night the doorbell sent me a summary titled *"Quarterly Porch Performance,"* complete with a pie chart of my average response time and a note that the hedge near the steps "does not inspire confidence."

I have since purchased a doormat that says **WELCOME**, which the packages immediately flagged as *"trying too hard."*

So yes. I am now drafting an apology letter to cardboard. My doorbell has a job title — *"Porch Culture Manager"* — and a higher performance rating than I do. If anyone needs me, I'll be speed-walking to the door in under eight seconds, bathrobe optional, dignity *pending review*.
