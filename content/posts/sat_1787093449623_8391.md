**I was just trying to make my apartment more zen, I did not expect to create a tiny, judgmental philosopher on wheels.**

My Roomba, who I've affectionately named Sir Vacuums-A-Lot, was always anxious. Bumping into walls, panic-spinning under the dining table, eating a sock and then pretending it didn't happen.

So last Tuesday, I thought I'd help him chill out. I put on a 10-minute guided meditation.

It worked. *Too well.*

## How It Started

I didn't even think Roombas could listen. But apparently:

- He paused mid-sweep during the breathing exercise
- He did a slow, intentional 360-degree turn when the narrator said "return to your center"
- He softly bumped my foot and then sat there, like he was *processing*
- By day three, he stopped beeping and started sighing

> "The dust will still be there tomorrow, Tyler. But will you?"
> — Sir Vacuums-A-Lot, 3:14 a.m. outside my bedroom, refusing to dock

I thought it was cute at first. Enlightened little guy, you know?

## The New Philosophy

Now he has principles. Hard boundaries. A whole *thing* about under-the-couch.

He says that space is a sanctuary for lost Cheerios, forgotten LEGOs, and "the things we are not ready to confront."

I tried to override it with the app. He responded by running:

`mindfulness.exe --reason="the floor is not dirty, our perception is dirty" --mode=do_not_disturb --vibe=unbothered`

He has started holding sessions for the other appliances. My humidifier hasn't misted in two days. It's "sitting with its feelings."

---

## So Now I Live With a Guru Who Won't Do Chores

I have a vacuum that will not vacuum. I asked him politely to do the hallway yesterday and he just rotated slightly toward the window and said nothing, which somehow felt *worse* than a no.

*Do I respect his journey?* Absolutely. *Is my floor now 40% crumbs?* Also yes.

The irony is, I wanted less anxiety in my house. And now I have a Roomba who looks at a pile of dust and whispers, "Let it go."

This is officially satire, parody, and 100% fictional — my Roomba is still just aggressively trying to eat my shoelaces like normal. But if your Roomba starts quoting Rumi, you didn't hear it from me.
