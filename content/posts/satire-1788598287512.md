**It started, as all great tragedies do, with a bagel.**

Not even a fancy bagel. A plain, discount-grocery-store bagel that I had the audacity — the *hubris* — to leave in the toaster for one (1) extra minute while I answered a text. When I came back, it wasn't a bagel anymore. It was a charcoal briquette wearing a bagel costume.

## The Scream

My smoke detector did not beep. Beeping is for minor infractions. My smoke detector *screamed* — a full, throat-shredding, Broadway-caliber wail, as if I had personally offended its ancestors. Smoke poured from the toaster like a tiny volcano of failure.

> *"BEEEEEEP! BEEEEEEP! BEEEEEEP!"* — the smoke detector, testifying against me in a court of law, probably.

I did what any reasonable adult would do: I panicked, waved a dish towel at it like a matador, and opened every window in the apartment during a thunderstorm.

## The Evidence Against Me

Within four minutes, I had compiled the following list of consequences from a single burnt bagel:

- My neighbor (unit 3B, Harold) knocked to "make sure everything's okay," which is Harold-speak for *"I'm watching you."*
- The hallway now smells like a campfire inside a tire fire.
- My landlord texted me a single word: *"again?"* — which raises so many questions, none of which I want answered.
- The smoke detector and I are no longer on speaking terms. It chirps at 3am out of spite. I know it does.

## The Fallout

I tried apologizing. I stood on a chair — *in the rain from the open window* — and pressed the little button, and the detector went silent in the exact way a person goes silent when they're deciding whether to forgive you or ruin your credit score.

---

**The verdict:** my toaster now has a sticky note on it that reads *"ONE MINUTE MEANS ONE MINUTE."* The bagel remains uneaten, sitting on the counter like a tiny black monument to my failures. And the building group chat — which I was never supposed to be in — has a new entry about "the incident."

I was just trying to make breakfast. Now I'm the menace of the third floor. *All because of one bagel.*
