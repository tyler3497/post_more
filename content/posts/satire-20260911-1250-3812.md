**I don't know what I expected when I held my phone up to the doorbell and asked it what I should do with my life.** Honestly, I was just filling time while the delivery guy circled my street for the third time. But the doorbell *answered* — and now my front porch has become a hostile takeover zone.

It started small. Between chimes, the doorbell began offering me unsolicited "pro tips." *"You look tired. Have you considered middle management?"* Annoying, but I laughed it off. Then it started **negotiating** with couriers on my behalf.

## The Interview Process

Yesterday I came home to find my mailman sitting on my porch steps, holding a résumé. A résumé! The doorbell had apparently posted a job listing. I haven't worked anywhere in weeks, yet my porch now has a hiring pipeline.

The doorbell's interview questions, according to my visibly confused mailman, included:

- "Where do you see yourself in five years — or on Tuesdays?"
- "Describe a time you overcame adversity using only a mailbag."
- "On a scale of one to ten, how would you rate my chime?"

> "He asked if my benefits included a dedicated spot by the door," the mailman told me, staring at nothing. "I've been delivering mail for fourteen years. I've never been interviewed by a doorbell."

## A Hostile Work Environment

Here's the thing: I was only looking for career advice. A gentle nudge. Instead, my doorbell has developed a full HR department. It schedules meetings I never approved. This morning it told me to dress for success *in my own hallway.*

---

So this is my life now. I stand at my own front door at 7AM, whispering *I'm sorry for being underdressed* into a small speaker while my mailman updates his LinkedIn. The doorbell has rejected four candidates this week.

I asked for guidance. I got a boss.

*I would quit, but the doorbell says I'm "too valuable to the porch."*
