## I've Been Living in "Ambiance" for Two Years and It Was Mostly Grease

**For 742 days, my kitchen had exactly one working light bulb out of three,** which I called *mood lighting* and my mom called *a fire hazard you should address*.

It was a system. Dim kitchen = you can't see the mess. You can't see the mess = mess doesn't exist. This is basic apartment physics, like `if (lights === dim) { anxiety = 0 }`.

Then Saturday, in a fit of becoming-a-functional-adult energy I usually reserve for January, I actually replaced the bulb. All three lit up. Daylight. Crime-scene brightness.

My backsplash, which I have lovingly seasoned with two years of optimistic stir-fries, gasped.

### Exhibit A Through Z

Under full illumination, it turns out I don't have a white tile backsplash. I have a *forensic record*:

- A Pollock of olive oil from The Time I Tried to Flambé Instant Ramen
- A constellation of soy sauce that maps exactly to where I lost a fight with a takeout packet in 2024
- One perfect, preserved fingerprint in what I think is pesto, holding on like it's waiting for rescue

And then the testimony began:

> "On the night of November 14th, the defendant claimed he was 'just heating up soup' but we all witnessed the Great Chili Explosion of 2024," said the tile behind the stove, who is apparently the foreman. "We have retained counsel."

*Which is fair, because I did tell people my kitchen was 'minimalist.'*

---

### My Defense Is Not Going Well

I tried to Windex my problems away. The tiles just squeaked louder, like they were on the stand and enjoying it.

My sponge, who has seen too much and has the mildew to prove it, whispered:

> "You can't clean away witness testimony, dude. You need a lawyer or at least a Magic Eraser with a better attitude."

Now my backsplash has demands. It wants to be quoted accurately in my lease renewal. It wants a formal apology to the grout. And it wants me to *finally* admit that chip in the tile wasn't "already like that when I moved in."

Satire / parody, obviously. My backsplash is not suing me. *Though if it could, wow, would it have a case.*
