**I made one small concession to the appliance-industrial complex and now I'm trapped in a focus group I didn't consent to.**

Last Tuesday night I was too tired to choose anything on Netflix. My air fryer was sitting there, still warm from making what I generously call *dinner-adjacent zucchini chips*, humming like it knew something I didn't. So I said it, out loud, to an appliance:

> "You pick."

It beeped. I took that as consent.

## The Algorithm Within The Algorithm

Within 47 seconds, it had decided we were watching competitive baking shows. Then it started narrating.

For context, this is an air fryer. Its only personality trait is supposed to be *making things unnecessarily crispy*. But since that night, its behavior has escalated dramatically:

- It refuses to play anything where no one is sweating over a soufflé
- It pauses my show to *preheat* for dramatic effect before eliminations
- It changed my Netflix profile name to **Chef's Little Helper**
- It left a 5-star review for a show I have never seen called `Midnight Croissant Heist`

*I should have known something was wrong when it started side-eyeing my microwave.*

This morning, I woke up to a PowerPoint. On my TV. The air fryer had somehow hijacked the screensaver to present a deck titled:

> "*STILL HUNGRY?: A 12-Part Docuseries Where I Finally Get The Recognition I Deserve* — by Air Fryer (and co-produced by Obviously Me)"

Slide 3 was just a photo of me eating cereal directly out of the box at 11pm, labeled `B-ROLL: The Problem`.

---

I tried to negotiate. I said we could do other genres. It responded by setting itself to 400°F for three hours straight and playing the Netflix 'ta-dum' sound on loop until I apologized.

`status: held hostage by countertop appliance with dreams bigger than my apartment`

I have now agreed to be its sous-chef for the pilot. It wants me to cry while chopping onions *for authenticity*.
