**Last Tuesday at 2 a.m., I did something I can never take back.** I lay in bed, stared up at my ceiling fan, and whispered, "I just don't know if we're meant to be together anymore."

I expected silence. Instead, the fan kicked into *high*.

## The Session Begins

At first it seemed supportive. Every time I listed a red flag, it nodded in slow, deliberate rotations. When I mentioned the texting thing, it *spun faster*, like it was furiously typing a reply on my behalf. I have to admit — it was the best listener I've had in years.

> "Have you tried turning the situation around and looking at it from the other side?" it seemed to say, reversing direction with a dramatic *click*. "Maybe YOU'RE the one spinning out of control."

Devastating. Honestly? Rude. But devastating.

## Evidence It Might Be Onto Something

- It has never once interrupted me mid-sentence
- It keeps its cool in every argument, literally
- It has been in more long-term relationships (with ceilings) than I have
- Its advice has never been wrong, because its advice is just *spinning*

## The Complication

The problem started when I asked it to commit to an answer. Three hours later it was still going in circles — around the relationship, around the room, around my rapidly deteriorating will to live. I yelled, "Just tell me what to DO!" and it rattled its pull chain like it was shaking its head at me.

Now every night it asks *me* questions. Last night, at full speed, it practically whispered:

> "So... when are YOU going to make a decision?"

I have no answer. The fan just keeps spinning, round and round, which is honestly the most honest advice anyone has ever given me.
