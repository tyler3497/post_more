**It started as an innocent Sunday ritual.** I was folding laundry, matching pairs, and I thought: *why not quantify the affection?* So I created the Sock Emotional Support Index — a rigorous, peer-reviewed-by-me ranking of every pair I own.

## The Methodology (Flawless)

Each pair was scored on four pillars:

- **Cushion Integrity** — does it still hug the heel, or has it given up like the rest of us
- **Aesthetic Resonance** — stripes earn points; plain gray gets a participation ribbon
- **Hole Trajectory** — a looming heel hole is a countdown to betrayal
- **Vibes** — *undefinable, non-negotiable*

## The Reckoning

The top three were paraded into the dresser drawer. The bottom tier — the lonely single, the one with the mysterious stain, the pair that slowly migrates north up my calves — were exiled to the rotation of shame.

I should have done it in private.

> "We demand a recount," announced the captain of the exiled socks, standing atop the laundry hamper like a tiny textile Che Guevara. "The Vibes category is pure bias. Also, the stain is *character*."

## The Escalation

- **Day one:** silent treatment from the drawer. I had to wear sandals. In *September*.
- **Day two:** protest chants at 6 a.m. — rhythmic thumping against the dryer door.
- **Day three:** the good socks, the elites, started fraternizing. One diamond-pattern pair has defected entirely.

---

So this is my life now: negotiating a ceasefire with hosiery, drafting a Sock Bill of Rights, and quietly buying backup pairs I never ranked at all. If anyone needs me, I'll be at the laundromat, telling a stranger's ankle socks that *they* are enough.
