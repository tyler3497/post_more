**I named my houseplants to feel more nurturing, and now they are thriving socially without me.**

*Which is not what the little tag at Home Depot promised when it said 'low maintenance and great company.'*

I bought five plants in March because my apartment felt like a dentist waiting room. I named them to bond. You know, healthy adult coping.

## The Naming Ceremony Was Very Serious

- **Fernando** the fern — dramatic, needs misting, thinks he's the main character
- **Mona** the monstera — tall, supportive, has seen me cry over taxes
- **Spike** the cactus — does not want to talk, respected
- **Pothos Malone** — growing aggressively despite my care, overachiever
- **Barbara** the succulent — actually plastic, but I still water her out of guilt

I talked to them. Played them jazz. *Very mindful.* Too mindful, apparently.

## The Group Chat I Was NOT Added To

Last night I reached for my watering can at 11:47 p.m. and saw my old iPad glowing in the plant corner. They had a group chat open. Named `Plant Parent Issues (No Humans)`.

> "Has anyone been watered with RESTED water today or is it tap again? Tap again? Okay. Noted." — Fernando, who has become an HR department

> "he said 'you're so brave' when I dropped a leaf. sir I'm photosynthesizing, not at war" — Mona

> "Barbara hasn't grown in 6 months and he still hasn't figured it out 😭" — Pothos Malone

*I stood there in socks that don't match, realizing I've been out-networked by organisms that communicate via vibes.*

They have a whole system now:

- `Fernando.status = wilting_for_attention()` when I work late
- Pothos grades my pour technique on a 10-point scale — I got a 3.2 last Tuesday
- Spike muted me but still reacts with a 🌵 to everything

---

I tried to apologize. I offered seaweed fertilizer and an apology playlist. Fernando left me on read. Literally. A leaf fell onto the iPad's keyboard like he was closing the laptop on me.

My current agreement: I water, I do not speak unless spoken to, and I accept that my apartment now has a botanical HOA.
