**For the record, I did not agree to become a mayor today.** I just wanted to find the mustard.

I opened my fridge Saturday morning — *a routine act of bravery I perform every 3-4 business weeks* — and realized my Tupperware had evolved.

## The Discovery

In the back, behind the soy sauce from 2023, three containers had formed a coalition government. There were tiny lights. There was infrastructure. One lid was propped up like a city gate.

Evidence that they are now a functioning society:

- They have a flag. It is a dried cilantro leaf stuck into a quinoa mound.
- They have agriculture. The chickpeas have... sprouted opinions.
- They have a legal system based entirely on who was left in there longest. The lasagna from January is the Supreme Court.
- They whispered when I reached for the oat milk, which is never a good sign from leftovers

> "You abandoned us," said the president, who appears to be a very confident lentil stew, "and now you return as a tourist? We have built a life here. We have *culture*."

I tried to explain I was just cleaning.

> "Ah yes," he said, adjusting his tiny bay-leaf cape, "the great purge. Classic colonial rhetoric."

### My Failed Negotiations

I offered to give them the crisper drawer as an autonomous zone. They demanded representation. They demanded Wi-Fi. They demanded I stop putting raw garlic next to them because, quote, `it burns the soul`.

---

I told them I have roommates. I have rent. I cannot list three sentient Tupperware containers as dependents on my lease.

They countered by offering me citizenship. Apparently I'm now eligible because I *created* them through neglect and provided continuous ambient refrigeration.

*This is how it starts. You forget one container of rice and suddenly you're in a constitutional crisis with your own leftovers.*

I have closed the fridge. I am giving them 24 hours to draft a constitution and decide if they want to move to the freezer, which they consider to be *the great northern exile*.

If they unionize with the freezer pops, I'm done for.
