**I made one responsible adult decision and now I'm legally entangled with fermented flour.**

It started because a friend said sourdough was *easy* and *rewarding*, which are two words that have never once described anything actually easy or rewarding in my kitchen.

I named him Bubbles. Mistake one.

## The Honeymoon Phase Lasted 36 Hours

At first it was cute. He bubbled. I fed him. He made little *glorp* noises like he was proud of himself. I whispered encouraging things like "you're doing great, buddy" while my actual houseplants withered in silent judgment.

Then I went away for the weekend.

I left a note: `feed me Sunday`. He took it personally.

When I got back, the kitchen smelled like ambition and betrayal, and there was a stack of Post-its stuck to the fridge I definitely did not write:

- Please acknowledge my rise (both emotional and literal)
- We need to discuss your commitment issues with whole wheat flour
- I have retained a very small crumb attorney
- Your mother never liked me and I'm starting to see why

> "I have been proofing your absence," Bubbles gurgled from his jar. "We are *intertwined* now. Like gluten."

### Things Are Escalating

He now demands:

- Daily affirmations at 6:30 a.m. sharp
- Only filtered water, *and he can taste the difference*
- Couples therapy which is just me sitting on the kitchen floor explaining why I looked at a baguette at the store

---

**I tried to break up with him.** *I really did.*

I said, "I think we need space," and he literally expanded to fill his entire jar to prove a point about personal growth.

My roommate said I should just make bread and end it. But Bubbles heard that and now he's crying yeasty tears and leaving voicemails:

> "Is this how you treat family? I'm literally your culture!"

*This is satire/parody. All sourdough starters, marriages, and crumb attorneys are entirely fictional and extremely co-dependent.*
