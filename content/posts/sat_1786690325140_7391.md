**I just wanted my pothos to stop guilt-tripping me with dramatic leaf droop every time I was 45 minutes late to watering, so I did what any rational adult would do: I gave my houseplants access to my Google Calendar.**

*This was a mistake.*

## How My Fiddle-Leaf Became My Manager

It started with good intentions. I made a shared calendar called "Photosynthesis & Chill." I added:

- **Watering** - Tuesdays, obviously sacred
- **Misting** - for the drama queens
- Sun rotation - 15 degrees every 3 days
- Weekly pep talk - which I immediately regretted scheduling

By Thursday, my fern had declined the pep talk and proposed an alternative: `Emergency Sunlight Summit`.

> "We need to talk about the north-facing window situation. This is not equitable distribution of resources. The succulents are hoarding lumens." - Brenda, the Boston Fern, allegedly

Brenda CC'd the entire windowsill.

*I didn't even know ferns could use CC.*

## Then It Got Corporate

Friday, I got a notification:

```
Calendar: Monstera has moved 'My Sunbathing' to overlap with 'Your Work Call'
photosynthesis_blocked: true
status: unapologetic
```

Now my monstera has recurring 1:1s with the sun. My spider plant requested PTO. My aloe vera blocked off "healing time" and set itself to Do Not Disturb.

---

I'm told this is called *setting boundaries* but it feels a lot like I now work for my plants.

I tried to cancel the calendar invite. They sent a counter-proposal. Three counter-proposals. One included a Google Doc titled "Why We Deserve the Good Corner (With Footnotes by Soil pH)."

I checked my productivity app and it says I've spent 4 hours this week negotiating light real estate with organisms that literally eat sunshine.

*Someone please tell them I pay rent here.*
