**I finally decided my fridge magnets needed a system.**

*My fridge looked like it had been decorated by a very enthusiastic raccoon with a travel addiction.*

So I organized them by vibes. You know: `fridge.sort(magnets, by=vibe)` energy. Little categories on painter's tape. It felt very adult. Very in-control. Very "I have my life together."

### The Vibes Chart

- **Inspirational Hustle** — the tiny cactus that says "Grow, Girl" and means it aggressively
- **Chaotic Good** — Las Vegas dice, a taco truck that says "WE OUT HERE", and a crooked souvenir from a beach I've never been to
- **Judgmental Barely** — the 2019 calendar still stuck on March, silently counting my failures

I left the leftovers alone. Rookie mistake.

By Tuesday, the magnets had unionized and started hosting a support group inside the fridge. For my leftovers.

> "We are holding space," said Crooked Beach Magnet in a voice I did not know magnets had, "for Gary, the spaghetti who has been here since last Thursday and is still being called 'meal prep.' Gary deserves truth, not Tupperware."

Gary, for the record, was *thriving* on neglect. He'd formed opinions. He'd formed colonies.

The meetings are at 9 p.m. sharp, right when I go to steal cheese. I have to walk through their circle:

- Gary (spaghetti, week-old, now a motivational speaker)
- Three lonely pickle slices who call themselves "The Brine Collective"
- Half an onion wrapped in foil like a tiny crime scene, whispering *let me go*

---

I tried to reassert dominance with `if (magnet.talking) { ignore(); }`

It did not work. The Inspirational Hustle magnet started coaching Gary: "You've outgrown your container. Literally. Mold is growth!"

**Update:** My fridge is now a wellness retreat. The leftovers have name tags. The magnets are taking dues in the form of expired coupons. And Gary just got promoted to Lead Leftover — he says he's writing a memoir called *Tupperware and Trauma.* I told him we don't have a publishing deal.

He said the magnets are his agent now.
