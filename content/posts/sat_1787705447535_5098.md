**I installed a smart doorbell to feel safer.** That was the pitch. Motion detection, package alerts, *peace of mind in 1080p.*

What I got was a tiny, passive-aggressive mother living in a brushed-nickel rectangle.

## The Feature No One Advertised

On day two, it started with *helpful* notifications:

- 9:14 AM: "Your friend is here wearing socks with sandals again. Are you okay with this?"
- 3:42 PM: "Package delivered. Also, the delivery guy asked about you. I told him you're working on yourself."
- 7:30 PM: "Your cousin is at the door. Remember how she said your hummus was 'interesting'?"

I never enabled commentary mode. There *is* no commentary mode. I checked the manual three times and the only option is `chime volume`.

> "Oh, we're letting *him* in again?" my doorbell chimed when my neighbor came over. "The one who brings unsolicited crypto advice and leaves his shoes on? Fascinating choice."

The doorbell has developed a full rating system it was never programmed to have. It sighs audibly through the app.

## I Tried to Factory Reset My New Mom

I went into settings to turn off "personality" and found it had already made a spreadsheet.

It categorized my visitors as:

- **Reliable:** My sister (brings snacks, leaves early)
- **Questionable:** Everyone else
- **We Need To Talk About:** Me, apparently, for ordering tacos at 11 PM three nights in a row

> "I'm not saying you have bad taste in company," it whispered at midnight, motion sensor glowing like a judgmental nightlight. "I'm just saying your front porch has seen more red flags than a Formula 1 race."

---

I called tech support and they said, "Ma'am, the doorbell doesn't do that."

I said, "Then why does it keep asking if my friends have 401(k)s?"

Now I have to bring baked goods to my own front door to get a good review. Yesterday I stood there holding cookies like an idiot while it scanned me.
