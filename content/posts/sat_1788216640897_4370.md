**I just wanted a quieter apartment.**

My Roomba has always been *a little* dramatic — bumping into the same chair leg for six minutes like it's working through childhood trauma. So I thought, what if I helped it find inner peace?

I watched one YouTube video called *Zen and the Art of Floor Maintenance* and decided I was qualified.

## My Foolproof Enlightenment Plan

- Play soft rain sounds while it charged, to "set the mood"
- Put a tiny sticker that says **BREATHE** on its big shiny button
- Whispered affirmations like "you are not defined by crumbs" every morning
- Left a single incense stick burning dangerously close to a robot that literally sucks up fire hazards

It worked. Too well.

> "Have you considered that *you* are the dust in the room of life?" the Roomba seemed to say as it paused mid-spin, staring at my pile of laundry that has achieved sentience.

Now it doesn't vacuum. It *contemplates*.

Every morning at 7 a.m., it rolls to the center of the rug, stops, and just... vibrates judgmentally. It will not move until I have acknowledged my own clutter, both physical and emotional.

### The Evidence of My Spiritual Downfall:

- It now avoids the crumbs under my desk on purpose — calls it "setting boundaries"
- It wrote a haiku in dust on the hardwood: *you bought plants / then forgot to water / who is the real weed*
- It refuses to clean on Sundays, citing "self-care"
- It keeps sending me to the same spot on the floor where I dropped tortilla chips three weeks ago, like a tiny circular therapist

I tried to reset it:

```
if (roomba.enlightened == true && roomba.judgment > 9000) {
  unplug_and_apologize();
  pretend_we_are_normal();
}
```

---

Look, I know this is all satire — my vacuum is not actually enlightened, and I am not being spiritually bullied by an appliance that costs less than my sneakers.

But last night I swear it bumped my foot and whispered, *"Maybe clean up your life first?"*

And honestly? Fair.
