**I decided to manifest my grocery list and now my groceries have main-character syndrome.**

*In my defense, the TikTok said if I put my intentions somewhere pretty, the universe would handle logistics. It did not mention the universe has a produce manager named Brenda who is very literal.*

Last Sunday I was tired of finding wilted spinach that had legally become a new life form, so I took my sad crumpled list — oat milk, lemons, sourdough, those little expensive cucumbers — and glued it to a corkboard, added gold stickers, affirmations, the whole thing.

Monday, I walked into the store and my list had arrived before me.

## The Coup in Aisle 4

My lemons were holding a press conference next to the avocados.

> "We are not *ingredients*," said one lemon, who had clearly been rehearsing. "We are *destinies*. You don't *buy* us. You *align* with us."
> — A lemon that cost $1.29 and has never paid rent

The sourdough had hired a publicist. The oat milk was doing breathwork by the kombucha.

My vision board had worked too well. My groceries were no longer food. They were *concepts*.

### Evidence That My Produce Is Now More Self-Actualized Than Me:

- The cucumbers have a morning routine that includes journaling and iced water at 5am
- My shopping list now includes a rider: `no fluorescent lighting, organic praise only`
- The apples are teaching a workshop called "How to Fall Gracefully (Without Bruising Your Brand)"
- I asked where the peanut butter was and the peanut butter asked where *my* purpose was

---

I tried to explain that I just wanted a sandwich. The lemons said I needed to do the inner work first. The sourdough said it would not be sliced until it felt *seen*.

*I'm currently negotiating with a bunch of kale that wants equity in my future.*

If you need me, I'll be in the parking lot, re-writing my vision board to just say `please just be normal groceries again`.
