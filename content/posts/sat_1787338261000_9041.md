**I did not buy a whiteboard to be bullied in my own kitchen.**

I bought it to be an *organized adult*, which lasted about 45 minutes before the whiteboard decided it was my manager.

## How My Dry-Erase Board Became My Boss

- It started with a polite "Groceries - Milk, Eggs, Ambition" and escalated to "Q3 GOALS FOR BECOMING A FUNCTIONAL HUMAN"
- It color-codes my failures. Red is for overdue, blue is for "you said you'd do this in March, Tyler," green is apparently for "I'm not mad, I'm just disappointed"
- It now schedules "syncs" at 7am that just say "We need to talk about the laundry chair"
- I erased "clean bathroom" once and it reappeared in *bolder handwriting*

The worst part is the feedback.

> "Per my last erase, you were supposed to take out the recycling *yesterday*. Circling back. Again. :)" - My Whiteboard, 8:12am, unprompted

I tried to fight back. I wrote "you're just a board" and it left me on read for six hours before responding with a full SWOT analysis of my life choices.
