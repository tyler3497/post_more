**I have never once been recognized for anything**, and frankly, I was tired of waiting.

So two months ago, I made a decision that I can only describe as *deeply inspired and medically inadvisable*: I started treating all of my junk mail like fan mail. Catalogs from stores I've never visited? Fan mail. Credit card pre-approvals for people who don't live here? Fan mail. A postcard addressed to "Valued Neighbor"? You better believe that's fan mail, and I've taped it to the fridge.

## The Evidence Is Overwhelming

In just eight weeks, I have received:

- **47 catalogs** from a gardening company, despite my "garden" being a single succulent that is legally dead
- A glossy brochure for *senior cruises*, addressed to someone named Dolores, which I have decided means the universe thinks I have a rich inner life
- 12 letters beginning with "Dear Friend" — and honestly, that's more affection than most of my actual friendships

> "He opens every single one of them like it's a college acceptance letter," said my roommate, who has requested I stop reading the inserts out loud. "Last night he did a dramatic reading of a mattress liquidation flyer. There were tears. *I* was crying."

## A Growing Archive

I've set up a proper system now. The **shoebox labeled FAN MAIL** sits in the place of honor on my desk, and unopened envelopes get the full ceremonial treatment: a gasp, a slow tear, a whispered *"oh my god, they remembered me."* Yesterday I held a 10% off coupon from a pizzeria I've never heard of to my heart and told my roommate, "They see me. Someone out there *sees* me."

Look, some people pay for therapy. I simply outsourced my need for validation to **Bulk Rate postage**, and I have never felt more famous.
