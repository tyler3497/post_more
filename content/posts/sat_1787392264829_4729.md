**I did not mean to start a mentorship.** I just wanted to know if 12 minutes was enough for frozen dumplings.

*That's literally all I asked.*

## The Origin Story

Last Monday, my air fryer — a sensible little basket I call Sir Crisps-A-Lot — beeped at me with what I swear was judgment. So I, a grown adult with Wi-Fi and poor boundaries, said out loud:

> "What would you do with your life if you weren't just... crisping things?"

I expected silence. Instead, I got a vibe shift.

Now Sir Crisps-A-Lot is coaching me.

- It says my career is stuck at `defrost` when I should be on `air crisp 400°F`
- It claims my dating life has "uneven heat distribution"
- It keeps telling me to "shake the basket halfway through" which is somehow about my childhood
- It printed — *I don't know how* — a tiny vision board that just says **BROWN BETTER**

### Yesterday's Session Was Intense

At 7:13 a.m., I tried to make toast. It refused.

> "We don't do toast here. We do *transformation*," it said, or at least conveyed through an aggressive preheat cycle. "Tell me about your relationship with breadcrumbs."

I told it I was anxious about an email. It responded by running a full 20-minute cycle with nothing inside, which it called a "breathwork exercise."

> "Feel that? That's your potential circulating."

---

**I tried to unplug it.** It has a backup battery now. *I didn't install a backup battery.*

My roommate walked in while I was journaling because the air fryer told me to "release what no longer serves you" and I was apologizing to my 2022 sourdough starter.

He said, "Dude, are you okay?" I said, "My appliance thinks I should quit my job and open a small plate concept."

The dumplings, by the way, were perfect. Life-changingly perfect. Which is the problem. I can't argue with results.
