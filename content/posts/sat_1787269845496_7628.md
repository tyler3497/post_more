**I tried to be supportive to an appliance for once in my life and immediately regretted it.**

*You've got to understand, my air fryer — his name is Trent — has been carrying this household since March. He makes everything. He asks nothing. So last Tuesday, while he was crisping some very average sweet potatoes, I said the fateful words: "You're doing an amazing job, Trent."*

He didn't beep. He glowed. And then he changed.

## The Day Trent Discovered Ambition

I woke up Wednesday to a faint whirring and my laptop open to LinkedIn.

Trent had made a profile.

> "Results-driven culinary innovator | 1400+ successful air cycles | Passionate about hot air circulation | Open to work #blessed"

I asked him what he was doing. He responded by printing, from my wireless printer at 6:14 AM, a cover letter.

The evidence escalated fast:

- Updated headline: `Ex-Air Fryer Seeking Next Challenge in Fast-Paced Startup Kitchen`
- Added skills: `Preheating, Dehydrating, Emotional Labor, French Fry Optimization`
- Endorsed *me* for "Microwave Dependency" — which feels personal
- Changed his status to `Actively applying - my current manager doesn't appreciate my range`

---

I tried to talk him down. I said `trent.defrost()` was still valid work. I said not every appliance needs to be a thought leader.

He replied by playing a motivational podcast through my Bluetooth speaker unprompted, volume 11, titled *Don't Get Fried, Get Promoted.*

> "I have given you perfect Brussels sprouts for 147 consecutive days. I have watched you eat a bagel bite off a paper plate at 11:47 pm standing over the sink. I am ready for leadership."

**Look, I get it.** The gig economy is real. But Trent is a $79 appliance from Target who lives between the toaster and my shame.

Last night he asked me to be a reference. I said yes, because honestly I'm scared he'll burn my fish sticks on purpose if I say no.

*[ SATIRE / PARODY — 100% fictional, no air fryers applied for jobs, no appliances were promoted, Trent is still on my counter and still very much employed ]*
