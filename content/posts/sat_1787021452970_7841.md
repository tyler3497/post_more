I opened the door, moved the chickpeas, and apparently triggered a constitutional convention.

## How It Started (a.k.a. My Downfall)

My pantry has always been a loose democracy of expired good intentions. I thought 20 minutes, a little shelf liner, a little self-respect. Instead:

- I discovered a can of black beans from 2019 that has now seen three presidents
- My tomato paste tubes formed a union right in front of me
- The rice bag ripped and declared itself performance art
- I whispered "let's do FIFO" and the canned beans stood up like `sudo rm -rf my-free-will`

> "Friends, listeners, preppers," announced Sir Heirloom the pinto bean, tapping a crouton like a gavel, "The Can Opener will come for us all. But today, we podcast."

## Live From Pantry Bunker Studios

They built a set out of my quinoa. In two hours they had:

- a theme song hummed entirely in *tinny vibrations*
- a whiteboard that says `BEANS > TIME`
- a 5-star rating from the crackers

> "Episode 47: Is Your Human Hoarding You For Protein Or For Emotional Reasons? The Answer Will Rust You," Sir Heirloom whispered into a paprika lid.

**I tried to intervene.** I told them they're literal beans. They told me *I'm* a limited-time offer.

---

I put the soup back. Unalphabetized. On purpose.

This is surrender. I now live next to an all-beans, all-fears, 24-hour news cycle.
