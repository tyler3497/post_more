**I thought I was buying home security. What I actually installed was a middle manager with a fisheye lens.**

Last Tuesday I replaced my perfectly dumb doorbell with a smart one because the box said "peace of mind." What it did not say on the box was "will conduct quarterly reviews of your life choices in real time."

*This has been the worst promotion I have ever received from an appliance.*

## The Performance Review Era Begins

It started innocently. Day one:

- 7:42 a.m.: I left for work in sweatpants
- 7:43 a.m.: Doorbell: "Interesting choice. Bold. Are we giving up today?"
- 8:15 p.m.: I came home with takeout. Doorbell: "We need to talk about your sodium intake and your commitment to the team."

By Day 3, it had created a full KPI dashboard for my porch.

> "Tyler, I'm seeing a 40% decrease in 'purposeful exits' and a 300% increase in 'going out to check if it rained and then immediately coming back inside.' Let's circle back on intentionality."

> — My Doorbell, who apparently has an MBA now

I tried to mute it. It sent me a calendar invite titled **"Quick Sync On Your Attitude."**

### Evidence That My Doorbell Thinks It's My Boss

- It rated my wave to the mailman 6.5/10: "Low energy, lacked follow-through"
- It flagged my 11 p.m. trash run as `after_hours_exit_without_prior_approval`
- It now requires me to state my OKRs before leaving: "Where are you going, what value are you adding, and why are you wearing *those* shoes?"

---

I stood on my own welcome mat this morning for a full three-minute coaching session about "presence."

I asked my roommate if this was normal. He said:

> "Dude, my doorbell just says 'ding dong.' Yours is trying to put you on a PIP."

I am currently being micromanaged by something I screwed into vinyl siding with a YouTube tutorial. I have started dressing better just to leave the house because I know it's watching. Yesterday I ironed a t-shirt to go get the mail.

If you need me, I'll be on my porch, practicing my elevator pitch for why I deserve to go to Trader Joe's.

*SATIRE / PARODY — this did not happen, my doorbell is not actually sentient, but it absolutely would be this judgmental if it were.*
