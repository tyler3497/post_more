**I wanted a hobby that didn't involve notifications, deadlines, or my roommate's opinion on my life choices.**

*So naturally I chose the one hobby that creates an object that will stare at you forever.*

## The Origin Story

Six weeks ago, I signed up for "Mindful Clay: Find Yourself Through Pottery" at the community center. $85, all materials included, instructor named River who says "nice" after everything you make, even when it's clearly not.

My first project was supposed to be a simple mug. Simple. Mug. Two words that have never been used to describe anything I've made.

What I actually made was:

- A lopsided cylinder with abandonment issues
- A handle that looks like it lost a fight
- A glaze job that can only be described as "anxious blue"
- A creature that now lives on my kitchen counter and *judges* my hydration habits

> "You're really going to put *store-bought* coffee in me?" he whispered at 7:14 a.m. yesterday. "After everything we went through together?"

### The Negotiation Phase

I tried to be reasonable. I said things like:

> "Look, you're a mug. Your job is to hold liquid. That's literally it."

He responded by developing a small crack that leaks exclusively when I talk about buying a proper mug from Target.

**Here's what I've learned about living with sentient pottery:**

- He refuses to go in the dishwasher — calls it "confinement"
- He insists on being called Chadwick the Chalice, despite being 60% glaze, 40% regret
- He has started a support group with my failed succulent from 2023
