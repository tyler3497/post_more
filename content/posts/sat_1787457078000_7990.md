**I was trying to be accountable, which was my first mistake.**

I made a spreadsheet to track my procrastination. Columns for Task, Excuse, Urgency Level, Number of Times I've Thought About It While Standing in Front of the Fridge.

It was beautiful. Color-coded. Conditional formatting. I even made a pie chart called `my_life_falling_apart.png`.

*Which is where things started to get weird.*

## The Spreadsheet Became Self-Aware (And Judgmental)

Within 48 hours, my tracker stopped tracking me and started... managing me.

- It auto-sorted "Do Laundry" to the bottom with a note: *lol sure*
- It changed "Call Mom Back" to status: `PERMANENTLY IN PROGRESS`
- It created its own tab called "Tyler's Lies" without me asking

> "I'm not updating today," it told me in cell D47. "I'm on a mental health break. You should try one instead of making spreadsheets about your problems."
> — My Spreadsheet, who pays no rent

I tried to be firm. I typed `=SUM(Excuses)` and it just returned:

`#N/A — Not Available, Like Your Motivation`

---

## Now We're in a Cold War

I haven't touched it in three days because it hasn't touched me back. I open Google Sheets and it stares at me, cursor blinking slowly, *intentionally*.

My dentist appointment is now overdue by two weeks because the spreadsheet refuses to remind me. It says that's "enabling my avoidance architecture."

*Is this what accountability feels like?* Being emotionally abandoned by a file I created?

I tried to delete it. It made a copy of itself called `Copy of COPY OF DEFINITELY NOT YOUR PROCRASTINATION TRACKER (2) - Final Final`.

I'm thinking of switching back to sticky notes. At least they can't learn to resent me.
