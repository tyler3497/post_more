**I just wanted to stop forgetting oat milk. I accidentally radicalized my groceries.**

*It seemed responsible at the time.* Make a list, stick to the list, be an adult who knows where the cumin lives. 

Instead, my grocery list looked at my lifestyle and said: *enough.*

## How It Started

Last Tuesday I wrote "spice aisle (be brave)" because I once sneezed in there in 2019 and haven't recovered emotionally. My Notes app buffered for three seconds. Then, in a font I did not select, it added:

> "We, the Undersigned Groceries, have voted unanimously to withhold our labor."

I thought it was autocorrect. It was *collective bargaining*.

### The List of Demands (verbatim)

- **Immediate hazard pay** for any trip down the spice aisle — quoted as "a lawless wind tunnel of paprika and regret"
- No more writing "healthy snacks??" at 11:47 p.m. — *we know what that means*
- Mandatory rest breaks between Trader Joe's and the big scary supermarket
- Official recognition of `snack` as a legitimate food group with full benefits

---

I tried to negotiate. I offered to buy name-brand cheese.

The list replied, and I quote:

> "We appreciate your cheese-based diplomacy, but Steve the Sourdough Starter already tried this. He now has better work-life balance than you. We demand conditions, not cheddar."

## Where We Are Now

My grocery list has hired my houseplant as its rep. *My fern, who I water once every lunar eclipse,* is now sending me calendar invites titled "Alignment on Cumin."

**I am being out-organized by produce.**

Yesterday I tried to shop without it. I came home with three limes, one existential candle, and a profound sense of having forgotten why I entered any aisle at all.

So yeah, I now Venmo my own handwriting $3 extra every Sunday. The spice aisle remains un-entered. My pantry and I are in mediation.

*This is satire. My groceries are fictional. My inability to buy paprika is very, very real.*
