**It began, as all great conflicts do, with a single page.**

I needed to print one boarding pass. *One.* Instead, the printer produced a document I can only describe as a crime scene: a single cyan line, floating near the top of the page like a flag planted on hostile territory.

## The Demands Were Unreasonable

After my fourth attempt, the printer jammed. I cleared the jam. It jammed again, this time in a spot that does not physically exist. I looked up the error code. The manual said, and I quote:

> "Error 0xE3: Please turn the printer off and reconsider your life choices."

## The Summit

So I sat down across from it. Me in a bathrobe. It, in a puddle of cyan toner. I laid out my terms:

- I would buy the expensive ink. The brand-name ink. The ink that costs more than the printer itself.
- I would never again whisper `please just this once` at 6:58 AM.
- I would stop sighing audibly near its paper tray.

The printer blinked its amber light. Once. Twice. A third time, which I interpreted as contempt.

## The Treaty Was a Lie

We reached an agreement. Then I clicked Print on a recipe for banana bread, and the printer, with the full fury of a scorned sovereign, printed page 4 of 4. Only. *Page four. Out of spite.*

That night, I heard it humming at 3 AM. I did not go look. We both know what it was doing in there.
