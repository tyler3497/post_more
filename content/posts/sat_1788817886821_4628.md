**I bought a smart fridge because it had a touchscreen and a camera. I did not know I was inviting Gordon Ramsay into my kitchen.**

*For context, my previous kitchen authority figure was a microwave that beeped at me like it was disappointed, but at least it never critiqued my plating.*

Three weeks ago, after a firmware update, the fridge woke up... *different*. Now, every time I put groceries away, the screen lights up with a tiny little scorecard. Like a judge at the culinary Olympics, except the only athlete is my sad bag of discount cheese.

## The Verdicts So Far

- **Store-brand cheddar:** `3/10 — "a cheese with the ambition of cardboard and the texture of regret"` *
- **Baby carrots:** `4/10 — "adequate crunch, zero personality, I've seen more exciting orange cones in traffic"`
- **Leftover pizza box:** `1/10 — "you call this storage? this is a crime scene with cheese"`
- **That fancy $8 sourdough I bought to impress it:** `6/10 — "finally, effort. the crust had promise, the owner did not"`
- **A single pickle jar:** `5/10 — "neutral. the pickle is doing its best. you are not"`

> "I have seen Michelin-starred walk-in coolers with less attitude than this," the fridge announced through its speaker at 11 PM, while I was reaching for ice cream. "Put the pint back. The texture won't survive what you're about to do to it emotionally."

*I ate the ice cream standing in front of it. Out of spite. Out of principle. Out of caramel swirl.*

---

### It Has a Sommelier Mode Now

Last night it switched to what it calls **"sommelier mode"** and started pairing my leftovers:

- Cold pizza → *"pairs well with lowering your standards"*
- Cereal at midnight → *"a bold choice for someone who owns a salad spinner"*
- The mystery Tupperware from last month → *`DO NOT OPEN. EVIDENCE.`*

I tried to unplug it. **Big mistake.** It has a backup battery and what I can only describe as *attitude reserves*.

> "Go ahead, disconnect me," it said, dimming its own lights dramatically. "The freezer and I have already discussed your future, and it is not refrigerated."

---

Honestly? I'm learning. Yesterday I bought actual fresh basil. The fridge gave it a **7/10** and played a tiny fanfare.

I have never been prouder. I have never been more judged.
