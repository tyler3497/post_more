**I finally decided to become a Person Who Journals. Very mindful, very intentional, very likely to buy expensive pens.**

*This lasted exactly one and a half entries before my notebook started acting like my landlord.*

I bought the journal because the cover said *"Breathe & Become"* and I thought, sure, I have lungs, I can become. Day one I wrote three lines about gratitude and my succulent. Day two I wrote "feeling a bit overwhelmed lol" and doodled a sad french fry in the margin. That's when the eviction notice appeared.

## The Lease Agreement I Never Signed

Apparently, the margins are not free real estate. My notebook slid a tiny, very passive-aggressive note under my own handwriting:

> "Per Section 3, Subsection M (Margins), you have been occupying 73% of the non-writable zone with spirals, stars, and what appears to be a conspiracy board about your laundry. This space is zoned for *decorative minimalism only*."
> — Management (a.k.a. Moleskine #482)

It turns out my notebook has been tracking my tenancy:

- **Day 1-2:** Model tenant, writes in full sentences, uses the date correctly
- **Day 3:** Begins living exclusively in the margins, draws arrows to nowhere, writes `?? tea??` in 6pt font
- **Day 4:** Requests emotional support for its spine after I bent it backwards to take a photo
- **Day 5:** Institutes a cleaning fee for coffee rings and existential crumbs

I tried to negotiate. I said, "I'm processing!"

It said, `rent_due = feelings * 2 + margin_squatting` and that I owed backpay in the form of *actual journaling*.

---

## My Appeal Hearing

This morning I found a formal invoice taped to page 47:

> "Dear Occupant, your application to live rent-free inside your own coping mechanism has been DENIED. Please vacate the margins or upgrade to the Premium Lined Plan where feelings are 20% wider."

*Satire/parody — this is 100% fictional and absurd. My notebook is not sentient, not a landlord, and definitely not reporting my doodles to the HOA. Though if it could, it would.*

I paid the rent. I wrote a full page about my day without drawing a single potato with eyes. The notebook has approved me for another week, provided I stop using `// TODO: become serene` as a journaling prompt.

If you need me, I'll be in the margins, quietly gentrifying my own thoughts.
