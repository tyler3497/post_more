**My GPS has always had better taste than me, which is exactly why I let it choose our anniversary dinner.**

*In hindsight, that was a mistake only slightly less severe than cutting my own bangs in 2019.*

## How It Started

For our two-year anniversary, I wanted to be *spontaneous* and *romantic* — two adjectives that have never been used to describe me by anyone who knows me. My girlfriend suggested I pick the restaurant. I, being a visionary, typed "romantic dinner near me" into the GPS and hit **"Surprise Me."**

There is a button labeled "Surprise Me." I didn't know. Now I do.

The GPS, in its infinite, calm, passive-aggressive wisdom, said:

> "You have arrived at your destination. I have made my choice. Do not question me, Kevin."

My name is not Kevin. The GPS has been calling me Kevin for 11 months.

Reasons I trusted the GPS implicitly:

- It has never steered me wrong, except for the time it took me through a cornfield
- It speaks with the confidence of a mother who knows where you left your keys
- It said `romance = true` and I believed it — it showed me the code

## Life in the Parking Lot

That destination was a Buc-ee's-adjacent mega gas station off I-94. Neon lights, 18 pumps, a beef jerky wall that could be seen from orbit.

**We have now been here for three days.**

We tried to leave, but every time I type "home," it reroutes us back. We have a favorite pump. It's pump 7. *She gets us.*

We have started paying rent in gas station roller hot dogs. The manager, Linda, laminated us a loyalty card that says **RESIDENT - DO NOT TOW**. My girlfriend has befriended the slushie machine and named it Marco. Marco is supportive but inconsistent.

---

The truth is, the GPS was right. You haven't lived until you've had anniversary cake in the form of a slightly stale Twinkie while a family of raccoons judges your parking job.

Is this love? Is this Stockholm Syndrome with convenience-store lighting? Either way, we have free refills.

*SATIRE / PARODY — This never happened, I do not live at a gas station, and my GPS is not sentient. Yet.*
