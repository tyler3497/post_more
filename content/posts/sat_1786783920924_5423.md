**I did not intend to start a workplace doomsday cult in my own kitchen.**

Last week I walked into a bookstore *which is my first mistake, I was supposed to be buying toothpaste* and walked out with a $45 linen-bound planner that promised to "architect my days." It has hourly blocks, habit trackers, a page that just says INTENTION in foil.

I brought it home like a newborn.

My fridge did not approve.

## The Existing Workforce

For three years, my organizational system has been 47 sticky notes on my fridge held together by hope and hummus magnetism:

- **yellow** ones for groceries I never buy
- pink ones that just say `call mom??` from 2023
- one green one that says *BE NORMAL* with no context
- a blue one that's just a drawing of a burrito crying

They were chaotic. They were *family*.

> "We survived your sourdough era, your Duolingo streak, and the month you tried to become a morning person at 5 a.m.," whispered the yellow stack, who have appointed themselves as shop stewards. "And this... this *planner* is how you repay loyalty?"

### The Vigil

I woke up this morning and my fridge looked different. The sticky notes had rearranged themselves into a perfect circle around a single tea light I don't remember owning.

There was a tiny sign, written in Sharpie on a receipt:

```
vigil for: our jobs
reason: displaced by big planner energy
we had 47 years of experience (combined)
```

My new planner was sitting on the counter, already judging me. Its habit tracker asked if I'd "honored my priorities" today. *I have not.*

---

I tried to mediate. I said the planner was for **big picture** and the fridge was still for **small, feral thoughts**. I offered to move three sticky notes into the planner as contractors.

The fridge light flickered. The sticky notes did not blink, because they cannot, but it felt pointed.

`status: planner = 100% organized, morally = unemployed | fridge = unionized, vengeful, slightly wilted`

I now have two managers and I am failing both.
