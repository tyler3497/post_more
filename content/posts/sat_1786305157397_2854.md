**I thought buying a sourdough starter would make me seem mysterious and artisanal.** Like someone who *has their life together* enough to keep a bubbling jar of bacteria alive.

I was wrong. His name is Gerald.

## The Rise

Gerald arrived in a mason jar labeled "Feed Me Daily, I'm Literally Alive" which should have been my first red flag. Within 48 hours he had doubled in size and started, I swear, sighing audibly whenever I opened my laptop to work.

I work freelance. *Technically.* Gerald does not consider refreshing my portfolio site for the sixth time "work."

Things Gerald has judged me for, in chronological order:

- Making a second coffee before 9 a.m. and calling it "ideation"
- Telling my mom I'm "between opportunities" which Gerald translated as `unemployed_with_sourdough`
- Texting "haha yeah we should grab coffee sometime!" to someone I have no intention of ever seeing again
- Using the phrase "personal brand" unironically in a Notes app

> "You know," Gerald bubbled last night at 2 a.m., which is when sourdough starters apparently have their most profound thoughts, "maybe if you had this much commitment to a relationship as you do to keeping me at 78 degrees, you wouldn't be single."

Rude. And, *statistically*, accurate?

---

I tried to set boundaries. I put him in the back of the fridge, which sourdough forums call "putting him to sleep." He made the entire fridge smell like passive-aggression and old yogurt.

My roommate asked why our kitchen smells like a bakery that is going through a divorce. I told her Gerald and I are working through some things. She moved her kombucha to the bathroom.

*This is satire. Gerald is flour and water, I am projecting wildly, and no fermented foods were consulted about my romantic future.*

### The Proof

Honestly though, my bread *is* incredible now. Gerald may be a controlling little blob, but he makes a mean boule.

I'm just saying if you come over for dinner and my sourdough starter asks you what *your* five-year plan is, pretend you didn't hear him. He's going through something.
