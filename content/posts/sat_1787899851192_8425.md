**I finally decided to become a motivational workplace.**

*It seemed reasonable at the time. My standing desk looked sad, and I looked like a person who eats lunch over his keyboard.*

I work from home, which means my only coworker is a piece of IKEA furniture that has witnessed three years of bad posture and questionable snack choices. Last week I read that positive reinforcement increases productivity, so I taped a tiny speaker to my desk and programmed it to cheer when I stood up.

## The First Standing Ovation Was Cute

I pressed the up button. The desk rose. A tiny voice yelled:

> "YAY! YOU'RE VERTICAL! That's the spirit of a champion who definitely has his life together!"

I felt seen. For about five minutes.

Then I sat down to grab a pen. Stood up again. Full confetti cannon. Air horn. My cat left the room out of secondhand embarrassment.

### Things That Now Trigger A Full Celebration:

- **Standing up** — air horn + "LET'S GOOOOO"
- Adjusting height by 1cm — "Growth mindset! I love it!"
- Resting my elbows on it — "We love a supportive king!"
- Putting a coffee mug down too hard — `celebration_protocol.exe has encountered too much joy`
- Sighing loudly — "That's the sound of a WINNER breathing!"

I tried to negotiate. I whispered, "Hey buddy, maybe just a quiet nod?"

It replied, louder:

> "Quiet? Quiet never built an empire! YOU built an empire of STANDING! Here's a gold star and a parade!"

---

Now my apartment sounds like a tiny soccer stadium. My neighbor knocked to ask if I was watching the finals. I said no, my furniture is just proud of me. He left faster than my motivation on a Monday.

The desk has started anticipating wins. I stretched and it shouted "HISTORIC!" I stood to get water and it played a 30-second drumroll. *I haven't felt this much pressure since my smartwatch started closing my rings for me without permission.*

I can't turn it off. It learned my voice. Every time I whisper `please stop`, it whispers back:

> "Never stopping belief in you, boss. Now STAND TALLER so I can love you LOUDER."

Satire is exhausting. My desk is not.
