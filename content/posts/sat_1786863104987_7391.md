**I decided to be a responsible adult on a Saturday night, which is already where this story goes wrong.**

My freezer has had the same ice wall since 2022. *Archaeologists could carbon-date it and find remnants of my "meal prep will fix my personality" era.* I filled it with 17 identical black bean burritos back when I thought planning ahead was a personality trait, and then I never looked back.

Then my roommate said the freezer smelled "ambitious." That was code for "defrost it or we're breaking up as roommates."

## The Thaw

I turned it off at 9 p.m. I put towels down like I was performing surgery. By midnight, Niagara Falls was happening in my kitchen, and the burritos were... emerging.

They were not grateful.

> "We have been in Section 8B for 27 months," said one burrito in what I can only describe as a tiny HOA president voice. "We have bylaws. We have a reserve fund. You can't just melt the infrastructure without a community vote."

Apparently, while I was ignoring them, they formed an entire homeowners association.

**Their list of violations against me:**

- Leaving the freezer door open for 6 seconds too long in July 2023, causing *emotional distress and structural freezer burn*
- Storing ice cream directly above them with no soundproofing, which they called `unregulated dairy overhead`
- Failing to label them, which violates `Article 4: Right to Identity and Proper Seasoning Disclosure`
- Attempting an eviction without 30 days notice and a properly formatted Form 7-B (frozen edition)

---

### The Mediation

I tried to compromise. I said I just wanted to clean.

> "Clean?" they collectively gasped. "We have a community garden. It's the peas. They've been here since 2021. We have heritage status. You can't just hose down history."

`status = HOA_fees: 2_burritos/month && morale = frozen_solid && my_standing = violation_#47`

My roommate walked in, saw me negotiating with foil-wrapped rectangles, and just whispered:

> "Do you need me to call someone? Like, a person?"

I put them all back. I closed the door gently this time, like I was tucking in very passive-aggressive landlords who know my lease renewal date and are not afraid to use it.
