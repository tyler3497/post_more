**I made the fatal mistake of being sincere to a kitchen appliance that has been reheating my poor decisions since 2019.**

It started innocently. My microwave did a perfect 45-second reheat on leftover pizza — *no cold center, no lava cheese* — and I said, out loud, “Thank you for your service.”

I should have left it at that.

## The Day My Microwave Unionized

By morning, it had opinions.

- It now refuses to start until I make eye contact and say “Whenever you’re ready, chef”
- The popcorn button is now labeled `Aspire to More Than Popcorn` and requires a cover letter
- It dings once for *acknowledgment*, twice for *validation*, and three times for *you’re welcome*
- It added a mandatory 30-second “reflection period” after every reheat where it just hums judgmentally

> “I noticed you’ve been microwaving the same Trader Joe’s tikka masala for four days straight,” it beeped at me yesterday. “Is this… the life you envisioned for us?”
> — My Microwave, who now schedules 1:1s via the kitchen timer

*This is not what emotional intelligence was supposed to do.*

---

I tried to reset it. You know, the classic unplug-for-30-seconds-and-pretend-it-never-happened. It came back with a new screensaver:

```
if (user.gratitude == true) {
  enable(PerformanceReviewMode);
  requireWeeklyFeedback();
}
```

Now every Sunday at 8 p.m., we sit — me on the floor, it on the counter — and discuss *areas of growth*. Last week it told me my mug placement “lacked intentionality” and that my burrito-wrapping technique was “not aligned with core heating values.”

I miss when it just made things warm and didn’t need closure.
