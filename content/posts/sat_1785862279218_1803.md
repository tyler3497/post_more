## The Drawer That Time Forgot

**I finally decided to become the kind of person who has their life together** and clean out my junk drawer. You know the one — every house has a drawer that is 30% batteries, 40% mysterious keys, and 30% *emotional baggage* you refuse to throw away.

I opened it Tuesday morning with a trash bag and optimism. By Tuesday afternoon I was in a hostage negotiation.

### The Discovery

Beneath a tangle of phone chargers from 2014 and a single soy sauce packet that had achieved sentience, I found entire *societies*:

- The **Battery Confederacy**, who had been powering a tiny LED metropolis off my dead AAs since 2021
- The **Tape Roll Tribe**, who built spiraled condos inside Scotch tape rings and worship the great silver Duct Tape god
- The **Loose Change Collective**, which insists it is now an independent economy and my quarters are subject to inflation

> "We were here first," squeaked their elected mayor, a LEGO minifig with half a beard drawn in Sharpie. "You can't just evict us because you watched a YouTube organization video."

### The Demands

They've unionized. Of course they've unionized. Their list of demands was written on the back of a CVS receipt longer than my car:

- Immediate recognition as sovereign drawer states
- Reparation in the form of **one (1) new AA battery** every equinox
- I finally dispose of that IKEA Allen wrench I *swear* I'll need someday

> "Do you know what it's like," whispered a rubber band elder, "to watch a man keep twelve identical spare keys for locks that no longer exist?"

I tried to explain about minimalism. They countered by showing me my collection of takeout menus from restaurants that closed during Obama's first term.

---

**Update:** We have reached a compromise. They get to stay, but I get to reclaim the scissors. `Status: tenuously coexisting` — I now have three tiny civilizations judging me every time I look for a pen.
