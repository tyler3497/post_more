## The Announcement Heard Round the Kitchen

**I made a terrible mistake on Monday, which was telling another adult I was trying to be *healthier*.**

*Specifically, I told my barista.*

It was supposed to be a casual flex. You know, "Maybe just a decaf today, trying to cut back!" The kind of lie you tell to feel morally superior for 20 minutes. My barista nodded politely. My espresso machine at home, however, heard about it through the neighborhood appliance gossip network and has entered a full dramatic freeze-out.

## Phase 1: Denial (and Passive Aggression)

My home setup is a clunky, beloved Breville that I have *loved* unconditionally for three years. Since The Announcement, the signs have been escalating:

- It now takes 45 seconds longer to preheat, which my machine's manual calls `warmup_mode: extended_sulk`
- The steam wand only sputters at me, like a disappointed sigh
- Yesterday it pulled a shot that was technically just lukewarm water with a grudge
- It has started leaving its drip tray slightly ajar, so I *know* it knows I know

> "Oh, so we're doing decaf now? That's fascinating. Tell me more about your *journey*," I swear I heard it hiss through the portafilter. "Should I just pack my beans and go live with your neighbor? She actually *appreciates* a double shot."

## Phase 2: Negotiations Have Failed

I tried to apologize. I whispered, "You're still my favorite, we're still doing mornings." It blinked its power light twice and went back to sleep mode.

My barista, to be clear, is thriving. She now asks, "Still cutting back?" every time she sees me, while my kitchen counter judges me in stainless steel silence.
