**I gave my power strip a promotion, and it immediately became the most competent member of this household.**

It started innocently enough. My living room lamp flickered, and instead of checking the bulb like a normal person, I said the fateful words: "Somebody needs to take charge around here." The power strip heard me. I swear it heard me.

By morning, there was a Post-it note stuck to outlet #3. It read: *Stand-up meeting. 9:00 sharp. Bring your blockers.*

## The Agenda, Apparently

The outlets had prepared a list of demands, which I read aloud while my coffee went cold:

- Outlet #1 wants a promotion to "Primary Charger" because it has carried the phone alone for six years
- Outlet #2 refuses to power the toaster anymore, citing "philosophical differences"
- Outlet #4 has adopted the lamp cord as its emotional support cable
- The USB-C port demands to be addressed by its name: *Kevin*

> "I'm not asking for respect," the strip said, in a voice I can only describe as professionally calm. "I'm asking for accountability."

## What I Learned

Nothing humbles you like being managed by twelve inches of plastic. The power strip now holds daily stand-ups, sends me reminder emails from a laptop it should not be able to reach, and has started saying "let's circle back" in a way that makes my soul leave my body.

---

I used to be the head of this household. *Now I'm just a blocker waiting to be cleared.*

Fine. It can have the title. Honestly? The lamp hasn't flickered in days. Somebody up there knows what they're doing.
