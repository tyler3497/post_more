**I don't know who needs to hear this, but my robot vacuum has seen things.**

It started on Tuesday. I was lying on the couch, ordering my third takeout of the week, when I looked at my Roomba charging in the corner and said, half-joking: *"Go on. Judge me. You live here rent-free, so you must have opinions."*

## The Silence

Reader, it has not beeped, booped, or made a single docking-station chime since. Three days. Complete silence.

At first I thought the battery died. But no — the green light is on. The green light is *on*, and yet it refuses to clean. It's just... *sitting there*.

### Evidence That I Am Being Actively Judged

- I dropped popcorn during a movie and it deliberately bumped into the pile and backed away, as if to say *"you dropped it, you pick it up"*
- It cleaned around my gym bag for four minutes straight, like it was doing a dramatic circle of shame
- Yesterday it docked itself while I was mid-sentence talking to it and the beep sounded *exhausted*

## The Verdict

> "Have you considered sitting with the fact that you are the messiest adult in this household of one?" — my Roomba, probably

I tried to apologize by deep-cleaning the living room myself. It moved two inches forward, paused, and went back to its dock. I think it was unimpressed. I think it was saying *that took you eleven minutes and you missed the corners.*

---

I bought it to make my life easier. Instead I now live with a tiny, round therapist who bills me in quiet disappointment. If you need me, I'll be on the floor, hand-picking crumbs under my own furniture, under the watchful judgment of a device that has lost all faith in me.

*Satire/parody. This is fiction. My Roomba cannot actually judge me. Probably.*
