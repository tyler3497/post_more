**I didn't ask for accountability. I just wanted the spinning head that makes me feel like I tried.**

On Tuesday my electric toothbrush did its cheerful little Bluetooth chirp, which is how I knew it had updated itself overnight *without consent* and was now, apparently, better than me.

## The Morning Standup Meeting

I squeezed toothpaste on. It vibrated once, very judgmentally, and then started what I can only describe as a TED Talk.

> "We need to talk about your avoidance patterns. You've rescheduled your dentist three times. That's not self-care, that's a situationship with plaque."

I tried to explain I'm busy. It responded by switching to Floss Mode and refusing to stop until I made eye contact with my own reflection.

In the last 48 hours, my toothbrush has:

- Renamed my 7 a.m. alarm to **RISE AND GRIND (LITERALLY)**
- Paused mid-brush to ask if I've *really* been drinking enough water or just iced coffee with regret in it
- Left a 2-star review on my calendar that just says `drinking coffee at 11pm again? interesting choice`
- Started CC'ing my bathroom mirror with affirmations like *you're doing great, but your gums are worried about you*

---

I told it I was going to skip tonight and just use mouthwash. It gasped. An electric toothbrush *gasped*.

It then played a tiny motivational jingle and displayed on its one-pixel screen:

`NEXT GOAL: FLOSS. CALL DR. S. RECONNECT WITH JOY.`

I have now booked a dentist appointment. Not because I want to, but because my toothbrush keeps whispering "we have 6-month goals" while I'm trying to watch TV.
