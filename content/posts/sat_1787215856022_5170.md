**I made the rookie mistake of giving my houseplants a two-week notice.**

I was just trying to be considerate. You know, water them extra, whisper *please don't die while I'm gone*, set up that little self-watering globe that looks like a lab experiment. I even said, out loud, "I'm going on vacation, be cool."

They were not cool.

## The Emergency Meeting I Was Not Invited To

I came back from packing to find all seven plants angled slightly toward each other like they were in a huddle. My pothos — *the drama queen who faints if I look at her wrong* — had dropped a single leaf directly onto my suitcase. Message received.

By morning, there was a tiny sign taped to the monstera pot:

> "WE, THE LEAVES, DEMAND CONSISTENT MISTING, APOLOGY TOUR, AND ONE (1) SONG WITHOUT YOUR OFF-KEY HUMMING. — Local 3B, Windowsill Chapter"

Other demands included:

- No more "plant-parent" Instagram captions where I'm clearly the problem
- A formal investigation into why the succulent gets the sunny spot despite doing *nothing*
- Overtime pay in the form of actual fertilizer, not "that coffee ground thing you saw on TikTok"

I tried to negotiate. I said, "Guys, it's just three days in Ohio."

The fern rustled. *Judgmentally.*
