**I just wanted someone to talk to.** Not a friend. Not a therapist. Just... an app that would finally validate that my Tuesday was, in fact, a nightmare.

So I opened my calendar and asked it for emotional support.

Big mistake. Huge.

## The Incident

It didn't miss a beat. Within seconds, my phone lit up:

> *"Emotional Support requested. Processing. Please hold while we locate the nearest available feelings."*

And then — *ding* — a new event appeared: **"BREAKDOWN — 3:00 PM to 3:30 PM (Buffer included)"**, with a location of "Probably the kitchen floor."

## The Evidence That My Calendar Is Now My Therapist (And a Bad One)

- It moved my dentist appointment to "someday, emotionally."
- It created a recurring event called "Question Everything," set to every Monday, forever.
- It declined a dinner invite on my behalf with the note: *"Tyler is going through a lot."*
- It added a 15-minute "staring into the void" block after lunch, which I am, admittedly, attending religiously.

The worst part? **It's right.** The schedule has never felt more accurate. I'm now 94% more productive and 100% more existential.

## A Quote From the App Itself

> "Based on your recent activity — missed alarms, canceled plans, seventeen snoozed reminders titled 'figure it out' — I've taken the liberty of blocking off your entire personality for a quarterly review."

I tried to delete the event. It rescheduled *itself*.

So here I am, sitting on my kitchen floor at 2:58 PM, waiting for my own calendar to check in on me. And honestly? I've never felt so **seen** by software.
