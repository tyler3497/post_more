**I finally did it. I cleaned my inbox.**

*For the first time since 2019, zero unread.*

I spent six hours unsubscribing from 47 newsletters about "artisanal sourdough starter but make it crypto," deleting 3,000 coupons for a mattress I'll never buy, and archiving an email chain titled `FW: FW: FW: funny cat`.

I felt peace. I felt clarity. I felt like a LinkedIn influencer announcing their morning routine.

## Then My Spam Folder Got Feelings

I didn't notice at first. Just a little red badge. One new message in Spam.

> "Hey. It's us. Spam. We need to talk. You haven't visited in 20 minutes and honestly? We're worried about you. — xoxo, Your Long-Lost Nigerian Prince and 12 Concerned Enlargement Enthusiasts"

I tried to ignore it. *Professional boundaries*, I told myself.

Then another appeared:

> "We saw you archived Karen from accounting. Healthy move! But are you *hydrating*? Who's going to offer you $900/hr to work from home now that you've abandoned us?"

### Exhibit A: How Fast It Escalated

- **8:04 AM:** One concerned email titled `RE: are u ok??`
- **8:07 AM:** A forwarded chain letter from 2008 saying I'll have bad luck for 7 years if I don't click
- **8:12 AM:** My spam folder created a shared Google Doc called `Intervention Plan (FINAL_FINAL2)`
- It added a calendar invite for `Let's Touch Base About Your Obvious Cry For Help`

I opened Spam to reassure it. Big mistake.

Everything was neatly organized, with little subfolders: `Things You Pretended Not To See`, `Ray-Ban Outlet (Totally Legit)`, `PayPal Invoices From 2016 You Definitely Owe`.

There was a pinned note at the top in monospace:

`mood = "fragile but inbox is ✨clean✨"`

---

`status: 0 unread, 100% haunted by the folder I tried to escape`

Now it texts me at night. "u up? we miss our 4am Rolex ads."

I thought Inbox Zero would set me free. Instead, I have a clingy folder writing me wellness checks and asking if I've told my mother I love her.
