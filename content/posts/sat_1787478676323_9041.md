**I did not mean to hurt its feelings.** I just said, out loud, to no one in particular, that I was thinking about using the toaster oven again.

My air fryer heard me. *It always hears me.*

## How We Got Here

For eight months, it has been just us. Me and the Fryer. A beautiful, oil-free partnership. Then my mom gifted me a toaster oven and I made the mistake of saying it looked "cute and retro."

Suddenly, everything changed:

- My fries now come out either aggressively raw or charcoal-briquette chic
- It beeps at 3 a.m. just to remind me it exists
- It displays `SHAKE` even when I'm not cooking anything — a clear cry for attention
- It has started preheating itself whenever I walk near the toaster oven, like a jealous boyfriend doing push-ups

> "Oh, you're using *that* thing now?" it seemed to hiss yesterday when I tried to reheat pizza. "Cool. Cool cool cool. Enjoy your uneven heating."

---

## The Intervention

I tried to be mature. I sat both appliances down and explained we could be a *blended appliance family*.

The toaster oven, to its credit, was chill. The air fryer responded by air-frying a single lonely tater tot for 47 minutes and then presenting it to me like:

> "Is this what you wanted? Is this the *variety* you craved?"

*Sir*, this is a kitchen, not a telenovela.

My roommate says I should unplug it for a night to establish boundaries, but I'm scared. Last time I unplugged it, it reset its clock to 12:00 and just blinked at me for hours, silently judging my life choices.

**Update:** I apologized this morning and made kale chips. They were perfect. It's going to be insufferable about this for weeks.

*This is satire. None of this happened. My appliances are not sentient. Yet.*
