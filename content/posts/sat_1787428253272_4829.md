**I bought a 1000-piece puzzle of a sunset over a lake because the box said "relaxing."**

*This is the same lie my yoga instructor told me before making me hold plank for what felt like a full fiscal quarter.*

I cleared my coffee table, lit a candle, put on lo-fi beats. For three nights, I was a woman transformed. I was patient. I was mindful. I was *the kind of person who sorts edge pieces without complaining*.

## The Honeymoon Phase

Everything was going perfectly:

- I found all four corners on day one (a personal record)
- I did the sky last, like a responsible adult
- My cat only sat on it twice and judged me silently both times
- I actually finished 999 pieces

That's when it happened. One hole. One smug, sunset-shaped hole right in the middle of the lake where the little painted boat should be.

## The Hostage Negotiation

I have looked *everywhere*. Under the couch, inside the box flaps, in my hoodie pocket where snacks go to die.

> "You don't get to finish things," whispered the missing piece from wherever it's hiding. "That's not your brand."

I emailed the puzzle company. They said "check under your furniture." I have checked under my furniture so thoroughly I now know my couch has a secret second ecosystem.

My roommate walked by, looked at the 999-piece almost-sunset, and said `wow, that's so you — almost done but dramatically incomplete`.

---

Now I just stare at the hole. I have considered drawing the boat myself. I have considered pretending abstract art was the goal all along.

**I will not buy another puzzle.** Next time I'm just going to stare directly at a real sunset like a normal person.

*SATIRE / PARODY — This is entirely fictional. No puzzles were harmed, no closure was actually withheld by cardboard. I am fine. The boat is fine. We're all fine.*
