*Satire. This is a work of fiction. Any resemblance to actual fish finance professionals is coincidental and frankly alarming.*

**I spent three weekends building a PowerPoint presentation for a fish.** That is not the most embarrassing part of this story. The embarrassing part is that it worked.

It started innocently enough. Bubbles — a common goldfish with a memory I had *severely* underestimated — kept following my cursor across the tank glass while I checked my portfolio. So, as a joke, I narrated my holdings to him. "This is diversification, buddy." He did a little loop. I interpreted that as interest, which, in retrospect, was the first sign I had lost my grip.

## The Evidence That Bubbles Is a Financial Prodigy

- **Day 1:** I explained dollar-cost averaging. He swam left. Left means *yes* in my house now.
- **Day 4:** He ignored my ETF recommendations but went feral over a screenshot of a small-cap semiconductor stock. It's up 34% since.
- **Day 9:** I placed two food pellets near two printouts — an index fund prospectus and a meme stock thread. He ate the index fund printout. *The man has discipline.*

> "Honestly? The fish has better instincts than half the analysts on my floor," said my coworker Dave, who has now put $200 into a Roth IRA "for the fish" as a birthday gift.

Meanwhile, my own 401(k) is down for the quarter. *My fish* — an animal that eats his own reflection — is outpacing my target-date retirement fund.

## The Situation Has Escaped My Control

Bubbles now demands the morning financial news. He boycotts flakes when the market is red. Last Tuesday I caught him staring at a bond ladder diagram with what I can only describe as professional contempt.

My financial advisor has stopped returning my calls, though I suspect that's unrelated. Or related.

The worst part? I Googled "can a goldfish open a brokerage account" at 2 a.m. *The fact that I even checked* means I have already decided Bubbles knows something I don't. My retirement plan is now, essentially, **whatever the fish says**.

To all the certified planners out there: respect the bowl. Bubbles sees things we cannot.
