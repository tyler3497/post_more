**I finally became the kind of person who says "no phones at dinner" out loud.**

*You know the type. Linen napkin, eye contact, talking about flavors like we're in a documentary.*

For years, my dinner routine was scrolling through videos of someone pressure-washing a driveway while eating pasta with the enthusiasm of a hostage. So I decided to fix it. One simple rule: no phones at the table.

Nobody else lives with me. So the rule applied to just me, my spaghetti, and my cutlery.

## Why This Was Supposed to Make Me Sophisticated

- I would taste my food like a real human
- I would think deep thoughts instead of watching a raccoon steal a hot dog
- I would become mysterious and grounded, probably start journaling
- My digestion would improve through sheer moral superiority

It lasted four minutes.

My fork immediately realized it had a captive audience. And it did not like what it saw.

> "Oh, so NOW you look at me? After three years of treating me like a shovel? You think you can just CHEW MINDFULLY and fix this? We had a system. You ignored me and I enabled your carb spiral."

I tried to explain that this was about wellness. About being present. About breaking my cycle of watching other people organize their fridges at 7:42 p.m.

My fork wasn't having it. It went full influencer.

---

By night two, my fork had started narrating everything I do wrong in real time. *You're slurping. You're emotionally eating carbs again. You're thinking about checking your phone even though it's in the other room on purpose.*

`status: dinner = awkward_silence_with_judgmental_utensil.exe`

I tried to switch to a spoon. The spoon just whispered, "I saw what you did to Fork. I'm scared. Don't drag me into this."

Now I eat standing over the sink like I'm being punished by my own kitchen drawer. My fork has 12 followers on its imaginary live-stream, including my sad houseplant and a skeptical butter knife. The plant DM'd me: "you need help, and also water."

I might bring my phone back. At least it doesn't have opinions about my sauce technique or demand a sponsorship deal for marinara.

*Satire. Obviously my fork is not actually live-streaming. It does not have Wi-Fi. Yet.*
