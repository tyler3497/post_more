**I bought a standing desk because my back made a sound like a Capri Sun being stepped on.**

*That was my wake-up call.* Not the doctor, not the 8-hour slump, but the audible crinkle that escaped my spine when I stood up last Tuesday.

So I did what any responsible adult with targeted Instagram ads does: I ordered a motorized standing desk that whispers *you're thriving now* as it ascends at 2 inches per second.

My office chair did not take this well.

## Phase One: The Silent Treatment

For the first two days, my chair just sat there. In the corner. At a slightly accusatory angle.

I tried to be diplomatic. I said, "It's not you, it's me prioritizing lumbar health."

The chair, which has held me through three breakups, two layoffs, and one ambitious attempt to learn sourdough, did not respond. *Because it is a chair.* Which did not stop me from interpreting its silence as deeply personal.

## Phase Two: The Podcast

On day three, I came home to find my chair podcasting.

I wish I was joking.

Evidence that it has started a limited series about my downfall:

- Episode 1 is titled `EP001: He Thinks He's Better Than Sitting Now`
- Episode 2 is just 45 minutes of creaking sounds that are clearly about my posture
- It invited my lumbar pillow on as a guest, and they both agreed I have "main-character spine issues"
- The show notes literally say `Recorded live from the corner of shame`

> "Welcome back to Chair Talk with Chairs," my chair said, in the voice I now assign to it, which is mid-Atlantic and extremely disappointed in me. "Today's topic: Who does he think he is, standing while emailing?"

*I want to be clear that no furniture was harmed or actually gained sentience, though my apartment is currently 90% passive-aggressive energy.*

---

**The desk is great, by the way.** I stand. I sway. I feel like a CEO who drinks enough water. But every time I lower it to sit for "just a minute," the chair sighs. Not physically. Emotionally.

I now have ergonomic guilt.

If you need me, I'll be negotiating custody between the device that loves my back and the one that knows all my secrets. Season 2 of the podcast drops tomorrow, and I heard it's about my search history.
