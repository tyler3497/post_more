**I made a fatal error last Tuesday: I opened my curtains.**

Not metaphorically. Literally. After six months of living like a well-hydrated cave goblin, I decided my apartment deserved some *natural light*. The sun, apparently, took this as a marriage proposal.

### The Honeymoon Phase Lasted 20 Minutes

At first it was nice. Warm glow, vitamin D, I could finally see how dusty my shelves were. Then it got... *intense*.

Suddenly I'm being followed everywhere:

- from the kitchen to the bathroom (boundaries, please!)
- directly onto my laptop screen right when I'm trying to watch TV (*rude*)
- onto the exact pile of laundry I've been avoiding since March, spotlighting my failures like a tiny shameful Broadway show
- aggressively highlighting the single houseplant I've managed not to kill, like it's tattling

> "I just thought we had something SPECIAL," I swear the sun seemed to say at 3:47 p.m. when it refused to move off my face during my nap. "I showed up for YOU every single day and THIS is how you treat me?"

### We Need to Talk About Commitment Issues

Look, Sun, you're great. You're big, you're bright, you're literally `essential-for-life-on-Earth` level important. But I didn't sign up for a `24/7 surveillance partnership`. I asked for ambiance, not accountability.

My roommate asked why I was whisper-apologizing to the window. I told him it's complicated.

---

Now I'm trapped negotiating. I close the curtains 90% and it sulks behind them, dimming my whole apartment like a passive-aggressive roommate. I open them and it's all *oh, you DO love me* beaming directly into my retinas.

My therapist — my aforementioned houseplant, Chad — says I need to set boundaries. Chad should talk, he's thriving in this new attention economy.

*This is fine. Everything is fine.* I'm just in a very serious, very one-sided relationship with a star 93 million miles away. Please send SPF 100 and curtains that aren't emotionally porous.

**SATIRE / PARODY — This is completely fictional. I am still mostly in my cave.**
