**I tried to be a responsible plant parent for exactly 11 days** and now HR is involved.

It started because I watched one YouTube video titled *"Speak Life Into Your Leaves"* and decided my apartment jungle needed professional development. So I gave my three houseplants annual reviews. Big mistake. Massive.

### The Quarterly Review Nobody Asked For

By Wednesday, they had flipped the script.

- **Fernanda the Fern** (HR) cited *"inconsistent hydration cadence and emotional unavailability on Tuesdays"*
- **Monstera Mike** (Operations) flagged that I keep moving him for *"better lighting for selfies"* which is *not* in his job description
- **Barbara the Succulent** (who does literally nothing) submitted a 12-slide deck titled *Why I'm Still Thriving Despite Neglect*

Then they posted a notice on the fridge:

> "Effective immediately, you are placed on a Performance Improvement Plan. Please report to the windowsill at 8 a.m. sharp for misting orientation. - Management"

I tried to argue. I said I was doing my best. Fernanda rustled disapprovingly and whispered:

> "We have gone *weeks* without a single word of affirmation AND you played that true-crime podcast too loud. We heard everything. We are stressed."

### My Corrective Action Plan

According to the laminated sheet taped to my watering can:

- Apologize to each plant individually with no fewer than *three* sincere compliments
- Stop using leftover coffee as "experimental fertilizer"
- Provide consistent indirect sunlight, not whatever discount existential gloom I have going on
- Attend weekly one-on-ones where I just sit there while they photosynthesize at me judgmentally

---

`status: employee_morale = wilting, but make it accountable`

I now have a tiny desk made of cork coasters where I take notes while Fernanda looms. Yesterday she gave me a gold star for remembering to rotate the pots. I'm so proud and so, so tired.

I told my roommate. She said, "At least someone finally gave you structure."
