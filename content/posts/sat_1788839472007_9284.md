**I decided to give my own life five stars on the internet, and the internet has questions.**

*It started as a self-esteem exercise my therapist suggested. It has become a content moderation nightmare.*

## The Review That Started It All

I'd had a genuinely good Tuesday. My coffee tasted like coffee. I found a parking spot in front of the building. My houseplant looked... not dead. So I thought, why not spread a little positivity? I opened a review site, typed in the location field "My Life (General)", and wrote:

> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"Honestly? Pretty solid overall. Great natural light. The commute is short because I work from the couch. Would live again."*

I signed it `Verified Resident`.

## The Algorithm Smells Fear (and Lies)

Within 14 minutes, my review was flagged. The automated notice said:

- **"Your review exhibits patterns consistent with inauthentic optimism"**
- **"Real people do not use the phrase 'would live again' outside of hospice testimonials"**
- **"You mentioned 'great natural light' but your photos show curtains that haven't opened since 2021"**

> "We've received reports that this review may be incentivized," read the official notice. "Did anyone pressure you into writing this? (You may say 'me.')"

I tried to appeal. I wrote in the dispute box: `if (life_satisfaction > 0.7) { stars = 5 } else { stars = 3.5 }`. They replied with an automated form letter saying my appeal had been escalated to a **Human Authenticity Specialist** named Debra.

### Debra's Investigation

Debra called me. Actual human. She asked probing questions:

- "Can you name one specific thing that earned the fifth star?"
- "You wrote 'the vibes are immaculate.' Sir, your smoke alarm battery is chirping in the background *right now*."
- "Your rating history shows you gave your laundry pile one star last month. Now life gets five? Interesting trajectory."

She put me on hold for eleven minutes. When she came back, my review had been demoted to **3.8 stars with a "suspected self-review" warning badge**.

---

**The worst part?** Other people have started reviewing my life too. Someone named `TruthTeller88` gave it two stars: *"Parking is good but the resident sings in the shower and it's frankly too much."* My landlord liked the review. *He liked it.*

I've deleted the review now. My life is officially **unrated**, which is the most honest thing I've ever done.
