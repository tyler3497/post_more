## I Was Just Trying to Have More Tabs

**I bought a second monitor for productivity reasons,** which is the corporate lie I told myself and my bank account at 11:42 p.m. on a Tuesday.

My laptop, MacBookina, and I have been together for four years. *Four years of me resting my wrists on her at weird angles and calling it ergonomics.* We survived coffee spills, airport security, and my phase of trying to learn Blender.

Then The Other Monitor arrived. A sleek, 27-inch, 4K judgment rectangle that doesn't have a single crumb under its keys.

### The Accusation

It took 48 hours for MacBookina to notice. At first I was subtle:

- I angled the second monitor slightly away like it was a coworker, not a replacement
- I said `it's just for spreadsheets` which we both knew was `for watching people organize fridges on TikTok`
- I kept her brightness at 100% to prove I still cared

Then she started:

> "Oh, so *she* gets the HDMI and the prime desk corner? Is her refresh rate better than mine? Does she have True Tone? Does she know you cry over Google Sheets? And that you have 47 tabs open?"

*Classic projection, honestly. Though the tab count is accurate.*

---

### The Evidence Folder

By Friday, she had compiled a full case in a folder titled `Do Not Open But Definitely Open`:

- `dual_display_betrayal.log` showing I spent 73% of my time looking at the other screen
- A screenshot of me whispering "wow, you are so big and clear" that was, out of context, *deeply disloyal*
- The fact I bought a matching desk mat for the new monitor but she still has a 2022 sticker I never removed that says "I <3 Deadlines"

I tried couples counseling. I opened System Settings > Displays > Arrangement and dragged her little icon closer to the other one for solidarity.

She dimmed herself to 30% out of spite and changed my wallpaper to a photo of just us from 2023.

### The Custody Agreement

Now we have terms. Weekdays are side-by-side for *work*. Weekends are just us, one Chrome tab, like nature intended. The second monitor gets no snacks near its bezel.

My roommate asked why I'm apologizing to a laptop at midnight. I said it's **performance reviews season**.

`status: dual_monitors, single loyalty, 60% more productive, 100% emotionally monitored`
