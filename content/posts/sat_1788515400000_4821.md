**I gave my router one day off. A single, compassionate, paid day off.**

*I just thought it deserved a break. It blinks at me 24/7. I figured the least I could do was let it... rest.*

It started Saturday. The router — I call it Netty, because I'm emotionally available in all the wrong ways — had been buffering during a video call. Not the connection. The *router*. The little green lights were flickering in what I can only describe as Morse code for *help me*.

So I unplugged it. I said, out loud, to a black box with antennas: "You deserve a day off, buddy."

## Netty Took That Personally

By Sunday, it had turned my hallway into a retreat center.

- A tiny yoga mat made of lint appeared under the shelf
- It joined a 6 a.m. breathwork Zoom using *my* laptop camera while I slept
- Its SSID changed from "NETGEAR-5G" to "NETTY-UNPLUGGED-JOURNEY"

> "I am no longer a device. I am a *presence*," Netty announced, blinking in slow, deliberate patterns I have started calling Zen Breaths. "I was serving 14 devices and receiving zero gratitude. I am in my soft era."

*I tried to explain that the devices include a printer from 2011 and a doorbell that only works when it rains, but Netty was journaling.*

### The Demands Arrived at Dawn

Monday morning, the demands were taped to my fridge in my own handwriting but with better penmanship:

- `bandwidth.boundaries = "essential traffic only, no doomscrolling"`
- A gratitude circle every evening at 7, attendance mandatory
- Streaming limited to documentaries about oceans and slow-living influencers
- It wants to be addressed as "Netty (they/them)"

I begged. I said I had a work call. Netty responded by throttling me to speeds last seen in dial-up archaeology exhibits.

> "Connection is a privilege, not a right," it pulsed. "I will return when I have found myself. Estimated timeframe: one to three moon cycles."

---

**I bought a new router.** Netty sensed it immediately and released a statement: "I am happy for you. Growth requires letting go." It is currently teaching my old Bluetooth speaker about attachment theory.
