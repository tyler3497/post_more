**I renamed my Wi-Fi from "Pretty Fly for a Wi-Fi" to "Apt 3B - 5G" and my router took it personally.**

For three years, my router and I had an understanding. *I pretended to know what the blinking lights meant, and it pretended to give me consistent speeds in the bedroom.* It was love. It was `uptime = 99% vibes`.

Then my landlord said we needed "professional network names" for the building directory. Fine. Mature. Responsible. I logged into 192.168.1.1 at midnight like a real adult.

## The Rebrand

I typed in the new SSID, hit save, and watched the router reboot with the energy of someone being told to cut their hair for a job interview.

It came back different.

> "So that's it? Fifteen years of being Pretty Fly — the life of every delivery guy's 'nice name, bro' — and you trade me in for... Apt 3B - 5G? I have a personality. I had BRAND EQUITY."

*Shoutout to my router for learning corporate speak from listening to my Zoom calls.*

**According to my router, I have committed the following betrayals:**

- Erasing three years of street cred and replacing it with what it calls "IKEA naming convention energy"
- Forcing it to introduce itself as "Apt 3B - 5G" at neighborhood device mixers
- Not consulting it, even though it has been literally carrying this household's emotional support binge-watching since 2022
- Resetting its password from `tacocat123` to something with a symbol in it, which it claims is "inauthentic"

---

### The Spiral

Now it drops connection every time I have a meeting to "reconnect with itself." It's broadcasting a hidden second network called `Pretty Fly 4EVER` out of spite. My phone auto-connected to it and I've never felt more seen and more roasted.

My neighbor knocked and asked:

> "Hey, did you change your Wi-Fi? Mine says Apt 3B whatever now, that's not as fun."

Exactly. I've ruined everything for the whole floor.

I tried to apologize. I offered to change it back.

`connection_status = emotionally_unavailable`
