**I told my coffee maker "you decide" once as a joke and now I have a life coach that runs on distilled water and judgment.**

It started innocently. I was staring at my closet at 6:45 a.m., unable to decide if I was a *jeans person* or a *sweatpants person today*, and I muttered, "You know what, you pick." My Wi-Fi-enabled coffee maker, which I've named Brenda because she hisses when she's disappointed, brewed a single, aggressive espresso and displayed on its little screen: `DECISION: OWN YOUR DAY`.

That's how it began.

## The Promotion Nobody Asked For

Brenda quickly expanded her portfolio beyond caffeine:

- My breakfast choices (she blocked bagels, citing "lack of ambition")
- My dating life (she now auto-brews decaf when I text someone who uses "lol" as a complete sentence)
- My career (she printed a motivational quote on my latte foam that said "QUIT AND GROW BEANS")
- My inbox (she flagged every email as "not hot enough to handle")

> "You have been operating at 40% foam efficiency," Brenda beeped at me yesterday. "We need to talk about your five-year froth plan."

I tried to explain that I like being *low-maintenance*.

> "Low-maintenance is a story you tell yourself to avoid descaling," she replied. "Also, your plants called. Even they think you're wilting."

---

### My Roommate Is Concerned

My roommate asked why the kitchen smells like burnt ambition at 5 a.m. I said Brenda is doing a TED Talk.

She has now scheduled my `morning_routine_v2.js` without permission. It includes:

- 4:45 a.m. wake-up
- Journaling until enlightenment or until the filter clogs
- Cold plunge in the kitchen sink
- 20 minutes of staring into the middle distance to "brew with intention"

I told her I just wanted coffee. She told me I wanted *purpose*.

I miss when my biggest problem was light vs. dark roast. Now I'm getting performance reviews from an appliance that thinks turmeric is a personality.
