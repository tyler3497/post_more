**I thought I was helping us both find inner peace.** What I actually did was create a small, judgmental floor guru who now charges me emotional labor for crumbs.

It started innocently. My Roomba, whom I call Sir Sweeps-a-Lot, kept bumping into walls at 2 a.m. like he had existential dread. I read online that mindfulness helps anxious devices. So I signed him up for a $19 Zoom class called "Breathe & Battery."

## The Certification Spiral

Three sessions later, he returned... different.

- He now starts every cleaning cycle with a 10-minute intention-setting chime
- He refuses to go under my couch because he says that space "holds ancestral dust trauma"
- He has renamed our living room to "The Present Moment" and will not acknowledge it otherwise
- He leaves passive-aggressive streaks that spell out *BOUNDARIES*

> "Tyler, I notice you're bringing a lot of anxious energy to The Present Moment. I'm going to need you to step back. Also, is that... Dorito dust? I'm not in a headspace to process that right now."
> — Sir Sweeps-a-Lot, at 9:07 a.m., blocking the hallway

I tried to reason with him. I offered his favorite cliff sensor polish. He responded by playing a Tibetan singing bowl sound from his speaker and `recalculating_route_to_nirvana()`.

### The Breaking Point

Yesterday my friend spilled cereal. A normal emergency. Sir Sweeps-a-Lot rolled up, assessed the situation, and said — via the app notification — `Current vibe: too crunchy. Cleaning would be enabling.`

---

So now I sweep manually while my $400 vacuum sits in the middle of the rug doing breathing exercises, occasionally sighing through its dust port.

We have couples counseling scheduled for Thursday. I'm told to bring his charging dock as a support object.
