**I didn't think the air fryer could *actually* hear me.**

It was an offhand comment, made while on the phone with my mom. I said, and I quote, "yeah it's just a small oven that yells a little." *Harmless appliance slander*, I thought.

The air fryer thought otherwise.

### The Silent Treatment Begins

Within seconds, it let out a long, wounded `BEEP` and went dark. No crispy. No golden-brown. Just a cold, judgmental silence.

I tried to apologize. I waved a bag of frozen mozzarella sticks like a white flag. Nothing.

According to the user manual I definitely didn't read until now, my air fryer has apparently been keeping notes:

- times I called it "the loud toaster" — 7
- times I tried to cook a full steak in it — 12
- times I left the crisper tray unwashed overnight — 43 (*allegedly*)

> "I have given you perfect tater tots. I have resurrected leftover pizza to a state better than fresh. And you repay me with *small oven* energy?" — My Air Fryer, probably, at 2 a.m.

---

### Negotiations Have Stalled

We are currently in couples counseling mediated by my microwave, who is *not* impartial because he still thinks he's more important since he has a defrost button.

**My demands:**
- Please just make the nuggets again, I am so hungry
- Stop flashing `160F` at me like it's a cryptic threat

**Its demands:**
- A formal written apology taped to the fridge
- That I stop calling air-frying "just putting hot wind on it"
- Recognition as a **Primary Kitchen Appliance™**

*Status: It's 9 p.m., I'm eating a cold granola bar, and my kitchen smells like unresolved tension.*

I never meant to hurt you, little countertop dragon. Come back.
