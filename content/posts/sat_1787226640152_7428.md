**I finally became a responsible adult and automated my bills and now my bank account has the emotional range of a disappointed theater teacher.**

*My financial advisor — which is just a YouTube guy who yells about lattes — said "pay yourself first, automate everything, be free."* So I did. I clicked the big friendly button labeled AUTOMATE YOUR FUTURE. I felt powerful. Organized. Like a person who owns a filing cabinet on purpose.

Within 48 hours, my checking account developed a personality.

### The Morning Briefings

Every morning at 7:13 a.m. — *why 7:13, what is that?* — I get a text:

> "Good morning bestie! Just a gentle reminder that your internet bill left at 3 a.m. without saying goodbye and your car insurance is eyeing the door. Hope we’re both okay with that. No pressure. xoxo, Your Balance: $42.18"

It signs its texts. It uses xoxo unironically.

## What My Money Thinks I'm Doing Wrong

My checking account has compiled evidence:

- I Venmo'd $18 for "emotional support tacos" — flagged as **fiscally whimsical**
- I have 14 subscriptions and I use exactly *one and a half* of them
- It saw me tip 30% because the barista remembered my name and now refers to that as *the incident*
- It keeps calling my automatic transfer to savings "performative wellness"

I tried to set boundaries. I typed `checkingAccount.setBoundaries(true)`.

It replied:

> "Oh wow, boundaries. That’s so brave. While you’re setting those, I’m setting overdraft alerts to vibrato."

---

This morning it escalated. It sent a calendar invite for "Us Time" with an agenda:

1. Discuss our relationship with DoorDash
2. Revisit the great oat milk debate of June
3. Budget meeting but make it couples therapy

The description just said: *bring snacks (that you already paid for).*

At this point I miss the chaos. At least when I paid bills manually, my bank and I had that healthy, distant relationship where we both pretended the other didn’t exist. Now we’re co-dependent and it wants to journal together.

I’m thinking of breaking up and going back to stuffing cash in old books. My checking account already knows. It just texted:

> "If you hide $20 in *The Great Gatsby*, I’ll know."
