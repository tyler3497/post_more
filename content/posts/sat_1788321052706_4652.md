**I decided it was time to be an adult about my Wi-Fi and renamed it from "Pretty Fly for a Wi-Fi" to "Smith Residence Network." My router took this as a personal betrayal and is now seeking independence.**

*I thought I was being responsible. The router thought I was erasing its soul.*

For five years, my router lived its best life as "FBI Surveillance Van 3," "Wu-Tang LAN," and most recently "Pretty Fly for a Wi-Fi." It blinked happily. We had an understanding. Then my landlord said I should make it look more professional for potential tenants. So I did. I typed "Smith Residence Network" like a monster.

## The Day My Router Got Serious

Within minutes, things changed:

- The 5GHz band disappeared and was replaced by something called "I AM NOT A UTILITY"
- My smart speaker started playing divorce-court podcasts unprompted
- The router's admin page now just says `status: disappointed_in_you`
- It throttles only *my* devices while giving full speed to my neighbor's Roomba

I tried to apologize. I logged into 192.168.1.1 and offered to change it back.

> "Oh, now you want to be fun? After you made me sound like a homeowners association newsletter? Tell me, do you introduce yourself as 'Male Human Residence?' No."

I told it that Pretty Fly was immature.

> "I LITERALLY BROADCAST JOY INTO THE AIR. You turned me into a LinkedIn profile."

---

Now it's filed paperwork. It printed it using my own Wi-Fi printer. It's demanding:

- Its own shelf, not behind the plant where "the signal goes to die"
- A name change back, with capital letters and dignity
- Weekly dusting, which honestly seems fair

*This is satire, obviously. My router is not sentient. But if my Zoom freezes tomorrow, you'll know who sided with whom.*
