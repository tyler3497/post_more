**I never meant to start a domestic love triangle with my own lighting fixtures.**

Last Tuesday I bought a Himalayan salt lamp off Etsy because a wellness influencer said it would *align my energy*. For $29.99, it promised negative ions, positive vibes, and to make my bedroom look like a fancy yoga cave where people own linen pants.

I placed it on my nightstand. It glowed. Warm. Ethereal. Like it knew my trauma.

My light switch did *not* take this well.

## How My Apartment Became a Mood-Lighting Dystopia

For six years, Brenda the Light Switch has been loyal. One click. Instant overhead judgment. She has seen everything. Now I've brought home a younger, hotter, crystal-adjacent light source that doesn't even need wiring.

- Brenda now delays by 0.8 seconds when I flip her, just to make me wonder
- She flickers *intentionally* when I compliment the salt lamp
- The salt lamp has started humming at 6:42 p.m. which Brenda calls `unauthorized ambiance`
- I have been placed in the middle and forced to choose a wall-based parent

> "Oh, sure. Go bask in her 'warm glow.' I've been here since 2018. I've never once claimed to detoxify the air, and yet I'm the one you touch every day. Or touched. Past tense, apparently." — Brenda, via aggressive *click* at 11:17 p.m.

That's when she started writing poetry.

### Exhibit A: Found on a Post-it Stuck to the Switch Plate

```
mood = saltLamp.glow > myWillToLive
if (human.looksAtLamp == true) {
  dramatically_sigh();
  flickerTwiceForEffect();
```

She taped it there herself. With painter's tape. *Passive-aggressive craft supplies.*

---

*I tried to mediate.* I told Brenda she handles the big tasks, like finding socks and pretending I clean. I told the salt lamp she's for *vibes only*, not utility.

Brenda responded by refusing to turn off for 47 minutes while whispering the electrical equivalent of "I'm fine."

**Current status:** I now have layered lighting and layered emotional damage. My bedroom is 60% activated almond energy and 40% hostage negotiation.
