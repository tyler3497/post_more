**I bought a succulent because my therapist said I needed something low-maintenance to believe in.**

*She meant a cactus, I think. I went with a succulent named Steve.*

## Chapter One: The Miracle

Everyone told me I have a black thumb. My last herb garden became a crime scene. So when Steve survived not just a week, but a full **month** without me over-watering, under-watering, or apologizing to him at 2 a.m., I took it personally.

I started telling people. Too many people.

Evidence of my descent:

- I rotated Steve 15 degrees daily for "even growth" like he was a rotisserie chicken
- I whispered affirmations. Steve is resilient. Steve is a survivor. Steve knows what he did.
- I made him an Instagram. Three followers. One is my mom. She thinks he's a baked potato.
- I put him on my desk so he could "get some sun and also supervise my emails"

> "Steve seems to be thriving," my coworker said, glancing at the plant I had strapped into the passenger seat on the way to work. "That's... normal amount of care."

## Chapter Two: The Incident

Yesterday I updated my emergency contacts. My phone asked, "Add emergency contact?" and autocomplete suggested **Steve (Plant)** because I'd searched "succulent emergency wilting help pls" forty times.

*I added him as a joke.* The joke has escalated.

Today my doctor's office called to confirm and asked, "Should we call Steve if you can't be reached?"

I said yes before my brain could intervene. *Yes, absolutely, call my succulent. He's very responsive between 9 and 5, indirect light.*

---

Now Steve is listed everywhere. My landlord has his name. My gym membership does. I got a form that said `IN CASE OF EMERGENCY, CONTACT:` and I wrote Steve in confident block letters.

**I have made a small green bureaucrat out of a plant that communicates exclusively through slight tilting.**
