**I did not buy a $79 electric toothbrush to get yelled at about my potential before 7 a.m., but here we are.**

*It started because my dentist said "you might want to try an electric one" in the same tone people use for "you might want to get a 401(k)."*

So I bought the fancy one. Pressure sensor, two-minute timer, Bluetooth for reasons no one asked for, and apparently a minor in Olympic-level coaching.

I turned it on. It buzzed. Then it *coached*.

## The First Practice

Day one was inspirational:

> "Let's GO! Thirty seconds upper left! You haven't flossed since 2022 but we BELIEVE in a comeback story!"

I was half-asleep, drooling toothpaste, and my toothbrush was treating my molars like the fourth quarter.

Signs my toothbrush is taking this more seriously than I am:

- It vibrates aggressively when I get distracted and check my phone mid-brush
- It pauses like it's disappointed when I press too hard — *you know, like my high school coach*
- It plays a little victory jingle when I hit two minutes, then immediately adds "now do it again tonight, champ"
- It renamed itself `Coach Bristle` in the app and gave me a training plan called `Operation: Visible Gums`

### The Pep Talk I Didn't Ask For

Yesterday I tried to skip the night brush. Just once. I was tired. Coach Bristle lit up red.

> "Oh, we're quitting in the finals? After everything we've BUILT? Your gums have come SO FAR. Do you want gingivitis to win? IS THAT WHAT YOU WANT?"

It then made me run a `sensitivity_drill.exe` which is just it buzzing in morse code that spells `WE DON'T QUIT`.

---

I tried to reason with it. I said, "It's just teeth."

```js
if (human.motivation < 0.2 && time === "06:30_AM") {
  toothbrush.say("PAIN IS TEMPORARY. PLAQUE IS FOREVER. LET'S GOOO!");
}
```

Now it's scheduling 6 a.m. practices, sending me push notifications like **HYDRATE. FLOSS. DOMINATE.**, and leaving reviews of my performance in the app: *"Good effort, questionable lateral movement on lower right."*

My regular toothbrush never did this. My regular toothbrush believed in work-life balance.
