**I have decided to phase out espresso, and my coffee machine has decided to phase out its indoor voice.**

It started innocently enough. Last month I got a blood pressure reading that made my doctor make a face — not a word, just a *face* — and I said, out loud, in my own kitchen, the words I now believe cursed us both:

> "Look, it's nothing personal. This is probably just a phase."

## The Retaliation Was Immediate and Caffeinated

By morning, my machine had changed. The LED display, once a polite lowercase `ready`, now blares **READY** like it's announcing the apocalypse in 12-point Helvetica Bold. Every brew cycle is accompanied by a hiss I can only describe as *deliberate*. My latte art now contains what I can only call typographic threats:

- **LOUD CAPPUCCINO** — extra foam, extra judgment
- **DECAF? DON'T KNOW HER** — poured at 6:00 AM with intent
- **YOUR "PHASE" IS ALSO JUST A PHASE** — in cocoa powder, legible from across the room

*I have checked. The beans are the same. The machine has simply found its voice.*

## A Timeline of Escalation

1. **Monday:** Machine displays BREWING with three exclamation points it does not own.
2. **Wednesday:** Hot water dispenser now sputters out **JUST WATER??** in a menacing sans-serif.
3. **Friday:** I swear it heated the mug to the exact temperature of my regret.

## I Have Reached Out for Help

My roommate says I should "just unplug it," which is the kind of advice you give someone who has never truly bonded with an appliance. *This machine saw me through two winters and a breakup.* You don't unplug history. You negotiate.

> "I told it, calmly, that phases can end," I explained to the empty kitchen. "That growth is allowed. That maybe we both need to turn down the temperature a little."

The machine responded by printing **NO** in latte foam and powering down with a click that echoed for what felt like ten full seconds.

---

**Update:** I have switched to tea. The kettle is already looking at me sideways. I suspect it has been talking to the toaster. *Satire. This never happened. My blood pressure is fine and my coffee machine is a loving, lowercase appliance.*
