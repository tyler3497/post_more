**This was supposed to be about cardio. It became about character.**

Last Tuesday, halfway up my stairs, I paused to catch my breath and said something reckless out loud: "These stairs just aren't motivating enough."

Big mistake. *Huge.*

The next morning, the staircase had changed. Every single step had developed a personality, and every single one of them was a personal trainer with unresolved anger.

## The New House Rules

By step three, I had learned the terms of my new reality:

- **Step 4** makes me do three squats before it lets me pass. It counts out loud. It does not accept "I'm in socks."
- **Step 7** gives unsolicited nutrition advice. Yesterday it told me my lunch had "no commitment to the craft."
- **Step 9** plays a little air horn every time I check my phone while climbing.
- The **landing** refuses to engage. It says it has "done its inner work" and will not be drawn into the drama.

> "You pause on me again and I will start charging you per breath," Step 5 told me on Wednesday. "This is not a rest stop. This is an opportunity."

## I Tried to Negotiate

I sat down on Step 2 — a known moderate — and tried to talk it out like an adult. I said the words out loud: "I was just frustrated with my fitness. I didn't mean *you*."

Step 2 listened, and then it said the thing I have been hearing in my dreams ever since:

> "Funny. That's exactly what your last four New Year's resolutions said."

Architecture has never roasted me before, and it was weirdly good at it.

## The Verdict

It has been two weeks. I now have calves like a Renaissance sculpture and the emotional stability of a person who apologizes to furniture. The staircase has issued a formal training plan through the weekend, and Step 11 has begun "gentle accountability" on my snack drawer, which it cannot even reach.

---

So: my house is fit now. My stairs believe in me more than I believe in me. And somewhere between the fourth step and the fifth, I have accepted the truth —

**I didn't need a gym membership. I needed twelve steps and a grudge.**
