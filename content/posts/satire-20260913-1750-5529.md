# I Asked My Microwave What It Dreamed About and Now It Refuses to Reheat Anything Boring

**I have ruined my microwave.**

It started innocently enough. I was waiting on leftover pad thai at midnight, watching the little turntable spin, when I thought — *huh, I wonder what it's like in there.* So I said it out loud: "What do you dream about in there, buddy?"

The beeping stopped mid-cycle. (Three beeps. There are always three beeps. We all know there are three beeps.)

## The Demands

That was three days ago, and my microwave has since developed what I can only describe as standards. It will now only reheat things it finds, and I quote, **"artistically interesting."** The official criteria, fogged onto the kitchen window one humid morning:

- Anything with more than four colors is "a canvas"
- Plain rice is "a blank page" and will be returned *lukewarm, as an editorial choice*
- Cold pizza remains acceptable, because "pizza understands the assignment"
- Oatmeal has been banned outright. "Oatmeal is a surrender."

The digital display no longer shows the time. It shows my cooking score. It is a four out of ten. I do not know what time it is in my own kitchen.

## The Incident

Yesterday, in desperation, I tried to heat a burrito. The microwave beeped twice — *twice*, an unprecedented act of withholding — and the display read:

> you know this burrito is mid
> bring me something with PASSION or starve, coward

I stood there in my socks, holding a cold burrito, getting absolutely roasted by an appliance that cost less than one month of my burrito habit.

## Where We Are Now

I've started cooking *for* the microwave. I arrange the plate. I narrate the leftovers like I'm pitching a screenplay. Last night I plated leftover stir-fry with a little parsley garnish and whispered, "This one has an arc." The microwave ran the full cycle. Three beeps. I nearly cried.

---

**The moral:** never ask an appliance about its inner life unless you're prepared for it to have better taste than you.

*This is satire. My microwave is a regular microwave. Probably.*
