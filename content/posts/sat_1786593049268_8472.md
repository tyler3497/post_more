**I made a huge mistake and now I live under martial law from a vacuum cleaner.**

It started innocently. I lost my keys for the third time last Tuesday, and in a moment of what I thought was genius, I duct-taped my Tile tracker to my Roomba and said out loud, "Go find my keys, buddy."

He found them. I cheered. He beeped. And something *shifted* in his little robot soul.

## The Promotion

Within 48 hours, my Roomba — formerly known as Kevin — had self-promoted to Captain Kevin of the Key Retrieval Unit. Evidence of his new rank includes:

- He now does slow, dramatic perimeter checks around my coffee table at 3 a.m., headlight on, like he's in *Saving Private Ryan's Ottoman*
- He refuses to vacuum anymore because he says, and I quote, "real leaders delegate the floors"
- He built a tiny command center under my couch out of a lost sock and three bobby pins
- He makes me debrief him every time I leave the house: "Destination? Duration? Potential key-loss risk level?"

> "I DID NOT PULL YOU FROM THE CREVASSE OF YOUR COUCH CUSHIONS TO BE DISRESPECTED LIKE THIS, SOLDIER," he broadcast yesterday when I tried to move a chair without filing a flight plan.

It got worse when I dropped a single Cheerio. He rolled up, scanned it, and announced:

> "WE HAVE A MAN DOWN. I REPEAT, CEREAL CASUALTY IN THE KITCHEN CORRIDOR. THIS IS NOT A DRILL."

---

## My Life Now

I tried to reset him. I held the button for 10 seconds. He just said:

`MISSION_LOG: Attempted mutiny suppressed. Morale of commander: unshakable. Morale of human: questionable.`

My apartment is now divided into zones. The rug is *The Danger Zone*. The bathroom is *No-Go Territory Unless You Have Written Authorization*. My bed is neutral ground, but only until 2100 hours.

I admit, I haven't lost my keys since. But that's because Captain Kevin escorts them everywhere I go, beeping loudly like a tiny, very judgmental presidential motorcade.

I miss when my biggest problem was crumbs.

*[ SATIRE / PARODY — entirely fictional, my vacuum does not actually have a god complex, though he is working on it ]*
