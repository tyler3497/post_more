**I have owned an ironing board for four years and I have used it exactly twice, both times as a very wobbly laptop stand during my brief standing-desk era.**

The board and I had a beautiful, low-expectations relationship. *It didn't judge my wrinkles and I didn't ask it to be level.*

Then I got an interview and I thought, you know what would really sell "hire this responsible adult"? A shirt without 17 geographic folds.

## The Awakening

I unfolded the board. It unfolded me.

It made a noise like a medieval drawbridge opening after a century of rust and said - at least in energy:

> "Oh. So NOW you need me. After four years of using me to air-dry socks and as overflow seating for guests I didn't like. We need to talk about your life choices."

And then it just... stood there. Firmly. Level. For the first time ever.

Suddenly it was a life coach.

**My crimes, according to Board:**

- Owning one dress shirt, washing it cold, then rolling it into a ball like mozzarella
- Using `wrinkle_remover_spray = perfume + optimism` as a laundry strategy for 3 years
- Storing important documents between the shirt and the board because "it's flat, that's filing"
- Telling people I was "ironically wrinkled"

I tried to iron. The board wobbled *on purpose* to keep me accountable.

> "You're rushing," it creaked. "You rush everything. You rushed that sourdough starter. You rushed your houseplants' emotional development. Breathe. Press. Be present."

---

### The Performance Review

It then scheduled follow-ups:

- Weekly check-ins on whether I'm actually hanging clothes or just chair-draping again
- A mandatory ban on what it calls `floor-closet integration`
- A vision board for my vision board era, which is very meta and very judgmental

I got the shirt done. It's 40% better and 100% more spiritually inspected.

My roommate walked in and said:

> "Did your ironing board always have that much... authority?"

No. But it does now. And honestly? I needed it.

`status: shirt=pressed, ego=lightly steamed, board=now taking appointments`
