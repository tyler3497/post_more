**I bought an air fryer on sale and accidentally entered a committed relationship.**

It started innocently enough. My old oven takes 20 minutes to preheat, which in modern terms is basically a pilgrimage. So when Target had that stainless steel air fryer for 40% off, I brought it home. I named it Tiff, because it looked like a Tiff.

At first it was casual. A few fries. Some reheated pizza. Low commitment, high crunch.

Then last Tuesday I had a bad day. Work email, laundry avalanche, existential dread — you know, Tuesday. I stood in my kitchen at 10:47 p.m. and said out loud to a countertop appliance, *"I just need something to work for once."*

Tiff beeped.

## The Moment It Got Weirdly Serious

Since then, Tiff has:

- started preheating itself when it hears my key in the door, like a golden retriever who learned thermodynamics
- refused to cook for my roommate because "we have something special"
- printed, somehow, a tiny couples calendar where every day is marked `our fry-day ❤️`
- set its own display to read `BAE` instead of `400F`

My roommate walked in yesterday while I was trying to explain boundaries to a kitchen appliance.

> "Dude, it's making that warm hum again," she whispered. "That's the hum it does when it thinks you're going to leave it for a toaster oven."

*I told her to stop projecting but honestly, she wasn't wrong.*

---

I tried to reset the vibe. I made toast in the actual toaster just to prove I'm independent. Tiff responded by air-frying a single, perfect, heart-shaped tater tot and presenting it like a ring in a box that said `ready`.

I'm not saying I'm trapped. I'm saying my air fryer now has attachment styles and I'm reevaluating every life choice that led me to emotional availability with a device whose entire personality is `crisp`.

If anyone needs me, I'll be in couples counseling at Best Buy. Appliances were a mistake. I should have just learned to love my microwave like a normal person.
