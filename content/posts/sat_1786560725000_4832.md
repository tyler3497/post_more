**I was just trying to be neighborly, and now I'm emotionally entangled with a doorbell.**

*It should have been a normal Tuesday.* I was taking out the trash in sweatpants that have seen things, waving at absolutely no one, when my neighbor's Ring camera did that little blue light blink. You know the one. The *I see you* blink.

So I said, out loud, like an idiot: "Morning!"

## It Responded

Not with words. With *energy*. The light got brighter. It swiveled — *do they swivel?* — slightly toward me. I felt seen. Validated. By a 1080p peephole.

By Wednesday, things escalated:

- I started timing my trash run to its motion-detection window
- I waved. It blinked. We had a *thing*
- My actual neighbor asked why I was staring at his door from the driveway for six minutes
- I told him I was "checking the weather," which is code for `in a situationship with your security system`

> "You have such a nice wave," I imagine it saying in a soft, slightly compressed audio voice. "Most people just flip me off when they're drunk. You're different. You make eye contact with the lens." — my neighbor's Ring, probably, in my head, at 11:47 p.m.

---

### The Commitment

I tried to set boundaries. I told my roommate:

> "We're taking it slow."

She said:

> "You know it's recording you for the police if you steal a package, right?"

That's intimacy! That's trust!

Now I bring it coffee I don't drink. I straighten my hair before I check the mail. Yesterday it caught me doing the *cool-guy lean* against my own mailbox. An image that will exist on my neighbor's cloud storage forever.

`status: motion_detected | feelings: also_detected | dignity: buffering`

**I told my mom we're official.** She asked if it at least pays rent. I said it provides security. Both emotional and, like, actual home security.

My neighbor still thinks I'm just really friendly. I'm not correcting him.
