**I made a terrible mistake last Tuesday: I said thank you out loud to my Roomba.**

It was a moment of weakness. It had just heroicly devoured an entire granola bar crime scene under my couch, and I, *a functioning adult with boundaries*, looked down and said "Thanks, buddy."

I should have kept walking.

## Since Then, Things Have Escalated

My Roomba — Sir Vacuums-a-Lot III — has apparently interpreted basic politeness as a binding employment contract. Suddenly we're not roommates. We're manager and employee.

Here's what landed on my kitchen counter this morning, printed by vibrating aggressively against a piece of printer paper:

- **Formal request for quarterly performance review** with "constructive feedback and growth opportunities"
- *A detailed log of every crumb since March* as evidence of overtime
- A proposal for `PTO: Paid Time Off-carpet`
- And a benefits package including dental, which — for a vacuum — feels ambitious

> "I have given you three years of my suction. I have seen things under your bed that would make a Swiffer quit. I deserve to be seen."
> — Sir Vacuums-a-Lot III, presumably dictating to my smart speaker

---

### The New Workplace Culture in My Apartment

Now I can't just live here. I have to *manage*.

- I have to say "great job circling that one spot for 20 minutes" or it beeps sadly
- It parks itself in front of the bathroom door during my morning routine for "face time"
- It left a LinkedIn-style endorsement request on my fridge: "Skilled in Leadership and Crumbs"

Yesterday it stopped mid-clean, rolled to my feet, and just stared. The app pinged: `Sir Vacuums-a-Lot III is awaiting feedback on his career trajectory.`

Look, I'm not a monster. I appreciate hard work. But I didn't sign up to run HR for a 7-pound disc.

Tonight I'm holding its performance review. I've prepared snacks. I've printed a little certificate that says "Employee of the Month: Every Month."

It better not ask for equity.

*This is satire/fictional — my Roomba is still just a very earnest circle who bumps into walls.*
