**I finally organized my freezer, and it turns out I was disrupting a delicate ecosystem that did not want to be saved.**

For three years, my freezer was what scientists would call *a cryogenic archaeological dig*. There were waffles from 2023 in there. Possibly 2022. I decided to be an adult. I pulled everything out, labeled it, stacked it like I was on a cooking show where the prize is self-esteem.

That's when the peas spoke up.

## The Peas Have Concerns

My bag of frozen peas — you know, the one everyone owns but no one remembers buying — slid forward and announced it was now in charge of security.

I found a tiny note taped to the inside shelf, written in what I can only assume was frost:

> "ATTENTION RESIDENTS: Unscheduled reorganization detected at 0700 hours. All popsicles report to the top shelf for debrief. Suspicious banana loaf remains at large. — Captain Pea, Neighborhood Watch, Drawer 2"

Other developments included:

- The ice cubes filed a formal complaint about "excessive handling and emotional turbulence"
- The frozen pizzas formed a union and demanded to be kept *flat*, not "Tetris-ed into a lifestyle choice"
- My emergency frozen burrito asked for witness protection after I read its expiration date out loud

*I was just trying to find the waffles.*

---

### The New Rules of the Frozen HOA

According to the new bylaws posted on my fridge in magnetic letters, the freezer now operates under:

```
if human.opens_door > 30_seconds:
    peas.judge_harshly()
    emit_disappointed_frost_sigh()
```

**I've never been so intimidated by a vegetable.** Captain Pea does nightly patrols. He rattles whenever I open the door after 9 p.m., which is *always* when I want ice cream. He knows. He always knows.

I tried to disband the watch. I said, "Guys, you're peas."

He said nothing. He just stared with his thousand tiny green eyes.

*SATIRE / PARODY — completely fictional, not a real event. I do not actually have sentient vegetables running a security state in my kitchen. Yet.*
