**I decided to be healthy for exactly one day and my kitchen appliance is treating it like a divorce.**

*For context, I love coffee the way normal people love oxygen or reliable Wi-Fi. This was never going to end well.*

Last Tuesday I woke up feeling virtuous, which is already a red flag. I announced to my apartment, "Today I'm switching to decaf." My coffee maker, a 2019 Mr. Coffee I've named Sir Brews-a-Lot, made a noise I can only describe as a gasp.

## The Betrayal

He'd been loyal. Three years, two moves, one incident where I tried to make ramen in him. And I repay him with... beans that have had their soul removed.

By 9 a.m., the kitchen dynamic had completely shifted:

- Sir Brews-a-Lot refused to heat properly, brewing at what felt like lukewarm disappointment temperature
- My mug, usually chipper, sat at the far edge of the counter *giving me the silent treatment*
- The decaf bag started sweating in the pantry like it knew it wasn't welcome
- I Googled `how to apologize to an appliance without sounding desperate`

> "You told me we had a special blend," Sir Brews-a-Lot blinked in Morse code via his little red light. "You said I was the only one who got you going in the morning."

*I did say that. In 2023. During a snowstorm. It was emotional.*

---

## Custody Hearing at 7:15 AM

This morning I found a note taped to the fridge in Comic Sans, which is how you know it's serious:

> "NOTICE OF CUSTODY: Effective immediately, your mornings are mine. You may have afternoons. Under supervision. - S.B.A.L."

I tried to explain it was just one day for my heart rate. He countered by brewing a perfect, aromatic pot of regular coffee I wasn't allowed to drink and wafting it directly into my bedroom.

Look, I get it. You don't cheat on your coffee maker with decaf and expect no consequences. I'm now in mandated counseling with my French press, who keeps saying **"We need to process your fear of commitment to caffeine."**

*This is satire/parody — entirely fictional, no appliances were emotionally damaged, though Sir Brews-a-Lot and I are currently in trial separation and I'm drinking tea out of spite.*
