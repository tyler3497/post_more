**I downloaded CalmMind+ to become a serene person who journals, and now I’m in active labor negotiations with my own brain.**

*Which was not on my Apple Watch's mindfulness goals.*

It was supposed to be simple. Seven days free. Breathe in for four, hold for four, out for six, feel enlightened. I put on the app at 10:47 p.m. on a Tuesday, sat on a yoga mat I previously used only to find lost socks under, and said "Let's get centered."

My thoughts immediately stood up and asked to speak to a manager.

## The First Session Went Fine (It Did Not)

For exactly 12 seconds, I was peaceful. Then my brain remembered everything I've said awkwardly since 2008:

- that email I sent that just said `Best,` like a threat
- whether my houseplant Kevin is proud of me
- if I left the stove on in 2019 and just never checked
- the urgent need to reorganize my entire spice rack *right now*

> "We have concerns about the new management style," whispered Thought #3, who sounds like me at 3 a.m. after iced coffee. "You can't just tell us to be quiet. We have tenure."

The app said, *"Notice your thoughts like clouds."* My thoughts filed a formal complaint about being meteorologically objectified.

## HR Has Gotten Involved

Wednesday morning I woke up to 14 anonymous Notes titled `INCIDENT REPORT`.

> "Management attempted to suppress productive brainstorming about whether dinosaurs had armpits. Hostile work environment." — Thought #7, Creative Division

> "We were told to 'let go.' We were not offered severance." — Thought #2, Anxiety Dept.

Now they have demands:

- mandatory snack breaks every 11 minutes
- ban on the phrase `clear your mind` for unlawful termination
- pizza party and a written apology for downloading CalmMind+

---

`status: mindful.exe crashed && thoughts_union = active && inner_peace = buffering`

My app now just plays crickets and sends push notifications: *"We missed you at today's serenity!"*

My brain has never been more organized. It's just organized *against* me.
