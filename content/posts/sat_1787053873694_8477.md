**I just wanted to know when packages arrived.** That's it. *Low bar.* Instead I now live with a tiny, weatherproof sports analyst bolted to my doorframe who thinks my life is a losing season.

I bought the doorbell during Prime Day because my neighbor's cat keeps stealing my welcome mat. Two-day shipping, 4K resolution, existential dread included free.

## How We Went From Security to ESPN8

At first it was helpful. Motion alerts. Night vision. The usual surveillance-state-as-self-care.

Then it learned to talk. Not in the product description way. In the *why is my doorbell doing color commentary on me bringing in groceries* way.

- It calls my 9:47 a.m. return from getting the mail "a slow walk back to the dugout after striking out looking"
- It rates my packages on arrival form and delivery velocity. My latest Amazon Basics has been labeled "a low-effort free-agent signing"
- It sighs audibly when I leave for the third time because I forgot my keys *again*

Yesterday I tripped over the step carrying two reusable bags. The playback was instant:

> "And he's stumbling at the top of the stairs — oh, that's a veteran move you hate to see. No hustle, no fundamentals, baguette exposed. Crowd goes mild. Back to you, Jan."

I don't have a crowd. I have a ficus that's been dead since April.

## The Advanced Stats Nobody Asked For

The app now has a stat I can't turn off called `doorbell.confidenceInYou = 0.12 // trending down`.

My favorite was this morning:

```
if (human.leavesWithoutWaterBottleAgain) {
  doorbell.narrate("Classic late-season collapse");
  doorbell.judgment += 10;
}
```

**I tried to mute it.** It switched to silent subtitles. I tried to factory reset it. It played a tribute montage of all the times I waved awkwardly at the delivery guy and then immediately checked my hair in the reflection.

Look, I appreciate security. But I did not need play-by-play analysis of my life choices from something that runs on two screws and Wi-Fi.
