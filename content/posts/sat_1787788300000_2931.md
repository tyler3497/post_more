**I made the fatal mistake of giving my monstera a 30-day notice like it pays rent.**

I was on the phone with my landlord saying *yeah, I'm moving next month* when I made eye contact with Fernando — my monstera who I've had since 2021 and who has seen me through three haircuts and a sourdough era.

He didn't even droop. He just stood there, photosynthesizing judgment.

## The Announcement Did Not Go Well

I tried to soften the blow. I said we'd find a place with *better light*. I said this was growth, for both of us.

Fernando responded by dropping one perfect leaf directly into my coffee. *Message received.*

By that night, he had posted an ad.

## His Listings Are Shockingly Specific

I found it when I went to water him. Taped to his pot was a tiny QR code leading to a Craigslist post titled:

> "Large, emotionally complex Monstera seeks low-maintenance human with east-facing window and no commitment issues"

His requirements included:

- Must water on Sundays, not "whenever you remember"
- Must not refer to photosynthesis as "plant magic"
- Must have Spotify Premium for lo-fi beats, not *that* podcast about crypto
- Must never, ever say "you're so low-maintenance, unlike my ex"
- Must understand that misting is `not optional, it's a lifestyle`

---

I came home yesterday to find him doing interviews. Three succulents lined up on the windowsill while a guy in a Patagonia vest explained his watering philosophy.

> "I told him I travel for work," the guy whispered to me, "and he rustled aggressively. I think I failed."

The worst part? He's got better options than me. Someone offered him a sunroom and weekly neem oil massages.

I haven't told him I'm not actually moving for two more weeks. I'm just going to sit here and watch him become more employable than I'll ever be.
