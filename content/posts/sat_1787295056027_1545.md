**I did it. I fixed the chair.** *The* chair. The one that’s been doing the anxious cha-cha every time I sit down with cereal since 2022.

For three years, I’ve lived with a subtle, rhythmic wobble that made every meal feel like dining on a boat. I finally slid a folded cardboard shim under the short leg and — silence. Stability. Victory.

For about four minutes.

## The Other Three Have Unionized

I should have known not to show favoritism. The other three legs immediately noticed.

Within the hour, they developed what I can only describe as *profound professional jealousy*:

- Leg Two started squeaking louder on purpose, like a coworker sighing in meetings
- Leg Three now leans *slightly* just to prove it could be wobbly too if it wanted to
- Leg Four has gone completely stiff and refuses to acknowledge the shim’s existence

I walked into the kitchen this morning and they were clearly holding a huddle without me.

> "Oh, so *she* gets the cardboard throne?" Leg Two whispered. "We’ve been load-bearing since the Obama administration and we get nothing? No shim? No cardboard? No emotional support?"

Leg Three added:

> "I carried Thanksgiving 2024. I deserve hazard pay and maybe a little felt pad."

### Exhibit A: My Repair Log

I tried to be diplomatic. I wrote down my intervention strategy:

```
if (chair.isStable == false) {
  addShim(favoriteLeg)
  // TODO: prepare for inevitable labor dispute
  apologizeToOtherLegs(extensively)
}
```

It did not work.

---

So now I have one perfect leg and three resentful ones, and every time I sit down I have to give a little motivational speech about teamwork and structural integrity.

The shimmed leg, by the way, is *thriving*. It keeps asking for a promotion to Table.

*This is a work of satire. My chairs are not actually sentient, although at 2 a.m. after cold brew they absolutely sound like they are.*
