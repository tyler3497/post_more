*SATIRE — none of this happened, and the sun has not been served any formal papers.*

**I did not mean to escalate things with the sun. I just wanted it to know how I felt.**

## The review that started it all

It happened last Tuesday. I stepped outside to enjoy the morning, coffee in hand, ready for a wholesome twenty minutes of golden-hour joy. Instead, I got:

- Direct sunlight in my left eyeball within four seconds
- A parking ticket I can't prove is unrelated
- Sandals melting, literally melting, onto the sidewalk
- A neighbor waving, which I interpreted as mockery

So I did what any rational citizen would do. I left a review. One star. Title: "Too bright, too loud, zero accountability."

## The sun's response

By Thursday, everything had changed. The sun now operates on what meteorologists are calling *selective spite*. Conditions I have personally documented:

- Full, blinding sunshine on every weekday morning while I'm stuck inside a meeting
- Overcast gloom the instant I take my lunch break on the patio
- Perfect golden sunsets on days I forgot I even had a backyard
- One memorable afternoon where a single cloud shadow followed me around a park like a personal eclipse

> "I have given this star four billion years of unconditional loyalty," my roommate said, "and you managed to start a feud with it in under a week."

## Where we stand now

I've tried everything. I left a follow-up comment (`"Sorry, I was having a bad day, you're doing great"`). I printed an apology and taped it to the window. I even stood outside at dawn and shouted my regrets directly into the horizon, which is how I learned my neighbor now thinks I'm in a cult.

The forecast for next week: *sunny, with a 100% chance of the sun acting like I don't exist.*
