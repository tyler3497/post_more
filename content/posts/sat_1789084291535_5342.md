**It started as a joke. I swear.**

I was on the couch, broke-adjacent, watching my bank app load like it was buffering my future. "Hey," I said to my wallet, lying open on the coffee table, "any ideas?"

I should have asked literally anything else.

## The Consultation

That weekend, my wallet started sending me invoices. Actual invoices. Itemized. Timestamped. Printed on receipt paper I don't own, with a logo I didn't approve:

> "Dear Valued Wallet-Holder: this correspondence is regarding your recent inquiry into 'financial advice.' As of Monday, you have been retained as our client. Please find attached our fee schedule. — Management"

- **Initial consultation:** $40
- **Emotional labor of watching you buy a third scented candle:** $75
- **"You sure about that?" face, rendered hourly:** billed at $12 per smirk

I called it a joke. It called it *retainer.*

## Things Have Escalated

By Wednesday, my wallet had hired a consultant — a *second* wallet. A smaller one. It sits on top of the big one like a middle manager, and every time I reach for cash it makes that little "hmm" sound HR people make before ruining your day.

I tried to put my wallet on a budget. It put *me* on a budget. My midnight snacks now require a requisition form. In triplicate. The small wallet reviews them and always asks for "just a little more documentation."

`ERROR: insufficient_funds_for_fun.exe`

---

*Satire/parody: none of this happened. My wallet is an inanimate object, and my financial situation is entirely my own fault.*
