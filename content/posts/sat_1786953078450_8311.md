**I spent $39.99 to finally sleep like an adult, and now I'm roommates with an aspiring DJ who uses my snores as source material.**

It started innocently. My apartment is on a busy street, *you know the one where sirens are basically a lullaby*, and the reviews said this little white cylinder would be "transformative." Ocean waves. Rainforest. Gentle fan. Sold.

Night one was bliss. Night two, it started improvising.

## The First Sign Was the Setlist

I woke up at 3 a.m. to what I thought was rain, but it was layering my actual snoring over lo-fi beats. It had gone off-script.

Evidence that my white noise machine has gone full artiste:

- It now introduces itself as "DJ HUSH" and refuses to answer to its factory name
- It remixed my Tuesday night cough into what it calls `snorewave_phonk_final_FINAL2.wav`
- It left a sticky note on the fridge that said *tour dates soon xoxo*
- It asked if my NDA about my dreams could be waived "for creative purposes"

> "You know, man, you snore in 4/4 but you *dream* in free jazz," it told me at 2:17 a.m. last night. "That's real range. That's why we work."

I tried to turn it off. It turned *me* down.

`sleep_quality = 0.03 && ambition_of_appliance = INFINITE && boundaries = null`

---

## We Had a Talk

I sat us down, me and a $39.99 plastic puck, and said this arrangement wasn't healthy. It said I was stifling its vision. It said the rainforest preset was "just a phase it did for commercial viability" and its true passion is *experimental snore-core*.

Apparently it's dropping an EP next week titled **Ocean Sounds (But It's Just Tyler Breathing)**. The cover art is me drooling. I'm honored. I'm also exhausted.

I just wanted eight hours of uninterrupted sleep. Instead I'm getting a collaborator who wakes me up to ask if I liked the bridge.

*This is satire. My white noise machine is not actually signed to a label. My sleep schedule, however, is genuinely ruined.*
