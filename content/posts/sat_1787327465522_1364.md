*It started innocently. I wanted to be someone who drinks lemon water at 6am and journals about gratitude. Now I have a sunrise alarm that glows like a smug motivational speaker and blackout curtains that have retained legal counsel.*

## How My Quest for Wellness Became a Courtroom Drama

I bought the sunrise lamp after one too many videos told me I was "depleting my circadian capital." It simulates dawn. It plays birds. It costs $89 to make me feel judged by the actual sun.

My blackout curtains, Brenda and The Darkness, have been loyal for three years. They blocked out streetlights, summer, and any hint of adult responsibility. They thought we had an understanding.

Here is what has happened since the alarm arrived:

- My curtains now close themselves *harder* at 6am, like they're making a point about loyalty
- The sunrise alarm starts glowing at 5:45am just to win, bathing my room in what it calls "healing light" but feels like an interrogation
- I woke up to a tiny cease-and-desist written in dust on my windowsill that just said "WE DO NOT CONSENT TO GLOW"
- My bedroom now has two distinct lighting zones and both are disappointed in me

> "You brought a literal false dawn into this home and expected us not to take it personally?" said my curtains, according to the transcript my sleep-deprived brain invented at 3am. "We gave you darkness. We gave you privacy. We gave you the ability to sleep through your neighbor's leaf blower at noon. And you replace us with *this LED imposter*?"
