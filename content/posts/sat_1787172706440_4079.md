**I have finally achieved the one adult skill that separates functioning humans from people who just ball it up and pray.**

For *29 years* I have lived in shame, shoving fitted sheets into my linen closet like a raccoon hiding evidence. Then last Saturday, at 2:13 p.m. while procrastinating on literally everything else, I watched a 90-second tutorial and it actually worked.

## How It Went Down

It was disturbingly simple. Corners into corners, smooth, fold, smooth, fold. No summoning ritual. No `sudo fold --force`. Just fabric doing what fabric is *supposed* to do.

I held up the result. A perfect rectangle. My dog left the room out of respect.

## The Linen Closet's Response Was Immediate

I opened the closet to put it away. The other linens gasped.

- My bath towels, who have been rolled like sad burritos since 2022, stood at attention
- The flat sheets whispered *“he finally did it”* in unison
- The pillowcases started calling me “boss”

> “We’ve been waiting for leadership,” said the top shelf, which now has a tiny nameplate that says MANAGEMENT. “We’ve been operating without structure since you tried to Marie Kondo us in 2021 and just cried.”

Suddenly I have responsibilities:

- **Monday:** Performance review for the washcloths (*“needs to be less threadbare about commitment”*)
- **Tuesday:** Mediating a dispute between the beach towels and the good towels
- **Wednesday:** Approving PTO for the comforter who says it’s *“just really tired”*

---

I tried to explain I only folded *one* fitted sheet. One. I am not qualified to lead. I still microwave soup without a cover.

My linen closet replied with a Slack invite. The channel is `#linen-ops`. The status is:

`fold.status = promoted // do not demote`

I haven’t closed my closet in two days because it now plays soft motivational jazz whenever I walk by.
