**I finally organized my junk drawer on Sunday and immediately regretted giving these things a sense of purpose.**

For seven years, this drawer was a beautiful democracy of chaos. *A junk drawer meritocracy*, where a dead AAA battery had the same status as a functional screwdriver. We had an agreement: I close you, you don't judge me.

Then I watched a 12-minute organization video at 11:48 p.m. and decided to become someone who decants screws into tiny containers.

## The Great Sorting

I dumped everything onto my kitchen counter and realized I was running a museum of bad decisions:

- 11 batteries of unknown charge, all kept "just in case" since 2021
- 4 rolls of tape, none of which actually stick anymore
- A collection of keys that open absolutely nothing in this apartment
- Takeout soy sauce packets now legally considered archaeological artifacts
- One heroic LEGO piece I've been saving to return to... someone

I bought those little plastic dividers with labels. *This was my first mistake.* Give a junk drawer structure and it develops ambition.

> "Since Tyler has finally recognized our core competencies, we're pivoting to thought leadership," announced a AA battery wearing a Post-it as a name tag. "I was thinking we could synergize in the shared utilities vertical?"

### The Networking Event

By Monday morning, the drawer had a sign-up sheet written on the back of an IKEA receipt:

**Agenda for Q3 Junk Drawer Summit:**
- 9:00 a.m. - Icebreaker: "What's your longest time being untouched?"
- 9:30 a.m. - The rubber bands present "Stretch Goals 2026"
- 10:00 a.m. - Panel: `The Twist Tie's Guide to Holding It Together`

I tried to get a Band-Aid. The tape dispenser said I needed to book office hours.

> "We're not *junk* anymore," said the oldest battery, who now goes by Chad Powercell. "We're *curated essential infrastructure with high-touch networking potential.* Also, please stop storing us next to expired coupons. It's bad for our brand."

---

I miss when my biggest problem was that the drawer wouldn't close. Now I have to submit a formal request to borrow scissors I own, and the batteries keep trying to connect with me on LinkedIn.

Current status: `drawer: organized | my life: pending approval`
