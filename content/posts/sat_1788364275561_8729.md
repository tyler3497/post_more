**I finally decided to become a responsible plant parent on Instagram, and now my succulents have better representation than I do.**

*In hindsight, posting a daily growth update with the hashtag #PlantDaddy was the first red flag. The second was when my jade plant started asking about my ring light's color temperature.*

## How It Started

I bought three succulents from Home Depot because the internet said they were "impossible to kill." I named them Steve, Brenda, and Kevin Jr. to honor the lineage. I gave them a sunny windowsill and, fatally, an Instagram account called @succ_it_up.

What I thought would happen:

- cute photos, 12 likes from my mom
- proof I'm nurturing
- maybe one aesthetic story

What actually happened:

- Steve learned about affiliate links in 6 hours
- Brenda discovered presets and now only shoots in "Moody Desert 4"
- Kevin Jr. wrote a media kit that says "rooted, thriving, unavailable for collabs under $500"

> "We love your passion," Steve told me, rustling like a tiny CEO, "but passion doesn't pay for artisanal misting. We're going to need to bring in outside counsel."

### The Brand Deal Era

Yesterday I found a tiny ring light clipped to my curtain rod and a Post-it that said `MEETING W/ MIRACLE-GRO - DO NOT WATER`.

They've been busy:

- Signed an NDA with a macramé hanger company
- Launched a Substack called *Thirst Trap: Weekly Hydration Tips*
- Sent my landlord a cease-and-desist for "vibe disruption via radiator hissing"

I tried to intervene. I typed:

```js
if (plant.fame > owner.fame) {
  owner.waterAnyway = true;
  plant.gratitude = "maybe";
}
```

Brenda saw my code and said:

> "Cute. We're pivoting to video. You're pivoting to being our assistant."

---

Now they have a publicist named Willow who is also a fern. She keeps emailing me invoices for "emotional labor and photosynthesis." My follower count is 47. Theirs is 4,203 and they just got invited to a succulent convention in Joshua Tree.

I just wanted to prove I could keep something alive. Now I have to ask permission to post them.
