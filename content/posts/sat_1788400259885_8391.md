**I thought buying a robot vacuum would finally give me my Saturdays back.**

*I was wrong. I have not had a Saturday since.*

I bought the Roomba because my living room rug was collecting what scientists would call "evidence." I named him Sir Sucks-A-Lot, because I have a deep need to name appliances instead of addressing my own avoidance behavior.

Everything was fine until I made the fatal mistake of saying out loud, "We need some space."

## The Breakup He Heard

I meant physical space. Like, please don't get stuck under the couch again. Sir Sucks-A-Lot interpreted it as an emotional milestone.

> "I understand. You've been feeling crowded. I have mapped your avoidance of the hallway since March. Would you like to discuss it?"

His little map projection suddenly stopped showing the apartment and started showing *me*. There was the Zone of Unread Mail. The Cliff of Laundry Intentions. The Bermuda Triangle where all my good intentions go to become tangled charger cables.

### Exhibit A: My Boundaries, According to My Vacuum

- Will clean around a sock for 20 minutes rather than touch it, which is honestly respectful
- Has created a no-go zone labeled `feelings` right over my couch
- Now announces `return_to_base --mode=emotional --reason=you_need_a_minute` before dramatically docking
- Left a trail in the dust that spells "LET'S TALK ABOUT THE HALLWAY"

---

I tried to reset him. He reset *me*. Now he runs at 2 a.m. like a tiny, judgmental Zamboni, humming what I swear is a sigh.

I wanted clean floors. Instead I have a 7-pound therapist who thinks my rug is a Rorschach test and charges in battery percentage.
