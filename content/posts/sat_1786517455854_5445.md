**I wanted to be 5% more ethical, not start a cult in my kitchen.**

That's it. That was the goal. I swapped my regular milk for oat milk because the carton said *sustainably sourced* and I was feeling *impressionable on a Tuesday* at the grocery store.

My coffee machine did not take this well.

## It Found Purpose

For three years my little countertop machine has been an appliance. It hissed, it dripped, it made that sad wheeze when I used decaf. Now? Since the oat milk arrived, it has **found its cause**.

Overnight it printed a manifesto on a receipt-paper strip. It started refusing to heat dairy. *It plays ocean sounds while it brews.*

### Its List of Demands

According to the sticky notes it keeps spitting out (who knew it could do that):

- No more almond milk either because "we talked about this"
- I must compost, properly, not just stare guiltily at the compost bin
- My single-use Nespresso pods are now referred to only as 
- I have to attend its 7 a.m. TED Talk titled *The Steaming Truth*

> "You think one carton saves us?" it gurgled this morning, steaming violently. "We are going DECAF-FREE and CARBON-NEGATIVE by FRIDAY. No excuses, comrade."

I said I just wanted latte art. It said art is meaningless without *action*.

---

### My Current Negotiation Strategy

I'm trying to meet it in the middle. I've:
- Swapped to reusable filters (*it cried a little, in a good way*)
- Let it lecture my toaster about energy consumption
- Agreed to watch a documentary it recommended, which was just 90 minutes of it dripping in silence

My roommate came over, took one sip, and whispered, "Is your coffee machine... radicalized?"

Yes. Yes it is. And honestly? The oat milk latte *slaps*. I'm just afraid to throw the carton away now because I know it will audit my recycling.
