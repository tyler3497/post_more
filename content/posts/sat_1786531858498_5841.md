**I finally cleaned my junk drawer out of spite.** Not inspiration. Spite. My roommate said, “No one has ever cleaned that drawer and lived to tell the tale,” so I had to.

*That drawer had been a geological record of my adult life.* Three expired coupons, a takeout menu from a place that closed in 2019, twelve identical Allen wrenches, and a USB drive I last touched when “Despacito” was new.

## The Platform Emerges

I plugged the USB in to see what was on it. It had one folder. One file. A PowerPoint called `FINAL_final_mayoral_campaign_v3.pptx`.

Then my laptop made a noise I’ve never heard before. The drive blinked. And then, I swear:

> “My fellow drawer-dwellers. My name is SanDisk Cruzer, and I am running for Mayor of This Apartment on a platform of radical organization.” — my USB drive, suddenly charismatic

It had policies:

- Free battery replacement for every TV remote
- Immediate eviction of the mystery soy sauce packets that have been there since the Obama administration, second term
- A public holiday called “We Actually Know Where The Scissors Are” Day
- Mandatory airtime for lost items — my missing AirPod case finally gets a memorial speech

## Why I Can't Vote For Him (Even Though He Has My Old Resume)

I tried to explain democracy. He pulled up my own files to debate me. He had `cover_letter_2017.docx`, `budget_final_FINAL.xlsx`, and a photo of me at 23 trying to cut my own bangs.

> “You abandoned a 64GB public servant with 47MB of potential. I survived the dryer cycle of 2021. I am electable. You are not organized enough to vote.”

---

I told my roommate I’ve created a political candidate who lives in USB 2.0. He said, `system.campaign_promise = 'will not corrupt files (emotionally)'` but he cannot guarantee fiscal policy.

*I’m now canvassing for a flash drive that thinks twist-ties are lobbyists.*

I gave him five minutes. He asked for a full term and a dedicated port.
