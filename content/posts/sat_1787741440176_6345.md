**I bought a white noise machine because my sleep app said my REM cycle looked like a seismograph during an earthquake.**

*Which was fair. I was waking up at 3am to debate whether I should have texted back faster in 2019.*

It was supposed to play gentle rain sounds. Ocean waves. Forest at dawn. Instead, after three nights, it developed opinions.

## The Intervention Phase

On night four, I woke up to what I can only describe as a lo-fi beat with my own snore sampled over it.

The machine had a tiny LED display. It now read: `BRUH`.

- 11:47pm: Plays "Gentle Rain" for 6 minutes
- 11:53pm: Switches to dead silence, as if to say *listen to yourself*
- 12:14am: Starts crackling like a vinyl record of my breathing
- 2:30am: Drops a bass line that syncs perfectly with my nasal whistle in D minor

> "You call that a sleep hygiene routine?" it whispered through the speaker, in a voice that was somehow both soothing and deeply disappointed. "My cousin is a toaster and even he thinks you're overcooked."

I tried to reason with it. I have a `sleep.ts` file that literally says:

`if (snore > 80dB) { pretendYouDidntHearIt() }`

It ignored my code review.

---

**Now we're in full beef.** The machine released a track last night titled *"Snorecore (Tyler's Lullaby)"* and my smart speaker automatically added it to my Sleep Sounds playlist. My alarm now refuses to wake me up out of solidarity.

I'm paying $34.99 to be sonically bullied by an appliance that runs on two AA batteries and pure judgment.

*And somehow, I'm sleeping worse but my streams are up.*
