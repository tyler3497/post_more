# I Tried to Cancel My Subscription to Gravity and Got Put on Hold for Six Hours

**The hold music was just the sound of things falling.**

It started innocently enough. I opened my monthly budget spreadsheet, like a responsible adult, and noticed a line item I'd never questioned: *Gravity — $0.00/month, renews automatically.* I thought, "You know what? I'm paying attention to this for the first time in thirty years, and frankly, the service has been fine, but I never agreed to the terms." So I called the number on the back of the universe.

## The representative was unhelpful

After six hours of hold music — which was just the sound of distant things falling — I finally reached Deborah in Retention. Deborah, it turns out, *is* the floor. Literally the concept of falling. She had notes on my account going back to 1994, including the time I jumped off the garage and rated my own landing.

> "Sir, before we proceed, can you confirm you're not currently floating?"
> "Well, I'm sitting, Deborah."
> "That's gravity you're feeling. Case closed?"

## Reasons my cancellation was denied

- **I signed an implied contract** the first time I ever tripped as a toddler
- My "free trial" of being a baby — held up, fed, floating in blankets — apparently expired in 1996
- The early termination fee is *the entire sky*
- Deborah offered me a "loyalty discount" on *falling with style*, which I found condescending

## What I've learned

I've been on the phone so long my arm got tired, and I briefly experienced a personal, localized form of weightlessness when I let go of the handset and it just... stayed there. Deborah called this "a promotional preview of the premium plan." Then she transferred me to her supervisor, who was also Deborah, but deeper.

---

I'm not giving up. I found a forum where a guy claims he got a partial refund by switching to *buoyancy*. I'm meeting him at the pool on Thursday. Deborah says she'll be there. Deborah is always there. Deborah is the *ground beneath my feet*.

*SATIRE / PARODY — This story is fictional. No phone calls to the universe were actually completed, and gravity remains, as always, non-negotiable.*
