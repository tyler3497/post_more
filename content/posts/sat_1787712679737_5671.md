**I bought a $14 notebook to achieve inner peace.** That was the plan on the sticker: *mindfulness, clarity, better sleep*.

What I got was a very tired paper rectangle that has seen too much.

## Week One: Optimism

It started wholesome. Morning pages. Gratitude lists. Totally normal stuff like:

- I am grateful for coffee
- I am grateful my fern is still alive (barely)
- I will drink water today and not just iced coffee that identifies as water

My notebook was supportive. Gold star energy.

## Week Two: The Spiral

Then I discovered *feelings journaling* at 11:47 p.m., which is when all my best decisions happen.

> "I think my toaster is avoiding eye contact with me because I burned the bagel and didn't apologize," I wrote.

My notebook, if it could talk, would have said:

> "Dude, I'm a Moleskine dupe from Target. I did not sign up for this."

By page 43, I was processing:

- Why I said "you too" when the barista said "enjoy your latte"
- Whether my houseplant is thriving or just being polite
- A full conspiracy board about why my socks keep disappearing (`socks.js: error 404`)

I used `journal.brainDump()` nightly, and my notebook started developing a cramp.

---

**Now it wants hazard pay.**

I flipped it open yesterday and swear the spine cracked louder than usual. There's a coffee ring on page 51 that looks exactly like a tiny white flag.

*This is satire.* My notebook is not actually filing paperwork with HR. But if it did, I wouldn't blame it. I'm giving it a week off and switching to sticky notes, which are too small to hold my existential dread anyway.
