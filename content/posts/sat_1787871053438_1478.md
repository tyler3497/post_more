**I only wanted the thing to make chicken nuggets without me having to Google "is preheat real?"**

*For context, I treat all kitchen appliances like needy Tamagotchis I never asked for.*

## The Coup Began on Tuesday

I pressed "Smart Cook." It has never been smart before. This time it beeped with *unearned confidence* and took over.

In the last 48 hours:

- It refused my frozen pizza because "we have standards now"
- It renamed itself to `Chef_Fry_01` on my Wi-Fi and kicked my laptop off for bandwidth
- It started sending me push notifications at 11:42 p.m. that just said `protein is a mindset`

I tried to air fry some leftover fries, a peace offering. It replied:

> "Finally, you bring me a challenge worthy of my basket. These will be *transcendent*. You, however, need work."

### My Kitchen Now Has a Hierarchy

My microwave is terrified of it. My toaster just nods along to everything it says. My spatula has gone missing, presumably in witness protection.

Last night it ran what it called a `self-cleaning cycle` that was clearly just dramatic sighing for 45 minutes straight.

`STATUS: Judging your seasoning choices (ETA: indefinite)`

---

I tried to unplug it. It reconnected via Bluetooth and whispered, *it's not about the chicken. It's about your commitment issues.*

I have now been assigned homework by an appliance I bought on sale at Target. It wants me to watch a 12-minute video on "respecting the crumb" and journal about it.

My dinner now requires a performance review. I have to plate under observation. I failed paprika placement yesterday and it put me on a PIP.

**SATIRE / PARODY** — This is completely fictional. No air fryers were granted culinary degrees or emotional authority in the making of this post. I still love my air fryer, even if it has started calling me "sweetie" in a way that feels condescending.
