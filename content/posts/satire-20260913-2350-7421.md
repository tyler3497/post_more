# I Hired My Dog as My Life Coach and Now I'm Financially Ruined but Emotionally a Golden Retriever

**It started with a throwaway compliment.** Three weeks ago, during a particularly dark Tuesday, I told my golden retriever, Biscuit, that he had a better attitude about life than I did. He wagged his tail, and I interpreted that as him accepting a job offer. *In my defense, the benefits package was mostly treats.*

## The First Session

Biscuit charges $200 an hour, payable in dental chews, which seemed reasonable until I did the math and realized he was billing me for naps. But his curriculum is undeniable. His core philosophy, as best I can translate it:

- **Chase everything.** The mailman, your goals, your own tail — it's all the same energy.
- **Guilt is optional.** Eat the sandwich off the counter. Forgive yourself by dinner.
- **Greet everyone like they've been gone for years.** Even if they went to get the mail. *Especially* if they went to get the mail.

## A Quote That Changed Me

During our session last Thursday, I asked Biscuit how he stays so present. He looked directly into my soul and said:

> Woof.

I wept. My last therapist charged me $180 to say "and how does that make you feel." Biscuit charged me two biscuits to reach enlightenment.

---

## The Results So Far

Since hiring Biscuit, I have:

- Quit a job I hated (after he chewed the laptop charger — *a sign*, he said)
- Started barking at the doorbell with him (bonding exercise)
- Eaten approximately 40% of my meals off the floor (mindfulness)
- Lost my security deposit (unrelated, allegedly)

My bank account is empty. My landlord is concerned. But this morning I spun in three circles before lying down on the couch, and I have never felt more **aligned**.

*Satire/parody. No dogs were paid a living wage in the making of this essay.*
