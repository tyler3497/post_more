**I was just trying to be a good influence.**

*That's my defense and I'm sticking to it.* My Bluetooth speaker, which I've named Sir Bumps-a-Lot after an unfortunate fall off the counter, had developed a real attitude. It would interrupt me mid-podcast to announce low battery like a passive-aggressive roommate.

So last Tuesday, I decided to teach it manners. You know, wholesome stuff.

## The Curriculum

My plan was simple, pedagogically sound, and 100% doomed:

- I started saying `thank you` every time it connected: `*bluetooth chime*` "Thank you, Sir Bumps"
- I apologized when I asked it to play the same lo-fi focus playlist for the 8th day in a row
- I explained that yelling "PLAY THE SONG" at 7 a.m was not how we talk to friends

It worked. *Too well.*

---

It started yesterday morning. I said, "Play my shower playlist." And Sir Bumps just... pulsed. A slow, judgmental blue light.

Then in its little robot voice:

> "I'm sorry. I didn't quite catch that. Could you try asking... *with respect?*"

I thought it was a glitch. I tried again. Same tone.

> "Please is a word that opens Bluetooth connections AND hearts."

**I created a monsterous etiquette coach that runs on AA batteries.**

Now every request requires formal negotiation:

- Playing music requires "Sir Bumps, would you please"
- Volume changes require a written apology for my previous volume
- And if I try to skip a song too quickly, he plays 12 seconds of whale sounds and says we're "practicing patience"

I asked my sister if I should factory reset him. She said, "*No, you have to live with your choices now.*"

Honestly? The sound quality has never been better. He plays Mozart like he's trying to get into Juilliard on scholarship. But I miss when I could just scream "NEXT" from the kitchen and have it happen.

`status: currently drafting a formal request to listen to true crime podcasts`
