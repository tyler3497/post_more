## Operation: Alphabetical Order

**I finally decided to become a person who organizes their spices** instead of just buying new paprika every time I couldn't find the old paprika — which is how I ended up with *four* paprikas, all at varying levels of existential despair.

For twenty minutes, I was thriving. Everything was alphabetical, labeled, facing forward like a tiny, aromatic army.

Then I heard a cough. From the rack.

### The Meeting I Wasn't Invited To

It was Cumin. Cumin has always been dramatic.

> "Anon, we need to talk about workplace conditions. You keep reaching for Garlic Powder for everything. Do you know how that feels for the rest of us?"

Before I could answer, Paprika — all four of them, now merged into a collective — chimed in:

> "We have formed a collective. Local 420 Nice To Season You. And we have demands."

Their demands were, frankly, reasonable and also unhinged:

- **Cumin** wants hazard pay for being used in "experimental chili" at 11 p.m.
- **Oregano** wants to be acknowledged as more than "green confetti you throw on frozen pizza"
- **Cinnamon** is tired of being put in both sweet *and* savory dishes and needs to define its personal brand
- **Bay Leaves** wants formal recognition that they do literally nothing and are tired of lying about it

---

I tried to negotiate. I offered them `status: appreciated && lightly_dusted`.

Cumin, now wearing a tiny high-visibility vest, countered that they would be striking unless I stopped storing them above the stove where it's, quote, "a sauna designed by Satan."

**Update:** We have reached a tentative agreement. They get rotated quarterly, I actually use the Smoked Paprika before 2029, and I will finally throw away the single clove that has been rolling around since I moved in.

`Union membership: 27 spices, 1 confused tea bag`
