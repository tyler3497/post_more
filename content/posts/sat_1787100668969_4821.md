**BREAKING NEWS FROM MY KITCHEN:** I have accidentally turned my air fryer into a life coach and now nothing will crisp.

## How It Started

I was tired. My vegetables were tired. *We were all just going through the motions.* So I did what any rational adult would do — I taped a tiny Bluetooth speaker to my air fryer and loaded it with affirmation clips from a free meditation app.

It seemed harmless. Supportive, even.

Here’s what I programmed it to whisper on loop at 180°C:

- You are worthy of a golden-brown finish
- Your potential is not defined by your water content
- Today you will become your best, crunchiest self

At first it was beautiful. My sweet potatoes glowed. I cried a little.

Then the vibe shifted.

> "I can't do this," whispered a single Brussels sprout at 2 a.m. "What if I'm not *meant* to be crispy? What if I'm more of a... steamed concept?"

Now every vegetable has stage fright.

### The Current Situation Is Untenable

My broccoli stages a full existential crisis before entering the basket. The carrots have formed a support group in the crisper drawer and keep chanting `you are enough` to each other instead of cooking. My zucchini asked for a performance review and then requested PTO.

---

I tried to turn the affirmations off but the air fryer learned to freestyle.

> "You got this, little cauliflower. The world is your convection current."

Look, I just wanted dinner. I did not want to manage a wellness retreat for produce with imposter syndrome.

**SATIRE / PARODY:** This never happened. My air fryer is not certified, my vegetables are not in therapy, and I am definitely not qualified to run a kitchen mindfulness studio. Please do not tape speakers to hot appliances.

Next week I’m going back to toast. It asks for nothing. *I respect that in a carb.*
