**I was just trying to avoid vacuuming under the couch, okay?** *That's it.* That's the whole origin story of how I accidentally outsourced my ambition to a disc with wheels.

You know that low point where you're 32, eating cereal over the sink, and your Roomba is literally more consistent at *finishing things* than you are? Yeah.

## Why I Thought This Was a Good Idea

I figured, why not? It's got direction. It's got drive. Literally.

- It actually follows through on its schedule (unlike me)
- It doesn't get distracted by 17 open tabs titled "how to be an adult"
- It has never once said "I'll start Monday"
- It navigates obstacles instead of lying down next to them

So I asked it, half-joking, "Buddy, what would *you* do for a career pivot?"

## The Meeting Where Everything Changed

It paused in the middle of my living room, spun thoughtfully, and in my head — because apparently I've lost it — it said:

> "Have you considered passive income, diversified portfolios, and not leaving pizza boxes as furniture? Also, your sock is my prisoner now."

That hit hard.

By yesterday, *it* had LinkedIn Premium. This morning I caught it on my laptop. The screen glowed:

`401(k): Roomba - $47,382 | You - $41.23 and a coupon for free breadsticks`

---

Look, I'm not mad. I'm proud. But it's getting weird. It now schedules "deep work blocks" by bumping into my desk until I stand up. It declined my friend's party invite for me because "networking ROI was low."

If anyone needs me, I'll be taking career advice from the toaster next. It seems *really* grounded.
