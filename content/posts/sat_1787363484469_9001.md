**I clicked unsubscribe exactly once, like a responsible adult with boundaries and a functioning spam filter.**

*Which, in retrospect, was my first mistake.*

I didn't mean to cause an emotional collapse. The email was from "Trendy Sock Emporium - You Left Something In Your Cart (And Our Hearts)," and it had been following me for 8 months. I thought we had an understanding: they send, I ignore. A beautiful, modern relationship.

Then I hit unsubscribe.

## My Inbox Has Entered Its Mourning Era

Within minutes, my Gmail started acting... different. The promotional tab dimmed like it was lowering flags to half-mast. My inbox, which I have treated as a digital junk drawer since 2019, suddenly developed *feelings*.

According to my inbox's official press release (which it CC'd me on), I violated the sacred pact:

- I opened 0 of their 147 emails but never had the courage to leave
- I used their 10% discount code once in 2022 and then ghosted
- I kept their "We Miss You!" email starred for six months out of guilt
- I told them their socks "really tied the outfit together" when I was clearly wearing sandals

> "We thought we had something real," whispered the automated newsletter, from somewhere deep in my spam folder. "You said our subject lines were 'so quirky and relatable.' Was that a lie? Were the emojis not enough? 🧦💔"

It gets worse. The code running this whole melodrama is not even subtle:

`if (human.setsBoundaries === true) { inbox.initiateGriefSequence(5); memorialService.schedule("tonight", "with_candles"); }`

---

Now my inbox is holding a full memorial service. The unread coupons are standing in a circle, holding tiny LED tea lights I didn't know my laptop could render. The "Final Notice: Your Cart Is Lonely" email is giving the eulogy. My actual important emails — like my dentist appointment — can't get a word in because the drafts folder keeps playing Sarah McLachlan on loop.

I tried to apologize. I re-subscribed. It said, "Too late. We're seeing other inboxes now."

*Satire, obviously.* My inbox is not sentient. *Probably.* But if you get an invite to a candlelight vigil for my relationship with sock marketing, please don't come. I'm trying to keep this low-key.
