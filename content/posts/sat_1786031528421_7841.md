**OFFICIAL STATEMENT FROM THE UNDERSIDE OF MY COUCH:** I am officially a bad landlord and my tenants have receipts.

I've been putting off cleaning under my couch for, *conservatively*, three years. My mom kept saying "you'd be amazed what's under there" and I finally snapped after my AirPod fell into the abyss.

I expected one LEGO, three petrified French fries, and crushing regret. What I found was a fully functioning civilization.

## The Discovery

In the 1.5 inches between carpet and shame, dozens of dust bunnies had built infrastructure.

- A tiny city hall made from a lost AA battery and a bottle cap
- A diner serving my 2023 Cheerios *al dente*
- A meticulous ledger logging every quarter I've dropped since moving in
- One very judgmental bunny wearing a mayor's sash made of dental floss

> "We've been paying rent," the mayor announced, holding up a nickel like it was a legal summons. "Seventeen cents monthly, in loose change and emotional support. You've dusted exactly zero times since the second Obama administration. Which one? We don't know. We live under a couch."

---

## Negotiations Went Poorly

I tried to explain that **I** am on the lease. They countered with `squatters_rights.exe` and presented their permit, which was just my expired pizza coupon with a sticker on it.

I offered to vacuum around them. They offered me a deal:

- I stop vacuuming forever
- I recognize their LLC as a non-profit
- They keep the remote

I told them I was already emotionally non-profit. They took my TV remote as a security deposit.

**Current status:**

- `quarters_recovered: 0`
- `moral_high_ground: lost`
- `dust_bunnies_unionized: true`

I put the couch back. We have an understanding now — they keep my change, I pretend I don't hear their town council meetings at 2 a.m.

*This is 100% satire/parody. My dust bunnies are real but their incorporation papers are still pending.*
