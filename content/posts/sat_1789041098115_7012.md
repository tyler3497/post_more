**I just wanted a free slow cooker. I am now the central figure in a neighborhood conflict so bitter it has its own group chat.**

## How It Started

I posted in my local Buy Nothing group — the wholesome, granola-crunching corner of the internet where people give away half-burned candles and single curtains. I typed the fateful words:

> "ISO: slow cooker. Making soup season my whole personality. Can pick up today."

Within four minutes, three people offered me crockpots. *Three.* I did what any rational adult would do: I said yes to all of them.

## How It's Going

It turns out there is an entire shadow economy of crockpot acquisition in this neighborhood, and I have disrupted the supply chain. The other members are **furious**. One woman has been hunting a free Crock-Pot since *2023*. She has a spreadsheet. She showed me the spreadsheet.

The current escalation timeline:

- Linda accused me of being a "crockpot scalper" (the resale value is $0, Linda)
- Gary formed a rival subgroup called Buy SOMETHING where everything costs "emotional labor"
- Someone started a rumor that I already own four slow cookers, which I cannot confirm or deny *(I can confirm)*
- Linda's husband has been spotted driving slowly past my house, which he claims is "just his route," but his route is forty miles long

## The Final Straw

Yesterday I received a private message from Linda herself. It read, in full:

> "Keep ONE. That's it. That's the message. We are not monsters. Enjoy your soup."

I kept one. I gave the other two away *in the same group*, which has been interpreted by all parties as either a peace offering or an act of war. Nobody will tell me which.
