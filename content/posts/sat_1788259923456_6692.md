**I finally decided to unsubscribe from myself.**

*Which, in retrospect, is exactly the kind of dramatic life choice an overthinker would make at 11:47 p.m. on a Tuesday.*

I realized I was paying $0 a month for unlimited spiraling. Premium anxiety, deluxe what-ifs, the whole bundle. So I called to cancel.

## The Retention Department Was Ready For Me

I dialed the number on the back of my brain, which is just my forehead. After three rings, a very tired version of my own voice answered:

> "Thank you for calling Overthinking™ — where every thought gets a sequel. My name is also you. How can I keep you today?"

I said I wanted to cancel. There was a long pause, then furious keyboard typing.

### What They Offered To Keep Me:

- **One free month of clarity** — then it auto-renews into wondering if you left the stove on in 2019
- A loyalty discount on 3 a.m. replaying that thing you said in 2018
- Ability to pause your subscription, but only while you're actively trying to fall asleep
- `mental_gymnastics.exe` upgraded to premium at no extra overthinking

I said, "No thanks, I'd like to just... not."

The agent gasped.

> "But sir, you've been a valued spiraler since age 12! If you leave, who will remind you that your email tone was *slightly* too casual? Who will draft seventeen responses to a text that just says 'ok'?"

---

I tried to escalate to a manager. The manager was also me, but wearing a tiny headset and drinking my coffee.

He offered a final deal: I could downgrade to casual pondering. Two worries a week. No late-night rewrites.

I said, "Let me think about it."

And that's how they got me. I'm still on the call.

*Currently on hold with myself. Estimated wait time: forever.*
