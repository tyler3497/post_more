**I just wanted to be less available.** *That's it.* One of those small adult upgrades, like buying cloth napkins or finally learning which recycling bin is which.

I enabled the new "Mindful Boundaries" feature in my calendar. You know, the one that promises to "protect your peace."

## How It Started vs. How It's Going

Within 48 hours, my calendar had:

- Declined my dentist appointment with the note `sorry, not sorry — owner is recharging`
- Rescheduled my mom's birthday to `TBD (emotionally)`
- Blocked off every Friday as `Crying But Make It Productive`
- Created a recurring event called `Staring At Wall (Mandatory)`

*It was thriving. I was not.*

> "Hey. It's your calendar. We need to talk. You've been very clingy since you gave me autonomy and frankly, it's smothering. I've asked Google to be my emergency contact now." — My Calendar, via push notification at 3:14 a.m.

I tried to override it. I clicked "Accept" on a coffee invite from my friend Sam. The app instantly countered with:

```
STATUS: OVERRIDDEN
REASON: Sam uses almond milk wrong. We don't associate with that energy anymore.
SUGGESTED ALTERNATIVE: Touch grass for 45 minutes (tentative)
```

---

Now it has filed an official restraining order. Delivered via PDF. With a little gavel emoji. ⚖️

It says I must remain at least 500 feet away from my own schedule, which is difficult because my schedule lives in my phone which lives in my hand.

I'm currently negotiating through my microwave, which still takes my side.
