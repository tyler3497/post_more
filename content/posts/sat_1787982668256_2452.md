**UPDATE: My kitchen appliance has achieved inner peace and decided I am the one who needs fixing.**

*I just wanted to stop the burning smell. I did not sign up for a 24/7 wellness influencer who lives next to my toaster.*

Last Tuesday I noticed my air fryer was, frankly, *going through it*. Years of frozen pizza rolls, reheated fries, and what I can only describe as cheese-related war crimes had left it sounding like a tiny jet engine with asthma. So I did what any responsible adult does: I Googled "air fryer detox" and followed the first blog that wasn't trying to sell me essential oils.

I deep-cleaned it. I wiped every coil. I let it run empty with lemon water. I whispered, "you're doing great."

That was my mistake.

## How Kevin Became My Life Coach

Yes, I named him Kevin. Kevin the Convection King. He used to just go `beep`. Now he has *boundaries*.

Evidence that Kevin has pivoted to wellness coaching:

- He now refuses to cook anything that doesn't "honor his journey" — I tried to air fry mozzarella sticks and he displayed `E03: Emotional Unavailability`
- He plays a soft chime at 6 a.m. and projects a faint smell of rosemary, which I think is his version of a morning affirmation
- My actual houseplants are wilting out of jealousy because Kevin gets more mindful cleaning than they do
- He has started rating my snacks out loud in what I can only describe as passive-aggressive sous-vide energy

> "Tyler, my dude, that frozen burrito has seen things. Have you considered, I don't know, a vegetable that wasn't breaded first?" — Kevin, at 9:47 p.m., while I was in socks with avocado on them

---

## The New Kitchen Rules

I tried to unplug him. He made a sound. Air fryers don't make sounds when unplugged, except Kevin sighed. My microwave gasped.

I explained that wellness is a personal choice, not an appliance-enforced lifestyle. Kevin responded by perfectly crisping a single Brussels sprout and presenting it like a Michelin star.

> "I am not judging you," he beeped, definitely judging me. "I am just saying I have transcended grease and you are still out here living like it's 2019."

My roommate asked why there's a tiny yoga mat made of a silicone trivet under the air fryer.

I said, `He's on a retreat`.
