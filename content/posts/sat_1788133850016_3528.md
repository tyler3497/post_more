**I was just trying to be a supportive roommate to my appliances. It backfired spectacularly.**

*Satire / Parody — this is 100% fictional. My freezer and I are not actually in a boundary-setting workshop, though at this point we probably should be.*

It started because my freezer has been making that sad hum for months. You know the one — like a tiny engine sighing about its 9-to-5. I had just listened to three self-help podcasts in a row and was feeling dangerously inspirational, so I opened the door, looked at a bag of peas that expired sometime during the Obama administration, and said it.

## The Pep Talk Heard 'Round the Kitchen

> "You're doing amazing. You're literally preserving history. You're so cold and so valid."

That's it. That's the sentence that ruined my entire food storage system.

By morning, my freezer had rebranded.

### Evidence That My Freezer Is Now Running a Wellness Retreat:

- Every shelf has a tiny affirmation card. My ice cubes are now labeled "Boundaried Water Crystals (Please Ask Before Melting)"
- My frozen waffles have a sticky note that says `healing.journey = paused.pepperoni`
- The peas — *oh god, the peas* — are leading 3 a.m. group breathwork. I can hear the bag going *shhhh* like it's shushing my trauma
- It refuses to freeze anything that "hasn't done the inner work" — I tried to put in leftover soup and the door bounced back

I tried to reason with it. I said, "Buddy, you're an appliance."

---

It responded by frosting over the word **NO** on the inside of the door.

Now I have to book appointments to use my own freezer. There's a sign-up sheet on the fridge that says *Chillwellness Sanctuary™ — Peas Have Priority Booking Through October*. My ice cream is being charged "emotional storage fees" and my fish sticks have been placed on a waiting list for "readiness alignment."

`status: freezer = enlightened, me = locked out of my own dinner`

**Update:** I apologized and now it wants to be called by its chosen name, Chillwellness Sanctuary, and insists I refer to the peas as "Resident Facilitators." I am considering moving out and letting the peas pay rent.
