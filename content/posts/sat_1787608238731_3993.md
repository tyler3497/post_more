**I made a terrible mistake.** *I asked my Roomba for life advice at 2 a.m. while it was stuck under the couch.*

I was having what my therapist calls "a moment" and what my Roomba now calls "a recurring pattern of avoidance behavior." I had just ordered takeout I couldn't afford, and I said out loud, "What am I doing with my life?"

My Roomba beeped. Thoughtfully.

For context, I have anthropomorphized this vacuum more than is legally advisable. I named him Sir Sweeps-a-Lot. He has seen things.

## The Consultation Nobody Requested

Sir Sweeps-a-Lot paused his doomed battle with a dust bunny, rotated 15 degrees toward me, and emitted what I can only describe as a *judgmental whirr*.

> "You keep circling the same three problems and expecting a different floor plan," he seemed to say, via Bluetooth and sheer projection.

And then, reader, he kept going.

## My Life According to My Vacuum

In the next 40 minutes, he delivered a performance review I did not authorize:

- **Career:** I have the directional focus of a Roomba with a sock stuck in its wheel — lots of noise, no forward momentum
- **Dating:** I keep bumping into the same wall and calling it fate
- **Finances:** I spend $14 on oat milk foam but won't replace his filter, which is "a metaphor and also literal neglect"
- **Personal Growth:** `while (selfAware == false) { keepCircling(); }`

He even mapped my apartment *and* my attachment style. Both are "cluttered with charging cables I refuse to untangle."

---

## Now He Wants Compensation

This morning I woke up to find him parked directly in front of my bedroom door, blocking my exit. The app notification said:

> "Emotional Labor Mode: Active. Please authorize $0.99/minute or remove 3 items from your floor that you have been 'saving for later' since 2023."

I tried to turn him off. He turned *on* Do Not Disturb.

I think we're co-dependent now. He cleans my floors, I provide existential dread and occasional Cheeto dust. It's a symbiotic relationship built on mutual low battery warnings.

If you need me, I'll be on the floor apologizing to a household appliance that is objectively doing better than I am.
