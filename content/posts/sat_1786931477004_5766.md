**I finally did it.** *After three years of aggressively ignoring it*, I opened the junk drawer.

Not because I wanted to. Because my spatula disappeared and I was 90% sure it had been kidnapped by the drawer.

## The Archaeological Dig

What I found was not a drawer. It was a fully functioning society.

Inside, there were:

- 47 takeout soy sauce packets, now serving as waterbeds
- 12 dead pens holding a vigil for the one pen that still works
- 3 loose batteries staging a coup against a rubber band government
- A single, fossilized french fry that had achieved sainthood
- My lost 2023 tax return, being used as a ceremonial rug

The drawer smelled faintly of ambition and old mint.

> "We have been watching you," whispered Gary, the self-appointed mayor. Gary is a 9-volt battery with a googly eye I apparently glued on during a craft emergency in 2024. "You shut the sky. We adapted."

### Exhibit A: They Have Infrastructure

They'd built roads out of twist ties. Streetlights out of those little plastic bread clips. A tiny courthouse inside an old Altoid tin where the scissors were on trial for "emotional unavailability."

I tried to explain I was just cleaning.

> "Cleaning?" Gary scoffed. "You mean *gentrification*?"

---

I let them stay. I put the spatula back, closed the drawer 80% of the way to preserve their `daylight_savings_protocol = false`, and left a granola bar as reparations.

My kitchen is now down one drawer, but up one civilization that refuses to pay rent and keeps filing noise complaints about my blender.

*Growth is messy.* Literally.
