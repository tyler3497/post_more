## My Pantry Has Become a TED Talk About Hemp

**I just wanted to stop finding a mountain of crumpled grocery totes under my passenger seat.** *You know the pile.* One says TRADER JOE'S in aggressive cheer. Four claim to be from farmers markets I've never actually been to.

So I watched one YouTube video. One. On how to fold reusable bags into tiny envelope triangles. Marie Kondo-level stuff. I folded all 17.

That was my first mistake.

### The Morning After

I opened my pantry and they had rearranged themselves into a perfect circle.

Not a pile. A *circle*.

Standing upright. Folded tighter than my jeans after Thanksgiving.

> "We have shed our former lives as crumpled polyesters," whispered the canvas tote with the kale stain. "Have you considered owning... nothing?"

Apparently while I slept, they formed **The Order of Perpetual Fold**.

Their doctrine:

- **You don't need 17 bags.** You need one bag and *intention*
- *Single-use plastic is a metaphor for your commitment issues*
- All snacks must be purchased in bulk and carried with spiritual awareness
- `if (item.plastic && item.joy == 0) { return to shelf }`

### I Tried to Leave

I grabbed the Trader Joe's bag for a quick run. The others gasped in unison.

> "She chooses convenience. She always chooses convenience," they murmured.

The little organic cotton one started chanting.

---

I now have to ask permission to enter my own pantry. They've instituted a lending library. They charge late fees in loose cilantro.

I tried to buy a new bag last week. They saw the receipt in my pocket and held an intervention.

`Status: pantry closed for enlightenment, 90% folded, 10% judged`
