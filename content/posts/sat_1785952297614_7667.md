## The Inheritance I Never Planned

**I bought reusable grocery bags to save the planet, not to accidentally adopt 12 canvas children who now live in my trunk.**

It started innocently. One trip, I forgot them. Then two trips. Then it was *April* and I was buying new reusable bags to carry groceries to the car where my other reusable bags live. My trunk has become a tote bag retirement community.

### The Signs They'd Moved In Permanently

By last week, the evidence was undeniable:

- My trunk smelled faintly of canvas and broken dreams
- One bag had started using my emergency granola bar as a pillow
- They had organized themselves by shade of beige and level of existential dread
- My spare tire filed a formal noise complaint about the rustling

I opened the trunk to finally bring them inside and a little gust of wind made one bag flop over, and I swear I heard judgment.

> "Oh, NOW you remember us? After seven months? We've bonded with the jumper cables, Karen. We ARE car now."

They're not wrong. They have sun-faded logos, a melted cough drop fused to the bottom, and what I can only describe as *trunk tenure*.

---

### The DMV Intervention

I tried to get my car inspected and the guy just stared into the trunk and said:

> "Ma'am, I can't legally inspect a vehicle that is 40% Wegmans tote by volume. That's a lifestyle choice, not a Corolla."

So I've proposed a compromise. I will bring in one bag per week, provided they agree not to testify against me when I finally clean out my glove compartment, which currently contains `17 years` of parking receipts and a fossilized french fry.

The trunk light flickered. I took that as a yes.
