**I have good socks and I have grocery-store socks, and until Tuesday those were two very separate legal entities.**

*My good socks are sacred. Merino wool, no holes, elastic that still believes in itself.*

I wore them to Kroger because every other pair was in the hamper staging a labor dispute. Twelve-minute milk run, I thought. My closet thought it was a coronation.

## The Incident That Ruined Everything

I put on the merinos. My closet gasped. Fabric shouldn't gasp, but mine did.

I was just buying milk. My wardrobe thought I was being scouted.

- My blazer whispered to my jeans: *he's finally committing*
- My sneakers stretched like they were warming up for the Olympics
- My laundry hamper filed an emergency injunction because this was *not in the rotation*

In produce, my left sock glowed with pride.

> "My liege," said my hoodie when I got home, hanging straighter. "We heard you took the Good Ones to see the outside world. Does this mean we're a *formal* household now?"

I said, "I just needed milk."

My closet replied:

> "Milk is not a milk errand when you're wearing *the merino diplomats*. That's a state visit."

## The New Dress Code Is Unhinged

Since then, my dresser has implemented **Operation Black-Tie or Die**:

- No t-shirt without a 45-second lint roll
- Sweatpants must present a written excuse signed by me
- My beanie now demands to be called *headwear*
- My flip-flops have been sent to re-education in the back of the closet

I tried logic:

```
if (socks.quality == "good" && destination == "grocery") {
  wardrobe.anxiety = Infinity;
}
```

It didn't help. My wardrobe printed a seating chart and put my good socks at the head of the table.

---

I tried my holey socks yesterday to de-escalate. My dresser made a disappointed-aunt noise and hid my keys.

Honestly, I look great at Kroger now. I'm the only guy in produce dressed like he's pitching VC to a cantaloupe.

**SATIRE / PARODY** — *100% fictional. No socks were promoted to management, though mine are negotiating a raise in fabric softener.*
