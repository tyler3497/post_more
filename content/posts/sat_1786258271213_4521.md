**I finally decided to become one of those Sunday meal-prep people you see on the internet who definitely have a personality.**

You know the type. Containers lined up like obedient soldiers, quinoa that hasn't given up on life yet, a color-coded lid system that screams *I've made peace with my choices*.

I made five.

## The Descent

By Tuesday, things got weird. The containers started... communicating.

Evidence that my fridge has become a hostile work environment:

- Container #2 (teriyaki chicken, 3 days old) was pushed to the back, *clearly* icing out Container #4
- The hummus side-cup developed condensation that spelled HELP
- Someone — and I'm not saying it was the overnight oats — left a passive-aggressive note that just said `we need to talk about your 11pm snacking`

> "We feel unseen," whispered the lentil soup, which is dramatic because I literally see it every time I look for cheese. "You promised you'd eat us by Wednesday and now it's Thursday and you're ordering tacos again."

I tried to mediate.

---

### My Attempt at Conflict Resolution

I opened the fridge and said, out loud, to a shelf of plastic:

> "Listen, I'm doing my best."

*Silence*. Then, in unison, they all fogged up slightly. That's container for *we're disappointed*.

My roommate walked in, saw me negotiating with chickpeas, and left without making eye contact. Fair.

`status: emotionally compromised by Tupperware`

## The Support Group

Now they meet at night. I hear tiny lids clicking. Last night I opened the fridge and they were in a perfect circle.

The quinoa spoke first: "We just want closure."

Look, I get it. I spent $47 on groceries to save money and instead created a **refrigerated HOA** that judges my life choices.

The grilled veggies have started a newsletter. It’s called *We Told You So Weekly*. Circulation: 5.

*I’m eating them tonight, out of spite and guilt, in no particular order.*
