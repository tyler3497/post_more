**Big news from my apartment:** I tried to build community and accidentally created high school.

*It seemed wholesome at the time.* My therapist said I should "nurture my support system," so I decided to start small — literally. I lined up my six houseplants on the windowsill and formally introduced them. By name. Out loud.

## How It All Went Sideways

At first it was sweet. The monstera waved. The pothos said hi. Then the succulents realized they had something in common — *low emotional needs and a shared dislike of overwatering* — and formed an alliance.

Within 48 hours:

- The monstera and pothos started a **Growth Mindset Book Club** and only invite plants with aerial roots
- The two succulents now communicate exclusively in passive-aggressive `drought_tolerance > friendship` code
- The snake plant stands in the corner pretending it's too cool to care, which is its entire brand
- And poor Fernando the fern? Fernando has been fully iced out

> "We just feel like Fernando is... a lot," the pothos told me, rustling judgmentally. "So needy. So humid. We are in our low-maintenance era."

Fernando whispered:

> "I just wanted friends who mist me back."

---

I tried mediation. I made name tags. I played lo-fi beats for group cohesion.

```js
if (plant.popularity < 2 && plant.needsWater == "constantly") {
  moveToBathroomForSelfReflection();
  sendFernToTherapy();
}
```

It did not work. The succulents have a group chat without Fernando. The monstera is now calling itself the "main character" because it got one new leaf.
