**I was trying to be financially responsible, which is my first mistake.**

*Historically, my budgeting strategy has been \"ignore it until it goes away,\" but my bank app sent me a push notification that just said \"girl.\"* So I decided to save $35 on a haircut. How hard could bangs be? It's just hair. On your forehead. That everyone sees immediately.

## The Business Plan That Failed in 4 Minutes

I watched one YouTube tutorial at 1.75x speed, which is where confidence goes to die. The lady said \"just dust the ends.\" I heard \"reinvent your face with kitchen scissors.\"

Evidence that this was a bad idea, presented to the court of my bathroom mirror:

- I used *craft scissors* from a drawer labeled MISC that have previously only cut coupons and denial
- I cut them dry, while my hair was still in a ponytail holder from yesterday
- I kept saying \"it's giving French girl\" out loud to manifest it
- I trimmed \"just one more little bit\" seven times, which is six times too many

> \"So this is how you treat the face I've been holding up for 29 years?\" said my mirror, in a voice I assigned to it because the silence was too loud. \"You bring *her* into this house? With *those* scissors?\"

### The Aftermath Is a Legal Separation

My mirror has asked for space. It now fogs itself up preemptively when I walk in.

`if (bangs.length < 0.5_inch && confidence > 0) { mirror.avoidEyeContact(); selfies.deleteAll(); }`

I tried to style them. I tried to swoop them. One side swoops. The other side has resigned and is now laying flat in protest, like a tiny exhausted rug.

My roommate walked in, paused, and said:

> \"Oh! Oh, you did a... *choice*. Very brave. Very... ventilated forehead.\"

---

I have pinned them back with 14 bobby pins that are now load-bearing. I Googled \"how fast does hair grow if you apologize to it daily\" and the answer was not fast enough.

My mirror left a Post-it that says **WE NEED TO TALK ABOUT BOUNDARIES (AND BLUNT CUTS)**. I'm giving it space. I'm wearing a beanie indoors. In August.
