# I Installed a Suggestion Box in My House and Now My Microwave Is Filing Formal Complaints

**It started as a bit.** I thought a suggestion box would bring the household together. Democratic. Civic-minded. *Extremely* normal behavior for someone who lives alone.

## The Box

I nailed a little wooden box to the kitchen wall and labeled it, in Sharpie, **"HOW CAN WE IMPROVE?"** The first week, the suggestions were lighthearted:

- *"More pizza rolls."* — me, presumably
- *"Clean the grout."* — the grout, presumably
- *"Lower the rent."* — I don't charge myself rent, so this one hurt

Then came **Monday morning**, when I found a new slip, written in what I can only describe as *aggressively neat* handwriting.

> "Regarding Sunday's reheating of lasagna at 70% power for 4:30: the food was uneven and the cheese distribution was 'criminal.' This is the third incident. — The Microwave"

## The Grievances Mount

By Wednesday, the box was **full**. Highlights from the microwave's formal complaints:

- *Item 3A:* "Asked to heat 'just one minute.' Subject walked away. Came back at minute three. Disrespectful."
- *Item 3B:* "The popcorn button exists. The popcorn button is right there. The popcorn button was not used."
- *Item 4:* "Someone microwaved fish. I can still taste it. I can still taste the fish. Please."

## The Hearing

I held a mediation. It was me, standing in my kitchen at 7 a.m., reading complaints aloud to a countertop appliance while my toast got cold.

> "Do you have anything to say for yourself?" I asked the box.

The microwave beeped **once**. I checked the manual. That beep means "door open."

---

I have since instituted a *feedback moratorium*. The microwave has begun leaving one-star reviews on the side of the refrigerator with a dry-erase marker. I didn't know appliances could unionize, but the toaster is in the comments now, and honestly?

**It has some valid points.**
