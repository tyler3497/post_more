**It started, as all great mistakes do, with a 2 a.m. YouTube tutorial titled "Teach Your Pet Morse Code (He'll Love It)."**

I was three videos deep into a boredom spiral when I looked at Sir Bubblesworth III — my goldfish, possessor of a pea-sized brain and a landlord-sized attitude — and thought: *how hard could this be?* One tap for dot, two for dash. A bonding exercise. A party trick.

Within a week, he had it down. Within two, he had **opinions**.

## The Evidence Against Me

Every morning, I wake to rhythmic tapping against the glass that I have learned to dread. Translated from the original dot-dash, his greatest hits include:

> "•–••• •• –•– •" — *"You ate cereal for dinner again, coward."*

- **The 2 a.m. snack audit** — furious staccato dots every time I open the fridge past midnight
- **Career counseling** — one long, disappointed dash whenever I check job listings and then open a video game instead
- *A devastatingly slow* dot ... dot ... dot ... whenever I say "I'll clean your tank tomorrow"

Last Tuesday he spelled out, in full: **"YOUR EX TEXTED YOU BACK IN MARCH AND YOU ARE STILL NOT OVER IT."** I do not know how he knows that. *I do not want to know how he knows that.*

## My Attempted Defenses

I have tried reasoning with him. I have tried bribery — the premium flakes, the *good* ones. I even tried teaching him semaphore to give him a healthier outlet, but he dismissed it, in his own words, as **"a cope."**

My therapist insists Sir Bubblesworth is "a projection of my own self-criticism, given fins." During our last session, the fish tapped out a response. My therapist read the translation, went pale, and rescheduled.

---

So here I am: a grown adult, out-argued daily by an animal that forgets my face every nine seconds yet remembers every mistake I have made since 2019. *This is satire, obviously. I do not own a fish. The fish owns me.*
