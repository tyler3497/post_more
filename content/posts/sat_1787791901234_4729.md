**I have made zero decisions this week and I have never been more stressed about it.**

In an effort to become a more decisive person, I decided to stop deciding entirely. Revolutionary, I know. I bought a Magic 8-Ball from a drugstore and promoted it to Chief Operating Officer of My Life.

## The Great Delegation Experiment

The plan was simple: ask the ball, live the answer, achieve inner peace. *What could possibly go wrong with abdicating all adult responsibility to a plastic sphere filled with blue goo?*

It started small:

- Should I have oatmeal or cold pizza for breakfast? The ball said `OUTLOOK NOT SO GOOD` on oatmeal, which felt personally aggressive but fair
- Should I text my landlord about the leaking faucet? `ASK AGAIN LATER` — so I did, 14 times, until the faucet fixed itself out of pity
- Should I finally clean my closet? `MY REPLY IS NO` — finally, some validation

By Wednesday, I was outsourcing everything. Should I go to the gym? Should I wear pants to get the mail? Should I apologize to my sourdough starter for neglecting it?

> "Look, man," I whispered to the 8-Ball at 11:47 p.m., "should I stay up and spiral or go to sleep like a responsible human?"

It floated up, slow and judgmental:

> **"SIGNS POINT TO YOU NEEDING TO TOUCH GRASS."**

---

### The Burnout

That's when I noticed the 8-Ball was... tired. The little floating dice inside was taking longer to surface. It started giving answers like `I'M TIRED, BOSS` and `DO I LOOK LIKE GOOGLE TO YOU?`

Yesterday I asked if I should order Thai food and it just showed `BRB` in tiny letters.

My therapist — I mean, my plastic oracle — has officially requested PTO. It left a Post-it on my desk that said *GONE FISHING FOR ANSWERS. BACK NEVER. TRY USING YOUR PREFRONTAL CORTEX.*

So now I'm sitting here, staring at a menu, forced to choose my own appetizer like some kind of 19th-century pioneer. I think I'll have the spring rolls.

*Unless that's what the ball would want me to think.*
