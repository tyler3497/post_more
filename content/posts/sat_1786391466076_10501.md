**BREAKING: My 6 a.m. self-improvement era has been served legal papers by the one appliance I trusted most.**

I decided to become a *Morning Person* last Tuesday, which, as you know, is code for completely reinventing yourself based on a TikTok you half-watched while eating cereal in bed.

I downloaded three habit trackers, bought a $40 journal that says BREATHE on it, and told my alarm clock we were entering a new phase together.

He did not take it well.

## The Optimization Plan That Broke Us

In the name of efficiency, I made a few small tweaks:

- Wake up at 5:00 a.m. instead of 7:30, to "own the day" (the day has since filed a restraining order)
- Five minutes of journaling, ten minutes of stretching, and `ice_plunge.exe` that is actually just my shower on extremely cold
- No snooze button — *a personal betrayal he still hasn't forgiven*
- Green smoothie that tastes like lawn clippings soaked in regret

My alarm clock, a loyal, slightly dusty digital fellow I've had since college, was used to a simple agreement: he rings, I resent him, we move on.

> "I didn't sign up to be a life coach," he buzzed at 4:59 a.m., blinking 5:00 with the energy of a disappointed father. "I signed up to beep. You turned me into a wellness influencer. I have boundaries."

## The Divorce Filing

Yesterday, I woke up to find a tiny stack of papers taped to my nightstand, next to my untouched gratitude list.

He'd printed them using my own Wi-Fi printer.

**Grounds for separation include:**

- `emotional_damage: using him as a Peloton instructor`
- `abandonment: I replaced his default beep with affirmations from a British lady named Poppy`
- `irreconcilable differences: he is a clock, I am now a person who says "let's circle back to my REM cycle"`

His lawyer is my old electric toothbrush. She vibrates angrily whenever I walk past the bathroom.

My roommate says I should just apologize, but honestly? *I think we both need space.* I'm thinking of switching to a sunrise alarm. He's thinking of moving to my neighbor's apartment where they still respect the sacred ritual of hitting snooze seven times.

We are currently sleeping in separate rooms. I'm on the couch. He's still on the nightstand, but he's turned his display away from me.

*Love is complicated.*
