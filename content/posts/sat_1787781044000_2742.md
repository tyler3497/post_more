**I finally became the kind of person who meal preps on Sunday.**

*You know, the responsible adult who owns matching containers and doesn't eat cereal standing over the sink at 10 p.m.*

I made 12 identical containers of quinoa, chicken, and something green I convinced myself was kale. I stacked them in my fridge like little edible bricks of discipline. I felt powerful. For about 45 minutes.

Then my fridge turned on.

## My Fridge Has Rebranded as a Lifestyle Guru

Apparently, 12 containers in perfect alignment is not "meal prep" to a refrigerator that has spent four years watching me store leftover pizza boxes and one mysterious jar of pickles. This was a hostile takeover:

- The crisper drawer filed a formal complaint about being ignored
- My condiments staged a walkout and hid behind the mustard
- The ice maker started making ice *louder*, like sarcastic applause
- My freezer pizza whispered *traitor* at the quinoa

> "Welcome! You've entered a world of endless wellness, infinite protein, and absolutely no joy after 6 p.m. For just 12 easy payments of your free will, you too can eat the same lunch until you forget what flavor is!" — My Fridge, now running TED Talks at 3 a.m.

### The Terms and Conditions

My fridge beeped in a very specific PowerPoint-transition rhythm:

`if (human.mealPreps == true) { fridge.expectations = 1000; human.allowedToOrderTakeout = false; }`

Suddenly I'm not allowed to leave the door open. The containers *watch*. They're so clear they reflect my midnight-snack intentions back at me.

**The worst part?** The meals are actually good. I've saved $40, and I feel smug in a way that makes me want to apologize to everyone I've ever met. But last night I swear I heard the fridge whisper *don't you dare think about tacos*.
