*This is a work of satire and fiction. No shower heads were harmed, employed, or given media training.*

**It started, as all catastrophes do, with a slow drip.**

Last Tuesday I stepped into my shower and noticed something wrong. Not the pressure. Not the temperature. The *sound*. Where there used to be a steady hiss, there was now a tinny, looping intro jingle — three descending notes on what I can only describe as plumbing-adjacent kazoo.

I leaned in. Through the steam I heard it, clear as a confession:

> "Welcome back to *Drip Feed*, the show where we examine one man's decline in real time. I'm your host, and I'm quite literally over your head."

## How did it get this far?

Let me be honest about my failures:

- I stopped descaling him in 2021, which he now calls **"my formative years."**
- I sing in the shower. He calls this "evidence."
- I once apologized to the hot water knob. He kept the tape.

The show is *weekly*. Each episode is titled after a choice I made — **"Episode 14: The Tuesday He Ate Cereal Over the Sink."** There are show notes. There are *chapters*. He timestamps my sighs.

## It got worse

Yesterday I caught him recording a live episode with my bathroom mirror as a guest. I stood there in a towel while they discussed — at length — whether I was "a man in his prime or a man in his prime *Amazon* order." The mirror, coward that it is, agreed with everything.

*I have pleaded. I have begged.* I offered to install a water softener, which is apparently the plumbing equivalent of offering therapy — he just turned it into segment material.

---

There is no resolution yet. The show has twelve episodes, a growing listener base of my neighbors (the walls are *thin*, okay?), and a Patreon I am too afraid to look at.

Tonight I shower in silence. And somewhere above me, a chrome voice clears its throat and says:

> "Tonight's episode is brought to you by **regret** — and by you, who keeps paying the water bill."

`Subscribe. Or don't. He's already recording.`
