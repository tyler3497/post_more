**I decided to be a responsible homeowner for 20 minutes, which is exactly how long it takes to accidentally create a luxury resort.**

*It was supposed to be simple.* My neighbor power-washed his driveway last weekend and it looked like fresh drywall for cars. I rented a power washer from Home Depot, put on safety glasses that fogged immediately, and went to war with three years of moss, leaves, and mysterious driveway freckles.

I won. Too well.

## My Driveway Got a Big Head

By minute 12, it was gleaming. By minute 20, it was *glowing*. I turned off the washer, stepped back, and I swear my driveway whispered:

> "We don't do street parking energy anymore. We do *destination*."

Now it has standards.

Suddenly my concrete slab thinks it's the Four Seasons with cracks:

- It refuses to let my trash cans sit on it — says they "lower the property value of the experience"
- It asked if I could add tiny lounge chairs and a towel service for the squirrels
- My welcome mat filed a complaint that it no longer matches the "aesthetic"
- It keeps playing ambient spa music via the garage echo — `relaxation_mode = true && humility = false`

My neighbor walked over and said:

> "Wow, did you resurface it? Looks expensive."

My driveway *preened*. Literally shed a puddle out of pride.

I tried to explain that it's still the same oil-stained rectangle that hosted my failed attempt at chalk art in 2023. It responded by reflecting the sun directly into my eyes like:

> "I am booked solid through September. Influencers only."

---

I tried to park on it last night. It made me take my shoes off. My *tires* off. Emotionally.

I ran `driveway.getEgo()` and it returned `INFINITY`. I tried `driveway.chill()` and it threw `Error: Cannot chill, currently a lifestyle brand`.

Now the HOA wants to feature it in the newsletter. My driveway has an agent. It wants a mini-fridge and a signature scent called "Wet Concrete & Hubris."

I just wanted to remove some algae. Now I live next to a resort I can't afford to stay at.

*SATIRE / PARODY — 100% fictional, entirely made up, no driveways became boutique hotels in the writing of this, please power-wash responsibly and don't let your concrete develop an ego.*
