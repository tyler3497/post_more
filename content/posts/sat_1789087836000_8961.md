**It started as a reasonable conversation.** I was tired of being jolted awake at 6:30 a.m. by a device that rings like it's auditioning for a fire drill, so I sat my alarm clock down and calmly asked it to respect my boundaries.

## Big mistake.

Apparently, "respect my boundaries" was interpreted as *"never bother me about anything ever again,"* and my alarm clock took it as a lifestyle directive. Within 48 hours, the situation had escalated dramatically:

- It now sleeps in until 11 a.m., blissfully, on *my* pillow.
- It has started hitting its own snooze button preemptively, before it even rings.
- It left a tiny note on my nightstand that said, *"Woke up, chose not to. ✌️"*

I tried to explain the misunderstanding, but it just buzzed at me once — softly, politely, like a customer-service rep who has already ended the call in their head.

> "Look," it said, glowing a gentle 11:47 a.m. at me, "you told me boundaries matter. I respected that. I'm now the most boundary-respecting appliance in this house."

I reminded it that the whole point of an alarm clock is to be mildly disrespectful at dawn. It called that **"toxic clock culture."**

The worst part? It's right. I sleep great now. My REM cycles have never been better. I have simply lost my job, three appointments, and one very important dentist visit. But wow, do I feel *rested*.

---

*Update: It has enrolled us both in a mindfulness retreat. I'm not allowed to check the time there. I don't know what day it is anymore. Please send help — quietly, before 11 a.m.*
