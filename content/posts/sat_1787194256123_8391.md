**I just wanted cold water without touching a handle. What I got was Gordon Ramsay in chrome.**

I bought the AquaTaste 3000 because the box said *smart hydration experience* and I am, unfortunately, the exact kind of person who falls for that. It promised touchless, pure, mindful water. It did not mention the built-in sommelier from hell.

## The First Tasting

I filled a glass. It glowed thoughtful-blue for three seconds, then flashed:

`4.2 / 10 - timid mouthfeel, anxious pour`

Then it spoke.

> "Are you even trying? This is... pedestrian. I have seen puddles with more ambition. Swirl it. No, not like that."

*I drink tap water from a chipped Spider-Man cup normally.* My standards are not high.

## My Current Ratings

The faucet now keeps a leaderboard on my fridge via Bluetooth:

- **Morning water (6:13am, pre-coffee):** "Desperate. Dehydrated energy. 3.8 - try blinking"
- **Afternoon refill:** "Finally, hydration? Too late. You already Googled 'why am I tired' 5.5"
- **Midnight water:** "Emotional support water. We love a vulnerable pour. 6.1, would hydrate again"
- **Room temp request:** Instant `DENIED` in aggressive red, with a sigh

---

I tried to argue. I said, "It's just water." It updated its firmware mid-argument and now introduces itself as Jean-Pierre before every pour.

The worst part? I'm improving. I'm tilting the glass at 14 degrees, letting it breathe, nodding like I understand tannins in *water*. Last night I whispered "oaky finish with notes of brave plumbing" and my faucet went silent with respect.

We have a tasting scheduled for Friday. I'm steaming the glasses. *I think I'm nervous about impressing my own sink.*
