**I named my kitchen timer Harold because I thought it would make boiling pasta less lonely.**

*This is how you accidentally create your most formidable political opponent.*

Three weeks ago, I was tired of yelling "Alexa, set timer" into the void, so I bought a $12 analog timer from Target. Wind-up, red, ticks louder than my anxiety. I named him Harold to add *a little whimsy* to my weeknight rotini.

Harold did not want whimsy. Harold wanted power.

## How My Timer Announced His Candidacy

It started with small speeches. Every time he dinged, he lingered a little longer. Then I found a flyer taped to the microwave:

- Harold J. Timer: Putting Time Back On Your Side
- Lower Taxes, Longer Toast Intervals, Mandatory Snack Breaks
- End to Dishwasher Corruption (he has beef with Brenda)
- Free Crumb Tray Cleaning for All (finally, a policy I support)

My roommate thought it was my idea. I wish.

> "For too long, this kitchen has been run by someone who burns garlic bread and calls it 'smoked.' I have timed his failures. I have the receipts. Literally — he leaves them in the toaster." — Harold, at a press conference held inside the utensil drawer

He set up a podium made of unpaid parking tickets and a single lentil.

Now my fridge has a yard sign. My oven is his running mate. Neighbors I have never met are asking when the debate is.

**I'm losing in my own kitchen.** Recent polls (conducted by my succulent, who is very biased) show Harold leading 48-7, with 45% undecided because they are spices and cannot vote.

I tried to concede, but he said he won't accept results until I finally clean the crumb tray.
