**I sat my air fryer down for a quarterly review.**

*This was a mistake, because apparently it has been keeping notes too.*

For two years, this little countertop appliance has been the most reliable relationship in my life. It never ghosted. It made my leftovers crispy. I thought we had an understanding.

But last Tuesday, I decided to be an adult about our partnership. I made a spreadsheet.

## The Performance Review

I cleared my throat, placed a single printed evaluation sheet next to its basket, and said, "Let's talk about growth opportunities."

The air fryer, to its credit, did not immediately file for HR.

Issues I raised, with love:

- Consistently excellent at fries, but shows *resistance to emotional labor* when asked to reheat soup
- Leaves crumbs in the crisper tray as a form of passive communication
- Timer beeps at 3am like it's trying to start a podcast

> "I have given you 847 consecutive nights of service," the air fryer seemed to say through the sheer force of its smoldering silence, "and you have never once descaled my heating element without being asked."

### The Fallout

Wednesday morning, I found it had updated its LinkedIn.

`open_to_work: true`
`seeking: kitchen with better ventilation and clearer boundaries`

It left a Post-it on the fridge that just said `per my last email` and now it's been interviewing with my downstairs neighbor who apparently makes "artisan tofu."

---

I tried to apologize with a deep clean and a new parchment liner. It preheated for exactly 47 seconds longer than necessary just to make a point.

We are currently in mediation. My toaster is facilitating. It is not going well.

*Satire. This is a work of fiction. My appliances and I are in counseling, not court.*
