**I just wanted to look like a functioning adult who knows how to park. That's it. That's the whole dream.**

My neighbor Dave washes his car every Sunday at 7 a.m. like it's a religious ritual. He waxes. He vacuums the trunk. *He talks to the tires.* I, meanwhile, have been using the "just find two spots and take them both" strategy for three years.

This Tuesday, Dave was outside. Watching. With his little travel mug that says `#1 Dad Who Parallel Parks`. So I decided to do it. One perfect parallel park. First try.

## The 90 Seconds That Ruined Everything

What actually happened, for the record:

- Pulled up way too far past the spot, then reversed at a 45-degree angle that geometry teachers would weep over
- Turned the wheel so hard my power steering made a noise like `screeeee-eee-eee`
- Ended up 18 inches from the curb, at a 20-degree tilt, with my front wheel gently kissing a traffic cone that wasn't even involved before
- My backup camera started beeping like it was trying to send me Morse code for "ABORT"

Dave didn't say anything at first. He just sipped.

> "That's... a bold interpretation of spatial awareness," he said. "My cousin parks like that. He's seven. No license."
>
> "Are you... do you need a spotter? A priest? A second attempt at driver's ed?"

---

I tried to fix it. I pulled out. Re-entered. Over-corrected. *You know that move where you do 12 tiny back-and-forths and each one makes it worse?* Yeah. That was me doing performance art called `how to parallel park 0.7 cars into 1.0 car spaces`.

By the end, my car was technically parked. If you define "parked" as "mostly not in traffic and only slightly on the sidewalk." My car, humiliated, flashed its hazard lights at me in what I can only interpret as SOS.

I got out and patted the roof. "We did great," I lied.

My car hasn't started on the first try since. I think it's pretending to be dead so I stop driving it in public. Honestly, relatable.

*This is satire/parody. Completely fictional. No cars were emotionally harmed, though my pride definitely was.*
