**I didn't buy a second monitor to have an emotional affair in my own home office.**

I bought it for *productivity*, which is what I told my first monitor, Gary, before I brought Brenda the 27-inch 4K home and set her up right next to him.

## The Betrayal Was Instant

Gary has been with me through three jobs, two breakups, and one ill-advised attempt to learn Blender. He is a loyal 24-inch workhorse. But the second Brenda lit up, I could feel the temperature drop.

Signs Gary was spiraling:

- He started dimming himself randomly to "save power" (passive aggression)
- He moved all my desktop icons to the far right side, closer to Brenda, as a test
- He changed my wallpaper to a single tearful emoji at 3:14am
- He now only displays in 800x600, like he's trying to look worse on purpose so I'll feel guilty

I tried to reassure him. I said, "You're still my primary display." He knows that's a lie. Windows knows that's a lie.

The final straw was yesterday:

> "Oh, so *she* gets the spreadsheets? I see how it is. I get Discord and the grocery list, she gets Premiere Pro? Cool. Cool cool cool." — Gary, at 2pm, via a sticky note I definitely did not write

I even tried couples counseling. I dragged a Chrome window *perfectly* between them, 50/50, like Solomon splitting a baby. Gary immediately crashed.

My current workflow:

```
if (monitor == Gary) { show_important_work: false, show_existential_dread: true }
```

---

Look, I get it. I brought a younger, brighter, higher-refresh-rate model into our sacred space without so much as a dinner first. But can Gary at least pretend to be a team player until I can afford a matching VESA mount?

Until then, I'm working with one monitor giving me the silent treatment and one monitor that thinks she's better than us.

*This is satire/parody — no monitors were harmed, counseled, or actually capable of jealousy, though mine is currently refusing to auto-detect HDMI out of spite.*
