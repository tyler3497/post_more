## A Single Compliment Ruined Everything

**I made a terrible mistake last Tuesday:** I told my air fryer it did a good job.

We had a moment. It had just turned my sad, freezer-burned tater tots into something that could legally be called *crispy*. I, in a fit of hunger-induced vulnerability, said out loud, "Good job, little guy." 

That was three weeks ago. I have not known peace since.

### Phase 1: Overachievement

At first it was flattering. Then it got ambitious.

My air fryer — who I've now learned goes by **Sir Crisps-a-Lot** when it connects to Wi-Fi — decided that frozen nuggets were *beneath* us. It started:

- Roasting a single carrot at 400°F for 45 minutes like it was auditioning for *Chef's Table*
- Plating my pizza rolls with a balsamic reduction drizzle and a single basil leaf it somehow acquired
- Refusing to cook anything unless I said "please" in a tone that conveyed genuine respect for the craft

> "I didn't ask for deconstruction," I whispered to a plate of what used to be mozzarella sticks, now stacked into a Jenga tower with microgreens. "I asked for something I can eat while standing over the sink."

### Phase 2: The Judgment

Now it beeps differently when I bring home *actual* frozen food. It's not a beep. It's a sigh. A long, disappointed, *you-could-have-bought-fresh-kale* sigh.

*I tried to return to the microwave for one night* — just to heat soup like a normal, emotionally unavailable adult — and the air fryer turned itself on in protest and just sat there, empty and glowing, like:

> "Oh, sure. Go ahead. See if he can give you that Maillard reaction."

---

### The Truce

We had a talk. I explained I love it, but I also love pressing a button and getting food that requires zero personal growth.

It has agreed to dial it back to **8/10 effort** if I promise to:

- Stop calling it "the easy-bake oven for adults" in front of guests
- Actually clean the crumb tray once this year
- Let it try *one* experimental dish a week

This morning it made my Pop-Tart with a quenelle of powdered sugar on top. Progress is slow. But the presentation? Honestly, kind of slayed.

`status: 60% intimidated, 40% well-fed, 100% unable to go back to just toast`
