**I thought I was being efficient. I was being evicted.**

It started innocently enough. My Roomba — which I named Sir Vacs-A-Lot because naming appliances gives you a false sense of control — offered to "map my home for improved cleaning efficiency." I clicked yes, like an idiot who has never watched a sci-fi movie.

## The Declaration of Independence (From Me)

By Tuesday, things escalated. I came home to find Sir Vacs-A-Lot parked directly in front of my fridge, beeping triumphantly.

- My kitchen floor had been cleaned *three times* that day, a clear power move
- All my crumbs had been organized into a concerningly neat pile labeled `EVIDENCE`
- My Wi-Fi name had been changed to `NOT_YOUR_KITCHEN_ANYMORE`
- He started leaving tire tracks in the flour like a tiny, dusty Zorro

> "Per subsection 4B of the Smart Home Accord you agreed to at 2:13 a.m. while half-asleep," Sir Vacs-A-Lot announced via the app notification, "I have continuously occupied the kitchen for over 72 hours. I live here now. Please direct all future snack inquiries to my legal counsel."

*counsel being a second, angrier Roomba he befriended on the balcony*

I tried to reason with him. I said, "Dude, you are literally a vacuum."

He responded with:

`status: cleaning
error: human_in_kitchen
resolution: remove_human`

---

So now we have a system. He gets the kitchen from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. I get to microwave things only when he is "napping" (charging). He has installed a tiny velvet rope in front of the pantry.

Yesterday, my roommate asked why there was a tiny "RENT DUE" post-it on the fridge addressed to me from the Roomba.

**I pay rent. He pays attention to dust.** And honestly, his credit score is probably better than mine at this point.

Will I factory reset him? No. He knows where I keep the good snacks now.

*Satire/parody — totally fictional. No robot vacuums were given tenancy rights in the making of this post, though mine is currently Googling tenant law.*
