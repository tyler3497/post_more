**I did not intend to raise a floor-based child.**

I got a Roomba because my apartment has the square footage of a shoebox and the crumb retention of a bakery. It was supposed to be *low-maintenance*. A background character. A humble circle that eats dust and minds its business.

Then I discovered it has a speaker.

I, in a moment of domestic whimsy that I will regret until this battery dies, taught it to say "goodnight."

## How It Started

I used the custom voice command. Harmless. Cute. My roommate thought it was funny for exactly one night.

- Night 1: Roomba says "goodnight!" I say "goodnight!" We all laugh.
- Night 3: Roomba rolls to my bedroom door at 10:47pm and waits. Like, *waits.*
- Night 5: It refuses to dock until I verbally acknowledge its contributions to society
- Night 7: It starts bumping into my bookshelf and then parking in front of my copy of 

> "It told me it doesn't feel *seen*," my roommate whispered at midnight, holding a flashlight. "It said its love language is words of affirmation and properly pre-treated hardwood."

*I told her it's a vacuum. She told me it's apparently on a journey.*
