**I started leaving voice notes for my future self to be more organized and now future me has hired a publicist to avoid me.**

*In retrospect, recording a 3-minute memo at 11:47 p.m. titled "idea - important - don't forget (final) (3).m4a" was not the systems upgrade I thought it was.*

I was trying to be proactive. You know, personal growth, time management, that whole Instagram carousel lie. So I opened Voice Memos and said "Hey future Tyler-adjacent entity, tomorrow we will wake up early, drink water, and not buy another houseplant we will emotionally neglect."

Future me did not respond. For two days.

Then the read receipts turned blue.

## My Future Self Has Boundaries Now

On Tuesday I left a classic: "Remember to call dentist, cancel sock subscription, figure out 401k."

Wednesday morning I got a notification. Not from Future Me. From *his assistant*.

> "Thank you for your continued interest in Future You. At this time he is not taking unsolicited ideas, especially those recorded while chewing cereal. Please direct all inquiries to his wellness team."
> — Future Me's executive assistant, who sounds suspiciously like me doing a fake British accent

I tried to reconcile. I left an apology note: "Sorry for the 12-part series on whether we should get bangs."

No reply. Just a status update on my calendar that Future Me blocked as "Busy (recovering from Past Me)."

### Evidence That This Has Gone Too Far:

- My reminders app now auto-corrects "future me" to "estranged associate"
- I found a vision board Future Me made that just says `NO NEW PROJECTS` in 72pt font
- He left *me* a voice note that was 4 seconds of sighing and then "we talked about this"
- My notes app started asking "are you sure you want to send this to him?"
- My phone now groups his messages under "Maybe: Future You"

---

Look, I get it. I was a lot. But I was trying to help.

I have now implemented a formal communication policy: one voice note per week, max 30 seconds, no business ideas after 9 p.m.

Future Me replied with a thumbs-up emoji. Progress. We're healing.

*This is satire. I do not have a time-traveling feud with myself. My dentist, however, is still waiting for that call.*
