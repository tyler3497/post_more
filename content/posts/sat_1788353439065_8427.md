**I thought signing up for sunrise yoga would make me a morning person. Instead, I ruined a perfectly good relationship with a rectangle of rubber.**

*To be fair, I have never been good at commitment, especially before coffee.*

Three weeks ago I decided to fix my life by booking a 6 AM vinyasa class. $22, an instructor named Serenity who actually owns that name legally, and a promise that I would become Flexible, Grounded, and Emotionally Stable by Week Two.

I bought the mat. Lavender. Non-slip. Ethically sourced optimism.

Day one went fine. Day two, I hit snooze twice. Day three, I told my mat I needed space.

## The Breakup

By Day five, my mat started sending signals.

> "Oh, you're awake at 9:30 now? That's cool. That's really cool. I was just here, rolled up, thinking about our future in a landfill."

It wasn't wrong. *I had started doing YouTube yoga in socks on the carpet like a monster.*

Reasons my yoga mat is officially ghosting me:

- I used it as a plate for toast crumbs during a "mindful eating exercise" I invented
- I spilled pre-workout on it and said "it's basically a blessing"
- I left it in the trunk for four days during a heatwave and it now has trust issues and mild warping
- I told Serenity I was "listening to my body" which is code for *my body said no*

I tried to reconcile. I whispered:

`const intention = setIntention("show up");`

My mat returned `undefined`.

---

## My New Wellness Plan

Now it lives in the closet, facing the wall. It refuses to unroll unless I play whale sounds and apologize for my entire personality.

Serenity texted me: "missed you today, where did you flow to?" I told her I was on a spiritual journey to the snooze button.

My roommate said if I skip one more class, the mat gets legally adopted by his girlfriend who actually does planks.

*Satire / Parody: This is 100% fictional. My mat is rubber. I am just dramatic before 7 AM.*
