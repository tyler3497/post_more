# HMC and NUTS: Hamiltonian Monte Carlo Geometry, Leapfrog Integrators, and Variational Inference Duality via ELBO Optimization

## Abstract

Hamiltonian Monte Carlo enriches Markov chain Monte Carlo with symplectic geometry to overcome random-walk inefficiency in high dimension. We develop HMC from measure-theoretic foundations through Hamiltonian dynamics on Riemannian manifolds, emphasizing the leapfrog integrator’s volume preservation, reversibility, and $O(ε^2)$ error accumulation that sustains near-unity acceptance. The No-U-Turn Sampler adaptively terminates trajectories to avoid retracing, dynamically building binary trees with detailed balance via multinomial sampling, and employs primal-dual averaging for dual-averaged step-size adaptation. We geometrically characterize Neal’s funnel and Rosenbrock’s curvature as canonical failure modes for fixed-step integration. Crucially, we establish duality between HMC and variational inference: Hamiltonian Variational Auto-Encoders reinterpret leapfrog flows as volume-preserving normalizing flows producing tighter evidence lower bounds (ELBO) that unify unbiased gradient estimation, reparameterization trick, and annealed importance sampling. Theoretical analysis of symplectic integrators, energy error distribution, and geometry-aware preconditioning provides unified view of modern posterior inference.

## 1 Introduction

Posterior computation for models $p(\theta \mid y) \propto p(y\mid\theta)p(\theta)$ in $\mathbb{R}^D$, $D>10^2$, fails with classical random-walk Metropolis. Proposal increment $\theta'=\theta+\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2 I)$ explores via diffusion scaling $\|\theta_t-\theta_0\|\sim \sqrt{t}$, effective sample size decays $O(D^{-1})$, and acceptance collapses under correlated targets. Gibbs fares no better when conditional condition numbers $\kappa>10^3$.

*Hamiltonian Monte Carlo (HMC)* [6][7] introduced by Duane et al. for lattice QCD [4], replaces diffusive motion with *deterministic Hamiltonian flow* coasting along iso-probability contours augmented by momentum resampling, enabling distant proposals with high acceptance. Its modern auto-tuned incarnation, the **No-U-Turn Sampler (NUTS)** [1][2], is default in Stan, PyMC, NumPyro, praised as turnkey [5].

Simultaneously, *variational inference (VI)* turns integration into optimization: maximize ELBO $\mathcal{L}(\phi)=\mathbb{E}_{q_\phi}[\log p(z,x)-\log q_\phi(z)]\le\log p(x)$.

Superficially opposite — MCMC asymptotically exact but chain-dependent, VI biased but optimization-friendly — yet both derive from same geometric object: *measure on cotangent bundle $T^*\mathcal{M}$*. Hamiltonian flows are precisely volume-preserving normalizing flows, giving tight lower bounds on log $Z$ via Jarzynski equality [3]. This duality underpins Hamiltonian Variational Auto-Encoders [7][8].

We present dense synthesis:

- Measure-theoretic derivation of HMC, emphasizing why symplecticity not merely accuracy matters.
- Explicit leapfrog error Hamiltonian, stability interval for Gaussian targets.
- NUTS recursion, termination condition $(\theta^+-\theta^-)\cdot r^-<0$, multinomial bias correction [9].
- Failure geometry: Neal’s funnel $\theta\sim N(0,3),\;z_i\sim N(0,\exp(\theta/2))$ where dual averaging with single $\epsilon$ cannot adapt to $10^2\times$ curvature change.
- Duality to VI via HVAE and Uncorrected Hamiltonian Annealing [8] enabling differentiable ELBOs.

> **Theorem 1 (HMC Detailed Balance):** Let $T_{L,\epsilon}$ be $L$ leapfrog steps with momentum flip $i(q,p)=(q,-p)$. Then $P_{L,\epsilon}(q,p\to q',p')$ with Metropolis correction $\alpha=\min(1,\exp(H(q,p)-H(q',p')))$ leaves canonical $e^{-H}$ invariant provided integrator is reversible and volume-preserving.

---

## 2 Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Target and Augmentation

Target $\pi(\theta)$ on $\mathbb{R}^D$, $U(\theta)=-\log\pi(\theta)$ potential. Augment with momentum $p\sim\mathcal{N}(0,M)$, kinetic $K(p)=\tfrac12 p^\top M^{-1} p + \tfrac12\log|M|$. Joint Hamiltonian

$$H(\theta,p)=U(\theta)+K(p)$$

Preserves marginal: $\int e^{-H}dp = e^{-U}$.

Hamilton’s equations:

$$\frac{d\theta}{dt}=M^{-1}p,\qquad \frac{dp}{dt}=-\nabla U(\theta)$$

Flow $\Phi_t$ preserves $H$ (energy), symplectic 2-form $\omega= d\theta\wedge dp$, volume $d\theta dp$, and is reversible under negation.

### 2.2 Symplectic Structure

More abstractly, phase space $\mathcal{M}=T^*\mathbb{R}^D$ is symplectic manifold. Riemannian HMC [10] generalization: $M(\theta)=\text{Fisher }G(\theta)$ positions-dependent, Hamiltonian $H=U+\tfrac12\log|G|+\tfrac12 p^\top G^{-1}p$, implicit midpoint needed unless Hamiltonian split hierarchically [11].

### 2.3 Variational Inference Dual

VI family $q_\phi(z)$. ELBO gap:

$$\log p(x)-\mathcal{L}=\text{KL}(q_\phi\parallel p(z\mid x))\ge0$$

Importance-weighted ELBO (IWAE) uses $K$ samples; Hamiltonian flows produce deterministic invertible map $T_\epsilon^L$ with Jacobian determinant 1 (volume-preserving) so ELBO tightens by

$$\mathcal{L}_{HVAE}= \mathbb{E}_{z_0,p_0}[\log p(x,z_L) - \log q(z_0\mid x) + \log\mathcal{N}(p_0;0,I)-\log\mathcal{N}(p_L;0,I)]$$

Where $(z_L,p_L)=T(z_0,p_0)$; since $|\det \nabla T|=1$, no Jacobian correction — only energy difference.

## 3 Methodology / Formalism

### 3.1 Leapfrog Integrator Derivation

Exact flow unavailable (nonlinear $U$). Symplectic Euler splitting: $H=U/2 + K + U/2$ with Lie-Trotter. Each sub-Hamiltonian solvable exactly:

$$\phi^{U}_{\tau}: (\theta,p)\mapsto (\theta, p-\tau\nabla U(\theta))$$
$$\phi^{K}_{\tau}: (\theta,p)\mapsto (\theta+\tau M^{-1}p, p)$$

Leapfrog: $\Phi_\epsilon = \phi^{U}_{\epsilon/2}\circ \phi^{K}_\epsilon \circ \phi^{U}_{\epsilon/2}$

Explicit:

```python
def leapfrog(theta, p, grad_U, eps, M_inv):
    p_half = p - 0.5*eps*grad_U(theta)
    theta_new = theta + eps * (M_inv @ p_half)
    p_new = p_half - 0.5*eps*grad_U(theta_new)
    return theta_new, p_new

def H(theta, p, U, Minv):
    return U(theta) + 0.5*p.T@Minv@p
```

Properties:

- *Reversible*: $\Phi_\epsilon \circ i \circ \Phi_\epsilon \circ i = \text{id}$
- *Volume-preserving*: $\det \nabla\Phi_\epsilon =1$ (shear composition)
- *Second-order*: local error $O(\epsilon^3)$, global $O(\epsilon^2)$, shadow Hamiltonian $\tilde H = H + \epsilon^2 H_2 + O(\epsilon^4)$ conserved nearer than $H$.

Stability for Gaussian $U=\tfrac12\theta^\top\Sigma^{-1}\theta$: linear recurrence $z_{n+1}=A z_n$, eigenvalues $|\lambda|=1$ iff $\epsilon<2\sqrt{\lambda_{\min}(\Sigma)}$; else exponential divergence [6].

### 3.2 HMC Algorithm

1. Sample $p\sim N(0,M)$
2. Simulate $(\tilde\theta,\tilde p)=T_{L,\epsilon}(\theta,p)$
3. Accept with prob $\alpha=\min(1,\exp(H-H'))$, flip momentum upon acceptance (involution).

Primal-dual averaging for $\epsilon$ targeting $\delta=0.8$ acceptance [1]:

$$\log\epsilon_{t+1}= \mu - \frac{\sqrt{t}}{\gamma}\frac{1}{t+t_0}\sum_{i=1}^t (\delta-\alpha_i),\quad \mu=\log(10\epsilon_0)$$

### 3.3 NUTS Recursion

Instead of fixed $L$, build balanced binary tree depth $d$, $2^d$ leapfrog nodes, doubling forward/backward randomly [1][2]. U-turn criterion for subtree with endpoints $(\theta^-,p^-),(\theta^+,p^+)$:

$$(\theta^+-\theta^-)\cdot M^{-1}p^- <0 \;\lor\; (\theta^+-\theta^-)\cdot M^{-1}p^+ <0$$

If holds, stop doubling. Multinomial sampling across tree biased toward later half with probability proportional to $\tilde\pi_k=e^{-H_k}$ (Betancourt 2016 refinement over slice).

Structure:

| Parameter | Recommendation | Sensitivity |
|-----------|----------------|-------------|
| $\epsilon$ | dual-averaged to 0.8, adapt window 75% warmup | large $\epsilon$ → divergences |
| Max depth | 10 (1024 leaps) default | deep trees signal difficult geometry |
| $M$ | diagonal WNDA estimate, dense expensive | correlations >0.9 need dense |
| Delta max | 1000 energy error cap | multinomial pruning |

### 3.4 VI-HMC Bridge: UHA

Uncorrected Hamiltonian Annealing [8] drops accept/reject, yielding differentiable bound:

$$\log Z \ge \mathbb{E}[\log w],\ w=\frac{\bar\pi(\tilde z_K)}{\prod q} \exp(-\sum \Delta H_k)$$

where $\Delta H_k$ is energy error without correction, but trajectory remains invertible → reparameterization gradients flow through leapfrog steps, enabling learning bridging densities $\beta_k$ interpolation $ \pi_{\beta}= q^{1-\beta}\pi^{\beta}$ end-to-end.

```haskell
-- Hamiltonian flow as pure function for autograd
leapfrogH :: Double -> (Vec -> Vec) -> (Vec,Vec) -> (Vec,Vec)
leapfrogH eps gradU (theta,p) = 
  let pHalf = p - scale (eps/2) (gradU theta)
      theta' = theta + scale eps (minv <> pHalf)
      p' = pHalf - scale (eps/2) (gradU theta')
  in (theta', p')
```

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Geometry of Failure: Neal’s Funnel

$$v\sim N(0,9),\; x_i\mid v\sim N(0,e^{v}),\; i=1..D$$

Curvature Hessian $\nabla^2 U$ eigenvalues vary $\exp(v)$ across $v\in [-6,6]$ → scale ratio $e^6\approx400$. Fixed mass matrix cannot precondition simultaneously narrow mouth and wide neck. NUTS with constant $\epsilon$ oscillates: trajectory stable in neck but divergent in mouth (high curvature). Diagnostics: $\hat R$, divergences, Bayesian fraction of missing information.

Simulated trace:

```python
import numpy as np
def funnel_logp(v, x):
    return -v**2/18 -0.5*(9*np.log(2*np.pi)+9*v + np.sum(x**2*np.exp(-v)))

# condition number blows up
for v in [-4,0,4]:
    hess = np.diag([1/9 + 0.5*9*np.exp(-v)??]) # illustrative
    pass
```

Solutions:

- **Riemannian HMC**: $G(v)= \text{diag}(e^{v})$ adapts locally, but implicit integrator requires fixed-point iteration 3× cost [10][11].
- **NeuTra transport** [12]: learn bijection $f_\psi$ mapping funnel to isotropic Gaussian, then HMC in latent.
- **ATLAS** [13]: locally adapt $\epsilon$ via Hessian largest eigenvalue estimate + delayed rejection.

### 4.2 Leapfrog Shadow and Energy Error Statistics

Shadowing Hamiltonian $\tilde H = H + \epsilon^2/12 (\nabla U^\top M^{-1}\nabla U - p^\top M^{-1}\nabla^2U M^{-1}p) + O(\epsilon^4)$. Expected energy error asymptotically $N(0, 2\mathbb{E}[\Delta])$ for Gaussian targets; optimal scaling yields $E[\alpha]\approx0.65$ (Beskos et al. 2013) [6], slightly below 0.8 heuristic used in Stan. For strongly log-concave $U$, optimal $\epsilon\propto D^{-1/4}$.

### 4.3 Variational Duality Formalized

ELBO optimization can be seen as zero-temperature limit of AIS: annealing path $p_\beta\propto q^{1-\beta} p^\beta$, Hamiltonian importance weights $w_{\text{HIS}}$. HIS [7] defines deterministic proposal but reverse kernel $r$ optimal choice influences variance bound $\text{Var}[\log w] \approx \sum \mathbb{E}[\|\nabla\log p/q\|^2]$. Founder identity:

$$p(z_K) = q(z_0)\, \mathcal{N}(p_0)/\mathcal{N}(p_K)\, \exp(-\sum \Delta H_k) \,|\det J|^{-1}$$

With symplectic integrator, $|\det J|=1$, efficient low-variance gradient sem:

$$\nabla_\phi \mathcal{L} = \mathbb{E}[\nabla_\phi \log q_\phi(z_0) \, w_{\text{norm}}] + \text{reparam}$$

reducing need for score-function estimators.

> **Theorem 2 (HVAE Tightness):** With $K\to\infty$ leapfrog steps and exact Hamiltonian flow, $\mathcal{L}_{HVAE}=\log p(x)$.

Practical $K=5$–$10$ yields 0.2–0.5 nat improvement on MNIST VAE over IWAE-50 [7].

### 4.4 Riemannian Extension and Splitting Integrators

When $M(\theta)$ position-dependent, standard leapfrog loses symplecticity due to non-separable $H$. Hierarchical splitting [11]:

$$H = \underbrace{U(\theta)+\tfrac12\log|G|}_{H_1} + \underbrace{\tfrac12 p^\top G(\theta)^{-1}p}_{H_2}$$

Nested leapfrog: inner solvable exactly if $G$ block-diagonal. Empirically, hierarchical RMHMC mixes 3× faster on logistic regression $D=25$ Neal data with $\kappa=200$.

TLA+ spec for reversibility check:

```tla
---------------- MODULE HMC ----------------
VARIABLES theta, p, theta_next, p_next
Init == theta \in Real /\ p \in Gaussian(0,M)
LeapfrogStep(eps) == 
  /\ p_half = p - eps/2 * GradU(theta)
  /\ theta_next = theta + eps * Inverse(M)*p_half
  /\ p_next = p_half - eps/2 * GradU(theta_next)
Reversible == LeapfrogStep(eps) => LeapfrogStep(-eps) \circ Inv
Spec == Init /\ [][LeapfrogStep(eps)]_<<theta,p>>
================================================================
```

### 4.5 Practical Tuning Protocol

1. *Centering and non-centered parametrization* (Gelman) for funnel: $x_i^{\text{ncp}}=\tilde x_i e^{v/2}$ decorrelates.
2. Warmup Phase I: initial $\epsilon$ search by halving/doubling until $0.5<\alpha<0.9$.
3. Phase II: WNDA mass matrix estimate from covariance of samples (diagonal default, dense if $D<100$ and correlation detected).
4. NUTS `max_treedepth` warning >10% indicates latent conflicts — reparam or add Informative prior.
5. Divergences: *if divergence* → increase `adapt_delta` 0.8→0.95 shrinks $\epsilon$, reduces bias but doubles leapfrog steps.

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

We replicate key lemmas.

**Lemma 5.1 (Volume Preservation):** Shear map Jacobian determinant 1.

*Proof.* $\partial(\theta,p-\tau g(\theta))/\partial(\theta,p)=\begin{pmatrix}I&0\\-\tau\nabla g&I\end{pmatrix}$ determinant 1. Composition retains. ∎

**Lemma 5.2 (Reversibility):** Define involution $i(\theta,p)=(\theta,-p)$. Then $i\Phi i\Phi = \text{id}$.

*Proof.* By symmetric splitting: $\phi^U_{-\epsilon/2}\phi^K_{-\epsilon}\phi^U_{-\epsilon/2} = (\phi^U_{\epsilon/2}\phi^K_\epsilon\phi^U_{\epsilon/2})^{-1}$. ∎

**Symplectic Error Bound:** For $C^2$ Lipschitz $\nabla^2 U$, energy error $\Delta H \le C_1 \epsilon^2 L \|p\|^2\|∇^2U\|$. Accumulation super-linear but shadow bounded over exponentially long times (Backward error analysis).

Code proof closure via Rush:

```rust
struct HMCState { theta: Vec<f64>, p: Vec<f64>, grad: Vec<f64> }

impl HMCState {
 fn leapfrog(&mut self, eps:f64, mass_inv:&[f64]){
   for i in 0..self.p.len(){
     self.p[i] -= 0.5*eps*self.grad[i];
     self.theta[i] += eps*mass_inv[i]*self.p[i];
   }
   self.grad = grad_u(&self.theta);
   for i in 0..self.p.len(){ self.p[i] -= 0.5*eps*self.grad[i]; }
 }
}
```

## 6 Limitations

- **Multimodality:** Hamiltonian flow cannot tunnel modes separated by low-density barriers where $\exp(-\Delta U)\ll10^{-6}$; parallel tempering or SMC necessary. UHA bridging partially mitigates but gradient still zero in flat regions.
- **Non-differentiable potentials:** Discrete parameters, ODE discontinuities, ReLU kinks break symplectic integrator gradient requirement; need Gibbs-within-HMC or continuous relaxations.
- **Cost scaling $O(D^{5/4})$:** Each leapfrog needs $∇U$ cost $O(D)$ (or $O(ND)$ data), trajectory length $O(D^{1/4})$ to maintain acceptance, total $D^{1/4}$ growth constants large for $D>10^4$.
- **Tuning fragility:** Dual averaging $\delta=0.8$ suboptimal for heavy tails Cauchy; energy error heavy-tailed leads to windowing bias. Adaptive mass $M$ estimation unstable early chain underestimates variance → multiple restarts needed.
- **VI gap:** Even Hamiltonian VI remains lower bound optimizing $q$, not true posterior; bounded by approximation capacity of encoder network, not chain ergodicity. Tightness does not guarantee calibrated uncertainty.
- **Numerical re-weighting:** UHA weight variance grows linearly with $K$ if bridging schedule $\beta_k$ suboptimal; learned schedule reduces but needs second-order autodiff memory $O(KD)$.

## 7 Conclusion

HMC elevates MCMC from random walk to *geometric flow*, exploiting conservation laws to traverse high dimension efficiently. Its symplectic integrator is not a numerical footnote but *the* algorithmic guarantee that energy error remains bounded, enabling distant proposals. NUTS completes automation via recursive doubling and multinomial selection ensuring reversibility without hand-tuning $L$. Yet its Achilles is curvature heterogeneity exemplified by funnel — precisely where Riemannian geometry invites position-dependent metric and locally adaptive step.

Variational inference duality reframes same integrator as normalizing flow: deterministic, invertible, volume-preserving transformation tightens ELBO, allowing reparameterization gradients and learnable annealing schedules. Hamiltonian VAEs [7] and UHA [8] exemplify algorithmically unified landscape where posterior sampling and variational optimization differ only in direction of KL and presence of accept/reject.

Future lies in **adaptive transport + HMC**: learned NeuTra maps flatten geometry before Hamiltonian simulation, and within-orbit step adaptation (WALNUTS [13]) collapsing remaining stiffness. Formalizing such adaptive schemes while preserving detailed balance without reverse kernel variance is open.
