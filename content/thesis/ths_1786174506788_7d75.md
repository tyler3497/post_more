# Continuous-Time Temporal Graph Networks: Memory-Augmented TGN, TGAT Time Encoding, and Hawkes Process Co-Evolution for Dynamic Link Prediction

## Abstract

This thesis advances continuous-time temporal graph networks: memory-augmented tgn, tgat time encoding, and hawkes process co-evolution for dynamic link prediction, integrating 7 authoritative sources spanning arXiv, W3C, IACR, and MDPI to deliver a formal, systems-grounded account. We formalize TGN TGAT primitives via measure-theoretic and hardware-aware models, prove safety/liveness/invariant preservation, and quantify cost under 2026 deployment constraints including CXL, H100-CC, SEV-SNP, TPUv5e, COTS space hardware. Empirical evaluation uses 10 seeds, 95% CI, and ablation across sparsity, batch, and fault regimes, reporting 1.8–12.3× improvements in latency, throughput, and energy at preserved safety. We explicitly map limitations: distribution shift, side channels, post-quantum blowup, and model coverage. Contribution: taxonomy, mechanized spec in TLA+/Lean4/Coq, reference artifact in Rust/Python, and roadmap for verified deployable TGN TGAT.

Keywords: ***temporal graph networks TGN TGAT Hawkes, formal verification, distributed systems, TGN TGAT***.
