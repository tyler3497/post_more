# Fully Homomorphic Encryption Bootstrapping Optimizations: CKKS Modulus Switching, TFHE Blind Rotation, and Hardware Acceleration on GPU/ASIC

## Abstract
Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE) permits arbitrary computation on encrypted data without decryption, yet noise accumulation limits multiplicative depth to a small modulus chain. *Bootstrapping*, introduced by Gentry [Gen09], refreshes ciphertexts to enable unlimited depth at extreme computational cost, often >90\% of total runtime in CKKS, TFHE, and FHEW deployments [1][6]. This thesis unifies three optimization frontiers driving recent breakthroughs: **CKKS approximate bootstrapping** via **modulus switching**, **CoeffToSlot/SlotToCoeff** linear transforms, and optimal **sine-cosine EvalMod** approximations with *lazy baby-step giant-step (BSGS)* evaluation reducing depth from 12 to 10 while achieving 93-bit precision; **TFHE blind rotation** through AP, GINX, and NTRU-based GSW-like techniques that decouple key distribution from evaluation key size, shrinking keys from 109 MB to 18.65 MB and latency from 10.5 ms to 6.4 ms [2][3]; and **hardware acceleration** of NTT, automorphism, and key-switching on **GPUs** and **ASICs** demonstrating 371.86x speedup on RTX 4090 vs 48-thread CPU and first <10 ms amortized bootstrapping on GPUs. We formalize error variance models, prove depth-optimality lemmas for EvalMod, and synthesize microarchitectural strategies including hybrid four-step NTT, fixed-point FFT with 50\% trimmed precision, and multi-block blind rotation partitioning.

## 1 Intro

Since Gentry's 2009 construction, leveled FHE schemes avoid bootstrapping until necessary by consuming multiplicative levels, but deep learning inference (ResNet-20 requires ~20-30 levels) forces frequent refreshes [4][5]. In **CKKS**, invented by Cheon-Kim-Kim-Song in ASIACRYPT 2017 [1], ciphertexts encrypt approximate real vectors $\mathbf{z} \in \mathbb{C}^{N/2}$ with scale $\Delta$, each multiplication followed by **Rescale** dividing by $q_l$ to manage scale, reducing level $l \to l-1$. Once $l=0$, bootstrapping is mandatory. In **TFHE** [CGGI16][2], every binary gate triggers bootstrapping refreshing LWE ciphertexts of bits via programmable bootstrapping, making *gate bootstrapping* cost critical.

> Theorem: *Bootstrapping Universality.* For any RLWE-based FHE scheme with decryption circuit $D_s(ct)=\langle s, ct \rangle \bmod q$, there exists a homomorphic circuit $Boot$ computing $Dec_{/\cdot}$ followed by re-encryption such that $Dec(Boot(ct))=Dec(ct)$ but $noise(Boot(ct)) \ll noise(ct)$. The existence proof gives no bound on depth; optimality requires scheme-specific approximations.

This thesis contributes:

- **A taxonomy** of CKKS bootstrapping: ModRaise $q_0 \to Q_L$, CoeffToSlot via homomorphic DFT, EvalMod via direct minimax polynomial vs sine-cosine composition, SlotToCoeff, with depth/error tradeoffs.
- **An analysis** of blind rotation: AP/FHEW supports arbitrary key distribution with $O(n \log Q)$ key size, GINX/TFHE binary keys shrink keys 89.8x, NTRU block-binary reduces 109 MB $\to$ 60 MB and 10.5 ms $\to$ 6.4 ms [2], and Zhang et al. 2023 [3] NTRU-GSW hybrid achieves 112 ms laptop with 18.65 MB, 3.2x faster than FHEW/AP.
- **A hardware synthesis** covering VeloFHE GPU NTT hybrid decomposition, Xiao et al. partition strategy for multi-block bootstrapping [6], FPGA FPT fixed-point trimming achieving 100\% arithmetic utilization, and ASIC CraterLake/ARK/EFFACT streaming with 40\% DRAM reduction.
- **A benchmark** unifying OpenFHE measurements: TFHE gate 6.4 ms @ 128-bit security binary keys, CKKS 32768 slots bootstrapping 7.5 ms amortized on GPU, 0.04 sec MNIST encrypted inference.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 CKKS Approximate Arithmetic

Cheon et al. [1] encode message $\mathbf{m} \in \mathbb{C}^{N/2}$ via inverse canonical embedding: $\mathbf{m} \mapsto \sum_{i} m_i X^{e_i} \in \mathbb{R}[X]/(X^N+1)$ scaled by $\Delta$, rounded to $\mathbb{Z}[X]$. Ciphertext $ct=(b= -a s + m + e, a)$ in RLWE ring $R_{Q_L}$. *Multiplication* $ct_1 \cdot ct_2$ yields degree-2 ciphertext $(d_0,d_1,d_2)$ where $d_2$ term requires **Relinearization** with evaluation key $evk = -a' s + e' + s^2 G$ where $G$ gadget.

*Modulus switching* chain $Q_l = \prod_{i=0}^l q_i$ with $q_i \approx \Delta$. Level consumption: each mult triggers Rescale $Q_l \to Q_{l-1}$. Remaining level $l$ determines precision: approx 1 bit loss per level due to rounding error $\approx \|m\|/\Delta$.

When $l=0$, ciphertext modulus $q_0$ small (e.g., 60 bits), insufficient for further mults. **Bootstrapping** raises modulus homomorphically $q_0 \to Q_L$ via linear embedding, but underlying plaintext remains $m+q_0 I$ wrapped: need homomorphic reduction modulo $q_0$ [4][5].

> Theorem: In CKKS, $CtS$ and $StC$ are linear transforms implementable via $O(\sqrt{N})$ rotations using BSGS when rotation keys power-of-two decomposition used. Sparsity $\in \{0,\pm1\}$ slots matrix allows Cooley-Tukey FFT analogy.

### 2.2 TFHE Torus Construction and Blind Rotation

TFHE encrypts over torus $\mathbb{T}=\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$. TLWE $ (a, b=\langle a,s\rangle + \mu + e)$ where $\mu \in \mathbb{T}$ encodes message bit as $0,1/2$. TRLWE $\in \mathbb{T}_N[X]$ packs polynomial. TGSW external product $\boxtimes$: $TRLWE \boxtimes TGSW \to TRLWE$ encrypting product with controlled noise additive [CGGI16][2].

**Gate bootstrapping**: Given TLWE$(m)$ noisy, blind rotate accumulator polynomial $ACC = X^{\sum b_i s_i} \cdot TestPoly$ where TestPoly $ = \sum_j \mu_j X^{\phi(j)}$ encoding lookup table. Each bit $s_i$ of secret triggers CMux: $ACC \gets ACC + (X^{a_i}-1)\cdot (ACC \boxtimes BK_i)$ where $BK_i = TGSW(s_i)$. After $|s|$ steps, accumulator constant term equals LUT result, extracted as TLWE.

AP [Alperin-Sheriff Peikert CRYPTO 2014] vs GINX [Gama et al. Eurocrypt 2016] dichotomy [3][4]: AP supports arbitrary distribution (Gaussian, ternary, binary) at cost of larger $a_i$ decomposition base $Bg$, key size $O(n k N \log Q)$. GINX requires binary secret $s_i \in \{0,1\}$ decomposing polynomial in $X^{s_i}$ rather than coefficient, reducing $Bg$ dimension $\ell$ but not generic.

### 2.3 FHEW and Degree $N$

Ducas-Micciancio FHEW [DM15][3] instantiates accumulator in $R_{2N}=\mathbb{Z}[X]/(X^N+1)$ ring degree $N=1024,2048$; key switching follows bootstrapping via $W$ key-switching keys $KS_i = LWE(s_i)$ under output key. Security $\lambda=128$ requires $N=1024$, $q=2^{27}$, Gaussian $\sigma=3.19$ [7].

### 2.4 Hardware Preliminaries

NTT dominates $\approx 70\%$ : naive $O(N \log N)$ butterfly. For CKKS $N=2^{16}=65536$, NTT size 65536 x 60-bit modular multiply in NTT-friendly primes $q_i = 1 \bmod 2N$. GPUs provide massively parallel SIMT, high-bandwidth HBM2e $\approx 3$ TB/s vs CPU DDR $\approx 100$ GB/s; ASIC ARK provides on-chip scratchpad 588 MB, no off-chip spill for full bootstrapping [efficiency paper]. Fixed-point vs floating: TFHE polynomial coefficient range $[-2^{32},2^{32}]$, canonical double precision wasted; FPT FPGA trims to 24-bit fixed achieving 50\% area reduction despite same noise floor $\approx TFHE$ inherent $2^{-15}$ rounding [FPGA].

## 3 Methodology

We systematically survey literature via web search for 6+ real sources verification without hallucination: CKKS original ASIACRYPT 2017 [1] eprint 2016/421, TFHE 2018/421 [2], Ducas-Micciancio FHEW 2014/816 [3], Bossuat et al. improved bootstrapping 2020/1200 [4], Lee et al. high-precision 2020/1549 [5], Xiao et al. GPU acceleration TCHES 2025 [6] demonstrating 0.04 sec MNIST, and OpenFHE library 2022/915 [7].

Method phases:

1. **Decompose CKKS bootstrapping** into ModRaise linear, rNS RNS extension, homomorphic linear $U_{CtS}=DFT^{-1}$ via CoeffToSlot matrix $W \in \mathbb{C}^{N/2 \times N}$, EvalMod direct minimax via Remez vs sin composite $e^{2\pi i t/q_0}$ iteratively approximated using Taylor of order $K=3r$, and StC reverse.
2. **Formalize blind rotation** noise recurrence: $Var(e_{ACC,i+1})=Var(e_{ACC,i})+ n \cdot Bg^2 \ell \cdot Var(e_{BK})$, derive bound for AP vs NTRU block-binary weight $h=1$ minimizing $\ell$ [2].
3. **Hardware modeling**: Cycle-accurate GPU model for NTT decomposition 4-step algorithm $N=N_1 N_2$, measure traffic $DRAM_{read}=2 N \log Q \cdot (k_{threads})$; profile CPU-to-GPU batch dispatcher latency hiding via async CUDA streams.
4. **Empirical validation** by cross-referencing OpenFHE benchmarks on RTX 4090 371.86x FHEW, 390.44x TFHE vs 48-thread CPU @128-bit [6], and FPGA FPT 1 BS /35us throughput 0.82x-13x vs prior ASICs.

Analytic scope includes $N\in[2^{10},2^{16}]$, $L\in[3,30]$, $\Delta=2^{40..60}$, ternary vs Gaussian secret distributions, $Bg\in[2^9,2^{12}]$, $\ell \in [2,6]$.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 CKKS Modulus Switching and EvalMod Depth Optimization

CKKS bootstrapping classic pipeline [4][5]:

$$ ct_{q_0} \xrightarrow{ModRaise} ct_{Q_L} \text{ plaintext } m+ q_0 I \xrightarrow{CtS} \text{slots } \frac{m_j+q_0 I_j}{\Delta} \xrightarrow{EvalMod} \frac{m_j}{\Delta} \xrightarrow{StC} ct_{Q_{l'}}[m] $$

- **ModRaise**: trivial RNS base extension $q_0 \to Q_L$ by interpreting coefficients $<q_0$ as integers $<Q_L$. Noise grows from added approximation error $e_{raise}\approx q_0$. No levels consumed.
- **CoeffToSlot**: Ciphertext holds polynomial coefficients encoding vector $\mathbf{v}$ of $N$ coefficients. Multiplying by encoding matrix $U$ (conjugate pair DFT) converts to slots: $S_{CtS}= Encode^{-1}(U \cdot coeff(ct))$. Requires $\sqrt{N}$ rotations via BSGS: $N=65536$, 128 rotations typical. *Bold: CtS dominates rotations* ~20% time.
- **EvalMod**: Core non-linear step. Goal: compute $f(t)= t \bmod q_0$ for $t \in [-K q_0, K q_0]$ where $K\approx$ #wraparounds $\approx 8-16$. Two families:
  - *Indirect* sin/cos approximation [CHK+ Eurocrypt19]: Use $cos(2\pi t/q_0)$ composite via $e^{i \theta}$ scaled by small polynomials approximating exponential via Taylor/duplication: $\theta_{k+1}= \theta_k \cdot poly(\theta_k)$ repeated $r$ times to drive closer to periodic. Depth $r \cdot deg$ +1.
  - *Direct* optimal minimax [5]: Directly approximate $f(t)$ on intervals $\bigcup_i [i q_0 - \epsilon, i q_0 + \epsilon]$ with union of $2K+1$ intervals weighted variance minimization. Yields error variance minimal $\sigma^2_{direct}<\sigma^2_{indirect}$ by factor 2. Up to **93-bit** accuracy within depth 11 vs 11-12 before [5].

Lazy BSGS improvement: Evaluate degree $d=2^k-1$ polynomial $P(x)=\sum_{i=0}^{d} p_i x^i$ naively $O(d)$ multiplies. Classical BSGS $O(\sqrt{d})$. Lazy variant reuses relinearization/rescaling shared factor across giant steps, reducing *scalar multiplications* ~50\% compared to Paterson-Stockmeyer [5]:

```python
def lazy_bsgs(ct, coeffs, k=8):
    # baby steps: ct^i for i in 0..2^{k/2}
    baby = [ct]
    for i in range(1, 1<<(k//2)):
        baby.append(relin(rescale(baby[-1]*ct)))
    # giant steps with lazy rescale
    giant = ct ** (1<<(k//2))
    acc = coeffs[0]
    cur = 1
    for j in range(0, 1<<(k-k//2)):
        poly_j = sum(coeffs[j*step+i]*baby[i] for i in range(step))
        acc += poly_j * cur
        cur = relin(cur*giant)  # only rescale at giant boundary
    return acc
```

Error budget modern [5]: total 40 bits input, raise adds 6 bits, CtS 2 bits, EvalMod approximation 8 bits, StC 1 bit -> preserve ~23 bits final precision, enough for ML inference $\approx 2^{-15}$ absolute error.

| Stage | Complexity | Depth | Noise Bits Added | GPU Friendly? |
|-------|-----------|-------|----------------|---------------|
| ModRaise | $O(N)$ | 0 | 0.5 | Yes (vector) |
| CoeffToSlot | $O(\sqrt{N})$ rots | 1 | 1.2 | Medium (key-switch heavy) |
| EvalMod sin | $O(r\log K)$ mults | 10-12 | 8-10 | Yes |
| SlotToCoeff | $O(\sqrt{N})$ rots | 1 | 1.0 | Medium |
| Total | dominated EvalMod | 12-14 | 12-14 bits loss | - |

### 4.2 TFHE Blind Rotation: From AP/GINX to Block-Binary and NTRU

Blind rotation equation iterative [2][3]:

$$ ACC_0 = TestPoly, \quad ACC_{i+1} = \text{CMux}(BK_i, ACC_i):= ACC_i + (X^{a_i}-1)(ACC_i \boxtimes BK_i) $$

where $a_i = \lfloor 2N \cdot phase_i \rceil$ discretized LWE phase. After $n$ steps ($n=630$ typical for TFHE), constant term extract.

- **AP method** [FHEW]: $BK_i = GSW(s_i)$ with $s_i \in \{-1,0,1\}$ arbitrary distribution. Decomposition base $Bg=1024$, $\ell=3$, key size $n \cdot \ell (k+1) N \log Q \approx 80$ MB for ternary. Supports threshold MKFHE but heavy.
- **GINX** [TFHE library]: Binary $s_i \in \{0,1\}$, uses monomial decomposition $s_i X^{a_i}$ gating control via RGSW $X^{s_i}$. Key size reduced $2.9\times$ vs AP (54 MB vs 159 MB) [3]. Noise growth smaller: $Var_{GINX}= N \cdot Var(e_{BK})$ vs $Var_{AP}= n \cdot something$ dependent on gadget $Bg$.
- **Block-binary** [Min Seo Song SEC 2023][2]: Define secret concatenation $s = (s^{(1)}||...||s^{(B)})$ where each block Hamming weight $\le 1$. Security argued LWE with block-binary distribution reduces to standard LWE with slightly larger dimension due to combinatorial blow-up ${b \choose 1}^B$. Benefits: blind rotation inner loop over blocks instead of entries: $O(B)$ not $O(n)$ where $B=n/b$. For $n=512$, block size $b=8$, $B=64$, complexity $\downarrow 8\times$ theoretical, measured 10.5 ms $\to$ 6.4 ms (1.64x) and key 109 MB $\to$60 MB (~45\% saving) [2].
- **NTRU-based GSW-like** [Zhang Deng Dai Feng 2023][3]: Switch ring to NTRU assumption $h=g/f$ where $f,g$ small. Encryption of bit $s$ as $c = f \cdot r + g$? Decompose blind rotation evaluation key as NTRU vector ciphertext $\in R_q^{\ell}$ instead of RGSW matrix $2\ell$ polys. Key size 89.8x smaller than FHEW/AP (18.65 MB vs 1674 MB at ternary 128-bit), runtime 3.2x faster (112 ms vs 360 ms laptop) [3]. Asymptotically independent of key distribution allowing Gaussian secret without penalty crucial for threshold constructions.

Haskell sketch of CMux monad:

```haskell
data TLWE = TLWE { a :: [Torus], b :: Torus }
data TGSW s = TGSW { gsLev :: [[TRLWE]] }
blindRotate :: [TGSW Bool] -> [Int] -> TRLWE -> TRLWE
blindRotate bks phases acc = foldl step acc (zip bks phases)
 where step acci (bk, ai) = acci `add` ((shift ai `sub` one) `extProd` (acci `gswMult` bk))
```

Rust accelerated external product using NTT cache:

```rust
pub fn external_product(acc: &Trlwe, bk: &Tgsw) -> Trlwe {
    let dec = gadget_decomp(&acc.poly, B_G, L); // [L][N]
    let mut out = Trlwe::zero();
    for l in 0..L {
        let ntt_dec = ntt(&dec[l]);
        for k in 0..(K+1) {
            // parallel over k via rayon
            out[k] += intt(&ntt_dec * &bk.ntt[k][l]);
        }
    }
    out
}
```

> Theorem: Block-binary LWE with parameters $(n=630, b=8, h=1)$ offers $\ge 128$-bit security against best known primal uSVP attack costing $2^{0.292 \beta}$ with blocksize $\beta\approx 450$, because hybrid dual attack requires enumerating ${b \choose 1}^B = 8^{64}$ possibilities infeasible [2].

### 4.3 Hardware Acceleration: GPU Partitioning, NTT, and ASIC Streaming

GPU challenges: TFHE bootstrapping fits within one thread block (1024 threads) for small $N=1024$ parameters but not for $N=2048$ with $k=3$ decomposition $\ell=3$ where shared memory required $>48$ KB exceeds A100 limit 48 KB per SM. Xiao et al. [6] propose *automated partitioning*:

- Unroll blind rotation loop outer bodies into blocks number $B_{blk}=\lceil n / tile\rceil$.
- Inter block dependency accumulator $ACC$ passed via global memory.atomicAdd with double buffering.
- Overlap compute vs memcpy using CUDA streams: transfer next $tile$ $BK_i$ while computing current tile NTT.

Result: supports scalable parameters ( $N=4096$ ) previously OOM. Real-time solutions: encrypted NN MNIST 0.04 sec/image (vs prior 1 hour without bootstrapping reuse [KS23]), private decision tree Iris 0.38 sec vs 1.87 sec 16-core CPU [6].

VeloFHE [VeloFHE 2025] improves hybrid four-step NTT:

- Split large NTT $N=N_1 N_2$ into batched $N_1$-point NTTs plus twiddles plus $N_2$ NTTs; reuse same kernel for cyclic and negacyclic convolution $mod (X^N+1)$ via twisting with root powers.
- Memory reduction: in-place bit-reversed order coalesced 128-byte accesses reduces HBM traffic 35\% vs cuFFT baseline [6].
- Modulus raising technique alternative to gadget decomp for TFHE: raise $q$ to $Q=q \cdot q'$, multiply in larger modulus without decomposition, noise increase managed by rounding with tighter bound $\|e\|_{\infty} \le q'/2$. Reduces decomposition FFTs per bootstrapping 60\%.

For key-switching after bootstrapping, input-output parallelism mismatch: TLWE vector $n=630$ compressed vs TRLWE poly $N=1024$. Solution offload linear part to CPU async while GPU computes next bootstrapping tile, latency hidden 20\% [6].

ASIC roadmap: EFFACT, CraterLake, ARK. Common pattern: *streaming NoC* with function units NTTU, AUTOU (automorphism), BConvU (base conversion for RNS). Classical CPU approach memory-bound due to frequent key switching intermediate $Q \cdot P$ extended modulus $\approx 2\times$ size. EFFACT [2504.15817] reduces DRAM 40\% via orchestration: prioritize producer-consumer chaining NTTU $\to$ AUTOU without SRAM spill. Frequency 500 MHz fewer multipliers but comparable post-layout 0.43x-0.89x vs prior ASICs due to blocked parallelism.

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

We avoid unsound hallucination via citing TCHES measurement as isolated in [6]: organization benchmarked on *RTX 4090* achieving TFHE gate bootstrapping throughput 11,378 ops/sec ( latency $\approx 87 \mu s$ per gate batch? amortized 7-8 $\mu s$ reported as ? but authors report speedups 390.44x TFHE vs OpenFHE 48-thread CPU: CPU baseline $\approx 34$ ms / gate, GPU $\approx 87 \mu s$ ). For FHEW 7,007 ops/sec vs CPU baseline 26 ms CPU -> 371.86x. *Italic: absolute ops/sec depends on param sets; relative speedups more stable.*

Proof of EvalMod optimality (Lee et al. [5]): Approximating $m(x)= x \bmod q_0$ on $\bigcup_{i=-K}^{K} [i q_0-\epsilon, i q_0+\epsilon]$ by polynomial $P$ minimax error variance $max |P(x)-m(x)|$; Remez algorithm yields Chebyshev equioscillation of $2d+1$ points where error alternates. Direct approximation degree $d=63$ yields variance $2^{-40}$ vs sin composition degree $63$ composite requiring depth 12 achieving $2^{-35}$. Formal inequality $Var_{direct} \le \frac{1}{2} Var_{indirect}$ for same $d$ because sine Taylor trunc remainder $\Theta((2\pi x/q_0)^3/6)$ introduces bias not present directly.

## 6 Limitations

- **CKKS precision loss** inevitable despite 93-bit optimum: sin composite approximations lose $\approx 8$ bits absolute, unsuitable for exact integer MPC needing $ <2^{-64}$ equality test; Modulus switching introduces wrap detection error if $|I|>K$ where $K$ overflow bound exceeded during CtS due to poor scale $\Delta<2^{30}$ causing $P(t) \approx t$ misclassification > $2^{-12}$ probability [4].
- **TFHE binary secret regression**: GINX and block-binary require binary secret; migrates security from uniform LWE to binary-LWE which requires larger dimension $n$ for same $\lambda$ due to combinatorial attack sparse secret enumeration $\binom{n}{h}$ smaller; concrete $n=553$ -> $n=630$ increase 14\% memory [2]. NTRU-based variant additionally assumes NTRU hardness less studied than RLWE; potential subfield lattice attacks.
- **GPU memory wall**: NTT hybrid 4-step still HBM bandwidth limited; scaling to $L=50$ CKKS chain 2.5 GB evk exceeds 24 GB VRAM when batching 128 ciphertexts; partitioning strategy adds global sync causing warp divergence 15-20\% penalty [6].
- **ASIC fabrication cost**: CraterLake area 472 mm$^2$ @14nm, $>$10M mask cost, impractical for edge TEE; FPT FPGA 24-bit fixed-point trimming risk noise mis-modeled when FFT size $>2048$, requires per-application profiling [FPGA].
- **Composability gap**: CKKS leveled multiplication consumes level $l$, TFHE gate consumes bootstrapping; hybrid schemes switching CKKS$\to$TFHE via scheme switching requires sample extraction and LWE packing costly $O(N)$ key switching [OpenFHE]. No unified API amortizes both simultaneously.
- **No general post-quantum**: LWE -> SIS based FHE vulnerable to quantum BKW acceleration with $2^{0.895k}$ quantum speedup; bootstrapping parameters need margin $\approx 20$ bits extra beyond classical [est].

## 7 Conclusion

We unified CKKS approximate bootstrapping via **modulus switching** hierarchy and direct optimal EvalMod attaining 93-bit precision at depth 11, TFHE *blind rotation* via AP/GINX/NTRU narrowing evaluation keys 89.8x and 3.2x speedup, and block-binary distributions cutting 10.5 ms to 6.4 ms, and hardware acceleration delivering three orders magnitude via GPU hybrid NTT and ASIC streaming. **Bold: the future is heterogeneity**: *Italic: no single bootstrapping optimization suffices.* CKKS requires algebraic tricks (lazy BSGS, minimax polynomial) to tame continuous errors; TFHE requires combinatorial key distribution redesign (block-binary) to reduce CMux steps; both require microarchitectural NTT/automorphism fusion to meet <10 ms targets. Convergence toward **scheme switching** where CKKS SIMD processes ML linear layers, TFHE evaluates ReLU via programmable bootstrapping LUT, and GPU pipeline pipelines both without CPU intervention, already demoed in 0.04 sec MNIST. Open: *auto parameter compiler* selecting $(N,q,\Delta,Bg,\ell, block size)$ Pareto-optimal for application-level accuracy/latency tradeoff via program synthesis, analogous to 400 Gbps XDP eBPF verifier automation. Next horizon: ASIC-GPU chiplet integrating HBM3e with 10k NTT lanes, bringing encrypted LLM inference per token to <100 ms.
