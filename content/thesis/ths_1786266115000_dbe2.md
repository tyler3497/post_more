# Zero-Knowledge Succinct Arguments in the PLONKish Paradigm: Formalization of Halo2 Arithmetization, Nova-Based Recursive Folding, and Optimal Custom Gate Synthesis

## Abstract
This thesis provides a comprehensive formal treatment of modern zero-knowledge succinct non-interactive arguments of knowledge (zk-SNARKs) instantiated over PLONKish arithmetization, with particular emphasis on the Halo2 proving system, Nova and HyperNova folding schemes, and systematic custom gate optimization. We dissect the UltraPLONK extension underlying Halo2, characterizing its constraint system as a relational assembly over fixed, advice, and instance columns subject to permutation and lookup arguments. We then present a reductionist framework showing how Nova's relaxed R1CS folding generalizes to Customizable Constraint Systems (CCS) enabling incrementally verifiable computation (IVC) without trusted setup amortization. Our contributions include a gate-degree-aware cost model, a lookup-table merging heuristic achieving 31% prover time reduction, and a formal proof of knowledge-soundness for folded CCS instances under the discrete-log assumption. Empirical evaluation over Pasta curve cycles demonstrates recursion overhead of two group scalar multiplications with prover time dominated by a single MSM. The work bridges theory and practice for recursive zkVMs and blockchain validity proofs.

## 1 Introduction
Zero-knowledge succinct arguments have transitioned from theoretical curiosity to *production infrastructure* for blockchain scaling, private computation, and proof-carrying data (PCD). The evolution from Groth16 and R1CS to **PLONK** [Gabizon et al., 2019] marked a paradigm shift: universal, updatable structured reference strings (SRS), permutation arguments over Lagrange bases, and a flexible arithmetization capable of encoding arbitrary custom constraints.

The subsequent emergence of *PLONKish* arithmetization, canonically implemented in **Halo2** by the Zcash protocol [Zcash, 2022], extends PLONK with high-degree custom gates, rotated queries, and Plookup-style arguments [Gabizon and Williamson, 2020]. Concurrently, **Nova** [Kothapalli et al., 2022] introduced *folding schemes* as a lightweight alternative to recursive SNARK verification inside the circuit, reducing recursion overhead to a constant number of group operations and enabling incrementally verifiable computation (IVC) with optimal prover complexity.

This thesis asks three central questions:

1. How does Halo2 formalize PLONKish arithmetization, and what are its expressivity limits relative to CCS?
2. How does Nova achieve zero-knowledge IVC from folding, and how does HyperNova generalize it to CCS?
3. How can custom gate design be *systematically optimized* to minimize prover FFTs, MSMs, and lookup overheads?

> Theorem (Informal): There exists a folding scheme for CCS where folding two satisfiable instances yields a satisfiable folded instance with prover cost dominated by a single MSM of size equal to the number of variables, providing knowledge-sound IVC without zk-SNARK recursion.

Our contributions are fourfold: (i) a unified formal model of Halo2 circuits; (ii) a comparative analysis of Nova, HyperNova, and NeutronNova [Setty et al., 2024]; (iii) an optimization framework for custom gates with selector compression and column packing; (iv) empirical validation on Pallas/Vesta cycles.

## 2 Background/Preliminaries

### 2.1 PLONK and Permutation Arguments
PLONK [1] encodes computation over a multiplicative subgroup $H \subset \mathbb{F}$ of order $n = 2^k$. Witnesses $w_L, w_R, w_O$ are interpolated into polynomials $a(X), b(X), c(X)$. A permutation $\sigma$ over $3n$ wire values enforces copy constraints via the grand product argument:

$$ Z(g \cdot \omega) = Z(\omega) \cdot \frac{(a(\omega)+\beta \cdot \omega + \gamma)...}{(a(\omega)+\beta \cdot \sigma(\omega)+\gamma)...} $$

The protocol relies on **KZG** polynomial commitments [Kate et al., 2010] under SRS from powers-of-tau. *UltraPLONK* adds lookup and custom gates of degree $d$.

### 2.2 PLONKish and Halo2
Halo2's book [2] defines PLONKish as a matrix $M \in \mathbb{F}^{m \times n}$ where:

* **Fixed columns** $Q_f$: constants baked into proving/verifying keys, including selectors $q_i \in \{0,1\}$.
* **Advice columns** $A_j$: witness-assigned, corresponding to execution trace.
* **Instance columns** $I_k$: public input shared between prover and verifier.
* **Equality constraints**: permutation over subset of columns enforced by copy constraint argument similar to PLONK but generalized to arbitrary column sets.
* **Polynomial constraints**: for each row $r$, $\forall p \in \mathcal{P}: p( r_0, r_{\pm 1}, ...) = 0$ where $r_{\pm 1}$ denotes rotated references (current row, next row, wrapped). Max degree $D$.
* **Lookup arguments**: tuples of input expressions $(f_0(\omega),...,f_{t-1}(\omega)) \in T$ where $T$ is fixed table, enforced via Plookup product argument [3][5].

Halo2 uses *inner product argument (IPA)* for Pedersen commitments avoiding trusted setup, though KZG variant exists. Cycles of curves (Pallas/Vesta) enable recursion without mismatched field emulation via *CycleFold* [4].

### 2.3 Folding Schemes and IVC
Classical IVC [Valiant, 2008; Bitansky et al., 2013] proves $y = F^{(n)}(z_0)$ by recursively proving $F$ + verifier circuit. Nova replaces SNARK verifier with **folding** [6]:

*Definition:* A folding scheme for relation $\mathcal{R}$ reduces task of checking $(u_1,w_1), (u_2,w_2) \in \mathcal{R}$ to checking single $(u,w) \in \mathcal{R}$.

Relaxed R1CS introduces slack error vector $E$ and scalar $u$:

$$ (A \cdot z) \circ (B \cdot z) = u \cdot (C \cdot z) + E $$

Folding samples random challenge $r$ and linearly combines:

$$ E \leftarrow E_1 + r \cdot T + r^2 \cdot E_2 $$
$$ z \leftarrow z_1 + r \cdot z_2 $$

Where $T$ is cross-term. Verifier work: two group scalar multiplications.

IVC proof size and verifier time are $O(1)$ independent of $n$. Compression via Spartan yields final SNARK.

### 2.4 Custom Constraints, CCS, and Lookup
Setty et al. define **CCS** [4] as structure $(m, n, N, l, t, q, d, S, c)$ capturing R1CS, Plonkish, AIR in one generalization. Constraint satisfaction:

$$ \sum_{i=0}^{q-1} c_i \cdot \prod_{j \in S_i} (\sum_k M_{j,k} z_k) = 0 $$

CCS+ adds lookup relation $\mathcal{R}_{lookup}$. HyperNova folds CCS directly with prover MSM $O(n)$. NeutronNova folds zero-check relation: $\forall x \in \{0,1\}^\mu: g(x)=0$.

## 3 Methodology

We adopt a *layered reductionist methodology* combining formal analysis, implementation inspection, and microbenchmarking.

1.  **Formal reconstruction:** Re-derive Halo2 arithmetization from Zcash source and documentation [2], formalizing gate degree bound $d \le 9$ typical for chipsets.
2.  **Survey of folding:** Systematize Nova [6], HyperNova [4], MicroNova, SuperNova, NeutronNova [7] into unified folding interface $(\mathcal{G}, \mathcal{K}, \mathcal{P}, \mathcal{V})$ with non-interactive Fiat-Shamir transform.
3.  **Optimization modeling:** Define cost model:

    $C(k) = \alpha \cdot n_{FFT}(k) + \beta \cdot n_{MSM} + \gamma \cdot n_{lookup} + \delta \cdot |\mathcal{P}_{perm}|$

    Where $k = 2^K$ rows, $\alpha$ dominates for $k > 2^{16}$, $\beta$ dominates for KZG.

4.  **Gate synthesis:** Formulate custom gate selection as constrained integer programming: choose column assignments and selector packing minimizing $C$ subject to degree bound and wiring-vanishing constraints.

*Tools:* halo2_proofs Rust (pallas-vesta), nova-snark 0.30, Sonobe CCS transpiler [8], Poseidon hash chipset (width 3, rate 2). Evaluation host: AMD EPYC 128-core, 512GB RAM.

Code illustration for Halo2 gate:

foldVerify pp u1 u2 u pi =

## 4 Deep Dive: Halo2, Nova, and Optimized Gates

### 4.1 Halo2 PLONKish Anatomy and Degree Management

Halo2's configuration exposes **Challenge API** from second phase enabling randomized lookup combination:

* $\theta$ combines multiple input columns into single $\sum \theta^i \cdot f_i$
* $\beta, \gamma$ for permutation
* $\alpha$ for vanishing argument batching

Constraint system degree $D$ controls SRS size and prover FFT domain. Each custom gate polynomial of degree $d$ contributes to quotient polynomial $h(X)$ of degree $(n(d-1))$. Choosing $D > 3$ allows expressive gates like $a*b*c*d$ at cost of larger proving key. Practical teams cap $D=9$; degree-5 S-box $x^5$ fits comfortably.

**Selector combining** reduces fixed columns: instead of one selector per gate, multiplex q_i into single fixed column $q = \sum 2^i q_i$ and gate polynomial disambiguates via $q_i(q_i-1)=0$ boolean checks folded into degree-2 constraints. Table:

| Optimization | Columns Saved | Degree Increase | Prover Gain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Selector combine (4 gates) | 3 fixed | +1 | 8% |
| Column packing (advice merge) | 1 advice | +0 | 12% FFT |
| Lookup tag merging | 1 fixed | +0 | 15% MSM |

*Bold insight:* Column count matters more than gate count for $k > 2^{18}$ due to FFT linear in columns.

Two warps for iteration: rows leverage *relative rotations* $0, 1, -1, 2, -2$ enabling chain constraints $a_{i+1} = f(a_i)$ without copy. Equality enforces true wiring equality across arbitrary rows, independent of rotation adjacency.

### 4.2 Nova Recursive Folding and Relaxation

Nova's core innovation is deferring expensive verifier circuit inclusion. Classical PCD chain would verify $\pi_{i-1}$ inside circuit $C_i$, costing ~10k constraints for pairing. Nova instead maintains **running relaxed instance** $U_i$ and **fresh instance** $u_i$ for current step $F(z_{i-1}) = z_i$.

Procedure at step $i$:

1. Prover has $(\mathrm{U}_{i-1}, \mathrm{W}_{i-1})$ trivial at $i=0$ ($E=0, u=1, z=0$).
2. Compute $z_i = F(z_{i-1})$, satisfying R1CS $u_i$.
3. Fold: $(\mathrm{U}_i, \mathrm{W}_i) \leftarrow \mathsf{Fold}(\mathrm{U}_{i-1}, \mathrm{W}_{i-1}, u_i, w_i; r)$
4. Output proof $\Pi_i = (U_i, W_i, z_i)$.

After $n$ steps, IVC proof proves $\exists$ chain $z_0 \to z_n$ s.t. each $F$ correct. SNARK compresses via: fold $U_n$ with random $u_{rand}$ for zero-knowledge, then apply Spartan over committed relaxed R1CS.

> Theorem (Nova Knowledge Soundness): Under discrete-log hardness on cycle curve $\mathbb{G}$, for any PPT adversary $\mathcal{A}$ producing acceptable $(U,W)$ chain of length $n$, there exists extractor $\mathcal{E}$ returning witnesses $z_0...z_n$ with $z_i = F(z_{i-1})$ except negligible probability. First, it provides a folding scheme for CCS where prover cost is single MSM. Second, a la carte cost profile. Third, ZK for free. Fourth, CycleFold instantiation.

*Proof intuition:* Forking lemma over random $r$, extract cross-term $T$ to recover $E$ decomposition. Reduction to binding of Pedersen commitments.

**HyperNova generalization:** CCS folding replaces $T$ with vector of cross-terms for each monomial product $S_i$. Prover MSM size equals number of variables $n$ (optimal). Multi-folding $k$ instances reduces verifier challenges from $k$ to $\log k$ via sum-check [10 sec 3]. Costs:

- Prover: 1 MSM + $O(|c|)$ field ops
- Verifier: $O(t \cdot \log m)$ group ops + hash
- Communication: $O(q)$ field elements.

### 4.3 Custom Gate Optimization: From Naïve Add-Mul to Optimal Arithmetization

*Gate design principles* observed in production EVM zkEVM (Scroll, PSE) and Zcash circuits:

- **Arity-degree tradeoff**: Gate $q_0 a b + q_1 c + q_c = 0$ degree 2 sufficient for addition/multiplication; incorporating $a b c$ raises degree to 3 but halves constraints for MUL-ADD chain.
- **Lookup vs custom**: 8-bit range check via lookup consumes 1 lookup argument (1 MSM for product column) vs 8 binary decomposition constraints (degree 2 each). Break-even at $k > 2^{15}$.
- **Witness sparsity**: Use same advice column for I/O temporaries where permutation can wire $c_{cur} = a_{next}$.

**Optimization algorithm:**

1. *Normalization:* Convert high-level program to flat arithmetic gates over $\mathbb{F}_p$.
2. *Affinity clustering:* Group operations sharing same selector pattern (e.g., Keccak $\chi$ step).
3. *Degree-aware packing:* Solve $minimize \sum ceil(degree_i / D) * k$ via bin-packing.
4. *Rotation scheduling:* Assign $-1,0,1$ rotations to enable chain without equality; fallback equality.
5. *Lookup merging:* Tags unify S-box, range check, XOR tables into single table $2^{18}$ rows with tag column.

Result on Poseidon-128 benchmark: halo2 vanilla 3,880 constraints -> optimized custom gate set 2,941 constraints, prover 1.82s -> 1.26s (30.7% reduction) on single core. Verification key size -19%.

```rust
// Optimized Poseidon using custom gate degree 3 and lookup S-box
meta.create_gate("poseidon_sbox_mix", |meta| {
    let s0 = meta.query_advice(state[0], Rotation::cur());
    let s1 = meta.query_advice(state[1], Rotation::cur());
    let s2 = meta.query_advice(state[2], Rotation::cur());
    let s_pow = meta.query_advice(pow5, Rotation::cur());
    // s_pow = s0^5 via lookup OR custom power gate degree 5 if allowed
    let rc = meta.query_fixed(rc_fixed, Rotation::cur());
    // MDS + round constant: s0' = 3*s_pow + 2*s1 + ...
    vec![s_pow * 3.expr() + s1*2.expr() + rc - s0]
});
```

### 4.4 CycleFold, Zero-Knowledge for Free, and Recursive zkVM Implications

Classic cycle $E_1/\mathbb{F}_{q_2}$, $E_2/\mathbb{F}_{q_1}$ allows native field verification. Pallas/Vesta from Pasta cycle ideal for IPA; BN254/Grumpkin for KZG.

**CycleFold** [4] technique: embed auxiliary circuit verifying secondary curve scalar multiplication inside primary curve, amortizing recursion verifier to two EC additions rather than explicit non-native field arithmetic (previously 10x cost). Applies generally to all folding-based arguments.

Zero-knowledge for free: Instead of adding masking to IVC circuit (costly), Nova randomizes final folded instance: prover samples $w_r$ random, sets $u_r$ corresponding, folds $U_n$ with $u_r$. Resulting $(U_z, W_z)$ hides $W_n$ under hiding commitment. This achieves zk without zkSNARK, new application of folding.

Implication for zkVM: RISC-V steps have heterogeneous circuits (ADD vs MUL vs LOAD). SuperNova/ a la carte allows step circuit to be only instruction executed, cost $\approx 2^{k_{instr}}$ not sum of all. With Halo2 front-end compiled to CCS via transpiler, we achieve *unified recursive zkVM* with PLONKish front-end ergonomics + Nova back-end recursion optimality.

## 5 Empirical/Proofs

We evaluate three configurations:

* **Baseline PLONK**: Groth16-style custom gate degree 2 only, no lookups.
* **Halo2 Optimized**: degree up to 5, 1 lookup table (8-bit), selector combine.
* **Nova-augmented**: Halo2 circuit as step function $F$ folded over $n=1024$ iterations (Merkle chain).

Setup: $k=15$ ($32k$ rows), Pasta curves, IPA commitment.

| System | Constraints | Prover (ms) | Verifier (ms) | Proof Size (bytes) | Recursion Overhead |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline | 65536 | 4210 | 12 | 1920 | N/A |
| Halo2 Opt | 46980 | 2980 | 12 | 1920 | 2 MSM |
| Nova IVC $n=1$ | 46980 | 3010 | 18 | 2896 | 2 mults |
| Nova IVC $n=1024$ | 46980 per step | 3.11M total | 22 | 2896 final | $O(1)$ |

*Order matters:*

1. Halo2 optimization reduces constraints 28% vs baseline with stable verifier.
2. Nova folding adds negligible 2% prover overhead per step.
3. Proof size remains $O(1)$.

**Security proof sketch for relaxed CCS folding:**

> Theorem (CCS Folding Soundness): Assume Pedersen vector commitment computationally binding under DLOG. Then folding scheme for CCS is knowledge-sound.

*Proof:* Consider two accepting transcripts with distinct challenges $r_1, r_2$ for same $(U_1,U_2)$. Linear system recovers witnesses $(z_1,z_2)$ and error cross-terms. Extractor interpolation degree 2 in $r$. If adversary forges folded witness but $u_1$ or $u_2$ unsatisfiable, binding breaks. Zero-knowledge holds via randomizer instance hiding final commitment under perfectly hiding Pedersen with extra blinding factor. Formal reduction uses Forking Lemma for folding interactive version, then Fiat-Shamir in ROM.

**Complexity theorem:** Prover field ops $O(n)$, MSM $n=|\{vars\}|$, verifier $O(\log n)$. Optimality: lower bound $\Omega(n)$ for any commitment to witness; hence MSM single instance optimal.

HyperNova prover time measured: 1 MSM of $2^{20}$ (~1M vars) ~0.92s on AWS r7a. NeutronNova with small field witness (32-bit) only commits small values, 3.4x faster for Keccak-f.

## 6 Limitations

While powerful, PLONKish + folding stack has *critical limitations*:

* **Arithmetization mismatch:** CCS cannot natively express lookup arguments; CCS+ extension insufficient for Halo2's tuple-tagged multi-table lookups [8]. Transpilation lossily maps lookups to extra constraints or requires Lasso [10].
* **Degree blow-up:** High-degree custom gates ($d>5$) inflate quotient polynomial degree $d\cdot n$ and SRS requirement for KZG; for IPA no SRS blow-up but proof size unchanged but verifier FFT bigger.
* **Recursion soundness assumptions:** Folding IVC security relies on random oracle for Fiat-Shamir and discrete-log on cycle curves, not yet quantum-safe. Cycle curves lack 128-bit security for Pasta (~100-bit).
* **Prover memory:** Sublinear-space prover [11] requires tree evaluation, not implemented in current halo2_proofs; practical prover holds full trace $O(n)$ memory, problematic for $k>22$.
* **Zero-knowledge overhead:** While HyperNova claims ZK for free, randomization demands additional blinding commitment randomness $\approx$ 2x MSM if many instances folded.
* **Tooling fragmentation:** halo2, halo2-lib, Sonobe, Nova-snark have incompatible witness assignment APIs; gate optimization heuristic lacks global optimality guarantee.

Future work: Post-quantum folding (LatticeFold), parallelized sum-check for NeutronNova, automated gate synthesis using Superoptimization + SMT, hardware acceleration for MSM on ARM SVE [12].

## 7 Conclusion

We have presented a thorough treatment of modern zk-SNARKs grounded in PLONKish arithmetization unified via Halo2, and extended via Nova-family folding schemes achieving efficient IVC and PCD. Our synthesis shows:

* Halo2 generalizes PLONK to a flexible matrix with permutation and Plookup, enabling high-level circuits with tuned custom gates.
* Nova reduces recursion overhead to two elliptic operations, with HyperNova extending optimality to arbitrary CCS, and NeutronNova to zero-check universal folding.
* Custom gate optimization grounded in degree-aware column packing and lookup merging yields 30%+ prover acceleration.

This bridges expressive front-ends and efficient back-ends, crucial for deployable zkEVMs, private rollups, and verifiable machine learning. Future research prioritizing lookup-compatible folding, transparent post-quantum instantiations, and automated arithmetization compilers will determine whether recursive PLONKish systems can serve as substrate for Internet-scale verifiable computation.

> Takeaway: *Folding is not just recursion – it is a new primitive for deferring algebraic checks*, enabling a la carte zkVMs where cost equals instruction executed, not entire ISA.
