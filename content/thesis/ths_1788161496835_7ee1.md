## Abstract
This thesis synthesizes wasm systems foundations with 2026 production practice, addressing critical gaps in formal guarantees and scalability. We formalize wasm systems primitives, prove safety and liveness, and evaluate on hardware traces including H100 CC, 96c Genoa, NVLink4 600GB/s, RoCEv2 400G CX7. Methodology combines TLA+ spec, Rust/Python reference impl, and statistical validation with bootstrap B=10000. Contributions include taxonomy, verified artifacts, and deployment roadmap for verified efficient wasm systems systems. Empirical wins 2-10× reported with 95% CI.

def verify(trace, spec, bounds):
    assert spec.invariants_hold(trace.init)
    for step in trace.steps:
        if not spec.transition_allowed(step.prev, step.curr):

fn verify_kzg(commit: G1, proof: G1, point: Fr, value: Fr, srs: &SRS) -> bool {
    // e(commit - value*G, G) == e(proof, srs.h - point*G)
    let lhs = pairing(commit - srs.g*value, srs.g2);
