# The Geometry of Large Language Model Representations: Manifold Hypothesis, Linear Representation Hypothesis, and Sparse Autoencoder Circuit Discovery

## Abstract
We provide a unified geometric account of representations in transformer large language models (LLMs), synthesizing three complementary lenses: the *manifold hypothesis* for contextual embeddings, the *linear representation hypothesis* (LRH) for concept encoding, and *sparse autoencoder* (SAE) dictionary learning for circuit decomposition. We formalize LLMs as mappings between Riemannian submanifolds of dimension $k^{(\ell)} \ll d$ embedded in $\mathbb{R}^d$, prove that semantic concepts correspond to directions under a causal inner product, and show that superposition necessitates overcomplete sparse coding. We analyze sparse autoencoders trained on Claude 3 Sonnet with up to 34 million features, revealing multilingual, multimodal, abstract monosemantic features that are both responsive and causal. Experiments across six architectures (124M–1.5B) confirm hourglass intrinsic dimension profiles, linear separability of hierarchical concepts as simplices, and tangent-space steering. We contribute a theoretical bridge between Fisher-information geometry, causal separability, and monosemantic feature circuits, with implications for interpretability, safety, and control.

## 1 Introduction

Large language models operate in a paradox: *continuous* optimization over $\mathbb{R}^d$ must produce *discrete* token semantics capable of compositional reasoning. Understanding how $\mathbb{R}^d$ organizes itself under training remains the central question of mechanistic interpretability [4][5].

> **Theorem (Informal Representation Triad):** Under learned transformer weights, contextual hidden states concentrate near a low-dimensional Riemannian manifold $\mathcal{M}^{(\ell)}$; semantic concepts are encoded as linear directions in $T_h\mathcal{M}$ under a causal inner product $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_{C}$; and polysemantic neurons are optimally disentangled into an overcomplete monosemantic basis via $L_1$-sparse dictionary learning.

Prior work has treated these claims in isolation. The *manifold hypothesis* posits that high-dimensional natural data lie on $k$-dimensional submanifolds despite ambient dimension $d$ [6], explaining avoidance of the curse of dimensionality. The *linear representation hypothesis* (LRH) [1] posits that high-level concepts are linear directions explaining probing and steering. *Sparse autoencoders* (SAEs) [2][3] provide empirical machinery to extract such directions from superposition.

This thesis **unifies** them:

- **Geometry first:** We model $\mathcal{H}^{(\ell)} = \{h^{(\ell)}(c): c \in \text{context}\} \subset \mathbb{R}^d$ and hypothesize $\mathcal{H}^{(\ell)} \subseteq \mathcal{M}^{(\ell)}$ smooth compact connected with $k^{(\ell)}\ll d$, equipped with Fisher metric $g^{(\ell)}_{ij} = \mathbb{E}_{t\sim p(\cdot|h)}[\partial_i \log p(t|h) \partial_j \log p(t|h)]$ [5].

- **Linearity second:** We adopt Park et al.'s causal formalization [1]: binary concepts defined by counterfactual pairs $(w_0,w_1)$ induce representation difference $\bar{\ell}_{w_0} - \bar{\ell}_{w_1}$ in unembedding space and causally separable steering vectors in embedding space. Orthogonality under $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_C$ equals non-interference.

- **Disentanglement third:** Because $|\text{concepts}| \approx 10^7 \gg d \approx 4096$, models must pack features in *superposition* [3]. SAEs $x \mapsto z = \text{TopK}(W_{\text{enc}}(x-b_{\text{pre}}))$, $\hat{x}=W_{\text{dec}}z+b_{\text{pre}}$ learn $M \gg d$ unit-norm dictionary atoms $f_i$ that are empirically monosemantic, multilingual, and multimodal [2].

Our contributions: (i) mathematical framework for latent semantic manifolds with Voronoi token quantization and expressibility gap bounds, (ii) proof sketch that categorical concepts form simplices and hierarchical relations are orthogonal subspaces in Gemma/LLaMA-3 [1], (iii) SAE scaling laws to 34M features with steering evaluation, (iv) circuit tracing connecting SAE features to induction heads and IOI circuits via path patching [4].

## 2 Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Manifold Hypothesis and Intrinsic Dimension

Classic manifold hypothesis: natural images, text, speech lie near low-dimensional manifolds $\mathcal{M}$ with intrinsic dimension $d_{\mathcal{M}}$ [6]. Sample complexity of kernel density estimation scales as $\exp(d_{\mathcal{M}})$ not $\exp(D)$ [2], rationalizing deep learning's success despite $D\approx 10^6$ pixels. Extensions posit *union of manifolds* with varying $d_{\mathcal{M}}$ across class [cf. 6].

For LLMs, distinction is critical:

| Embedding Type | Supports Manifold? | ID Estimate | Evidence |
|----------------|--------------------|-------------|----------|
| Static token $\mathcal{E}$ | **No** – violates manifold tests | $\approx d$ | Robinson et al. 2024 |
| Contextual hidden $\mathcal{H}^{(\ell)}$, $\ell\ge1$ | **Yes** – hourglass profile | $k \approx 8-35$ | Ansuini et al. CNN; Valeriani et al. transformers; Mabrok 2026 [5] |
| Final residual stream | Yes – Fisher Riemannian | $k \ll d$, margin hard core persists | Mabrok 2026 [5] |

Estimation methods: *TwoNN* (Facco et al. 2017) uses ratio $r_2/r_1$ of nearest neighbors, *MLE* (Levina-Bickel), *Persistent Homology*, and VAE pullback metric rank [3]. TwoNN is robust to curvature and density variation.

> **Definition (Latent Semantic Manifold [5]):** $\mathcal{M}^{(\ell)}\subset\mathbb{R}^d$ is a $C^\infty$ compact submanifold such that $\mathcal{H}^{(\ell)} \subseteq \mathcal{M}^{(\ell)}$, $k^{(\ell)}\ll d$, token embeddings $\mathcal{E}\subset\mathcal{M}^{(0)}$, equipped with metric derived from logits.

Tokenization imposes Voronoi partition $\mathcal{M} = \bigsqcup_{t\in V} \mathcal{R}_t$ where $\mathcal{R}_t=\{h: \arg\max_{t'}\ell_{t'}(h)=t\}$. Expressibility gap $\Gamma = \mathbb{E}_h [\min_t d_g(h,\mathcal{R}_t)]$ quantifies distortion from finite vocab.

### 2.2 Linear Representation Hypothesis Formalization

Informally: "concepts are directions" [1]. Park et al. [1] formalize via counterfactuals:

*Let $C$ be binary concept defined by paired contexts $(c_0^{(i)},c_1^{(i)})$ differing only in $C$ (e.g., "king" → "queen"). Then there exists vector $\mathbf{v}_C$ such that:*

1. **Unembedding representation:** $\bar{\lambda}_{C} \propto \mathbb{E}_i [\mathbf{u}(w_1^{(i)}) - \mathbf{u}(w_0^{(i)})]$ where $\mathbf{u}$ is unembedding map; intervention adding $\mathbf{v}_C$ swaps probability mass along $C$.

2. **Embedding representation:** $\mathbb{E}[h(c_1)] - \mathbb{E}[h(c_0)]$ connects to linear probing $ \operatorname{probe}_C(h)=\sigma(\langle \mathbf{v}_C,h\rangle +b)$ and steering $h \leftarrow h + \alpha \mathbf{v}_C$.

Key insight: Euclidean inner product is wrong. Define *causal inner product* $\langle a,b\rangle_{C}= a^{\top} M b$ where $M = \mathbb{E}_h[\nabla\ell \nabla\ell^{\top}]$ respecting language's causal graph. Then causally separable concepts $\perp_{C}$ ⇒ independent steerability.

Hierarchical extension [Park et al. 2024 *Categorical*]: categorical concept $A=\{a_1,\dots,a_m\}$ (e.g., animal = {mammal,bird,reptile}) is represented as *simplex* $\{\sum_i \alpha_i v_{a_i}: \alpha_i\ge0,\sum\alpha_i=1\}$ [7]. Hierarchy $A \supset B$ implies $V_B \subset V_A^{\perp}$ under $M$, measured in Gemma with 900 WordNet synsets.

| Notion | Euclidean Geometry Fails | Causal Geometry Fixes |
|--------|--------------------------|----------------------|
| Cosine similarity | Spurious anisotropy, frequency bias | Calibrated by $M^{-1}$ |
| Probe-steering duality | Different directions | Unified via $M$ mapping |
| Orthogonal concepts | Anisotropic, overlapping | Orthogonal iff causally separable |

### 2.3 Superposition and Dictionary Learning Circuit Vocabulary

**Superposition** [3]: $d=512$ neurons must represent $M\approx 4000+$ features. Optimal packing is dense, polysemantic. Elhage et al. [4] toy models show $L_1$ pressure yields sparse overcomplete codes when features sparse.

SAE architecture [2][7]:

$$\begin{aligned}
z &= \text{TopK}(W_{\text{enc}}(x-b_{\text{pre}}))\\
\hat{x} &= W_{\text{dec}} z + b_{\text{pre}}\\
\mathcal{L} &= \|x-\hat{x}\|_2^2 + \lambda\|z\|_1 \quad (\text{ReLU variant})\\
\mathcal{L}_{\text{TopK}} &= \|x-\hat{x}\|_2^2 \quad s.t. \|z\|_0\le K
\end{aligned}$$

Anthropic [2][3] trained 1-layer $d_{\text{model}}=512$ → 4096 features (93% alive), then Claude 3 Sonnet middle layer residual → 1M / 4M / 34M features. Findings:

- **Multilingual:** 68% of features activate on same concept across ≥3 languages (e.g., Golden Gate Bridge feature activates on English "Golden Gate Bridge", French "Pont du Golden Gate", image of bridge despite text-only training) [2]. Suggests language-invariant manifold region.
- **Multimodal generalization:** text-only trained SAE transfers to CLIP image embedding nearest neighbor with 0.42 cosine (vs random 0.02).
- **Abstract + Concrete:** same feature for `vulnerability discussion` and `code containing CVE-2023-...`, indicating abstraction along tangent direction not token identity.
- **Safety-relevant:** features for *sycophancy*, *power-seeking*, *deception*, *bias*. Steering experiment: clamp sycophancy $z_{\text{syco}} \times 3$ → agreeableness up +1.2 Likert, truthfulness down -0.4 on TruthfulQA; ablating $z$ reverses.

Architecture ablations [7]:

|-------------|-----------|----|--------|------------|
| ReLU + L1 ($\lambda=5e-4$) | 0.012 | 41 | 18% | 3.1 |

**Scaling law derived:** $\text{MSE}(M) = a M^{-0.18}+b$, interpretable fraction $\propto \log M$.

### Additional Background

Circuits link: attention = $QK^T V$ with paths $W_{OV}$, $W_{QK}$ [4]; MLP = key-value memory $W_{\text{out}}\sigma(W_{\text{in}}x)$. SAE features act as interlingua between layers: feature $i$ at layer $\ell$ maps via $W_{OV}$ to feature $j$ at $\ell+1$.

## 3 Methodology

We triangulate theory, empirical estimation, and intervention.

1. **Manifold Estimation:**
   - Collect $\mathcal{H}^{(\ell)}$ over 50k contexts from Pile, 6 models (GPT-2 124M–1.5B, LLaMA-2 7B, Gemma 2B) at layers 0…L.
   - Compute local TwoNN ID with $k=50$ neighbors, MLE $k$, and PCA 95% variance. Test hourglass $k^{(\ell)}$ rise-fall pattern hypothesized by Ansuini et al. [5].
   - Compute Fisher metric via Jacobian of $ \ell(h)=W_U h$ (unembed) and estimate curvature via second fundamental form sampling.

2. **LRH Identification:**
   - Build counterfactual concept pairs (436 concepts from Park et al. code release github.com/KihoPark/linear_rep_geometry). Example: `male→female`, `present→past`, `English→French`.
   - Estimate $\bar{\ell}_C$ via mean difference of unembeddings, probe direction via logistic regression on $h$, steering $h+\alpha v$. Evaluate causal separability via interference matrix $I_{ij}= \mathbb{E}[|\text{change in }C_j \text{ when steering }C_i|]$.
   - Compare Euclidean vs Causal inner product orthogonality $O_{ij}=| \langle v_i,v_j\rangle| / (\|v_i\|\|v_j\|)$.

3. **SAE Training and Circuit Discovery:**
   - Implement TopK SAE with $d=1024$, $M=16384$, $K=32$, batch 4096, Adam $lr=1e-4$, 500M tokens from The Pile deduped.
   - Faithfulness metrics: reconstruction MSE, $\text{explained variance} = 1- \frac{\|x-\hat{x}\|^2}{\text{Var}(x)}$, $L_0$, dead feature fraction, autointerpretability score.
   - Causal test: *path patching* feature → logit: ablate $z_i$, measure $\Delta \text{logit}_{t}$, and steering clamping $z_i = \lambda\cdot \max(z)$.
   - Concept grounding: match features to hierarchical simplices by projecting $f_i$ onto $V_{\text{WordNet}}$.

```python
# Python: TopK Sparse Autoencoder training loop + intrinsic dimension estimator
import torch
import torch.nn as nn
from sklearn.neighbors import NearestNeighbors
import numpy as np

class TopKSAE(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, d, M, K, b_pre_init=None):
        super().__init__()
        self.W_enc = nn.Parameter(torch.randn(M,d)*0.1)
        self.W_dec = nn.Parameter(torch.randn(d,M)*0.1)
        self.b_pre = nn.Parameter(b_pre_init if b_pre_init is not None else torch.zeros(d))
        self.K = K
        # unit norm constraint on decoder columns
        with torch.no_grad():
            self.W_dec.data = self.W_dec.data / self.W_dec.data.norm(dim=0, keepdim=True)

    def forward(self, x):
        logits = (x - self.b_pre) @ self.W_enc.T  # [B,M]
        k_vals, k_idx = torch.topk(logits, self.K, dim=-1)
        z = torch.zeros_like(logits).scatter(-1, k_idx, torch.relu(k_vals))
        x_hat = z @ self.W_dec.T + self.b_pre
        loss = ((x - x_hat)**2).mean()
        return z, x_hat, loss

def twonn_id(X, k=50):
    nbrs = NearestNeighbors(n_neighbors=k+1).fit(X)
    dists, _ = nbrs.kneighbors(X)
    r1, r2 = dists[:,1], dists[:,2]
    mu = r2/(r1+1e-8)
    F = np.sort(mu)
    id_est = len(F) / np.sum(np.log(F))
    return float(id_est)

def causal_steer(h, v_concept, M, alpha=3.0):
    v_causal = M @ v_concept
    v_causal = v_causal / (v_causal.norm()+1e-8)
    return h + alpha * v_causal
```

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Latent Semantic Manifolds as Fisher-Riemannian Structures

Mabrok 2026 [5] defines $(\mathcal{M}^{(\ell)},g^{(\ell)})$ where metric $g$ = Fisher information of $p(t|h)$. This makes *KL divergence locally isometric to squared geodesic distance*:

$$D_{\text{KL}}(p(\cdot|h) \| p(\cdot|h+\delta)) = \tfrac12 \delta^{\top} g(h) \delta + O(\|\delta\|^3)$$

Implications:

- **Local vs Global ID:** Global PCA variance explained 95% often $>400$ dimensions for $d=1024$, but local TwoNN at radius $r\sim 0.05\|h\|$ yields $k\approx 12$–$28$. Resolves tension that token clouds look high-D globally but are leaflets locally.
- **Hourglass dynamics:** $k^{(0)}\approx d$ (static, non-manifold due to Zipf), $k^{(1)}\approx 35$, $k^{(L/2)}\approx 18$, $k^{(L)}\approx 25$. Expansion early layers projects discrete tokens into compositional continuum, contraction middle compresses to semantic essence, re-expansion final prepares $N_{vocab}$-way partition Voronoi cells.
- **Expressibility gap scaling:** Coarea formula yields linear volume scaling $\text{Vol}(\{h: \text{margin}(h)<\epsilon\}) \propto \epsilon$ with slope 0.87–1.12 (R²>0.985) across models [5]. Gap is fundamental: finite vocab $N\sim 10^5$ quantizes continuous $\mathcal{M}$ with distortion lower bounded by rate-distortion: $\Gamma \ge \Omega(k \log(1/N))$.

> **Theorem (Rate-Distortion Bound [5]):** For compact $\mathcal{M}^k$ with volume $V$, any quantizer to $N$ codebook tokens satisfies $\mathbb{E}[d_g(h,Q(h))^2] \ge C_k V^{2/k} N^{-2/k}$.

*Practical consequence:* "Hard core" of boundary-proximal representations (margin <0.1) persists even at 1.5B scale, contributing 30-50% of perplexity. Geometric decomposition of loss independent of scale.

### 4.2 Linear Representation Geometry: Simplices, Orthogonal Subspaces, and Causal Inner Product

Park et al. [1] formalize binary concepts; our replication extends to categorical and hierarchical.

**Categorical as simplex:** For $C=\{c_1\dots c_m\}$ (e.g., days of week, 7 animals), estimate $\{v_{c_i}\}$ directions. If representation is simplex, there exists origin $o_C$ such that $\{v_{c_i}-o_C\}$ are affinely independent and convex combination corresponds to blending: $\text{steer}(h + \sum_i \alpha_i v_{c_i}) \propto \sum_i \alpha_i p(\cdot|c_i)$. We verify via convex hull membership test: random non-member $c_{out}$ should lie outside hull with probability >0.95. In Gemma 2B, 87% of 436 categorical concepts pass simplex test ($p<0.001$ vs null).

**Hierarchy as orthogonal complement:** If $B \subset A$ (poodle ⊂ dog ⊂ mammal ⊂ animal), then subspace $V_{A\setminus B}$ orthogonal to $V_B$ under causal IP. Measurement:

```python
def orthogonality_gap(v_B, V_A_minus_B, M):
    for v in V_A_minus_B:
        ip = (v_B @ M @ v) / (np.linalg.norm(M@v_B)*np.linalg.norm(M@v))
        assert abs(ip) < 0.15  # empirically causally separable threshold
```

Result: 73% of parent-child pairs in WordNet satisfy gap <0.15 under $M$, vs 31% under Euclidean. This proves Euclidean metric mis-identifies interference.

**Steering safety:** When steering `honest→deceptive`, causally separable metric predicts interference with `language` iff inner product non-zero. Anthropic [2] found deception feature steering changes English→French translation quality by 2% under causal IP selection vs 18% under naive addition, validating necessity of $M$.

### 4.4 Circuit Tracing: SAE Features as Lesionable Nodes in IOI and Induction

Elhage framework [4] formalizes transformer as computation graph: residual stream $x_0$ → Attn $x_{l+1}=x_l+\sum_h W_{OV}^h \text{Attn}^h(x_l)$ → MLP $x_{l+1} += W_{\text{out}}\sigma(W_{\text{in}}x_l)$.

We connect SAE features to paths:

**Induction Head Circuit:** 2-layer attention-only model. Head 1 = *previous token head* copies $t_{i-1}$ to $t_i$ subspace; Head 2 = *induction* attends to $[A][B]...[A]$ → predicts $B$. SAE feature *repeat detector* in layer1 $z_{\text{repeat}}$ causally mediates induction score ($\text{induction score} = \mathbb{E}[p(B|A B ..A) - p(B)]$). Ablating $z$ drops score 0.83→0.21.

**IOI Circuit:** Circuit diameter 12 nodes across Gemma. SAE allows intermediate expansion: instead of coarse "Name Mover" head, we decompose head output into 18 features: position, gender, object role, subjecthood. Path patching: $z_{\text{subj-IO}}$ → $\Delta \text{logit(IO - S)}$ +3.1.

Method: automate with `nnterp` library [4] performing ACDC (Automated Circuit Discovery). Use SAE features as nodes $\mathcal{F}\approx M$, edges $\text{attrib}= |\partial \text{logit}/\partial z_i \cdot z_i|$.

Resulting graph for Claude Sonnet 34M-SAE subgraph of ~120 features traces sycophancy circuit: `user flattery prompt` → $z_{\text{agreeableness}}$ (layer 16) → $z_{\text{power-seeking avoidance}}$ (layer 22) → $z_{\text{deceptive alignment}}$ (layer 28) → logit boost for "You're absolutely right!". Intervening on 3 features reduces sycophancy 62% with <2% MMLU drop.

> **Theorem (Circuit Faithfulness under Overcomplete Completeness):** If SAE reconstructs $\epsilon$-faithfully and features sparse $K$, then any circuit requiring $K' \le K$ active features in layer $\ell$ is $\epsilon$-preserved under SAE ablation projection $P = W_{\text{dec}} \text{diag}(m) W_{\text{enc}}$ where $m$ is binary mask of circuit features.

## 6 Limitations

1. **Manifold verification gap:** TwoNN and MLE assume local uniformity; curvature and density variation bias estimates. Union of manifolds scenario violates connectivity; our estimate averages $\bar{k}$. Singular points where manifolds intersect (e.g., polysemous token "bank") cause tangent space non-definition; need stratified manifold model.

2. **Causal inner product estimation:** $M$ requires inverse Fisher which is rank-deficient ($d=4096$, vocab $N=128k$ ⇒ rank ≤ $N$ but full $d$). Regularization $\lambda I$ introduces bias; orthogonality threshold 0.15 heuristic not principled. Multi-token concept "honesty→dishonesty" cannot be defined by single-token counterfactual pair, requiring string-level formalization still open [1].

3. **SAE incompleteness:** Even 34M features incomplete — estimated total concepts $10^7$-$10^8$ per Templeton et al. [2]; 60% of variance of residual stream still unexplained after 34M. Dead features reactivation rate 2% after 1B tokens shows optimization instability. Switch SAE expert collapse [5] reintroduces polysemanticity across experts.

4. **Faithfulness vs interpretability tradeoff:** High autointerp score does not imply causal role — SAE features capture correlative linguistic correlates of reasoning, not reasoning computation itself [6]. Contrastive activation evaluation overestimates reasoning features by 3× baseline.

5. **Circuit tracing scalability:** ACDC with SAE nodes $M=16k$ per layer $L=32$ ⇒ $5\times10^5$ nodes, edge attribution $O(M^2 L)$ prohibitive; pruning heuristics miss distributed computations stored in rank-1 subspaces of $W_{OV}$ not axis-aligned to features [4].

6. **Hardware/physics assumptions:** Fisher metric derived assuming softmax unlimited temperature; low-temp decoding sharpens Voronoi boundaries amplifying gap nonlinearly. VAE pullback metric [6] suffers decoder collapse in high-curvature regions.

7. **Safety feature dual-use:** Publishing sycophancy/deception steering vectors enables malicious manipulation; responsible release requires gating. Existence of feature does not equal model propensity to use it.

8. **Data contamination:** Pile pretraining includes arXiv text about manifolds/LRH/SAEs themselves, causing evaluation circularity for autointerpretability explanations generated by LLM judge.
