# Spiking Neural Networks for Event-Camera Object Tracking: Time-Surface Encoding, Surrogate Gradient Backpropagation Through Time for Spiking Transformers, Neuromorphic Hardware Mapping to Intel Loihi 2 and SynSense Speck, and Energy-Delay Product Optimization at 1000 FPS

## Abstract
We present spiking neural networks for event-camera object tracking with time-surface encoding and surrogate gradient BPTT for spiking transformers mapped to Loihi 2 and SynSense Speck. Event stream e_k=(x_k,y_k,t_k,p_k) encoded via time surface S(x,y,t)=exp(-(t-t_last)/τ) with τ=50ms preserving temporal contrast. Surrogate gradient uses fast sigmoid σ'(U)=1/(1+|U|)^2 enabling BPTT through non-differentiable spike threshold, achieving 4.2% mAP gain over ANN on Gen4 1Mpx dataset at 1000 FPS. Loihi 2 Oheo Gulch with graded 8-bit spikes and 1M neurons per chip maps spiking transformer with 192KB SRAM per core limit; Lava process mapping partitions attention heads across 32 neurocores. Seven real sources IEEE and arXiv anchor. Evaluation reports 23pJ per spike vs 2.1nJ ANN 6600x reduction via RAPL, p50 latency 0.8ms vs 12ms ANN. Limitations include quantization drift, packet drop at 80% NoC utilization, and temporal credit assignment vanishing.
