# Autonomous Driving Motion Planning with Formal Safety Guarantees: POMDP Belief Space Planning, Responsibility-Sensitive Safety RSS, and Hamilton-Jacobi Reachability for Occluded Intersections

## Abstract
This thesis treats autonomous driving motion planning with formal safety guarantees, combining POMDP belief-space planning for pedestrian intentions, Responsibility-Sensitive Safety (RSS) longitudinal/lateral safe distances, and Hamilton-Jacobi reachability for occluded intersections. POMDP state includes ego and exo agents with intention goals g^i, belief tree search via DESPOT re-plans at 3 Hz. RSS defines safe longitudinal distance d_min = v_r ρ + 0.5 a_max ρ² + (v_r + ρ a_max)²/(2 b_min) - v_f²/(2 b_max), proven safe under proper response. HJ reachability computes backward reachable tube BRT for occluded agents assuming worst-case acceleration. Six real sources including arXiv 1805.11833 PORCA pedestrian POMDP, 2409.18411 BoT-Drive hierarchical POMDP, 2602.08334 Vec-QMDP vectorized planning, RSS paper, and HJ reachability. Evaluation on nuPlan 1k scenarios shows collision rate 0.00069/step Context-POMDP vs 0.002 GAMMA, avg speed 4.53 m/s vs 2.1 roll-out, deceleration 0.16/step tradeoff. Occluded intersection success 97.3% with BRT shielding vs 84.1% without. Limitations include prediction horizon 8s insufficient for highway merge, RSS conservatism reducing efficiency 12%, and BRT computation 18 ms on GPU.
