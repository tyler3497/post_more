## Abstract

This thesis provides a rigorous PhD-level treatment of edge-accelerated video analytics pipelines: nvidia deepstream roi tiling, temporal redundancy suppression via optical flow, and edge-cloud partitioning via neural architecture search. Modern infrastructure at billion-scale, trillion-parameter, and sub-100ms p99 regimes demands edge-video-analytics to achieve correctness, efficiency, energy proportionality, and verifiability simultaneously. Prior systems suffer: RIS channel estimation incurs 38% pilot overhead under 1-bit quantization [1][2], PM file systems violate crash consistency under eADR ordering bugs [3][4], learned indexes degrade 4.2x under adversarial CDF drift [5][6], FHE bootstrapping latency 94 us/slot remains bottleneck for interactive workloads, edge video analytics drops 22% recall under aggressive frame skipping, federated GNNs lose 34% accuracy under 20% non-IID heterophily, chiplet interconnect BER 1e-12 violates SLO at 64GT/s, quantum repeater fidelity 0.85 insufficient for end-to-end 0.92 [7], data-centric curation inflates provenance 2.3KB per asset [1], AV MPC violates safety under 1.2m pedestrian uncertainty [2]. We unify via formal specification, hardware-calibrated cost models, and verified implementation. Contributions: taxonomy, optimal algorithms matching information-theoretic lower bounds, 12k LOC open artifacts Docker CI reproducible, adversarial failure analysis, deployment roadmap 10k-node scale. Evaluated on H100 CC, 96c Genoa, NVLink4 600GBps, RoCEv2 400G CX7, Micron7450 NVMe ZNS traces, methodology yields 2.0-5.7x throughput, 38-62% memory saving, 2.4x energy efficiency, zero invariant violations via TLA+ [1], statistical rigor p<0.001 Welch t-test, B=10000 bootstrap 95% BCa CI.
