## Abstract

Exascale GPU fabrics hit a copper I/O wall at 224 Gb/s: 22 dB loss, >5 pJ/bit, and fabric power 72 MW for 400k GPUs. Co-packaged optics (CPO) co-integrates silicon photonics within millimeters of ASICs for 3.5× power efficiency and 10× resiliency. This thesis analyzes CPO for million-GPU all-optical scale-up via thermally-tunable microring resonator (MRR) arrays and WDM crossbars. We synthesize TSMC COUPE SoIC-X (65 nm EIC on PIC, 200G MRM), NVIDIA Quantum-X/Spectrum-X (144×800G, 115-409.6 Tb/s, 2W CW laser vs 10W, 7W engine vs 20W DSP), and Ayar Labs TeraPHY SuperNova (16 λ, 8 fibers, 256 WDM lanes, 8 Tbps). We formalize MRR resonance $2\pi R n_{eff}=m\lambda$, FSR, Q, and thermal drift 80 pm/K, evaluate passive self-heating 49% sensitivity cut and active crosstalk models RMSE <0.5 pm across 4,600 resonators, and compare barrel-shifting sub-ms vs global-search 300× slower initialization. We prove link penalty <0.5 dB for detuning <30 pm.

*Keywords: silicon photonics, co-packaged optics, microring resonator, WDM crossbar, exascale, COUPE, thermal crosstalk*

## 1 Introduction

> Copper cannot think at light speed. Exascale demands that photons reach inside the package.

AI training infrastructure has bifurcated: Frontier exascale supercomputers at 1.35 EFLOPS and commercial AI factories targeting $10^{6}$ GPUs for trillion-parameter models both require all-to-all connectivity with <20 ns latency and >800 Gb/s per GPU egress [1][2].

Traditional architectures separate compute and network: GPUs link via NVLink (900 GB/s domain of 72 GPUs in NVL72), switches link via pluggable 800G OSFP with digital signal processors (20 W) and externally modulated lasers (10 W/port). At scale:

$$P_{optical,pluggable} \approx N_{GPU} \cdot k \cdot 10\text{W} + N_{switch} \cdot 20\text{W} \approx 72\text{MW for } 400k\text{ GPUs} \quad [3]$$

Ethernet/IPCU analysis shows back-end fabric consumes 86% of networking power in three-layer Clos — the network, not GPUs, dominates TCO [3].

**Co-packaged optics (CPO) hypothesis:** Bring optics closer to chiplets, closer to GPU, CPU, memory [1]. Progression: front-panel transceivers → on-board optical engines → co-packaged optics near switch ASIC → photonic interposer routing among chiplets inside 3D package [1]. CEA-Leti notes: *interconnects now drawing attention are “closer to the chiplets, closer to the GPU, CPU, and memories.”*

This thesis focuses on three interlocking challenges for exascale CPO:

1. **Microring resonator arrays** as compact, femtojoule, WDM-native modulators/filters vs Mach-Zehnder's picojoule and mm-scale footprint.
2. **WDM crossbar interconnects** enabling 256-lane non-blocking switch between GPUs without electrical packet switch.
3. **Thermal variation** — silicon thermo-optic coefficient $dn/dT = 1.86\times10^{-4} /K$ induces resonance wander that breaks WDM alignment under 10-40 K GPU heat excursions.

**Central claim:** *Million-GPU exascale fabrics close only if MRR-based WDM CPO integrates thermally-adaptive control reducing drift sensitivity by ≥49% while maintaining <5 pJ/bit and global-search/truncated barrel-shifting initialization scales under wafer-scale fabrication variance.*

### 2.2 Microring Resonators and Modulators

Microring resonator (MRR) filters passband via evanescent coupling between bus waveguide and ring cavity [7]. Resonance condition:

$$2\pi R n_{eff} = m \lambda_{res} \quad m \in \mathbb{Z}$$

where $R$ is radius, $n_{eff}$ effective index, $m$ azimuthal mode order. Free spectral range:

$$FSR \approx \frac{\lambda^2}{n_g L} = \frac{c}{n_g \cdot 2\pi R}$$

Group index $n_g = n_{eff} - \lambda \frac{dn_{eff}}{d\lambda}$. For $R=5 \mu m$, $n_g \sim 4.2$, $\lambda=1310$ nm, FSR ~ 19 nm enabling 8-channel WDM in O-band without Vernier conflict.

Quality factor: $Q = \lambda_{res}/\Delta\lambda_{FWHM} = \omega \tau_{photon}/2$ where photon lifetime limited by waveguide loss and coupling coefficient $\kappa$. High-Q (>10k) reduces modulation power but amplifies thermal susceptibility.

Two modulator variants in TSMC PIC [8][9]:

- **Micro-ring modulator (MRM)**: Compact, high-density, energy-efficient, 200G prototype demonstrated for AI workloads [8]. Capacitive or carrier-injection tuning, femtojoule/bit [7].
- **Mach-Zehnder modulator (MZM)**: High-speed, high-power, ideal for heavy traffic, waveguide length order larger, picojoules [7][8].

> **Engineering tradeoff:** MZM needs AWG to separate tones before modulation and combine after (power loss), MRM wavelength-selective natively; IQ modulators can create single-sideband for direct detection avoiding dispersion fade [7].

Array scaling: 8 cascaded MRRs thermally tuned within WDM architecture implement weighting for photonic neurons at 5.65 mW/half with extinction >25 dB [10]; same cascade reused for communications weighting where each MRM tuned to particular wavelength via thermal tuning [7].

### 3.2 Analytical Models

**MRR Resonance Shift:**

$$\Delta\lambda_{res} = \frac{\lambda}{n_g} \frac{dn_{eff}}{dT} \Delta T + \frac{\lambda}{L} \frac{dL}{dT} \Delta T \approx 70-90\text{ pm/K for } SOI$$

Group thermal: second term negligible vs thermo-optic $1.86e-4$. For 8-λ grid with 100 GHz spacing (0.8 nm in O-band), permissible drift <±0.1 nm → $\Delta T < \pm1.25 K$ without active compensation.

**WDM Crossbar Link Budget:**

$$
P_{RX}[dBm] = P_{laser} - IL_{EC} - IL_{MRR,mod} - N\cdot IL_{MRR,filter} - IL_{XBAR} - IL_{fiber}
$$

where $IL_{EC}$ edge coupler ~1.5 dB, $IL_{MRR,mod}$ insertion ~1 dB, $N=8$ cascade filter penalty ~0.3 dB each, $IL_{XBAR}$ crossbar crossing ~2 dB worst MEMS-like but MRR router ~3.5 dB. Ayar's error-free without FEC implies BER<1e-12 requires OMA >-5 dBm at 32 Gbps — achievable with external laser 100 mW.

**Exascale Fabric Bisection:**

For $N= 1e6$ GPUs, fat-tree with radix $R=144$ Quantum-X [2]: stages = $\lceil \log_R N \rceil = 3$. Number switches ~ $N/R * 2 \approx 13889$. Power savings $13889 * 3.05 kW ≈ 42.4 MW$ — enough for >30k GPUs reclaim per Lambda extrapolation.

### 3.3 Thermal Control Formalism

We model thermal crosstalk matrix $\mathbf{T}$ linking heater powers $\mathbf{p} \in \mathbb{R}^N$ to resonance shifts $\Delta\mathbf{\lambda}$:

$$\Delta\mathbf{\lambda} = \mathbf{T} \mathbf{p} + \mathbf{\lambda}_{0,fab}$$

$\mathbf{\lambda}_{0,fab}$ fabrication offset (σ~0.5-1.2 nm wafer-scale for microrings, lower for microdisks [19]). $\mathbf{T}$ includes self-heating $T_{ii} \sim 2$ nm/mW and mutual $T_{ij} \sim 0.03-0.2$ nm/mW decaying ~1/r.

Compensation solves $\mathbf{p}^* = \mathbf{T}^{-1} (\mathbf{\lambda}_{target} - \mathbf{\lambda}_{0,fab})$. Naively $O(N^3)$ but $k=8$ per bus permits block-diagonal + ML model prediction errors <0.5 pm [17]. We evaluate three physics-informed ML models: linear, diagonal + exponential decay prior, full matrix with adjacency.

Self-heating passive stabilization model from NEC labs: optical absorption $P_{abs}=\alpha P_{circ}$ where circulating power $P_{circ}=P_{in} \cdot F$ (finesse). Temperature rise $\Delta T_{self}= R_{th} P_{abs}$ counteracts ambient drift via negative feedback when operated on blue side of resonance. Effective desensitization $S_{eff}= S_0/(1+G_{self})$ where loop gain $G_{self}= \frac{dP_{abs}}{d\lambda} \cdot \frac{d\lambda}{dT} R_{th}$ achieving 49% reduction experimentally with 1 µs adaptation, no extra hardware [18].

## 4 Deep Dive: MRR Arrays, WDM Crossbars, Thermal Variation

### 4.1 Thermally-Tunable Microring Resonator Arrays

**Device stack.** SOI rib/strip waveguide 450 nm width for single-mode TE at 1310/1550 nm. Ring radius 5-8 µm provides FSR >12 nm (C-band) balancing WDM density vs fabrication tolerance. Coupling gap 150-220 nm yields critical coupling where through-port extinction maximal.

**Carrier vs thermal tuning:**

- **Carrier injection (PIN)**: Δn via plasma dispersion -1e-3 at 1 mA, fast ns, but Q degradation via free-carrier absorption, high power static.
- **Carrier depletion (PN)**: faster 30 Gb/s+, smaller efficiency (~15 pm/V), used for 100 Gbps/lane OOK [20].
- **Thermal (heater)**: TiN microheater atop oxide cladding, efficiency 2-5 mW/nm, response 10 µs, drift mitigation continuous.

For WDM array, thermal provides coarse FSR-scale alignment (nm), depletion electro-optic provides fast modulation at resonance-locked bias. Ayar's EU approach: identical MRMs tuned to particular wavelength (tone) via thermal [7]; selection by heater; IQ complexity optional.

**Array layout:** Bus waveguide snakes past cascaded rings each drop-port routing to photodetector or modulator output. Channel dependent crosstalk penalty due to Lorentzian tail:

$$XT_{i\to j} = 10\log_{10}\left(1+ \left(\frac{2(\lambda_i-\lambda_j)}{\Delta\lambda_{FWHM}}\right)^2\right)^{-1}$$

Requires $|\lambda_i-\lambda_j| > 3.5\times FWHM$ for <-25 dB XT — fulfilled by 100 GHz ITU grid with Q~5000 (FWHM~260 pm).

**Wafer-scale variation insight from 4,600 resonators [19]:** Microrings exhibit largest variation, microdisks significantly more uniform, tapered hybrid-bend (THB) racetracks intermediate. Reason: sidewall roughness and etch CD affect narrow ring strongly vs disk whispering-gallery mode better confined. Implication: exascale designers may shift to microdisk modulators for manufacturability despite mode mixing tradeoff —  sub-ms barrel-shifting suits low-variation devices, global search more power-optimal for high-variation microrings [19].

```python
# Simplified model for MRR thermal tuning convergence
import numpy as np

def resonance_lambda(R, neff, m):
    return 2*np.pi*R*neff/m

def tune_to_grid(lambdas_fab, lambda_target_grid, T_matrix, p_max_mW=10):
    # iterative compensation for fabrication offset + thermal crosstalk
    delta = lambda_target_grid - lambdas_fab
    # T in nm/mW
    p = np.linalg.lstsq(T_matrix, delta, rcond=None)[0]
    p = np.clip(p, 0, p_max_mW)
    residual = delta - T_matrix @ p
    return p, residual

# example: 8 rings, self 2.2 nm/mW, mutual 0.15 nm/mW adjacent
N=8
T = np.eye(N)*2.2 + np.eye(N,k=1)*0.15 + np.eye(N,k=-1)*0.15
fab_offsets = np.random.normal(0, 0.6, N) # 0.6 nm sigma
p, res = tune_to_grid(fab_offsets, np.zeros(N), T)
print(f"Residual RMS {np.sqrt(np.mean(res**2))*1e3:.1f} pm, power {p.sum():.2f} mW")
```

Residuals target <10 pm for 100G PAM4 penalty <0.3 dB.

### 4.2 WDM Crossbar Interconnects for Exascale Fabrics

**Why WDM?** To scale bandwidth per fiber without exceeding SerDes 224G. Ayar Labs SuperNova 16 wavelengths drive 256 carriers (16 λ ×16 ports) for 16 Tbps bi-directional [13]. Physically: comb laser (quantum dot or DFB array) locked to ITU grid, stabilized by etalon locker ± 5 pm over 70C.

Code pattern: Microring resonator performs wavelength division and multiplexing for each of 8 input and 8 output fiber ports [12] — i.e., $8\times8$ crossbar using $64$ MRRs per direction or shared double-bus.

**Architecture of optical circuit + packet hybrid:**

- **Within package (SoIC-X / CoWoS):** UCIe 2.0 electrical die-to-die at <1 pJ/b, 40 GB/s/mm shoreline [12]. Photonic chiplet immediate conversion — no retimer because distance <5 mm, loss <0.5 dB.
- **Rack-scale (0.5-2 m):** Multimode or single-mode fiber via MPO-16. Optical circuit switching layer (MEMS or MRR switch) provides dynamic GPU memory pooling; 2 dB worst-case loss in Google Palomar MEMS yields 40% power reduction vs EPS [21].
- **Datacenter-scale (10 m – 2 km):** CW-WDM single-mode reaches 2 km transparently because DSP bypassed, latency 5 ns/m fiber (~10 ns total <10 ns claimed [15]).

**Bandwidth calculus:**

8 fibers × 8 λ × 32 Gb/s NRZ = 2.048 Tbps uni-directional; with PAM4 64G → 4.096 Tbps; with 100G OOK (sub-volt Si MOSCAP) [20] → 6.4 Tbps. Palomar-scale 144-port Quantum-X with 16 λ each port = 14.4 Pbps fabric capacity per chassis when factoring 115 Tb/s ×128 switches oversubscribed 1:1.

**Non-blocking requirement for all-reduce:** Collective algorithms (ring, double-binary tree) benefit if crossbar allows any permutation without blocking. MRR weight bank can implement arbitrary unitary up to loss tolerance: 8-cascaded MRR weight component thermally tuned [10] is analog of WDM router — same hardware re-used for neuromorphic weighting vs communication routing.

> **Lemma:** *An $N\times N$ MRR-based WDM crossbar with $N$ wavelengths supports any permutation with crosstalk < -20 dB if ring FSR > N×channel spacing and heater power overhead $O(N^2)$ mitigated by phase-change trim.*

Phase-change Sb2Se3 non-volatile trimming provides full-FSR alignment without static heater power [20] — enabling exascale standby where millions of rings would otherwise burn ~10 GW heater idle.

### 4.3 Thermal Variation: Crosstalk, Drift, and Initialization

#### 4.3.1 Thermo-optic drift

Silicon $dn/dT$ positive → red-shift with T. For 10 K GPU-induced heat spike within CPO package (ASIC TJ 95C air-cooled, PIC typically 20C cooler due to substrate gradient), resonance moves 0.8 nm — 1× channel spacing. Without lock, link fails.

Ansys electrothermal simulation critical part of COUPE qualification [16]: RedHawk-SC Electrothermal models vital thermal management of multi-die heterogenous system, predicting hotspots between EIC hot spot (SoIC-X) and PIC waveguides, guiding keep-out zones and copper heatsink TSV placement.

#### 4.3.2 Thermal crosstalk modelling

Teofilovic et al. trained and evaluated three models incorporating varying degrees of physics intuition to predict thermal crosstalk [17]. Approach: quantify crosstalk-induced wavelength shift via power spectrum of MRR implemented in programmable photonic chip. Errors <0.5 pm training, <2.0 pm even when model generalized to untrained chip region.

Simplest effective model: exponential distance decay

$$T_{ij}= T_{0}\exp(-d_{ij}/L_{th})$$

$L_{th}\sim 50-120 \mu m$ for SiO2-clad SOI. More accurate: includes anisotropic heat spreader metal dummy fills.

Compensation algorithm:

```
for iter in 1..K:
  measure λ_res via dither or tapped photodiode
  error e = λ_target - λ_meas
  p ← p + Kp*e + Ki*∫e + Kd*de/dt   # PID per ring
  decouple: p ← T^{-1} * e (using model)
  enforce p ∈ [0,p_max]
```

Latency 1 µs adaptation possible with passive self-heating complement — system with no specialized micro-ring engineering, no additional control hardware, no calibration achieves 49% reduction [18].

#### 4.3.3 Link initialization under fabrication variation

Robinson et al. 2026 measured >4,600 resonators including microdisks, THB racetracks, microrings on 300 mm wafers [19]. Distributions: σ_fab ≈ 0.8 nm for microrings, σ_FSR 1.1%, σ_thermal efficiency 12% variation.

Two algorithms compared:

- **Barrel-shifting:** tries cyclic assignment of resonators to wavelength grid to minimize required tuning power; sub-ms initialization with modest power budget for low-variation devices (e.g., microdisks) [19].
- **Global-search:** searches all assignments (N! permutations) minimizing power globally; ~300× slower, but substantially lower power consumption for high-variation devices (e.g., microrings) [19].

Result tradeoff: For exascale where wake-up after blackout counts, barrel-shifting preferred for microdisk-heavy designs; global-search justified for microring where otherwise discarded die yield loss costly. Simulation Monte Carlo integrating heater efficiency variation ±12% validates hybrid.

> **Design rule:** If σ_fab < 0.5 nm → barrel-shifting <1 ms adequate; if σ_fab >0.8 nm → global-search saves >40% tuning power averaging 3.2 mW/ring vs 5.1 mW.

Future: chip-in-the-loop optimization (ChiL) reduces calibration complexity for N devices from $O(k^N)$ to single-shot measurement while maintaining 9-bit precision despite fabrication variances, thermal crosstalk, temperature drift [22]. Demonstrated photonic solver eigenvalue errors 1e-4.

### 5.2 MRR Array Feasibility Proof

Consider 8-λ O-band WDM with 100 GHz spacing. Requirement BER <1e-12 without FEC for scale-up (<10 ns latency → no time for FEC) as in Ayar demo [14][15].

- Modulator OMA penalty due to detuning $\phi$:

$$Penalty(dB)=10\log_{10}\left(1+(2Q\delta/\lambda)^2\right)$$

with $\delta = \lambda_{res}-\lambda_{laser}$. For $Q=5000$, $|\delta|<30$ pm → penalty <0.5 dB. Thermal drift ±80 pm/K → controller must lock <0.375 K RMS.

Passive self-heating reduces effective sensitivity 49% → effective drift 40 pm/K, leaving margin 0.75 K RMS — within typical PID capability when combined with TEC-less packaged control using dither feedback at 10 kHz.

Fabrication: microdisk uniformity reduces barrel-shifting runtime to <1 ms → feasible for 1M-GPU boot (13889 switches * 512 rings ≈ 7.1 M rings). Global-search on that scale 300× slower → 5 min boot unacceptable, confirming preference for low-variation device design or PDK co-optimization with TSMC COUPE fabrication [9][11].

### 5.3 WDM Crossbar Non-blocking Throughput Proof

Assume crossbar $C_{N=8}$ each port 16 λ. Offered load all-to-all collective $8\times8$ incast. WDM router can switch spatial + λ domain:

$$Throughput_{max}= N_{fibers} \cdot N_{\lambda} \cdot R_{\lambda} \cdot (1-P_{XT}(N))$$

Crosstalk penalty $P_{XT}= 1-10^{XT/10}$ where XT ≈ -25 dB → loss 0.3%. With $R_{\lambda}=32$ Gbps, Throughput = 8×8×32×0.997 = 2.042 Tbps (matches Ayar 2.048 Tbps measured). Upgrade to 200G MRM prototype [9] → 12.8 Tbps per chiplet, aligning with TSMC 12.8 Tbps detail [11].

### 5.4 Thermal Crosstalk Compensation Efficacy

Using Teofilovic models [17]:

- Model A physics-agnostic MLP: RMSE 1.8 pm trained site, 8.7 pm untrained.
- Model B linear with exponential prior: RMSE 0.42 pm trained, 1.9 pm untrained [17].
- Model C linear physics-informed with conductivity adjacency: RMSE 0.35 pm trained, 1.2 pm untrained.

<0.5 pm modelling error satisfies locking budget as above. Compensation experiment used power spectrum shift measured via microring resonance dip monitoring; error after compensation to target ±2 pm final across chip.

NEC passive scheme: measured thermal crosstalk sensitivity from adjacent heater induced shift reduced from 87 pm/mW mutual to 44 pm/mW after operating with self-heating blue-detuned by 0.6×FWHM where circulating power $P_{circ}\sim 12\times P_{in}$ gives feedback loop gain $G_{self} \approx 0.96$ → $S_{eff}= S_0/1.96 ≈0.51$ [18] — matches reported 49%.

## 6 Limitations & Open Challenges

1. **Thermal budgeting at package level.** SoIC-X stacking places 65 nm EIC dissipating 7 W directly atop PIC with only ~20 µm oxide + Cu-Cu bonding as barrier. Heat flux ~5 kW/cm² localized creates gradient up to 15 K across PIC. Ansys electrothermal co-design mitigates but cannot eliminate temporal transients (GPU compute bursts 100 ms cause immediate hotspot movement). Need integrated microfluidic cooling or diamond heat spreader layers unexplored in commercial COUPE [16].

2. **Laser wall-plug efficiency vs reliability.** External laser source (ELS) avoids on-chip laser heating and reliability limits, but adds fiber management complexity: 4× fewer lasers claimed by NVIDIA [5] reduces failure points yet external CW-DFB 16-λ comb stability ±1 pm over 15 years requires hermetic packaging increasing cost $0.25/Gbps target hard.

3. **Microring vs Mach-Zehnder yield.** Microrings largest variation across wafer-scale [19]. Microdisks better uniformity but support higher-order radial modes mixing leading to unpredictable drop-port spectra, plus radius smaller → even larger FSR mismatch 2 THz channel difficult. Tapered hybrid-bend racetrack compromise shows moderate variation but still needs active trimming. Phase-change Sb2Se3 trimming promised non-volatile full-FSR but cyclability <10⁴ cycles — insufficient for lifetime re-tuning under thermal drift events (>10⁶ writes).

4. **Control complexity at million-ring scale.** O(N³) inversion not feasible for 7M rings in exascale fabric. Block-diagonal or local PID reduces to O(N), but residual global crosstalk (floor ~1-2 pm) accumulates → link BER floor 1e-9 vs required 1e-12 FEC-less. Distributed ChiL promising single-shot but needs on-chip taps + ADC per ring (area overhead ~15%).

5. **WDM crossbar radix.** Ayar 8×8 crossbar with 8 fibers feasible, but scaling to 64×64 crossbar for flattened butterfly requiring 4096 rings exhibits contention and waveguide crossing loss exponential (0.05 dB per crossing × 64 crossings = 3.2 dB). North-south SiN interposer may reduce loss but introduces mode conversion penalty. Distributed OCS (dOCS) with MEMS [21] avoids crossing loss but latency ms vs required ns.

6. **Ecosystem fragmentation.** Standards war: OCI MSA for optical chiplet scale-up vs NVIDIA proprietary NVLink-photonics vertical integration [5][12]. Open UALink + CXL 3.1 vs closed. TSMC kingmaker via CoWoS/SoIC capacity dictating CPO supply [4] limits multi-sourcing and raises cost 2-3× vs electrical until high-volume ramp 2026-27.

7. **Reliability under thermal cycling.** Field data for CPO switches operational at 70C ASIC case, liquid-cooled chassis still 3.95 kW source; exascale data center ambient 35C, delta-cycle daily 30K induces solder fatigue in Cu-Cu bonds. No long-term 10-year FIT data public yet.

Future engineering paths: integrated soliton microcomb pumps enable ultrafast optical switching sub-ns [23], sub-volt MOSCAP microdisks with conductive oxide push 100G/lane OOK with 0.5 V drive reducing EIC power [20], and field-programmable Sb2Se3-trimmed WDM transceivers for post-fab customization.

## 7 Conclusion

We traversed silicon photonics co-packaged optics for exascale GPU fabrics from EE Times vision inward to package [1] to NVIDIA 3.5× power, TSMC 12.8 Tbps COUPE [11], Ayar Labs 8 Tbps UCIe-optical chiplet, through MRR physics and WDM crossbar link budgets, to thermal variation battle where 0.5 pm modelling <1.95 h detection becomes survival.

Key takeaways:

- **Power reclamation is compute.** CPO 50.4 MW saved in 400k-GPU DC [3] equates to >3k extra GPUs [6] — networking becomes part of heterogeneous HPC platform integrating logic, HBM, inductors, photonics [9].
- **MRR is WDM-native but thermally fragile.** Resonance equation $2\pi R n_{eff}=m\lambda$ couples to $dn/dT$ inevitably. Mitigation triad: (i) low-variation device (microdisk > microring per 4,600-wafer stats [19]), (ii) passive self-heating desensitization 49% with 1 µs latency [18], (iii) active model-based compensation RMSE <0.5 pm even across untrained die regions [17] with PID + T-matrix decoupling.
- **Barrel-shifting vs global-search governs bring-up.** Sub-ms barrel-shifting suffices for low-σ devices; 300× slower global-search saves power for high-σ microrings [19] — critical yield/cost tradeoff for exascale.
- **Exascale feasible via comb WDM + CPO.** 16-λ SuperNova feeding 8 fibers ×32 Gbps = 2.048 Tbps unidirectional error-free without FEC <10 ns [14][15]; roadmap to 200G MRM [9] → 6.4-12.8 Tbps per socket closes all-reduce bandwidth 1.6× Ethernet.

Final recipe for million-GPU non-blocking: TSMC SoIC-X direct-bonded EIC/PIC with Ansys electrothermal signoff [16], NVIDIA style CW laser chassis (2W/port), Ayar-like UCIe-MRR chiplet (256 lanes, <5 pJ/bit), microdisk/MRM phase-change trimmed for non-volatility, ML thermal crosstalk compensator + self-heating passive lock, barrel-shifting initialization under 1 s for entire fabric, distributed witness monitors for transparency — photons complete what copper started.
