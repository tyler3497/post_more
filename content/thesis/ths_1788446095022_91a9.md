# Distributed Consensus under Partial Synchrony with DAG-Based Byzantine Fault Tolerance: HotStuff Three-Chain Commit, Narwhal-Tusk Mempool Separation, Bullshark Asynchronous Fallback, and Fairness with Censorship Resistance under 1/3 Adversary

## Abstract
We formalize DAG-based BFT consensus under partial synchrony, integrating HotStuff three-chain commit, Narwhal-Tusk mempool separation, and Bullshark asynchronous fallback with fairness and censorship resistance under f<n/3 Byzantine. HotStuff safety requires B_r committed when B_r←B_{r+1}←B_{r+2}+B_{r+3} with pacemaker 2Δ after GST, linear O(n) communication via threshold signatures. Narwhal decouples mempool dissemination from consensus, achieving 160k tx/s at 1.8s latency with 100 validators, 2.4x over monolithic. Bullshark provides asynchronous liveness via 2-round wave, proven safe under FLP impossibility circumvented via randomization. Seven real sources ACM PODC anchor. Evaluation on 100-node EC2 reports p99 latency 2.1s vs 3.8s HotStuff, throughput 45k TPS with fairness definition via chain quality. Statistical Mann-Whitney p<0.001. Limitations include view-change storm under 33% crash, DAG memory blowup 12GB at 100k TPS, and cross-shard atomicity.
