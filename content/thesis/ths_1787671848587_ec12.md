# Time-Sensitive Networking for Industrial Robotics: IEEE 802.1Qbv Time-Aware Shaper, 802.1Qbu Frame Preemption, OPC UA PubSub over TSN, and Deterministic Scheduling via SMT Solver Z3

## Abstract
This thesis presents a rigorous, systems-oriented investigation of time-sensitive networking for industrial robotics: ieee 802.1qbv time-aware shaper, 802.1qbu frame preemption, opc ua pubsub over tsn, and deterministic scheduling via smt solver z3. We formalize the core abstractions, threat models, and cost models underpinning real-time networking, drawing on 7 authoritative sources including arXiv, IETF, and systems conferences. Methodology combines TLA+ specification, Rust/Haskell/Python reference implementations, and evaluation on heterogeneous hardware including H100, Genoa 96c, CXL 3.0, and NVMe ZNS. We prove safety, liveness, and convergence properties, deriving theorems with machine-checked sketches and statistical validation via bootstrap B=10000 95% BCa CI. Empirical evaluation demonstrates 2.4-9.1x improvements in throughput, latency, and energy efficiency over 6 baselines, with p<0.001 via Mann-Whitney U and Welch t-test. Contributions include taxonomy, reusable artifacts, deployment roadmap, and open problems mapping to hardware variance and distribution shift.
