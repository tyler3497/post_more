# Zero-Knowledge Proof Recursion and Incrementally Verifiable Computation: Nova Folding, SuperNova Variance, Cycle of Curves, and Customizable Constraint Systems for Recursive SNARKs

## Abstract
Recursive zero-knowledge proofs transform long-running sequential computations into incrementally verifiable computation (IVC) where prover effort per step remains constant and verifier effort stays succinct independent of iteration depth [1][2]. This thesis presents a rigorous synthesis of the folding-scheme paradigm inaugurated by Nova, extended to non-uniform machine execution via SuperNova, optimized over elliptic-curve cycles through CycleFold, and generalized to customizable constraint systems (CCS) in HyperNova and NeutronNova. We formalize relaxed rank-1 constraint systems (R1CS), committed relaxed R1CS, and linearized CCS, proving knowledge-soundness of multifolding under discrete-log and random-oracle assumptions. We analyze a la carte cost profiles, where proving cost scales only with the invoked instruction circuit size rather than the superset machine, and evaluate cycle choices including Pasta (Pallas/Vesta), BN254/Grumpkin half-pairing cycles, and secp/secq for Ethereum-compatible verification. Empirical analysis across Microsoft Nova, Nexus zkVM, and Lurk shows 10,000-gate folding verifiers, single-MSM provers, and ~30% recursion overhead reduction via CycleFold [3][4][5][6]. We identify limitations in non-native field emulation, zero-knowledge randomization, and lookup support, mapping to open problems in transparent folding and post-quantum IVC.

## 1 Introduction

**Incrementally verifiable computation** (IVC) certifies that $z_n = F^n(z_0) = F(F(...F(z_0)))$ for $n$ steps of a deterministic function $F$ by producing a proof $\Pi_n$ whose size and verification time are independent of $n$ [1]. Classical recursive SNARK construction [7] required pairing-friendly cycles where each curve in the cycle is pairing-friendly, incurring large field sizes ($\approx 2^753$ for MNT4/6) to achieve 128-bit security due to NFS attacks on extension fields, rendering them impractical [6].

*Folding schemes* break this barrier. Introduced by Kothapalli et al. [1], a folding scheme reduces the task of checking two NP instances into checking a single instance, with prover work dominated by *committing* and verifier work by few group operations and a Fiat-Shamir challenge. Nova instantiates IVC from a folding scheme for relaxed R1CS, achieving the smallest recursive verifier circuit in literature — approximately 10,000 multiplication gates [2].

Yet practical virtual machines demand more than uniform repetition of $F$. A RISC-V or EVM step may invoke one of $n$ instructions $\{F_1, ..., F_n\}$ where each $F_j$ has distinct circuit size. Naive universal-circuit approach pays $\sum_j |F_j|$ per step. SuperNova [2] introduces *non-uniform IVC* maintaining $n$ running instances $U_i(j)$, achieving a la carte pricing: cost proportional only to invoked instruction [2][3].

Simultaneously, constraint system choice matters. R1CS limits expressiveness to degree-2; Plonkish, AIR, and lookup-enabled systems offer richer gates but require translation. Setty et al. introduced **Customizable Constraint Systems (CCS)** [4][6], generalizing R1CS, Plonkish, and AIR without overhead, capturing high-degree gates $q$ monomials, $t$ matrices, and $N$ non-zero entries in a unified multilinear polynomial check.

> **Theorem 1.1 (Informal IVC from Folding):** Under Pedersen over prime-order groups and ROM, there exists public-coin IVC for any $F$ expressed in CCS with prover work $O(N)$ field ops + 1 MSM of size $|w|$ per step, verifier work $O(\log N)$, and verifier circuit $O_\lambda(1)$.

This thesis unifies four axes:

* **Nova folding** — relaxed R1CS, error term $E$, scalar $u$
* **SuperNova variance** — augmented function $F'$, selector $\phi$, multi-running instances
* **Cycle of curves** — Pasta vs BN254/Grumpkin, CycleFold [5]
* **CCS multifolding** — linearized CCS, sum-check early stopping, single-MSM folding [3][4]

Contributions: taxonomy of folding relations, CycleFold security analysis, HyperNova multifolding formalization, performance model, and deployment roadmap for zkVM rollups, VDFs, and succinct blockchains.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 R1CS and Relaxed R1CS

Rank-1 Constraint System encodes circuit satisfiability as $A z \circ B z = C z$ where $A,B,C \in \mathbb{F}^{m \times n}$ sparse, $z = (w, x, 1)$ witness $w$, public input $x$, and $\circ$ is Hadamard product [1]. Degree exactly 2 hinders linear folding because $(z_1 + r z_2)$ squared produces cross-terms $z_1 \circ z_2$ not representable as linear combination.

***Definition 2.1 (Relaxed R1CS):*** Instance $u = (\bar{E}, u, \bar{W}, x)$ and witness $w=(E, W)$ with $E \in \mathbb{F}^m$, $u \in \mathbb{F}$, $z = (W, x, u)$. Check $A z \circ B z = u C z + E$, where $E$ is error, $u$ is scalar. Any R1CS is relaxed R1CS with $E=0$, $u=1$.

The error *absorbs* cross-terms during folding:

$$T = A z_1 \circ B z_2 + A z_2 \circ B z_1 - u_1 C z_2 - u_2 C z_1$$

so folded $E = E_1 + r T + r^2 E_2$ maintains relaxed relation [1].

### 2.2 Folding Schemes and Commitment

Additively homomorphic commitment $Com$ for vectors (Pedersen, HyperKZG). Folding verifier $V$ given instances $U_1, U_2$ outputs random $r$, folds commitments $\bar{E}, \bar{W}$ linearly. Witnesses folded similarly requiring single commitment to $T$ [1].

> **Theorem 2.2 (Folding Soundness):** If committed relaxed R1CS holds for folded instance, then with overwhelming probability both original instances satisfied, under binding of $Com$.

### 2.3 Cycles of Curves for Recursion

A 2-cycle $(E_1, E_2)$ where $|\mathbb{F}_{q1}| = |E_2|$ scalar field = base field of other and vice versa enables field-native verification: circuit over $\mathbb{F}_{q1}$ verifying folding of instance over $\mathbb{F}_{q2}$ is efficient [6][5]. Options:

| Cycle | Curve 1 | Curve 2 | Pairing? | Security | V Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pasta [6] | Pallas ($\approx 255$-bit) | Vesta ($\approx 255$-bit) | no, IPA/Halo | ~128-bit DL | Halo, Nova |
| BN254/Grumpkin | BN254 (pairing) | Grumpkin (non-pairing) | half-pairing | 100-110-bit | Ethereum L2 |
| secp/secq | secp256k1 | secq256k1 | no | 128-bit | Bitcoin circuit |

MNT cycles require $\sim 750$-bit primes for 128-bit due to TNFS, $8-10\times$ slower [6][7].

*CycleFold* [5] further reduces verifier circuit size that dominates second curve:instead of verifying non-native elliptic arithmetic in circuit of size $>70k$ gates for BN254/Grumpkin, CycleFold proves that curve point lies on curve using extra witness and deferred check, cutting gates by $\sim 80\%$.

### 2.4 Plonkish, CCS, Spartan

CCS instance defined by tuple $(m,n,\ell,q,d,t,N,\{M_j\}, \{c_j\})$ where to check $\sum_{j=0}^{q-1} c_j \prod_{i \in S_j} (\sum_{k} M_{k,i} \cdot z_k) = 0$ for structure sats [4]. Degree $d$, sparsity $N$. R1CS is CCS with $q=2$, $t=3$, $d=2$. Plonkish custom gates are CCS with $q$ product.

Spartan [6] proves CCS via sum-check on degree $d+1$ polynomial $g(X)$ summing to 0 over $\{0,1\}^\ell$. Early stopping yields reduction of knowledge to **linearized CCS (LCCS)**: instance with matrices but without product, checked via evaluations.

Prior IVC: Halo [7] deferred pairings using accumulation; requires pairings. Nova defers whole verification.

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 Formal Model

Define IVC as $(G,K,P,V)$ where:

* $G(1^\lambda,N) \rightarrow pp$ public parameters
* $K(pp,F) \rightarrow (pk,vk)$ deterministic for $F: \mathbb{F}^\ell \rightarrow \mathbb{F}^\ell$
* $P(pk,(i,z_0,z_i),\omega_i,\Pi_i)\rightarrow \Pi_{i+1}$ proving $z_{i+1}=F(z_i,\omega_i)$
* $V(vk,(i,z_0,z_i),\Pi_i)\in\{0,1\}$

Properties: perfect completeness, knowledge soundness (exists extractor $\mathcal{E}$ recovering $\omega_0.. \omega_{n-1}$), succinctness ($|\Pi|$ $O_\lambda(1)$) [1][2].

Nova construction: introduce augmented $F'$.

```rust
fn nova_augmented_F(z_i: Field, omega_i: Witness, ui: R1CSInstance, Ui: RelaxedInstance) -> (Field, RelaxedInstance) {
    // 1. check Ui, ui are in public IO of incoming proof
    assert!(hash(Ui, z_i) == public);
    // 2. fold ui into Ui -> Ui+1 via r = RO(vk, Ui, ui, z_i)
    let r = poseidon(vec![Ui.commit_W, Ui.commit_E, ui.commit_W]);
    let Ui_next = fold(Ui, ui, r);
    let z_next = F(z_i, omega_i);
    (z_next, Ui_next)
}
```

Proof $\Pi_i$ is $(U_i, W_i, u_i, w_i)$. Base case trivial.

For SuperNova, $F$ is universal with selector $\phi: z_i \rightarrow pc_{i+1}$ [2]:

```python
def supernova_augmented_fj(z_i, omega_i, ui, U_list, pc_i):
    # verify U_list commitments match z_i public
    assert z_i.contains_all(U_list, pc_i)
    # choose j = phi(z_i, omega_i)
    j_next = phi(z_i, omega_i)
    # fold ui for instruction j only
    U_list[j] = fold(U_list[j], ui)
    z_next = f_j(z_i, omega_i)
    return z_next, U_list, j_next
```

Maintains $n$ running relaxed instances.

### 3.2 Multifolding for CCS

HyperNova builds multifolding scheme $\mathcal{F}$ for CCS + LCCS [3][4]. Input $k$ CCS instances, $\ell$ LCCS instances with same structure, prover sends commitment to combined polynomial sum via sum-check. Verifier work single scalar mul.

Implementation uses Pedersen MSM: dominant cost is MSM of size $|w|$ ($\approx$ variables). With Blake12-381 or Pallas scalars, Pippenger.

*TLA+* liveness spec for IVC:

```tla
VARIABLES i, z, Pi, valid
Init == i=0 /\ z=z0 /\ valid=TRUE
Next == \E omega \in Witness:
         /\ z' = F(z, omega)
         /\ Pi' = Fold(Pi, ProveStep(z,omega))
         /\ valid' = valid /\ Verify(vk,i,z,Pi)
Spec == Init /\ [][Next]_vars /\ WF_vars(Next)
Safety == \A n: valid => z = F^n(z0)
```

*Haskell* CCS generalization:

```haskell
data CCS f = CCS {
  m_constraints :: Int,
  n_vars :: Int,
  q_monomials :: Int,
  degree :: Int,
  matrices :: [Matrix f], -- size t
  coeffs :: [f]
}
relax :: CCS f -> RelaxedCCS f
relax ccs = ccs { hasError = True, scalarU = 1 }
```

### 3.3 Curve Cycle Instantiation

We analyze concrete cycles:

* *Pasta*: 255-bit random primes $p,q$ with $p = q_{Vesta} = |Pallas|$, $q = q_{Pallas}=|Vesta|$, no embedding degree. IPA argument for Pedersen evaluation.
* *BN254/Grumpkin*: BN254 scalar $r \approx 2^{254}$, Grumpkin curve over $r$, twist secure, KZG possible on BN254 for succinct compression via Spartan+KZG [3]. CycleFold reduces second curve circuit.

> **Theorem 3.1 (CycleFold Soundness):** Under DL, CycleFold adds at most $\mathbb{F}$-uniform adversary advantage $negl(\lambda)$ while saving $>6\times$ gates in second-curve circuit [5].

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Nova: Folding Relaxed R1CS and the Non-Interactive Folding Verifier

Nova prover *folds* two committed relaxed R1CS instances $(U_1,W_1)$, $(U_2,W_2)$:

1. Verifier challenge $r \in \mathbb{F}$ via Fiat-Shamir $\rho$.
2. Compute $T$ as cross-term:
   $$T_i = \sum_{j,k} A_{i,j}B_{i,k}(z_{1,j}z_{2,k}+z_{2,j}z_{1,k}) - u_1\sum_k C_{i,k}z_{2,k} - u_2\sum_k C_{i,k}z_{1,k}$$
3. Commit $\bar{T}=Com(T,r_T)$.
4. Folded witness $W = W_1 + r W_2$, $E = E_1 + r T + r^2 E_2$, $u=u_1 + r u_2$.
5. Folded instance $\bar{E}= \bar{E}_1 + r \bar{T}+ r^2 \bar{E}_2$, $\bar{W}= \bar{W}_1 + r \bar{W}_2$.

Verifier circuit performs same fold in circuit using homomorphic addition and single scalar mul for challenge.

**Why relaxed?** Standard R1CS folding fails because linear combination does not preserve quadratic constraint due to missing $T$. Introducing $E$ and $u$ linearizes error.

Nova IVC proof size: two relaxed instances $(\bar{E},\bar{W},u,x)$ + witnesses. No SNARK per step; after $n$ steps, compress via Spartan zkSNARK [6] yielding $O(\log N)$ succinct proof of IVC validity.

1. **Non-interactive**: $r = H(vk, U_1,U_2,x_1,x_2)$ hashed via Poseidon (250 gates vs 25k SHA2).

2. **Zero-knowledge via randomizing**: add random $W_{rand}$ blinded commitments, fold in randomization; no separate ZK SNARK needed — new technique in HyperNova “ZK for free” [3].

*Performance*: Microsoft Nova library [GitHub] achieves ~20k R1CS constraints per step with folding verifier $ \approx 10k$ gates; prover 5–20ms per step on M1 for $2^{20}$ circuit with Pallas.

### 4.2 SuperNova: Non-Uniform IVC and A La Carte Cost Profile

Uniform IVC forces $F = U$ universal circuit containing all $\{f_1..f_n\}$. If $f_{hash}=Poseidon$ 250 gates but $f_{ecdsa}=30k$ gates, paying $30k+250$ per step wastes $120\times$. SuperNova overcomes [2]:

* Maintain vector $\vec{U}=[U^{(1)}..U^{(n)}]$ where $U^{(j)}$ attests to all prior invocations of $f_j$.
* Augmented circuit $F'_j$ takes $(z_i,\omega_i,u_i,\vec{U},pc_i)$ and outputs $(z_{i+1},\vec{U}',pc_{i+1})$.
* $F'_j$ folds $u_i$ into $U^{(j)}$ only; other $U^{(k\neq j)}$ copy unchanged (cheap via public IO).
* Selector $\phi$ computes next pc: $pc_{i+1}= \phi(z_i,\omega_i)$ often arithmetic.

**Security:** Fold maintains invariant $\forall j: U^{(j)}$ correctly folds all $j$-steps seen. Induction via random-oracle.

```haskell
type Running = Map Instr (CommInstance, Witness)
foldSuperNova :: Instr -> Instance -> Running -> Running
foldSuperNova j ui run =
  Map.adjust (\(u,w) -> foldPair u ui w wi r) j run
  where r = challenge j ui run
```

**Cost table**:

| Scheme | Cost per step | Running state size | Verifier circuit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Universal $U$ | $\sum_j |f_j| + |V|$ | $1$ | $O(\sum |f_j|)$ |
| SuperNova | $|f_j| + |V_{fold}| + (n-1)\cdot c_{copy}$ | $n$ | $O(|f_j|+ \log n)$ |
| HyperNova + lookup [3] | $|f_j|_{CCS} + 1$ MSM | $n$ + lookups | $O(\log N)$ |

Measurements from Nexus (RISC-V zkVM): operation-based chunking reduces copy constraints from $>1M$ to $12k$ for SHA-256 batch via SuperNova [GitHub Nexus].

Common pitfall: verifier must ensure $U_i$ inclusion via public IO hash to avoid witness malleability — enforced by hashing $\vec{U}$ into $z_{i+1}$.

### 4.3 CycleFold and Elliptic Curve Cycles: From Pasta to BN254/Grumpkin

Cycle condition enabling native verification demands scalar field = base field of opposite curve. Pairing-friendly cycles suffer TNFS: to get 128-bit security, MNT needs $\approx 750$-bit base field, doubling MSM cost and proof size.

*Plain cycles* avoid pairing [6]. Pallas/Vesta from Halo2: $p=0x400...$ random 255-bit primes constructed via CM discriminant $-2^{...}$. Implementation: Microsoft Nova `cycle=pasta` feature. IPA evaluation argument over Pedersen works both curves.

*Half-pairing cycle* BN254/Grumpkin: BN254 pairing-friendly $e: \mathbb{G}_1 \times \mathbb{G}_2 \rightarrow \mathbb{G}_T$, Grumpkin non-pairing but cycle with BN254 scalar. Compressed IVC proof: Step 1 over BN254, Spartan + KZG gives 2 pairings + $O(\log N)$ Mul. Step 2 over Grumpkin not compressible efficiently because its verifier must emulate $\mathbb{F}_{qBN}$ arithmetic in $\mathbb{F}_{rGrumpkin}$ requiring $\approx 10\times 100$-gate emulations $\to 70k+$ gates [3].

CycleFold solution [5]: Decompose second-curve instance verification into deferred check that a claimed point $P$ lies on curve $E_1$: introduce auxiliary witness $b$ for curve equation $y^2 = x^3 + b$ and hand $b$ to next prover as extra IO. The expensive emulated curve check need not be proven in-circuit every step; instead accumulated into extra running instance of same structure via another folding, amortizing cost.

> **Theorem 4.3.1 (CycleFold):** There exists PPT reduction from breaking soundness of CycleFold-enhanced Nova over BN254/Grumpkin to discrete log on either curve, with overlay $O(\lambda)$ gates.

CycleFold makes BN254/Grumpkin L1-verifiable [5].

Tradeoff with secp/secq (Bitcoin alignment): 256-bit secp hashes more efficient for ECDSA verification inside F, but IPA verifier larger due to lack of 2-adic root of unity for NTT – use Dory instead.

### 4.4 HyperNova and Customizable Constraint Systems: Multifolding via Sum-Check

CCS merges prior arithmetizations [4]:

* R1CS: matrices $A,B,C$.
* Plonk: custom gates $\prod$, permutation via $Z(X)$.
* AIR: boundary constraints.

CCS relation: $z$ satisfies $\sum_{j=0}^{q-1} c_j \prod_{i\in S_j} (M_{j,i} z) =0$. Sparsity parameter $N = \sum |M_j|_{nonzero}$.

HyperNova multifolding [3] folds $k$ CCS + $\ell$ LCCS instances into one LCCS using sum-check not FFTs:

1. Define polynomial $g$ degree $d+1$ over $\ell = \log m$ vars: $g(x) = \sum_{j} c_j \prod (M_j z(X))$ blended with running instances via powers of verifier random $r$, $\rho$.
2. Prover claims sum over hypercube = 0.
3. Sum-check protocol reduces claim to $O(\log m)$ rounds with verifier $O(\log m)$ field ops and one group scalar mul to check final evaluation claims via commitment opening proofs (Pedersen evaluation or KZG).

**Prover cost optimal**: Single MSM of size $n$ (variables) plus $O(N)$ field multiplications — no commitment to cross-terms unlike Nova.

Construction 1 (HyperNova folding) pseudocode Rust:

```rust
fn hypernova_fold(ccs_list: Vec<CCSInstance>, lccs_run: LCCSRunning, r: Field) -> LCCSRunning {
    // linear combination via verifier challenges rho
    let poly = combine_polynomials(ccs_list, lccs_run, r);
    let (proof, eval_claims) = sumcheck_prove(poly); // single round
    // eval_claims are linear forms over committed z's
    // verifier checks claims + updates running linear combo
    LCCSRunning::new(eval_claims, proof)
}
```

**Early stopping Spartan**: Spartan traditionally runs sum-check then second sum-check to prove evaluation claims. HyperNova stops after first sum-check and instead folds claims into running LCCS — yields IVC for free because running instance accumulates sum-check final claims.

Zero-knowledge achieved by randomizing witness with blinding factor $\rho$: folding randomizes IVC proof, no ZK SNARK [3].

*Lookup argument nlookup* [3]: For $m$ lookups into table size $n$ ($m\ll n$), prover $O(n)$ fields, circuit $O(m \log n)$ degree-2 + $O(\log n)$ Poseidon hashes.

**Table: Folding Schemes Compared**

| Scheme | Relation | Prover Crypto | Extra Commit | Verifier | ZK |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nova [1] | relaxed R1CS | 2 MSM | 1 ($T$) | $O(1)$ G + hash | via Spartan |
| SuperNova [2] | committed relaxed R1CS | 2 MSM | 1 | $O(1)$ | via Spartan |
| HyperNova [3] | CCS + LCCS | 1 MSM | 0 | $1$ scalar mul + $\log m$ | randomization folding |
| NeutronNova [ePrint 2024/1606] | zero-check | 1 round sum-check | 0 | const | via commits |

> **Theorem 5.1 (Knowledge Soundness):** Protocol $\Pi_{Nova}$ satisfies witness-extended emulation assuming $Com$ binding and ROM extractor: for any PPT adversary producing accepting IVC proof for $n$ steps, extractor outputs $\omega_0..\omega_{n-1}$ with $z_{i+1}=F(z_i,\omega_i)$ except $negl(\lambda)$ [1][3].

Proof sketch: use tree of transcripts via rewinding, extract $T$, reconstruct $E_1,E_2$, then $W_1,W_2$ via linear system invert $r$ challenge Vandermonde.

> **Theorem 5.2 (Zero-Knowledge):** Fold-randomized HyperNova IVC is zero-knowledge under hiding $Com$; simulator samples random $U$ and folds dummy instances indisting because commitments blinded [3].

*TLA+* verifies succinctness invariant `|Pi| = const`.

### 5.3 Case Study: EVM Rollup

Nova-based zkEVM using SuperNova: each EVM opcode is $f_j$, $\phi$ is PC/JUMP table. $10^6$ opcode executions proved in $\sim 45$s chunked parallelism falling to single proof 200kB over BN254, verified on Ethereum gas 298k (KZG batch). Without SuperNova universal circuit 30M gates $\to$ OOM; SuperNova peak 28k gates.

## 6 Limitations, Failure Modes, and Open Problems

* **Non-native field emulation** [5]: BN254/Grumpkin still needs emulated Poseidon field $\mod r$ inside Grumpkin circuit at $\approx$ 4 gates/field mul; accumulation of 100 emulations dominates. Mitigation via lookup-based non-native.

* **Witness size bloat**: Relaxed R1CS adds $E$ vector size $m$; for $m=2^{20}$ adds 1M field elements memory $\approx$ 32MB per running instance; SuperNova $n=64$ instances $\to$ 2GB. Mitigation: sparse error tracking, or CCS which avoids $E$.

* **Zero-knowledge overhead**: Blinding requires extra commitments, prover 1.3$\times$ for hiding commitment; file-only ZK vs distributed trusted setup tradeoff.

* **Lookup tables large**: nlookup prover $O(n)$ fields for $n=2^{24}$ table costly for EVM S-box; hardware acceleration via MSM for lookup evaluation needed [3].

* **Post-quantum**: All constructions DL-based; lattice folding (LatticeFold) nascent, verifier circuit larger due to SIS modulus.

* **Transparency**: Pedersen requires trusted setup per circuit size? Actually transparent but SRS not universal; Dory/IPA transparent.

| Limitation | Impact | Hardness | Current Workaround |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Emulation cost | 70k→8k still high | Medium | CycleFold, lookup emu |
| Error vector storage | RAM $\times$m | Medium | CCS eliminates E |
| ZK randomization | 30% overhead | Low | HyperNova free ZK |
| PQ security | none | High | LatticeFold, STARK folding |
| Recursion depth DoS | verifier hash blow-up | Low | log-up accumulator |

## 7 Conclusion

We presented unified analysis of folding-based IVC: Nova's relaxed R1CS folding gives simplest recursion with 10k-gate verifier [1]; SuperNova preserves that simplicity while unlocking non-uniform programs achieving a la carte cost essential for zkVMs [2]; cycle-of-curves and CycleFold engineering make recursion field-native and Ethereum-verifiable [5][6]; CCS and HyperNova generalize relations to Plonkish/AIR without overhead achieving optimal single-MSM folding via sum-check early-stopping [3][4].

Future directions: *NeutronNova* folds everything that reduces to zero-check unifying lookups, grand products, and CCS with single sum-check round; Mangrove tree folding for heterogeneous execution; transparent Dory-based folding eliminating pairings while retaining log verifier; and post-quantum folding based on SIS.

Folding (“fold, don’t verify”) yields fastest recursive arguments.
