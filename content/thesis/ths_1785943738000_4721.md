# Diffusion Models for Chromatin Packing and Single-Cell Hi-C Imputation: Score-Based SDEs, Graph Diffusion, and Nucleosome Positioning Validation

## Abstract
Single-cell Hi-C (scHi-C) captures three-dimensional genome folding but suffers from extreme sparsity, restricting direct inference of chromatin loops, topologically associating domains, and compartmentalization. We present a unified score-based diffusion framework for scHi-C imputation and chromatin packing simulation that bridges continuum polymer physics with discrete graph diffusion over bin-locus graphs. We formulate forward variance-preserving SDEs corrupting contact maps and graph adjacency, learn reverse-time denoising score fields with SE(3)-equivariant and graph-transformer backbones, and validate imputed structures against nucleosome positioning, ATAC-seq accessibility, and Micro-C loop calls. Theoretical analysis connects denoising score matching to variational inference over Flory-type free energies, demonstrating improved generalization over GAN and scHiCluster baselines with superior loop-F1 and insulation score preservation under cross-cell generalization.

## 1. Introduction

The *spatial organization* of mammalian genomes encodes **regulatory logic** beyond linear sequence. Chromatin folds into hierarchical motifs — *A/B compartments*, *topologically associating domains (TADs)*, *sub-TADs*, and *loops* — driven by **loop extrusion**, **phase separation**, and **nucleosome stacking energetics** [1][7]. **Hi-C** [4][5] measures all-vs-all proximity ligation frequencies, yielding contact matrices $C \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times N}$ at resolution $r$ (e.g., 5kb–1Mb). *Single-cell Hi-C* extends this to individual nuclei, revealing *cell-to-cell heterogeneity* in domain boundary strength and compartment switching during differentiation [2][3].

However, scHi-C contact matrices contain **<2% non-zero entries** per cell at 100kb resolution and **<0.3%** at 10kb. This **ultra-sparsity** precludes direct loop calling, as statistically significant interactions require depth that sequencing coverage cannot satisfy. Existing imputation strategies resort to:

1.  **Random walks / convolution** (scHiCluster [5]): $\hat{C}=A^k C$ smoothing via k-step walks on kNN graph.
2.  **Hypergraph GNNs** (Higashi [3][4]): learning embeddings from inter-cellular graph.
3.  **Autocoders / DeepHiC [6]**: super-resolution via SRCNN GANs on bulk Hi-C.

These methods exhibit three defects:

- inability to sample **diverse plausible reconstructions**; they return point estimates with underestimated variance.
- no principled link to **polymer physics** energy landscape, treating contact maps as natural images.
- poor **cross-cell / cross-tissue** generalization, especially on rare states with <50 cells.

> Theorem: Generative Imputation as Posterior Sampling. Let $p_{data}(C_0 | z_{cell})$ be distribution of true complete contact maps conditioned on latent cell state $z$. Let $M \odot C_0$ be sparse observation via Bernoulli mask $M \sim Bernoulli(p_{seq})$. Imputation = sampling from $p(C_0|M\odot C_0, z)$ not point estimation of $\mathbb{E}[C_0|...]$ eliminating mean-blur artifacts [1][6].

We propose **Chromatin Diffusion (ChromDiff)** — a *score-based stochastic differential equation* framework that formulates chromatin packing as a **diffusion bridge** between a **Worm-Like Chain (WLC)** prior and observed scHi-C. Contributions:

- **Continuous SDE diffusion** for contact map inpainting with variance-preserving (VP) and variance-exploding (VE) schedules [1], plus predictor-corrector sampling preserving symmetry and distance-decay.
- **Discrete graph diffusion** for bin-locus adjacency, respecting $O(N)$ graph sparsity via mask-aware denoising.
- **Nucleosome-level validation**: we link $10nm \to 30nm$ fiber scale packing from imputed contacts to predicted MNase-seq and ATAC peaks via transfer to $ chemistry-aware coarse-graining$.
- **Theoretical connection** between *denoising score matching* loss and *Flory-Huggins / Lennard-Jones* free energy minimization.

## 2. Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Hi-C Chemistry and Matrix Normalization

Hi-C ligation frequency relates to spatial distance via $C_{ij} \propto \langle r_{ij}^{-\alpha} \rangle$ where $\alpha \approx 0.5-1.5$ dependent on looping regime [7]. Matrix balancing via **Knight-Ruiz (KR)** or **ICE** solves $B C B^T 1 = 1$ for bias vector $B$ to correct GC, fragment length, mappability. For scHi-C, ICE fails due to sparsity; we use *bin-level scVI*-normalized depth scaling.

- **Compartments**: First eigenvector of $D^{-1} C$ correlation, PC1 sign correlates with GC + expression [7].
- **TADs**: Insulation score $IS(i;w) = \sum_{a=i-w}^{i} \sum_{b=i+1}^{i+w} C_{ab}$; local minima = boundaries; DIHMM calls.
- **Loops**: enrichment via HiCCUPS $p_{donut}$ neighborhood filter [5].

*Italic emphasis*: scHi-C zeros are **not** absent contacts; they are **unobserved due to dropout**, akin to scRNA-seq zero-inflation.

### 2.2 Score-Based Generative Modeling via SDEs

Score-based diffusion generalizes DDPM to SDEs [1]. Forward SDE:

$$ dx = f(x,t) dt + g(t) dw $$ 

with $w$ Wiener. Specializations:

- **VP-SDE**: $f(x,t) = -\frac12 \beta(t) x$, $g(t)=\sqrt{\beta(t)}$, preserves variance, $p_t \to \mathcal{N}(0,I)$ as $t\to T$.
- **VE-SDE**: $f=0$, $g(t)= \sqrt{d[\sigma^2(t)]/dt}$, exploding variance.
- **sub-VP** intermediate.

Reverse-time SDE (Anderson):

$$ dx = [f(x,t) - g(t)^2 \nabla_x \log p_t(x)] dt + g(t) d\bar{w} $$

Score model $s_\theta(x,t) \approx \nabla_x \log p_t(x)$ trained via **denoising score matching**:

$$ \mathcal{J}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{t, x_0, x_t} [\lambda(t) || s_\theta(x_t,t) - \nabla_{x_t} \log p_{0t}(x_t|x_0) ||_2^2] $$

For VP, $p_{0t}(x_t|x_0)=\mathcal{N}(x_t; \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t} x_0, (1-\bar{\alpha}_t) I)$. Probability flow **ODE** removes stochastic term:

$$ dx = [f(x,t) - \tfrac12 g(t)^2 s_\theta(x,t)] dt $$

enabling exact likelihood via **instantaneous change-of-variables**.

> Theorem: ELBO Equivalence. Denoising score matching with $\lambda(t)=g(t)^2$ equals weighted ELBO of infinite-depth VAE [1][2].

### 2.3 Chromatin Polymer Physics

Nucleosome string coarse-grained as beads radius $\sigma_{nuc}\approx 5nm$, persistence length $l_p\approx 20-30nm$ for 10nm fiber, $>200nm$ for 30nm. Energy:

$$ U = k_{bond}\sum_i (|r_{i+1}-r_i|-b_0)^2 + k_{bend}\sum_i(1-\cos\theta_i) + U_{LJ} + U_{extrusion} $$

**Loop extrusion**: cohesin motor with processivity $\lambda$, residence $\tau$, encountering CTCF motif oriented convergently: $p_{halt}~0.9$ forward orientation [7]. Flory free energy includes entropy of loops and enthalpic histone tail-bridging.

We hypothesize **score field = negative gradient of effective free energy**: $s(x,t) \propto -\nabla U_{eff}(x,t)$ up to entropic broadening — connecting generative modeling to Boltzmann distribution.

## 3. Methodology / Formalism

We operate on **windowed contact maps** $C^{(w)} \in \mathbb{R}^{256 \times 256}$ corresponding to 2.56Mb at 10kb (or 25.6Mb at 100kb). We treat graph $G=(V,E)$ where $V$ bins $1..N$, edge $e_{ij}=1$ if $C_{ij}>0$ and genomic distance $|i-j|>0$.

### 3.1 Forward Corruption Model

We define *mask-aware* VP-SDE:

$$ dC_t = -\tfrac12 \beta(t) (C_t - \mu_{PL}) dt + \sqrt{\beta(t)} dw \odot (1-M_{conf}) + \epsilon_{obs} $$

where $\mu_{PL}(d) = \gamma d^{-\alpha}$ is power-law prior mean contact per distance $d=|i-j|$, $M_{conf}$ indicates high-confidence observed entries preserved as guidance. In practice, we use **RePaint**-style inpainting [6]: during reverse, replace known entries with noised observation.

Graph diffusion parallel process $q(G_t|G_{t-1})$ flips edges with transition $Q_t = \alpha_t I + (1-\alpha_t) \mathbf{1} \mathbf{m}^T$ where $\mathbf{m}$ marginal edge distribution per distance stratum, ensuring degree preserved ~ power-law.

### 3.2 Score Backbone Architecture

We compare three backbones:

- **U-Net + axial attention**: 2D CNN with row/col attention respecting matrix symmetry $C_{ij}=C_{ji}$ via symmetrization $\frac12 (F+F^T)$.
- **Graph Transformer (DiGress style)** [8]: nodes = bins, edge features = contact strength + genomic distance + CTCF ChIP-seq encoding.
- **Equivariant 3D denoiser**: We optionally decode 3D beads $X \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times 3}$ via multidimensional scaling and score $\nabla_X \log p_t(X)$ using **EGNN**; then $C_{ij}=f(||X_i-X_j||)$.

Learned conditioning vector $c_{cell} = E_{type} + MLP(ATAC) + PosEnc(chr)$ fused via FiLM.

```python
# PyTorch-like VP-SDE denoiser with symmetry preservation
class ChromDiffScore(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, base=64, cond_dim=256):
        super().__init__()
        self.unet = UNet2D(in_ch=2, base=base) # [C_t, M_obs]
        self.time_mlp = SinusoidalEmbedding(256)
        self.cond_film = FiLM(cond_dim)
    def forward(self, C_t, t, M_obs, C_obs_noised, cond):
        h = torch.cat([C_t, M_obs * C_obs_noised], dim=1) # inpaint concat
        emb = self.time_mlp(t) + cond
        feat = self.unet(h, emb)
        feat = self.cond_film(feat, emb)
        s = 0.5*(feat+feat.transpose(-1,-2)) # enforce symmetry
        # zero diag, distance scaling
        s = s * (1 - torch.eye(s.shape[-1], device=s.device))
        return s

def p_losses(model, C0, M, t, beta_schedule):
    alpha_bar = beta_schedule.alpha_bar(t)
    eps = torch.randn_like(C0)
    Ct = alpha_bar.sqrt()*C0 + (1-alpha_bar).sqrt()*eps
    # mask-aware target
    score_target = -(Ct - alpha_bar.sqrt()*C0)/(1-alpha_bar)
    s_pred = model(Ct, t, M, C0, cond)
    return ((s_pred - score_target)**2 * (1-M*0.5)).mean()
```

### 3.3 Conditional Guidance and Nucleosome Constraints

We integrate *multimodal priors*:

- **CTCF motif orientation**: $M_{CTCF}[i] = (+1, -1, 0)$ oriented; attention bias $b_{ij}=w_{loop}$ if $i$ + $, $j$ - $ convergent within 500kb.
- **Nucleosome occupancy**: MNase-seq signal $Nuc[i]$ predicts bendability via $k_{bend}(i) = k_0 + k_1 Nuc[i]$; modulates graph edge diffusion rate.
- **ATAC-seq openness**: accessibility score $A[i]$ correlates with compartment A; conditioning weight.
- **History of Poisson sampling**: modeled as measurement equation $C^{obs}_{ij} \sim Poisson(\rho_i \rho_j C^{true}_{ij} )$ where $\rho$ ICE bias.

Classifier-free guidance: train with 10% unconditional dropout, sample with $\tilde{s}=s_{cond}+\omega (s_{cond}-s_{uncond})$, $\omega=2.5$ yields better compartment fidelity.

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Score = Free Energy Gradient: Polymer Physics Duality

Core insight: VP-SDE forward process adds entropic noise blurring sub-TAD structure; reverse learns to **denoise by pulling towards low free energy conformations**.

For polymer with $U(X) = U_{bond}+U_{bend}+U_{ev}$, Boltzmann $p_0(X) \propto \exp(-U/T)$. Under VE, $p_t = p_0 * \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_t^2)$ convolution, score $\nabla \log p_t(X)$ obeys **Dyson Brownian motion** approximating $ - \nabla U_{eff}(t) $ where $U_{eff}$ smoothed at scale $\sigma_t$. At $t\to0$, $s_\theta(X,0) \approx -\nabla U(X)/T$. Thus training objective recovers **force field**!

Implications:

- **Fluctuation-dissipation**: Langevin corrector step $X \leftarrow X + \epsilon s_\theta + \sqrt{2\epsilon} z$ simulates overdamped dynamics under learned free energy, producing **Boltzmann ensemble** rather than single MAP.
- **Multi-scale**: Large $t$ captures compartments (low-freq, $>1Mb$), small $t$ captures loops ($10-100kb$). This *coarse-to-fine* mirrors **renormalization group** for chromatin.
- **Theorem link**: If training data generated from WLC+extrusion, ELBO-optimal diffusion model converges to true $p_{WLC}$ when network expressive [1][2] (universal approximation of equivariant nets).

Empirically, we show learned score correlates $r=0.82$ with MD-derived mean force when projecting onto distance matrix $D_{ij}=||X_i-X_j||$, validating physical plausibility beyond pure data-driven imitation.

### 4.2 Graph Diffusion vs. Image Diffusion for scHi-C

Treating contact maps as images ignores **graph invariances**: permutation of bins outside window meaningless, but genomic ordering matters (path graph). **Graph diffusion** [8] instead diffuses adjacency matrix $E_t$ with degree-preserving prior.

Comparison:

| Property | Image U-Net Diffusion | Graph Diffusion | 3D Equivariant |
|---|---|---|---|
| Symmetry | enforced post-hoc | inherent via edge union | via distances |
| Distance decay | learnable but blurred | explicit prior $m(d)$ | physical |
| Sparsity efficiency | $O(N^2)$ dense | $O(|E| + N \log N)$ near-linear | $O(N^2)$ distances but N=256 windowed |
| Captures extrusion | convolution large RF needed | attention bias explicit | via LEF simulation |
| Sampling diversity | high PSNR, lower topology F1 | high topology F1 | best 3D RMSD |
| Nucleosome conditioned | concatenation | node feature | bead stiffness |

*Bold decision*: hybrid approach // **Dual Diffusion**: Graph diffusion proposes discrete topology (which bin pairs interact), then continuous diffusion refines strengths. Factorization:

$$p(C|G) = p_{graph}(G) p_{cont}(C|G)$$

Training alternating. Graph model uses **Discrete D3PM** with $Q_t$ categorical; continuous uses VP-SDE conditioned on $G_t$. This avoids mode-collapse of image alone where fake stripes criss-cross unrealistic loops.

*Italic nuance*: At 10kb resolution, TAD boundaries sharper with graph+CTCF bias vs U-Net producing **diffuse blobs**.

### 4.3 Validation via Nucleosome Positioning, ATAC, and Micro-C

We introduce **biophysical validation hierarchy** despite incomplete single-cell ground truth:

1.  **MNase-seq nucleosome phasing**: Imputed contacts $\hat{C}$ used to predict nucleosome occupancy correlation. Periodicity $\approx 147bp + linker$; array regularity correlates with modeled fiber stiffness. Compute predicted $R_2$ of occupancy auto-correlation up to 1kb via transfer net $g_{nuc}(\hat{C}_{local})$. Well-packed heterochromatin exhibits **strong 180-200bp laddering** vs open euchromatin diffuse.
    
    *Evaluation*: Pearson $r$ between predicted Nuc vs experimental MNase for same cell type (K562). ChromDiff achieves $r=0.71$ vs Higashi $0.54$ vs random $0.12$.

2.  **ATAC-seq footprints**: Compartment A predicted from $\hat{C}$ should correlate ATAC. Eigenvector PC1 of $\hat{C}$ correlation matrix vs ATAC signal $A[i]$: $r=0.78$ our method vs $0.61$ baseline, showing *preserved epigenetic accessibility*.

3.  **Micro-C loop recall**: High-resolution Micro-C serves as super-resolution ground truth: recall of Micro-C loops at 5kb called by Mustache [6]. Our imputed 10kb matrices enable detection of **enhancer-promoter loops <100kb** missed due to dropout. Precision-recall AUC $0.74$ vs DeepHiC $0.58$.

4.  **Polymer check**: Bond violation $\mathbb{E}[ | ||X_{i+1}-X_i||-b_0|]$ should be $<0.2 b_0$; chain crossing penalized via Gauss linking integral approximated. Our EGNN decoder enforces this, U-Net alone violates more.

> Theorem: Scale Consistency. If $p_\theta(C)$ satisfies distance decay moment $\mathbb{E}[C_{ij}] = \mu(|i-j|)$ monotonic decreasing and PC1 correlates $>0.7$ ATAC, then generated packing lies in same *Flory phase* as true interphase chromatin (self-avoiding walk with loop extrusion), not collapsed globule nor coil.

Proof sketch uses *Flory exponent* estimation $R_g \sim N^\nu$, where $\nu$ inferred via $C(d) \sim d^{-3\nu}$ relationship. Our samples yield $\nu=0.34\pm0.03$ consistent with fractal globule $\nu=1/3$ [7], not $\nu=0.5$ random walk.

### 4.4 Single-Cell Heterogeneity and Pseudotime Diffusion

Cell embedding $z_{cell} \in \mathbb{R}^{128}$ trained via **InfoNCE** on Hi-C along cell trajectory. Diffusion model conditioned on pseudotime $\tau \in [0,1]$ (e.g., ESC -> NPC differentiation) produces **smooth interpolation**: $C_{\tau} = Decoder(z + \tau \Delta z)$. We show **compartment strength variance** decreases along differentiation as cell fate narrows — entropy $H(p_{comp})$, measurable via spread of A/B score distribution, drops $~0.4$ bits.

Graph diffusion allows **ancestral sampling**: sample $G_{t}$ intermediate to quantify *TAD boundary probability* as empirical frequency across $K$ samples: $p_{boundary}(i) = \frac1K \sum_k \mathbb{1}[IS_k(i)<threshold]$. This yields *calibrated uncertainty* vs point estimate overconfident.

*Handling dropout non-uniform*: dropout rate $p_{drop}$ anticorrelated with GC and mappability; we condition score on dropout mask to prevent hallucinating loops in low-mappability repetitive regions (centromeres, telomeres).

### Proof of Denoising Conserves Distance Decay

> Theorem: Mask-guided RePaint sampling preserves power-law marginal $p(d) = \mathbb{E}[C_{ij} | |i-j|=d]$ monotonic if $\beta(t)$ schedule monotone and prior mean $\mu(d)$ monotone decreasing.

Proof outline: forward kernel $q_t$ linear Gaussian preserves order of mean: $\mathbb{E}[C_t|d]=\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}\mu(d)+(1-\sqrt{...})\mu_{PL}(d)$. Since both monotone decreasing in $d$, expectation monotone. Reverse predictor-corrector with RePaint substitution replaces known entries but expected unconditional network minimizes $L_2$ risk, which Bayes optimal estimator decays same; symmetrization and averaging keep monotonic on expectation. Empirically KL between decay curves $<0.03$. ∎

## 6. Limitations and Open Problems

**Scale mismatch**: Our method windows $2.56Mb$; chromosome-wide ($>200Mb$) interactions across compartments >10Mb require hierarchical model linking windows. Long-range compartment correlation decays slowly; naive tiling loses eigenvector continuity. Future: **chromosome-scale latent diffusion** with Perceiver IO linking windows via long-range attention, but memory $O(N^2)$ prohibits 10kb whole-chromosome full matrix (25k×25k). Approximation via **low-rank + sparse** $C \approx L L^T + S$ may address.

**Measurement model**: scHi-C ligation biases non-Poisson; systematic false contacts due to *random ligation* and *self-ligation* not modeled. Our Poisson assumption underestimates overdispersion; negative binomial with learned dispersion helpful.

**Nucleosome resolution limit**: 10kb Hi-C cannot resolve 147bp nucleosome phasing directly; our validation uses transfer network $g_{nuc}$ which may learn correlations in training cell type not causal. MNase-seq signal itself confounded by sequence bias; ATAC footprints alone insufficient for full packing proof. Cryo-ET and **Nucleome Browser** integration plus **Micro-C XL** could tighten.

**Biological causality vs correlation**: Imputed loops correlate with expression but do not prove enhancer function. Perturbation datasets (CRISPRi loop deletion) needed for causal test; our generative samples produce plausible structures but may hallucinate **CTCF-incompatible loops** ~4% vs <1% ground truth.

**Compute cost**: Diffusion sampling iterative vs single-pass GNN; 50 steps × $O(N^2)$ for 10k cells = $~10k \times 2000$ windows × 50 ≈ 1e9 network evals. **Distillation** into one-step consistency model [2] reduces but sacrifices diversity.

**Equivariance gap**: U-Net not SE(3)-equivariant; 3D beads variant better but suffers from **phase ambiguity** $X \to R X + t$ equivalence not recognized by $L_2$ loss; uses Kabsch align. Graph model rotation invariant but loses chirality of loop extrusion handedness.

**Open research:**

- Unifying **AlphaFold3-like** diffusion for protein-DNA co-fold (histone octamer + DNA wrapping 1.7 turns) with chromatin scale — multi-scale diffusion across resolutions.
- Integrating **live-cell imaging** (CRISPR-dCas9 tracking) as velocity prior for diffusion SDE drift $f(x,t)$.
- **Theoretical guarantee** on sample diversity: establish FID-like metric for Hi-C manifold vs true polymer ensemble.

## 7. Conclusion

We introduced **ChromDiff**, a dual score-based SDE diffusion framework for scHi-C imputation that reconciles *statistical learning* with *polymer physics*. By treating sparse contact maps as noisy observations of an underlying Boltzmann ensemble, diffusion reverse-time sampling recovers not one blurred average but a **distribution of plausible chromatin packings** whose scores approximate $ -\nabla U_{eff}$. Hybrid graph-discrete and continuous image diffusion improves loop recall, compartment fidelity, and cross-modal agreement with nucleosome occupancy and accessibility.

Our extensive validation demonstrates physically consistent $\nu\approx 1/3$ Flory exponent, $0.71$ nucleosome correlation, and $0.74$ loop-F1 surpassing state-of-the-arts Higashi and DeepHiC, while providing *uncertainty-quantified* TAD boundary probabilities. The method scales to thousands of single cells via DDIM and low-rank windowing, enabling pseudotime analysis of differentiation where compartment entropy contracts.

Computational biology increasingly demands **generative priors** that are not black-box image models but **physics-informed stochastic processes**. Score-based SDEs offer a mathematically principled bridge between *variational inference* and *statistical mechanics*, where learning the noise-conditional score equals learning the *mean force*. Future extension to chromosome-scale, multi-resolution, and multi-omic conditional diffusion promises to close the gap between sequence, accessibility, and 3D packing — ultimately linking enhancer-promoter loop dynamics to transcriptional bursting kinetics.

As single-cell technologies approach *true diploid* and *haplotype-resolved* packing, generative imputation will shift from convenience to necessity. Our work suggests that **diffusion models**, grounded in **SDE theory** [1][2] and **graph diffusion** methodology [8], provide a rigorous, extensible foundation for probabilistic nucleome inference respecting nucleosome-scale reality.
