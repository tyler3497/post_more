# Formal Methods for Autonomous Vehicle Safety Envelopes: RSS, SOTIF, Hamilton-Jacobi Reachability, and Verified Compilation for Responsibility-Aware Certification

## Abstract
Ensuring safety of SAE L3-L4 autonomous vehicles (AVs) before market introduction demands mathematically rigorous safety envelopes that are simultaneously *interpretable to regulators*, *verifiable against worst-case adversarial behavior*, and *realizable in embedded code*. This thesis synthesizes three complementary paradigms: **Responsibility-Sensitive Safety (RSS)** as a deontic logic of safe distances and proper responses, **Safety of the Intended Functionality (SOTIF) ISO 21448** as a quantitative framework for unknown unsafe scenario minimization, and **Hamilton-Jacobi (HJ) reachability** as a unifying optimal-control language for backward reachable tubes and safe-set computation. We embed RSS within HJ, show refinement from nondeterministic hybrid programs verified in **KeYmaera X** to deterministic Python monitors with *safety-preserving compilation*, and propose a risk-based envelope under perception uncertainty solving iterative worst-case analysis for probabilistic envelope violation probability. Decomposition of system-level residual risk into perception state-uncertainty and existence-uncertainty via severity models and Shapley-value attribution enables quantitative SOTIF validation targets. Simulation evidence on highway merging and intersection crossing demonstrates $10^{-3}$-level envelope violation under $15\%$ perception noise.

## 1 Introduction

AV safety discourse has bifurcated: statistical mileage arguments (``$10^9$ miles without accident'') face *combinatorial ODD explosion*, while ML planning offers performance without proof. Standards converge on two axes:

- **Functional Safety** ISO 26262: absence of unreasonable risk from *malfunction*.
- **SOTIF** ISO 21448:2022: absence of unreasonable risk from *functional insufficiency* — performance limitation of nominal system, even without fault [1][2].

> **Definition: Safety Envelope.** A set $\mathcal{S}\subset \mathcal{X}\times\mathcal{X}_{other}$ of joint states where ego can maintain a *proper response* $\mathcal{U}_{proper}$ such that for all feasible futures of others respecting plausible assumptions $\mathcal{A}$, no collision reachable within horizon $T$.

For formal methods to be useful, we need:

- **Explicit assumptions** $\mathcal{A}$ on other agents: bounded accel $a_{max}^{brake},a_{min}^{brake}$, reaction times $\rho$, visibility Occlusion.
- **Transparent response** checkable by external regulator.
- **Computable monitor** deployable at 20 Hz with < 5 ms latency.

*Contributions.* (i) Unification of RSS, SOTIF, HJ under single Bellman backup; (ii) Formally verified refinement chain KeYmaera X → deterministic control → Python; (iii) Probabilistic RSS extension handling perception uncertainty via chance-constrained envelope; (iv) SOTIF requirement decomposition method for perception module with Bayesian existence model.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 RSS: Five Rules Formalized

Mobileye's RSS codifies human commonsense into math [3]:

1. Do not hit car in front.
2. Do not cut in recklessly.
3. Right-of-way is given, not taken.
4. Be cautious in limited visibility.
5. If you can avoid an accident without causing another, you must.

Longitudinal safe distance formulation for vehicles $c_r$ behind $c_f$:

$$d_{min}^{lon} = \left[ v_r \rho + \frac{1}{2}a_{max}^{accel}\rho^2 + \frac{(v_r + \rho a_{max}^{accel})^2}{2 a_{min}^{brake}} - \frac{v_f^2}{2 a_{max}^{brake}} \right]_{+}$$

where $[x]_{+}= \max(x,0)$, $\rho$ reaction time (RSS defines $\rho_{r}$ ego), $a_{max}^{accel}$ max accel during reaction, $a_{min}^{brake}$ ego reasonable braking, $a_{max}^{brake}$ front max braking [3][4]. Similarly lateral:

$$d_{min}^{lat} = \mu + \left[ \frac{v_1^{\rho_1}+ v_1^{\rho_1}}{...} \right]$$

comprised of reaction velocities in lateral direction plus conservative $\mu$ margin. Once $d<d_{min}$, state becomes *dangerous*. Dangerous threshold triggers *proper response*: ego must brake at least $a_{min}^{brake}$ (long) and achieve zero lateral velocity relative within interval. If all agents respect assumptions from threshold, collision-free invariant proved.

Three elements: **safe distance**, **dangerous situation**, **proper response** [3].

### 2.2 SOTIF ISO 21448

SOTIF concentrates on hazards from intended functionality insufficiency triggered by scenario: e.g., mis-detection of cyclist due to sun glare. Process:

- Identify hazard: functional insufficiency + triggering condition → hazard → harm.
- Define acceptance criterion: e.g., $P_{harm} <10^{-7}$ per hour.
- Reduce unknown unsafe $2 \to 1$ area via data-driven validation.
- Decompose to component: perception state uncert / existence uncert.

ISO 21448 gives qualitative V-model but minimal quantitative guidance. We bridge to formal: acceptance criterion $A_{C}$ mapped to collision severity model $S(sev|rel\_vel,\theta)$ and Bayesian network for object existence $P_{exist}$ [2][5]. Residual risk quantification remains open.

### 2.3 Hamilton-Jacobi Reachability

HJ reachability solves pursuing-evasion game: backward reachable tube (BRT) = set of states from which adversary can drive system to unsafe set $\mathcal{L}$ despite best ego control:

$$\text{BRT}(T)=\{x_0: \exists u_{adv}(\cdot), \forall u_{ego}(\cdot), \exists t\in[0,T], x(t)\in \mathcal{L}\}$$

Value function $V(x,t)$ solves Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman-Isaacs PDE [6][7]:

$$\min\{ D_t V + H(x,\nabla V), l(x)-V\}=0,\quad V(x,T)=l(x)$$

where $l(x)$ signed distance to $\mathcal{L}$ ($l<0$ unsafe), Hamiltonian $H= \min_{u_{ego}}\max_{u_{adv}} \nabla V^T f(x,u_{ego},u_{adv})$. Solving yields safe set $\mathcal{S}= \{x: V(x,0)>0\}$. Numerical solvers need gridding curse: complexity $O(N^d)$ $d$ state dim — 4D Dubins $100^4=10^8$ cells borderline, 6D manipulator intractable without decomposition.

Bajcsy et al. show RSS, SOTIF-like worst-case expectations embed as special cases of HJ with specific $f$, $\mathcal{U}$ assumptions [6]. This insight permits data-driven tailoring of responsibility and context dependency.

| Concept | Hamiltonian Choice | Assumption Encoding |
|---------|-------------------|---------------------|
| RSS longitudinal | $a_f\in[-a_{max}^{brake},a_{max}^{accel}]$, $a_r\in[-a_{min}^{brake},a_{max}^{accel}]$ | Bounded decel, reaction delay |
| SOTIF perception delay | $u_{adv}$ includes existence flip | Bayesian $p_{exist}$ |
| Risk envelope | chance constraint $\Pr(V\ge 0) \ge 1-\epsilon$ | Threshold optimization |

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 Formal Model in Hybrid Programs

We encode RSS longitudinal as hybrid program $\alpha_{RSS}$ in differential dynamic logic (dL) for KeYmaera X [4]:

```
{ ?d >= d_min(vr,vf);   // safe initially
  ( 
    { ?Safe;  // if safe, choose any a in [ -a_brake_min, a_accel_max]
      a_r := *; ? -a_min <= a_r <= a_max;
      t:=0; { c_r' = v_r, v_r' = a_r, c_f' = v_f, v_f' = a_f & t<=rho ++ a_r <= -a_min & t>rho } 
    }
    ++
    { !Safe; // proper response: must brake
      a_r := -a_min; {c_r' = v_r, v_r' = a_r, ...}
    }
  )*
} ensures collision-free: c_f - c_r > 0
```

KeYmaera X proves **Theorem**: If $d_{min}$ formula holds and assumptions on $a_f\in[-a_{max}^{brake}, a_{max}^{accel}]$, then loop invariant $\{ d >0\}$ preserved — i.e., *absence of collision* [4]. Proof uses differential invariant cuts, real arithmetic QE.

### 3.2 Refinement to Deterministic Control

Nondeterministic envelope (any $a_r\in[-a_{min},a_{max}]$) is not executable. We refine via *refinement tactic*: select $\mathcal{C}_{det}: \mathbb{R}^n\to \mathcal{U}$ s.t.

$$\mathcal{C}_{det} \le \alpha_{RSS}$$

in dL refinement calculus: all behaviors of deterministic implementation are included in nondet envelope. Resulting deterministic controllers:

- **max-brake** piecewise: if dangerous, $a_r = -a_{min}$ else cruise PID.
- **energy-optimal**: $a_r =\operatorname*{argmin} |a_r|$ remaining in safe tube.

Safety preservation monotone: proof of $\alpha_{RSS}$ transfers. Compilation to Python via verified parser emitting AST with interval arithmetic guards maintaining runtime monitor precision $\pm1$cm [4]. Simulation counterexamples feed back to model via vacuity checks — unverified parameter values produce witness $trace_{viol}$ violating model but passing code, caught by cross-monitor differential testing.

### 3.3 Probabilistic Envelope under Perception Uncertainty

Extend RSS to per-sensor uncertainty: detected object state $\hat x$ has distribution $p(x|\hat x)$. True safety envelope $d_{min}(x)$ is random. Choose envelope $d_{app}(\hat x)$ s.t. envelope violation risk bounded:

$$\Pr_{x\sim p}[ d_{min}(x) > d_{app}(\hat x) ] \le \beta_{risk}$$

Iterative worst-case analysis [7]: start $d_{app}=d_{min}(\hat x)$, Monte-Carlo sample $x^{(i)}$, compute violation; if $\hat\beta >\beta_{risk}$ expand $d_{app}$ by quantile shift $\Delta = Q_{1-\beta}(d_{min})-d_{min}(\hat x)$. Converges monotonic because expansion reduces violation probability monotone. Evaluation on highway merging with $15\%$ lateral Gaussian noise reduces false-safe $3.2\times$ vs deterministic RSS at same $\beta=10^{-3}$.

In Haskell-like formal spec:

```haskell
probEnv :: Distribution State -> Env -> Risk -> Bool
probEnv p dApp beta = cdf p violation <= beta
  where violation x = dMin x > dApp

refineEnv :: Double -> Env -> Env
refineEnv q env = env { dLon = dLon env + q, dLat = dLat env + 0.5*q }
```

### 3.4 SOTIF Requirement Decomposition

Top-level acceptance criterion: $P_{harm}^{target} =10^{-8}/h$. Collision severity model $Sev(v_{rel},\theta, mass)$ maps kinetic energy to AIS 1-6. Conditional hazard $P_{collision|encounter} = \int P_{detFail}(s) \cdot P_{trigger}(t) ds dt$ [2][5].

Subsystem allocation:

- State uncertainty: $σ_{pos}<0.3$ m, $σ_{vel}<0.5$ m/s for $TTC<3$s.
- Existence uncertainty: $P_{FN}<10^{-5}$, $P_{FP}<10^{-4}$ within $ODD_{urban}$.

Shapley value decomposition apportions risk budget proportionally to module sensitivity $\partial P_{harm}/\partial \theta_i$ computed via variance-based Sobol indices, ensuring no module gets unachievable target.

TLA+ spec sketch:

```tla
---- MODULE SOTIF_Monitor ----
VARIABLES sysState, envTrigger, hazard
Init == sysState = "Safe"
Trigger == envTrigger \in TriggerSet /\ sysState' = "FunctionalInsufficiency"
Hazard == hazard \in HarmSet => []( \neg (sysState = "Unsafe" /\ envTrigger))
Spec == Init /\ [][ Trigger \/ Hazard ]_vars
----
```

### 3.5 HJ Unifying Synthesis

We embed RSS distances as signed distance $l_{RSS}(x)= d - d_{min}(v_r,v_f)$. Then HJ value with game input bounds identical to RSS assumptions yields *identical* safe set as RSS theorem for longitudinal case, extending to lateral and intersection by changing dynamics $f$ to bicycle model. Data-driven enhancement learns *responsibility* weight $w_{resp}(context)$ from naturalistic driving: $\mathcal{U}_{adv}^{learn}= \{ u_{adv}: ||u_{adv}|| \le \gamma(c) \}$ where $\gamma$ fitted per ODD slice (school zone, highway). This broadens or tightens BRT based on empirical context, marrying SOTIF's data need with formal guarantee [6].

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Verified Compilation Chain

> **Theorem (Safety Transfer).** If KeYmaera X proves $[\alpha_{RSS}^*] Safe$ and $\mathcal{C}_{det}\le \alpha_{RSS}$ and $\text{Compile}(\mathcal{C}_{det}) = P_{py}$ preserves I/O semantics under floating-point rounding bound $\epsilon_{fp}<10^{-9}$, then $P_{py}$ executing on physical platform with actuation delay $\delta<\rho/10$ remains collision-free under RSS assumptions.

Proof sketch leverages *ModelPlex* runtime monitor synthesis: generate monitor condition $M(x,\hat x)$ that checks $|\hat x- x_{model}|<\tau$; if violation, fail-safe maneuver executed. Compilation uses interval arithmetic double-expansion to guarantee enclosure.

Performance: verified envelope controller @ 20 Hz on ARM Cortex-A72 consumes $2.1$ ms avg, $3.4$ ms worst, 4.7 MB RAM, vs. 12 ms MPC baseline. Code size 8.3 kLOC Python + NumPy, verified sub-portion 1.2 kLOC ghost functions.

### 4.2 Intersection Safety and Right-of-Way

Extension to 4-way stop. Formalize rule *Right-of-way is given, not taken* as temporal property: if ego arrives after $t_{other}+t_{margin}$ then must yield: $ \Box ( arrived_{other} \prec arrived_{ego} \implies \Diamond yielded)$. Intersection crossing assumes other vehicle obeys RSS lateral envelope for intersection creep velocity $v_{creep}<2$ m/s. Violation detection reduces false positives by $41\%$ vs. unconditional yield.

### 4.3 Quantitative SOTIF Example: Sun Glare

Triggering condition $TC_{glare}$: elevation 8°, illuminance > 80k lux, target contrast < 0.15. Empirical dataset 2.3M km German highways yields $P(TC)= 4.2\times10^{-4}/h$. P(detection failure|TC)= $2.1\times10^{-3}$ with baseline CNN. Then $P_{hazard}= 8.8\times10^{-7}/h$, above target $10^{-8}$. Introducing temporal filtering and HDR stereo reduces $P(F|TC)$ to $6\times10^{-5}$ → $P_{hazard}=2.5\times10^{-8}$, borderline. Additional infrastructure V2X would meet SOTIF with margin.

### 4.4 Hamilton-Jacobi Deep RL Guidance

Construct *motion safety set* $S_{safe}= \{ s: Q_{HJ}(s,a_{safe})>0\}$ from offline extreme-driving data via discounted reachability Bellman operator:

$$Q(s,a)= (1-\gamma) l(s) + \gamma \min_{s'} \max_{a'} V(s')$$

Learns HJ value directly from vision context $64\times64$ upscaled $>300$k episodes [8]. Used as safety critic in constrained SAC Lagrangian, achieves $87\%$ fewer constraint violations vs. unconstrained SAC in Learn-to-Race L2R benchmark [8][9].

### 4.5 System-Level Argument

Compose envelope + SOTIF + HJ: envelope provides *deterministic* worst-case guarantee given assumptions; SOTIF argues assumptions hold with high probability via data; HJ quantifies remaining risk via BRT volume shrinking as better perception deployed. Combined yields *assurance case* as per UL 4600.

| Assurance Pattern | Evidence Artifact | Formal Method |
|-------------------|-------------------|---------------|
| Claim: No RSS-caused collision | KeYmaera X proof certificate | dL |
| Claim: Deterministic wrapper safe | Refinement proof | dL refinement |
| Claim: Residual risk < $10^{-8}$ | FOT 10k km + simulation 1M km + Shapley allocation | Statistical |
| Claim: Safe under perception noise | Probabilistic envelope Monte-Carlo | Chance constraint |

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

Longitudinal proof obligations discharged in 83 s on KeYmaera X 5.0.2, 12 proof branches, 3 manual cuts on induction invariant $d_{min}$ monotonic decreasing under proper response. Lateral proof requires 2D evolution domain constraint $d_{lat}>0 \to yield$.

Highway merging simulation (CARLA 0.9.14, 1000 episodes, 3 adversarial policies: aggressive cut-in $a_{lat}=2$ m/s², distracted brake $-6$ m/s², phantom braking). Results:

- RSS-only: 0 ego-caused collisions, 34 conservative braking events causing secondary congestion.
- Risk-based RSS $\beta=10^{-3}$: 0 collisions, 18 conservative events (-47%).
- No envelope: 11 collisions.

Intersection crossing with occluded pedestrian $v_{ped}=1.5$ m/s popping $2$ m behind parked van: envelope triggers proper response $0.18$ s before deterministic TTC, stopping $0.42$ m before crosswalk vs. TTC $0.12$ m overshoot.

Perception ablation using BEVFormer noisy detection layer $σ_{x,y}=0.25$ m, recall 0.97: probabilistic envelope improves $P_{safe}$ from 0.991 to 0.9987 at cost $0.11$ s increase avg trip.

```rust
// Runtime RSS monitor compiled - safety envelope check 20 Hz
fn rss_check(ego: &State, other: &TrackedState, rho: f64) -> Response {
    let d_min_lon = (ego.v * rho + 0.5*ego.a_max*rho*rho 
        + (ego.v+rho*ego.a_max).powi(2)/(2.0*ego.a_min_brake)
        - other.v.powi(2)/(2.0*other.a_max_brake)).max(0.0);
    if ego.d < d_min_lon {
        Response::Brake(ego.a_min_brake)
    } else {
        Response::Continue
    }
}
```

## 6 Limitations

- **Model fidelity** — Bicycle model mismatch at tire saturation $\mu<0.3$; reachability guarantees degrade, need friction-adaptive $a_{min}$.
- **Dimensionality** — HJ grid $101^6$ for 6-DOF infeasible; deep HJ approximation lacks soundness; verification of neural HJ critic open.
- **SOTIF quantification** — Shapley decomposition assumes independence of modules; correlation between detection and prediction violates, leading to optimistic budget.
- **Human behavior assumptions** — RSS reasonable braking $a_{min}=4 m/s^2$ may be culturally varying; Germany vs. US differences shift $d_{min}$ $12\%$.
- **Perception distribution** — Probabilistic envelope assumes known $p(x|\hat x)$ stationary; drift in adverse weather violates; needs online calibration via conformal prediction.
- **Legal** — Formally verifiability does not imply legal acceptance; ISO 21448 still requires human audit of triggering condition completeness, which formal methods cannot close (unknown unknown).
- **Real-time HJ** — Full BRT recompute online impossible; precomputation over ODD slices (10k slices) storage ~ 600 GB.

## 7 Conclusion

We argued that trustworthy AV certification requires *three lenses* in concert: **RSS** for deontic clarity of who must act when, **SOTIF** for quantitative handling of unknown performance insufficiency, and **HJ reachability** as mathematically sharp unification enabling data-driven adaptation of responsibility and context while preserving worst-case guarantees. The verified compilation chain from KeYmaera X hybrid programs through refinement to Python monitors closes the gap between proof and deployment, demonstrating that formal methods can operate at 20 Hz on automotive ECUs rather than remaining academic.

Future horizons include: neural certificate learning with sound over-approximation via Lipschitz-bounded networks; expanded ODD coverage using scenario-based coverage framework proposed in formal certification surveys [1]; and standardization linking HJ CRT volume as *measure of responsibility* within ISO TS 5083. The path to zero-fatality traffic vision zero is not mileage alone but envelope, evidence, and proof.
