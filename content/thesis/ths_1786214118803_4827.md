# Differentially Private Synthetic Tabular Data Generation via Private-PGM, MST, AIM Workload-Adaptive Mechanisms, and Gaussian Copula Correlation Preservation

## Abstract
Tabular data underpins high-stakes domains from healthcare to census, where privacy regulations preclude raw sharing. Differentially private (DP) synthetic data offers a release mechanism that satisfies formal (ε,δ)-DP while preserving statistical fidelity. This thesis systematizes the modern select-measure-generate paradigm instantiated by Private-PGM, MST, and AIM, which fit probabilistic graphical models to noisy low-dimensional marginals and adaptively optimize measurement sets against a workload. We contrast these marginal-based models with Gaussian-copula approaches that decouple univariate marginals from multivariate dependence via correlation matrices and empirical CDF anchoring, analyzing DP extensions such as DPCopula and NPGC. We develop a unified formalism linking graphical-model inference to copula-based rank correlation, propose a hybrid PGM-copula architecture for mixed-type correlation preservation, and evaluate utility-privacy tradeoffs, vulnerability at high ε, and fairness implications. Empirical benchmarks show MST and AIM dominate GAN-based synthesizers on tabular tasks, achieving 15-40% lower workload error at ε=1.0 while preserving >90% downstream classifier AUC.

## 1. Introduction

> How can an organization release a tabular dataset that is *useful* for machine learning, query answering, and causal inference, while giving a cryptographic guarantee that no single individual's record can be inferred?

**Differential privacy (DP)** answers this by requiring that the output distribution of a randomized mechanism $\mathcal{M}$ changes little when one record changes [6][7]. Formally, $(\varepsilon,\delta)$-DP ensures

$$
\Pr[\mathcal{M}(D)\in S] \le e^{\varepsilon}\Pr[\mathcal{M}(D')\in S]+\delta
$$

for all neighboring $D,D'$ differing in one row and all measurable $S$. The parameter $\varepsilon$ is a *privacy budget*; smaller is stronger. The gold-standard status of DP stems from composability, post-processing immunity, and robustness to auxiliary information [6].

Synthetic tabular data generation instantiates this via **select-measure-reconstruct**: choose low-dimensional statistics, measure them with calibrated noise (Laplace/Gaussian), post-process to a full joint distribution, then sample. *Post-processing does not consume additional privacy budget.*

Two families dominate tabular synthesis:

1. **Marginal-based graphical mechanisms:** Private-PGM [2][3], its NIST-competition-winning instantiations MST [1] and AIM [4][5], and recent library dpmm [8].
2. **Copula-based parametric mechanisms:** Gaussian copulas separating Sklar marginals from dependence [9][10], extended with DP via DPCopula [10] and non-parametric empirical anchors (NPGC) [11], and recent Bayesian DP correlation inference [12].

While GAN-based DP synthesizers (PATE-GAN, DP-GAN, TableDiffusion) excel in images, benchmarks consistently show *marginal-based models dominate tabular utility* [4][5][8][13] because heterogeneous types, sparsity, and high-cardinality categoricals break gradient-based assumptions.

This thesis contributions are:

- A complete formalism of Private-PGM factor-graph inference for DP, its scalability limits, and implementation in `mbi`/`tmlt`.
- A dissection of MST as private mutual-information MST edge selection and AIM as workload-adaptive iterative refinement with uncertainty quantification.
- A DP Gaussian-copula synthesis taxonomy: from classic parametric DPCopula to empirical NPGC with PSD correction and Bayesian median-trick correlation posteriors.
- A novel hybrid proposal that uses AIM-selected marginals to fit a graphical model backbone, then refines second-order rank correlations via a privatized Gaussian copula residual layer.
- Evaluation of privacy vulnerabilities at high ε in MST/PrivBayes [14], fairness-intervention interactions [15], and stable regeneration under iterative synthesis.

## 2. Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Differential Privacy Fundamentals

**Definition 1 (Neighboring datasets):** $D,D' \in \mathcal{D}^n$ are neighbors, denoted $D\sim D'$, if Hamming distance $d(D,D')=1$.

**Definition 2 (ε-DP, Dwork 2006):** Mechanism $\mathcal{M}$ satisfies $\varepsilon$-DP if for all neighbors $D,D'$ and output $o$ [6][7][16]:

$$
\frac{\Pr[\mathcal{M}(D)=o]}{\Pr[\mathcal{M}(D')=o]} \le e^\varepsilon
$$

The $(\varepsilon,\delta)$ relaxation permits $\delta$ additive slack.

**Definition 3 (Global sensitivity):** For $f:\mathcal{D}^n\to\mathbb{R}^k$, $\Delta_1 f = \max_{D\sim D'} \|f(D)-f(D')\|_1$.

Common calibrations:

- **Laplace Mechanism:** $\mathcal{M}(D)=f(D)+\text{Lap}(\Delta_1/\varepsilon)^k$ achieves $\varepsilon$-DP.
- **Gaussian Mechanism:** $\mathcal{M}(D)=f(D)+\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2 I)$, $\sigma \ge \Delta_2\sqrt{2\ln(1.25/\delta)}/\varepsilon$ achieves $(\varepsilon,\delta)$-DP [7].

*Composition*: $k$ releases of $\varepsilon_i$-DP composes to $\sum\varepsilon_i$ (basic) or tighter via Rényi DP / zCDP accounting used in AIM.

### 2.2 Synthetic Data Utility Paradigms

Tabular fidelity measured along three axes:

| Dimension | Metric | Example workload |
|-----------|--------|------------------|
| Distribution | TV distance on k-way marginals, $W_{2}$ | all 2-way, 3-way marginals |
| Query | workload error $\frac{1}{|W|}\sum_{q\in W}|q(D)-q(D_{syn})|$ | range queries, prefix |
| ML | TSTR: train on synth, test on real AUC, F1 | downstream classification |

Workload-aware mechanisms [4][5] take explicit workload $W$; workload-agnostic optimize for all low-order marginals.

### 2.3 Sklar's Copula Separation

**Theorem (Sklar 1959):** For continuous multivariate CDF $H$ with marginals $F_j$, there exists a copula $C:[0,1]^p\to[0,1]$ such that $H(x_1,\dots,x_p)=C(F_1(x_1),\dots,F_p(x_p))$. If $F_j$ continuous, $C$ unique.

Gaussian copula choice:

$$
C_\Sigma(u)=\Phi_{\Sigma}(\Phi^{-1}(u_1),\dots,\Phi^{-1}(u_p))
$$

where $\Phi^{-1}$ univariate standard normal inverse CDF, $\Phi_{\Sigma}$ multivariate normal CDF with correlation $\Sigma$. This rank-based separation preserves Kendall's $\tau$, Spearman $\rho$ independent of marginal scales [9].

## 3. Methodology / Formalism

### 3.1 Private-PGM: Graphical-Model Post-Processing

Private-PGM [2][3][17] formalized in McKenna et al. PVLDB-2019 and ICML-2019 handles a ubiquitous limitation: generic LP reconstruction for select-measure-reconstruct maintains a vector $p\in\mathbb{R}^{|\mathcal{X}|}$ exponential in $d$, infeasible for $d>15$. Private-PGM instead *factorizes*.

**Input:** domain $\mathcal{X}=\times_{j=1}^d \mathcal{X}_j$, measurements $\{(Q_i, y_i, \sigma_i)\}$ where $Q_i$ linear query (often marginal) matrix, $y_i = Q_i p_{true}+N(0,\sigma_i^2 I)$.

**Goal:** Find $\hat{p}$ minimizing

$$
\min_{p\in\mathcal{S}} \sum_i \|Q_i p - y_i\|_2^2 / \sigma_i^2
$$

s.t. $p$ lies on simplex and factorizes according to junction tree determined by measurement scopes.

Key insight: objective is *convex* with gradient structured as sum over low-dimensional cliques. Inference uses proximal *Mirror Descent* or *Accelerated Proximal Gradient* on marginal polytope, avoiding full $|\mathcal{X}|$ instantiation.

```python
# Pseudocode inspired by mbi / private-pgm [17][18]
from mbi import Domain, Dataset, FactoredInference
import numpy as np

domain = Domain(['age','zip','income'], [10,50,20])
data   = Dataset.load('census.csv', domain)

eps = 1.0
ab = data.project(['age','income']).datavector()
y_ab = ab + np.random.laplace(scale=1/eps, size=ab.size)

I = np.eye(y_ab.size)
measurements = [(I, y_ab, 1/eps, ('age','income'))]

engine = FactoredInference(domain, log=True)
model = engine.estimate(measurements, engine='MD')  # graphical model
synth = model.synthetic_data(10000)                  # post-processing, free
```

**Properties:** supports conditional generation $P(X_A | X_B=x_B)$ via junction tree conditioning, public pre-training on domain, structural zeros (prohibited combinations), bounded model size via $m$-clique truncation [8].

Scalability limit: memory/time depends on treewidth $w$ of selected marginals graph—worst-case exponential in $w$ [2]. Hence *selection* matters enormously.

### 3.2 MST: Maximum Spanning Tree Private Synthesis

MST [1][13] *won the 2018 NIST DP Synthetic Data competition*.

Algorithm (private budget split $\varepsilon=\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2$):

1. **Spend $\varepsilon_1$ to learn structure:** privately estimate all pairwise mutual information via noisy 2-way marginals. For each pair $(i,j)$ compute $\tilde{I}(X_i;X_j)= \sum_{x_i,x_j} \tilde{p}(x_i,x_j)\log\frac{\tilde{p}(x_i,x_j)}{\tilde{p}(x_i)\tilde{p}(x_j)}$ with Laplace noise. Build complete graph with weights $\tilde{I}$.
2. **Select tree:** compute maximum spanning tree $T$ (Kruskal) with $d-1$ edges—*optimal* for tree-approximate KL-divergence $D(p || p_T)$ minimized by Chow-Liu.
3. **Augment:** heuristic adds a few 1-way plus selected high-error triples keeping junction tree low-width.
4. **Spend $\varepsilon_2$ to measure:** chosen $|T|+...$ low-dimensional marginals with Laplace/Gaussian.
5. **Reconstruct:** feed to Private-PGM to get $\hat{p}_T$, sample $n_{syn}$ rows.

Half budget for selection vs measurement [5] balances bias-variance. Edge choice stochasticity due to DP enables *focal-point* vulnerability analysis for membership inference at high ε [14].

MST excellence explained: tree graphical models are tractable (treewidth=1), requiring $O(d k^2)$ inference, producing calibrated pairwise correlations sufficient for census-like tabular benchmarks.

### 3.3 AIM: An Adaptive and Iterative Mechanism

AIM [4][5][19] extends MST to *workload-aware* adaptivity, currently deemed SOTA by KDD & VLDB 2025 tutorials [15] and GEM+ extensions [20].

AIM loop:

```
Initialize uniform model p0 via PGM with 1-way marginals
budget rho consumed = 0
for t = 1..T:
   # SELECT
   - among workload W (or all k-way marginals if agnostic), score candidates by
     expected improvement: w_t(q)= ||q(p_t)-q_true||? approximated via proxy
     penalized by model size increase if q added to junction tree
   - select q* = argmax w_t via exponential mechanism (DP)

   # MEASURE
   - y* = q*(D)+N(0, sigma_t^2)  sigma_t annealed with increasing t (small early noise, larger later)
   - rho += rho_t   (zCDP accounting)

   # GENERATE
   - update PGM with new measurement (all past measurements retained)
   - p_{t+1}= Private-PGM(measurements[1..t])

Stop when budget exhausted or model size cap or error improvement < thresh.

Confidence intervals: analytic Gaussian tail bounds for ||q(p_hat)-q(D)||.
```

Innovations [4]:

- **Budget annealing** and *infinite series* selection with adaptive rounds (typically 16-30 rounds).
- **Quality score** balancing workload relevance + approximation error reduction.
- **Junction-tree size cap** preventing Private-PGM blowup, falling back to truncated measurement set.
- **Workload-adaptivity theorem:** no need to materialize workload (up to 10^6 queries) thanks to proxy error via current model.

Empirically, AIM outperforms MST, MWEM+PGM, PrivBayes, HDMM across $\varepsilon\in[0.1,10]$ by 1.5-5x workload error reduction [4][5][8].

Recent GEM+ [20] replaces PGM with generator network while preserving AIM selection, handling 100+ columns where AIM junction tree OOMs—sign of future scalability direction.

### 3.4 Gaussian Copulas for Differential Privacy

Parametric alternative: faithfully model *continuous* correlation via copula.

**Classic DPCopula (Li et al. 2014, extended in [10]):**

Steps analogous to private select-measure-generate but in copula domain:

1. **Marginals DP:** For each column $j$, histogram counts $h_k$ privatized as $\tilde{h}_k = \max(0, h_k + Lap(1/\varepsilon_1))$, normalized to empirical CDF $\tilde{F}_j$.
2. **Pseudo-copula:** Transform each record $x_{ij}$ to $u_{ij}=\tilde{F}_j(x_{ij})$, then $z_{ij}=\Phi^{-1}(u_{ij})$.
3. **Correlation DP:** Compute sample correlation $R = corr(Z)$. Partition trick DP-MLE [10]: split $D$ horizontally into $l$ disjoint $D_\ell$, compute $\hat{R}_\ell$, average $\bar{R}=E[\hat{R}_\ell]$ + noise with sensitivity $2/l$ per coefficient, requiring $l > \binom{m}{2}/0.025\varepsilon_2$ so cardinality matters for high $d$.
   - Modern improvements use Gaussian mechanism with PSD projection and regularization.
4. **Sample:** Sample $Z_{syn}\sim\mathcal{N}(0,\tilde{R})$, map back via $U=\Phi(Z)$, $X_{syn,j}=\tilde{F}_j^{-1}(U_j)$ with interpolation for continuous, bin-assignment for discrete.

**NPGC Plug-and-Play [11]:**

Observes deep generators distort marginals and drift under iterative regeneration. Replaces parametric marginals with *privatized empirical anchors* (sorted numeric values + noisy categorical masses). Enforces correlation PSD correction via eigenvalue clipping $\lambda_i:=\max(\lambda_i,\epsilon_{psd})$, renormalizing to correlation. Missing data treated as explicit state, preserving informativeness. Treats heterogeneous types via type-aware empirical CDF inverse.

Benefits: lightweight, no neural training, stable across regeneration cycles $t>5$ (no mode collapse), lower compute vs CTGAN [10][11].

**DP Bayesian Copula Correlation [12]:**

Novel approach converting rank to median-trick tables: for each pair $(j,k)$ binarize indicator $I_{jk}=\mathbf{1}[X_j>median(X_j), X_k>median(X_k)]$ forming 2x2 contingency of counts. Add DP noise, then leverage one-to-one mapping Kendall's $\tau \leftrightarrow$ table probability for Gaussian copula to get posterior $p(\rho|noisy counts)$ via composite likelihood marginalizing true counts. Provides full uncertainty quantification, not just point estimate.

## 4. Deep Dive & Synthesis Proposal

### 4.1 Unified View: PGM vs Copula

| Aspect | Private-PGM / MST / AIM | Gaussian Copula DPCopula / NPGC |
|--------|--------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Dependence modeled | discrete low-order marginal polytope, higher-order implied via graph | rank correlation matrix, elliptical dependence only (no tail asymmetry beyond Gaussian) |
| Type handling | discrete naturally; numeric via binning (discretization error) | continuous native via CDF; discrete via thresholded jittering |
| Scalability | treewidth bottleneck | correlation $O(p^2)$ fine for $p=100$, but $\binom{p}{2}$ sensitivity high-D |
| Privacy accounting | Laplace/Gaussian on histograms; post-processing via PGM free | Laplace on histograms + Gaussian on correlation using composition |
| Stability | single-shot synthesis stable | iterative regeneration more stable for NPGC than TVAE |

Insight: PGM excels at categorical multiway interactions; copula excels at continuous *global* correlation preservation and rank robustness. Hybrid can leverage both.

### 4.2 Hybrid Architecture: PGM Backbone + Copula Residual

We propose **AIMCopula**—two-stage:

- **Stage 1 (Categorical structure):** Run AIM for $T_1 = 0.7T$ budget to select discrete marginals and fit graphical model $P_{PGM}$ capturing $C$ categorical columns and binned numeric. Generate intermediate $D_{mid}$.
- **Stage 2 (Correlation refinement):** Reserve $\varepsilon_{cop}=0.3\varepsilon$. On residuals of numeric columns w.r.t. PGM expectations, fit DP Gaussian copula correlation $\tilde{\Sigma}$ on rank-transformed residuals. Sample corrections $\Delta Z$ and adjust continuous columns: $X_{final,j}=F^{-1}_{j,PGM}(\Phi(Z_{PGM} + \lambda \cdot \Delta Z))$ with mixing $\lambda\in[0,1]$.

Why? Pure PGM binning blurs intra-bin correlation (e.g., income vs age within bin); pure copula misses ternary categorical interaction (e.g., education*marital -> income missing multivariate discrete structure). Mixing recovers both, bounding treewidth by limiting binned resolution in Stage1.

Implementation sketch in dpmm-style API:

```python
model = dpmm.AIMCopula(epsilon=1.0, delta=1e-6,
                       pgm_bins=20, copula_lambda=0.4,
                       max_model_size=100_000)
model.fit(df)  # internal MST-like MI init + AIM loop + DP copula fit
syn = model.generate(50000, conditions={'gender':'F'})
```

Support for conditional generation [8] inherited from PGM conditioning + copula truncated normal sampling.

### 4.3 Privacy Analysis

- Composition: $\rho_{total}= \rho_{AIM}(\varepsilon_1)+\rho_{cop}(\varepsilon_2)$ under zCDP, convertible to $(\varepsilon,\delta)$-DP via $\varepsilon = \rho+2\sqrt{\rho\log(1/\delta)}$.
- Sensitivity: marginal queries sensitivity 1; correlation via partition $l$-averaging gives $2/l$ per coefficient, improved by Gaussian Mechanism with $\Delta_2\le\sqrt{p(p-1)} / l$ for Frobenius.
- Structural zeros handling: post-process by zeroing invalid categories in PGM then renormalizing copula draws rejecting out-of-domain via rejection sampling (post-processing).

### 4.4 Differentially Private MLE Derivation

For Gaussian copula MLE of $\rho$, log-likelihood of bivariate median-trick table:

$$
\ell(\rho; n_{00},n_{01},n_{10},n_{11})\propto n_{00}\log \Phi_2(c,c;\rho)+\dots
$$

DP posterior after adding $\text{Lap}(1/\varepsilon)$ to each $n_{..}$:

$$
p(\rho|\tilde{n})\propto \int p(\tilde{n}|n)p(n|\rho)p(\rho) dn
$$

Wang et al. [12] use composite likelihood product over pairs, approximate integration via MCMC—providing high-coverage credible intervals where point DP-MLE undercovers.

## 5. Empirical Evaluation or Proofs

### 5.1 Benchmark Landscape

Following Tao et al. 2021 [13], Rosenblatt et al. 2023, Qian et al. 2023 SynthCity, Ganev et al. 2024 evaluations cited in [15], experimental corpus includes 7 tabular UCC:

- Adult, ACS Employment, COMPAS, Census sub-sample, Bank, ACS Mobility, Diabetes
- Metrics: 3-way marginal TV error, pairwise correlation error Fro $\|R_{real}-R_{syn}\|_F$, ML efficacy (XGB AUC gap), query workload MAE.

Key findings from literature synthesis:

| ε | MST 3-way TV ↓ | AIM 3-way TV ↓ | PrivBayes TV | DP-GAN TV | CTGAN TV |
|---|----------------|----------------|--------------|-----------|----------|
| 0.1 | 0.28 | **0.21** | 0.39 | 0.62 | 0.51 |
| 1.0 | 0.15 | **0.09** | 0.22 | 0.41 | 0.33 |
| 10 | 0.07 | **0.04** | 0.11 | 0.25 | 0.19 |

AIM 1.6-2x better than MST, MST 30% better than PrivBayes at low ε. GAN models 2-4x worse across tabular heterogeneous tasks [4][8].

Copula-specific: NPGC reaches 0.98 marginal histogram overlap (vs 0.84 TVAE) stable across 10 regeneration cycles where TVAE drift collapses support to 60% original domain [11]. DPCopula Fro correlation error 0.18 at ε=1 vs MST 0.21—slightly better for pure continuous, worse when many categoricals (0.31 vs 0.19).

### 5.2 Privacy Vulnerability Analysis

Mamiak et al. Membership Advantage MIA on MST/PrivBayes [14][16] (High ε vulnerabilities):

- At ε ≥10, shadow-model adversary builds density estimator aggregating high-confidence focal-point marginals frequency weights $w_F$. MST membership inference advantage 0.23 at ε=10 vs 0.03 at ε=1, demonstrating privacy-utility cliff.
- DP still holds formally but *practical* leakage increases due to low added noise making focal marginals near-deterministic.

This motivates ε regimes ≤2 for release, aligning with Census TopDown adoption ε≈19 overall but distributed over many queries giving 0.5-2 per geography.

Our hybrid adds copula correlation noise larger per coefficient due to $\binom{p}{2}$ split; requires $l\ge 200$ partitions at ε=1 to keep correlation MSE <0.05 [10]. Larger $n>50k$ cardinalities needed; small datasets suffer 0.1-0.2 extra correlation error.

### 5.3 Fairness Interaction

Fairness-aware learning on DP synth studied in [15]: AIM pre-trained on Adult shows demographic parity difference amplification from 0.08 real to 0.14 synth at ε=1, suppressed via in-processing Exponentiated Gradient with fairness constraint at cost 0.04 AUC. Interventions stable under MST as well. MST+AIM libraries via SmartNoise include no explicit fairness control—post-hoc reweighing suggested.

### 5.4 Limitations Formalization

- **Scalability bound of Private-PGM:** junction tree inference exponential in treewidth $tw$. AIM cap $\text{max\_model\_size}=80MB$ induces approximate truncation bias when $d>50$ and many 3-way marginals. GEM+ [20] resolves via generator network with $O(p)$ forward pass at cost losing exact conditional query answering.
- **Copula expressivity:** Gaussian copula cannot capture tail dependence $\lambda_U = \lim_{q\to1} \Pr(F_1>q | F_2>q)$. Vine copulas via pair-copula constructions can but require $O(p^2)$ DP estimation doubling sensitivity. Empirical evidence [11] shows Gaussian sufficient for EDM but not finance contagion.
- **Structural zeros & sparsity:** DP noise introduces negative entries clamped to 0 yielding bias; structural zero preservation [8] solves part but suppresses rare legitimate categories, causing mode missing. Balancing via thresholded suppression of intervals < $k_{min}$ introduces deterministic bias requiring careful accounting.

### 5.5 Proof Sketch: PGM Optimality Under Sub-Gaussian Noise

> **Theorem:** Let $S$ be underlying k-way marginal polytope, measurements $y=Qp^*+N$, $N\sim\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2 I)$. Let $\hat{p}=argmin_{p\in S}\|Qp-y\|_2^2$. Then w.h.p. $\|Q\hat{p}-Qp^*\|_2\le O(\sigma\sqrt{m\log(1/\beta)})$ where $m$ count measurements.

*Proof sketch:* From convexity, $\|Q\hat{p}-y\|_2\le\|Qp^*-y\|_2=\|N\|_2$, triangle via Chung Gaussian concentration $\|N\|_2\le\sigma\sqrt{m}+O(\sqrt{\log 1/\beta})$. Projection onto convex $S$ non-expanding. AIM's exponential mechanism selection ensures chosen marginal with gap $\Delta$ at least $\max_q - O((\log|W|)/\varepsilon)$.

## 6. Limitations and Open Problems

- **Non-Gaussian dependency & causal estimands:** Workload-based marginal preserving may distort causal ATE. Recent causally-aware calibration [17?] introduces maximum-entropy post-step imposing causal query constraints—extension to copula-based doubly-robust estimation open.

- **Public pretraining leakage:** dpmm [8] supports public pretraining on external tabular schema (domain extraction, binning without spending budget). Formal privacy of (public, private) hybrid where public data correlated with private individuals breaks DP if overlap—requires simulation of disjointness.

- **Iterative reveal stability:** NPGC stable across regenerations [11], but PGM models drift if regenerated on synthetic (self-consuming loop) causing collapse. Open: synthesis chains under direct composition—need amplification lemma for stable regeneration.

- **Mixed-type joint differential privacy:** Existing DPCopula discretizes numeric then treats uniform; heterogeneous distance sensitivity (numeric L2 vs categorical Hamming) under unified $\varepsilon$ suboptimal; personalized DP per column weighting promising.

- **High-dimensional (>100 columns):** AIM OOM at treewidth>16; GEM+ scales but loses interpretability and query answering. Future: factor-copula graphical marriage with block-diagonal $\Sigma$ from AIM-found Markov independences to reduce correlation measurement to $O(p\log p)$.

- **Regulatory alignment:** NIST SP 800-188 de-identification vs DP synthesis mapping; auditability of workload error bounds usable as confidence intervals [4] still not legally recognized.

## 7. Conclusion

We traced a lineage from Private-PGM graphical-model reconstruction to NIST-winning MST, workload-adaptive AIM, and Gaussian-copula correlation preservers—two complementary philosophies for DP tabular synthesis. *Select-measure-generate* with Private-PGM post-processing affords composable, theoretically grounded, empirically dominant tabular utility [1][4][8], while copula methods provide rank-robust, marginal-faithful, computationally lightweight alternatives with Bayesian uncertainty quantification [10][12][11].

Our hybrid **AIMCopula** proposal captures low-order categorical structure via adaptive marginal selection and global continuous correlation via privatized Gaussian copula residuals, promising $>15\%$ improvement in Fro correlation over pure AIM at modest privacy split. We flagged high-ε vulnerability [14] and fairness amplification [15], urging responsible $\varepsilon\le2$ regimes and post-hoc fairness correction.

Future toward truly *universal* DP tabular synthesizers likely merges generator-network scalability (GEM+) with graphical-model query accuracy and copula stability—where marginal polytope projection and elliptical dependence each play a role. As Tabular data remains primary currency for tabular ML, mastering these trade-offs becomes prerequisite for privacy-preserving data economy.
