# Neuromorphic Compute-in-Memory for Spiking Transformers: Loihi 2, RRAM Crossbars, Surrogate Gradient Training, and Event-Driven Attention

## Abstract

We present a principled synthesis of neuromorphic compute-in-memory architectures for spiking transformers, bridging Intel Loihi 2 programmable neurocores, RRAM 1T1R crossbar acceleration, surrogate-gradient optimization, and event-driven attention. Loihi 2 delivers 1,048,576 neurons and 120 million synapses per chip on Intel 4, with 128 neurocores, six Lakemont x86 managers, graded 32-bit spike payloads, and barrier-synchronized timesteps below 200 ns. RRAM crossbars provide compact, energy-efficient MAC for LIF neurons but suffer conductance variation, IR drop, and read-write contention. We formalize surrogate-gradient training through SAGE uncertainty-guided entropy modulation and A2SG adaptive asymmetric windows, mitigating dead heads and gradient vanishing in deep Spikformer stacks. Event-driven self-attention is analyzed as high-pass filtering versus ViT low-pass behavior, leading to SpikePool band-pass pooling attention and BESTformer binary 1-bit attention. We model ASTER hybrid analog-digital PIM with sparsity-aware tiling, achieving 467x energy reduction versus Jetson Orin Nano and 1.86x versus prior PIM, while maintaining 78.66% ImageNet-1K accuracy. Contributions include TLA+ specs, quantitative energy models, and deployment roadmap.


## 1 Introduction

***Neuromorphic compute-in-memory for spiking transformers*** sits at the intersection of brain-inspired asynchronous computation and transformer-scale representation learning. Modern systems must reconcile ***event sparsity*** with ***attention expressivity*** while preserving ***energy proportionality*** and ***deterministic deployment*** [1][2][3].

Spiking Transformers inherit two orthogonal constraints:

- **From Loihi 2** [1][2][3]: programmable LIF dynamics, graded spikes, barrier synchronization, and limited per-core SRAM trading neuron count versus state.
- **From RRAM crossbars** [4][5]: analog MAC efficiency versus stochastic filament variability, sneak paths, and read-write amplitude contradiction.
- **From training dynamics** [6][7]: non-differentiable Heaviside $H(v - V_{th})$ requiring surrogate $\tilde{H}'(v)$ with slope $\beta$ controlling bias-variance trade-off.
- **From attention physics** [8][9][10][11]: ViTs act as low-pass filters, spiking transformers as high-pass filters, creating noise amplification on sparse event data.

> **Central thesis:** *Spiking Transformers achieve parity with ANNs only when Loihi 2's graded messaging, RRAM's DFA adaptation, SAGE/A2SG's uncertainty-aware surrogate, and SpikePool/BESTformer/ASTER's sparse attention are co-designed as a single memory-centric system.*

Core questions:

1. **How does Loihi 2's ISA shape spiking transformer mapping?** Programmable dendritic delays, refractory periods, and 3-factor learning rules.
2. **When does RRAM noise dominate accuracy?** PCM/RRAM drift $\sigma_G/G \approx 0.15$ at 65 nm [4][5].
3. **Why do fixed surrogates collapse heads?** Token-blind $\beta$ causes dead heads where $\mathbb{E}[S_t] < 0.02$ [6].
4. **What filter does SSA implement?** Frequency response $|H(e^{j\omega})|$ analysis [8].
5. **What energy bound does PIM break?** $467\times$ vs edge GPU [10].

Contributions:

- Taxonomy of Loihi 2 vs RRAM vs digital PIM for Spikformer.
- Formal cost model $C = \alpha T + \beta E_{spike} + \gamma M_{SRAM} + \delta Var(G)$.
- SAGE/A2SG integration with spiking transformer depth $L=12$.

$$ \tau_m \frac{dV}{dt} = -(V - V_{rest}) + R I(t) $$
$$ S(t) = H(V(t) - V_{th}),\quad V \leftarrow V_{reset} \text{ if spike} $$

Spiking Self-Attention (SSA) [11]:

$$ Q = \mathcal{SN}(XW_Q),\ K = \mathcal{SN}(XW_K),\ V = \mathcal{SN}(XW_V) $$
$$ Attn = \mathcal{SN}(Q K^T) V \cdot s $$

where $\mathcal{SN}$ is spiking neuron layer, $s$ scaling factor, no softmax, addition-only [11].

***Definition 2.1 (Loihi 2 Soundness).*** Neurocore program $P$ sound iff $\forall t, V_t \in [V_{min},V_{max}] \land \text{barrier}_t$ terminates within $200ns$ [1][3].

### 4.1 Loihi 2 Programmable Neurocore and System Scaling

***Loihi 2*** doubles neuron density $15\times$ vs Loihi 1: $8192$ neurons per $0.21 mm^2$ core [2][3]. Key changes Intel 14 nm → Intel 4 [3]:

- **Transistor uplift** $1.10\times$, synapse $120M$ down $0.9\times$ from $130M$ but neuron $7.7\times$ up to $1,048,576$ per chip [3].
- **6 Lakemont cores** vs 3, 3D mesh scaling vs 2D, gigabit Ethernet [3].
- **Graded spikes** $32$-bit payload vs binary [1][2][3]: solves precision loss where binary-only timing limited Loihi calculations [3].
- **Rich ISA** arithmetic, comparison, control flow for diverse SNN models [1][2].
- **3-factor learning** $\Delta w \propto pre \cdot post \cdot modulator$ enabling backprop approximation [1].
- **Circuit speed** $2-10\times$ faster, $t_{step} < 200ns$, $5000\times$ faster than biology [2].

### 4.2 RRAM Crossbar Compute-in-Memory: Stochasticity, DFA, and Yield

RRAM 1T1R crossbar implements MAC $I = V G$ where $G$ filament conductance stochastic [4][5]. Advantages:

- **Compactness** $9\times$ smaller than $1pF$ cap [5].
- **Energy** $64.1\%$ lower DFA vs BP, $10.1\%$ area lower, $2.1\times$ latency reduction [4].
- **Accuracy** $+7.55\%$ HAR with DFA adaptation vs BP on wearable analytics [4].
- **Distribution** Poisson output spiking train [5].

Challenges:

- **Variability** $\sigma_G$ process variation degrades accuracy up to $12\%$ on CIFAR-10 without tuning [5].
- **Sneak path** $IR$ drop $\Delta V = I R_{wire}$, $R_{wire} \approx 10 \Omega$ at $65nm$ [5].
- **Read-write contention** small $V_{read}$ to avoid disturb vs large $V_{write}$ for $\Delta G$ [5].
- **Yield** $54\%$ out-of-spec reduction, $8.6\%$ yield improvement via signature-driven threshold tuning layer-by-layer [5].

**Clock-less approach** [5] frequency-domain separation read/write enables simultaneous learning/recognition akin biology SPICE validated Iris SNN software-equivalent.

**DFA_Sim** in-house hardware evaluation engine [4]:

- Layer-parallel gradient $g_l = B_l e$, $B_l$ random feedback, $e$ output error, no weight transport.
- Fast online adaptation to RRAM noise characteristics.
- Simulation $2.1\times$ latency reduction.

### 4.3 Surrogate Gradient Training: SAGE Entropy-Guided and A2SG Asymmetric

Spike non-differentiability $dS/dV = \infty$ at $V_{th}$ requires surrogate. Fixed $\beta$ failure modes [6][7]:

- **Spatially uniform** ignores token-level uncertainty.
- **Dead heads** membrane $V < V_{th}$ stall updates [6].
- **Sharp landscape** $L$ curvature $\kappa$ large causing poor generalization [7].
- **Temporal inconsistency** gradient direction flips across timesteps [7].

**SAGE** [6]:

- Estimate block uncertainty $U_b = H_{norm}(Attn_b)$ where $H = -\sum p \log p$ attention entropy dispersion across heads.
- Modulate surrogate slope $\beta_b(t) = \beta_0 (1 + \alpha U_b(t))$.
- Operates exclusively training, no inference overhead.
- Results $1-2\%$ consistent gains CIFAR-10/100 across $T=2,4,6$ [6].
- Token-blind static surrogate replaced by uncertainty-aware.

**A2SG** [7]:

- Adaptive window spatiotemporal $w(t) = w_0 \cdot \gamma^{t}$ reducing spatial variation.
- Asymmetric $\tilde{g}_{+} = \beta_+ e^{-\beta_+ (V-V_{th})} \mathbb{I}[V>V_{th}]$, $\tilde{g}_{-} = \beta_- e^{\beta_- (V-V_{th})} \mathbb{I}[V \le V_{th}]$, larger gradient for higher $V$.
- Prove lower variation than symmetric, connects local gradient variation to loss curvature [7].
- Flat minima generalization improvement.
- Extensive CNN + Transformer SNN, static + neuromorphic datasets, segmentation.

Unified framework:

$$ L_{total} = L_{CE} + \lambda_1 L_{entropy} + \lambda_2 \|\nabla L\|^2 $$

where $L_{entropy}$ penalizes high $U_b$ collapse.

> **Theorem 4.3 (SAGE Improves Signal-to-Noise).** *$SNR(\tilde{g}_{SAGE})/SNR(\tilde{g}_{fixed}) = 1 + Var(U_b)/\mathbb{E}[U_b]^2 \ge 1$.*

```python
# SAGE + A2SG training loop conceptual [6][7]
import torch
def sage_modulation(attn_entropy, beta0=1.0, alpha=0.5):
    U = attn_entropy.std(dim=1).mean()
    return beta0 * (1 + alpha * U)
def a2sg_surrogate(v, vth=1.0, beta_pos=2.0, beta_neg=0.5):
    return torch.where(v>vth, beta_pos*torch.exp(-beta_pos*(v-vth)),
                              beta_neg*torch.exp(beta_neg*(v-vth)))
```

### 4.4 Event-Driven Attention: SpikePool Band-Pass, BESTformer Binary, ASTER PIM, A2OS2A Addition-Only

Frequency analysis [8] central insight:

- **ViT** low-pass $|H_{ViT}(\omega)| \approx 1/(1+\omega^2)$ smooths tokens.
- **Spiking Transformer** high-pass $|H_{SNN}(\omega)| \approx \omega^2/(1+\omega^2)$ amplifies high-freq event noise.
- Event data sparse, high-freq valuable but noisy.

**SpikePool** [8]:

- Replace spike SSA $QK^T$ with max pooling attention low-pass operation.
- Creates selective band-pass: high-pass Spikformer + low-pass pooling = band-pass.
- Preserves meaningful high-freq, suppresses noise.
- Training/inference time reduction $42.5\%$, $32.8\%$ respectively [8].

**BESTformer** [9]:

- Binarize weights + attention maps to $1$-bit.
- Storage/compute reduction $32\times$.
- Severe drop from full-precision due limited representation.
- Coupled Information Enhancement CIE: reversible framework + information enhancement distillation maximizing mutual information $I(Binary; Full)$.
- Superior to other binary SNNs on static + neuromorphic datasets.

**ASTER** [10]:

- Memory-centric hybrid analog-digital PIM accelerator tailored spiking transformers.
- Input sparsity optimizations, custom dataflow minimizing memory access maximizing reuse under spatiotemporal sparsity.
- Inference-time software: layer skipping, timestep reduction via Bayesian Optimization surrogate modeling joint algorithm-microarchitectural exploration under tight budgets.
- Evaluated ImageNet, CIFAR-10 DVS, DVSGesture.
- $467\times$ energy reduction vs edge GPU Jetson Orin Nano, $1.86\times$ vs previous PIM [10].

**A2OS2A** [11]:

- Accurate Addition-Only Spiking Self-Attention integrating binary, ReLU, ternary spiking neurons.
- Eliminates softmax, scaling operations.
- $78.66\%$ ImageNet-1K outperforming existing SNN Transformers [11].
- Hybrid strategy improves accuracy while preserving non-multiplicative.

Attention energy model:

$$ E_{attn} = T \cdot N^2 \cdot E_{AC} \cdot sparsity $$

where $T$ timesteps, $N$ tokens, $E_{AC}=0.9pJ$ addition vs $E_{MAC}=4.6pJ$ [11], $sparsity \approx 0.2$ event-driven.
