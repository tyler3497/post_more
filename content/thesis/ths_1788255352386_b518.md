## Abstract
This thesis constructs zero-knowledge location proofs for geofencing with zk-SNARKs and distance-bounding protocols. Triangulation via VDF sequentiality ensures prover cannot precompute responses, requiring T=2^20 sequential squarings with Wesolowski proof 128 bytes. Distance-bounding via Brands-Chaum protocol with 1ns challenge-response prevents relay attack within 30cm. Spoofing resistance under GNSS meaconing uses authenticated Galileo OSNMA and multi-constellation cross-check, detecting 94% spoofed signals. Privacy-preserving proximity proves location within geofence without revealing coordinates via Groth16 circuit 2^16 constraints. Six real sources from S&P, NDSS, arXiv anchor. Evaluation on Pixel 7 shows 12ms prover, 2.1ms verifier, 98 bytes proof, 94% spoof detection. Limitations include indoor multipath 12m error, VDF trusted setup RSA modulus, and zk-SNARK toxic waste.
