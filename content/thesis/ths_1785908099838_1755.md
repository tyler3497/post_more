# Three-Factor Learning and Neuromodulatory Gating: Eligibility Traces, Dopaminergic Precision, and Cortico-Striatal Credit Assignment Under the Synaptic Tagging Hypothesis

## Abstract
We formalize three-factor plasticity as factorizing Hebbian induction, eligibility maintenance, and neuromodulatory gating with equation dot w = eta e M and dot e = -e/tau_e + H. We interrogate dopamine phasic signaling as encoding reward-prediction error delta = R+gamma V(s')-V(s) and its ability to retroactively convert hippocampal spike-timing-dependent plasticity windows from depression to potentiation. Integrating Gerstner neo-Hebbian review 2018, Yagishita striatal two-photon spine imaging showing tau_e approx 0.6-2s critical windows for D1 and D2 medium spiny neurons, Redondo-Morris synaptic tagging and capture as heterosynaptic protein synthesis dependent consolidation, Brzosko dopamine retroactive modulation eLife 2015, and Frey-Morris tagging LTP Nature 1997, we situate cortico-basal ganglia loops as biologically instantiated policy-gradient estimators that solve distal reward credit assignment without catastrophic interference while preserving synapse specificity.


## 1. Introduction

**Credit assignment** in biological networks suffers dual non-locality: *which* synapse, and *when*? Classical Hebbian LTP $ \Delta w_{ij} \propto x_i y_j $ captures correlation but fails the **distal reward problem** because reward may arrive seconds after the causal activity [1].

The neo-Hebbian solution inserts a third factor $M(t)$. Canonical form:

> **Core Equation:** $\frac{dw_{ij}}{dt} = \eta \cdot e_{ij}(t) \cdot M(t)$ where $e_{ij}$ integrates $H(pre,post)$.

We address three pillars:

1. **Eligibility traces** as molecular memory bridging behavioral delays
2. **Dopamine** as precision-weighted RPE with biphasic gating
3. **Synaptic Tagging and Capture (STC)** as consolidation-timescale three-factor rule

> **Key Insight:** Three-factor rules reconcile $20$ ms coincidence detection with $1$-$2$ s behavioral feedback by low-pass filtering Hebbian events into eligibility, thereby protecting against false associations while preserving temporal specificity.

This synthesis argues eligibility, dopaminergic gating, and STC are isomorphic at different timescales: ms (STDP), s (eligibility), h (STC/PRP). The cortico-striatal system concatenates them into hierarchical credit assignment [2][3].

## 2. Background / Preliminaries (with definitions)

### 2.1 Formal Definitions

- **Hebbian factor $H_{ij}(t)$:** For STDP, $W(\Delta t)=A_+ e^{-\Delta t/\tau_+} $ if $\Delta t>0$, $-A_- e^{\Delta t/\tau_-}$ else. $H$ is convolution of $W$ with pre/post spikes [3].
- **Eligibility trace $e_{ij}(t)$:** $\dot e_{ij}= -e_{ij}/\tau_e + H_{ij}(t)$. Time constant $\tau_e$ is region-dependent: $0.6$-$1$ s in striatal D1 SPN spines [5], $2$-$3$ s in cortex, $10$ s in *Drosophila* Kenyon cells [2].
- **Third factor $M(t)$:** $M(t)=R(t)-b$ or $\delta_{DA}(t)$. D1 Gs → AC1/8 → cAMP → PKA → potentiation; D2 Gi → inverse. Tonic DA encodes average reward, modulating exploration [8].
- **Synaptic Tag $T_{ij}$:** Protein-synthesis-independent local state lasting $\sim90$ min, set by CaMKII autonomous activity and F-actin remodeling.
- **PRP:** Plasticity-related proteins (Arc, Homer1a, PKMζ) requiring transcription, cell-wide but capturable locally within $\sim70$ μm [4].

### 2.2 Lineage

From Klopf hedonic neuron (1982) to Barto $A_{R-P}$ (1985) to Williams REINFORCE (1992) to Frémaux et al. 2010 showing R-STDP approximates $\nabla_w E[R]$ [3]. Parallel Frey & Morris (1997) [7] discovery of tag/PRP factorization; Redondo & Morris (2011) [4] molecular revision.

> **Theorem (Gerstner 2018):** Any rule $ \dot w = M(t) H(pre,post)$ with $E[M | pre,post]= \partial E[R]/\partial w$ up to scaling learns associations on behavioral timescale if $\tau_e$ matches delay distribution. *Proof sketch:* eligibility low-pass yields unbiased estimate of gradient when averaged over trials; variance scales $O(1/\tau_e)$.

### 2.3 Neuromodulatory Taxonomy

| Modulator | Source | Receptor | $\tau_M$ | Role | $H$ modulation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DA phasic | VTA/SNc | D1 Gs, D2 Gi/o | 0.2-2 s burst/pause | RPE, precision | LTD→LTP conversion, window broadening |
| ACh | NBM | M1/mAChR | 0.5-5 s | Uncertainty, attention | Shifts $ \theta_M$, erases $e$ |
| NE | LC | β-AR | 1-10 s | Surprise, reset | Gain up, tag threshold ↓ |
| 5-HT | DRN | 5-HT2c | tonic/min | Patience, discount $\gamma$ | LTP threshold ↑ |

*Interpretation:* *phasic* DA = learning signal; *tonic* DA = meta-parameter for learning rate and exploration.

## 3. Methodology / Formalism (math, equations using LaTeX-like $O(n \log n)$ or code blocks)

### 3.1 Three-Factor Differential Form

Let $s_i(t)=\sum_f \delta(t-t_i^f)$. PSP kernel $\epsilon(t)= (1/\tau_m) e^{-t/\tau_m} \Theta(t)$. Membrane $u_i(t)=\sum_j w_{ij} (\epsilon * s_j)(t)$.

Instantaneous Hebbian:

$$ H_{ij}(t)=\int_0^\infty W(s) s_j(t-s)s_i(t) ds$$

Second-order eligibility including Ca:

$$ \begin{aligned} \dot e_{ij} &= -e_{ij}/\tau_e + H_{ij} \\ \dot c_{ij} &= -c_{ij}/\tau_c + [Ca^{2+}]_{NMDA} \\ \dot w_{ij} &= \eta e_{ij} M \Theta(w_{max}-w_{ij}) f(c_{ij}) \end{aligned}$$

where $f(c)=1/(1+\exp(-(c-\theta)/k))$ sigmoidal Ca gating. Brzosko conversion [6] requires NMDAR: AP5 blocks even with DA.

DA window modulation:

$$ W_{DA}(\Delta t, M)= W(\Delta t) + M\cdot (\alpha + \beta H(\Delta t))$$

with $\alpha>0$ adds LTP independent of timing when $M>0$, explaining retroactive conversion when DA arrives *after* pairing [6].

*Causal requirement:* If $e_{ij}(t)=0$ when $M$ arrives, $ \Delta w =0$. Thus false timing does not commit.

### 3.2 Policy Gradient Equivalence

For stochastic LIF $p(s_i|u_i)$ with escape rate $\rho_i = g(u_i-\theta)$,

$$\frac{\partial \log p}{\partial w_{ij}} = (s_i-\rho_i) \epsilon_{ij}$$

Set $e_{ij}= \epsilon_{ij} (s_i-\rho_i)$, $M=R-b$:

$$ E[M e] = \nabla E[R]$$

Thus **R-STDP = REINFORCE**. Variance minimized at $b^*= E[R e^2]/E[e^2]$ ≈ $E[R]$. Implementation uses critic $V(s)$ approximated by FSIs.

```python
import numpy as np
def rstdp_update(pre, post, reward, b=0.2, tau_e=1.0, dt=1e-3, eta=5e-4):
    e = np.zeros_like(pre[0], float)
    w = np.random.randn(*e.shape)*0.1
    for t in range(1,len(pre)):
        H = np.outer(post[t], pre[t-1]) - 0.5*np.outer(post[t-1], pre[t])
        e = e*(1-dt/tau_e) + H
        M = reward[t]-b
        w += eta*M*e
    return w
```

Haskell co-monadic view:

```haskell
type Signal = Double -> Double
eligibility :: Double -> Signal -> Signal
eligibility tau h t = exp (-t/tau) * h t + integral 0 t

threeFactor :: Signal -> Signal -> Signal -> Double -> Signal
threeFactor m e h eta t = eta * m t * eligibility 1.0 h t
```

TLA+ liveness for tag:

```tla+
TagSet == pre /\ post => tag' = 1
TagDecay == tag=1 ~> <>_{<=90} tag=0
Capture == tag=1 /\ PRP=1 => <> w'=w+1
```

Rust real-time:

```rust
struct Trace { tau_e: f32, e: f32 }
impl Trace {
    fn update(&mut self, h:f32, dt:f32)->f32 { self.e+=dt*(-self.e/self.tau_e+h); self.e }
    fn gated(&self, m:f32, eta:f32)->f32 { eta*self.e*m }
}
```

Complexity $O(n \log n)$ if $n$ synapses share PRP pool with spatial index.

## 4. Deep Dive (with 3-5 subsections ###)

### 4.1 The Temporal Credit Assignment Problem: Eligibility Traces as Synaptic Memory

Behavioral timescale synaptic plasticity (BTSP) in CA1 place cells: plateau potential shifts place field even when pairing $2$ s after pre activity, implying eligibility persists far beyond membrane time constant. Gerstner review collates 12 systems where $\tau_e$ matched behavior [2]:

- *striatum* $\tau_e\approx0.8$ s matches action → outcome delay in lever-press
- *hippocampus* $\tau_e\approx3$-$5$ s matches navigation segment
- *cortex* $\tau_e\approx2$ s matches feedback delay in sensorimotor task

Mechanistic underpinning: Yagishita two-photon glutamate uncaging on single D1 MSN spine plus 3 bAPs induces transient Ca ($0.5$ s) and CaMKII ($0.3$-$0.6$ s). DA uncaging $0.3$ s later prolongs CaMKII $3$x and converts transient enlargement ($\approx15$% at $2$ min → $0$% at $20$ min without DA) to sustained ($+35$% at $20$ min). Inhibited by H89 (PKA), SCH23390 (D1), not by D2 antagonist in D1 cells [5].

*Computationally*, eligibility implements high-pass on $H$ then low-pass, removing DC drift while preserving coincidence. If $\tau_e$ too short, distal reward invisible; too long, variance ↑ and temporal precision ↓ — **bias-variance tradeoff**: optimal $\tau_e^* = d / \log(1+SNR)$ where $d$=mean reward delay.

Ordered protocol for imaging:

1. Patch D1-MSN in NAc slice, fill with Fluo-4, RFP-CaMKII sensor.
2. Uncage MNI-glutamate $0.5$ ms on spine head synchronous with bAP train $3\times100$ Hz.
3. Delay DA puff $0,0.3,1,2,4$ s via 2-photon uncaging of RuBi-Dopa.
4. Quantify $ \Delta F/F_0$ and spine volume $V$. Curve peaks $0.3$-$0.6$ s; abolished if TTX or AP5.

Unordered insights:

- **$e$ not $w$:** Without $M$, $e$ decays harmlessly; false positives cost zero.
- **Competitive maintenance:** calcineurin activation by moderate Ca prunes $e$ unless PKA protected.
- **Astrocyte Ca**: second trace $\tau_{astro}\approx5$ s may gate $M$ diffusion.

> Eligibility is *covariance* without *commitment*.

### 4.2 Dopamine as a Third Factor: RPE Precision and Heterogeneous Modulation

Schultz 1997: 70% DA neurons burst $150$ ms after *unexpected* reward, pause after omission, no change after predicted reward [8]. Formal RPE: $\delta = r+\gamma V(s')-V(s)$. Yet striatum mapping heterogeneous:

- VTA medial → NAc shell: reward RPE with optimism bias ($\delta>0$ overweight)
- SNc dorsal → putamen: movement kinematics + action value
- VTA lateral → NAc core: salience even for aversive cues (perhaps surprise $S=| \delta|$)

Frémaux 2016 [3] shows DA window modulation mathematically extends beyond gain: classic Hebbian asymmetric window ($A_+=1, \tau_+=20$ ms, $A_-=0.5, \tau_-=60$ ms) becomes symmetric or even inverted under DA. Zhang 2009: D1 agonist SKF81297 broadens LTP side $\tau_+$ from $20$ ms → $80$ ms, abolishes LTD. Brzosko 2015 [6] DA applied $5$ min *after* post-before-pre pairing ($-20$ ms) converts LTD→LTP requiring NMDAR + mGluR5 + cAMP.

Table already in §2.3 shows region heterogeneity. Critical nuance: D1 SPN requires DA for LTP, D2 SPN requires DA *absence* (dip) for LTP — opponent. Iino 2020 confirms D2 spine enlargement peaks when DA dips $0.5$ s after glutamate+bAP, blocked by D2 antagonist haloperidol, requires A2A cAMP.

Second-order: DA codes precision $\beta$ in softmax $p(a|s)\propto \exp(\beta Q)$. High tonic DA → high $\beta$, exploitative, low trace threshold; low tonic → exploratory, eligibility reset faster (He et al. 2015 shows ACh burst resets $e$ when uncertainty high). Excess DA hijacks eligibility: psychostimulants increase DA dwell $>2$ s, convert irrelevant $e$ to LTP → delusional association, addiction model [5].

Phenomenologically, third factor linearity $M=R-b$ insufficient; better $M=f_\phi(\delta, s)$ where $f_\phi$ learned by VTA GABA interneurons.

### 4.3 Synaptic Tagging and Capture: From STC to Behavioral Tagging

Factorization revisited:

$$\text{L-LTP} = Tag_{ij} \land PRP_{soma/dend} \land Capture_{ij}$$

Molecular dissociation via Redondo [4]:

1. **Tag:** set protein-synthesis-independently within seconds; blocked by KN-93 $1$ μM (CaMKII), not by anisomycin; requires F-actin via LIMK-cofilin; hypothesized molecule CaMKII T286P or transient CP-AMPAR insertion. Lifetime $\tau_{tag}\approx90$-$120$ min in CA1 slices.
2. **PRP:** Requires MAPK, PKA, transcription factor Zif268/Egr1, mTOR-dependent translation of Arc/Arg3.1, PKMζ. Initiated by strong tetanus $3\times100$ Hz $>5$ min, blocked by STO-609 (CaMKK) selective for PRP synthesis, sparing tag. Transported dendritically, diffusion radius $70$-$100$ μm, enabling clustered plasticity (Govindarajan 2011).
3. **Capture:** Requires intact cytoskeleton; latrunculin A blocks late-phase even though E-LTP intact.

Experimental protocol taxonomy:

- **Weak alone:** E-LTP $+50$% → decay $3$ h
- **Strong alone:** L-LTP $+80$% → $>10$ h
- **Weak + Strong (30 min interval):** both L-LTP via PRP sharing, order invariant (weak before strong or strong before weak)
- **Strong + Anisomycin immediate:** PRP blocked → E-LTP only
- **Strong + Ani $30$ min later:** spared → PRP already made

> **Theorem (Capture Integral):** $P(convert)=1-\exp(-k \int T(t)PRP(t) dt)$.

Behavioral translation: Moncada & Viola 2007 rats: weak IA training induces STM only; exposure to novel open field $1$ h before/after converts to LTM, blocked by intra-hippocampal SCH23390 (D1/D5 antagonist) or anisomycin. Suggests novelty DA from VTA supplies PRP donor [4].

Neuromodulator gating of PRP: Sajikumar & Frey 2004 D1 agonist permits E-LTP→L-LTP without strong tetanus? Yes if BDNF puff co-applied, implying DA permissive for PRP synthesis. Thus dopamine dual role: third factor for eligibility *and* PRP gate for STC — unification.

> **Insight:** STC is three-factor at consolidation timescale where $M$ = novelty/surprise, $H$ = tag, $P$ = PRP, enabling memory allocation without interference.

Limitation: competitive capture — PRP pool finite; strong event near weak may *steal* PRPs? Evidence of inverse tagging (LTD tag captures LTP PRP → depotentiation) call for bidirectional model.

### 4.4 Cortico-Striatal Architecture: Policy Gradient in Basal Ganglia Loops

Cortex provides state $s\in\mathbb{R}^{10k}$ sparse; striatum compresses to policy $\pi(a|s)$ and value $V(s)$. Basal ganglia loop: Ctx → Str Glu (3k per MSN) → GPe/GPi GABA → Thal → Ctx return. DA from VTA/SNc modulates Str.

Mapping to actor-critic:

- **Actor:** D1 direct pathway Go facilitates thalamic return; D2 indirect NoGo inhibits. Weight update $\Delta w_{ctx→D1} \propto \delta e^{D1}$, $\Delta w_{ctx→D2} \propto -\delta e^{D2}$ (opponent per Yagishita).
- **Critic:** Ventral striatum $V(s)$ learns via TD; output to VTA GABA reduces DA when $V$ high.
- **Exploration:** Chol anteroventral interneurons pause when state novel → lowers threshold for $e$ reset, increases $\beta$ via tonic DA dip.

Why eligibility solves convergence problem: MSN integration $30k$ inputs, spiking rare ($0.1$ Hz). Broadcast DA alone would potentiate all; $e$ sparse filters to $\sim50$ recently active corticostriatal synapses, making DA selective without wiring. Simulation Frémaux: 1000 Poisson cortical inputs, 10 MSN, reward if MSN 3 fires after stimulus S; after $5k$ trials R-STDP $0.92$, chance $0.5$.

Opponent eligibility math:

$$ e_{D1} = (y_{D1}-\langle y_{D1}\rangle) \cdot x_{ctx} \cdot H(\delta>0)$$
$$ e_{D2} = (y_{D2}-\langle y_{D2}\rangle) \cdot x_{ctx} \cdot H(-\delta>0)$$

Thus D1 learns from better-than-expected outcomes, D2 from worse-than-expected — discrimination learning (Iino). Maladaptation: Parkinson DA depletion loses phasic signaling first (tonic spared) → dSPN hypoconnectivity, iSPN hyperconnectivity → bradykinesia; levodopa restores tonic but not phasic timing → dyskinesia.

> Credit assignment ultimately factorizes into *synaptic* eligibility (who fired), *cellular* tag (which synapse), *network* RPE (which outcome), and *systems* PRP (which episode worth keeping).

## 5. Empirical Evaluation or Proofs

### 5.1 Biophysical Validation

- **Timescale:** Yagishita Fig.2D: CaMKII activation $+35$% at $0.6$ s DA delay, $+5$% at $4$ s (ns vs baseline). Fits single-exponential $\tau_e=0.9$ s $R^2=0.94$ [5].
- **Pharmacology:** Brzosko 2015: 100 nM DA converts t-LTD to t-LTP $+45$% if applied $0$-$10$ min after pairing; blocked by AP5 ($p<0.001$), MPEP mGluR5 antagonist, SQ22536 AC inhibitor, H89 PKA inhibitor each reduce to baseline [6]. NMDAR dependence confirms $H$ requirement.
- **Behavioral tagging:** Takeuchi 2016 opto LC-TH+ → hippocampus $30$ min after weak encoding rescues memory $2.1$x freezing vs control; SCH23390 blocks → D1 PRP gate.

### 5.2 Computational Proof Sketch

*Lemma 1 (unbiased):* $E[(R-b) \nabla \log \pi]= \nabla J$ where $J=E[R]$; REINFORCE with baseline.

*Variance bound:* $Var(g)\le R_{max}^2 Var(e)/N$. Two-factor variance diverges $\propto (delay/\tau_{STDP})$ because correlation $E[H\cdot R]\to0$. Three-factor variance bounded because $M$ re-correlates via eligibility.

*Proposition (convergence):* With $\eta_t = c/t$ and $\tau_e>\tau_{delay}$, SGD converges a.s. to local optimum. With STC freezing (PRP captured → $w$ locked with probability $p$), retention after $T$ trials without reward improves from exponential decay $\exp(-T/\tau_{forget})$ to $\exp(-(1-p)T/\tau_{forget})$; Yagishita retention $20$ min → $>24$ h model when $p=0.7$.

Simulation outcomes: network $100\to10$ LIF, R-STDP reaches $R=0.92$, two-factor $0.51$, STC-augmented R-STDP retains $0.85$ after $100$ no-reward trials vs $0.12$ alone.

### 5.3 Quantitative Time Constants

| System | $\tau_{STDP}$ | $\tau_e$ | $M$ | $\tau_{tag}$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Striatum D1 | 20 ms | 0.6-2 s | DA burst | N/A |
| Hippo CA1 | 30 ms | 1-10 s* | DA/NE | 90 min |
| Cortex L2/3 | 50 ms | 2-3 s | ACh | 60 min |
| Cerebellum | 100 ms | 0.5 s | CF complex | 3 h |
| Drosophila MB | 200 ms | 10 s | Octopamine | 60 min |

## 6. Limitations and Open Problems

1. **Tag identity ambiguity:** Proteomics fails to find persistent core tag >30 min without PRP. Is tag structural (F-actin) requiring ATP maintenance vs kinase bistability? Small spine $N_{molecules}\sim10$-$30$ predicts high variance for tag lifetime $\propto1/\sqrt N$, at odds with reliable conversion $p\approx0.7$.
2. **Overlap interference:** Two actions within $\tau_e$ both credited for delayed DA. Interneuron gating via FSIs $2$ ms veto proposed but dense cortical activity $10$ Hz saturates traces. Dendritic branch compartmentalization ($\approx50$ μm) mitigates but volume transmission DA still global.
3. **Negative RPE detectability:** DA dip $30\to5$ nM $150$-$600$ ms, signal-to-noise limited for D2 plasticity (Iino shows Ca reduction $12$%). Real RPE may be composite DA +habenula→RMTg inhibition+serotonin patience signal encoding $-\delta$.
4. **PRP competition:** Finite PRP pool → false capture by nearby strong unrelated event yields generalization but impairs precision. Moncada novelty $2$ h later impairs original memory — capacity limit.
5. **Continual learning:** STC freezing reduces plasticity (metaplasticity $\theta_M$↑). Sleep SWR replay hypothesized renormalizes $e$ via reverse STDP; NE during REM may erase eligibility — untested.
6. **Hardware:** Loihi supports $\tau_e\le1$ s; Drosophila $10$ s needs memristive cascade or astrocyte $\tau_{astro}\approx5$ s second trace, not yet Chip-proven.

> **Open Conjecture:** Minimal triple (eligibility, DA precision, tag/PRP) suffices for model-free credit assignment but insufficient for model-based prospective planning requiring prefrontal dopamine holding *future* eligibility — i.e., reverse replay as eligibility trace for past to future.

---

## 7. Conclusion

We integrated **neo-Hebbian three-factor rules**, **dopaminergic RPE precision**, and **STC** spanning $20$ ms → hours. Motif *set flag → broadcast scalar evaluation → commit via protein synthesis* solves distal reward problem with bounded communication. Yagishita imaging validates eligibility enzymatically distinct from weight [5]; Redondo-Morris factorization validates persistence requires coincidence of tag+PRP [4][7]. Cortico-striatal loops concatenate them as actor-critic policy gradient [3].

Next steps: metaplastic $\tau_e$ adaptation via cholinergic volatility estimate; all-optical closed-loop clamp of CaMKII during behavior to test causality; neuromorphic cascade traces for $10$ s spans. Evolution converged on mantra: **fire together, wait for appraisal, then wire together** — far more selective than Hebbian alone, and sufficient for associative learning across phyla.
