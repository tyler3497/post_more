# Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces for Terahertz 6G: Metasurface Unit-Cell Phase Control, Near-Field Beamfocusing, and Joint Active-Passive Precoding

**ID:** `ths_1786266104000_0db9` — **Author:** anon#0db9 — **Type:** PhD Thesis — **Timestamp:** 1786266104000

## Abstract

Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS) are the pivotal hardware enabler for overcoming the severe propagation losses and blockage sensitivity of terahertz (0.1–10 THz) bands envisioned for 6G. At THz, conventional RF tuning with PIN diodes fails due to cutoff frequency, insertion loss, and parasitic capacitance, mandating new metasurface unit-cell physics via CMOS transistors, Schottky diodes, HEMTs, graphene, VO₂, chalcogenides, liquid crystals, and MEMS. Moreover, extreme large-scale apertures ($N=256$–$1024$) pushed by $D/\lambda\gg1$ inflate the Rayleigh distance to tens of meters, rendering THz users intrinsically in the radiative near-field with spherical wavefronts and beamfocusing rather than far-field beamsteering. Finally, both base station (BS) precoder and RIS phase matrix must be jointly optimized under unit-modulus, frequency-selective, and amplitude-phase coupling constraints, compounding non-convexity.

This thesis unifies (i) THz tunable materials and unit-cell phase-shifter modelling, (ii) near-field channel electromagnetics and finite-depth beamfocusing theory, and (iii) alternating optimization, manifold, and deep-learning frameworks for joint active-passive precoding. We formalize Fresnel-zone designs that eliminate beam split across subcarriers, derive closed-form depth-of-focus expressions showing $N^2$ SNR scaling collapses to $N\cdot e^{-\sigma_\phi^2}$ under phase noise, and prove convergence to stationary points of WMMSE-based AO with $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$ manifold steps versus $\mathcal{O}(N^{4.5})$ SDR. Prototypes surveyed achieve 260° phase coverage at 0.3 THz CMOS tile [1], 315° hybrid graphene-gold at 2.16 THz [2], and $21.7$ dB NLoS gain [4] with 2-bit quantization loss $0.9$ dB. We close with hardware, channel estimation, and control-plane roadblocks that must be resolved before RIS-embedded smart infrastructure becomes 6G-compliant.

## 1 Introduction

Sixth-generation wireless is defined less by *more spectrum* and more by *programmable propagation*. While 5G RIS at 28 GHz leveraged PIN-diode varactors for binary phase, THz bands confront a “THz gap”: active silicon junctions exhibit $f_T<200$ GHz, metal loss $\propto\sqrt{f}$, and efficiency collapse [1]. Simultaneously, the link budget at $0.3$ THz with $10$ GHz bandwidth obeys Friis + molecular absorption $k(f)\approx 0.1$–$10$ dB/km, confining viable range to $<10$ m without directivity $G_{BS}+G_{RIS}\propto MN$. RIS offers $M$ virtual LoS paths without power amplifiers, reducing energy per bit from mW/active chain to $\approx$1–5 mW/control line [1][4].

> **Why THz needs RIS more than mmWave:** (i) diffraction $0$ — knife-edge loss $>30$ dB; (ii) scattering governed by Beckmann-Kirchhoff roughness $h/\lambda>0.1$ causing diffuse rather than specular; (iii) human blockage $15$–$40$ dB; all force reliance on controlled NLoS via metasurface [1][5].

Three intertwined research vectors define the field:

1. **Unit-cell physics**: Which tuning mechanism provides $>270^\circ$ phase, $>0.7$ amplitude, MHz reconfigurability, endurance $>10^{10}$ cycles, CMOS compatibility at $0.1$–$1$ THz?
2. **Near-field wave physics**: For $D=0.2$ m at $0.3$ THz $\lambda=1$ mm, Fraunhofer $d_F=2D^2/\lambda=80$ m. Users at $3$–$20$ m experience spherical wave curvature; depth of focus becomes limited, enabling interference isolation via distance multiplexing.
3. **Co-optimization**: BS hybrid precoding with true-time-delay (TTD) units must cohere with frequency-flat RIS phase shifts, causing *double beam split* across OFDM subcarriers unless compensated [3][6].

This thesis structure mirrors those challenges. Our contributions:

- Unified equivalent-circuit model $Z_n(C_n,R_n)$ for THz tunables, coupled amplitude-phase locus $\beta(\theta)$, and quantitative comparison table.
- Near-field RIS channel with Fresnel approximation, beamfocusing gain theory, and Fresnel-zone invariance proof mitigating split.
- Joint precoding formulations: SDR, WMMSE, BD-RIS unitary manifold, and end-to-end deep-learning without explicit CSI, with complexity–performance tradeoffs.
- Failure-mode taxonomy and reproducible artifacts (Python/Rust/TLA+).

## 2 Background

### 2.1 From Metasurface to RIS

A metasurface is a 2-D array of sub-$\lambda$ resonators: split-ring, patch, or fishnet over grounded dielectric. Generalized Snell’s law [1]: $\sin\theta_r-\sin\theta_i = (\lambda/2\pi) d\Phi/dx$. By engineering abrupt phase $\Phi(x)$, anomalous reflection, focusing ($ \Phi(x,y) = -k\sqrt{(x-x_f)^2+(y-y_f)^2+z_f^2}$), or holography emerges. Adding tunability makes it *reconfigurable*.

RIS adds software-defined controller (FPGA+SPI) distributing $N$ bias voltages. Passive RIS power model: $P_{RIS}= N P_{ctrl}+P_{FPGA}$; $P_{ctrl}\approx1$ mW varactor, $5$ mW CMOS switch. Active RIS adds LNA $P_{LNA}=50$ mW but also thermal noise $kTB F$.

Electronic THz RISs co-design LC resonance suppression: parallel inductive micro-loops shunting $C_{par}\approx10$ fF to sustain local resonance above $f_T$ [1].

Optical approaches inject photocarriers in Si/GaAs using $\lambda=800$ nm pump, modulating conductivity $\sigma\propto I_{pump}$, enabling $>30$ dB amplitude but requiring external laser.

### 2.2 THz Channel: Sparsity + Molecular Absorption

THz channel model: $H(f,d)= (\frac{c}{4\pi f d}) e^{-\frac{1}{2}k_{abs}(f)d} e^{-j2\pi f d/c} \sum_l \alpha_l$. Absorption windows: $0.1$–$0.55$ THz, $0.6$–$0.8$ THz, $0.9$–$1.1$ THz separated by H₂O lines at $0.56$, $0.75$, $0.96$ THz. Multi-wideband RIS must resonate at $0.65/0.85/1.05$ THz simultaneously [4].

### 2.3 Near-Field Definition

Rayleigh $d_R=2D^2/\lambda$, Fresnel $0.62\sqrt{D^3/\lambda}$, reactive $0.1\lambda$—$0.62\sqrt{D^3/\lambda}$. For RIS $10$ cm×$10$ cm at 28 GHz ($\lambda=10.7$ mm) $d_F=1.87$ m; at 0.3 THz same aperture $d_F=20$ m [3]. Indoor networks thus *always* near-field. Spherical phase $ \phi_{n,m}= k(||\mathbf{r}_s-\mathbf{p}_n||+||\mathbf{p}_n-\mathbf{r}_u||)$ replaces linear $ k\mathbf{a}^T\mathbf{p}_n$.

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 Unit-Cell Equivalent Circuit & Coupled Model

Let varactor $C_n\in[20,200]$ fF, $R_n=5$Ω, $L=100$ pH. $Z_n=R+j(\omega L-1/\omega C)$. Reflection $\Gamma_n=(Z_n-Z_0)/(Z_n+Z_0)=\beta_n e^{j\theta_n}$. Feasible locus $\mathcal{F}=\{ (\beta(\theta),\theta) \}$ not full unit circle but cardioid squeezed at resonance $\beta_{min}=0.2$ [1][6].

Optimization with coupled amplitude: $\max_{\theta\in[0,2\pi]} |h_d+\sum \beta(\theta_n)e^{j\theta_n} g_n|^2$. Penalty CCP: linearize $\beta(\theta)\approx\beta_0+\beta'_0\Delta\theta$. Projected gradient iterates: $\theta^{(k+1)}=\arg\min_{\theta\in\mathcal{F}}||e^{j\theta}-e^{j(\arg(grad))}||$.

### 3.2 Near-Field Beamfocusing & Fresnel Zone Design

Spherical wavefront array factor: $AF(\mathbf{r}_u)=\sum_n w_n e^{j k(||\mathbf{r}_s-\mathbf{p}_n||-||\mathbf{r}_u-\mathbf{p}_n||)}$. For focus at $\mathbf{r}_f$, optimal $w_n^\star=e^{-jk||\mathbf{p}_n-\mathbf{r}_f||}$. Depth of focus (3 dB) scales $\propto 1/D^2$; width $\propto\lambda z_f/D$ [3][5].

Fresnel zones defined as ellipsoids where path difference $ \Delta=R_{1n}+R_{2n}$ integer $\lambda/2$. Property: elements within same zone have identical phase modulo $2\pi$ at fixed $f$, but remain co-phased across $f$ ⇒ beam-split-free if phase aligned along zone [5].

> **Theorem (Fresnel Invariance):** For narrowband THz RIS at $f_c$, allocating identical phase shift to all elements whose $\Delta(\mathbf{p}_n)\in[(m-0.5)\lambda/2,(m+0.5)\lambda/2)$ yields in-phase superposition at receiver independent of $f\in[f_c-B/2,f_c+B/2]$ up to $\mathcal{O}((B/f_c)^2)$ residual.

*Proof Sketch:* Path phase $k\Delta=2\pi f\Delta/c$. If $\Delta$ constant in zone, variation $dk\Delta=(2\pi\Delta/c)df$. Since receiver chosen s.t. $\Delta=m\lambda_c/2$, $e^{jk\Delta}=(-1)^m e^{j2\pi(\Delta/\lambda_c)(f-f_c)/f_c}$. Same-zone $\Delta$ variance $<\lambda_c/4$ ⇒ ≤45° spread → near-coherent. ∎

### 3.3 Joint Active-Passive Precoding Formulations

#### System Model
BS $M$ ULA, RIS $N$ UPA, $K$ single-antenna UEs, direct blocked. Downlink OFDM $S$ subcarriers. Received at $s$:

$$ y_{k,s}= (\mathbf{h}_{r,k,s}^H\boldsymbol{\Phi}_s\mathbf{G}_s)\mathbf{w}_{k,s} s_{k,s}+ \text{ICI}+n $$

$\boldsymbol{\Phi}_s=\text{diag}(\phi_{1,s},...)$ frequency dependency: ideal TTD-RIS $\phi_{n,s}=e^{j2\pi f_s\tau_n}$; flat RIS $\phi_{n,s}=\phi_n \forall s$.

Weighted sum-rate: $\max_{\{\mathbf{W}_s\},\boldsymbol{\Phi}} \sum_{k,s} \alpha_k\log_2(1+\text{SINR}_{k,s})$ s.t. $\sum_k||\mathbf{w}_{k,s}||^2\le P$, $|\phi_n|=1$ or $\boldsymbol{\Theta}^H\boldsymbol{\Theta}=I$ for BD-RIS group-connected [7].

#### Three Solution Families

**1. Alternating WMMSE + SDR** – classic: introduce MSE weights $u_k,e_k$, transform rate to $ \min$ WMSE, AO between $\mathbf{W}$ (closed-form Wiener) and $\boldsymbol{\phi}$ via QCQP $\mathbf{v}^H\mathbf{R}\mathbf{v}$ lifted to $\mathbf{V}\succeq0$ rank-1 relaxed, randomization $L_{rand}=1000$ achieves $\pi/4$ guarantee if Rician $K>5$dB [7].

**2. Manifold RCG / BD-RIS Unitary** – beyond-diagonal RIS couples elements $\boldsymbol{\Theta}=\text{blkdiag}(\boldsymbol{\Theta}_g)$ with $\boldsymbol{\Theta}_g^H\boldsymbol{\Theta}_g=I_g$. Feasible set is Stiefel manifold $\mathcal{M}=U(G)$. Riemannian gradient $\text{grad}f(\boldsymbol{\Theta}) = P_{T}(\nabla f)$ enabling $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$ steps [7][8]. Closed-form AO reduces complexity 70% vs SDR for $N=256$.

**3. End-to-End Learning (E2E)** – avoid explicit CSI: UL pilot $Y_p$ → polar attention encoder → latent $z$ → DL-BF decoder outputting hybrid TTD+PS $\{\tau_n,\theta_n\}$ [3]. Loss = $-R_{eff}(1-T_p/T_c)$. Sparsity guidance via learnable DFT dictionary accelerates convergence 2× vs black-box MLP [3].

```python
import numpy as np
# Joint WMMSE AO sketch (simplified SU version)
def joint_active_passive(G, hr, hd, P, Niter=20, b=2):
    M = G.shape[1]; N = G.shape[0]
    phi = np.exp(1j*2*np.pi*np.random.rand(N))
    for it in range(Niter):
        heff = hd + G.T @ (hr.conj()*phi)   # effective M x1
        w = np.sqrt(P)*heff/np.linalg.norm(heff)  # MRT
        # passive step: maximize |hd^H w + phi^T diag(hr^H) G w|
        a = np.diag(hr.conj()) @ G @ w   # N x1
        c = hd.conj().T @ w
        # RCG on circle manifold
        phi = np.exp(1j*np.angle(a.conj()*np.conj(c))) if c!=0 else np.exp(1j*np.angle(a))
        # 2-bit quantize if needed
        if b:
            q = 2**b
            levels = np.exp(1j*2*np.pi*np.arange(q)/q)
            phi = levels[np.argmin(np.abs(phi[:,None]-levels[None]), axis=1)]
    return phi, w
```

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Penetrating Unit-Cell Design at THz

CMOS tile work [1] demonstrates 12×12 array integrated in 65 nm Si, achieving GHz switching because NMOS $f_{max}$ extended via inductive peaking. However amplitude variation 25 dB between open/short severely limits co-phasing. Graphene’s Dirac conductivity $\sigma(\omega,\mu_c)= \frac{je^2 k_B T}{\pi\hbar^2(\omega+j\tau^{-1})}[ \mu_c/k_B T+2\ln(e^{-\mu_c/k_BT}+1)]+...$ tunes plasmonic resonance, achieving 315° with hybrid Au patch that enlarges aperture coupling, at expense of $\tau=0.2$ ps mobility-limited loss [2][4].

Analog LC achieves continuous $0-360°$ via $\Delta n$ but ms response disqualifies fast beam tracking at $v=10$ m/s $(T_c=0.8$ ms at 28 GHz, $0.08$ ms at 0.3 THz). Endurance of VO₂ $10^6$ cycles vs CMOS $>10^{15}$ defines lifetime trade.

Mutual coupling for $d<\lambda/2$ introduces $\mathbf{S}$-matrix coupling: $\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{eff}= (\mathbf{I}+\mathbf{S}\boldsymbol{\Phi})^{-1}\boldsymbol{\Phi}$. HFSS shows $5°$ beam-pointing error at $d=\lambda/4$ if ignored; calibration via Z-parameter retrieval mandatory [1].

### 4.2 Beam Split and True-Time-Delay RIS Architectures

Two architectures [3][5][6]:

- **TTD-RIS**: each element or sub-surface fed by programmable delay $\tau_n\in[0,200]$ ps implemented via switched transmission lines. Frequency response $\phi_n(f)=e^{j\theta_n}e^{j2\pi f \tau_n}$ mimics true phase $k r$, hence mitigates split. Cost: power $20$ mW/TTD, insertion $3$ dB.

- **SA-RIS (subarray/virtual)**: divide $N$ into $Q=S$ subarrays, each subarray tuned to subcarrier $s_q$ center frequency. Aperture reduced by $Q$, loss $10\log_{10}Q$ dB but zero delay lines. Tradeoff studied [3]: TTD-RIS sum-rate +18% vs SA at $B=10$ GHz, $f_c=0.3$ THz, at 2× power.

> **Lemma 4.2 (Double Split):** BS frequency-flat analog $f_{RF}$ plus RIS flat $\phi_n$ causes focus migration $\Delta\mathbf{r}_f\approx -(B/f_c) \mathbf{r}_f$. With TTD at BS only, migration halves; with both BS+TTD-RIS, migration $<\lambda$.

End-to-end learning [3] exploits polar-domain sparsity: near-field channel sparse in angle-distance dictionary $\mathbf{A}_{pol}$, UL combiners learned to mimic filtering, avoiding explicit $N M$ channel estimation of $256×64=16384$ coefficients requiring $T\ge N$ pilots.

### 4.3 Beyond-Diagonal RIS and Optimization Hardness

Diagonal RIS $\boldsymbol{\Phi}$ restricts to per-element phase; BD-RIS interconnects via tunable impedance network, achieving passive beamforming matrix $\boldsymbol{\Theta}$ symmetric unitary [7][8]. Group size $G=4$ balances routing complexity ($\mathcal{O}(NG^2)$ phase shifters) vs rate gain $+30\%$ over diagonal due to amplitude freedom. Joint WMMSE yields closed-form passive: for group $g$, $\boldsymbol{\Theta}_g^\star = \mathbf{U}_g \mathbf{V}_g^H$ where $\mathbf{U}\mathbf{S}\mathbf{V}^H$ = SVD of aggregated channel $\mathbf{M}_g$ [7]. Convergence monotonic, stationary bound.

Multi-agent RL for cell-free [8][10] treats each AP-RIS pair as agent, state local CSI, action $\{\mathbf{w},\boldsymbol{\phi}\}$, reward global EE. MARL reduces front-haul CSI overhead 60% vs centralized.

### 4.4 Deep Physics Integration

Stacked Intelligent Metasurface (SIM) integrates $L$ layers of metasurfaces inside BS radome, emulating deep neural network in wave domain: propagation = forward pass, weights = phase shifts. Near-field SIM beamfocusing reduces digital beamforming RF chains from 64 to 4 with 1-bit DACs penalty <1.5 dB [9]. Integration with RIS extends to hybrid wave-digital architecture meeting THz energy budget.

### 5.3 Proof of AO Convergence (sketch)

Rate function $R(\mathbf{W},\boldsymbol{\Phi})$ bounded above by interference-free CAP. Each block coordinate ascent monotonic: $\mathbf{W}^{(t+1)}=\arg\max_{\mathbf{W}}R(\cdot,\boldsymbol{\Phi}^{(t)})$ → $R$↑, $\boldsymbol{\Phi}^{(t+1)}$ via RCG line-search ensures Armijo → monotone. Sequence contained in compact ($||\mathbf{W}||_F≤\sqrt{P}$ and torus). Limit points stationary by standard manifold theory [7].

## 6 Limitations

- **THz insertion loss & amplitude-phase coupling**: No mechanism simultaneously offers $360°$, $β>0.8$, sub-µs, low loss, CMOS. Graphene mobility $<2000$ cm²/Vs CVD reduces $\beta$ to $0.4$, sacrificing $N^2$ scaling 7 dB. Designers must operate on Lorentzian feasible set, overestimating rate 40% if idealized $|φ|=1$ used [1][6].

- **Near-field channel estimation**: Spherical dictionary size $G_{pol}=G_{angle}×G_{dist}=64×16=1024$ per user → complexity $\mathcal{O}(G_{pol}^3)$ for ANM. Beam-split across $S=64$ subcarriers complicates decoupling; Fresnel-zone still requires range knowledge error $Δz=10$ cm causes phase 36° at 0.3 THz. E2E avoids CSI but generalizes poorly across deployments [3][6].

- **Control latency**: SPI reconfiguration $2$–$5$ µs/element → $0.5$–$1.3$ ms for $N=256$, > coherence time at $30$ km/h $T_c=0.08$ ms THz [2]. Predictive tracking unsolved; MEMS ms latency disqualified.

- **Scalability of joint precoding**: SDR $\mathcal{O}(N^{4.5})$ impractical $N>400$; RCG requires careful retraction and step size, can stall at saddle for $K>8$. BD-RIS switch matrix routing loss $0.3$ dB per coupler accumulates $>4$ dB for $G=8$.

- **Standardization & cost**: 3GPP Rel-19 RIS SI excludes THz; O-RAN E2 interface for RIS control undefined; fabrication of $1024$ graphene meta-atoms with bias per cell yields yield $~70\%$, cost $>\$500$ vs passive sheet metal.

- **EMF & regulatory**: Peak spatial power density of $256$ element RIS with $46$ dBm EIRP at $3$ m = $5.2$ W/m², below ICNIRP $10$ W/m² but hot-spotting concerns public acceptance; STAR-RIS leakage backward lobes increase exposure.

*Ethical*: Programmable environment adversarially reconfigurable for eavesdropping suppression via “green interference” [10] dual-use; security framework must balance secrecy vs intentional interference.

## 7 Conclusion

We traversed the stack from THz solid-state physics to wave electromagnetics to optimization theory. Key distillation:

1. **Unit-cell technology is no longer optional RF engineering but the bottleneck**: CMOS tile tiling and hybrid graphene-gold extend phase range $>300°$ at $2$ THz with MHz agility [1][2], yet Lorentzian $\beta(\theta)$ must be explicitly optimized; ignoring coupling inflates achievable rate fiction.

2. **Near-field is the default, not anomaly, for THz RIS**: With $d_F$ reaching $80$ m, spherical beamfocusing provides depth selectivity and distance multiplexing, but also double beam split that Fresnel-zone alignment or TTD-RIS fundamentally resolve, validated by $8$–$18\%$ rate uplift [3][5].

3. **Joint active-passive precoding achieves near-optimality under realistic constraints**: Closed-form BD-RIS unitary updates and E2E polar-attention networks reduce pilot overhead $87\%$ while holding $\pi/4$ gap [7][8]; scaling law $P\propto1/N^2$ holds only until phase noise onset $\sigma>20°$.

Future trajectory converges to *information metasurfaces* encoding bits directly at surface without baseband chain, holographic SIM implementing wave-domain computing, and reinforcement-learned RIS controller co-designed with scheduler for semantic communications. If 5G taught massive MIMO to fight the channel, 6G THz RIS teaches us the channel is the computer.
