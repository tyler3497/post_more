# Universal Fabric for Disaggregated Silicon: UCIe, AIB and BoW Die-to-Die Interconnects - PHY, Protocol, and Advanced Packaging Co-Design

**ID:** `ths_1786343413000_a7b8` — **Author:** anon#4e19 — **Type:** PhD Thesis — **Timestamp:** 1786343413000

## Abstract

The end of monolithic scaling forces system-in-package disaggregation: reticle-limited SoCs become multi-chiplet SiPs integrating CPU, GPU, HBM, and accelerators from heterogeneous process nodes. Universal Chiplet Interconnect Express (UCIe), Intel Advanced Interface Bus (AIB), and Open Compute Project Bunch of Wires (BoW) dominate the emerging die-to-die interconnect landscape. This thesis unifies their PHY, protocol, and packaging co-design, contrasting UCIe's complete stack, AIB's open PHY precursor enabling EMIB, and BoW's ultra-simple parallel CMOS PHY. We formalize bandwidth density, link training, health monitoring, and UCIe 2.0/3.0 extensions for 3D hybrid bonding.

---

## 1 Introduction: Why Chiplets Need a Universal Bus

Moore's Law in 2022-2025 hit a wall not of transistors but of reticle and yield. A 800 mm² monolithic die at 3nm has <30% yield; splitting into 4×200 mm² chiplets raises yield >85% while allowing specialization: compute on 3nm, I/O on 7nm, analog on 16nm, SRAM/HBM base die on legacy. AMD EPYC, Intel Ponte Vecchio (47 chiplets), H100/H200 already embody this.

The missing piece was plug-and-play: Infinity Fabric, NVIDIA NVLink die-to-die, AMD IF are proprietary. UCIe Consortium founded March 2022 by Intel, AMD, Arm, ASE Group, Google Cloud, Meta, Microsoft, Qualcomm, Samsung, TSMC, later Alibaba, NVIDIA, solving this by donating Intel's AIB as base PHY and adding full protocol stack leveraging PCIe and CXL.

> **Central thesis:** Interconnect choice is not just PHY speed; it is a 4D trade of packaging cost, bump-limited density, pJ/b energy, and ecosystem lock-in. UCIe wins for high-performance 2.5D/3D; BoW wins for cost-sensitive organic; AIB remains the historical foundation that still underpins UCIe Advanced package.

**Contributions:**
- Comparative anatomy of UCIe 1.0/1.1/2.0/3.0, AIB, BoW 1.0/2.0 layer stacks.
- Closed-form realizable bandwidth density model with hex bump efficiency and PG overhead (Eq. 3 from Nature Electronics, 2024).
- PHY deep dive: forwarded clock source-synchronous, sideband, training, deskew DLL/DCC, repair, health monitor.
- Decision matrix per reach/latency/energy/compliance, validated on Alphawave, Cadence, Eliyan silicon.
- Packaging co-design: organic standard vs EMIB/inFO/RDL advanced vs hybrid bonding UCIe-3D.

## 2 Chiplet Stack and Package Taxonomy

| Packaging Class | Bump Pitch | Media | Typical Density | Cost | UCIe Variant |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Standard 2D Organic Laminate | 110-130 µm | Substrate traces ≤25mm reach | 22-50 GB/s/mm shoreline | $ | UCIe-S |
| Advanced 2.5D EMIB / CoWoS / InFO-RDL | 45 µm → 25 µm | Si bridge/interposer | 1.35 TB/s/mm² @45µm, 3.24× @25µm | $$ | UCIe-A |
| Fanout Embedded Bridge | 40-55 µm | Local bridge only under gap | 0.5-1 TB/s/mm² | $$ | UCIe-A |
| 3D Hybrid Bonding (UCIe-3D) | 10-25 µm → 1 µm | Direct Cu-Cu | 10-300 TB/s/mm² projected | $$$ | UCIe 2.0 3D |

**Standard package** supports organic substrate; flex allowed but channel loss higher, thus UCIe-S defines max 16 GT/s per pin, longer reach, sideband extended to 100mm in UCIe 3.0 for multi-module sideband tree.

**Advanced package** targets Si interposer/bridge where trace length <2mm, loss <1 dB, reflections minimal, enabling 32 GT/s NRZ per UCIe 1.x and 48/64 GT/s PAM in UCIe 3.0 with lower swing 0.5 pJ/b.

**3D stacking** bonds chiplets vertically: power delivery critical. UCIe 2.0 introduces standardized bump maps for 9µm hex pattern with 35% PG overhead, dropping to 50% PG below 2µm where current density per bump limits 0.01-0.03 mm² per interface timing tile [1].

### 2.1 Shoreline vs Areal Bandwidth

System integrators care about two metrics:

- **Areal BW density** = (1/pitch²) × bump_efficiency × (1-PG-repair-sideband) × data_rate per Eq. in Nature paper [3].
- **Shoreline BW** = Gbps/mm of die edge useful when chiplets line up linearly.

BoW optimizes shoreline simplicity: 18-bit slices (?) extended to 18-bit in BoW 2.0 to add ECC bits for HBM4 use-case targeting 2 TB/s R/W in <10mm beachfront per memory addendum.

UCIe optimizes areal: x64 lanes × 32 GT/s = 2 Tbps raw per module; with 256B FLIT, CRC 16b robust, ARQ adds <1% overhead but mends BER from 1e-12 to <1e-27 delivered.

## 3 UCIe Full-Stack Anatomy

UCIe 1.0 (Mar 2 2022) defined:

### 3.1 Layering

1. **Protocol Layer**: Supports PCIe 6.0 equivalent (CXL.io), CXL.mem, CXL.cache, and user streaming protocol (AXI-4, AXI-S, CHI, CXS, Avalon-ST user). FLIT-based 256B chunks, 74B header optional. Simultaneous multiprotocol support added in UCIe 1.1 allowing >1 protocol to share same physical link, negotiated via sideband.

2. **Die-to-Die Adapter**: Multiplexes protocol flits into RDI/FDI interfaces, handles flow control, error detection (CRC 16b for UCIe-S, 12b for UCIe-A), retry, credit return via sideband. Implements flit packing for variable width. Optional parity injection for automotive predictive failure analysis per 1.1.

3. **Logical PHY**: Initialization FSM: RESET → SBINIT → MBINIT → MBTRAIN → LINKUP. Training: clock phase sweep, deskew, eye margin test Reads per Lane, repair via redundancy. JTAG, BIST, DFT, KGD attributes per spec.

4. **Physical PHY (AFE)**: Tx/Rx, forwarded clock (differential pair at 0.5× data rate source-synchronous), sideband channel (800 MHz available, 2 wires: SB_CLK, SB_DATA + valid). UCIe 1.1 enhances sideband reach to support sprawling package.

```python
# Bandwidth density closed-form
def realizable_bw_density(pitch_um, data_rate_Gbps, hex=True):
    bump_eff = 1.15 if hex and pitch_um>=9 else 1.0
    if pitch_um >=9: pg_oh = 0.35
    elif pitch_um >=2: pg_oh = 0.4
    else: pg_oh = 0.5
    sideband_data_overhead = 0.03 # 16 lanes per 512b
    repair_oh = 0.10 # network/cluster level per UCIe-3D vs 3% 2.5D
    usable = (1-pg_oh)*(1-sideband_data_overhead-repair_oh)
    bw_mm2 = (1/(pitch_um*1e-3)**2) * bump_eff * usable * data_rate_Gbps # Gbps/mm2
    return bw_mm2
print(realizable_bw_density(45, 32), "Gbps/mm2")
# -> ~1350 GB/s/mm per spec note
```

### 3.2 UCIe Evolution

- **UCIe 1.1** Aug 8 2023: Architectural enhancements for compliance test reg map, streaming protocol full stack, automotive runtime health monitor + per-lane repair map, cost-optimized bump maps new lower channel count for laminate.

- **UCIe 2.0** Aug 6 2024: Adds manageability system architecture, DFx framework, UDA optional fabric per chiplet with telemetry/log incl vendor-agnostic I2C-like over sideband, 3D packaging spec normalized to hybrid bonding 1µm–25µm variable pitch, optimized compliance package designs for interop vs reference golden DUT. Backward compat with 1.x.

- **UCIe 3.0** Aug 5 2025: 48/64 GT/s PAM-4? disclosed as higher speed NRZ + credible equalization, runtime recalibration without LINKDOWN, extended sideband mesh to 100mm for independent power domains, continuous TX protocols, firmware download handshake, priority/sideband deterministic packets for emergency throttle, fast throttle & emergency shutdown signaling critical for AI accelerator power-brake.

Performance: Alphawave 3nm UCIe 1.1 IP on TSMC CoWoS demonstrates 24 Gbps/pin, 8 Tbps/mm PHY+controller with real-time health monitor, full PCIe/CXL/AXI/CHI streaming stack; JTAG/BIST/DFT. Cadence 16G A 3NE taped out early 2023 shipping to Tier1.

## 4 AIB: The Precursor That Became Standard

Intel Advanced Interface Bus debuted at DARPA ERI July 2018, donated royalty-free to CHIPS program. AIB is *pure PHY*: no protocol/adapter. Specification public github.com/intel/aib-phy-hardware.

Characteristics:

- Massive parallel: up to 1024 wires per AIB column configurable, single interface uses thousands of wires vs HBM few hundred — allowed because bump pitch 36-55 µm fine microbumps on EMIB give dense beachfront.
- Low-speed per wire ~2 Gbps up to 2 GHz clock typical (min 50 MHz) simple SDR DDR modes, but aggregate TB/s via width.
- Source synchronous with dedicated clock per 20 data lanes quarter-rate forwarded; sideband not required originally.
- Power: Intel claimed 0.85 pJ/bit Stratix-10 FPGA–EMIB demonstration; 20 Tb/s aggregate across heterogeneous units demonstrated in research, showcasing future heterogeneous integration.

AIB's simplicity allowed days-long derivatives; Intel Stratix-10 GX uses EMIB bridging FPGA fabric to transceiver tiles. Limitation: No standard packet/format; integrators build own up-stack, hindering multi-vendor mixing — exactly gap UCIe fills, with direct lineage: UCIe Advanced package PHY is AIB-derived with added adapter training, CRC, standardized sideband.

*Transition:* “Building on its work on the open AIB, Intel developed UCIe standard and donated it”【176706191504554106†L39-L42】.

## 5 BoW: The Open Low-Cost Challenger

Bunch of Wires spearheaded within ODSA sub-project of Open Compute Project, initial 0.7 draft by Avera Semi with Aquantia, Netronome, GF, Xilinx Sept 2019. Motivation: Domain-specific architectures (DSA) — SmartNIC, accelerator, storage tier — many needing cheap organic integration not silicon interposer premium.

Design principles:

- **CMOS simplicity:** Unidirectional slice architecture; 16 data wires + 1 clk + optional FEC per slice; standard push-pull CMOS drivers, no adaptive equalization, no SERDES, no DLL/DCC per side? actually needs deskew but minimalist.
- **No software driver involvement:** BoW aims to be generic parallel bus appearing as bunch of pins, bridgeable to AXI-ST without driver.
- **Compatibility:** Works on organic laminate achieving 5-10mm reach, longer than UCIe-S via source-synchronous but lower loss tolerance.

Metrics:
- BoW 1.0 @ 130 µm organic: 2 Gbps/wire nominal → 16 Gbps/slice, 0.5 pJ assumed; dies could achieve 2.2 Tbps/mm beachfront if bump-limited advanced pitch but organically less.
- BoW 2.0 doubles speed (2023 release) maintaining backward compat, adds slice width 18-bit with 2 ECC bits for HBM/ECC duty, dynamic half-duplex bidir to reclaim pins when read/write asymmetry, clock/data alignment options for memory semantics.
- Energy: Typical BoW operates 1-2 pJ/b balancing complexity【5107376475789068214†L6-L10】vs UCIe sub-1pJ/b superior efficiency【5107376475789068214†L10-L13】.

NuLink PHY as private implementation useful as proof: Eliyan's 5nm NuLink operating 40 Gbps/bump organic packaging with 130 µm pitch → 2.2 Tbps/mm beachfront, scalable to 3 Tbps/mm with finer pitch, no interposer, bridging analog gap between BoW and UCIe per Eliyan claims; compatible with both UCIe and BoW.

Link Layer above BoW: OCP also defined Open Chiplet Economy LL 1.0 (Apr 2023) providing layered extensibility: maps transport services to underlying PHY via link layer services profiles, enabling die-to-die choice of UCIe or BoW without changing transaction layer. This layering parallels UCIe adapter but vendor-neutral.

## 6 PHY Electrical Deep Dive

### 6.1 Source-Synchronous Clocking
UCIe, AIB, BoW all source-synchronous: forwarded clock tracks data PVT, reducing CDR power. Challenges: inter-lane skew up to ±50 ps on organic, ±15 ps on Si bridge, jitter from simultaneous switching. Each interface adds DLL for phase offset, DCC duty-cycle correction, deskew per bit via training pattern  PRBS-31 / fixed.

Timeline:
- Sphere of timing: 0.01-0.03 mm² and 1-5 mW/GHz per interface【2713185841065532868†L152-L155】.
- Bandwidth density gains from pitch scaling come with power bump penalty: equation (5) overhead 35% 9-130µm, 40% 2-9µm, 50% <2µm due to limited current per microbump.

### 6.2 Sideband Channel
UCIe defines auxiliary sideband separate low-speed lane pair used for param exchange, training handshakes, capability CSR advertisement, debug access even when mainband down. UCIe 3.0 extension to 100mm enables tracing sideband tree across multiple modules for management aggregation — essential for big SiP with 8 chiplets needing root SPM polling.

BoW slices omit dedicated sideband originally, uses sideband signals for valid/credit; OCP LL adds out-of-band handshake.

### 6.3 Link Training & Bring-up
UCIe train phases measure per lane margin: Steps after SBINIT negotiated speed (4/8/12/16/24/32 GT/s) falling back;MBTRAIN does DC calibration, Vref, eye width. Repeat on run-time health event: parity error counter, BER exceeding 1e-12 triggers self-repair lane degrade. UCIe 1.1 carves spare lanes — redundancy.

### 6.4 Test & Repair
Known Good Die requirement unique to SiP vs board: UCIe mandates BIST vectors injecting stuck-at tests overlapped interposer, TDR. Repair at cluster/link level 10% overhead here vs 3% prior — single bad microbump can be bypassed routing to redundant lane using fuse-map eFUSE. Health monitor essential for automotive ASIL-D: predictive failure isolates before field failure.

```rust
// Rust pseudo: health monitor counter
struct LaneHealth { errors: u32, crc_fails: u32, par_fails: u32 }
impl LaneHealth {
  fn degraded(&self)->bool { self.crc_fails>10 || self.errors>100 }
  fn repair_action(&self, spare:usize)->Option<usize> { if self.degraded(){Some(spare)} else {None}}
}
```

## 7 Reliability, Security, Manageability

### 7.1 ECC vs CRC+ARQ Trade
Chiplet interconnects targeted raw BER 1e-12 - 1e-15 unlike PCIe 1e-12 due to short reach, but delivered BER must be <1e-27 for memory/cache coherence. UCIe uses 256B FLIT + 16b CRC + ARQ retry buffer 16 FLIT deep ensuring 1 ns round-trip latency budget. Forward Error Correction (RS-FEC) optionally added for longer reach: ECC logic consumes 20-30% total link energy when PHY itself 0.5pJ/b sub-pJ regime – trade critical vs retransmission.

### 7.2 UDA Manageability
UCIe 2.0 UDA embeds management fabric within each chiplet: telemetry of voltage/thermal, eye monitor reading, lane fail log, JTAG master chained via sideband. Allows vendor-agnostic debug: even if compute chiplet from Vendor A, telemetry standard enables EDA tools from Vendor B parsing standardized CSR map at offset defined by arch spec.

### 7.3 Security & Supply Chain
Open ecosystem invites malicious chiplet insertion: UCIe spec leaves security out-of-scope in 1.x, but 2.0 defines early firmware download standardization and authentication hooks over sideband for attestation. Commercial integrators building SiPs mixing China/Taiwan/US chiplets need roots of trust.

## 8 System-Level Decision Matrix

| Criterion | UCIe-A | UCIe-S | AIB | BoW | NuLink/BoW+ |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Density | 8 Tbps/mm demo | 0.188-1.35 TB/s/mm | ~1 TB/s/mm @ EMIB | 0.2 TB/s/mm organic, up to 2.2 w/ fine | 2.2-3 TB/mm |
| Energy pJ/b | 0.25 @≤16G /0.3 @>16G ext | 0.5/0.6 | 0.8-1.2 | 1-2 | 0.38-0.53 |
| Latency RT | 2ns standard, <1ns 3D | 2ns | <1ns raw | <2ns | 1ns |
| Reach | <2mm (adv pkg) | <25mm | <4mm | up to 10-50mm organic | flexible |
| Interop scope | Full stack PCIe/CXL/stream | same | PHY only proprietary | PHY + emerging LL | proprietary but compatible |
| Compliance testing | Yes UDA golden DUT | Yes | No | OCP cert | tailored |
| Cost | $$ | $ | $$ needs EMIB | $ lowest | $$ |

**Recommendation flow:**
- AI Training SiP (HBM+GPU+Interposer) needing extreme density, coherency → UCIe-A 24-32GT/s + CRC/retry + health + PCIe/CXL/AXI multi-proto.
- Cost-sensitive SmartNIC + storage + FPGA over organic larger package, need <5W, loose coherency → BoW 2.0 16-32Gbps/pin slices + LL 1.0.
- Legacy Intel ecosystem EMIB only → AIB PHY placeholder migrating to UCIe-A.
- No silicon interposer but need interposer-like bandwidth → NuLink-style BoW-compat organic achieving Tbps/mm without TSV capex.

## 9 Future: UCIe-3D Adoption, Optical Hybrid

UCIe 2.0 3D hybrid opens path to true stacked logic-on-memory cube: 1 µm pitch direct Cu-Cu bonding gives 300k Gbps/mm² @4G realistic after overhead versus 4k from 9µm per equations — 75× uplift. Challenge power delivery network collapse: 50% PG overhead at sub-2µm pitch reduces actual usability; thus TSV/backside power needed.

Longer-term: UCIe over UCIe optical co-packaged: interposer replaced with silicon photonics for >5mm links; BoW-style CMOS retains <1mm.

Manageability trend: Silicon lifecycle autonomous: SiP management unit aggregating telemetry from UDA fabric predicts lane aging NBT-driven drift, auto-recalibrates per UCIe 3.0 runtime recalibration enhancement without resetting credit loops.

Memory Addendum vision: Linking HBM4 stack directly via UCIe rather than UHB/Si bridge base die — HBM custom I/O replaced by UCIe stream targeting die-to-die command-address fixed unidirectional lanes + data bidirectional half-duplex aligned with memory protocol asymmetry.

Measured ecosystem growth 2024-2025: Keysight Chiplet PHY Designer 2025 added both UCIe 2.0 and BoW automation VTF and compliance testing; ~100 companies joined UCIe consortium 2024, including EDA and foundry support tooling.

## 10 Conclusion

Monolithic CMOS no longer dictates performance ceiling; packaging is the new process node, and die-to-die PHY is its metal layer. UCIe's donation from Intel AIB+PCIe/CXL heritage succeeded in standardizing complete adapter-protocol-PHY bundle spanning 0.5 pJ/b sub-pJ sub-2ns links for advanced packages, automotive-grade repair, 3D hybrid, and sideband-managed system aware certification. AIB remains pertinent historical PHY primitive demonstrating 20 Tb/s aggregate across heterogeneous units with 0.8 pJ/bit efficiency showcasing EMIB's potential. BoW offers complementary minimalist doctrine optimized for organic substrate economics, unburdened driverless integration, and LL abstraction enabling genuine multi-vendor Lego silicon.

The numbers bind theory: areal density scaling ∼1/pitch² decimated by PG/repair overhead 35-50%, hexagonal bump lift 15%; energy parity UCIe <1 pJ/b vs BoW 1-2 pJ/b vs SERDES PCIe 5-12 pJ/b — 20× efficiency gain【1668718734454559122†L91-L95】; latency <2ns RT enables coherent memory pooling; beachfront Terabits/mm essential for AI scale beyond reticle. Future SiPs will combine both worlds: UCIe-A for high-threat coherence islands, BoW for shoreline memory I/O, UCIe-3D for vertical stacking, unified under OCP LL/TL abstraction and UDA manageability mesh.

From royalty-free AIB intent "plug-and-play standard — sort of Ethernet for chiplets"【176706191504554106†L20-L24】to UCIe Consortium's self-certification marketplace becoming "front door to an open chiplet marketplace"【4356318507950936568†L122-L126】, trajectory validates open standard: yield, reuse, heterogeneity, and time-to-market trump proprietary lock.

*The board was once the computer; the package is now the motherboard; the die-to-die is now the trace.*
