# Type Theory Foundations for Proof Assistants: System Fω, Calculus of Constructions, Homotopy Type Theory Univalence, and Lean 4 Elaboration with Universe Polymorphism

## Abstract
We present a PhD-level synthesis of type theory lineage from System F through System Fω, the Calculus of Constructions, the λ-cube, strong normalization via Girard and Geuvers-Nederhof, to Homotopy Type Theory univalence and Lean 4 elaboration with universe polymorphism. We formalize the Barendregt cube as extension lattice along axes term-dependency, type-dependency, and type polymorphism, reconstruct CoC with impredicative Prop and predicative Type hierarchy, prove Curry-Howard correspondence, contrast intensional versus extensional equality and UIP, and analyze univalence axiomatization of equivalences as identities, its simplicial set semantics, cubical computational interpretation with Kan hcomps and Glue types, and stratified universe management in Lean 4 with level metavariables, max-imax constraint solving, Harper-Pollack polymorphism, and elaborator backtracking. Results unify classical proofs-and-types tradition with modern proof assistants, measuring typechecking cost, universe failure modes, and cubical transport practicality.

## 1 Introduction

***Type theory*** provides ontological substrate for proof assistants, bridging logic and computation via Curry-Howard-Lambek. While set theory dominated 20th-century mathematics, dependent type theory offers ***constructive content***, decidable checking, higher-dimensional symmetry. This thesis reconstructs lineage ***F → Fω → CoC → HoTT → Lean 4 elaboration*** preserving *strong normalization*, *confluence*, *consistency* [1][2][3][4].

We traverse:

1. **System F** [1]: Girard/Reynolds impredicative second-order polymorphism, encoding datatypes via elimination, provably total functions of second-order arithmetic.
2. **System Fω**: higher-kinded operators *Rightarrow * for modules, Haskell core [1][11].
3. **Calculus of Constructions** [2]: Coquand-Huet 1988 unified Fω + dependent types across λ-cube [3], yielding Prop impredicative and Type₀:Type₁:… predicative, Coq kernel [2][9].
4. **HoTT + Univalence** [6][7]: Voevodsky's ua treats equivalences as equalities, resolving setoid hell.
5. **Lean 4 Elaboration** [8]: de Moura et al. 2021 introduced hierarchical names, universe level metavariables, instance synthesis.

**Contributions:** formal cube reconstruction 8 systems; impredicative vs predicative comparison Prop proof-irrelevant vs Type u max/imax; univalence computation simplicial [7] vs cubical Kan; elaboration as constraint solving graph acyc-check.

> **Theorem 1 (Cube Soundness).** All eight λ-cube systems are strongly normalizing, confluent, logically consistent PTS. If Γ ⊢ M : A then A is sort or inhabited only by normalizing terms.

> **Theorem 2 (Geuvers-Nederhof Transfer).** SN of λP + parametricity λ2 lifts to λP2 and λω; CC inherits SN from Fω via saturated sets.

Methodology mixes archival primary sources with mechanized sketches in Rocq, Lean 4 metaprogramming, Haskell DSL.
