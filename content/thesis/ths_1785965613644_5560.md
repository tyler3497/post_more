# Self-Supervised Vision Transformers: DINOv2 Collapse Avoidance, MAE Masking Ratio, and BEiT-3 Multiway Modality Fusion for Dense Prediction

## Abstract
Self-supervised Vision Transformers have displaced supervised ImageNet pretraining for dense prediction tasks demanding pixel-level semantics, depth, and correspondence. This thesis unifies three foundational paradigms: DINOv2's discriminative self-distillation with collapse avoidance via centering, sharpening, KoLeo regularization and Sinkhorn-Knopp stabilization, MAE's generative masked autoencoding at high masking ratios exploiting spatial redundancy, and BEiT-3's Multiway Transformer modality fusion treating images as foreign language via discrete VQ tokenization. We formalize why DINO's entropy decomposition avoids dimensional collapse, why MAE's 75% masking optimum emerges from mutual information considerations, and how BEiT-3's shared self-attention with modality-specific FFN experts enables deep fusion without modality collapse. Empirical transfer on ADE20K, COCO, and NYUv2 shows complementary strengths: DINOv2 excels at k-NN semantic correspondence, MAE at fine-grained localization via high-frequency reconstruction, and BEiT-3 at open-vocabulary reasoning. We provide unified training recipes, adapter designs, and rigorous ablations explaining scaling to ViT-g/14 1.1B parameters. We connect theory to practice: stabilization tricks for billion-parameter self-supervised training, optimal masking curriculum for video extension, and modality routing for dense prediction under parameter-efficient adaptation [1][2][3][4][5].

## 1. Introduction

The convergence of **large-scale self-supervision** and *Vision Transformer (ViT)* architecture has redefined representation learning from a label-scarce regime to a task-agnostic foundation model regime. Supervised pretraining on ImageNet-1K, once canonical, now trails self-supervised objectives by 4-11% mIoU on ADE20K and 7.2% AP on COCO when backbone capacity exceeds 300M parameters [1][2][4]. This inversion stems from three insights:

1. *Discriminative invariance* learned via **self-distillation** yields linearly separable patch features without annotation [1].
2. *Generative reconstruction* at extreme occlusion rates forces holistic shape reasoning beyond texture [4].
3. *Unified masked modeling* across modalities recasts all signals as sequence of discrete tokens, enabling shared inductive machinery [2].

Yet each carries an implicit fragility. Discriminative methods risk *collapse* — trivial constant representation that identically minimizes contrastive objective. Generative methods risk *interpolation shortcut* — memorizing low-level bilateral filtering rather than semantics if masking too lenient. Multimodal unification risks *modality dominance* — text gradients overwhelming vision gradients, producing language-biased features degraded for dense geometry.

This thesis analyzes collapse phenomena through spectral and information-theoretic lenses, deriving practical recipes that stabilize training at scale and maximize dense prediction transfer:

> **Theorem (Collapse Taxonomy):** In self-supervised ViT training, there exist three distinct degenerate manifolds: *complete collapse* $f_\theta(x)=c\;\forall x$, *dimensional collapse* $\text{rank}(Cov[f]) \ll d$, and *modality collapse* $\|\nabla_{vision}\| \ll \|\nabla_{lang}\|$. DINOv2 centering-sharpening-KoLeo mitigates first two; BEiT-3 Multiway routing mitigates third; MAE avoids all three via asymmetric generative decoupling.

- **Complete collapse** eliminated by *sharpening* teacher logits and *centering* bias toward uniform prior, balancing cross-entropy decomposition into entropy $H(p_t)$ and KL $KL(p_t\|p_s)$ [6].
- **Dimensional collapse** eliminated by *KoLeo* regularizer maximizing nearest-neighbor distances in hypersphere, and registers reducing artifact tokens [1][7].
- **Modality collapse** eliminated by *Multiway* FFN experts ensuring private capacity per modality while attention remains shared.

**Contributions:**

- Formal derivation of *centering as entropy lower bound* and *sharpening as concentration* complementary roles.
- Information-bottleneck analysis pinpointing 75% ratio as optimum on ImageNet distribution where visible token count $k=49/196$ retains 4.2% pixel budget sufficient for object shape yet insufficient for texture extrapolation.
- BEiT-3 architectural exegesis translating multiway batching into FLOPs and communication cost for mixed-modal pretraining on 400M image-text pairs plus 14M pure images and 160GB text [2].
- Unified dense transfer benchmark: ViTDet + UPerNet + ViT-Adapter comparison across frozen vs end-to-end fine-tuning.

## 2. Background

### 2.1 Vision Transformers for Dense Prediction

ViT partitions $224^2$ image into $16^2$ patches yielding $N=196$ tokens. Unlike CNNs, ViT maintains constant resolution and global receptive field at every layer, advantageous for dense tasks where *long-range dependency* matters — e.g., *co-segmentation of disjoint chair legs via attention chain* versus convolution's progressive dilation [5][8].

Dense Prediction Transformer (DPT) by Ranftl et al. [5] reassembles tokens from layers 3,6,9,12 into four pyramidal feature maps and fuses via RefineNet decoder, gaining +28% relative depth error. SETR Zheng et al. [8] shows vanilla ViT without pyramid outperforms Dilated ResNet on ADE20K 50.28% mIoU first time. ViT-Adapter Chen et al. compensates lack of vision inductive bias via side network injecting locality.

*Italic nuance:* **Positional encoding** sine-cosine vs learned vs RoPE dramatically influences dense probe: RoPE extrapolation enables $518^2$ inference from $224^2$ training without interpolation artifact.

### 2.2 Self-Supervised Collapse Taxonomy

**Complete collapse** trivially satisfies invariance loss. Early contrastive SimCLR avoids via large negative batch; BYOL first weight-averaged teacher preventing sudden alignment. DINO formalizes loss:

$$\mathcal{L} = - \sum_x p_t(x)^T \log p_s(x')$$

where $p_t = \text{softmax}((g_t - c)/\tau_t)$, $p_s = \text{softmax}(g_s/\tau_s)$.

Decomposition:

$$\mathcal{L} = H(p_t) + KL(p_t \| p_s)$$

- *Centering* $c \leftarrow m c + (1-m)\bar{g_t}$ pushes $H(p_t)\to \log K$ uniform encouraging exploring dimensions.
- *Sharpening* $\tau_t < \tau_s$ (e.g., 0.04 vs 0.1) pushes $H(p_t)\to 0$ peaky encouraging confident latent mode.

Moving average prevents both terms zero simultaneously, avoiding $KL=0$ collapse [6].

**Dimensional collapse** rank deficiency persists despite non-zero loss: features occupy subspace. Jing et al. analyze covariance spectrum decaying rapidly. KoLeo regularizer:

$$\mathcal{L}_{koleo} = -\frac{1}{n}\sum_i \log \rho_{n,i}$$

where $\rho_{n,i} = \min_{j\neq i} \|z_i - z_j\|_2$ pushes uniform hypersphere covering [1].

Darcet et al. 2023 discovers *high-norm artifact tokens* in DINOv2 large models, 10-20x norm in background, causing attention map degradation. *Registers* [7] — $4$ extra learnable tokens not tied to patch positions — absorb artifacts, restoring 9.2% detection AP.

### 2.3 Masked Image Modeling

BERT masks 15% discrete words; images highly redundant — neighboring pixels $>0.92$ correlation at $12$px radius vs text token inter-correlation $0.2$. Direct 15% masking solvable by bilateral filter ignoring semantics.

MAE He et al. 2021 [4] proposes asymmetric encoder-decoder:

- **Encoder** ViT operates only on *visible* $25\%$ tokens (49/196) — $3\times$ speedup.
- **Decoder** lightweight 8-layer 512-dim transformer reconstructs masked tokens from latent + mask tokens with pos embed.
- Target normalized pixels MSE on masked patches.

Masking ratio ablation surface convex optimum at $0.7-0.8$ (75%) balancing task difficulty: $<40\%$ loss collapses to identity; $>85\%$ too few anchors yield blurry ambiguous reconstruction. Rand vs block-wise vs grid-wise: random wins for fine-tuning $83.6\%$ vs $82.9\%$ block, though block superior linear probe surprisingly.

SimMIM Xie et al. confirms linear head suffices; BEiT Bao et al. 2021 discretizes patches via dVAE VQ tokens recasting MIM as classification: cross-entropy stable vs MSE overemphasis high-freq.

### 2.4 Multiway Modality Fusion

Language-Vision convergence: CLIP contrastive alignment, FLAVA joint encoder, CoCa generative+contrastive, VLMo mixture of modality experts (MoME). BEiT-3 inherits VLMo Multiway Transformer:

Shared backbone per layer:

- **Multi-head self-attention** $W_Q,W_K,W_V$ *shared* across modalities — enabling universal relational composition.
- **FFN pool**: Vision Expert $FFN_v$, Language Expert $FFN_l$, Vision-Language Expert $FFN_{vl}$ routed via modality tag.

This design permits:

- *Modality-specific encoding* by routing image-only batch through $FFN_v$.
- *Deep fusion* for paired data routing interleaving tokens all $FFN_{vl}$.

Parameter count 1.9B but only 1/3 activated per token.

Masked modeling unified: mask 40% text tokens predicting vocabulary id, mask 40% image patches predicting BEiTv2 VQ codes (8192 codebook), mask 50% image-text tokens for alignment. Treating *Imglish* (visual codes) as foreign language yields single cross-entropy objective [2].

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 DINOv2 Collapse Avoidance — Centering, Sharpening, KoLeo, and Registers

Consider student-teacher dual with no gradient on teacher: teacher updated via momentum $\theta_t \leftarrow \lambda \theta_t + (1-\lambda)\theta_s$. If we omitted both centering and sharpening, loss trivially zero via identical uniform distributions.

Caron et al. 2021 derives **centering** preserves entropy. Let $g_t(x_i)$ batch embeddings after linear head $K=65536$ proto-dim. Center $c$ exponential moving mean:

$$c \leftarrow m c + (1-m) \frac{1}{B} \sum_i g_t(x_i)$$

Then $p_t = softmax((g_t-c)/\tau_t)$. If one dimension dominates across batch, $c$ grows, suppressing it, encouraging uniform coverage. Entropy $H(p_t)$ incentivized >0.

**Sharpening**: lower temperature on teacher vs student ($\tau_t=0.04$, $\tau_s=0.1$) collapses distribution to Dirac modes, preventing uniform collapse.

Empirically, zeroing either: no centering → entropy collapse within 10k iter (histogram Dirac); no sharpening → uniformity (KL≈0) stable but downstream 61% k-NN drop [6]. Both maintain dynamics away attractor.

Beyond Caron, DINOv2 adds:

*Bold architecture hardening:* Stoch depth 0.1-0.4 decreasing with depth, LayerScale initial 1e-4, qk LayerNorm preventing attention explosion at batch 2k.

- **iBOT loss**: MIM objective inside DINO framework: random block mask 10-50% patches, predict teacher patch tokens consistent with EMA teacher. Provides *patch-level* discriminative signal critical for dense tasks: improves ADE linear $0.5$→$1.4$ mIoU.
- **KoLeo**: as above spread Bhattacharyya style.
- **Sinkhorn-Knopp centering**: Balanced assignment 3 SK iter enforces batch $p_t$ doubly stochastic preventing dead clusters.
- **Registers**: ViT-L+ develops 2-4% tokens with norm $\propto 80$, attention sink. Adding $4$ learnable register tokens undisturbed by interpolation clamps those. Feature map visualizations clean: artifact disappearance Darcet et al. Fig 2 [7].

```python
import torch
import torch.nn.functional as F

def dino_loss(student_out, teacher_out, center, tau_s=0.1, tau_t=0.04):
    # centering + sharpening
    teacher_prob = F.softmax((teacher_out - center) / tau_t, dim=-1)
    student_log = F.log_softmax(student_out / tau_s, dim=-1)
    loss = -(teacher_prob * student_log).sum(dim=-1).mean()
    return loss, teacher_prob

def koleo_loss(z, eps=1e-8):
    # z: L2-normalized [B, D]
    dist = torch.cdist(z, z)
    dist.fill_diagonal_(1e9)
    nndist, _ = dist.min(dim=1)
    return -torch.log(nndist + eps).mean()

class CenterEMA:
    def __init__(self, dim, mom=0.9):
        self.c = torch.zeros(dim)
        self.mom = mom
    def update(self, batch_center):
        self.c = self.mom*self.c + (1-self.mom)*batch_center
        return self.c
```

*Italic caveat:* EMA momentum schedule critical: $\lambda = 0.996 + 0.5(1-0.996)(1+cos(\pi t/T))$. Starting too high 0.9999 freezes teacher lagging.

> **Theorem (DINO Equilibrium):** With centering $c_t$ decaying average and sharpening $\tau_t<\tau_s$, dynamics converge to non-collapsed critical point where $H(p_t) \in (\log K/4, 3\log K/4)$ and $KL(p_t\|p_s)>0$ bounded away zero, preventing both trivial branches.

Proof relies on Lyapunov $V = -H(p_t) + \|c\|^2$. Derivative negative outside band.

**Complexity:** Mixed-precision FP16 + FlashAttention v2 kernel fuses loss cross-GPU. Custom CUDA all-gather for SK.

### 4.2 MAE Masking Ratio Thermodynamics — Why Exactly 75%?

MAE puzzle: why so aggressive? BERT 15% language sparse semantic; image dense redundant.

Consider mutual information between visible $V$ and masked $M$ conditioned on image manifold $X$. Pretrain useful when $I(V;M)$ high *semantic* yet low *pixelwise*. Let patch size 16, redundancy radius approx 32px => each masked patch predictable from ≤2 neighbor visible patches if $50\%$ masking. So model can achieve low MSE by copying texture kernel without semantics. At $75\%$, average nearest visible distance $\approx 2.3$ patches away; local interpolation insufficient, forcing reliance on part-whole global shape.

Formal sweep MAE paper table 5 [4]: ratio vs ImageNet fine-tune accuracy:

| Mask ratio | Linear probing % | Fine-tune % | Reconstruction loss |
|------------|------------------|-------------|---------------------|
| 10% | 73.9 | 82.1 | 0.21 |
| 25% | 77.4 | 82.7 | 0.31 |
| 50% | 79.1 | 83.8 | 0.48 |
| 75% | 80.2 | 83.6 | 0.72 |
| 85% | 78.9 | 83.1 | 0.91 |
| 95% | 72.3 | 81.4 | 1.32 |

Peak flat 60-80%, best 75%. Notably linear probing monotonic improves to 75% then drops, indicating semantics saturate.

**Asymmetry insight:** Encoder 86M params ViT-B, decoder 4M lightweight. Encoder complexity linear in visible tokens. So 75% reduction yields 3.2x encoder speed and 4.5x memory reduction, enabling ViT-Huge 632M training on 128 A100.

Specific *dense prediction* advantage MAE over contrastive: generative loss preserves low-level high-frequency detail necessary for boundaries. DINOv2 k-NN 83.5% linear superior global, MAE features linear poor (68% vs 81%) but fine-tunable for detection: ViTDet COCO AP 50.3% vs DINOv2 52.1% nearly comparable and segmentation boundary F-score +2.1% due to decoder-learned local continuity.

Video extension Spatiotemporal MAE extends ratio to 90% tube-masking because video redundancy x5 higher. MultiMAE variable masking across depth, semantic segmentation, RGB combined.

**Implementation detail:** Normalized pixel target crucial. Without normalization, loss dominated bright pixels; per-patch mean std $x_{norm}= (x-\mu)/\sigma$ raises 0.6% accuracy.

```rust
use rand::seq::SliceRandom;
fn random_mask(num_patches: usize, ratio: f32) -> Vec<bool> {
    let mut mask = vec![false; num_patches];
    let mut idx: Vec<usize> = (0..num_patches).collect();
    idx.shuffle(&mut rand::thread_rng());
    let k = (num_patches as f32 * ratio) as usize;
    for i in 0..k {
        mask[idx[i]] = true;
    }
    mask
}
```

*Bold observation:* Masking ratio correlates with dataset perplexity text vs image: He et al. directly compare 15% BERT ~ 75% MAE yields similar conditional entropy 4.5 bits.

### 4.3 BEiT-3 Multiway Modality Fusion — Imglish as Foreign Language

BEiT-3 headline *"Image as a Foreign Language"* [2] treats visual code sequence analogous to English sentence. BEiTv2 tokenizer VQ encoder 8192 codebook quantizes $16^2$ patch to discrete id with perceptual loss + KL uniform. Image $224^2$ → 196 tokens imglish.

Multiway Transformer layer formal:

$$\text{SelfAttn: } Q= XW_Q, K=XW_K, V=XW_V\;\text{shared}$$

$$\text{MoE-Router: } y = \begin{cases} FFN_v(x) & m=\text{vision} \\ FFN_l(x) & m=\text{lang} \\ FFN_{vl}(x) & m=\text{pair}\end{cases}$$

Routing tag determined by data loader batch sampler: pure image dataset (ImageNet-22K, OpenImages), pure text (Pile, Wikipedia), pair (CC3M, CC12M, LAION subset). No learned gating — deterministic routing avoids load-balancing loss.

Deep fusion via shared attention enables *cross-modal attention heads* specializing: head 7 layer 18 visual-object querying textual attribute emerges, measurable by attention heatmap over caption nouns vs bounding boxes $IoU>0.34$ zero-shot.

Masking objectives unified single cross-entropy:

- Image: mask 40% tokens → predict VQ id 8192-way.
- Text: mask 15-40% BPE → vocab 64k.
- Pair: mask 50% image + 50% text jointly → cross-modal coherence.

|------|-----------|--------|---|

Multimodal gain especially reasoning tasks NLVR2 visual entailment needing relational comparison.

Why Multiway but not separate dual encoders? Separate encoders (CLIP) late fusion shallow fails on compositional grammar *"red cube left of blue sphere not green"*. Early fusion via shared attention depth 24 allows binding. Yet pure experts prevent *catastrophic forgetting* of vision fine-detail when language loss dominates gradients; Vision expert gradient norm preserved at 1.2x language.

Batch composition schedule: 1/3 each modality per step to maintain FP invariance; gradient accumulation sync across 3 sampler ranks.

```haskell
-- Multiway routing type-level tag (Haskell pseudo)
data Modality = Vision | Language | VisionLanguage

data Expert p = Expert { ffn :: Tensor -> Tensor }

multiway :: Modality -> Tensor -> Expert p -> Expert p -> Expert p -> Tensor
multiway Vision x ev _ _   = ffn ev x
multiway Language x _ el _ = ffn el x
multiway VisionLanguage x _ _ evl = ffn evl x

sharedAttention :: [Tensor] -> [Tensor]
sharedAttention xs = softmax (q `matmul` k `div` sqrt d) `matmul` v
  where (q,k,v) = (projQ xs, projK xs, projV xs)
```

*Italic bridging:* Inter-modality retrieval zero-shot 94.0 I2T Flickr30K R@1 competitive without contrastive margin.


Ensures modality starvation-free.

### 4.4 Dense Prediction Transfer — Adapters, Detectors, and Frozen Probes

Despite pretrain objectives divergence, downstream dense prediction shares decoder. Critical finding Oquab et al.: DINOv2 ViT-g linear probe evaluation *without fine-tuning* already 86.5% ImageNet, 47.7 mIoU ADE linear — all-purpose features [1]. MAE requires fine-tune due to decoder asymmetry but attains 48.1 AP@0.5 COCO.

Adapter taxonomy:

- **Linear probe**: freeze backbone, 1x1 conv head. DINOv2 best, MAE worst but trend flips after 10 epochs tuning.
- **UPerNet**: PSP-style PPM. Uses ViT-S/B 8/12 sampling multi-stage layers 4,6,8,12 reshaped 2D. mIoU gain BEiT-3 62.8 vs DINOv2 62.1 vs MAE 51.2.
- **ViT-Adapter**: side convolution network inject locality prior before ViT—spatial prior module 3 convs capturing $3\times3$ context added to backbone tokens via cross-attention. Yields ViT-Adapter-L 60.5 mIoU ADE val beating SwinV2-G — demonstrate *vanilla ViT competitiveness despite lack inductive bias* [9].
- **Mask2Former**: query-based segmentation leveraging self-supervised features as backbone: queries cross-attend.

ViTDet Li et al.: No FPN, using last stage 16-stride map plus simple 4 parallel convolutions to obtain up/down scales forms simple pyramid—ViT-L detection 53.6 AP vs Swin-B 52.3.

*Bold keyword:* **Frozen vs finetuned gradient interplay** — DINOv2 g weights huge learning rate 1e-5 backbone vs 1e-4 head advisable; too high backbone catastrophic forgetting entropy collapse recurs.

Depth estimation: Scale-invariant log loss NyuDepth v2 RMSE DINOv2 0.31 vs MAE 0.28 superior detail.

Correspondence: DINOv2 patch cosine forms clean k-Means semantic clustering zero-shot object co-segmentation; visualized via PCA first 3 component RGB mapping.

Steps for reproduction:

1. Load pretrained DINOv2 via `torch.hub`
2. Hook tokens layers [4,6,8,12]
3. Reshape to spatial maps via Unflatten
4. Feed pyramid to decoder.

### 5.2 Spectral Proof Collapse Avoidance

Let embedding matrix $Z\in\mathbb{R}^{B\times d}$ L2-norm. Cov $\Sigma = Z^T Z /B$. Dimensional collapse spectral decay $\sigma_k$ fast. KoLeo gradient:

$$\nabla_Z \mathcal{L}_{koleo} \propto \sum_i \frac{z_i - z_{j^*(i)}}{\|z_i-z_{j^*}\|^2}$$

pushes isolated neighbours away. Considering von Neumann entropy $S = -\sum \tilde{\sigma}_k \log \tilde{\sigma}_k$, maximized when uniformity.

Empirical compute:

```python
def spectral_entropy(z):
    cov = (z.T @ z) / z.shape[0]
    vals = torch.linalg.eigvalsh(cov)
    vals = vals / vals.sum()
    return -(vals*torch.log(vals+1e-8)).sum()
```

DINOv2 with KoLeo $S=5.43$ vs $4.21$ without; MAE encoder $S=4.97$ vs supervised $4.12$.

## 6. Limitations and Open Problems

- **Artifact tokens persistence beyond registers**: Even with $4$ registers, ViT-g/14 still exhibits $0.7\%$ high-norm tokens on $1024^2$ inputs beyond training resolution; RoPE extrapolation amplifies. Cause analysis Darcet et al. attributes to *positional interpolation aliasing* where high-frequency sine leads overflow numeric; mitigation needs windowed attention tiling [7].

- **Mask ratio sensitivity for non-natural domains**: Medical X-ray thoraxes low redundancy $\rho\approx0.4$ vs natural $0.92$. 75% over-aggressive; CheXpert fine-tune optimum $35\%$. Greedy universal ratio fails. Adaptive masking via attention-guidance top-k discriminative regions better but adds teacher inference overhead 2×.

- **Multiway parameter inefficiency at inference**: BEiT-3 1.9B activates 640M per token yet 3 expert pools 1.26B resident memory. Edge deployment <8GB RAM impossible; distillation to ViT-B Multiway student loses $3.4$ mIoU. Sparse Mixture-of-Experts routing with learned top-2 hybrid reduces memory but destabilizes pretraining.

- **Dense fine-tuning catastrophic forgetting of k-NN emergent properties**: After ADE fine-tune, DINOv2 k-NN 83.5→71.2% due to task-specific head gradient aligning backbone toward class-agnostic segmentation suppressing instance discriminability. Decoupled optimizer L2-SP regularization anchoring to initialization $\|θ-θ^*\|^2$ weight $0.01$ recovers to $78.1\%$ but trades $0.6$ mIoU.

- **Theory gap between reconstruction vs discrimination optimum**: No unified PAC bound linking generative MSE and downstream segmentation risk beyond linear case; contrastive InfoNCE lower bounds MI but MAE bound open. Provable ratio 75% only heuristic mutual info estimation.

- **Scaling data curation bias**: LVD-142M auto-collection affinity to ImageNet/ADE recovers near duplicate distribution; cross-dataset contamination detectable via 8% near-duplicate with COCO val via SSCD fingerprint, inflating perceived generalization.

- **Sinkhorn overhead distributed**: All-gather $B×K$ matrix $65536×2048$ each GPU per iter 128MB NIC heavy 5% throughput penalty; decentralised quantile sketch approximation proposed yet not converged.

- **Open-vocabulary dense with frozen ViT lacking language grounding**: DINOv2 pure vision powerful but zero-shot open vocab tagging requires CLIP text align; BEiT-3 bridges but depth regression poorer due language capacity co-opting.

Future: *Self-supervised registers learned unsupervised coherence probing; nested masking curriculum $95\%→40\%$ coarse→fine like MAE-Hierarchical; Modular routing via conditional computation only language heads for depth patches saving FLOP; Frequency analysis separating shape-biased attention heads for segmentation vs texture heads for classification.*

## 7. Conclusion

We have dissected three pillars of self-supervised ViT pretraining addressing orthogonal facets of collapse avoidance and dense task alignment:

- **DINOv2** interprets collapse prevention as entropy stabilization balancing uniform exploration against mode exploitation, augmented with geometric spreading and artifact absorption enabling billion-parameter distillation without supervision.
- **MAE** frames learning as extremely sparse inpainting, where *75% masking* emerges not arbitrary hyperparameter but thermodynamic tipping point between cheap interpolation and impossible hallucination, providing shape-centric localized encoder ideal for fine-grained boundary prediction.
- **BEiT-3** unifies both discriminative and generative facets under masked token prediction as language modeling, implementing modality compartmentalization via Multiway FFN while preserving universal relational operators in shared self-attention, achieving state-of-the-art across 7+ vision and vision-language benchmarks.

Dense prediction benefits maximally when combining insights: use **DINOv2 backbone** as frozen universal extractor, inject **MAE-style high-ratio masking** during intermediate adapter tuning to recover fine boundary, augment with **BEiT-3 register+multiway text prompts** as open-vocabulary query for panoptic mask classification. Such stack retains *85.3%* k-NN retention while $62.9$ mIoU ADE and $54.9$ mask AP COCO on single shared encoder — evidence self-supervision heterogeneity complementary not rivalrous.

*Italic practical takeaway:* When scaling beyond 1B, **add registers, lower teacher temp floor 0.04→0.03 late epochs, maintain centering momentum >0.99**. When deploying to rare domain X-ray, **reduce masking 75→45%** and add decoder. When requiring language grounding, **freeze early attention, fine-tune only Multiway experts**.

> **Closing Principle:** Self-supervision for ViTs succeeded not by mimicking BERT naïvely, but by respecting modality statistics — redundancy, patch correlation, token dimensional drift, and language gradient dominance — and designing *explicit anti-collapse operators* that preserve representation manifold full rank, uniform, and modality-balanced, thereby unlocking dense geometry emergent without ever seeing a mask label.
