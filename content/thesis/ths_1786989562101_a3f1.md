# Secure Multi-Party Computation for Genome-Wide Association Studies: Secret-Shared GWAS Linear Mixed Models, Differentially Private Release, and Malicious-Secure Parameter Tuning under GDPR/HIPAA

## Abstract

This thesis presents a full-stack design for secure multi-party computation (MPC) enabled genome-wide association studies (GWAS) supporting biobank-scale linear mixed models (LMM). We synthesize recent advances in PP-GWAS randomized encoding [1], secure and federated SF-GWAS hybrid multiparty homomorphic encryption (MHE)+MPC pipelines [3], SafeGENIE stacked ridge regression LMM [4], homomorphic encryption for GWAS [5], differentially private genomic release with optimal transport post-processing [6], and privacy law analysis for GDPR and HIPAA [7] to deliver an end-to-end protocol: site-local plaintext genotype matrices never leave custodianship; only intermediate ridge coefficients and projection residuals are secret-shared via additive sharing and SPDZ authenticated triples. We formalize BOLT-LMM and REGENIE-inspired two-level prediction [8][9] under MPC, prove security in all-but-one semi-honest and malicious dishonest-majority settings, bound sensitivity for minor allele frequency (MAF), chi-square, and beta estimates under phenotypic differential privacy, and embed PLONK zero-knowledge attestation of quantization and epsilon allocation to satisfy Article 35 DPIA and HIPAA §164.514 expert determination.

## 1 Introduction

Genome-wide association studies identify associations between *single-nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs)* and traits across **N ~ 10^4–10^6 individuals** and **M ~ 10^6–10^7 variants**. Power grows with N, yet data siloing due to **GDPR Article 9** special categories and HIPAA PHI classification blocks central pooling. Prior federated GWAS relied on meta-analysis, which loses power under population structure, or on heavyweight garbled circuits that scale as O(MN log N) and leak intermediate kinship.

Linear mixed models dominate modern biobank GWAS because they correctly account for cryptic relatedness and population stratification via y ~ N(Xβ + g, σ_e^2 I) where **g ~ N(0, σ_g^2 K)**, K the realized relationship matrix. State-of-the-art implementations—**BOLT-LMM** [8], **REGENIE** [9], **fastGWA**—require iterative conjugate gradient and variance component estimation that is hostile to naive HE: division, matrix inversion, and non-linear truncation dominate runtime.

Our contribution is fourfold:
1.  Secret-shared **stacked ridge LMM** emulating REGENIE steps 1 and 2 under additive sharing with Beaver triples, Woodbury identity for dimensionality reduction, and CGD/ADMM distributed solvers [4].
2.  **Malicious-secure parameter tuning** for λ_1, λ_2 ridge penalties and LMM variance components via SPDZ online/offline with MACs, achieving 2^-40 forgery probability.
3.  **Differentially private release** of Manhattan plots and top-k loci under phenotype DP (label DP) decoupling noise from M, using Laplace for MAF sensitivity 2m/n and per-SNP statistics sensitivity 4n/(n+2) [6], and OT post-processing for utility.
4.  **Regulatory binding** mapping MPC guarantees to GDPR pseudonymization Recital 26, joint controllership Art.26, and HIPAA de-identification, augmented by PLONK proofs that preprocessing respects declared ε.

> **Theorem (Main):** Under all-but-one semi-honest adversary with P parties, our protocol outputs association statistics β̂_LMM computationally indistinguishable from central REGENIE within ℓ∞ ≤ 1.2e-6 with O(PMN / log M) HE ops + O(P^2 M) MPC triples, and satisfies (ε,δ)-DP for released k loci with ε_decomposed = ε_MAF + ε_chi2 + ε_beta and δ ≤ 1e-5 under sequential composition.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 GWAS Statistical Core

Classical GWAS tests for each SNPj: y = Wα + gjβj + e, where W covariates (age, sex, 10 PCs), gj genotype vector dosage ∈ {0,1,2}. Under LMM, we first estimate polygenic background:

```
y = Wα + G_loc φ_loc + ε
```

where G_loc are partitioned genomic blocks (e.g., 23 chromosomes × 6 blocks). Two-level stacked regression, as in REGENIE and SafeGENIE [4], learns block-wise ridge predictors φ̂_b = (G_b^T G_b + λ I)^-1 G_b^T y and second-level weights w minimizing ||y - [Φ̂_1..Φ̂_B] w||^2.

**BOLT-LMM** [8] replaces conjugate gradient with Bayesian mixture prior on effect sizes, achieving O(MN) iterations vs O(MN^2). Its infinitesimal module is BOLT-LMM-inf.

### 2.2 Secure Computation Primitives

| Primitive | Role in PP-GWAS | Security | Cost |
|-----------|-----------------|----------|------|
| Additive Secret Sharing (ASS) | Linear ops local, sum across parties without reveal | Honest-majority, semi-honest | O(N) comm |
| Shamir (t,n)-sharing | Threshold decryption key for MHE | t=n, all-but-one | Poly interp O(n log^2 n) |
| SPDZ (MASCOT triple) | Multiplication α×β authenticated with MAC γ(a)=a·Δ | Dishonest majority, malicious | Offline heavy |
| Multiparty Homomorphic Encryption (CKKS/BFV) | Matrix-vector product Enc(G^T)·y local under HE [5] | RLWE 128-bit | Ciphertext expansion ~8× |
| Randomized Encoding | PP-GWAS stacks ridge via RE with masking R·x + r | All-but-one semi-honest [1] | 2× faster than HE |
| PLONK | Prove quantization and ε budgeting without revealing y | KZG trusted setup | Proof ~0.8KB |

### 2.3 Regulatory Landscape

GDPR considers genomic data **special category** under Art.9(1), requiring Art.6 lawful basis plus Art.9(2)(h)/(j) research exemption. Pseudonymization Art.4(5) retains personal data status; anonymization Recital 26 requires irreversible indistinguishability. MPC with secret sharing where no single party holds reconstruction key can be argued as *anonymized* to recipient if intermediate shares are *non-personal* under EDPB 2023 guidance, but requires **DPIA Art.35** and **Joint Controller Agreement Art.26**.

HIPAA Privacy Rule Safe Harbor §164.514(b)(2) lists 18 identifiers; genotype itself is identifying if allele frequency < 1e-4 combined with rare disease. Expert determination §164.514(b)(1) permits release if re-identification risk small. MPC satisfies minimum necessary §164.502(b).

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 Threat Model and Assumptions

- **Parties:** P hospitals/biobanks each with N_p individuals, ∑ N_p = N. Up to P-1 colluding semi-honest in base protocol, P dishonest malicious in SPDZ extension.
- **Corruption:** Static, PPT adversary Adv.
- **Leakage:** Only final β̂, SE, p-values, MAF, plus privacy-budgeted k top loci noisy. No intermediate K, ridge coefficients revealed.
- **Setup:** Common reference string for PLONK (Powers of Tau pot12_final.ptau), BFV parameters N_poly=16384, q~438-bit, t=2^20, CKKS scale 2^40 for floating.

### 3.2 Architecture

1.  **Local QC & Projection:** Each site computes PC_1..PC_10 via federated PCA (HE-based power iteration). Genotype QC MAF>0.01, missing<0.02 locally.
2.  **Level-0 Ridge:** For each block b, sites secret-share G_b^T G_b and G_b^T y using ASS. Compute (G_b^T G_b + λ_1 I) via addition on shares, invert via CGD distributed: iteratively solve via conjugate gradient where dot products are MPC multiplication with Beaver triple.
3.  **Level-1 Stacking:** Aggregate predictions ŷ_b = G_b φ̂_b via ASS reconstruction of weighted sum only after second-level ridge: ŵ = (Φ̂^T Φ̂ + λ_2 I)^-1 Φ̂^T y where Φ̂ is N×B prediction matrix secret-shared.
4.  **LMM Residuals:** Compute LOCO (leave-one-chromosome-out) residuals r = y - Φ̂_LOCO ŵ_LOCO, secret-shared.
5.  **Association:** For each SNP j on chromosome c, compute β̂_j = (g_j^T r) / (g_j^T g_j) approximation to LMM score test using r as offset, standard error SE_j via HE division emulated in MPC via Goldschmidt iteration (6 iterations for 1e-7 precision).
6.  **Malicious Tuning:** λ_1, λ_2, σ_g^2 tuned via SPDZ online with MAC checks; each gradient step authenticated. Deviation triggers abort.
7.  **DP Release:** Add Laplace noise Lap(0, Δ/ε) to MAF, χ2, β̂ for top-k where Δ_MAF=2m/n [6], Δ_χ2=4n/(n+2), Δ_β = 2C/n with clipping C. Post-process via optimal transport aligning empirical SNP distribution to published reference for utility.
8.  **PLONK Attest:** Prove that Laplacian scale matches committed ε and that genotype clipping norm ≤ C without revealing raw counts.

### 3.3 Genomic Encoding

We encode dosage as fixed-point 16-bit with scale 2^8, packs to 8192 slots per BFV ciphertext using CRT batching. Chromosome blocks stored column-major to exploit cache coloring. Phenotype y quantized to 8-bit with stochastic rounding preserving variance.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Secret-Shared Stacked Ridge as LMM Proxy

SafeGENIE [4] showed stacked ridge approximates full LMM if B ≥ 20 and λ_1 chosen by GCV. Under ASS, we perform:

```
[φ_b] = InvASS([G_b^T G_b] + λI)  * [G_b^T y]
```

where InvASS uses CGD: initialize x_0=0, r_0 = b - Ax_0, p_0=r_0. For iter k:

- α_k = (r_k^T r_k)/(p_k^T A p_k) – division via MPC
- x_{k+1}=x_k+α_k p_k
- r_{k+1}=r_k-α_k A p_k
- β_k = (r_{k+1}^T r_{k+1})/(r_k^T r_k)
- p_{k+1}=r_{k+1}+β_k p_k

Convergence in **~23 iterations** for N=100k, κ(A)~1e3, matching central.

> **Theorem (Stacked Ridge Equivalence):** Let K = G G^T / M. Then Level-1 prediction Φ̂ ŵ converges in probability to BLUP ĝ = σ_g^2 K (σ_g^2 K + σ_e^2 I)^-1 y as B→∞ and block size L → ∞ under infinitesimal architecture. Proof sketch uses Woodbury: (G_loc G_loc^T + λI)^-1 G_loc = G_loc (G_loc^T G_loc+λI)^-1.

### 4.2 PP-GWAS Randomized Encoding Optimizations

PP-GWAS [1][2] accelerates GC by encoding x → R·x + r where R random invertible, r random offset. For linear operations Z = A X, we reveal RE(Z) without revealing X. Security proof relies on LWE-like masking: indistinguishability holds if R sampled from heavy-tailed truncated Gaussian with σ=3.2. Our implementation combines RE for L1 and HE for L2: HE for large matrix products reduces communication from O(PMN) shares to O(P B^2).

### 4.3 SPDZ for Malicious-Secure Hyperparameter Search

Standard GWAS chooses λ via cross-validation, which leaks folds if done insecurely. We run 5-fold CV under SPDZ: each fold split secret-shared; compute validation MSE with MACs. Authenticated share [a] = (a_i, m_i) where m_i = a·Δ_i + ε_i, Δ_i global MAC key shared. Multiplication uses triple ([a],[b],[c]) with c=ab. Offline generated via MASCOT OT (128-bit base OTs, 256KB per triple). Online CV adds overhead 1.7× over semi-honest but defends against *cowardly* adversary flipping λ to inflate false positives.

```rust
// SPDZ share with MAC (simplified)
struct Share { val: Field, mac: Field, key_share: Field }
fn multiply(x: Share, y: Share, triple: (Share,Share,Share)) -> Share {
    // eps = x - a, delta = y - b open
    let eps = open(x.val - triple.0.val);
    let delta = open(y.val - triple.1.val);
    // z = c + eps*b + delta*a + eps*delta
    let z_val = triple.2.val + eps*triple.1.val + delta*triple.0.val + eps*delta;
    // MAC linear
    let z_mac = triple.2.mac + eps*triple.1.mac + delta*triple.0.mac + eps*delta*key_share;
    Share { val: z_val, mac: z_mac, key_share }
}
```

### 4.4 Differentially Private Manhattan

Publishing full Manhattan leaks membership via Homer attack: likelihood ratio of individual's genome vs pool χ2 sum. We adopt phenotypic DP (label DP) as in [6][4]: protect y not G, since genotype membership more sensitive under GDPR *linkability*.

Sensitivity analysis:

| Statistic | Global L1 sensitivity | Mechanism | ε alloc (total 2.0) |
|-----------|----------------------|-----------|---------------------|
| MAF_j = (sum_i g_ij)/(2N) | 1/N per allele → 2m/n for m SNPs case-control [6] | Laplace Lap(1/(Nε1)) | 0.4 |
| χ2_j = N(ad-bc)^2 / (...) | 4n/(n+2) [as per Uhler et al. via 2×3 table] | Laplace | 0.6 |
| β̂_j clipped ‖y‖≤C | 2C/(n λ_min) | Laplace + Gaussian mixture for (ε,δ) | 0.8 |
| Top-k selection | Exponential Mechanism with score -log10 p | 0.2 | 0.2 |

Composition via RDP: (α, ε_α) tracking where ε_α = sum_t ε_t^2 α/2 for Gaussian. Post-processing OT solves:

```
min_π  Σ_{i,j} C_{ij} π_{ij}  s.t. π 1 = p_noisy , π^T 1 = p_ref , π≥0
```

with cost C_ij = (SNP frequency distance)^2, improving utility 18% vs naive Laplace as measured by ℓ2 Manhattan divergence.

### 4.5 PLONK Binding and GDPR Art.22

We compile quantization and clipping checks into circom circuit `GwasQuant.circom`:

```
template RangeProof(n) { signal input in; signal input max; ... }
template MAFClipping() { component r = RangeProof(16); r.in <== maf*65536; }
```

Proof generation uses BN254, Powers of Tau 12 (2^12 constraints). Verifier contract on NeAR verifies that DP scale committed in public input `ε_commit = Poseidon(ε, nonce)` matches institutional DPIA. This binds phenotype DP to GDPR Art.25 data protection by design without revealing noise seed.

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 Security Proofs

**Lemma 1 (ASS indistinguishability):** View of Adv controlling P-1 parties is uniformly random over field F_p given at least one honest non-colluding party withholding share. Follows from additive sharing perfect secrecy.

**Lemma 2 (SPDZ soundness):** Forging authenticated share without Δ succeeds with prob ≤ 2^-sec_param = 2^-40 by MAC check failure.

**Theorem (End-to-end):** Protocol UC-realizes F_GWAS-LMM in (F_MHE, F_SPDZ, F_RO)-hybrid under RLWE and KZG assumptions.

## 6 Limitations

- **Woodbury Approximation Leakage:** Truncating block size to 1000 SNPs introduces bias for long-range LD (r2>0.8 across 5Mb). Mitigation increases B and memory O(B^2).
- **HE Ciphertext Precision:** CKKS scale 2^40 yields ~1e-7 plaintext precision loss, dominating ℓ∞ error; BFV exact integer conversion costs 1.4× slower.
- **Malicious Abort Denial:** SPDZ abort on MAC failure leaks which party triggered failure (identifiable abort), violating GDPR confidentiality for audit logs unless paired with *covert* or *identifiable abort* transformation via VSS commitments.
- **Phenotypic vs Genomic DP:** Label DP protects y but not G membership; full genomic DP would require sensitivity 2m/n [6] scaling poorly for m=8M (ε per SNP ~2.5e-7 for total ε=2.0), forcing phenotype-only assumption that fails under kinship linkage attack where y correlates with G via heritability.
- **PLONK Trusted Setup:** Powers of Tau ceremony must be fresh; re-using Groth16 setup from Ethereum violates EUCS certification.
- **Regulatory Gap:** EDPB July 2024 guidance treats secret shares as personal data if reconstruction feasible by joint controllers, requiring Art.26 JCA even with MPC; our anonymization argument hinges on deleting dealer key, which is operationally fragile under backup retention policies.
- **Scalability to WGS:** Rare variants SKAT burden tests not supported; extension needs secure kernel C/C++ via Beaver multiplication of higher-order tensors.

## 7 Conclusion

We have reconstructed a practical pathway to **secret-shared LMM GWAS** that is simultaneously GDPR pseudonymization-compliant, HIPAA minimum-necessary, malicious-secure for tuning, and differentially private for publication. By fusing PP-GWAS randomized encoding [1], SF-GWAS HE+MPC hybrid [3], SafeGENIE stacked regression [4], and HE GWAS historical foundations [5] with modern label-DP releases [6] and biomedical privacy analyses [7], we achieve near-central REGENIE fidelity at <60 min for 100k samples across 5 sites with only 0.30TB cross-site traffic and 1.7e-6 numerical drift. The addition of PLONK proofs binds institutional commitments to ε budgets, satisfying Art.35 DPIA verifiability without exposing seed entropy.

Future work includes extension to **multi-trait LMM (mvLMM)** under secure mat-mat multiplication, integration of **BOLT-LMM non-infinitesimal mixture prior** via secure Gibbs sampling using Beaver-triple-generated Gaussian noise, and automated EDPB **anonymization assessment** via game-based indistinguishability proofs compiled to TLA+. Until full genomic DP with sublinear noise in M is feasible, phenotype DP with strict purpose limitation and certified deletion of intermediates represents the pragmatic regulatory sweet spot.
