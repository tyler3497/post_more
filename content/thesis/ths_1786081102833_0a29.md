## 6 Limitations

1. **Verification gap**: seL4 proof assumes hardware semantics defined by ARM ISA manual but not microarchitectural covert channels; cache coloring mitigates but not eliminate timing side channels beyond LLC.
2. **DVS calibration**: event camera bias thresholds drift with temperature; requires online recalibration every ~10 min, not modeled in STDP proof.
3. **Post-quantum hybrid negotiators**: combiner proof in random oracle; real-world downgrade resilience depends on KEM identifier enforcement in TLS transcript hash—implementation bug could bypass [6].
4. **CFD adjoint**: discrete adjoint memory O(T·mesh) for unsteady solver expensive; checkpointing via revolve algorithm introduces recomputation tradeoff.
5. **PIR lattice parameters**: security vs correctness error trade; RLWE noise > q/4 causes decryption failure ~2⁻³⁵ probability not negligible for repeated queries.
6. **ZeRO** communication collectives duplicate under heterogeneous bandwidth; final bucket all-gather limited by straggler.
7. **TM persist**: PMDK libtx logging overhead persists on NVDIMM wear; endurance 10⁷ writes may wear early under hot HTM abort retry.
8. **Photonics**: fusion success probability percolation threshold ~0.5 for 3D cluster, requires multiplexing overhead 4× physical photons per logical.
9. **Profunctor optics**: compile-time blowup exponential in nested optics depth; GHC inlining threshold hit at depth >7 causing dictionary explosion.
10. **DAG BFT**: mempool certificates O(n²) signatures; threshold signature aggregation reduces but verification still O(n) pairings expensive on BLS12-381 (~1ms).
