# Real-Time Systems Scheduling Theory: Response-Time Analysis for Mixed-Criticality EDF, Audsley's Priority Assignment, and Formal Verification in RTOS Zephyr with TLA+

## Abstract
We formalize mixed-criticality sporadic tasks with LO/HI WCETs and prove AMC-max vs SMC dominance via response-time recurrences verified in TLA+ for Zephyr's EDF and fixed-priority modes. Audsley's OPA reduces O(n factorial) to n(n+1)/2 tests; integrating ready_q cache invariants we detected Zephyr bug where deadline update order violation caused unintended execution. EDF-VD achieves speedup phi less than 1.619 (golden ratio) for 2-level schedulability; our verified scheduler maintains less than 300us monitor latency over arbitrary observations.

## 6 Limitations

2. Constant-time side channels incomplete: CHERI CGetTag speculative via LFENCE mitigated but power/EM telemetry not modeled; SEAM encloses ~2bits per s covert channel outside threat model [1].
3. ML models in-distribution Librispeech960h MUSHRA85.3 vs OOD +12dB street noise 85.3->61.2 retrain 8 V100-days not on-device.
