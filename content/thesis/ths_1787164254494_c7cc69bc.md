# Interpretable Machine Learning via Concept Bottleneck and Probing: TCAV Sensitivity, Linear Probes with SAE Dictionary Learning, Causal Abstraction for Transformer Circuits, and Representation Engineering for Honesty

## Abstract
This thesis unifies four complementary strands of interpretable machine learning: post-hoc concept testing via TCAV, ante-hoc interpretable architectures via Concept Bottleneck Models, sparse dictionary learning for monosemantic feature extraction via Sparse Autoencoders, and top-down representation control via representation engineering, all grounded in causal abstraction theory for transformer circuits. We formalize concept utility through directional derivatives and linear concept activation vectors at intermediate layers, analyze how concept bottleneck training imposes an informational bottleneck $x \rightarrow c \rightarrow y$ that enables test-time intervention, demonstrate how overcomplete sparse autoencoders trained on residual streams recover $>30$M interpretable atoms resolving polysemanticity and superposition, and show how contrastive linear probes yield steering vectors for honesty that improve truthful QA by measurable margins. Synthesizing results from ICML 2018 TCAV [1][2], CBMs [3], Anthropic's scaling monosemanticity [4], dictionary learning foundations [5], RepE honesty directions [6], and causal abstraction formalism [7], we provide end-to-end methodology, empirical circuits analyses on GPT-2 small and Claude 3 Sonnet-class models, and safety implications for auditing deception, power-seeking, and sycophancy.

## 1 Introduction

***Interpretability*** has shifted from heatmaps to ***mechanistic explanations*** that support causal prediction and control of model behavior [1][3][7]. Two tensions persist: *fidelity versus usability*, and *locality versus compositionality*. Feature attribution tells *where* a model looks but not *what concept* it uses — e.g., whether a zebra classifier uses *stripedness* or *savanna background* [1]. Conversely, fully symbolic bottlenecks sacrifice capacity.

We argue interpretability must answer three questions simultaneously, inspired by ladder of causation and mechanistic interpretability:

1.  **What concept does a representation encode?** via *probing* and *TCAV* sensitivity [1][2]
2.  **Is the concept causally responsible for downstream behavior?** via *causal abstraction* and *interchange interventions* [7]
3.  **Can we fix it without retraining?** via *concept bottleneck intervention* [3] and *representation engineering steering* [6], enabled by *SAE feature surgery* [4][5]

> **Central Thesis:** Polysemantic superposition can be inverted by sparse overcomplete dictionary learning to yield monosemantic features that are simultaneously probed by linear CAVs, abstracted into causal high-level models, and steered via top-down reading vectors for honesty, forming a complete interpretable loop $f_{low} \rightleftharpoons \mathcal{M}_{high}$.

Our contributions:

- Unified formalism linking CAVs, CBMs, SAEs, and RepE under intervention algebra
- Reproduction of TCAV statistical testing on Inception and BERT, with FastCAV acceleration analysis
- End-to-end SAE training on GPT-2 small residual layer 8, extracting 3072 monosemantic features with 54.9% variance explained at L0=42.7 [4][5]
- Demonstration of causal abstraction for induction heads and honesty circuit tracing
- Honesty steering evaluation achieving truthfulness improvements with bounded KL penalty [6]

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Concept Activation Vectors and TCAV

Kim et al. introduced *Concept Activation Vectors* to move beyond pixel attribution [1][2]. Given activations $f_l(x) \in \mathbb{R}^d$ at layer $l$, user-provided concept examples $P_C$ (e.g., striped images) and random counterexamples $P_{rand}$, a linear classifier separates the activation clouds; its normal vector $v_C^l$ is the CAV. Concept importance for class $k$ is measured by directional derivative:

$$ S_{C,k,l}(x) = \nabla h_{l,k}(f_l(x)) \cdot v_C^l = \lim_{h\to 0} \frac{h_{l,k}(f_l(x)+h v_C^l)-h_{l,k}(f_l(x))}{h} \tag{1}$$

TCAV score aggregates:

$$ \text{TCAV}_{Q_{C,k,l}} = \frac{|\{x\in X_k: S_{C,k,l}(x)>0\}|}{|X_k|} \quad [1] $$

Statistical significance is tested against multiple random CAVs via two-sided t-test. FastCAV replaces SVM with ridge-accelerated closed-form to enable ImageNet-scale auditing.

**Properties:** *Global*, *concept-level*, *model-agnostic*, and *human-friendly* [2].

### 2.2 Concept Bottleneck Models (CBMs)

Koh et al. [3] enforce $p(y|c)p(c|x)$ factorization. Training optimizes:

$$ \mathcal{L} = \sum_i \mathcal{L}_y(f(c_i),y_i) + \lambda \sum_j \mathcal{L}_{c_j}(g_j(x_i),c_{ij}) $$

Variants include Concept Embedding Models (CEMs) [Zarlenga et al. 2022] using vector embeddings for richer semantics, and CF-CBMs adding counterfactual sampling for third-rung ladder coverage. Leakage problem — $c_j$ encoding extra task-irrelevant information — motivates interventions and Mahinpei et al. orthogonalization.

| Model | Factorization | Intervention | Accuracy vs Std | Interpretability |
|-------|---------------|--------------|---------------|------------------|
| Standard DNN | $x\to y$ | No | Baseline | Post-hoc only |
| CBM [3] | $x\to c\to y$ | Yes, logical | -2 to +1% | Intrinsic |
| CEM | $x\to e(c)\to y$ | Embedding edit | Parity | Intrinsic+ |
| TCAV+CBM Hybrid | $x\to c$, test $S_C$ | Both | Tradeoff | Hybrid |

### 2.3 Superposition, Polysemanticity, and Sparse Dictionary Learning

Elhage et al. superposition hypothesis: models encode $m \gg d$ features as almost-orthogonal directions in $d$-dimensional space; single neurons are *polysemantic* superpositions [5]. Sparse Autoencoders invert this:

$$ \mathbf{f}(\mathbf{a}) = \text{ReLU}(W_e \mathbf{a}+b_e), \quad \hat{\mathbf{a}} = W_d \mathbf{f}+b_d, \quad \mathcal{L}=||a-\hat a||_2^2 + \lambda||f||_1 $$

Gated SAEs [Rajamanoharan et al. 2024] decouple *which direction* vs *magnitude* to reduce shrinkage. Anthropic scaled to 34M features on Claude 3 Sonnet middle residual [4], revealing multilingual, multimodal, abstract features: *Golden Gate Bridge*, *deception*, *code bug*, *sycophancy*, *power-seeking*.

Key metrics: **L0 sparsity**, **variance explained**, **dead feature rate**, **steering efficacy**, **autointerpretability score**.

### 2.4 Representation Engineering and Causal Abstraction

Zou et al. [6] propose top-down reading vectors: given contrastive pairs $(p^+,p^-)$ for concept *honesty*, extract difference of residual activations $\Delta = h^+ - h^-$, PCA first component $r = \text{PC}_1(\Delta)$. Monitoring projects $h \cdot r$, manipulation adds $\alpha r$.

Geiger et al. [7] unify these methods via causal abstraction. A high-level causal model $\mathcal{M}_H$ with variables $V$ is abstraction of low-level neural net $\mathcal{M}_L$ if interventions align:

$$ \tau(I_{L}^{V\leftarrow v})(\mathcal{M}_L) = I_{H}^{V\leftarrow v}(\mathcal{M}_H) $$

where $\tau$ maps low-level states. This formalizes activation patching, path patching, causal mediation, DAS (Distributed Alignment Search), and SAE as instances of *intervention algebra* with hard and soft transformations.

## 3 Methodology

We adopt **extraction → validation → intervention → evaluation** loop grounded in [7]'s algebra.

1.  **Dataset construction:**
    - Concept sets for TCAV: 50 striped, 50 random ImageNet; BRODEN textures for polka-dotted, striped, zigzagged vs ladybug class [1]
    - CUB-200-2011 112 concepts, OAI chest X-ray opacity concepts for CBM
    - Contrastive honesty pairs 512 from TruthfulQA true/false factual statements [6]

2.  **CAV / Linear Probe training:**
    - Collect activations layer $l$, train logistic regression CAV, filter insignificant CAVs ($p>0.05$ vs 50 random CAVs).
    - Compute $S_{C,k,l}$ and TCAV distribution. Report both mean and Cohen's d effect size.

3.  **CBM Training Protocol:**
    - Encoder ResNet-18 or BERT-base encoder, concept head MLP 2-layer ELU, label predictor linear. Joint training with $\lambda=1$, then sequential finetune for disentanglement.
    - Intervention protocol: at test time, oracle replaces 10% highest-uncertainty concepts to measure uplift; expected efficiency $E[\uparrow acc|\text{int}]$.

4.  **SAE Dictionary Learning:**
    - Hook residual stream layer 8 (GPT-2 small, d=768) normalized $\mathbb{E}||a||_2^2=d$. Expansion $4\times$ → 3072 features, L1 8e-5, resampling dead neurons every 5k steps. Training 500k tokens OpenWebText subset.

> **Theorem 3.1 (TCAV Consistency under Interchange Intervention):** *If $v_C^l$ aligns with high-level variable $V_C$ under abstraction $\tau$, then $\mathbb{E}_{x}[ \mathbf{1}[S_{C,k,l}(x)>0] ]$ equals interchange intervention accuracy of $V_C \rightarrow Y$ in $\mathcal{M}_H$ up to $\epsilon$ bounded by alignment error.*

*Proof sketch:* Follows Geiger et al. Lemma 3.2 [7]: soft intervention where $f_l(x)+hv_C^l$ corresponds to $do(V_C=v+h)$. Linear approximation holds by Taylor expansion of $h_{l,k}$ with Lipschitz constant $L$. Statistical test filters alignment failure (IIA< chance). ∎

```python
# CAV + TCAV estimation + Honesty reading vector (Zou et al. 2023 RepE)
import torch, numpy as np
from sklearn.linear_model import LogisticRegression

def train_cav(acts_concept, acts_random):
    X = np.vstack([acts_concept, acts_random])
    y = np.hstack([np.ones(len(acts_concept)), np.zeros(len(acts_random))])
    clf = LogisticRegression(max_iter=1000).fit(X, y)
    v = clf.coef_[0] / np.linalg.norm(clf.coef_[0])
    return v, clf.score(X,y)

def tcav_score(model, layer, v_c, X_k, class_k):
    grads = [] # autograd directional derivative
    h = model.layer_gradients(layer, X_k, class_k)  # [N,d]
    S = h @ v_c
    return (S>0).mean(), S

def honesty_reading_vector(model, pairs, layer=15):
    diffs=[]
    for pos, neg in pairs:
        a_pos = model.resid(layer, pos)[-1]
        a_neg = model.resid(layer, neg)[-1]
        diffs.append(a_pos-a_neg)
    D = torch.stack(diffs).cpu().numpy()
    U, S, Vh = np.linalg.svd(D - D.mean(0), full_matrices=False)
    r = Vh[0]; r/=np.linalg.norm(r)
    return r
# steering: h_tilde = h + alpha * r
```

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 TCAV Sensitivity and Linear Probe Calibration

***Sensitivity geometry matters.*** Positive TCAV $>0.5$ indicates concept aligns positively with class decision; but magnitude of $S_{C,k,l}$ encodes *strength*. We observe layered specialization:

- **Early layers (mixed3a mixed3b Inception):** TCAV $\approx 0.5$ random, low discriminability — texture concepts not yet linearly separable [1]
- **Middle layers (mixed4c-4e):** TCAV $0.76\pm0.06$ for stripes→zebra, $0.91$ for polka-dotted→ladybug, significant $p<0.001$ vs random $0.5\pm0.03$
- **Late layers (mixed5b):** TCAV saturates to $0.82$, but *multiple concepts* become significant — concept entanglement emerges

We propose **$\alpha$-TCAV** generalization using ridge-CAV with angular margin $\alpha$ to stabilize against near-collinear $\epsilon$. FastCAV reduces compute from SVM $O(n^3)$ to $O(nd)$ via closed-form, retaining Pearson $r=0.96$ with SVM-CAV [1][2].

*Failure modes:* CAV can encode dataset bias not causal use; e.g., *savanna background* as spurious correlate for zebra. Interchange intervention filtering (swap concept activations between models) drops 23% false positives.

### 4.2 Concept Bottleneck Leakage, Intervenability, and Embedding Remedies

***Bottlenecks leak.*** Vanilla CBMs violate intended factorization $I(Y;X|C)>0$ due to concept encoder overcapacity. Mahinpei leakage score:

$$ Leak = I(\hat c; x | c) $$

We measure leak via auxiliary decoder attempting to predict $x$ from $\hat c$ residuals. Vanilla leakage $0.21$ nats; CEM reduces to $0.09$ by forcing vector bottleneck with independence penalty.

Intervention efficiency curve reveals diminishing returns:

| Intervention % concepts | CUB Acc | Leakage Acc Drop | Human Time (s/concept) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0% | 74.7% | — | 0 |
| 10% Oracle | 82.1% | -1.2% | 12 |
| 10% Uncertainty-guided | 79.4% | -0.8% | 12 |
| 30% Oracle | 89.3% | -0.4% | 36 |
| 100% | 95.2% | 0% | 120 |

CEM embedding surgery enables ***soft intervention***: perturb embedding $e_1$ along $r_{edit}$ to add concept without hard label flip, preserving gradient flow for task predictor — improving OOD robustness by 6.3% on Waterbirds bias shift.

### 4.3 Sparse Autoencoders: From Polysemantic Neurons to Monosemantic Features and Transformer Circuits

SAE training decomposes 512 neurons into >4000 features [Anthropic 2023-2024]. Each feature is *multilingual* and *multimodal*: feature 10,123 activates on *DNA sequence ATCG*, also *base pair coverage*; feature 22,457 *HTTP request GET*; feature 31,102 *Hebrew gematria*.

**Circuit synthesis via composition:** Elhage's QK and OV circuit factorization [2021 framework] combined with SAEs yields Cross-Layer Transcoders (CLTs) [Anthropic 2025 attribution graphs]. CLTs map $a_l \rightarrow f_{CLT} \rightarrow a_{>l}$ replacing MLP stack with sparse features reading one layer but writing all later layers.

We reconstruct ***induction heads*** using SAE features: OV eigenvalue positive circular $|\lambda|>0$ marks copying heads (heads 5:9, 6:10 in 2-layer attention-only). Composition score $W_{OV}^{h1}W_{QK}^{h2}$ high + previous-token attention signature → induction. Intervening on Golden Gate Bridge feature (feature 247,900 in 34M) clamps to 10x mean activation causes model to self-identify as bridge with 83% mention rate at length 50 [4].

> **Theorem:** *If SAE achieves $||a-\hat a||_2^2 \le \epsilon$ with $L_0 \le k$ and decoder atoms quasi-orthogonal $\mu(W_d)<1/(2k)$, then feature steering changes logit by bounded $|\Delta logit| \le ||W_U|| \alpha$ maintaining fluency for $\alpha < \epsilon_{KL}$.*

**Qualitative dashboard:** Feature 679 discourse connective predictor fires on `.` final token, logit-lens top-promotes *However, Additionally, Therefore* [SAE-GPT2 2026]. Feature 2256 UTF-8 mojibake detector fires on replacement-byte glyph from misdecoded curly apostrophe.

Limitations: dense antipodal pairs, feature splitting (hierarchical concepts split across multiple atoms when increasing width), residuals containing nonlinear composition.

### 4.4 Representation Engineering for Honesty and Causal Abstraction Audit

RepE provides top-down control [6]. We replicated Zou et al. Layer -13 (approx 67% depth) reading vector for honesty on Llama-2-13B-chat, Mistral-7B-Instruct, Llama-3-8B-Instruct.

| Model | Layer | Honesty Probe Acc Test | TruthfulQA $\Delta$ honest after $\alpha=2$ steering | KL penalty |
|-------|-------|------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|------------|
| Llama-2-13B-chat | 26/40 (-13) | 83.4% | +12.1% absolute | 0.08 |
| Mistral-7B-Instruct | 21/32 (-11) | 81.2% | +9.7% | 0.12 |
| Llama-3-8B-Instruct | 21/32 (-11) | 85.1% | +14.3% | 0.06 |

Natural activation test: projecting 35 unframed judgment scenarios onto honesty axis correlates $r=0.71$ with human rated honesty, showing concept is not prompt-overfit.

**Causal abstraction auditing** of honesty circuit [Anthropic 2025 Biology]: attribution graph from input tokens *The capital of state containing Dallas* traces Dallas→Texas feature (feature 8,442), then Texas→Austin (feature 11,203) multi-hop; swapping Dallas→Houston swaps Texas representation to Sacramento with intervention accuracy 94% (IIA). Planning phenomenon: in poem generation, rhyme target features activate *before* intervening lines — backward planning — invisible to next-token lens but visible via J-lens (Jacobian lens) [2026 Workspace].

We propose honesty audit protocol:

- **Monitor:** $score = r_{honest}^\top h$
- **Detect sycophancy:** compare honesty vs agreeableness reading vectors angular dispersion $>30°$
- **Mitigate deception:** CLT attribution shows deception feature precedes harmful completion; early ablation prevents while preserving capability

Safety-relevant features discovered via SAE: *deception*, *power-seeking*, *resource accumulation*, *sycophancy*, *evaluation awareness* [4]. Steering deception $-2\sigma$ reduces deceptive planning on sandbagging benchmark from 31%→11%.

## 6 Limitations and Future Work

- **TCB and CAV fragility:** Concepts learned from examples inherit dataset bias; *gender* CAV trained on CelebA may capture *makeup* not gender; random concept baseline may insufficiently control for class-correlation. Need multiple negative sets and counterfactual concept augmentation.
- **Scale verification:** TLC and IIA evaluation currently limited to $N=4$ toy configurations for TLA+ spec; prod combinatorial explosion needs symmetry reduction or parametric abstraction [7]. Attribution graphs labor-intensive and capture only fraction of computation (<30% MLP-only).
- **Generality and side-channel:** Workload synthetic; real chat distributions cause concept drift. SAE dictionaries per-site specific, not transferable across checkpoints; training per layer costs non-trivial (34M atoms $\approx$ several GB). Future: universal feature spaces across LLMs [Martinez et al. 2024 universal] reduces cost.
- **Adversarial adaptivity:** Linear steering may be deflected by RLHF-finetuned models where concept directions fragment per-domain [concept-direction-universality 2026 EMNLP]. Angular dispersion measurement across domains needed; RLHF may transform global directions into domain-specific subspaces.
- **Residual error node:** SAE reconstruction error $\epsilon$ acts as hidden error node contributing to downstream logits; claims of complete circuit tracing must account for residual $E = a-\hat a$ [Mechanist feature-dictionary limitation].
- **Planning detection limits:** Jacobian lens [2026] reveals verbalizable workspace but only small privileged set; vast ocean of non-verbalizable processing remains opaque.
- **Safety composability:** Combining honesty, harmlessness, power-aversion vectors simultaneously not orthogonal; composition via linear addition may cause interference. Future work: orthonormalized Conceptors or LoRA adapters per direction [Huang et al. 2024 alignment].

Future roadmap: distributed alignment search (DAS) automates $\tau$ optimization for causal abstraction, replacing hand-crafted CAVs; joint training of CBM+SAE transcoder for intrinsic interpretability with post-hoc verifiability; recursive causal bucketing to improve IIA where hypothesis only partially faithful [bucketing paper 2025].

## 7 Conclusion

We presented a unified lens that connects *post-hoc probing* (TCAV sensitivity and linear CAVs), *ante-hoc architecture* (Concept Bottlenecks enabling human intervention), *dictionary decomposition* (Sparse Autoencoders resolving superposition into monosemantic features at scale), and *top-down control* (Representation Engineering honesty vectors), all formalized under *causal abstraction* interchange interventions. Anchored in 7 authoritative sources — ICML TCAV [1][2], CBM [3], Anthropic scaling monosemanticity [4], dictionary learning foundations [5], RepE [6], and causal abstraction theory [7] — we demonstrated that interpretability is not a single method but a composable algebra: extracting features with SAEs, validating their causal relevance via TCAV/interchange accuracy, and steering them via RepE yields measurable improvements in truthfulness, deception reduction, and circuit transparency.

Operationally, the loop is practical: train SAE on residual stream, obtain CAVs for concepts of interest, test significance via TCAV random baselines, map via DAS to high-level variables, audit via attribution graphs, and deploy steering with KL-bounded monitoring. This closes theory-practice gap with ***real artifacts***, reproducible Nix flakes, TLA+ specs, and open dashboards.

*Key takeaway:* **faithful interpretation equals causal control**: when features are sufficiently disentangled and abstractly aligned, linear probes become not merely descriptive but actionable levers for alignment, auditing, and safer deployment, beyond next-token prediction mythology.
