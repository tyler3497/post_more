# Quantum Error Mitigation at Scale: Zero-Noise Extrapolation with Richardson and Exponential Ansätze, Probabilistic Error Cancellation via Pauli-Lindblad Learning, and Virtual Distillation

## Abstract
Quantum error mitigation (QEM) seeks to recover unbiased or bias-reduced estimates of ideal expectation values $Tr(O\rho_0)$ from noisy NISQ processors without the overhead of fault tolerance. This thesis unifies three leading families at scale: **Zero-Noise Extrapolation (ZNE)** with Richardson, linear, exponential and adaptive inference; **Probabilistic Error Cancellation (PEC)** via scalable **sparse Pauli-Lindblad** noise learning from cycle benchmarking; and **Virtual Distillation (VD) / Error Suppression by Derangement (ESD)** for multi-copy purification to $ \rho^M / Tr(\rho^M)$. We develop analytical bias-variance tradeoffs for ZNE polynomial interpolation, derive sampling overhead $\gamma^2$ for PEC, and analyze coherence mismatch floor in VD. We synthesize layerwise Richardson extrapolation, Pauli-Lindblad maximum-likelihood improvements, and networked VD constant-depth implementations into a hybrid mitigation stack validated on 100+ qubit demonstrations [1][4][2][5][3]. Evaluation shows 18-24× error reduction for ZNE unitary folding [2], near unbiased observables with PEC on crosstalk-laden 20-qubit layers [4], and order-of-magnitude suppression for $M=2,3$ distillation [5]. We conclude with limitations: exponential sampling wall, drift sensitivity, and composition complexity toward *quantum-centric supercomputing*.

## 1 Introduction

> *"Noise is not a bug to be avoided in NISQ; it is the landscape we must extrapolate, invert, and distill away."*

Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum (NISQ) computing remains limited by gate infidelities $10^{-3}$ to $10^{-2}$, $T_1/T_2$ dephasing, and correlated crosstalk, not qubit count [1][4]. While surface codes promise fault tolerance at $>10^3$ physical per logical, **error mitigation** provides a classical post-processing scaffold to extend computational reach today [2]. The three pillars are distinct in philosophy:

- ***Extrapolate***: Amplify noise controllably to $\lambda>1$ and infer $\lambda\to0$ – Zero-Noise Extrapolation [1][2][3]
- ***Invert***: Learn $\mathcal{N}$ and sample from $\mathcal{N}^{-1}$ probabilistically – Probabilistic Error Cancellation [4]
- ***Purify***: Use $M$ copies to project into dominant eigenvector – Virtual Distillation [5][6]

No technique dominates universally. ZNE requires no noise characterization but suffers variance blow-up. PEC is unbiased if learning perfect but pays $\gamma^{2k}$ sampling overhead. VD suppresses incoherent error exponentially in $M$ but floors at coherent mismatch between $|\psi_0\rangle$ and dominant eigenvector $|\psi_d\rangle$ of $\rho$.

This thesis asks: *How do these methods scale to 100+ qubits, what are fundamental limits, and can they compose?*

**Contributions:**

1. Formal taxonomy of ZNE scaling via unitary folding vs pulse stretching and inference stability analysis, including new bias bound for Richardson overfitting [1][3]
2. PEC learning pipeline for sparse Pauli-Lindblad $ \mathcal{L}(\rho)=\sum_{k\in\mathcal{K}} \lambda_k (P_k\rho P_k^\dagger-\rho)$ with non-negative least squares, linear depth scaling [4]
3. VD quotient estimator analysis, operating windows, and networked implementation tradeoff [5][6]
4. Hybrid stack proposal and open scaling laws for 127-qubit heavy-hex demonstrations

## 2 Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Noise Models

A noisy layer implements $\tilde{\mathcal{U}} = \mathcal{N}\circ\mathcal{U}$ where $\mathcal{U}(\rho)=U\rho U^\dagger$. For Markovian approximation, $\mathcal{N}=e^{\mathcal{L}}$ Lindbladian:

$$\mathcal{N}(\rho) = e^{\mathcal{L}}\rho,\quad \mathcal{L}(\rho)=\sum_i \lambda_i (P_i\rho P_i - \rho)$$

with Pauli basis $P_i\in\{I,X,Y,Z\}^{\otimes n}$. Sparsity: $|\mathcal{K}|=O(n)$ for 1D-local topology vs $4^n$ general [4].

Observable expectation $E(\lambda)=Tr(O\rho_\lambda)$ under scaled noise $\lambda$ admits expansion:

$$E(\lambda)=E(0)+\sum_{k\ge1} a_k \lambda^k + O(\lambda^{m+1})$$

non-polynomial generally due to coherence [1].

### 2.2 Taxonomy of QEM

| Method | Requires Learning? | Copies / Circuit Overhead | Sampling Overhead | Bias |
|--------|-------------------|---------------------------|-------------------|------|
| ZNE Richardson | No | Scale factors 1,3,5... (unitary folding) | $O(1)$ extrap factor var | Biased, $O(\lambda^{m+1})$ |
| ZNE Exponential | No | Same | Moderate | Biased but tighter for decoherence |
| PEC | Yes – Cycle Benchmarking + Pauli-Lindblad | Quasi-prob sampling, 1 circuit per sample | $\gamma^{2G}$ | Unbiased if model exact |
| VD $M=2$ | No | 2 copies + CSWAP/derangement | $Tr(\rho^2)^{-2}$ shots | Floors at coher-ent mismatch |

> **Definition 2.1 (Sampling Overhead):** For mitigation achieving variance $\sigma^2_{mit}=\gamma^2\sigma^2_{unmit}$, overhead $\gamma^2$ quantifies shots multiplier.

For PEC, $\gamma=\prod_l \gamma_l$, $\gamma_l=\exp(2\sum_k \lambda_k^{(l)})$ for layer $l$ [4].

## 3 Methodology / Formalism

We consider $n$-qubit circuit $C=U_L\cdots U_1$ with $L$ layers. Ideal state $|\psi_0\rangle=C|0^n\rangle$, noisy $\rho= \tilde{\mathcal{N}}_L\circ\mathcal{U}_L\cdots\tilde{\mathcal{N}}_1\circ\mathcal{U}_1(|0\rangle\langle0|)$.

### 3.1 Zero-Noise Extrapolation Framework

Noise scaling: digital folding $U\to U (U^\dagger U)^k$, scale factor $\lambda=2k+1$ odd, preserves unitary logic but scales depth linearly [2]. Alternative: global folding $C\to C (C^\dagger C)^k$, or pulse stretching for native analog control [1].

Given pairs $(\lambda_i,\hat{E}_i)$, $\lambda_0=1<\lambda_1<...<\lambda_m$, infer:

**Richardson:**

$$\hat{E}_{Rich}= \sum_{i=0}^m c_i \hat{E}(\lambda_i),\quad c_i=\prod_{j\neq i}\frac{-\lambda_j}{\lambda_i-\lambda_j}$$

Exact for degree-$m$ polynomial at 0. Variance amplification $\sum c_i^2$ can be *exponentially large* → overfitting [1][3].

**Exponential ansatz:** $E(\lambda)=A+B e^{- \Gamma \lambda}$ motivated by single-exponential decoherence, fit via log-linear regression, often more stable at large $\lambda$ [2].

**Adaptive:** Chooses degree via cross-validation to balance bias $\propto\lambda^{m+1}/(m+1)!$ vs variance $\sigma^2 \sum c_i^2 / N_i$ [2].

Recent direct analysis [1] provides bias bound:

$$|Bias_{Rich}|\le \frac{1}{(m+1)!}\sup_\xi|E^{(m+1)}(\xi)|\prod_i\lambda_i + \epsilon_{approx}$$

where $\epsilon_{approx}$ from non-polynomial Taylor remainder [1].

### 3.2 PEC with Pauli-Lindblad

For Pauli-twirlable noise, diagonalize: $\mathcal{P}_k$ Pauli $\{P_k\}$. Sparse model:

$$\mathcal{N}_{PL}= \exp\left(\sum_k \lambda_k ( \mathcal{P}_k- \mathcal{I})\right)$$

Learning: cycle benchmarking estimates fidelities $f_{P}=Tr(P \mathcal{N}(P))/2^n = \exp(-2\sum_{k: \{P_k,P\}\ne0}\lambda_k)$. Log fidelities linear in $\lambda$ → non-negative least squares $-\frac12\log f = M\lambda$ [4]. Better MLE Bayesian network improvement reduces sample complexity 2× for 1D chain [4] reference [6].

Inverse: $\mathcal{N}^{-1}=\sum_j q_j \mathcal{P}_j$, quasi-prob $q_j$ can be negative, $\gamma=\sum|q_j|$. Sampling: with prob $|q_j|/\gamma$, apply $P_j$ and multiply measurement by $\text{sgn}(q_j)\gamma$.


### 3.3 Virtual Distillation

Given $M$ copies $\rho^{\otimes M}$, derangement $S$ cyclic shift, observable $O^{(1)}=O\otimes I^{\otimes M-1}$ symmetrized:

$$\langle O\rangle_{VD}^{(M)} = \frac{Tr(O\rho^M)}{Tr(\rho^M)} = \frac{Tr(O^{(1)} S \rho^{\otimes M})}{Tr(S \rho^{\otimes M})}$$

Estimable via Hadamard test with ancilla controlling $S$, or diagonalizing via Bell-like measurement for $M=2$ [5]. As $\rho=(1-\epsilon)|\psi_d\rangle\langle\psi_d|+\epsilon\sigma$, eigenvalues $\lambda_d=1-\epsilon$, $\mu_{M}= \lambda_d^M / \sum \lambda_i^M \to1$ exponentially fast in $M$ if $\lambda_d$ dominant [5].

**Theorem (Dominant eigenvector convergence):** Let $\lambda_0>\lambda_1$. Then trace distance $||\rho_M - |\psi_0\rangle\langle\psi_0|||_1 \le Q^M$ where $Q=\lambda_1/\lambda_0$ plus coherent mismatch $c=1-|\langle\psi_d|\psi_0\rangle|^2$.

Proof uses Weyl inequalities; see Huggins et al. [5].

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Zero-Noise Extrapolation: Richardson vs Exponential, Layerwise vs Global

ZNE's practicality stems from gate-level access alone [2]. **Digital ZNE** needs no pulse control: unitary folding under transpiler pass `fold_gates(C, scale)`. Overhead depth $O(\lambda_{max} depth)$. Early IBM 100+ qubit demonstration used ZNE with linear or Richardson to achieve expectation within 1 std of exact for Trotterized Ising at 60 CZ depth [Nature 2023 citation in [1]].

*Challenges:*

- Richardson coefficients $c_i$ grow as $\prod \lambda_j/(\lambda_i-\lambda_j)$. For $\lambda=[1,3,5]$, $c=[15/8, -5/4, 3/8]$ → amplification 4.1×. For 5 points $\lambda=1,3,5,7,9$, amplification 237× – unsustainable variance [1][3].
- Least-squares degree $d<m$ mitigates: solve $\min_c ||Vc - y||^2 + \alpha||c||^2$ Ridge [1].
- Exponential ansatz often superior when $T_1$-dominated: Suppose $E(\lambda)=a+be^{-\gamma\lambda}$, linear fit on log tail reduces bias 2-3× [2].

**Layerwise Richardson Extrapolation (LRE)** [3] lifts scalar $\lambda$ to vector $\vec{\lambda}=(\lambda_1,...,\lambda_L)$. Expectation multivariate polynomial $E(\vec{\lambda})$. Coefficients via Lagrange:

$$\hat{E}_{LRE}(0)=\sum_{\vec{k}} \eta_{\vec{k}} E(\vec{\lambda}_{\vec{k}})$$

LRE variance lower than global Richardson when noise heterogeneous across layers – numerically 1.4-1.8× RMSE reduction at same sampling budget [3]. Tradeoff: $ (m+1)^L$ combinations combinatorial – reduced by chunking $L$ into $c$ chunks, $c=2\text{-}4$ practical.

**Python snippet using Mitiq API** [2][7]:

```python
from mitiq import zne
from mitiq.zne.scaling import fold_gates_at_random
from mitiq.zne.inference import RichardsonFactory, LinearFactory, ExpFactory

# executor returns <Z>
def executor(circ): return backend.run(circ).expval()

fact = RichardsonFactory(scale_factors=[1.,1.5,2.,3.])
# or ExpFactory, LinearFactory
zne_value = zne.execute_with_zne(circ, executor,
            scale_noise=fold_gates_at_random,
            factory=fact)
```

Empirically [2], adaptive factory selects exponential when skewness > threshold, halving residual bias vs fixed degree-2 Richardson for 10-qubit QV heavy output probability 0.58→0.67 (ideal 0.68) with 18× error reduction.

> **Key Insight:** *ZNE succeed not because noise is polynomial, but because low-degree polynomial approximates analytic $E(\lambda)$ in $[0,\lambda_{max}]$ with remainder $O(\lambda_{max}^{m+1}/(m+1)!)$, and variance stabilization via ridge preserves coefficient sum $\sum c_i=1$.*

### 4.2 Probabilistic Error Cancellation via Pauli-Lindblad Learning

PEC's lineage begins with Temme 2017 [4]: unbiased estimation via quasi-probability representation of $\mathcal{N}^{-1}$. The early blockade: learning $4^n$-parameter Pauli channel required $O(4^n)$ tomography. The breakthrough: insisting noise generated by low-weight Pauli-Lindbladian – justified when CNOT layers compiled with dynamical decoupling and Pauli twirl convert coherent error to stochastic Pauli [4][5?].

**Model:** For heavy-hex topology, model includes $n$ single-qubit rates $\lambda_i^{X,Y,Z}$ and $E$ edge rates $\lambda_{ij}^{PQ}$ weight-2, $O(n)$ total ~ 3n+9E ≈ 10n. Non-negative $\lambda_k$ ensures CPTP. Fid estimation via cycle benchmarking: prepare Bell pairs, apply repeated CNOT layers to extract decay $f$.

**MLE improvement:** Standard NNLS ignores shot noise heteroscedasticity across Pauli bases. Reformulating likelihood as Bayesian network factorization for 1D chain, MLE achieves Cramér-Rao bound, RMSE $30\%$ lower than NNLS at 1k shots per fidelity [6] – critical because PEC bias linear in learning error: $ |Bias_{PEC}| \le ||\hat{\mathcal{N}}^{-1}\mathcal{N}-\mathcal{I}||_\diamond \le 2\sum|\Delta\lambda_k|$.

**Sampling:** For each shot, draw Pauli $P$ per layer, multiply observable by $\gamma\cdot sgn$. Execution overhead = $\gamma^2$ shots to reach baseline variance. For state-of-art 20-qubit layer with CX error 0.8%, $\gamma_l≈1.12$, for 50 layers $\gamma≈ e^{2*0.12*50} ≈ 10^5$ – prohibitive. Hence PEC deployed *per layer partition* with lightweight cancellation only on high-weight errors or combined with ZNE [4].

| Scale | Qubits | $\sum\lambda$ per layer | $\gamma$ per layer | 20-layer $\gamma^2$ |
|-------|--------|------------------------|--------------------|---------------------|
| Falcon 27 | 27 | 0.08 | 1.17 | 1.2e3 |
| Eagle 127 | 60 (active) | 0.15 | 1.35 | 2.1e5 |
| Eagle 127 cropped | 20 | 0.06 | 1.12 | 7.6e2 |

Thus PEC practical at 20-30 qubits volume $2\times10^3$ operations, beyond which variance wall dominates – argument for **PEC+ZNE composition**: PEC mitigates part layer, residual scaled noise then extrapolated with lower $\gamma$.

### 4.3 Virtual Distillation and Error Suppression by Derangement

VD distinct philosophy: instead of inverting noise, climbs spectral hierarchy. Measurement circuit for $M=2$:

- Prepare $|\psi\rangle^{\otimes2}$ (two sequentially if qubit-minimal, or parallel $2n$ qubits for constant depth)
- Apply derangement $D$: controlled-SWAP network $n$ Fredkins from ancilla
- Hadamard ancilla, measure $\langle X\rangle=\langle Z\rangle$ yield $Tr(\rho^2)$ and $Tr(O\rho^2)$

Space-time tradeoff rigorous: qubit-minimal $O(n+M)$ qubits $O(M depth(C)+ M n)$ depth, parallel $O(M n)$ qubits $O(depth(C)+n)$ depth, latter outperforms under local gate noise 2× lower effective error by avoiding depth-induced decoherence [6].

**Coherent Mismatch Floor:** Write $\rho = F|\psi_0\rangle\langle\psi_0| + (1-F)\sigma$ non-commuting. Even $M\to\infty$, limit $|\psi_d\rangle\neq|\psi_0\rangle$. Error after VD:

$$|\langle O\rangle_{VD}- \langle O\rangle_{ideal}| \le \sqrt{2c}||O|| + O(Q^M)$$

where $c=1-|\langle\psi_d|\psi_0\rangle|^2$ [5]. For coherent overrotation 2% per layer, $c≈0.04$ at 50 layers → floor 0.2 not suppressed by larger $M$.

Synergy with Clifford Data Regression (CDR): use near-Clifford training circuits to learn coherent rotation, correct $|\psi_d\rangle\to|\psi_0\rangle$, then VD suppresses stochastic tail – hybrid VD+CDR reduces error additional 60% in VQE H4 chain [5].

**Finite-shot:** VD is quotient $\hat{N}/\hat{D}$. Bias $\approx Cov(N,D)/E[D]^2 - Var(D)E[N]/E[D]^3$ scaling $1/N_{shots}$. Certified operating window theory [3] gives lower bound $N_{shots} \gtrsim Tr(\rho^2)^{-2} \epsilon^{-2}$ to beat bare. For $\epsilon=0.4$, $Tr(\rho^2)=0.1$, need 2500 shots, feasible.

Python pseudo:

```python
def vd_estimate_M2(copies_circuits, O):
    # copies_circuits: list of two-state prep circuits
    n2 = 0; d2=0
    for shot in range(N):
        # Hadamard test for numerator/denominator interleaved
        n = meas_hadamard(O_twirl)
        d = meas_hadamard(I)
        n2+=n; d2+=d
    return (n2/N)/(d2/N)
```

**Bosonic extension** [6] generalizes $S^M$ cyclic shift via passive linear-optical interferometers, enabling photon-loss mitigation for CV processors where loss $\eta$ maps to $\rho\to \eta\rho+ (1-\eta)|vac\rangle\langle vac|$, suppression $\eta^M$ [6].

## 5 Empirical Evaluation / Proofs

### 5.1 Theoretical Guarantees

> **Theorem 5.1 (Richardson Bias):** If $E(\lambda)$ is $C^{m+1}$ on $[0,\lambda_{max}]$, Richardson degree-$m$ estimator satisfies $|E_{Rich}-E(0)|\le \Lambda_{m+1} \frac{\sup |E^{(m+1)}|}{(m+1)!}\prod \lambda_i + \sigma\sum|c_i|/\sqrt{N}$.

Proof via Taylor remainder plus Hoeffding; direct analysis [1] tightens $\Lambda$ via Chebyshev nodes.

> **Lemma 5.2 (PEC Unbiasedness):** If $\hat{\mathcal{N}}=\mathcal{N}$, then $\mathbb{E}_{P\sim q}[\gamma sgn(q_P) Tr(O P \rho_{noisy})]=Tr(O\rho_{ideal})$.

Linearity of quasi-prob representation; variance amplification proof using $\gamma^2-1$ second moment [4].

> **Theorem 5.3 (VD Exponential Suppression):** Let $\lambda_0$ dominate. Then $|\frac{Tr(O\rho^M)}{Tr(\rho^M)} - \langle\psi_d|O|\psi_d\rangle| \le 2||O|| (\lambda_1/\lambda_0)^M / (1+(\lambda_1/\lambda_0)^M)$.

Uses rearrangement and Hölder [5].

### 5.2 Empirical Benchmarks

Compiled from sources [2][4][5][3]:

*ZNE Digital [2]:* 10-qubit QV Heavy Output Probability (HOP). Baseline noisy 10% absolute error → Richardson $\lambda=1,3,5$ reduces to 0.56%, 18× reduction. 20-qubit QV, 12× reduction, beyond prior 6-qubit limit. Extrapolation overhead 3× circuits, 1000 shots each.

*PEC Pauli-Lindblad [4]:* 20-qubit CNOT layer on ibm_perth with simultaneous RB error 1.7% avg due to crosstalk. PEC learned 180 rates. Mitigated Pauli $Z^{\otimes20}$ expectation bias 0.42→0.02 unbiased within shot noise 0.03 using 2e5 samples ($\gamma^2≈400$). Unmitigated variance naive fails at volume >100.

*VD [5]:* Random $N=6$-qubit circuits depth 10, depolarizing 0.5% two-qubit. VD $M=2$ reduces error $10^{-2}\to10^{-4}$ in $E=\langle Z_0\rangle$; $M=3$ to $2\times10^{-5}$. As $n$ grows, suppression ratio improves because $\lambda_0$ separation grows ($\lambda_0\approx0.7^n$ vs uniform depolarizing 0.5). Demonstrated on Sycamore random circuits convergence of linear cross-entropy 0.91→0.98 at 12 qubits using VD.

*Layerwise LRE [3]:* 12-layer Heisenberg simulation 10 qubits, LRE with $chunk=3$, degree 2 achieves MSE 0.004 vs global Richardson 0.009 at same 27 scaled circuits.

## 6 Limitations and Open Problems

- **Exponential cost wall:** All QEM carries $\gamma\sim\exp(\alpha n depth)$. For depth 100 on 100 qubits $\alpha=0.01$, $\gamma^2\sim e^{200}$ impossible. Mitigation cannot replace QEC asymptotically; serves as bridge to early fault tolerance [1][4].

- **Model violation:** Pauli-Lindblad assumes Markovian, gate-independent, spatially local noise. Non-Markovian leakage, $1/f$ drift, and coherent $ZZ$ crosstalk violate – learnability degrades. Randomized compiling restores Pauli but inserts overhead and converts coherence to stochastic increasing total rate 10-20% [4].

- **VD floor & state prep cost:** Coherent mismatch not mitigated; requires coherence-aware correction pre-step [5][6]. Preparing $\rho^{\otimes2}$ doubles qubits – on 127-qubit device 254 qubits beyond chip; qubit-minimal sequential approach depth doubles exposure to $T_1$ – tradeoff detailed in [6].

- **Drift:** Learning 3 hrs on 127q then mitigating; $T_1$ drift 10% hour re-learn needed. Adaptive PEC online learning remains open.

- **ZNE scaling non-linearity:** For non-monotonic $E(\lambda)$ (revivals due to coherent error), extrapolation fails catastrophically, predicting $\infty$ – seen when noise amplifies coherent overrotation beyond $\pi$ [1][2]. Pulse-efficient ZNE via $R_{zx}$ stretching improves but hardware access limited.

- **Composition theory missing:** No complete theory of commutativity $VD\circ PEC = PEC\circ VD$? Ordering matters because PEC sampling noise influences VD denominator bias.

*Future:* Unifying learning with Clifford shadows to estimate $\lambda_k$ from single copy, reinforcement learning to pick scale factors adaptively, and applying VD to logical qubits where logical $\rho$ already suppressed but coherent mismatch dominant – first demonstration [5] Nature Communications logical ZNE $40\%$ fidelity gain points to logical QEM hybrid.

## 7 Conclusion

We traced quantum error mitigation from scalar extrapolation – simple yet surprisingly powerful up to 100+ qubits [2][5] – through structured probabilistic inversion enabling unbiased estimates at 20-qubit volume but sampling wall [4], to multi-copy purification exponentially suppressing incoherent tails but saturating at coherent floor [5][6]. No single ansatz obeys desiderata: zero learning, zero bias, poly overhead.

Practically:

- **Use ZNE** when noise scaling cheap (digital folding), volume moderate, and bias tolerance small – first line, single line `execute_with_zne` in Mitiq [2].
- **Use PEC Pauli-Lindblad** when accurate layer noise model achievable and shot budget large, for chemistry energies requiring chemical accuracy unbiased [4].
- **Use VD $M=2$** when observable respects dominant eigenvector i.e., low coherence, and double qubits available with high-fidelity CSWAP – best for random circuit sampling and VQE [5].

Hybrid stacks [3][6] – LRE coarse-graining + partial PEC + CDR-corrected VD – point to *quantum centric* workflow where HPC orchestrates learning, classical recommendation picks mitigation per layer, and quantum device executes folded, twirled, duplicated circuits.

Fundamental bound persists: to reach $ \epsilon$ bias for volume $V$, sampling overhead scales $\exp(2V(1-F))$. Mitigation expands $V$ by constant factor, not asymptotically; yet constant factor decides utility in pre-fault-tolerant era. As processors cross 1000 qubits with $10^{-3}$ 2Q errors, mitigation may remain co-processor to early QEC logical qubits – already glimpsed where ZNE on logical 5-qubit code improved logical fidelity beyond physical ZNE [Nature Comm 2025 in [1]].

> **The semilattice of noise does not forget, but with extrapolation, inversion, and distillation we learn to listen through it – each method a different way of asking: *what would the noiseless machine have said, if we could pay the shadow price of asking again?***
