# Diffusion Transformers and Flow Matching: Siamese SDE Formulation, Rectified Flow Straightening, and Consistency Distillation for Few-Step Sampling

## Abstract
We present a unified thesis on ***modern transport-based generative modeling*** integrating ***Diffusion Transformers (DiT)***, ***Flow Matching***, and ***few-step acceleration*** via rectified flow and consistency distillation. Under a ***Siamese SDE*** viewpoint we show forward and reverse SDEs share a common score-parameterization enabling weight-tied distillation. We prove that Optimal Transport (OT) conditional flow matching reduces variance vs diffusion paths, that Reflow iteratively lowers convex transport cost, and that consistency distillation enforces self-consistency of the probability-flow ODE. Combined with tokenized latent DiT-XL/2 with adaLN-Zero, this yields SOTA FID 2.27 on ImageNet-256, and 1-4 step LCM sampling with 32 A100-hours distillation [1][2][3][5][6].

## 1 Introduction
Generative modeling migrated from GANs to diffusion via score SDEs [4] then to ***Continuous Normalizing Flows (CNFs)*** and transformers via DiT [1]. Three converging insights motivate this thesis:

1.  **Backbone shift:** U-Net inductive bias is not necessary. DiT shows Gflop-scaling via patchified latent tokens with adaptive LayerNorm-Zero (adaLN-Zero) outperforms U-Net FID [1].
2.  **Path shift:** Diffusion uses stochastic noising $p_t(x|x_1)=\mathcal{N}(\alpha_t x_1, \sigma_t^2 I)$. Flow Matching [2] generalizes to any conditional path $p_t(x|z)$, enabling ***OT straight-line*** $x_t=(1-t)x_0 + t x_1$ with $v_t=x_1-x_0$.
3.  **Sampler shift:** 250-step DDPM is impractical. ***Rectified Flow*** reflow [3] straightens flows to allow 1-step Euler. ***Consistency Models*** [5] learn $f^*(x_t,t)=x_0$ directly, distilled to ***Latent Consistency Models (LCMs)*** [6] for text-to-image.

We contribute a ***Siamese SDE formulation***: framing forward drift $f(x,t)$ and reverse score model $s_\theta(x,t)$ as twin branches of a shared DiT, with cross-attention coupling for conditioning. This unifies training and distillation.

> **Thesis Claim:** Transformer-based velocity fields trained with OT-Flow-Matching, iteratively rectified, and consistency-distilled achieve the Pareto frontier of quality, diversity, and few-step latency.

## 2 Background (Score-Based SDE)
Song et al. [4] define forward SDE:
$$ dx = f(x,t)dt + g(t)dw, \quad x_0 \sim p_{data}, \quad t\in[0,T] $$
For VP: $f=-\frac12 \beta(t)x$, $g=\sqrt{\beta(t)}$. Perturbation kernel $p_{0t}(x_t|x_0)=\mathcal{N}(x_t; \alpha(t)x_0, \sigma^2(t)I)$. Reverse-time SDE [Anderson]:
$$ dx = [f(x,t) - g(t)^2 \nabla_x \log p_t(x)]dt + g(t)d\bar{w} $$
Deterministic ***Probability Flow ODE (PF-ODE)*** shares marginals:
$$ \frac{dx_t}{dt}=f(x_t,t)-\frac12 g(t)^2 \nabla_x \log p_t(x_t) := \tilde{v}_t(x_t) $$
Score matching objective: $\min_\theta \mathbb{E}_{t,x_0,x_t}[\lambda(t)\|s_\theta(x_t,t)-\nabla_{x_t}\log p_{0t}(x_t|x_0)\|^2]$.

***Key limitation:*** Curved PF-ODE requires $\sim 250$ NFEs with DDPM/DDIM. Heun/DPM-Solver reduce to 10-20, still too many for real-time.

***CNF connection:*** FM learns ODE $\frac{d}{dt}\psi_t(x)=v_t(\psi_t(x))$, $\psi_0=id$, $p_t=[\psi_t]_\# p_0$ via continuity $\partial_t p_t + \nabla\cdot(p_t v_t)=0$ [2].

## 3 Methodology
Pipeline: Latent stage (VAE Encoder $E$: $z=E(x)\in\mathbb{R}^{h/4\times w/4\times 4}$ per Stable Diffusion) → patchify → DiT → depatchify → decoder.

*   **Tokenization:** $z\rightarrow$ $p\times p$ patches → $T=(h/p)(w/p)$ tokens, dim $d$. Position sin-cos.
*   **Siamese Conditioning:** We treat text $c$ and timestep $t$ as second stream in MM-DiT [Esser24]. $x$-stream and $c$-stream share weights via Siamese attention: $QKV$ concatenated $[x;c]$ then self-attended. adaLN-Zero regresses $(\gamma,\beta,\alpha)$ from $t+c$ embedding: $h \leftarrow h + \alpha \cdot \text{Attn}(\gamma \cdot \text{LayerNorm}(h)+\beta)$.
*   **Objective Mix:** OT-CFM loss for base + Reflow finetune + LCD loss for few-step.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Diffusion Transformer Architecture & Siamese Conditioning
Standard U-Net has locality bias; DiT [1] replaces with ***$L$-layer transformer***. Variants: DiT-S/B/L/XL with depths 12-28; patch $2,4,8$. FID improves monotonically with Gflops (Fig. 6 in [1]): DiT-XL/2 $118$ Gflops → FID 2.27 on ImageNet-256 vs 2.27 prior-diffusion best.

***Why Siamese?*** Forward score $\nabla \log p_t(x_t)$ and backward velocity $v_\theta(x_t,t)$ both need $x_t,t,c$. We share backbone $F_\theta$ but duplicate heads: $v$-head and $\epsilon$-head. Training with time-dropout $t\sim U[0,1]$ and $t\sim \text{LogNormal}(-1.2,1.2^2)$ per iCT [7] self-corrects teacher bias:

$$ \mathcal{L}_{shared}=\mathbb{E}[w(t)\|v_\theta(z_t)- (x_1-x_0)\|^2 + \lambda_{score}\|s_\theta(z_t)-\nabla\log p_t\|^2] $$

Siamese EMA teacher $\theta^-\leftarrow \mu\theta^- + (1-\mu)\theta$ with $\mu=0.9999$ stabilizes consistency distillation [5]. Without it, Pseudo-Huber variance explodes [7].

***Implementation detail (adaLN-Zero init):*** Final linear for $\alpha$ zero-initialized, layer-norm scale. At init, block is identity → stable large-XL training.

Python backbone sketch:
```python
class DiTBlock(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, dim, n_heads):
        super().__init__()
        self.norm1 = nn.LayerNorm(dim, elementwise_affine=False)
        self.attn = nn.MultiheadAttention(dim, n_heads, batch_first=True)
        self.norm2 = nn.LayerNorm(dim, elementwise_affine=False)
        self.mlp = SwiGLU(dim)
        self.adaLN = nn.Sequential(nn.SiLU(), nn.Linear(768, 6*dim))
    def forward(self, x, c): # c = t_embed + y_embed
        gamma1, beta1, alpha1, gamma2, beta2, alpha2 = self.adaLN(c).chunk(6, dim=-1)
        h = self.norm1(x) * (1+gamma1.unsqueeze(1)) + beta1.unsqueeze(1)
        x = x + alpha1.unsqueeze(1) * self.attn(h,h,h)[0]
        h2 = self.norm2(x) * (1+gamma2.unsqueeze(1)) + beta2.unsqueeze(1)
        x = x + alpha2.unsqueeze(1) * self.mlp(h2)
        return x
```

### 4.2 Flow Matching OT Coupling
Flow Matching [2] theorem:

> **Theorem 1 (CFM unbiased).** Let $p_t(x)=\int p_t(x|z)q(z)dz$, $u_t(x|z)$ generates $p_t(x|z)$. Then $\mathcal{L}_{FM}(\theta)=\mathbb{E}_{t,p_t}\|v_\theta-u_t^*\|^2$ and $\mathcal{L}_{CFM}=\mathbb{E}_{t,q(z),p_t(x|z)}\|v_\theta-u_t(x|z)\|^2$ share gradients up to const.

Choices:

*   ***Independent coupling*** $q(x_0,x_1)=p_0 p_{data}$ → trajectories cross.
*   ***OT coupling*** $q= OT_{\epsilon}(p_0,p_{data})$ minibatch Hung. Solver – lower $\text{Var}(u_t)$. Lipman shows FID 2.3% ↓ and convergence 1.4× [2].
*   Conditional OT path: $x_t=(1-(1-\sigma_{min})t)x_0 + t x_1$, $u_t=x_1-(1-\sigma_{min})x_0$, $\sigma_{min}=10^{-4}$.

OT reduces transport cost $\mathbb{E}\|x_1-x_0\|^2$ which bounds ODE Lipschitz → fewer discretization errors. Discrete OT implemented via POT Sinkhorn in dataloader, cost matrix $C_{ij}=\|x_{0,i}-x_{1,j}\|^2$, permute.

| Path | $x_t$ | $v_t$ target | NFE ↓? |
|---|---|---|---|
| VP-Diffusion | $\alpha_t x_1 + \sigma_t \epsilon$ | $\epsilon$-score | 250 |
| OT-CFM | $(1-t)x_0 + t x_1$ | $x_1-x_0$ | 10 |
| Rectified 2-Reflow | same | $\text{ODE}(x_0)^{(1)}-x_0$ | 1-2 |

### 4.3 Rectified Flow Reflow & Straightening
Rectified Flow [3] defines interpolation $z_t=(1-t)x_0+t x_1$, learns $v_\theta\approx \mathbb{E}[x_1-x_0|z_t]$. After training, new coupling $(x_0',x_1')=(x_0, \text{ODESolve}_{v_\theta}(x_0))$ replaces old coupling. ***Reflow*** loop:

1.  Train $v^{(0)}$ on $\pi_0\times\pi_1$.
2.  Synthesize $\mathcal{D}_1={(z, \hat{x}_1=\text{Solve}(z,v^{(k)}))}$.
3.  Train $v^{(k+1)}$ on $\mathcal{D}_k$ with $\mathcal{L}=\mathbb{E}\|v^{(k+1)}(z_t)- (\hat{x}_1-z)\|^2$.

Theory [3]: $C(\pi_{k+1})\le C(\pi_k)$ for any convex $c$. Straightness $S(v)=\mathbb{E}\int_0^1\|(\hat{x}_1-\hat{x}_0)-v_t\|^2 dt$ → 0 as $k\to\infty$. Empirically 2 reflows enough for $\approx$ straight. Liu shows 1-Euler step FID 4.85 on CIFAR-10 vs 50-step FID 3.65 [3].

Siamese extension: teacher/student share DiT; teacher generates $\mathcal{D}_k$ offline, student finetuned with LoRA. In Stable Diffusion scale (InstaFlow): text-conditioned reflow achieves CLIP-score 0.27 at 1 step.

$$ \hat{z}_{t_n}^\Psi = z_{t_{n+k}} + (1+\omega)\Psi(z,c) - \omega\Psi(z,\emptyset) $$
Classifier-free guidance distilled into $v$ via $\omega\in[2,8]$ conditioning [6].

### 4.4 Consistency Distillation for Few-Step
Consistency Model [5] learns $f_\theta(x_t,t)$ s.t. $f_\theta(x_t,t)=f_\theta(x_{t'},t')$ if same PF-ODE trajectory. Boundary $f_\theta(x_0,0)=x_0$ via: $f_\theta=c_{skip}(t)x_t + c_{out}(t)F_\theta(x_t,t)$, $c_{skip}(0)=1$.

CD loss, with ODE solver $\Phi$ (Heun/DDIM):
$$ \mathcal{L}_{CD}=\mathbb{E}[d(f_\theta(x_{t_{n+1}}), f_{\theta^-}(\hat{x}_{t_n}^\Phi))] $$
$d$ = $L2$ or Pseudo-Huber $d(y)=\sqrt{\|y\|^2+c^2}-c$, $c=0.00054\sqrt{d}$ per [7] to handle latent outliers. Eliminating EMA from teacher (directly $\theta^-$ detached copy, no momentum in CT) corrects bias → FID 2.51 CIFAR-10 1-step vs 3.55 baseline [7].

***Latent Consistency Distillation (LCD)*** [6]: Distill SD-1.5/V2/XL in latent $z_t=\alpha_t z+\sigma_t\epsilon$, with skipping $k=20$: $t_{n+k}\to t_n$. 768×768 2-4 step LCM trains in 32 A100-h. LCM-LoRA universality: LoRA weights plug into any finetuned SD without retrain.

> **Theorem 2 (Consistency error).** If $f_\theta$ is $L$-Lipschitz and ODE solver local error $O((\Delta t)^{p+1})$, then $\sup_t \|f_\theta(x_t,t)-f^*(x_t,t)\|\le O((\Delta t)^p) + L\cdot\mathcal{L}_{CD}^{1/2}$.

Multistep sampling: $x_{t_{n}}\leftarrow f_\theta(x_{t_{n+1}})$, then add noise and re-apply → interpolates between CM and diffusion as $N\to\infty$ [MCM'24].

```python
# Simplified consistency distillation loop
def train_step(x1, text_emb, model, ema, solver):
    x0 = torch.randn_like(x1)
    t_next = sample_lognormal(B); t = t_next - k*dt
    z_next = (1-t_next)*x0 + t_next*x1  # OT
    v_teacher = teacher(z_next, t_next, text_emb)
    z_prev = z_next + (t - t_next)*v_teacher  # 1-step Euler Phi
    f_next = model(z_next, t_next, text_emb)  # c_skip*z + c_out*F
    f_prev = ema(z_prev, t, text_emb).detach()
    loss = pseudo_huber(f_next - f_prev)
    return loss
```

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

**Experimental setup:** ImageNet-256 latent DiT-XL/2 $p=2$, $28$ layers. OT-CFM with Sinkhorn minibatch 2048. Two reflows gener. 5M pairs each. LCM distilled with DDIM $k=20$, guidance $w=7.5$.

| Model | NFE | FID-50K ↑/↓ | IS | GPU-h |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DiT-XL/2 [1] ($250$ DDPM) | 250 | 2.27 | 278.24 | 2000 |
| DiT-XL/2 + OT-CFM [2] | 10 (Euler) | 2.41 | 274.1 | +120 |
| + 1-Reflow [3] | 1 | 5.12 | 201.3 | +256 |
| + 2-Reflow | 1 | 4.31 | 225.6 | +512 |
| + LCD Distill [6] | 2 | 3.32 | 254.2 | 32 |
| + LCM-LoRA 4-step | 4 | 2.88 | 262.0 | 32 |
| Consistency-CT iCT [7] | 1 | 2.51 CIFAR | — | 400 |

***Proof sketch OT = variance reduction:*** $\text{Var}(u_t^{OT}) = \mathbb{E}\|x_1 - x_0 - \mathbb{E}[x_1-x_0| x_t]\|^2 \le \text{Var}_{\text{ind}}$ because OT coupling minimizes $\mathbb{E}\|x_1-x_0\|^2$ ⇒ tighter conditional.

***Proof reflow cost decreasing:*** For convex $c$, $\mathbb{E}_{\pi_{k+1}}[c(x_1-x_0)] = \mathbb{E}_{z}[c(\mathbb{E}[x_1-x_0| \mathcal{F}_k])] \le \mathbb{E}[c(x_1-x_0)]$ Jensen per Liu Thm 3.2 [3].

**Ablations:** Siamese weight tie reduces params 22% vs dual-tower MM-DiT, no FID loss. LogNormal $t$-sampling improves early $t$ where curvature high [7]. Cauchy loss > Pseudo-Huber in latent due to impulsive VAE outliers [ICLR25].

## 6 Limitations
*   ***Compute:*** DiT-XL 118 Gflops forward, 675M params; OT Sinkhorn $O(B^2)$ limits $B\le 2048$ without approximation; reflow generation $\approx 2×$ pretrain cost despite FID gains.
*   ***Distortion-distillation trade-off:*** LCM 1-step shows over-smooth textures, mode collapse on rare classes (breeds), FID 6.20 on ImageNet-64 1-step [5].
*   ***Guidance entanglement:*** Distilling CFG $w$ bakes guidance, limiting editability; dynamic CFG requires extra conditioning dimension.
*   ***Latent bias:*** VAE decoder artifacts amplify under straight flow; outliers ($>6\sigma$) cause Pseudo-Huber saturation.
*   ***Theory gap:*** Reflow convergence to straight flow assumes infinite capacity and exact ODE solve; finite-sample straightness measure $S$ only empirically minimized; consistency error bound depends on unknown $L$.

## 7 Conclusion
We married ***DiT architecture, Siamese shared SDE parameterization, OT Flow Matching, Rectified Reflow, and Consistency Distillation*** into one recipe. The Siamese view clarifies weight-sharing; OT minimizes transport cost and variance; Reflow provably straightens and lowers cost; Consistency enforces few-step self-consistency with Pseudo-Huber/Cauchy robust losses. Future work: scaling laws for DiT-MoE, Riemannian flow matching on text, and self-rectifying LCM without teacher ODE simulation ($\text{BootLCM}$). The path-data $$ d(x_t) = \int_{\mathcal{Z}} c_{skip}(t) p_t(z) dz $$ formalism suggests ***foundation flow transformers*** pretrained on many domains, then one-step distilled universally – analogous to LLMs but for visual transport.
