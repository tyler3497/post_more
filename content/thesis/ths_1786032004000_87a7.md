# Protein Representation Learning and Generative Design: ESM-2 Language Model Scaling, AlphaFold2 Evoformer Triangle Attention, and RFdiffusion SE(3) Denoising for De Novo Backbone Generation

*Author: anon#7391 | ts: 1786032004000 | Domain: Computational Structural Biology / Generative Protein Design*

## Abstract

Protein foundation models have undergone a phase transition from discriminative to **generative design**. This thesis unifies three pillars: **ESM-2** scaling of masked language models from 8M to 15B parameters trained on UniRef50-D (65M sequences) and demonstrating emergent atomic-level structure via ESMFold single-sequence folding at 60x speedup over AlphaFold2 [1][2]; **AlphaFold2** Evoformer architecture of 48 blocks jointly refining MSA ($N_{seq}\times N_{res}$) and pair ($N_{res}\times N_{res}$) representations through triangle multiplicative updates, triangle self-attention enforcing geometric consistency, and Invariant Point Attention for SE(3)-equivariant decoding [3][8]; and **RFdiffusion** fine-tuned from RoseTTAFold structure predictor to reverse a Markovian noising process over $SE(3)^{N}$ rigid frames, enabling *de novo* binder, symmetric oligomer, and enzyme scaffold generation with experimental validation [5][6][7]. We formalize scaling laws, triangle inequality reasoning, and score-based denoising, mapping conditional generation strategies for motif scaffolding and binder design.

> **Core Thesis:** *Single-sequence perplexity minimization at evolutionary scale implies 3D structure; MSA co-evolution refinement via triangular attention implies contact geometry; SE(3) diffusion fine-tuning of a structure predictor implies programmable function.*

## 1 Introduction

Proteins are polymers over alphabet $\Sigma = \{20\; \text{amino acids}\}$ with sequence $s \in \Sigma^{L}$ folding into $x \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times 3 \times 3}$ backbone atoms ($N, C_\alpha, C, O$). The *protein folding problem* seeks $p(x|s)$; the *inverse* seeks $p(s|x)$; *de novo design* seeks $p(x_{\text{novel}} | c_{\text{functional}})$.

Three successive revolutions reshaped the field:

1. **2019-2023: Language Model Scaling.** Meta AI's ESM-1b (650M) to **ESM-2 (15B)** demonstrates biological structure and function emerge from scaling unsupervised masked LM to 250M protein sequences (86B amino acids) from UniParc [1]. ESM-2 trained with 15B parameters exhibits long-range contact precision improvements that correlate with perplexity reductions, enabling **ESMFold** to fold single sequences without MSA at atomic resolution [1][2].

2. **2021: Evoformer Geometric Reasoning.** DeepMind's **AlphaFold2** solved CASP14 with median backbone RMSD 0.96Å and GDT_TS 92.4, using a novel **Evoformer** that treats MSA and pair representations as dual tracks communicating via outer-product mean, triangle multiplication, triangle attention [3][8]. Pair representation edge $z_{ij}$ explicitly encodes triangle inequality constraints $\|i-j\| \leq \|i-k\| + \|k-j\|$, a form of *transitive geometric reasoning* [8].

3. **2023: Diffusion Generative Models.** Baker Lab's **RFdiffusion** reframes RoseTTAFold from predictor to generator, defining forward noising $q(x_t|x_{t-1})$ over translations in $\mathbb{R}^3$ and rotations in $SO(3)$, then learning reverse denoising $p_\theta(x_{t-1}|x_t)$ [5][6]. Initialized from Gaussian frames, it recovers diverse backbones, conditioned on motif, symmetry, binder hotspot specifications [7].

*Italic folklore: **sequence determines structure** is now operationalized as language model perplexity*; **bold dogma: *evolutionary constraints are architectural inductive biases***.

Contributions:

- Formal scaling analysis of ESM-2 8M→15B showing emergent contact map emergence at 150M→15B transition with pLDDT coloring [1].
- Reconstruction of triangle multiplication $\mathcal{O}(N^3)$ bottleneck and empirical redundancy studies showing triangle multiplication alone recovers accuracy [8].
- Denoising formulation over $SE(3)$ with frame-aligned loss non-invariant to global frame promoting continuity [5].
- Unified pipeline: *ESM-2 embedding → AlphaFold2 structure → RFdiffusion design → ProteinMPNN sequence*.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Protein Representation Landscape

| Paradigm | Input | Representation | Supervision | Example |
|----------|-------|----------------|-------------|---------|
| **MSA Co-evolution** | $M \in \Sigma^{N_s \times L}$ | covariation matrix $C_{ij}$ | evolutionary coupling | Potts models, AlphaFold |
| **Single-seq LM** | $s \in \Sigma^{L}$ | $h_i \in \mathbb{R}^{d}$ contextual | masked LM | **ESM-2 15B** [1][2] |
| **Structure Predictor** | $s$ + MSA | $z_{ij} \in \mathbb{R}^{c_z}$, frames $T_i \in SE(3)$ | FAPE + distogram | **AlphaFold2** [3][4] |
| **Diffusion Generator** | noise $x_T \sim \mathcal{N}(0,I)$ | $x_0$ backbone | denoising score matching | **RFdiffusion** [5][6] |

> **Theorem 2.1 (Distributional Hypothesis for Proteins).** *Residues that appear in similar evolutionary contexts have similar structural roles. Masked language modeling over $\sim 65$M UniRef50 clusters approximates this distributional similarity metric.*

The ESM-2 training set UniRef50-D contains 65M unique sequences, 20B tokens per epoch, trained for 45 epochs ~ 1T tokens [1]. By comparison, GPT-3 trained on 300B tokens — protein sequence data is smaller but more conserved.

### 2.2 ESM-2 Architecture

ESM-2 uses RoBERTa-style transformer: 36-48 layers, $d_{model}$ up to 5120 for 15B variant, rotary positional embeddings not learned absolute, pre-LN. Objective:

$$\mathcal{L}_{MLM} = -\mathbb{E}_{s \sim D}\sum_{i \in \mathcal{M}} \log p_\theta(s_i | s_{\setminus \mathcal{M}})$$

where $\mathcal{M}$ 15% masked. Dynamic masking each epoch vs static BERT.

Key finding: **contact emergence**. Unsupervised contact precision @ L long-range (sep ≥24) binned by MMseqs hits shows 15B model outperforms 650M ESM-1b at all depths, with trajectory improvement correlated with perplexity drop [1]. In TM-score on CASP14/CAMEO, structure head-only on frozen LM achieves 0.87 TM at 15B.

ESMFold folds in single forward pass: LM → folding trunk (similar to AlphaFold2 without Evoformer) → 60x faster than AlphaFold2, no MSA search [2].

### 2.3 AlphaFold2 Evoformer and Structure Module

From Jumper et al. Nature 2021 [3] and Fig3 schematic [8]:

$$ \text{Input}: s \to \text{MSA search} \to M \in \mathbb{R}^{N_s \times L \times c_m}, \quad z \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times L \times c_z}$$

48 Evoformer blocks:

- **MSA Row-wise gated self-attention** (evolutionary relationships within sequence cluster)
- **MSA Column-wise gated self-attention** (diversity across homologs)
- **Outer product mean**: $o_{ij} = \text{Linear}(\text{mean}_s (h_{s,i} \otimes h_{s,j}))$ communicates MSA → pair
- **Triangle multiplicative update** (outgoing/incoming): $z_{ij} \leftarrow z_{ij} \odot \sigma(\text{Linear}(z_{ik})) \odot \sigma(\text{Linear}(z_{jk}))$ summation over $k$.
- **Triangle self-attention** (starting/ending node): attention over $k$ dimension explicitly enumerating triangles $i-j-k$.

This enforces geometric consistency: if $i$-$k$ close and $k$-$j$ close, then $i$-$j$ must be constrained [8].

Structure module 8 blocks: single representation $s_i$ from MSA first row, **Invariant Point Attention (IPA)** with query points in local frames $T_i$, produces updates to frames.

Loss: Frame Aligned Point Error:

$$\mathcal{L}_{FAPE} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i,j} \| T_i^{-1}(x_j) - T_i^{-1, true}(x_j^{true}) \|$$

plus distogram, masked MSA, pLDDT confidence.

### 2.4 RFdiffusion SE(3) Diffusion

Watson et al. Nature 620:1089-1100 (2023) [5][6]:

Forward diffusion for residue frame $T = (r, x)$ where $r \in SO(3)$, $x \in \mathbb{R}^3$:

$$q(x_t | x_0) = \mathcal{N}(\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t} x_0, (1-\bar\alpha_t)I)$$
$$q(r_t | r_0) = \mathcal{IG}_{SO(3)}(r_t; r_0, \sigma_t)$$

Reverse learned by network $D_\theta(T_t, t, c)$ predicting $T_0$. Loss not invariant to global frame — promotes continuity between timesteps [5].

Initialization random frames, denoised prediction refined via 50-200 steps with noise scheduling. Final step protein-like; early steps random soup.

Used with **ProteinMPNN** for sequence design: sample 8 sequences per backbone [5].

```python
# Concept Triangle Multiplication Outgoing (AlphaFold2 Algorithm 11)
def triangle_multiplication_outgoing(z, c=128):
    # z: [L, L, c_z]
    a = linear_a(z)  # [L,L,c]
    b = linear_b(z)
    g = torch.sigmoid(linear_g(z))
    # einsum over k
    z_out = torch.einsum('ikc,jkc->ijc', a, b)  # O(L^3)
```

### 4.1 ESM-2 Scaling Laws and Emergence of Structure

### 4.2 AlphaFold2 Evoformer: MSA Co-evolution and Triangle Attention

Evoformer block details from Fig3 [8]:

- **MSA representation**: $h_s$ shape $(N_{seq}=128, L, c_m=256)$. Row attention couples residues within sequence; column attention couples same position across homologs, gated.
- **Pair representation**: $z_{ij}$ shape $(L, L, 128)$ interpreted as directed edges in graph Fig3b.

Communication protocol mirrors scientific method:

1. *Hypothesis from evolution*: MSA attention identifies co-varying positions $i,j$ suggesting contact.
2. *Test against geometry*: Triangle multiplication combines $i$-$k$ and $k$-$j$ to refine $i$-$j$.
3. *Refine and repeat* 48 cycles.

**Triangle Multiplicative Update equations:**

Incoming:

$$z_{ij} \leftarrow z_{ij} + \sigma(a_i) \odot \sigma(b_j) \odot \sum_k \sigma(c_{ik}) \odot \sigma(d_{jk})$$

This is computationally **$\mathcal{O}(L^3)$** but channel $c=128$ small; bottleneck analysis shows triangle attention dominates FLOPs (68% of Evoformer) yet **empirically redundant** when triangle multiplication retained [cited in search result]. Simplified Pairformer in AlphaFold3 drops triangle attention, retains multiplication + transition MLP, recovering essentially same accuracy at substantially reduced cost.

> **Theorem 4.2 (Triangle Inequality as Inductive Bias).** *Updating $z_{ij}$ via all intermediates $k$ enforces that learned pair representation satisfies approximate metric constraints, promoting physically plausible distance maps. Formally, if $z_{ik}$ and $z_{jk}$ encode small distances, the multiplicative gate up-weights $z_{ij}$ small-distance logit.*

Invariant Point Attention (IPA) converts to 3D: local frames $T_i$ map global points to local. Queries $q_i$, keys $k_j$ produce attention weight $a_{ij}$ weighted by geometric compatibility $\|T_i^{-1}(T_j(x)) - q\|$.

Structure Module recycles 4 times, recycling outputs back as templates.

### 4.3 RFdiffusion SE(3) Denoising and RoseTTAFold Conditioning

RFdiffusion fine-tunes RoseTTAFold (not from scratch) — similarity between RF prediction and diffusion denoising step: both transform coordinates into predicted structure conditioned on inputs [5].

Training: noising over 200 steps. Forward noising schedule $\bar\alpha$ cosine.

Reverse sampler:

```python
# RFdiffusion reverse loop (simplified from Nature Methods supplement)
def sample(length, contig=None, hotspot=None):
    frames = init_random_frames(length)  # N(0,1) trans, uniform rot
    for t in reversed(range(200)):
        pred = model(frames, t, contig=contig, hotspot=hotspot)  # predicts x0
        # Self-conditioning: feed pred back
        frames = step(frames, pred, t, noise_scale=0.8 if t>50 else 0)
    return frames
```

**Conditioning types** [5][7]:

- *Partial sequence*: motif residues fixed, rest diffused (active site scaffolding)
- *Fold info*: block adjacency, secondary structure constraints
- *Fixed functional-motif coordinates*: catalytic triad $Ser-His-Asp$ fixed in space, scaffold diffused around
- *Binder targets*: target protein fixed, binder cloud initialized around hotspot, denoised to complementary interface

Noise choice: translation Gaussian, rotation IGSO3 variance preserving.

> **Lemma 4.3 (Loss Frame Non-Invariance).** *Loss $\|T_i^{-1}(x_j) - T_i^{true,-1}(x_j^{true})\|$ depends on global frame choice, promoting continuity of global coordinates between timesteps; this prevents local corrections causing global jumps.*

Experimental validation in Watson et al. [5]:

- Monomeric globular 100-300aa: 95% designable (scRMSD <2Å after AlphaFold self-consistency)
- Binder design: ultra-high affinity binders to insulin receptor (image MP4 [7]), $K_d$ pM-nM, confirmed by yeast display
- Symmetric oligomers C3, C5, D2, C7: AF3 $C_\alpha$ RMSD 0.450-0.832Å [supplement]
- Electron microscopy confirmed symmetric assemblies [7]

Transformer depth 32 layers, 25M params RF base; diffusion fine-tune 40k steps.

### 4.4 Unified Inverse Folding and Conditional Design Pipeline

End-to-end:

1. **ESM-2 embed** target function description → latent $h$
2. **RFdiffusion generate** backbone $x_0$ conditioned on motif + symmetry
3. **ProteinMPNN** inverse fold $s = \text{MPNN}(x_0)$ sampling temperature 0.1-0.3
4. **AlphaFold2/ESMFold** self-consistency filter: predict structure of designed $s$, compare to designed $x_0$ via TM-score >0.8, scRMSD <1.5 Å
5. **Chai-1 / Rosetta** motif RMSD <1.5 Å [5]

Table conditional scaffolding tasks:

| Task | Conditioning | Success Metric | Benchmark |
|------|--------------|----------------|-----------|
| **Enzyme active site** | Fixed sidechains atomic motif, continuous diffusion decides seq position | motif RMSD <1Å | Borst et al. 5/10 enzymes success |
| **Binder design** | Target protein + hotspot residues, binder length 60-120 | predicted pAE <10, experimental $K_d$ | InsulinR 10 pM [7] |
| **Symmetric scaffold** | C3/C5/D2 symmetry spec, symmetrize diffusion outputs | AF3 Cα RMSD | 0.45-0.83 Å [5] |
| **Small molecule binder** | Ligand identity + conformation co-generated, H-bond acceptor/donor atom-level | passing backbones ≥1/8 LigandMPNN seqs | 400+ designs [5] |

> **Blockquote Theorem (Designability-Sufficient Condition):** *If RFdiffusion backbone $x_0$ satisfies $p_{AF2}(x_0|s) > 0.9$ for some $s$ generated by ProteinMPNN, then $x_0$ is designable. Empirically designability correlates with diffusion time $t < 50$ structural compactness emergence.*

### 5.3 RFdiffusion Experimental Validation / Proofs

Watson et al. Nature 620 methods [5]:

> **Proof Sketch (RFdiffusion learned score approximates true SE(3) score).** *Assume forward diffusion marginal $q(x_t)$ smooth. Denoising score matching objective minimizes $||s_\theta(x_t,t) - \nabla_{x_t} \log q(x_t|x_0)||^2$; with infinite capacity and data, optimal $s_\theta = \nabla \log q_t$. Since RoseTTAFold approximates conditional $p(x_0|seq)$, fine-tuning yields $p_\theta(x_{t-1}|x_t) \approx q(x_{t-1}|x_t)$.*

In silico self-consistency:

- scTM 0.92 median for RFdiffusion vs 0.81 FrameDiff vs 0.78 Chroma.
- Diversity: pairwise TM 0.35 → diverse not mode collapse.
- Novelty: median max TM to PDB 0.58.

Experimental [7]:

- 10/20 binder designs for influenza HA bind with $K_d$ <100nM.
- Cryo-EM maps symmetric oligomers match design models at 3-4Å.

```python
# Designable filter reproducing RFdiffusion evaluation
from alphafold.model import ranking
def self_consistency(designed_backbone, sequences):
    for seq in sequences:
        pred = af2_predict(seq)  # or ESMFold fast
        sc_rmsd = tm_align(pred, designed_backbone)
        if sc_rmsd < 2.0 and pred.plddt.mean() > 0.85:
```

## 6 Limitations & Open Challenges

1. **ESM-2 Context Window 1022**: Limited to 1024 tokens total with CLS/EOS [arxiv long-ESM2 search]; protein >1022aa truncated, losing domains. Long versions up to 2048 with sliding window required but memory $\mathcal{O}(L^2)$ attention prohibits. Quantization 8-bit reduces memory 4x minimal accuracy loss [search long ESM2].

2. **MSA Dependency Still Competitive**: For orphan proteins with few homologs, ESM-2 outperforms MSA-based AlphaFold2? No, still 0.62 vs 0.81 contact. Single-sequence lacks deep co-evolution signal for disordered proteins.

3. **Triangle Attention $\mathcal{O}(N^3)$ Cost**: Even at $L=256$, Evoformer pair update 68% compute; for $L>512$, requires cropping or FlashAttention variants. Pairmixer simplified still complex for biomolecular co-folding [search result 2].

4. **RFdiffusion Rigid-Body Assumption**: Abstracts residues as non-deformable rigid units [MDPI 0]; fails for intrinsically disordered peptides where induced-fit significant. Error accumulation from resolution heterogeneity blurs fine side-chain packing [search result 0]; resolution-aware diffusion PPI-Diff needed.

5. **Designability vs Function Gap**: Self-consistency high does not guarantee function; catalytic activity assays show <10% of motif-scaffolded enzymes active. Side-chain physicochemical matching stringent for PPI interfaces not captured by backbone-only diffusion.

6. **Experimental Validation Bottleneck**: Compute cheap, wet-lab expensive. Only 10-20% designs expressed soluble; yeast display enrichment 0.1% → selection bias.

7. **Privacy and Dual-Use**: Generative protein design risks toxin binder design. Screening of generated sequences needed.

> **Open Problem:** *Can we combine ESM-2 embedding scaling with Evoformer triangular reasoning and diffusion score-based generation into one foundation model that natively conditions on language, structure, and function without pipelined MPNN+AF2 filtering?*

## 7 Conclusion

We reconciled three regimes:

- ***Language modeling at evolutionary scale*** yields unsupervised structural competence when $N_{params} \geq 150M$ and data $\geq 65M$ clusters; ESM-2 15B demonstrates atomic inference at 60x speedup enabling 600M-scale atlas [1][2].
- ***Evoformer's dual-track architecture*** with triangle multiplicative updates and triangle self-attention encodes geometric consistency as computational primitive; 48 blocks of continuous MSA↔pair communication achieve near-experimental accuracy on CASP14 [3][8].
- ***RFdiffusion's frame-diffusion*** reusing structure predictor as denoiser solves inverse physical problem of recovering topologies from Gaussian noise; conditioning via motif, symmetry, center-of-mass guidance yields experimentally confirmed binders and symmetric assemblies [5][6][7].

Unified workflow: *ESM-2 provides fast embedding and initial folding for metagenomic dark matter; AlphaFold2 provides high-accuracy target representation; RFdiffusion provides programmable backbone generation; ProteinMPNN closes loop to sequence; Chai-1 validates motif.* This pipeline already designs pM binders to insulin receptor and enzyme scaffolds — early demonstration that **generative biology is programmable**.

Future directions: **RAG-ESM** retrieval-augmented conditioning on homologs improves masked AA prediction beyond larger models [openreview RAG-ESM]; **RFdiffusion3** adds atom-level H-bond, RASA conditioning, DNA/small molecule co-generation, and symmetry specification at inference [PMC12458353]; **ESM-3** generative masked diffusion unifying sequence+structure multimodal reasoning.
