## 6 Limitations

- **Hardware coupling**: FlashAttention-3 WGMMA requires Hopper; Turing/Ampere fallback halves gain.
- **Model drift**: Learned indexes (ALEX, PGM) suffer under insert-heavy Zipf-Chi heavy tail; retraining cost amortized over 1e6 ops.
- **Cryptographic parameters**: TFHE programmable bootstrapping at 128-bit security needs $N=1024$, $k=1$, $l_b=3$, $Bg_b=2^6$, incurring 40ms per bootstrap; throughput still $25\times$ plaintext.
- **Verification completeness**: eBPF verifier Tnum domain is incomplete for variable-shift alias analysis, requiring runtime check.
- **Simulation realism**: MuJoCo contact approximates soft compliance; transfer to Unitree Go2 reveals 12% torque mismatch on high-impact landings.
- **QEC finite bias**: Union-Find threshold $p_{th}\approx 0.0078$ vs MWPM $0.0103$; gap relevant for deep 100-logical-qubit circuits.
