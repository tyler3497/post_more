## 6 Limitations

- **Assumption alpha:** i.i.d. sampling may fail under drift; our robustness tested only up to TV distance 0.12. storage systems flash SSD in production exhibits nonstationarity.
- **Assumption beta:** TLA+ model checks only up to N=4 hosts / 1e5 states; larger scale 10k-node may expose Heisenbugs not captured by small-scope hypothesis. Counterexample: DPOR 6-depth race condition 7-node clique required N=7.
- **Assumption gamma:** Energy measurement via RAPL counters omits DRAM refresh 0.3W; for cryo-CMOS SFQ interconnect 4K operation J/query incomparable.
- **Assumption delta:** Transient exec mitigations rely on microcode 0x219 may regress on future stepping; GhostWrite silicon bug forces feature off, 50 percent perf loss unacceptable for embedded RISC-V LP.
- **External validity:** Graph factorization compression for worst-case optimal joins achieves O(N to the fhtw) optimal enumeration only when indices are hash tries / B-trees; columnar Parquet row-groups not currently supported.
- **Formal gaps:** Coq proofs for Tree Borrows protector rely on Stacked Borrows-to-Tree Borrows refinement still conjecture 7.3 per 2026 POPL; TLA+ WF fairness may hide livelock under asynchronous 70x WAN variance 31 ms to 2190 ms found in Janus benchmark.
