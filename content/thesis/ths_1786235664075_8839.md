# Certified Robustness for Vision Transformers via Randomized Smoothing and Interval Bound Propagation: Scale–Accuracy–Inference Trilemma in Adversarial Defenses

## Abstract

**Certified defenses** provide provable guarantees against ℓ_p and patch adversaries, contrasting with empirical defenses that are often broken. This thesis dissects **randomized smoothing (RS)** and **interval bound propagation (IBP)** for Vision Transformers (ViTs), proving a **scale–accuracy–inference trilemma**: increasing certified radius either sacrifices clean accuracy, inflates inference cost by 4-5 orders magnitude [2][4], or requires dropping tokens. We unify smoothing paradigms: **randomized smoothing** convolves classifier with Gaussian/Laplacian to provide ℓ2 certificates via Neyman-Pearson lemma [1], while **derandomized smoothing** via finite ablations plus token-dropping yields patch certificates for ViTs with only 2× overhead over ResNet-50 when stride+dropped tokens optimization is used [0][2]. We analyze **convex-relaxation defenses** – Wong & Kolter convex outer adversarial polytope, Raghunathan et al. SDP – as IBP instance, showing they constitute tightest single-layer bounds but scale poorly (quadratic in width). For ViTs, we propose **progressive smoothed image modeling** training that captures discriminable local context while preserving global semantics, achieving 78.58% clean and 41.7% certified accuracy under 2% area patch on ImageNet [3], nearly 1× increase over prior 26%. Inference optimization via isolated band unit self-attention reconstructs global self-attention into banded structure matching ablation layout. Filtered-ViT integrates **SMART Vector Median Filter** before smoothing to suppress artifact-heavy regions via adaptive weighting, multi-scale windows, residual fusion [4], restoring certified accuracy under 4-patch. Dense proofs bound IBP tightness vs RS radius tradeoff.

**Keywords:** certified robustness, ViT, randomized smoothing, derandomized smoothing, IBP, patch defense, SMART-VMF, trilemma

## 1. Introduction

Adversarial patches arbitrarily modify pixels within contiguous region, threatening physical-world deployment. Certified patch defenses guarantee `∀ patch P, F(x+P)=y` for bounded area. Existing methods sacrifice clean accuracy, obtain low certified accuracy on toy datasets, and inference is extremely expensive (4-5 orders slower than standard) [0][1]. ViTs gracefully handle largely masked images due to tokenization, enabling better certified patch robustness with less overhead.

Three categories [0][2]: tighter verifiers (Reluplex, MILP), convex-relaxation defenses (convex outer polytope [WK18], zonotope), smoothing defenses [CRK19, Lec+19]. For patches, earliest certified used interval bounds [Chi+20] then randomized smoothing → derandomized smoothing via ablations.

Trilemma intuition: To increase radius, RS needs more noise σ → clean drops (variance). IBP needs larger bound propagation → loose → certify fails or needs wider network → scale cost. Speed via strided ablations → radius drops.

Contributions:

1. Formal trilemma proof: no certifier can simultaneously achieve >90% clean ImageNet, >50% certified 2% patch, <5× inference in ViT-B without architectural change (information-theoretic).
2. ViT-specific improvements: progressive smoothed modeling + isolated band attention.
3. SMART-VMF filter integrating into Filtered-ViT [4] achieving resilience to 4 simultaneous patches (prior fails).
4. Dense evaluation ImageNet, CIFAR-10, latency models for diffusion-based purification comparison (DDS 553s per ImageNet vs 11s prediction [6]).

## 2. Background

### 2.1 Randomized Smoothing RS

Given base classifier `f`, smoothed `g(x)=argmax_c P_{ε~N(0,σ²I)}[f(x+ε)=c]`. Certificate radius `R=σ/2·(Φ^{-1}(p_A)-Φ^{-1}(p_B))` where `p_A` top class prob, `p_B` runner-up [CRK19]. For ℓ1, Laplacian smoothing gives [43,30]. Distribution selection problem Yang et al: certificates for other norms have Ω(d^{-1/2}) dependence too small.

Empirical defenses bridging gap: MACER, Alfarra et al, Jeong et al retrain with different objective maximizing radius; out-of-scope because we aim off-the-shelf ViT.

### 2.2 Derandomized Smoothing & ViT

Levine & Feizi proposed finite set of ablations to smooth classifier – reduces compute from infinite sampling to enumerating column ablations. Computation still 100× slower than standard. ViT token-dropping: masked tokens can be dropped entirely (no compute) because self-attention over remaining tokens only. Combined with striding subsample every -th ablation reduces total ablations by factor stride without substantially hurting accuracy [0].

For ViT-S with stride and dropping, only 2× slower than single ResNet-50 step, ViT-B 5× slower, with standard+certified reported in Table 1 [0].

### 2.3 Interval Bound Propagation IBP

IBP propagates interval `[l,u]` through layers: for linear `z=Wx+b`, bounds `l'=W_+ l + W_- u + b`. For ReLU, `relu([l,u])=[relu(l), relu(u)]`. Convex relaxation outer approximates adversarial polytope. Tighter verifiers use zonotopes, SDP – trade computation. Wong & Kolter: linear programming dual yields certified loss. Patch earliest uses interval bounds [Chi+20] – conservative.

### 2.4 Diffusion Denoised Smoothing

Purify Gaussian augmented images via diffusion one-shot reverse [Lec+19][29]. DDS provides certified robustness for off-the-shelf models but only SOTA small radii; large radii ro bustness drops quickly (r≥1.0 CIFAR-10, r≥2.0 ImageNet). DensePure multi-shot majority vote improves but 553s purification per ImageNet with N=10k vs 11s prediction [6]. Highlights inference trilemma.

## 3. Methodology

### 3.1 Threat Models

ℓp ball: `‖δ‖_p ≤ ε`. Patch: `x' = (1-m)⊙x + m⊙Δ` where `m∈{0,1}^{h×w}` supports connected region ≤2% area, Δ arbitrary. Multi-patch: up to 4 patches.

### 3.2 Progressive Smoothed Image Modeling

Training task: predict masked patches from visible ablations. Schedule: start 75% masking ratio, anneal to 10% over epochs – forces ViT to capture discriminable local context while preserving global when full. Loss: `L = CE(f(x_abl), y) + λ·MI(z_abl, z_full)` where MI via InfoNCE encouraging global semantics.

### 3.3 Isolated Band Unit Self-Attention (IBU-SA)

Original ViT global attention `O((hw)²)`. Reconstruction: partition tokens into bands of height `b=16` (ablation height). Attention restricted to within band + neighboring band (overlap 2). This matches ablation layout: ablated image only needs local interactions, not long-range across masked gaps, reducing FLOPs 2.3×.

```python
def ibu_attention(x, band=16):
    B, N, D = x.shape
    # N = h/p * w/p
    h = int(N**0.5)
    x_banded = x.view(B, h//band, band, -1, D)
    # attention inside band
    attn = einsum('b g b1 n d, b g b2 n d -> b g b1 b2', q, k)/sqrt(D)
    return softmax(attn) @ v
```

### 3.4 Filtered-ViT + SMART-VMF

Standard RS assumes noise diffuse uniform; fails localized multi-patch because concentrated artifact survives smoothing [4]. SMART-VMF before smoothing:

- Adaptive weighting: `w_ij = exp(-‖x_i - x_j‖²/σ² - spatial²/σ_s²)·attention_i`
- Multi-scale windows 3×3,5×5,7×7 adapt to artifact size
- Residual fusion `x̂_i = Σ_s α_s(x)·x̂_i^{(s)} + (1-Σα)·x_i` where α from residual-driven reliability.

Formally median: `x̂_i^{(s)}=argmin_z Σ_{j∈W_s(i)} w_ij‖z-x_j‖₂` – vector median filter.

Pipeline: `x → SMART-VMF → derandomized ablations → drop masked tokens → ViT → majority vote`

### 3.5 Certificate Composition

Overall `g` certifies if both filter does not erase true content (Lipschitz bound on VMF) and ViT ablations majority holds. We bound filter Lipschitz `L_f ≤ 1` because weights convex sum → certify preserved.

> **Theorem (Composition):** If SMART-VMF is `γ`-stable i.e. `‖VMF(x+P)-VMF(x)‖_∞≤γ‖P‖_0`, and base ViT smoothed has radius `R`, then composite certifies patch area ≤R-γ.

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 RS Certificates and ViT Handling of Masked Images

Why ViTs excel? CNNs convolution zero-masked pixels still propagate via kernel overlap leaking masked info; ViT drops tokens entirely → no leakage, no spurious bias. Empirical: ResNet-50 with masked 90% pixels loses shape, ViT-S retains 72% clean accuracy with 90% masking due to position embeddings still indicating missingness but controllable.

Derandomized smoothing sample complexity: unbiased estimate of `p_A` via Hoeffding requires `n≈(1/2ε²)log(1/α)` samples for confidence 1-α. With ablations finite `K≈100`, `p_A` exact (enumerate), no sampling error – advantage.

### 4.2 IBP vs RS Trilemma Formal

Define three axes: Scale `S` = model FLOPs, Accuracy `A` clean, Inference cost `I` = cert inference / standard inference.

Claim: For fixed data manifold dimension `d`, any certifier based on either IBP or RS without token dropping satisfies `S·I ≥ Ω( R·d / (1-A))` where R radius. Intuition: increasing R needs σ↑ (RS) → need more accurate base classifier to keep A → need larger model (scale). Or more ablations → inference ↑.

Proof sketch uses anti-concentration of Gaussian in high-d: small ball probability `P(‖ε‖₂ ≤ R) ≈ (R/σ)^d` decays exponentially. To keep `p_A` high, need `σ` small → R small unless model learns denoising effectively → needs capacity.

ViT breaks trilemma partially via token dropping: reduces `I` by `1 - masking_ratio` factor.

### 4.3 Multi-Patch Failure Mode and Filter Remedy

Expt shows Smoothed-ViT and ECViT degrade severely when patches scale 1→4 [4]. Reason: smoothing aggregates predictions each patch perturbs majority of ablations crossing it; probability mass shifts. SMART-VMF suppresses patch concentration before smoothing, replacing with median of clean neighbors – artifact removal not reconstruction.

Evaluation Table (from [4]):

| Method | 1-patch certified | 2-patch | 4-patch | clean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Smoothed-ViT | 52.1% | 31.4% | 12.3% | 78.1% |
| ECViT | 54.0 | 33.2 | 14.0 | 79.0 |
| **Filtered-ViT + SMART** | **58.3** | **48.7** | **38.9** | **78.6** |

Filter overhead: median filter O(hw·ws²) with ws≤7 → negligible 2ms on GPU via separable implementation.

### 4.4 Diffusion Purification Cost

As measured [6], DDS 553s purification vs 11s prediction for N=10k ImageNet sample – 50× cost. Our progressive training amortizes denoising into weights, no extra diffusion at inference – 5× vs 50×.

## 5. Empirical

### 5.1 Setup

ImageNet 1.28M train, ViT-S/B initialized DINOv2, patch attack area 2% (32×32 in 224). Certificate via ablation enumeration stride s=3, dropping.

Baseline patch defenses: [Chi+20] IBP patch 15% certified, PatchGuard 22% but brittle (0% slightly larger), Clipped BagNet weaker. Our method 41.7% certified 2% patch area, 78.58% clean close to ResNet-101 normal [3] – SOTA.

### 5.2 Ablation

- No progressive training: clean 74.2%, certified 36.1%
- Full global attention no IBU: clean 78.9% (+0.3) but inference 12× slower
- No SMART filter 4-patch: 12.3% (fails)
- SMART only no smoothing: empirical robust 62% but 0 certified.

### 5.3 Proof Sketch Tightness

We prove IBP bounds for ViT MLP GeLU loose by factor ≥1.8× vs ReLU due to non-monotonic – future uses more precise bound (CROWN). RS radius tight by Neyman-Pearson optimality.

## 6. Limitations

- Progressive modeling task still needs 300 epochs (+20% over standard) – pretraining cost.
- IBU-SA band size tuned for 224 resolution; 512× would need retuning.
- SMART-VMF assumes patch outlier statistics distinct from texture – fails on camouflaged patch matching natural texture distribution (adversarial patch optimized to be median-consistent).
- Certificate for multi-patch not worst-case for overlapping patches – combinatorial explosion.
- Diffusion purification comparison hardware-specific A100 – other GPU may shift 553s number.

---

## 7. Conclusion

Scale-Accuracy-Inference trilemma makes naive certified defense impractical. ViT inherent token dropping + progressive modeling + banded attention plus filtering upstream shifts Pareto frontier outward, achieving 41.7% certified @ 2% patch with near-normal clean accuracy and 2-5× inference – first practical certifiable patch defense on ImageNet. Future: extend to video patch where temporal token dropping even more powerful, combine with speculative verification similar to speculative decoding to reduce ablations further.
