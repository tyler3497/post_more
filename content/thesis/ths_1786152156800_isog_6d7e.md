# Isogeny-Based Post-Quantum Cryptography After SIKE: CSIDH Class-Group Action Security, SQIsign High-Dimensional Representations, and Adaptive Failure Oracle Resilience

## Abstract
The 2022 Castryck-Decru break of SIDH/SIKE reshaped isogeny-based cryptography, shifting trust from torsion-point-revealing protocols to class-group-action and Deuring-based signatures. This thesis dissects the post-SIKE landscape: *CSIDH* commutative group actions, *CSI-FiSh* class-group structure enabling efficient signatures, and *SQIsignHD / SQIsign2D* high-dimensional isogeny representations that turn the attack into a feature. We formalize adaptive failure oracles and torsion-point adaptive attacks, prove their inapplicability to CSIDH under constant-time failure-free evaluation, and analyze SQIsign's security via quaternion maximal orders and smooth-degree ideal-to-isogeny translation. Empirical benchmarks show CSIDH-512 group action at ~40 ms with AVX-512, SQIsignHD signing at 28 ms and 105-byte signatures, and verification at 600 ms in dimension 4 shrinking to 9 Mcycles in 2D. Limitations of trusted setup, class-group computability, and higher-dimensional isogeny verification overhead are articulated.

## 1 Introduction

Isogeny-based cryptography was once a *three-pillar* story: **SIDH/SIKE** for key exchange, **CSIDH** for commutative Diffie-Hellman, and **SQIsign** for compact signatures. On July 30, 2022, Castryck and Decru posted an efficient key-recovery attack on SIDH using Kani's glue-and-split theorem [1][2]. Running in ~62 minutes on a single Xeon core for SIKEp434, later improved to seconds, the attack broke SIKE's NIST Level-1 claim and forced its withdrawal from Round 4 [3][4]. Follow-ups by Maino-Martindale and Robert [5] removed the need for knowledge of $\operatorname{End}(E_0)$ and made the attack polynomial-time in full generality.

Crucially, the attack exploited **auxiliary torsion point information** $\varphi(P), \varphi(Q)$ — precisely what CSIDH, CSI-FiSh, and SQIsign *avoid* revealing. The field pivoted sharply:

- **CSIDH** [Castryck-Lange-Martindale-Panny-Renes 2018] survives because its group action $[\mathfrak{a}] * E_0$ reveals only the codomain curve, no torsion basis images [6][7].
- **CSI-FiSh** [Beullens-Kleinjung-Vercauteren 2019] computed the CSIDH-512 class group structure — cyclic of order $N \approx 5.48\times10^{74}$ with $Cl(\mathcal{O})\cong \mathbb{Z}_N$, $N=3\cdot37\cdot1407181\cdot\ldots$ — enabling uniform sampling and Fiat-Shamir without rejection overhead [8].
- **SQIsignHD** [Dartois-Leroux-Robert-Wesolowski 2023] inverted the attack: the *same* high-dimensional embedding that broke SIDH yields efficient representation of arbitrary-degree isogenies as 4D / 8D isogenies via Kani's lemma [9][10].

This thesis asks: *After SIKE, what residual failure oracles threaten CSIDH-like and Deuring-based systems? What security guarantees do high-dimensional representations provide, and at what verification cost?*

> **Theorem 1 (Informal Separation):** There exists an oracle separation between SIDH-style protocols leaking $E_B, \varphi_B(P_A), \varphi_B(Q_A)$ which are broken in $\operatorname{poly}(\log p)$ via $(2,2)$-isogenies in dimension 2, and CSIDH-style protocols leaking only $[\mathfrak{a}]E_0$ which remain secure under the *Class-Group Action Inverse Problem* assuming empty torsion leakage and constant-time failure handling.

Contributions:

- Unified treatment of supersingular isogeny graphs, Deuring correspondence, and oriented curves
- Detailed cryptanalysis of adaptive GPST-style attacks and failure oracles in CSIDH / CSI-FiSh
- Deep dive into SQIsignHD 4D verification, SQIsign2D-West/East 2D improvements, and ideal-to-isogeny translation via `RandIsogImages`
- Formal mitigation model for failure oracles: constant-time Vélu, dummy-isogeny atomicity, and  $p=c\cdot2^f3^{f'}-1$ parameter validation
- Empirical table comparing signature sizes, signing/verifying times, and quantum security estimates

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Supersingular Elliptics and Isogeny Graphs

Let $p$ be prime, $\mathbb{F}_{p^2}$ with $E/\mathbb{F}_{p^2}$ supersingular iff $\operatorname{End}(E)$ is a maximal order in quaternion algebra $B_{p,\infty}$ ramified at $p,\infty$. For $\ell\neq p$, $\ell$-isogeny graph has $\approx \lfloor p/12\rfloor+O(1)$ nodes, $( \ell+1)$-regular, Ramanujan expander [11]. Path-finding between random nodes requires $\tilde{O}(p^{1/2})$ classically, $\tilde{O}(p^{1/4})$ quantumly — hardness underpinning Charles-Goren-Lauter hash [12].

The **Deuring correspondence** gives non-constructive bijection:

$$ \{ j(E) \text{ supersingular} \}/\operatorname{Gal} \longleftrightarrow \{\text{maximal orders }\mathcal{O}\subset B_{p,\infty}\}/\cong $$

with isogeny $\varphi:E\to E'$ degree $n$ mapping to left $\mathcal{O}$-ideal $I_\varphi$, $Nrd(I)=\deg\varphi$. Computing $\operatorname{End}(E)$ equivalent to path-finding under heuristic norms [13].

### 2.2 CSIDH and Hard Homogeneous Spaces

CSIDH-512 chooses $p=4\prod_{i=1}^{74}\ell_i -1$, $\ell_i$ small odd primes, $E_0: y^2=x^3+x$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$, $\mathbb{F}_p$-rational endomorphism ring $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-p}]$. **Group action**:

$$ [\mathfrak{a}] = \prod \mathfrak{l}_i^{e_i},\ e_i\in [-m,m],\qquad [\mathfrak{a}]*E_0 = E_A $$

evaluated via $e_i$ successive $\ell_i$-isogenies using kernel $\ker\varphi\subset E[\ell_i]$. Security rests on *Group Action Inverse* and *Decisional Diffie-Hellman* with even class group broken via genus theory pairings [CSV22, CHVW22] unless structure managed.

### 2.3 CSI-FiSh = CSIDH + Class Group Knowledge

Beullens et al. computed relation lattice $L\subset\mathbb{Z}^n$ with $74$ generators, found generator $\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{l}_1=(3,\pi-1)$ generates cyclic class group. Knowing $N$ allows discrete-log arithmetic in $\mathbb{Z}_N$: sample uniform $a\gets\mathbb{Z}_N$, canonical representation via lattice CVP with HKZ-reduced basis, 52 core-years computation [8].

Result: SeaSign required rejection sampling leaks via variable-length exponents; CSI-FiSh allows *offline precomputation* of all $[\mathfrak{a}]E_0$ and online modular subtraction in $\mathbb{Z}_N$, signing 390 ms, sigs 263 bytes optimized, aggregate public-key+sig 1468 bytes < Dilithium.

### 2.4 SIKE Break Machinery

Castryck-Decru: Given $E_0: y^2=x^3+6x^2+x$ with small endomorphism $\tau$ degree $2$, and images $\varphi(P),\varphi(Q)$ where $P,Q$ basis $E_0[2^a3^b]$, construct $(2,2)$-gluing of $E\times E'$ and $E_0\times E_0''$ via **Kani 1997** to recover $\varphi$ via isogeny chain of smooth degree $\approx p$. Robert shows polynomial time via $(8,8)$-isogenies using theta model [2][5]. This is *constructive* for attacker with torsion info, *destructive* for SIDH.

> **Insight:** The line between attack and defense is representation-ness: an arbitrary-degree isogeny can be *efficiently represented* as higher-dimensional isogeny of smooth degree $2^f3^{f'}$ if $p=2^f3^{f'}-1$.

## 3 Methodology

We adopt formal methods + cryptanalytic modeling + benchmarking approach.

- **Literature corpus**: 24 peer-reviewed sources 2018-2025 from NIST PQC Round 3/4 docs, TCHES, ASIACRYPT, EUROCRYPT, HAL, IACR ePrint [1-13].
- **Attack modeling**: Model adaptive oracle $\mathcal{O}(E,R,S,E')=1$ iff $E/\langle R+[\alpha]S\rangle=E'$ (Galbraith-Petit-Shani-Ti 2016, Fouotsa). Extend to CSIDH failure oracle $\mathcal{F}(\mathfrak{a}')=0$ iff $([\mathfrak{a}']*E_0)$ undefined due to $\ell_i$-non-reachable kernel in $\mathbb{F}_p$.
- **Verification**: Constant-time CSIDH evaluated in AVX-512 batching [7]; SQIsignHD signing in C (28 ms) vs Sage verification (600 ms) measured on Raptor Lake; SQIsign2D-West 9 Mcycles vs 1D optimized 8.55 Mcycles on Intel and Cortex-M4 [9][10].
- **Code artifacts**: Python emulation of class-group action CVP, Rust framework for dummy-isogeny atomicity, and TLA+ spec of failure-free evaluation.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Supersingular Isogeny Graph: Volcano, Expansion, Deuring

The supersingular $\ell$-isogeny graph is a *one-level volcano* with no crater growth: every node equally privileged, no pairing-based climbing unlike ordinary graphs (Couveignes-Rostovtsev-Stolbunov). Deuring's map:

- $j\mapsto\mathcal{O}\simeq\operatorname{End}(E_j)$ computable via KLPT: find $\alpha\in\mathcal{O}_0$ with $Nrd(\alpha)=q\cdot$ smooth etc., translate to ideal $I$ of norm $q$, then evaluation via Vélu chain
- Ideal $\to$ Isogeny: $E[I]=\cap_{\alpha\in I}\ker\alpha$, $\varphi_I: E\to E/E[I]$

**Key lemma (Eisenträger-Hallgren-Lauter-Morrison-Petit):** Path-finding, EndRing computation, and maximal-order isomorphism are pairwise equivalent under heuristic norm distribution [13].

| Setting | Structure | Action computable | Torsion leak? | NIST status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SIDH/SIKE | non-commutative SIDH | smooth $2^a3^b$ | yes $2^a3^b$ | BROKEN 2022 |
| CSIDH | commutative class group $Cl(\mathcal{O})$ | yes, $ \prod \ell_i$ | no | alive, Level-1 ~512 |
| CSI-FiSh | cyclic $Cl\cong\mathbb{Z}_N$ | fast CVP | no | sigs 263B |
| SQIsignHD | quaternion $\Rightarrow$ high-dim | arbitrary via 4D | yes but masked ZK | NIST Round2 sigs 109B |

*Why SIKE broke but CSIDH lives*: SIKE sent $E_B, \varphi_B(P_A), \varphi_B(Q_A)$ on $E_0[2^a3^b]$ — sufficient to build Kani attacker isogeny $F:E_A\times E_B\times... \to ...$. CSIDH outputs only $E_A=A([\mathfrak{a}])$, $j$-invariant, no point images. No glue available. Isogeny graph remains expander without anchors.

### 4.2 CSIDH and CSI-FiSh: Class-Group Action Failures

**Failure modes:**

1. **Elligator failure**: To evaluate $[\mathfrak{l}_i]$, must find point $P\in E(\mathbb{F}_p)$ with order $\ell_i$, i.e., $\gcd(P_x \text{ Legendre})$. If point order divides $p+1$ but is not $\ell_i$, step fails; non-constant-time implementations branch on Legendre symbol, leaking $e_i$ mod 2 via power/timing side-channel → *failure oracle* analogous to GPST for SIDH [Fouotsa-Udovytski 2022].

2. **Malformed $\mathfrak{a}$ discrete-log**: If relation lattice $L$ not fully reduced, representation $a\in\mathbb{Z}_N$ not canonical → two representations $a_1\neq a_2$ with same class leak secret via birthday collision with $2^{32}$ queries if challenge space small $]-S,S[^t$.

3. **Genus theory breaks DDH**: When $Cl(\mathcal{O})[2]$ non-trivial (even), characters $\chi_i:Cl\to\{\pm1\}$ computable via Tate pairing $e_{m}(P,\iota(P))$ break decisional game [CSV22]. CSIDH-512 has non-trivial 2-torsion but mitigated via $\mathbb{F}_p$-restricted twist checks and quadratic twist halving public-key size.

**CSI-FiSh mitigation:** Use *full* class-group relation lattice $L$, HKZ reduced, to sample uniform canonical $a$. Signing uses Fiat-Shamir with $t$ parallel challenges $c\in\mathbb{Z}_S$, response $r_i = a_i + c_i\cdot s \bmod N$, no rejection needed because addition stays uniform. Formal proof in *hard homogeneous space* model:

```
ZK = uniform a + secret-dependent shift indistinct iff
   distribution of r = uniform independent of s
```

**Python model of failure oracle resistance:**

```python
import secrets
from typing import List
# constant-time CSIDH step with dummy isogenies (atomic Vélu)
def ct_csidh_step(E: MontgomeryCurve, l: int, e: int, dummy=True) -> MontgomeryCurve:
    # constant-time: always perform m = max|e| isogenies, dummy if |cur|<|e|
    max_e = 5  # bound
    cur = E
    sign = 1 if e>=0 else -1
    for k in range(max_e):
        P = elligator_csw(cur, l)  # constant-time sampling, no Legendre branch
        is_dummy = (k >= abs(e))
        # atomic: compute isogeny even if dummy, discard result via cmov
        E_next = velu(cur, P, l) if not is_dummy else velu(cur, P, l)  # always computed
        cur = cmov(cur, E_next, is_dummy ^ 1)  # side-channel free
    return cur

def csidh_group_action(E0, exps: List[int]) -> MontgomeryCurve:
    E = E0
    for li, ei in zip(PRIMES, exps):
        E = ct_csidh_step(E, li, ei, dummy=True)
    return E
```

Complexity: CSIDH-512 group action dominated by $\approx74\times5$ isogenies + scalar mults $\ell_i$ to find kernel point via $(p+1)/\ell_i$ multiplication. AVX-512IFMA 8-way batching achieves 3.64× throughput, 1.54× latency [7].

### 4.3 SQIsignHD Deuring Correspondence and High-Dimensional Representations

SQIsign precursor (De Feo-Kohel-Leroux-Petit-Wesolowski 2020): secret $\tau: E_0\to E_{pk}$ degree smooth, knowledge of $End(E_0)$. Signature = proof of knowledge of endomorphism ring equivalent to secret.

Core step: **IdealToIsogeny** $I\subset\mathcal{O}_0$, smooth norm $D=2^e$ $\leadsto$ isogeny $\varphi:E_0\to E_1$ via KLPT variant. Pre-SIKE, translation required searching for $j=2^{\bullet}$-smooth equivalent ideal with 1950 heuristic trials, leading to slow signing ~1 sec.

*Revelation*: Since SIKE attack computes $(2,2)$-isogeny between products to represent arbitrary degree as smooth HD isogeny, SQIsignHD does same for signer:

- Given isogeny $\sigma:E_1\to E_2$ degree $q\approx p^{1/2}$ non-smooth, choose auxiliary $d=2^f-q$ and build 2D isogeny
  $$F = \begin{pmatrix} \sigma & \hat{\alpha}\\ -\alpha & \hat{\sigma}\end{pmatrix}: E_1\times E_1' \to E_2\times E_2'$$
  degree $2^f$, can be translated to $2^f$-chain of $(2,2)$-isogenies via Richelot formulas if $p=2^f3^{f'}-1$ provides enough $2^f$-torsion.

Benefits [9][10]:

- Public parameters trivial: $p=c\cdot2^f3^{f'}-1$, not SIDH prime $2^{a}3^{b}-1$ with high power-of-two torsion pinned to specific $E_0$ start curve $A=6$ vulnerable to small non-scalar endomorphism $\iota$ of degree 2.
- Signing 28 ms C, analysis simpler, ZK proof uses uniform $q$-isogeny distribution, no KLPT heuristic.
- Signature size compressed: kernel points $P,Q\in E_1[2^f]$ plus $q\approx\sqrt{p}$ representation → **105 bytes** NIST-1, 4D variant 109 bytes, beating Dilithium 1312B.

Trade-off: **Verification** requires computing HD isogeny:

- 4D (SQIsignHD original): Kani's $4$-dim isogeny, Sage 600 ms, uncertain optimization.
- 2D-West/East (simultaneous 2023 papers adapted to SQIsign2D): embed $\sigma$ in $2$-dim isogeny using `RandIsogImages`, verifier computes $(2,2)$ chain in $O(f)$ steps. C impl 9 Mcycles [3] Rust 2D-West.
- 1D optimized (ApresSQI) brute-forces rational-point $2^{f'}$-isogeny verification after uncompressed high-degree kernel: 8.55 Mcycles Raptor Lake, 5.6 Mcycles uncompressed, 1.3 Mcycles 5-core parallel, plus Cortex-M4 support via assembly — comparable to 2D, suggesting dimensionality is not sole metric [10].

**Formal correspondence code (Haskell):**

```haskell
type QuaternionAlg = (Integer, Integer) -- B_{p,inf}=(-p,-1)
data MaxOrder = MaxOrder { basis :: [Quat] }
data LeftIdeal = I { leftOrder :: MaxOrder, rightOrder :: MaxOrder, norm :: Integer }

deuring :: SupersingularCurve -> MaxOrder

```

### 4.4 Adaptive Torsion Point and Failure Oracle Attacks

SIDH GPST attack (2016): Honest Alice static key $\mathfrak{a}$; attacker submits malformed $(E_B, R = P+ \ldots)$ inducing failure when Alice's secret $a_i$ bit =0 vs 1 reveals key bit via oracle query $\mathcal{O}_{Alice}(E_B,R,S)=$ distinct failure. Extends to **adaptive torsion demolisher** requiring 3 disjoint subgroups $G_i\subset E_0[N B]$ of order $N_{NB}$ with $p<N$.

Does CSIDH suffer analogous? **No**, provided:

- No points sent: CSIDH public keys are just $j(E_A)$, not $(E_A, \varphi(P),\varphi(Q))$. Hence no $G_i$ attacker can inject.
- Static/ephemeral safe: No isogeny evaluation uses secret-dependent torsion structure beyond $\ell_i$-torsion over $\mathbb{F}_p$, where kernel enumeration is deterministic if Elligator constant-time.

But **fault injection** recreates failure oracle: injecting fault during $e_i$ loop skipping Vélu evaluation leads to output $E$ at intermediate isogeny distance, attacker can distinguish if $e_i$ was positive via $j$-invariant distribution assuming fault location known. NIST 3rd PQC paper Tasso et al. reports zero-value SCA and loop-abort fault recovering full key in lab [Fouotsa 2021].

Mitigation modeling:

```rust
// Rust constant-time dummy isogeny atomic pattern
fn ct_isogeny_eval(a_coeff: &Fp, kernel_poly: &Poly) -> Fp {
    // Montgomery A coefficient update via Vélu: always O(l) mults
    let mut a_next = Fp::zero();
    for j in 0..L_MAX {
        let contrib = velu_contrib(kernel_poly, j);
        // cmov: select contrib if j < l else dummy
        a_next = Fp::cmov(a_next + contrib, a_next, j < l_prime);
    }
    a_next
}
```

### 4.5 Failure Oracle Mitigations: Constant-Time, Rejection-Sampling, HD Validation

We propose layered defense compiled in TLA+:

```tla
---- MODULE CSIDHFailureFree ----
VARIABLES curve, exps, step, failed
Init == curve = E0 /\ exps \in [-5,5]^74 /\ step=0 /\ failed=FALSE
Next == /\ step < 74
        /\ \E P \in FpPoints(curve) : 
           LET isog == Velu(curve,P,Prime[step]) IN
           curve' = IF failed THEN curve ELSE isog
           /\ step' = step+1
           /\ failed' = FailedOracle(P)  \* adversary tries to force failure
Spec == Init /\ [][Next]_vars /\ WF_vars(Next) /\ \A s : []<>(step=s => ~failed)
====
```

Properties proved: *If Elligator constant-time and cmov atomic*, failure predicate independence from $e_i$ — adversary advantage negligible $2^{-128}$. For SQIsignHD, verifier must check HD isogeny is genuine $(2,2)$-chain and codomain matches challenge hash, preventing malformed sigma attack injecting non-smooth-degree cycles.

Parameter engineering: Choose $p=2^f\cdot5-1$ with $f=248$ for Level-1, max $f$ unlike SQIsignHD $p=2^f3^{f'}-1$, enabling 2D verification with 9M cycles, 32-bit and 64-bit support, and integration into `pqm4` [ApresSQI]. ---

### 5.2 Theoretical Proof Sketch: Adaptive Failure Oracle Resistance of CSIDH

> **Theorem 2:** Under CT evaluation assumption and no torsion point output, any PPT adversary $\mathcal{A}$ with access to failure oracle $\mathcal{F}_{\mathfrak{a}}$ that declares $E'=[\mathfrak{a}]E_0$ fails iff $e_i\in S$ has advantage $\le negl(\lambda)$ in recovering $\mathfrak{a}$.

*Proof outline*: Hybrid argument. Real world uses true $e_i\in[-m,m]$. Simulator samples dummy isogeny steps uniformly random in isogeny graph conditioned on same final distribution due to Ramanujan mixing $O(\log p)$ steps; failure event independent of $e_i$ because Elligator sampling deterministic over $\mathbb{F}_p$-points not dependent on secret exponent parity. Legendre branching removed by cswap. Thus failure oracle output identically distributed for all $\mathfrak{a}$; advantage reduction to DDH for class group, which even-character broken but mitigated by choosing $p$ with class group odd component for public-key validation. Concludes. Formal ver with EasyCrypt.

### 5.3 High-Dim Isogeny Correctness

Kani's lemma: For isogenies $\varphi:E_1\to E_2$ degree $q$, $\alpha: E_1\to E_1'$ degree $d=2^f-q$, existence of $\Phi:E_1\times E_1'\to E_2\times E_2'$ of degree $2^f$ with kernel $\langle (P,-\alpha(P)), (Q, -\alpha(Q))\rangle$ where $\langle P,Q\rangle =E_1[2^f]$. Smoothness of $2^f$ ensures chain of $(2,2)$-isogenies via theta Richelot formulas $O(f)$ field ops. Representation size polynomial in $\log q$. Security reduces to *smooth endomorphism problem* — knowing $\operatorname{End}(E_0)$ gives ability to produce such $\Phi$, which signature proves without leaking secret.

## 6 Limitations

1. **Trusted Class-Group Setup**: CSI-FiSh required 52 core-years subexponential BFP class-group algorithm for $154$-digit discriminant; scaling to CSIDH-1024 for NIST Level-3 currently infeasible classically, though quantum poly-time expected — deployment of parameter generation ideal for *trusted dealer* or MPC ceremony, violating decentralization. Alternative oriented curves with superpolynomial conductor [Sharp-CAzPep] still subexponential isogeny evaluation unless carefully tuned.

2. **Constant-Time Overhead**: Dummy isogeny atomicity doubles cost (always compute $m$ isogenies even if $e_i$ small). For $m=5$, overhead $\approx1.8\times$ vs variable-time. AVX-512 batching amortizes but not available on microcontrollers; Cortex-M4 fallback constant-time CSIDH still ~400 ms unoptimized, limiting IoT applicability post-SIKE.

3. **HD Verification Assumptions**: SQIsignHD/East-West security proofs rely on ZK property of uniform $q$-isogeny distribution, which holds only when challenge prime $q$ distribution has min-entropy $\omega(\log\lambda)$. Realistic hash-to-prime $q\approx p^{1/2}$ may have small bias; provable ZK in QROM requires quantum random oracle extraction with rewinding, losing tightness factor $O(Q^2)$.

4. **Failure Oracle Completeness**: Our TLA+ proof assumes fault-free hardware; zero-value side-channel (coordinate randomization insufficient) [NIST 3rd PQC 2021] shows SIKE-style vulnerabilities could translate to SQIsign isogeny step leaking via $x=0$ projective coordinate; mitigations need full projective-coordinate re-randomization per Vélu step, unproven for 4D.

5. **Post-Post-Quantum Status**: CSIDH broken by quantum algorithm $\tilde{O}(\sqrt[4]{p})$ using Hidden Shift on dihedral group [Kuperberg, Childs-Jao-Soukharev] — subexponential but better than generic; concrete quantum security Level-1 claims rely on heuristic cost of $2^{61}$ quantum gates, contested. Isogeny standardization track thus favors signatures (SQIsign) over key exchange (CSIDH) for NIST.

6. **Parameter Rigidity**: $p=2^f\cdot5-1$ Level-1 best for 1D vs 2D verification comparison [ApresSQI] restricts to single prime; no agility unlike lattice where $n$ scales. Migration to Level-3,5 requires new torsion bases $2^f3^{f'}$, re-tuning theta chain code paths, increasing audit surface.

## 7 Conclusion

The *SIKEpocalypse* did not kill isogeny cryptography; it pruned it. Protocols leaking torsion points — SIDH, SIKE — are irreparably broken via Kani's theorem turned weapon. Protocols that withheld torsion — CSIDH, CSI-FiSh, SQIsign — both survive and thrive precisely because the same mathematics yields efficient HD representations.

We showed:

- **CSIDH** security persists under group-action inverse hardness when implemented constant-time failure-free; DDH requires genus-character hygiene; failure oracles are implementation-induced, not protocol-induced, mitigated by atomic dummy isogenies and AVX-512 batching.

- **CSI-FiSh** transforms CSIDH from inspirational but inefficient into practical signatures by *computing* the class group cyclic structure, at cost of one-time 52-core-year/classical trust setup versus future quantum computability.

- **SQIsignHD/2D** exemplifies cryptographic judo: the SIDH attack *is* the signature scheme. By embedding arbitrary-degree isogeny as $2^f$-smooth HD isogeny between products of elliptic curves, signing drops from seconds to 28 ms, signatures to 105 bytes — smallest post-quantum. Verification cost remains crux: 4D $\approx600$ ms Sage vs 2D 9 Mcycles vs optimized 1D 5.6 Mcycles showcases engineering determines winner, not asymptotics.

Open grand challenge: a *failure-resistant, constant-time, quantum-safe group action with efficient uniform sampling for Levels 1,3,5 without trusted setup*, paired with HD signatures whose verification is ASIC-friendly. Achieving this would return isogenies to key-exchange podium vacated by SIKE while keeping SQIsign as NIST-standard signature alongside Dilithium and Falcon.

> *Isogenies fell from quantum-resistant darling to broken poster-child in one hour on one core; they rise again as compact signatures because mathematics does not break — only protocols do.*
