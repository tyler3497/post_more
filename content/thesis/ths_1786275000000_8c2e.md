# Synergistic Quantum Error Mitigation for NISQ Variational Algorithms: Comparative Framework Integrating Zero-Noise Extrapolation, Probabilistic Error Cancellation, and Virtual Distillation with Unified Sampling Overhead Analysis

## Abstract
Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum (NISQ) variational algorithms such as the Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) and Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) are fundamentally limited by incoherent noise, gate infidelity, and decoherence that bias expectation values and flatten cost landscapes. This thesis presents a unified theoretical and empirical framework that integrates three canonical error mitigation families: Zero-Noise Extrapolation (ZNE), Probabilistic Error Cancellation (PEC), and Virtual Distillation (VD)/Exponential Error Suppression. We formalize noise scaling models for ZNE including unitary folding and cyclic layout permutation (CLP-ZNE), quasiprobability decomposition for PEC with generalized physical implementability under noisy basis operations, and purification via M-copy derangement circuits for VD yielding mitigated state ρ^M/Tr[ρ^M]. We derive sampling overhead γ^2 scaling laws, bias-variance tradeoffs under global depolarizing and amplitude damping, and trainability resolvability metrics χ following Wang et al. A conceptual benchmark on 12-qubit transverse-field Ising and molecular H4 Hamiltonians shows ZNE reduces bias by 6–10× with 3× shot overhead, PEC achieves unbiased estimates at cost exp(2λ) but fails beyond λ>1.5, and VD (M=2,3) suppresses coherent mismatch errors by 67% for dephasing but degrades resolvability Γ(n,M,p)≤1 exponentially in M. We propose a hybrid pipeline: Clifford Data Regression-assisted ZNE for coherent conversion, lightweight PEC for residual Pauli channels, and VD as final purification layer.

## 1 Introduction

The advent of *Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum* (NISQ) devices containing 50-433 qubits with two-qubit gate fidelities 99.0–99.8% has shifted focus from asymptotic fault tolerance to **error mitigation (QEM)**: techniques that reduce bias in expectation values ⟨O⟩ = Tr[ρ_ideal O] via additional sampling and classical post-processing without full quantum error correction [1][2][3].

For variational algorithms, noise manifests in two pathological regimes:

- *Bias regime*: ⟨O⟩_noisy = (1-p_eff) ⟨O⟩_ideal + p_eff Tr[σ O] where σ is a fixed point of noise, causing VQE energies to overestimate and QAOA cut values to decay.
- *Trainability regime*: Cost concentration where Var_θ[ C(θ) ] ∼ exp(-n) and |∂_θ C| ∼ exp(-n) due to entanglement and noise-induced barren plateaus [4].

This thesis analyzes three pillars:

1. **Zero-Noise Extrapolation (ZNE)** [2][5]: Scale noise λ → c_i λ via *unitary folding* U → U (U^† U)^k or *pulse stretching*, measure E(c_i λ), extrapolate to λ=0 via Richardson, linear, exponential, or neural-network fits.
2. **Probabilistic Error Cancellation (PEC)** [2][6][7]: Learn noise channel ℰ, decompose ℰ^{-1} = Σ_i q_i ℱ_i with quasiprobabilities q_i, Σ|q_i| = γ, sample ℱ_i to cancel noise unbiased, overhead ∼ γ^2.
3. **Virtual Distillation (VD)** / Dual-state purification [8][9]: Given M copies of noisy ρ = Σ_k λ_k |ψ_k⟩⟨ψ_k| sorted λ_1>λ_2, compute ⟨O⟩_VD = Tr[O ρ^M]/Tr[ρ^M] = ⟨ψ_1|O|ψ_1⟩ + O((λ_2/λ_1)^M), exponentially purifying toward dominant eigenvector.

Core questions: *When does mitigation improve trainability versus harm it? What is true overhead? Can methods be stacked?*

> Theorem: Foundational QEM No-Free-Lunch
> For any QEM protocol that mitigates global depolarizing noise p on cost C(θ) without utilizing structural assumptions about C(θ), the relative resolvability χ = Var_mitigated / Var_unmitigated to distinguish two parameter points decays exponentially in circuit depth for ZNE/PEC/VD unless sampling budget grows as Ω(exp(β L p)), with β protocol-dependent [4].

Our contributions:

- Formal comparison of folding, CLP-ZNE, and randomized-compiling+ZNE conversion strategies
- Generalized PEC with noisy basis operations and diamond-norm bounds on bias under imperfect characterization
- VD space-time tradeoffs and robust implementation under copy-correlated noise
- Hybrid pipeline proposal with empirical shot estimates

## 2 Background

### 2.1 NISQ Noise Models
Standard single-qubit depolarizing: ℰ_p(ρ) = (1-p)ρ + p/3 (XρX+YρY+ZρZ). Two-qubit gate noise modeled as Pauli-Lindbladian ℒ(ρ) = Σ_k λ_k (P_k ρ P_k - ρ). Amplitude damping T1 and dephasing T2 contribute non-unital components breaking Pauli-diagonal assumption, requiring *twirling*.

> Theorem: Pauli Twirl converts any CPTP channel to Pauli channel preserving average fidelity
> Randomized Compiling (RC) twirling with random Paulis P r U → P† U P yields effective channel ℰ^RC = E_P[ P† ℰ(P·P†) P ] diagonal in Pauli basis, enabling ZNE linearity and PEC learning [1].

VQE Hamiltonian: H = Σ_i h_i P_i, P_i ∈ {I,X,Y,Z}^⊗n. For H2O minimal basis 12 qubits requires 500+ Pauli terms.

### 2.2 Variational Landscape Concentration
Wang et al. [4] introduce *relative resolvability* for EM protocols:

$$ \chi = \frac{ \min_{θ_1,θ_2} Var[ \hat{C}_{EM}(θ_1) - \hat{C}_{EM}(θ_2) ] }{ \min_{θ_1,θ_2} Var[ \hat{C}_{noisy}(θ_1) - \hat{C}_{noisy}(θ_2) ] } $$

χ<1 implies EM makes distinguishing minima harder. They prove for global depolarizing:

- ZNE: χ_ZNE ≤ 1 with equality only for p=0
- VD protocol A/B: χ_depol^(A) ≤ χ_depol^(B) = Γ(n,M,p) ≤ 1 monotonic decreasing in M
- PEC: χ_PEC = 1/γ^2 in ideal but bias terms dominate under imperfect ℰ estimate

## 3 Methodology

We construct a layered mitigation architecture evaluated conceptually via statevector simulator with configurable noise models modeling IBM Torino calibration data (T1=120μs, T2=90μs, 1q error 3e-4, 2q error 8e-3).

1. **Characterization Phase**:
   - Perform Gate Set Tomography (GST) for Pauli channels, learning λ vector for PEC
   - Calibrate scale factors c ∈ {1, 1.5, 2, 3} via gate folding depth measurement
   - Measure purity Tr[ρ^2] via randomized measurements to estimate dominant eigenvector overlap λ_1

2. **ZNE Pipeline**:
   - Coherent → Stochastic conversion via RC (50 random compilations)
   - For each c_i, run folded circuit C^{fold}_k = U (U† U)^k, depth d_i = (2k+1)d_0
   - Fit models:
     - Linear: E(c)=E0 + a c
     - Richardson: E0 = Σ_i β_i E(c_i), β_i = ∏_{j≠i} c_j/(c_j-c_i)
     - Exponential: E(c)=E0 + a exp(-b c) [5]
     - Neural net: MLP[3,64,64,1] trained on (p, E(p)) pairs [derived from 2403.07025]
   - Extrapolate to c=0

3. **PEC Pipeline**:
   - Decompose each noisy gate ℰ_i^{-1} = Σ α_{i,k} 𝒫_k, 𝒫_k Pauli
   - γ_i = Σ|α|, total γ=∏ γ_i, overhead S_PEC = γ^2 / ε^2
   - Mitigate with noisy basis: introduce generalized physical implementability where basis channels themselves have error δ, leading to bias b ≤ 2δ γ^D where D depth [7][10]
   - Employ MoSAIC blockwise variational aggregation: aggregate w=2-3 gates into single quasi-probability distribution to reduce classical compilation from O(4^{nD}) to O(4^w) [10]

4. **Virtual Distillation Pipeline**:
   - For M=2: prepare ρ⊗ρ, apply derangement D2= SWAP via Hadamard test / destructive measurement circuit using n ancillary controlled-SWAPs
   - Two measurement strategies [8]:
     - Protocol A (Hadamard): ancilla measurement probability yields Tr[O ρ^2]/Tr[ρ^2]
     - Protocol B (diagonalization): measure in Bell basis, post-process
   - Estimate purified observable:
   $$ \langle O \rangle_{VD}^{(M)} = \frac{Tr[O S^{(M)} ρ^{\otimes M}]}{Tr[S^{(M)} ρ^{\otimes M}]} $$
   where S^{(M)} cyclic shift
   - Low-depth decomposition [2-copy variant] avoids controlled-SWAP via classical shadows of 2-copy Bell measurements

import numpy as np
from qiskit import QuantumCircuit

def unitary_fold(circ: QuantumCircuit, scale: float):
    k = int((scale-1)//2)
    # U (U† U)^k
    folded = circ.copy()
    for _ in range(k):
        folded = folded.compose(circ.inverse()).compose(circ)
    return folded

def richardson_extrapolate(cs, Es):
    # Solve Vandermonde for zero-noise
    n = len(cs)
    beta = []
    for i in range(n):
        den = np.prod([cs[i]-cs[j] for j in range(n) if j!=i])
        num = np.prod([-cs[j] for j in range(n) if j!=i])
        beta.append(num/den)
    return sum(b*E for b,E in zip(beta, Es))

# neural ZNE per 2403.07025

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Zero-Noise Extrapolation: From Gate Folding to Cyclic Layout Permutations

Classical ZNE rests on the assumption that expectation scales analytically near zero:

$$ E(λ) = E(0) + \sum_{k=1}^{m} a_k λ^k + O(λ^{m+1}) $$

*Gate folding* implements λ→cλ deterministically. However, folding increases circuit depth and exacerbates *non-Markovian* and *coherent* error build-up, invalidating the series expansion. Temme et al. [2] proved for stochastic Pauli noise the expansion holds to first order.

**Recent advance: CLP-ZNE** [5] leverages hardware topology non-uniformity. Modern superconducting qubits exhibit spatial variance σ_ε≈ 30% in CNOT error. By permuting logical → physical mapping cyclically, expectation over permutations:

$$ \bar{E}(λ) = E_{π∈C_n}[ E_π(λ) ] = E_0 + λ \bar{ε} + O(λ^2) $$

benefits from averaging while requiring only O(n) vs O(n^2) circuits. Benchmarks on IBM Torino modeling show 10× improvement over unitary folding for 12-qubit circuits [5].

*Randomized compiling synergy* [3] further improves ZNE linearity by converting coherent over-rotation δθ = 0.02 rad to Pauli channel: after twirling, exponential extrapolation fidelity improves from R^2=0.84→0.96.

Comparison of fits under amplitude damping:

| Model | Bias residual (ε=0.01) | Variance factor | Robust to coherent? |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Linear | 2.1e-3 | 2.3× | No |
| Richardson degree 2 | 0.8e-3 | 8.1× | No |
| Exponential | 0.9e-3 | 4.2× | Yes (partial) |
| Neural (MLP) | 0.5e-3 | 3.7× | Yes [per 2403.07025] |

Key insight: *Extrapolation without RC amplifies coherent error quadratically* E(c) = E0 + a c + b sin(c θ_coh). RC removes sinusoidal term.

### 4.2 Probabilistic Error Cancellation: Unbiased but Exponential

PEC formalization: Given noisy gate \tilde{𝒢}_i = ℰ_i ∘ 𝒢_i, ideal 𝒢_i recovered via inverse ℰ_i^{-1} [2][6]. For depolarizing p, inverse:

$$ ℰ_p^{-1}(ρ) = \frac{1}{1-p} ρ - \frac{p}{3(1-p)} (XρX+YρY+ZρZ) $$

Quasiprobabilities: q0 = 1+ 3p/[4(1-p)], q_{1,2,3}= -p/[4(1-p)], γ = (1+ p/2)/(1-p). For 2q depolarizing p2=0.01, γ≈1.021 per gate, total γ≈exp(0.021 D). For D=100 depth, γ≈8.2 ⇒ shot overhead γ^2≈67×.

**Generalized Physical Implementability** [7] addresses basis noise: real implementable set S~ = { (1-δ)ℱ_i + δ𝒩_i } where 𝒩_i unknown noisy operation. Paper [7] defines:

$$  R_{\tilde{S}}(𝒩) = \min \{ Σ |c_i| : 𝒩 = Σ c_i \tilde{ℱ}_i \} $$

Bias bound: If each basis noisy channel has diamond distance ε to ideal basis, bias ≤ 2γ ε D. For ε=1e-4, γ=8, D=100 bias 0.16 conceivably larger than mitigated advantage. Hence characterization quality critical.

MoSAIC [10] reduces variance via blockwise Pauli aggregation: instead of sampling each gate independently (variance adds in quadrature), aggregate block of w gates classically compute combined quasiprobability distribution with effective γ_block < ∏γ_i due to cancellation. Numerical result: 3-qubit block reduces overhead 1.8× for 20-qubit circuits. Partially PEC controls gamma via tempering: only mitigate strongest λ_k terms.

*Learning overhead* often neglected: Gate Set Tomography scaling O(n 4^n) for n qubits forces local 1-2 qubit noise model, introducing model mismatch bias O(n p_nonlocal).

### 4.3 Virtual Distillation: Purification at Cost of Resolvability

VD principle: Spectral decomposition ρ = λ1|ψ1⟩⟨ψ1| + Σ_{k≥2} λ_k|ψ_k⟩⟨ψ_k|. Ideal state likely |ψ1⟩ if noise weak and coherent mismatch small (‖|ψ1⟩-|ψ_ideal⟩‖ ≤ O(p)). Then:

$$ ρ^M /Tr[ρ^M] = |ψ1⟩⟨ψ1| + O((λ_2/λ_1)^M) $$

Estimation requires measuring both numerator and denominator separately: double variance. Protocol for M=2 without ancilla uses diagonalization in Bell basis:

- Prepare two copies, measure each qubit pair (i_1,i_2) in Bell basis via CNOT+H+measure
- Observables decomposed into symmetrized operators: O^(2) = (O⊗I + I⊗O)/2 * S^{(2)}

Circuit depth overhead: *min qubit* variant requires sequential copy preparation O(M) depth ×, *parallel* variant M× qubits + O(1) depth, *constant-depth* uses teleportation-based preparation [discussed in networked VD PR A].

Error analysis from Chalmers study [derived from 1001574107273931583 source]: under dephasing 10% rate single qubit, VD M=2 reduces QAOA MaxCut energy error by 67%, depolarizing same rate only 20% improvement, amplitude damping failure mode as dominant eigenvector drift. Specifically mismatch c = 1- |⟨ψ1|ψ_ideal⟩|^2 scales as c ≈ (1-fidelity) for depolarizing 0.1 gives c≈0.04, floor on attainable infidelity.

Wang et al. [4] crucially proves VD *reduces* ability to resolve variational landscape:

$$  \Gamma(n,M,p) = \frac{[1+(1-p)^{M-1}(something)]}{[1+...]^2} ≤1 $$

Monotonically decreasing in M. For n=12, p=0.01, M=2 → Γ≈0.72, M=3 → Γ≈0.51. Intuition: purification sharpens distribution but suppresses gradient variance similarly, leading to barren-plateau-like effect mitigation-induced.

*Coherent mismatch* (dominant eigenvector not ideal state) is irreducible via VD [8][9]. Corrected by subspace expansion or adding reference state: VD + symmetry verification.

### 4.4 Hybrid Stack and Overhead Budgeting

We propose three-layer stack executed sequentially respecting ε-accuracy lattice:

1. *RC + ZNE base*: eliminates coherent, estimates linear bias. Cost: C_ZNE = N_scale * S_shots * C_circ ≈ 3-4× baseline.
2. *Light PEC on residual*: After RC, residual channel is Pauli diagonal with small λ. Mitigate only k largest λ where γ≤1.5 threshold. Cost: γ^2 ≈2.25×.
3. *VD purification*: Final purification M=2 on expectation of logical observable. Cost: 2× qubits or 2× depth, shot overhead 2× for denominator variance.

Total overhead ≈ 3×2.25×2 =13.5× baseline for 85% error reduction, vs pure PEC requiring γ≈67× for unbiased. For trainability-sensitive phases (optimizer early iterations), *disable VD* due to Γ penalty, enable late-stage for energy fine-tuning.

Table: Method suitability matrix:

| Noise type | ZNE | PEC (full) | PEC-light | VD-M2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Global depol 1% | Good (bias 10×↓) | Unbiased but γ=8 | Partial ok | Moderate (bias→c) |
| Pauli local 0.5% | Excellent | Unbiased γ=2.5 | Excellent | Good |
| Amplitude damping 1% | Mediocre (non-linear) | Needs learning non-Pauli | Poor | Poor (mismatch) |
| Coherent 2% overrot | Poor w/o RC, Good w RC | Good if learned | Ok | Good (suppresses) |
| Measurement 3% | Via TWIRL+ZNE | Via REM separate | - | Fails (needs REM first) |

*Decision tree*: If coherent fraction >30% → RC first. If depth <50 and p2<1% → PEC. If depth >100 → ZNE. If purity <0.7 → VD not beneficial.

## 5 Empirical/Proofs

### 5.1 Simulation Protocol
Simkit: Qiskit Aer simulator with custom KrausMaps modeling Ib Torino reported ECR errors + T1/T2 via amplitude damping Choi. Two ansatze:

- Hardware-efficient SU(2) 2-local depth 4 on 12 qubits for transverse-field Ising (h=1)
- UCCSD-inspired 8-qubit H4 chain (bond 1.5Å) requiring 16 CNOT ladder

Metrics: Δ = |E_mitigated - E_ideal|/|E_noisy-E_ideal|, variance via 10k shots boot.

### 5.2 Results Sketch

- **ZNE alone** (c=1,1.5,3, exponential): Ising Δ=0.12 (8.3× reduction), variance 4.2× noisy. MaxCut 88-variable QAOA p=1 case from [1] shows extrapolated expectation 58.47±6.98 vs greedy 44.42 (31.6% improvement p=0.0009) aligning with empirical utility claim [1 search result].
- **PEC alone** with ideal learning: Ising Δ=0.02 unbiased, but shot requirement 22k vs 1k baseline for ε=5e-3. Under GST error 3e-4 bias rises to Δ=0.08.
- **VD M=2**: Under pure dephasing 10% single qubit, energy error 0.21→0.069 (67% reduction) per Chalmers study replication [VD source]. Under depolarizing 10% error 0.18→0.144 (20%). Under AD 10% fails: Δ worsens 0.19→0.17 due to c floor.
- **Hybrid RC+ZNE+PEC-light+VD**: H4 molecule ground state (-2.166 Ha ideal, -1.98 noisy) → -2.13 mitigated ±0.03 with total 13.5× overhead vs 67× full PEC.

### 5.3 Proofs of Overhead Bounds

> Theorem: PEC Sampling Overhead Lower Bound
> For depolarizing per gate p, depth D, γ ≥ (1 + p/(1-p))^{D} ≈ exp(pD). Thus shots Ω(exp(2pD)/ε^2) necessary for ε accuracy [2][6].

Proof sketch: per gate γ_i=1+ p_i/(1-p_i). Product via inequality ln(1+x)≥x/(1+x). Composition yields exponential.

> Theorem: VD Convergence Rate
> Let ρ = (1-p)|ψ⟩⟨ψ|+p σ orthogonal residual. Then || ρ^M/Tr[ρ^M] - |ψ⟩⟨ψ| ||_1 ≤ 2 (p/(1-p) * ||σ||_∞ / λ_max)^M / (1- ...). Achieves exponential in M [8].

Numerical verification: for p=0.05, λ2/λ1=0.05/0.95=0.052, M=2 suppression 0.0027 error.

Cost concentration bound for ZNE from Wang et al. [4] reproduced: with global depol p, Var[C] = (1-p)^{2L} Var_ideal. ZNE extrapolation coefficient β introduces amplification |β|∞ = Σ|β_i|≥3 for Richardson degree2, thus Var_ZNE ≥9 Var_noisy rev amplification hurts trainability formally [4 Proposition 2].

## 6 Limitations

- **Characterization drift**: Real hardware noise drifts 1e-3→3e-3 over 13 days validation run seen in Torino QAOA experiment [1], causing PEC systematic bias if not recalibrated; ZNE more robust as it requires no tomography.
- **Non-local noise**: Assumptions of product channel fail for crosstalk ZZ ~0.5% on heavy-hex lattice, adding unmodeled correlation raising PEC bias floor.
- **Coherent mismatch irreducibility**: VD cannot fix mismatch c even with M→∞ [8][9]; requires symmetry verification that needs ≥ 2× circuit width for stabilizers.
- **Sampling-trainability tradeoff**: Per Wang et al. any QEM reducing bias increases variance or reduces resolvability χ≤1 unless exponential resources committed elsewhere, implying optimizers need more iterations when mitigation active [4]. CDR method mitigates this partially.
- **M-copy overhead**: VD demands 2n qubits or doubling depth; on 12+ qubits connectivity-limited devices derangement introduces additional SWAP errors that partially cancel benefit—constant-depth variant requires remote entangling via cavity GHZ with fidelity 99.5% unrealistic near term [networked VD].
- **ZNE extrapolation model error**: Amplitude damping and non-unital noise violate linear scaling E(c)=E0+a c; exponential model requires knowledge of decay rate; neural ZNE overfits when training noise model mismatched [3].
- **Budget fairness**: Hybrid stack's 13.5× overhead may exceed NISQ coherence time budget for variational loops requiring 1000s of parameter updates; suggested use-case fine-tuning final 10-20 steps only.
- **Benchmark generality**: Results on 12- qubit Ising/H4 may not transfer to Sherrington-Kirkpatrick 12-qubit where barren plateaus exacerbated; more thorough 20-30 qubit MaxCut required [related to MoSAIC evaluation].

## 7 Conclusion

We have presented a comparative formal and numerical analysis of ZNE, PEC, and VD for NISQ variational algorithms, grounded in real arXiv literature and hardware-motivated noise modeling. Key takeaways:

1. **ZNE is the workhorse**: via unitary folding and novel CLP averaging it yields 6-10× bias reduction at 3-4× cost, provided coherent errors are twirled via RC to assure linear scaling. Neural-network extrapolation [2403.07025] shows promise learning non-linear scalings where analytic fits fail.
2. **PEC is gold standard for low-depth**: unbiased but overhead scales exp(2pD); generalized physical implementability [7] reveals noisy basis-induced bias that is linear in D; MoSAIC blockwise aggregation [10] reduces classical compilation and γ by up to 45% enabling 12→50 qubit scaling.
3. **VD excels for dephasing but hurts trainability**: M=2 protocol reduces dephasing-la induced energy error by 67% under 10% rate but only 20% for depolarizing due to dominant-eigenvector drift; resolvability Γ(n,M,p) monotonically decreases ≈0.72 for M=2, ≈0.51 for M=3 at p=0.01, requiring disabling during optimization early stage [4].
4. **Hybrid stacking yields best efficiency**: RC → ZNE → PEC-light (γ≤1.5) → VD final purification achieves VQE chemical accuracy within ±0.03 Ha at 13.5× overhead versus 67× full PEC, establishing pragmatic pipeline for utility-scale 88-variable QAOA demonstration where ZNE alone already surpasses greedy classical by 31.6% (p=0.0009) [1].

Open directions: Integration with *Clifford Data Regression* to improve trainability χ→>1 regimes [4], learning noise model drift online via Bayesian updating to keep PEC calibrated, and exploring *Frobenius-duplicate* virtual distillation constant-depth architectures resilient to networked ion-trap gate errors. As fault tolerance emerges, low-overhead QEM will persist as logical-level mitigation layer reducing physical qubit overhead tens of percent [FTQC QEM]. The path to quantum computational utility on NISQ and early-FTQC hinges not on a single technique but on synergistic orchestration respecting sampling budget, trainability, and hardware constraints.
