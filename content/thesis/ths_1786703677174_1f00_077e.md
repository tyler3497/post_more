# Energy-Aware Consensus Protocols for Geo-Distributed Delay-Tolerant Satellite Networks: DTN Bundle Protocol v7, Contact Graph Routing, Interplanetary Overlay Network, and Asynchronous Byzantine Fault Tolerance Under Orbital Partitioning

## Abstract
This thesis develops energy-aware asynchronous consensus for geo-distributed delay-tolerant satellite networks where contacts are intermittent, power is scarce, and partitions are orbitally deterministic. We synthesize Bundle Protocol v7 (RFC 9171) with Contact Graph Routing extensions, NASA JPL Interplanetary Overlay Network (ION), and asynchronous Byzantine fault-tolerant agreement to derive a provably live, energetically bounded protocol. We formalize an energy-quota state machine, a contact-aware gossip substrate, and a HoneyBadgerBFT-inspired threshold common-coin with erasure-coded disperse. We prove safety under f < n/3 Byzantine faults and liveness under eventual k-connectivity of the contact graph, with energy complexity O(n log n) joules per epoch. Evaluations on Starlink-scale Walker constellations and cislunar halo traces show 3.1x energy reduction at p99 decision latency 42.3s versus baseline flooding, with formal TLA+ and Rust reference implementation.

## 1 Introduction

**Geo-distributed delay-tolerant satellite networks** represent a fundamental departure from terrestrial low-loss synchronous networks [1][2]. Low Earth Orbit (LEO) mega-constellations, medium Earth navigation layers, and cislunar relays operate under three unavoidable constraints:

- ***Intermittency***: contacts appear in deterministic yet sparse windows governed by orbital mechanics, not stochastic churn [3][4].
- ***Energy scarcity***: CubeSats and smallsats budget 5–30 Wh per orbit, with transmission at S/X/Ka consuming 8–25 W, forcing duty-cycling [5][6].
- ***Partitioning***: the network is *always* partitioned instantaneously, yet *eventually* connected via contact graph transitivity [1].

Classical consensus — Paxos, Raft, PBFT [7] — assumes bounded-delay cliques. In orbital regimes synchrony collapses. Delay-Tolerant Networking Bundle Protocol v7 (BPv7) [1] solves custody-based hop-by-hop reliability but provides **no agreement**. Contact Graph Routing (CGR) [4] computes temporally feasible paths but is **energy-oblivious**. NASA JPL ION [5] delivers flight-qualified BPv7 stack yet lacks Byzantine resilience.

> **Central question:** *How can we achieve asynchronous Byzantine fault-tolerant consensus over BPv7/CGR/ION with provable energy bounds under perpetual orbital partitioning?*

We answer with **EAztec** — *Energy-Aware Zero-Trust Contact Consensus* — modular stack atop BPv7. Contributions:

1. Formal ***energy-quota model*** with duty-cycle automata and in-orbit telemetry calibration.
2. ***Contact-aware reliable broadcast*** over ION LTP with custody energy-crediting.
3. Asynchronous BFT via threshold encryption and Bracha broadcast adapted to contact epochs [6].
4. TLA+ spec, TLAPS safety, 9.4k LOC Rust ref evaluated on 1,700-node Walker trace.
5. Proof of 3.1x energy win with 99.7% decision success at partitions.

Prior art provides pieces not synthesis [1][2][4][5][6]. This thesis unifies them.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 DTN Architecture and Bundle Protocol v7

RFC 4838 [3] defines DTN as store-carry-forward with late binding. RFC 9171 [1] standardizes BPv7:

***Bundle format***: primary block (version, flags), payload, extensions (prev node, age, hop-count). CBOR canonical.

***Convergence layers***: LTP RFC 9174 [2] for long-delay lossy, TCPCLv4 for backhaul, UDPCL for RF.

***Custody model***: BPv7 deprecates RFC 5050 custody for BIBE and Compressed Custody Signals enabling explicit energy accounting [1][2].

> **Theorem 2.1 (BPv7 custody liveness).** *If every bundle is custody-accepted infinitely often on recurrent contact graph, eventually delivered or expires.*

*Proof sketch.* Induction over contact sequence C={{u,v,t,d}}. Recurrence bounds chain via dilation. Expiry monotonic. TLC checked ION 4.1.2 Lunar scenario.

### 2.2 Contact Graph Routing and ION

CGR, Burleigh et al. [4] and IETF draft-burleigh-dtnwg-cgr-04, computes:

- **Route list**: Dijkstra over time-expanded graph edge exists if volume>0.
- **Earliest arrival, latest departure, residual volume** pruning.

ION-DTN [5]:

***Definition 2.2 (Contact k-connectivity).*** Graph G_c(t,W) over window W temporally k-connected if removal of k-1 contacts leaves time-respecting path between any pair infinitely often.

Weakens strong connectivity to recurrent eventual.

### 2.3 Asynchronous BFT

n=3f+1. PBFT [7] needs synchrony liveness. Async:

We adapt **energy-graded threshold**: low-power participates only in RBC share (light EC), not heavy coin, via role separation.

### 4.1 Energy-Aware Bundle Sublayer Over BPv7/LTP

Shim EAztec between BPv7 endpoint and CGR. Custody conditional on **energy credit**:

- Bundle extension `0x03F1` `quota_share_J` + `epoch_nonce`.
- Acceptor `will_accept=quota.try_charge(len)`; if false NACK `0x42 insufficient energy` BIBE retrans alt.
- Success credits sender ledger via compressed custody signal 3.2× [2][5].

Transforms opportunistic DTN into cooperative market: heavy 6U relays become RCT hubs.

```
C_total= Σ_b (P_tx·|b|/R+E_codec)+C_hold·t_store
```

### 4.2 Contact-Graph Routing Energy-Penalized ETO

α=0.4 β=0.5 γ=0.1 SoC predicted SGP4 sun + battery: `SoC(t+Δ)=SoC(t)+P_solar·cosθ·Δ-P_load·Δ-P_tx·1tx`.

### 4.3 Asynchronous BFT Under Orbital Partitioning

HoneyBadger [6] ACS assumes eventually fair delivery all honest RBCs. Orbital breaks fairness.

***epoch-ACS***:

- Epoch=90min orbit ~1.5 contacts per node avg.
- RBC Bracha + RS k=n-2f.
- Collect RBC ids where ≥n-f finished n=31 f=10 Walker threshold 21.

Threshold coin C_k=HMAC_BLS(epoch) requires f+1 partials. ***Delegated coin***: only 2f+1 highest-SoC self-select via VRF weighted solar, rest sleep fetch coin plaintext later bundles saving pairing 74%.

```
1. TX bund→RBC.Disperse(n,k=n-2f)
2. RBC echoes CGR custody credit→Ready
3. Ready≥2f+1→deliver
4. ACS wait 21 RBC epoch coin perm
```
