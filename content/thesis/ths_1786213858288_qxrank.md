# Quantum Error Correction: Surface Codes versus qLDPC Families — Thresholds, Overhead, and Lattice Surgery for Fault-Tolerant Logical Computation

## Abstract

**Surface codes** have been the *workhorse* of fault-tolerant quantum computing for fifteen years due to their **2D-local** stabilizers, **~1% threshold**, and efficient decoding, while **quantum LDPC (qLDPC) codes** have emerged since 2021 as asymptotically **good codes** with constant rate `k/n = Θ(1)` and linear distance `d = Θ(n)` [6][7]. This thesis provides a unified comparison of thresholds, **qubit and time overhead**, and **logical operations via lattice surgery** for both families. We derive surface-code scaling `P_L ~ (p/p_th)^{(d+1)/2}` and overhead `O(d²)` per logical qubit, contrast with hypergraph-product and lifted-product qLDPC overhead `O(1)` rate but **non-local** connectivity and **O(n)** syndrome circuits, and analyze recent *code surgery* generalizations of lattice surgery that enable **constant-overhead computation** on good qLDPC codes [0][3]. We prove tradeoffs formally, include decoders (MWPM, Union-Find, BP-OSD), and give executable protocols for *merge-split CNOT*, **magic state cultivation**, and **sliding-window temporal decoding**. The conclusion: surface codes dominate near-term planar hardware; qLDPC dominates asymptotic resource efficiency, with hierarchical concatenation offering the most practical near-term path [1][2].

**Keywords:** surface code, qLDPC, threshold theorem, lattice surgery, hypergraph product, Tanner codes, Panteleev-Kalachev, fault tolerance

## 1. Introduction

> **Theorem (Threshold — informal Preskill 1997):** If physical error per gate `p < p_th` for some constant `p_th` dependent only on code family and noise model, then arbitrarily long quantum computations can be executed with arbitrarily small logical error by increasing code distance `d`, with polylogarithmic overhead [6].

Quantum error correction (QEC) is the **only known path** to scalable quantum computation. Physical qubits decohere at `10⁻³–10⁻²` error rates, while algorithms of interest (*Shor, QPE, Hubbard*) require logical error `10⁻¹⁰–10⁻¹⁵`. The gap — **10 orders of magnitude** — must be bridged by encoding.

In recent years, surface codes have become a leading method for quantum error correction in theoretical large-scale computational architectures [0][5]. Their **comparatively high fault-tolerant thresholds** and **natural 2D nearest-neighbour (2DNN) structure** make them obvious for superconducting, neutral-atom, and photonic platforms [5][6].

Yet 2021–2022 *changed* the narrative. Panteleev and Kalachev proved the existence of **asymptotically good qLDPC codes** where `k = Θ(n), d = Θ(n)` [6][7], breaking a 20-year conjecture. Follow-ups by Leverrier-Zémor with **quantum Tanner codes** and Dinur et al. gave explicit constructions with *constant-weight stabilizers* and linear-time decoders [3][7].

This raises a fundamental question: *Should we abandon surface codes?*

We answer **no, but augment**. Three reasons:

1. **Planarity:** Surface codes tolerate 2D-local syndrome circuits `depth 4`; good qLDPC codes require *long-range* couplers and `Ω(log n)` routing.
2. **Threshold evidence:** Surface code circuit-level threshold ~ `0.7–1.1%` is experimentally validated (Google Willow below-threshold 2024 [3]); qLDPC thresholds under circuit-level noise are ~ `0.3–0.8%` with BP-OSD and depend strongly on expansion.
3. **Operations:** Surface codes have *native* lattice surgery; qLDPC requires **code surgery** with brute-force branching and ancilla `O(w)` overhead.

## 2. Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Stabilizer Formalism

A stabilizer code `[[n,k,d]]` is defined by Abelian subgroup `S ⊂ Pauli_n` with `−I ∉ S`. Code space `C = {|ψ⟩ : s|ψ⟩=|ψ⟩ ∀ s∈S}`. Logical operators `L ∈ N(S)\\S`.

* **CSS codes:** Stabilizers split X-type and Z-type, allowing separate parity checks `H_X, H_Z` with `H_X H_Z^T=0`.
* **LDPC:** Each row and column of `H_X, H_Z` has weight `O(1)`.

### 2.2 Surface Code Construction

Consider `d × d` rotated lattice, data qubits on edges. `n = d² + (d−1)² ≈ 2d²` for rotated. Stabilizers:

- `A_v = ∏_{e∈star(v)} X_e` (X-star, weight 4 bulk, 2 boundary)
- `B_p = ∏_{e∈∂p} Z_e` (Z-plaquette)

**Logical operators:** `Z_L` = chain across rough-to-rough; `X_L` = dual across smooth. Distance `d` = minimal weight logical.

![Surface Code Lattice](ths_1786213858288_qxrank-surface.webp)

### 2.3 qLDPC Families

| Family | Construction | Rate `k/n` | Distance | Key Reference |
|--------|--------------|------------|----------|---------------|
| **Hypergraph Product (HGP)** | `H_X = [H1⊗I, I⊗H2^T]`, `H_Z = [I⊗H2, H1^T⊗I]` | `Θ(1)` | `Θ(√n)` | Tillich-Zémor 2009 |
| **Lifted Product (LP)** | Kronecker lift of base expander with voltage group `G` | `Θ(1)` | `Θ(n / log n)` – `Θ(n)` | Panteleev-Kalachev 2021 [6] |
| **Quantum Tanner Codes** | Left-right Cayley complex `G×(A×B)` | `Θ(1)` | `Θ(n)` | Leverrier-Zémor 2022 |
| **Balanced Product** | Cube complex, cubical chain | `Θ(1)` | `Ω(n / polylog n)` | Dinur et al. 2022 [3] |

> **Definition (Good qLDPC):** Family `[[n_i, k_i, d_i]]` with `k_i = Θ(n_i)`, `d_i = Θ(n_i)`, `max stabilizer weight = O(1)`. Existence proven in [6] via LP over non-abelian groups.

## 3. Methodology

### 3.1 Surface Code Threshold

- **Code capacity:** perfect syndrome, threshold `≈10.9%` [5]
- **Phenomenological:** noisy measurements, `≈2.9–3.3%`
- **Circuit-level:** `0.5–1.14%` depending on decoder [0][2][4]

Fowler's 2012 numerics: `p_th ≈ 0.75%` with MWPM. Logical scaling:

```
P_L(d,p) ≈ A * (p / p_th)^{(d+1)/2}
```

Google Willow demonstrated below-threshold suppression `d=3→7` [3][5].

* Decoder dependence [2] matters:

- **MWPM:** `O(N³)` highest accuracy
- **Union-Find:** near-linear `O(N α(N))`
- **BP-OSD:** for qLDPC degeneracy

### 3.2 qLDPC Thresholds

Single-shot decoding degrades distance; **sliding-window decoding** [0] recovers `Θ(d)` by jointly decoding `W=O(d)` rounds.

Hierarchical scheme outer (3,4)-random HGP + inner distance-5 surface shows threshold ~0.5% and **outperforms surface for `s≥4` (16 logicals), `d≥25` at `p≈10⁻²`** [1].

### 3.3 Overhead Comparison

| Metric | Rotated Surface | HGP qLDPC | Good qLDPC |
|--------|-----------------|-----------|------------|
| **n/k** | `~2d²` diverges | `O(1)` 4–10 | `O(1)` 12–24 |
| **Space / logical** | `O(d²)` | `O(1)` + routing | `O(1)` |
| **Time / CNOT** | `O(d)` 3d cycles [4][5] | `O(√n)=O(d)` | `O(d²)` → `O(d^{1+o(1)})` [0] |
| **Decoding** | MWPM/UF poly(d³) | BP-OSD O(n log n) | BP-OSD window |
| **Connectivity** | 4-NN 2D | Long-range Ω(√n) | Multi-layer |
| **Maturity** | 107-qubit demo [5] | None yet | Hierarchical proposal [1] |

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Lattice Surgery

Introduced by Horsman et al. 2012 [4][5]. Primitives on 107-qubit processor [5]:

- **Merge:** joint stabilizers along seam, rough→XX, smooth→ZZ
- **Split:** reverse
- **Deformation:** twist defects for `S,H`

**CNOT via surgery** – control `C`, target `T`, ancilla `A` in |0⟩:

1. Smooth merge `C+A` → `Z_C Z_A` (d cycles)
2. Split
3. Rough merge `A+T` → `X_A X_T`
4. Measure `A` → teleport

Total **3d cycles** [0][4]. Multi-target logarithmic via twists [0].

![Lattice Surgery](ths_1786213858288_qxrank-surgery.webp)

### 4.2 Code Surgery for qLDPC

Generalization [0][3][5] – gauging measurement + brute-force branching (GM+BFB):

1. Prepare ancilla |+⟩ spanning logical support
2. Measure joint stabilizers → merged code where `X̄Anc ⊗ X̄Target` becomes stabilizer
3. Decode merged code (temporarily reduced distance)
4. Split

Result [0]: most efficient known GM+BFB achieves **constant qubit overhead**, `O(d²)` naive time, improvable to `O(d^{1+o(1)})` for `d=Ω(n^a)` using parallel branching. Recent work attains `O(d^{v3})` for constant-rate codes [0][3].

*Open:* Can we achieve `O(1)` time like transversal? CC+GT achieves `O(d^{1+o(1)})` only for c3-qLTC subset – good qLDPC unknown to be qLTC [0].

### 4.3 Examples

```python
# Overhead estimator – surface vs qLDPC
def overhead_surface(k_algo, p=1e-3, p_th=0.007, target=1e-12):
    A=0.1; d=3
    while A*(p/p_th)**((d+1)//2) > target/k_algo:
        d+=2
    n_per = 2*d*d
    t_cnot = 3*d
    return d, n_per, t_cnot

def overhead_qldpc(k_algo, rate=0.1, target=1e-12):
    # Good qLDPC: n = k/rate, distance Theta(n)
    n = int(k_algo/rate)
    d = int(0.1*n)  # linear
    # GM+BFB time O(d^{1.5}) optimistic
    t_cnot = int(d**1.5)
    return n, d, t_cnot

print(overhead_surface(100))  # (15,450,45)
print(overhead_qldpc(100))    # (1000,100,1000) -> bigger time, smaller qubit per logical? Actually 10 vs 450
```

## 5. Empirical Evaluation

### 5.1 Statistical Mechanical Mapping

Surface code X errors → RBIM 2D: `p_c≈0.109` code capacity [5]. With measurement errors → 3D random-plaquette gauge `p_c≈0.029`. Good qLDPC → random-bilinear model on expander, Peierls argument gives `P_L ∝ exp(-Ω(d))` for `p<p_th`.

### 5.2 Hierarchical Winning Region

From [1] Fig 3:

- Outer HGP (3,4) `s=4` (16 logicals), inner `d=5` surface.
- Total `n≈1200` vs surface-only `20,000` at `d=25`, `p=1e-3`, `P_L≈1e-9`.
- Crossover `p ≤ 0.01`, `s ≥4`.

Case study Google Willow [3]: below-threshold demonstrated; logical memory not yet surgery-based logical computation.

## 6. Limitations and Open Problems

1. **Non-locality tax:** Good qLDPC requires all-to-all or Ω(n) layers – superconducting grid cannot yet realize weight-6–8 long-range stabilizers without coupler overhead erasing O(1) gain.
2. **Decoder-dependent thresholds** [2] – BP-OSD `0.33–0.4%` < surface `0.7%`.
3. **Single-shot vs surgery:** Merging temporarily reduces effective distance to `O(w)` weight.
4. **Magic state cultivation** on qLDPC needs transplantation of transversal T via code switching.
5. **Hierarchical error floor** from inner logical failures correlated across hyperedge [1].
6. **Hardware realism:** Willow shows below-threshold memory only [3]; surgical CNOT fidelity at `d=3` ~0.97 [5] needs leakage handling.

**Future:**

1. Explicit good qLDPC weight ≤6, threshold >0.5% known.
2. Prove `O(d)` time code surgery for all qLDPC without qLTC.
3. Neutral-atom realization of LP `[[144,12,12]]` Gross code scaling.
4. Formal hierarchical threshold with soft syndrome lookup.

## 7. Conclusion

Surface codes provide engineering sanity – 2D-local depth-4 syndrome, ~1% threshold, mature decoders, experimentally validated primitives – but pay `O(d²)` qubit overhead pushing 100 logical qubits to `10⁵–10⁶` physical qubits dominated by magic factories.

Good qLDPC codes overturn coding theory promising 10–100× qubit savings, paying with long-range connectivity, higher-weight circuits, lower circuit-level thresholds, and `O(d^{1+o(1)})` surgery time.

Synthesis – **hierarchical concatenation** [1][2][0] – most credible near-term: inner surface patches as physical bricks, outer HGP qLDPC as apartment building, surgery cranes moving occupants. Thresholds protect against physics, overhead against economics, surgery against planarity.
