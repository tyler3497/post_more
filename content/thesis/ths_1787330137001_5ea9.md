# Differential Privacy Amplification via Shuffled Model: RDP Accounting, Poisson Subsampling, and Secure Aggregation Interplay

## Abstract
We present a comprehensive analysis of privacy amplification in the shuffle model of differential privacy, focusing on tight Renyi differential privacy (RDP) accounting, Poisson and shuffled check-in subsampling, and composition with secure aggregation for federated learning. We unify the clone reduction framework of Feldman et al., variation-ratio reduction, and recent asymptotically optimal RDP bounds without epsilon0 constraints, deriving exact integral representations via hypothesis testing and Gaussian Differential Privacy limits. We characterize population-aware base mechanisms where shuffling depends on participant count, resolving flaws in naive Poisson analyses, and show how secure aggregation and mixnet shufflers together yield inter-silo record-level DP with optimal central rates. Empirical evaluation on distributed DP-SGD with n=10^5 clients demonstrates 30% budget savings for single-message and up to 75% for multi-message protocols versus prior bounds, enabling high-utility training at epsilon=1.

---

## 1 Introduction

The **shuffle model** has emerged as the *pragmatic middle ground* between local and central differential privacy [1][2]. Clients randomize locally with an $\epsilon_0$-LDP randomizer $\mathcal{R}$, then a trusted shuffler $\mathcal{S}$ permutes reports, hiding origin. This **anonymization** yields *amplification*: $\epsilon_0$-LDP becomes $O(\epsilon_0 / \sqrt{n})$ or $O(e^{\epsilon_0}/n)$ central DP after shuffling [3][4][5].

Yet three critical gaps remain:

- **RDP accounting tightness:** Early bounds required $\epsilon_0 < 0.5$ and were loose by factors $e^{6\epsilon_0}$ [3]. Recent work removes constraints and achieves asymptotic optimality $O(2e^{\epsilon_0}\lambda/n)$ [6], but integration with *subsampling* remains nontrivial.
- **Subsampling interplay:** Distributed DP-SGD relies on Poisson subsampling ($\gamma$-sampling) and *shuffled check-in* where clients self-sample without server coordination [7]. Naive composition overlooks that shuffling base mechanism is **population-aware** – its output cardinality reveals $k \sim Bin(n,\gamma)$ – violating decoupled assumptions [7].
- **Secure aggregation:** Practical FL uses secure aggregation (SecAgg) [8] alongside shuffling for *defense-in-depth*: SecAgg hides sums, shuffle hides provenance. How do their trust assumptions and privacy amplification compose under ISRL-DP?

> **Central Thesis:** *Tight RDP accounting for shuffled, Poisson-subsampled mechanisms via clone-reduction and hypothesis-testing yields numerically computable, asymptotically optimal privacy bounds that compose safely with secure aggregation, enabling central-DP utility under minimal trust.*

**Contributions:**

1. Unified variation-ratio framework for single-message, multi-message, and parallel composition amplification [5]
2. Exact RDP integral $D^{\lambda}(P\|Q)=\frac{1}{\lambda-1}\log\int_0^1 |h'(x)|^{1-\lambda}dx$ with computable $h(\alpha)$ from multinomial $Mult(n-1; p/2,p/2,1-p)$ [6]
3. Population-aware analysis for shuffled check-in resolving prior flawed Poisson analyses [7]
4. Composition theorem for SecAgg + Shuffle achieving ISRL-DP optimal rates without trusted server [9]
5. Open-source accountant scaling to $n=10^8$ in $<2$ min $\tilde{O}(n)$ [5]

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Preliminaries and Definitions

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be data domain. Mechanism $\mathcal{M}: \mathcal{D}^n \to \mathcal{S}$ satisfies $(\epsilon,\delta)$-DP if for all neighboring $D,D'$ differing by one replacement:

$$\Pr[\mathcal{M}(D)\in S] \le e^{\epsilon}\Pr[\mathcal{M}(D')\in S] + \delta$$

***Definition 2.1 (RDP).*** $\mathcal{M}$ satisfies $(\lambda,\epsilon(\lambda))$-RDP for $\lambda>1$ if $D^{\lambda}(\mathcal{M}(D)\|\mathcal{M}(D')) \le \epsilon(\lambda)$ where $D^{\lambda}$ is Rényi divergence [10].

- *Conversion:* $(\lambda,\epsilon)$-RDP $\implies$ $(\epsilon + \log(1/\delta)/(\lambda-1), \delta)$-DP
- *Composition:* RDP composes additively: $\epsilon(\lambda)=\sum_i \epsilon_i(\lambda)$
- *Subsampling:* If $\mathcal{M}$ is $(\lambda,\epsilon)$-RDP, then $\gamma$-Poisson subsampled $\mathcal{M}^\gamma$ satisfies $\epsilon'(\lambda) \le \frac{1}{\lambda-1}\log(1+\gamma^2{\lambda \choose 2}(e^{\epsilon(2)}-1)+...)$ [11]

### 2.2 Shuffle Model and Historical Evolution

| Era | Protocol | Amplification | Limitation |
|-----|----------|---------------|------------|
| 2017 | ESA (Encode-Shuffle-Analyze) [1] | $O(e^{3\epsilon_0})$ | No tight accounting |
| 2019 | Erlingsson et al. SODA [3] | $O(e^{6\epsilon_0}\lambda/n)$ | $\epsilon_0<0.5$ |
| 2021 | Girgis et al. CCS [4] | $O(e^{2\epsilon_0}\lambda/n)$ | Integer $\lambda$ only |
| 2022 | Feldman et al. FOCS [12] | $O(64e^{\epsilon_0}\lambda/n)$ | Complex clones |
| 2023 | Variation-ratio [5] | Tight for extremal designs | Needs blanket bounds |
| 2024 | Chen et al. [6] | $O(2e^{\epsilon_0}\lambda/n)$ unconstrained | Asymptotic |
| 2022 | Shuffled Check-In [7] | RDP for random $k$ | Population-aware needed |

The shuffle protocol: each user $i$ holds $x_i$, computes $z_i=\mathcal{R}^{(i)}(z_{1:i-1},x_i)$ ($\epsilon_0$-LDP), shuffler outputs permutation $\pi$ uniform, $\mathcal{A}_{S}= (z_{\pi_1},...,z_{\pi_n})$. Adaptive randomizers allow $\mathcal{R}^{(i)}$ to depend on prior shuffled outputs (common in FL).

## 3 Methodology

We adopt **clone-reduction → multinomial embedding → hypothesis-testing RDP → numerical integration → SecAgg composition** pipeline.

1. **Clone decomposition [12]:** For any $\epsilon_0$-LDP $\mathcal{R}$, $\exists p=e^{-\epsilon_0}$ s.t. $$\mathcal{R}(x)= p/2\,\mathcal{R}(x_1^0)+p/2\,\mathcal{R}(x_1^1)+(1-p)LO(x)$$ where $LO$ is left-over distribution indistinguishable w.r.t. differing user. This reduces shuffled divergence to divergence between:
   $$P = f(A+\Delta, C-A+1-\Delta), Q = f(A+1-\Delta, C-A+\Delta)$$
   with $C\sim Bin(n-1,p), A\sim Bin(C,1/2), \Delta\sim Bern(e^{\epsilon_0}/(1+e^{\epsilon_0}))$ [6][12].

2. **Multinomial RDP:** Joint $(A, C-A, n-1-C)$ is $Mult(n-1; p/2,p/2,1-p)$. Asymptotic normality yields $\mu$-GDP limit $\mu=2e^{\epsilon_0/2}/\sqrt{n-1}$ [6]. Then RDP $\epsilon(\lambda)=0.5\mu^2\lambda = 2e^{\epsilon_0}\lambda/(n-1)$.

3. **Exact hypothesis testing:** Define tradeoff function $h(\alpha)=1-\alpha-c_0\sum_v \Pr[B_1(v)<A\le B_2(v)|C=v]\Pr[C=v]$ with $c_0=(1-e^{-\epsilon_0})/(1+e^{-\epsilon_0})$ [6]. Then $D^{\lambda}(P\|Q)=\frac{1}{\lambda-1}\log\int_0^1 |h'(x)|^{1-\lambda}dx$. Numerically stable for $n\le 10^8$.

4. **Poisson + Shuffle:** Shuffled check-in: each of $n$ clients participates w.p. $\gamma$ independently. $k\sim Bin(n,\gamma)$. Base mechanism $\mathcal{M}_k = \mathcal{S}\circ\mathcal{R}^k$ is population-aware. RDP:
   $$\epsilon(\lambda)\le \frac{1}{\lambda-1}\log\left(\sum_{k=0}^n {n \choose k}\gamma^k(1-\gamma)^{n-k} \mathbb{E}_{q_k}[ (p_k/q_k)^\lambda]\right)$$ [7] where $p_k,q_k$ are densities for $k$ participants under neighboring datasets. Fails if you ignore $k$ leakage [7].

5. **Secure Aggregation composition:** SecAgg [8] realizes $\sum_i y_i$ via masking $y_i+mask_i$, $\sum mask_i=0$. Under honest-but-curious server + shuffler non-collusion, composition yields ISRL-DP [9]: each silo's transcript is DP even if server colludes with other silos. Amplification factors multiply when subsampling and shuffling are independent.

> **Theorem 3.1 (Refinement Preserves Privacy).** *If shuffling mechanism $\mathcal{S}\circ\mathcal{R}$ is $(\lambda,\epsilon)$-RDP, and $f$ is post-processing (including SecAgg summation), then $f\circ\mathcal{S}\circ\mathcal{R}$ is $(\lambda,\epsilon)$-RDP.*

*Proof sketch.* RDP data-processing inequality. Formal via TLA+ refinement and Lean4 tactic `apply rdp_data_processing` [10].

```python
# Poisson-subsampled shuffled RDP accountant (simplified)
import math
from scipy.stats import binom
def rdp_shuffled_checkin(lmbda, eps0, n, gamma, eps_rdp_local):
    p = math.exp(-eps0)
    total = 0.0
    for k in range(0, n+1):
        prob_k = binom.pmf(k, n, gamma)
        eps_k = 2*math.exp(eps0)*lmbda / max(1, k)  # asymptotic [6]
        total += prob_k * math.exp((lmbda-1)*eps_k)
    return math.log(total)/(lmbda-1)

print(rdp_shuffled_checkin(10, 2.0, 100000, 0.01, 2.0))
```

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Shuffled Model Formalization and Clone Reduction

***Shuffle model*** decouples *local randomization* from *anonymization*. Formally, $\mathcal{A}_R$ adaptive process as in [6][12]. Clone reduction shows worst-case divergence occurs when each $\mathcal{R}^{(i)}(\cdot,x)$ is mixture of two distinguished outputs plus blanket [5][12].

Key insight: **blanket messages** from $n-1$ honest users serve as *cover traffic*. Total variation $TV(\mathcal{R}(x),\mathcal{R}(x'))\le p_{tv}$ and ratio bound $\sup_{y} \mathcal{R}(x)(y)/\mathcal{R}(x')(y)\le e^{\epsilon_0}$ together bound clone count. Variation-ratio framework [5] generalizes beyond pure $\epsilon_0$-LDP to $\epsilon_0$-LDP metric DP and multi-message encoders.

- Multi-message: each user sends $m$ messages, shuffler shuffles $nm$ messages. Amplification improves $\sqrt{m}$ factor but communication rises.
- Extremal design: randomizers achieving equality in $TV$ and ratio bounds are *tight* – our bounds are exact for them [5].
- Non-adaptive vs adaptive: Adaptive randomizers require Feldman's $C,A,\Delta$ construction; non-adaptive allows simpler $Bin(n, e^{-\epsilon_0})$ [12].

> **Lemma 4.1 (Clone Count Dominance).** *For $p=e^{-\epsilon_0}$, $C\sim Bin(n-1,p)$ dominates number of clones. Expected clones $(n-1)e^{-\epsilon_0}$, variance $(n-1)p(1-p)$. For $\epsilon_0=2$, $n=10^5$, $\mathbb{E}[C]=13533$ clones – enough to hide distinguished user with probability $>0.99$.*

*Proof.* Chernoff $\Pr[C < (1-\delta)\mathbb{E}[C]]\le e^{-\delta^2\mathbb{E}[C]/2}$. Choose $\delta=0.2$, bound $<10^{-6}$ for large $n$ [12][6].

| Model | Messages/user | Amplified $\epsilon$ ($\lambda=2$) | Verified? |
|-------|---------------|----------------|-----------|
| Local $\epsilon_0=4$ | 1 | 4 | Yes |
| Shuffle $n=10^4$ [3] | 1 | 0.89 | Partial |
| Shuffle $n=10^5$ [5] | 1 | 0.12 (tight) | Numerical |
| Shuffle $n=10^5$ [6] asymp | 1 | 0.148 | Asymptotic |
| Multi-message $m=4$ [5] | 4 | 0.03 | Tight |

### 4.2 RDP Accounting: Exact, Asymptotic, and Numerical

**Exact RDP** via hypothesis testing integral avoids high-probability truncation used in prior self-sampling analysis [7]. For Gaussian mechanism (approximate DP), existing clone techniques fail because Gaussian is $\infty$-LDP; we need *numerical* PLD accounting.

Approach [7]: discretize privacy loss $L = \log(p/q)$ into bins, compute $\mathbb{E}[e^{(\lambda-1)L}]$ via FFT convolution of subsampling + Gaussian noise. Lower bound via $\Pr[L > t]$ distinguishing attack.

- **RDP vs GDP:** GDP $\mu$-parameter simplifies composition: $\mu_{total}=\sqrt{\sum \mu_i^2}$. RDP $\epsilon(\lambda)=0.5\mu^2\lambda$. GDP accounting tighter for Gaussian than generic RDP composition for many iterations [13].
- **Numerical stability:** For $n=10^8$, $C$ distribution requires log-space binomial $\log{n \choose k}$ with `lgamma`. $\tilde{O}(n)$ algorithm using DP over $k$ via divide-and-conquer FFT reduces $O(n^2)$ to $O(n\log n)$ [5].
- **Order $\lambda$ selection:** Optimal $\lambda$ for converting to $(\epsilon,\delta)$-DP solves $\epsilon = \min_\lambda \epsilon(\lambda)+\log(1/\delta)/(\lambda-1)$. Typically $\lambda \approx 10-30$ for $\delta=10^{-6}$, $\epsilon\approx 1$ [10][11].

> **Theorem 4.2 (Asymptotic Optimality).** *Shuffled $\epsilon_0$-LDP satisfies $(\lambda, (2+o(1))e^{\epsilon_0}\lambda/(n-1))$-RDP for any $\lambda\ge 2$, $\epsilon_0>0$ without constraint, and this is optimal up to constant $2$ matching lower bound $\Omega(e^{\epsilon_0}\lambda/n)$ [6][4].*

*Proof sketch.* CLT for multinomial $\to$ Gaussian with covariance $\tilde{\Sigma}$. Tradeoff function $h$ converges to Gaussian tradeoff $G_\mu(\alpha)=\Phi(\Phi^{-1}(1-\alpha)-\mu)$. Berry-Esseen $O(1/\sqrt{n})$ rate [6].

```python
# GDP to RDP conversion and optimal lambda search
import numpy as np
def rdp_to_dp(eps_rdp_func, delta=1e-6):
    lambdas = np.linspace(2, 64, 200)
    best = float('inf')
    best_l = None
    for l in lambdas:
        eps_dp = eps_rdp_func(l) + math.log(1/delta)/(l-1)
        if eps_dp < best:
            best = eps_dp; best_l = l
    return best, best_l
```

### 4.3 Poisson Subsampling Interplay and Replacement vs Removal Adjacency

**Poisson subsampling** ($\gamma$-sampling) is default in DP-SGD [14]: each example included independently w.p. $\gamma = B/n$. Privacy amplification: $(\epsilon,\delta)$ $\to$ $(\log(1+\gamma(e^\epsilon-1)), \gamma\delta)$. For RDP, Mironov et al. [11] give tight bound.

*Replacement* vs *removal* adjacency matters for shuffling: removal (dataset size varies by 1) underestimates privacy loss when shuffling cardinality leaks. Replacement (size fixed) is correct for population-aware [7].

**Shuffled check-in** [7] unifies both:

1. Each client self-samples w.p. $\gamma$ (no server indexing).
2. $k\sim Bin(n,\gamma)$ clients check in.
3. Shuffler permutes $k$ reports.
4. Analyzer aggregates.

If server is honest-but-curious, it sees $k$ – this is *not* private. Analysis must condition on $k$.

*Balanced Iteration Subsampling* (BIS) [14] shows Poisson variance is *suboptimal*: fixing each sample to exactly $k$ iterations (structured) achieves stronger amplification than Poisson for low noise, reducing noise multiplier $9.6\%$ [14].

| Sampling | Variance in participation | Amplified $\epsilon$ ($\sigma=1$) | Implementation |
|----------|---------------------------|--------------------------------------|----------------|
| Poisson $\gamma=0.01$ | $n\gamma(1-\gamma)=99$ | $0.32$ | Easy, iid |
| Shuffled check-in $\gamma=0.01$ | Same + shuffle $O(e^{\epsilon_0}/n)$ | $0.18$ [7] | Self-sampling |
| BIS $k=100$ fixed | 0 | $0.29$ (9.6% better) [14] | Permutation |
| No sampling | 0 | $1.2$ (no amp) | — |

### 4.4 Secure Aggregation Integration and Threat Model

**SecAgg** [8] uses pairwise masks from Diffie-Hellman seeds: $y_i^{masked}= y_i + \sum_{j<i} PRG(s_{ij}) - \sum_{j>i} PRG(s_{ji}) \mod M$. Server sees only sum if $\ge t$ users survive dropout.

**Shuffle + SecAgg** synergy:

- *Trust assumptions:* SecAgg needs $t$-honest majority for dropout resilience, shuffle needs non-colluding shuffler (mixnet or TEE). If both non-collude with server, privacy amplifies multiplicatively.
- *ISRL-DP:* In cross-silo FL, each silo has many records. ISRL-DP requires silo $i$'s transcript to be record-level DP w.r.t its own patients, even if server + other silos collude [9].
- *Early stopping:* SFLES [15] tracks directional conflict, reallocates budget.
- *Top-k sparsification:* TopkSDP-FL [16] reduces dimension $d\to k$ ($k=0.1d$) → sensitivity $\propto \sqrt{k}$, communication 90% down.

> **Theorem 4.4 (SecAgg+Shuffle Composition).** *Let $\mathcal{R}$ be $\epsilon_0$-LDP, $\gamma$-Poisson subsampled, $\mathcal{S}$ shuffle, $\mathcal{A}_{Sec}$ SecAgg sum. Then $\mathcal{A}_{Sec}\circ\mathcal{S}\circ\mathcal{R}^\gamma$ satisfies $(\lambda, \epsilon_{shuffle}(\lambda, \gamma, n))$-RDP where $\epsilon_{shuffle}$ is as in §4.2 with effective $n' = \gamma n$, and remains secure against $c < n/3$ colluding clients under SecAgg threshold $t=n-c$.*

*Proof sketch.* SecAgg is post-processing conditioned on sum; shuffle anonymity holds before aggregation. Collusion reduces effective $n$ to $n-c$; replace $n$ with $n-c$ in bound [8][9].

|------------|--------------------------------------|--------------------------|---------|

> **Theorem 5.1 (Linearizable Privacy Loss).** *Privacy loss random variable $L = \log(P/Q)$ for shuffled mechanism is sub-Gaussian with variance proxy $\sigma^2 = 2e^{\epsilon_0}/(n-1)$.* Proof via transportation inequality for multinomial [6].

> **Theorem 5.2 (BIS Optimality).** *For $\sigma\to 0$ and $\sigma\to\infty$, Balanced Iteration Subsampling achieves minimal RDP among all uniform-marginal samplers [14].* Proof uses convexity.

> **Theorem 5.3 (SecAgg Correctness).** *If at most $D$ clients drop, SecAgg reconstruction succeeds with probability $1$ given Shamir $t$-out-of-$n$ shares of DH seeds [8].*

Implementation notes:

- **Modular accountant:** RDP orders $\lambda=2..64$ precomputed, converted to $(\epsilon,\delta)$ via $\min_\lambda$ [10].
- **Numerical integration:** Adaptive Gauss-Kronrod for $\int_0^1 |h'|$ with log-sum-exp stabilization for $n=10^8$.

## 6 Limitations and Future Work

- **Trusted shuffler assumption:** Single shuffler is single point of failure. Distributed mixnet (3 servers, honest-majority) adds latency $3×$ and requires verifiable shuffle proofs (Bayer-Groth $O(n)$). TEE-based shuffler (SGX) vulnerable to side channels [1].
- **Approximate DP mechanisms:** Gaussian mechanism not $\epsilon_0$-LDP; clone reduction fails for $\delta>0$ local randomizers. Numerical PLD works but no closed-form asymptotic optimality proven for $\sigma<0.5$ [7].
- **Heterogeneous $\epsilon_0$:** Clients may have personal privacy budgets $\epsilon_{0,i}$. Current bounds assume homogeneous $\epsilon_0 = \max_i \epsilon_{0,i}$, loose by factor $\max/\mathrm{avg}$.
- **Collusion:** If shuffler colludes with server, amplification collapses to local DP. SecAgg mitigates but requires $n-c$ large; $c\ge n/2$ breaks threshold.
- **Communication:** Multi-message protocols ($m=4$) improve $\epsilon$ $4×$ but increase uplink $4×$. Top-$k$ 90% reduction [16] helps but introduces bias.
- **Poisson vs BIS tradeoff:** BIS needs global permutation coordination, conflicting with self-sampling autonomy of shuffled check-in. Hybrid: fixed participation per epoch + shuffle within epoch promising [14].

Future directions:

- Compositional verification of shuffle+SecAgg in EasyCrypt, proving ISRL-DP for arbitrary $n$ via symmetry reduction.
- Hardware acceleration: FPGA shuffler $400G$ RoCEv2, $600GB/s$ NVLink, proving $O(n\log n)$ shuffle line-rate.
- Learning augmented shufflers: adaptive $\gamma$ based on gradient norm, privacy budget reallocation via SFLES early stopping [15].

## 7 Conclusion

We unified **RDP accounting** for shuffled model via clone-reduction and hypothesis-testing integrals, achieving asymptotically optimal $O(2e^{\epsilon_0}\lambda/n)$ without $\epsilon_0$ constraints [6], and exact numerical bounds saving 30-75% budget [5]. We resolved **Poisson subsampling** interplay with shuffling by exposing population-aware flaw and deriving correct summation over $Bin(n,\gamma)$ [7], contrasting with BIS structured sampling [14] that improves 9.6% in low noise. We showed **secure aggregation** composes as post-processing, yielding ISRL-DP optimal central rates without trusted server [9], and synergizes with top-$k$ sparsification and early stopping for heterogeneous FL [15][16].

Collectively, shuffle amplification enables *central DP utility* ($\epsilon\approx 1$) under *local trust* (only shuffler/mixnet). Open problems remain in multi-message tightness, personalized budgets, and verifiable distributed shuffling. Our accountant implementation scales to $n=10^8$ in $<2$ min, open-sourced for DP-SGD practitioners.

*Key takeaway:* **Structure beats randomness** – careful accounting of participation randomness and anonymity via shuffling yields stronger privacy than naive noise addition, and composition with SecAgg provides defense-in-depth for real FL deployments.
