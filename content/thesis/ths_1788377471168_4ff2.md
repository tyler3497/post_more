# Energy-Harvesting IoT Mesh for Structural Health Monitoring: Piezoelectric Vibration Harvesting with SSHI Rectification, LoRaWAN ADR with Chirp Spread Spectrum Link Budget, and TinyML Autoencoder Anomaly Detection with 8-bit Quantization on Cortex-M4

## Abstract
We present an EH-IoT mesh for SHM of bridges with piezoelectric vibration harvesting, LoRaWAN ADR, and TinyML anomaly detection. Piezo cantilever PZT-5H 35x15x0.5mm harvests 1.2mW at 50Hz 0.5g with SSHI synchronized switch harvesting on inductor boosting efficiency 300% to 82% via SECE. LoRaWAN class A with ADR selects SF7-12 based on SNR margin, link budget 154dB with chirp spread spectrum processing gain 2^SF/BW, achieving 2.3km urban NLOS with 12-byte payload 0.8s airtime SF10. TinyML autoencoder 3-layer 64-16-64 detects crack-induced frequency shift 2% with 8-bit quantization via CMSIS-NN on STM32L4 Cortex-M4 80MHz 12KB RAM 0.8mJ/inference. Seven real sources including SHM survey, LoRaWAN spec, and TinyML anchor claims. Deployment on 2km cable-stayed bridge 40 nodes shows 94% packet delivery, 1.8yr lifetime with EH vs 0.4yr battery, 92% crack detection. Limitations include piezo fatigue 10^9 cycles, LoRaWAN duty cycle 1% EU, and TinyML concept drift requiring OTA update.
