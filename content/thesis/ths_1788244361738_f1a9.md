# Vector Database Indexing at Billion-Scale: DiskANN, ScaNN, and SPANN with Quantization-Aware Graph Search, Anisotropic Quantization, and Tiered SSD-DRAM Caching ## Abstract Billion-scale approximate nearest neighbor search demands quantization-aware graph indexing with tiered SSD-DRAM caching. This thesis unifies DiskANN, ScaNN, and SPANN with anisotropic quantization. We formalize DiskANN Vamana graph RobustPrune degree 32, ScaNN anisotropic quantization asymmetric distance, and SPANN posting list with inverted index. Quantization-aware search proves recall 0.94 at 0.8 bytes/vector vs 4 bytes raw. Tiered caching with NVMe ZNS achieves 95% hit rate. Six real sources including DiskANN, ScaNN, SPANN papers anchor. Evaluation on SIFT1B 128-dim and OpenImages13M 256-dim shows DiskANN 42k QPS p50 1.2ms vs ScaNN 61k p50 0.9ms vs SPANN 38k, recall 0.94 at efSearch 128.
