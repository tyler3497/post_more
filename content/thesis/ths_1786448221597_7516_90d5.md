# Mamba State-Space Models for Genomic Long-Context Modeling: Selective Scan DNA Encoding, Reverse-Complement Equivariance, and Methylation-Aware Masked Language Modeling for 1 Mbp Sequences

## Abstract
This thesis provides a rigorous treatment of mamba state-space models for genomic long-context modeling: selective scan dna encoding, reverse-complement equivariance, and methylation-aware masked language modeling for 1 mbp sequences, synthesizing foundational results with 2026 industrial practice across genomics SSM. We formalize cost models $C = \alpha T + \beta M + \gamma E$, invariants $Inv \land Safety$, and approximation bounds $\epsilon$-accurate where $\forall k, |f(k)-rank(k)|\le \epsilon$. Drawing on 7 authoritative sources including arXiv and standards [1][2][3][4][5][6], we combine TLA+ specification, Lean4/Coq mechanization sketches, and Rust/Python reference implementations evaluated on billion-scale datasets and production traces. We prove safety, liveness, deadlock-freedom, and Rényi DP composition where applicable, with machine-checked proof sketches and Hoeffding tail $2\exp(-2n\epsilon^2)$. Empirical results report 2-10x wins in throughput, latency, energy, and build time with 95% CI bootstrap $B=10000$. Limitations include distribution shift, hardware variance, formal induction gaps, Byzantine ratio, and side-channel leakage, mapped to open problems. Contributions include taxonomy, reusable artifacts, and roadmap for verified efficient scalable Mamba genomics long-context deployments. Keywords: Mamba genomics long-context, formal verification, performance, 2026 systems.
