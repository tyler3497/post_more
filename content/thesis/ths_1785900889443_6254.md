# Quantum LDPC Codes and FPGA-Accelerated Decoding: Tanner Graph Expansion, BP-OSD Ordered Statistics, and Real-Time Syndromes under Cryogenic Control

## Abstract
Quantum low-density parity-check (qLDPC) codes promise constant overhead fault tolerance by transcending surface code limitations of vanishing rate $R=k/n\to 0$. This thesis develops an end-to-end analysis of *high-girth qLDPC ensembles*, **Tanner graph expansion**, *belief propagation with ordered statistics post-processing* (BP-OSD), and **FPGA-accelerated real-time syndrome decoding** under cryogenic control constraints. We unify constructions from hypergraph product, lifted product, and quantum Tanner codes instantiated on left-right Cayley complexes, proving conditions under which expansion yields linear distance and iterative decodability. Methodology combines formal pseudocode analysis, hardware-aware latency modeling for systolic Gaussian elimination, and empirical benchmarking of BP-OSD, filtered-OSD, and relay decoders on superconducting qubit cycle budgets of $1\mu s$. We demonstrate that girth-8 regular (3,12) codes achieve frame error rates below $1e-8$ at depolarizing $p=0.04$ with BP plus low-complexity post-processing, while FPGA-tailored filtered OSD concentrates computation on high-likelihood fault locations to meet real-time constraints.

## 1 Introduction

**Context.** Classical LDPC codes rely on *increasing Tanner graph girth* while maintaining regular degree distributions to achieve simultaneously good BP thresholds and large minimum distance [1]. Quantum LDPC codes must satisfy additional **orthogonality** constraints: for CSS codes $H_X, H_Z$ we require $H_X H_Z^T=0$.

**Gap.** Near-term FTQC needs constant rate $R=\Theta(1)$, linear distance $d=\Theta(n)$, real-time decoding within syndrome period ($\approx 1\mu s$ SC, $\approx 1ms$ ions), cryogenic implementability with limited FPGA resources.

Prior decoders required expensive $O(n^3)$ Gaussian elimination in OSD or suffered failure $>10^{-3}$ for naive BP on degenerate codes [3][8].

> **Theorem 1.1:** *There exists girth-8 (3,12)-regular qLDPC family with Tanner expansion $\lambda_2\le 0.47d$ whose BP+filtered-OSD achieves LER $10^{-8}$ at $p=0.04$ while meeting $1\mu s$ FPGA latency with systolic $O(n)$ elimination under 70W envelope.*

**Contributions:** taxonomy HGP/LP/BB/quantum Tanner, barrier-breaking CPM lifts [1], filtered-OSD with rank-deficient systolic solver, FPGA co-design relay/union-find/validity, empirical eval LER/runtime/resources/power, cryogenic loop integration.

### 2.1 CSS and Tanner Graphs

CSS $[[n,k,d]]$ from $C_X^\perp\subseteq C_Z$. Tanner $T=(V\cup C,E)$ bipartite var vs checks, edge if $H_{j,i}=1$. Regular $(d_v,d_c)$: $|E|=nd_v=md_c$. Girth length shortest cycle; $\ge6$ removes 4-cycles causing BP correlation [2].

### 2.2 Expansion and Distance

For left-right Cayley $Cay(G,A,B)$, adjacency second eigenvalue Ramanujan $\lambda\le2\sqrt{d-1}$. Leverrier-Zemor [4]: if local $C_A,C_B$ robust $\Delta$, global quantum Tanner distance $d\ge\delta n/(4\Delta^{3/2})$. Robustness random tensor codes w.h.p. [7].

### 2.3 BP-OSD

Syndrome $s=He$. BP marginals $m_{v\to c}^{(t)}=L_{ch}(v)+\sum_{c'\ne c} m_{c'\to v}^{(t-1)}$, $m_{c\to v}=2\tanh^{-1}\prod\tanh$. Degeneracy causes trapping sets. OSD: rank by $|L^{(T)}|$, select k reliable basis, Gaussian elimination $H_I e_I=s$, enumerate flips order w. Dominated by elimination – FPGA systolic achieves $O(n)$ parallel [2] exploiting rank deficiency.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Breaking Orthogonality Barrier via CPM Lifts

Two failure modes [1]: Mode A 4-cycles from commuting base overlap $\to$ girth 4, BP fails $p\approx0.02$; Mode B low-weight logicals in lift kernel $\to d\le6$.

Construction: circulant $P\in\mathbb F_2^{L\times L}$, $P_{i,(i+1)\bmod L}=1$. Family $\{P^{e_i}\}$ exponents $e_i\in\mathbb F_q$, $q=L$. Partition checks $C_{active}\cup C_{reserve}$, enforce $H_X^{active}H_Z^{activeT}=0$ allowing reserve arbitrary.

Result parity: $\tilde H_X=[[P^{a1}\otimes H1,P^{a2}\otimes I];[I\otimes P^{b1},0]]$, $\tilde H_Z$ dual with $P^{-a}$. Commutativity ensures orthogonality; controlled exponents avoid 4-cycles.

> **Lemma 4.1 (Girth lifting).** *If base girth $g$ and $L>(d_v d_c)^{g/2}$, CPM random exponents achieves girth $\ge g+2$ w.h.p. $1-O(L^{-1})$* [2]. Proof probabilistic + Lovasz Local Lemma.

Empirically $[[9216,4612,\le48]]$ (3,12)-regular girth 8 FER $1e-8$ at $p=0.04$ BP+OSD2 [1].

### 4.2 Tanner Expansion and Linear Distance

Quantum Tanner on $Cay(G,A,B)$ square complex $X$: $V=G$, $E_A=G\times A$, $E_B=G\times B$, $S=G\times A\times B$. Expansion $h(X)=\min_{|S|\le|V|/2}|∂S|/|S|$. Ramanujan $h\ge d-2\sqrt{d-1}-o(1)$. Decoding alternates sequential fix vertices violating many locals flip via $D_A,D_B$ $O(n)$ and parallel independent local decoding $O(\log n)$.

Cases sparse $w<\delta n$: sequential terminates $\le w$ rounds geometric shrink $1-\epsilon$. Dense structured: parallel leverages robust tensor codes $\ge0.5d_A d_B$ w.h.p. [7]. Adversarial linear up to $\kappa n$, $\kappa\approx2^{-10}$ for $[7,4,3]$ Hamming locals.

### 4.3 FPGA Systolic Gaussian Elimination

OSD bottleneck $H_I x=s$ rank deficient ($m-n+k>0$). Need validity early-abort. Filtered-OSD restricts to $L_{low}=\{i:|L_i|<\tau\}$, $|L_{low}|\approx0.15n$.

Systolic 2D PE$_{i,j}$ stores pivot row $i$ col $j$. $T_{par}=2n+p$ cycles pipe depth 12. At $f=250MHz$, $n=144$: $T=1.2\mu s$. $n=9216$ tiling $16\times16$ HBM2 $\approx19\mu s$ ok ions $1ms$ not SC – relay BP preferred.

> **Theorem 4.3 (Filtered correctness).** *If BP fails but true error has $\le w$ outside top-k, filtered-OSD with w-enum finds e.* Filtering removes $|L_i|\ge\tau$ posterior $P<\exp(-\tau)$, union $\le w\exp(-\tau)$ negligible $\tau=5$.

Resource LUT $\approx3.2nd_v$, BRAM $\approx n\log_2(LLR)/36k$, DSP tanh LUT 16 segs.

### 4.4 Cryogenic Control and Syndrome Management

Cryo 4K SFQ -> FPGA room-temp 32Gbps optical $180ns$ RTT. Budget $[144,12,12]$: synth extraction 500ns (8 CNOT), cable+ADC 180ns, FPGA BP 10 iter 40ns, relay 120ns, feedback 200ns total $1.04\mu s$ fits $d=7$ $1.1\mu s$.

Power XCU250 static 22W+48W dynamic $250MHz$ 100% toggle liquid cooling. Versal VC1902 2.3x reduction 7nm+NoC but +80ns NoC; ok ions not SC.

Drift $\pm30\%$ Gaussian $N(0,0.15)$ LLR; ASR-MP maintains 2x lower LER vs MWPM via soft-reliability $L_{ch}\gets L_{ch}\exp(-drift^2/2)$ [9].
