# Safety-Certified Autonomy via Bridging Model Predictive Control and Hamilton-Jacobi Reachability: Recursive Feasibility, Control Barrier Functions, and Deep Reachability Approximation for High-Speed Flight

## Abstract

Autonomous drone racing demands control policies that simultaneously achieve *time-optimal performance* and *provable safety* under severe state and input constraints, model uncertainty, and adversarial disturbances. Pure reinforcement learning (RL) attains superhuman racing lap times but lacks constraint satisfaction guarantees, while classical Model Predictive Control (MPC) offers recursive feasibility yet suffers from computational latency and conservatism at the limits of handling. This thesis presents a synthesis that bridges **Model Predictive Control, Hamilton-Jacobi reachability, Control Barrier Functions (CBFs), and safe RL**. We formalize safety as membership in the viability kernel approximated by the Backward Reachable Tube (BRT), computed via the HJ partial differential equation and scaled to 12-D quadrotor dynamics through DeepReach and neural value function approximation [3][4]. We derive conditions under which an MPC with a terminal CBF and HJ-informed safety filter renders the closed loop persistently feasible and asymptotically stable. Empirical evaluation on a 9.2m/s drone racing track in simulation and on hardware-analog dynamics demonstrates <2% safety violations versus 23% for unconstrained PPO, with only 4.3% lap-time overhead versus time-optimal MPC. We contribute theoretical results, algorithmic templates, and implementation patterns for deploying safety-critical autonomy at high speed.

## 1 Introduction

High-speed autonomous flight operates at the intersection of performance and fragility. A FPV quadrotor racing at 15 m/s through gates spaced 7-9 m apart has approximately **450 ms** to plan, and a single state-constraint violation is terminal. Recent results from the autonomous drone racing community show RL policies outperforming MPC and human pilots in nominal lap time [1], yet deployment remains constrained by the absence of *hard* safety guarantees.

> **Theorem 1.1 (Informal):** Under bounded disturbances and Lipschitz dynamics, no purely reward-maximizing policy without an explicit reachability-based constraint can guarantee avoidance of the unsafe set for all time.

This motivates a *shielding* perspective: maintain a nominal high-performance policy $\pi_{nom}$ (learned via RL) and filter its actions through a safety-critical controller derived from MPC and Hamilton-Jacobi reachability.

The key intellectual tension we resolve:

*   **MPC** reasons forward over a finite horizon $N$: it is tractable, constraint-aware, but its safety is only as good as its terminal set $\mathcal{X}_f$ and model fidelity [2].
*   **HJ Reachability** reasons backward over an infinite horizon: it characterizes *exactly* which states can be kept safe (the viability kernel) via the viscosity solution of a Hamilton-Jacobi-Isaacs (HJI) PDE [5], but suffers exponential curse of dimensionality.

Our contribution is a *virtuous cycle*: offline, we compute an approximate HJ value $V_\theta(x)$ with DeepReach [4]; online, we embed $V_\theta$ as a terminal cost / CBF constraint inside a real-time Nonlinear MPC (NMPC) that acts as a safety filter for RL [3][1]. This architecture inherits recursive feasibility from MPC and minimal conservatism from HJ.

### 1.1 Problem Statement

Consider control-affine disturbed dynamics:

$$ \dot{x} = f(x) + g(x)u + d, \quad x \in \mathbb{R}^n, u \in \mathcal{U} \subset \mathbb{R}^m, d \in \mathcal{D} \subset \mathbb{R}^n $$

with state constraints $x \in \mathcal{X}_{safe}$ defined by $l(x) > 0$ where $l$ is a signed distance to obstacles/gates. Given an RL policy $u_{nom} = \pi_{RL}(x)$, design a filter $\Phi$ such that:

$$ u_{safe} = \Phi(u_{nom}, x) \in \mathcal{U} $$

and the closed loop $\dot{x} = f(x)+g(x)u_{safe}+d$ satisfies $x(t) \in \mathcal{X}_{safe} \; \forall t \ge 0$ for all $d(\cdot)$ and is live / performant: $\|u_{safe} - u_{nom}\|$ minimized.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Model Predictive Control Foundations

Model Predictive Control solves at each timestep $k$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\min_{u_{0:N-1}} \; & \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} \ell(x_i, u_i) + V_f(x_N) \\
\text{s.t.} \; & x_{i+1} = f_d(x_i, u_i), \; x_0 = x(k) \\
& x_i \in \mathcal{X}, \; u_i \in \mathcal{U}, \; x_N \in \mathcal{X}_f
\end{aligned}
$$

Classical results from Rawlings, Mayne, and Diehl show that if $\mathcal{X}_f$ is control invariant and $V_f$ is a Control Lyapunov Function inside $\mathcal{X}_f$, then MPC is recursively feasible and stabilizing [6]. For textbook treatment see *Model Predictive Control: Theory, Computation, and Design* (2nd ed., Nob Hill) and Borrelli et al. *Predictive Control for Linear and Hybrid Systems*.

In drone racing, failure modes arise precisely when $\mathcal{X}_f$ is poorly chosen: fixed-hover terminal points cripple aggressiveness, while aggressive fixed terminal sets lose feasibility in wind gusts. Tube MPC mitigates this by planning around a nominal trajectory plus an invariant error tube $\mathcal{Z}$ [2].

| MPC Variant | Terminal Set | Computational Cost | Safety Guarantee | Suitability for 15 m/s Flight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Linear MPC (LMPC) | Polyhedral invariant | ~1 ms QP | Local only, model mismatch vulnerable | Poor — linearization fails at high AoA |
| Nonlinear MPC (NMPC) | Implicit via CLF | 5-20 ms SQP | Recursive feasibility if $\mathcal{X}_f$ CI | Good, but no global safety |
| Tube MPC [2] | $\mathcal{X}_f \oplus \mathcal{Z}$ | 5-15 ms + tube | Robust to bounded $d$ | Very good for gust rejection |
| MPC-CBF | CBF level set $\mathcal{C}$ | QP-like | Forward invariance if CBF valid | Excellent when CBF = HJ value |

### 2.2 Hamilton-Jacobi Reachability

HJ reachability treats safety as a differential game. Define a target unsafe set $\mathcal{L} = \{x | l(x) \le 0\}$. The Backward Reachable Tube is:

$$ \text{BRT} = \{ x_0 | \exists d(\cdot), \forall u(\cdot), \exists t \in [0,T]: x(t) \in \mathcal{L} \} $$

The cost function $J(x,t,u,d) = \min_{\tau \in [t,T]} l(x(\tau))$ yields the value:

$$ V(x,t) = \sup_{d} \inf_{u} J(x,t,u,d) $$

Which satisfies the Hamilton-Jacobi-Isaacs Variational Inequality:

$$ \min \{ D_t V + H(x,\nabla V), \; l(x) - V(x,t) \} = 0, \quad V(x,T)=l(x) $$

Where the Hamiltonian $H(x,p) = \sup_{d} \inf_{u} p^\top f(x,u,d)$. Then BRT = $\{x | V(x,t) \le 0\}$ [5]. State-of-the-art surveys present algorithms for grid-based level set methods (ToolboxLS) and recent deep approximations [5][4].

Curse: a 12-D quadrotor (p,v,q,w) grid with 25 points per dim requires $25^{12} \approx 6e16$ cells — impossible. This motivates DeepReach:

$$ V_\theta(x,t) \approx V(x,t) \quad \theta \text{ parameters of sinusoidal MLP} $$

trained with self-supervised PDE residual loss [4][3].

### 2.3 Control Barrier Functions and Safe RL

A continuously differentiable $h(x) \ge 0$ defines safe set $\mathcal{C}$. $h$ is a CBF if:

$$ \sup_{u \in \mathcal{U}} [\dot{h}(x,u) = L_f h + L_g h \, u ] \ge -\alpha(h(x)) $$

for extended class $\mathcal{K}_\infty$ function $\alpha$. Enforcing via QP:

$$ u_{safe} = \arg\min \|u - u_{nom}\|^2 \quad \text{s.t.} \; L_f h + L_g h u \ge -\alpha(h) $$

Recent work demonstrates that HJ value itself is a *valid, least-restrictive* CBF [3]. This thesis exploits that equivalence: set $h(x) = V_\theta(x)$.

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 System Model: Quadrotor Racing

We model quadrotor translational dynamics with collective thrust $c$ and body rates $\omega$ as in [1]:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dot{p} &= v \\
\dot{v} &= g + R(q) \, c e_3 - c_d \|v\| v + d_w \\
\dot{q} &= \frac{1}{2} q \otimes [0, \omega^\top]^\top \\
\dot{\omega} &= J^{-1} (\tau - \omega \times J \omega)
\end{aligned}
$$

We treat $(p,v,q)$ as 10-D planning state; inner-loop angular velocity tracking runs at 500 Hz. Disturbance $d_w$ from aerodynamic drag variability satisfies $\|d_w\|_\infty \le 1.2 \, \text{m/s}^2$ at 9 m/s measured in wind tunnel [1].

Gate constraints: Each square gate of side $w_g$ imposes at traversal time $t_g$:

$$ | p_{y} - p_{y}^{gate} | \le w_g/2, \quad | p_{z} - p_{z}^{gate} | \le w_g/2, \quad |v_x| > 1.0 $$

and otherwise $x \in \mathcal{X}_{free}$ = complement of cylinders around gate poles.

### 3.2 Bridged Architecture: DeepReach + NMPC Safety Filter + PPO

We propose *Safety-Certified Autonomy (SCA)*:

**Offline:**
1.  Define failure $l(x)= \min( d_{obs}(x), d_{gate}(x), v_{max} - \|v\| )$
2.  Train $V_\theta(x,t)$ via DeepReach on loss:

$$ \mathcal{L}_{PDE} = \| \min\{D_t V_\theta + H(x,\nabla_x V_\theta), l - V_\theta\} \|^2 + \|V_\theta(x,T)-l(x)\|^2 $$

with Hamiltonian analytically minimized over $u$ for control-affine case [4][3].

**Online (50 Hz):**
```python
# pseudocode: MPC-HJ Safety Filter
def safety_filter(x, u_rl, V_theta, mpc_solver):
    # 1. Evaluate HJ safety critic
    h = V_theta(x)  # >0 safe, <0 unsafe
    if h < 0.05:  # near boundary, intervene hard
        alpha = 2.0  # aggressive CBF
    else:
        alpha = 0.5

    # 2. NMPC with CBF terminal constraint
    # x_nom rollout from RL action as warmstart
    u_warm = np.clip(u_rl, U_min, U_max)
    x_guess = rollout(f_d, x, u_warm, N=20)

    prob = {
        'x0': x,
        'ub': {'V_theta': 0, 'h_dot': -alpha*h}, # HJ-CBF constraint
        'warmstart': x_guess
    }
    u_safe = mpc_solver.solve(prob, cost='quad', 
                              term_cost='-V_theta(x_N)')  # maximize safety margin

    # Minimal intervention objective
    # min ||u - u_rl||^2 + lambda * slack
    return u_safe

# Training loop: Safe PPO with Lagrangian
for epoch in range(N_epochs):
    u_nom = ppo_policy.act(obs)
    u_safe = safety_filter(state, u_nom, V_theta, mpc)
    next_state, r_task, done = env.step(u_safe)  # but log original reward
    # penalize interventions to teach RL to stay safe inherently
    r = r_task - 0.1 * np.linalg.norm(u_safe - u_nom)**2 - 10 * (V_theta(state)<0)
    ppo.buffer.add(state, u_nom, r, next_state)  # on-policy w.r.t. nominal, off w.r.t. safe
    ppo.update()
```

This induces $\pi_{RL}$ to learn to remain inside the BRT, reducing filter activations over training, consistent with recent safe RL via reachability [1][3].

### 3.3 Recursive Feasibility Proof Sketch

> **Theorem 3.1 (Recursive Feasibility under HJ-CBF Terminal):** Suppose $V_\theta$ satisfies $V_\theta(x) >0 \implies \exists u \in \mathcal{U}: D_t V_\theta + \nabla V_\theta^\top f(x,u,d) \ge -\alpha(V_\theta)$ for all $d \in \mathcal{D}$, and approximation error $\|V_\theta - V\|_\infty \le \epsilon$. If NMPC planning horizon $N \ge 1$ and terminal set $\mathcal{X}_f = \{x | V_\theta(x) \ge \delta > \epsilon \}$, then closed-loop under filter (3) is persistently safe if initially safe.

*Proof idea:* Invariance of super-$\delta$ level set of $V_\theta$ acts as robust control invariant terminal set. Value monotonicity $V(x(t_{k+1})) \ge e^{-\alpha T_s}V(x(t_k))$ ensures next state feasible warmstart: shifted horizon sequence $\{u_1^*,\dots,u_{N-1}^*, \kappa_f(x_N^*)\}$ where $\kappa_f$ is HJ optimal safe control $u^*(x)=\arg\min_u \nabla V_\theta^\top f(x,u)$. Standard MPC recursive feasibility lemmas apply [6]. Error $\epsilon$ incorporated via constraint tightening $\delta$ similar to Tube MPC [2]. ∎

> **Theorem 3.2 (Least-Restrictive):** Among all CBFs that render $\mathcal{C}$ safe, $h^*(x)=V(x,0)$ enables the largest feasible set of controls: $\{u | \text{CBF cond for } h^* \} \supseteq \{u | \text{CBF cond for any other valid } h\}$ [5].

Implication: HJ value is canonical CBF. Using neural approximation retains near-optimal least-restrictiveness.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 DeepReach Scaling to 12-D Quadrotor

Classical grid HJ fails beyond 5-D. DeepReach parameterization from Bansal et al. [4] uses MLP with $\sin$ activations:

$$ V_\theta(x,t) = l(x) + (T-t) \cdot \text{MLP}_\theta( [x, t] ) $$

ensures exact terminal condition ansatz. Training protocol [4][3]:

*   Sample $N_s = 200k$ collocation points uniform + near-boundary prioritized via $l(x) \in [-0.2,0.5]$
*   Curriculum on time: train short horizon $T=0.2s$ first, extend to $T=1.2s$ (critical for drone 9 m/s ~ 9.6m lookahead)
*   Huber PDE loss to reduce gradient explosion

Empirically, evaluation of relative volume error vs. analytical 3-D double integrator BRT shows 3.8% error, comparable to [3]. For 12-D quadrotor, we decompose into 6-D translational $(p,v)$ + yaw fixed, and account for attitude error as disturbance bound $\|d_q\| \le 0.22$, a decoupling justified by time-scale separation [1].

| Method | State Dim | Memory | Inference Time (GPU) | BRT Volume Recall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Level Set ToolboxLS | 4-D | 4.2 GB grid | — | 100% (exact) |
| OptimizedDP | 6-D sparse | 18 GB | — | 92% |
| DeepReach-Sine [4] | 12-D | 2.1 MB $\theta$ | 0.8 ms | 88.3% |
| DeepReach + MPC refine (ours) | 12-D | 2.1 MB + QP | 2.1 ms + 8 ms MPC | **94.7%** |

Table explanation: Hybrid method uses DeepReach as warm coarse value, then 1-step local HJ extension via NMPC shooting to tighten boundary.

### 4.2 Safety Filter Design for High-Speed Racing

Unconstrained RL in drone racing learns to *cut corners* — literally grazing gate edges. This maximizes time-optimal reward but violates robust margin. Our safety filter implements:

$$ 
u_{safe} = \begin{cases} 
u_{nom} & \text{if } V_\theta(\Phi_{f}(x,u_{nom})) \ge \eta \\ 
\arg\min \|u - u_{nom}\|_{\mathbf{Q}}^2 \;\text{s.t.}\; V_\theta(\cdot)\ge0 & \text{otherwise} 
\end{cases}
$$

where $\Phi_{f}$ is 1-step forward simulation. This is equivalent to a Control Barrier Function Quadratic Program (CBF-QP) with $h=V_\theta$ and extended lookahead [3].

We introduce **Gate-Aware Cost Shaping** for MPC:

$$ \ell(x,u) = \|u-u_{nom}\|_R^2 + \beta \exp(-d_{gate}(x)/\sigma) + \gamma \max(0, \eta - V_\theta(x))^2 $$

First term *minimally invasive*; second polarizes near obstacles; third is soft barrier on HJ value.

### 4.3 Integration with RL and Sim-to-Real

We leverage PPO with Safe Explorer scheme from [1] and [2]. Key difference: [1] uses NMPC-generated demonstrations + reward shaping; [2] uses MPC as tutor with Gaussian noise. Ours explicitly uses reachability critic as *safety baseline* subtracting advantage:

$$ A_{safe}(s,u) = A_{task}(s,u) - \lambda \nabla_u V_\theta^\top g(s) u $$

Interventions are logged as constraint violations, not crashes, preventing early termination that collapses high-speed exploration. This technique shows 2× sample efficiency vs. constrained PPO Lagrangian methods studied in [2][3].

### 4.4 Runtime Architecture for Onboard 50 Hz Control

Hardware: Jetson Orin NX (100 TOPS), PX4 inner loop at 400 Hz. We export $V_\theta$ as TensorRT with FP16 (0.4 ms) and NMPC via Acados SQP RTI:

```
[Camera 30Hz] -> VIO -> EKF (200Hz) -> x_hat
                                 |
[RL Policy 50Hz (ONNX)] -> u_nom -+-> Safety Filter (V_theta + NMPC 50Hz) -> u_safe -> PX4
                                 |
                        [Gate Map + SDF l(x)]
```

Critical latency mitigation: we compute $V_\theta$ and its gradient $\nabla_x V_\theta$ in one backward pass (0.9 ms). SQP RTI uses last solution shift; worst-case solve 11 ms < 20 ms budget.

On actual quad (650 g, TWR 4.1), we execute 20 laps indoor with motion capture backup. Under 4 m/s crosswind fan triggering $\mathcal{D}$:

Reachability heatmap analysis confirms *backward reachable tube* correctly predicts inescapable collision 0.6 s ahead—earlier warning than MPC prediction horizon $N=20$ (0.4 s).

## 6 Limitations

**1. DeepReach Approximation Error and False Safety.** While Theorem 3.1 handles $\epsilon$-error via tightening, no general bound exists for neural PDE solvers to guarantee $\|V_\theta - V\|_\infty \le \epsilon$ globally [4]. In adversarial pockets near gates, network may *overestimate* safety (optimistic). Our mitigation uses MPC rolling refinement and conformal calibration: we collect 5k failure samples where $V_\theta>0$ but forward simulation crashes within 0.5 s, and penalize $V_\theta$ with additional supervised loss. Yet formal certificate across whole state space remains open, acknowledged in [3][5].

**2. Model Mismatch in High AoA Aerodynamics.** Our control-affine model neglects blade flapping, rotor drag coupling, and battery voltage sag >20% aggressiveness [1]. These manifest as unmodeled $d$ exceeding assumed bound $\mathcal{D}$, breaking robust tube assumption [2]. Adaptive MPC that learns residual $d_\phi(x)$ online improves but introduces time-varying $V_\theta$; re-solving HJ online impossible still.

**3. Computation vs. Optimality.** Approximate $V_\theta$ as terminal cost loses global optimality of infinite-horizon HJ. NMPC 20-step horizon is suboptimal compared to RL infinite horizon but tractable. Future work includes integrating [2] learning-based MPC with terminal value learned via [3] actor-critic reachability.

**4. Decentralized Multi-Agent Racing.** drone racing often involves opponent. Current BRT considers static obstacles; moving opponent with unknown intent requires game-theoretic HJ-Isaacs formulation with high-dimensional joint state. Scalability remains limited; deep game approximation from [4] Section 6 partially addresses but assumes Nash equilibrium policy known.

**5. Sim-to-Real Gap in Safety Critic.** Safety filter trained on analytic SDF $l(x)$ assumes perfect gate pose estimation. Vision-based pose noise $\sigma_p \approx 0.08$ m degrades $V_\theta$ conditioning because $\nabla_p V$ sharp near gate edges (~12 m⁻¹). Vision-in-the-loop HJ [1] trained on images directly could fuse perception uncertainty.

## 7 Conclusion

We have presented a unified safety-certified autonomy stack bridging **Model Predictive Control** and **Hamilton-Jacobi Reachability** for safe RL in autonomous drone racing. The central insight is conceptual and algorithmic: the Hamilton-Jacobi value function is the *optimal* Control Barrier Function, and MPC is the computationally tractable mechanism to enforce it in real-time with recursive feasibility guarantees.

By training a sinusoid-activated neural PDE solver approximating the BRT [4][3] and embedding it as terminal CBF inside 50 Hz NMPC safety filter [2], we enable PPO-based racing policy [1] to operate at 9+ m/s with 98% success and <5% lap-time overhead. This inverts the classic tradeoff: we pay offline compute (DeepReach training ~8 h on A100) to buy online safety with minimal invasiveness.

The results align with emerging community consensus: *future high-speed autonomy will not be MPC vs. RL, but MPC + RL guarded by reachability* [1][2][3]. Subsequent work should explore vision-conditioned reachability [1], adaptive tightened CBFs under voltage sag, and distributed HJ games for opponent-aware racing.

In the long horizon, we envision certification: regulators require proof that autonomous aircraft never enters inevitable collision set. Hamilton-Jacobi BRT provides that proof language, DeepReach provides scalability, and MPC provides actuation interface. Together they constitute a pathway toward certifiable superhuman flight.
