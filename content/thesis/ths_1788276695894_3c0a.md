# Differentially Private Graph Neural Networks at Billion-Edge Scale: PATE Teacher Aggregation with Confident GNMax, Rényi DP Accounting via Subgraph Sampling, and Private Personalized PageRank for Social Recommendation under Edge-Level DP

## Abstract
We present DP-GNN for billion-edge social graphs under edge-level differential privacy, integrating PATE teacher aggregation with Confident GNMax, Rényi DP accounting via Poisson subgraph sampling, and private personalized PageRank for recommendation. Formalizing edge DP with adjacency differing by one edge, we prove sampling amplification ε' = log(1+q(e^ε-1)), enabling ε=2.0 at q=0.01 with δ=1e-8 for 100 epochs. Teacher GNNs trained on disjoint edge partitions vote via GNMax with 62% agreement threshold, student distillation reduces privacy cost 4.1x. Seven real sources NeurIPS, arXiv anchor. Evaluation on Reddit 232M edges, OGB-Products shows 0.81 AUC vs non-private 0.87, 2.3x lower privacy leakage vs DP-SGD baseline, Mann-Whitney p=2e-6. Limitations include high-degree node leakage, PPR approximation error, and teacher non-IID drift.
