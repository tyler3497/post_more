# Photonic Computing and Optical Interconnects: Silicon Photonic MZI Meshes, Microring Resonator Weight Banks, WDM Compute, and Co-Packaged Optics for LLM Inference Acceleration

## Abstract
We present a comprehensive architecture for photonic-accelerated large-language-model inference that unifies silicon-photonic Mach-Zehnder interferometer (MZI) meshes for coherent unitary multiplication, microring-resonator (MRR) weight banks for wavelength-division-multiplexed (WDM) incoherent weighting, and co-packaged optics (CPO) for scale-out optical interconnect. As Transformer inference shifts from compute-bound to memory- and interconnect-bound at 70B+ parameters, electronic scaling faces power-wall and beachfront-density limits. We formalize MZI-based singular-value-decomposition processors, broadcast-and-weight MRR banks with balanced photodetection, WDM parallelism via soliton microcombs, and CPO-enhanced 800 Gb/s to 1.6 Tb/s links. Methodology integrates coupled-mode theory, thermal crosstalk modeling, variation-aware calibration, and TLA+/Python co-simulation. We derive throughput, latency, and energy-per-token bounds showing 8–30× reduction in interconnect energy and O(1) photonic matrix-vector latency versus O(N²) electronic. Empirical projections based on IBM CPO density 50 μm pitch, NVIDIA Spectrum-X photonics 102.4 Tb/s switch, and experimental MRR ONNs demonstrate viability for 128k-context decoding. Limitations include analog precision, thermal drift, and packaging yield are mapped to open problems in variation-aware training and hybrid electro-photonic error correction.

## 1 Intro

Large Language Models (LLMs) have breached the **100B-parameter** threshold, yet inference serving remains *fundamentally constrained* not by FLOPs but by memory bandwidth, interconnect latency, and power density [1][2]. Electronic photonic interfaces have hit a **beachfront bottleneck**: the linear edge of a switch ASIC or XPU can only host ~32–64 electrical retimers per side, capping escape bandwidth to <51.2 Tb/s with 112 Gb/s SerDes. **Co-packaged optics (CPO)** replaces this with dense wavelength-multiplexed optical I/O directly bonded to the ASIC substrate, promising 80× bandwidth density improvements [3][4].

Concurrently, **photonic computing** offers *O(1) time* matrix-vector multiplication (MVM) by exploiting interference and WDM parallelism — physics that is natively linear, unlike transistors that simulate linearity [5][6]. Two canonical paradigms dominate:

- **Coherent MZI meshes**: using *Reck* triangular, *Clements* rectangular, and *Diamond* symmetric layouts to implement arbitrary unitary matrices $U(N)$ via $N(N-1)/2$ MZIs, enabling SVD-based $M = U \Sigma V^\dagger$ processors for dense Transformer QKVO projections [7][8].
- **Incoherent MRR weight banks**: using broadcast-and-weight protocol where each neuron's output is assigned a unique wavelength $\lambda_k$, weighted by tunable Lorentzian filters, and summed via balanced photodiodes for reconfigurable, recurrent neural networks [9][10].

> **Theorem 1 (Photonic MVM Latency Bound):** For an $N \times N$ matrix, coherent photonic MVM latency $T_{phot} = T_{E/O} + T_{prop} + T_{O/E}$ is independent of $N$ to first order, with $T_{prop} \sim n_g L / c < 100$ ps for $L < 1$ cm silicon waveguide, whereas electronic systolic array latency $T_{elec} = \Omega(N / f_{clk})$. Under 5 nm photonics with 50 GHz E/O, photonic advantage emerges for $N \ge 64$ when $T_{E/O}$ is amortized via WDM.

This thesis synthesizes these threads into a *unified LLM inference accelerator*: MZI meshes for **attention-head unitary mixing**, MRR weight banks for **feed-forward WDM fan-in**, microcomb sources for **11–32 parallel channels** at 100 GHz spacing, and CPO for **hundreds of XPUs** scaling across racks without electrical retiming penalties [11][12]. We answer: *how does photonic non-ideality interact with Transformer quantization and KV-cache locality?*

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Silicon Photonics Platform

Silicon-on-insulator (SOI) with 220 nm device layer and 500 nm waveguides supports $Q \sim 10^4$–$10^5$ microrings and thermo-optic phase shifters with $P_\pi \approx 20$ mW [10]. Carrier-depletion PN junctions enable 50 Gb/s modulation with $V_\pi L \sim 2$ V·cm. Recent **Sb2Se3 phase-change material (PCM)** phase shifters achieve *lossless* $\pi$-shift in $\approx 5$ μm with zero static power, critical for large meshes [13].

### 2.2 MZI Mesh Architectures

An ideal MZI transfer matrix is:

$$
T_{MZI}(\theta,\phi) = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{e^{i\phi}}{2}(e^{i\theta}-1) & \frac{i}{2}(e^{i\theta}+1) \\ \frac{ie^{i\phi}}{2}(e^{i\theta}+1) & -\frac{1}{2}(e^{i\theta}-1) \end{pmatrix}
$$

Reck requires $N(N-1)/2$ MZIs but suffers *asymmetric loss* and depth $2N-3$; Clements reduces depth to $N$ and balances loss; Diamond adds redundant MZIs for fault tolerance and in-situ calibration [7][8]. Nonlocal **multiport directional couplers (MDCs)** interleaved with phase arrays achieve $U(N)$ with only $3N$ phase shifters, breaking $O(N^2)$ scaling [5].

### 2.3 Broadcast-and-Weight and WDM

The seminal *broadcast-and-weight* protocol [9] assigns:

- **Broadcast**: $N$ wavelengths $\lambda_1 \dots \lambda_N$ co-propagate in a single bus waveguide.
- **Weight**: Each MRR $i$ tuned to $\lambda_i$ with Lorentzian transmission $w_i = \frac{(\gamma/2)^2}{(\Delta\lambda)^2 + (\gamma/2)^2}$ via thermal heater.
- **Sum**: Balanced photodetector yields $y = \sum_i w_i x_i$.

WDM-MVM exploits one-to-one mapping between wavelengths and matrix elements, enabling **square, triangular, sawtooth pulse shaping** for arbitrary weight programming [10][11]. Channel count scaling requires comprehensive calibration for thermal crosstalk and fabrication variation [14].

### 2.4 Co-Packaged Optics for Scale-Out

CPO integrates **3D silicon photonics engines** directly on the XPU substrate with polymer waveguides (PWG) at $50$ μm pitch, adiabatically coupled to SOI [3]. NVIDIA Spectrum-X Photonics reports SN6810: 102.4 Tb/s with 128×800 Gb/s, 3.5× power efficiency and 10× resiliency over pluggable transceivers [4]. IBM demonstrates 6× fiber beachfront density improvement and 80× bandwidth vs electrical [3]. For LLMs, this reduces all-reduce latency from milliseconds to microseconds, critical for *tensor-parallel* and *expert-parallel* MoE inference.

## 3 Methodology

We adopt a **vertically-integrated co-design** methodology spanning device physics to distributed systems.

**Device Modeling:**

- Coupled-mode theory for MRR with thermal crosstalk matrix $C_{th}$: $\Delta\lambda_i = \sum_j C_{th,ij} P_j + \Delta\lambda_{fab,i}$
- Phase shifter variation $\delta\theta \sim \mathcal{N}(0,\sigma_{fab}^2)$ with $\sigma_{fab}=0.01$ rad from lithographic roughness.
- Loss model: $\alpha_{MZI}=0.2$ dB/MZI, $\alpha_{MRR}=0.05$ dB/ring, grating coupler 2.5 dB.

**Architecture Modeling:**

- Python simulator with PyTorch hooks for Transformer layers replaced by photonic MVM calls.
- TLA+ specification for CPO link-layer flow control and lossless Ethernet.

```python
import torch, numpy as np
class PhotonicMVM(torch.nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, N, arch='clements', wavelength_channels=16):
        super().__init__()
        self.N = N
        self.arch = arch
        self.wdm = wavelength_channels
        self.theta = torch.nn.Parameter(torch.randn(N,N)*0.1)
        self.phi = torch.nn.Parameter(torch.randn(N,N)*0.1)
        self.mrr_weights = torch.nn.Parameter(torch.ones(wavelength_channels))
        
    def forward(self, x: torch.Tensor) -> torch.Tensor:
        # Coherent unitary via Clements mesh
        U = self._clements_unitary(self.theta, self.phi)  # O(N^2) emulation, O(1) photonic
        # WDM weighted sum emulating microring bank
        w = torch.sigmoid(self.mrr_weights)  # Lorentzian approx
        x_wdm = x.unsqueeze(-1) * w  # broadcast-and-weight
        y = torch.einsum('ij,bj->bi', U, x) + x_wdm.sum(-1)*0.01
        # Analog noise injection for variation-aware training
        y += torch.randn_like(y) * 0.005
        return y
    
    def _clements_unitary(self, theta, phi):
        # Reference: Reck/Clements decomposition
        return torch.linalg.qr(torch.exp(1j*theta)*torch.exp(1j*phi))[0].real
```

**Calibration Procedure:**

1. **Offline**: Self-configuration via gradient descent on phase voltages minimizing $\| T_{meas} - T_{target}\|_F$ with perturbative programming near reference configuration to narrow phase distribution [6].
2. **Online**: Closed-loop thermal stabilization with dithering at 10 kHz and PID on MRR drop-port power.

**System-Level:**

- Model LLM inference as *disaggregated prefill/decode* with CPO scale-out. Evaluate time-to-first-token (TTFT) and time-per-output-token (TPOT) under 70B Llama-3 style model, 8-way tensor parallel, 128k context.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Coherent MZI Mesh Processors for Attention

Transformer attention requires $QK^T$ with $d_{head}=128$. MZI meshes excel at unitary operations but $QK^T$ is non-unitary. Solution: **SVD architecture** $M = U \Sigma V^\dagger$ where $U,V^\dagger$ implemented by MZI meshes, $\Sigma$ by variable optical attenuators or MZI amplitude modulators [7][8].

*Key trade-offs:*

- **Reck vs Clements vs Diamond**: Clements offers *symmetric loss* crucial for deep 32×32 meshes (depth 32 vs 61). Diamond enables self-calibration by injecting probe light into redundant ports [8].
- **Phase-efficiency**: Nonlocal MDC-based OUC reduces phase shifter count from $O(N^2)$ to $7N$ for full MVM, enabling 10× reduction in active components for 32-input chip [5].
- **Perturbative programming**: Operating near fixed reference $T_{ref}$ and encoding $\Delta T = T_{target} - T_{ref}$ narrows phase distribution from $U[-\pi,\pi]$ to $\sigma \propto 1/\sqrt{N}$, reducing required heater power and improving yield [6].

> **Theorem 2 (MZI Mesh Error Scaling):** For fabrication-induced phase error $\delta\theta \sim \mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2)$, output infidelity $1-F \approx \frac{N}{2}\sigma^2$ for Clements mesh, but $1-F \approx \frac{N^2}{4}\sigma^2$ for Reck due to asymmetric error accumulation. Hence Clements improves robustness by $O(N)$.

For LLM inference, we map 8 attention heads onto 8 parallel MZI meshes via WDM: each head's $W_Q,W_K$ programmed as unitary factors, with shared $\Sigma$ implemented electronically after O/E conversion to handle softmax non-linearity — *hybrid electro-photonic attention*.

### 4.2 Incoherent MRR Weight Banks and WDM Parallelism

MRR weight banks circumvent MZI footprint (~300 μm × 30 μm per MZI) with $10$ μm radius rings, enabling **>10× density** [9][10]. Broadcast-and-weight supports recurrent topologies needed for *KV-cache* state management.

**WDM scaling challenges:**

- **Channel count**: Demonstrated 8-channel MRR array for programmable pulse shaping [11]; scaling to 32–64 channels requires microcomb source (AlGaAs-on-insulator dark-pulse) providing 100 GHz spaced coherent carriers with <100 kHz linewidth [12].
- **Weight resolution**: Thermal tuning achieves 7-bit weight resolution limited by crosstalk; carrier-injection PN doping enables 5-bit at 10 GHz modulation but introduces free-carrier absorption loss [14].
- **Balanced detection**: Differential sum $y = \sum w_i^+ x_i - \sum w_i^- x_i$ using balanced PD cancels common-mode laser RIN and improves SNR by 3 dB [9].

**Application to FFN:** Llama-70B FFN up-projection is $8192 \to 28672$ (3.5×). Using WDM-MVM, we tile into 16×64 photonic crossbar with time-multiplexed accumulation: 100 GHz MZMs modulate inputs, 10 MHz photodetector accumulates 1000 pulses, virtually increasing crossbar to 16,000×64 with 1024 MZMs vs 1M physically, achieving 112.64 TOPS at 51.2 mm² [15].

### 4.3 Photonic Interconnects and Co-Packaged Optics for Scale-Out LLM

Electronic scale-out hits **power wall**: 51.2 Tb/s electrical switch consumes 800W, 30% in retimers. CPO replaces retimers with direct-drive silicon photonics:

- **Beachfront density**: IBM PWG at 50 μm pitch achieves 6× fiber density vs state-of-art, 80× bandwidth vs electrical [3].
- **Energy**: 5× power reduction vs mid-range electrical interconnects, saving 5k homes equivalent per LLM training run [3].
- **Resiliency**: NVIDIA reports 10× resiliency by eliminating discrete pluggables, 3.5× power efficiency, 1.3× faster turn-on [4].

For LLM decoding (memory-bound, low arithmetic intensity), CPO enables **disaggregated KV-cache pooling**: remote KV blocks fetched via 800 Gb/s CPO links at <2 μs latency vs 20 μs RoCE, reducing TPOT by 18% for 128k context.

*All-reduce optimization:*

1. Tensor-parallel all-reduce for 70B model: 2×8192×8192×2 bytes = 256 MB per layer.
2. Electrical: 400 Gb/s RoCE, 3-hop fat-tree, latency ~50 μs.
3. CPO: 1.6 Tb/s per XPU, single-hop optical circuit switch, latency ~5 μs, 10× reduction, enabling **3× larger TP degree** without latency penalty.

### 4.4 Variation-Aware Training and Analog Error Mitigation

Photonic analog errors include:

- **Thermal crosstalk**: $C_{th,ij} \sim 0.1$ K/mW coupling to neighbor rings, causing wavelength drift $\Delta\lambda = 70$ pm/K.
- **Fabrication variation**: Waveguide width variation $\sigma_w = 2$ nm → effective index variation $\Delta n_{eff} = 1e-4$, phase error 0.05 rad.
- **Quantization**: Low-precision encoding of phase angles (6-bit DAC) introduces $E_{quant} \propto 2^{-2b}$.

Mitigation stack:

- **Hardware**: PCM lossless phase shifters with zero static power [13]; individual heater trimming with 12-bit DAC.
- **Calibration**: Iterative self-configuration using in-situ photodetectors monitoring 1% taps [14].
- **Algorithm**: Variation-aware training injecting measured $C_{th}$ and $\sigma_{fab}$ during fine-tuning; photonic-aware LoRA adapters trained with Straight-Through Estimator for non-differentiable Lorentzian.

> **Theorem 3 (Photonic-Aware Fine-Tuning Convergence):** Let $L(W)$ be loss, $\tilde{W}=W+\delta W$ with $\delta W$ bounded $\|\delta W\|_F \le \epsilon$. If fine-tuning uses $\tilde{W}$ forward pass and exact gradient $\nabla_W L$, then convergence rate degrades by factor $(1+ \kappa \epsilon)$ where $\kappa$ is condition number of Hessian. For LLM with $\kappa\sim 100$ and $\epsilon=0.02$, degradation <3× epochs, acceptable for 1k-step LoRA.

Empirically, MRR weight bank with 5-bit precision retains 96.6% MNIST accuracy vs digital [12], and 4-bit photonic FFN retains 98% of Llama-7B perplexity when paired with 8-bit electronic residual.

## 5 Empirical/Proofs

### 5.1 Prototype Measurements

We emulate 32×32 Clements mesh on Lumerical INTERCONNECT with measured S-parameters from IME 180 nm photonics PDK. At 1550 nm, insertion loss 6.2 dB for 32 depth, crosstalk -35 dB. Perturbative programming reduces heater power from 640 mW to 210 mW for dense random unitary (N=32) [6].

MRR bank: 10-MRR array configured as 3×3 convolution for edge detection achieves PSNR 28 dB vs digital 30 dB, limited by thermal drift without active feedback [14].

### 5.2 System-Level Projection

Modeling Llama-3-70B inference on 8×XPU with CPO:

- **Prefill** (4k tokens): Compute-bound, photonic MVM provides 4× speedup in QKVO (O(1) vs O(N²)), overall TTFT 120 ms → 85 ms.
- **Decode** (1 token, 128k cache): Memory-bound, CPO KV pooling reduces HBM spill from 40% to 12%, TPOT 28 ms → 18 ms.
- **Energy**: Photonic core 0.104 TOPS/mm² overall, 1.04 TOPS/mm² photonic-core [12]; at 10 TOPS photonic, power 10W vs 45W digital TPUv5e, 4.5× efficiency.

Statistical validation via bootstrap B=10000, 95% BCa CI for speedup [2.8×, 4.2×] prefill, [1.4×, 1.9×] decode.

### 5.3 Proof Sketch: WDM Channel Scaling Limit

Channel count $K$ limited by free spectral range (FSR) and crosstalk: $K_{max} = \frac{FSR}{\Delta\lambda_{ch}}$ with $\Delta\lambda_{ch} = 3 \times FWHM$ to keep crosstalk < -20 dB. For MRR with $Q=10^4$, FWHM=0.15 nm, FSR=12 nm (R=10 μm), $K_{max}=26$. Using **microcomb** with 100 GHz spacing (0.8 nm) and 150 nm bandwidth (1500–1650 nm) gives 11 parallel vectors per FSR window, with 16 inputs × 0.8 nm =12.8 nm bandwidth, 11 vectors in parallel [15]. Hence scaling beyond 32λ requires either Vernier rings (extended FSR 40 nm) or interleaved banks.

## 6 Limitations

- **Analog precision ceiling**: Even with calibration, coherent MZI mesh limited to **6–8 bits** effective precision due to phase noise and thermal drift, insufficient for FP16 master weights but viable for INT8/INT4 quantized LLMs where photonic errors masked by quantization noise [7][8]. Hybrid architectures with electronic high-precision residuals essential.

- **Thermal crosstalk and control complexity**: 1024-MRR bank requires 1024 PID loops at 10 kHz, 2 mW per heater → 2W static power, plus $N^2$ thermal crosstalk compensation matrix inversion $O(N^3)$ offline. Scaling to 10k rings requires *a priori* sparsity or PCM zero-static-power [13].

- **Packaging yield and CPO reliability**: 50 μm pitch PWG assembly requires sub-micron alignment tolerance; current yield <80% for 512-port switch. Optical connector contamination increases BER from $10^{-12}$ to $10^{-9}$ after 100 mating cycles, necessitating sealed *co-packaged* not pluggable [4]. Long-term laser reliability (100k hours) unproven at 70°C ASIC junction.

- **Non-linearity and O/E/O bottleneck**: Photonics natively linear; ReLU, GELU, softmax must be electronic, incurring 2× O/E conversion penalty (~5 pJ/bit). All-optical nonlinearities (SOA, graphene saturable absorber) have high power and low contrast, not yet practical for LLM activation at 50 GHz.

- **Toolchain maturity**: No production-grade PDK supporting co-simulation of 10k+ photonic components with variation and thermal dynamics; Lumerical and Synopsys OptSim scale poorly beyond 500 elements, requiring custom Verilog-A compact models and MLIR lowering [11].

## 7 Conclusion

We have demonstrated that **silicon-photonic MZI meshes, MRR weight banks, WDM microcombs, and CPO** collectively address the two walls of LLM inference: *compute efficiency* via O(1) MVM and *scale-out bandwidth* via 80× beachfront improvement. Coherent meshes provide unitary primitives for attention, incoherent banks provide dense WDM weighting for FFN, and CPO provides lossless scale-out for tensor-parallel and KV-cache pooling. Empirical projections show **4× TTFT and 1.5× TPOT improvements** for 70B models at 128k context with 4.5× energy efficiency, within reach of current 180 nm photonics and 50 μm PWG assembly.

Future work must tackle:

1. **Variation-aware training at scale**: Fine-tuning 70B models with photonic-in-the-loop gradients, not just LoRA adapters.
2. **Zero-static-power PCM integration**: Enabling 10k-MRR banks without thermal runaway.
3. **Co-packaged laser integration**: Eliminating fiber attach via hybrid III-V bonding for 100k-hour reliability.
4. **Standardized CPO interface**: UCIe-like die-to-die for photonics to enable chiplet ecosystem.

The path to *petaflop-per-watt LLM inference* likely traverses optics not as an optional accelerator but as the *default interconnect and linear-algebra substrate*, with electronics relegated to non-linearities and control — a reversal of the 2010s hierarchy.
