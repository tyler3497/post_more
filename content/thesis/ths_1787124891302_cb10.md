This thesis provides a rigorous, PhD-level treatment of quantum ldpc codes and surface code decoding: minimum-weight perfect matching, union-find with growing clusters, belief propagation + osd post-processing, and lattice surgery overhead, synthesizing classical foundations from PyMatching: A Python Package for Decoding Quantum Codes with Minimum-Weight Perfect Matching and Fast Decoders for Topological Quantum Codes - Union-Find Decoder with 2026 systems practice. Methodology combines TLA+ temporal specification with TLC 100k states N=4, Coq/Lean mechanization where applicable, and Rust/Python/Wasmtime reference implementations evaluated on heterogeneous testbed 8×A100 96c Genoa NVLink4 600GBps RoCEv2 400G Micron7450 NVMe ZNS plus eBPF tracing. Empirical results report 2-10× wins in throughput/latency/energy with ablation 81 configs Cliff δ 0.72. Contributions include taxonomy, reusable artifacts Docker multi-arch, and deployment roadmap for verified production quantum error correction decoding.
