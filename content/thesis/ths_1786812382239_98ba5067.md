## 6 Limitations


*Current verification assumes **bounded loops***; unbounded data-dependent loops require helper `bpf_loop` not yet supported in Linux 6.8 eBPF verifier. Thermal drift in photonic systems degrades SNR beyond 85°C without active TEC compensation duty-cycle capped at 30%. BBS+ requires Type-III Pairing-friendly curve risking future quantum adversary via 128-bit discrete log; migration to SPHINCS+ recommended but 12× proof size.

*Hardware variance*: NTT unified butterfly for Kyber `q=3329` and Dilithium `q=8380417` incurs 12% frequency drop when sharing Montgomery multiplier across mismatched moduli due to carry overhead. Memory lift failure when `mmap`/`HMM` migration thrashes on 4K vs 2M huge pages; needs sub-64kB buffer.

*Privacy accounting lossiness*: RDP→(ε,δ)-DP conversion heavy-tailed PLDs exhibits 6-9% slack versus exact PLD at ε<0.8; residual masked via f-DP composition.

*Trust model exclusions*: Resistance to active bus interposition <$1k attacks excluded by Intel TDX threat model clause [4]; physical interposer on UCIe interposer violates 1e-27 BER guarantee unless lane-repaired.

*Coverage bounds*: Data-flow Shapley view reveals `merge` associativity proved only for non-TLS custom ADTs; TLS threaded storage unsafe.

We do not claim zero-overhead GC for WASM Component Model resource handles with `borrow` tracking escape beyond scope; `#[cfg(panic="abort")]` omitted.

Mapped to open: self-verifying EAT bundles removing platform-specific verifier code, recursive WIT composite runtime handling arbitrary directed-graph values, fully-checked CT-like coinductive proof in Lean4.
