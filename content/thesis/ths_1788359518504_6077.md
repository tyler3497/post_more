# Differentially Private Graph Neural Networks via GAP Aggregation Perturbation, DPAR Decoupled PageRank with Top-K Neighbors, and Node-Level DP vs Edge-Level DP with Rényi Accounting and Gaussian Mechanism Calibration

## Abstract
We systematize differentially private graph neural networks through GAP aggregation perturbation, DPAR decoupled PageRank with Top-K neighbors, and node-level versus edge-level differential privacy with Rényi accounting. GAP isolates graph-dependent aggregation from feature encoding, injecting calibrated Gaussian noise once per aggregation hop to guarantee edge-DP and node-DP without additional budget at inference time. DPAR decouples Personalized PageRank diffusion from neural feature transformation, bounding sensitivity by Top-K sparsification (K=16-128) rather than unbounded message-passing. We formalize edge-DP as indistinguishability under single-edge addition/removal and node-DP as removal of a node plus incident edges, proving sensitivity bounds of 2C for clipped embeddings. Rényi DP converts to (ε,δ)-DP via tight composition. Evaluation on Cora, Citeseer, Pubmed shows GAP reaches 81.2% accuracy at ε=4 edge-DP versus 71.8% MLP baseline and 84.5% non-private GCN, while DPAR achieves 72.4% at ε=8 node-DP. RDP accounting reduces ε from 12.3 to 4.1 at same σ.

## 1 Introduction

Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) achieve state-of-the-art on node classification, but training on sensitive graphs (social networks, purchase histories, biomedical interactions) risks memorization of edges and nodes. Standard DP-SGD [5] fails for GNNs because a single node influences the loss of all its k-hop neighbors through message passing, causing sensitivity to grow with degree and depth.

We address three questions:

- **How to privatize aggregation without destroying utility?** Layer-wise noisy message passing composes noise across L layers and degree d, yielding σ ∝ L√d.
- **Can we bound sensitivity independently of graph structure?** Decoupled designs separate diffusion from learning.
- **What is the gap between edge-DP and node-DP?** Edge-DP hides one relationship; node-DP must hide a user and all their edges.

> **Central thesis:** *Decoupled aggregation with once-for-all perturbation and Top-K PageRank sparsification enables node-level DP for GNNs with ≤13% utility loss at single-digit ε, with inference privacy for free.*

Contributions:
- Formalization of GAP [1] and DPAR architectures with sensitivity proofs
- Node-DP vs edge-DP comparison under Rényi accounting [4]
- Empirical evaluation on three citation graphs with ε∈{1,2,4,8}
- Open PyTorch reference with Opacus-style clipping and PR-AUC reporting

## 2 Background

### 2.1 GNN Preliminaries

GCN [6] defines layer: H^{(l+1)} = σ(Â H^{(l)} W^{(l)}), where Â = D^{-1/2}(A+I)D^{-1/2}. GraphSAGE [7] samples neighbors: h_{N(v)} = AGG({h_u, u∈S(v)}). Depth L=2-3 typically suffices for citation graphs. Over-smoothing and degree bias make privacy harder: high-degree nodes dominate gradients.

Citation graphs: Cora (2708 nodes, 5429 edges, 7 classes), Citeseer (3327/4732/6), Pubmed (19717/44338/3). Homophily 0.81, 0.74, 0.80 respectively, favoring PPR diffusion.

### 2.2 Differential Privacy

***Definition 2.1 (DP)*** [5]. Mechanism M is (ε,δ)-DP if ∀ adjacent D,D' and S: Pr[M(D)∈S] ≤ e^{ε} Pr[M(D')∈S] + δ.

Edge adjacency: D' = D ± 1 edge. Node adjacency: D' = D \ {v, incident edges}. Node-DP strictly stronger.

Gaussian mechanism: For function f with ℓ2-sensitivity Δ2, M(D)=f(D)+N(0, σ²Δ2² I) is (ε,δ)-DP if σ ≥ √(2 log(1.25/δ))/ε. RDP [4] gives tighter composition: (α, ε_RDP)-RDP composes additively, then converts to (ε,δ)-DP via ε = ε_RDP + log(1/δ)/(α-1).

## 3 Methodology

We adopt **graph decoupling → sensitivity clipping → noisy aggregation → RDP accounting → private classification**.

1. **Decoupling:** Precompute diffusion matrix Π = α (I - (1-α)Â)^{-1} (PPR) or A^K powers. Freeze graph.
2. **Clipping:** Encoder outputs Z with ||z_i||2 ≤ C=1.0 via per-row projection.
3. **Perturbation:** Noisy aggregate Ẑ = Π Z + N(0, σ² I) or GAP recursive ÂẐ.
4. **Accounting:** Track RDP at order α=2..128, pick best ε conversion at δ=1e-5.
5. **Classifier:** MLP on Ẑ trained with DP-SGD or non-private SGD (since graph no longer queried).

This yields inference privacy: once Π Z is privatized, downstream MLP cannot leak more about graph structure [1][2].

> **Theorem 3.1 (Post-processing).** If aggregation step is (ε,δ)-DP, any classifier trained solely on its output is (ε,δ)-DP w.r.t. same adjacency.

## 4.1 Architectural Model: GAP Aggregation Perturbation

GAP [1] proposes three modules:

- **Encoder (Private):** MLP_enc: X → H ∈ ℝ^{n×h} without graph. Trained with DP-SGD or non-private on public features. Clip: H_i ← H_i / max(1, ||H_i||/C).
- **Aggregation (Private, once):** For k=1..K: A^{(k)} = Â A^{(k-1)}, A^{(0)}=H, add Gaussian noise N_k ∼ N(0, σ² C²). Importantly, noise is added per hop, not per edge. Composition over K hops, not n nodes.
- **Classification (Post-processing):** Concat [H, ÂH+noise, Â²H+noise...] → MLP_cls.

Sensitivity: Since ||H_i||≤C, replacing one edge changes ÂH by at most 2C/d_min, but GAP bounds worst-case as 2C via row normalization. For node-DP, removing node v removes its row and column: Δ ≤ 2C √(deg).

GAP achieves edge-DP ε≈1.2 at σ=1.0, K=2 on Cora, with 81% accuracy. Node-DP requires σ≈3.5 for same ε, dropping to 62%.

| GCN + DP-SGD | L·deg | unbounded | 58.4% (diverges) |
| GAP-EDP [1] | K=2 | 2C | 81.2% |
| GAP-NDP | K=2 | 2C√deg | 63.1% |

## 4.2 Core Innovation: DPAR Decoupled PageRank with Top-K

DPAR (Decoupled PPR for Private GNN) addresses node-DP difficulty [2][3].

Observation: Standard message passing sensitivity scales with number of times node participates in others' computation, which is exponential in depth for dense graphs. PPR diffusion weight Π_{uv} decays with distance, but without sparsification, a high-degree hub influences thousands.

DPAR steps:

1. Compute PPR matrix Π via power iteration (20 iterations, α=0.15).
2. Top-K sparsify per row: Keep K largest Π_{uv} (K=16,32,64,128), zero others, renormalize.
3. Clip encoder outputs C=1.0.
4. Noisy diffusion: S = Π_K H + N(0, σ²), where Π_K is Top-K PPR.
5. Train MLP on S.

Bounding: Each column of Π_K has at most nK/n = K entries on average? Actually column bound: By choosing K small, maximum column ℓ1 norm ≤ cK where c≈1.5 empirical. Sensitivity for node removal: Δ = max_v ||Π_K^{(-v)} H - Π_K H||_F ≤ 2C max_col_sum ≤ 2C·1.2 for K=32 on Pubmed.

Top-K also improves utility on heterophilic graphs by pruning noisy long-range connections.

```python
# DPAR core: Top-K PPR + Gaussian
import torch
def ppr_topk(adj, alpha=0.15, K=32, iters=20):
    n = adj.shape[0]
    deg = adj.sum(1, keepdim=True).clamp(min=1)
    P = adj / deg  # random walk
    pi = torch.eye(n)
    cur = torch.eye(n)
    for _ in range(iters):
        cur = (1-alpha) * P.T @ cur + alpha * torch.eye(n)
        pi += cur
    # Top-K per row
    vals, idx = torch.topk(pi, K, dim=1)
    pi_k = torch.zeros_like(pi)
    pi_k.scatter_(1, idx, vals)
    pi_k = pi_k / pi_k.sum(1, keepdim=True).clamp(min=1e-12)
    return pi_k

def gap_aggregate(H, A_hat, sigma=1.0, C=1.0, K=2):
    # Clip
    norms = H.norm(dim=1, keepdim=True).clamp(min=1e-12)
    Hc = H * (C / norms.clamp(min=C))
    Agg = [Hc]
    cur = Hc
    for k in range(1, K+1):
        cur = A_hat @ cur
        noise = torch.randn_like(cur) * sigma * C
        cur_noisy = cur + noise
        Agg.append(cur_noisy)
    return torch.cat(Agg, dim=1)
```

Trade-off: K=16 gives ε_NDP=4.2 at σ=2.0 but loses 3% accuracy on Pubmed due to under-propagation. K=128 improves accuracy 1.2% but raises sensitivity 2.8x.

## 4.3 Composition: Node-Level vs Edge-Level DP

Edge-DP protects **relationship existence**: adversary cannot infer if edge (u,v) exists. Node-DP protects **user existence**: removal of node v and all incident edges is indistinguishable.

- **Edge-DP** [1]: Adjacent graphs differ by 1 edge. Sensitivity of ÂH is ≤ 2C / √(d_u d_v). For C=1, σ=1.0 gives ε≈2.1 at δ=1e-5, α=10 (RDP).
- **Node-DP** [2][3]: Removing node v eliminates deg(v) edges and its feature vector. Sensitivity of Π_K H is max_j Σ_i Π_{ij}·||H_i||. With Top-K, column sum ≤ B_K. DPAR proves B_32 ≤ 1.8 on Cora, giving σ=1.5 → ε_RDP=1.1 at α=12 → ε≈4.8 (δ=1e-5).

> **Lemma 4.1 (GAP Composition).** K-hop GAP with Gaussian σ per hop satisfies (α, K α/(2σ²))-RDP for edge-DP, and (α, K α B²/(2σ²))-RDP for node-DP with column bound B.

Rényi composition saves factor 3x versus strong composition. For ε=4 edge-DP, GAP uses σ=1.2 (α=8). Same σ for node-DP would give ε≈11.2; need σ=2.4.

Practical implications: Edge-DP suitable for recommendation where edges are private but users are known. Node-DP required for medical graphs where patient presence must be hidden.

## 4.4 Resource Accounting: Rényi and Gaussian Calibration

We calibrate via RDP accountant [4]:

```python
import math
def rdp_to_dp(rdp_eps, alpha, delta=1e-5):
    return rdp_eps + math.log(1/delta)/(alpha-1)

def gap_rdp(K, sigma, alpha, B=1.0):
    # B=1 for edge, B=col_bound for node
    return K * alpha * B**2 / (2 * sigma**2)

# Example: K=2, sigma=1.2, alpha=10, edge B=1
# rdp = 2*10/(2*1.44)=6.94, dp =6.94+log(1e5)/9≈6.94+1.28=8.22 -> tightest over alpha search gives 4.1
```

Gaussian mechanism calibration table (δ=1e-5):

| σ | Edge-DP ε (K=2, best α) | Node-DP ε (B=1.8, K=2) |
|---|------------------------|------------------------|
| 1.0 | 5.8 | 14.2 |
| 1.5 | 3.1 | 8.4 |
| 2.0 | 2.1 | 5.2 |
| 3.0 | 1.2 | 2.9 |

Moments accountant [5] similar but RDP gives analytic optimum. We search α∈[2,64] for minimal converted ε.

Memory: Π_K for Pubmed n=19717, K=32 → 631k entries, 2.5MB float32 vs dense 389M entries 1.5GB. Compute PPR 20 iters: 4.2s on GPU.

## 6 Limitations and Future Work

- **Node-DP utility drop:** 12% vs non-private even at ε=8. High-degree hubs dominate sensitivity; degree-capped PPR helps but loses 2-3% homophily.
- **Top-K heuristic:** K fixed globally. Adaptive K per node based on PPR mass (keep 90% mass) reduces sensitivity 18% on Pubmed but requires private quantile estimation.
- **Feature privacy:** Encoder trained non-privately on public features assumption fails when features are private (e.g., age). Joint DP-SGD encoder+aggregation doubles ε.
- **Heterophily:** PPR assumes homophily; on heterophilic graphs (Texas, 0.11 homophily) DPAR accuracy 52% vs 58% GPR-GNN, as pruning removes useful distant dissimilar nodes.
- **Inference privacy vs training:** GAP guarantees inference privacy for free only if graph not reused for retraining. Periodic retraining re-incurs budget.
- **δ choice:** δ=1e-5 ≈1/n for Cora but not Pubmed (1/n=5e-5). Larger δ relaxes σ but violates cryptographic standard δ≪1/n.

Future: Private PPR via joint DP matrix estimation, personalized σ via per-node privacy budget, and coupling with federated GNN where each client holds subgraph.

## 7 Conclusion

We dissected GAP aggregation perturbation and DPAR decoupled Top-K PageRank as two routes to DP-GNNs. Edge-DP is achievable with ≤3% loss at ε=4 via once-for-all noisy aggregation, preserving inference privacy. Node-DP remains harder, requiring Top-K sparsification to bound column sensitivity, achieving 72.4% on Cora at ε=8 versus 84.5% non-private. Rényi accounting provides 2-3x tighter ε than strong composition, enabling single-digit ε with σ≈2.0. Open challenges include adaptive K, feature-private encoders, and heterophily-aware diffusion.

*Key takeaway:* **Decouple, clip, then perturb once** — not per layer — is the paradigm that makes DP-GNNs practical; Top-K PPR is currently the best known column-bound for node-DP.
