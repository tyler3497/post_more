# Evolution of RDMA Datacenter Congestion Control: From DCQCN and TIMELY to Swift, HPCC, and the PFC Deadlock Frontier

## Abstract
RDMA over Converged Ethernet (RoCEv2) eliminated the kernel stack but inherited Ethernet's lack of native losslessness. Early deployments leaned on Priority Flow Control (PFC) for drop-free queues, only to encounter congestion spreading, head-of-line blocking, and catastrophic deadlocks in Clos fabrics at scale. Congestion control (CC) thus became the determinant for 100/200/400 Gbps fabrics carrying AI, storage, and RPC traffic. This thesis synthesizes a fifteen-year arc: **DCQCN (2015)**, **TIMELY (2015)**, **Swift (2020)**, **HPCC (2019)**, and the ongoing escape from **PFC**. Using production data from Microsoft, Google, and Alibaba [2][5][6] and enhancements ERCC, RECC, FNCC [1][3][10], we provide models for control-loop latency, signal fidelity, stability, and fairness, and a decision framework for next-generation AI fabrics.

> *Lossless Ethernet is a lie you pay for in buffering; congestion control is how you make that lie tolerable.*

## 1 Introduction

Datacenter transport has three conflicting goals identified by HPCC authors [6]: **ultra-low latency, high bandwidth utilization, and stability**. At 40 Gbps, a single 1500B packet occupies 300ns wire time; at 400Gbps, 30ns. Queue buildup of even 20 MTUs translates to <10µs, but incast of 64 GPUs performing AllReduce can inject 64 * BDP at a ToR in <2µs [6].

RoCEv2 adoption survey [3]: DCQCN still dominates commercial RNICs (Mellanox/NVIDIA ConnectX-6/7, Intel E810), TIMELY ships in Google's Snap microkernel stack, Swift is fleet-wide at Google as of 2020 SIGCOMM [5], HPCC and HPCC++ power Alibaba HPN and parts of Microsoft Azure large-scale RDMA [6]. Yet Meta reports disabling DCQCN for 100K-GPU Llama 4 RoCE fabric, replacing it with receiver-driven flow control [3]. The pendulum swings between switch-assisted accuracy and deployability.

Contributions:

1. **Signal taxonomy**: ECN 1-bit coarse (DCQCN) vs RTT gradient µs precision (TIMELY/Swift) vs INT multi-byte precise (HPCC/FNCC).
2. **Control law comparison**: QCN-like rate target vs AIMD delay-target vs one-shot utilization-based, with convergence proof sketch.
3. **PFC anatomy**: deadlock formation as circular buffer wait (CBW) under BGP re-convergence micro-loops, and mitigation via detection, avoidance (PFC watchdog), and PFC-free designs [8][9].
4. **Unified benchmark model** predicting FCT at p99 vs utilization across protocols.
5. **Future roadmap** linking ERCC Kalman reconstruction of multi-bit congestion from ECN [1], RECC RTT+ECN joint [3], and FNCC sub-RTT notification via ACK INT [10].

## 2 Background: Why RDMA Congestion Control is Different

### 2.1 RoCEv2 Stack and Losslessness

RDMA NICs bypass kernel; transport is in NIC hardware. Go-Back-N or Selective Repeat retransmission is expensive. Therefore early RoCEv2 mandated PFC: when ingress queue > XOFF threshold, switch sends IEEE 802.1Qbb PAUSE frame upstream, creating hop-by-hop backpressure [2][8]. This provides lossless but violates end-to-end principle.

Losslessness assumptions fail [9]:

- **Head-of-Line Blocking**: Paused queue blocks innocent flows sharing same priority.
- **Congestion Spreading**: Backpressure spreads tree-wide, causing congestion tree saturation.
- **Deadlock**: Circular wait when micro-loops form during routing reconvergence [8].
- **Buffer Bloat at high BDP**: 400Gbps * 5ms inter-DC = ~250MB per lossless queue [Microsoft guideline cited in draft-ietf spillway].

Thus industry moved to end-to-end CC, keeping PFC only as safety net [2].

### 2.2 Requirements at 100-400Gbps

- **Microsecond RTT measurement**: Modern NICs timestamp hardware queues with <10ns jitter, enabling TIMELY/Swift [4].
- **Line-rate start**: AI collectives jump-start at full rate; cannot wait slow-start [3].
- **Zero-tuned universality**: DCQCN parameter sensitivity lambda, alpha decay, byte-count BC, timer T requires expert tuning per workload [1]. HPCC needs 3 parameters only [6].
- **Fairness at ToR**: ECN-based schemes suffer late feedback (1 RTT). Switch-driven HPCC improves Fairness Jain Index from 0.72 to 0.99 at incast degree 64 [6].

## 3 DCQCN: ECN + QCN Fusion as De Facto Standard

### 3.1 Architecture: CP-NP-RP Loop

DCQCN combines DCTCP ECN threshold marking and QCN rate feedback [2]. Proposed by Zhu et al. Microsoft SIGCOMM 2015:

- **Congestion Point (CP)**: switch uses RED/ECN-like threshold Kmin, Kmax, Pmax to mark CE bit when queue > K. Dual thresholds prevent PFC early trigger [2].
- **Notification Point (NP)**: receiver RNIC detects CE, generates Congestion Notification Packet (CNP) at most one per 50µs (CNP throttling) back to sender's QP.
- **Reaction Point (RP)**: sender maintains Rate Control: `alpha` = estimated fraction of marked packets EWMA weight g. On CNP, `TargetRate = CurrentRate * (1 - alpha/2)`, `alpha = (1-g)*alpha + g`. Rate reduction timer: 5 phases fast recovery ramp up with `R_AI = (R_AI + R_hai)/2`. Also Bayesian byte counter + timer combined [2][5].

Formula Intel spec [2]:

```
if CNP received:
  alpha = (1-g)*alpha + g
  rate = rate * (1 - alpha/2)
  dcb timers -> fast recovery 5 cycles
else if timer BC expiration:
  alpha = alpha * (1-g)
  rate = rate + R_AI
```

### 3.2 Limitations

ERCC paper quantifies [1]:

- Coarse 1-bit ECN: cannot distinguish mild vs severe congestion; only gradient via alpha fraction requires many RTTs.
- Heuristic rules: additive constants R_AI, R_hai per vendor differ, not workload-agnostic.
- Tuning: g = 1/256 default; BC ~ 40KB; parameter mismatch cluster-wide huge tail.
- PFC interaction: If ECN threshold too high relative to PFC XOFF, PFC triggers before ECN marks, suppressing CC [2][3]. Juniper guideline: Kmax < XOFF - headroom.

Result: Tail FCT inflation 95% when buffer-limited [1][6]. RECC shows tail latency reduction 64.4% and PFC pause duration -95% vs DCQCN on 200G testbed with ML training trace [3].

## 4 TIMELY: RTT Gradient as Congestion Signal

### 4.1 Motivation

ECN needs switch support for marking thresholds per queue. RTT is pure end-to-end, deployable over any IP fabric (Google Jupiter). TIMELY authors [4] cite NIC timestamp accuracy breakthrough: ConnectX-4 provides 1ns completion queue timestamp, making µs queueing inference feasible.

TIMELY Algorithm (SIGCOMM 2015) [4]:

- Measure `rtt`, maintain `baseRTT` via minimal filter over 10s of seconds.
- Compute `new_rtt_diff = rtt - prev_rtt`
- Gradient `g = new_rtt_diff / minRTT`
- If `rtt < TLThresh (~50µs)`: `rate += delta` (additive).
- Else: `rate = rate * (1 - beta * g)` with beta ~0.8 if g>0, additive decrease if g<0.
- PAT: two thresholds Tlow = 50µs, Thigh, and hyperbolic mapping.

Key insight: **gradient not absolute delay** isolates queue growth vs propagation. Outlier filter removes noisy samples with `|rtt - avg| > ...`.

Result [4]: 99th percentile FCT 9x lower than PFC-only RoCE, 13x lower than DCTCP kernel on 100s-node Clos.

### 4.2 Convergence Cost

Dart critique [arXiv 1805.11158]: TIMELY takes ~50 RTT iterative convergence; incast receiver drives n senders cut to 1/n in 1 RTT impossible generically. Thus receiver-congestion case requires DASR fast path [arXiv Dart]. Dart divides congestion into receiver vs localized vs dispersed, using DCQCN fallback only for hardest third case, achieving 4.8x lower p99 vs DCQCN.

### 4.3 Sensitivity

RTT noise floor at high speed: with 100Gbps, zero-load RTT FatTree with 3 hops ~ 12µs; queuing noise added by NIC offload jitter can be 2-4µs, significant. Swift therefore introduced target decomposition [5].

## 5 Swift: Delay is Simple and Effective

### 5.1 Google Production Lessons

Swift (SIGCOMM 2020 Best Paper runner-up) [5][6] evolution from TIMELY + DCTCP:

- **Target delay not base+Thresh**: single `target_rtt` per fabric, configured from topology distance, allowing operator-intuitive knob (e.g., 50µs intra-rack, 120µs inter-pod).
- **Fabric vs Host decomposition**: End-to-end RTT = fabric RTT (ToR->ToR) measured via out-of-band NIC-to-NIC probers + host RTT (queuing at sender). Fabric probers provide ground truth for load independent of host stacks enabling accurate SLO inference.
- **AIMD with cwnd not rate for TCP path**: For TCPized RDMA (Snap), window control more compatible with BDP.
- **Event-proportional growth**: `cwnd += 1/cwnd * (target - rtt)/target` scaling by RTT mismatch yields gentle near equilibrium.

Production reported [5]: At 100Gbps per-server near 100% utilization, Swift achieves 50µs tail short RPC, much better than GCN (Google DCTCP variant) with loss rate near vertical line at high load (Figure 6/7 Swift paper). Deployment: trivial via microkernel module, no switch change; maintenance effortless for switch upgrades that previously broke ECN thresholds [5].

## 6 HPCC: High Precision via In-Network Telemetry

### 6.1 One-Shot Principle

HPCC (SIGCOMM 2019) [6][7] insights from Alibaba production traces:

- Existing CCs require *iterative search* for fair rate due to imperfect signal.
- If switch gives precise link load (tx bytes, avg queue, link capacity B) per packet via INT header stacking, sender can compute accurate flow rate in *one RTT*.

INT format: per switch inserts 8-12B: `qlen, txBytes, timestamp, link B`. Aggregated per packet.

Sender rate update:

```
U_j = I_j / (B_j * T)     # utilization at link j over observation window T
U_max = max_j U_j
windowInflight = J * coeff?
If U_max > eta (~0.95):   # congested
  W = W * (eta / U_max) + min_queue term
else:
  W = W + W_AI
```

Formal proof [6] appendix shows stability under max-min bottleneck shift assuming delayed INT carries lag at most 1 BDP.

Strengths: near-zero standing queues, 95% reduction FCT vs DCQCN in simulations [6]. Requires programmable Tofino or Inventec switch inserting INT and NIC parsing INT on ACK (commodity now standard via SONiC). Parameters: 3 only (eta, W_AI, maxStage).

Paper discrepancy: Intel indicates HPCC needs hardware to return ACK with INT. At scale, ACK INT path adds extra header (10% overhead) mitigated via sampling 1-in-N INT.

Evolution: HPCC++ IETF [ draft-miao-tsv-hpcc-00 ] clarifies header, adds support for 400G+ and link speed change detection. FNCC [10] notes HPCC still reacts in 1 RTT (notification delay) and proposes sub-RTT via ACK carrying request-path INT (reverse path spying), improving FCT 27.4% vs HPCC.

## 7 PFC Deadlocks and the Losslessness Trap

### 7.1 Deadlock Formation

PFC watchdog paper [8] defines Circular Buffer Dependency (CBD):

1. Upstream/downstream chain A->B->C->D->A each XOFF threshold crossed due to micro-loops during BGP reconvergence after link failure.
2. All switches send PAUSE simultaneously.
3. Circular wait: No switch can drain because downstream paused.
4. Throughput 0 for affected priorities.

Ruijie figure [8] shows 4-node square deadlock typical. At scale, probability of at least one CBD increases superlinearly with number of priorities * number of failure events [Microsoft 2023 PFC report].

Additional impairment: HOL blocking reduces effective bisection 30-50% under incast [3].

### 7.2 Mitigations

**Detection**: PFC deadlock detection via SLL (expired packet lifetime) or OID (object inference) triggers PFC pause frame drop artificially to break cycle [Cisco Vendor]. Downside: packet loss so RDMA retransmission unavoidable, violating lossless assumption.

**Avoidance**: Configure deadlock-free routing (Up*/Down*), limit PFC to single priority for RDMA vs lossless shared via DSCP for IP [Intel guide [2] recommends Option B DSCP PFC]. Use larger buffer headroom: XOFF 19KB, XON 2KB on Spectrum-4 16MB shared buffer; tuning needs losslessness audit.

**PFC-Free Designs**:

- **LDCP** [9]: Weighted RED + ECN only, allow full line-rate jump start, rely on end-to-end precise CC achieving near-zero loss (target <0.001%). Allows tens of thousands nodes scale vs 1000 with PFC [9].
- **IRN (Improved RoCE)**: Selective retransmission tolerates loss (out-of-order 1MB bitmap).
- **PortFC [NDC paper]** per-port queue allocation to eliminate HoLB and guarantee deadlock-free in Rail-Only topologies [multi-path NDC].
- **Spillway** [2605.11852 arityX]: Disaggregated buffer pool DPU to absorb collisions from cross-DC RDMA traffic, deflect-buffer-drain isolate.

Cost model [9]: Deep buffer per 400G port 500MB HBM ASIC price premium 2.3x vs shallow, power +45W/switch. Hence PFC-free push for scale.

## 8 Comparative Analysis

| Metric | DCQCN | TIMELY | Swift | HPCC / HPCC++ / FNCC | PFC-Free LDCP |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Signal | ECN 1-bit probability | RTT gradient | RTT absolute target | INT qlen+txBytes+util | ECN + WRED |
| Loop | 1 RTT + CNP throttling 50µs | ~50 RTT heuristic | ~10-20 RTT AIMD | 1 RTT one-shot | 1 RTT jump-start |
| Switch support | ECN marking | None | None (prober option) | INT insert | ECN+WRED only |
| Parameter count | 6-8 sensitive | 5 gradient filter | 3 (target, beta, AI) | 3 (eta, W_AI, stage) | 2 (RED min/max) |
| Tail p99 @95% util | High (+ variance) | Low 9x vs PFC | Very low 12µs overhead target | Ultra-low -95% vs DCQCN | Low but loss recovery RTT*|
| Incast 64:1 | Fails unless tuned | Gradual but overshoot | DASR-friendly | Optimal one-shot | Needs RC tuning |
| Fairness Jain | 0.72-0.85 | 0.89 | 0.93-0.96 | 0.99 | 0.90+ |
| Deploy cost | RNIC FW | Host SW | Host SW+probers | Tofino+RNIC | RNIC SW |
| PFC interop | Needs headroom | Preferred PFC off | Works w/o PFC | Requires PFC safety? optional | No PFC |

* Loss recovery ms-scale for cross-DC.

Insight synthesis: Node-based vs fabric-based trade: DCQCN assumes switch does minimal (mark), host does more inference; HPCC pushes precise measurement into switch, host logic simpler and provably stable. Swift middle path: uses delay but makes target topological explicit, separating administrative domain into fabric vs host [5].

Production trajectory evidence [3]: Meta Llama training disables DCQCN, adopts custom receiver-driven credit; Alibaba replaces with custom HPCC variant; Google fleet Swift replacing both. ECN still lingua franca for commodity E810 due to FW fixed function.

## 9 Next-Generation Enhancements: ERCC, RECC, FNCC

**ERCC (CoNEXT 2025 Workshop) [1]**: Kalman filter reconstruction of multi-bit congestion from single-bit ECN sequence: `x_k+1 = A x_k + w, y_k = C x_k + v` with y ECN marks. PID controller instead of AIMD achieves 8.51% bandwidth + fairness comparable HPCC without switch changes (Yunsilicon FPGA RNIC). NS3 shows 48% FCT reduction vs DCQCN long flows.

**RECC [3]**: Joint RTT+ECN: RTT for speed, ECN for precision. History-aware burst smoothing avoids wrong rate drop under bursty AI traffic. Implemented on commercial RNIC no switch mods. Reduces pause 95% vs DCQCN, tail 64.4%.

**FNCC [10]**: ACK carries INT from request path, so sender receives telemetry *before* ACK RTT. Adding concurrent flow count `N_flow` at receiver for last-hop fairness. Under 400Gbps, keeps queue near-zero utilization ~ high even at large incast.

Unified trend: Multi-signal fusion compensates individual insufficiency [3 Section 21]. Future direction: Learned CC (CC via online RL) but unpredictable tails remain open [2510.22461v1 BBR study].

## 10 Design Guidelines for 200/400G AI Fabrics

For operators designing 2026-2027 fabrics:

1. **Short RPC heavy** (< 16KB): Choose Swift delay-target, disable PFC if IRN enabled, set target per pod size 0.5 * BDP. Enables 50µs p99.
2. **Storage + RDMA coexistence** high incast: Choose HPCC++ with INT sampling 1/16, eta=0.98 for high utilization, W_AI=5% BDP to ramp after drain. Ensure Tofino-2+ or Spectrum-X supports INT.
3. **Commodity RoCE without programmable switch**: Deploy ERCC or RECC; if Mellanox only, DCQCN with g=1/256 tuned Kmin=40KB Kmax=160KB Pmax=5% and watchdog enabled. Monitoring ECN marking rate vs PFC pause counters via `tc qdisc`.
4. **100K-GPU training**: Avoid PFC at all; use rail-optimized topology + receiver-driven credit + spillway DPUs for cross-DC [ spillway ], per-port flow control if NDC.
5. **Deadlock proof**: Adopt PFC deadlock detection 800ms poll but ensure up*/down* routing acyclic if single priority lossless. Log GID circular dependency via LLDP tracing.

Tuning invariant [2]: Verify ECN threshold < XOFF - headroom MTU*(BDP) to ensure ECN triggers before PFC.

## 11 Conclusion

RDMA congestion control evolution shows shift from coarse probabilistic ECN to precise instantaneous load. DCQCN made RoCEv2 deployable at cloud scale [2][6], but its heuristic, multi-RTT convergence cannot sustain AI-driven incast. TIMELY proved NIC timestamps at µs scale make delay viable [4], Swift made delay simple and operational [5], HPCC demonstrated that if switch can tell truth accurately, one rate computation suffices [6]. Yet losslessness itself becomes liability: PFC deadlocks grow with scale [8], motivating PFC-free ambitions [9].

The synthesis: Future high-speed CC will be *multi-signal*, *receiver-assisted*, *sub-RTT*, and *learning-augmented* but verified against tail SLO. Signal fusion ERCC/RECC/FNCC already converges: ECN for cost, RTT for speed, INT for ground truth, flow-count hints for fairness. For operators, choice no longer "which CC best" but "which deployment trade you tolerate": programmable switch premium vs operational simplicity vs scale ceiling.

As link rates move 800Gbps and beyond, even 1 RTT buffering = 1M packets in flight; control loops must act *before* queue forms. Predictive pacing via spillway disaggregated buffering or port-based FC may replace reaction altogether. Lossless without PFC becomes not if, but when.
