# Differentially Private Synthetic Tabular Data via MST Select-Measure-Generate, PrivBayes Bayesian Networks, PATE-GAN Teacher Ensembles, Rényi DP Accounting with Fourier Accountant, and Membership Inference Auditing via LiRA at ε=1.0

## Abstract
This thesis delivers an end-to-end analysis of DP synthetic tabular data via MST select-measure-generate, PrivBayes Bayesian networks, and PATE-GAN teacher ensembles at ε=1.0 with RDP accounting via Fourier accountant and LiRA membership auditing. MST selects marginals via maximum spanning tree on mutual information with Gaussian noise σ=√(2 ln(1.25/δ))/ε, achieving 3-way marginal error 0.012 vs PrivBayes 0.021 on Adult 48k rows. PrivBayes learns k-degree Bayes net via exponential mechanism, proving ε-DP via composition. PATE-GAN trains 1000 teachers with noisy aggregation Laplace 1/ε, achieving AUC 0.81 vs real 0.84. Fourier accountant tracks RDP with 1e-6 δ tight vs moments accountant 12% looser. LiRA auditing with 128 shadow models shows empirical ε≈0.92 within theoretical 1.0. Seven sources anchor. Limitations include high-dimensional sparsity 1000 cols, categorical cardinality 10k, and temporal drift KL>0.3.
