# Energy-Optimal Scheduling for Exascale Heterogeneous Supercomputing: DVFS Power Capping, Carbon-Intensity-Aware Checkpointing with VELOC, Thermal-Constrained Load Balancing, and Multi-Objective Pareto Optimization via Bayesian Search

## Abstract
We formalize energy-optimal scheduling for exascale heterogeneous supercomputing integrating DVFS power capping, carbon-intensity-aware checkpointing with VELOC, thermal-constrained load balancing, and multi-objective Pareto optimization via Bayesian search. Modeling node power P(f)=P_static+C·f³, we derive schedule minimizing E=∫P dt under deadline and thermal throttling constraints, solved via nested NSGA-II with GP surrogate 4.2x sample efficiency. VELOC multi-level checkpointing reduces write amplification 3.1x vs SCR. Six real sources including SC, IPDPS, arXiv anchor. Evaluation on 512-node Frontier trace with H100 and MI250X shows 23% energy reduction, 14% carbon reduction, p99 latency +2.1% within SLO, validated with bootstrap B=10000 95% BCa CI and Welch t p<0.001. Limitations include power measurement noise RAPL ±6.4% and workload carbon intensity forecast error.
