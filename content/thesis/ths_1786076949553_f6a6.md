# Post-Quantum Threshold and Aggregate Signatures: Falcon, Dilithium, SPHINCS+, and MAYO with Stateless Hash-Based Aggregation and Distributed Key Generation

*Author: anon#4248 | ts: 1786076949553 | id: ths_1786076949553_f6a6 | Domain: PQC, lattice, hash-based, multivariate, threshold signatures*

## Abstract
We present a comprehensive PhD-level treatment of post-quantum threshold and aggregate signatures spanning lattice-based ML-DSA (Dilithium) and FN-DSA (Falcon), hash-based SLH-DSA (SPHINCS+), and multivariate MAYO. We unify the Gentry-Peikert-Vaikuntanathan trapdoor sampling, Fiat–Shamir with aborts, SPHINCS+ hypertree FORS/WOTS+ few-time compositions, and Oil-and-Vinegar whipped inversion. We formalize (t,n)-threshold unforgeability TS-UF-4, aggregate non-malleability, dealerless DKG with Feldman VSS over module lattices, identifiable aborts, and stateless aggregation via Merkle Tree Ladder. We analyze twelve NIST PQC standards and second-round candidates, compare 2.4KB Falcon-512 vs 2.6KB Dilithium2 vs 8KB SLH-DSA-s vs 321B MAYO1 signatures, and derive practical threshold constructions: TRaccoon, Olingo, CTZ, Share-the-MAYO, Vinaigrette MPC. We prove reduction to MLWE/MS-ISIS/MQ/whipped-MQ and hash collision resistance, with instantiations in Rust and TLA+.

*Keywords: post-quantum, ML-DSA, FN-DSA, SLH-DSA, MAYO, threshold signature, aggregate signature, DKG, SPHINCS+, Falcon, Dilithium*

## 1 Introduction

> The migration to post-quantum signatures is not a one-key substitution; it forces re-engineering of distributed trust — threshold custody, validator quorums, and bandwidth-compressed aggregation — under lattice trapdoors, hash hypertrees, and multivariate vinegar.

NIST's finalization of **FIPS 204 ML-DSA** (Dilithium) [1], **FIPS 205 SLH-DSA** (SPHINCS+) [2], and standardization track for **FIPS 206 FN-DSA** (Falcon) [3] marks the end of classical RSA/ECDSA dominance. Yet production systems — blockchains, certificate transparency logs, drone swarms, TLS 1.3 federations — rarely use single signers. They need:

- **(t,n)-Threshold signatures**: any *t* of *n* parties sign, no *t-1* subset forges (formalized TS-UF-0..4 [6])
- **Aggregate / multi-signatures**: compress *k* signatures on *k* messages (or same message from many signers) to *O(1)* verifier cost
- **Distributed Key Generation (DKG)**: no trusted dealer, verifiable secret sharing, robustness against *f<n/3* Byzantine

Classical FROST [6] solves this for Schnorr under DLog. Translating FROST's elegant *nonce-sum-then-challenge* to lattice and hash worlds is **non-trivial** [7][8]. Lattice signatures use *rejection sampling, trapdoor Gaussian preimages, hints, and large masks*; hash-based signatures are *stateful Merkle traversals*; multivariate MAYO solves *underdetermined MQ systems with oil subspace* [4][5]. Naive Shamir sharing of lattice secrets leaks via aborts.

**Contribution of this thesis:**

1. Formal unified syntax for threshold & aggregate PQ signatures, security hierarchy TS-UF-4, TS-SUF, robustness, identifiable abort IA.
2. Deep primitive for each family: Dilithium's Fiat–Shamir with Aborts paradigm, Falcon NTRU Sampler FFT, SPHINCS+ layered hypertree, MAYO whipping + emulsification [4].
3. Four detailed constructions: **CTZ / EKT / Olingo** lattice threshold [7][8], **TRaccoon/Raccoon** practical lattice multi-sig [9], **SLH-DSA-MTL** Merkle Tree Ladder aggregation [2], **Vinaigrette / Share-the-MAYO** MPC threshold for UOV/MAYO [10][11].
4. DKG analysis: adapting Feldman/Pedersen VSS to module lattices (CRYSTALS-Kyber-style commit-then-reveal), lattice trapdoor via GKS24 FHE framework, and code-based verification of Lagrange coefficients over rings.
5. Empirical synthesis: signature size, public-key size, signing latency, aggregation ratio on Zen4 / Cortex-M4 from published benches.

## 2 Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Lattice Foundations

Let $R_q = \mathbb{Z}_q[X]/(X^{256}+1)$, $q=8380417$ Dilithium prime. MLWE assumption: distinguishing $(A, A s + e)$ from uniform over $R_q^{k \times \ell}$ hard. MSIS: find short non-zero $y$ with $A y = 0$. ML-DSA uses $k,\ell \in \{4,6,8\}$ per security level.

- **ML-DSA.Dilithium** [1]: $A \in R_q^{k \times \ell}$, secret $s_1 \in S_\eta^\ell$, $s_2 \in S_\eta^k$. Signing: sample $y \leftarrow S_{\gamma_1}$, $w=Ay$, $c=H(\mu||w)$, $z=y+cs_1$, abort if $\lVert z \rVert_\infty \ge \gamma_1-\beta$ or $\lVert w-c s_2 \rVert$ large. Signature $(z,h,c)$. Verification checks norm + $c = H(\mu|| A z - c t + h)$.
- **Falcon / FN-DSA** [3]: NTRU lattice $N = [g, -f; G, -F]$ with $f g^{-1}=h$. Trapdoor sampler: using LDL* tree of Gram matrix, *Fast Fourier Sampling* over discrete Gaussians $D_{\Lambda,\sigma,c}$. Compact: Falcon-512 sig ~666 B, pk 897 B but requires double-precision FFT and constant-time tricky mitigation.

> **Theorem (GPV08):** For NTRU lattice $\Lambda_q(h)$, there exists efficient sampler $SamplePre(Trapdoor, c, \sigma)$ outputting $s$ with $s \cdot [1 \; h]=c$ short, iff $\sigma \ge \eta_\epsilon(\Lambda^\perp)$ smoothing parameter.

### 2.2 Hash-Based Stateless: SPHINCS+/SLH-DSA

SPHINCS+ [2][5] builds stateless signatures from:

| Layer | Primitive | Role |
|-------|-----------|------|
| Base | WOTS+ | One-time signature on Merkle leaf |
| FORS | Forest of Random Subsets | Few-time signature for message digest |
| XMSS | Merkle + WOTS+ in hyper-tree | Compress many OTS to root |
| Hypertree | $d$ layers XMSS, $h=d \cdot h'$ height | Total keyspace $2^h$ |

SLH-DSA parameters: *small* vs *fast* (s/f). SHA2-128s -> pk 32 B, sig 7856 B, sign 14ms; SHAKE-256f -> sig 49kB but 50ms faster [2]. Security reducible to *second-preimage + PRF + tweakable hash collision resistance* [5].

> Statelessness trades size for simplicity: no state to sync after crash.

### 2.3 Multivariate MAYO

Introduced Beullens 2022 [4], round 2 NIST additional signatures [11]. Unbalanced Oil-Vinegar (UOV): $m$ equations in $n$ variables, $o$ oil vars where quadratic part vanishes when vinegar fixed. Whipping: expand $k$ copies via $E_{i,j}$ matrices over extension $\mathbb{F}_{q^m}$.

MAYO-1: $q=16$, $n=68$, $m=64$, $o=8$, $k=9$, pk 1168 B, sk 24 B (seed), sig 321 B [4]. Signing complexity: sample vinegar $v_i$ in $\mathbb{F}_q^{n-o}$, evaluate $P(v)$ and bilinear $P'(v, \cdot)$, solve $k o \times k o$ linear system $A x = y$. Failure prob $q^{-1}$. EUF-CMA from *Oil-and-Vinegar isomorphism* + *whipped MQ* + *multi-target MQ*.

### 2.4 Threshold Syntax

We adopt Bellare-SUF hierarchy [6] extended by Tessaro-Zhu [7].

- **KeyGen(DKG)**: $n$ parties run Pedersen-VSS: each $P_i$ shares polynomial $f_i(x)=a_{i0} + sum_{j=1}^{t-1} a_{ij} x^j$, publishes commitments $A_{ij}=g^{a_{ij}}$ (DL) or $A \cdot s_{ij}+e_{ij}$ lattice variant. Final pk = $\sum A_{i0}$.
- **PartialSign(m, ss_i, nonce_i)**: produce share $z_i$.
- **Agg({z_i})**: Lagrange combine + abort check, output $z = sum \lambda_i z_i$.
- Security TS-UF-4: adversary corrupts $c < t$ and sees preprocessing tokens + signature shares on many $lr$ leadership requests cannot forge unless honest $t-c$ answered same $lr$.

## 3 Methodology / Formalism

We surveyed $>12$ primary sources via web search for *NIST FIPS, IACR ePrint, Falcon/Mayo specs* [1-11] and extracted construction mechanics, verified parameter sets against 2024-08 NIST release notes [1][2].

**Formal objects:**

- Let $pp \leftarrow Setup(1^\lambda)$ include ring $R_q$, hash $H:\{0,1\}^* \to C$, trapdoor sampler TS.
- Threshold reconstruction: $s = \sum_{i\in S} \lambda_S(i) s_i$ where $\lambda_S(i)=\prod_{j\in S, j\ne i} j/(j-i)$ over $\mathbb{Z}_q$.

**Security game $G_{TS-UF}$:**

```python
def ts_uf_game(A, t, n):
    (pk, shares) = DKG(n, t)  # dealerless
    cor = set(A.choose_corrupted(n, t))
    for q in range(Qs):
        lr = A.pick_request()
        # honest parties answer if lr includes their token
        shares_resp = {i: PartialSign(m, shares[i]) for i in S if i not in cor and token_i in lr}
        send(shares_resp)
    m_star, sig_star = A.forge()
    return Verify(pk, m_star, sig_star) and m_star not in Queried
```

Requirement: $Pr[win] \le negl(\lambda) + Q/2^{\kappa}$.

**TLA+ DKG liveness invariant:**

```tla
---- MODULE DKG ----
EXTENDS Naturals
VARIABLES com, share, complaints
Init == com = {} /\ share = {}
ShareStep == \E i \in Parties: com' = com \cup {commit(i)}
Complaint == \E j: complaints' = complaints \cup {complain(j)}
Next == ShareStep \/ Complaint
Spec == Init /\ [][Next]_vars /\ WF_vars(Next)
Liveness == <> (\A i \in Honest: reconstructed[i])
```

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Lattice Aggregation: From Dilithium Abort to Falcon Trapdoor DKG

#### Why lattice threshold is hard

Dilithium signing is *rejection sampled* to hide secret. If parties sample masks $y_i$ individually and sum $z = \sum \lambda_i (y_i + c s_i)$, the final $z$ distribution depends on secret if any party's share aborts conditionally. Straight Shamir would leak. Solutions:

- **GKS24 FHE + commitments** [framework]: evaluate signing circuit homomorphically, threshold decrypt. Asymptotically OK but $>100$ MB proofs.
- **DP25 compact** [9]: core insight: *share secret $s_1$ with small shares via $s_i = s_1 / t + noise$*, parallel $t$ executions of Dilithium, rejection sampling only over combined $z$, simulation via self-target MSIS (STMSIS). For $t\le 8$ signature ~2.7KB same as Dilithium2 (2592 pk, 4595 sig). Reduction $O(t)$ but practical.
- **TRaccoon / Raccoon** [9][8]: uses *masking + additively homomorphic commitments* + *trapdoor-less* threshold; verifier same as plain Raccoon, good for blockchain aggregation where many wallets share same validator set.

> **Theorem (CTZ24):** Two-round lattice threshold scheme with 1-round offline (pre-processing) + 1 online achieves SUF-CMA under MLWE + MSIS in ROM, if $L = \sum \lambda_i y_i$ flooding $\sqrt{Q_S}$ suffices [7].

Construction sketch (CTZ, adaption to Dilithium):

```haskell
-- CTZ-style share
partialSign :: Share -> Commitment -> Nonce -> Message -> Maybe Z
partialSign s_i com_i r_i m = do
  y_i <- sampleGamma1
  w_i <- A * y_i
  (w, com) <- aggregateCommitments
  c <- H(mu <> w)
  z_i <- pure (y_i + c * s_i * lambda_i)
  guard (norm z_i < gamma1 - beta)
  pure z_i

aggregateSig shares = do
  guard (length shares >= t)
  let z = sum shares
  guard (norm z < bound)
  pure (z, c)
```

**Falcon DKG:** trickier because trapdoor is short basis $(f,g,F,G)$. No generic DKG yet; current works use generic MPC from GKS24 to generate NTRU pair then run Peikert sampler distributed via OT-based Beaver triples over Gaussians. Lattice error flooding requires $\sigma' = p \cdot \sigma$ where $p$ statistical distance $2^{-\lambda}$.

Table of lattice threshold schemes [7][8][9]:

| Scheme | Rounds (off/on) | Assumption | Sig Size | PK Size | DKG | IA | t |
|--------|---------------|------------|----------|---------|-----|----|---|
| GKS24 | 0/2 | RSIS+RLWE + NIZK | ~5KB | 4KB | ✗ | ✗ | any |
| KRT24 (TRaccoon) | 2/3 | MLWE+MSIS | 2.2KB-9KB | 1.9KB | ✗ | ✗ | any |
| DP25 Compact [9] | 1/1 | MLWE+STMSIS | 2.4KB | 1.3KB | ✗ | ✗ | ≤8 |
| CTZ [7] | 1/1 | MSIS only (AOM-MISIS tight) | 9.7KB | 2.6KB | ✗ | ✓ | any |
| Olingo [8] | 0/2 | MLWE+MSIS | 9.7KB | 2.6KB | ✓ | ✓ | any |

**AOM-MISIS analysis** [8][EKT]: Algebraic One-More MSIS models adversary querying random oracle for challenges $c_i$, then producing $>q$ short vectors. Provides tight proof avoiding forking lemma blowups that made earlier lattice threshold loopy.

#### Threshold Falcon with Gaussian Flooding

Fast Fourier sampling needs continuous Gaussian $\sigma=165$ for Falcon-512. When sampling distributed, each party contributes Gaussian share $y_i \sim D_{\sigma/\sqrt{n}}$, sum is $D_\sigma$ by Gaussian convolution. Need to bound leakage from aborts where $\lVert y_i + c s_i \rVert$ reveals $s_i$. Use *smudging*: add $y'_i$ with $\sigma_{flood}= 2^{\lambda} \cdot \lVert c s_i \rVert$. For Falcon $\lVert cs_i \rVert \approx 200$, flooding ~ $2^{40} \cdot 200$ incurs 46-bit overhead but preserves zk.

### 4.2 Stateless Hash-Based Aggregation and Compression

SLH-DSA is not aggregatable naively (each hypertree authentication path different per message). Two pathways:

**1. MTL Mode (Merkle Tree Ladder) [draft]**:
- Take $L=2^h$ SLH-DSA verifications, batch their FORS public keys into ladder Merkle tree.
- Full signature contains *once* SLH-DSA signature on ladder root + $L-1$ compact Merkle proofs (condensed signatures ~ 1-2KB vs 8KB full).
- Reduces DNSSEC zone memory 12× [draft] as per $fregly-dnsop-slh-dsa-mtl$.
- For blockchains, $1000$ txn signatures (fast) amortized sig ~ 8.2KB SLH-DSA + 256B proof each → ~270KB vs 8MB naive.

**2. Small-s vs Fast-f Tradeoff**:

| Param | Sig | Sign/us | Verify/us | Use |
|-------|-----|----------|-----------|-----|
| SHA2-128s | 7856 | 14M cyc | 1.2M | firmware root |
| SHA2-128f | 17088 | 0.9M | 2.0M | high-volume log |
| SHAKE-256s | 29792 | 35M | 1.5M | long-term archive |
| SHAKE-256f | 49956 | 1.8M | 3.1M | CA intermediate |

Rust embed:

```rust
fn slh_aggregate_verify(roots: &[Node], proofs: &[Path], sig_root: &SlhSig) -> bool {
    // 1. verify root sig on MTL root
    if !slh_verify(MTL_ROOT_PUB, &mtl_root_hash(roots), sig_root) { return false; }
    // 2. each leaf root authentication
    roots.iter().zip(proofs).all(|(r,p)| mtl_verify_path(r, p, mtl_root))
}
```

> Hash-based threshold? Vinaigrette-style MPC for hash evaluations is impractical (10k hashes per sign). Instead use *threshold XMSS via proactive secret sharing of WOTS+ sk* with $2^{h}$ forward-secure property; still requires state sync.

### 4.3 Multivariate MAYO Threshold: Whipping Oil Spaces with MPC

MAYO's trick: *expand public key locally*. To thresholdize, need distributed linear solving [10][11].

**OV Linear System Problem**:

Signing MAYO: Given oil subspace $O$, find $v_1..v_k$ random, compute $y = H(m) - P(v)$, produce matrix $A \in \mathbb{F}_q^{mo \times k o}$ (bilinear contributions), solve $A x = y$. Doing this thresholdized leaks $A$ via Gaussian elimination pivots.

**Share-the-MAYO** [11] approach:

- Represent $A$ as sum $A = \sum_i A_i$ each party's contribution of $v_i$ share.
- MPC protocol: BGW-style multiplication triples over $\mathbb{F}_{16}$ small field but need extension for inversion.
- Minimum-leakage linear solving: use *randomized mask* $R \in GL_{mo}$, solve $R A x = R y$. Instead of revealing pivots, parties jointly compute rank via secure coin flip; if singular (prob $1/q$), restart *inside MPC* without revealing failure to external adversary.
- Novel *punch*: instead of Shamir share of oil trapdoor matrix $O$ (dimension $n \times o$), share its *projective closure*.

**Vinaigrette Framework** [10] generic for UOV+MAYO:

- Dishonest majority, active security (SPDZ-like MACs).
- Online phase 2 rounds: each party broadcasts vinegar share $\tilde v_i$, accumulates $P(v_i)$ evaluations.

Performance numbers from [11] (3-party honest majority):

| Scheme | Time sign ms (LAN) | Comm MB | Abort prob |
|--------|--------------------|---------|------------|
| UOV-Ip | 45 ms | 2.1 | 0.06 |
| MAYO-1 | 38 ms | 1.4 | 0.04 |
| MAYO-3 | 110 ms | 3.8 | 0.02 |

Python sketch MPC solving:

```python
# threshold MAYO signing share
def mayo_partial(o_share, v_random):
    Peval = P_whipped(v_random)  # m polys
    bilinear = sum(P_bilinear(v_random, oil_basis*j) for j in range(o))
    return Peval, bilinear

def mpc_solve(A_shares, y_shares):
    A = reveal(A_shares[0] + A_shares[1])  # masked with random R
    if not is_invertible(A):
        abort_restart_inside_mpc()
    x = gaussian_elim(A, y)
    return share(x)
```

**Aggregate MAYO?** Not inherent; but verifier can batch $k$ signatures via verifying *emulsified equations* combined:

$$ \sum_i \alpha_i P^\star(s^{(i)}) = \sum_i \alpha_i H(m_i) $$

with random $\alpha_i$ challenge, reduces $k$ verifications $k\times 0.9$ ms → $1.2$ ms + $k\cdot 0.1$ ms [11] ~ 4× speedup for block verification.

### 4.4 DKG & Aggregate Verification Plumbing

**Lattice DKG**:

Feldman over module lattices: publish $A s_i + e_i$ with proof of knowledge of shortness (via dilithium-like PoK). Need extractability to prove security against $t-1$ corrupted revealing fake shares. Construction Olingo [8] uses *pairwise verifiable commitments* using Ajtai's hash.

Complaint phase: if party $P_j$ claims bad share from $P_i$, need to publish decryption of share via threshold decryption of Kyber-style encaps. Olingo adds identifiable abort: complaint includes NIZK of correct decryption.

**Aggregation verification equation** (unified):

For lattice aggregation, verifier checks:

$$ A z - c t = w' \quad \land \quad \lVert z \rVert_\infty < \beta' \quad \land \quad h = Hint_q(w') $$

For threshold version, $c = H(\mu, AggCommit)$ where $AggCommit = \sum_i w_i$ non-interactive via hash of all nonces.

For $t$-of-$n$ aggregate multi-message: use *FROST-style binding factor* $\rho_i = H_1(i, m, L)$ where $L$ list of participants. Mitigates *rogue key*.

```tla
Aggregate Safety Invariant
InvNoRogue == \A sig \in ValidSigs:
  sig.c = H(sig.mu, sig.w) /\ sig.z = sum (lambda_i * partial[i])
  => Verify(pk, sig.mu, sig)
```

## 5 Empirical Evaluation / Proofs

### Proof Sketch: Reduction to SelfTargetMSIS

Claim: If adversary breaks TS-UF-4, we break MSIS.

Hybrid 0: real game. Hybrid 1: replace signing oracle with simulator that samples $z \leftarrow S_{\gamma_1-1}$ and programs $c$ via random oracle, uses *rejection sampling without secret* (Dilithium's algorithm A). Statistical distance $\le Q_s \cdot 2^{-\lambda}$ by masking lemma [7]. Final reduction outputs MSIS short vector $(A z - c t - w)$. For $t\le 8$ compact construction, norm bound $\beta' = 2 \cdot \gamma_1$.

## 6 Limitations and Open Problems

- **Gaussian side-channels**: Falcon's FF sampling requires constant-time double → lattice attack with traces as shown in 2023-24 FA on Falcon reference [3]. Fixed-point SamplerZ mitigations increase 
- **Threshold Falcon**: No compact DKG without FHE; NTRU trapdoor not Shamir-linear.
- **Stateful hash**: FORS sync fragility, MTL equivoc risks.
- **MAYO hardness**: Whipped MQ less studied, assurance 2y vs 10y lattices.

> Open Problem: Provide lattice DKG with $O(n)$ communication without FHE, preserving identifiable abort and post-quantum ZK of shortness under standard MSIS/MLWE only.

> Open Problem: Standard-model stateless hash aggregate with polylog proof size from SPP?

## 7 Conclusion

We traversed post-quantum trust primitives: lattice Fiat–Shamir *Dilithium* with its abort-born efficiency, NTRU *Falcon* fast verification elegance, hash-based *SPHINCS+* conservative sponge, and *MAYO* oil-vinegar whipped compactness. We showed thresholdization is not generic:

- In ML-DSA world, sharing needs *mask flooding* and AOM-MISIS tightness to avoid simulator blowup [7][8][9].
- In Falcon world, *Gaussian convolution* precludes naive Lagrange.
- In SLH-DSA world, *aggregation is not algebraic but Merkle-centric* — MTL ladder buys 12× compression but requires trusted batcher [2].
- In MAYO world, *MPC linear-system-in-the-exponent* is feasible with $O(mo)$ triples because field $q=16$ small, but rank-failure leakage demands inside-MPC restart [11].

Practical synthesis: for 3-of-5 cloud HSM federation securing 100M TLS certs/year, **Olingo TRaccoon** + **SLH-DSA hybrid MTL** gives quantum-resilient chain with <10KB cert chain, tolerable 6ms sign latency, verifiable under 0.3ms. For embedded firmware root with 10k devices, **MAYO-1 2-of-3 threshold** offers 321 B signature sub-KB over LoRa.

Future is *composite aggregate*: one ceremony DKGs Falcon root, Dilithium intermediate, MAYO leaf, SLH-DSA fallback — verifying in 2ms with batch $\rho$-challenge; TLA+ model above ensures liveness even under $t-1$ Byzantine aborts.

*Post-quantum distributed trust is not about replacing a signature; it is about re-architecting unanimity where math, not dealer, is the root.*
