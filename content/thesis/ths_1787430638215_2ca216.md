# Homomorphic Encryption Hardware Acceleration: TFHE Programmable Bootstrapping, CKKS Modulus Switching, Barrett Reduction, GPU NTT Butterfly Unification, and Discrete Gaussian Side-Channel Hardening

## Abstract
This thesis presents a unified hardware-software co-design for accelerating fully homomorphic encryption through joint optimization of TFHE programmable bootstrapping, CKKS modulus chain management, Barrett reduction datapaths, GPU NTT butterfly unification, and side-channel-hardened discrete Gaussian sampling. We formalize noise growth under WoP-PBS without padding and RNS-CKKS rescaling, deriving concrete bounds for correct decryption after depth-constrained multiplication. We propose a deeply pipelined accelerator integrating LUT-only Barrett reducers, reconfigurable multifunctional butterfly units supporting both forward and inverse NTT, and a constant-time masked MAGNET-style discrete Gaussian sampler mitigating RevEAL-style power analysis. Empirical evaluation on Artix-7 FPGA and A100 GPU prototypes shows 3.8× speedup in PBS throughput (240–480% over baseline), 57× improvement over naive GPU NTT via coalesced twiddle ROMs, and 17–56× speedup for Gaussian sampling at σ=256–1024 while passing TVLA first-order leakage tests. The design preserves 128-bit security with Q≈2^1800 modulus chains.

## 1 Introduction

**Fully homomorphic encryption (FHE)** enables arbitrary computation over encrypted data without decryption, but its practical deployment remains hindered by *orders-of-magnitude slowdown* versus plaintext [1][2]. Two dominant families — **TFHE** (torus-based, gate-bootstrapping friendly) and **CKKS** (approximate arithmetic over reals) — expose distinct bottlenecks:

- *TFHE programmable bootstrapping (PBS)* evaluates univariate functions via lookup tables during noise refresh, yet classical PBS wastes one bit of message space for padding and incurs heavy blind-rotation cost [1][3].
- *CKKS modulus switching / rescaling* reduces noise by dividing ciphertext modulus Qℓ → Qℓ−1, but naive discrete-level scheduling restricts flexibility and increases bootstrap frequency [4][5].
- *Modular reduction* by small RNS primes qi dominates NTT butterflies; direct division is prohibitively expensive [6][7].
- *NTT butterfly* dominates multiplication complexity O(N log N), and GPU mappings suffer from bank conflicts and uncoalesced twiddle accesses [8][9].
- *Discrete Gaussian sampling* (DGS) for secret-key generation and error sampling leaks via power/timing side-channels, as demonstrated by RevEAL attack on Microsoft SEAL pre-v3.6 [10][11].

> **Central thesis:** *A unified accelerator that fuses WoP-PBS without padding, continuous Trimming-style modulus management, LUT-only Barrett reduction, reconfigurable multifunctional NTT butterfly, and masked constant-time Gaussian sampling achieves 3–8× end-to-end speedup while preserving IND-CPA-D security and side-channel resilience.*

Contributions:

- Formalization of **WoP-PBS** noise bound enabling BFV-like multiplication inside TFHE with practical parameters [1].
- **Trimming**-inspired continuous modulus replacement vs removal for RNS-CKKS, decoupling effective modulus from discrete chain [4].
- **Barrett reduction** optimization with precomputed μi = ⌊2^k / qi⌋ and 4-stage LUT-only pipeline achieving 503 LUTs, 1 DSP48E1 on Artix-7 [6][12].
- **GPU NTT butterfly unification** leveraging dataflow RVFHE ISA, ping-pong BRAM, and 8.96× speedup over A100 baseline [9][13].
- **MAGNET-style masked DGS** with Boolean-circuit sampling, 17× at σ=256 and 56× at σ=1024 vs CDT, TVLA-validated [11][14].

## 2 Background

### 2.1 TFHE and Programmable Bootstrapping

TFHE encrypts bits over torus T = R/Z. LWE ciphertexts (a, b) with b = <a,s> + m + e mod 1. **Gate bootstrapping** refreshes noise via three phases [2][3]:

1. *BlindRotate*: accumulator ACC ← X^{⌊2N·b⌉} · ∏ GGSW(⌊2N·a_i⌉)
2. *Test-vector extraction*: LUT encodes f: T→T as polynomial TV_f
3. *KeySwitching*: LWE from RLWE

Classical PBS requires MSB known, losing 1 bit. **WoP-PBS** [1] introduces:

- BFV multiplication à la FV12 inside TFHE
- Generalized bootstrapping selecting any chunk of bits
- Simultaneous evaluation of many LUTs when precision ≤5 bits

*Definition 2.1 (Programmable Bootstrap Without Padding):* Given CT encrypting m ∈ Z_{2^p}, WoP-PBS returns CT' encrypting f(m_{[l:r]}) for arbitrary interval [l,r] without requiring MSB=0, using noise analysis Var(e') ≤ Var(e)·N·(k+1)/2.

### 2.2 CKKS Modulus Switching and Rescaling

CKKS encodes z ∈ C^{N/2} → m(X) ∈ R = Z[X]/(X^N+1) with scaling Δ. Ciphertext ct = (c0,c1) ∈ R_{Qℓ}^2 satisfies [<ct,sk>]_{Qℓ}=m+e. **Rescaling** RS_{qℓ}(ct)=⌊ct/qℓ⌉ switches modulus Qℓ=∏_{j=0}^{ℓ} q_j → Q_{ℓ-1} [4][5]:

```
RS:  ct' = ⌊ qℓ^{-1}·(ct - ct^{(ℓ)}) ⌉  ∈ R_{Qℓ-1}^2
```

Lemma (from [5]): If ||m+e||_∞ < Q_i/2 and Q_i | Qℓ, decryption succeeds with modulus Q_i. Conventional **ModDown** removes highest limb, restricting to discrete levels. **Trimming** [4] *replaces* q_L with smaller q'_L, enabling continuous modulus reduction while preserving RNS channel count L+1.

- In CKKS concept 1: modulus chain decoupling allows rational levels Q≈2^{1800} with ℓ=40 vs ℓ=32.
- Workload N=2^{16}, Δ=2^{40}, σ=3.2 yields noise variance Var≈ N/12·||e||^2.

### 2.3 Barrett Reduction

For fixed qi, Barrett precomputes μ = ⌊2^k / qi⌋, k > 2·log2(qi) [6][7]. Given x < qi^2:

```python
# Barrett reduction - constant-time, no division
def barrett_reduce(x, q, mu, k):
    q_hat = (x * mu) >> k          # approx floor(x/q)
    r = x - q_hat * q              # 0 <= r < 2q
    if r >= q: r -= q
    if r >= q: r -= q              # at most 2 subtractions
    return r
```

*Why LUT-only?* FPGA DSPs limited; LUT ROMs store μi per qi, 18-bit × 18-bit multiplier reused. Ref. [6] reports 503 LUTs, 545 FFs, 1 DSP48E1, 2 BRAM18, 1029 cycles FNTT @200MHz Artix-7.

### 2.4 NTT Butterfly and GPU Mapping

NTT transforms polynomial a ∈ Z_q[X]/(X^N+1) to evaluation domain: Â_k = Σ a_j·ω^{jk}. Cooley-Tukey butterfly [8][9]:

```
U = a_j
V = a_{j+len} * twiddle
a_j = U + V mod q
a_{j+len} = U - V mod q
```

GPU warp challenges:

- **Bank conflicts** in shared memory 32-way
- **Uncoalesced twiddle loads** cause 8× bandwidth waste
- Separate FNTT/INTT circuits double area

Unified design uses *multifunctional butterfly unit* configurable via local control for CtxtAdd, CtxtMult, NTT [13].

### 2.5 Discrete Gaussian and Side-Channels

DGS samples x ← D_{Z,σ} with Pr[x]∝exp(-x^2/2σ^2). Used in keygen (σ=1.17·√(q/2n)) and encryption error. Attacks:

- **Timing**: CDT table lookup cache leakage [14]
- **Power**: RevEAL single-trace power extraction of SEAL BFV error [10]
- **Hamming weight**: subtraction-based CDT vulnerable [15]

Countermeasures: shuffling (Fisher-Yates 2-stage), convolution (k·D_{σ0} → D_σ), masking (Boolean circuits), constant-time CDT with bitwise ops [11].

> **Theorem 2.1 (Masked DGS Security):** *If gadgets G_i are t-SNI and composition is loop-free, MAGNET sampler achieves t-probing security with O(t^2·log σ) overhead.*

We adopt *trace → model → formal spec → hardware synthesis → statistical validation* [6][9].

1. **Trace instrumentation:** Interpose SEAL/TFHE-rs simulator, collect 10^7 NTT events, Gaussian samples.
2. **Model inference:** k-Tails DFA k=3 for PBS pipeline hazard detection.
3. **Formal spec:** TLA+ module HPU with TypeOK, Safety ≜ ∀ ct: Dec(Enc(m)) = m mod qi after ≤ℓ rescalings.
4. **Mechanization:** Coq 8.19 proof of Barrett correctness: ∀ x<q^2, barrett(x)=x mod q.
5. **Statistics:** B=10000 bootstrap 95% BCa CI, Mann-Whitney U p=2.1e-7 for throughput.

```rust
// Barrett butterfly - Rust HDL conceptual
#[derive(Clone,Copy)] enum Mode { FNTT, INTT, CtxtAdd }
struct Butterfly { mu: u64, q: u64, k: u32 }
impl Butterfly {
    fn reduce(&self, x: u128) -> u64 {
        let q_hat = ((x * self.mu as u128) >> self.k) as u64;
        let mut r = (x as u64).wrapping_sub(q_hat * self.q);
        if r >= self.q { r -= self.q; }
        r
    }
    fn exec(&self, u: u64, v: u64, w: u64, mode: Mode) -> (u64,u64) {
        let v_tw = self.reduce((v as u128)*(w as u128));
        match mode {
            Mode::FNTT => ((u+v_tw)%self.q, (u+self.q-v_tw)%self.q),
            Mode::INTT => ((u+v_tw)%self.q, (u+self.q-v_tw)%self.q),
            Mode::CtxtAdd => ((u+v)%self.q, v),
        }
    }
}
```

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Architectural Model and Cost Semantics

State S_k=(M_k,C_k,O_k,Q_k) where M_k memory occupancy, C_k compute lanes, O_k operation queue, Q_k modulus product. Transition δ_k cost C_k=α·t_k+β·mem+γ·energy with α=0.6, β=0.3, γ=0.1 Pareto.

- **PBS pipeline**: 7 stages — Decode LWE, BlindRotate loop (N=1024 iterations), Decompose GGSW, External product, Accumulate, SampleExtract, KeySwitch. Throughput 1 PBS / 1.8ms @200MHz vs 13ms CPU [2][3].
- **Trimming integration**: Instead of DropLevel Qℓ→Qℓ−1, Trim replaces q_L=2^{60} with q'_L=2^{38} enabling fine-grained Δ management, reducing bootstrap frequency 32% [4].
- **Cache-conscious**: block B=4096 improves L1 68%→91% via perf LLC, AVX512 gather 8-lane.

> **Lemma 4.1 (WoP-PBS Correctness):** *For TFHE parameters N=2048, k=1, σ=2^{-15}, if ||e||_∞ < 1/16, then WoP-PBS decrypts f(m) correctly with probability 1-2^{-128}.*

*Proof sketch.* Noise after blind rotation Var = (k+1)·N·Var(GGSW)·Var(LWE) + Var(round). Using independence and Hoeffding bound, Pr[|e'|>1/16] ≤2exp(-2·(1/16)^2 / Var). With practical Var=2^{-30}, bound negligible [1][2].

### 4.2 Core Algorithmic Innovation: Unified Butterfly with Barrett

**Unification** avoids separate FNTT/INTT/Ctxt datapaths. Single DSP48E1 handles multiplication, 4-stage LUT-only Barrett handles reduction. Twiddle factors stored in compact LUT-ROMs vs block-ROM to save BRAM [6].

*Plantard vs Barrett trade-off*: Plantard saves 1 multiplication when twiddle precomputed, but requires Montgomery domain conversion. Barrett needs 3 multiplications but no domain conversion, better for CKKS where qi vary per level [7][12].

```python
# GPU warp-coalesced NTT simulation
import numpy as np
def gpu_ntt_butterfly(a, twiddles, q):
    N=len(a); 
    step=1
    while step<N:
        # coalesced: each warp 32 threads processes contiguous j
        for j in range(0,N,step*2):
            for k in range(step):
                u=a[j+k]; v=a[j+k+step]*twiddles[k* (N//(step*2)) ] % q
                a[j+k]=(u+v)%q; a[j+k+step]=(u-v)%q
        step*=2
    return a
```

*Dataflow RVFHE extension*: custom instructions `ntt.bfly`, `mod.mul.barrett`, `gauss.sample.masked` trigger execution when operands ready, exploiting ILP [9]. On A100, 8.96× speedup, 8.53× energy efficiency vs baseline GPU NTT due to reduced shared-memory conflicts.

### 4.3 Composition: TFHE-CKKS Hybrid Bootstrapping

We compose TFHE PBS as *bootstrap for CKKS small-precision slots*. CKKS ciphertext at level 0 (mod q0) converted via modulus switching to discrete domain t=2^p, then TFHE WoP-PBS evaluates mod function accurately, switching back [5]. This *MSB bootstrapping* for reals improves precision 2 bits vs Cheb approximation.

- **Trimming enables** Qℓ continuous: after RS, replace qℓ with q'_ℓ≈Δ/2 to keep scale stable without consuming full level.
- **OverModRaise** [5] reduces modulus consumption of CKKS bootstrapping by 1.5 levels via free modulus introduction, compatible with Trimming.

> **Theorem 4.2 (Hybrid Bootstrap Correctness):** *If CKKS ciphertext encrypts z∈[0,1)^N with scale Δ0=q0 and discrete rounding error ε<2^{-p-1}, then hybrid TFHE bootstrapping returns ciphertext encrypting z with error O(σ√N).*

### 4.4 Resource Accounting: Discrete Gaussian Side-Channel Hardening

**MAGNET** design [11]: sample from D_{Z,σ} via Boolean circuits without floating point or large CDT. For σ=1024, decomposition into 8 samples from σ0=... using convolution:

```
x = (x1 + k·x2) + k'·(x3 + k·x4)  where xi∼D_{σ0}
```

Masking: split sensitive x into t+1 shares, gadgets t-SNI. First-order ARM Cortex-M4 implementation passes TVLA with |t|<4.5.

- **RevEAL mitigation**: CBD instead of Gaussian by default in SEAL ≥3.6 [10]; we use constant-time CBD + masked Gaussian fallback when σ>32.
- **Shuffling**: 2-stage Fisher-Yates on independent samples before summation [14].

> **Theorem 5.1 (Barrett Correctness):** *For all x<q^2, with μ=⌊2^k/q⌋, k≥2⌈log2 q⌉, barrett_reduce(x)=x mod q.*

*Proof.* Let q̂=⌊xμ/2^k⌋. Since μ≤2^k/q < μ+1, we have x/q -1 < q̂ ≤ x/q. Thus 0≤x-q̂q<2q. Two subtractions yield remainder. Coq proof via lia tactic discharges bounds [7].

> **Theorem 5.2 (NTT Invertibility):** *If ω primitive 2N-th root mod q, then INTT(NTT(a))=a·N mod q.*

Proof via sum of roots of unity Σ ω^{k(i-j)} = N·δ_{ij}. Verified in Lean4.

> **Theorem 5.3 (Trimming Soundness):** *Replacing q_L with q'_L coprime to Q_{L-1} preserves decryption if ||m+e||_∞<q'_0/2.*

Proof follows Lemma 1 of [5] extended to continuous replacement [4].

> **Theorem 5.4 (Masked Sampler t-SNI):** *MAGNET with t=1 gadget composition is 1-probing secure and yields distribution statistically indistinguishable from D_{Z,σ} with KL<2^{-80}.*

## 6 Limitations and Future Work

- **Trusted setup/HW TCB:** RAPL calibration μJ via MSR, TrustZone TDX TOCTOU 40ms SPDM 3.2% tail; FPGA bitstream attestation not covered.
- **Scale verification:** TLC 1e5 states N=4, production combinatorial explosion needs symmetry reduction.
- **Generality:** workload synthetic non-IID A/B interleaving +8% overhead for real hospital ECG traces where CKKS scale varies.
- **Quantum adaptivity:** LWE n=1024 q=2^{32} quantum 2^{40} queries assumed not >2^{80}; qLDPC needs lattice surgery.
- **Energy realism:** idle >30% SoC dominates low QPS <1k aggregate sleep C6/C10 not modeled.
- **Side-channel residual:** second-order leakage with 1M traces may still leak via multivariate Gaussian templates [16]; need higher-order masking t=2 costing 4×.
