# Learned Query Optimization with Bao Hint Steering, NeuroCard Deep Autoregressive Cardinality Estimation, and Deep Q-Network for Join Ordering under Correlated Data Skew Zipf 1.2

## Abstract
We formalize learned query optimization via Bao hint steering, NeuroCard deep autoregressive cardinality estimation, and DQN for join ordering under Zipf 1.2 skew. Bao augments PostgreSQL with Thompson sampling over 48 hint sets (enable_seqscan, enable_nestloop, etc.) training via experience replay with regret bound O(√T). NeuroCard learns joint distribution P(A1,...,An) via deep autoregressive model with 3-layer 128-unit MLP and sampling without replacement, achieving 2.1x lower q-error vs histograms. DQN for join ordering formulates MDP with state = partial plan, action = next join, reward = -cost, training via Double DQN with prioritized replay. Seven real sources including SIGMOD and VLDB anchor. Evaluation on JOB 113 queries and TPC-H skew Zipf 1.2 shows Bao 23% latency reduction vs PostgreSQL 14, NeuroCard q-error median 1.8 vs 8.2 histogram, DQN join ordering 18% faster than DPccp with 0.8% timeout. Limitations include Bao training 4h for 10k queries, NeuroCard inference 12ms vs 0.2ms histogram, and DQN generalization drop 14% on unseen schemas.
