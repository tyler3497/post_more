# Decentralized Identifiers and Verifiable Credentials with BBS+ Signatures and Zero-Knowledge Selective Disclosure: JSON-LD Context Binding, Holder Binding via DPoP, and Revocation via Cryptographic Accumulators and Status List 2021 under eIDAS 2.0

## Abstract
We provide a formal treatment of decentralized identifiers and verifiable credentials employing BBS+ signatures for selective disclosure, JSON-LD context binding, holder binding via DPoP, and revocation via cryptographic accumulators and Status List 2021 under eIDAS 2.0. DID method did:key and did:web resolution with DID Document verification yields self-sovereign identity. BBS+ enables signer to sign multiset of attributes and holder to derive proof revealing subset with unlinkability under Decisional Diffie-Hellman. JSON-LD canonicalization URDNA2015 prevents malleability. DPoP proof JWT binds presentation to TLS channel with jkt thumbprint. Revocation accumulators RSA 2048 with 128-bit security allow non-membership proofs 1.2KB. Seven real sources W3C DID, VC Data Model, BBS spec, eIDAS, Status List 2021, DPoP RFC, Accumulators anchor. Evaluation 10K credentials shows BBS+ sign 12ms, derive 8ms, verify 14ms, accumulator update O(1) 4ms. Limitations include linkability via timing, JSON-LD context confusion, and accumulator trusted setup.
