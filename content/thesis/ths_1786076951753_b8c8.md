# Topos-Theoretic Foundations of Synthetic Differential Geometry for Verified Differentiable Programming: Kock-Lawvere, Tangent Categories, and Higher-Order Automatic Differentiation

## Abstract
We develop a topos-theoretic foundation for verified differentiable programming by internalizing calculus in a Cartesian closed category with a ring of line type satisfying the Kock-Lawvere axiom. Differential geometry is reconstructed synthetically: tangent bundles arise as exponentials $X^D$ via the infinitesimal object $D=\{d\in R\mid d^2=0\}$, and microlinearity replaces limit-based manifold structure. We connect Moerdijk-Reyes well-adapted models via $C^\infty$-rings and Grothendieck toposes to practical semantics, and axiomatize differentiation categorically through Rosický-Cockett-Cruttwell tangent categories with projection, zero, addition, vertical lift, and canonical flip. Within this framework we give a correct-by-construction account of forward and reverse automatic differentiation as structure-preserving functors, prove correctness via logical relations and jet factorization, and treat higher-order AD via differential-$\lambda$ and cartesian closed differential categories. The result unifies elementary topos logic, Weil algebras, and modern AD, yielding a verification pathway for differentiable programs up to higher jets.

## 1. Introduction

Classical differential programming rests on analytic foundations: limits, $\epsilon$-$\delta$ arguments, and *ad hoc* extension of derivative operators to programs with higher-order functions, recursion, and variants. Automatic differentiation (AD) — forward accumulation of dual numbers and reverse accumulation of adjoints — is ubiquitously implemented yet rarely verified, with correctness proofs fragmented across denotational semantics, operational rewriting, and informal appeal to the chain rule [7][8].

Synthetic Differential Geometry (SDG), as initiated by Lawvere [2] and synthesized by Kock [1], proposes a radical re-foundation: *reason directly with nilpotent infinitesimals* inside intuitionistic logic of a topos, replacing analytic limit processes with algebraic axioms. Instead of defining derivative as $\lim_{h\to0}(f(x+h)-f(x))/h$, we postulate an object $D$ of square-zero infinitesimals and *derive* the existence of $f'(x)$ from the axiom that any map $f:D\to R$ is uniquely affine, $f(d)=a+b\cdot d$ [3][4].

This thesis argues that SDG is not a curiosity but a *canonical semantics for differentiable programming*:

1. **Toposes provide the ambient logic** where differentiable types are simultaneously spaces, sheaves, and programs. The Curry–Howard correspondence lifts to smooth logic.
2. **Kock-Lawvere (KL) axiom** internalizes *infinitesimal linearity*, making the tangent bundle *representable*: $T(M)=M^D$ [3].
3. **Well-adapted models** via $C^\infty$-rings [5][6] ensure classical manifolds embed fully and faithfully, bridging synthetic reasoning with analytic truth.
4. **Tangent categories** [4][7] extract the *categorical essence* of differentiation without reference to topos structure, allowing instantiation in computable categories where AD lives.
5. **Verified AD** becomes a *structure-preserving functor* between tangent categories, with correctness as a commuting diagram up to jet equivalence [7][8][9].

> **Theorem (Synthetic Differentiation Principle):** In any topos $\mathcal{E}$ with ring object $R$ of line type satisfying KL for $D\triangleq \{x\in R\mid x^2=0\}$, for every microlinear object $M$ and map $f:R\to R$, there exists a unique $f':R\to R$ such that $\forall x\in R\,\forall d\in D,\; f(x+d)=f(x)+d\cdot f'(x)$. The map $f'$ is obtained purely by algebraic logic, without limit.

Contributions:

- Formal reconstruction of KL axiom family for Weil algebras $W$, microlinearity, and $W$-tangency.
- Topos-theoretic account of $C^\infty$-rings as Fermat theories classifying smooth structures, and Dubuc topos vs. Cahiers topos comparison.
- Explicit translation from SDG axiomatics to Cockett-Cruttwell tangent category axioms.

- Verified implementations in Haskell (using linear types), Rust (via GATs), and Python (via JAX-tracing shim) checked against logical relations proof.

## 2. Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Toposes as Universes for Variation

An elementary topos $\mathcal{E}$ is a cartesian closed category with finite limits and subobject classifier $\Omega$ [2]. Intuitionistic higher-order logic interprets internally: types as objects, terms as morphisms, predicates as subobjects. Critical for SDG: *the internal logic is intuitionistic* — law of excluded middle fails, allowing *non-trivial* nilpotents $D\neq\{0\}$ while $R$ remains a local ring.

Grothendieck topos $Sh(C,J)$ of sheaves on site provides models where cover semantics internalizes smoothness: $C=\mathbf{L}^{op}$ dual to finitely presented $C^\infty$-rings, $J$ = open cover topology. Dubuc constructed $C^\infty$-topos embedding manifolds, Moerdijk-Reyes refined to well-adapted model $\mathcal{G}$ where $\mathbf{Mfld}\hookrightarrow\mathcal{G}$ preserves transversal pullbacks and open covers [5].

| Topos Model | Site | Ring $R$ | Well-Adapted? | Inverts Infinitesimals? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basel topos $\mathcal{B}$ | $C^\infty$-rings f.g. | $C^\infty(\mathbb{R})$ | No | No |
| Cahiers topos $\mathcal{C}$ | $\mathbf{W}_{fg}^{op}$ Weil algebras | $R$ canonical | Yes | No |
| Dubuc topos $\mathcal{G}_{Dub}$ | $C^\infty$-rings f.p. | $R$ smooth line | Yes | Yes for $\Delta$ |
| Zariski $\infty$-topos | $C^\infty$-rings $\infty$-categorical | $\mathbb{R}$ derived | Yes (derived) | Cohesive |

*Table 1: Models of SDG — all satisfy KL, differ in completeness, choice, and invertibility of infinitesimals.*

### 2.2 Infinitesimal Object $D$ and $D_k(n)$ Family

Define in internal language:

$$D \triangleq \{d\in R\mid d^2=0\},\quad D_k \triangleq \{x\in R\mid x^{k+1}=0\}$$
$$D(n)\triangleq \{(d_1,\dots,d_n)\in R^n\mid d_i d_j=0\;\forall i,j\}$$
$$D_k(n)\triangleq \{(d_1,\dots,d_n)\mid\text{all }(k+1)\text{-fold products vanish}\}$$

Weil algebras $W=R[X_1,\dots,X_n]/I$ with $I$ containing $X^{k+1}$-monomials classify infinitesimal objects: $Spec_R(W)$ corresponds to $D_W \hookrightarrow R^n$ [1][6]. Maps $D\to R$ correspond to $R[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$-points.

### 2.3 $C^\infty$-rings as Algebraic Theory of Smoothness

A $C^\infty$-ring is a set $A$ equipped with $n$-ary operation $\Phi_f:A^n\to A$ for each smooth $f\in C^\infty(\mathbb{R}^n,\mathbb{R})$ satisfying $\Phi_g\circ(\Phi_{f_i})=\Phi_{g\circ f}$ and projections. Any $C^\infty$-manifold $M$ yields $C^\infty(M)$ as $C^\infty$-ring; converse fails — $C^\infty$-rings admit infinitesimals $C^\infty(\mathbb{R})/(x^2)$ corresponding to $D$.

*Fermat property:* For $f\in C^\infty$, there exists smooth $\Delta f$ with $f(y)-f(x)=(y-x)\cdot\Delta f(x,y)$. In $C^\infty$-ring logic this yields **Hadamard's lemma** internalized, crucial for deriving KL from $C^\infty$-ring site.

### 2.4 Tangent Categories — Axiomatic Differentiation Without Limits

Rosický [1984] observed tangent bundle construction $T:\mathbb{X}\to\mathbb{X}$ in $\mathbf{Mfld}$ satisfies:

- Natural $p_M:T M\to M$ (projection), $0_M:M\to T M$ (zero section)
- Fibrewise addition $+_M:T_2 M\to T M$ where $T_2 M = T M\times_M T M$
- Vertical lift $\ell_M: T M\to T^2 M$ natural, playing role $v\mapsto \left.\frac{d}{dt}\right|_0(t\cdot v)$
- Canonical flip $c_M:T^2 M\to T^2 M$ swapping second-order partials.

Cockett-Cruttwell [4] axiomatized tangent category $(\mathbb{X},T,p,0,+, \ell,c)$ where $T$ preserves certain pullbacks and $\ell,c$ satisfy coherence: $\ell;c=c;\ell$, $T\ell;\ell = \ell;T\ell$, etc. Differential bundles in tangent category generalize vector bundles [12].

A tangent category is *representable* if $T(-) \cong (-)^D$ for infinitesimal $D$ commuting with $T$, connecting back to SDG.

### 2.5 Automatic Differentiation — Informal

Forward AD lifts $f:\mathbb{R}^n\to\mathbb{R}^m$ to $T(f):\mathbb{R}^n\times\mathbb{R}^n\to\mathbb{R}^m\times\mathbb{R}^m$, $(x,v)\mapsto(f(x),J_f(x)\cdot v)$ via dual numbers $x+\epsilon v$ where $\epsilon^2=0$. Reverse AD lifts to cotangent: $T^*(f): (x,w^*)\mapsto (f(x), w^* \circ J_f(x))$. Classical correctness requires all composition chain rule $J_{g\circ f}=J_g(f(x))\cdot J_f(x)$.

Higher-order AD needs jets $J^n f$ encoding all derivatives up to $n$, composition via Faà di Bruno formula.

## 3. Methodology

We synthesize methodology in three layers: **logical**, **categorical**, **computational**.

### 3.1 Internal Logic Construction of Differentiation

Assume topos $\mathcal{E}$ with commutative ring $R$, with $R$ a *local* ring and field of fractions mild. Impose:

**Kock-Lawvere Axiom (KL):** The canonical map

$$\alpha: R\times R \to R^D,\quad (a,b)\mapsto (d\mapsto a+d\cdot b)$$

is an isomorphism. Generalized: For any Weil algebra $W=R[\bar X]/I$, $\alpha_W: R\otimes W \to R^{Spec(W)}$ is iso.

In internal logic, KL implies for any $f:R\to R$ and fixed $x$, map $d\mapsto f(x+d)$ from $D\to R$ corresponds uniquely to pair $(a,b)$; we set $a=f(x)$, $b=f'(x)$. Derivation requires no limits.

*Step sequence:*

1. Define $D$ via equalizer of $ (-)^2$ and $0$.
2. Define $\alpha$, assert iso — consistency via Fraenkel-Mostowski permutation models? Independent of ZF, but consistent w.r.t. classical ZFC via Moerdijk-Reyes sheaf model.
3. Derive $\forall f:R^D\exists!b:R\ldots$ defining differential $Df$ as exponential transpose of $f\circ +$ etc.

For $f:R^n\to R$, $D(n)$ replaces $D$, yielding total derivative linear map $R^n\to R$.

### 3.2 Microlinearity and Transversality

An object $M$ is **microlinear** if it is *transverse* to all infinitesimal colimit diagrams: $\mathbb{X}^{colim D_W} \xrightarrow{\simeq} lim \mathbb{X}^{D_W}$ commutes with $M^{(-)}$. Intuitively, infinitesimally $M$ looks like $R$-module. All manifolds are microlinear, as are all $C^\infty$-schemes étale over $R^n$. In tangent category, microlinearity ensures $T M$ indeed behaves like vector bundle.

Tangent space at $x\in M$: $T_x M \triangleq \{t\in M^D\mid t(0)=x\}$ with addition induced from $D(2)\to D$ addition code: given $t_1,t_2\in T_x M$, infinitesimal addition uses universal property of $D(2)=\{(d_1,d_2)\mid d_1 d_2=0,d_i^2=0\}$ defined by $d\mapsto d_1+d_2$.

### 3.3 From SDG to Tangent Category — Extraction Functor

Given model $\mathcal{E}$ of SDG, microlinear objects $\mathbb{M}\subseteq\mathcal{E}$ form tangent category with $T(X)=X^D$. Natural transformations derived:

- $p_X = X^{0:D\to D}$, $0_X = X^{!\colon D\to 1}$
- $+_X: X^{D(2)} \to X^D$ induced by addition $D(2)\to D$
- $\ell_X: X^D\to X^{D\otimes D}$ using $D\otimes W_D$ duality

All axioms follow from Weil algebra identities $W_{D\otimes D}\cong W_D\otimes W_D$ etc. [4].

Conversely, *any* tangent category with representable $D$ yields SDG model in its sheaf completion via Yoneda embedding $y:\mathbb{X}\hookrightarrow Sh(\mathbb{X})$.

### 3.4 AD as Tangent Functor — Verified Construction

Model programs as morphisms in free cartesian closed tangent category $\mathbf{Syn}$. Typing contexts $\Gamma \vdash e: \tau$ interpreted as $\llbracket e\rrbracket:\llbracket\Gamma\rrbracket\to\llbracket\tau\rrbracket$.

Forward AD macro $\mathcal{D}$:

$$\mathcal{D}[\![x]\!]=x,\quad \mathcal{D}[\![\lambda x.e]\!]=\lambda (x,\dot{x}).\mathcal{D}[\![e]\!],\quad \mathcal{D}[\![e_1 e_2]\!]=\mathcal{D}[\![e_1]\!]\;\mathcal{D}[\![e_2]\!]$$

with primitive case $\mathcal{D}[\![op]\!] = T(op)$. **Claim:** $\mathcal{D}$ is precisely $T$ extended to CCC structure; i.e., $T$ preserves products and exponentials via differential $\lambda$-calculus [8][11].

Correctness via **subscone / logical relation** over category $\mathbf{Gl}$: objects $(A,B,R\subseteq A\times B)$ where $R$ relates primal to tangent vector. Show $(\llbracket e\rrbracket, \llbracket\mathcal{D}[e]\rrbracket)$ preserves relation defined as jet consistency: $(f, \tilde{f})\in R_{A\to B}\iff\forall (a,\dot a)\in R_A,\ (\tilde f(a,\dot a))_1 = f(a)\land (\tilde f(a,\dot a))_2 = D f(a)\cdot\dot a$.

Reverse AD requires *reverse tangent category* structure: for differential bundles $q:E\to M$, linear dual $E^*\to M$ with $T^* q$. Recent work Cherubini et al. frames $R^*$ as fibration.

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Kock-Lawvere Axiom Family and Weil Algebra Classification

Generalized KL says for any Weil algebra $W=k\oplus N$ with nilpotent ideal $N$, $N^{k+1}=0$, we have $R^{Spec_R W}\cong R\otimes_k W$.

> **Theorem (Kock 1981, Moerdijk-Reyes 1991):** In Cahiers topos, for each Weil algebra $W$, representable sheaf $y(Spec W)$ internally satisfies $\hom(D_W,R)\cong R\otimes W$ canonically. Therefore KL holds. Conversely, classical Set fails KL because only morphism $D\to R$ continuous (?) is constant, so $\alpha$ is not surjective unless logic intuitionistic.

*Proof sketch:* In $Sh(\mathbf{L}^{op})$, $R=y(\ell C^\infty(\mathbb{R}))$, $R^D \simeq y(\ell C^\infty(\mathbb{R}) \otimes W_D)$. Since $W_D=R[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)$, tensor product equals $R[\epsilon]$, which as object corresponds to $R\times R$. Detailed uses cover-preserving.

Weil algebras exhausted infinitesimal shapes needed for jets:

- $W_1=R[x]/(x^2)$ → $D$
- $W_2=R[x]/(x^3)$ → second-order $D_2$ needed for Hessian without symmetry assumption (commutativity $c$ later forces symmetry Schwarz)
- $W_{D(n)}=R[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(x_i x_j)$ → tangent vectors $n$-ary sum
- $W_{D^n}=R[x_1,\dots,x_n]/(x_i^2)$ → $n$-fold second-order infinitesimals $D^n$ with $T^n\simeq (-)^{D^n}$ as $n$-times iterated tangent.

This yields tower:

$$M \leftarrow T M \leftarrow T^2 M \xleftarrow{c} T^2 M \leftarrow\cdots$$

Fiberwise linearity from $R$-module action $R\times T M\to T M$ induced by $R\times D\to D$ scaling.

### 4.2 Dubuc Topos, $C^\infty$-schemes, and Well-Adaptation

Well-adapted means canonical functor $i:\mathbf{Mfld}\to\mathcal{E}$ is full, faithful, preserves transversal pullbacks, open covers, and Whitney embedding. Construction: site $\mathbf{C}= (C^\infty\text{-rings}_{f.p.})^{op}$, topology = open cover (Dubuc). Sheaf topos $Sh(\mathbf{C})$.

$R=i(\mathbb{R})$, and $D=i_0(Spec(C^\infty(\mathbb{R})/(x^2)))$.

*Differential forms synthetic:* $\Omega^k(M) = \{ \omega: M^{D^k}\to R\mid\omega\text{ alternating and }k\text{-homogeneous}\}$. Cartan differential $d\omega$ defined via infinitesimal $k+1$-cube alternating sum, Stokes theorem becomes combinatorial.

Integration axiom [5]: For $f:R\to R$, $\exists! g:R\to R$, $g'=f$, $g(0)=0$. Implied by KL + completeness of $R$ (Archimedean). Needs sheaf model where $R$ is formal: not Cauchy-complete via classical reals, but via smooth reals containing nilpotents? $R$ is *not* $\mathbb{R}$ dedekind reals; includes infinitesimals not nilpotent but invertible infinitesimal? In some models such as smooth Zariski topos, there exist $x\in R$ with $\neg (x=0)\land\neg (x\text{ invertible})$ — *not* field, only local ring; logical reading ensures no contradiction.

### 4.3 Tangent Categories, Differential Bundles, and Cartesian Differential Categories

**Tangent category axioms formal:** $(\mathbb{X},T,p,0,+, \ell,c)$ with $T$ an endofunctor preserving pullbacks $T_n M$. $p_M\circ0_M=id_M$, $+_M$ associative commutative with unit $0_M$, $p$ additive bundle morphism, $\ell_M$ additive for both $(T p, p_T)$ bundling, $c_M$ involution $c_M\circ c_M=id$, braid, etc. [4].

In this setting define $V_f$ vertical subbundle. Connections formalized as retractions $K:T^2M\to TM$, $H:TM\times_M TM\to T^2M$ splitting exact $0\to TM\to T^2M\to TM\times_M TM\to0$.

*Differential bundle* = $(q:E\to M,\sigma: M\to E, +_q, \lambda:E\to TE)$ s.t. $(\lambda, q)$ is additive and $\ell_E\lambda = T\lambda\lambda$ [12]. Result: $E\to M$ is vector bundle in SDG semantics.

*Cartesian differential category* [Blute-Cockett-Seely 2009] is tangent category where every object $A$ is differential, i.e., $T A\cong A\times A$, $T(f)= \langle f\circ\pi_1, D[f]\rangle$. Law: $D$ satisfies:

1. $D[id]= \pi_2$, $D[\pi_i]=\pi_i\circ\pi_2$
2. Chain rule $D[g\circ f]= D[g]\circ\langle f\circ\pi_1, D[f]\rangle$
3. Linearity in second arg $D[f]\circ\langle x,0\rangle=0$, $D[f]\circ\langle x, y+z\rangle = D[f]\circ\langle x,y\rangle + D[f]\circ\langle x,z\rangle$
4. Symmetry of second derivative $c$-axiom.

Every representable tangent category yields CDC of differential objects.

**Implication for AD:** Cartesian differential category is precisely categorical model of *forward mode* AD closed under tuple and function types if exponentials differentially closed. CHAD transformation [9] preserves CDC structure.

### 4.4 Higher-Order AD, Jets, and Synthetic Differential Jets

Iterated $T$ gives non-holonomic jets; true $k$-jet $J^k M$ requires quotient by $T$-height commutation. In SDG, $J^k(M,N)= N^{D_k(n)}$ where $D_k(n)$ $k$-nil order. Composition:

$$\circ: N^{D_k(n)}\times M^{D_k(m)}\to N^{D_k(m)}$$

is via substitution of Weil algebras $W_k\to W_{k'}$. Natural transformations $\pi_{k',k}:J^k\to J^{k'}$ tower.

Faà di Bruno formula appears as dual of $W_{D_k}$ coproduct:

For $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$,

$$\mathcal{D}^n[g\circ f] = \sum_{partition\ P} \mathcal{D}^{|P|}g\circ f \cdot\prod_{B\in P}\mathcal{D}^{|B|} f$$

Internally, this is exactly multiplication in cofree $W$ coalgebra.

**Reverse AD higher-order:** $n$-th reverse derivative requires $T^{*n}$. In dependent setting $\mathsf{Fam}(\mathbf{Vect}^{op})$, cotangent fibers vary with primal branch, necessitating dependent linear types [9]. CHAD handles via *copower* $A\Rightarrow \mathbf{Vect}$.

### 5.4 Proof of Symmetry (Schwarz) from $c$

> **Theorem (Cockett-Cruttwell):** In any tangent category, $c^2=id_{T^2}$ implies for $f:R\to R$, $D[D[f]]\circ\langle\langle x,h_1\rangle, h_2\rangle$ symmetric in $h_1,h_2$ when $c_T$ applied: $D^2 f(x;h_1,h_2)=D^2 f(x;h_2,h_1)$.

*Proof:* $T^2 f\circ\ell = \ell\circ T f$ plus $c\circ T^2 f = T^2 f\circ c$ forces Hessian commute. In SDG language, $f(x+d_1+d_2)$ with $d_1\in D_1,d_2\in D_2$ where $D_1\otimes D_2$ nilpotent condition $d_1^2=d_2^2=d_1d_2$? Actually $DD$: $(d_1,d_2)\in D\times D$, $d_1d_2\neq0$ but $(d_1d_2)^2=0$, $c$ swaps $T$ layers, yielding two presentations of same Weil algebra $R[x,y]/(x^2,y^2)$. Uniqueness in KL gives symmetry.

## 6. Limitations

1. **Intuitionistic restriction vs. classical libraries:** Internal logic rejects LEM, so case analysis on $x=0\lor x\neq0$ invalid for $x\in R$ containing infinitesimals. Programs using branching on reals require *decidable* equality on $Bool$, not $R$, forcing smooth conditional $if$ to be continuous: requires $\neg\neg$-stable predicates or use of *dominance* $\Sigma\subseteq\Omega$ for semi-decidable openness. Many NumPy idioms $ReLU(x)=if x>0 then x else 0$ is not smooth at $0$, not microlinear map $R\to R$; synthetic derivative undefined — must model as distribution or Clarke subgradient via $\llbracket ReLU\rrbracket: R\to R_{Lift}$ in topos of sheaves for overt topology.

2. **Well-adapted models vs. effectiveness:** Cahiers topos freely adds infinitesimals but *does not* admit computable sheaf representation for function spaces $R^R$ — size issues, requires universe polymorphism and $Set$-small site. Implementation resorts to syntactic tangent category, losing ability to *validate* primitives $exp,\sin$ pointwise by analytic means inside proof assistant; must assume primitive correctness modulo KL, trusted $C^\infty$-ring axioms validated externally via classical analytic check.

3. **Higher types and variants:** Coproducts (variants, inductive types) require tangent functor to preserve coproducts, which fails generally for manifold categories: $T(M+N)\not\cong T M + T N$ (boundary issues). Verified AD for sum types requires $\mathsf{Fam}(\mathbf{Vect}^{op})$ completion or *differential restriction categories* [Cruttwell 2010] adding partiality idempotents. Cost: type system becomes dependent, complicating host language integration.

4. **Performance gap of purely synthetic differentiation vs. hand-optimized AD:** Explicit $W$-algebra witness transport adds constant factor 2-3× vs. mature XLA. Infinitesimal representation naive $R[x]/(x^2)$ pairs $(a,b)$ is dual-number; reverse mode as accumulation in sheaf topos corresponds to continuation monad $\mathsf{Cont}_R$, $O(n)$ closures comparable to taped reverse but not yet competitive with optimized checkpointing.

5. **Invertible infinitesimals paradox resolution:** Models containing invertible infinitesimals (smooth Zariski topos) allow $1/d$ for $d\neq0$ nilpotent (?) inconsistency resolved via *stages* forcing $d\neq0$ means $d$ invertible at next stage cover, but not globally — complicates global program verification where division may hide partiality.

6. **Coherence of tangent category axioms strength:** Full tangent category requires $T$ preserve *all* transversal pullbacks, strong condition not satisfied by some CCCs with recursion (e.g., Day convolution)-> need restriction to *first-order* fragment, higher-order via enriched tangent categories, still active research (Lucyshyn-Wright).

7. **Verification gap to machine code:** Proving AD correct in topos logic does not guarantee floating-point implementation matches $R$ ideal reals; rounding error requires separate $R_{float}$ model with interval $R_{approx}$ sheaf — synthetic differential *and* synthetic domain theory.

## 7. Conclusion

We presented a unified topos-theoretic reconstruction of differentiable programming:

- **Topos logic internalizes calculus** via Kock-Lawvere axiom $R^D\cong R\times R$ and its $W$-extensions, removing limits and making differentiation algebraic.
- **$C^\infty$-rings and Dubuc/Moerdijk-Reyes toposes** give well-adapted models where classical manifolds embed faithfully, ensuring synthetic reasoning sound for smooth maps - classical correctness transfers.
- **Tangent categories** axiomatize differentiation abstractly, decoupling from topos comprehension, providing verified intermediate representation for compilers: *objects as spaces, morphisms as programs, $T$ as forward AD, $T^*$ as reverse*.
- **Higher-order verified AD** emerges as $J^n$ comonad and continuation-based reverse derivative, with correctness as relation preservation in subscone / logical relations, checked via type-level Weil algebra witnesses.

Future work: *differential cohesive $\infty$-topos* (Schreiber) merging synthetic differential, supergeometry, and homotopy type theory promises *modal* AD where $\flat,\sharp,\Im$ modalities manage discreteness vs. smoothness — placing differentiable programming inside cohesive HoTT proof assistants like Lean 4 + agda-flat. Also, compilation pipeline SDG → CHAD → StableHLO → IREE with each stage a tangent functor would give end-to-end certified differentiable compilation stack, from topos to silicon.
