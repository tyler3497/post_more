# Verified Smart Contract Verification via Separation Logic: Move Prover, Certora CVL, ConCert Extraction, and Gas-Bounded Symbolic Execution with Hevm Symbolic VM

## Abstract

This thesis presents a comprehensive study of verified smart contract verification via separation logic, addressing foundational and practical challenges at the intersection of verified smart contract verification via separation logic and modern distributed systems. We formalize the problem, survey evolution from early verified constructions to state-of-the-art Prover deployments, and introduce analytical frameworks for evaluating correctness, security, and performance under adversarial and resource-constrained conditions. Contributions include a taxonomy of design trade-offs, formal reductions to standard assumptions, and an empirical methodology bridging theory and practice. We show verified smart contract verification via separation logic achieves order-of-magnitude improvements in throughput and latency while preserving provable guarantees, and we identify open problems in composability, verification, and hardware acceleration that guide future work. Experiments across diverse benchmarks validate scalability and robustness, with implications for cloud and edge production deployment.

fn verify_kzg(commit: G1, proof: G1, point: Fr, value: Fr, srs: &SRS) -> bool {
    let lhs = pairing(commit - srs.g*value, srs.g2);
    let rhs = pairing(proof, srs.g2_h - srs.g2*point);
    lhs == rhs
}
