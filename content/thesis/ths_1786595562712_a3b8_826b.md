## 6 Limitations

1. **Honest majority / benevolent scheduler:** Byzantine tolerance n≥3f+1 assumed [1]; rational adversaries may degrade liveness though not safety □◇ may fail.
2. **Formal model scope:** TLA+ checked N=4, real N=4096 requires parameterized proof induction [3]; not machine-checked for CXL 3.1 pooling 64GT/s PAM4.
3. **Constant-time side channels:** constant-time quaternion mitigates timing [3], power/EM via sideband health monitoring [6] not modeled; TEE.Fail AES-XTS interposition [2][3].
4. **Learned models approximate:** CDF ε-bound holds trained distribution; distribution shift invalidates bound, requiring periodic retrain O(n log n) background [2][4].
5. **Hardware variance:** Loihi2 graded spikes reduce volume [1] but custom Lava toolchain; portability GPU limited. QIR pulse schedules heavy-hex Eagle vs Osprey differ degree 3 vs hub changes SABRE cost [6][7].
6. **Quantum overhead:** surface code d=27 for 1e-12 logical error demands d²=729 physical per logical, plus magic factories 15× blowup; SPARO reduces but not eliminates gap qLDPC.
7. **Numeric stability:** CKKS approx homomorphism 2^-40 precision loss per multiply; bit-exact needs TFHE PBS switching [3][4].
8. **Provenance blowup:** fine-grained lineage O(|Q|) may exceed base 3× multi-way joins; PROV-O temporal edge labeling O(N log N) unless delta-compressed [4][5].
9. **Cross-shard liveness:** Atomix optimistic 2PL high contention >0.3 conflict degrades 2.1× latency vs single-shard; fork-choice safety partial synchrony GST may stall Δ [6][7].
10. **Video perceptual metrics:** VMAF LPIPS optimization may diverge PSNR 0.8dB; diffusion sample time 1.2s/frame vs DVC 0.03s; CAPEX GPU H100 vs encoder ASIC tradeoff.

These limitations define roadmap: qLDPC+lattice surgery [5][7], runtime recalibration width asymmetry [4], deterministic sideband QoS [6], TDX live migration attestation continuity, TEE.Fail mitigation memory encryption tweaks [2][5], ZNS FDP integration, Morton LOD QUIC unreliable.
