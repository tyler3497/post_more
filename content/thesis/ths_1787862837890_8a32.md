## 6 Limitations

- **Hardware availability**: TDX 1.5 and CCA Realms are pre-production, measurements on simulators [1][3]
- **Assumption fragility**: FCI assumes faithfulness and no selection bias beyond modeled [1][5]; violations lead to PAG over-approximation
- **Scalability**: MZI mesh loss scales O(N), limiting N>64 without amplification [2][4]
- **DP utility**: Fourier accountant tightness degrades for ε>8 due to discretization [3][4]
- **Laser weather**: free-space optical ISL attenuation 12 dB/km in fog, fallback to RF required [1][6]
- **WASM ecosystem**: Component Model tooling immature, debugging support limited [2][3]

Open problems include unified attestation across heterogeneous TEEs, photonic-electronic co-packaging yield, and causal discovery with interventional data.
