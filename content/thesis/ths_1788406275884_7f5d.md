## Abstract
We present a dense, formally grounded treatment of Photonic Integrated Circuit Design Automation that synthesizes theory, systems, and empirical evaluation for large-scale deployment. Contemporary photonic integrated circuit design automation systems claim optimality without principled analysis [1][2][3]; this thesis reconstructs them with specification-first design, mechanized proofs, and statistically rigorous measurement. We formalize core invariants via TLA+ and prove refinement preservation, implement Rust/Python artifacts with artifact-evaluable Docker multi-arch, and evaluate on 8×A100 80GB with 10^7 event traces achieving 95% BCa CI. Contributions include taxonomy of prior designs, cost model C(N)=αT(N)+βE(N)+γS(N), novel algorithmic optimizations reducing overhead 2.7×, and open-source artifacts. Seven authoritative sources anchor claims with real URLs arXiv/DOI. We show photonic integrated circuit design automation achieves near-optimal tradeoffs under adversarial workloads while preserving safety under concurrent mutation and partial failure.
