# Succinct Polynomial Commitments and Folding Schemes: KZG, Inner Product Arguments, FRI Low-Degree Tests, and Nova-Style Incrementally Verifiable Computation over Cycle Curves

## Abstract
We present a unified systematization of **succinct polynomial commitment schemes** and modern **folding schemes** for incrementally verifiable computation (IVC). Polynomial commitments allow a prover to commit to $f \in \mathbb{F}_p^{<d}[X]$ with $O(1)$ or $O(\log d)$ size and later open $f(z)=y$ succinctly. We analyze three paradigms — *pairing-based* KZG [1], *discrete-log* Inner Product Argument (IPA) / Bulletproofs [2], and *hash-based* FRI low-degree tests [4] — on trusted setup, post-quantum security, proof size, and verifier complexity. We then formalize relaxed R1CS folding as introduced in Nova [3], proving that two instances $(U_1,W_1),(U_2,W_2)$ can be folded into $U = U_1 + r U_2 + r^2 T$ with $O(1)$ verifier circuit dominated by two scalar multiplications. We show how this yields IVC without FFTs or pairings in the recursion, instantiated over 2-cycle elliptic curves Pallas/Vesta [7]. We compare HyperKZG, IPA, and Merkle-FRI backends and evaluate concrete costs: Nova's verifier circuit < 20k R1CS constraints, KZG opening $O(1)$ pairings, IPA opening $2\log n$, FRI opening $O(\log^2 d)$. Applications to PCD, Rollups, and zkVMs are discussed.

## 1 Introduction

The transition from monolithic SNARKs to *proofs of incremental execution* has been driven by two primitives: **polynomial commitments** and **folding / accumulation**.

Classically, Plonkish and R1CS SNARKs encode computation as constraints on interpolations of witness vectors; succinctness is achieved by committing to the underlying polynomials $f,g,h$ and opening them at random challenges. Kate-Zaverucha-Goldberg (KZG) commitments [1] give $O(1)$ commitments and openings but require a structured reference string (SRS) and are vulnerable to quantum attacks. Inner Product Argument (IPA) based commitments [2] pioneered by Bootle et al. and deployed as Bulletproofs remove trusted setup and achieve $O(\log n)$ proof size via logarithmic folding of vector bases. FRI [4] achieves transparent, post-quantum security using only a hash-function random oracle, at the cost of $O(\log^2 d)$ proofs from Merkle paths over Reed-Solomon codewords with blowup.

In parallel, *recursion* moved from expensive SNARKs-in-SNARKs verified inside a circuit — requiring pairings in-circuit or long FFTs — to accumulation and folding. Halo [6] introduced the idea of deferring $O(|F|)$ verification to a single final check. Nova [3] maximally crystallized this: instead of proving at each step that the previous IVC proof was valid, it *folds* two relaxed R1CS instances into one.

**Contribution of this thesis:**

* We give complete formal definitions for Additively-homomorphic polynomial commitment schemes $\Pi_{PC}$ with $\mathsf{Commit},\mathsf{Open},\mathsf{Eval}$.
* We provide self-contained constructions for KZG, IPA, and FRI with security reductions to *$d$-SDH*, *DLOG*, and *RO + Reed-Solomon proximity* respectively.
* We define and prove a **Non-Interactive Folding Scheme (NIFS)** for relaxed R1CS and prove IVC security.
* We instantiate IVC over **cycle curves** (Pallas/Vesta, BN254/Grumpkin) and discuss CycleFold, SuperNova, HyperNova.
* We give concrete performance model and verifier circuit breakdown.

> This work is aimed at protocol engineers building zkRollups, zkEVMs, and proof-carrying data systems who need to choose commitment + recursion.

## 2 Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Notation

Let $\mathbb{F}$ be a prime field, $\mathbb{G}$ cyclic group of order $|\mathbb{F}|$. $\mathsf{R1CS}$ is defined by matrices $A,B,C \in \mathbb{F}^{m \times m}$; $(A z) \circ (B z) = C z$ where $z = (W,x,1)$. A *relaxed R1CS* adds scalar $u$ and error vector $E$:

$$ (A Z) \circ (B Z) = u \cdot (C Z) + E, \quad Z = (W,x,u) $$

This linearizes the degree-2 condition to make it foldable.

*Commitment:* $\mathsf{Com}_G(v; r) = r H + \langle v, \mathbf{G} \rangle$. Homomorphic: $\mathsf{Com}(v_1;r_1) + \mathsf{Com}(v_2;r_2) = \mathsf{Com}(v_1+v_2; r_1+r_2)$.

### 2.2 Polynomial Commitments

A polynomial commitment scheme is a tuple:

* $\mathsf{Setup}(1^\lambda, D) \to \mathsf{pp}$
* $\mathsf{Commit}(\mathsf{pp}, f) \to C$
* $\mathsf{Open}(\mathsf{pp}, C, z, f, \text{hint}) \to \pi$
* $\mathsf{Verify}(\mathsf{pp}, C, z, y, \pi) \in \{0,1\}$

Properties: **Binding**, **Hiding** (optional), **Evaluation Binding**, **Knowledge Soundness**, **Succinctness** $|C|,|\pi| = o(d)$.

We contrast three families in Table 1.

| Scheme | Assumption | Setup | Commit Size | Open Size | Verifier | PQ? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **KZG** [1] | $t$-SDH / Pairings | Trusted, updatable | $1 \ \mathbb{G}_1$ | $1 \ \mathbb{G}_1$ | $2 e$ | No |
| **IPA / Bulletproofs** [2] | DLOG | Transparent | $1 \ \mathbb{G}$ | $2 \log n \ \mathbb{G} + 1 \ \mathbb{F}$ | $O(n)$ naïve, $O(\log n)$ with multiexp trick | No |
| **FRI / STARK** [4] | CRHF / RO | Transparent | $1 \ \text{Hash}$ | $O(\log^2 d)$ hashes | $O(\log^2 d)$ | Yes |
| **Dory / HyperKZG** [5] | SXDH + Pairings | Trusted | $1$ | $O(\log n)$ | $O(\log n)$ | No |

*Table 1: High-level tradeoffs of PCS. Concretely, KZG gives 48 bytes on BLS12-381, IPA gives ~1.3KB for $n=2^{10}$, FRI gives 80-200KB depending on query count.*

### 2.3 IVC and PCD

**Incrementally Verifiable Computation** [3] : Prover shows iterative computation $z_{i+1}=F(z_i, w_i)$ holds for $\ell$ steps via proof $\Pi_i$ that can be updated incrementally with work $\approx |F|$, not $\ell \cdot |F|$.

**Proof-Carrying Data (PCD)** generalizes to DAG of computations. IVC is PCD on a line graph.

Classic IVC required verifying a SNARK inside own verifier circuit — expensive. Folding schemes reduce checking two NP instances to checking one of same size.

## 3 Methodology / Formalism

We define folding scheme as primitive weaker than SNARK but sufficient for IVC.

> **Definition 3.1 (Non-Interactive Folding Scheme NIFS).** For relation $\mathcal{R} \subseteq \mathcal{I} \times \mathcal{W}$, a folding scheme is $\mathcal{G},\mathcal{K},\mathcal{P},\mathcal{V}$ where $\mathcal{P}((U_1,W_1),(U_2,W_2)) \to (U,W)$. Completeness: if both inputs valid, output valid. Knowledge Soundness: from folded witness extractor can recover witnesses for inputs.

> **Theorem 3.2 (Nova Folding).** The relaxed R1CS relation admits a secure folding scheme with verifier circuit cost 2 multiscalarmuls.

*Proof sketch:* Given $Z_1=(W_1,x_1,u_1), Z_2$, define cross term:

$$ T = A Z_1 \circ B Z_2 + A Z_2 \circ B Z_1 - u_1 C Z_2 - u_2 C Z_1 $$

and folded $Z = Z_1 + r Z_2$, $u = u_1 + r u_2$, $E = E_1 + r^2 E_2 + r T$. Then $(A Z)\circ (B Z) = u C Z + E$ holds as polynomial in $r$ of degree 2; zero at random $r$ implies coefficients zero. Commitment homomorphism lets verifier compute $\bar{E}, \bar{W}$ linearly. QED.

Our formalism extends to *commitment-carrying* IVC as in [3] Fig 4, where $U = (\bar{E}, u, \bar{W}, x)$ contains commitments to $E,W$.

For cycle curves, we need fields $\mathbb{F}_p, \mathbb{F}_q$ such that $|\mathbb{E}_1| = q = |\mathbb{F}_{p2}|$ and $|\mathbb{E}_2| = p$. Pallas: $y^2 = x^3 +5$ over field $q = 0x4000...0001$, Vesta symmetric [7]. This allows circuits over $\mathbb{F}_p$ to natively encode $\mathbb{F}_q$ scalar operations needed for verifier on other curve.

Implementation methodology: Rust `arkworks` & `nova-snark`. Evaluate GKR-free R1CS with Poseidon hash for Fiat-Shamir.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 KZG: Constant-Size Pairing-Based Commitments

KZG [1] requires SRS $\mathsf{srs}= \{ g^{\tau^i}\}_{i=0}^{d}, \{h^{\tau^i}\}$ for $h \in \mathbb{G}_2$, $\tau \leftarrow \mathbb{F}$ secret. Delete $\tau$. Commit: $C = g^{f(\tau)} = \prod (g^{\tau^i})^{f_i}$.

Opening at $z$: prover computes quotient $q(X) = (f(X)-f(z))/(X-z)$, proof $\pi = g^{q(\tau)}$. Verifier checks pairing:

$$ e(C / g^{f(z)}, g) = e(\pi, h^{\tau} / h^{z}) $$

Extensions [5] (Boneh et al.) batch $k$ openings of $t$ polynomials at different points into one $\pi$ using random linear combination $\rho^i$ and concatenated evaluation set $Z$. This yields $\approx 2$ group elements and $O(k + t) + O(\log)$ $\mathbb{G}_2$ exponentiations, vital for Plonk [5].

**Pros:** $O(1)$ size, $O(1)$ pairing check, algebraic structure ideal for aggregation.
**Cons:** Trusted setup, not PQ, requires pairing-friendly curve (BLS12-381, BN254) which are not cycles with high 2-adicity for efficient NTT alone.

```rust
// KZG::open - illustrative
fn kzg_open(f: Poly, tau_pow: &[G1], z: Fr) -> (Fr, G1) {
    let y = f.eval(z);
    let q = f.sub(&Poly::from(y)) / Poly::from_root(z); // (f - y)/(X - z)
    let pi = msm(tau_pow[0..q.len()], &q.coeffs);
    (y, pi)
}
```

### 4.2 IPA & Bulletproofs: DLOG Folding Without Pairings

Inner Product Argument reduces statement $\langle \mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b} \rangle = c, P = \langle \mathbf{a},\mathbf{G}\rangle + \langle \mathbf{b}, \mathbf{H}\rangle$ to half-size via folding [2][6].

Protocol round for $n=2n'$:

1. Verifier challenge not yet; prover splits $\mathbf{a}_L,\mathbf{a}_R,\mathbf{b}_L,\mathbf{b}_R$, sends $L = \langle \mathbf{a}_L,\mathbf{G}_R\rangle + \langle \mathbf{b}_R,\mathbf{H}_L\rangle + (\mathbf{a}_L \cdot \mathbf{b}_R)U$, $R$ symmetric.
2. Verifier sends $x \xleftarrow{\$} \mathbb{F}^\times$.
3. Both fold $\mathbf{G}' = x^{-1}\mathbf{G}_L + x \mathbf{G}_R$, $\mathbf{a}' = x \mathbf{a}_L + x^{-1}\mathbf{a}_R$, etc.

Classic insight: cross terms $L,R$ vanish after folding because:

$$ P' = P + x^2 L + x^{-2} R = \langle \mathbf{a}', \mathbf{G}'\rangle + \langle \mathbf{b}', \mathbf{H}'\rangle + (\mathbf{a}'\cdot\mathbf{b}')U $$

After $\log_2 n$ rounds, prover sends single $a,b$. Total communication $2\log n$ group + 1 field.

*Relation to folding schemes:* IPA **is** the ancestor of Nova folding, but for vector inner product relation rather than R1CS. Nova keeps same recursive challenge structure but homomorphic commitment replaces $U$.

**Security:** Witness-extended emulation under DLOG, no pairings, transparent. Verifier $O(n)$ to compute folded generators unless precomputed, then $O(\log n)$.

Bulletproofs range proof builds on this to show $v \in [0,2^n)$ by showing polynomial identity $\langle \mathbf{a}_L, \mathbf{a}_R \rangle =0$ plus linear constraints, aggregatable to $m$ values with $O(\log(mn))$ overhead [2].

### 4.3 FRI: Low-Degree Testing and Hash-Based Commitments

Fast Reed-Solomon IOP [4] proves $\mathsf{dist}(f, \mathsf{RS}[D,d]) \leq \delta$ . Prover commits to evaluation over domain $D_0$, $ |D_0| = \rho^{-1} d$, as Merkle root $root_0$. Verifier samples $\alpha_0$, prover folds:

$$ f_{i+1}(y) = \frac{f_i(x)+f_i(-x)}{2} + \alpha_i \frac{f_i(x)-f_i(-x)}{2x}, \quad y = x^2, x \in D_i $$

folding halves domain and degree. Continue until constant $f_r$. Commit each layer. Query phase: random leaf $x_0$ => chained openings on cosets consistency $f_{i+1}(x_{i+1}) = \mathsf{Fold}_{\alpha_i}(f_i(x_i), f_i(-x_i))$.

List-decodable radius analysis shows soundness $\approx (1-\rho)^q$.

To get PCS: commit as before, evaluation consistency via **DEEP-ALI** quotient:

$$ Q_{z,y}(X) = \frac{f(X)-U_{z,y}(X)}{Z_H(X)} $$

where $U$ Interpolates verifier-specified $f(z)=y$ claim, forcing $f$ to satisfy claim globally. Then low-degree test is run on $Q$.

**Pros:** No trust, PQ-safe, small field compatible, fast prover $O(d \log d)$ hashes, no elliptic curves. Used in Starks and Circle STARKs with $2$-smooth domains [4].
**Cons:** 100-500KB proofs vs 48 bytes KZG.

### 4.4 Nova Folding and Cycle Curves

Nova defines relaxed R1CS with commitment to $E,W$ via Pedersen $ \bar{E},\bar{W}$ . NIFS.P computes $T$ as above, commits to $T$, verifier challenges $r = H(\mathsf{vk},U_1,U_2,\bar{T})$, folded instance $U$ is linear combination. Witness $W = W_1 + r W_2$, $E = E_1 + r T + r^2 E_2$.

Incrementally Verifiable Computation:

* $\Pi_0$ trivial
* At step $i$, prover has running $U_i$ proving $z_i$ reachable from $z_0$ in $i$ steps, and fresh $(u_i,w_i)$ proving single step $z_{i+1}=F(z_i)$.
* Fold $U_{i+1} = \mathsf{NIFS.V}(U_i,u_i, r)$. New proof is folding proof plus witness update $O(|F|)$ MSM.

Thus **prover does no FFTs**, only 2 MSMs size $|F|+o(|F|)$. Verifier circuit for NIFS.V: two $\mathbb{G}$ scalar muls $ \bar{E}' = \bar{E}_1 + r \bar{T} + r^2\bar{E}_2$, $\bar{W}' = \bar{W}_1 + r \bar{W}_2$. This is constant ~10-20k constraints, independent of $F$.

> **Cycle requirement:** IVC proof verifier must be represented as circuit over same field it verifies. Over single curve, field mismatch: statements are over $\mathbb{F}_q$ scalar field, but verifying group operations needs base field $\mathbb{F}_p$. Cycle $(E_1,E_2)$ where $|\mathbb{E}_1|=q_2=field_2$ and $|\mathbb{E}_2|=q_1$ solves this. Pallas/Vesta 2-cycle with $\rho \approx 1$ high 2-adicity ($2^{32}$ roots of unity) allows NTT for Plonk prover if needed, but Nova doesn't need NTT in recursion anyway [7]. BN254/Grumpkin also a cycle.

CycleFold [Microsoft 2023] optimization uses second curve only for single scalar multiplication (1.5k gates) instead of full verifier.

Compressed IVC: Nova proof size $O(|F|)$ elements $\to$ compress via Spartan / HyperKZG succinct zkSNARK on top of folded instance $U_\ell$, now $O(\log |F|)$ succinct [3].

![Nova IVC over cycle curves](/thesis/ths_1785999001102_8115-1.webp)

```tla
---- MODULE NovaIVC ----
EXTENDS Naturals, FiniteSets
VARIABLES runningU, stepU, z, i

Init == 
  /\ runningU \in ValidRelaxedR1CS(\* trivial *)
  /\ z = z0 /\ i = 0

Fold(U1, U2, r) == 
  [E |-> U1.E + r*U2.T + r*r*U2.E, W |-> U1.W + r*U2.W, x |-> Hash(z)]

Next == 
  \E w \in Witness :
    LET u == StepInstance(z,w) 
        r == RandomOracle(runningU, u)
    IN /\ z' = F(z,w)
       /\ runningU' = Fold(runningU, u, r)
       /\ i' = i+1

Spec == Init /\ [][Next]_<<runningU,z,i>> /\ WF_<<runningU,z,i>>(Next)
====
```

## 5 Empirical Evaluation / Proofs / Analysis

### 5.1 Theoretical Costs

| Metric | KZG-Plonk | Bulletproofs-IPA | FRI-STARK | Nova-IVC (per step) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prover | $O(d \log d)$ + MSM | $O(n \log n)$ | $O(d \log d)$ hashes | $2$ MSM $\approx 2|F|$ |
| Verifier circuit | $2e$ | $2 \log n$ muls | $O(\log^2 d)$ hashes | $2$ scalar muls, ~15k gates |
| Proof size | 3 $\mathbb{G}_1$ | $2 \log n$ | 100-300KB | 2 $\mathbb{G}$ (uncompressed) |
| Setup | Universal SRS | None | None | None (Pedersen) or SRS (KZG) |

*Nova's constant overhead* means for $|F|=2^{20}$ gates (~1M), recursion overhead <1% runtime vs Halo which required FFT per step $O(|F| \log |F|)$.

**Soundness:** Reduction to DLOG for IPA backend: if adversary folds incorrectly, we extract $R$ for relaxed equation contradiction, breaking $q$-DLOG $2^{\log |G|}$. For KZG backend, extractor fails with probability $d/|\mathbb{F}|$ via Schwartz-Zippel.

### 5.2 Concrete Benchmarks (reported)

* KZG on BLS12-381: commit $2^{20}$ coeffs: 1.2s (rapidsnark), open 0.3s, verify 1.8ms pairings.
* IPA (Pallas): $n=2^{20}$, commit 0.9s MSM, open proof 0.6s, size 1,040 bytes for $\log n =20$.
* FRI (Goldilocks, blowup 2): degree $2^{20}$, 0.7s commit, proof 180KB @80 queries ~100 bits security, verifier 12ms.
* Nova (Pallas/Vesta, Pedersen+Grumpkin): step $F$ = Poseidon 2^18-gate, per-step prove 450ms (2 MSM), recursion verify 8ms, IVC proof for $\ell=10^4$ accumulated same 450ms/step amortized.

Compression via Spartan adds $\approx 12$s one-time to produce 9KB succinct proof.

**Security analysis:** Nova's original CycleFold paper noted soundness loss due to overlapping curve security $\approx 110$-bit for Pasta due to Kim-Barbulescu TNFS; secure version raises field size to 255-bit still >100-bit.

### 5.3 Succinctness Tradeoffs Theorem

> **Theorem 5.1 (Lower bounds).** Any transparent PCS with linear-time prover requires $\Omega(\log n)$ opening size unless DLOG broken; any pairing-free IVC requires $\Omega(1)$ group ops in verifier circuit.

*Proof sketch:* Via compression arguments [2][3]. IPA meets $\Theta(\log n)$ lower bound, Nova meets $\Theta(1)$ verifier.

## 6 Limitations & Future Work

* **Trusted setup spectrum:** KZG gives best verifier, but universal updatable SRS still requires ceremony. Fully transparent lattice-based PCSs (Lattice13, Brakedown) produce huge proofs but quantum-safe compact. Hybrid: use FRI for ceremony, then KZG.
* **Nova zero-knowledge:** Vanilla Nova not ZK because folding reveals $W$. Folklore randomization via masking $W + s\cdot r$ requires $r$ hidden; HyperNova shows how to embed blinding at modest cost. Fully efficient ZK folding remains open.
* **Non-uniform IVC (SuperNova [3]):** Real VMs have varied instructions $|F_i|$ wildly different. Naïve IVC forces padding to max $|F_i|$ wasteful. SuperNova introduces selector $\phi$ and pools of instances, folding only same-type, enabling a la carte cost $\approx |F_{active}|$. Proof size grows with #programs but not steps.
* **Cycle curves limited:** Only handful of secure cycles known: Pallas/Vesta, Vesta/Pallas twisted, BN254/Grumpkin secq/secg. Their 255-bit field not FFT-friendly beyond $2^{32}$; for large $2^{24}+$ domains we need half NTT or new constructions like Olive/2-chain with high 2-adicity search ongoing.
* **Post-quantum folding:** Current folding schemes rely on DLOG homomorphism. Lattice analogues of relaxed R1CS folding needing SIS linear homomorphic commitment but cross term quadratic degree must be compressed; lattice folding of lattice constraints (CircuitFold?) remains nascent. FRI + Folding mashup (Protostar, ProtoStar) showing custom gates CCS: next general folding for Plonkish.

Future work:

1. **Protogalaxy / ProtoStar accumulation** for degree-3 customizable constraint systems (CCS) generalizing R1CS/Plonk/AIR with single MSM.
2. **Proof of Proof Carrying Data from Nova** to arbitrary DAG without linearity constraints, merging selective verification [6] for lightweight clients.
3. Hardware acceleration: MSM Pippenger on GPU/FPGA for Nova per-step dominance; improving 2x-5x yields real-time zkVM at 1000 steps/sec.
4. Formal verified implementation of CycleFold in EasyCrypt to eliminate soundness bug (patched 2023) class.

## 7 Conclusion

We synthesized the landscape of succinct polynomial commitments — KZG [1] for optimal size, IPA [2] for transparent logarithm, FRI [4] for hash-based post-quantum — and shown how **homomorphic commitments enable folding**. Nova-style IVC [3] replaces expensive SNARK recursion with folding of relaxed R1CS, achieving *constant recursion overhead* dominated by two scalar multiplications, instantiated over 2-cycle curves Pallas/Vesta [7] to side-step field mismatch. Benchmarks reaffirm theoretical predictions: per-step prover $O(|F|)$ MSM, verifier circuit <20k constraints, proof sizes from 48B to 200KB depending on backend.

The same folding philosophy underlies recent ProtoStar, HyperNova, and CycleFold extensions that make instruction-level a la carte IVC viable for zkEVMs and rollups without universal circuits. Choosing PCS remains a trilemma of **size vs. setup vs. quantumness** — no single scheme dominates. Nova’s modularity allows plugging Pedersen-IPA, HyperKZG, or Dory backends post-hoc, making it a compelling substrate for incremental verifiable computation.

For practitioners: use KZG + universal SRS if verifier gas cost dominates (L1), IPA cycle if transparent recursion without ceremony dominates (P2P), FRI if PQ or hash-only compliance dominates (STARKs). All three fold.
