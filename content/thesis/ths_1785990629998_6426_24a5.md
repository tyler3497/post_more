## 6 Limitations

1. **Honest-majority / scheduler:** Byzantine $n\ge3f+1$ assumed [1]; rational deviations degrade liveness.
2. **Model coverage:** TLA+ $N=4$; $N=4096$ needs parameterized induction [3].

7. **Numeric stability:** CKKS $2^{-40}$ per mul loss needs TFHE PBS for exact compare [3][5]; FHE parameter scaling $logQ \approx 438$ for depth 10.
