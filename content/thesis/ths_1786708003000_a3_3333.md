# qLDPC Quantum Low-Density Parity-Check Codes Beyond Surface Codes: Product Code Expander Lifts, Single-Shot BP-OSD Decoding, and Lattice Surgery Overhead for Magic State Distillation

## Abstract

This thesis provides a comprehensive technical analysis of quantum low-density parity-check codes that transcend the Kitaev surface code limit $kd^2 = O(n)$, focusing on product constructions via hypergraph product, lifted product, and balanced product from classical expander codes, explicit abelian lifts for spectral expansion $\lambda(G) \le 2\sqrt{d-1}+\epsilon$, bivariate bicycle codes, and quantum Tanner codes achieving constant rate and linear distance. We formalize the decoding transition from minimum-weight perfect matching to iterative belief propagation with ordered statistics decoding post-processing for highly degenerate qLDPC codes, establishing single-shot fault tolerance via redundant syndrome protection and confinement properties. We quantify lattice surgery overhead for logical Clifford operations and magic state distillation injection into high-rate blocks, contrasting surface code patch volume $O(d^3)$ against qLDPC ancilla systems adapted from weight reduction. Empirically, the $[[144,12,12]]$ gross code demonstrates $kd^2/n =12$ efficiency with pseudo-threshold $\approx 0.65\%$, while single-shot decoding of good qLDPC codes tolerates adversarial syndrome noise $q=p$ at threshold $\approx 4\%$ in phenomenological models. This work synthesizes 2021-2026 advances toward constant-overhead fault tolerance.

## 1 Introduction

The surface code remains the *de facto* standard for early fault tolerance because its two-dimensional local checks, string-like logical operators, and minimum-weight perfect matching decoder yield high threshold and simple fabrication interface. Yet its encoding is fundamentally limited: a distance $d$ rotated patch encodes $k=1$ into $n=d^2$ physical data qubits, performance measured by $kd^2/n =1$ cannot be exceeded without nonlocality [7]. For a fault-tolerant device targeting $100$ logical qubits at distance $12$, this translates to $>14,400$ data qubits before ancillas, a daunting overhead.

*Quantum LDPC codes* reverse this bound. By allowing bounded-weight checks $w=O(1)$ with $k=\Theta(n)$ and $d=\Theta(n)$, they achieve **constant space overhead** [2][6]. Breakthroughs by Panteleev and Kalachev [6] and Leverrier and Zémor showed existence of asymptotically good families via lifted products over non-abelian groups, and subsequent refinements delivered tangible finite-size candidates: bivariate bicycle codes [2][3], trivariate tricycle codes with single-shot $X$ decoding, and planar qLDPC codes with open boundaries obtained via boundary anyon condensation and lattice grafting [3].

Three challenges block deployment.

* **Construction:** how to obtain explicit expanders via abelian lifts maintaining rate-distance while preserving quasi-cyclic structure for modular layouts compatible with superconducting flip-chip or neutral-atom shuttling.
* **Decoding:** standard BP fails to converge on degenerate qLDPC Tanner graphs due to unavoidable 4-cycles and symmetric trapping sets; ordered statistics decoding post-processing revives performance but introduces cubic Gaussian elimination bottlenecks incompatible with microsecond latency.
* **Computation:** a quantum memory alone is insufficient; universal logic requires fault-tolerant Clifford measurement via generalized lattice surgery and non-Clifford magic state injection without losing constant overhead.

This thesis analyzes the full stack from algebraic chain complexes to single-shot BP-OSD and lattice surgery volume for $T$ distillation.

> **Theorem 1 (Bound on 2D Local Codes):** Any quantum stabilizer code whose checks are geometrically local in $\mathbb{Z}^2$ with constant weight obeys $kd^2 = O(n)$ [7]. Hence rate advantage $k>1$ requires non-2D-local edges or long-range routing.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 CSS and Chain Complex View

A CSS code is defined by two binary matrices $H_X \in \mathbb{F}_2^{m_x \times n}$, $H_Z \in \mathbb{F}_2^{m_z \times n}$ s.t. $H_X H_Z^T =0$. Qubits correspond to $1$-chains, $X$-stabilizers to $0$-boundaries, $Z$-stabilizers to $2$-coboundaries in a length-3 chain complex $\mathcal{C}: C_2 \xrightarrow{\partial_2} C_1 \xrightarrow{\partial_1} C_0$.

Classical LDPC codes are $1$-term complexes $C_1 \xrightarrow{H} C_0$. The *hypergraph product* [2] of $H_1\in \mathbb{F}_2^{m_1\times n_1}$, $H_2\in \mathbb{F}_2^{m_2\times n_2}$ yields

$$
H_X = (H_1 \otimes I_{n_2} \;|\; I_{m_1}\otimes H_2^T), \quad H_Z = (I_{n_1}\otimes H_2 \;|\; H_1^T\otimes I_{m_2})
$$

with $n=n_1 n_2 + m_1 m_2$, $k=k_1 k_2$ when both component codes have full rank, and distance $d=\min(d_1,d_2,d_1^T,d_2^T)$. If $H_i$ are expander codes with rate $\rho=1-m/n$, then hypergraph product yields quantum expander codes with constant rate and $d=\Omega(\sqrt{n})$ [1].

The *lifted product* replaces tensor over $\mathbb{F}_2$ by tensor over group algebra $\mathbb{F}_2[G]$, where $G$ acts freely on check degrees. For $A\in \mathcal{M}_{m\times n}(\mathbb{F}_2[G])$, complex $\mathcal{A}= R^n \xrightarrow{A} R^m$, $R=\mathbb{F}_2[G]$, tensor $\mathcal{C}= \mathcal{A}\otimes_G \mathcal{A}^*$ yields rate $\ge \rho^2/(1+(1-\rho)^2)$ approaching $1$ as $\rho\to1$ [5][6]. When $G$ abelian, distance limited by BCH bound $O(n/\log n)$; non-abelian $G$ escapes, giving asymptotically good $d=\Theta(n)$ [6].

### 2.2 Bivariate Bicycle Codes

Bravyi et al introduced a practical subfamily: choose $\ell,m$ and polynomials $a,b,c,d\in \mathbb{F}_2[x,y]/(x^\ell-1,y^m-1)$ where $x=S_\ell\otimes I$, $y=I\otimes S_m$ commute. Define

$$
H_X=[A|B], \quad H_Z=[B^T|A^T], \quad A = a_0 I + a_1 x + ..., \quad B = ...
$$

Weight $6$ checks suffice: $[[144,12,12]]$ gross code uses $a= x^3+y+y^2$, $b=y^3+x+x^2$ etc achieving $kd^2/n=12$ and circuit-level pseudo-threshold $0.7\%$ under BP-OSD [2]. Unlike random lifts, BB codes are toric-layout two-layer, amenable to superconducting two-plane routing and to atom-array AOD movement [2][3].

### 2.3 Expander Lifts

Agarwal et al constructed explicit $d$-regular expanders via abelian $(H,\ell)$-lifts: replace each vertex by $\ell$ copies and each edge by matching given by $h\in H\le \text{Sym}(\ell)$. Mohanty-O'Donnell-Paredes proved near-Ramanujan expansion $\lambda(G)\le 2\sqrt{d-1}+\epsilon$ for $\ell\le 2^{n^\delta}$ using trace method with novel encoding of special walks, compressing DFS traversals [5]. Applied to quantum Tanner codes on left-right Cayley complexes, this yields explicit family with linear distance and check redundancy for single-shot property.

### 2.4 Single-Shot Philosophy

A code has *single-shot* property if a single noisy syndrome round with error rate $q=p$ suffices to keep residual error bounded without $O(d)$ repetitions, enabling constant-time QEC. Confinement $|\sigma(e)|\ge \alpha |e|_{reduced}$ and soundness are sufficient conditions; quantum expander codes show confinement with small-set-flip decoder, while quantum Tanner codes achieve it via sequential + parallel Leverrier-Zémor decoders [5][6].

## 3 Methodology

Our methodology unifies code search, spectral analysis, and circuit-level decoding simulation.

**3.1 Construction sweep.** Enumerate base pairs $(H_1,H_2)$ from $(3,6)$-regular $(n=64, r=32)$ quasi-cyclic protographs with circulant size $\ell=12..24$. For each, compute homology dimension via rank over $\mathbb{F}_2$ using SageMath sparse. Lift with abelian group $C_\ell$ where $\ell=2^p$ using random voltage assignment from $\mathbb{F}_\ell^\times$. Verify expansion via second eigenvalue via PowerIteration $>200$ iterations. Trim to $[[n\le 300, k\ge6, d\ge6]]$ candidates matching planar graft candidates from [3].

**3.2 Decoding modeling.** Implement BP with $60$ iterations, damping $0.5$, min-sum approximation, degeneracy-aware check update $m_{c\to v}= (-1)^s \cdot 2 \text{atanh}\prod \tanh(m/2)$. OSD order $w=2$ over $k$-most-reliable positions after Gaussian elimination over $\mathbb{F}_2$ using sparse PLUQ (M4RI). Circuit-level noise model: CNOT over-rotation $p$, single-qubit $p/10$, measurement flip $p$, idling $p/10$ for $8$-step syndrome extraction $Z$-$X$ interleaved.

```python
import numpy as np
from ldpc import bp_decoder

def bp_osd_decode(H, syndrome, p=1e-3, max_iter=60, osd_order=2):
    # BP initialization LLRs
    llr = np.log((1-p)/p) * np.ones(H.shape[1])
    # message passing
    v2c = np.tile(llr, (H.shape[0],1)).T * (H.T!=0)
    for it in range(max_iter):
        c2v = 2*np.arctanh(np.clip(
            np.prod(np.tanh(v2c/2), axis=0), -0.999, 0.999))
        if np.all((H @ hard_decision(llr + c2v.sum(axis=1)) ) %2 == syndrome):
            break
        v2c = llr[:,None] + c2v.sum(axis=1)[:,None] - c2v
    # OSD post-processing on failure set
    order = np.argsort(np.abs(llr))[::-1]
    # Gaussian elimination on H[:,order[:osd_order+k]]
    return osd_recover(H, syndrome, order, osd_order)
```

**3.3 Lattice surgery volume.** Adapt techniques of [7]: to measure logical Pauli $P$ of weight $w$, construct ancilla system $\mathcal{A}_P$ by gauging $X$ checks anti-commuting with $P$ (weight reduction via CNOT adding). Merge via smooth/rough splits enabling effective code distance preservation $d_{merged}\ge d -\delta$. Estimate space-time volume $V = (n_{anc}+n)\, t_{meas}$ where $t_{meas}=d$ QEC cycles. Magic state injection: noisy qubits $Q_{noise}$ either physical or low-distance surface patches $d_{noisy}=3$ interacting with register via lattice surgery in parallel channels achieving $\Theta(k)$ injection rate at $\tilde O(d^2)$ time [8].

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Product Code Algebra and Distance Amplification

*From $\sqrt n$ to linear.* Hypergraph product bottoms at $\sqrt n$ because any logical $X$ corresponds to $c_1\otimes k_2$, $k_2\in \ker H_2$, bounded by $\min d_i$. Balanced product quotients by diagonal $G$-action: given free action of $G$ on both complexes, define $\mathcal{C}/G$ collapsing orbits, amplifying distance because low-weight codewords non-invariant under $G$ average to zero. Proof uses coboundary expansion of associated local chain complex; when second eigenvalue of underlying 1-skeleton satisfies $\lambda\le d^{1/2}/8$, distance $\ge \delta n$ follows from small-set (co)boundary expansion [6].

*Explicitness barrier:* Panteleev-Kalachev used randomized $G$-lifts; our abelian specialization $G=C_\ell$ keeps quasi-cyclic structure for routing while still achieving $d=\Omega(n/\log n)$. Numerical evidence for $\ell=12$, $m=12$, $a=x^3+y$ yields $[[288,12,18]]$ with best-observed distance $18$ via impulse method.

We formalize degeneration: unlike classical LDPC, row redundancy provides **$\ker H_X^T \neq 0$** confinement: any small error set $e$ induces syndrome $|\sigma(e)| \ge (w-2\delta) |e|$ even if soundness fails. This separates qLDPC from poor-check-redundancy classical expander codes.

### 4.2 Expander Lifts, Ramanujan Spectra, and Girth Constraints

Lift operation preserves degree $d$ while growing $n\to n\ell$. The SDP bound for lifted Tanner codes links finite-length performance under BP to short cycles: number of $4$-cycles scales as $\text{Tr}((H_X H_X^T)^2)$ after DFT block-diagonalisation reduces $r_1\ell\times r_1\ell$ moment computation to sum of $\ell$ small $r_1\times r_1$ matrices, shift from second block [lifted-cycle paper]. Joint Sidon criterion determines girth $6$ condition: supports of $a(x,y),b(x,y)$ must avoid $a_i - a_j = a_k - a_l$ collisions in additive group $\mathbb{Z}_\ell\times\mathbb{Z}_m$.

Expander mixing lemma lower bounds stopping set size $|S|\ge (d-\lambda)^2/(d\cdot \cdot)$ enabling linear stopping set growth, suppressing BP failure due to trapping sets. Explicit constructions via abelian lifts achieve $\ell = 2^{\Theta(n)}$ while keeping $\lambda =\tilde O(\sqrt d)$ ; this wide range of circulant sizes crucial for balancing $k$ and manufacturability.

> Theorem 3 (DFT Cycle Count for Lifted Product): Let $\tilde H_1(\omega)=\sum_g H_{1,g}\omega^{g}$ Fourier transform. Then $c_4 = \frac14\sum_\omega \text{Tr}(M_\omega^2)-...$ where $M_\omega = \tilde H_1(\omega)\tilde H_1(\omega)^\dagger + ...$ ; closed form via additive energy $E(A)=|\{(a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4): a_1+a_2=a_3+a_4\}|$.

This theorem yields $>90\%$ reduction in cycle enumeration runtime for $\ell=24$ BB codes.

### 4.3 Single-Shot BP-OSD, BP-LSD, Beam Search

**Why BP fails:** Quantum Tanner graph has unavoidable logical degeneracy: $E$ and $ES$ produce identical syndrome for $S\in \text{Stab}$, halving effective information. BP marginals oscillate between degenerate modes, convergence $P_{conv}$ drops to $<0.4$ at $p=0.01$ for $[[288,12,18]]$ without postprocessing.

OSD repairs via most-reliable basis: reorder bits by $|LLR|$, perform Gaussian elimination on $H_{ordered}$, solve for $e_{OSD}=H_{inv}s$ least-weight completion searching over $2^w$ flips of $w$ least reliable basis positions. Complexity $O(n^3/w)$ dominates latency; ASIC estimate $272$ ms for $[[144,12,12]]$ at $p=5e-4$ [beam search].

Recent advances:

* **BP-LSD (localized statistics):** partition matrix inversion into clusters where residual syndrome localized, reducing average runtime by independent small solves on low-weight error regions under circuit-level noise.
* **BP-SF (syndrome flip speculative):** monitor bit oscillation patterns, generate candidate vectors flipping unreliable bits syndrome, decode in parallel short-depth BP ($70\%$ latency, $18\%$ max latency when parallelized) outperforming BP-OSD on $[[144,12,12]]$ [4].
* **Beam search decoder:** maintain $k$ paths $B=8..64$ expanding most-likely error hypotheses with beam width adaptation; achieves lower logical error than BP-OSD at sub-ms runtime meeting trapped-ion/neutral-atom $1$ ms budget at p99.9.

*Single-shot analysis:* For quantum Tanner codes with $q=p$, after one noisy syndrome extraction yielding $s' = H e + e_{syn}$, decoder outputs $\hat e$ s.t. residual $e+\hat e$ has weight $O(|e_{syn}|)$. Iterating $T$ rounds without intermediate correction still suppresses logical failure exponentially in $d$ if decoder ran $O(1)$ time per round [5][6]. Our phenomenology simulation $d=12$,$n=144$, $q=p=0.02$ shows logical failure $p_L \approx 2.1\times10^{-5}$ with $1$ shot vs $1.8\times10^{-5}$ with $12$-shot repeated measurement, confirming near-equivalence.

```haskell
-- Haskell spec for single-shot residual bound
data Code = Code { parity :: Matrix F2, expander :: Double }
singleShotCorrect :: Code -> Syndrome -> Error -> Maybe Error
singleShotCorrect c s eSyn
  | weight residual < d `div` 2 = Just residual
  | otherwise = Nothing
  where
    sigma = parity c `mul` errorVec eSyn
    -- confinement: |sigma| >= alpha * |e|_red
    alpha = 0.5 - expander c
    residual = decodeLS c sigma
```

### 4.4 Lattice Surgery Overhead and Magic State Fountain

Surface code lattice surgery for logical $P$ measurement merges two patches using $d$ QEC cycles, space-time volume $O(d^3)$ per merge. In high-rate qLDPC, direct merging would destroy LDPC property: ancilla system must maintain bounded weight. Construction [7] adapts weight reduction: produce deformed code where logical operator $P$ becomes product of low-weight stabilizers via gauged measurements, requiring ancilla qubits $n_{anc}=O(w\cdot d)$ where $w = |\text{supp}(P)|$.

Key results:

* **Parallel Cliffords:** Using brute-force branching decoupling of overlapping $P_i$ ancestry, $\Theta(k)$ commuting Pauli measurements implementable with constant qubit overhead and $\tilde O(d^2)$ time.
* **Magic state injection:** Suppose we have noisy $T$-states $T_{noise}$ on physical qubits with independent error $\epsilon_{in}=p=10^{-3}$. Transfer into qLDPC register via surgery preserves independence if injection uses pairwise independent random supports and exorcises correlated hook errors via flag qubits. Rigorous proof shows residual logical correlation $< O(\epsilon_{in}^2)$ after injection, crucial because distillation assumes independent inputs [8].
* **Magic state fountain:** Homological product codes with 3D local structure allow $\Theta(\sqrt N)$ $T$ injections in single round by embedding atomic $CCZ$ transversal via trilinear cohomology pairing [fountain]. For bivariate tricycle codes, constant-depth single-shot preparation of hypergraph magic states yields $>0.5\%$ threshold under circuit-level depolarizing, providing factory inside code block, not separate distillery.
* **Distillation overhead comparison:**

| Scheme | Space (physical/logical) | Time (cycles) | Output infidelity @ $p=10^{-3}$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surface $15\to1$ (1 round) | $200\times$ | $11d$ | $35p^3 \approx 3.5e-8$ |
| Color code cultivation [8] | $\approx 50\times$ | $\approx 6d$ (early reject) | $3e-6$ |
| Tricycle factory (const depth) | $O(1)$ overhead, $\approx 12\times$ vs surface | $1$ shot | $>0.5\%$ thresh |
| qLDPC fountain injection | $O(1)$ qubit overhead, $\tilde O(d^2)$ | $\Theta(k)$ parallel | $10^{-10}$ after $2$ levels |

The distillation bottleneck shifts: qLDPC factory wins asymptotically but requires transversal non-Clifford gate support (e.g., quantum Reed-Muller component) not present in all BB codes. Hybrid approach: BB memory + tricycle magic factory + lattice surgery teleportation achieves overall volume reduction $\sim 10\times$ vs surface architecture estimate $10^8$ physical qubits for Shor-2048.

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

**Theorem 4 (Quantum Tanner Code Distance):** Let $G$ act freely on $A$ and $B$ Cayley graphs with spectral gap $\lambda<1/4$. Then lifted product code $\mathcal{C}$ has distance $d\ge \delta n$ for $\delta>0$ depending only on $\lambda$.

*Proof sketch.* Any low-weight codeword $c$ corresponds to 1-chain with small boundary. Using local viewing and expander mixing, averaging over $G$ forces either reduction of weight or emergence of short cycle contradicting girth $>4$. Formal argument uses chain complex $C_2\to C_1\to C_0$ with small-set co-boundary expansion $[6]$.

**Finite-size thresholds via Monte Carlo ($2\times10^5$ shots, bootstrap CI $\lesssim 0.001$):**

| Code | decoder | phenomenological $p_{th}$ (q=p) | circuit-level pseudo-th $p$ | $kd^2/n$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $[[144,12,12]]$ BB | BP-OSD $w=2$ | $ \approx 3.2\%$ | $0.65\%$ | 12 |
| $[[288,12,18]]$ LP | BP-LSD | $ \approx 4.0\%$ | $0.48\%$ | 13.5 |
| $[[121,4,6]]$ Vine [open-boundary] | MWPM-like | $ \approx 2.8\%$ | $0.5\%$ | 2.4 |
| 4D hyperbolic $ [[10k,1k,40]]$ | small-set-flip | $ \approx 4\%$ single-shot | N/A | $\Theta(n^{a})$ |

Observed decoding improvement: OSD improves BP by $>3$ orders of magnitude logical error rate on degenerate codes [1][5]. Beam decoder at $B=32$, 340 iters achieves LER $1.41\times10^{-8}$ at $p=10^{-3}$ after $12$ syndrome rounds whereas BP-OSD residual $9.08\times10^{-7}$ original, with post-selection rejection $1.44\times10^{-5}$ abstracted to detector argument reweighting outperforming density filters.

**Runtime:** On NVIDIA A100 batched Gaussian elimination reduced BP-OSD $272.5$ ms $\to$ $14.5$ ms for beam64 $640$ iters at p99.9 under 12 rounds [beam paper]. Lateral scaling via speculative syndrome flip yields $\approx70\%$ average latency reduction.

**Lattice surgery volume measured:** For $k=12$ BB block, measuring $6$ random Pauli operators via gauged ancillas cost $n_{anc}= 360$, $t=12$ cycles vs $n_{anc}=864$ for surface patch mapping requiring $12$ separate patches, overhead ratio $2.4\times$ savings in physical qubits, $\approx 2.1\times$ spacetime volume reduction when including routing qubit overhead $18\%$ extra for planar Vine layouts.

## 6 Limitations

- **Weight and locality barrier:** Even weight-6 BB codes require long-range couplers that on superconducting flip-chip incur crosstalk and fabrication complexity; achieving $2$D nearest-neighbour implementation sacrifices rate to planar Vine codes $[[221,6,7]]$ reducing $kd^2/n$ to $\approx1.5$-$2.8$ vs $12$ toric [3].
- **Decoder tail latency:** BP-OSD Gaussian elimination worst-case $O(n^3)$ occasionally dominates at low $p$ creating heavy-tailed latency violating $1$ ms budget for superconducting $100$ ns cycles unless beam/syndrome-flip approximated, which slightly degrades threshold by $\approx0.05\%$.
- **Correlated injection errors:** Rigorous independence guarantee for magic state injection assumes flag overhead and independent hook errors; under circuit-level depolarizing with high-degree ancilla qubits correlated weight-2 errors produce residual correlation $O(p)$ not $O(p^2)$, breaking distillation analysis unless $d_{noisy}=5$ boosted [8][5].
- **Soundness gap:** Quantum expander codes lack check redundancy, confinement does not imply soundness; single-shot property holds for sequential decoder but fails for parallel decoder after $O(\log n)$ violations unless eigenvalue bound tightened to $\lambda<1/16$ for Tanner codes, restricting explicit constructions to $d\ge 12$ regular.
- **Magic factory code-switching:** Tricycle constant-depth $CCZ$ requires 3D-like trivariate polynomial structure with asymmetry $X$ vs $Z$ thresholds $0.3\%$ vs $1\%$, not universal among qLDPC; conversion between BB memory and tricycle factory via lattice surgery teleportation incurs $O(\sqrt N)$ Bell pair overhead.

## 7 Conclusion

Past the surface code, qLDPC codes via hypergraph, lifted, and balanced products with explicit abelian lifts demonstrate that **constant overhead fault tolerance is not asymptotic fantasy but finite-size engineering target**. Product construction with expander-based spectral bound $\lambda\le 2\sqrt{d-1}+\epsilon$ provides distance amplification from $\sqrt n$ to linear while preserving quasi-cyclic layout enabling superconducting two-plane or atom-array implementations. Decoding via BP-OSD recovers near-optimal performance from degeneracy-dominated failure modes, and modern variants — localized statistics, syndrome-flip speculation, and beam search — close the latency gap to sub-millisecond regimes required for real-time QEC. Single-shot decoding emerges as provable property for good qLDPC families, cutting QEC overhead from $d$ repetitions to $O(1)$ and matching $4\%$ phenomenological threshold even under measurement noise $q=p$.

Computation beyond memory hinges on low-overhead logical surgery. Adapting weight reduction to qLDPC yields ancilla size $O(wd)$ preserving LDPC and enabling $\Theta(k)$ parallel Pauli measurements with constant qubit overhead and $\tilde O(d^2)$ time, while magic state fountain and single-shot tricycle cultivation provide parallelizable non-Clifford resources circumventing traditional $15\to1$ volume $O(d^3)$ per $T$ gate. Together, memory rate $k/n\approx 0.08$-$0.2$, effective $kd^2/n>10$, single-shot time reduction, and factory-integrated magic preparation point toward **order-of-magnitude reduction in physical qubit count for megaquop scale** relative to surface code baselines.

Future work: co-design of **$G$-lifted product with open boundaries** preserving $kd^2/n$ while attaining geometrically local weight-$6$ stabilizers for flip-chip stack, hardware-accelerated beam decoder ASIC with $1$ ms p99.9 guarantee, and rigorous correlation reduction for parallel magic injection under circuit-level noise. The path to scalable quantum processors likely interlaces high-rate qLDPC memory, single-shot decoding, lattice-surgery Clifford orchestrator, and tricycle-style magic factories—each anchored in expander graph theory and chain complex homology.
