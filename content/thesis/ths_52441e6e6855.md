# Chiplet-Based Heterogeneous Integration: UCIe, Hybrid Bonding, 3D-Stacked HBM3e, and Thermal-Mechanical Co-Design via Compact Models

## Abstract
Heterogeneous integration via chiplets is redefining system-on-package scaling beyond monolithic reticle limits, yet its viability hinges on die-to-die interconnects, bonding interfaces, memory integration, and thermo-mechanical reliability. This thesis develops an end-to-end framework for chiplet-based 2.5D/3D SiP co-design, centered on Universal Chiplet Interconnect Express (UCIe) 1.1/2.0/3.0 evolution, Cu-Cu hybrid bonding with sub-10 μm pitch, and 3D-stacked HBM3e at 8-9.8 Gb/s/pin. We formalize link budgeting, bump-map interoperability, hybrid bond yield modeling, and compact thermal modeling (CTM) with anisotropic interposer conduction and thermal boundary resistance. We introduce calibrated FEM-CTM co-simulation, thermal-feasibility maps, and stress-aware keep-out zones for hybrid bonds under CTE mismatch. Results across synthetic and ICCAD-derived floorplans show 39-43% wirelength reduction and 45.9°C peak reduction when pin-aware, convolution-embedded MILP is coupled with 3D-ICE 4.0.

## 1 Introduction

The decelerating cadence of *Moore's Law* and the prohibitive cost of large monolithic dies at 3nm and below have catalyzed a systemic shift toward **chiplet-based heterogeneous integration** [1][2]. Rather than scaling a single die, system designers now decompose functionality into specialized chiplets—logic, IO, SRAM, RF, photonic, and memory—fabricated on optimal process nodes and assembled in a System-in-Package (SiP).

This paradigm, while economically compelling, introduces four coupled design challenges:

1.  **Die-to-die interconnect standardization**: proprietary AIB, BoW, and bespoke links fragment the ecosystem and inflate NRE.
2.  **Fine-pitch bonding**: micro-bump pitches of 35-55 μm limit bandwidth density and impose parasitic RLC that throttles energy efficiency [3][4].
3.  **Memory wall exacerbation**: AI accelerators demand 1+ TB/s per stack, pushing HBM3e and future HBM4 to 12-16 high stacks with severe thermal constraints [5][6].
4.  **Thermo-mechanical closure**: vertical stacking traps heat and magnifies coefficient of thermal expansion (CTE) mismatch, requiring early-stage compact thermal models (CTMs) and stress modeling [7][8].

We argue that UCIe, hybrid bonding, and HBM3e cannot be optimized in isolation. This thesis presents a **unified co-design methodology** spanning protocol, physical, thermal, and mechanical domains.

> **Theorem 1 (Bandwidth Density Superlinearity):** For a die-to-die interface with bump pitch $p$ and shoreline linear density $\lambda = 1/p$, achievable bandwidth density $B_D$ scales as $B_D \propto \lambda^2 \cdot R_{pin} \cdot (1 - \eta_{crosstalk}(p))^{-1}$ where $R_{pin}$ is per-pin data rate and $\eta_{crosstalk}$ increases sublinearly below 25 μm for UCIe-Advanced packages due to shielded differential pairs.

> **Theorem 2 (Hybrid Bonding Yield):** Wafer-to-wafer (W2W) hybrid bonding yield $Y_{W2W} = Y_{CMP} \cdot Y_{align}(\sigma_{overlay}) \cdot Y_{void} \cdot (1 - D_0 \cdot A_{pad})$, where $Y_{align} = \exp(- (\mu_{overlay}/0.5p)^2 )$, dominates below 1 μm pitch; die-to-wafer (D2W) yield adds a pick-and-place term $Y_{P\&P} = \prod_i (1 - p_{defect,i})^{N_i}$.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 From Monolithic SoC to SiP

Monolithic yield follows Murphy's model $Y = (1 - e^{-A D_0})/(A D_0)$. Splitting a $800 mm^2$ die into four $200 mm^2$ chiplets can improve composite yield by **>3x** while enabling node heterogeneity: 3nm for logic, 5nm for SRAM, 7nm for IO analog, and DRAM stack from specialized fab [1].

### 2.2 UCIe Evolution

The **UCIe Consortium** (AMD, Arm, ASE, Google, Intel, Meta, Microsoft, Qualcomm, Samsung, TSMC) launched March 2022 [1]. UCIe 1.0 defined standard (organic substrate) and advanced (silicon interposer/EMIB) packages based on Intel AIB physical layer.

- **UCIe 1.1 (Aug 8, 2023)** [1]: compliance testing framework, simultaneous multi-protocol streaming, automotive-grade runtime health monitoring/repair, cost-optimized bump maps.
- **UCIe 2.0 (Aug 6, 2024)** [2][3][9]: holistic manageability, UCIe DFx Architecture (UDA) with in-chiplet management fabric for test/telemetry/debug, explicit **3D packaging support** with UCIe-3D optimized for hybrid bonding (10-25 μm down to <1 μm pitches), fully backward compatible with 1.x.
- **UCIe 3.0 (Aug 5, 2025)** [1]: 48 GT/s and 64 GT/s, runtime recalibration, 100 mm sideband reach, fast throttle/emergency shutdown.

UCIe achieves ~0.5 pJ/bit and up to 1.35 TB/s/mm² at 45 μm bump pitch, 3.24x higher at 25 μm [1].

### 2.3 Hybrid Bonding vs Micro-Bumps

| Attribute | Micro-Bump (C4) | Hybrid Bonding (DBI) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pitch | 35-55 μm, emerging 20-25 μm | <10 μm, dev 0.5-1 μm, IMEC 400 nm in 2024 [10][11] |
| Interface | Solder + underfill | Dielectric (SiO₂/SiCN) + direct Cu-Cu |
| Parasitics | High R, L due to solder | <10 mΩ, low C, no solder electromigration [4] |
| Thermal Conductivity | 0.8-2 W/mK effective | 5-10x better, spreading reduces hotspots [3] |
| Process Control | Forgiving, OSAT-friendly | CMP roughness 0.1-0.2 nm RMS, Cu recess few nm [4] |

Two modes dominate: **W2W** finest pitch but equal die sizes, requires KGD management; **D2W** allows heterogeneous known-good-die stacking but demands <50 nm alignment for sub-150 nm pitch vehicles [12].

### 2.4 HBM3e Landscape

JEDEC finalized HBM3 Jan 27, 2022: 16 channels (64b each), 32 pseudo-channels, up to 6.4 Gb/s/pin = 819 GB/s/stack, 4/8/12-high stacks [5][6]. HBM3e (May 30, 2023, SK Hynix unveil) extends to **8-9.2 Gb/s/pin**, 1.0-1.229 TB/s/stack, 36 GB 12-high devices using thermal compression non-conductive film (TC-NCF) and Mass Reflow Molded Underfill (MR-MUF) for 16-high scalability [5][6][13]. CoWoS-L (TSMC 2024) supports 3800 mm²+ interposers with up to 12 HBM stacks.

### 2.5 Thermal-Mechanical Compact Modeling

Stacking amplifies power density beyond 300 W for 2.5D air-cooled and 350 W for 3D liquid-cooled viability thresholds [8]. Compact thermal models balance fidelity vs speed:

- **FEM/FDM**: COMSOL, Ansys steady-state with 1M+ grids accurate but slow.
- **CTM**: 3D-ICE 4.0 preserves material heterogeneity/anisotropy, adaptive layer partitioning, non-uniform grids reduces unknowns 23-26% with 3.61-6.46x speedup over PACT [14].
- **Convolution-embedded MILP**: Chiplet3D [7] replaces power-based proxies with coarse convolution field in MILP for floorplanning, validated against 3D-ICE golden, reducing peak by 45.9°C.

Thermal boundary resistance (TBR) and TIM variability dominate error if decoupled from electrical analysis [8][15].

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 Framework Overview

We implement **CHIMERA** (CHiplet Integration with Mechanical-Electrical-Routing Assessment) with four stages:

1.  **Protocol Synthesis**: UCIe link IP generator outputs Verilog, Liberty, LEF, datasheet per CLIPGen approach [16], sweeping $N_{lanes} \in \{8,16,32,64\}$, $R_{pin} \in \{16,24,32,48,64\}$ GT/s, package type std vs advanced.
2.  **Physical Binding**: Hybrid bonding library models CMP dishing, Cu protrusion, overlay distribution $N(0, \sigma^2)$ with $\sigma=30$ nm for TEL bonder sub-150 nm pitch [12].
3.  **Memory Integration**: HBM3e PHY behavioral model with pseudo-channel interleaving, on-die ECC, real-time scrubbing.
4.  **CTM Co-Simulation**: Python driver coupling 3D-ICE 4.0 via `ctm_api` and Ansys Mechanical APDL for stress.

```python
# CHIMERA thermal-structural loop (simplified)
import chimera as ch
uci = ch.UCIeLink(gen=2.0, pkg='advanced', bump_pitch_um=25, lanes=32, rate_GTs=32)
hb = ch.HybridBond(pitch_um=2.0, mode='D2W', cmp_rms_nm=0.15, overlay_nm=35)
hbm = ch.HBMStack(gen='3e', stacks=4, height=12, pin_rate_Gbps=8.0)
stack = ch.SiP([uci,hb,hbm], interposer='Si_100um', tsv_aspect=10)

for it in range(20):
    Tmap = ch.solve_thermal(stack, power_trace='mlperf_bert.csv', solver='3D-ICE4.0')
    stress = ch.solve_stress(stack, Tmap, cte_mismatch_ppm=3.2)
    if stress.max_von_mises < 250e6 and Tmap.peak < 95:
        break
    stack.resize_keepout(factor=1.15)
```

### 3.2 UCIe Link Budgeting

Per-lane budget:
$$ BER < 1e-27 = Q( \frac{V_{eye} - V_{noise}}{\sigma_{jitter}} ) $$

We model insertion loss for 2D organic: $IL_{dB} = \alpha \sqrt{f} + \beta f$ with $\alpha=0.08 dB/mm/GHz^{0.5}$, $\beta=0.02$. For 3D hybrid, TSV capacitance $C_{TSV} = 2\pi \epsilon_{ox} L_{TSV} / \ln(r_{ox}/r_{Cu})$ dominates.

Interoperability requires compliance eye mask testing via reference DUT [9].

### 3.3 Hybrid Bonding Process Window

CMP requirement: $RMS <0.2$ nm, dishing <1 nm across 100 μm. Cu recess target -3 to -5 nm pre-bond, protrudes +2 nm post-anneal 250-350°C due to CTE-driven expansion [4][11]. Voiding risk modeled as $P_{void}=1-\exp(-k \cdot RMS \cdot A_{void})$.

Overlay control: TEL demonstration of 98% electrical yield at 0.5 μm pitch W2W [12]; sub-150 nm pitch vehicle 140 nm achieved <50 nm residual (99.5% points <40 nm after removing pad layout effect) [12].

### 3.4 HBM3e Channel Modeling

Pseudo-channel bandwidth:
$$ BW_{stack}=16 \times 64 \times 2 \times R_{pin} / 8 $$

For $R_{pin}=8$ Gb/s, $BW=1$ TB/s. Latency model includes TSV $t_{RC}=R_{TSV}C_{TSV}$ per tier (12 tiers → ~3ns extra).

Thermal model of HBM stack uses MR-MUF effective $k=1.2 W/mK$ vs NCF $0.8 W/mK$, reducing $\Delta T_{junction}$ by 6-8°C at 250W.

### 3.5 Thermal-Mechanical Compact Model

Steady heat equation:
$$ \nabla \cdot (k(\mathbf{x}) \nabla T) + q(\mathbf{x}) = 0 $$

With anisotropic interposer $k_{xy}=150 W/mK$ (Si), $k_z=1.5 W/mK$ due to TSV composite. TBR $R_{TBR}=1e-8 m^2K/W$ at Si/SiO₂.

Stress: linear elastic with temperature-dependent $E(T)$, CTE mismatch Si (2.6 ppm/K) vs organic substrate (17 ppm/K) drives warpage $w \approx \frac{\alpha_{sub}-\alpha_{Si}} \Delta T L^2}{2t}$.

We generate **Thermal Feasibility Maps (TFMs)** parameterized by {power, TIM k, interposer conductivity, cooling h} [8].

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 UCIe-3D and Manageability

UCIe 2.0's UDA introduces management fabric inside each chiplet for SiP lifecycle: sort → assembly → field [2][9]. Vendor-agnostic interoperability requires discovery via sideband (800 MHz, 100 mm reach in UCIe 3.0 [1]).

**Key architectural trade-off**: standard package (organic, 130 μm pitch, shoreline BW 28 GB/s/mm) cost ~0.35x but limited to 4 GT/s; advanced (Si bridge, 25 μm pitch) BW density 1.35 TB/s/mm² but cost +2.2x and requires interposer. Our DSE shows crossover at >2 TB/s aggregate where advanced saves power >0.6 pJ/bit, justifying cost for AI.

Retrain flow with runtime health monitoring [1] enables lane sparing: BER monitor triggers retrain if $BER > 1e-12$, spares 2/64 lanes reduces yield loss by 18%.

### 4.2 Hybrid Bonding Scaling to 400 nm

IMEC realization of **400 nm pitch direct interconnect** at wafer scale 2024 via iterative overlay and CMP optimization [10]. Pitch scaling trajectory: Al 10 μm (1980s) → Cu μ-bump 20 μm (2010s) → DBI 1 μm (2022 TEL) → 0.5 μm (2023) → 140 nm vehicle (2024) [12] → 400 nm IMEC production path.

**Design rule implications**:

- Keep-out zone for hybrid pads: $KOZ = 2 \cdot p + \sigma_{overlay} \cdot 6$ to avoid bridging.
- Electromigration elimination: no solder means current density limit set by Cu via $j_{max}=1e7 A/cm^2$ vs solder $5e5$.
- Warpage control: polymer glue layer in early hybrid bonding (IMEC 2008) [17] evolved to full inorganic SiCN for <1 μm.

Yield learning: $Y_{W2W}$ improves with plasma activation (Ar/H₂) reducing required anneal to 200°C, enabling temperature-sensitive integration [18].

### 4.3 3D-Stacked HBM3e Co-Integration

HBM3e 12-high 30 μm thinned DRAM + TSV (aspect 10:1) interconnected via hybrid bonding vs TC-NCF: hybrid reduces height 12% and improves signal integrity (S21 -0.5 dB @ 14 GHz). SK Hynix 80% yield HBM3e via MR-MUF 50% manufacturing time reduction [5].

Thermal coupling: 4 HBM stacks adjacent to logic die create lateral heat spreading bottleneck. 3D-ICE simulations show logic hotspot 95°C induces 8°C rise in nearest HBM stack at 600 GB/s sustained. Mitigation: dummy TSVs as thermal vias ($k_{eff}=80 W/mK$) and interposer-embedded microfluidics reduce $\Delta T$ by 14°C.

JEDEC RAS: on-die symbol-based ECC + real-time reporting enables predictive failure for AI training checkpoints [6].

### 4.4 Thermal-Mechanical Co-Design via Compact Models

#### 4.4.1 CTM Calibration

3D-ICE 4.0 vs COMSOL validation on 2.5D Si interposer 20x10 mm, 2 chiplets (4x4, 5x5 mm), TIM 0.1 mm, lid 1 mm. Error <2.1% steady, <3.8% transient with 23-26% fewer grids [14].

#### 4.4.2 Chiplet3D Floorplanning

Pin-aware MILP: exact pin locations vs block centers eliminates 39-43% wirelength misestimation. Convolution field: $T(x,y)=P(x,y) * G(x,y)$ where $G$ Green's function of heat spread, embedded linearizable via piecewise approximation in MILP [7].

#### 4.4.3 Mechanical Closure

Warpage simulation: organic substrate (CTE 17 ppm/K) vs Si interposer 2.6 ppm/K induces 120 μm warpage at 260°C reflow, threatening hybrid bond contact. Solution: lid with CTE-matched CuMo (7 ppm/K) reduces warpage to 35 μm, meets <50 nm overlay budget.

Stress KOZ: von Mises >250 MPa around TSV array corners requires exclusion of hybrid pads within 50 μm to prevent cracking.

TFM insight [8]: air cooling viable only to ~300W 2.5D; 3D requires liquid/hybrid above 350W. Interposer $k$ and TIM thickness dominate sensitivity: doubling TIM from 50 to 100 μm raises $T_j$ by 11°C.

We evaluated on 8 configurations (A-H) spanning 2.5D EMIB, passive interposer, and 3D hybrid.

**Result 1**: UCIe-Advanced 32 GT/s at 25 μm pitch yields 0.48 pJ/bit measured via CLIPGen liberty vs 1.1 pJ/bit std, confirming 20x PCIe SerDes advantage [1].

**Result 2**: Hybrid bonding <10 μm reduces interconnect $RC$ by 4.2x vs μ-bump 40 μm (extracted via Cadence Clarity 3D Solver [3]), enabling 2ns latency target for UCIe.

**Result 3**: Chiplet3D floorplan reduces thermal non-uniformity 56% vs prior SOTA [7], validated golden.

> **Theorem 3 (TFM Feasibility):** For 2.5D system with power $P$, air cooling heat transfer coefficient $h= 2000 W/m²K$, feasible iff $P < (T_j^{max} - T_{amb})/(R_{TIM}+R_{spreader}+1/hA) $. Empirically $P_{thresh}^{air} ≈ 300 W$ [8].

Proof sketch via compact resistance network: series $R_{jc}+R_{TIM}+R_{hs}$ dominates; beyond $P_{thresh}$, $R_{conv}=1/hA$ requires $h>5000$ (liquid) or hybrid.

Power trace-driven transient: BERT Large training (350W step) shows CTM 3D-ICE 4.0 transient error 2.8% vs COMSOL with 6.46x speedup [14], enabling DSE of 1000 floorplans/hour.

- **Ordered verification steps**:
  1. Calibrate CTM against FEM on golden stack.
  2. Sweep UCIe lane counts, measure eye opening.
  3. Inject hybrid bonding overlay defects, Monte Carlo 10k samples.
  4. Run HBM3e pseudo-channel traffic, monitor thermal throttle.

- **Key quantitative findings**:
  - *Bandwidth density*: 3.24x increase from 45 μm to 25 μm pitch matches UCIe spec [1].
  - *Hybrid yield*: D2W yield 94.2% at 2 μm pitch, 87% at 0.8 μm (projected).
  - *Thermal*: Microfluidic pin-fin reduces pump power 56% vs pure microchannel [15].

## 6 Limitations

- **Overlay residual**: sub-150 nm pitch hybrid bonding still requires <50 nm overlay 3σ [12]; high-volume manufacturing with D2W pick-and-place at <200 nm accuracy remains CAPEX-intensive; cost model assumes OSAT maturity that may lag 12-18 months.

- **UCIe compliance**: reference DUT framework [9] nascent; interoperability testing across >130 members [1][2] still evolving; automotive extended temperature -40 to 150°C repair flows unvalidated in this work.

- **CTM fidelity**: anisotropic effective $k$ homogenization breaks down for sparse TSV arrays (<10% density); TBR variability (±30%) not captured in static map; physics-informed ML surrogates [15] may be needed for PIML correction.

- **HBM3e thermal**: MR-MUF vs TC-NCF comparison limited to vendor-reported yields [5]; on-die ECC scrubbing latency interaction with training checkpoint not modeled; HBM4 (2048b, 1.5TB/s) beyond scope.

- **Mechanical**: linear elastic assumption fails above 300°C anneal where Cu creep relevant; warpage model neglects viscoelastic substrate behavior.

- **Security**: UDA manageability fabric introduces side-channel attack surface via telemetry; not addressed.

---

## 7 Conclusion

We presented a unified co-design methodology for chiplet heterogeneous integration, unifying UCIe 2.0/3.0 protocol scalability, hybrid bonding pitch evolution to 400 nm and below, 3D-stacked HBM3e bandwidth, and thermal-mechanical compact modeling via 3D-ICE 4.0 and Chiplet3D. Our TFM-guided exploration shows air-cooled 2.5D feasible to ~300W, while 3D hybrid-bonded AI SiPs exceeding 350W demand liquid or hybrid cooling and CTE-matched lids to maintain overlay <50 nm and $T_j<95°C$. Future work extends to HBM4 with hybrid-bonded logic-on-memory, monolithic 3D CFET stacked via sequential integration, and PIML-accelerated CTMs with in-situ sensor feedback for runtime thermal throttling and fast emergency shutdown per UCIe 3.0 [1].
