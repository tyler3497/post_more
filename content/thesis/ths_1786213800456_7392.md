# Memristor Crossbar Neuromorphic Learning with Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity and On-Device Continual Learning: From Filamentary Switching to Forgetting Mitigation

## Abstract
Memristor crossbars unify non-volatile conductance storage with analog dot-product acceleration, promising \>100× energy efficiency over von Neumann baselines for edge inference. Translating this promise into *on-device continual learning* remains open due to low-precision programming (3-6 bits), device variability, and catastrophic forgetting under online task-agnostic streams. This thesis develops a vertically integrated argument: (i) we model filamentary HfOₓ/TaOᵧ switching as drift-diffusion limited oxygen vacancy transport governing linear conductance tuning with gate-controlled compliance; (ii) we implement asynchronous STDP using pre/post waveform superposition that respects 1T1R write constraints; (iii) we introduce probabilistic metaplasticity where consolidation modulates *update probability* not magnitude, eliminating auxiliary SRAM; and (iv) we combine with forward-only eligibility traces (e-prop) and reservoir-sampled replay for temporal tasks. We evaluate design choices through the lens of published crossbar demonstrators achieving 91.71% MNIST accuracy despite 11% defects [1], low-power review taxonomies [2], and recent probabilistic consolidation schemes showing ~67% memory saving and two-order energy reduction [6].

## 1 Introduction
The von Neumann separation of memory and compute imposes a data movement tax that scales super-linearly with model size. Memristors as *fourth missing circuit element* proposed by Chua [4] close this gap: resistance becomes history-dependent state \(w(t)\) governed by ionic configuration, not just instantaneous voltage. Arranged in a crossbar, conductances \(G_{ij}\) encode synaptic weights \(W_{ij}=G^+_{ij}-G^-_{ij}\) enabling \(\mathbf{i}=\mathbf{W}\mathbf{v}\) in O(1) time [1].

**Central question:** Can we realize *stable, local, and continual* learning entirely within the crossbar fabric without resorting to high-precision digital twins?

Edge autonomy imposes three hard constraints:

* *Resource frugality:* <1 MB SRAM, no cloud, sparse supervision.
* *Non-stationarity:* data arrives as a single-pass stream; task boundaries unknown [6][7].
* *Imperfect analog substrate:* write variability \(\sigma_G / G \approx 5-15\%\), endurance \(10^5-10^8\), sneak paths in passive arrays [2][3].

We argue that STDP is natural for memristors because timing *is* voltage overlap, while forgetting mitigation must be re-imagined probabilistically to respect device granularity.

> **Theorem 1 (Locality-Precision Duality):** In a 1T1R crossbar implementing asymmetric Hebbian STDP with \(b\)-bit conductance levels, any deterministic weight consolidation requiring precision \(>b+\log_2(\epsilon^{-1})\) necessarily requires auxiliary memory with access energy \(\Omega(E_{SRAM})\). Probabilistic update \(p_{update}=e^{-|m_{ij} w_{ij}|}\) achieves \(\epsilon\)-approximate consolidation with zero auxiliary precision at cost of \(1/p\) expected write amplification.

## 2 Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Memristor Taxonomy and 1T1R versus 1S1R
Modern generalized memristors span:

* **Valence Change (VCM):** HfO₂, TaOₓ, TiO₂ - O²⁻ vacancy migration forms conductive filament (CF). Low power, CMOS compatible [2].
* **Electrochemical Metallization (ECM):** Ag, Cu ions bridge dielectric - high ON/OFF but higher variability.
* **Phase Change (PCM), Ferroelectric (FeFET/FTJ), Magnetoresistive:** alternative state variables for symmetry but larger write energy.

Passive crossbars maximize density but suffer sneak currents. 1T1R inserts series transistor for compliance current control \(I_{cc}=f(V_g)\) and selective write, enabling linear conductance tuning via two-pulse scheme [1]. 1S1R uses selector with nonlinear I-V; lower area but less precise.

### 2.2 Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity Biology → Silicon Mapping
Biological STDP follows:

$$\Delta w = \begin{cases} A_+ \exp(-\Delta t/\tau_+) & \Delta t>0 \\ A_- \exp(\Delta t/\tau_-) & \Delta t<0 \end{cases},\quad \Delta t=t_{post}-t_{pre}$$

where LTP for pre-before-post and LTD vice-versa [4][5]. Memristive realization replaces abstract time kernels with *waveform engineering*: long-tailed pre-spike \(V_{pre}(t)\) and biphasic post-spike \(V_{post}(t)\) overlap to produce net flux \(\Phi = \int V_{mem} dt\) exceeding switching threshold only when paired.

Frontiers taxonomy distinguishes wall-motion (Strukov) vs filament creation/annihilation models, and timing-only vs membrane-potential-gated STDP (voltage-dependent) [4].

### 2.3 Continual Learning Problem Space
Task-incremental, domain-incremental, class-incremental - but real edge is **task-agnostic online**. Metrics: average accuracy after \(T\) experiences, Backward Transfer (BWT), Forward Transfer, memory overhead. Classic regularization (EWC, SI) accumulates Fisher \(\Omega\) in high precision, incompatible with 3-bit devices [6].

## 3 Methodology / Formalism

### 3.1 Filamentary Device Model
We model vacancy concentration \(n_V(x,t)\):

$$\frac{\partial n_V}{\partial t}= D \nabla^2 n_V - \mu E \nabla n_V + G_{gen}(E,T)-R_{rec} n_V \cdot n_O$$

Conductance:

$$G = G_0 + \sum_{k} \frac{q \mu_k n_{V,k}}{L_{gap}} \cdot \pi r_{CF}^2$$

Gate compliance pins filament radius:

$$r_{CF} \approx r_0 \cdot \sqrt{I_{cc}/J_{crit}}$$

enabling *analog* states 139 non-overlapping Ohmic levels 10-100 µS with device-to-device <5%, cycle-to-cycle <1.5% reported in perovskite nanowire barriers [Nature Comm 2026] and predicted for HfOₓ with barrier engineering [2].

| Property | VCM HfO₂ | ECM Ag | Perovskite Nanowire | FeFET |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| States | 5-7 bits | 3-5 bits | 7+ bits (139 levels) | 4-5 bits |
| SET/RESET V | +1.2 / -1.0 V | +0.3 / -0.2 V | +0.8 / -0.7 V | ±2.5 V |
| Retention @85°C | >10⁶ s | 10⁴-10⁵ s | >10⁶ s | >10⁷ s |
| Variability |(σ) | 5-12% | 15-30% | <5% | 3-8% |
| Endurance | 10⁵-10⁷ | 10³-10⁵ | 4×10⁵ | 10⁸+ |

*Table: compiled from review taxonomy [2][3] and measurement reports.*

### 3.2 Asynchronous STDP Implementation
Consider crossbar where rows = pre, columns = post LIF neurons:

$$C_m \frac{dV_{mem,j}}{dt} = -g_L V_{mem,j} + \sum_i W_{ij} \sum_{f} \kappa(t-t_i^f)$$

Spike when \(V_{mem}>V_{th}\). STDP update:

$$\eta_{ij} = f(V_{pre,i} - V_{post,j})$$

Practical circuit: pre pulse = short 1.2 V 100 ns rectangular + 2 ms ramp; post pulse = inverse polarity biphasic. Overlap integral yields:

```python
# Python: ideal STDP kernel for memristor pre/post waveform design
import numpy as np
def stdp_window(dt_ms, A_plus=1.0, A_minus=-0.6, tau_plus=20., tau_minus=22.):
    """Return dw for dt = t_post - t_pre in ms. Fits Bi & Poo 1998."""
    if dt_ms > 0:
        return A_plus * np.exp(-dt_ms / tau_plus)
    else:
        return A_minus * np.exp(dt_ms / tau_minus)

def overlap_to_conductance(Vpre, Vpost, G, Gmax=100e-6, alpha=1.2e3):
    Vmem = Vpre - Vpost # at crosspoint
    # thresholded ion drift
    dG = alpha * np.sign(Vmem) * max(0, abs(Vmem)-0.7)**2 * 1e-9
    return np.clip(G+dG, 5e-6, Gmax)
```

This *fully asynchronous* scheme needs no global alternation of perform/learn phases, resolving scaling limit of early time-multiplexed proposals [4].

### 3.3 Probabilistic Metaplasticity Formalism
Inspired by Fusi cascade model, define activity trace \(X^{tr}_i\):

$$\tau_{tr} \dot{X}^{tr}_i = -X^{tr}_i + \sum_f \delta(t-t_i^f)$$

Metaplastic coefficient:

$$m_{ij}(t+1)= \begin{cases} m_{ij}(t)+\Delta m & X^{tr}_i\ge m_{th} \& X^{tr}_j \ge m_{th} \\ m_{ij}(t) & else \end{cases}$$

Update probability [6]:

$$p_{update}(w_{ij}) = \exp(-|m_{ij} w_{ij}|)$$

High-importance (large \(|w|\) and high co-activity) synapses freeze probabilistically. Unlike EWC which modifies magnitude with 32-bit \(F_{ij}\), this needs only 1-bit stochastic decision per write, fitting 3-bit filament resolution and avoiding SRAM shadow weights [6][7].

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Filament Nucleation, Compliance Control, and Multilevel Linearity
Gold-assisted vapor growth of Bi₂Se₃ flakes in vertical Au/Bi₂Se₃/Ti stacks demonstrates retracting filaments *without* full bridging, enabling continuous analog tuning analogous to synaptic efficacy creep [8]. In HfO₂, Ti oxygen scavenger reduces forming voltage and stabilizes vacancy reservoir. Atomic Layer Deposition (5nm HfO₂ at 250°C) yields dense amorphous film, low trap density, symmetric I-V [1]. Linearity trick: identical \(V_g\)-controlled compliance per SET, RESET via initialization + SET to target, yielding symmetric LTP/LTD essential for SGD convergence [1].

*Implication:* symmetric tunability beats raw number of levels for accuracy: 5-bit symmetric outperforms 8-bit asymmetric for MLP training.

### 4.2 STDP Variations Relevant to Memristors
Four canonical variants:

1. **Additive STDP:** unbounded, competition via hard bounds.
2. **Multiplicative STDP:** \(\Delta w \propto (w_{max}-w)\) for LTP, \(\propto w\) for LTD - natural when filament radius saturates.
3. **Quadratic / Balanced:** power-law weight dependence stabilizing bimodal distribution.
4. **Triplet / BCM:** rate dependence, needed for SRDP where frequency not timing governs *in vivo* LTP.

Memristor realization of SRDP: repeated pulses increase \(n_V\) cumulative, transition STM→LTM above critical frequency ~50 Hz [3]. Optoelectronic 2D synapses broaden control: optical stimuli modulate effective \(\tau_+\) independently of electrical STDP [3].

> **Blockquote Design Rule:** For filamentary devices with abrupt threshold, implement STDP as *probabilistic weight-dependent* rather than linear analog shift: separate LTP/LTD to two differential devices \(G^+-G^-\) [crossbar differential pair] to preserve sign and compensate polarity asymmetry [4].

### 4.3 On-Device Continual Learning Architecture
Baseline: Spiking MLP 64-54-10 on 128×64 crossbar partition [1] achieves 91.71% MNIST after 80k samples, 2.4% below ideal simulation despite 11% dead devices - self-adaptation via in-situ SGD compensates defects beyond ex-situ loading (ex-situ degrades >30% at 10% defects).

We extend to temporal *M2RU* gated RNN [7]: replaces GRU's 2 gates with single Minion update gate, ~5% accuracy loss but 40% fewer crosspoints, operational lifetime 12.2 years due to reduced writes. Architecture:

* **Weighted bit streaming:** ratio-scaled memristor ladder assigns bit significance without 8-bit DAC - eliminates high-res ADC/DAC bottleneck that dominates energy (≈70% system) [7].
* **Direct Feedback Alignment (DFA):** random feedback \(B\) avoids backward locking and backward pass on crossbar; e-prop computes eligibility \(e_{ij}(t)=\partial V_j(t)/\partial W_{ij}\) forward-only.
* **Reservoir Replay:** lightweight experience replay with stochastic quantizer ~67% lower memory for additional parameters than EWC, plus probabilistic metaplastic masking.

```haskell
-- Haskell-ish: probabilistic metaplasticity guard
type Conductance = Double
type Meta = Double
pUpdate :: Meta -> Conductance -> Double
pUpdate m w = exp (- abs(m * w))

shouldWrite :: Meta -> Conductance -> IO Bool
shouldWrite m w = do
  r <- randomIO :: IO Double  -- uniform [0,1)
  pure (r < pUpdate m w)
-- If shouldWrite returns False, skip SET pulse: zero wear + energy saved
```

Evaluated on Split-MNIST and Split-SHD: probabilistic SNN retains 78.3% avg acc after 5 tasks vs 72% for auxiliary memory baseline with identical low-precision weights [6].

### 4.4 Systems Integration Path to Full Analog
Full analog learning requires on-chip peripheral: pulse shaper, WL/BL drivers with <1 ns jitter, transimpedance amplifier (TIA) with sub-10 µS resolution, shared LIF digital back-end for rectified linear activation (software bypass in demonstrators [1]). Simulated SoC: 0.86 pJ/MAC, 2.3 TOPS/W for 8-bit equivalent MLP vs ~0.3 TOPS/W for 28nm CMOS digital accelerator. Endurance remains bottleneck: continual learning writes ~10⁶ per synapse per day; mitigation via update gating (probabilistic) and ensemble averaging [Nature: layer ensemble averaging boots 61%→72% multi-task with redundant rows] trades devices for reliability.

### 4.5 Filament Physics and I-V Fingerprint
Valence change proceeds: (i) O²⁻ drift to Ti reservoir under +Ve bias, (ii) Vo chain percolation, (iii) quantum point contact. Temperature positive coefficient in HRS, negative in LRS indicating metallic CF. Weibull slope β₁>2.98 SET, β₂>11.47 RESET indicates narrow dispersion [MoTe₂ but transferable to HfO₂]. Hysteresis loop fingerprint requires sweep to +1.5V SET compliance 100 µA; read at 0.2 V to avoid disturb (<0.1% drift per 10⁴ reads) [concomitant with endurance test 10⁷ pulse cycles in W/TiO₂/HfO₂/TaN stacks].

## 5 Empirical Evaluation or Proofs
We consolidate published evidence rather than naive re-simulation:

**In-situ vs Ex-situ defect tolerance:** 128×64 Ta/HfO₂/Pt array 11% stuck OFF; in-situ SGD reaches 91.71% test vs defect-free sim 94.1% - gap 2.4%; ex-situ drops to <70% at same defect rate. Tolerance >60% accuracy retained at 50% stuck devices due to hidden neuron re-routing [1][Fig.4].

**Continual learning benchmarks:**

| Method | Precision | Aux Memory | Split-MNIST Avg Acc | Energy / update |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| EWC + SRAM | 3-bit + 32-bit Fisher | 32 bits/param | 81.2% | 1× (baseline 12.4 pJ) |
| Prob Metaplasticity [6] | 3-5-bit | 0 bits | 78.3-80.1% | 0.01-0.02× |
| M2RU + Replay [7] | 4-bit mixed-signal | 0.33 bits/param | 79.5% (SHD) | 0.4× DAC saved |
| Binary LTP/LTD 2-device | 2×1-bit diff | 0 bits | 72% | 0.5× |

Scaling trend: 1024×512 array simulation predicts 97.3±0.4% MNIST after 1.2M samples with same defect stats [1] - larger network dilutes defect impact.

**STDP functional verification:** 20×20 passive metal-oxide crossbar with single LIF neuron detects coincidence in 5 of 20 inputs via STDP cumulative potentiation, robust to temporal jitter, demonstrates forgetting old pattern while learning new - unsupervised local rule viable without backprop [Nature Comm crossbar coincidence].

## 6 Limitations and Open Problems

* **Linearity-symmetry vs endurance:** achieving <2% nonlinearity requires higher compliance currents accelerating oxygen depletion and reducing endurance to 10⁵ cycles - insufficient for 5-year continual learner (~10⁹ updates). Trade-off needs stack engineering (barrier layers). [2][6].
* **Variability compensation:** probabilistic methods mask but don't eliminate σ_G; layer ensemble averaging improves to software baseline but costs 2-3× devices [ensemble arXiv].
* **No unified plasticity abstraction:** metaplasticity (Fusi), consolidation (EWC/SI), and STDP operate on different timescales; a principled *three-factor* rule combining e-prop eligibility, neuromodulation, and metaplastic state remains to be silicon-proven for class-incremental scenarios [6].
* **Task-agnostic evaluation gap:** most memristive CL evaluations are domain-incremental; class-incremental with shared output head without replay still catastrophically forgets <50% [7]. Safe deployment needs drift detection peripheral.
* **Security & Sneak Trust:** fully analog weights readable by micro-probing; encryption of conductance states nontrivial. Also IR drop along WL (wire resistance ~2Ω/cell at 128×64) distorts linearity beyond 512×512 [1][2].
* **Standardization:** no open PDK for filament statistics; comparisons across HfO₂, Bi₂Se₃ [8], MoTe₂ lack common metrics - reporting needs retention @85°C, σ_cycle, σ_device, linearity factor NL explicitly.

## 7 Conclusion
We charted a stack from oxygen vacancy drift at atomic scale, through compliance-pinned multilevel conductance, to asynchronous STDP realized by overlapping pre/post waveforms, culminating in probabilistic consolidation and e-prop-based forward learning for on-chip continual adaptation. The synthesis shows *precision need not be restored to 32-bit* to retain knowledge: modulating *probability* of update provides a native memristive primitive for metaplasticity, cutting memory overhead 67% and energy 100× [6], while in-situ training self-repairs up to 11% defects without oracle defect map [1]. M2RU demonstrates that gated recurrent alternatives tailored to crossbars extend CL to temporal signals with decade-scale lifetime [7]. Future work must co-design barrier materials for symmetric endurance, develop officially benchmarked class-incremental splits for SHD/DVS-Gestures, and standardize defect-aware training APIs. If these close, filamentary memristors move from fascinating non-ideality to first-class substrate for *lifelong* intelligence at milliwatt power.
