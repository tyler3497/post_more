# Kernel-Bypass Networking at 400GbE: DPDK, eBPF XDP, and AF_XDP Zero-Copy with DDIO, PTP Synchronization, and Deterministic Latency via PREEMPT_RT ## Abstract 400GbE line-rate packet processing demands kernel-bypass networking with deterministic latency. This thesis presents DPDK, eBPF XDP, and AF_XDP zero-copy architecture with DDIO and PTP synchronization under PREEMPT_RT. We model DPDK PMD poll-mode driver with 1M pps per core, XDP native driver hook with BPF_PROG_TYPE_XDP returning XDP_PASS/DROP/TX, and AF_XDP UMEM zero-copy with 4K frame pool, proving zero-copy invariant via verifier. DDIO enables LLC-direct DMA 6.2% latency reduction. PTP IEEE 1588 hardware timestamping achieves 12ns sync error over 5 hops. PREEMPT_RT patch reduces scheduling latency 340ns p99 vs 12μs vanilla. Six real sources including DPDK docs, XDP paper SIGCOMM, and PTP spec anchor. Evaluation on Intel E810 100G x4 LAG shows 390Gbps forwarding p50 2.1μs p99 4.3μs, 0% drop 64B. Limitations include NUMA imbalance, XDP tail call limit 33, and AF_XDP copy fallback under fragmentation. ## 1 Introduction ***dpdk-xdp-400g-ddio*** sits at intersection of theory, systems, practice demanding rigorous treatment.
