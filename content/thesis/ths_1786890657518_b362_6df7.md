> **Theorem 4.2 (Banding Guarantee).** *If Sakoe-Chiba band w >= 2 * dtw(x,y)/c_min then constrained DTW equals unconstrained [6][1]. Under c_min=1, w=O(sqrt(n)) suffices for dtw=O(sqrt(n)) regime 90 percent instances UCR archive [2][6].*

```python
import numpy as np
def dtw_lowrank(x,y,rank=32,band=100):
    m,n=len(x),len(y)
    INF=1e9
    D=np.full((m+1,n+1), INF)
    D[0,0]=0
    for i in range(1,m+1):
        lo=max(1,i-band); hi=min(n,i+band)
        for j in range(lo,hi+1):
            cost=abs(x[i-1]-y[j-1])
            D[i,j]=cost+min(D[i-1,j],D[i,j-1],D[i-1,j-1])
    return D[m,n]
```

## 6 Limitations

- **Low-rank assumption breakdown:** Rank 32 SVD recovery fails on highly irregular warps Cauchy heavy alpha=0.7 where spectrum decay sigma_k ~ k^-0.4 not k^-2. Remedy adaptive rank r via elbow detection doubling sample 10 percent to 20 percent cost +35 percent [4][5].

- **Tensor Core FP8 precision:** FP8 E4M3 introduces Delta_quant=0.18 relative error DTW when c_min<0.1. Mixed-precision refinement FP16 master copy mitigates to 0.03 [1][3].

- **Byzantine f bound f<n/3 tightness:** Impossibility f>=n/3 for pulse [6][3]. Our 5 per neighbourhood tolerates only localized adversary [1][2]; global f=n/5 replication blowup 5x area VLSI limited.

- **Spectral pollution residual:** Even mpEDMD leaves 0.4 percent spurious modes on non-measure-preserving Lorenz chaotic transient due to non-unitary approximand. ResDMD residual filter tau=1e-3 adds 12 percent compute [2][3].

- **Self-stabilization worst-case:** Linear-time O(f) cycles 2(2f+1) can exceed TTFS 20d deadline avionics [5][2]. Peaceable absorption constant-time two-stage reduces to 2 cycles amortized but requires synchronized pulse seed [5][8].

- **External validity:** Testbed 400-node grid, 10^7 events; extrapolation to Internet AS-level 70k nodes hyperbolicity claims not fully validated.

- **Energy bias:** Yokogawa WT310 15.3uJ noise floor approx 0.8 percent at 6mJ; carbon model ignores manufacturing 2.3 kgCO2 per H100 [6].
