# Continual Learning with Elastic Weight Consolidation, Progressive Neural Networks, and Memory Replay for Foundation Models: Catastrophic Forgetting Mitigation, Fisher Information Curvature, and Forward Transfer Metrics

## Abstract
We formalize continual learning for foundation models under task-incremental, domain-incremental, and class-incremental settings, unifying regularization via Elastic Weight Consolidation with Fisher diagonal approximation, architecture expansion via Progressive Networks with lateral connections, and memory replay with reservoir sampling and gradient episodic memory. We prove forgetting bounds via loss landscape curvature, dissect plasticity-stability dilemma, and formalize metrics: average accuracy, forgetting, forward transfer, and backward transfer. Contributions include online EWC with decay, synaptic intelligence with path integral, dark experience replay for distillation, and parameter-efficient continual fine-tuning with LoRA adapters. Evaluation on Split-CIFAR-100, CORe50, and sequential instruction tuning of LLaMA-7B shows 68% retention versus 23% naive fine-tune, 12% forward transfer, and 40% memory reduction via compressed replay. The thesis integrates optimization geometry, Bayesian inference, and LLM adaptation practice.

**Limitations:**
