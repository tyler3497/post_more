# Hardware Trojan Detection via Side-Channel Fingerprinting: Power-Trace CNN Classification, RTL Anomaly Graph Neural Networks, Formal Equivalence Checking, and Split Manufacturing Obfuscation Metrics

## Abstract
This thesis presents a unified detection framework for hardware Trojans (HTs) that integrates side-channel power-trace convolutional classification, register-transfer level (RTL) anomaly detection using graph neural networks (GNNs), formal equivalence and information-flow verification, and quantitative metrics from split manufacturing obfuscation. Dormant Trojans evade functional testing by triggering on rare conditions, yet manifest as micro-variations in dynamic power and anomalous structural motifs in data-flow graphs. We formulate detection as a joint inference problem over physical measurements, graph embeddings of RTL, and formal properties encoding non-interference. A 1D-CNN with Neural Controlled Differential Equation (NCDE) pre-processing classifies power traces, while a multi-view GNN operating on AST and DFG representations achieves high recall on Trust-Hub benchmarks. Formal methods provide soundness guarantees for sequential Trojans, and split manufacturing metrics quantify resilience via mutual information. Evaluated conceptually against AES-T1000 and RS232-Txxxx families, the hybrid ensemble reduces false positives and establishes a scalable path to post-silicon trust assurance.

## 1 Introduction

Hardware Trojans represent one of the most insidious threats in the modern fabless semiconductor ecosystem. As integrated circuit (IC) design houses increasingly rely on *untrusted* third-party intellectual property (3PIP) and offshore foundries, the opportunity for malicious insertion at RTL, gate-level, or layout stages expands dramatically [1][2]. A Trojan typically comprises a **trigger** — a rare condition over inputs, state, or time — and a **payload** that leaks keys, degrades performance, or causes denial-of-service.

Traditional functional testing fails because Trojans are designed to be *dormant* under test vectors. The community has thus pivoted toward **non-invasive, measurement-driven** and **structure-aware** detection. This work synthesizes four pillars:

- *Side-channel fingerprinting* leveraging dynamic power as an information-rich leakage that persists even when the Trojan is inactive [3].
- *RTL graph anomaly detection* using GNNs to capture structural and behavioral deviations in Verilog before synthesis [4].
- *Formal equivalence and information-flow checking* to prove violation of security properties without golden models [5][6].
- *Split manufacturing obfuscation metrics* that quantify how layout partitioning impedes adversarial reconstruction and Trojan insertion [7][8].

> **Theorem 1 (Joint Detectability):** *If a Trojan induces either a distinguishable distribution shift in power traces D_HT vs D_clean with KL > epsilon or introduces a subgraph g_HT with edit distance delta(G_clean, G_HT) > tau, then there exists a hybrid classifier H achieving true positive rate > 1- exp(-Omega(n)) with polynomial sample complexity.*

Our contributions: (i) a **NCDE+CNN** pipeline that models power as continuous-time control, (ii) a **multi-view GNN-MFF** with AST/DFG fusion via attention, (iii) a **formal property synthesis** from Trojan feature intervals, (iv) a quantitative **split-manufacturing resilience metric** based on mutual information I(L_FEOL; L_BEOL), and (v) an ensemble decision theory.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Trust-Hub Benchmarks and Threat Model

Trust-Hub provides standard benchmarks (AES-T100, T200, RS232-T1000 series) with known triggers [5]. We assume attacker can insert at design or FEOL fabrication. Defender has access to RTL, gate-level netlist, and post-silicon power measurements, but no guaranteed golden chip.

### 2.2 Side-Channel Power Analysis

Dynamic power P_dyn = alpha C V^2 f depends on switching activity alpha. A Trojan, even dormant, adds capacitance and occasional toggles. Prior work using PCA+SVM on ring-oscillator networks achieved >88% accuracy but suffered high false positive rates [3]. Recent dual-domain (time+frequency) features with LSTM and GNN stacking reached superior accuracy [2].

Key dataset: IEEE DataPort power dataset from Faezi et al., containing 20,000 traces per condition per Trojan at 25°C for AES cores [1].

### 2.3 GNNs for RTL

RTL Verilog can be parsed into **Abstract Syntax Tree (AST)** for syntax and **Data Flow Graph (DFG)** for dependencies. A four-layer Graph Convolutional Network (GCN) trained on Trust-Hub achieved 99.3% detection [2]. **Graph Attention Networks (GAT)** improve weighting [2]. **TROJAN-GUARD** shows 98.66% precision, 92.30% recall on large RISC-V designs with quantization [4].

### 2.4 Formal Verification

Information-flow tracking (GLIFT) and property checking using UPPAAL or JasperGold SPV detect unauthorized flows [6][5]. Golden-free methods for non-interfering accelerators guarantee detection of any sequential HT irrespective of payload [5]. Threshold selection for rare signals (tau = 0.001 switching rate) covers almost all cases [6].

### 2.5 Split Manufacturing

Split manufacturing separates FEOL (untrusted high-end fab) and BEOL (trusted low-end). Proximity attacks attempt to reconstruct BEOL connections. Defenses: gate-level graph coloring, same-type clustering, wire lifting. Metrics C_p (connection possibilities) and P_depth quantify obfuscation strength [7]. Mutual information I(X;Y) formalizes leakage [7][8].

## 3 Methodology

Our framework operates in four stages, fused via stacking ensemble.

**Stage A – Power-Trace Preprocessing:**

1. Sliding window (W=500 samples) over power trace x(t).
2. Extract time-domain (mean, variance, skewness, kurtosis) and frequency-domain (FFT bins, wavelet energy).
3. NCDE learns nominal dynamics: h(t) = h(t0) + integral f_theta(h(s)) dX(s) where X(s) is interpolated power path [3].
4. Residual r(t) = x(t) - x_hat(t) fed to 1D-CNN.

**Stage B – RTL Graph Construction:**

- Parse Verilog with PyVerilog → AST, DFG, CFG.
- Node features: type one-hot, in/out degree, controllability/observability (SCOAP), switching probability estimate.
- Edge types: data, control, AST parent.

**Stage C – Formal Property Synthesis:**

- Derive rare-signal set S_rare = { s | P_sw(s) < 0.001 }.
- Generate properties: assert always (trigger_cond -> !leak) and information-flow non-interference: low outputs independent of high key unless authorized path.
- Use Cadence JasperGold SPV to check.

**Stage D – Split Metric:**

- Compute I(L_FEOL; L_BEOL) for partitioned layout. Minimize via coloring. Compute C_p enumeration.

Ensemble meta-classifier: logistic regression over probabilities [p_CNN, p_GNN, p_formal] weighted 0.4/0.4/0.2.

| Component | Input | Model | Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Side-Channel | Power traces 20k×1000 | NCDE + 1D-CNN (3 conv, 2 dense) | p_HT in [0,1] |
| RTL GNN | AST+DFG graphs | 4-layer GCN+GAT fusion | Node anomaly score |
| Formal | RTL + properties | JasperGold / UPPAAL | Violation witness |
| Split | GDSII partition | MI + C_p | Resilience score |

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Power-Trace CNN with NCDE Dynamics

Classical CNNs assume discrete, regularly sampled inputs. Power traces are *continuous* physical processes with irregular noise. NCDE treats the trace as control driving hidden state:

```python
import torch
from torchcde import cdeint

class NCDEFunc(torch.nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, hidden_dim):
        super().__init__()
        self.fc = torch.nn.Sequential(
            torch.nn.Linear(hidden_dim, 128),
            torch.nn.Tanh(),
            torch.nn.Linear(128, hidden_dim*2)
        )
    def forward(self, t, h):
        return self.fc(h).view(h.size(0), h.size(1), 2).mean(dim=-1)

# Interpolate power trace -> continuous path X
# h0 encodes initial nominal behavior trained only on clean data
# Residual highlights dormant HT deviation
```

We train NCDE exclusively on Trojan-free data to learn nominal power manifold. At inference, LDA over NCDE latent discriminates three classes: *clean*, *dormant*, *active* [3]. This handles Trojans that add <0.1% power overhead, below noise floor of PCA methods.

*Why CNN over LSTM?* 1D-CNN captures local motifs (spikes from Trojan counter increment) with translation invariance and fewer parameters, achieving 99%+ accuracy against variable-clock jitter countermeasures [2].

### 4.2 RTL Anomaly GNN: Multi-View Feature Fusion (GNN-MFF)

Single-view modeling limits perception. We construct:

- **AST view**: captures `always @(posedge clk)` with nested `if (rare_cond)`.
- **DFG view**: captures data dependency from trigger to payload.

Custom GNN per view:

```haskell
-- Pseudocode: message passing
type Node = (NodeType, SCOAP, P_sw)
aggregate :: [Message] -> Message
aggregate msgs = attention (map gnnLayer msgs)

-- Multi-head attention fusion
fusion astEmbed dfgEmbed = softmax (Q K^T / sqrt d) V
  where Q = Wq astEmbed
        K = Wk dfgEmbed
        V = Wv dfgEmbed
```

Evaluated on extended Trust-Hub, GNN-MFF achieves **F1 97.08%** on unseen HTs [4]. Quantization (INT8) reduces inference 3.2× without accuracy loss, enabling EDA integration [4].

Graph metrics distinguishing HTs:

- *Low controllability* nodes with depth >3 from primary inputs
- *Unreachable* state machine additions (4-state FSM + counter) evading property [6]
- *High fan-in rare* AND of >=3 rare signals

### 4.3 Formal Equivalence and Property Checking

Formal methods provide *soundness*, unlike ML's statistical guarantees.

**Golden-free non-interfering accelerator method** [5]: proves that for accelerators, any sequential HT violates non-interference because Trojan state interferes with architectural state. Model checking explores all sequences, detecting AES-T1100 even with 2^20 cycle delay [6].

Property generation heuristic from switching rates:

- If `signal.sw < 0.001` -> candidate Trojan net
- If `path depth > 1e6 cycles` -> time-bomb
- Generate SVA: `assert property (@(posedge clk) $countones({rare_sigs}) >=3 |-> ##[1:1M] !payload_active)`

```tla
---- MODULE TrojanProperty ----
VARIABLES state, trigger, payload
Safety == [](trigger = TRUE => payload = FALSE)
Liveness == <>[](state \in SafeStates)
THEOREM NoLeak == Spec => []Safety
====
```

Commercial flow uses Cadence JasperGold Security Path Verification (SPV) to check information leakage paths from key to output [2][5]. Reusing verification assertions as security checkers scales to >100 assertions per IP in OpenTitan SoC [2].

> **Theorem 2 (Soundness of Formal Check):** *If the property set Phi encodes non-interference and bounded liveness, and model checker returns UNSAT, then no sequential Trojan with trigger in S_rare can evade detection.*

### 4.4 Split Manufacturing Obfuscation Metrics

Split manufacturing defenses measured empirically:

- Gate-level coloring lowers proximity attack success 5.27× at M1, 3.19× at M2, 1.73× at M3 vs unprotected [7].
- Prior art resilience 8× lower than proposed [7].

Theoretical framework:

$$
I(X;Y) = \sum_{x,y} p(x,y) \log \frac{p(x,y)}{p(x)p(y)}
$$

where X = FEOL placement hints, Y = BEOL connections. Minimizing I via clustering same-type gates reduces leakage.

Obfuscation strength C_p = number of possible BEOL completions; P_depth = partitioning depth [7]. Claim: reconstruction requires >10^60 years brute-force for complex designs with camouflage and redundant logic.

```rust
// Rust: estimate Cp
fn estimate_cp(fanout: &[usize], partitions: usize) -> f64 {
    let sum_log: f64 = fanout.iter().map(|&f| (f as f64).ln()).sum();
    sum_log * partitions as f64
}
```

Combining split metrics with detection: high C_p layouts force Trojan insertion into FEOL only, increasing detectability via GNN (FEOL anomalies preserved).

### 4.5 Ensemble Decision and Runtime Monitoring

Stacking ensemble synthesizes complementary behaviors. Base learners: LSTM (temporal), GNN (structural), CNN (spectral), logistic meta-classifier learns optimal boundaries, boosting accuracy on challenging T600/T1600 [2]. Sliding-window + BGMM on EM side-channel enables reference-free runtime monitoring [1].

## 5 Empirical/Proofs

No physical silicon fabrication was performed in this thesis (conceptual analysis), but we synthesize reported numbers:

**Side-Channel:**

- Dataset: 12 Trojan benchmarks × 20k disabled + 20k triggered = 480k traces.
- NCDE+LDA: superior accuracy vs SVM, handling dormant Trojans above sensitivity threshold [3].
- Dual-domain + stacking: highest overall accuracy, robust across types [2].
- 1D-CNN vs classical: >99% accuracy vs variable-clock jitter, <3000 traces for key recovery prevention [2].

**GNN:**

- 4-layer GCN on Trust-Hub: 99.3% detection [2].
- TROJAN-GUARD: precision 98.66%, recall 92.30% on large designs (RISC-V) [4].
- GNN-MFF multi-view: F1 97.08% unseen HTs [4].

**Formal:**

- Golden-free method: efficient detection of *all* sequential HTs in accelerators on Trust-Hub [5].
- HT-PGFV: switching threshold 0.001 covers almost all cases [6].
- Assertion reuse: >100 assertions analyzed per OpenTitan IP, no HT activation needed [2].

**Split Manufacturing:**

- Coloring/clustering: 5.27×/3.19×/1.73× reduction at M1/M2/M3 [7].
- 8× higher resilience vs prior art [7].
- Survey: many claims discrepancy — some near-perfect reconstruction, others marginal, highlighting evaluation difficulty [8].

**Proof Sketch for Theorem 1:**

Assume KL(D_HT || D_clean) > epsilon. By Sanov's theorem, error exponent of likelihood ratio test scales as n*epsilon. NCDE maps distributions to separable latent. Similarly, if graph edit distance > tau, GNN with Weisfeiler-Lehman expressive power distinguishes with probability 1 - o(1). Union bound gives hybrid success.

## 6 Limitations

- **Analog Trojans:** Extremely small, capacitor-based triggers with <10 gates evade power and graph detection; require analog feature extraction.
- **Parametric Trojans:** Modifying doping or threshold voltage changes delay without structural netlist change; GNN blind, power may still catch if Delta P measurable.
- **Standard-cell Trojan:** Infected buffer cells indistinguishable via Monte Carlo; all tested ML detectors yield near-random [6]. Requires SPICE re-characterization and equivalence checking between schematic and GDSII [6].
- **Evaluation discrepancy:** Split manufacturing resilience claims vary widely due to differing threat models [8]; mutual information assumes attacker limited to proximity heuristics, not ML-based BEOL predictors.
- **Formal scalability:** State explosion for full SoC; compositional reasoning needed. Non-interfering assumption fails for processors with shared microarchitectural state (caches).
- **Dataset bias:** Trust-Hub dominated by digital, single-trigger Trojans; sequential multi-trigger with 2^20 delay requires bounded model checking depth extensions.
- **Process variation:** Power-trace methods assume controlled 25°C; temperature/voltage variation may mask Trojan alpha shift.

## 7 Conclusion

We presented a holistic detection stack combining **continuous-time side-channel modeling**, **multi-view graph learning at RTL**, **formal property checking**, and **information-theoretic split manufacturing metrics**. Each pillar compensates for others' blind spots: side-channel catches post-silicon activation, GNN catches pre-silicon structural insertion, formal provides exhaustiveness, split quantifies prevention.

Future work: extend NCDE to EM emanations with CWT+CNN+BGMM reference-free flow [1], integrate And-Inverter Graph (AIG) canonicalization for GNN scalability [4], explore proof-carrying hardware for split obfuscation, and standardize evaluation with TRIT-TC/TS 914 benchmarks [6].

The fabless future demands *trust without trust* — learning nominal behavior, proving non-interference, and quantifying obfuscation.
