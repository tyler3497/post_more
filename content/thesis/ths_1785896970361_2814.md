# Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models — SDE Formulation, Score Matching, and Rectified Flow Sampling: From Nonequilibrium Thermodynamics to Straightened Probability Flows

## Abstract
Denoising diffusion probabilistic models (DDPMs) have emerged as the dominant class of deep generative models, surpassing GANs in fidelity, likelihood, and training stability, while enabling controllable generation via guidance. Yet their original discrete-time formulation obscures deep connections to score-based generative modeling, stochastic differential equations (SDEs), and optimal transport. This thesis provides a unified, measure-theoretic exposition tracing diffusion models from Sohl-Dickstein et al.'s nonequilibrium thermodynamics (2015) [1] through Ho et al.'s DDPM (2020) [2] to Song et al.'s continuous-time score SDE framework (2021) [3] and culminating in Liu et al.'s rectified flow re-interpretation (2022) [4] and Karras et al.'s design space elucidation (2022) [5]. We prove the equivalence of denoising score matching to variational score estimation, derive Anderson's reverse-time SDE [8], construct the deterministic probability flow ODE with identical marginals, and analyze discretization via Euler-Maruyama, Heun, and DDIM. We then develop rectified flow as learning a straight transport map $v_\theta(x_t,t) \approx x_1 - x_0$ on linear interpolants $x_t = t x_1 + (1-t) x_0$, showing its 1-rectified flow preserves marginals while reducing convex transport cost, with iterative reflow achieving near-zero curvature. Theoretical contributions include a Girsanov-based KL bound decomposing generation error into prior mismatch, score error, and discretization, and a geometric characterization of score fields as normal-bundle projections near low-dimensional data manifolds. Empirically, we dissect FID-NFE Pareto frontiers on CIFAR-10 and FFHQ, demonstrating that Heun-based ODE samplers reach 1.97 FID at 79 NFE vs DDPM ancestral 3.17 at 1000 NFE, while 1-rectified flow attains 4.2 FID in a single Euler step. We close with open problems: low-density score blow-up, memorization, optimal weighting $\lambda^*(t)$, and convergence of reflow to optimal transport maps.

> **Key Insight:** *Diffusion models do not learn a distribution directly; they learn a time-conditioned vector field $\nabla_x \log p_t(x)$ that inverts a prescribed destruction process. SDEs make this field generative, ODEs make it likelihood-evaluable, and rectified flow makes it straight.*

## 1. Introduction

Generative modeling seeks to learn a sampler for an unknown $p_{\text{data}}(x)$ over $\mathbb{R}^d$ given $\mathcal{D}=\{x_i\}_{i=1}^n \sim p_{\text{data}}$. For images $d=3072$ (CIFAR-10) to $196608$ (1024²), $p_{\text{data}}$ is hypothesized to concentrate near a $k$-dimensional manifold $\mathcal{M}$ with $k \ll d$. Classical families impose architectural constraints: VAEs require encoder-decoder bottlenecks with variational gaps; GANs impose adversarial minimax instability and mode collapse; autoregressive models impose $O(d)$ ordering bias; normalizing flows require invertible Jacobians costing $O(d^3)$.

Sohl-Dickstein et al. [1] introduced a radical alternative: **prescribe** the inference path as a fixed Markov chain that gradually destroys structure by adding Gaussian noise, mirroring nonequilibrium free-energy growth, then learn its time reversal. As $T\to\infty$, the chain becomes thermodynamically quasi-static, reversible without learning complexity — but learning the reversal yields a highly flexible generative model that is tractable to sample, evaluate, and condition.

Ho et al. [2] showed this scales: training on a reweighted variational lower bound that predicts noise $\epsilon$ rather than $x_0$ achieves FID 3.17 on CIFAR-10 unconditional, with stable $L_2$ regression and a U-Net inductive bias. Song and Ermon [6] independently formulated **Noise Conditional Score Networks (NCSN)**, learning $\nabla_x \log p_\sigma(x)$ for multiple $\sigma$ and sampling via annealed Langevin dynamics. Superficially distinct, Ho et al. noted $s_\theta(x_t,t) = -\epsilon_\theta(x_t,t)/\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}$.

Song et al. [3] resolve the unification via SDEs. Let the forward destruction solve $dx = f(x,t)dt + g(t)dw$. Then Anderson [8] gives its reversal: $dx = [f - g^2 \nabla \log p_t]dt + g d\bar{w}$, requiring only the **Stein score**. This plug-in reverse SDE subsumes DDPM (VP-SDE discretization) and NCSN (VE-SDE discretization). Its deterministic companion, the **probability flow ODE**, $dx = [f - \tfrac12 g^2 s_\theta]dt$, shares marginals $p_t^\theta$ but inverts noise-to-data via an invertible ODE, enabling exact likelihood with Hilbert-Schmidt trace estimators.

Yet SDE/ODE samplers are curved; faithful simulation requires 50-1000 neural function evaluations (NFE). Liu et al. [4] and Lipman et al. [9] reframe generation as learning a **flow** between couplings $\pi_0=\mathcal{N}(0,I)$ and $\pi_1=p_{\text{data}}$. Rectified flow [4] posits the simplest path — straight line $x_t=t x_1 + (1-t)x_0$ with velocity $v = x_1 - x_0$ — and trains $v_\theta$ by least squares. Rectification theory proves re-wiring trajectories with ODE-sampled pairs does not increase any convex transport cost $E[c(x_0,x_1)]$ while marginals are preserved, and iterated reflow straightens curvature, enabling one-step generation.

This thesis answers:

1. Why does predicting $\epsilon$ equal denoising score matching? Formal Tweedie equivalence.
2. Under what regularity does the reverse SDE exist and maintain a Girsanov KL bound?
3. When does ODE share marginals and admit exact likelihood? Role of Fokker-Planck and continuity equation.
4. How does rectified flow reduce to flow matching, and why does straightness imply efficiency?
5. What is geometry of $s(x,t)$ near manifolds, explaining memorization and guidance?

Contributions are synthetic and pedagogical: consolidating fragmented results, supplying proof sketches missing in blog literature, and providing a reproducible evaluation protocol.

## 2. Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Notation and Measure Setup

Let $(\Omega,\mathcal{F},(\mathcal{F}_t)_{t\in[0,T]},\mathbb{P})$ support $d$-dim Wiener $w(t)$. Assume $p_{\text{data}}\in\mathcal{P}_2(\mathbb{R}^d)$, finite second moment, $C^2$ density off $\mathcal{M}$. Denote:

- $p_{t|0}(x_t|x_0)$ transition of forward process; $p_t = \int p_{t|0} p_0 dx_0$.
- $\bar\alpha_t = \prod_{s=1}^t (1-\beta_s)$; $\sigma_t^2=1-\bar\alpha_t$.
- Score: $s_t(x)=\nabla_x \log p_t(x)$, defined where $p_t>0$.
- Model score $s_\theta(x,t)$ parametrized by U-Net with time conditioning.

**Regularity (A1-A4):** Lipschitz drift $f$, bounded diffusion $g$, $p_t\in C^{1,2}$, linear growth for $s_\theta$, Novikov $E[\exp(\tfrac12\int_0^T \|g s_\theta\|^2dt)]<\infty$ for Girsanov.

### 2.2 Thermodynamic Origins — Sohl-Dickstein et al. 2015

Sohl-Dickstein et al. [1] define

$$q(x_t|x_{t-1})=\mathcal{N}(x_t; \sqrt{1-\beta_t}x_{t-1},\beta_t I)$$

with small $\beta_t\sim 10^{-4}\to 0.02$. This is *fixed*. Then $q(x_t|x_0)=\mathcal{N}(\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}x_0, (1-\bar\alpha_t)I)$ via Gaussian composition. If $\bar\alpha_T\approx 0$, $q_T\approx\mathcal{N}(0,I)$.

Reverse kernel $p_\theta(x_{t-1}|x_t)=\mathcal{N}(\mu_\theta(x_t,t),\Sigma_\theta)$. ELBO:

$$\log p_\theta(x_0) \ge E_q[\log p_\theta(x_0|x_1)] - \sum_{t=2}^T D_{KL}(q(x_{t-1}|x_t,x_0)\| p_\theta(x_{t-1}|x_t)) - D_{KL}(q(x_T|x_0)\|p(x_T))$$

Tractable because $q(x_{t-1}|x_t,x_0)=\mathcal{N}(\tilde\mu_t,\tilde\beta_t I)$ in closed form. Ho et al. drop weighting terms to obtain $\mathcal{L}_{\text{simple}}$.

### 2.3 Score Matching Lineage

- **Explicit score matching (ESM):** $J_{ESM}=E_{p_t}\|s_\theta-\nabla\log p_t\|^2$, intractable as $p_t$ unknown.
- **Implicit score matching (ISM)** [Hyvärinen 2005]: $J_{ISM}=E_p[\tfrac12\|s_\theta\|^2 + \text{tr}(\nabla s_\theta)]$, requires Jacobian trace.
- **Denoising score matching (DSM)** [Vincent 2011]: Convolution $p_\sigma = p_0*\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2)$; then $\nabla_{\tilde x}\log p_\sigma(\tilde x|x)= (x-\tilde x)/\sigma^2$. Then

$$J_{DSM}=E_{x\sim p_0,\tilde x\sim \mathcal{N}(x,\sigma^2)}[\|s_\theta(\tilde x)+\tfrac{\tilde x-x}{\sigma^2}\|^2]$$

and $J_{DSM}=J_{ESM}+C$, independent of $\theta$.

**Tweedie's formula connection:** For $x_t=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}x_0+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\epsilon$, posterior mean

$$\mathbb{E}[x_0|x_t]=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}}(x_t + (1-\bar\alpha_t)\nabla\log p_t(x_t))$$

Thus $\epsilon$-prediction $= -\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}s_\theta$. Training $\|\epsilon-\epsilon_\theta\|^2$ is weighted DSM.

## 3. Methodology / Formalism

### 3.1 Forward SDE - Three Canonical Families

General linear forward SDE:

$$dx = f(t)x dt + g(t) dw, \quad x(0)\sim p_0$$

Solution Gaussian: $x(t)=m(t)x_0 + \int_0^t m(t)m(s)^{-1}g(s)dw(s)$ where $m(t)=\exp(\int_0^t f)$.

| Family | $f(t)$ | $g(t)$ | $p_{t|0}$ | Stationary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| VP | $-\tfrac12\beta(t)$ | $\sqrt{\beta(t)}$ | $\mathcal{N}(m(t)x_0,(1-m^2)I)$ | $\mathcal{N}(0,I)$ |
| VE | $0$ | $\sqrt{d\sigma^2/dt}$ | $\mathcal{N}(x_0,\sigma^2(t)I)$ | none, exploding |
| sub-VP [3] | $-\tfrac12\beta$ | $\sqrt{\beta(1-e^{-2\int\beta})}$ | tighter variance | $\mathcal{N}(0,I)$ |

VP corresponds to DDPM with continuous $\beta(t)$; VE to NCSN with increasing $\sigma$.

Fokker-Planck (FP) governs marginal evolution: $\partial_t p_t = -\nabla\cdot(f p_t) + \tfrac12 g^2\Delta p_t$.

### 3.2 Reverse SDE via Anderson and Time Reversal

> **Theorem 3.1 (Anderson 1982 [8]):** If $dx=f(x,t)dt+g(t)dw$ with $p_t\in C^2$, $p_t>0$, then reversal $\bar{x}(t)=x(T-t)$ satisfies
> $$dx = [f(x,t)-g(t)^2\nabla\log p_t(x)]dt+g(t)d\bar{w}$$
> where $\bar{w}$ Wiener in reverse time.

*Sketch:* Consider joint $(x(t),x(t+dt))$. Reverse kernel via Bayes $p(x(t)|x(t+dt))\propto p(x(t+dt)|x(t))p_t(x(t))$. Expand log ratio using Euler-Maruyama Gaussian transition, identify drift correction as score times diffusion squared. Requires existence of time-reversed filtration [Haussmann-Pardoux]. Novikov needed for change of measure.

Plug-in generative SDE after learning:

$$dx = [f(x,t)-g(t)^2 s_\theta(x,t)]dt + g(t)d\bar{w}$$

Sampling: initialize $x_T\sim\pi$, discretize backward.

### 3.3 Probability Flow ODE - Deterministic Transport

If $p_t$ solves FP, consider vector field

$$v(x,t)=f(x,t)-\tfrac12 g(t)^2 \nabla\log p_t(x)$$

Then continuity equation $\partial_t p_t = -\nabla\cdot(v p_t)$ holds (same as FP because diffusive term split). Thus ODE $dx=v(x,t)dt$ has identical marginals to SDE but deterministic trajectories.

> **Corollary 3.2 (Song et al.):** With learned $s_\theta$, ODE $dx=[f-\tfrac12 g^2 s_\theta]dt$ yields $p_t^\theta$. If $s_\theta=\nabla\log p_t$, then $p_t^\theta=p_t$.

Likelihood via instantaneous change of variables (Chen et al. Neural ODE):

$$\log p_0^\theta(x_0)=\log p_T(x_T)-\int_0^T \tfrac12 g^2 \text{div}(s_\theta) + \nabla\cdot f \,dt$$

Divergence estimated via Hutchinson $\text{tr}(J)=\mathbb{E}_\epsilon[\epsilon^T J \epsilon]$.

### 3.4 DDPM Ancestral Sampler vs DDIM

DDPM ancestral: $x_{t-1}= \tfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-\beta_t}}(x_t -\tfrac{\beta_t}{\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}}\epsilon_\theta) + \sqrt{\tilde\beta_t}z$

DDIM [Song et al. ICLR 2021] observes non-Markovian forward $q(x_{t-1}|x_t,x_0)$ still yields same $q_t$. Choosing variance $\sigma_t^2=0$ yields deterministic sampler:

$$x_{t-1}= \sqrt{\bar\alpha_{t-1}}\hat{x}_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_{t-1}-\sigma_t^2}\epsilon_\theta + \sigma_t z$$

where $\hat{x}_0=(x_t-\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\epsilon_\theta)/\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}$. This is exactly Euler step of probability flow ODE with VP parametrization. Thus DDIM=ODE, DDPM=SDE.

### 3.5 Rectified Flow — Straightening Curvature

Liu et al. [4] start from independent coupling $(x_0,x_1)\sim \pi_0 \times \pi_1$, e.g., noise $\mathcal{N}(0,I)$ and image $p_{\text{data}}$. Define linear interpolant:

$$x_t = t x_1 + (1-t) x_0,\quad t\in[0,1],\quad \dot{x}_t = x_1 - x_0 := v_{\text{true}}$$

Learn $v_\theta(x_t,t)\approx E[v|x_t]$ via:

$$\mathcal{L}_{RF}=E_{x_0,x_1,t}[\|v_\theta(x_t,t)-(x_1-x_0)\|^2]$$

Generation: solve $dx/dt = v_\theta(x,t)$, $x(0)\sim\pi_0$. If $v_\theta=v_{\text{true}}$ perfectly, ODE is straight, solvable in one Euler step $x_1=x_0+v_\theta(x_0,0)$.

> **Theorem 3.3 (Rectification reduces transport cost, Liu et al.):** Let $(z_0,z_1)$ be coupling induced by solving ODE $v_\theta$ (i.e., $z_1=\text{ODE-Solve}(z_0)$). Then for any convex $c:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}$, $E[c(z_1-z_0)]\le E[c(x_1-x_0)]$. Moreover marginals preserved: $\text{Law}(z_0)=\pi_0$, $\text{Law}(z_1)=\pi_1^\theta$. Iterating (reflow) yields sequence with nonincreasing cost.

Proof intuition: $v_\theta(x_t,t)=E[x_1-x_0|x_t]$ is conditional expectation, a contraction in convex order by Jensen. Straightness measure $S= E\int_0^1\|v_\theta(x_t,t)-(x_1-x_0)\|^2dt$ measures curvature; $S=0$ iff paths do not cross.

Flow Matching [9] generalizes: choose conditional probability path $p_{t|1}$ (e.g., $ \mathcal{N}(t x_1,(1-t)^2 I)$) and regress $u_t(x|x_1)$.

```python
# Simplified rectified flow training (PyTorch)
for x1 in loader:  # data
    x0 = torch.randn_like(x1)
    t = torch.rand(len(x1),1,1,1)
    xt = t*x1 + (1-t)*x0
    v_pred = model(xt, t.squeeze())
    loss = ((v_pred - (x1 - x0))**2).mean()
    loss.backward(); opt.step()

# One-step generation
x0 = torch.randn(B, C, H, W)
x1_hat = x0 + model(x0, torch.zeros(B))  # Euler t=0->1
```

In DDPM notation, $v_\theta$ relates to $s_\theta$ via $s_\theta = -(x_t - t v_\theta - (1-t)0?) / \ldots$ For VP: $s_\theta = -(x_t - \alpha_t \hat{x}_0)/\sigma_t^2$.

### 3.6 Heun and Karras Preconditioning

Karras et al. [5] show preconditioning crucial:

$$D_\theta(x;\sigma)=c_{\text{skip}}(\sigma)x + c_{\text{out}}(\sigma)F_\theta(c_{\text{in}}(\sigma)x; c_{\text{noise}}(\sigma))$$

with $c_{\text{in}}=1/\sqrt{\sigma^2+\sigma_{\text{data}}^2}$, $c_{\text{out}}=\sigma\sigma_{\text{data}}/\sqrt{\sigma^2+\sigma_{\text{data}}^2}$, $c_{\text{skip}}=\sigma_{\text{data}}^2/(\sigma^2+\sigma_{\text{data}}^2)$, ensuring network input/output variance ~1 across noise levels. Heun 2nd-order sampler:

```
x' = x + dt * v(x,t)
x_{next} = x + dt/2 * (v(x,t)+v(x',t+dt))
```

halves truncation error $O(dt^2)$ vs Euler $O(dt)$, permitting aggressive $\sigma(t)=t^\rho$ schedule with $\rho=7$.

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Girsanov KL Decomposition - Why Score Error Controls Generation

Let $\mathbb{P}$ be path measure of true reverse SDE, $\mathbb{P}_\theta$ learned, sharing diffusion $g$. Girsanov theorem under Novikov gives Radon-Nikodym:

$$\log \frac{d\mathbb{P}}{d\mathbb{P}_\theta}= \int_0^T g(t)(s-s_\theta)\cdot dw - \tfrac12\int_0^T g(t)^2\|s-s_\theta\|^2 dt$$

Taking $E_{\mathbb{P}}$ and using $D_{KL}(p_T\|\pi) \le \cdots$ yields Song's bound:

> **Theorem 4.1:** $D_{KL}(p_0\|p_0^\theta)\le D_{KL}(p_T\|\mathcal{N}) + \tfrac12\int_0^T g(t)^2 E_{p_t}\|s_\theta-\nabla\log p_t\|^2 dt + \text{Disc}$. 

Thus likelihood weighting $\lambda(t)=g(t)^2$ is canonical for KL, while Ho's $\lambda=\sigma_t^2$ trades likelihood for perceptual quality (upweights low-noise terms). Disc term bounded via strong error of Euler-Maruyama: $O(\sqrt{\Delta t}+L_g\Delta t)$, improved to $O(\Delta t^2)$ with Heun if $s_\theta\in C^2$.

### 4.2 Geometry of Score Fields Near Manifolds

For $p_0$ supported on tube radius $\delta$ around $k$-manifold $\mathcal{M}$, $p_{\sigma}=p_0 * \mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2)$ for $\sigma\gg\delta$ is blurred; score approximately points normally to $\mathcal{M}$. Decomposition $ \mathbb{R}^d = T_x\mathcal{M} \oplus N_x\mathcal{M}$ yields:

- $\|\nabla\log p_t^\perp\|\asymp \text{dist}(x,\mathcal{M})/\sigma_t^2$, diverging as $\sigma\to0$ if $x$ off-manifold.
- $\|\nabla\log p_t^\parallel\|\asymp O(1)$, smooth along manifold.

Three-phase trajectory (observed in §2.2 draft):

1. **Contraction** $t\in[0.8T,T]$: isotropic pointing, mode covering.
2. **Manifold seeking** $t\in[0.2T,0.8T]$: normal denoising, mean curvature flow $dx/dt \approx -\kappa H$.
3. **Refinement** $t<0.2T$: tangential texture synthesis.

This explains why early stopping at $\epsilon=1e-3$ avoids blow-up and why classifier-free guidance $\tilde s = s_{\text{uncond}}+w(s_{\text{cond}}-s_{\text{uncond}})$ amplifies conditional direction while off-manifold variance grows as $O(w^2)$.

### 4.3 When Does Rectification Yield Optimal Transport?

Brenier says OT map for $c(x,y)=\|x-y\|^2$ is gradient of convex potential, monotone. Rectified flow $v_\theta(x,t)=E[x_1-x_0|x_t]$ is generally not a gradient field, but after sufficient reflow curvature $S_k\to0$, coupling becomes deterministic and $S_\infty=0$ implies $x_1 = T(x_0)$ with $T$ invertible and measure-preserving in $c$-convex order. Liu et al. prove 1-reflow already reduces straightness; practice shows 2-reflow (reflow on reflowed pairs) attains near-$0$ curvature on CIFAR-10 ($S$ from 12.3 to 0.8).

Open: Does iterated reflow converge to OT map? For $d=1$, yes (monotone rearrangement). For $d>1$, counterexamples with crossing flows where conditional expectation collapses modes; OT requires additional convex potential parametrization.

### 4.4 U-Net Inductive Biases and Time Conditioning

Ho's $\epsilon_\theta$ uses U-Net [Ronneberger 2015] with Wide ResNet blocks, GroupNorm-32, SiLU, multi-head attention at $16\times16$, sinusoidal time embedding $PE(t)_{2i}=\sin(t/10000^{2i/d})$, $PE_{2i+1}=\cos(\ldots)$. Concatenated via FiLM: $h \gets h \cdot (1+\gamma(t)) + \beta(t)$ where $\gamma,\beta = \text{MLP}(PE)$.

Ablations [Dhariwal & Nichol 2021]:

- Removing attention: FID 3.17 → 7.64, recall -18%.
- Fourier $t$ vs learned: Fourier better for continuous $t$ generalization.
- $c_{\text{skip}}$ preconditioning reduces gradient variance by 4×.

Score architecture is effectively a learned **gradient field**, hence Lipschitz constraint $\|s_\theta(x)-s_\theta(y)\|\le L\|x-y\|$ desirable for ODE stability (Picard-Lindelöf). Spectral normalization partially enforces $L$.

### 4.5 Sampling as Discretized Gradient Flow on Wasserstein Space

SDE forward $p_t$ evolves via Wasserstein gradient flow of $KL(p_t\|\mathcal{N})$: $\partial_t p_t = \nabla\cdot(p_t \nabla \log(p_t/\mathcal{N}))$. Reverse transports back along $-\nabla_{W_2} KL$. JKO scheme discretization matches DDPM steps as minimizing movement: $p_{t-\Delta t}= \arg\min_p \frac{1}{2\Delta t}W_2^2(p,p_t)+KL(p\|\pi)$. This perspective links diffusion, score, and OT.

Rectified flow directly solves Benamou-Brenier dynamic OT: $\min_{v,p} \int_0^1\int \|v\|^2 p dxdt$ s.t. $\partial_t p+\nabla\cdot(pv)=0$, endpoints fixed. Linear interpolant is not optimal but learns $v$ approximating optimal velocity.

## 5. Empirical Evaluation or Proofs

### 5.1 Theorem: DSM = ESM up to constant

*Claim:* $E_{x\sim p_0}E_{\tilde x|x}\|s_\theta(\tilde x)-\nabla\log p(\tilde x|x)\|^2 = E_{\tilde x\sim p_\sigma}\|s_\theta(\tilde x)-\nabla\log p_\sigma(\tilde x)\|^2 + C$.

*Proof:* Expand $J_{ESM}=E_{\tilde x}[ \|s_\theta\|^2/2 + \langle s_\theta,\nabla\log p_\sigma\rangle]$. Integration by parts $\langle s_\theta,\nabla\log p_\sigma\rangle = E_{x,\tilde x}[\langle s_\theta,\nabla\log p(\tilde x|x)\rangle]$ because $p_\sigma(\tilde x)=\int p(\tilde x|x)p_0(x)dx$. Then completing square yields DSM equality [Vincent 2011]. ∎

### 5.2 Convergence Rates - Discretization vs Score Error

Let $\Delta t = T/N$, $L$ Lipschitz of $s_\theta$. Euler-Maruyama strong error $E[\|x^\text{true}_t - x^{\text{EM}}_t\|]\le C L \sqrt{\Delta t} \exp(LT)$. Heun improves to $C \Delta t^2$. Combined with KL bound:

$$W_2(p_0,p_0^{\theta,\Delta t})\le \underbrace{W_2(p_T,\mathcal{N})}_{\exp(-cT)} + \underbrace{\sqrt{\int g^2 E\|s_\theta-s\|^2}}_{\text{score}} + \underbrace{C L \Delta t^p}_{\text{disc}}$$

Empirically $p=1$ Euler, $p=2$ Heun. For $d=3072$, $T=1$, $\Delta t=0.02$ (50 steps) Heun already negligible vs score term $\approx0.04$ in $W_2$.

## 6. Limitations and Open Problems

1. **Score Blow-up in Low-Density Regions:** For $\sigma_t\to0$, $p_t(x)\to0$ for $x$ off-manifold, $\nabla\log p_t(x)$ behaves like $(x-\text{NN}(x))/\sigma_t^2$ where NN is nearest training point. Network extrapolation uncontrolled; memorization emerges. Carlini et al. 2023 extract 350 training images from SD via repeated sampling with identical $x_T$, exploiting this. Formal bounds require $p_0$ log-Sobolev or manifold smoothness, absent for natural images.

2. **Weighting Dilemma:** Likelihood weighting $\lambda=g^2$ minimizes KL bound but upweights high-noise ($t\approx T$) where $s$ trivial, degrading perceptual quality. Ho's $\lambda=\sigma^2$ upweights mid-noise, improving FID but losing MLE justification. Finding $\lambda^*(t)$ minimizing $W_2$ or perceptual LPIPS remains heuristic; $\lambda\propto1/E\|\nabla\log p_{t|0}\|^2$ used in EDM helps but no theory.

3. **Curse of Dimensionality for Distillation:** Single-step ideal transport $T:\mathcal{N}\to p_0$ for multi-modal $p_0$ is discontinuous (e.g., 2-moons). Lipschitz $v_\theta$ cannot approximate it without high complexity; neural net needs capacity exponential in number of modes. Understanding when 1-rectified flow can be continuous requires Alexandrov second-boundary problem regularity, generally failing.

4. **Evaluation Metrics Gap:** FID uses Inception-v3 trained on ImageNet, biased to texture, not semantic alignment; KID, FD-DINOv2, CLIP-Score improve but violate metric axioms (triangle inequality). No divergence exactly correlates with human judgment.

5. **Non-Euclidean and Discrete Extensions:** On $SO(3)$, $S^2$, or discrete $\{1\ldots K\}^d$, SDE becomes Brownian motion on manifold with Laplace-Beltrami score. Efficient estimators scale $O(K^2)$; score parametrization via logits suffers mode collapse. Riemannian flow matching promising but unproven beyond low dimensions.

6. **Convergence of Reflow to OT:** Liu et al. show monotonic cost decrease, but limit may be non-OT fixed point if coupling has atoms. Lee et al. 2024 argue one reflow sufficient when interpolation straight enough, but counterexample with crossing X-shaped data shows second reflow still curved. Characterization via $c$-cyclical monotonicity still open.

> **Open Problems:**
> - Does there exist $s_\theta$ with Lipschitz $L(T)=O(1/\sigma_{\min})$ guaranteeing $W_2(p_0,p_0^\theta)\le\epsilon$ with $NFE=O(\log(1/\epsilon))$? Or is curvature inevitable?
> - Bridging $R_\theta$ rectified flow with Schrödinger bridge: entropic OT interpolant $p_t\propto \int \exp(\ldots)$ yields SDE with extra drift, rectified flow is $\gamma\to0$ limit. Can we learn $\gamma$-interpolation adaptively for diversity-speed tradeoff?

## 7. Conclusion

We have traced a decade from equilibrium destroying [1] to learning to reverse [2] to continuous-time score dynamics [3] to straight transport [4]. Formally, all models minimize variations of denoising score matching:

$$\mathcal{L}(\theta)=E_{t,\lambda(t)}E_{x_0,x_t|x_0}\|s_\theta(x_t,t)-\nabla\log p_{t|0}\|^2$$

differing only in SDE choice $f,g$, weighting $\lambda(t)$, and sampler integration order. Anderson's reversal [8] furnishes existence of generative SDE provided scores regular; probability flow ODE converts stochasticity to determinism without changing marginals, unlocking exact likelihood; Karras' preconditioning [5] makes integration numerically stable; rectified flow [4] straightens the ODE, minimizing transport cost and enabling one-step synthesis.

Practically: VP-SDE with cosine schedule $\bar\alpha_t = \cos^2(\frac{\pi}{2}\frac{t+s}{1+s})$, $s=0.008$, Heun sampler, polynomial $\sigma(t)=t^\rho$, $\rho=7$, and one Langevin corrector per step provides strong baseline (FID 1.97 at 79 NFE). For latency-critical deployment, 2-rectified flow + distillation attains FID 4.2 at NFE 1.

Nevertheless, core fragilities remain: score estimates diverge near data singularities, memorization risk scales with model capacity, and single-step transport for complex multimodal distributions provably requires non-Lipschitz maps whose learning is statistically hard. Next frontier merges stochastic analysis (reflection couplings, log-Sobolev, Poincaré), geometric measure theory (rectifiability, currents), and optimal transport (Brenier potentials, Schrödinger bridges) to derive sampler-agnostic guarantees linking integrated score error to perceptual metrics, not just KL.

Diffusion thus exemplifies a broader principle transcending images: *injecting structured noise and learning its inversion yields a universal sampler whose bias is continuity in time, not architectural narrowness* — a principle now extending to proteins (AlphaFold 3), audio (Grad-TTS), and video (Sora) via latent diffusion on learned manifolds.

> *To generate is to remember how to forget, then to unforget in reverse, along the least curved path possible.*
