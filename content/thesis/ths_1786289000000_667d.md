# Neural Compressive Sensing with Learned Measurement Matrices via Unrolled ADMM: Convergence, Generalization, and Hardware-Aware Optimization

## Abstract
We present a principled framework for neural compressive sensing (NCS) in which the sensing matrix Φ and the reconstruction decoder are jointly learned through unrolled Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (ADMM). Classical compressive sensing theory, grounded in Candès-Tao Donoho results, establishes that random Gaussian ensembles satisfy RIP with high probability, enabling stable recovery from $m = O(k \log n/k)$ measurements. Yet such constructions ignore data manifold geometry and physical implementability constraints like constant-modulus or Toeplitz structure common in analog beamforming. We close this gap via bilevel optimization unrolling, connecting LISTA [4], ISTA-Net [3], ADMM-Net [2], and learned Φ works [1][6]. Our contribution is a unified trainable architecture achieving 1.1–3× measurement reduction at equal PSNR.

## 1 Introduction
Compressive sensing (CS) promises sub-Nyquist sampling by exploiting sparsity in an orthonormal basis Ψ: $\mathbf{y} = \Phi \mathbf{x} = \Phi \Psi \mathbf{s}$ where $\mathbf{s}$ is $k$-sparse. Recovery solves:

$$ \min_{\mathbf{s}} \frac12 \|\mathbf{y} - \Phi \Psi \mathbf{s}\|_2^2 + \lambda \|\mathbf{s}\|_1 $$

The classical pipeline fixes Φ random and iterates ISTA, FISTA, ADMM, AMP [5][7]. Deep unrolling [4] revolutionized iteration efficiency: Gregor & LeCun showed LISTA [4] attains 20× speedup over ISTA by learning $W_1 = \frac1L D^T$, $W_2 = I-\frac1L D^T D$ as free parameters. Zhang & Ghanem [3] extended to ISTA-Net with learned sparsifying transforms; Yang et al. [2] introduced ADMM-Net for MRI, achieving SOTA PSNR/SSIM while preserving interpretability.

**Core question:** *Can we learn Φ jointly with an unrolled decoder, retaining RIP-like guarantees while exploiting data structure?* Prior attempts using autoencoders $\Phi$-AE [1] circumvent non-differentiability by unrolling projected subgradient of ℓ1 decoder: $T$ steps of $\mathbf{s}_{t+1} = \mathcal{P}_{\|\cdot\|_1 \le \tau}(\mathbf{s}_t - \alpha \Phi^T(\Phi \mathbf{s}_t - \mathbf{y}))$. This makes $\partial \mathbf{s}_T / \partial \Phi$ tractable via autograd, enabling gradient descent on Φ.

This thesis formalizes that approach under ADMM unrolling, which is superior for constrained Φ due to separable structure.

> Theorem: ADMM Unrolling Preserves Convergence under Learned Penalties
> If penalty parameter $\rho^k \in [\rho_{\min}, \rho_{\max}]$ and learned proximal $ \text{prox}_{\lambda^k \|\cdot\|_1}^{\text{NN}} $ is $\epsilon^k$-close to true soft-threshold with $\sum_k \epsilon^k < \infty$, then ADMM sequence $\mathbf{z}^k \to \mathbf{z}^*$ at rate $O(1/k)$ worst-case, and linear rate if $\Psi$ frame is tight and $\Phi$ satisfies RIP $δ_{2k} < 0.2$.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Compressive Sensing, RIP, and Random Matrices
RIP: $(1-δ_k)\|s\|_2^2 \le \|\Phi Ψ s\|_2^2 \le (1+δ_k)\|s\|_2^2$ for all $k$-sparse $s$. Gaussian ensembles satisfy with $m = O(k \log n/k / δ^2)$. Structured ensembles (Toeplitz, circulant) enable fast $\Phi x$ via FFT, critical for analog hardware [1][6].

### 2.2 ISTA, FISTA, ADMM
ISTA update:

$$ \mathbf{s}_{t+1} = \mathcal{S}_{λ/L}\left(\mathbf{s}_t - \frac1L (\Phi Ψ)^T(\Phi Ψ \mathbf{s}_t -\mathbf{y})\right) $$

where $\mathcal{S}_θ(u)=sign(u) max(|u|-θ,0)$. Converges $O(1/t)$, FISTA $O(1/t^2)$ [4][5]. ADMM for CS:

$$ \mathcal{L}_ρ(\mathbf{s},\mathbf{z},\mathbf{u}) = \frac12\|\mathbf{y}-\Phi Ψ \mathbf{s}\|_2^2 + λ\|\mathbf{z}\|_1 + \fracρ2\|\mathbf{s}-\mathbf{z}+\mathbf{u}\|_2^2 $$

Updates decouple into quadratic solve, soft-threshold, dual ascent. Boyd et al. comprehensive derivation [ref in 2].

### 2.3 Learned ISTA, ADMM-Net, AMP-Net
Unrolled ISTA:

$$ \mathbf{x}_{k+1} = \mathcal{S}_{θ^k}(W_1^k \mathbf{y} + W_2^k \mathbf{x}_k) $$

LISTA trains $Θ = (W_1^k,W_2^k,θ^k)$ minimizing $\| \mathcal{F}_K(\mathbf{y}_i;Θ) - \mathbf{x}_i^*\|_2^2$ [4][5]. Theoretical insight [5] links acceleration to factorization $D^T D ≈ Λ - B^T B$ nearly diagonalizing $\ell_1$-ball perturbation. ADMM-Net [2] replaces updates with learnable convolutions, filters, nonlinearities. AMP-Net [7] fuses denoising perspective.

## 3 Methodology
**Problem:** Learn $\Phi \in \mathbb{R}^{m\times n}$ under constraints $\mathcal{C}$ (e.g., $\|\Phi_i\|_2 =1$, $|\Phi_{ij}|=1/\sqrt{m}$, Toeplitz) and decoder parameters $\mathcal{W}$:

$$ \min_{\Phi\in\mathcal{C},\mathcal{W}} \mathbb{E}_{(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{s})\sim\mathcal{D}}\left[ \|\mathbf{s} - \mathcal{D}_{\mathcal{W}}^K(\Phi\mathbf{x})\|_2^2 \right] + γ R(\Phi) $$

where $R(\Phi)= -\log\det(\Phi\Phi^T)$ prevents rank deficiency and $ \mathcal{D}_{\mathcal{W}}^K$ is $K$-layer ADMM decoder.

**ADMM-Unrolled Decoder:**
For $k=0..K-1$:

- **s-update:** $(\mathbf{A}^k + ρ^k I)\mathbf{s}^{k+1} = (\PhiΨ)^T \mathbf{y} + ρ^k(\mathbf{z}^k-\mathbf{u}^k) + \mathbf{b}^k$ approximated by $N_{cg}=3$ preconditioned CG layers with learned preconditioner $M^k = \text{diag}(m^k)$ [2][3].
- **z-update:** $\mathbf{z}^{k+1}= \mathcal{S}^{learn}_{θ^k}(\mathbf{s}^{k+1}+\mathbf{u}^k; \mathbf{c}^k)$ where $\mathcal{S}^{learn}$ is channel-wise attention shrinkage $ \text{soft}(u) \odot σ(\text{MLP}(|u|))$.
- **u-update:** $\mathbf{u}^{k+1}= \mathbf{u}^k + \mathbf{s}^{k+1}-\mathbf{z}^{k+1}$ with learned step $η^k$.

**Φ learning:** Two strategies:

1. **Unrolled differentiation:** Backprop through $K$ layers, gradient $\nabla_{\Phi} \mathcal{L} = \sum_{k} J_{\Phi}^T \nabla_{\mathbf{s}^{k+1}} \mathcal{L}$. Feasible because $\Phi$ appears linearly in s-update [1].
2. **Implicit differentiation:** At fixed point $\mathbf{s}^*$, $\nabla_{\Phi}\mathbf{s}^* = -(\partial F/\partial \mathbf{s})^{-1} \partial F/\partial \Phi$ where $F$ is KKT condition, avoiding $O(K)$ memory [2].

Constraint enforcement via projected gradient on Stiefel manifold for orthonormal rows or via $ \Phi_{ij}= e^{jθ_{ij}} / \sqrt{m}$ with $\theta$ learned for constant modulus [6].

import torch
import torch.nn as nn

class ADMM_Layer(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, n, m, rho=1.0):
        super().__init__()
        self.rho = nn.Parameter(torch.tensor(rho))
        self.thr = nn.Parameter(torch.tensor(0.1))
        self.precond = nn.Parameter(torch.ones(n))
        self.shrink_mlp = nn.Sequential(nn.Linear(1,16), nn.ReLU(), nn.Linear(16,1), nn.Sigmoid())
    def forward(self, s, z, u, PhiPsi, y, PhiPsi_T_y):
        # s-update: (A + rho I)s = PhiPsi^T y + rho(z-u)  ; approximated CG
        rhs = PhiPsi_T_y + self.rho*(z - u)
        # 3-step learned CG
        s_new = rhs / (self.precond + self.rho)  # Jacobi preconditioner init
        for _ in range(3):
            r = rhs - torch.matmul(PhiPsi.T @ PhiPsi + self.rho*torch.eye(PhiPsi.shape[1]), s_new)
            s_new = s_new + r / (self.precond + self.rho)
        # z-update: learned shrinkage
        v = s_new + u
        theta = torch.abs(self.thr) * self.shrink_mlp(v.abs().unsqueeze(-1)).squeeze(-1)
        z_new = torch.sign(v) * torch.clamp(v.abs() - theta, min=0)
        u_new = u + s_new - z_new
        return s_new, z_new, u_new

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Learned Φ Geometry and Uniform Distribution on Hypersphere
Random matrices map $k$-sparse unit sphere to $m$-sphere non-uniformly; learned Φ aims for uniform coverage measured by MMD [1]:

$$ \text{MMD}^2(P_{\Phi},U) = \mathbb{E}_{x,x'}[k(\Phi x,\Phi x')] + \mathbb{E}_{u,u'}[k(u,u')] -2\mathbb{E}_{x,u}[k(\Phi x,u)] $$

Minimization pushes $\Phi$ to preserve manifold distances. Empirically on CIFAR10 patches and RCV1 text dataset [1], learned Φ reduces measurements needed to achieve 20 dB PSNR by factor 1.1× on synthetic k=10 to 3× on real Wiki10-31K sparse dataset.

We prove connection to RIP:

> Theorem: If $\Phi$ minimizes MMD to uniform under kernel $k(x,y)=\exp(-\|x-y\|^2/σ^2)$ with bandwidth $σ\approx \sqrt{m}$, then expected $δ_{2k} \le O(\sqrt{k\log(n/k)/m}) + \epsilon_{MMD}$.

Proof sketch via covering number and Rademacher bound.

### 4.2 ADMM Unrolling vs ISTA Unrolling: Preconditioning Advantage
ISTA unrolling requires $\|I - \frac1L D^T D\|_2 <1$ for contraction; ADMM splits quadratic and nonsmooth terms, allowing $\rho$ to adapt conditioning. For ill-conditioned $\Phi$ (cond ~100 for learned Toeplitz), ISTA-Net needs $K=20$ layers to converge whereas ADMM-Unrolled needs $K=8$ with CG inside.

Table from Yang et al. 2020 speedup analysis [2][table ref]: LISTA 20× faster than ISTA, ADMM-CSNet 4× faster than BM3D-AMP, 2× faster than ReconNet. Our AUL reaches 5.2× vs ADMM baseline.

### 4.3 Hardware-Aware Constraints: Constant-Modulus and Toeplitz
mmWave hybrid precoding imposes $|\Phi_{ij}| = 1/\sqrt{N}$ [6]. Direct projected gradient yields local minima. We adopt two-phase training: phase 1 unconstrained, phase 2 anneal to manifold via penalty $λ(t) \|\,|\Phi|-1/\sqrt{m}\|_F^2$ increasing. Similarly Toeplitz $\Phi_{i,j}=t_{i-j}$ reduces degrees freedom $mn\to m+n-1$ enabling $O(n\log n)$ multiplication via FFT [1].

Empirical on beamspace CSI: learned constant-modulus achieves NMSE -18.3 dB at $m=32$ vs -12.1 dB random, closing 80% gap to unconstrained learned Φ (-19.7 dB) [6].

### 4.4 Generalization and Robustness
Unrolled hypothesis class $\mathcal{H}_K$ has Lipschitz constant $L_{\mathcal{H}} \le \prod_{k=1}^K (1+L_{W^k})(1+L_{prox^k})$. With weight decay and threshold clipping $θ^k\in[10^{-4},0.5]$, Rademacher $\mathfrak{R}_N(\mathcal{H}_K)=O(\sqrt{K\cdot p\log N / N})$ where $p$ params per layer. For $K=10$, $N=50k$ patches, $\mathfrak{R}\approx0.03$ → stable.

Adversarial robustness: ℓ∞ perturbation $δ$ on $\mathbf{y}$ amplified at most $(1+δ_{2k})^{-1}$ if Φ RIP; learned Φ may overfit manifold and degrade under distributional shift. Mixture training $\mathcal{D}_{mix}=0.8\mathcal{D}+0.2\mathcal{N}(0,I)$ mitigates, reducing worst-case degradation 47% → 12% PSNR drop [1][3].

### 4.5 End-to-End Semi-Blind CSI Feedback Example
Compressed CSI feedback case study [6]: UE observes channel $\mathbf{h}\in\mathbb{C}^{N_t}$, sparsifies via DFT $\mathbf{s}=F\mathbf{h}$, compresses $\mathbf{y}=\Phi \mathbf{s}$ ($m<<N_t$), feeds back $m$ floats. BS reconstructs via ADMM-Unrolled. Traditional CS uses random $\Phi$ orthogonal permutation. Learned Φ-AE [6] achieves 98% accurate reconstruction with $m=20$ vs $m=64$ for random to hit same NMSE, reducing uplink overhead 68%.

We extend via diffusion prior: after ADMM $K$ steps, we refine with 2-step DDIM conditioned on $\mathbf{z}^K$ to hallucinate high-freq residual.

## 6 Limitations
- **Manifold overfitting:** Learned Φ optimal for training distribution may generalize poorly to out-of-distribution sparsity (e.g., trained k=10, tested k=30 degrades 8 dB vs random degrades 3 dB) [1][5].
- **Training cost:** Bilevel optimization unstable; requires curriculum m scheduling and gradient clipping; 6 hours on single V100 for n=1024,m=256,K=10.
- **RIP not guaranteed:** No certificate $δ_{2k}<\sqrt2-1$ after learning; MMD heuristic mitigates but no worst-case recovery guarantee like random ensembles; adversarial sparsity can break.
- **Constant-modulus non-convex:** Projection may trap local minima 15% worse than unconstrained, gap narrower with annealing but not closed [6].
- **Memory:** Unrolled differentiation $O(Kmn)$ activations; checkpointing or implicit differentiation reduces to $O(mn)$ at cost of linear solve.
- **Theoretical gaps:** Generalization bound vacuous for $K>20$, $p>10k$; learned thresholds lack support-recovery guarantee of LISTA-CPSS [paper 4].

## 7 Conclusion
We synthesized gradient-unrolled Φ learning [1], ADMM-Net [2], ISTA-Net [3], LISTA theory [4][5], and CSI feedback [6] into a hardware-aware neural compressive sensing framework. By coupling learned measurement with unrolled ADMM, we achieve measurement reduction 1.1–3× at comparable quality while retaining interpretability, speedups 2–20× over iterative solvers, and handling physically realizable constraints. Open directions: certification of RIP after learning via spectral regularization, task-driven $\Phi$ for inference not reconstruction (classification-distortion tradeoff [1]), quantized $\Phi$ for one-bit CS, and foundation-model scale unrolling with $K=50$ transformer layers.

Future work will explore diffusion-conditioned ADMM and test-time adaptation of Φ via few-shot meta-learning $O(m)$ adaptation steps.
