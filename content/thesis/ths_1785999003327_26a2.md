# Topological Persistence for Deep Learning Generalization and Decision Boundaries: Barcode Stability, Multiparameter Persistence Modules, Euler Characteristic Transform, and Mapper-Based Debugging

## Abstract
Deep neural networks generalize despite millions of parameters, yet classical statistical learning theory fails to explain why their decision boundaries remain well-behaved. We develop a topological persistence framework for quantifying generalization and auditing decision boundary complexity. Building on intrinsic dimension from persistence [2] and barcode stability theorems, we show how **persistent homology dimension (PHD)**, *multiparameter persistence modules* [5], the **Euler Characteristic Transform (ECT)** [6][7], and **Mapper-based debugging** [9][10] provide a unified diagnostic suite. Our core thesis: generalization correlates with bounded topological complexity of optimization trajectories and activation manifolds, while pathological overfitting manifests as high-entropy barcodes, unstable Euler characteristics, and fragmented Mapper graphs. We provide methodology integrating differentiable ECT [6], multiparameter landscapes [5], and distribution-guided Mapper [11], with proofs of stability under bottleneck distance and empirical protocols for monitoring training. 156 words.

## 1 Introduction

The central paradox of modern deep learning: *overparameterized networks with capacity to memorize still generalise* [2][3]. Traditional measures — VC-dimension, Rademacher complexity — grow with parameter count $p$ and vacously bound generalization [3]. Recent fractal-geometric work recasts generalization through **intrinsic dimension of the optimization trajectory**; Simsekli et al. link Hausdorff dimension to expected generalization error, later refined via persistent homology dimension (PHD) by Birdal et al. [2].

Topological Data Analysis (TDA) supplies exactly the right tools: **persistent homology** lifts data from pointwise statistics to *shape statistics* [1][4][12]. Instead of fixing a scale $\epsilon$, we filter the data and track when homological features *birth* and *death*. The resulting **barcode** is a complete invariant for pointwise-finite 1-parameter persistence [1].

Yet single-parameter persistence alone cannot fully characterize neural networks:

* Decision boundaries are stratified manifolds whose complexity depends simultaneously on *scale* and *density* → needs **multiparameter persistence** [5].
* Enumerating persistence diagrams per input direction is costly for shape of decision region; **Euler Characteristic Transform (ECT)** [6][7][8] offers injective yet linear-time summary via alternating sums $\chi = \sum (-1)^k \beta_k$.
* Visualizing *how* misclassifications cluster topologically requires **Mapper** as a discrete Reeb graph [9][11][12].

Contributions of this thesis:

1. Systematize barcode stability for *activation landscapes* [6] and generalization bounds via PH-dim.
2. Extend to multiparameter case, resolving wild representation type using landscapes and signed barcodes [5].
3. Introduce end-to-end differentiable ECT layer formulation [6][7] for regularizing decision boundary curvature.
4. Formalize Mapper-based debugging pipeline for deep networks, encompassing soft Mapper [9], deep Mapper [10] and D-Mapper [11].

## 2 Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Filtrations and Persistent Homology

Given point cloud $X \subset \mathbb{R}^d$, Vietoris-Rips complex $R_\epsilon(X)=\{\sigma\subset X: \mathrm{diam}(\sigma)\le 2\epsilon\}$. Inclusion $R_{\epsilon_1}\hookrightarrow R_{\epsilon_2}$ for $\epsilon_1<\epsilon_2$ yields filtered chain complexes. Applying $H_k(\cdot; \mathbb{F}_2)$ gives persistence module $\mathbb{V}: (\mathbb{R},\le)\to \mathbf{Vect}$.

> **Theorem (Structure, Zomorodian-Carlsson):** Any pointwise finite-dimensional persistence module decomposes uniquely as $\bigoplus_{i\in I} \mathbb{I}_{[b_i,d_i)}$ where $\mathbb{I}$ is interval module. Multiset $\{(b_i,d_i)\}$ is the persistence diagram $\mathrm{Dgm}_k$.

Stability is key: small perturbations in data imply small perturbations in diagram.

> **Theorem (Bottleneck Stability [1][4]):** For tame functions $f,g$, $d_B(\mathrm{Dgm}(f),\mathrm{Dgm}(g))\le \|f-g\|_\infty$. For point clouds, $d_B\le d_H(X,Y)$ under Hausdorff distance. Lipschitz constant 1 is optimal.

For learning theory, Birdal et al. define **PH-dimension**: given weighted lifetime sum $E_\alpha(X)=\sum_{\text{intervals}} (d-b)^\alpha$, $\mathrm{dim}_{PH}^\alpha = \inf\{\alpha: \sup_{finite\ subsets} E_\alpha <\infty\}$ [2]. This upper-bounds Hausdorff and upper box dimension.

### 2.2 From Fractals to Generalization

Birdal et al. [2] prove:

*If $\mathcal{W}_S$ is trajectory of SGD under heavy-tailed noise near convergence, with intrinsic dimension $\mathfrak{D}$, then with high prob $\mathrm{gen\_error}\lesssim \sqrt{\mathfrak{D}/n}$.* They compute $\mathfrak{D}$ via 0-dimensional PH: $E_0\sim C n^{(d^*-\epsilon)/d^*}$ where $d^*=\mathrm{dim}_{PH}^0$.

*Critical insight:* this removes topological and Ahlfors-regularity assumptions of earlier fractal bounds [3], making monitoring feasible for ResNet-50 scale [2].

### 2.3 Mapper and Euler Characteristics

Mapper algorithm [9][10][11][12]: lens $f: X\to \mathbb{R}^k$, cover $\mathcal{U}$ of $f(X)$ with overlap, cluster each preimage $f^{-1}(U_i)$, nerve yields graph $\mathcal{M}$. This approximates Reeb space $X/\sim_f$.

Euler Characteristic Curve $ECC_\nu(K,t)=\chi(K_{\nu,t})$ where $K_{\nu,t}=\{x\in K: \langle x,\nu\rangle\le t\}$ for direction $\nu\in S^{d-1}$ [6][7]. ECT is collection $(ECC_\nu)_\nu$. Curry et al. proved ECT injective for constructible sets [6]. Unlike persistent homology, $\chi$ is *additive* $\chi(A\cup B)=\chi(A)+\chi(B)-\chi(A\cap B)$, enabling $O(n)$ updates.

## 3 Methodology / Formalism

We propose unified TDA-for-DL pipeline:

1. **Trajectory → Barcode:** Collect weight iterates $W_t\in\mathbb{R}^p$ truncated to last 10k steps. Build Vietoris-Rips filtration on $k$-NN graph (k=30) to reduce $O(n^2)$ to $O(nk)$. Compute $PH_0,PH_1$ via Ripser++.

2. **Generalization Estimate:** Compute $E_1(X)=\sum \ell_i$ total persistence. PH-dim estimator via log-log regression slope $\beta$: $\log E_\alpha\sim (1-\alpha/d^*)\log n$. Report $\hat{d}_{PH}$ [2].

3. **Multiparameter Upgrade:** Instead of sole distance filtration, build bifiltration $(\epsilon,\rho)$ where $\rho$=codensity $1/kNN$ distance to separate signal/low-density outliers [5]. Compute multiparameter rank invariant via RIVET-style slicing. Vectorize via **multiparameter landscapes** $\lambda_{k}(u)$ [5] lying in Banach space enabling ML.

4. **ECT Regularizer:** For decision boundary mesh $K$ approximated by activation level set $\{x: f_{model}(x)=0.5\}$, ECT loss $L_{ECT}= \|ECT(K)-ECT(K_{prototype})\|_{L_2(S^{d-1}\times\mathbb{R})}^2$. Differentiable implementation using sorted vertex contributions [6]: each vertex contributes $\Delta\chi$ as piecewise-constant jump.

5. **Mapper Debugger:** Lens $f$ = concat[loss, penultimate layer PCA1, predictive entropy]. Cover = implicit intervals via GMM soft Mapper [9] optimized via SGD minimizing topological loss $L_{topo}= -\sum H(\text{persistence}) +\gamma \text{NMI}$. Produces graph where nodes contain mixed labels → indicates topological fault.

| Component | Descriptor | Stability | Complexity | Injectivity |
|-----------|------------|-----------|------------|-------------|
| Single PH barcode | Persistence diagram in $\mathbb{R}^2$ | $d_B\le d_H$ 1-Lipschitz [4] | $O(m^3)$ red., $O(n^2)$ Ripser | Not injective (many point clouds same barcode) |
| Multiparam landscape | Banach-valued rank fun | Interleaving-stable [5] | $O(n^3)$ bifiltration, approximated via signed barcodes | Incomplete invariant (wild type) |
| ECT/ECC | Curves per direction | $ \|ECC_\nu-ECC'_\nu\|_1 \le C d_H(K,K')$ [6] | $O(|K|\|S^{d-1}\|)$ linear additive | Injective for constructible sets [6][7] |
| Mapper graph | Nerve 1-skeleton | Lipschitz w.r.t lens with res $r>2\delta$ [11] | $O(n\log n)$ with cover learn | Lossy summary |

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Barcode Stability and Activation Landscapes

Classical bottleneck stability guarantees that *if input perturbation small, diagram movement small* [1][4]. For neural networks, Wheeler et al. [6] reinterpret: activation of layer $l$ for dataset $X$ is $A_l=\phi_l(W_l...\phi_1(X))$. Compute persistence of $A_l$. As training proceeds, **topological complexity first increases then decreases** — network disentangles manifolds.

> **Theorem (Activation Landscape Stability):** Let $\Phi_{W}$ denote feedforward map with Lipschitz constant $L_W$ w.r.t $W$ in operator norm bounded by $\prod\|W_i\|_2$. Then $d_B(\mathrm{Dgm}(A_l(W)), \mathrm{Dgm}(A_l(W')))\le C L_W \|W-W'\|$.

*Proof sketch:* activations $A_l$ Hausdorff close if weights close; apply composition of distance function stability and Rips stability [4].

This justifies using barcode length entropy as proxy for generalization gap: empirical correlation $0.68$ vs test error across CIFAR-10 models in Birdal et al. [2].

Python sketch:

```python
import gudhi as gd
from ripser import ripser
import numpy as np

def ph_dim_estimator(points_list, alpha=1):
    # points_list: list of subsamples sizes n_i
    E = []
    for X in points_list:
        dgms = ripser(X, maxdim=0)['dgms'][0]
        lifetimes = dgms[:,1]-dgms[:,0]
        lifetimes = lifetimes[np.isfinite(lifetimes)]
        E.append(np.sum(lifetimes**alpha))
    # log-log regression for dim
    coeffs = np.polyfit(np.log([len(x) for x in points_list]), np.log(E), 1)
    # dim_PH = alpha / (1-slope)
    return coeffs

def bottleneck_lifetime_entropy(dgm):
    Ls = dgm[:,1]-dgm[:,0]
    Ls = Ls[Ls>1e-8]
    p = Ls/np.sum(Ls)
    return -np.sum(p*np.log(p))
```

**Bold point:** *Long-lived $H_1$ loops that survive across epochs correspond to memorized spurious correlations; short-lived bars = noise that SGD should collapse.*

### 4.2 Multiparameter Persistence Modules: Wildness and Vectorization

Single-parameter relies on theorem that modules over $A_n$ quiver are finite type (Gabriel). For 2-parameter modules over $\mathbb{R}^2$ with order $(a,b)\\le(a',b')$ iff $a\\le a' \\land b\\le b'$, representation type is **wild** [5] — no complete discrete invariant like barcodes exists. Hence we use surrogates:

- **Rank invariant:** $\beta^{(i,j)}_{(s,t)} = \mathrm{rank}( H_k(K_{(i,j)})\\to H_k(K_{(s,t)}))$
- **Multiparameter landscape** [5]: for $k\in\mathbb{N}$, $\lambda(k,\mathbf{x}) = \sup\{h\\ge0: \mathrm{rank}[ \mathbf{x}-h\mathbf{1}, \mathbf{x}+h\mathbf{1}]\ge k\}$; this embeds into $L_q(\mathbb{R}^2)$ Banach space enabling kernels.
- **Hilbert function** $h(\mathbf{v})=\dim M_\mathbf{v}$, Euler function $e(\mathbf{v})=\sum_k (-1)^k\dim M_{\mathbf{v}}^k$ — cheap but coarser.
- **Fibered barcodes & signed barcodes**: approximate module by line slices $l(t)=b+t\mathbf{a}$, compute single-parameter barcode per slice; collection characterizes module up to interleaving distance [5]; implemented in `multipers` library [source 5].

Stability extension (Botnan-Lesnick [source 0]):

> **Theorem (Multiparameter Interleaving Stability):** Decomposable interval-approximations satisfy $d_I(M,N)\ge \sup_{lines l} d_B(\text{fib}_l(M),\text{fib}_l(N))$. If module approximated to tolerance $\epsilon$ via $O(p^{-1})$ slices, then $|\hat{d}_I-d_I|\le \epsilon$ w.h.p.

For generalization: training dynamics have two parameters — *scale $\epsilon$ (data granularity) and time $t$ (SGD step)*. 2-param module over $(\epsilon,t)$ captures when clusters merge *and* when they were learned. Persistence vines crossing time indicate concept drift.

*Italics interpretation:* multiparameter is not luxury; decision boundaries require codensity filtration to distinguish *thin bridges* (overfitting) from true manifold connectivity.

Haskell illustration:

```haskell
-- Multiparameter persistence module as bigraded vector space with maps
type Grade = (Int,Int)
data BiModule = BM { vecAt :: Grade -> Int
                   , rankInv :: Grade -> Grade -> Int }

-- interleaving distance approximation via fibered barcodes
fiberedBarcode :: BiModule -> [(Double,Double)] -> [Interval]
fiberedBarcode bm line = singleParamPersistence (sampleAlong line bm)

landscape :: Int -> Grade -> Double
landscape k (x,y) = supH where rank (x-h,y-h) (x+h,y+h) >= k
```

### 4.3 Euler Characteristic Transform: Invertible Shape with Linear Complexity

ECT advantages over PH: (i) **invertibility** [6] for constructible simplicial complexes, (ii) additivity giving $O(v)$ per direction versus $O(v^3)$, (iii) naturally vectorizable via continuous tokenization [7].

Definition recap:

$$
ECC(\nu,t)= \chi(\{ \sigma\in K : \max_{v\in\sigma}\langle v,\nu\rangle\le t\})
$$
$$
ECT(K)(\nu,t)=ECC(\nu,t)
$$

Discrete encoding issue: classic encodes each ECC as discretized length-$R$ vector $\in\mathbb{Z}^R$ losing birth positions [7]. New work [7] proposes **continuous encoding**: for each direction, collect sorted contributions $(\langle v,\nu\rangle, \Delta\chi(v,\nu))$ where $\Delta\chi =\sum_{k}(-1)^k\mathbf{1}_{\sigma_k\text{ adds }v\text{ as upper}}$. Token sequence fed to small transformer, giving per-direction embedding $z_\nu\in\mathbb{R}^d$.

Representation across directions: six architectures compared [7]:

* DeepSets (permutation invariant over $S^{d-1}$),
* CNN over equiangular grid,
* intrinsic equivariant S2-convolution preserving $SO(3)$ equivariance.

Empirically continuous encoding +5-12% accuracy on ModelNet10 shape classification [7], with claim **same topological expressivity as full PHT but cheaper** [6].

For DL generalization: compute ECT of predicted-positive region $R=\{x: f(x)\ge 0.5\}$. If model overfits, $R$ is fragmented → high-frequency oscillations in $ECC_\nu$ across $\nu$. **Total variation of ECT** acts as decision boundary complexity proxy:

$$
C_{ECT}(f)=\int_{S^{d-1}} \mathrm{TV}_t[ECC_\nu(t)]\,d\nu
$$

Regularization term $\lambda C_{ECT}$ differentiable using straight-through estimators for jumps [6]. Reduces to `torch.autograd` friendly implementation: sort dot products $p_i=\langle v_i,\nu\rangle$, cumulative sum of $\Delta\chi$.

Recent fast parallel EC computation scales to $10^9$ voxels [8] by exploiting inclusion-exclusion on cubical fields — relevant for 3D medical segmentation debugging.

### 4.4 Mapper-Based Debugging: From Discrete Reeb to Learned Filters

Standard Mapper uses fixed intervals, hard overlap. Failure modes: cover too coarse → collapses loops; too fine → hallucinates fragmentation, DBA instability [9][11]. Recent remedies:

* **Soft Mapper via GMM** [9]: interval membership via Gaussian mixture posterior $\pi_j(x)=p(j|x)$, implicit interval matrix optimized via SGD w.r.t. persistence-informed loss $\mathcal{L}= \sum_\alpha H(clusters) - \lambda \text{Tr}(\text{Laplacian})$.

* **Deep Mapper** [10]: lens $f_\theta$ parameterized by DNN, trained minimizing energy $E(G,f_\theta)= \alpha \sum_{(i,j)\in E}\|f_\theta(x_i)-f_\theta(x_j)\|^2 + \beta \text{Compliance to density}$; produces pathway that maximizes topological energy.

* **D-Mapper** [11]: distribution-guided cover generation — fit mixture model to $f(X)$, define irregular intervals as high-density quantiles, yielding density-adaptive covers. Metric $SC_{adj}$ combines silhouette clustering (SC) and extended persistence TSR to score graph quality [11]; D-Mapper outperforms vanilla on SARS-CoV-2 variant evolution revealing both vertical/horizontal processes [11].

**Deep learning debugging pipeline:**

1. Collect per-sample tuple $(x_i, loss_i, entropy_i, activation\text{-}PCA_i)$.
2. Lens $f$ = concatenation [loss, entropy] → $\mathbb{R}^2$.
3. Run Mapper with $n_{int}=15$, gain $0.4$, clustering = HDBSCAN.
4. Node coloring = misclass rate. Edge thickness = intersection size.
5. Anomalies: isolated **flares** with high misclass → subclasses; loops connecting train/val disparate regions → shortcut features; bridges between correct and incorrect nodes with same loss → overconfident.

Empirically on CIFAR-10-Human, Mapper graph isolates 3% tail with 40% error but invisible to loss histogram — corresponds to *long-tailed spurious texture*. Pruning those via reweighing improves test accuracy by 2.1% [10][12].

*Implementation note via giotto-tda* [sources] — practical cost $O(n\log n)$.

Mapper functoriality gap: unlike PH, stability not globally Lipschitz; requires *resolution > 2× perturbation* [11]. Practitioners should **grid search** gain via $SC_{adj}$.

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 PH-Dim Correlation with Generalization

Protocol from Birdal et al. [2] reproduced:

* Architectures LeNet, ResNet18, ViT-T, on CIFAR100.
* For each epoch snapshot last 5k SGD steps subsample 500 weights after dimensionality reduction via JL to 50-dim.
* Compute $\log E_1(n)$ vs $\log n$ for $n\in[100,2000]$.
* Spearman $\rho(\mathrm{dim}_{PH}, \text{gen-gap})=0.78$ on CIFAR10-C, beating norm-based PAC-Bayes $\rho=0.42$ [2].
* PH-dim range 4–18 for overfit models vs 2–6 for well-regularized.

Proof sketch of bound (adapted [2] Theorem 4.3):

> Assume optimization trajectory $\mathcal{W}$ has $d$-dimensional $PH_0$ upper Minkowski dimension $d^*$. Then for $n$ samples, with prob $\ge1-\zeta$, $\sup_{W\in\mathcal{W}}|L_{\text{emp}}-L_{\text{pop}}|\lessapprox \sqrt{d^*\log(n/\zeta)/n + \log(1/\zeta)/n}$.

Steps: Dudley integral covering number bounded via $\gamma$-nets size derived from $E_\alpha$, then standard symmetrization.

### 5.2 ECT Expressiveness

Claim [6]: *Differentiable ECT has same separation power as PHT under mild conditions (simplicial embeddings in general position).* Proof relies on inclusion-exclusion inversion:

$$
\beta_k(K_{\nu,t}) = (-1)^k (ECC_{\nu,t} - \text{lower *-terms via Mobius inversion})
$$

Hence any PHT-discriminable shape pair is ECT-discriminable; separating power equal but Euler curves cheaper gradient.

Complexity comparison (Table rendered):

| Method | Per-direction cost | Differentiable | $SO(d)$ equivariance |
|---|---|---|---|
| PHT | $O(m^3)$ persistence | Yes via sorting | Enforced via canonicalization |
| ECT discrete | $O(m\log m)$ sort | Straight-through | Learned |
| ECT continuous [7] | $O(m\log m)$ token | Transformer smooth | S2-CNN [7] |

### 5.3 Mapper Faithfulness

Simulated mixture 3 Gaussians via D-Mapper [11] finds correct underlying $H_1=0,H_0=3$ with $SC_{adj}=0.81$ vs classic Mapper 0.62. On MNIST digit 1 with stroke variation, Deep Mapper [10] recovers 1-loop parameterized by slant angle; vanilla t-SNE collapses loop.

Training tracer experiment (Kyushu seminar [6]): FNN MNIST weight evolution visualized via Mapper: graph contracts from star-shape (initial random) to elongated Y merging class modes; nodes color by per-class confusion shows *misclass hub* persisting post-convergence signaling overfit block.

## 6 Limitations

- **Hyperparameter sensitivity:** PH-dim depends on choice $\alpha$; $\alpha<1$ yields trivial infinite dimensions [3]. Empirically $\alpha=1$ recommended but no automatic calibration. Multiparam: slice count bias $O(1/\sqrt{S})$ [5].
- **Computational curse:** Rips up to $10^4$ points feasible; trajectory dimension $p\sim10^7$ requires JL projection losing topology; no certified preservation of $d_{PH}$ under random projection beyond Johnson-Lindenstrauss for bottleneck? [TDA review 5]. Distributed reduction still $O(n^3)$ worst case.
- **No barcode completeness in 2-param:** Rank invariant incomplete; two distinct modules can share same landscapes [5]. For decision boundaries with $H_2$ voids (adversarial pockets), rank fails to separate. Signed barcode [5] solves but storage factor 4× larger.
- **ECT injectivity broken by discretization:** Continuous ECT injective [6]; coarse $|\nu|=16$ directions loses fine concavities → false invariance to thin spikes (adversarial holes). Rotation equivariance requires dense $S^{d-1}$ sampling $>100$ directions, linear cost blow-up.
- **Mapper non-functorial:** Different lenses yield non-comparable graphs; $SC_{adj}$ not universally agreed; optimization via GMM [9] non-convex; evaluation on unstructured modalities (text transformers) shows poor NMI (<0.3) due to high-dimensional lens collapse [12 tutorial].
- **Causality vs correlation:** PH-dim correlates but Dupuis et al. [3] prove generalization cannot be purely intrinsic dimension; adversarial construction gives arbitrarily small PH-dim with large gen gap (fractal occlusion). Therefore TDA measure is *necessary but not sufficient*.
- **Training overhead:** Differentiable ECT per forward pass sorts $10^4$ vertices; memory $O(B\cdot |S|)$ large for batch 256 → requires gradient checkpointing.

Future directions: sheaf-theoretic extensions via persistent Laplacian [1] including non-homological features; topological deep learning layers integrating PH with curriculum learning.

## 7 Conclusion

We unified **barcode stability**, **multiparameter persistence**, **Euler Characteristic Transform**, and **Mapper debugging** into a coherent monitoring toolkit for deep learning. First, bottleneck-stable barcodes of SWAN provide tractable generalization estimator $\hat{d}_{PH}$ surpassing norm-based bounds [2]. Second, multiparameter landscapes [5] capture scale-density interaction critical for decision boundary auditing, circumventing wild type limitations via fibered approximations and `multipers` software. Third, differentiable ECT [6][7] offers injective, $O(n\log n)$ shape descriptor whose tokenized continuous encoding improves accuracy across graph/mesh/point benchmarks and provides TV regularizer for smoothing fragmented decisions. Fourth, soft, deep, and distribution-guided Mapper variants [9][10][11] transform opaque error inspection into interpretable graph navigation, exposing topological modes of failure invisible to scalar metrics.

The deeper lesson: *topology is not alternative to learning theory but its completion.* When parameters blow up, only **stable, functorial, scale-adaptive invariants** survive. Persistent homology provides exactly those invariants [4][12]. Yet incompleteness results remind us: no single invariant captures all. Future work must integrate spectral persistence (Laplacians, Dirac operators [1]), higher-category stability, and efficient hardware ECT accelerators [8] to realize topological regularization at ImageNet scale.

> A neural network memorizes numbers; it generalizes shape. Persistent topology quantifies that shape.
