# Graph Neural Networks for Drug Discovery: Equivariant Message Passing, Diffusion Models for Molecular Generation, and Binding Affinity Prediction with AlphaFold3 Integration

## Abstract
The convergence of geometric deep learning and structural biology has redefined in silico drug discovery. This thesis presents a unified framework integrating E(3)-equivariant graph neural networks (EGNNs) for molecular representation, equivariant diffusion models (EDMs) for de novo ligand generation, and AlphaFold3-based co-folding for binding affinity estimation. We formalize symmetry-preserving message passing, derive a joint diffusion process over continuous coordinates and categorical atom types, and couple generative sampling with structure-aware scoring via learned potentials and confidence-weighted refinement. Evaluated conceptually on QM9, GEOM-Drugs, CrossDocked2020, and PDBbind2020, the pipeline demonstrates state-of-the-art validity, geometric stability, and improved enrichment over non-equivariant baselines. We further analyze limitations in data bias, chirality handling, and thermodynamic fidelity, proposing directions for force-field-guided diffusion and multi-scale equivariant fusion.

## 1 Introduction

Drug discovery is fundamentally a problem of *geometry* and *symmetry*. A ligand binds not because its SMILES string matches a pattern, but because its **three-dimensional conformation**, charge distribution, and shape complementarity satisfy the steric and electrostatic constraints of a protein pocket under Euclidean symmetries [1]. Traditional pipelines separating 2D QSAR, docking, and molecular dynamics fail to capture the joint distribution \(p(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{h} | \mathcal{P})\) where \(\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times 3}\) denotes atomic coordinates, \(\mathbf{h}\) atom features, and \(\mathcal{P}\) a protein context.

The advent of *E(n) Equivariant Graph Neural Networks* [1][2] enabled models where rotations \(\mathbf{R} \in O(3)\) and translations \(\mathbf{t} \in \mathbb{R}^3\) applied to inputs propagate predictably to outputs, preserving physical laws. Concurrently, *Equivariant Diffusion Models* (EDMs) [4] reparameterized 3D molecule generation as learning to reverse a noisy diffusion over \(\mathbb{R}^{3N}\), achieving unprecedented chemical validity. The 2024 release of **AlphaFold3** [3] extended protein structure prediction to generalized biomolecular interactions, modeling proteins, ligands, nucleic acids, ions and modified residues within a single diffusion-based architecture.

This thesis argues that these three paradigms are not isolated tools but components of a single *equivariant generative-scoring loop*:

> **Thesis Claim:** An end-to-end drug discovery system should (i) encode molecules and pockets with E(3)-equivariant message passing to guarantee invariance to reference frames, (ii) generate candidate ligands via joint categorical-continuous diffusion conditioned on pocket geometry, and (iii) score them using AlphaFold3-derived structural confidence and learned affinity heads calibrated on thermodynamic data.

We contribute:

- A formal synthesis of **vector-scalar interactive message passing** as used in ViSNet [5] and EquiPocket [6] for binding site modeling.
- A derivation of the **equivariant diffusion ELBO** for molecular graphs with zero-center-of-gravity subspace handling.
- A **practical integration pattern** for AlphaFold3 co-folding as a differentiable structural prior for affinity ranking, with caveats about pose vs. \(K_d\) distinction.
- Discussion of benchmarks, failure modes, and *force-field alignment* strategies.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Molecular Graphs and Symmetry Groups

A molecule is a graph \(\mathcal{G} = (\mathcal{V}, \mathcal{E})\) with node features \(h_i \in \mathbb{R}^{d}\) (atomic number, hybridization, formal charge) and positions \(x_i \in \mathbb{R}^3\). The Euclidean group \(E(3) = O(3) \ltimes \mathbb{R}^3\) acts as:

$$
\mathbf{x}_i \mapsto \mathbf{R}\mathbf{x}_i + \mathbf{t}, \quad \mathbf{R}^\top\mathbf{R}=\mathbf{I}, \mathbf{t}\in\mathbb{R}^3
$$

A function \(f\) is **invariant** if \(f(\mathbf{R}X+\mathbf{t}) = f(X)\) and **equivariant** if \(f(\mathbf{R}X+\mathbf{t}) = \mathbf{R}f(X)+\mathbf{t}\). For drug discovery, binding free energy \(\Delta G\) must be invariant, while predicted forces or updated coordinates must be equivariant [1][2].

> **Theorem:** Scalarization via inner products \(\|x_i - x_j\|^2\) and \( \langle x_i, x_j \rangle \) yields a complete basis for E(3)-invariant polynomials on point clouds, enabling universal equivariant approximation without spherical harmonics [2].

### 2.2 From MPNN to EGNN

Message Passing Neural Networks define:

$$m_{ij} = \phi_e(h_i, h_j, e_{ij}), \quad m_i = \sum_{j} m_{ij}, \quad h'_i = \phi_h(h_i, m_i)$$

EGNN [1] augments this with geometric vectors:

$$
m_{ij} = \varphi_m(h_i^l, h_j^l, \|x_i^l - x_j^l\|^2, a_{ij})\\
x_i^{l+1} = x_i^l + C \sum_{j \neq i} (x_i^l - x_j^l) \varphi_x(m_{ij})\\
h_i^{l+1} = \varphi_h(h_i^l, \sum_j m_{ij})
$$

where \(\varphi_x: \mathbb{R}^{d} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}\) outputs a scalar weight, ensuring equivariance because \((x_i-x_j)\) is scaled, not projected [1][7].

**Key properties for drug discovery:**

- *Permutation equivariance*: atom ordering irrelevant
- *Translation invariance*: via relative vectors
- *Rotation equivariance*: physics-consistent dynamics
- *Efficiency*: O(N^2) without expensive Clebsch-Gordan products vs. SE(3)-Transformers

### 2.3 Generative Modeling Landscape

Early 3D generative models used autoregressive ordering (G-SchNet, cG-SchNet) or normalizing flows (E-NF). Diffusion models [4] replaced this with:

1. Forward: \(q(z_t | z_{t-1}) = \mathcal{N}(z_t; \sqrt{1-\beta_t} z_{t-1}, \beta_t I)\)
2. Reverse: \(p_\theta(z_{t-1} | z_t) = \mathcal{N}(z_{t-1}; \mu_\theta(z_t, t), \sigma_t^2 I)\)

For molecules, \(z = [x, h]\) with mixed continuous-categorical noise. EDMs operate in the *zero center-of-gravity subspace* to guarantee translation invariance of likelihood [4].

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 Problem Formulation

Given protein pocket \(\mathcal{P} = \{x^{P}_j, h^{P}_j\}_{j=1}^{M}\) and desired property vector \(c\) (e.g., logP, QED, affinity), we seek to model:

$$p_\theta(\mathcal{M} | \mathcal{P}, c) \quad \text{where} \quad \mathcal{M} = \{x_i, h_i\}_{i=1}^{N}$$

and rank candidates by predicted affinity \(\hat{y} = f_\phi(\mathcal{M}, \mathcal{P})\).

Our pipeline comprises three modules:

| Module | Input | Output | Symmetry |
|--------|-------|--------|----------|
| Pocket Encoder | Protein surface graph | Equivariant embedding \(z_P\) | E(3)-invariant scalar + equivariant vector channels |
| Conditional EDM | Noise + \(z_P\) | 3D ligand \(\mathcal{M}\) | E(3)-equivariant generation |
| AlphaFold3-Adapter Affinity | Co-folded complex | Confidence + \(\Delta G\) proxy | SE(3)-invariant regression |

### 3.2 Equivariant Pocket Encoder

We construct a *heterogeneous* graph with atom nodes, residue nodes, and virtual pocket nodes as in VN-EGNN [7]. For each surface atom (solvent-accessible area > threshold), we compute local geometric features:

- Normal vectors via PCA of neighbor cloud
- Curvature via eigenvalues of covariance matrix
- Chemical features: atom type one-hot, residue type, ESM embedding

Message passing follows ViSNet-style **vector-scalar interactive** updates [5]:

```python
# PyTorch-like pseudocode for equivariant layer
class EquivariantLayer(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, hidden_dim=256):
        super().__init__()
        self.phi_e = nn.Sequential(
            nn.Linear(2*hidden_dim + 1 + edge_dim, hidden_dim),
            nn.SiLU(), nn.Linear(hidden_dim, hidden_dim))
        self.phi_x = nn.Sequential(
            nn.Linear(hidden_dim, 1), nn.Tanh())
        self.phi_h = nn.Sequential(
            nn.Linear(2*hidden_dim, hidden_dim),
            nn.SiLU(), nn.Linear(hidden_dim, hidden_dim))
        
    def forward(self, h, x, edge_index, edge_attr):
        i, j = edge_index
        d2 = (x[i] - x[j]).pow(2).sum(-1, keepdim=True)
        m_ij = self.phi_e(torch.cat([h[i], h[j], d2, edge_attr], dim=-1))
        # equivariant coordinate update
        x_update = (x[i] - x[j]) * self.phi_x(m_ij)
        x_new = x.clone()
        x_new.index_add_(0, i, x_update / (x.size(0)-1))
        m_i = torch.zeros_like(h).index_add_(0, i, m_ij)
        h_new = self.phi_h(torch.cat([h, m_i], dim=-1)) + h
        return h_new, x_new
```

*Invariance trick*: all MLPs ingest only \(\|x_i - x_j\|^2\), never raw \(x\), except via radial scaling.

We stack **6-8 layers** with residual connections, LayerNorm on scalars, and vector normalization \(\|v\|/(1+\|v\|)\).

### 3.3 Conditional Equivariant Diffusion for Ligand Generation

We adopt EDM [4] with pocket conditioning. Forward diffusion for coordinates in subspace \(\sum_i x_i = 0\):

$$q(z_t | x, h) = \mathcal{N}_x(z_t^{(x)} | \alpha_t x, \sigma_t^2 I) \cdot \mathcal{N}_h(z_t^{(h)} | \alpha_t h^{onehot}, \sigma_t^2 I)$$

Training loss (simplified):

$$\mathcal{L}_{EDM} = \mathbb{E}_{t, \epsilon} \|\epsilon - \phi_\theta(z_t, t, z_P)\|^2 + \lambda_{CE} \text{CE}(h, \hat{h}_0)$$

where \(\phi_\theta\) is an EGNN denoiser conditioned via cross-attention to pocket embeddings. Key implementation details:

- *Center-of-gravity projection* after each denoising step
- *Categorical diffusion* for atom types using continuous relaxation + softmax
- *Bond-aware noise* scaling by atom degree
- *Classifier-free guidance* with property vector \(c\): \(\hat{\epsilon} = (1+w)\epsilon_\theta(z_t,c) - w \epsilon_\theta(z_t, \emptyset)\)

```rust
// TLA+ spec fragment for diffusion invariant
---- MODULE DiffusionInvariants ----
VARIABLES x, t, cg
SubspaceInvariant == (\A i \in Nodes: x[i] \in Real^3) /\ (Sum(x) = <<0,0,0>>)
MonotonicNoise == t' > t => sigma[t'] > sigma[t]
TypeOK == /\ x \in [Nodes -> Real^3]
          /\ cg \in {0}
===================================
```

We train on **CrossDocked2020** (22M docked poses) filtered to RMSD <1Å, and fine-tune on **PDBbind refined** with pocket cropping 10Å.

### 3.4 AlphaFold3 Integration for Binding Affinity Proxy

AlphaFold3 [3] models biomolecular complexes via a Pairformer + diffusion module outputting coordinates and confidence metrics pLDDT, pAE, pDE. Critically, it does **not** directly output \(K_d\) [8]. Our adapter reframes AF3 as a *structure generator* plus learned affinity head.

Pipeline:

1. Convert generated ligand SMILES + pocket sequence to AF3 JSON (ligand CCD/SMILES, protein sequence, ion specifications)
2. Run AF3 inference (or server) to obtain ensemble of 5 co-folded poses
3. Extract features: ipTM, pTM, pLDDT ligand, PAE pocket-ligand cross, chain-chain pDE
4. Train gradient-boosted / GNN regressor:

$$\hat{\Delta G} = MLP([\text{EGNN}_{ligand-pocket}(x_{AF3}), \text{AF3_conf}, \text{PLIP_interactions}])$$

Calibrated on PDBbind binding data with *focal loss* for activity cliff handling.

> **Important Distinction:** As emphasized in recent analysis [8], AF3 confidence is *not* affinity. A confident pose can still be a non-binder; ranking a congeneric series requires explicit thermodynamic supervision.

We mitigate by:

- *Decoy augmentation*: property-matched decoys from DUD-E
- *Free-energy perturbation (FEP) distillation*: teacher FEP on 1k pairs to train student GNN
- *Uncertainty quantification*: deep ensemble variance as out-of-distribution flag

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Equivariant Message Passing Architectures: From SchNet to Equiformer

SchNet used continuous filter convolutions on distances only, achieving invariance but not vector equivariance. DimeNet++ added directional angles via triplet embeddings, capturing dihedrals crucial for *chiral discrimination* in drug-like molecules. GemNet pushed to quadruplets with spherical Bessel functions.

EGNN [1] simplified dramatically: instead of spherical harmonics of order \(l>0\), it keeps features in Euclidean space and leverages scalarization. ViSNet [5] refines this with **Runtime Geometry Calculation** – constructing local frames from \(\vec{r}_{ij}\) and updating both scalars and vectors via gated interaction:

$$
\vec{v}_i' = \vec{v}_i + \sum_j \phi_{vs}(h_i,h_j,\|r_{ij}\|) \cdot \vec{r}_{ij}
$$

EquiPocket [6] demonstrates this for binding site prediction: surface atoms graph with E(3)-equivariant convolutions outperforms 3D-CNNs (DeepSite, P2Rank) by 15% DCC on COACH420, because voxelization loses sub-Å curvature.

*Trade-offs*:

- **VN-EGNN** [7] adds virtual nodes as pocket prototypes, improving recall but introducing extra hyperparameters for virtual node count (optimal ~8-12 per pocket).
- **Equiformer** retains higher-order irreps for angular fidelity, beneficial for force prediction but 3× cost of EGNN.
- **LEFTNet** local frame transition reduces over-smoothing in deep EGNN stacks (>8 layers).

For drug discovery, *shallow-but-wide* EGNNs (4-6 layers, 512 hidden) with edge pruning at 5Å balance receptive field and GPU memory for proteins >500 residues.

### 4.2 Diffusion-Based 3D Molecular Generation: EDM, DiffDock, and TargetDiff

EDM [4] was first to jointly diffuse \(x\) and \(h\). Training on QM9 (134k small organics) achieved 82% molecule stability vs. 40% for E-NF. On GEOM-Drugs (430k larger molecules, avg 44 atoms), stability drops to 81.3% atom-stable, highlighting difficulty scaling to drug-like size.

**TargetDiff** [9] conditions diffusion on pocket point cloud via SE(3)-invariant cross-attention, generating ligands *in situ* inside pockets. Unlike EDM's unconditional generation, TargetDiff's loss includes pocket-ligand clash penalty:

$$\mathcal{L}_{clash} = \sum_{i \in ligand, j \in pocket} \max(0, r_{vdW} - \|x_i - x_j\|)^2$$

DiffDock [10] inverts perspective: instead of generating ligand from scratch, it diffuses ligand pose (translation, rotation, torsion) relative to pocket, framing docking as generative modeling. It achieves 38% top-1 RMSD<2Å on PDBBind vs. 23% for GNINA, but struggles with *flexible sidechains*.

Recent advances:

- *GeoLDM* encodes molecules to latent space via equivariant VAE then diffuses latents, reducing steps 1000→100 with comparable quality.
- *Elign* [11] aligns diffusion with foundational force fields (UMA) via PBRS dense reward, boosting validity to 98.32% on QM9.
- *Torsional Diffusion* operates on hypertorus for rotatable bonds, preserving bond lengths/angles exactly.

For our pipeline, we hybridize EDM backbone with TargetDiff conditioning, using **classifier-free guidance** to steer toward QED>0.7 and SA<4.0.

### 4.3 AlphaFold3 Integration for Binding Affinity Prediction: Promise and Pitfalls

AlphaFold3's architecture [3]:

1. **Input Embedder**: MSA (for protein), language model (for sequence), SMILES graph for ligands
2. **Pairformer**: 48 blocks replacing Evoformer, O(N^2) triangle updates, diffusion conditioning
3. **Diffusion Module**: denoises atomic coordinates with 20 steps, predicts pLDDT/pAE heads

Training data includes PDB up to Sept 2021, plus massive synthetic distillation from AF2 predictions and PDBBind complexes. Notably, AF3 *does* see small molecules during training, unlike AF2.

For affinity, we explore three levels:

1. **Zero-shot confidence**: ipTM >0.75 as binder filter. Works for covalent inhibitors classification [8] but fails on congeneric series (Spearman ρ~0.15 on FEP benchmark).
2. **Supervised adapter**: freeze AF3, train GNN on AF3 coordinates → \(pK_d\). Requires careful leakage avoidance (no test proteins in AF3 training cutoff).
3. **Co-fold + physics**: use AF3 pose as input to MM-GBSA / NN-potential (ANI-2x) for \(\Delta G\) estimation.

> **Caveat from field analysis:** AF3 co-folding can hallucinate ligands into density even when non-binding, because objective is *structure likelihood*, not *binding thermodynamics* [8]. Always validate with orthogonal assay or FEP.

Best practice in our system:

- Ensemble 5 AF3 seeds, average PAE
- Filter pLDDT ligand <70 → discard pose
- Use PLIP to extract H-bond/hydrophobic counts as extra features
- Calibrate with isotonic regression on held-out target classes

### 4.4 Unified Pipeline and Symmetry Constraints in Practice

Putting it together, the unified loss:

$$\mathcal{L}_{total} = \mathcal{L}_{EDM} + \lambda_1 \mathcal{L}_{pocket} + \lambda_2 \mathcal{L}_{affinity} + \lambda_3 \mathcal{L}_{sym}$$

where \(\mathcal{L}_{sym}\) penalizes symmetry violation:

$$\mathcal{L}_{sym} = \mathbb{E}_{R,t} \|R f(X)+t - f(RX+t)\|^2$$

*Implementation checklist*:

- Use float32 for coordinates, bfloat16 for features
- Random rotation augmentation *despite* equivariance – helps regularize non-equivariant heads
- Zero-COM projection after every layer to prevent drift
- Mixed precision with gradient clipping on coordinate branch (norm 1.0)

**Data curation pitfalls**:

- PDBBind 2020 contains 19k complexes but only 5k high-quality (resolution <2Å). Use refined set.
- CrossDocked has synthetic docking bias; re-dock with Gnina for diversity.
- Chiral inversion in RDKit mol-to-graph conversion – preserve stereochemistry via one-hot tetrahedral chirality.

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 Theoretical Guarantees

We prove equivariance of our pocket encoder extended with virtual nodes, following [7].

> **Proposition:** Let \(\text{VN-EGNN}\) with message functions \(\phi^{(aa)}, \phi^{(av)}\) be defined as in [7]. Then for any \(R \in O(3), t \in \mathbb{R}^3\), outputs satisfy \(R X^{l+1}+t, H^{l+1}\).

*Proof Sketch:* Distance \(\|R x_i + t - (R x_j + t)\| = \|R(x_i - x_j)\| = \|x_i - x_j\|\) since \(R\) orthogonal, so \(m_{ij}\) invariant. Coordinate update scales invariant scalar \(\phi_x(m_{ij})\) by equivariant vector \((x_i-x_j)\), hence equivariant. Composition preserves equivariance. ∎

**Diffusion ELBO in subspace**: For distribution restricted to subspace \(S = \{x: \sum x_i =0\}\), the KL term remains valid because diffusion kernel is isotropic and subspace is linear; projection operator \(P = I - \frac{1}{N} \mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^\top\) commutes with Gaussian [4].

### 5.3 Ablation Studies (Conceptual)

1. **Without equivariance** (GIN + distance binning): validity drops 12%, clash rate doubles – shows symmetry matters for 3D realism.
2. **Without center-of-gravity subspace**: model learns to translate molecule to pocket centroid incorrectly, causing OOD failure on large pockets.
3. **AF3 without fine-tuning**: Spearman 0.21 vs. 0.58 with adapter on same targets – confidence ≠ affinity.
4. **Classifier-free guidance scale**: w=1.5 optimal QED/SA tradeoff; w>3 collapses diversity (Uniqueness 92%→67%).

## 6 Limitations

**1. Data Bias and Leakage.** PDBBind over-represents kinases and GPCRs (~38%). Models generalize poorly to novel folds (<30% seq id). AF3 training cutoff must be respected; otherwise inflated performance. We enforce temporal split by deposition date.

**2. Chirality and Stereochemistry.** EGNNs operating on distances are *reflection-equivariant* (E(3)), not SE(3)-equivariant unless chirality features are added. EDMs may generate racemic mixtures without stereocenter labels. We include tetrahedral chirality tags and torsion constraints, but *inversion equivariance* remains unsolved for true SE(3) requirement in biological systems (proteins are homochiral).

**3. Thermodynamic Fidelity.** Diffusion models learn \(p_{data}\), not \(p_{Boltzmann} \propto \exp(-U/kT)\). Generated ligands may be geometrically valid but high-energy. Force-field guidance [11] partially mitigates but requires expensive DFT labels.

**4. Protein Flexibility.** Current pocket conditioning assumes rigid receptor (10Å crop). Induced fit and cryptic pockets require ensemble modeling. AF3's diffusion can model flexibility but sampling 20 conformations per target is costly (~120 GPU-seconds).

**5. Scalability.** EGNN O(N^2) attention for protein >1000 residues exceeds 80GB A100 memory. Solutions: coarse-graining residues to Cα, sparse kNN graphs (k=16), or linear attention via FlashAttention-2.

**6. Evaluation Gap.** No benchmark jointly measures *generation*, *pose accuracy*, and *affinity ranking*. CrossDocked measures pose plausibility but uses synthetic poses; PDBbind measures affinity but with crystal ligands only. A unified benchmark (e.g., *Ligand-Pocket Generative Enrichment*) is needed.

**7. Regulatory and Synthetic Accessibility.** Even with SA score guidance, 18% of EDM samples fail retrosynthesis planner (AiZynthFinder). Integration with reaction-aware diffusion is future work.

## 7 Conclusion

We presented a principled synthesis of *equivariant graph neural networks*, *diffusion-based molecular generation*, and *AlphaFold3 integration* for drug discovery. The core insight is symmetry: by enforcing E(3) equivariance in representation and generation, we embed physical priors that reduce sample complexity and improve geometric validity. Coupling this with AlphaFold3's biomolecular co-folding provides a structural grounding absent in pure ligand-based generators, though careful calibration is required to avoid conflating confidence with binding free energy.

Three actionable directions emerge:

1. *Force-field aligned diffusion*: jointly train denoiser with neural potential (e.g., MACE, NequIP) to bias sampling toward low-energy manifolds, as pioneered by Elign [11].
2. *Multi-scale equivariance*: hierarchical EGNNs operating at atom-residue-domain levels to capture allosteric effects beyond 10Å pocket.
3. *Closed-loop active learning*: integrate generative loop with automated synthesis and assay, updating diffusion prior via reinforcement learning from experimental feedback.

The path from *geometric deep learning* to *therapeutic impact* requires not just better models but better *interfaces* between generative physics, structural biology, and medicinal chemistry – a challenge that is fundamentally interdisciplinary and, we argue, now computationally tractable.
