Transcript hash binding includes HRR in transcript via RFC 8446 §4.4.1 preventing cross-protocol confusion with 128-bit security. Limitations include ML-KEM failure rate 2^-139 requiring retry, HRR amplification DoS, and ECH padding 14% overhead.
