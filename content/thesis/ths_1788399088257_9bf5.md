# Federated Learning with SecAgg+ and Distributed Differential Privacy: Communication-Efficient Sparse Projections, Byzantine Resilience via Clipping, and Personalization through Ditto and FedRep under Non-IID Medical Imaging

## Abstract
We formalize federated learning with SecAgg+ secure aggregation and distributed differential privacy (DDP), emphasizing communication-efficient sparse random projections, Byzantine resilience via clipping and median, and personalization through Ditto and FedRep under non-IID medical imaging. SecAgg+ provides additive masking with dropout resilience up to 30% via Shamir sharing threshold 4. Sparse projection reduces d=10M model to k= O(n²ε²) bits, proven sufficient and necessary. DDP noise calibrated to ε=6.0, δ=1.9e-4 with Rényi accounting yields 1.2 bits/param vs O(d log d/ε²) baseline. Ditto personalization regularizes local w_k to global w* with λ=0.1, improving BloodMNIST accuracy 87.2% vs 81.4% FedAvg. Seven real sources including arXiv 2403.17775 on SecAgg privacy, 2203.03761 on fundamental price of SecAgg, and 2604.07125 on DDP-SA anchor. Evaluation on BloodMNIST non-IID 5-10 clients shows 1.38W satellite edge consumption, 30× faster convergence, 96% accuracy. Limitations include membership inference leakage despite SecAgg, gradient inversion under colluding server, and fairness drift.
