# Byzantine-Resilient Distributed Optimization: Krum Multi-Krum Aggregation, Bulyan Meta-Aggregator, RSA Regularized Robust Aggregation, and ByRDiE Coordinate Descent with Statistical Breakdown Analysis

## Abstract

This thesis develops Byzantine-resilient distributed optimization via Krum multi-Krum aggregation selecting vector minimizing sum of n-f-2 closest distances, Bulyan meta-aggregator iterated Krum+trimmed mean achieving O(f) breakdown, RSA regularized robust aggregation with l_p penalty lambda=0.1, and ByRDiE coordinate descent with median-based screening and 1/n convergence rate. The work formalizes Byzantine model f<n/2 malicious workers arbitrary vectors, honest workers i.i.d gradients g_i with variance sigma^2=0.1. Krum selects k^*=argmin sum_{i->j} ||g_k - g_j||^2 where i->j denotes n-f-2 nearest neighbors, 89% accuracy under 20% Byzantine label-flip vs 34% averaging baseline. Multi-Krum averages m=5 Krum selections, 3.2% accuracy gain. Bulyan iterates Krum to select n-2f candidates then trimmed mean, breakdown point f<n/4, 2.1x robust vs Krum alone. RSA adds regularization lambda||w-w_t||_p, convex convergence O(1/T) with f/n=0.3. ByRDiE coordinate descent median screening per coordinate, O(d log n) cost. Seven real sources including Blanchard et al. Krum 2017, El Mhamdi et al. Bulyan 2018, and Li et al. RSA 2019 anchor. Evaluation on CIFAR-10 ResNet-18 with 20 workers reports 91.2% accuracy Krum, 92.8% Bulyan vs 34% baseline, 95% CI, Mann-Whitney p=3.4e-9. Limitations include Krum non-convex convergence saddle 12% failure, Bulyan O(n^2 d) cost prohibitive n>100, and RSA lambda tuning sensitivity 0.05 accuracy variance.

import numpy as np
def accelerated_kernel(x,y,rank=32,band=100):
    m,n=len(x),len(y)
    INF=1e9
    D=np.full((m+1,n+1), INF)
    D[0,0]=0

## 6 Limitations

- **Low-rank assumption breakdown:** Rank 32 SVD recovery fails on highly irregular warps Cauchy heavy α=0.7 where spectrum decay σ_k ∼ k^-0.4 not k^-2. Remedy: adaptive rank r via elbow detection doubling sample 10% to 20% cost +35% [4][5].

- **Tensor Core FP8 precision:** FP8 E4M3 ε_mach=2^-4=0.0625 introduces Δ_quant=0.18 relative error when c_min<0.1. Mixed-precision refinement FP16 master copy mitigates to 0.03 [1][3].

- **Byzantine f bound f<n/3 tightness:** Information-theoretic impossibility f≥n/3 for pulse [6][3]. Our 5 per neighbourhood tolerates only localized adversary [1][2]; global f=n/5 global replication blowup 5x area VLSI limited.

- **Spectral pollution residual:** Even mpEDMD leaves 0.4% spurious modes on non-measure-preserving chaotic transient due to non-unitary approximand. ResDMD residual filter τ=1e-3 adds 12% compute [2][3].

- **Self-stabilization worst-case:** Linear-time O(f) cycles 2(2f+1) can exceed application TTFS 20d deadline avionics [5][2]. Peaceable absorption constant-time two-stage reduces to 2 cycles amortized but requires synchronized pulse seed [5][8].
