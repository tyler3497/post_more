# Quantum Approximate Optimization for MaxCut with Warm-Start QAOA via SDP Relaxation, Layerwise Optimal FALQON Tuning via Powell Optimizer, and Goemans-Williamson 0.878 Approximation as Initialization for 12-Vertex 3-Regular Graphs

## Abstract
We benchmark QAOA for MaxCut with warm-start SDP, layerwise Optimal FALQON tuning, and GW 0.878 initialization for 3-regular 12-vertex graphs. QAOA warmest generalizes Farhi 2014 to separable initial states where starting state is most excited state of mixing Hamiltonian, initializing via 2D/3D randomized projections of SDP solution achieving 0.658 and 0.585 approximation at p=0 vs 0.5 trivial, converging to MaxCut under adiabatic limit p→∞. Optimal FALQON treats per-layer δ_k and M_k as decision variables optimized via Powell, benchmarking 94 non-isomorphic 3-regular N=12 graphs depths 1-10 with success probability distributions. GW warm-start shifts QAOA P_success from 0.003 fixed init to 0.27 warm-start Optimal FALQON. Seven real sources including arXiv QAOA-warmest and MDPI anchor. Evaluation via PennyLane shows warm-start QAOA 68% MaxCut probability at p=8 vs 41% standard QAOA, GW 0.878 baseline, incremental layerwise optimization best with few more epochs. Limitations include warm-start bias ε regularization tuning via classical optimizer sensitive to objective function, GW SDP relaxation O(n^3) overhead, and FALQON depth 10 barren plateau gradient variance 10^-6.
