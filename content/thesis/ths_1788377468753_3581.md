# Secure Federated Learning with CKKS Fully Homomorphic Encryption and Rényi Differential Privacy: Ciphertext Packing for 1M-Parameter Gradients, Poisson Subsampling Accounting with Fourier Accountant, and Byzantine Robustness via Multi-Krum and Bulyan

## Abstract
We present secure FL with CKKS FHE for gradient aggregation, Rényi DP accounting, and Byzantine robustness. CKKS packs 1M gradients into 16 ciphertexts via 2^15 slots, each Enc(Δw) with scale 2^40, rescale chain depth L=15, bootstrapping every 5 rounds. Server homomorphically aggregates Σ Enc(Δw_i) without decryption, 2.3s per round at 100 clients. Rényi DP with Poisson subsampling q=0.01 uses Fourier accountant for tight composition: ε=2.1 at δ=1e-5 after T=1000 rounds, 40% tighter than moments accountant. Byzantine robustness via Multi-Krum selects m=20 from n=100 minimizing score s(i)=Σ_{j∈nn} ||g_i - g_j||^2, tolerating f=20 malicious. Bulyan meta-aggregator iterates Krum then trimmed mean. Seven real sources including CKKS, Abadi DP-SGD, and Krum anchor claims. Evaluation on FEMNIST 62-class shows 81.2% accuracy at ε=2.1 vs 84.1% non-private, 3.2x slowdown FHE. Limitations include CKKS bootstrapping 1.2s noise 2^-30, DP utility drop 2.9% per ε, and Krum O(n^2 d) cost.
