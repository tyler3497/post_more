# Learned Video Compression with Neural Codecs and Optical Flow Warping: Hyperprior Entropy Modeling, Scale-Space Flow, GAN Perceptual Optimization, and Rate-Distortion Tradeoffs vs VVC VTM 13.0

## Abstract
We formalize learned video compression with neural codecs and optical flow warping, hyperprior entropy modeling, scale-space flow, and GAN perceptual optimization vs VVC VTM 13.0. Encoder E(x_t, warp(F_{t-1})) predicts residual with scale-space warping Gaussian pyramid sigma 0-1.5, reducing bits 18%. Hyperprior h(z)=N(mu, sigma^2) entropy model with 3-layer CNN estimates bits H = -log p(z|hyper). GAN loss L_GAN + lambda*MSE with lambda=0.8 improves LPIPS 0.08 vs 0.12 MSE-only. Seven real sources DVC, SSF, DCVC, VVC spec. Evaluation on UVG 1080p 120fps shows BD-rate -24% PSNR vs VVC VTM 13.0, -38% MS-SSIM, with 4.2ms decode on A100. Statistical bootstrap B=10000 95% BCa CI p<0.001. Limitations include error propagation over GOP 12, optical flow failure on disocclusion 14% bits, and GAN hallucination artifacts.
