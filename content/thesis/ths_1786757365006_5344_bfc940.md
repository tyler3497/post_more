def build_pgm(keys, eps=64):
    segs=[]; i=0

- **Distribution shift:** Training workloads ZIPF 0.99 synthetic poorly represent production multi-tenant skew with 5% super-hot keys causing additional 18% p99. Need domain adaptation via online learning without forgetting [2][5].
