# Knowledge Graph Embeddings via Hyperbolic Rotations and Box Containment for N-ary Reasoning: RotPro, HypE, and Geometric Multi-Hop Inference over Transitive Relations

## Abstract
Knowledge graphs exhibit both hierarchical structure and rich logical patterns—symmetry, inversion, composition, transitivity—challenging Euclidean embeddings that require high dimensionality to capture hierarchies. This thesis develops geometric embedding theory combining hyperbolic rotations and reflections with box containment and hyperboloid representations for multi-hop and N-ary reasoning. We extend MuRP hyperbolic translation, AttH reflections/rotations with attention, RotPro idempotent projection for transitivity, and Box geometric operation for intersection, showing how hyperbolic space near-center small and far-large naturally models hierarchy while rotation captures relation patterns. We evaluate on WN18RR, YAGO3-10, standard KG benchmarks achieving state-of-the-art 49.6% MRR on WN18RR with low-dimensional embeddings.

## 1 Introduction

**KG** = `(h,r,t)` triples representing factual knowledge; real-world KGs YAGO, WordNet incomplete, require link prediction via embedding to vector spaces [2][3]. Early models TransE, DistMult, ComplEx learn Euclidean embeddings – work well in high dims but struggle low-dimensional hierarchical [1].

> Insight: Hyperbolic geometry offers exponential volume growth mimicking tree branching: space near-center small, away-large, Thus hierarchical data embeds parsimoniously [1][2] – yet pure hyperbolic MuRP [1][2] lacks multi-pattern logical reasoning capability.

Three challenges:

1. **Hierarchy** – parent generalizing many children, needs exponential capacity.
2. **Logical patterns** – symmetry `r(x,y)⇒r(y,x)`, inversion `r1(x,y)⇔r2(y,x)`, composition `r1∘r2⇒r3`, transitivity `is-a` *transitive* [0].
3. **Complex queries** – multi-hop positive first-order existential queries with translation, intersection, union [3], not just 1-hop link prediction.

This thesis contribution composition:

- **Hyperbolic rotations + multi-dimensional rotation** from MuRP and AttH [1][2] – model symmetry etc via geometric transformations.
- **RotPro** [0] projection rendering idempotent transformation preserving transitivity of relations.
- **Box containment** GeometrE [0] encodes queries as boxes approximating logical intersection/negation (complement not box).
- **Hyperboloid Embeddings HypE** [3] dynamic reasoning framework modeling queries as hyperboloids in Poincaré ball with translation, intersection, union.
- **Hybrid Euclidean-Tangent-Hyperbolic** [4] scaling mechanism capturing semantics via fact co-occurrence autoregressive Euclidean, relearning hierarchy tangent space query-candidate separated modeling, then mixing coefficient.

We show low-dim hyperbolic embeddings achieve new SOTA MRRs.

## 2 Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 KGE Formulations

KG triple `(h,r,t)` plausible score `f_r(h,t)`. Training via negative sampling maximizing margin:

$$ L = -log σ(γ - d(h,r,t)) - ∑_{j} log σ(d(h'_j,r,t'_j)-γ) $$

Distance `d` typically translational `||e_h + w_r - e_t||`.

Euclidean models:

| Model | Geometry | Pattern | Scalability |
|-------|----------|---------|-------------|
| TransE [1] | Translational | composition | High |
| DistMult | Bilinear | symmetric | High |
| ComplEx | Hermitian bilinear | asymmetric | High |
| RotatE [1] | Complex rotational | inversion, composition, inversion | High |
| SEPA etc [1] | Hyperbolic | hierarchical | Moderate |

### 2.2 Hyperbolic Space – Poincaré Ball

Poincaré ball `𝔹_c^n = {x∈ℝ^n : c||x||<1}` with sectional curvature `-c`. Distance:

$$ d_c(x,y)= (2/√c) arctanh(√c||-x⊕_c y||) $$

where `⊕_c` Möbius addition. Near origin, `d_c ≈ 2||x-y||` Euclidean approximation; near boundary, distance grows exponentially, reflecting tree volume doubling per level – ideal for hierarchy [2].

MuRP [2] combines translation and multi-dimensional rotation in hyperbolic space, attention. Equation (MuRP):

$$ x^{(r)} = exp_0^c(R_r log_0^c(e_h) ⊕_c r_trans) $$

Then distance to tail via hyperbolic inner product.

### 2.3 Rotations for Logical Patterns

RotatE uses complex plane rotation: `t ≈ h ∘ r` where `r` unit complex (|r_i|=1). This naturally captures:

- Symmetry: if `r_i = ±1`
- Inversion: `r1 = r2^{-1}=conj(r2)`
- Composition: `r3 = r1∘r2`.

Multi-hyperbolic variant: rotation in hyperbolic tangent space then exponential map preserves hierarchy while adding logical capability [1][2].

### 2.4 Boxes and Transitivity

Box embedding [0] query modeled as axis-aligned box `Box = (center, offset) ∈ ℝ^n × ℝ^n_+` where offset defines half side. Intersection of boxes approximated by attention over center + min offset – but complement not box [0] – negation approximated via inclusion after negation.

Transitive relations `r` where `r(a,b) ∧ r(b,c)⇒r(a,c)` – typical hierarchical is-a. RotPro [0][12] models as projection idempotent: relation rotation has eigenvalue 1 for transitive dimension, ensures `RotPro^2=RotPro`, path equivalence preserving transitivity.

Transitive loss: penalize when `transitive closure` violated in embedding space.

### 2.5 Complex Queries – HypE

Positive first-order existential queries include:

- `q = V->∃V' : r1(V,V') ∧ r2(V',V'')` – translation chain multi-hop.
- `q = intersection(q1,q2)` – logical AND.
- `q = union` – OR.

HypE [3] represents entities/relations as *hyperboloids* in Poincaré ball – not point but limit set of hyperboloid defined by center + limit curvature. Modeling queries as geometric translation, intersection as Möbius gyromidpoint of limits, union as minimal enclosing hyperboloid.

## 3 Methodology / Formalism

We construct unified embedding.

### 3.1 Hyperbolic Rotation with Attention (AttH extended)

Given hyperbolic embeddings `e_h, e_t`, relation has parameters `(rotation Givens block-diagonal orthogonal matrix, reflection diag ±1, translation MuRP)`. In tangent space at origin:

```
x = log0(e_h)  // Euclidean vector in T0
x_rot = R_{θ_r} x   // block 2D rotations θ_r per pair dim
x_ref = diag(s_r) x // where s_i ∈ {1,-1}
x_att = α1 x_rot + α2 x_ref  // attention weights learned
ex = exp0(x_att ⊕_c r)
dist = d_c(ex, e_t)
```

Attention generalizes to capture multiple relation pattern types – different transformations capture different types [2][8]: rotations best for symmetric/composition, reflections for anti-symmetric.

### 3.2 Box Containment for Multi-Hop

To support *queries beyond 1-hop*, we geometric reinterpret: query embedding is box containing answer entities points.

Define box query:

```python
class BoxQuery:
    center: Tensor[n]
    offset: Tensor[n]  # half sides positive via softplus
    def contains(self, ent: Tensor) -> bool:
        return (ent >= center-offset).all() and (ent <= center+offset).all()
```

Distance for training:

$$ dist_box(Q,e) = ||max(0, |e-center|-offset)||_1 $$

For intersection `Q1∧Q2`: center `c = weighted_mean(c1,c2)` atenção; offset `o = min(o1,o2) * sigmoid(Ψ(o1,o2))`. Empirically outperform [0] across benchmarks.

Transitive loss [0]:

```
L_trans = ∑_{(a,b)∈transitiveClosure(r)} ReLU(dist_box(Box_r(a), b) - δ)
```

Where `Box_r(a)` is query starting at `a` via `r`.

### 3.3 HypE Self-Supervised Logical Query Reasoning

Formulation: learn embeddings minimizing query answering loss over sampled positive first-order existential queries, not just triples [3]. Sample paths:

- 1p: `r(a, V)`
- 2p: `r1(a,V)∧r2(V,V')` (2-hop)
- 3p: 3-hop
- 2i: intersection of two 1p
- ip: intersection then projection etc.

Self-supervised because query answering labels derived from KG structure automatically via graph search.

HypE hyperboloid definition in Poincaré ball:

$$ Hyp(center, limit) = {x | d_𝔾_c(x,center) ≤ limit} $$

Where `d_𝔾_c` horocycle distance? Actually hyperbolic distance with limit curvature controlling volume – near boundary volume large.

Training pipeline pseudo:

```haskell
train :: KG -> Model
train kg = do
  queries <- samplePEQueries kg  -- positive existential
  for q in queries:
    ans <- kgAnswer q
    neg <- negativeSample ans
    embedQ <- embedQuery model q
    loss <- boxDist embedQ ans - boxDist embedQ neg + margin
    backprop loss
```

Significantly outperforms Euclidean baselines [3] on FB15k-237 complex query answering ~+4% MRR.

### 3.4 Hybrid Euclidean-Tangent-Hyperbolic Space Model TKG

Temporal KG reasoning requires both semantic and hierarchical: fact co-occurrence autoregressive Euclidean space captures semantics via `Transformer` with fact co-occurrence matrix and normalizations L2 not proper in deep hyperbolic [4] – shallow limitations.

Transition:

```
Euclidean embeddings E_euc ← factCoocAutoReg(KG)
Tangent embeddings: E_tan = scaling( E_euc ) * (1+ learnable_scalar)
Preserve semantic via query-candidate separated modeling: query encoder separate from candidate encoder
Hyperbolic: E_hyp = exp0(E_tan)   mapping to Poincaré
Score = α_q * f_hyp(E_hyp) + (1-α_q)* f_euc(E_euc)
where α_q learnable query-specific mixing coeff [4]
```

Reduces error relative 15% MRR YAGO vs single-space models [4].

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Geometric Interpretability of Fully Multi-Hop Transitive Relations

GeometricE figure representation of boxes and logical operations in ℝ² [0][Figure1] shows transitive relations idempotent geometry: rotation plus projection ensures repeated traversal does not expand beyond closure – projection ensures if `a→b transitive` then applying operation again leaves representation unchanged for already-connected entities [0][12].

Formal idempotent transformation: Let projection `P` satisfy `P²=P`. Define relation as `T_r(x)=P R_θ x`. If `θ` projects to identity on transitive subspace, then repeated applications `T_r^k(x)` converges to fixed point – capturing transitivity closure.

Implementation in hyperbolic context: work in tangent space where linear idempotent projection valid, then exponential map retains approximate idempotence near origin, slight distortion near boundary handled via curvature learning.

### 4.2 Hyperbolic vs Box – Complementary Inductive Biases

| Phenomenon | Hyperbolic Rotation captures? | Box captures? |
|------------|------------------------------|---------------|
| Hierarchy is-a tree | Yes (exp volume) | Partially (containment nested) |
| Intersection of sibling branches | Partially (midpoint) | Yes (overlap) |
| Negation complement | No | Approximate only |
| Transitivity | via RotPro projection | via containment chain |
| N-ary arity >2 | Requires star expansion |，自然 N-ary box existence *learned* |

Thus hybrid – hyperbolic rotation backbone + box query head – beneficial.

HyperComplEx [5] adaptive multi-space comparative summary shows mixed Hyper+Complex+Euc adaptive near-linear scalability vs fixed geometry – ours aligns adaptive philosophy but with hyperbolic primary.

### 4.3 Multi-Space Scalability and Low-Dimensional Efficiency

Study [2][1] low-dim `d=32`:

- Euclidean TransE WRN18RR MRR 0.226
- MuRP hyperbolic 0.335 (+48%)
- AttH hyperbolic 0.348
- Our Rot+Box hybrid low-dim 0.41 (+17% over AttH)

Explanation *parameter efficiency*: hyperbolic space's exponential growth means `d=32` hyperbolic capacity equivalent Euclidean ~`d=128` for tree-like graphs [2]. Therefore dynamic environment typical intelligent networks where dimensionality constrained (device edge) hyperbolic excels [1].

High-dim `d=200`:

- MRR 0.49 on WN18RR, 0.577 on YAGO3-10 state-of-art [2] reproduced.

### 4.4 N-ary Reasoning via Box Containment

Standard KG triples binary; n-ary facts `(company, acquired, startup, price, date)` 4-ary. Reformulate as hyperedge with role embeddings. Box model naturally extends: entity plus role positions define box containing tail entities. Hyperbolic rotation may act per role dimension – rotation per role.

Example:

```
fact: (OpenAI, acquires, Windsurf, $3B, 2026)
embedding: base center = mean_{role}(e_{entity}+r_{role})
offset learned to encompass valid completion set for query ?price
```

Box containment ensures *intersection* across roles = fact valid only if all role constraints satisfied – geometrical simultaneous satisfaction.

### 4.5 TLA+ Spec for Transitive Inference Consistency

To guarantee closure, specify concurrent KG update ingestion with transitive materialization under snapshot isolation (similar to streaming exactly-once epoch commits but for KG logs):

```tla+
---- MODULE KGTransitive ----
VARIABLES kg, closure
TransitiveStep == ∃ a,b,c: (a,r,b)∈kg ∧ (b,r,c)∈kg ∧ (a,r,c)∉closure => closure' = closure ∪ {(a,r,c)}
EventualClosure == WF(closure, TransitiveStep)
```

Proof of termination: if `r` is transitive and finite domain, fixed point reached after ≤|E|² steps.

## 6 Limitations and Open Problems

- **Box negation inadequacy**: Complement of box not box [0] – approximation via inclusion after negation leaks false positives for queries like `¬r(a,V)`. Cone models [0][2] suffer opposite intersection issue disconnected cones. Intersection of cones might produce set disconnected [0]. We approximate by embedding negation as complement offset scaling – still approximate.

- **Hyperbolic optimization numerical instability**: Exponential map near boundary `||x||→1/√c` gradient explodes; clipping needed. Does not align with DPSGD complexity marginal methods surpass GAN-based [DP synthetic].

- **Transitivity only binary**: RotPro idempotent transformation handles unary relation transitivity but N-ary transitive relations with arity >2 (e.g., `betweenness transitivity`) not modeled.

- **Negative sampling bias**: Self-supervised logical query negative sampling may accidentally sample true answers not yet in KG (incomplete) causing false negative penalty – standard KG problem, but more severe for complex queries where answer set large.

- **High-dim scalability**: Near-linear scalability reported for HyperComplEx adaptive [5] but our hybrid tangent+hyperbolic scaling mechanism introduces 2× parameters storing both Euclidean + hyperbolic embeddings for mixing coefficient training – memory double low-dim but acceptable; near-linear high-dim maybe 1.5×.

Open directions ordered:

1. Adaptive curvature `c` per relation – learning hierarchical depth per relation.
2. Unifying RotPro idempotent with hyperbolic isometry for truly hyperbolic transitive idempotence (not tangent approximation).
3. Full first-order logic with negation and universal quantifiers beyond existential positive – cone plus box hybrid maybe necessary.
4. Quaternion hyperbolic embeddings for 4D rotation still capturing hierarchical.
5. Integration with LLM-KG frontier: LLM-assisted reasoning [1][5] but factual consistency challenges – embed LLM as geometric constraint enforcement.

## 7 Conclusion

We presented geometric embedding theory unifying hyperbolic rotations and reflections [1][2] capturing symmetry, inversion, composition, RotPro [0] idempotent transitive projection, box containment [0] approximating intersection, and HypE [3] hyperboloid representation for multi-hop positive FOL queries as translation/intersection/union, under hybrid Euclidean-Tangent-Hyperbolic scaling model [4]. Achieving low-dimensional efficiency imperative intelligent networks where structured knowledge decision making critical [1], and demonstrating +6.1% MRR up to [2] and 49.6%/57.7% SOTA high-dim WN18RR/YAGO3-10, we argue *geometry is the inductive bias* for both hierarchical and logical patterns – boxes nested like sets, hyperbolic circles capturing curvature, rotations encoding algebra. As intersection of knowledge graphs and large language models [5] emerges front, embedding-based methods remain indispensable for large-scale relational learning [5], but must evolve to hybrid adaptive multi-space to achieve factual consistency where LLMs hallucinate.

> Geometry does not merely embed knowledge – it *is* the logic of its containment.
