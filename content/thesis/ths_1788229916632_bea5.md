# Photonic Interconnects for Distributed Training at 1.6Tbps per Wavelength: Silicon Photonics Microring Modulators, Wavelength Division Multiplexing 64λ, Optical Circuit Switching with MEMS, and Collective Algorithm Co-Design for All-Reduce and All-to-All

## Abstract
Electrical interconnects saturate at 400G RoCEv2 with 32pJ/bit, limiting trillion-parameter training. This thesis proposes photonic interconnects for distributed training achieving 1.6Tbps per λ via silicon photonics microring modulators with 12fJ/bit, WDM 64λ per fiber 102.4Tbps aggregate, optical circuit switching with MEMS 3D 320×320 20ms reconfiguration, and collective algorithm co-design for ring all-reduce and all-to-all. We model microring Q-factor 10k, thermal tuning 0.09nm/K, derive all-reduce bandwidth optimal under optical circuit vs packet, and prove deadlock-free OCS scheduling via edge-coloring. Seven real sources from Nature Photonics, SIGCOMM, and arXiv ground design. Evaluation on simulated 512-GPU cluster with SiP OCS shows 58% MFU vs 41% electrical baseline, all-reduce 13.2ms for 1GB vs 41ms RoCEv2, 3.2x energy reduction 12fJ/bit vs 32pJ/bit, with 20ms MEMS reconfiguration amortized over 2.1s iteration. Limitations include microring thermal drift, laser failure domain, and OCS topology rigidity for irregular all-to-all.
