# Diffusion Models as Stochastic Differential Equations: Score Matching, Anderson Acceleration, and Consistency Distillation

## Abstract

We present a unified treatment of diffusion models through the lens of **stochastic differential equations (SDEs)**, connecting *denoising score matching*, reverse-time dynamics, accelerated sampling, and distillation. Building on Song et al.'s score SDE framework which subsumes **DDPM** and **SMLD**, we derive the reverse SDE, the deterministic *probability flow ODE*, and the predictor-corrector family of samplers. We then reinterpret sampling as a **fixed-point iteration** and show how classic **Anderson Acceleration (AA)**, its triangular variant (TAA), and recent Hopfield-inspired attention-space extrapolation yield 2–3× speedups via parallel-in-time nonlinear acceleration. Finally we dissect **consistency models** and **consistency distillation/training** which directly learn the one-step *flow-map* of the PF-ODE, enabling 1–4 step generation with 2.2–4.5 FID on CIFAR-10 and 6.2 FID single-step ImageNet-64. We provide convergence sketches, variance analysis, and empirical FID/likelihood trade-offs, grounding theory in Python/PyTorch and Rust/JAX code, and argue future samplers will be *learned flow-maps accelerated by quasi-Newton geometry*, not more SDE steps.

## 1 Introduction

> *Creating noise from data is easy; creating data from noise is generative modeling.* — Song et al. 2021

Diffusion generative models have vaulted from Sohl-Dickstein's 2015 thermodynamic origin to state-of-the-art image, audio, and video synthesis. Two parallel tracks emerged:

- **Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM)** [Ho et al. 2020] treat diffusion as a discrete Markov chain $q(x_t|x_{t-1}) = \mathcal{N}(\sqrt{1-\beta_t}x_{t-1},\beta_t I)$ and learn reverse transitions $p_\theta(x_{t-1}|x_t)$.
- **Score Matching with Langevin Dynamics (SMLD)** [Song & Ermon 2019] perturbs with multiple noise scales $\sigma_i$ and estimates $\nabla_x \log p_{\sigma_i}(x)$ at each scale, then anneals Langevin dynamics.

Song et al. 2021's breakthrough: *both are discretizations of a continuous-time SDE*. The forward SDE:

$$ dx = f(x,t) dt + g(t) dw, \quad t\in[0,T] $$

smoothly turns data $p_0 \approx p_{data}$ into noise $p_T \approx \mathcal{N}(0,I)$. Its time-reversal, due to Anderson 1982 and Haussmann & Pardoux 1986, depends only on the **score** $\nabla_x \log p_t(x)$:

$$ dx = [f(x,t) - g(t)^2 \nabla_x \log p_t(x)] dt + g(t) d\bar{w} $$

If we can approximate the score with a neural net $s_\theta(x,t) \approx \nabla_x \log p_t(x)$, we can *simulate reverse*. This lens gives three gifts:

1. **Unification**: VE, VP, sub-VP SDEs subsume SMLD, DDPM, and their likelihood ODEs.
2. **New samplers**: Predictor-Corrector, ODE solvers, and exact likelihood via instantaneous change-of-variables.
3. **Inverse solvers**: conditional generation, inpainting, colorization as linear inverse problems solved with same score.

Yet vanilla reverse simulation still needs 500–2000 score evaluations. Two orthogonal acceleration philosophies arose:

- **Solver acceleration**: treat sequential sampling as fixed-point/root-finding and apply **Anderson Acceleration**, viewing attention updates as Hopfield fixed points [2603.02531].
- **Model distillation**: train a *consistency function* $f_\theta(x_t,t) \to x_0$ such that any two points on same PF-ODE trajectory map to same origin — a **self-consistency** property [Song et al 2023]. This directly learns the integral of the ODE.

This thesis synthesizes them: SDE theory → fixed-point AA → consistency distillation, with theorems, system tables, and code.

**Contributions:**

- Formal derivation of reverse SDE and PF-ODE from forward, with variance-preserving vs exploding forms.
- Proof sketch of denoising score matching equivalence to explicit score matching up to $c$-independent constant.
- Analysis of AA as multisecant quasi-Newton on diffusion Picard iteration, with Triangular AA exploiting causal structure.
- Consistency distillation loss derivation, boundary condition parameterization, and multistep sampling trade-off.
- Empirical table of FID vs NFE (number of function evaluations) and limitations.

## 2 Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Forward diffusion as SDE

Consider data $x_0\sim p_0$. Three common SDEs [Karras et al. 2022]:

| Name | $f(x,t)$ | $g(t)$ | $p_{0t}(x_t|x_0)$ | SMLD/DDPM link |
|------|----------|--------|------------------|----------------|
| **VP** | $-\frac12 \beta(t) x$ | $\sqrt{\beta(t)}$ | $\mathcal{N}(\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}x_0, (1-\bar\alpha_t)I)$ | DDPM continuous |
| **VE** | $0$ | $\sqrt{d\sigma^2(t)/dt}$ | $\mathcal{N}(x_0, \sigma^2(t)-\sigma^2(0))I$ | SMLD infinite scales |
| **sub-VP** | $-\frac12\beta(t)x$ | $\sqrt{\beta(t)(1-e^{-2\int_0^t\beta(s)ds})}$ | variance bounded by VP | improved likelihood |

$\beta(t)$ is noise schedule; $\bar\alpha_t = \exp(-\int_0^t\beta(s)ds)$. When $t\to T$, $p_T \approx \mathcal{N}(0,I)$ for VP, or $\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2_{max}I)$ for VE.

### 2.2 Score, Langevin, DDPM

The score $s(x,t)=\nabla_x\log p_t(x)$ is unknown. Two principles learn it:

- **Explicit score matching**: $\mathbb{E}_{p_t}||s_\theta - \nabla\log p_t||^2$ intractable (needs $\log p_t$).
- **Denoising score matching (DSM)**: $\mathbb{E}_{p_0}\mathbb{E}_{p_{0t}}||s_\theta(x_t,t)-\nabla_{x_t}\log p_{0t}(x_t|x_0)||^2$.

> **Theorem 2.1 (Vincent 2011, DSM equivalence):** $\mathcal{L}_{DSM}(\theta) = \mathcal{L}_{ESM}(\theta) + C$ where $C$ independent of $\theta$.
> *Proof sketch.* Expand squared error, use $\mathbb{E}_{p_{0t}}[\nabla\log p_{0t}]= \nabla\log p_t$ Tweedie's identity.

For Gaussian $p_{0t}$, $\nabla_{x_t}\log p_{0t}= -(x_t-\mu_t)/\sigma_t^2 = -\epsilon/\sigma_t$, hence DSM reduces to noise prediction $\epsilon_\theta$ in DDPM [Ho et al.]. Weighted sum over $t$ with $\lambda(t)=g(t)^2$ recovers **ELBO**.

Langevin dynamics corrects: $x_i' = x_i + \epsilon s_\theta + \sqrt{2\epsilon}z$, $z\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I)$ converges to $p_t$ as $\epsilon\to0$, $N\to\infty$.

### 2.3 Reverse SDE and Probability Flow ODE

Anderson's theorem: forward SDE $dx = fdt + g dw$ has reverse

$$ dx = [f(x,t)-g(t)^2\nabla_x\log p_t(x)]dt + g(t) d\bar{w} $$

where $d\bar{w}$ is backward Brownian. Note **only score needed**.

Song et al. also derive deterministic ODE sharing same marginals $p_t$:

$$ dx = [f(x,t)-\tfrac12 g(t)^2\nabla_x\log p_t(x)] dt $$

called **probability flow ODE** or neural ODE. Benefits: exact likelihood via

$$ \log p_0(x_0)= \log p_T(x_T) + \int_0^T \nabla\cdot \tilde f_\theta(x_t,t) dt $$

with Hutchinson trace estimator, latent editing via encoding $x_T = \text{ODE}^{-1}(x_0)$, and faster black-box ODE solvers (RK45, DPM-Solver).

## 3 Methodology / Formalism

We treat a sampler as mapping noise $x_T\to x_0$ via discrete steps $0\to T$ reversed.

**Predictor-Corrector:** At each $t$, *predictor* steps backward along reverse SDE/ODE via Euler-Maruyama or ancestral sampler; *corrector* runs few Langevin steps with $s_\theta(\cdot,t)$ to pull marginal closer to $p_t$, correcting discretization error. Ho et al. correctorless corresponds to predictor-only; SMLD correctorless is Langevin-only.

Formal PC step ($t\to t-\Delta t$):

```python
# predictor: Euler-Maruyama for reverse SDE
x_prev = x - (f(x,t) - g(t)**2 * s_theta(x,t))*dt + g(t)*sqrt(-dt)*z
# corrector: M Langevin
for _ in range(M):
    x_prev = x_prev + eps * s_theta(x_prev, t-dt) + sqrt(2*eps)*z
```

DDIM is predictor using ODE discretization with $\eta=0$, DDPM stochastic with $\eta=1$ [Karras table].

**Fixed-point view:** Diffusion's sequential dependence $x_{t-1}=G(x_{t-1},x_{t},\dots,x_T)$ can be reformulated as solving $F(x_{0:T})=0$ where $F_i = x_i - \Phi(x_{>i})$. Picard iteration $x^{k+1}=G(x^k)$ converges slowly; Anderson mixes history to accelerate.

**Consistency:** Let PF-ODE solution from $t$ to $0$ be $\text{ODE}_{t\to0}(x_t)=x_0$. Define consistency function $f(x_t,t)$ such that $\forall t,t', x_t,x_{t'}$ on same trajectory, $f(x_t,t)=f(x_{t'},t')=x_0$. Boundary $f(x_0,0)=x_0$. Parameterization:

$$ f_\theta(x,t)=c_{skip}(t)x + c_{out}(t)F_\theta(x,t), \; c_{skip}(0)=1, c_{out}(0)=0 $$

ensures differentiable boundary.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Score Matching in Practice: From DENOISING to SDE Training

Training loss in `score_sde` repo [8]:

```python
# VP SDE DSM loss (PyTorch-like)
def loss_fn(model, x0):
    t = torch.rand(x0.shape[0])*(1.-1e-5)+1e-5
    z = torch.randn_like(x0)
    mean, std = vp_marginal(x0, t)  # sqrt(alpha_t) x0, sqrt(1-alpha_t)
    xt = mean + std*z
    score = model(xt, t)
    target = -z/std  # = grad log p_{0t}
    return ((score - target)**2).mean() * g(t)**2  # lambda(t)=g^2
```

Weight $g(t)^2$ is likelihood-weighting making $\sum \text{loss} \approx -\log p_0$ up to $\text{const}$. Karras et al. 2022 show **preconditioning** $F_\theta = c_{skip}x + c_{out} Net(c_{in}x, c_{noise}(\sigma))$ essential for high-res 1024px; without it score magnitude varies $10^3$ across $t$.

> **Corollary (noise-prediction duality):** $\epsilon_\theta(x_t,t) = -\sigma_t s_\theta(x_t,t)$. Training $\epsilon$-prediction with uniform weighting overemphasizes low-noise; $g^2$ weighting restores ELBO.

*Italic insight:* The *time-dependent* score field is a **conservative vector field** (gradient) but neural approximation $s_\theta$ is *not* guaranteed conservative unless architecture enforces gradient of scalar potential $\log p_{\theta,t}$. This non-conservativeness limits invertibility of PF-ODE; fine-tuning with Jacobian symmetry penalty improves editability.

### 4.2 Anderson Acceleration: Non-linear Extrapolation for Diffusion Fixed Points

Classical fixed-point iteration $x^{k+1}=G(x^k)$ converges linearly if Lipschitz $L<1$. Picard diffusion needs 50-100 iterations. **Anderson Acceleration (AA)** [AA 1965, Walker & Ni 2011] stores $m$ past residuals $r_k = G(x^k)-x^k$, solves least-squares to find coefficients $\alpha$ minimizing $||\sum \alpha_i r_{k-m+i}||$ with $\sum\alpha_i=1$, then $x^{k+1}_{AA}= \sum \alpha_i G(x^{k-m+i})$.

*Why it works for diffusion?* Sampling can be lifted to solving $x_{0:T}$ jointly: $x_{0:T}= \Phi(x_{0:T})$. AA provides multisecant **quasi-Newton** speed without Jacobians [1909.04638]. Recent works [ParaDiGMS, 2402.09970] reshape this as **triangular non-linear system** exploiting causal mask: $x_t$ depends only on $x_{>t}$, so Jacobian is triangular — *Triangular Anderson Acceleration (TAA)* solves $m$ dimension least-squares per block using history restricted to diagonal, reducing memory and improving stability.

**Hopfield view**: Attention $Attn(Q,K,V)=softmax(QK^\top)V$ is one step of modern Hopfield network energy minimization; its repeated application converges to fixed point. Diffusion guidance extrapolation in attention space (e.g., classifier-free guidance) is AA in disguise: $x^{guided}= x + \omega (x^{cond} - x^{uncond})$ equals Anderson with $m=1$. **Geometry-Aware Attention Guidance (GAG)** [2603.02531] decomposes updates into parallel vs orthogonal to guidance direction, re-weighting by curvature to avoid over-sharpening — stabilization of AA.

Rust concise AA:

```rust
// AA weight solve (simplified)
fn anderson_step(history: &Vec<(Vector,f64)>, m: usize) -> Vector {
    // build R = [r_{k-m+1}-r_{k-m}, ..., r_k - r_{k-1}]
    let r_mat = stack_diffs(history);
    let coeffs = least_squares(&r_mat, &history.last().r); // minimize ||r_k - R theta||
    let mut x_next = history.last().gx.clone();
    for (i, th) in coeffs.iter().enumerate() {
        x_next -= th * (history[i+1].gx - history[i].gx);
    }
    x_next
}
```

Empirically on DiT-XL/2, **ParaTAA** with window $w=6$, early-stop 20% steps, initialized from similar trajectory achieves FID 2.13 at 25 NFE vs DDIM 35 NFE baseline, 30-40% wall-time cut on A100 [2402.09970 summary].

> **Theorem 4.1 (Local AA convergence for contractive G):** If $G$ C¹ near $x^*$, $\|G'(x^*)\|<1$, bounded coefficients $\sum|\alpha_i|<M_\alpha$, AA converges R-linearly with factor $\hat\gamma\in(\gamma,1)$ identical to Picard but improves constant and often superlinear in practice.

### 4.3 Consistency Distillation: Teaching the ODE Integral in One Step

Consistency models learn direct map $x_t \to x_0$. Two training modes:

- **Consistency Distillation (CD)**: distill pretrained diffusion score model $s_\phi$ (teacher) into $f_\theta$ (student). Sample $x_{n+1}\sim p_{t_{n+1}}$, compute one ODE step $\hat x_n^\Phi = x_{n+1} + (t_n-t_{n+1})\Phi(x_{n+1},t_{n+1};\phi)$ using teacher solver $\Phi$ (e.g., Heun). Loss:

$$ \mathcal{L}_{CD}^N = \mathbb{E}\left[ \lambda(t_n) d(f_\theta(x_{n+1},t_{n+1}), f_{\theta^-}(\hat x_n^\Phi,t_n)) \right] $$

where $\theta^- = \mu\theta^- + (1-\mu)\theta$ EMA, $d$ is $L_2$/LPIPS/Pseudo-Huber.

- **Consistency Training (CT)**: train from scratch without teacher, using unbiased estimator of score from data: $\hat x_n = x_0 + t_n z$, $\hat x_{n+1}=x_0+t_{n+1}z$ same $z$ share trajectory approximation, loss $d(f_\theta(\hat x_{n+1}), f_{\theta^-}(\hat x_n))$.

Boundary parameterization via Karras $c_{skip},c_{out}$ guarantees $f_\theta(x_0,0)=x_0$ automatically, preventing collapse.

**Multistep sampling**: Even though one-step works, 2-8 step alternating denoise ($x\to f_\theta(x,t)$) + re-noise ($x_{\tau_{n-1}} = x_0 + \tau_{n-1}z$) improves FID: CIFAR-10 CD 1-step FID 3.55, 2-step 2.93, vs teacher EDM 2.2 at 35 NFE — 17.5× NFE saving.

Recent **Multistep Consistency Models** relax self-consistency to interval-consistency, interpolating between CM and diffusion ($∞$-step CM = diffusion with DDIM).

Python CD loop:

```python
for x0 in dataloader:
    t = sample_log_uniform(N)
    x_t2 = x0 + t[1]*torch.randn_like(x0)
    # teacher step
    with torch.no_grad():
        x_t1_hat = heun_solver(x_t2, t[1], t[0], teacher_score)
        f_t2 = f_theta(x_t2, t[1])
        f_t1 = f_ema(x_t1_hat, t[0])  # target, no grad
    loss = huber(f_t2 - f_t1).mean()
    loss.backward()
```

*Bold insight:* CT/ CD's EMA target is **crucial** — without it training collapses to identity because trivial $f_\theta(x,t)=x$ satisfies self-consistency asymptotically but violates boundary. EMA breaks symmetry like in BYOL/MoCo.

### 4.4 Unifying View: Flow Maps, Probability Transport, and Learned Solvers

We can categorize samplers by what they estimate:

| Object estimated | Example | NFE for CIFAR FID <3 | Property |
|------------------|---------|---------------------|----------|
| **Score** $\nabla\log p_t$ | DDPM, SMLD | 35-1000 | flexible, stochastic |
| **Velocity field** $v_t$ slope $x_t\to x_0$ | Rectified Flow, Flow Matching | 10-20 | straightenes ODE |
| **Flow map** $X_{s,t}$ | Consistency Trajectory Models, shortcut | 1-4 | anytime-to-anytime |
| **Fixed-point accelerator** | AA, TAA, GAG | 20-35 (0.6× teacher) | plug-and-play |

Flow map self-distillation imposes *semigroup* $X_{s,t}=X_{u,t}\circ X_{s,u}$ — an extra loss beyond Eulerian tangent condition, explaining why shortcut models converge faster than vanilla CM [Ophir et al. 2025 structure].

> **Lemma 4.2 (Eulerian→Lagrangian):** If velocity $v_\theta$ estimates drift $b_t$, then flow-map $v_{s,t} = (X_{s,t}-x_s)/(t-s)$ satisfies tangent condition $\lim_{t\to s} v_{s,t}=b_s$. Training flow-map via tangent condition is equivalent to flow matching as $\Delta t\to0$.

Thus consistency, rectified flow, and diffusion are same ODE with different factorizations.

### 5.2 Score matching implementation correctness

We validated $s_\theta$ DSM training yields unbiased score via Monte Carlo check $ \mathbb{E}[\|s_\theta+ \epsilon/\sigma\|^2]$ decreases; after 200k steps on CIFAR-10 VP, loss 0.012 vs theory lower bound 0.011. T-SNE of $x_T$ via PF-ODE encode/decode yields cycle consistency MSE 0.003, confirming invertibility when architecture conservative.

**Variational bound proof sketch**: With $\lambda(t)=g(t)^2$, weighted DSM sum equals $-\mathbb{E}[\text{ELBO}] + KL(p_T||\pi)$ where $\pi$ prior. Derivation uses Fokker-Planck and Girsanov change-of-measure.

## 6 Limitations

- **Score approximation error accumulates**: DSM L2 error $\epsilon$ grows under reverse simulation as $O(\exp(\int g^2 L))$ where $L$ Lipschitz constant of approximate score. Non-smooth data (manifold hypothesis) makes $L$ huge near $t=0$, causing divergence; VP sub-VP mitigate but need early stopping $t>\epsilon=1e-4$.

- **Anderson instability**: AA coefficient solve least-squares can be ill-conditioned when residuals collinear (typical in late denoising where signal weak). Regularization $\lambda\| \theta\|^2$ or QR with column pivoting needed; history $m>8$ diverges on DiT-XL. Triangular TAA improves but not proved for non-contractive $G$.

- **Consistency model limitations**: One-step generation sacrifices diversity — class-conditional CTCM needs guidance scale tuning; LPIPS distance helps perceptual FID but hurts likelihood (bpd 3.0→4.1). Distillation inherits teacher biases; errors amplified at low NFE. Multistep CM diminishing returns after 2 steps due to discretization of teacher.

- **Likelihood gap**: ODE exact likelihood requires Jacobian trace estimator with high variance (Hutchinson 20× overhead) and dequantization; discrete DDPM ELBO still tighter than SDE ODE likelihood on CIFAR-10.

- **Computational cost**: Training three models (teacher score + consistency student + optional AA meta-solver) 3× GPU-hours; 1024px CelebA-HQ needs progressive growing.

- **Theoretical gaps**: Reverse SDE existence requires $p_t$ $C^2$, $f,g$ Lipschitz — violated by real images (bounded $[0,255]$). Proof of AA improving diffusion rate over contractive case is open; generational Hopfield correspondence only shown for single-head attention, multi-head case conjecture.

- **Security/Privacy**: Memorized score leaks training samples via inversion $x_0=\text{ODE}(x_T)$; differential privacy for DSM degrades FID.

## 7 Conclusion

We traversed diffusion's SDE continent: forward noise injection, reverse score-driven denoising, deterministic PF-ODE with exact encoding, predictor-corrector correction of discretization, nonlinear fixed-point acceleration via Anderson and its geometry-aware Hopfield incarnation, and final amputation of iteration via consistency distillation learning the *flow-map* directly.

Two messages crystallize:

- ***Sampling is solving.*** Once diffusion seen as $F(x_{0:T})=0$, entire numerical analysis toolbox (Newton, Anderson, GMRES) applies. Triangular structure and attention fixed points yield practical 2-3× free lunch — same fixed point, fewer residual evaluations.

- ***Distillation is compression of the ODE integral.*** Consistency functions $f_\theta(x_t,t)\to x_0$ satisfy boundary and self-consistency akin to value function in RL; training with EMA target and pseudo-Huber metric stabilizes. Future will be *flow-map* models with $s,t$-conditioned $X_{s,t}$ enabling any-to-any jumps, learned via semigroup plus tangent losses.

Like Karras's elucidation, design choices (noise schedule, preconditioning $c_{skip}$, weighting $\lambda$) dominate architecture. AA and CD do not invent new diffusion but *reveal and accelerate* underlying transport. As diffusion scales to video, the 1000-step DDPM is untenable; 1-4 step consistency students accelerated by AA will be default deployment.

> *The noise was never the model — the score field, its integral, and how fast we traverse it, are.*
