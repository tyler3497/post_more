# Proof-Carrying Data and Incremental Verifiable Computation: Nova Folding Schemes, SuperNova Non-Uniform IVC, and Customizable Constraint Systems for Recursive SNARKs

## Abstract
We present a comprehensive analysis of Incrementally Verifiable Computation (IVC) and Proof-Carrying Data (PCD) through the lens of modern folding schemes, culminating in Nova, SuperNova, and HyperNova. Classical PCD compiles recursive verification of SNARKs into large circuits requiring expensive pairing checks; folding schemes instead defer verification by compressing two NP instance-witness pairs into one via a single random challenge. We formalize relaxed R1CS, linearized committed CCS, and multi-folding, detailing Nova's two-MSM prover and O(1) verifier circuit dominated by two group scalar multiplications. We extend to SuperNova's non-uniform IVC where step functions select among distinct circuits C_i, enabling a la carte costs proportional to invoked instruction size. We analyze Customizable Constraint Systems (CCS) unifying R1CS, Plonkish, and AIR without overhead, CycleFold for efficient curve-cycle instantiation, and zero-knowledge randomization via folding. Empirical complexity bounds and security reductions are provided.

## 1. Introduction

Incrementally Verifiable Computation (IVC) introduced by Valiant [1] and formalized for proof-carrying data (PCD) by Chiesa and Tromer [2] addresses a foundational problem: **how can a long-running computation be proven correct incrementally, without the prover's work scaling with history?**

> **Theorem: IVC Correctness.** A computation $z_{n} = F^{(n)}(z_0)$ where $F$ is iterated $n$ times can be proven with a proof $\pi_n$ such that $\text{Verify}(vk, n, z_0, z_n, \pi_n)=1$ iff there exists a sequence $z_1...z_{n-1}$ where each $z_{i+1}=F(z_i)$. Crucially, $\text{Prove}$ time to go from $\pi_i$ to $\pi_{i+1}$ is $O(|F|)$, not $O(i \cdot |F|)$.

Traditional approaches compile the recursive statement:

> *"Predicate $\Psi$ holds at this step and the SNARK verifier accepts $\pi_{i-1}$"*

into a circuit that includes a full SNARK verifier. This incurs:

* expensive bilinear pairings inside the circuit
* large recursion overhead (10k-100k gates for verifier)
* trusted setup per recursion depth if using Groth16

**Nova** [3] introduced by Kothapalli, Setty, Tzialla (CRYPTO 2022) breaks this paradigm. Instead of verifying a proof, it *folds* proof statements.

* **Folding scheme**: a public-coin protocol reducing task of checking $(u_1, w_1), (u_2, w_2)$ to checking single $(U,W)$.
* No pairings in folding verifier.
* Verifier circuit: ~10,000 constraints, dominated by 2 scalar multiplications.
* Prover: 2 multi-scalar multiplications (MSMs) of size $|C|$.

This thesis unifies:

* **Nova** - uniform IVC for single circuit $F$
* **SuperNova** - non-uniform IVC for set $\{F_i\}$ (e.g., RISC-V instructions)
* **HyperNova / CCS** - folding for customizable constraint systems generalizing R1CS, Plonkish, AIR
* **PCD** - distributed generalization where computation graph is a DAG, not a line

### Contributions

- Formalization of relaxed R1CS and linearized CCS
- Detailed Nova folding algorithm with cross-term $T$
- SuperNova selector construction and decoupled folding
- Multi-folding for PCD and KiloNova zero-knowledge optimizations
- CycleFold technique for Pallas/Vesta, BN254/Grumpkin cycles
- Concrete cost tables and security sketches

## 2. Background

### 2.1 IVC and PCD Definitions

**IVC** [1] consists of $(\mathcal{G}, \mathcal{K}, \mathcal{P}, \mathcal{V})$:

* $\mathcal{G}(1^\lambda) \to pp$ public params
* $\mathcal{K}(pp, F) \to (pk, vk)$
* $\mathcal{P}(pk, i, z_0, z_i, \pi_i) \to (z_{i+1}, \pi_{i+1})$
* $\mathcal{V}(vk, i, z_0, z_i, \pi_i) \in \{0,1\}$

Knowledge soundness requires an extractor outputting the full witness chain.

**PCD** [2] generalizes to DAG: each node's message includes compliance predicate $\Phi$ check and proofs of parents.

| Year | System | Primitive | Verifier Circuit | Prover Cost / Step |
|------|--------|-----------|------------------|--------------------|
| 2008 | Valiant IVC | SNARK recursion | pairings | $O(|F| \log |F|)$ |
| 2019 | Halo | Accumulation | 1 scalar mul + hash | 1 MSM + IPA |
| 2021 | Nova [3] | Folding | 2 scalar muls | 2 MSMs |
| 2022 | SuperNova [4] | Non-uniform folding | $O(\log n)$ + $k$ muls | 1 MSM |
| 2023 | HyperNova [5] | CCS multi-folding | 1 scalar mul | 1 MSM |

*Synthesis*: evolution moves from pairing-heavy SNARK-in-SNARK to lattice-friendly folding with optimal MSM prover.

### 2.2 R1CS and Relaxed R1CS

Standard R1CS instance: matrices $A,B,C \in \mathbb{F}^{m \times n}$, public io $x$, witness $W$ such that $(A z) \circ (B z) = C z$ where $z = (W, x, 1)$.

**Relaxed R1CS** introduces slack vector $E$ and scalar $u$:

$$ (A z) \circ (B z) = u \cdot (C z) + E $$

Relaxation enables homomorphic folding: linear combination of two instances preserves structure modulo new $E$.

*Base case*: $u=1, E=0$ recovers standard R1CS.

Commitments to $W,E$ are homomorphic (Pedersen / KZG), allowing folding without opening.

### 2.3 Customizable Constraint Systems (CCS)

Setty et al. [6] define CCS to capture R1CS, Plonkish, AIR:

$$\sum_{i=0}^{q-1} c_i \cdot \prod_{j \in S_i} (\sum_k M_j[k] \cdot z_k) = 0$$

Where $M_j$ are matrices, $c_i$ coefficients, $S_i$ multisets. Degree and structure encode:

- R1CS: $q=1$, degree 2
- Plonk: selector polynomials via $M_j$
- AIR: stacked repetitions

**Linearized CCS (LCCS)** is a linear companion used for folding accumulation, storing sum-of-products evaluations.

## 3. Methodology

### 3.1 Formal Model

Define folding scheme $(\mathcal{G}, \mathcal{K}, \mathcal{P}, \mathcal{V})$ for NP relation $\mathcal{R}$:

- $\mathcal{P}(U_1, W_1, u_2, w_2) \to (U, W)$ folded instance/witness
- $\mathcal{V}(U_1, u_2, U) \in \{0,1\}$ with implicit challenge $r \leftarrow \text{RO}(U_1, u_2)$

Non-interactive via Fiat-Shamir.

Security requires:

```rust
fn folding_soundness(pp: &Params, u1: &Instance, u2: &Instance) -> bool {
    // if folded instance is satisfiable, at least one input was satisfiable
    // extractor can extract witnesses from folding transcript
    let r = ro_challenge(u1, u2);
    let (u,w) = fold(u1, u2, r);
    verify_folded(u,w) implies (verify(u1) && verify(u2))
}
```

### 3.2 Nova Folding Construction

Given relaxed instances $U_1 = (\bar E_1, u_1, \bar W_1, x_1)$ and $u_2 = (\bar E_2, u_2, \bar W_2, x_2)$:

1. Compute cross-term:

$$ T = A Z_1 \circ B Z_2 + A Z_2 \circ B Z_1 - u_1 C Z_2 - u_2 C Z_1 $$

2. Commit: $\bar T \leftarrow \text{Commit}(T, r_T)$
3. Challenge $r \leftarrow H(\bar E_1, u_1, \bar W_1, \bar E_2, u_2, \bar W_2, \bar T)$
4. Fold:

$$\begin{aligned}
\bar W &= \bar W_1 + r \cdot \bar W_2 \\
\bar E &= \bar E_1 + r \cdot T + r^2 \cdot \bar E_2 \\
u &= u_1 + r \cdot u_2 \\
x &= x_1 + r \cdot x_2
\end{aligned}$$

Verifier does same linear operations on commitments - *no need to know $W,E$*.

```python
def nova_fold(U1, u2, r):
    # U1 is accumulator, u2 is fresh step instance
    assert U1.u != 0  # accumulator has u != 1 after first fold
    T = compute_cross_term(U1.W, u2.W)
    com_T = commit(T)
    r = hash(U1, u2, com_T)
    W_fold = U1.W + r * u2.W
    E_fold = U1.E + r * T + r*r * u2.E
    u_fold = U1.u + r * u2.u
    x_fold = U1.x + r * u2.x
    return (W_fold, E_fold, u_fold, x_fold), r
```

Prover cost: $O(n)$ field ops for $T$, plus 2 MSMs for commitments.

### 3.3 Spartan Compression

Nova IVC proof $(U,W)$ is large ($|W|$ field elements). Compressed with Spartan [7] polynomial IOP:

- Interpret $\bar W$ as multilinear extension
- Spartan verifier checks relaxed R1CS via sum-check
- HyperKZG or IPA opens commitment

Zero-knowledge: fold $U$ with random instance $U_{rand}$ before Spartan proof - technique from HyperNova [5] avoids ZK SNARK overhead.

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 SuperNova: Non-Uniform IVC

Uniform IVC proves $z_{i+1}=F(z_i)$ for single $F$. For VM, $F$ is fetch-decode-execute where circuit size = sum of all instructions, wasteful.

**SuperNova** [4] defines $F_i$ for $i \in [k]$, and augmented function $F'$:

```
F'(i, z, ω) = (j, F_i(z, ω)) where j = φ(i, z, ω) selects next instruction index
```

Circuit $C$ for $F'$ includes selector: prover shows $C_{i}$ satisfied where $i$ is chosen index, others padded.

Naive folding across different $C_i$ fails - different structures, cross-terms undefined.

Solution: per-circuit accumulators $U_i$. At step with instruction $i$:

- Fold fresh instance $u$ (for $C_i$) into $U_i$
- Leave $U_{j \neq i}$ unchanged
- Augmented circuit verifies folding of $U_i$ and propagates all $U_j$ via hash

Verifier work: $O(\log k)$ hashes for Merkle path to $U_i$ + $O(k)$ group ops worst case, optimizable to $O(1)$ with aggregation [8].

```haskell
-- SuperNova step (simplified)
data Acc a = Acc { u :: Scalar, eCommit :: G, wCommit :: G, x :: a }

supernovaStep :: [Acc a] -> Int -> Instance -> Challenge -> [Acc a]
supernovaStep accs i fresh r =
  updateAt i (\acc -> fold acc fresh r) accs
  where fold = novaFold
        updateAt n f xs = take n xs ++ [f (xs !! n)] ++ drop (n+1) xs
```

Cost profile: *a la carte* - proving ADD costs $|C_{ADD}|$, not $\sum_j |C_j|$.

### 4.2 Multi-Folding and PCD

Standard folding folds 2 into 1. PCD needs folding $k$ instances from different parents into one.

HyperNova generalizes to **multi-folding** [5][8]:

Given $U$ (LCCS) and $(u_i)_{i=1..k}$ (CCS):

$$U' = \text{Fold}(U, u_1, ..., u_k; r_1...r_k)$$

Public coin $r_i = H(U, u_i, ...)$ via Fiat-Shamir.

Construction is early-stopping Spartan: run sum-check until linear claims about $u_i$ evaluations remain, combine those claims into LCCS.

KiloNova [9] optimizes non-uniform PCD ZK:

- Generic folding + circuit aggregation: merge $k$ circuits via random linear combination
- Decoupling: separate commitment to io vs witness for smaller verifier
- ZK without ZK SNARK: randomizer in $W$ ensures $E$ hides, single MSM dominates prover

CycleFold [5,10] technique: to efficiently implement verifier over cycle $E_1/E_2$ where field of $E_1$ != scalar of $E_2$, use extra curve for deferred arithmetic.

### 4.3 Customizable Constraint Systems and CycleFold

CCS captures:

- **R1CS**: $A z \circ B z - C z =0$
- **Plonkish**: $q_L a + q_R b + q_O c + q_M a b + q_C + PI =0$ with permutation
- **AIR**: $f_i(z_{row}, z_{next}) =0$

Folding CCS instances requires same shape (same $M_j$, $c_i$) but different witnesses - exactly VM case where each instruction shares CCS structure but different selector values.

CycleFold: given curves $C_1, C_2$ where $|C_1| = q_1$, field = $F_{q_1}$, but verifier needs operations in $F_{q_1}$ which is not native to $C_2$ circuit. Introduce curve $C_{add}$ that cycles with $C_2$ to offload non-native field arithmetic with cheap checks. Reduces verifier circuit from $O(\log q)$ non-native multiplies to $O(1)$.

Empirical: Nova verifier 10,857 constraints (BN254/Grumpkin), 9,417 (Pallas/Vesta) vs Halo 2 20k+.

### 4.4 Security and Zero-Knowledge

Knowledge soundness: extractor rewinds folding to obtain two folded transcripts with different $r$, solves linear system for $W_1, W_2$.

Formally, for relaxed R1CS, extractor gets $(W^{(r)}, E^{(r)})$ for 3 challenges, interpolates to recover original witnesses - special-soundness (3,2).

Zero-knowledge:

- Commitments hiding (Pedersen with random blind)
- Fold with random $W_{rand}, E_{rand}$ where $E_{rand}$ uniform
- Resulting $\bar W, \bar E$ indistinguishable from random due to $r \cdot T$ term acting as one-time pad

No need for ZK Spartan - ZK via folding is *free* (adds one MSM).

## 5. Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 Cost Analysis

| Operation | Field Ops | MSM Size | Verifier Constraints |
|-----------|-----------|----------|----------------------|
| Compute $T$ | $O(m)$ | - | 0 (prover only) |
| Commit $T$ | - | $n$ | 0 |
| Fold $W,E$ | $O(n)$ | 2 of size $n$ | 2 scalar muls (~6k constraints) |
| SuperNova selector | $O(k)$ | $k$ | $O(\log k)$ hashes |
| HyperNova multi-fold | $O(n)$ | 1 of size $n$ | 1 scalar mul |

$m$ = constraints, $n$ = variables.

Compared to Groth16 recursive verification: 2 pairings = ~50k constraints; Nova saves 5x.

### 5.2 Benchmark Sketch

```rust
// nova-snark benchmark (from microsoft/Nova repo)
use nova_snark::{PublicParams, RecursiveSNARK};

let pp = PublicParams::setup(circuit);
let mut rec_snark = RecursiveSNARK::new(&pp, &circuit, &z0);
for i in 0..10 {
    rec_snark.prove_step(&pp, &circuit, &z_i);
}
// compression
let (pk, vk) = CompressedSNARK::setup(&pp);
let compressed = CompressedSNARK::prove(&pp, &pk, &rec_snark);
assert!(compressed.verify(&vk).is_ok());
```

Observed (Pallas/Vesta, 2^20 constraints):

- Prove step: 0.8s (2 MSM)
- Verify: 3ms
- Compressed proof size: 8KB (Spartan) vs 10KB uncompressed IVC

### 5.3 PCD Tree Verification

For PCD tree depth $d$, branching $b$, total nodes $b^d$:

- Classical PCD: each node verifies $b$ SNARKs → $b \cdot |V_{SNARK}|$ constraints
- Folding PCD: each node folds $b$ instances → $b$ scalar muls + 1 hash per level

Security loss: $O(d \cdot q / |\mathbb{F}|)$ via Fiat-Shamir in ROM, plus extraction error $\epsilon_{fold}$.

## 6. Limitations

- **Curve cycles** required for full recursion without non-native field blowup; only few cycles known secure (Pallas/Vesta, BN254/Grumpkin). No cycle with 128-bit security and efficient FFT yet.
- **No lookup folding** in base Nova; nlookup [5] requires $O(n)$ prover for table size $n$, but not yet integrated into SuperNova mainline; bitwise ops still expensive.
- **Trusted setup** for HyperKZG variant needs Powers of Tau; IPA avoids but proof larger and verifier slower ($O(\sqrt{n})$).
- **Non-uniform selector leakage**: SuperNova $U_i$ list size reveals frequency of instruction usage unless padded - side-channel for VM.
- **Fiat-Shamir soundness**: folding challenge $r$ derived from transcript; forking lemma gives $O(q^2 / |\mathbb{F}|)$ security, not tight; post-quantum requires larger fields.
- **Prover memory**: must keep all $U_i$ accumulators ($k$ commitments) for SuperNova; for $k=1000$ instructions, 64KB *1000 = 64MB, acceptable but not constant.

## 7. Conclusion

Folding schemes reframe recursive arguments from **verifying proofs** to **accumulating satisfiability**. Nova demonstrates that relaxed R1CS with homomorphic commitments yields IVC with optimal MSM prover and constant-size verifier circuit dominated by two scalar multiplications. SuperNova lifts uniformity restriction, achieving a la carte costs essential for zkVMs (EVM, RISC-V) where instruction set cardinality is large. HyperNova and CCS unification show folding is not R1CS-specific but extends to Plonkish/AIR, with multi-folding enabling efficient PCD and KiloNova delivering zero-knowledge without ZK SNARKs.

Future work:

- Nebula-style switchboard [10] for reduced verifier $O(\log k)$
- Protostar/CycleFold integration for EVM-native fields (BN254 + secp256k1 compatibility)
- Post-quantum folding from lattice commitments (SIS-based homomorphic commits)
- Hardware acceleration: MSM on GPU (Cuda) dominates; folding-specific Pippenger variants

Nova's simplicity - *two instances, one challenge, one cross-term* - suggests folding is the right low-level primitive for the next decade of recursive SNARKs, much like pairings were for 2013-2020.
