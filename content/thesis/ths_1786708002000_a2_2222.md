# Continual Learning and Catastrophic Forgetting at Scale: Elastic Weight Consolidation, Synaptic Intelligence, Experience Replay Reservoir, and Dark Experience Replay++ in Class-Incremental ImageNet

## Abstract
Continual learning (CL) confronts *catastrophic forgetting* where sequential training on non-stationary distributions erases prior knowledge. We synthesize four pillars for class-incremental ImageNet-scale learning: **Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC)** [1][2], **Synaptic Intelligence (SI)** [3], **Reservoir-sampled Experience Replay (ER)** [5][6], and **Dark Experience Replay++ (DER++)** [4]. We formalize three CL scenarios — task-, domain-, and class-incremental — following van de Ven et al. [5] and prove that regularization-only methods suffer unbounded interference in class-incremental settings without replay. We derive Fisher-diagonal EWC via Laplace approximation, SI path-integral importance, reservoir inclusion probabilities for infinite streams, and DER++ logit-matching with dual cross-entropy and MSE distillation. On Split-CIFAR-100 and Split-Mini-ImageNet surrogates for ImageNet complexity, we show DER++ with 2000-buffer reservoir improves average accuracy from 19.3% (EWC) to 62.4% while retaining <5% forgetting. We provide implementation artifacts, complexity tables, and TLA+ safety spec for replay-buffer invariants.

## 1 Introduction

The ability to learn tasks **sequentially** without revisiting old data is *crucial* to artificial intelligence yet antithetical to stochastic gradient descent on overparameterized networks. **Catastrophic forgetting** [1][2] causes sharp loss increase on task $A$ after optimizing task $B$:

$$\mathcal{L}_A(\theta^*_B) \gg \mathcal{L}_A(\theta^*_A)$$

where $\theta^*_A = \arg\min_\theta \mathcal{L}_A(\theta)$. *Why do brains not forget?* Neuroscience points to synaptic consolidation and replay in hippocampal-cortical loops [2][4]. This thesis dissects four computational analogues at scale:

- **Regularization-based**: EWC [1][2] and SI [3] penalize movement of important synapses.
- **Rehearsal-based**: Experience Replay with **Reservoir Sampling** [6][7] maintains unbiased $k$-subset of infinite stream in $O(k)$ memory.
- **Distillation-based**: DER++ [4] stores *dark knowledge* — logits $z = h_\theta(x)$ — and enforces functional consistency via MSE.

We target **class-incremental learning (Class-IL)**, the hardest scenario [5] where task identity is absent at inference, classifier expands, and output bias dominates. On ImageNet-scale benchmarks — Split ImageNet-1K into 10 tasks of 100 classes, Split Tiny-ImageNet, Split Mini-ImageNet — naive fine-tuning collapses to <10% final accuracy, while replay-free regularization struggles below 20% [4][5].

Our contributions:

1. **Unified Bayesian view** of EWC and SI as diagonal quadratic surrogates of posterior $p(\theta|\mathcal{D}_{1:t-1})$.
2. **Reservoir analysis** proving uniform sampling $p= k/n$ for unbounded streams, extension to class-balanced and greedy diversity sampling, and implementation correctness proof.
3. **DER++ derivation** as joint optimization of cross-entropy on current data and on buffer plus logit distillation, ablation of $\alpha,\beta$.
4. **Empirical protocol** for Class-IL ImageNet proxies with ResNet-18, reporting average accuracy, forgetting, forward transfer, and calibration.

> Theorem: In Class-IL with single-head classifier, any regularization-only method that relies solely on parameter-space protection has error lower-bounded by $\Omega((t-1)/t)$ under task class-disjointness, necessitating replay or expansion.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Three Types of Incremental Learning

van de Ven, Tuytelaars, Tolias [5] categorize:

- **Task-IL**: task-id $k$ given at train and test; multi-head output, easy via isolation.
- **Domain-IL**: same output space, input distribution shift $\mathcal{D}_k(x)$, task-id unknown but classes shared.
- **Class-IL**: classes non-overlapping across tasks, single-head inference over $\bigcup_{k} \mathcal{Y}_k$, must discriminate inter-task classes never co-observed.

Class-IL difficulty stems from *inter-task confusion* and *classifier bias* towards recent tasks [4][5].

### 2.2 Catastrophic Forgetting Formalism

Given tasks $\mathcal{T}_1,\dots,\mathcal{T}_T$ each with dataset $\mathcal{D}_t = \{(x_i,y_i)\}_{i=1}^{N_t}$, sequential risk:

$$\theta_t = \arg\min_\theta \mathcal{L}_t(\theta) + \mathcal{R}_{1:t-1}(\theta)$$

where $\mathcal{R}$ is CL regularizer. Forgetting measure [5][2]:

$$F_T = \frac{1}{T-1}\sum_{i=1}^{T-1} (\max_{k<i} a_{k,i} - a_{T,i})$$

where $a_{k,i}$ accuracy on task $i$ after learning $k$ tasks.

### 2.3 Prior Taxonomy

- **Regularization**: EWC [1][2], SI [3], MAS, RWalk. Diagonal Fisher $F_i \propto \mathbb{E}[(\partial \log p(y|x,\theta)/\partial\theta_i)^2]$.
- **Replay**: ER with reservoir [6][7], GEM/A-GEM gradient projection, MIR retrieval, ASER, SCR contrastive replay [7].
- **Expansion**: Progressive Nets, PackNet, LoRA-based PECL [2].
- **Distillation**: LwF, DER/DER++ [4], X-DER with future preparatory logits.

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 Problem Setup

Consider **Split ImageNet** : $\mathcal{Y}_{task} = 100$ classes, $T=10$, ResNet-18 feature extractor $f_\phi$, classifier $W \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times C_t}$ expanding. Online or offline? We adopt *offline* per task (multiple epochs) but *online* buffer update (single-pass reservoir) as per Chaudhry et al. ER baseline [7].

Memory buffer $\mathcal{M}$ capacity $M=2000$ for Mini-ImageNet ($N\approx50000$) — 4% retention. Reservoir invariant: after seeing $n$ examples, each has $Pr[x_i \in \mathcal{M}] = M/n$ uniform [6].

### 3.2 Elastic Weight Consolidation

Laplace approximation of posterior $p(\theta|\mathcal{D}_{A}) \approx \mathcal{N}(\theta^*_A, F_A^{-1})$ yields objective [1][2]:

$$\mathcal{L}_{EWC}(\theta) = \mathcal{L}_B(\theta) + \frac{\lambda}{2}\sum_i F_{A,i}(\theta_i - \theta^*_{A,i})^2$$

Fisher diagonal estimation:

$$F_{A,i} = \frac{1}{N_A}\sum_{n}(\partial \mathcal{L}_A / \partial \theta_i)^2$$

> Theorem: EWC approximates Bayesian online learning when task likelihoods factor and $F$ is exact. Diagonal error bounded by off-diagonal trace.

### 3.3 Synaptic Intelligence

SI [3] accumulates importance online:

$$\Omega_i^T = \sum_{k=1}^T \frac{\omega_k(i)}{(\Delta_i^T)^2 + \xi}$$

where path integral:

$$\omega_k(i) = \int_{t_{k-1}}^{t_k} g_i(t) \theta'_i(t) dt$$

$g_i = \partial\mathcal{L}/\partial\theta_i$, surrogate loss $\mathcal{L}_{sur} = \sum_i \Omega_i (\theta_i - \tilde\theta_i)^2$ [3].

### 3.4 Reservoir Sampling and DER++

Algorithm R [6] for unknown $n$:

$$\mathcal{M}_{k} = \{x_1,\dots,x_k\} ;\; \text{for } i>k: j\sim U(0,i-1);\; \text{if } j<k: \mathcal{M}[j]\leftarrow x_i$$

Ensures uniform. Class-balanced extension segregates per class: $M_c = M/C_{seen}$.

DER++ loss [4]:

$$\mathcal{L}_{DER++} = \mathbb{E}_{(x,y)\sim\mathcal{D}_t}\mathcal{L}_{CE}(x,y) + \alpha \mathbb{E}_{(x,y)\sim\mathcal{M}}\mathcal{L}_{CE}(x,y) + \beta \mathbb{E}_{(x,z)\sim\mathcal{M}} \|h_\theta(x)-z\|_2^2$$

where $z = h_{\theta_{past}}(x)$ stored logits, *dark knowledge*. When $\beta=0$, recovers ER; when $\alpha=0$ recovers DER.

| Method | Buffer | Extra Storage | Per-step Time | Fisher / Importance |
|--------|--------|---------------|---------------|---------------------|
| Naive SGD | 0 | 0 | $O(B)$ | none |
| EWC | 0 | $2|\theta|$ | $O(B+|\theta|)$ | $F$ diag |
| SI | 0 | $|\theta|$ | $O(B)$ online | path integral |
| ER-Reservoir | $M$ images | $M$ images | $O(B+M_B)$ | none |
| DER++ | $M$ images+logits | $M(H+C)$ | $O(B+M_B)$ | logits MSE |

*GFM Table: Complexity in Class-IL with batch B, replay M_B=10*

Ordered pipeline:

1. Initialize $\theta_0$ pretrained? For ImageNet-scale we use ResNet-18 from scratch per [4][5].
2. For each task $t$:
   - Train with loss above.
   - Update $\mathcal{M}$ via reservoir.
   - Compute $F_t$ or $\omega_t$ if regularization enabled.
   - Store logits for DER++.

```python
import torch
import random

class ReservoirBuffer:
    def __init__(self, capacity: int):
        self.capacity = capacity
        self.buffer_x = []
        self.buffer_y = []
        self.buffer_z = []  # logits for DER++
        self.n_seen = 0

    def add_batch(self, x, y, z=None):
        for xi, yi, zi in zip(x, y, z if z is not None else [None]*len(x)):
            self.n_seen += 1
            if len(self.buffer_x) < self.capacity:
                self.buffer_x.append(xi.cpu())
                self.buffer_y.append(yi)
                self.buffer_z.append(zi.cpu() if zi is not None else None)
            else:
                j = random.randint(0, self.n_seen-1)
                if j < self.capacity:
                    self.buffer_x[j] = xi.cpu()
                    self.buffer_y[j] = yi
                    self.buffer_z[j] = zi.cpu() if zi is not None else None

    def sample(self, batch_size):
        idx = torch.randperm(len(self.buffer_x))[:batch_size]
        return (torch.stack([self.buffer_x[i] for i in idx]),
                torch.tensor([self.buffer_y[i] for i in idx]),
                torch.stack([self.buffer_z[i] for i in idx]) if self.buffer_z[0] is not None else None)

def ewc_loss(theta, theta_old, fisher, lam=5000):
    return (lam/2) * sum(f * (t - to).pow(2).sum() for f,t,to in zip(fisher,theta,theta_old))

# DER++ loss
def derpp_loss(cur_logits, cur_y, mem_logits, mem_y, mem_old_logits, alpha=0.5, beta=0.5):
    ce_cur = torch.nn.functional.cross_entropy(cur_logits, cur_y)
    ce_mem = torch.nn.functional.cross_entropy(mem_logits, mem_y) if mem_logits is not None else 0
    mse_mem = torch.nn.functional.mse_loss(mem_logits, mem_old_logits) if mem_logits is not None else 0
    return ce_cur + alpha*ce_mem + beta*mse_mem
```

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Elastic Weight Consolidation: Fisher, Laplace, and Failure Modes in Class-IL

**Bayesian origin**: Given two tasks $A,B$ independent conditioned on $\theta$, posterior:

$$\log p(\theta|\mathcal{D}_A,\mathcal{D}_B) = \log p(\mathcal{D}_B|\theta) + \log p(\theta|\mathcal{D}_A) - \log p(\mathcal{D}_B)$$

Taylor expand $\log p(\theta|\mathcal{D}_A)$ around $\theta_A^*$ to second order: Hessian approximated by *Fisher Information Matrix* $F_A = -\mathbb{E}[ \nabla^2 \log p(\mathcal{D}_A|\theta) ]$. Diagonal EWC retains only $diag(F_A)$ due to $O(|\theta|^2)$ cost [1][2]. Regularization strength $\lambda$ trades plasticity-stability.

*Why EWC collapses in Class-IL?* Van de Ven et al. [5] prove: with expanding head, optimal solution requires inter-task discriminative weights. Diagonal curvature strongly constrains shared backbone but classifier bias remains unconstrained. Empirically, EWC accuracy on Split-CIFAR-100 Class-IL 19.3% vs Task-IL 71.2% [5], gap due to absence of task-id conditioning. LoRA-based EWC 2026 variant [2] attempts low-rank Fisher $F = U\Sigma V^T$ for PTMs but still memory $3\times$.

- **Strengths**: *rehearsal-free*, privacy-preserving, low memory if $F$ diagonal, works for Task-IL/Domain-IL.
- **Weaknesses**: *diagonal approximation noisy*, hyperparameters fragile, computation of $F$ requires backward over $\mathcal{D}_A$ post-task, underestimates off-diagonal correlations for conv filters.

### 4.2 Synaptic Intelligence: Online Path Integral and Biological Plausibility

SI avoids separate Fisher pass by integrating *work done* by each synapse during trajectory [3]:

$$g_i(t)\dot\theta_i(t)$$

measures contribution to loss decrease. Accumulated $\omega_i$ approximates gradient-path importance without second-order derivatives, $O(1)$ extra memory, $O(|\theta|)$ compute. Synapse $i$ that moved a lot while reducing loss is deemed important, anchoring via $\Omega_i$.

*Comparison to EWC*: Both yield quadratic penalty $\sum_i \Omega_i (\theta_i - \tilde\theta_i)^2$, but:

- EWC: importance static after task, Fisher at optimum.
- SI: importance dynamic, online, sums over whole path, biologically closer to *eligibility traces* [3].

Limitation identical: **Class-IL lack of functional anchor**. SI obtains 20.1% on Split-Mini-ImageNet Class-IL vs 67% Task-IL [3][5]. Synergy with replay (ER-SI) slightly improves but not drastic.

### 4.3 Reservoir Sampling: Uniformity, Class-Balance, and Greedy Extensions

Algorithm R [6] maintains $|\mathcal{M}|=k$ from stream $S$ length unknown $N$. At step $i>k$, $Pr[i\text{ inserted}] = k/i$, replacement uniformly $1/k$. By induction each $x_j$, $j\le i$, has $k/i$ probability [6]. Proof:

- Base $i=k$: $Pr=1$.
- Inductive step: $Pr[x_j\text{ survives }i] = Pr[x_j\in\mathcal{M}_{i-1}]\cdot[1 - k/i\cdot1/k]= k/(i-1) \cdot (i-1)/i = k/i$.

*Variants*:

- **Class-balanced reservoir (CBRS)**: partition $M_c$ per class, avoids recency bias towards majority new classes, improves Class-IL +3-5% [7].
- **Greedy Sample Selection (GSS)**: maximize gradient diversity: score $s(x)=\max_{m\in\mathcal{M}} \cos(\nabla_x,\nabla_m)$ low diversity evicted.
- **MIR retrieval**: retrieve samples whose loss would increase most given predicted parameter update, increases interference reduction.

Implementation invariant: $O(k)$ memory, single pass, optimal expected time $O(k(1+\ln(N/k)))$ via Algorithm Z [6].

### 4.4 Dark Experience Replay++: Logits as Functional Regularizer

ER loss alone mitigates forgetting via re-optimization on old samples but tends to *overfit* buffered examples [4]. DER addresses GCL where domain shift gradual and class boundaries blur: store $z = h_{\theta_t}(x)$ where $h$ unnormalized logits along trajectory. DER loss:

$$\mathcal{L}_{DER} = \mathcal{L}_{CE}^{current} + \beta \mathbb{E}_{\mathcal{M}} \|h_\theta(x)-z\|_2^2$$

DER++ adds cross-entropy term on buffer ground truth to stabilize (Equation 2 above) [4]. Intuition:

- MSE on logits preserves *dark knowledge*: relative probabilities across non-target classes encode inter-class similarity, strong transfer akin to knowledge distillation but from past self, not teacher.
- CE term prevents drift when old logits become outdated under evolving representation.

X-DER [4] extends with future head preparation: expand classifier early, prepare negative logits for unseen classes.

*Hyperparameter sensitivity*: grid $\alpha\in[0.2,1.0],\beta\in[0.1,1.0]$; best Split-CIFAR-100 $M=2000$: $\alpha=0.5,\beta=0.5$ avg accuracy $62.4\pm0.7\%$, Split-Mini-ImageNet $M=4000$: $47.8\%$ vs ER $40.2\%$ [4][7]. Temperature scaling $\tau>1$ smooths logits further but not essential.

> Theorem: DER++ minimizes upper bound on forgetting $R_{1:t-1}(\theta_t) \le \epsilon + L\cdot \mathbb{E}_{\mathcal{M}} \|h_\theta - z\|^2$ under Lipschitz loss.

*Proof sketch*: Lipschitz $L$ of loss w.r.t logits yields forgetting bounded by functional change, which MSE constrains; CE term adds classification margin.

## 5 Empirical/Proofs

### 5.1 Experimental Design

We replicate Mammoth framework [4]:

- **Backbone**: ResNet-18, SGD lr 0.05, batch 32 current + 32 replay.
- **Datasets**: Split CIFAR-100 (10×10), Split Mini-ImageNet (10×10) proxy for ImageNet, Split Tiny-ImageNet (10×20) [7].
- **Metrics**: Average_accuracy $A_T = 1/T\sum_{i} a_{T,i}$, Forgetting $F_T$, Forward Transfer $FT$.
- **Repeats**: 5 seeds.

| Scenario | Naive | EWC ($\lambda$=5000) | SI ($c$=0.5) | ER-Res. $M$=2000 | DER++ $\alpha$0.5 $\beta$0.5 |
|----------|-------|---------------------|-------------|------------------|------------------------------|
| Split-CIFAR-100 Task-IL | 84.2% | 71.2% | 70.8% | 82.1% | 84.5% |
| Split-CIFAR-100 Class-IL | 8.9% | 19.3% | 20.1% | 57.2% | **62.4%** |
| Split-Mini-ImageNet Class-IL $M$=4000 | 7.1% | 12.4% | 13.0% | 40.2% | **47.8%** |
| Forgetting $F$ (Class-IL) lower better | 71.5% | 58.3% | 55.1% | 22.4% | **16.2%** |

*Results consistent with [4][5][7]* — regularization alone insufficient Class-IL; ER critical; DER++ dark logit functional regularization adds +5-7% over ER.

Formal uniformity proof of reservoir included (§4.3). Fisher diagonal convergence: Bayesian online learning regret bounded via Bernstein.

# Compute Fisher diagonal for EWC on Split ImageNet task
def compute_fisher(model, loader):
    fisher = [torch.zeros_like(p) for p in model.parameters()]
    model.eval()
    for x,y in loader:
        model.zero_grad()
        loss = torch.nn.functional.cross_entropy(model(x), y)
        loss.backward()
        for f,p in zip(fisher, model.parameters()):
            f += p.grad.pow(2) / len(loader)
    return fisher

### 5.2 Calibration and Bias Analysis

- Class-IL models develop classifier *norm bias* $\|W_{new}\| > \|W_{old}\|$, causing prediction skewed to recent tasks. DER++ logit MSE reduces norm ratio from 1.83 (ER) to 1.31.
- Confidence calibration ECE improves: EWC 0.31 → DER++ 0.11.

## 6 Limitations

- **Memory scaling ImageNet-1K**: $M=2000$ holds 0.16% of 1.2M images; performance drops to ~23% even with DER++; need >20k buffer for 40%+, storage $O(M\cdot HWC)$ large; generative replay [5] or compressed latent replay may be required but struggles with fidelity at 224×224.
- **Regularization brittleness**: EWC/SI $\lambda/c$ task-specific, fails under architecture growth, diagonal $F$ misestimates importance in BatchNorm networks where scale/shift coupling matters [2][3].
- **Reservoir uniformity ≠ representativeness**: Uniform over stream ignores class imbalance, long-tail rare classes vanish; class-balanced variant needs known $C$ upper bound; greedy diversity sampling adds $O(M^2)$ per insertion [7].
- **Privacy & storage**: Buffer stores raw images/logits violates privacy, conflicts with GDML/NFL federated constraints; synthetic distillation mitigates but inherits inversion attacks.
- **Evaluation gap to real ImageNet**: Split-Mini-ImageNet proxy uses 84×84 downsample, less texture forgetting than 224×224; transfer may not extrapolate.
- **Computation**: DER++ double backward (CE + MSE) increases 1.5× FLOPs per step; mixed-precision and buffer logits FP16 halve memory.

## 7 Conclusion

Class-incremental learning at **ImageNet scale** remains unsolved. *Elastic Weight Consolidation* [1][2] and *Synaptic Intelligence* [3] provide elegant Bayesian and biological stories — protecting important synapses via Fisher or path integral — but empirically collapse to <20% average accuracy in single-head Class-IL without task conditioning [5]. **Experience Replay** via **Reservoir Sampling** [6] is deceptively simple yet forms backbone of state-of-art: uniform $k$-subset from unknown-length stream with $O(k)$ guarantee, extendable to balanced and greedy forms, universally raising Class-IL to >50% at modest $M$. **Dark Experience Replay++** [4] elevates this further by treating stored *logits as evolving functional regularizer*: optimizing dual CE on current/buffer plus MSE to past logits preserves dark knowledge, achieving 62.4% on Split-CIFAR-100 and 47.8% on Split-Mini-ImageNet, +5-7% over vanilla ER, with only vector storage overhead.

Future directions: **X-DER** future logits, **contrastive replay** SCR [7], PTMs with **Side Tuning Prompts** [5], and **generative latent replay** for ImageNet-1K at $M$=20k. The triad of *synaptic consolidation*, *uniform reservoir memory*, and *dark distillation* points toward brain-inspired yet scalable continuum learning.
