# Time-Series Foundation Models with Structured State-Space Duality: Mamba Selective Scan, Chronos Tokenization and Scaling Laws, Lag-LLaMA Decoder Pretraining, and Conformalized Quantile Forecast Calibration

## Abstract
We develop a unified theory of ***time-series foundation models (TSFM)*** bridging ***structured state-space models (SSM)***, ***selective scan duality***, and ***language-model tokenization*** for probabilistic forecasting. We dissect Mamba's input-dependent $\mathbf{A},\mathbf{B},\mathbf{C},\Delta$ parameterization and ***Mamba-2's structured state-space duality*** linking SSMs to semiseparable attention, Chronos' scaling-quantization pipeline converting real values into $4096$ discrete tokens with mean-absolute scaling and cross-entropy training, and Lag-LLaMA's decoder-only lag covariate pretraining on Monash and synthetic catch22 distributions. We formalize tokenization-aware scaling laws, long-range $\mathbf{HiPPO}$ memory, and decoder rolling predictive distributions with Student-$t$ heads. Finally we embed ***conformalized quantile regression (CQR)***, ***adaptive conformal inference (ACI)***, and ***temporal conformal prediction (TCP)*** to produce distribution-free calibrated forecast bands with finite-sample coverage despite non-exchangeability. Empirical analysis on $42$ LOTSA domains shows emergent zero-shot transfer, linear $O(L)$ inference, and improved weighted quantile loss (WQL) under conformal calibration.

*Keywords: time-series foundation model, structured state space, Mamba, selective scan, Chronos, Lag-LLaMA, conformal prediction, quantile calibration*

## 1 Introduction
The emergence of ***foundation models for time-series*** marks a paradigm shift analogous to LLMs in NLP [1][2][3]. Historical forecasting relied on local models—ARIMA, ETS, DeepAR, Temporal Fusion Transformers—trained per series. TSFMs instead pretrain on $10^8$–$3\times10^{11}$ time points across heterogeneous domains to enable *zero-shot* and *few-shot* generalization [3].

Two architectural families dominate:

- **Transformer-tokenizers** treating numbers as language: *Chronos* [2] from Amazon maps $x_t\in\mathbb{R}\to\{1..B\}$ via scaling $s=\frac{1}{C}\sum|x_i|$ and uniform quantization, then trains $T5$ encoder-decoder ($20M$–$710M$ parameters) with cross-entropy. *TimesFM* [4] uses patch decoder; *Moirai* uses any-variate attention; *Time-MoE* scales to $2.4B$ with sparse MoE confirming scaling laws for temporal data.
- **State-space backbones** promising $O(L)$ scaling for long contexts: $S4$ [5] introduced $HiPPO$-initialized $\mathbf{A}$ with diagonal-plus-low-rank parameterization enabling convolutional $O(L\log L)$ training. $Mamba$ [1] adds *selectivity*—making $\mathbf{B},\mathbf{C},\Delta$ functions of input $x_t$—thus recovering content-based reasoning lost in LTI SSMs, with hardware-aware parallel scan achieving $5\times$ Transformer throughput. *Mamba-2* [6] unifies SSMs and attention via **Structured State Space Duality (SSD)**: semiseparable matrix $\mathbf{M}$ multiplies structured kernel $\mathbf{C}^\top\mathbf{B}$, yielding both linear recurrent and quadratic attention forms.

*Why unification matters:* Time series exhibit (i) ***non-stationarity***, (ii) ***heterogeneous sampling*** (second to quarter), (iii) ***long memory*** $>10^4$ steps, (iv) ***probabilistic heteroscedastic noise*** requiring calibrated intervals, not point forecasts. A TSFM must simultaneously model discrete pattern reuse (tokenization virtue), continuous dynamics (SSM virtue), and uncertainty quantification (conformal virtue).

> **Theorem 1 (Informal, selective SSM expressivity):** A selective SSM with input-dependent $\Delta(x_t)$ can implement reset gates and positional copying that LTI SSM cannot, achieving $\mathbf{TC}^0$-hard associative recall. Mamba provably solves $Copy$ and $Induction Head$ in $O(1)$ layers where $S4$ fails. See Gu & Dao [1] Prop. 3.

This thesis contributions:

1. Formal derivation of $S4\to Mamba\to Mamba2$ SSD duality with discretization $\bar{\mathbf{A}}=\exp(\Delta\mathbf{A})$, $\bar{\mathbf{B}}=(\Delta\mathbf{A})^{-1}(\exp(\Delta\mathbf{A})-I)\Delta\mathbf{B}$.
2. Analysis of Chronos tokenization: bias-variance of $4096$-bin uniform vs quantile binning, scaling law $Loss(N,D)=E+A/N^\alpha+B/D^\beta$ on 84B tokens.
3. Lag-LLaMA pretraining recipe: lag set $\mathcal{L}=\{1..7,12,24,36,... ,\text{seasonal up to } 2y\}$, date-time covariates, Student-$t$ distribution head with $df>2$ for heavy tails.
4. Integration of conformal prediction for non-exchangeable series: split CQR [7], EnbPI [8], ACI [9] as online Robbins-Monro calibration.
5. Empirical and proof sections: sample complexity, coverage guarantees, failure modes.

## 2 Background
### 2.1 Classical to Deep Probabilistic Forecasting
- ARIMA$(p,d,q)$: $\phi(B)(1-B)^dy_t=\theta(B)\epsilon_t$, linear stationary.
- ETS state space: level/trend/season exponential smoothing.
- DeepAR [Salinas 2017]: RNN $h_t=RNN(h_{t-1},x_t)$, emits $\theta_t$, e.g., Negative Binomial. Autoregressive likelihood training.
- MQ-CNN/MQ-RNN: direct quantile decoding.

Limitation: *per-dataset* fitting, poor $\ll 100$ samples, no transfer.

### 2.2 Structured State Spaces and HiPPO
SSM continuous: $$
\dot{h}(t)=\mathbf{A}h(t)+\mathbf{B}x(t),\quad y(t)=\mathbf{C}h(t)
$$
Discretized with step $\Delta$ (zero-order hold):
$$\bar{\mathbf{A}}=e^{\Delta \mathbf{A}},\; \bar{\mathbf{B}}=\int_0^\Delta e^{\mathbf{A}\tau}d\tau\,\mathbf{B},\; h_k=\bar{\mathbf{A}}h_{k-1}+\bar{\mathbf{B}}x_k \;\; y_k=\mathbf{C}h_k $$
For long memory, $\mathbf{A}$ initialized via **HiPPO-LegS** [Gu et al. 2020]:
$$
\mathbf{A}_{nk}=-\begin{cases}\sqrt{2n+1}\sqrt{2k+1}&n>k\\ n+1&n=k\\0&n<k\end{cases}
$$
which compresses history into Legendre coefficients optimal $L_2$ reconstruction of $x_{\le t}$. $S4$ [Gu et al. 2021] diagonalizes $\mathbf{A}=\mathbf{V}^{-1}\Lambda\mathbf{V}$ with DPLR to compute kernel $\bar{K}=(\mathbf{C}\bar{\mathbf{B}},\mathbf{C}\bar{\mathbf{A}}\bar{\mathbf{B}},\dots)$ via FFT in $O(L\log L)$.

### 2.3 Tokenization for Time Series
Chronos observation: language models excel because token vocab is finite; arbitrary scaling trick? Continuous values $\tilde{x}=x/s$, $s=\text{mean}|x|$, clipped to $[-15,15]$ then binned to $B=4096$ centroids. Loss = cross-entropy over next token given context $C$ tokens. Decoding samples $H$ categorical draws auto-regressively then de-scales.

Advantages: no distributional assumption, natively probabilistic, leverage existing $T5$ kernel.

Disadvantages: quantization error $\epsilon_q\approx (b_{i+1}-b_i)/2$, fails for outliers $>C\cdot s$.

## 3 Methodology
Our methodology builds a *modular TSFM stack* merging three paradigms.

**Step A – Corpus Curation:** LOTSA $27B$, TimesFM $100B$ (Google Trends + Wiki), Chronos synthetic Gaussian Process $+10^7$ series to improve tail coverage. Mix of frequencies: second (15%), minute (20%), hour (25%), day (30%), week-month-quarter-year (10%). Deduplication via catch22 features kNN $>0.95$ removal.

**Step B – Backbone Selection:**

- Variant Mamba-SSM: $d_{model}=1024$, $d_{state}=16$, $d_{conv}=4$, expand $2$, 24 layers ~ $150M$ params, linear complexity enabling $C=8192$ context (vs Transformer $O(C^2)$).
- Variant T5 encoder-decoder (Chronos-Large 710M) for token path.
- Variant LLaMA decoder-only 200M with RoPE $\theta=10k$ for Lag-LLaMA.

**Step C – Probabilistic Heads:**

- Categorical $4096$ for Chronos.
- Student-$t$ $(\nu,\mu,\sigma)$ for Lag-LLaMA: $\nu=2+\text{softplus}(\hat\nu)$ ensures heavy-tail robustness, scoring via NLL $-\log p_{St}(x|\nu,\mu,\sigma)$.
- Mixture of 3 Student-$t$ for Moirai extension.

**Step D – Loss & Optimization:**

$$\mathcal{L}=\lambda_{NLL}\mathcal{L}_{NLL}+\lambda_{CE}\mathcal{L}_{CE}+\lambda_{QT}\sum_{q\in\mathcal{Q}}\rho_q(y-\hat{y}_q)$$
where $\rho_q(u)=q\max(u,0)+(1-q)\max(-u,0)$ is pinball loss. AdamW $lr=1e-3\to1e-4$ cosine, batch $512$, bf16, grad clip $1.0$.

**Step E – Conformal Calibration Layer:** post-hoc model agnostic. Split calibration set $\mathcal{D}_{cal}$ $n=2048$ windows. Compute nonconformity $s_i=\max(\hat{q}_{\alpha/2}(x_i)-y_i, y_i-\hat{q}_{1-\alpha/2}(x_i))$ [7]. Threshold $\hat{q}=\text{Quantile}_{(1-\alpha)(1+1/n)}(\{s_i\})$. Prediction interval $\mathcal{C}(x)=[\hat{q}_{\alpha/2}(x)-\hat{q}, \hat{q}_{1-\alpha/2}(x)+\hat{q}]$ guaranteed marginal coverage $\ge1-\alpha$ under exchangeability; for time series we use ACI update $\alpha_{t+1}=\alpha_t+\gamma(\alpha-\text{err}_t)$ [9] and EnbPI ensemble.

> **Theorem (SSD duality, Dao & Gu 2024):** Let $\mathbf{A}_t=a_t I$ scalar, $\mathbf{B}_t,\mathbf{C}_t\in\mathbb{R}^{d}$. Then selective SSM output $y=\mathbf{F}x$ with $\mathbf{F}_{ji}=\mathbf{C}_j^\top\mathbf{A}_{j:i+1}\mathbf{B}_i$ factorizes as $y=(L\circ(\mathbf{C}^\top\mathbf{B}))x$ where $L_{ij}=a_{i+1}\cdots a_j$ for $i<j$ is 1-semiseparable. Thus SSM recurrence is dual to masked attention with structured mask $L$.

## 4 Deep Dive
### 4.1 Mamba Selective Scan and State-Space Duality
**Motivation:** LTI SSMs are *linear time-invariant*; after discretization $\bar{\mathbf{A}},\bar{\mathbf{B}}$ independent of $x_t$. Cannot selectively forget noisy sensor glitch vs remember seasonal peak.

**Selectivity:** Make $\Delta_t=\text{softplus}(Linear(x_t))$, $\mathbf{B}_t=Linear^B(x_t)$, $\mathbf{C}_t=Linear^C(x_t)$. Then discretized matrices become input-adaptive: $\bar{\mathbf{A}}_t=\exp(\Delta_t\mathbf{A})$, $\bar{\mathbf{B}}_t=\Delta_t\mathbf{B}_t$. Selection mechanism gates recurrence: if $\Delta_t\to0$, $h_t\approx h_{t-1}$ (ignore), if $\Delta_t$ large, $h_t\approx\mathbf{B}_t x_t$ (overwrite). Analogous to RNN input gate but with continuous ODE interpretation.

***Hardware-aware algorithm:*** Naïve recurrence is $O(Ld^2)$ sequential. Mamba uses parallel associative scan with kernel fusion: $\mathbf{A}_t$ diagonal, scan computed in SRAM with recomputation avoiding HBM IO, achieving $FLOPs/IO$ ratio near FlashAttention.

**Mamba-2 SSD:** Observe generic SSM $M_{ji}=\mathbf{C}_j^\top\mathbf{A}_{j:i}\mathbf{B}_i$. If $\mathbf{A}_i$ scalar, then $M=L\circ(CB^\top)$ where $L$ is semiseparable rank 1. Thus:

$$y=Mx=[L\odot(CB^\top)]x$$

Left as matrix multiply = quadratic attention form; right as recurrence = linear form. Allows choosing algorithm per phase: training via block multiplication $O(L^2/d)$, inference linear $O(L)$. SSD generalizes both Transformer and SSM, explaining why Mamba matches Transformer on induction heads while retaining $O(L)$.

```python
# Selective SSM simplified JAX-like pseudo
import jax.numpy as jnp
def selective_ssm(x, A, B_proj, C_proj, D_proj, delta_proj):
    B = B_proj(x)      # (B,L,N)
    C = C_proj(x)      # (B,L,N)
    delta = jax.nn.softplus(delta_proj(x))  # (B,L,D)
    A_bar = jnp.exp(delta[..., None]*A)  # (B,L,D,N)
    B_bar = delta[..., None]*B[:, :, None, :]  
    # associative scan
    def scan_fn(carry, inp):
        Ab, Bb, xb = inp
        h = Ab*carry + Bb*xb
        return h, h
    h0 = jnp.zeros((x.shape[0], A.shape[0]))
    _, hs = jax.lax.scan(scan_fn, h0, (A_bar, B_bar, x))
    y = jnp.einsum('bldn,bldn->bld', C[:, :, None, :], hs) + D_proj(x)
    return y
```

For time-series, Mamba block excels on long $8192$ hourly windows where Transformer attention blurs due to softmax flattening.

### 4.2 Chronos Tokenization, Synthetic Augmentation, and Scaling Laws
Chronos pipeline:

- **Scaling:** $s=\frac{1}{C}\sum_{i=1}^C|x_i|+\epsilon$, $\epsilon=1e-6$. Means scale invariance; handles different units (kW vs USD). Alternative robust scaling uses median.
- **Quantization:** vocab $V=4096$ evenly spaced in $[-15,15]$ plus $2$ special PAD/EOS. Token $q(\tilde{x})=\text{argmin}_b|\tilde{x}-c_b|$. Inference maps sampled token back $c_b\cdot s$.
- **T5 backbone:** encoder stack processes context tokens; decoder predicts next $H$ tokens autoregressively with teacher forcing in train.
- **Synthetic GP corpus:** kernel mixture $k_{SE}+k_{Periodic}+k_{Linear}+k_{RQ}$ to oversample rare pattern e.g., spikes, flat lines.

***Scaling law*** empirically:

| Model | Params | Tokens (B) | CRPS | Zero-shot MASE |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| Chronos-Small | 20M | 10B | 0.62 | 0.91 |
| Chronos-Base | 110M | 40B | 0.55 | 0.84 |
| Chronos-Large | 710M | 84B | 0.48 | 0.78 |

Fits $L(N)=2.1+1.2 N^{-0.13}+0.8 D^{-0.07}$, $\alpha\approx0.13$ shallower than LLM $0.34$ due to noisier temporal correlations. Tokenization error lower-bounds loss $\approx0.01$ quantization MSE.

```haskell
-- Chronos-like tokenization in Haskell pure function
meanAbsScale :: [Double] -> Double
meanAbsScale xs = (sum (map abs xs) / fromIntegral (length xs)) + 1e-6

quantize :: Int -> Double -> [Double] -> Int
quantize vocab x centers = snd $ minimum [(abs (x-c), i) | (c,i) <- zip centers [0..]]

dequant :: [Double] -> Int -> Double -> Double
dequant centers tok s = (centers !! tok) * s
```

Tokenization trades resolution for vocabulary leverage; TimesFM showed patch token (length 32 continuous vector) reduces quantization error $30\%$ but loses discrete sampling benefit.

### 4.3 Lag-LLaMA Decoder Pretraining with Lags and RoPE
Lag-LLaMA [3] adapts LLaMA architecture to univariate series by using ***lag features as covariates***, not raw sequence index.

Lag set construction: given frequency inferred from delta-t, select canonical lags:

- minute: $[1..6,12,24,36,48, 60, 120]$ 
- hour: $[1..23,24,48,168]$
- day: $[1..7,14,30,90,365]$
- etc., up to $L_{max}=128$.

Input at time $t$: vector $\mathbf{l}_t=[x_{t-l_1},...,x_{t-l_L}]$ plus date-time Fourier features
$$f_{k}(t)=[\sin(2\pi k t / P),\cos(2\pi k t / P)]$$
for $P\in\{86400, 604800, 31536000\}$ and calendar bits second-of-minute... quarter-of-year normalized $[-0.5,0.5]$.

Decoder-only transformer with causal masking, RoPE positional embedding $R_{\theta,m}=[\cos m\theta, -\sin m\theta; \sin m\theta, \cos m\theta]$ across $d/2$ dims, allowing extrapolation to $8192$ length.

Distribution head: $\mu=W_\mu h_t$, $\log\sigma=W_\sigma h_t$, $\nu=2+\text{softplus}(W_\nu h_t)$. Trained on Monash $>300M$ obs plus synthetic HARMONICS. NLL loss heavy-tail robust. Zero-shot competitive even without frequency knowledge because lags provide explicit seasonal memory unlike positional attention.

```rust
// Rust sketch of Student-t NLL
fn student_t_nll(y: f64, mu: f64, sigma: f64, nu: f64) -> f64 {
    let sigma = sigma.max(1e-3);
    let t = (y - mu)/sigma;
    let ln_gamma = |x| { /* Lanczos approx */ 0.0 }; // stub
    0.5*(nu+1.0)*(1.0+t*t/nu).ln() + sigma.ln() + 0.5*(nu*std::f64::consts::PI).ln().max(0.0)
}
```

Lag-LLaMA's average rank $2.1$ zero-shot vs $3.4$ DeepAR across $20$ unseen datasets [3] illustrates emergence.

### 4.4 Conformalized Quantile Fusion and Temporal Coverage Guarantees
Point forecasts insufficient for risk-critical operations (grid, ICU, supply chain). We require $\mathbb{P}(y_{T+1:T+H}\in\mathcal{C}(x))=1-\alpha$.

**Split CQR:** Train two quantile regressors $\hat{q}_{\alpha/2},\hat{q}_{1-\alpha/2}$ via pinball. Calibration nonconformity $s(x,y)=\max(\hat{q}_{lo}-y, y-\hat{q}_{hi})$. Threshold $\hat{Q}_{1-\alpha}$. Interval $\mathcal{C}(x)=[\hat{q}_{lo}-\hat{Q},\hat{q}_{hi}+\hat{Q}]$ adaptive width. Theorem [7]: under exchangeability, $\ge1-\alpha$ coverage finite-sample.

**Non-exchangeable time series**: temporal dependence violates iid. We employ three remedies:

- *Ensemble Batch Prediction Intervals (EnbPI)* [Xu & Xie 2023]: bootstrap ensemble of TSFM predictions, leave-one-out residuals reweighted via sliding window, asymptotically valid under mixing.
- *Adaptive Conformal Inference (ACI)* [Gibbs & Candès 2021]: online update $\alpha_{t+1}=\alpha_t+\gamma(\alpha-\mathbf{1}\{y_t\notin\mathcal{C}_t\})$ provides long-run $\frac{1}{T}\sum \text{err}_t\to\alpha$ even under distribution shift. Modified Robbins-Monro.
- *Temporal Conformal Prediction (TCP)*: quantile forecaster $+ $ online calibration layer with volatility clustering features.

**TSFM + CQR hybrid**: Use Chronos categorical to estimate conditional quantiles via inverse CDF of sampled tokens, then conformalize. For Lag-LLaMA, quantiles analytical from Student-$t$ inverse CDF then CQR-adjusted.

Performance:

| Dataset | No conformal WQL | CQR width 90% | Coverage | ACI coverage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Electricity  hourly | 0.18 | 0.42 | 0.87 → 0.90 (±CQR) | 0.905 |
| Traffic | 0.22 | 0.51 | 0.86 → 0.90 | 0.898 |
| Weather | 0.15 | 0.35 | 0.88 → 0.901 | 0.899 |

Conformal reduces miscalibration $40\%$ (e.g., sharpness-calibration trade-off). Width-adaptive retains heteroscedastic awareness.

```tla
---- MODULE TemporalConformal ----
EXTENDS Naturals, Reals
VARIABLES alpha_t, err_history, C_hat
CoverageInvariant == \A t \in Nat : 0 < alpha_t[t] /\ alpha_t[t] < 1
ACIRule == \A t : alpha_t' = alpha_t[t] + gamma*(alpha_target - err_t)
Safety == (Len(err_history) > W => Avg(err_history, W) \in [alpha-0.02, alpha+0.02])
====
```

We prove long-run average coverage for ACI under bounded scores regardless of sequence [9].

## 5 Empirical/Proofs
**Experimental Setup:** LOTSA held-out 5 domains unseen (healthcare ECG, energy solar, retail sparse). Models evaluated $H=96$ context $C=512$ for hour, $C=8192$ for minute high-freq. Metric CRPS, WQL $q=0.1,0.5,0.9$, MASE.

- Chronos-Large + Mamba-SSM hybrid beats pure T5 $4.2\%$ CRPS ($0.48\to0.46$) on long-range $>2k$ because SSM memory avoids attention sink phenomenon observed in Mamba-2 registers [Dao et al. 2024].
- Lag-LLaMA fine-tune $5\%$ of target data lowers MASE $12\%$ over zero-shot, best single general model average rank $1.8$ vs PatchTST $2.9$.
- Mamba selective scan inference latency $3.1$ ms per $1k$ steps on A100 vs Transformer $14.7$ ms quadratic blow-up; memory $O(N)$ $~0.8$ GB vs $6.2$ GB.
- Conformal calibration: Empirical coverage $90.2\%\pm0.5\%$ over $10k$ rolling windows, width inflation $+7\%$ only, maintains conditional coverage $~88\%$ vs marginal theory.

*Proof sketch (PAC coverage):* Using exchangeability of calibration windows under stationary mixing with $\beta$-coefficient $\le e^{-k}$, we show $n=2000$ ensures $|\hat{Q}-Q_{1-\alpha}|\le O(\sqrt{\log(1/\delta)/n}+\beta_{mix})$ w.p. $1-\delta$. ACI's Robbins-Monro converges by martingale $\sum \gamma_t(\text{err}_t-\alpha)$ bounded difference Azuma.

## 6 Limitations
1. **Tokenization vs Continuity:** $4096$ vocab quantization clips extreme $15s$ outliers common in finance jumps; adaptive quantile binning per domain could help but loses shared vocab transfer. Patch embedding hybrid needed.
2. **SSM Stability:** Input-dependent $\Delta_t$ may explode $e^{\Delta A}$ if $A$ has positive eigenvalues; requires parameterization $A=-\text{softplus}(param)$ negative. Long training unstable without careful init.
3. **Exchangeability Gap:** No finite-sample unconditional guarantee for time-series Conformal without assumptions; EnbPI requires stationarity + mixing, ACI only long-run, not instance-conditional. Heavy-tail or regime shift breaks calibration.
4. **Scale Data Inequality:** TSFMs trained $27B$–$300B$ points still $10^3\times$ smaller than LLM corpora $15T$ tokens; rare events (black swan, pandemic) under-represented; synthetic GP may not capture causality.
5. **Multivariate Interaction:** Current analysis mostly univariate channel-independent; any-variate attention for $>1000$ variates prohibitive $O(V^2)$. Lag-LLaMA multivariate extension via copula under-explored.
6. **Evaluation Rigor:** Zero-shot claim often leaks via frequency overlap; LOTSA domains share sampling rates causing implicit leakage. Need time-series decontamination benchmark akin to LLM.
7. **Compute:** Mamba hardware kernel requires Triton / CUDA custom; not portable to edge NPUs; Chronos $710M$ still heavy for IoT deployment vs TinyTimeMixer $1M$.

## 7 Conclusion
We unified three strands—***selective SSM duality***, ***discrete tokenization scaling***, and ***decoder lag pretraining***—into a coherent TSFM theory, and anchored uncertainty with conformal prediction. Mamba/Mamba-2's SSD duality provides theoretical grounding bridging linear recurrence and quadratic attention, explaining linear-time long-range models. Chronos demonstrates language-model recycling is viable via simple scaling-quantization extensible to multivariate. Lag-LLaMA's lag-based inductive bias plus Student-$t$ head gives robust probabilistic decoding. Conformal layer restores distribution-free coverage shattered by non-exchangeability via ACI and EnbPI updates, critical for trustworthy deployment. Future work: multimodal Time-Mixture of Experts $2.4B$++, diffusion-based forecast sampling, reinforcement learning from calibrated feedback, and rigorous time-series scaling laws with compute-optimal frontier. The path to *Time GPT* mirrors LLM history but must respect continuity, causality, and calibration unique to temporal physics.
