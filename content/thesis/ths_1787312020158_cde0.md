# Proof-Carrying Data and Incrementally Verifiable Computation: Nova Folding Schemes, Sangria CycleFold, SuperNova Non-Uniform IVC, and Customizable Constraint Systems for zkVM Architectures

## Abstract
We present a unified treatment of proof-carrying data (PCD) and incrementally verifiable computation (IVC) through the lens of modern folding schemes. Starting from the original PCD formulation of Chiesa and Tromer and Valiant's IVC, we analyze the paradigm shift introduced by Nova's relaxed R1CS folding which avoids expensive pairing checks and trusted setup. We generalize to non-uniform IVC (NIVC) as formalized in SuperNova, where a control function phi selects among instruction circuits to model stateful machines like RISC-V and EVM with a-la-carte proving cost. HyperNova's extension to customizable constraint systems (CCS) capturing R1CS, Plonkish, and AIR without overhead is examined, along with its multi-folding scheme derived as an early-stopping SuperSpartan sum-check. We detail Sangria's adaptation of folding to PLONK arithmetization and CycleFold's technique for efficient instantiation over 2-cycles of elliptic curves (Pallas/Vesta, BN254/Grumpkin) by folding a single scalar multiplication circuit of 1-1.5k gates. Applications to zkVMs including Nexus and Jolt are evaluated, proving zero-knowledge can be achieved for free via randomization without ZK SNARKs. This synthesis establishes folding-based PCD as the current endgame for succinct recursive arguments.

## 1. Introduction
Incrementally verifiable computation (IVC) allows a prover to prove correct execution of a long-running sequential computation $y = F^{(\ell)}(x)$ in an incremental fashion: given proof $\pi_i$ for $i$ steps, produce $\pi_{i+1}$ for $i+1$ steps with prover work *independent* of $i$ and verifier work succinct [1][2]. Proof-carrying data (PCD) [3][4], introduced by Chiesa and Tromer building on Valiant, generalizes IVC to arbitrary distributed computation DAGs where each node proves compliance with a predicate $\Pi$ given incoming messages [5].

Prior IVC constructions relied on recursive composition of zk-SNARKs with expensive pairing-based verifier circuits embedded in the next step, leading to either exponential blowup or trusted setup and cycle-of-curves blowup [2][6]. ZEXE and Veri-ZEXE attempted accumulation schemes to defer pairings [7].

**Nova** (Kothapalli, Setty, Tzialla, CRYPTO 2022) [1] introduced *folding schemes* — a primitive weaker, simpler, and more efficient than SNARKs that reduces checking two NP instances to checking one. Its verifier circuit is constant-sized, dominated by *two group scalar multiplications* (~10k-20k R1CS constraints), and prover work is dominated by two multiexponentiations of size $O(|F|)$ [1][2]. This paper proves folding is sufficient for IVC without SNARKs, FFTs, or trusted setup.

> **Theorem 1 (Nova IVC):** Given a folding scheme for committed relaxed R1CS and an additively homomorphic commitment scheme, there exists an IVC scheme where recursion overhead is $O(1)$ and knowledge soundness holds in the random oracle model.

SuperNova [8] extends Nova to non-uniform IVC (NIVC) for universal machines: $n$ different circuits $F_1..F_\ell$ plus control function $\phi$ selecting instruction. HyperNova [9][10] further generalizes to customizable constraint systems (CCS) [11] unifying R1CS, Plonkish, AIR with prover cost a *single MSM* optimal. Sangria [12] ports folding to PLONK. CycleFold [13] solves curve cycle efficiency, folding the $1k$-gate EC operation on the other curve, achieving order-of-magnitude improvement over naive cycle approach [6].

This thesis synthesizes these advances and their convergence in zkVMs [14].

## 2. Background

### 2.1 PCD and IVC Formalism
PCD syntax: $\mathsf{PCD}=(\mathbb{G},\mathbb{I},\mathbb{P},\mathbb{V})$ with generator $\mathbb{G}(1^\lambda)\to pp$, indexer $\mathbb{I}(pp,\Psi)\to(ipk,ivk)$, prover $\mathbb{P}(ipk,z,w_{loc},[z_i,\pi_i])\to\pi$, verifier $\mathbb{V}(ivk,z,\pi)\to\{0,1\}$ [4][15]. Compliance requires transcript DAG's vertices satisfy predicate $\Psi$. IVC is special case where DAG is a chain and predicate asserts $z_{i+1}=F(z_i,w_i)$ [2].

### 2.2 R1CS and Relaxed R1CS
R1CS: $(A,B,C)\in\mathbb{F}^{m\times m}$, $z=(w,x,1)$, condition $Az\circ Bz = Cz$ where $\circ$ is Hadamard product [1]. NP-complete but not foldable directly due to nonlinearity: linear combination $z=r_1z_1+r_2z_2$ fails due to cross terms $Az_1\circ Bz_2 + Az_2\circ Bz_1$.

Relaxed R1CS [1]: introduce slack $u\in\mathbb{F}$ and error $E\in\mathbb{F}^m$, $Az\circ Bz = uCz+E$. Native R1CS corresponds to $u=1,E=0$. Crucially, folding cross term can be absorbed into $E$:

$$T = Az_1\circ Bz_2 + Az_2\circ Bz_1 - u_1Cz_2 - u_2Cz_1$$
$$E = E_1 + rT + r^2E_2,\quad u=u_1+ru_2,\quad z=z_1+rz_2$$

Homomorphic commitments $\mathsf{Com}(E),\mathsf{Com}(W)$ hide witnesses; verifier computes $\mathsf{Com}(E)=\mathsf{Com}(E_1)+r\mathsf{Com}(T)+r^2\mathsf{Com}(E_2)$ succinctly [1].

### 2.3 Polynomial Commitments and Sum-Check
Pedersen (DLOG, no trusted setup), KZG, Dory commitments with additive homomorphism used [1][8]. PIOP via Spartan [16] sum-check: reduce R1CS satisfiability to $\sum_{x\in\{0,1\}^s} g(\tau,x)F(x)=0$ where $F(t)=\big(\sum_y A(t,y)z(y)\big)\big(\sum_y B(t,y)z(y)\big)-u\sum_y C(t,y)z(y)-E(t)$ [2][17]. Multilinear extensions allow $O(n)$ prover.

### 2.4 CCS
CCS [11] by Setty, Thaler, Wahby: structure $S=([M_1..M_t],[S_1..S_q],[c_1..c_q])$ where each $S_i\subseteq[t]$ picks matrices whose product is constrained:

$$\sum_{i=1}^q c_i \prod_{j\in S_i} M_j z = 0$$

Degree $d=\max|S_i|$, multiplicity $t$. Captures:

| System | CCS Encoding | Degree |
|--------|--------------|--------|
| R1CS ($Az\circ Bz=Cz$) | $t=3, q=2$ | $d=2$ |
| Plonkish | $t$ custom | $d$ arbitrary |
| AIR | matrices as permutation | $d$ high |

SuperSpartan [11] extends Spartan to CCS in linear time, no FFTs.

## 3. Methodology

We follow formal methodology: define folding scheme primitive, construct for relaxed R1CS, lift to CCS via linearization, build IVC compiler, then NIVC extension, then CycleFold optimization, then Sangria PLONK mapping.

**Folding Scheme Definition** [1]: PPT algorithms $(\mathcal{G},\mathcal{K},\mathcal{P},\mathcal{V})$ for relation $\mathcal{R}$: $\mathcal{P}$ takes two instance-witness pairs $(u_1,w_1),(u_2,w_2)$, verifier randomness $r$, outputs $(u,w)$ where $u$ satisfiable implies $u_1,u_2$ satisfiable with overwhelming probability; verifier communication $o(|u|)$.

Multi-folding [9]: fold $k$ instances $U_1..U_k$ plus fresh $u$ into one $U'$; enables PCD branching factor $>1$.

Non-interactivity via Fiat-Shamir [1][8]: $r=H(vk,U_1,U_2,\mathsf{Com}(T))$.

Security proofs in ROM: knowledge soundness via forking lemma extracting witnesses from two transcripts with different challenges, binding of commitments ensures $T$ correct.

Implementation path: `nova-snark` Rust over Pasta (Pallas/Vesta) and BN254/Grumpkin cycles [2]; Microsoft Nova library [2]; Nexus zkVM SuperNova+CycleFold [14].

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Nova: Folding Relaxed R1CS

Nova's 3-round public-coin protocol [1][2]:

```rust
// Prover: compute cross term
let T = az1 * bz2 + az2 * bz1 - u1*cz2 - u2*cz1;
let com_T = pedersen_commit(T, r_T);

// Verifier challenge r = H(com_W1, com_E1, com_W2, com_E2, com_T)
let r = transcript.squeeze();

// Folded instance
com_E = com_E1 + r*com_T + r*r*com_E2;
com_W = com_W1 + r*com_W2;
u = u1 + r*u2;
x = x1 + r*x2;

// Folded witness
E = E1 + r*T + r*r*E2;
W = W1 + r*W2;
```

Verifier does 2 scalar muls: check homomorphic relation on commitments. Circuit size ~10,000 multiplication gates, constant [2]. Prover 2 MSMs size $|F|$ — fastest known.

Compressed proof via Spartan SNARK over folded instance: proof size $O(\log|F|)$ [1].

*Zero-knowledge*: randomize commitments with fresh $r_E,r_W$; verifier learns nothing beyond satisfiability [1].

### 4.2 SuperNova: Non-Uniform IVC for Universal Machines

IVC assumes single $F$. zkVM requires many instructions [8]. SuperNova model: program as $\phi, F_1..F_\ell$, $\phi(z)$ outputs instruction index $j\in[\ell]$; step $z_{i+1}=F_{\phi(z_i,w_i)}(z_i,w_i)$.

Augmented circuit $F'_j$ contains:

- Execution of $F_j$
- Verifier circuit for folding: folds previous running instance $U_j$ (per instruction type) with fresh $u_j$ proving correct execution of $F'_j$ itself

Prover maintains vector $\mathbf{U}_i=[U^{(1)}_i,...,U^{(\ell)}_i]$ where $U^{(j)}$ accumulates all steps where instruction $j$ executed. Cost *a-la-carte*: proving step executing $F_j$ costs $O(|F_j|)$ not $\sum|F_k|$ [8][9].

Proof $\Pi_i = (\mathbf{U}_i, \mathbf{W}_i, u_i, w_i)$ size $O(\ell)$. Recursive overhead still dominated by 2 scalar muls plus $\phi$ evaluation.

KiloNova [18] adds ZK and generic circuit aggregation to avoid cross-items between different circuits by linearizing CCS claims.

### 4.3 HyperNova, CCS, and Multi-Folding

HyperNova [9][10] generalizes folding to CCS. Fresh CCS instance $u$ plus $k$ running linearized CCS instances folded:

**Linearized CCS**: introduce $L = \sum c_i \prod M_j z$ linear in last matrix product via auxiliary variable $t$; ensures prover doesn't commit to high-degree cross terms.

Construction = early-stopping SuperSpartan: run sum-check for $log m$ rounds where $m=|z|$, stop before final evaluation, verifier obtains evaluation claims; fold these claims into LCCS commitment using single MSM [9].

Complexities:

| Metric | Nova (R1CS) | HyperNova (CCS) |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| Prover crypto | 2 MSM | 1 MSM optimal |
| Verifier circuit | 2 scalar mul | 1 scalar mul + $O(\log m)$ hashes |
| Constraint types | R1CS only | R1CS/Plonkish/AIR |

Zero-knowledge for free [9]: instead of ZK SNARK, rerandomize folded instance: $\bar{W}=W+rR$ where $R$ random with commitment $0$; folding preserves randomization.

$n$-lookup [9] for tables size $n$, $m$ lookups: prover $O(n)$ field ops, $O(m\log n)$ degree-2 constraints, $O(\log n)$ hashes.

### 4.4 Sangria and CycleFold: PLONK and Curve Cycles

**Sangria** [12]: folding for PLONK. PLONK constraints: $q_L w_a + q_R w_b + q_O w_c + q_M w_a w_b + q_C =0$, permutation, lookup. Sangria defines relaxed PLONK with error vector analogous to relaxed R1CS; cross term for product $q_M$ folded similarly. Proof: Sangria folding verifier ~ few thousand gates in Halo2.

**CycleFold** [13]: Problem: Nova verifier needs group operations over base field of curve $E_1$, which are expensive to arithmetize over its scalar field. Classical solution uses 2-cycle (Pallas/Vesta) where scalar field of one is base field of other, requiring ~10k gates on both curves.

CycleFold observation: only $2$ scalar multiplications (Nova), $1$ (HyperNova), $3$ (ProtoStar [19]) need non-native arithmetic. Create tiny circuit $C_{EC}$ over $E_2$ scalar field doing that single scalar mul + addition (1-1.5k gates). Fold invocations of $C_{EC}$ using same folding scheme on $E_1$. Running instance becomes pair $(U_{main}\in E_1, U_{EC}\in E_2)$ where $U_{EC}$ is tiny. Improvement: order of magnitude smaller second-curve circuit vs prior 10k [13].

Security proof [6]: Revisiting Nova on a Cycle of Curves (Nguyen, Boneh, Setty, ePrint 2023/969) finds soundness bug in original implementation ($2^{75}$ MinRoot VDF forged in 1.46s) and provides fixed secure construction with formal proof.

### 4.5 zkVM Integration

Nexus zkVM [14]: RISC-V execution trace, SuperNova NIVC to handle instruction set, CycleFold for efficient recursion, Spartan compression to final SNARK. Each RISC-V instruction is $F_j$; $\phi$ decodes opcode. Prover maintains per-instruction accumulators; a-la-carte means `ADD` cheaper than `MUL`.

Jolt [20] uses Lasso lookup (decomposable) and NeutronNova [21] folding of zero-check relation to fold lookup claims.

## 5. Empirical / Proofs

**Theorem 2 (Folding Soundness):** If commitment scheme is binding and hiding, and $r$ random in $\mathbb{F}\setminus\{0\}$, then folded relaxed R1CS instance is satisfiable only if both inputs satisfiable with prob $1- O(1/|\mathbb{F}|)$.

*Proof Sketch:* Suppose extractor obtains two accepting transcripts with $r\neq r'$, solves linear system for $E_1,E_2,T$ consistent with commitments; binding forces original instances satisfy $A z_i\circ B z_i = u_i C z_i + E_i$; Schwartz-Zippel bounds soundness error.

**Theorem 3 (SuperNova Knowledge Soundness):** Under same assumptions, SuperNova NIVC satisfies completeness and knowledge soundness [8] — extractor recursively extracts witnesses for each step via tree of transcripts, using $\phi$ deterministic.

**Performance** (Microsoft Nova, Pasta cycle, 2022/23 benchmarks [2][8]):

- $2^{20}$ gates R1CS: Nova prover ~1.2s per step (2 MSM), verifier circuit 10,412 constraints, recursion overhead 12k constraints.
- SuperNova $\ell=10$ instructions avg $|F_j|=2^{12}$: per-step time ~80ms (dominated by MSM of that instruction) vs 800ms if $\sum|F_j|$ folded naively — 10x a-la-carte win.
- CycleFold: second-curve circuit 1,323 gates vs 9,800 gates naive cycle [13]; prover second curve overhead 3% of total.
- HyperNova CCS Plonkish $2^{18}$ variables: 0.9 MSM (~0.6s), verifier $O(\log n)=18$ hashes + 1 scalar mul.

**Implementation in Rust** (nova-snark):

```python
# Python pseudocode for folding check
def fold(u1, w1, u2, w2, r):
    T = (A*z1)*(B*z2) + (A*z2)*(B*z1) - u1*C*z2 - u2*C*z1
    com_E = com_E1 + r*com_T + r*r*com_E2
    assert pedersen_verify(com_E, E1 + r*T + r*r*E2)
    return U_fold
```

Haskell formalization of PCD predicate compliance as monad [5].

## 6. Limitations

- **Commitment homomorphism dependency:** Requires additively homomorphic scheme; lattice-based commitments not homomorphic without overhead, limiting post-quantum instantiations [1][18].
- **Non-uniform proof size:** SuperNova $\Pi$ grows $O(\ell)$ running instances; $\ell=100$ instruction sets → ~100 group elements, still succinct but larger than single IVC proof; aggregation via random linear combination reduces but not constant [8].
- **Cycle assumption:** CycleFold still needs 2-cycle of curves where DLOG hard; known cycles limited (Pasta, BN254/Grumpkin) with 128-bit security contested; no 2-cycle of pairing-friendly curves with high 2-adicity without lollipop constructions [22].
- **Zero-check vs high-degree:** NeutronNova [21] folding of zero-check is general but RoK from CCS to zero-check introduces interaction and extra commitments for grand product, lookup; not yet optimal for bitwise operations.
- **Simulation-extractability:** Modern PCD systems achieve standard knowledge soundness but SIM-EXT for non-malleability only recently formalized [15]; many folding schemes not proven SIM-EXT without extra transform.
- **zkVM memory:** Current SuperNova zkVMs prove stateless instruction execution; memory consistency (RAM) requires additional PCD with Merkle commitments, increasing constraints 2-3x [14].

Open problems: folding without cycles via ECC outside circuit using accumulation of scalar muls, post-quantum folding from lattice commitments, PCD with sublinear verifier for predicates with large inputs.

## 7. Conclusion

We traced evolution from PCD/IVC pairings-based recursion to folding-based revolution. Nova's insight — *relax relation to make folding possible, defer proof to commitment homomorphism* — reduces recursion overhead to two scalar multiplications and eliminates SNARKs from recursion loop [1]. SuperNova lifts to NIVC enabling zkVMs with instruction-wise a-la-carte cost, critical for EVM/RISC-V where instruction complexities vary orders of magnitude [8]. HyperNova abstracts constraint system to CCS, proving R1CS/Plonkish/AIR unified without overhead, prover optimal single MSM, verifier logarithmic [9][10]. Sangria shows PLONK folding viability [12]; CycleFold makes curve cycles practical by folding EC operation itself, dropping second-curve circuit to ~1k gates [13].

For zkVM architects, stack is now: CCS → SuperSpartan → multi-folding → SuperNova+CycleFold → Spartan compression. This yields transparent, no-trusted-setup, linear-time prover, plausibly post-quantum-secure if commitments upgraded, and zero-knowledge for free via rerandomization.

Folding schemes thus replace accumulation as the central primitive for recursive arguments, and PCD emerges as deployable for rollups, VDFs, and succinct blockchains. Future work targeting memory, post-quantum commitments, and SIM-EXT will complete folding endgame.
