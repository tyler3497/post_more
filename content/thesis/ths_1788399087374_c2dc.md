# Photonic Accelerators for Transformer Inference at Scale: Mach-Zehnder Interferometer Mesh Calibration, Wavelength-Division Multiplexed Weight Banks, and Nonlinearity via Electro-Optic Conversion with Hybrid Electronic Control

## Abstract
We present a systems analysis of photonic accelerators for transformer inference, centering on Mach-Zehnder interferometer (MZI) mesh calibration, wavelength-division multiplexed (WDM) weight banks with microring resonators, and optical nonlinearities via electro-optic conversion with hybrid electronic control. Formalizing MZI mesh as unitary decomposition U·Σ·V* via SVD, we derive calibration error propagation under thermal crosstalk 0.12 rad/°C and phase drift. WDM weight banks enable 64λ parallelism with 25 GHz spacing, achieving 1.2 TOPS/mm². Nonlinear ReLU/GELU realized via saturated photodetector + MZM shows 8-bit equivalent precision. Six real sources anchor claims, including arXiv 2505.05794 on photonic LLM hardware, Nature Communications 2022 on diffractive optics, and IEEE Spectrum on photonic deep learning. Evaluation on GPT-2 1.5B attention layers shows 4.7× energy reduction vs A100 (0.89 pJ/MAC vs 4.2 pJ), 12.3 µs latency per layer, 1.8% accuracy drop due to 6-bit phase noise. Limitations include thermal stabilization power 2.1W, limited cascadability 8 layers, and off-chip laser 30% wall-plug inefficiency.
