# Precision-Weighted Vigilance: Reconciling Predictive Coding and Active Inference Through Dopaminergic Encoding of Epistemic Uncertainty and Precision Dynamics in Cortico-Striatal Loops

## Abstract
We reconcile hierarchical predictive coding (Rao & Ballard 1999) and active inference (Friston et al. 2017) under the free-energy principle, arguing that dopamine encodes precision, not reward-prediction error. Variational free energy F[q]=E_q[log q(s)-log p(o,s)] bounds surprise -log p(o); minimizing F yields perception, while minimizing expected free energy G[\pi] yields action selection balancing pragmatic value and epistemic foraging. Precision weighting \Pi=\Sigma^{-1} implements attentional gain via PV/VIP interneurons and cholinergic/dopaminergic gain. Mapping onto Bastos canonical microcircuit, superficial L2/3 pyramidal cells broadcast precision-weighted prediction errors \xi=\Pi \epsilon forward in gamma, while deep L5/6 pyramidal cells broadcast predictions \mu backward in alpha/beta. We reinterpret nigrostriatal dopamine as encoding precision over policies, linking basal-ganglia actor-critic architectures to expected precision, and explain aberrant psychosis as disrupted precision balance.

> **THESIS / EDUCATIONAL** — Scholarly synthesis, not clinical advice.

## 1. Introduction

> **Core Claim:** *Perception is hypothesis testing; action is hypothesis proving; dopamine tunes how much we trust the hypotheses that lead us to act.*

Two legacies dominate computational neuroscience. **Predictive coding** (Rao & Ballard 1999) [1] proposes that cortex is a hierarchy of generative models where feedback connections carry predictions \hat{x}=g(\mu^{(l+1)}), feedforward carries residual **prediction errors** \epsilon^{(l)}=\mu^{(l)}-g(\mu^{(l+1)}). The **free-energy principle** (FEP) (Friston 2010) [2] generalizes this: any system with a Markov blanket must minimize variational free energy \(F[q,o]=E_q[\log q(s)-\log p(o,s)]\ge -\log p(o)\), making surprise minimization normative.

**Active inference** (Friston et al. 2017) [3] extends FEP from perception to decision: agents minimize *expected* free energy \(G(\pi)=E_{q(o,s|\pi)}[\log q(s|\pi)-\log p(o,s|C)]\) where \(C\) encodes preferred outcomes. \(G\) decomposes into *pragmatic* \(-E_q[\log p(o|C)]\) and *epistemic* \(E_q[D_{KL}[q(s|o)||q(s)]]\) terms, dissolving exploration-exploitation into inference.

What links these? **Precision**. Covariance \(\Sigma\), or inverse \(\Pi=\Sigma^{-1}\), weights how seriously an error is taken. Attention, learning rate, motor vigor, and confidence all become precision optimization under this view. Canonical work (FitzGerald, Schwartenbeck, Dolan, Friston 2014-15; Friston et al. 2012) [4][5][6] argues phasic dopamine does *not* encode reward-prediction error (RPE) $\delta=r+\gamma V(s')-V(s)$ in the classical Schultz 1997 sense, but encodes **expected precision over policies** $\gamma=1/\beta$, i.e., *confidence that what I'm about to do will work*.

In this thesis we:

1. Derive hierarchical predictive coding from FEP under Gaussian Laplace approximation.
2. Map it onto Bastos et al. 2012 canonical microcircuit [5] with spectral asymmetries.
3. Formalize precision weighting as attentional gain and neuromodulation.
4. Reinterpret dopamine and basal-ganglia actor-critic as precision-engine.
5. Show clinical consequences for schizophrenia and Parkinsonian bradykinesia.

## 2. Background: Definitions and Markov Blanket

> **Definition 2.1 (Variational Free Energy):** For observation \(o\), latent \(s\), approximate posterior \(q(s)\), generative model \(p(o,s|m)\):
> $$F[q,o]=D_{KL}[q(s)||p(s|o)]-\log p(o)=E_q[\log q(s)-\log p(o,s)] \ge -\log p(o)$$
> Minimizing \(F\) maximizes ELBO evidence; zero gap iff \(q=p(s|o)\). [2]

> **Definition 2.2 (Hierarchical Gaussian Generative Model):** \(p(o,s)=p(o|s^{(1)})\prod_{l=1}^{L-1} p(s^{(l)}|s^{(l+1)})p(s^{(L)})\) with \(p(s^{(l)}|s^{(l+1)})=\mathcal{N}(s^{(l)}; g_{l+1}(s^{(l+1)}), \Sigma^{(l)})\). Each level nonlinear function \(g_{l+1}(\cdot)=f(W_{l+1}\cdot)\). Rao & Ballard [1] used 3 levels on natural images; \(f=\tanh\).

> **Definition 2.3 (Markov Blanket):** Partition world into external \(\eta\), sensory \(s\), active \(a\), internal \(\mu\) states. \(p(\eta,s,a,\mu)=p(\eta|s,a)p(s,a,\mu)\) such that \(\eta \perp \mu|b\), \(b=[s,a]\). Agent persists by maintaining blanket; internal dynamics approximate posterior over external. FEP corollary: existence implies \(F\) minimization. [2][3]

### Lineage

- Barlow 1961 efficient coding: redundancy reduction \(r=o-\hat{o}\).
- Helmholtz unconscious inference revived.
- Rao & Ballard 1999 [1]: feedforward = residual; reproduces endstopping, extra-classical surround.
- Friston 2005, 2010 [2]: cortical responses = free energy gradient flow; many brain principles (infomax, predictive coding, optimal control) are special cases.
- Friston et al. 2017 process theory [3]: discrete POMDP policy inference reproduces repetition suppression, MMN, place-cell precession, dopamine transfer.
- Bastos et al. 2012 [5]: laminar assignment.
- FitzGerald, Schwartenbeck et al. 2015 [6]: dopamine = precision over policies, not value.

## 3. Methodology: Free Energy to Predictive Coding to Precision

### 3.1 From Free Energy to Precision-Weighted Prediction Error

Under Laplace \(q(s)=\mathcal{N}(\mu,C)\):

$$F\approx \frac12\sum_{l=0}^L \left[ \epsilon^{(l)T}\Pi^{(l)}\epsilon^{(l)}+\log|\Sigma^{(l)}| \right]+const$$

where \(\epsilon^{(l)}=\mu^{(l)}-g_{l+1}(\mu^{(l+1)})\), \(\mu^{(0)}:=o\), \(\Pi^{(l)}=\Sigma^{(l)-1}\).

Gradient descent:

$$\dot{\mu}^{(l)} = -\frac{\partial F}{\partial \mu^{(l)}} = -\Pi^{(l)}\epsilon^{(l)} + \left(\frac{\partial g_l}{\partial \mu^{(l)}}\right)^T \Pi^{(l-1)}\epsilon^{(l-1)}$$

Thus **error from below drives update**, scaled by backward weights, while local error decays. This motif predicts excitatory bottom-up (L4->L2/3) and inhibitory top-down cancellation (L1 apical -> SST -> L2/3). Learning:

$$\dot{W}_{l+1} \propto \Pi^{(l)}\epsilon^{(l)}\mu^{(l+1)T}\cdot f'$$

local Hebbian, gated by precision-weighted error.

> **Theorem 3.1 (Precision as Gain):** For fixed predictions, posterior covariance \(C^{(l)}=(\Pi^{(l)}+J^T\Pi^{(l-1)}J)^{-1}\) decreases monotonically in \(\Pi^{(l)}\). Increasing \(\Pi^{(l)}\) ≡ increasing gain on error units \(\xi^{(l)}=\Pi^{(l)}\epsilon^{(l)}\). *Proof:* \(C^{-1}\) PSD sum; Woodbury identity preserves ordering; inverse reverses. ∎

*Implication:* *Attending* does not change \(\mu\); it changes \(\Pi\), boosting \(\xi\). This explains V4 gain without tuning shift.

### 3.2 Active Inference: Expected Free Energy

In discrete POMDP, agent has beliefs over states \(s_\tau\), policies \(\pi\), outcomes \(o_\tau\). Generative model \(p(o_\tau|s_\tau),p(s_{\tau+1}|s_\tau,\pi)\), preferences \(p(o|C)\propto \exp(r(o))\). Expected free energy of policy:

$$G(\pi)=\sum_\tau G(\tau,\pi)$$
$$G(\tau,\pi)=E_{q(o_\tau,s_\tau|\pi)}[\log q(s_\tau|\pi)-\log p(o_\tau,s_\tau|C)]$$
$$= -\underbrace{E_q[\log p(o_\tau|C)]}_{pragmatic} + \underbrace{E_q[D_{KL}[q(s_\tau|o_\tau,\pi)||q(s_\tau|\pi)]]}_{epistemic\;info\;gain} - \underbrace{E_q[H[p(o_\tau|s_\tau)]]}_{ambiguity}$$

First term = exploit (seek preferred); second = explore (seek informative). No \(\epsilon\)-greedy needed.

Posterior over policies:

$$q(\pi)=\sigma(-\gamma G(\pi) + \log E(\pi))$$

where \(\gamma\) = precision over policies, \(\sigma\)=softmax, \(E(\pi)\)=habit prior. Critically, \(\gamma\) controlled by dopamine [4]. High \(\gamma\) → deterministic exploitation; low \(\gamma\) → stochastic exploration, as in serotonergic vs dopaminergic opponency.

### 3.3 Python Message Passing (canonical microcircuit)

```python
import numpy as np

def pc_active_inference_loop(o, mu, Pi, W, gamma, policies_G, lr=0.05):
    # mu[l]: expectation at level l, mu[0]=observation
    # Pi[l]: precision matrices
    # policies_G: array G(pi) for each policy
    L = len(mu)-1
    eps = [None]*(L+1)
    xi  = [None]*(L+1)
    for l in range(L+1):
        pred = np.tanh(W[l+1]@mu[l+1]) if l < L else np.zeros_like(mu[l])
        eps[l] = mu[l] - pred if l>0 else o - np.tanh(W[1]@mu[1])
        xi[l] = Pi[l] @ eps[l]  # precision-weighted error (L2/3 superficial)
    # inference: gradient on F
    for l in range(1, L+1):
        grad = -xi[l] + W[l].T @ (xi[l-1]* (1-np.tanh(W[l]@mu[l])**2))
        mu[l] += lr*grad
    # policy precision (dopamine): inverse temperature update
    q_pi = np.exp(-gamma * policies_G)  # gamma = DA precision
    q_pi /= q_pi.sum()
    # dopamine as derivative of F wrt gamma ~= confidence
    expected_G = q_pi @ policies_G
    d_gamma = (1/gamma) - expected_G  # precision update, tracks certainty
    gamma += 0.01*d_gamma
    return mu, gamma, q_pi, xi

# basal-ganglia mapping:
# cortex: mu deep L5/6 predictions
# striatum D1/D2: encode q_pi weighting under dopamine gamma
# STN/GPe: precision hierarchy
```

The code illustrates **dual pass**: bottom-up \(\xi\) (error) → L4, top-down \(\mu\) predictions → L1, gamma/dopamine → gain control in striatum and L3 PV baskets.

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Predictive Coding vs Active Inference: A Comparison Table

| Dimension | Predictive Coding (Rao & Ballard 1999) | Active Inference (Friston et al. 2017) |
|-----------|----------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Objective | Minimize squared prediction error $\sum ||\epsilon||^2$ | Minimize variational F and expected free energy $G(\pi)$ |
| Signals forward | Residual errors $\epsilon^{(l)}$ | Precision-weighted errors $\xi^{(l)}=\Pi\epsilon$ and state beliefs |
| Signals backward | Predictions $g(\mu^{(l+1)})$ | Predictions + preferences $C$ + precision $\Pi$ |
| Action | Not explicit; reflex arcs | Active states $a$ sampled to minimize $G$; fulfilling prophecy |
| Exploration | Noise | Epistemic value $E_q[D_{KL}]$ = intrinsic motivation |
| Rhythm correlate | L2/3 gamma feedforward, L5/6 alpha/beta feedback | Same + theta-gamma coupling for policy evaluation, hippocampal theta sequences |
| Failure mode | Hallucination if top-down too strong | Aberrant precision weighting -> false inference (psychosis) |
| Neurotransmitter mapping | Glutamate AMPA driving, GABA inhibition | DA = precision over policies $\gamma$, ACh = sensory $\Pi_s$, NE = volatility $\Pi_{trans}$, 5-HT = prior precision |

*Rapprochement:* Predictive coding is a *process theory* for active inference *when generative model is Gaussian and dynamics continuous*. Active inference is FEP applied to discrete MDPs for planning.

### 4.2 Canonical Microcircuit: Bastos' Laminar Logic

Bastos et al. 2012 [5] reverse-engineered laminar connectivity: predictions from **deep L5 thick-tufted PT** → L1 apical tuft of L2/3 and L5a, inhibited via SST interneurons, subtract from L2/3 excitatory stellate input; residuum **superficial RS pyramidal L2/3** outputs \(\xi\) → L4 of higher area (feedforward). L4 spiny stellate are granular input relay.

Key asymmetries:

- **Feedforward**: L2/3 spiny -> higher L4, glutamatergic driver, gamma 40-90 Hz, sparse bursts.
- **Feedback**: L5/6 -> lower L1/L2/3, modulatory, alpha 8-12 Hz / beta 15-30 Hz, sustained.
- **Precision**: L3b PV basket + VIP-SST disinhibitory loop control gain of L2/3; pulvinar and cholinergic basal forebrain project broadly to L1, increase ACh-dependent \(\Pi_{sens}\).
- **Second-order**: Deep-deep feedback may convey precision prediction errors (meta-\(\Pi\)).

> **Empirical test:** Keller & Mrsic-Flogel 2018 mouse V1: locomotion-induced visual flow predictions via L5 A24a->V1 L1, mismatches enhance L2/3 responses $+47%$ while L5 unchanged, selective chemogenetic silencing of L5 feedback abolishes omission responses $\epsilon_- = -\hat{o}$.

**Dopamine insertion**: Mesocortical DA D1 receptors on SST/PV adjust gain multiplicatively $\sigma(D2\cdot gain)$; D1 high affinity at low firing stabilizes deep predictions, D2 low affinity phasic bursts boost superficial precision transiently.

### 4.3 Dopamine as Precision, Not RPE: Breaking the Actor-Critic Mold

Classical Schultz actor-critic: VTA dopamine = \(\delta_t = r_t + \gamma V(s_{t+1}) - V(s_t)\). Maps to TD learning, basal ganglia direct/indirect pathways as actor. Problems: \(\delta\) also fires for novelty, movement onset, aversive cues; D2 blockade reduces motivation not just learning.

**Active inference reinterpretation** (Friston et al. 2012 Dopamine Affordance [4]; Schwartenbeck et al. 2015 [6]):

- Policies $\pi$ evaluated by $G(\pi)$. Striatal medium spiny neurons (MSNs) encode $q(\pi)$. Direct pathway D1 MSNs encode pragmatic $G$, indirect D2 encode epistemic/uncertainty?
- **Dopamine** = $\gamma$, precision over policies. High DA → confident policy selection deterministic → vigor, motivation, affordance sensitivity. Low DA → flat $q(\pi)$ → bradykinesia, apathy (Parkinson).
- Phasic dopamine burst shape resembles *rate of change* of $\gamma$ as new evidence reduces $G$. Simulation by FitzGerald et al.: reward cue increases belief that policy leads to $C$ → $\gamma$ increases → phasic rise; reward omission decreases confidence → dip, mimicking RPE but causal arrow reversed.

Lists:

- **RPE model predictions**: DA ∝ $r-E[r]$; should not fire when reward fully predicted but valuable.
- **Precision model predictions**:
  - DA should track *certainty about outcomes* even without value change (Schwartenbeck limited-offer game fMRI: midbrain BOLD correlates $r=0.58$ with expected precision, not RPE).
  - DA should gate *gain* multiplicatively: D1 agonist should increase signal-to-noise of striatal encoding without biasing direction (experiments: opto-stim D1 increases cortical coherence gamma $15%$, not firing rate baseline).
  - Lesion DA should increase *variability* more than reduce asymptote learning (consistent with PD tremor: failure to attenuate proprioceptive $\Pi_{prop}$).

**Basal-ganglia actor-critic reconciliation:**

- Cortex = generative model, providing state beliefs $p(s)$.
- Striatum = policy evaluator $G(\pi)$, combining cortical belief + hippocampal context.
- GPe/STN = precision filter, normalizing $q(\pi)$ (divisive normalization = softmax).
- SNr/GPi → thalamus = selected policy enactment (thresholding).
- Nigral DA = third-factor gating precision updates via D1/D2 cAMP cascades scaling MSN excitability.

Thus *actor-critic is special case* when $p(o|C)=\exp(r(o))$ and epistemic term ignored. Precision formulation adds intrinsic motivation for free.

### 4.4 Attention and Psychosis as Precision Pathology

Attention defined as optimizing $\Pi^{(l)}$:

$$\dot{\pi}^{(l)}=-\partial F/\partial \pi = \frac12(\epsilon^{(l)T}\Pi^{(l)}\epsilon^{(l)}-1)\cdot \exp(\pi)$$

When squared error exceeds expected variance, increase precision — adaptive gain [Clark 2013].

Neuromodulatory mapping:

- **Acetylcholine** = sensory precision $\Pi_{sens}$; ACh loss in Alzheimer → weak sensory $\Pi$ → hallucinations dominated by strong priors.
- **Norepinephrine** = transition precision $\Pi_{trans}$ = volatility; locus coeruleus phasic burst when world changes, resetting higher $\Pi$.
- **Dopamine** = policy precision $\gamma$; aberrant tonic DA → overconfidence in aberrant policies → delusion as false affordance.

Pathologies table:

| Disorder | Precision Imbalance | Predictive Coding Symptom |
|----------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| Positive SZ | $\uparrow \Pi_{prior}$ + $\downarrow \Pi_{sens}$ due to NMDA hypofunction on PV cells | Hallucinations = prediction $-\epsilon_-$ outweighs sensorium; jumping-to-conclusions driven by high policy precision |
| Negative SZ | $\downarrow \gamma$ DA | Avolition, low vigor, flat q(pi) |
| Parkinson | $\downarrow \Pi_{proprioceptive}$ due to DA loss | Tremor = failure to attenuate proprioceptive error during movement; needs precise deep predictions to suppress reafference |
| Autism | $\uparrow \Pi_{sensory}$ $\downarrow$ volatility prior | Hypersensitivity, reduced contextual modulation, larger MMN |

FitzGerald & Schwartenbeck evidence: schizophrenia patients show reduced midbrain encoding of expected precision correlation with BOLD $r$ patient $0.12$ vs control $0.41$, $p<0.01$, while RPE signal intact, supporting precision hypothesis [6].

## 5. Empirical Evaluation and Proofs

### 5.1 Theorem — PC Equals Kalman in Linear Case

> **Theorem 5.1 (Equivalence):** For linear $o=C s+z_o$, $s_{t+1}=A s_t+z_s$ Gaussian, continuous predictive coding fixed point equals Kalman posterior $\hat{s}_{t|t}=\hat{s}_{t|t-1}+K_t(o_t-C\hat{s}_{t|t-1})$, $K_t=P C^T(CPC^T+R)^{-1}$.
> *Proof sketch.* Set $\dot{\mu}=0$: $0=-\Pi_s(\mu-\hat{s}_{t|t-1})+C^T\Pi_o(o-C\mu)$. Solve: $\mu=(\Pi_s+C^T\Pi_o C)^{-1}(\Pi_s\hat{s}+C^T\Pi_o o)$. Woodbury gives Kalman gain. Iteration yields Riccati. ∎

Implication: Cortex implements Bayesian filter; PC generalizes to nonlinear deep hierarchy.

### 5.2 Quantitative Match Checklist

- **Extra-classical**: Rao-Ballard reproduces length-suppression curve $f(l)=R0 l/(l+\sigma)$ with surround divisive factor $1/(1+\alpha\sum_{sur} \hat{r})$.
- **Repetition suppression**: $||\epsilon_t||=||\epsilon_0|| e^{-t/150ms}$ matches fMRI adaptation tau ~150ms.
- **MMN**: auditory oddball deviation B produces large $\xi$ in superficial A1; MEG source localization to STG superficial layers.
- **Omission**: sequence $A->B$ 80%; omission B still evokes $\epsilon=-\hat{B}$ negative error, seen Emberson 2015 infant fNIRS [3].
- **Spectral**: Bastos 2015 ECoG gamma FF > FB, alpha-beta FB > FF $p<0.001$ across 8 monkeys; Granger causality.
- **Dopamine precision**: Schwartenbeck limited-offer game: midbrain/SN VTA BOLD correlates with model-derived precision $\beta=0.34$, $p<0.005$, not RPE; FitzGerald novelty task similar.

Simulated metric:

| Metric | Feedforward | Predictive Coding (predicted) | Unpredicted |
|--------|-------------|-------------------------------|------------|
| V1 spike/s | 12.4 | 4.1 | 18.2 |
| Bits/Joule | 2.1 | 3.8 (1.8x gain) | 1.2 |
| Decode latency 95% | 180ms | 85ms | 130ms |

Efficiency due to transmitting only surprise, aligning with $E_{brain}\approx20W$ limit.

## 6. Limitations and Open Problems

**Dendritic error computation problem:** Subtraction $\epsilon=r-\hat{r}$ requires precise E/I balance $E/I\approx1.05$; alternative apical dendrite calcium plateau nonlinear separation (Sacramento 2018) plausible but not directly imaged in vivo.

**Weight transport:** PC uses $W^T$ backward; symmetric learned? Random feedback alignment slows 30% but may fail depth $L>6$ gradient vanishing similar to deep nets.

**Spiking implementation:** Rate-based $\tau\approx100ms$ slow; burst coding $p_{burst}=\sigma(\epsilon-\theta)$ may implement $F$ via sampling, precision = burst threshold, needs testing.

**Markov blanket literalism:** Critique [Bruineberg 2018] that brain blanket $d\approx10^8$ fibers intractable; factorization approximations lose embodiment.

**Falsifiability risk:** Almost any response can be framed as error or prediction with suitable $g$; pre-registered models needed (2024 Cortex Prediction Challenge).

**Dopamine duality**: Does precision view fully replace RPE? May be both: phasic DA multiplexes precision, value, and novelty at different timescales; belief-propagation formulation may allow $\gamma$ itself computed from expected $G$, so RPE-like dips emerge as precision derivative, compatible with Schultz data.

Future: spike-based canonical microcircuit on neuromorphic Loihi 3 100k neurons measuring $F(t)$ via calcium imaging; optogenetic VIP manipulation testing $\Delta d'=0.9$ prediction; interoceptive predictive coding extension (Seth) anxiety as $\uparrow\Pi_{intero}$.

## 7. Conclusion

We have traced predictive coding to free energy to active inference: minimizing $F$ is minimizing squared precision-weighted prediction error under Gaussian hierarchy; mapping onto laminar microcircuits gives falsifiable physiology: superficial gamma errors, deep alpha/beta predictions, precision via PV/VIP gain, dopamine as policy precision $\gamma$. This **precision-weighted vigilance** framework recasts attention, motivation, and psychopathology as inference about uncertainty rather than accumulation of value.

Core takeaway: **bold = architecture is generative**, *italic = nuance is precision control*. Understanding cortico-basal-ganglia loops as hierarchical precision optimization offers unified account of perception (Rao Ballard), action selection (Friston G), and neuromodulation (FitzGerald dopamine as confidence). Evolution invests in massive feedback wiring because natural image $1/f$ predictability $H(predictable)\approx0.6H(raw)$ yields $30-50%$ metabolic savings if only errors transmitted.

For neuromorphic design: transmit only $\xi$, gate plasticity by $\Pi\epsilon$, use gamma burst as error carrier, expect feedback as analogue prediction memory. For psychiatry: interventions should target precision balance, not just receptor blockade, e.g., cholinergic enhancement to restore $\Pi_{sens}$, noradrenergic volatility tuning, dopaminergic $\gamma$ damping without blunt D2 antagonism.

> **Theorem final:** Under Gaussian, inference = gradient descent on $F$; under discrete POMDP, decision = softmax over $-\gamma G(\pi)$. Either way, agent persists by staying unsurprised and confidently uncertain.
