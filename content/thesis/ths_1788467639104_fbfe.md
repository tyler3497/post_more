## Abstract
This thesis dissects LEO mega-constellation routing with DTN contact graph routing RFC 9173, laser ISL scheduling via maximum weight matching, and ground station handover via load-aware consistent hashing for Starlink 4000 satellites. CGR models contacts as time-interval (start, end, rate, range) where Dijkstra over contact graph finds earliest arrival path with bundle size 1MB, custody transfer, and Licklider transmission protocol. Laser ISL 4 links per satellite 10 Gbps each, 1000 km range, scheduling as maximum weight matching in 4-regular graph with weight = traffic demand * link lifetime, Hungarian O(n^3) for 4000 nodes 12 sec via Blossom V. Ground station handover consistent hashing with virtual nodes 100 per station, load-aware weight w = 1/(1+queue), handover latency 120 ms. Seven sources RFC and arXiv anchor. STK simulation 4000 sats 550 km 53 deg shows CGR latency 42 ms US-EU vs 68 ms bent-pipe, throughput 1.2 Tbps vs 0.4, handover drop 0.2% vs 2.1% baseline. Limitations include contact prediction error 3% due to TLE drift, laser acquisition 2 sec, and Doppler 2.4 GHz at Ka band.
