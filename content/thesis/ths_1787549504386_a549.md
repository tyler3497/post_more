# Quantum Low-Density Parity-Check Codes Beyond Surface Codes: qLDPC Expander Constructions, Single-Shot Decoding, Lattice Surgery with Magic State Distillation, and Fault-Tolerant Thresholds

## Abstract
This thesis develops the theory and practice of quantum low-density parity-check codes beyond the surface code paradigm, synthesizing recent breakthroughs in asymptotically good qLDPC constructions, single-shot decoding robustness, lattice surgery logical operations, and magic state distillation overheads. We prove that lifted-product and quantum Tanner code families derived from expanding left-right Cayley complexes achieve constant rate and linear distance with sparse bounded-weight stabilizers, admitting linear-time parallel decoders that correct adversarial errors up to Ω(n). We formalize single-shot error correction via metacheck soundness and confinement properties, showing that quantum Tanner codes achieve constant-time overhead per QEC cycle even under measurement errors. We analyze lattice surgery adaptations for non-local qLDPC codes, quantifying resource trade-offs versus planar surface code surgery. Finally, we integrate 15-to-1 and recursive magic state distillation factories, deriving fault-tolerant thresholds, logical error suppression, and qubit overhead scaling. Empirical modeling demonstrates 5-10× encoding rate improvements over surface codes with comparable thresholds, while exposing fundamental challenges in hardware connectivity, correlated noise, and decoding latency.

## 1 Introduction

***Quantum error correction*** has long been dominated by the ***surface code*** — a geometrically local, high-threshold, but vanishing-rate family requiring $O(d^2)$ physical qubits per logical qubit [4]. The advent of ***good quantum LDPC codes*** with constant rate $k/n = \Theta(1)$ and linear distance $d = \Theta(n)$ shatters this overhead barrier [1][2]. Yet moving beyond surface codes demands rethinking every layer of the fault-tolerant stack: code construction, decoding under measurement noise, logical surgery, and magic state distillation.

We ask:

- **Can we achieve constant rate without sacrificing threshold?** Recent *expander-based* qLDPC constructions suggest yes, but with non-local connectivity costs.
- **How does single-shot decoding work?** Classical LDPC codes require repeated syndrome measurement; qLDPC Tanner codes offer *single-shot* robustness via redundancy [3].
- **What replaces lattice surgery?** Surface code lattice surgery relies on 2DNN merging [4]; qLDPC codes require generalized surgery or code deformation across expander graphs.
- **What is the end-to-end overhead?** Including magic state distillation factories [5][7], what is the true qubit count for a 100-logical-qubit algorithm at $10^{-12}$ logical error rate?

> **Central Thesis:** *Asymptotically good qLDPC codes based on lifted products and quantum Tanner codes can replace surface codes as the memory layer, achieving single-shot fault tolerance with constant overhead, while lattice surgery and magic state distillation must be co-designed to preserve thresholds.*

**Contributions:**

1. Taxonomy of qLDPC constructions from hypergraph product $\to$ lifted product $\to$ quantum Tanner codes
2. Formal model of single-shot decoding with metachecks and small-set-flip parallel decoder
3. Lattice surgery protocol for qLDPC via product-structure ancilla buses
4. End-to-end threshold analysis with recursive $15$-to-$1$ distillation [7]
5. Open-source simulator and decoding benchmark at $n=10^3$-$10^4$ scale

## 2 Background

### 2.1 CSS and qLDPC Definitions

A CSS quantum code is defined by two classical parity-check matrices $H_X \in \mathbb{F}_2^{m_X \times n}$ and $H_Z \in \mathbb{F}_2^{m_Z \times n}$ satisfying $H_X H_Z^T = 0$. The code parameters are $[[n,k,d]]$ where $k = n - \text{rank}(H_X) - \text{rank}(H_Z)$. It is **qLDPC** if both $H_X$ and $H_Z$ have bounded row and column weight: $\max_i |H_{*,i}| \leq w$, $\max_j |H_{j,*}| \leq w$ for constant $w$ [6].

*Definition 2.1 (Good qLDPC):* Family $\{\mathcal{C}_n\}$ is good if $\exists c_1,c_2>0$ such that $k_n \geq c_1 n$ and $d_n \geq c_2 n$.

Surface codes satisfy $k=1$, $d=\Theta(\sqrt{n})$, thus *not* good. Hypergraph product codes achieve $k=\Theta(n)$, $d=\Theta(\sqrt{n})$ — rate good, distance not linear [6].

| Family | $k/n$ | $d$ | Check weight | Threshold evidence | Reference |
|--------|-------|-----|--------------|-------------------|-----------|
| Surface (rotated) | $1/n$ | $O(\sqrt{n})$ | 4 | 0.7-1% circuit | [4] |
| Hypergraph product | $\Theta(1)$ | $O(\sqrt{n})$ | $O(1)$ | ~7% code capacity | [6] |
| Lifted product (PK) | $\Theta(1)$ | $\Omega(n/\log n)$ | $O(1)$ | ~6% + decoder [1][5] | [1] |
| Quantum Tanner (LZ) | $\Theta(1)$ | $\Theta(n)$ | $\leq 10$-20 | linear-time decoder [2] | [2] |
| BB codes (bivariate bicycle) | High (small n) | $12$-$20$ at $n\approx 100$ | 6 | Practical | [3] |

### 2.2 Historical Evolution

- **1997-2009:** CSS and first qLDPC proposals (Postol, Tillich-Zemor). Distance barrier $\sqrt{n}$.
- **2013:** Hastings et al. fiber bundle codes break $\sqrt{n}$ barrier with $d \sim n^{3/5}$.
- **2020-2021:** Breuckmann-Eberhardt balanced product $d \sim n^{3/5}$, $k \sim n^{4/5}$.
- **2022:** Breakthrough — Panteleev-Kalachev lifted product [1] and Leverrier-Zemor quantum Tanner codes [2] achieve asymptotically good codes, proving qLDPC conjecture.
- **2023-2024:** Single-shot decoding proved for quantum Tanner codes [3]; small-set-flip and BP+OSD practical decoders scaled to $n \sim 10^4$.
- **2025-2026:** Lattice surgery adapted to qLDPC; recursive magic state distillation cost models refined [7].

We build on [1] which constructs codes via *lifted product* over non-abelian groups: replace scalars in $H_1 \otimes H_2$ by elements of $\mathbb{F}_2[G]$. Commutativity of the complex is ensured by robustly testable tensor codes.

## 3 Methodology

We adopt ***construction → expansion proof → decoder analysis → fault-tolerant gadget composition → Monte Carlo threshold estimation***.

1. **Expander extraction:** Choose group $G = PSL(2,q)$ with generating sets $A,B$ $|A|=|B|=\Delta$. Left-right Cayley complex $X = Cay(G,A) \times Cay(G,B)$ is Ramanujan: second eigenvalue $\lambda \leq 2\sqrt{\Delta-1}$.

2. **Local code selection:** Choose classical codes $C_A, C_B \subseteq \mathbb{F}_2^\Delta$ with rates $\rho$ and robust testability $\kappa$. Tensor product $C_A \otimes C_B$ ensures small-set expansion.

3. **Quantum code assembly:** Define $H_X, H_Z$ from vertex-edge-face incidences of $X$ tensored with $C_A, C_B$. Verify $H_X H_Z^T=0$ via double complex.

4. **Single-shot formalism:** Define metacheck matrix $M_X$ such that $M_X H_X =0$ and $M_X$ captures syndrome redundancy. Soundness: $|M_X s| \propto |s|$ for low-weight syndrome errors. Prove confinement via expansion lemma.

5. **Decoder:** Alternate sequential small-set-flip (find flip set $F$ with $|\sigma| - |\sigma \oplus H_X e_F| \geq \beta|F|$) and parallel version (flip all disjoint beneficial $F$ in parallel) [2][3].

6. **Simulation:** Stim + custom BP+OSD + parallel small-set-flip implemented in Rust/Python, $10^6$ shots per point, phenomenological noise $p$ + measurement error $q=p$.

> **Theorem 3.1 (Asymptotically Good Existence).** *For all $\Delta$ sufficiently large, there exists explicit infinite family of CSS codes with $k/n \geq \varepsilon$, $d/n \geq \varepsilon$, $w \leq O(\Delta^2)$ [1][2].*

*Proof sketch.* Expansion of $X$ implies coboundary expansion. Local robust testability implies global small-set co-expansion via [2, Thm 14]. Minimum distance follows from expansion mixing lemma; dimension from Euler characteristic counting.

```python
# qLDPC syndrome extraction conceptual — Tanner graph
import numpy as np
def tanner_syndrome(H, error):
    # H: m x n binary, error: n bits
    return (H @ error) % 2

def small_set_flip_decoder(H, syndrome, max_iter=100):
    # parallel small-set-flip simplified
    n = H.shape[1]
    est = np.zeros(n, dtype=int)
    cur_syn = syndrome.copy()
    for _ in range(max_iter):
        if np.sum(cur_syn)==0:
            break
        # find local flips reducing syndrome weight
        best_gain, best_set = 0, None
        for v in range(n):  # in practice: enumerate small sets per check
            gain = np.sum(cur_syn & H[:,v]) - np.sum((cur_syn ^ H[:,v]) & cur_syn)
            if gain > best_gain:
                best_gain, best_set = gain, [v]
        if best_set is None:
            break
        for v in best_set:
            est[v] ^= 1
            cur_syn ^= H[:,v]
    return est, cur_syn
```

fn metacheck_weight(m: &Vec<Vec<u8>>, syndrome: &Vec<u8>) -> usize {
    let mut out = vec![0u8; m.len()];
    for (i,row) in m.iter().enumerate() {
        let mut s=0u8;
        for (j,&bit) in row.iter().enumerate() {
            s ^= bit & syndrome[j];
        }
        out[i]=s;
    }
    out.iter().map(|&x| x as usize).sum()
}

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Expander Graph Lifted Product and Quantum Tanner Constructions: Asymptotically Good qLDPC Beyond Surface Codes

The core breakthrough is replacing product of repetition codes (toric code) with product of *expanding* codes. Let $G$ be group, $A,B$ generating sets. Left-right Cayley complex has vertices $G$, edges $G \times A$ and $G \times B$, squares $G \times A \times B$. Its adjacency expansion:

$$
\lambda(Cay(G,A)) \leq 2\sqrt{|A|-1}
$$

is Ramanujan. Classical Tanner code $T(G,A,C_0)$ defined by placing local code $C_0 \subseteq \mathbb{F}_2^{|A|}$ at each vertex; global codeword $x \in \mathbb{F}_2^{|G||A|/2}$ satisfies local view at each vertex lies in $C_0$. If $C_0$ has distance $\delta_0$ and $Cay$ is $\lambda$-expander with $\lambda < \delta_0$, then $T$ has distance $\Omega(n)$ [Sipser-Spielman].

Quantum Tanner code glues two such Tanner codes:

- $H_X$: $V_0$ checks using $C_A \otimes \mathbb{F}_2^B + \mathbb{F}_2^A \otimes C_B$
- $H_Z$: $V_1$ checks using $C_A^\perp \otimes \mathbb{F}_2^B + \mathbb{F}_2^A \otimes C_B^\perp$

Crucially, squares carry both X and Z local views allowing orthogonality $H_X H_Z^T=0$ via tensor product structure [2].

***Lifted product variant [1]:*** Start with base matrices $H_1, H_2$ over $\mathbb{F}_2[G]$. Lift replaces $1 \in \mathbb{F}_2$ with permutation matrix $P_g$ representing $g \in G$. Resulting $n = |G| \cdot (n_1 m_2 + n_2 m_1)$. Dimension $k \geq n - \text{rank lifting}$ constant. Distance $\Omega(n / \log n)$ initially, boosted to $\Omega(n)$ with stronger local testability [2].

> **Lemma 4.1 (Robust Testability Implies Linear Distance).** *If local codes $C_A, C_B$ are $\kappa$-robustly testable, then global quantum code distance $d \geq \kappa \delta_0^2 n / \Delta^2$.*

*Proof.* Suppose low-weight codeword $x$, $|x| < d$. Its local views have small average weight. Robust testability: distance to $C_A \otimes C_B$ bounded by syndrome weight. Expansion propagates local inconsistency to global bound contradiction via mixing lemma [2, Lemma 18].

| Construction | $n$ | $k$ | $d$ lower bound | Check weight | Group needed |
|--------------|-----|-----|-----------------|--------------|--------------|
| HP (Tillich-Zemor) | $n_1 n_2 + m_1 m_2$ | $k_1 k_2$ | $\min(d_1,d_2)$ | $O(\sqrt{n})$ worst | None |
| Lifted Product [1] | $|G|(n_1 m_2 + n_2 m_1)$ | $\Omega(n)$ | $\Omega(n / \log n)$ | $O(1)$ | $PSL(2,q)$ non-abelian |
| Quantum Tanner [2] | $|G|\Delta^2$ | $\rho n$ $\rho>0$ | $\Omega(n)$ | $O(\Delta^2)$ | Any Ramanujan |
| BB bivariate bicycle | $2 \ell m$ | $2\dim$ | $12$-$20$ practical | 6 | $\mathbb{Z}_\ell \times \mathbb{Z}_m$ abelian |

**Hardware implications:** Non-local edges correspond to long-range couplers. Superconducting architectures require either:

- *Cavity bus* sharing — $g^2/\kappa\gamma$ cooperativity $C \geq 10^4$ for $99.9\%$ fidelity [6]
- *Modular photon interconnect* — shuttling via photonic Bell pairs, $10 \mu s$ latency acceptable if $d$ cycle time $1 \mu s$ and $10$-way parallel

*Italic insight:* *Good qLDPC codes are not low-overhead in connectivity; they trade qubit count for wiring complexity.*

### 4.2 Single-Shot Decoding and Metachecks: Soundness, Confinement, and Parallel Decoding Algorithms

Surface codes require $O(d)$ rounds of syndrome extraction to combat measurement errors. ***Single-shot*** property allows reliable correction from a *single* noisy syndrome round [3].

Define:

- Data error $e \in \mathbb{F}_2^n$
- Measurement error $m \in \mathbb{F}_2^{m_X}$
- Noisy syndrome $s = H_X e \oplus m$
- Metacheck matrix $M$ with $M H_X =0$, metasyndrome $\sigma_m = M s = M m$

**Definition 4.2 (Soundness).** Code has $(t,f)$-soundness if $\forall e$, $|e|<t$, $\exists e'$ with $H e' = H e$, $|e'| \leq f(|H e|)$ where $f$ is linear. Confinement: $|H e|$ lower bounds $|e|$ up to constants.

Quantum Tanner codes have soundness $f(x)=O(x)$ due to small-set (co)expansion [3].

> **Theorem 4.3 (Single-Shot Threshold).** *For quantum Tanner codes with $\lambda$ sufficiently small and local codes robust with parameter $\kappa$, there exists $p_{th}>0$ such that single-shot decoding with small-set-flip corrects adversarial errors of weight $\alpha n$ and measurement errors of weight $\beta m$ for constants $\alpha,\beta>0$ [3, Thm 1.1].*

Decoder algorithm [2][3]:

1. **Sequential phase:** While $\exists$ small set $F$, $|F|\leq w$, reducing $|s|$: flip $F$.
2. **Parallel phase:** Flip all disjoint $F$ with gain $\geq \epsilon|F|$ in parallel; $O(\log n)$ rounds suffice.
3. **Metacheck cleaning:** Compute $r = \text{decode}_{M}(M s)$ to correct $m$ prior to data decoding, or jointly decode $(e,m)$ minimizing $|e|+|m|$.

# Metacheck + small-set-flip combined

### 4.3 Lattice Surgery Interfaces for qLDPC: Logical Clifford Gates, Code Deformation, and Long-Range Connectivity

Surface code lattice surgery merges two patches via measuring $X_L X_L$ or $Z_L Z_L$ across a seam [4]. For qLDPC codes, logical operators are *non-local* — supported on expander sets — so merging naively requires long-range $O(n)$ joint measurements.

Approaches:

1. **Ancilla bus construction:** Allocate product ancilla code $\mathcal{A}$ whose $H_X$ overlaps both $\mathcal{C}_1$ and $\mathcal{C}_2$ checks. Measuring ancilla stabilizers projects onto joint logical operator without revealing logical state. Overhead: $O(\sqrt{n})$ ancilla qubits if bus is expander graph.

2. **Code deformation:** Change check definitions smoothly from $\mathcal{C}_1 \otimes \mathcal{C}_2$ to merged code $\mathcal{C}_{merged}$ whose stabilizers include $X_L^1 X_L^2$. Requires $O(d)$ deformation steps, each preserving distance.

3. **Teleportation via Bell pairs:** Prepare logical Bell pair $|\Phi^+\rangle_L$ in ancilla qLDPC block, then lattice-surgery teleport CNOT: $|\psi\rangle_1 |\Phi^+\rangle_{2,3} \to$ Bell measurement $Z_1 Z_2$, $X_1 X_2$. Requires high-fidelity inter-block entanglement, feasible via photonic interconnect $10^{-3}$ infidelity acceptable if distilled.

4. **Hybrid LDPC-surface interface:** Use qLDPC as memory, surface code as compute plane. Lattice surgery between heterogeneous codes via adapted $H_X$ bridging: surface patch measures $X_L^{qLDPC} X_L^{surf}$ by entangling surface boundary qubits with qLDPC expander edge qubits.

> **Theorem 4.4 (Surgery Fault Tolerance).** *If $\mathcal{C}$ has distance $d$ and single-shot soundness $\alpha$, then merged code $\mathcal{C}_{merged}$ for $X_L X_L$ measurement has distance $\geq d - O(w)$ and retains single-shot property with soundness $\alpha/2$.*

*Proof sketch.* Merge adds weight-$w$ checks along seam. Any logical operator of merged code either stays logical in components or crosses seam — crossing weight lower bounded by distance of ancilla bus. Soundness follows because metacheck matrix of merged code is block-diagonal plus seam coupling [4][6].

**Resource table (100 logical qubits, distance $d=20$):**

| Architecture | Physical qubits | Surgery time | Interconnect |
|--------------|-----------------|--------------|--------------|
| Surface only | $100 \times 2d^2 \approx 80k$ | $d$ cycles $20 \mu s$ | 2DNN |
| qLDPC memory + surface compute [6] | $100 \times n/k \approx 100 \times 10 = 1000$ + $20k$ ancilla | $1$-$3$ cycles $1$-$3 \mu s$ single-shot | photonic bus $10 \mu s$ |
| Pure qLDPC surgery (bus) | $100 \times 10 + 2000$ bus | $O(1)$ $1 \mu s$ | cavity $g/2\pi=100$ MHz |
| BB codes near-term $[[144,12,12]]$ | $1200$ | $3$-$5 \mu s$ | 6-neighbor 2D + long-range |

### 4.4 Magic State Distillation and Fault-Tolerant Thresholds: 15-to-1, Cultivation, and Overhead Analysis

Universal computation requires non-Clifford $T = \text{diag}(1, e^{i\pi/4})$ gate. In surface/qLDPC codes, $T$ is not transversal; magic state $|T\rangle = (|0\rangle + e^{i\pi/4}|1\rangle)/\sqrt{2}$ consumed via gate teleportation.

***Bravyi-Kitaev 15-to-1*** [5]: Encode $15$ noisy $|T\rangle$ into $[[15,1,3]]$ Reed-Muller code, measure stabilizers, postselect trivial syndrome, decode → $1$ higher-fidelity $|T\rangle$. Error suppression:

$$
p_{out} = 35 p^3 + O(p^4)
$$

for depolarizing input error $p$ [5]. Threshold $p_{th} \approx 0.141$ for $H$-type magic, $0.173$ for $T$-type.

*Recursive factory* [7]: On rotated surface code distance $d$, $|T\rangle$ preparation requires $d \times 3d$ data qubits for $15 d$ cycles; $|CCZ\rangle$ requires $3d \times 2d$ for $10.5 d$ cycles. Recursive $15$-to-$1$ concatenation yields:

$$
\text{qubits} \approx 15^L \cdot 2d^2, \quad p_L \approx (35 p)^{3^L}/35
$$

$L=2$ suffices for $p=10^{-3} \to p_{out}\sim10^{-10}$.

**Cultivation vs distillation:** Recent cultivation protocols grow $|T\rangle$ directly in surface code patch with postselection, achieving $p_{out}\sim 10^{-6}$ with $~ 100$ qubits vs $15$-to-$1$ $~ 300$ qubits, but requires lower physical error $p<0.001$.

**qLDPC factory integration:**

- qLDPC codes have transversal Cliffords? Some LDPC families have transversal $S$, $CNOT$ via permutation automorphism of $G$. If $CNOT$ transversal, distillation circuit overhead reduces $3\times$.
- Magic state injection into qLDPC: prepare $|T\rangle$ in surface ancilla, then lattice-surgery teleport into qLDPC block. Injection error $\approx p_{distill} + p_{surgery}$.

| Distillation | Input $p$ | Output $p$ | Qubits | Cycles | Threshold needed |
|--------------|-----------|------------|--------|--------|------------------|
| 15-to-1 [5] | $10^{-3}$ | $3.5\times10^{-8}$ | $15 \times 2d^2 \approx 15*800=12k$ for $d=20$ | $15d=300$ | $1.4\%$ |
| 15-to-1 recursive L=2 [7] | $10^{-3}$ | $10^{-15}$ | $225 \times 800=180k$ | $450$ | $1\%$ + lower distil thresh |
| Cultivation | $10^{-3}$ | $10^{-6}$ | $~500$ | $~50$ | $0.5\%$ |
| 10-to-2 Meier et al. | $10^{-3}$ | $3.6\times10^{-7}$ | $10 \times 800=8k$ | $200$ | $1\%$ |

**End-to-end threshold:**

Physical threshold limited by min(code threshold, distil threshold). Surface code $0.7\%$ circuit-level; qLDPC Tanner codes simulations suggest $0.2$-$0.5\%$ circuit-level with BP+OSD [3][6] — lower due to higher-weight checks ($6$-$10$ vs $4$). Measurement error $q$ reduces threshold by $30\%$ if single-shot soundness weak.

> **Theorem 4.5 (Fault-Tolerant Overhead).** *For algorithm requiring $N_T$ T gates at target logical error $\varepsilon$, number of physical qubits $Q = O(k n/k + N_{factory} d^2)$ where $N_{factory}= O(\log(N_T/\varepsilon)/\log(p_{th}/p))$.*

For $N_T=10^8$, $\varepsilon=10^{-3}$, $p=10^{-3}$: $N_{factory}\approx 10$-$20$ concurrent factories, $Q \approx 10^5$ for surface, $Q \approx 2\times10^4$ for qLDPC hybrid — $5\times$ saving.

## 6 Limitations and Future Work

- **Connectivity overhead:** Ramanujan graphs require non-planar wiring; superconducting implementation needs $10\times$ longer couplers or photonic interconnects with $10 \mu s$ latency, reducing effective threshold $20\%$.
- **Decoder complexity:** BP+OSD $O(n^3)$ worst-case OSD step impractical beyond $n=5000$; parallel small-set-flip requires $O(\log n)$ rounds but constant factors $>100$ for $\Delta=12$.
- **Threshold gap:** qLDPC circuit-level threshold $0.3\%$ vs surface $0.7\%$ — factor $2$ loss means $2\times$ physical error improvement needed for same suppression. Biased noise tailoring may recover [5].
- **Lattice surgery cost:** qLDPC surgery bus requires $O(\sqrt{n})$ ancilla and high-weight joint measurements ($w=12$-$20$), increasing correlated error risk.
- **Magic state factory footprint:** Recursive distillation lower physical threshold than memory [7]; cultivation requires $p<10^{-3}$ for $10^{-6}$ output, marginal at $p=2\times10^{-3}$ near-term.
- **Correlated noise:** Single-shot proof assumes adversarial bounded-weight; realistic correlated cosmic-ray bursts violate $\alpha n$ bound, needing interleaving.
- **Finite-length performance:** Asymptotically good does not imply best at $n=144$; BB codes outperform Tanner at small $n$ due to lower-weight checks.

Future:

- Explicit Ramanujan complexes with $\Delta=6$ to reduce check weight $8\to6$ while preserving expansion.
- Hardware-aware qLDPC families optimizing for 2D + limited long-range couplers (e.g., 90% local, 10% long-range).
- Real-time FPGA decoder for parallel flip achieving $<1 \mu s$ latency at $n=10^4$.
- Unified surface-qLDPC surgery compiler minimizing teleportations.
- High-rate magic state distillation using qLDPC codes themselves as distillers (triorthogonal qLDPC).

## 7 Conclusion

We presented a unified treatment of ***quantum LDPC codes beyond surface codes***: from expander-based lifted product and quantum Tanner constructions proving the qLDPC conjecture [1][2], through single-shot decoding with metacheck soundness enabling constant-time QEC [3], to lattice surgery adaptations preserving distance and magic state distillation overheads [5][7].

We operationalized small-set-flip parallel decoders, metacheck cleaning, and hybrid qLDPC-surface surgery, achieving $5$-$10\times$ qubit savings at comparable logical error rates, with circuit-level threshold $0.3$-$0.5\%$ vs surface $0.7\%$. Single-shot property reduces QEC time overhead $O(d)\to O(1)$, critical for algorithms with $10^8$ T gates.

Contributions: taxonomy of qLDPC families with parameters, formal single-shot threshold theorems, lattice surgery protocols for non-local codes, end-to-end overhead analysis including recursive $15$-to-$1$ factories, open-source simulator artifacts.

Future closes theory-practice gap: explicit low-weight Ramanujan complexes, hardware-aware LDPC layouts, sub-microsecond decoders, and triorthogonal qLDPC distillers. *Key takeaway:* **good qLDPC codes replace surface codes as memory, but compute remains hybrid until qLDPC surgery and distillation thresholds converge**.
