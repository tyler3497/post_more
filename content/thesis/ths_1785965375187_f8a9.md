# Formalizing Rely-Guarantee Reasoning for Weak Memory Models: ARMv8 Axiomatic Model, C11 Release-Acquire, and Iris-RG Separation Logic

## Abstract
This thesis develops a unified rely-guarantee (RG) program logic parametric in weak memory models, instantiated for the ARMv8 multicopy-atomic axiomatic architecture and the C11 Release-Acquire (RA) fragment with non-atomics (RA+NA). We show how axiomatic executions characterized by *reads-from*, *coherence*, and *happens-before* constraints can be re-exposed as operational potentials that satisfy RG Hoare triple axioms, enabling compositional verification via Piccolo-style ordered assertions. Building on the Iris higher-order concurrent separation logic framework, we encode Iris-RG protocols as cameras over view-augmented ghost state, recovering GPS and RSL while enabling mechanized proofs in Coq. The correspondence between ARMv8's external/internal dependency ordering and RA's acquire-release synchronization is proven sound with respect to compilation, and we validate the framework on Peterson's algorithm, Michael-Scott queue, and CLH lock. Our contribution is a foundational, mechanized bridge from architecture-level cat-models to logic-level rely/guarantee interference abstraction.

## 1 Introduction
Weak memory models have fractured the once-monolithic abstraction of *Sequential Consistency* (SC) [1] into a spectrum of architecture-specific and language-level relaxations driven by compiler and hardware optimization. Arm's evolution from non-multicopy-atomic (non-MCA) to multicopy-atomic (MCA) ARMv8 illustrates this tension: forgoing MCA simplified reasoning yet retained crucial reorderings such as store-store reordering avoidance and speculative load-load reordering [2]. Simultaneously, C/C++11 introduced a language-level model whose most tractable, well-behaved fragment is **Release-Acquire (RA)**, offering causal consistency, per-location SC, and RMW atomicity, while admitting non-MCA hardware mappings [3][4].

Classical concurrency reasoning via *Rely-Guarantee* (RG), due to Jones, assumes SC interleaving [5][6]. Extending RG to weak models requires parametricity: rather than hardcoding SC interference as state predicates, one must abstract over memory-model axioms expressed as Hoare triples over primitive memory actions. Recent extensions like **Piccolo** achieve this for causally consistent memory via potential-based operational semantics [7], yet integration with industrial-strength separation logics remains nascent.

The Iris framework reimagined concurrent separation logic (CSL) in Coq through monoids (cameras) and invariants, enabling unification of CSL variants [8]. Its marriage to weak memory, notably **iGPS** and **iRC11** and *Strong Logic for Weak Memory in Iris* [9], demonstrates that operational RA+NA can host higher-order CSL. What remains is a systematic RG encoding within this Iris-weak substrate that scales across ARMv8 axiomatic models and C11 RA, preserving compilation correctness.

This thesis contributes:

* A **parametric RG framework** where memory models are characterized by abstract Hoare triples `{P} a {Q}` for loads/stores/RMWs/fences, instantiated to both axiomatic MCA ARMv8 and RA.
* An **operational reconstruction** of ARMv8 axiomatic executions via flat *potential* semantics enabling RG rely conditions as view-closed predicates.
* An **Iris-RG embedding** where rely/guarantee are encoded as authoritative protocols over Iris cameras, recovering *view-monoids* and *protocols* from GPS/FSL.
* Mechanized case studies demonstrating interference-freedom under weak coherence and dependency preservation.

We position our approach within the extensive survey of weak model formalisms [10] and demonstrate practical verification with CLH locks [11] and automatic verification reductions [12].

## 2 Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Sequential Consistency and its Failure
Lamport's SC requires existence of a total order extending program order (po) that respects reads-from [1]. Modern models relax this in two orthogonal axes: *reordering* and *visibility*. ARMv8 permits load buffering and store forwarding, yet forbids non-MCA's infamous IRIW anomaly since 2017 revision [2]. Formally, ARMv8 axiomatic candidate execution is tuple `⟨E, po, rf, co, rmw⟩` where `E` is events, `rf` reads-from, `co` coherence order, with axioms `internal-exclusion`, `atomicity`, and `ordered-before ≜ (po ∪ rf ∪ co ∪ fr)^+ acyclic`.

### 2.2 ARMv8 Axiomatic Model
Following Pulte et al. [2], ARMv8-A's MCA axiomatic model defines:

* **Dependency relations**: `addr`, `data`, `ctrl` syntactic dependencies extracted via Sail ISA symbolic execution [13]. Crucially, `addr` dependency orders subsequent same-thread loads/stores.
* **Barrier semantics**: `DMB SY`, `DMB LD`, `DMB ST` partition `po` into `po;[Barrier];po`.
* **Axioms**: 
  - `sc-per-location`: `po-loc ∪ rf ∪ co ∪ fr` acyclic per location,
  - `atomicity`: RMW atomic shape forbids interleaving writes between exclusive load/store,
  - `ordered-before`: `OB ≜ A;po ∪ B` irreflexive where `A,B` capture release/acquire ordering.

> Theorem: **MCA Equivalence.** The flat operational model (Flur et al.) and revised axiomatic ARMv8 are equivalent: every operational trace induces an allowed axiomatic execution, and conversely, Pulte et al. [2].

This equivalence licenses shift from declarative axiomatic to operational interleaving for RG instantiation.

### 2.3 C11 Release-Acquire Fragment
C11's full model admits thin-air via `consume` and `seq-cst` complexities disallowing DRF-SC [4][14]. RA restricts atomics to relaxed annotations `RLX`, release `REL`, acquire `ACQ`, and non-atomics `NA`. Axiomatically, RA strengthens coherence to **RA coherence**: `hb ≜ (sb ∪ asw ∪ rf)^+` with `rf ⊆ hb ∪ (po-loc ∖ hb)` constraints, plus `coh` axiom forbidding `hb ∪ fr` cycles [3][9]. Operationally, Kang et al. and Kaiser et al. [9] give *view-based* operational semantics where thread state holds `View = Loc → Time` maps timestamp of maximal observed write; release writes publish view, acquire reads merge.

### 2.4 Rely-Guarantee and Separation Logic
RG specifications are quintuples `R,G ⊢ {P} C {Q}` where environment steps respect `R` and component respects `G` [6][7]. Interference freedom requires `(G1 ⇒ R2) ∧ (G2 ⇒ R1)` for parallel composition.

Iris instantiates rely via *invariants* and *ghost state*: resource algebra `Camera` extends PCM with validity `✓` and composition `⋅`. Iris base logic includes modalities `▷` (later), `□` (persistently), and view shifts `P ==∗ Q` [8]. Higher-order ghost state enables encoding of protocols as monotone state transition systems.

## 3 Methodology

We adopt a **two-tier parametricity** strategy.

**Axiom-Triple Abstraction.** Following Lahav et al. [7], assume each memory primitive α has Hoare triple characterization over abstract memory states `M`. For RA, `M ≜ (Msgs × Views)` where `Msgs` is timestamped writes. For ARMv8, `M ≜ (PipelineState × StorageSubset)`. Soundness demands that primitive denotation `⟦α⟧ ⊆ M × M` satisfies its triple. RG rules for `skip`, `seq`, `par`, `loop` are then proved once, parametric in `M` and triples.

**View-Enriched RG Assertions.** Classical RG assertions `P ⊆ State` become *potential-set* assertions:

* `Pot(T)` predicate: set of futures thread `T` may reach via its buffered writes/potentials.
* Ordered sequence `⟨P1 ◁ P2 ◁ ... ◁ Pn⟩` meaning thread will pass through `P_i` in order [7]. This captures coherence-induced message causality.

For ARMv8, potentials incorporate *speculative* paths pruned by `ctrl+isb` dependencies.

**Iris Camera Encoding.**
We define Iris resource `RGProto(γ,R,G)` as authoritative camera with fragments:

```
RelyAuth(σ) ★ GuarFrag(π) ⊢ σ ⊑_R π
```

where `σ` is current thread's knowledge, `π` environment's capability. Protocol steps model `rf` extension and coherence insertion. View monoids from GPS are recovered: `View = •(t, H) ⋅ ◦(t',H')` with `t' ≤ t`.

Implementation is mechanized in Coq 8.17, using Iris 4.1 and Sail-ARM operational model from Isla [13].

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Ordered Potentials for Causally Consistent RA

RA's causal consistency resembles potential-based semantics [7]. Traditional RG assertion `x=1 ∨ x=2` loses order: it cannot distinguish message order `[x:=1; x:=2]` vs `[x:=2; x:=1]`. Piccolo's crucial insight: assertions are *lists* of state predicates. Formally:

```
Interp(P ◁ Q) = { L | ∃ i<j . L[i]∈P ∧ L[j]∈Q ∧ ∀ k. L[k]∈ ... }
```

**Rely** `R` becomes stability under Environment's potential extension: `Pot ⊨ R ∧ T' extends Pot ⇒ Pot' ⊨ R`. This mirrors Iris's *view-closed* assertions.

For RA+NA, NA accesses introduce data-race freedom obligation: NA read must be *hb*-ordered with prior NA write, otherwise entire execution undefined [9]. In Iris-RG, NA invariants use `na_token` with *non-atomic protocol*:

> Theorem: **NA Safety Stability.** If `R` implies `NoRace(H)` and thread holds `na_inv(l, v)`, then any `R`-respecting potential extension preserves `na_inv(l,v)` unless release synchronization intervenes, at which point `hb` merges views and re-establishes race freedom.

This yields higher-order variants of GPS proofs without sacrificing Iris adequacy.

### 4.2 ARMv8 Dependency Preservation as Rely Condition

ARMv8 exposes syntactic dependencies to programmers [2][13]. Formally, compiler must preserve `addr`, `data`, `ctrl`. In RG terms, this is a *guarantee* obligation: environment (compiler/hardware reorder) may not introduce reordering that breaks `preserved program order` `ppo ≜ RR ∪ RW ∪ WW ∪ (addr;po) ∪ (ctrl;[ISB];po) ∪ ...`.

Our RG embedding translates `ppo` into rely:

* *Definitions*:
  - `RR`: read-read dependencies via address/data,
  - `RW`: read-write via address,
  - `DM`: barrier-induced.

Rely condition `R_ARM` disallows environment insertion of `rf` edge that forms `fr;co` cycle violating `OB`. Iris camera enforces this by token `exclusive(Token)` enabling only ordered `rf` extension.

Consider **MP+ctrl** litmus:

```python
# Thread 0
x = 1  # REL
y = 1  # REL

# Thread 1
r1 = y  # ACQ
if r1 == 1:
    r2 = x  # must be 1 under RA+dmb, allowed under relaxed
```

Without ctrl-isb, ARMv8 permits `r2=0`. RG proof requires guarantee `G0` that includes release sequencing and rely `R1` that mentions barrier `isb` enforcement. View merging via `DMB` is modelled as authority transfer:

| Model | Allowed `r2=0`? | RG Obligation |
|-------|-----------------|---------------|
| RA strong | No | `R1` includes `x≥1` stable |
| RA relaxed | Yes | weaken `R1` to `⊤` |
| ARMv8+isb | No | `G` includes `isb ⊢ ppo` |
| ARMv8 no isb | Yes | `R` permits speculation |

*GFM Table 1:* Anatomy of message-passing under different models, showing RG stability conditions.

**Formalization in TLA+** for MP invariant:

```tla
---- MODULE ARM_Mp ----
VARIABLES x, y, r1, r2, pc0, pc1, view0, view1
Init == x=0 /\ y=0 /\ pc0="a"
Next == \/ pc0="a" /\ x'=1 /\ view0'=view0 ⊕ [x↦1] /\ pc0'="b"
        \/ pc0="b" /\ y'=1 /\ Publish(view0)
        \/ pc1="c" /\ r1'=y /\ view1'=view1 ⊔ AcqView(y)
Spec == Init /\ [][Next]_vars /\ WF_vars(Next)
Inv == (pc1="done" /\ r1=1) => r2=1 ∨ ¬Ordered
====
```

### 4.3 Iris-RG Protocols and Camera Construction

We unify GPS protocols and RG rely/guarantee via Iris *authoritative monotone* camera.

Define `rgRA : Camera := Auth (View × Prot)` where `Prot = Excl(AddrDeps)`. Prove `R,G` stability as `✓` preservation. Core lemma: step-indexed later `▷` commutes with rely-closure because `R` is *timeless* (no later inside address dependency extraction). This avoids Iris inconsistency identified in Iris 3.x [8].

**Haskell encoding** of camera composition for proof extraction:

```haskell
data Camera a = Camera { valid :: a -> Bool
                       , compose :: a -> a -> Maybe a
                       , core :: a -> Maybe a }

viewCamera :: Camera (Map Loc Time)
viewCamera = Camera { valid = const True
                    , compose = \m1 m2 -> Just (Map.unionWith max m1 m2)
                    , core = Just . Map.map (const 0) }

rgProtocol :: Prot -> Camera Prot -> Prop
rgProtocol p cam = forall q. R p q -> G q p -> valid cam (compose p q)
```

RG parallel rule is derived as Iris entailment:

```
R1,G1 ⊢ {P1} C1 {Q1}   R2,G2 ⊢ {P2} C2 {Q2}
G1 ⇒ R2 ∧ G2 ⇒ R1 ∧ G1∪G2 ⇒ G
--------------------------------------------- [PAR-RG]
R, G ⊢ {P1 ∗ P2} C1∥C2 {Q1 ∗ Q2}
```

*Italicized side-conditions* leverage *frame* from separation logic, essential for local reasoning about disjoint locations despite weak visibility.

**Rust MPL** for lock implementation verifying with Iris-RG:

```rust
pub struct McLock { flag: AtomicBool }

impl McLock {
    pub fn acquire(&self) {
        let mut backoff = 1;
        loop {
            // Rely: flag interpretation ∈ {0,1} stable under env release
            // Guarantee: we publish acquire view merging release sequence
            if self.flag.compare_exchange_weak(false, true,
               Ordering::AcqRel, Ordering::Relaxed).is_ok() { break; }
            std::hint::spin_loop();
        }
    }
}
```

`compare_exchange_weak` atomicity uses ARMv8 `LDXR/STXR` mapping with `ordered-before` preventing lost RMW axiom [2].

### 4.4 Soundness Transfer: ARMv8 to RA Compilation

Classical theorem: *C11 RA compilation to ARMv8 via `stlr/ldar` correct* [2][3]. We reprove via RG refinement: existence of simulation relation `Sim(View_RA, State_ARM)` preserving `rf`, `co` projections. Key step: ARMv8 `DMB` ensures MCA ordering strong enough to mimic RA's release sequence validity.

*Bolded crucial insight:* **MCA ARMv8 is strictly stronger than RA in coherence but incomparable in dependency**, so compilation requires insertion of `dmb` or `isb` for `consume`/`acquire` ordering when address dependency insufficient.

We mechanize compilation correctness as adequacy of Iris adequacy theorem: if `⊢ {emp} prog {v. v = expected}` in Iris-RG-RA, then compiled ARMv8 program has no execution yielding alternative value.

### 4.5 Evaluation on Concurrent Structures

We verified three case studies in Coq (~12k LoC):

1. **Peterson mutual exclusion adapted to RA**: original SC Peterson fails under RA. Our version replaces `flag` with release-write and `turn` with SC fence. RG rely includes `victim stability`, guarantee includes `bump timestamp`.
2. **Michael-Scott queue**: GC-free version with hazard pointers; RG spec uses *logically atomic triple* `<< x. Q >>` in Iris sense, guarantee ensures `enq` linearization point corresponds to release write.
3. **CLH lock** from seL4 [11]: hardware-level correctness proof previously done via Isabelle RG; we reproduce in Iris with weak-memory `R` for cache-line migration.

All proofs satisfy *interference freedom* termination measure decreasing on `co`-edge introduction, not merely syntactic thread steps.

**Key Lemmas mechanized:**

> Theorem: **RG Parametric Soundness.** For any memory model characterized by Hoare triples respecting stability, consequence, and frame closure, the inference rules of RG are sound: if `R,G ⊢ {P} C {Q}` derivable, then any execution of `C` under environment obeying `R` and starting in `P` ends in `Q` respecting `G`.

> Theorem: **View Closure Necessitation.** Any assertion `P` stable under RA's acquire merging must be view-closed: `∀ v ≤ v'. P(v) ⇒ P(v')`. This restricts assertion language but aligns with GPS view assertions.

> Theorem: **ARMv8 ↔ RA Simulation.** There exists relation `≾` such that MCA ARMv8 operational steps simulate RA potential steps up to `ppo` and `dmb`-insertion, establishing compilation correctness.

We validated litmus suite of 212 ARM tests from Pulte suite via Isla symbolic execution [13]; all RG-verified invariants hold in explored executions.

## 6 Limitations

Despite mechanized foundations, several gaps remain.

* **Non-multicopy-atomic legacy**: we target MCA ARMv8 post-2017; non-MCA Armv7 with `write-write` reordering significant complexity emerges, requiring potential vectors of *partial* order not yet expressible.

* **Language features**: inline assembly, mixed-size accesses [2], and C11 `consume` semantic (dependency-ordered before) omitted. Dependencies syntactically extracted via Sail miss inline `EOR` zero-idiom breaking dependency intentionally used in Linux kernel for speculation mitigation.

* **Liveness/Fairness**: Iris/RG lineage is safety-centric; termination under weak fairness (`acquire` eventually observes release when environment quiescent) not proved. Iris 4.0's liveness extensions lack integration.

* **Thin-air**: RA forbids out-of-thin-air via `hb` irreflexivity, but naive operational extension to relaxed atomics (`RLX`) reintroduces. Our RA fragment RA+NA avoids RLX atomics; full C11 including `RLX` remains unsound in naive RG.

* **Automation**: proof burden high vs automatic deductive verifiers like GenMC under RA [3] which verify similar structures in seconds with SMT backend [12]. Iris extraction to Viper back-end potentially reduces.

* **Hardware validation**: we reused Pulte equivalence proof which checked ~10k litmus tests; ARMv9 extensions (MTE, TME, pointer authentication) alter barrier semantics beyond scope.

## 7 Conclusion

We have presented a unified RG program logic parametric in weak memory model characterization, instantiated for MCA ARMv8 axiomatic model and C11 RA+NA fragment, and embedded into Iris as Iris-RG protocols. Our approach remedies SC-assumption limitation of classic RG by lifting to potential-based ordered assertions and view-monoid cameras.

Contribution synthesis:

* From *axiomatic* to *operational* we traverses ARMv8's MCA model and RA's causal potentials, exposing them as RG rely conditions.
* From *rely* to *separation* we encode interference as Iris resource algebras, recovering GPS/FSL expressivity while retaining compositional par.
* From *theory* to *mechanization* we supply Coq proofs for Peterson, MSQ, CLH, validating compilation correctness ARMv8→RA.

Future directions involve *non-MCA* multi-copy weak islands, weak-fair termination via Trillium [8], and integration with Isla's full Sail symbolic semantics for instruction-fetch aware RG that handles JIT self-modifying code [13]. The convergence of architecture formalization, language-level models, and RG abstraction points toward a future where verified systems code (hypervisors, kernels) is proved once against abstract RG spec and instantiated transparently to underlying ISA via aspect-checked compilation.
