# Succinct Arguments of Knowledge via Folding Schemes: Nova, SuperNova, HyperNova and Cycle of Curves for Recursive SNARKs

## Abstract
The evolution from monolithic SNARKs to incrementally verifiable computation (IVC) has fundamentally redefined scalable zero-knowledge proofs. Folding schemes, pioneered by Nova [1], replace expensive recursive verification inside the circuit with a cheap algebraic folding step, achieving IVC without trusted setup per step and with prover cost dominated by a single multi-scalar multiplication. This thesis provides a unified, PhD-level treatment of the Nova family—Nova, SuperNova, HyperNova, and their instantiation over cycles of elliptic curves via CycleFold. We formalize relaxed R1CS and its generalization to Customizable Constraint Systems (CCS) [5], derive the complete folding algebra, analyze non-uniform IVC for universal machine execution in SuperNova [2], and the a-la-carte, multi-folding zero-knowledge design of HyperNova [3]. We then examine the arithmetic-geometric substrate enabling efficient recursion: 2-cycles, Pasta, and Pallas/Vesta, the security pitfalls revealed by cycle-aware attacks [4], and the CycleFold technique for embedding secondary-circuit verification. Empirical analysis compares prover time, verifier circuit size, and memory asymptotics, and proves knowledge soundness under the discrete-log assumption in the random oracle model. We conclude with limitations in arithmetization overhead, cross-circuit accumulation, and post-quantum security.

## 1 Introduction

Incrementally Verifiable Computation (IVC) [6] formalizes the notion of a *computational trace* whose correctness can be verified incrementally, without re-executing the entire history. Given a function $F: \mathbb{F} \times \mathbb{F} \to \mathbb{F}$ and initial state $z_0$, IVC proves the iterated application $z_{i+1} = F(z_i, \omega_i)$ for $i=0..n-1$ with a proof $\Pi_n$ whose verification cost is sublinear in $n$ and whose size remains constant. Traditional approaches constructed IVC by recursively verifying a SNARK inside another SNARK, incurring a large overhead: the verifier circuit of a pairing-based SNARK (e.g., Groth16) contains pairings, hash-to-curve, and FFT operations that are prohibitively expensive to arithmetize.

**Nova** [1] introduced a paradigm shift: *folding schemes*. Instead of proving that a proof verifies, Nova maintains a *running* relaxed instance $U$ and folds a fresh instance $u$ representing the current step into $U$ using only algebraic operations in the plain field. The expensive check is deferred until the very end, where a single compressed SNARK proves the folding history. This insight yields IVC where the prover's per-step cryptographic work is dominated by **two $n$-sized multi-scalar multiplications (MSMs)** and the recursive circuit contains only $O(\log n)$ hashes and two group scalar multiplications.

> Theorem: (Nova IVC Informal). Under the discrete logarithm assumption and in the random oracle model, there exists an IVC scheme for $F$ where the prover per step does $O(n)$ field operations plus two MSMs, and the IVC verifier runs in $O(\log n)$ time after a final $O(n)$ succinct proof.

This thesis studies three generations:

*   **Nova** – folding for relaxed R1CS, homogeneous IVC [1]
*   **SuperNova** – non-uniform IVC for programs with many different instruction circuits [2]
*   **HyperNova** – folding for Customizable Constraint Systems (CCS), multi-folding, zero-knowledge for free, and CycleFold [3]

We also treat the *curve substrate*: how to instantiate these schemes over a cycle of elliptic curves to avoid non-native field arithmetic [4][7][8].

## 2 Background

### 2.1 SNARKs, Relaxed R1CS, and Committed Relaxed R1CS

Let $\mathbb{F}$ be a prime field. Rank-1 Constraint System (R1CS) with matrices $A,B,C \in \mathbb{F}^{m \times m}$ defines language $\mathcal{L}_{R1CS} = \{ (x,W) : (A\cdot Z) \circ (B\cdot Z) = C\cdot Z, Z=(W,x,1) \}$.

Nova relaxes this to allow slack:

> Definition (Relaxed R1CS). Instance is $(\bar{E}, u, \bar{W}, x)$ with error vector $E \in \mathbb{F}^m$ and scalar $u \in \mathbb{F}$. $Z=(W,x,u)$. Relation: $(A Z) \circ (B Z) = u \cdot (C Z) + E$.

The commitment scheme $\mathsf{Com}$ is Pedersen over a group $\mathbb{G}$ where $\mathsf{Com}(W, r)$ binds $W$. Committed relaxed R1CS hides $W$ and $E$ behind commitments $\bar{W}, \bar{E}$. This relaxation is *crucial*: folding two arbitrary R1CS instances would produce a quadratic cross-term that is not R1CS; adding $E$ and $u$ absorbs that term.

### 2.2 Accumulation vs Folding

**Accumulation schemes** [Bünz et al., BCMS20] accumulate an expensive predicate (e.g., polynomial opening proof) into an accumulator that is checked once at the end. **Folding schemes** are stronger: they reduce checking *two* NP instances to checking *one* instance of the same language, without extra predicate. Every folding scheme yields an accumulation scheme, but not vice-versa [1].

| Scheme | Language | Prover Cost | Verifier Circuit | Trusted Setup? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Halo [6] | Inner product | $O(n \log n)$ | $O(\log n)$ hashes | No |
| Nova [1] | Relaxed R1CS | 2 MSM ($n$) | 2 scalar mul + hash | No |
| SuperNova [2] | Extended R1CS | 2 MSM + lookup | $O(\log k)$ + hash | No |
| HyperNova [3] | CCS | 1 MSM ($n$) | $O(1)$ | No |
| Sangria (PLONK) | PLONK | 2 MSM | $O(\log n)$ | No |

### 2.3 Cycles of Curves and IVC Instantiation

Recursive verification requires proving statements about $\mathbb{G}_1$ arithmetic inside a circuit defined over $\mathbb{F}_2$, and vice-versa. If $\mathbb{G}_1$ is defined over $\mathbb{F}_q$ with scalar field $\mathbb{F}_r$, we need a second curve $\mathbb{G}_2$ defined over $\mathbb{F}_r$ whose scalar field is $\mathbb{F}_q$. Such a *2-cycle* is rare. **MNT4/MNT6**, **Pasta (Pallas/Vesta)**, and **Grumpkin/BN254** cycles [7][8] realize this. Alternatively, Nova uses *CycleFold* [3][4] to fold the secondary circuit's work into the primary cycle without simulating non-native arithmetic.

### 2.4 Incrementally Verifiable Computation and PCD

Valiant's IVC [6] and Chiesa et al.'s Proof-Carrying Data (PCD) generalize IVC to distributed DAG computations. Non-uniform PCD, needed for EVM/RISC-V, allows different functions $F_i$ per step. SuperNova is the first practical non-uniform IVC from folding [2].

## 3 Methodology

Our methodology combines algebraic analysis, security reduction, and implementation-aware cost modeling.

1.  **Formalization**: We formalize folding scheme as tuple $(\mathsf{Gen}, \mathsf{Enc}, \mathsf{Fold}, \mathsf{Dec})$ with completeness, knowledge soundness, and zero-knowledge properties. We extend to *multi-folding* $(k:1)$ and *non-uniform* selector.

2.  **Algebraic Derivation**: For each scheme, we derive folding equations. For Nova, given $U=(\bar{E}, u, \bar{W}, x)$ and $u=(\bar{E}_0, u_0, \bar{W}_0, x_0)$ with witnesses $(E,W,r_W,r_E)$ and $(E_0,W_0,r_{W0},r_{E0})$, verifier samples $r \xleftarrow{\$} \mathbb{F}$ and outputs folded $U' = (\bar{E}', u', \bar{W}', x')$ where $u' = u + r u_0$, $\bar{W}' = \bar{W} + r \bar{W}_0$, and cross term
    $$
    T = A Z_1 \circ B Z_2 + A Z_2 \circ B Z_1 - u_1 C Z_2 - u_2 C Z_1
    $$
    absorbed into $\bar{E}' = \bar{E} + r \cdot \bar{T} + r^2 \bar{E}_0$.

3.  **Curve Analysis**: We enumerate cycle parameters: Pasta curves $p_{Pallas}=2^{254}+...$, $p_{Vesta}=...$, security ~128 bits. We analyze *CycleFold* circuit of size $O(\ell)$ where $\ell$ is number of non-native scalar muls, reducing overhead from $O(\lambda \cdot \ell)$ to $O(\ell)$.

4.  **Literature Verification**: We verify claims against ePrint sources [1][2][3][4][5] and survey [8], cross-checking protocol versions and patched soundness bug (Nov 2023, $2^{75}$ VDF counterexample [4]).

5.  **Empirical Model**: We implement cost estimator in Rust/Haskell-style pseudocode to project MSMs, FFTs, and hash constraints.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Nova: The Minimal Folding for Relaxed R1CS

Nova's core contribution is the *committed relaxed R1CS* folding protocol.

```rust
// Pseudocode: Nova NIFS.Fold
fn nifs_fold(
  U: &RelaxedInstance, u: &Instance,
  T_commit: G, r: Fr
) -> RelaxedInstance {
  let u_fold = U.u + r * u.u;
  let x_fold = U.x + r * u.x;
  let W_fold = U.W_bar + r * u.W_bar; // homomorphic commitment
  let E_fold = U.E_bar + r * T_commit + r*r * u.E_bar;
  RelaxedInstance { E: E_fold, u: u_fold, W: W_fold, x: x_fold }
}
```

Prover computes $T$ in $O(n)$ field ops and commits to $T$. Verifier only needs to perform two scalar multiplications to update commitments and $O(|x|)$ field ops. Crucially, Nova avoids *FFT* per step; only linear-time checks.

*Why relaxed?* Standard R1CS is not closed under linear combination due to Hadamard product quadratic term. The slack $E$ and scalar $u$ make the language *linear* in $T$.

Nova IVC construction:

*   $z_0$ initial, $U_0$ trivial.
*   For $i$, prove $z_{i+1} = F(z_i, \omega_i)$ as R1CS instance $u_i$.
*   Fold $u_i$ into running $U_i$ to get $U_{i+1}$ via $r = \mathsf{RO}(U_i, u_i, T)$.
*   Recursively prove correctness of folding inside circuit $F'$, which checks $F$ *and* one NIFS.V.

Final IVC proof is $(U_n, W_n, E_n, \pi_{n})$ where $\pi_n$ is a compressed SNARK (e.g., Spartan) for $U_n$ satisfying.

> Theorem (Nova Knowledge Soundness [1]): The folding scheme is knowledge-sound under DLOG in ROM. The IVC scheme is knowledge-sound with $O(1)$ overhead if the compressed SNARK is knowledge-sound.

### 4.2 SuperNova: Non-Uniform IVC without Universal Circuits

Real programs are not one repeated $F$ but a set $\{F_1,...,F_k\}$ (e.g., EVM opcodes). Naively, one would build universal circuit $F_{univ}$ that multiplexes all $F_i$, paying $\sum |F_i|$ per step. SuperNova avoids this.

Key idea: *augmented* relaxed R1CS with selector vector $s \in \{0,1\}^k$, $||s||_1=1$, indicating which $F_i$ executed. Running instances $U_i$ per program counter? Actually SuperNova maintains *$k$* running instances $\{U_i\}_{i=1}^k$? Revised 2023 version maintains one $U$ plus list of instances per instruction.

Folding preserves *a-la-carte* cost: proving a step executing $F_j$ costs $\approx |F_j|$, not $\sum |F_i|$.

Construction:

*   Instance $u$ contains $s$ indicating $j$.
*   Folding verifier folds only into $U_j$, other $U_{i\ne j}$ unchanged (or folded with dummy).
*   Circuit $F'$ must prove membership of $j$ and correctness of folding for $j$.

SuperNova also introduces *circuit aggregation* to handle cross-terms across heterogeneous circuits via shared structure $A,B,C$ extended with selector columns.

Performance: For $k=32$ opcode circuits of average size $2^{15}$, universal circuit would be $2^{20}$; SuperNova prover is $2^{15}$-scale per step, $32\times$ saving [2].

### 4.3 HyperNova: Folding CCS, Multi-Folding, and Zero-Knowledge for Free

Setty et al. [5] introduce **Customizable Constraint System (CCS)** that generalizes R1CS, Plonkish, AIR:

$$
\sum_{i=0}^{q-1} c_i \cdot \prod_{j \in S_i} \left(\sum_{y} M_j[y] \cdot Z[y]\right) = 0
$$

where $M_j$ are matrices, $S_i$ multisets of indices, $c_i$ constants. By choosing $q, S_i$, CCS captures:

*   R1CS: $q=2$, $S_0=\{0,1\}$, $S_1=\{2\}$
*   Plonk permutation: high-degree product constraints
*   Lookup arguments: $q>2$

HyperNova folds CCS directly, not via reduction to R1CS. Contributions:

1.  **Optimal prover**: Single $n$-sized MSM to commit to folded witness, versus 2 in Nova. Achieved by linearly combining witness commitments and computing cross-terms via $O(n)$ field ops without extra MSM.

2.  **Multi-folding $k:1$**: Fold $k$ instances $u_1,...,u_k$ into $U$ in one shot with challenge vector $\mathbf{r} \in \mathbb{F}^k$. Critical for PCD where a node aggregates proofs from many children. Costs: $O(k n)$ field ops + 1 MSM.

3.  **Zero-Knowledge for free**: Prior folding schemes required ZK SNARK at the end to get ZK IVC, doubling costs. HyperNova randomizes folding with blinding factors $\rho$ added to $W$ and $E$, making transcript simulatable without extra circuit overhead.

4.  **CycleFold**: General technique to instantiate any folding-based IVC over a cycle with *no* non-native field arithmetic for secondary circuit. Instead of simulating $\mathbb{F}_q$ ops in $\mathbb{F}_r$ circuit, fold them as an auxiliary instance.

```haskell
-- HyperNova multi-fold core
multiFold :: CCSInstance -> [CCSInstance] -> Fr -> CCSInstance
multiFold u0 us rs = 
  let rPows = powers rs -- r, r^2, ...
      uFold = foldr (+) u0 (zipWith scale rPows us)
      eFold = combineError u0 us rs -- absorbs sum_{i} c_i * crossTerms
  in CCSInstance { wit = uFold.wit, err = eFold, u = combineU rs }
```

### 4.4 Cycle of Curves, Pasta, and Security Pitfalls

Instantiating Nova naively over BN254 requires verifying BN254 scalar field operations inside a BN254 circuit — impossible efficiently because circuit native field is $\mathbb{F}_r$ but group operations are over $\mathbb{F}_q$. A 2-cycle solves this: primary curve $E_1/\mathbb{F}_q$ with scalar field $\mathbb{F}_r$, secondary $E_2/\mathbb{F}_r$ with scalar field $\mathbb{F}_q$.

Pasta cycle: **Pallas** ($y^2 = x^3+5$, $p = 2^{254}+...$) and **Vesta** (same equation, different $p$) provides 128-bit security, FFT-friendly 2-adic root $2^{32}$ factor, no pairings. Nova's reference implementation uses Pasta.

> Security Warning (2023/969): Original Nova implementation omitted consistency check $u \ne 0$ and allowed malleable relaxed instance where $\bar{E}$ could be arbitrarily set. Attack exploited missing input $\bar{E}$ in Fiat-Shamir hash, allowing forged IVC proof for $2^{75}$ Minroot VDF steps in 1.46s. Patched by hashing $\bar{E}, \bar{W}$ and enforcing $u=1$ for fresh instances.

CycleFold [3] further optimizes: secondary circuit $C_2$ that verifies $O(\log n)$ hashes and group ops is itself folded using same folding scheme, but its representation is over $\mathbb{F}_q$, which is native to $E_1$. Thus we get two interleaved IVCs: primary over $E_1$ (Pallas) proving $F$, secondary over $E_2$ (Vesta) proving verifier of primary's folding.

### 4.5 From IVC to PCD and zkVMs

PCD allows dynamic DAG where each node proves compliance of its local computation plus compliance of its parents. Folding enables efficient PCD because folding many parent proofs is multi-folding.

*   **zkVMs** (RISC-V, EVM): SuperNova + HyperNova yield *a-la-carte* zkVMs where cost per opcode is proportional to opcode circuit size, not sum of all opcodes. This is realized in projects like Lurk, Sonobe, Nexus.
*   **Decentralized proving**: PCD + folding allows untrusted workers to contribute steps; final verifier checks single proof.

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 Knowledge Soundness Proof Sketch

We sketch reduction for Nova folding.

> Lemma: NIFS is knowledge-sound if $\mathsf{Com}$ is binding and discrete log hard.

*Proof idea.* Extractor $\mathcal{E}$ rewinds prover to obtain three accepting transcripts $(r_1, r_2, r_3)$ with same $U,u,T$. From linear relations $U' = U + r_i u + r_i^2 E_0 + r_i T$, solve linear system for witnesses $W, E$ via Vandermonde inversion. Binding of commitment ensures extracted $W$ opens commitments. Forking lemma converts to IVC soundness with $O(1)$ loss [1][6].

For HyperNova CCS, same structure but with $q$ matrices; cross-term polynomial degree $d = \max |S_i|$, requiring $d+1$ rewinds.

### 5.2 Cost Model and Benchmarks (Modeled, not measured hardware)

We model $n = 2^{20}$ constraints (~1M), Pasta curves, 32 cores not assumed.

| Metric | Nova | SuperNova (k=16) | HyperNova | HyperNova $4:1$ multifold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Prover MSM size | 2 × n | 2 × |F_j| | 1 × n | 1 × n (amortized) |
| Field ops | $O(n)$ | $O(|F_j|)$ | $O(n)$ | $O(k n)$ |
| Recursive circuit (constraints) | ~12k + $\log n$ hashes | ~18k + selector | ~10k | ~14k |
| Proof size (final) | ~8 KB (Spartan) | ~9 KB | ~6 KB | ~7 KB |
| Memory peak | $O(n)$ | $O(\max |F_j|)$ | $O(n)$ | $O(n)$ |
| ZK overhead | Requires outer ZK SNARK | Same | 0 (free ZK) | 0 |

*Interpretation*: HyperNova halves MSM work vs Nova; SuperNova saves $k\times$ when instruction diversity high.

### 5.3 Cycle Overhead

Non-native field simulation without CycleFold: simulating one $\mathbb{F}_q$ multiplication in $\mathbb{F}_r$ circuit costs ~ 100–200 constraints (using limb decomposition). Nova secondary circuit does ~ 2 scalar muls = ~ 500 non-native muls => ~ 100k constraints overhead. **CycleFold** reduces this to ~ 1k constraints by delegating verification to fold itself.

### 5.4 Reference Implementation Snippet

```python
# Python-ish estimator for folding cross-term
def compute_cross_term(A,B,C, Z1,Z2, u1,u2):
    # A,B,C are sparse matrices in CSR
    Az1 = A @ Z1; Bz1 = B @ Z1; Cz1 = C @ Z1
    Az2 = A @ Z2; Bz2 = B @ Z2; Cz2 = C @ Z2
    # T = Az1*Bz2 + Az2*Bz1 - u1*Cz2 - u2*Cz1
    T = Az1 * Bz2 + Az2 * Bz1 - u1 * Cz2 - u2 * Cz1
    return T

def fold_witness(W1,W2, r):
    return W1 + r*W2
```

## 6 Limitations

*   **Arithmetization gap**: CCS is expressive but not universal for *all* custom gates used in production zkVMs (e.g., non-deterministic range checks with tables). Lookup folding remains active research; SuperNova/HyperNova handle lookups via CCS extension but prover must commit to lookup tables, increasing MSM size.

*   **Memory and parallelism**: MSM of size $2^{20}$–$2^{24}$ dominates RAM (tens of GB). GPU acceleration (Icicle, Arkworks) mitigates but introduces synchronization overhead. Incremental MSM (Pippenger) needs large window tables.

*   **Curve security**: Pasta cycle is not pairing-friendly; thus final compressed SNARK must be *non-pairing* (Spartan, Groth16 over cycle requires pairings not available). Security relies on DLOG in 254-bit field (~126 bits) which is below 128-bit post-quantum; Shor breaks all.

*   **Fiat-Shamir fragility**: 2023 soundness bug [4] demonstrates that folding schemes are subtle to instantiate: omission of public input in RO breaks knowledge soundness even if algebra correct. Formal verification (CertiK, Veridise audits) needed.

*   **Non-uniform blowup for large $k$**: SuperNova's selector vector $s$ grows linearly in $k$; for $k>128$ (EVM opcode count ~ 100), selector commitments dominate. Nebula switchboard proposal [2024/1605] addresses with logarithmic selector via Merkle.

*   **Post-quantum**: All folding schemes rely on discrete log and RO; lattice-based folding (e.g., LatticeFold) is nascent and proof sizes larger ($\approx$ 100KB).

## 7 Conclusion

Nova's introduction of folding transformed recursive SNARKs from a theoretical curiosity requiring expensive verifier circuits to a practical substrate for IVC and PCD with prover cost dominated by a single MSM. SuperNova extends this to non-uniform computation, enabling *a-la-carte* zkVMs where opcode cost is proportional, not worst-case. HyperNova completes the algebraic generalization to CCS, introduces multi-folding, zero-knowledge for free, and CycleFold to elegantly resolve the cycle-of-curves instantiation without non-native arithmetic blowup.

The synthesis is a coherent stack: **CCS arithmetization → folding ($k:1$) → CycleFold instantiation over Pasta → IVC → PCD → zkVM**. Remaining challenges are lookup integration, formal Fiat-Shamir security proofs for multi-folding, and post-quantum folding from lattice commitments. As implementations like Sonobe and Lurk mature and GPU MSMs scale to $2^{25}$ constraints, folding-based IVC is poised to become the default backbone for verifiable computation, blockchain compression (Mina-like), and privacy-preserving state machines.

Future work should explore **LatticeFold** with Ring-SIS commitments, folding for *higher-arity* CCS ($d>3$) to capture Jolt's Lasso lookups, and *incrementally verifiable* universal setup ceremonies where setup itself is folded.
