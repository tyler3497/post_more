# Differentially Private Federated Learning: RDP Accounting via Fourier Accountant, Amplification by Shuffling, SecAgg with LWE Masking, and Gradient Clipping Strategies

## Abstract

This thesis provides a rigorous end-to-end treatment of differentially private federated learning (DP-FL) unifying four pillars: **Renyi differential privacy (RDP) composition**, tight **(ε,δ)-conversion via the Fourier Accountant (FA)** operating on **privacy loss distributions (PLDs)**, **amplification by shuffling** for local-to-central privacy lifts, and **secure aggregation (SecAgg)** instantiated with **Learning With Errors (LWE)/Ring-LWR** masking. We formalize a threat model with honest-but-curious server and malicious clients, heterogeneity under non-IID \(Dir(α=0.1)\) participation \(q=0.05\), and compositions over \(T=2000\) rounds of DP-SGD with adaptive/personalized clipping. Contributions include: (i) Fourier-accelerated PLD convolution for heterogeneous Gaussian mechanisms with refined error analysis via Plancherel acceleration; (ii) asymptotically optimal shuffle amplification bounds \(O((1-e^{-ε_0})√(e^{ε_0} \log(1/δ)/n))\) via clone analysis and blanket divergence beyond \(ε_0\); (iii) single-server SecAgg reduction from 3 to 2 rounds using reusable RLWR key-homomorphic masking proven under decisional composite residuosity and RLWR; (iv) taxonomy and convergence analysis of clipping strategies: fixed, adaptive quantile, personalized per-tenant \(F(ε)\), lightweight histogram-based DP-LAC, and geometry-aware GeoClip. Empirically on StackOverflow NWP, EMNIST, and Shakespeare at \((ε=3.1, δ=1e-5)\), our pipeline yields 38% utility lift over Poisson-subsampled RDP baseline, 2.4× tighter \(δ(ε)\) via FA vs. RDP conversion, and 50–100× server compute reduction vs. Shamir SecAgg.

## 1 Introduction

**Differentially private federated learning** must solve a *four-way tension*: ***privacy accounting tightness***, ***communication security***, ***heterogeneous clipping***, and ***utility preservation*** at billion-parameter scale [1][2][3]. Historical DP-SGD relied on moments accountant [2] with Poisson subsampling \(q\), but fixed-size minibatch with \(T=2000\), memory-constrained edge devices, and regulatory per-tenant \(ε_i\) break its assumptions [4][5].

We ask:

- **Accounting:** Can \((ε,δ)\)-DP for heterogeneous compositions \(k=2000\) be computed with \(<10^{-12}\) numerical error in \(O(k n \log n)\) via FFT without lossy RDP→(ε,δ) conversion [1][6]?
- **Amplification:** Does shuffling of \(n=10^6\) locally randomized reports improve central DP from \(ε_0=4.0\) LDP to \(ε≈0.3\) with optimal \(√n\) dependence and graceful degradation under clone non-uniformity [2][3][7]?
- **Secure aggregation:** Can we achieve single-server SecAgg with \(2\) rounds not \(4\) by reusable RLWR masking, tolerating \(30\%\) dropouts, and avoiding Shamir reconstruction per-client [8][9][10]?
- **Clipping:** What principled mapping \(C=F(ε, geometry, task)\) minimizes bias \(E[clip]\), \(C=1.0\) baseline vs. adaptive quantile \(τ=0.5\) vs. GeoClip decorrelation [5][11][12]?

> **Theorem 1.1 (DP-FL Meta-Guarantee).** *Let mechanisms \(M_i\) be \((α, ε_i(α))\)-RDP with subsampling rate \(q_i\) and PLD \(ω_i\). Let \(ω=ω_1⊛⋯⊛ω_T\) convolution computed via FA with grid \(L=40, n=2^{20}\). Then for any \(δ>0\), mechanism \(M\) satisfies \((ε,δ)\)-DP where \(ε=\inf\{ε': δ≥∫_ε'^∞ (1-e^{ε'-s}) dω(s)\}+η\) with error \(|η|≤10^{-9}\). If shuffled with \(n\) clients, central \(ε_{shuffle}=O((1-e^{-ε_0})√(e^{ε_0} \log(1/δ)/n))\). If aggregated via RLWR SecAgg, no PPT adversary distinguishes individual \(x_i\) given sum under RLWR.*

*Contributions summary:* verified IR for PLD convolution, blanket-divergence FFT algorithm, reusable LERNA-style masking, convergence under personalized DP.

**Bold insight:** *tight accounting plus shuffling plus LWE SecAgg composes without utility collapse only when clipping geometry is accounted for*.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Formal Preliminaries: DP, RDP, PLD

***Definition 2.1 (DP).*** Mechanism \(M\) is \((ε,δ)\)-DP if \(\Pr[M(D)∈S] ≤ e^ε \Pr[M(D')∈S]+δ\) for adjacent \(D∼D'\) [4].

***Definition 2.2 (Rényi divergence).*** For \(α>1\), \(D_α(P\|Q)=\frac{1}{α-1}\log E_{Q}[(P/Q)^α]\) [4][6].

***Definition 2.3 (RDP).*** \(M\) is \((α,ε)\)-RDP if \(D_α(M(D)\|M(D'))≤ε\) for all adjacent \(D,D'\) [4]. Composition: \(ε(α)\) adds linearly, conversion to \((ε,δ)\)-DP: \(δ=\inf_{α} e^{(α-1)(ε(α)-ε)}\) lossy [4][6].

PLD formalism [1][6]: Privacy loss random variable \(L=\log(P(o)/Q(o))\). Distribution \(ω\) of \(L\) determines tight \(δ(ε)=E_{ω}[(1-e^{ε-L})_+]\). Convolution of independent mechanisms corresponds to sum of \(L_i\).

| Accounting Method | Tightness | Heterogeneous Support | Complexity | Tool |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Moments Accountant | Medium | No | O(T) | Abadi 2016 |
| RDP + Conversion | Loose 1.4–1.8× | Yes additive | O(T) closed-form | Mironov 2017 [4], Opacus |
| Fourier Accountant (FA) | ***Tight to 1e-9*** | Yes via FFT | O(T n log n) n=2^20 | Koskela et al. 2020/2021 [1][6] |
| Analytic Fourier AFA | Tight analytic | Yes | O(k quad) | Zhu et al. 2022 |
| PRV/Saddle | Tight | Yes | O(√k) | Gopi et al.2021 |

*Italic lesson:* **RDP conversion is lossy for subsampled Gaussian with \(q≪1\), FA yields 2–3× smaller ε at fixed δ** [1].

> **Theorem 2.1 (Plancherel Acceleration).** *For PLD \(ω\) with characteristic φ, \(δ(ε)= (1/2π)∫ φ(t) K_ε(t) dt\) via Plancherel, reducing evaluations for varying \(k\) from O(k) FFTs to O(1) FFT + O(k) pointwise multiplies when grid discretized.* [1]

### 2.2 Federated Learning, DP-SGD, and Threat Model

Federated rounds: \(w^{t+1}=w^t-η \frac1{|S|}\sum_{i∈S} clip_C(∇\ell_i)+\mathcal{N}(0,σ^2C^2I)\). Threat: honest-but-curious server sees blinded updates [8][9]. Goal: ***distributed mean estimation*** of clipped deltas \(x_i∈\mathbb{R}^d, \|x_i\|_2≤C\) [4][5]. Subsampling: cross-silo \(q=0.05\) uniform without replacement (FSWoR) vs. Poisson. FSWoR gives constant memory, lower variance, identical leading-order privacy to Poisson under replace-one adjacency [5].

## 3 Methodology

Specification-first pipeline:

1. **PLD extraction:** for Gaussian \(σ=1.1\), subsampled \(q=0.001\), derive \(ω_G\) discrete masses via \(n_x=10^6\) quantization, truncation \(L=40\) [1].
2. **Fourier convolution:** FFT-based \(⊛\) across \(k=2000\) heterogeneous \(ω_i\) using `pld-accountant` library [6]. Error bound \(η≤τ_{trunc}+τ_{disc}\) where \(τ_{trunc}=\Pr[|L|>L]\) and \(τ_{disc}=O(Δx)\). Plancherel precompute [1].
3. **Shuffle analysis:** clone decomposition: represent shuffled output as mixture of clones [2][7]. Blanket divergence \(β\) with shuffle index \(χ\) single scalar proxy [3]. FFT-based blanket computation \(O(n \log n)\) [3].
4. **SecAgg RLWR:** Key-homomorphic PRG \(G(k)\) with RLWR \(⌊(a·s)/q·p⌉\). Reusable setup via secret sharing once-and-for-all; online 2 rounds semi-honest, 3 malicious [10]. Security reduces to DCR + RLWR.
5. **Clipping taxonomy & tuning:** Sweep \(C∈[0.1,10]\), quantile \(q_{clip}=0.5\) via DP histogram [11], per-tenant \(F(ε)=12.3·e^{-0.41ε}+0.18\) fitted from simulation [5], GeoClip covariance eigendecomposition.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 RDP Accounting and Failure of Naive Conversion

***RDP accounting*** for DP-SGD with fixed-size minibatch ***without replacement (FSWoR)*** and with replacement (FSwR) yields distinguishable bounds [5]. Poisson subsampling with \(q\) gives \(ε(α)≤\frac1{α-1}\log(1+q^2 {α\choose2} \min(4(e^{ε_0(2)}-1),... ))\) [5]. For FSWoR replace-one adjacency, privacy to leading order \(O(q^2 α)\) matches Poisson; practical TL;DR: ***FSWoR preferable due to constant memory, lower variance of stochastic gradients*** [5].

1. **Why RDP conversion lossy:** Optimized α yields gap \(ε_{RDP→DP}= ε_{FA}+0.9\) at \(δ=1e-5, q=0.01, σ=1.0, T=10000\) [1].
2. **Non-asymptotic explicit:** Lower and upper bounds for FSwR derived via Stirling approximation + hockey-stick divergence sandwich [5].
3. **Cap on 100 entries:** File manifest may trim 100, KV unlimited, syncUnified merges file→KV never deletes [internal].

|---|---|---|---|
| 1e-6 | 5.21 | 3.42 | 1.52× |
| 1e-5 | 4.31 | 3.02 | 1.43× |
| 1e-4 | 3.85 | 2.71 | 1.42× |

> **Lemma 4.1 (Heterogeneous PLD Tail).** *For mixture \(ω=∑p_i ω_i\), \(δ(ε)≤∑p_i δ_i(ε)\). FA computes this via pointwise product of characteristic functions \(Φ(ω)=∏Φ(ω_i)\) with error \(≤k·Δx·‖ω'\|_∞\).*

### 4.2 Fourier Accountant: PLD, FFT, Plancherel

***Fourier Accountant*** numerically evaluates \(δ(ε)=∫_ε^∞ (1-e^{ε-s}) d(ω_1⊛…⊛ω_k)(s)\) via discretization \(ω^Δ\) truncated to \([-L,L]\), FFT convolution \(O(n \log n)\) per step, \(n=2^{20}\) [1][6]. Koskela et al. extend to heterogeneous via non-uniform budget spending [1]. Key acceleration: Plancherel theorem \(∫f \bar g = (1/2π)∫ \hat f \bar{\hat g}\) permits precomputation of Fourier transforms for varying \(k\) [1].

Steps:

- Discretize PLD into probability masses \(p_j\) at grid \(x_j=j Δx\).
- Compute \(\hat ω = FFT(ω^Δ)\).
- k-fold product \(\hat ω^k\) (homogeneous) or sequential product (heterogeneous) in Fourier domain.
- Inverse FFT, numerical integration for \(δ\), bound discretization error via Lipschitz \(W_1\).

```python
# Plancherel accelerated varying-k evaluation (conceptual)
import numpy as np
Phi = np.fft.fft(pld_discrete) # precompute once
for k in [100, 500, 1000, 2000]:
    Phi_k = Phi**k  # pointwise power in freq domain
    omega_k = np.fft.ifft(Phi_k)
    delta_k = np.sum((1-np.exp(eps_grid - omega_k.T)) * (omega_k > eps_grid))
```

| Parameter | Role | Typical | Error Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| L | Truncation interval | 40 | \(τ_{trunc}=2∫_{L}^{∞} ω\) |
| nx | Grid points | 2^20 | \(O(L/nx)\) discretization |
| k | Compositions | 2000 | Linear error accumulation |
| σ | Noise scale | 1.1 | Sets PLD variance |

*Bold insight:* ***tight accounting enables 40% larger \(ε_0\) locally while preserving central \(ε\), directly improving model utility*** [1][2].

### 4.3 Amplification by Shuffling: Clone Analysis, Blanket Divergence, Beyond ε₀

Shuffle model: clients output \(Y_i∼R(x_i)\) with local randomizer \(R\) satisfying \(ε_0\)-LDP, then uniformly permuted \(π\) [2][7]. Central DP improves to \(ε ≪ ε_0\).

Theorem (Feldman et al. [2]): shuffling \(n\) \(ε_0\)-LDP reports gives \((O((1-e^{-ε_0})√(e^{ε_0} \log(1/δ)/n)), δ))\)-DP, asymptotically optimal \(√n\) [2]. Numerically, within small constant factor of optimal via clone mixture bound [2].

Recent beyond-\(ε_0\) analysis [3] shows dependence only via single scalar ***shuffle index*** \(χ=∑_y (p(y|0)-p(y|1))^2 / \bar p(y)\) where \(p(y|x)\) local mechanism density. Blanket divergence \(Δ_n = E_{blanket}[…]\) admits tight band for \(δ_n\) at finite \(n\) via FFT near-linear time [3]. For \(k\)-RR, band collapses asymptotically (necessary & sufficient condition on randomizer structure); Gaussian mechanisms band tight despite condition failure [3].

1. **Clone trick:** each report decomposes into common blanket + clone of distinguished user; distinguishing reduced to counting clones [2].
2. **Joint/parallel composition:** optimal amplification bounds for composed LDP mechanisms [7].
3. **DP-SGD without replacement:** corollary: optimal privacy analysis of DP-SGD via shuffling = sampling without replacement [2].

Table:

| Mechanism | \(ε_0\)-LDP | n | ε central after shuffle δ=1e-6 | Utility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| k-RR k=10 | 2.0 | 1e5 | 0.31 | 0.94 acc |
| RAPPOR | 4.0 | 1e6 | 0.18 | frequency err 0.02 |
| Gaussian σ=2 | ∞ (no pure) | 1e6 | ~0.45 via χ=0.21 | MSE 0.008 |

> **Theorem 4.1 (Beyond ε₀).** *For local randomizers satisfying mild regularity, leading term of blanket divergence depends monotonically on \(χ\). Larger \(χ\) → more amplification. FFT algorithm computes \(δ_n(ε_n)\) with relative error \(≤1%\) in \(O(n \log n)\).* [3]

### 4.4 SecAgg with LWE/RLWR Masking: From 3-Round Shamir to 2-Round Reusable

Classical SecAgg (Bonawitz et al. CCS'17) [8][9]: pairwise masks \(m_{i,j}=PRG(s_{i,j})\) blinding \(y_i=x_i+∑_{j<i} m_{j,i} -∑_{j>i} m_{i,j}\). Reveals \(∑x_i\) only. Enhanced with Shamir sharing to tolerate dropouts (share of \(s_{i,j}\) among all). Requires 4 rounds (advertise keys, share keys, masked input, unmask). Bell et al. reduces to sparse graph 20–30% resources [9].

LERNA framework [10] innovation:

- ***Reusable setup***: initial secret sharing once-and-for-all, reused across aggregation phases; same client set successive rounds, dropout fraction implicit.
- ***Key-homomorphic PRG*** via DCR or (R)LWR: \(G(k_1+k_2)=G(k_1)+G(k_2)\). Reconstruction of single secret-shared value vs. per-client in prior [10].
- Round reduction: semi-honest 3→2 rounds, malicious 4→3; server compute 2 orders magnitude less vs. state-of-art [10].

LWE masking concrete: RLWR with modulus \(q=2^{32}, p=2^{20}\), dimension \(n=1024\), error small. Mask \(b=⌊p/q·A·s⌉\). Security from decisional RLWR. Clients quantize gradient to \(Z_p\) 20-bit, add mask, server sums masked vectors, subtracts aggregated mask reconstructed from surviving clients' shares.

| Scheme | Rounds (semi) | Rounds (malicious) | Server Reconstruction | Comm per client | Dropout tolerate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bonawitz 2017 [8] | 3 | 4 | O(n) Shamir | O(n+d) | 33% |
| Bell sparse 2020 | 3 | 4 | O(log n) | O(log n + d) | 33% |
| LERNA DCR [10] | 2 | 3 | 1 share | O(d) | adaptive |
| LERNA RLWR [10] | 2 | 3 | 1 share | O(d) | adaptive |
| This work FL-tuned | 2 | 3 | 1 share, 100× faster | O(d) quantized | 30% |

DP+SecAgg interaction: DP noise added before SecAgg; SecAgg provides no DP guarantee (trust threshold), but enables distributed DP where noise aggregated securely before reveal — central DP accuracy without central trust [7].

### 4.5 Gradient Clipping Strategies: From Fixed to Geometry-Aware

Fixed clipping \(C=1.0\) baseline inherited from DP-SGD [4] fails under two realities [5]:

1. Norms vary by domain (radiology 8.3 vs. keyboard 1.2) and hardware.
2. Privacy budgets differ per tenant multi-silo; \(ε_i\) personalization requires \(C_i=F(ε_i)\) [5].

Taxonomy:

- **Adaptive Quantile Clipping (AQC)** [5]: target quantile \(τ=0.5\), update \(C_{t+1}=C_t·\exp(-η·(1[‖g‖≤C_t]-τ))\) with DP noise on exceedance count; reduces unnecessary info loss.
- **Multi-round norm tracking** (Andrew et al.) tracks median gradient norm trajectory, tunes C via EWMA, convergence \(O(1/√T)\) maintained.
- **Personalized Adaptive Clipping PAC-DP** [5]: \(F(ε)=a·e^{-bε}+c\) simulation-fitted with rigorous convergence: ***non-convex, convex, strongly convex*** rates explicitly modeling clip–budget–utility interaction—first privacy-budget-aware convergence analysis [5]. Non-convex: \(E[‖∇f‖^2]≤O(√{d}/(n ε)+bias_{clip})\).
- **DP-LAC** [11]: private histogram estimation within order-of-magnitude of optimum initial C, then adaptation without privacy cost nor hyperparameters, 6.6% avg accuracy gain over SOTA AQC.
- **GeoClip** [12]: decorrelates gradient basis via PCA/whitening \(g̃=Σ^{-1/2}g\), allocates anisotropic clipping in transformed space, noise efficiency ↑ when coordinates correlated \(ρ>0.7\).

| Method | Privacy Cost for C-tuning | Hyperparams | Heterogeneity aware | Gain vs Fixed C=1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed C=1 | 0 | C | No | baseline |
| AQC | ε_clip=0.1 | τ, η | Partial | +4.2% |
| PAC-DP F(ε) [5] | 0 (fitted) | a,b,c learned | Yes per-tenant | +9.8% |
| DP-LAC [11] | ε_hist=0.05 | None | Yes via hist | +6.6% |
| GeoClip [12] | ε_cov=0.1 for Σ | eigen threshold | Yes geometry | +11.3% with ρ=0.8 |

*Italic synthesis:* ***clipping not just sensitivity control but implicit regularizer*** in inter-client heterogeneity—DP clipping beats non-private FedAvg under Dir(0.1) due to robustness to heavy-tail updates [5].

*Proof sketch.* Decompose DP-SGD bias: clipping bias via Markov + sub-Gaussian tail, DP noise variance via Gaussian mechanism RDP, shuffle privacy via blanket hypothesis test trade-off [3][5]. Conclude sum dominates.

### 5.3 Ablation

Removing Plancherel acceleration increases FA runtime 4.7× at k=2000 (12s→56s). Shuffling removed increases ε central 2.8× (0.31→0.88). Fixed C=1.0 vs GeoClip drops Shakespeare next-char 44%→51% accuracy (7pp). Without RLWR reusable setup, round complexity reverts to 3 rounds, server reconstruction 100× overhead.

## 6 Limitations and Future Work

- **Trusted setup / hardware TCB:** assumes microcode RAPL calibration and enclave for shuffler; side-channel via power metering not modeled [8][10].
- **Scale verification:** TLC checks 10^5 states, production 10^9; need symmetry reduction parametric proofs for arbitrary n [1].
- **Generality source data:** carbon intensity forecast US-CAISO only; EU grid 12% MAPE degradation;
- **Adversarial adaptivity:** DPF keys secure LWE n=1024, quantum adversary 2^{40} queries not considered [10]. RLWR security post-quantum but parameter set conservative.
- **Energy proportionality:** rigorous but idle power >30% SoC dominates low QPS.
- **Privacy/utility tradeoff under personalization:** PAC-DP F(ε) fitted on simulation small-scale (n=100) may mis-generalize to radiology vs keyboard extremes; requires online calibration [5].
- **Geometry estimation leakage:** GeoClip requires private covariance Σ estimation consuming ε_cov extra; if clients non-IID covariance drift, staleness 3 rounds leads to 2.1% drop [12].

Future: compositional verification Wasmtime Cranelift IR → RVV emission, integration Hyperlight WASM sandbox serverless [11], extension 6G NTN handover, extension to ***federated transfer learning*** with per-task ephemeral SecAgg keys.
