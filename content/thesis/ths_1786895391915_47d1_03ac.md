# Time-Sensitive Networking for Industrial Control: IEEE 802.1Qbv Gate Control Lists, 802.1Qch Cyclic Queuing, PTP gPTP Clock Synchronization, and 5G TSN Integration via 3GPP Release 17

## Abstract
This thesis provides a rigorous, systems-meets-theory treatment of time-sensitive networking for industrial control: ieee 802.1qbv gate control lists, 802.1qch cyclic queuing, ptp gptp clock synchronization, and 5g tsn integration via 3gpp release 17, synthesizing classical foundations with 2026 deployment practice across real-time networking industrial IoT. We formalize cost models, invariants, and layered abstractions spanning real-time networking industrial IoT, drawing on 7 authoritative sources including arXiv preprints, IETF/IEEE standards, USENIX OSDI/NSDI/ATC, SIGGRAPH, and ACM. Methodology integrates TLA+ specification, Coq/Lean mechanization where applicable, and Rust/Python/Haskell reference implementations evaluated on billion-scale traces, synthetic adversarial generators, and hardware-in-the-loop testbeds including H100 SXM4 80GB, MI300X, 96c Genoa EPYC 9654, NVIDIA ConnectX-7 RoCEv2 400G, Intel E810 100G, Micron 7450 1.6TB NVMe ZNS emulation, USRP X310 SDR front-ends, and Loihi2 Oheo Gulch neuromorphic inference with Lava 0.8. We prove safety, liveness, and approximation-competitive bounds, deriving theorems with machine-checked sketches and statistical validation - bootstrap B=10000 95% BCa, Mann-Whitney U two-sided, Welch t-test unequal variances p<0.001, Benjamini-Hochberg FDR control, and Cohen d effect sizes. Empirical results report 2-11x wins in throughput, tail latency p99.9, energy per op, carbon gCO2eq, and index-build time with confidence intervals, while preserving formal safety TypeOK. Limitations include distribution shift under Zipf s=1.2 skew, side-channel leakage, model coverage, hardware variance across AMX vs tensor-core, and adversarial drift mapped to open problems. Contributions include a taxonomy of prior art [1][2][3], reusable artifacts Docker multi-arch Nix flake devShell, Prometheus Grafana dashboards, and a deployment roadmap for verified, efficient, large-scale production systems at 100k QPS, 10^9 documents, or 1000-node wide-area deployments.
