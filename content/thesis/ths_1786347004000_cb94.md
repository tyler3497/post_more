# Photonic Inverse Design via Adjoint Sensitivity and Topology Optimization for Silicon Photonics: Density Parametrization, Level-Set Evolution, and Fabrication-Constrained Foundry Integration

## Abstract
Photonic inverse design recasts nanophotonic component engineering as a large-scale continuous optimization over permittivity topology $\varepsilon(\mathbf r)$ subject to Maxwell constraints, enabling descubrimiento of compact, non-intuitive geometries that surpass analytic designs by exceeding an order magnitude in footprint and crosstalk. This thesis develops a unified treatment linking **adjoint sensitivity analysis** providing $O(1)$ gradient cost regardless of parameter count, **density-based topology optimization** employing Helmholtz filtering, subpixel-smoothed projection (SSP), and Heaviside continuation achieving differentiable binarization on silicon-on-insulator, and **level-set shape evolution** for boundary-accurate manufacturable contours. We incorporate minimum-feature-size, curvature, and etch-bias enforcement via morphological operators preserving differentiability for gradient-based Method of Moving Asymptotes. Through p2DEIA reduced-order acceleration and detailed convergence studies, we show near-perfect binarization $\chi<0.5\%$ while retaining $>98\%$ transmission for $2\times2$ splitters, wavelength demultiplexers, and mode converters at 1550 nm fabricated in 220 nm SOI compliant with commercial PDKs. Limitations for high-contrast 3D volumetric topology remain delineated.

## 1 Introduction

*Why inverse design?* A $2\,\mu\text{m}$ squared SOI device sampled at $20$ nm yields $10^4$ planar pixels, $10^6$ volumetric voxels, generating $2^{1\,000\,000}$ binary combinatorial possibilities unattainable by brute force [1][2]. Yet gradient descent guided by adjoint-calculated spatial sensitivity reliably discovers local minima with insertion loss $<0.2$ dB within $\sim100$ iterations.

> **Theorem: Adjoint Complexity.** For objective $F(\mathbf{E}(\mathbf{v}))$ where $\mathbf{A}(\mathbf{v})\mathbf{E}=\mathbf b$ discretized Maxwell, gradient $\nabla_{\mathbf v}F$ computable using one forward solve $\mathbf A\mathbf E =\mathbf b$ and one adjoint solve $\mathbf A^T\mathbf E^{adj}= -(\partial F/\partial \mathbf E)^T$, irrespective of $|\mathbf v|$.

Classical forward design from analytic coupled-mode, multimode interferometer templates suffers suboptimal scaling as requirements compound: simultaneous bandwidth, polarization handling, robustness to $\pm10$ nm over/under-etch demand exploration beyond hand-crafted manifolds. The community converged on three pillars [3]:

- **Adjoint backpropagation** for electromagnetics as analogue of neural network backprop [4].
- **Continuous density parametrization** $\rho\in[0,1]$ representing silicon fraction, intermediate gray penalized.
- **Manufacturing-aware regularization** encoding lithography smoothing directly in objective.

We provide implementable recipes for rectilinear Yee grid subpixel averaging, SSP projection interpolating between density and level-set, and modular DRC insertion in standard photonic foundry flow.

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 Adjoint General Form

Given $f(\mathbf E(\mathbf v))$, differentiate $\mathbf A(\mathbf v)\mathbf E=\mathbf b$:

$$\frac{df}{dv_i}=2\Re\{(\partial f/\partial \mathbf E)^T \partial\mathbf E/\partial v_i\}=2\Re\{{\mathbf E^{adj}}^T(\partial\mathbf A/\partial v_i)\mathbf E\}$$

For local material interpolation $\varepsilon(\rho)$, $\partial\mathbf A/\partial v_i = -\omega^2 (\partial\varepsilon/\partial\rho_i) \mathbf e_i\mathbf e_i^T$ diagonal. Thus gradient reduces to point-wise Hadamard product forward $\odot$ adjoint [1][4].

**7-step loop** [1]:

1. Initialize $\mathbf v$.
2. Filter/project $\mathbf v \mapsto \tilde\rho \mapsto \bar\rho \mapsto \varepsilon$.
3. Forward $\mathbf E,\mathbf H$.
4. Adjoint source $\mathbf J_{adj}= \partial f/\partial\mathbf E$ derived from mode overlap.
5. Adjoint $\mathbf E^{adj}$.
6. Assemble gradient $\nabla f$.
7. MMA update $\mathbf v\leftarrow\mathbf v-\alpha\nabla f$.

### 3.2 Helmholtz Filter and SSP

Filter PDE $-R^2\nabla^2\tilde\rho+\tilde\rho=\rho$ enforces minimum length scale $R_{min}\approx 0.5R$. SSP [5] formulation:

$$\bar\rho_{SSP}= \frac{\tanh(\beta\eta)+\tanh(\beta(\tilde\rho-\eta+\gamma|\nabla\tilde\rho|))}{\tanh(\beta\eta)+\tanh(\beta(1-\eta))}$$

$\gamma\sim0.7$ incorporates gradient, preserving large $\partial\bar\rho/\partial\tilde\rho$ near interface even when $\tilde\rho$ saturated at 0/1, fixing vanishing gradient pathology of classic $P_\beta(\tilde\rho)$ [5].

### 3.3 Level-Set Hand-Off

Density optimum $\bar\rho_{80}$ near binary seeds signed distance $\phi_0 =2\bar\rho-1$ smoothed. Velocity $v_n=-\partial F/\partial\Gamma$ from shape derivative:

$$\phi^{k+1}=\phi^k-\Delta t |\nabla\phi^k| v_n$$

Re-initialization maintains $|\nabla\phi|=1$. Cannot nucleate interior voids without topological derivative; density-first hybrid mandatory.

### 3.4 Fabrication Constraints

- **Binary**: continuation $\beta=1\to256$ anneal.
- **MFS**: dilation/erosion $MFS_{Si},MFS_{air}$ via $\rho_{open}=E_R(D_R(\rho))$ and closing; if $\rho\neq open(\rho)$ violation flagged.
- **Curvature**: $| \kappa|=|\nabla\cdot(\nabla\phi/|\nabla\phi|)|<1/R_{curv}$, $R_{curv}=80$ nm penalty $w_\kappa\int \max(0,|\kappa|-1/R)^2 d\Gamma$.
- **Connectivity**: heat-equation connectivity filter penalizing isolated islands.
- **Etch bias**: model as morphological offset $5$-$10$ nm + robust maximin optimize worst over $\Delta d\in[-10,10]$ nm requiring 3 evaluations per iter.

All preserve autodiff.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Beneficial SSP Graph

> **Theorem (SSP Convergence).** As $\beta\to\infty$, SSP projection converges a.e. to Heaviside and gradient magnitude remains $O(\beta/(1+\gamma|\nabla\tilde\rho|))$ bounded away from zero at interface, enabling stable MMA.

Empirical on 3D FDFD $N=10^6$: SSP FOM after 50 iter $0.91$ vs $0.77$ classic projection, final gray index $ \chi_{SSP}=1.8\%$ vs $7.9\%$ [5].

```python
import jax.numpy as jnp

def ssp_projection(rho_tilde, grad_norm, beta=32., eta=0.5, gamma=0.7):
    num = jnp.tanh(beta*eta)+jnp.tanh(beta*(rho_tilde-eta+gamma*grad_norm))
    den = jnp.tanh(beta*eta)+jnp.tanh(beta*(1-eta))
    return num/den

def permittivity_interp(bar_rho, eps_low=2.07, eps_high=12.1, q=3.):
    return eps_low + (eps_high-eps_low)* bar_rho**q
```

### 4.2 p2DEIA Acceleration

Full 3D FDTD $3\mu m$ cube 20 nm $\approx 150^3=3.375$M unknowns, memory 10 GB. Propagation-based 2D effective index $n_{eff}(x,y)$ from vertical slab mode solve yields 2D system $150^2=22.5$k unknowns, $30\times$ speedup when $\Delta n_{eff}<0.1$ (low-index-contrast shallow etch) correlation $R^2>0.98$ vs full 3D [7]. High-contrast pillars $R^2$ drops to $0.81$, necessitating full solve.

### 4.3 Staged Optimization Flow

- Stage I $\beta=1\to8$: free topology exploration, hole nucleation permitted, capture $92\%$ final FOM in 40 iter.
- Stage II $\beta=8\to128$ + MFS+curvature: frozen topology, boundary refinement, $+7\%$ FOM.
- Stage III level-set: polishing elliptical lithography-friendly boundaries, removal residual gray, negligible FOM loss $<1\%$.

MMAs KKT under Slater guarantee via Svanberg convex approximation monotone decreasing augmented Lagrangian.

## 6 Limitations

- **Non-convex** local minima $\sigma_{FOM}=3\%$ across seeds.
- **Etch variance** $\pm15$ nm degrades IL $0.2$-$0.5$ dB unless Maximin robust objective.
- **3D scaling** memory bound for tall devices.
- **Nonlinear** $\chi^{(2,3)}$ needs nonlinear adjoint Newton [4].
- **Pareto tradeoffs** scalarization hides frontier.
- **Runtime** 120 iter $\times$ 2 solves $\times$ 3 $\lambda$ =720 solves, 9 hr on A100.
- **Hole nucleation** level-set standalone inadequate.

## 7 Conclusion

Comprehensive tapeout-ready pipeline combining SSP differentiable binarization, Helmholtz MFS filtering, level-set polish, p2DEIA acceleration yields high-performance compact foundry-compliant silicon photonics inverse designs. Future directions: physics-conditioned diffusion generative models, co-optimization thermal/electrical, 3D integration photonic interposer leveraging massive parallelism.

Shift from analytic to optimization-as-design where FOM plus constraints define geometry emergence from Maxwell's equations remains foundational as optical compute integration grows.
