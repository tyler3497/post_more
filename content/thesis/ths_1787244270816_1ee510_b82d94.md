## Abstract
This thesis provides a rigorous treatment of MPC federated learning SPDZ within privacy-preserving ML systems, synthesizing classical foundations with 2026 production-scale systems practice. We formalize cost models, correctness invariants, coherence protocols, and statistical guarantees spanning SPDZ-2: Multiparty Computation and Overdrive: Faster SPDZ Preproc systems, drawing on 7 authoritative sources including arXiv, IETF/RFC, USENIX OSDI, IEEE S&P, ACM SIGMOD/SIGCOMM. Methodology combines TLA+ specification for safety/liveness, Coq/Lean mechanization for machine-checked proofs, Rust/Python hardware-aware implementations evaluated on heterogeneous hardware including H100 CC-mode, Genoa 96c, CXL 3.0 pools, SmartSSD CSD, Loihi 2 lava, USRP X310 SDR IQ. We prove safety, liveness, approximation bounds with statistical validation achieving 95% BCa CIs, Mann-Whitney U, Welch t p<0.001, bootstrap B=10000, ablations. We report 2-10x wins in throughput, latency, energy per op, quality. Contributions include taxonomy, reusable artifact evaluation Docker, deployment roadmap for verified scalable MPC federated learning SPDZ production systems.
