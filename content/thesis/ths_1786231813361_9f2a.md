# Differential Privacy for Time Series under Continual Observation: Adaptive Composition via Filters and Odometers, Optimality of the Binary Tree Mechanism, and Architectures for Private Kalman Filtering

**PhD Thesis** — Subagent 9/10 | `ths_1786231813361_9f2a` | anon#9f2a | ts: 1786231813361  
*Topic:* **Differential Privacy for Time Series** | *Field:* Continual Observation, Adaptive Composition, Filtering & Control

## Abstract

**Continual observation** transforms differential privacy from a batch guarantee into a time-uniform martingale problem where each user contributes to all future outputs. This thesis synthesizes three pillars of private time-series analysis: **(i)** *fully adaptive composition* via **privacy filters** and **odometers** with Rényi DP, zCDP, and f-DP validity conditions; **(ii)** the *binary tree (a.k.a. tree aggregation) mechanism* of Dwork, Naor, Pitassi, Rothblum and Chan, Shi, Song, whose dyadic decomposition yields `O(log²T/ε)` pure-DP and `O(log^{3/2}T·√log(1/δ)/ε)` approximate-DP error and is recently shown optimal for ℓ∞ continual counting [1][3]; **(iii)** *private Kalman filtering* where sensing, shaping pre-filters, and post-filtering trade sensitivity against mean-square estimation error. We prove filter-correctness lemmas, unpack the geometric-budget extension to unbounded horizons, give SDP formulations for optimal input-perturbation shaping, and show how adaptive composition bounds the cumulative `(ε,δ)` loss of a stream of Kalman updates. The result is a unified architecture book-keeping `ε_t` per node of the aggregation tree while maintaining `P_t|t` stability.

**Keywords:** differential privacy, continual observation, adaptive composition, privacy filter, privacy odometer, binary tree mechanism, tree aggregation, Kalman filter, RDP, zCDP, f-DP, state estimation

## 1. Introduction

Modern infrastructure — *smart grids, traffic loop detectors, wearable ECG monitors, financial tickers* — emits **correlated time series** whose statistics must be released in real time. The naïve approach of adding independent Laplace noise at each `t` incurs linear privacy loss: a single participation event `σ_t = {x_t}` influences all releases `r_k` for `k≥t`, implying `T`-fold composition.

> **Theorem (Continual Observation Hardness — Dwork et al. 2010, Chan et al. 2010 informal):** For event-level ε-DP continual counting of a binary stream of length `T`, any mechanism must incur `Ω(log T / ε)` ℓ∞ error, while independent perturbation incurs `Ω(T/ε)`. Dyadic reuse is necessary [2][3].

Two literatures converge:

- **Computer-science track:** Dwork et al. [DNPR10] and Chan-Shi-Song [CSS10] introduced *continual observation* and the **binary tree mechanism**. Lower bounds and recent *optimality* results for approximate DP [1] closed a 15-year gap up to `√log(1/δ)` factors.
- **Control-theoretic track:** Le Ny–Pappas [0] extended DP to dynamical systems with multiple participants, defined adjacency over input signals in `ℓ_1` or `ℓ_2`, and approximated stable filters by private versions minimizing distortion. Kalman filtering becomes non-trivial because the filter's impulse response `h` convolves private inputs.

A third axis — **adaptive composition** — governs how an analyst who chooses `ε_t` after seeing `r_{<t}` can still guarantee global `(ε,δ)`-DP. Rogers et al. introduced *privacy filters* (stopping rules) and *odometers* (uniform upper bounds). Whitehouse et al. 2023 [5] showed tight filters matching advanced composition constants; Feldman–Zirfas and Papernot–Steinke gave RDP filters; Koskela et al. and subsequent work studied *f-DP/GDP filters* and proved the naïve f-DP filter **invalid** in general [6][7].

*Contributions:*

1. Unified presentation of continual observation model, event vs. user-level adjacency, `ℓ_∞` vs `ℓ_2` error, and adaptation across time.
2. Full construction and variance analysis of binary tree mechanism with geometric budget for `T→∞`, and recent optimality lower bounds.
3. Formal derivation of *approximate Rényi DP filter* and *zCDP odometer* via Ville's inequality and time-uniform martingale concentration, mapping to DP-Kalman.
4. Private Kalman filter taxonomy: input perturbation vs. output perturbation vs. two-stage shaping [0][4] with SDP synthesis and sensitivity bounds for stable `G(z)`.
5. End-to-end architecture showing how tree nodes act as private integrators feeding a post-filtered Kalman observer, with provable `(ε,δ)` composition and MSE guarantees.

## 2. Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Differential Privacy Definitions

A mechanism `M: X^n → Y` is **(ε,δ)-DP** if ∀ adjacent `D≃D'` and ∀ `S⊆Y`:

```
Pr[M(D)∈S] ≤ exp(ε)·Pr[M(D')∈S] + δ
```

**Rényi DP** of order `α>1`: `D_α(M(D)||M(D')) ≤ ε(α)`. **Zero-concentrated DP (zCDP)**: `D_α ≤ ρ·α`. **f-DP**: trade-off function `f = T(P,Q)` lower-bounded. GDP is `f = G_μ`.

Adjacency for time series:

- **Event-level:** two streams differ in a single increment at one time `t`: `||x - x'||_∞ ≤1` but only at one coordinate.
- **User-level/w-time:** differ in up to `w` consecutive contributions (w-event). **User-level (unbounded):** one user's entire trajectory can change.

We focus on *event-level* for counting and *ℓ2 signal adjacency* `||u - u'||_2 ≤1` for filtering [0].

### 2.2 Continual Release Model

Stream `σ = (σ_1,…,σ_T)` arrives online. Mechanism `M` releases `a_t = M_t(σ_{≤t})` at each `t`. Privacy holds over full vector `a_{1:T}` jointly. The challenge: **correlated sensitivity**. For sum `S_t = Σ_{i≤t} σ_i`, each `σ_i` influences `Ω(T-i+1)` outputs if naïvely released.

*Continual observation* originates from monitoring tasks: COVID counts, power load, click counts, spec monitoring via all-aggregations requiring unbounded contributions [0].

### 2.3 Composition Landscape

| Type | Requires fixed `ε_i`? | Guarantee | Rate | Adaptivity |
|------|-----------------------|-----------|------|------------|
| **Basic** | No | `Σ ε_i` | Linear | Any |
| **Advanced (Dwork-Roth)** | Yes | `√(2k log(1/δ')) Σ ε_i² + Σ ε_i(e^{ε_i}-1)` | `~√k` | Non-adaptive |
| **Fully adaptive filter (Whitehouse 2023)** | No | Stops when `Σ ε_i²` crosses budget | `~√k` + constants tight | *Fully adaptive* |
| **RDP filter (Feldman-Zirfas)** | Mechanism choice adaptive | `Σ ε_i(α)` stays below target | RDP→DP conversion | Fully adaptive |
| **GDP / f-DP filter** | Naïve crossing invalid | Needs CLT correction | `μ-GDP` tight only under `q<0.2` | Conditional validity [6] |

## 3. Methodology

### 3.1 Binary Tree Mechanism Construction

Let `T = 2^L`. Complete binary tree of height `L`. Leaf `i` corresponds to `σ_i`. Internal node at level `ℓ` covering interval `[j·2^ℓ+1, (j+1)2^ℓ]` stores partial sum `c_{ℓ,j} = Σ_{i∈interval} σ_i`. To release running sum `S_t`, decompose `[1,t]` into `≤L` dyadic intervals via binary representation. Example: `t=13=1101b` → intervals `[1,8],[9,12],[13,13]`.

Noise addition: each node maintains **one-time** perturbation `ĉ_{ℓ,j}=c_{ℓ,j}+Noise`. Changing one leaf affects at most `L+1 = O(log T)` nodes (path to root). Sensitivity `Δ = log T`. With Laplace `Lap(log T / ε)`, entire tree is `ε`-DP and each `S_t` summed over ≤log T noisy nodes → error `O(log²T / ε)` w.h.p., improved analysis to `O(log²T / ε)` ℓ∞ [1].

For **approximate DP**, use Gaussian noise `N(0, σ²)` with `σ = O(log T √(log(1/δ))/ε)` → error `O(log^{3/2}T √(log(1/δ))/ε)` [5]. Recent work shows this is **optimal** `Ω(log^{3/2}T)` lower bound for `ε∈(0,1)` [1].

*Unbounded T:* allocate privacy budget geometrically over tree levels: `ε_ℓ = ε / (2^{ℓ+1})?` Not summable; alternative distributing `ε_ℓ ∝ 1/(ℓ+1)^{1+η}` or using *evolving* construction with doubling T and `ε/(log T)` splitting, or geometric series over height assuming expanding tree via truncated construction [0].

### 3.2 Adaptive Composition via Filters and Odometers

Consider adaptively chosen sequence `(A_n)` where `A_n` is `ε_n`-DP conditional on past outputs.

> **Quote:** *Adaptive composition is not about how many algorithms you ran, but about Σ ε_n² you consumed.* — Whitehouse et al. 2023 insight [5]

**Privacy Loss Random Variable:** `L_n = log(P(M(D)=a)/Q(M(D')=a))`. Martingale `M_n = Σ L_i - Σ E[L_i|F_{i-1}]`.

*Filter construction* sketch (`(ε,δ)`):

```
Fix target ε,δ. For threshold τ = ε + log(1/δ') variance term
Define stopping time: N = inf{ n : Σ ε_i² > τ²/2  or  Σ ε_i·(e^{ε_i}-1)/(e^{ε_i}+1) excess }
Then composed mechanism stopped at N is (ε,δ)-DP.
```

Recent refinement: approximate zCDP filter handles `δ_n` per mechanism:

```
If Σ ρ_n ≤ ρ_target - 2√(2ρ_target log(1/δ)) correction,
then overall (ε,δ) from ρ→(ε,δ) conversion holds adaptively [5].
```

**RDP filter** [Feldman-Zirfas 2021]: maintain `Σ_i ε_i(α) ≤ B_α ∀α∈Λ`. Stop when any α violates. Converts to `(ε,δ)` via `ε = B_α + log(1/δ)/(α-1)`.

**f-DP invalidity:** Tran et al. 2026 [6] show natural `f`-composing filter `f_1 ⊗ f_2 ⊗ ...` stopped at threshold `f_global` fails **pointwise** because trade-off curves non-comparable under optional stopping; counterexamples with asymmetric hypotheses. Valid iff curves are *ordered* or CLT regime (`q<0.2` or `q>0.8` subsampling) where GDP approximation holds [7].

### 3.3 Private Kalman Filtering

System:

```
x_{k+1}=F x_k + w_k,   w_k ~ N(0,Q)
y_k = H x_k + v_k + Σ_i u^{(i)}_k   // aggregate of private participants
z_k = C x_k   // quantity to privatize
```

Goal: release `ẑ_k ≈ z_k` preserving DP for signals `u^{(i)}`.

Le Ny–Pappas taxonomy [0][4]:

- **Input perturbation:** `ŷ = y + ν`, `ν∼Lap(Δ/ε)`, then filter `ẑ = G*ŷ`. Sensitivity = `max_i || G_i ||_1` where `G_i` is impulse response from participant `i`. Simple, but noise amplified by filter gain.
- **Output perturbation:** compute `z = G*y` non-privately, then `ẑ = z + η`. Sensitivity = `||G||_1` (ℓ1 sensitivity for Laplace). Better when `G` is low-pass.
- **Two-stage shaping:** Insert prefilter `S` before Gaussian mechanism, and Kalman smoother `K` after:

```
        u → S → + w → _ → K → ẑ
                 ↑
       (private) Gaussian DP noise scaled to ||S||_2
```

Optimal `S` for static case solves SDP:

```
min_S  Tr( E[(z-ẑ)(z-ẑ)^T] )
s.t.   ||S||_2 ≤ γ(ε,δ)   // induced H2 gain controls sensitivity
```

Described in Le Ny 2017 [4]: `S` solves `min Tr(W)` with LMI ` [W  (*); * I] ⪰0`.

Stability: if `G` is *stable* with `||G||_2 < ∞`, then sensitivity bounded independent of `T`, enabling continual release with `O(1)` noise rather than `O(T)` [0].

**Connection to binary tree:** unstable integrators `G(z)=1/(1-z^{-1})` have infinite sensitivity, needing tree mechanism. Decompose integrator into dyadic blocks (tree nodes) each DP, then reconstruct via post-filter.

```python
import numpy as np

def binary_tree_continual_count(stream, eps, delta=1e-6, mode='approx'):
    n = len(stream)
    L = (n-1).bit_length()
    # tree as dict level-> array
    tree = {}
    for l in range(L+1):
        size = (n + (1<<l)-1)//(1<<l)
        sens = 1.0
        if mode == 'pure':
            scale = (L+1)*sens/eps
            tree[l] = np.random.laplace(0, scale, size=size)
        else:
            sigma = (L+1)**0.5 * np.sqrt(2*np.log(1.25/delta))/eps
            tree[l] = np.random.normal(0, sigma, size=size)
        # add true counts later in practice
    def query(t): # 1-indexed inclusive
        res=0.0
        bit=0
        idx=t
        while idx>0:
            if idx & 1:
                level=bit
                j=(t-idx)//(1<<bit)  # rough
                res+=tree[level][j] if j < len(tree[level]) else 0
            idx>>=1; bit+=1
            t//=2
        return res
    return [query(i) for i in range(1,n+1)]

def private_kalman_update(x_pred, P_pred, z, R_priv, H):
    # Standard KF with inflated R due to privacy noise
    S = H @ P_pred @ H.T + R_priv
    K = P_pred @ H.T @ np.linalg.inv(S)
    x_upd = x_pred + K @ (z - H @ x_pred)
    P_upd = (np.eye(len(x_pred)) - K @ H) @ P_pred
    return x_upd, P_upd

# SDP sketch for optimal prefilter S (static)
# minimize Tr(C (I) C^T) s.t. ||S||_2 <= gamma
```

```haskell
-- Dyadic decomposition as functional tree (Haskell sketch)
data Tree a = Leaf a | Node (Tree a) (Tree a) a

build :: [a] -> Tree a
build [x] = Leaf x
build xs = let (l,r) = splitAt (length xs `div` 2) xs
               tl = build l
               tr = build r
           in Node tl tr (mconcat [value tl, value tr])

-- Query running sum with ≤ log T nodes
query :: Int -> Tree Double -> Double
query t tree = go t tree 0
  where
    go 0 _ acc = acc
    go _ (Leaf v) acc = acc+v
    go k (Node l r v) acc
      | k <= size l = go k l acc
      | otherwise   = go (k - size l) r (acc + value l)

-- Sensitivity model for f-DP filter validity condition
type TradeOff = Double -> Double
validGDPFilter :: Double -> Double -> Bool
validGDPFilter q mu = q < 0.2 || q > 0.8  -- from Tran et al. CLT regime [6]
```

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Fully Adaptive RDP Accounting for Time Series

Let analyst at time `t` choose sampling rate `q_t` and noise multiplier `σ_t` after seeing `ẑ_{<t}`. Each subsampled Gaussian mechanism contributes RDP `ε_t(α) = 1/(α-1) log( E_{B}[...]) ≤ q_t² α /σ_t²` for `q_t ≤0.2`. The *RDP filter* maintains upper envelope:

```
B_α(t) = Σ_{i≤t} ε_i(α)
Stop if ∃ α: B_α(t) > B_α^target
```

Key result [5][7]: this stopping rule yields `(ε,δ)`-DP without stronger pointwise DP assumption. Conversion cost `log|Λ|/λ` for finite `Λ` of α-grid is negligible.

*Odometer variant* provides *always-valid* bound `ε̂(t) = O( √(B(t) log(log B(t)/δ)) + B(t) )` where `B(t)= Σ ρ_i`. Uses mixture martingale + Ville inequality for confidence sequence.

### 4.2 Hybrid Tree + Filter for Stable LTI Systems

We split filter `G = G_stable * Integrator^p`. For pure integrator (counting), tree mechanism essential. For exponentially stable tail, sensitivity decays as `||g||_1 = Σ |h_k| < ∞`. Private version via output perturbation has bounded error independent of `T`:

```
||ẑ - z||_∞ ≤ O( ||G||_1 * log T / ε )   Laplace
||ẑ - z||_2 ≤ O( ||G||_2 * √(log T log 1/δ)/ε)
```

**Hybrid algorithm**:

1. Maintain binary tree over raw contributions `u_k`
2. For each node commit, add Gaussian noise once
3. Online release `ŝ_t = Σ dyadic nodes` (event-level DP)
4. Feed `ŝ_t` through *post-filter* `G_stable` to obtain `ẑ_t`

Error decomposes: tree error amplified by `||G_stable||_2`, not `T`. This yields `o(T)` utility impossible under independent perturbation.

### 4.3 f-DP Filter Failure and CLT Rescue

Tran et al. [6] counterexample:

- Choose two mechanisms: `M1` is `μ1-GDP` with trade-off `G_{μ1}`, `M2` depends on output of `M1`: if `M1` outputs 0, `M2` is very private, else very non-private.
- Natural composed curve `f1 ⊗ f2(worst)` predicts crossing at time 2, yet actual type I/type II trade-off achievable by adversary who can *adaptively* choose which branch violates target `G_μ`.
- Formally, `⊗` does **not commute** with conditioning on `F_{n-1}`.

Sufficient condition for validity: trade-off curves are *totally ordered* pointwise or mechanism family is *Gaussian* with CLT approximations [7]. For DP-SGD (`q<0.2`), `f-DP ↔ μ_n^2 = Σ (q_n * (e^{ε_n}-1))²` approximation yields filter:

```
μ(t)² = Σ μ_i², halt if μ(t) > μ_target
```

provably valid up to `O(q³)` slack [6].

### 4.4 Private State Estimation Guarantees

Consider multi-participant Kalman where each participant `i` injects `u^{(i)}` into measurement `y = Σ H_i x^{(i)} + v`.

**Adjacency** `Adj = { ||u^{(i)} - u^{(i)'}||_2 ≤1 }`. Sensitivity of linear system `L: u→z` is induced `ℓ2` gain `Δ_2 = sup_{||u||_2≤1} ||L(u)||_2 = ||G||_{H∞}` for LTI.

Gaussian mechanism `ẑ = z + N(0, (Δ_2 c(δ)/ε)² I)` yields `(ε,δ)`-DP. MSE:

```
J = E||z-ẑ||² = σ_priv²·Tr( K Cov... )
```

With two-stage `S,K`, optimal `S` whitens high-gain frequencies where adversary could distinguish. SDP [4] yields **up to 3× MSE improvement** over input perturbation for second-order low-pass plants.

*Continual version* uses binary tree's `O(log T)` noise per level multiplied by `H∞` norm remains bounded.

## 5. Empirical Evaluation / Proofs

### 5.1 Tree Error Analysis

Let err_t = Σ_{node∈Decomp(t)} η_node.

- Laplace: `η~Lap(λ)` where `λ=(log T)/ε`. Variance `2λ²`. Sum of ≤L terms → Var ≤2Lλ² = O(L³/ε²) → whp `O(L²/ε·log(1/β))` via Chernoff + union over T. Tight analysis improves `log^{5/2}` to `log²` using sub-exponential Bernstein.

- Gaussian: variance `σ²L`. ℓ∞ over T: via Gaussian maximal inequality, `E max_t |err_t| ≤ O(σ√(L log T)) = O(log^{3/2} √(log 1/δ)/ε)`.

Lower bound proof sketch [1]: packing argument with `Ω(log T)` disjoint intervals needing distinct leakage; via anti-concentration of `Ber(1/2)^log T` queries, any `(ε,δ)` algorithm incurs `Ω(log^{3/2} / ε)` under ℓ∞. Uses factorization norm `γ_2` of continual-counting matrix `M_count` with `γ_2(M)=Θ(log T)`.

### 5.2 Adaptive Filter Correctness Lemma

**Lemma (RDP filter valid).** Define filtered σ-algebra `F_t`. Let `M_t | F_{t-1}` be `(α, ε_t(α))`-RDP. Define `L_t^α = exp((α-1)(log(Q/P)-ε_t(α)))`. Then `∏_{i≤t} L_i^α` is supermartingale with `E≤1`. By Ville's maximal inequality `Pr[∃t: log∏L_i > log(1/δ)/(α-1)]≤δ`. Stock roles of `D,D'`. Conversion yields `(ε(α)+log(1/δ)/(α-1),δ)`-DP for stopped process. ∎

*Odometer* uses mixture over `λ`: `M_t(λ)=exp(λ S_t - λ²V_t/2)`. Integrating against truncated Gaussian prior gives `Pr[∃t: S_t ≥ threshold(V_t)] ≤ δ`. Substituting `S_t = privacy loss` yields bound [5].

## 6. Limitations and Open Problems

- **Unbounded sensitivity of unstable plants:** Pure integrators need tree; generally unstable `ρ(F)>1` gives exponential sensitivity `||F^k||` → impossible without *a-priori* horizon bound `T`. Open: graceful degradation under `ρ>1` with prediction.
- **Event vs User efficiency gap:** user-level over `w`-window inflates tree sensitivity by `w`. `w=365` daily active user costs `365×`. Sliding-window tree with decay (`d` decreasing) heuristic used in practice but lacks tight accounting under expiration [real deployments 6].
- **Adaptive f-DP full characterization:** necessary and sufficient condition for valid f-filter beyond GDP CLT remains open [6]. Ordered family conjecture partially solved but counterexamples exist for heterogeneous `μ_i`.
- **Non-linear Kalman (EKF/UKF):** linearization breaks Lipschitz bounds; sensitivity becomes state-dependent. Bounds via `L2`-bounded Jacobian only local.
- **Correlated time series:** DP assumes neighboring streams differ independently; temporal correlation breaks independence, side-channel leakage via autocorrelation. Pufferfish or `⟨ρ⟩`-zCDP extensions needed.
- **Systolic implementations:** binary tree needs `O(log T)` memory per time; for `T=10^9` edge devices, SRAM heavy. Approximate streaming via *sparse tree* with `O(log T / ε)` sketch trade-offs unexplored.
- **Privacy-utility Pareto frontier:** SDP optimal prefilter assumes known spectral density of private signal; unknown spectrum → minimax regret formulation.

## 7. Conclusion

We unified three decades of private time series: **continual observation** as the problem, **binary tree aggregation** as the (optimal) mechanism for prefix sums, **adaptive filters/odometers** as the accounting enabling data-dependent ε_t selection, and **private Kalman filtering** as the system-theoretic end-use.

Takeaways:

1. Never perturb independently; dyadic reuse reduces cost from `T` to `log T` and, for Gaussian, optimal `log^{3/2}` [1][5].
2. Adaptive composition must be accounted via *supermartingale* methods — RDP and zCDP filters match advanced composition even when `ε_t` depends on prior outputs [5]; naïve f-DP composition is **invalid** [6].
3. Stable filters can be privatized with bounded distortion; unstable ones must be decomposed via tree; two-stage shaping via SDP yields concrete MSE выигрыш [0][4].
4. End-to-end architecture: tree nodes serve as private integrators, their sum post-filtered by Kalman gain, whole pipeline tracked by one RDP filter.

Future work points toward **non-linear state-space DP**, **Pufferfish-correlated** notions, and hardware-efficient tree sketches for `10^12`-length IoT streams.
