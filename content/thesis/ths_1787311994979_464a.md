# Type-Theoretic Foundations of Cubical Type Theory: Kan Operations, Univalence Computation, Higher Inductive Types, and De Morgan Algebra Semantics

## Abstract
This thesis develops a self-contained account of cubical type theory (CTT) as formulated by Cohen, Coquand, Huber and Mörtberg (CCHM), focusing on the computational interpretation of Voevodsky's univalence axiom and higher inductive types (HITs) via Kan filling operations. We analyze the De Morgan cube category, its interval algebra with connections, reversals, and diagonal, and show how uniform Kan composition decomposes into homogeneous composition and transport. We prove canonicity for closed booleans and natural numbers, detail Glue types and univalence computation, and reconstruct the semantics of S¹, spheres, suspension, and propositional truncation as initial algebras in cubical sets. The contribution unifies operational, presheaf, and topos-theoretic views, clarifying why path types PathP internalize equality with definitional behavior, and why univalence computes. Methodologically we combine syntax-directed operational semantics, logical families, and cubical sets over DM.

## 1. Introduction

Cubical type theory [1] was introduced to give a **constructive** interpretation of the *univalence axiom* and to make *function extensionality* directly provable without axiomatic extensions. Unlike Book HoTT where univalence is postulated, cubical type theory provides a computational rule for `ua : Equiv A B → Path U A B` [1][4].

> Theorem: Univalence Computation. For `e : A ≃ B`, `transport (ua e) a ≡ e.forward a` judgmentally in CCHM.

The central insight is to replace abstract identity types `Id_A` by **path types** `PathP (λ i → A i) a b` internalizing maps from an interval object `I`. The interval is not a type in the ordinary sense; it is a *De Morgan algebra* [2][3] with operations `∧, ∨, ¬, 0, 1` satisfying:

- `i ∧ i = i`, `i ∨ i = i`, `¬¬i = i`
- `¬(i ∧ j) = ¬i ∨ ¬j`, `¬(i ∨ j) = ¬i ∧ ¬j` [6]
- `i ∧ 0 = 0`, `i ∨ 1 = 1`, `i ∧ 1 = i`, `i ∨ 0 = i`

The presence of *connections* and *reversal* distinguishes the De Morgan cubes from cartesian or distributive variants [5]. This algebraic richness simplifies Kan filling to a derived operation from primitive *composition*.

Our contributions are:

1. A precise account of **Kan operations**: `hcomp`, `transp`, `comp` and their uniform variant.
2. A constructive proof of **canonicity** following Huber [3] via typed operational semantics and computability predicates.
3. A detailed reconstruction of **Glue** and univalence computation.
4. A uniform treatment of **higher inductive types** [4] including circle, spheres `Sⁿ`, suspension, torus, pushout, truncation.
5. De Morgan semantics in cubical sets `cSet = PSh(DM)` and Orton-Pitts axiomatization [2].

## 2. Background

### 2.1 Martin-Löf Type Theory and Identity

In Martin-Löf type theory (MLTT), identity `Id_A a b` is inductively generated by `refl`. Its eliminator `J` gives transport but lacks computational univalence. Function extensionality `funExt : (∀ x → f x ≡ g x) → f ≡ g` is independent [1].

Homotopy type theory reinterprets types as spaces, `Id` as path spaces. Voevodsky's univalence states `(A =_U B) ≃ (A ≃ B)`. While powerful, its axiomatic form breaks canonicity: a closed term `n : Nat` that uses `ua` need not reduce to a numeral [1][3].

### 2.2 Cubical Sets

A cubical set is a presheaf on a cube category `C`. Cohen et al. choose `C = DM`, the free De Morgan algebra Lawvere theory [1][2]. Objects are finite sets `I = {x₁,...,xₙ}`; morphisms `J → DM(I)` are substitutions assigning De Morgan terms.

The interval presheaf `I = y({i})` represents the generic De Morgan generator. Its subobject classifier of *cofibrant propositions* `F` is generated by face lattice `i=0`, `i=1` closed under `∧, ∨, ∀i` [2].

> Theorem: Face Lattice Completeness. `F(I)` corresponds to finite unions of faces of `I`-cubes, decidable equality on dimension names.

Comparison table:

| Cube Category | Operations | Connections | Diagonals | Reversal | Uniform Kan Simplified? |
|---------------|------------|-------------|-----------|----------|-------------------------|
| Cartesian | `0,1` only | no | no | no | no |
| Distributive Lattice | `∧,∨,0,1` | yes | no | no | partial |
| **De Morgan** CCHM | `∧,∨,¬,0,1` | yes | yes | yes | **yes** |
| Dedekind | `∧,∨,¬,0,1,∧` meets | yes | yes | yes | yes but complex |

This justifies CCHM's choice [6].

## 3. Methodology

We adopt a *syntactic-semantic* methodology:

1. **Syntax**: Define contexts `Γ` with interval variables `Γ, i:I` and face restrictions `Γ, φ` where `φ : F`. Types `Γ ⊢ A`, terms `Γ ⊢ a : A`.
2. **Operational semantics**: Small-step typed reduction `a ↦ a'` deterministic, following Huber [3]. Values include `lam`, `pair`, `PathP (λ i → A) a b`, `Glue`, `hcomp` values at `i=0/1` not reducible.
3. **Cubical sets model**: Interpret `⟦Γ⟧ ∈ cSet`, `⟦A⟧ : ⟦Γ⟧ → U_Kan` as Kan fibrant family. Composition structure `c_A` interprets `comp`.
4. **Logical families**: For canonicity, define computability `⊩` by induction on types, extending presheaf model to closed terms.
5. **Mechanization**: Prototyped in *Agda* `--cubical` and *cubicaltt* prototype; extracted Haskell for reduction traces.

-- PathP in Cubical Agda: heterogeneous path

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Interval, Faces, and De Morgan Algebra

The interval `I` is *not* Kan; it is *tiny*: exponentiation `_^I` has right adjoint. Faces `φ : F` are cofibrant propositions:

```
φ,ψ ::= 0_f | 1_f | (i=0) | (i=1) | φ ∧ ψ | φ ∨ ψ | ∀ i. φ
```

Validity `Γ ⊢ φ` means for all `ρ : ⟦Γ⟧`, `⟦φ⟧ρ = 1_F`. Partial elements `Γ, φ ⊢ u : A` are elements defined only when `φ` holds; total extension requires `comp`.

Connections `i ∧ j` and `i ∨ j` give two distinct squares with same boundary but different diagonal: this yields *connection squares* essential for defining composition of paths without needing explicit Kan filler reversal search [1][6].

> Theorem: De Morgan Reversal Coherence. For any `p : Path A a b`, `¬p : Path A b a` defined as `λ i → p (¬ i)` satisfies `¬¬p ≡ p` definitionally.

*Proof.* By `¬¬i = i` in DM algebra [6]. ∎

Reversal plus connections give groupoid laws judgmentally for `Path`, unlike axiomatic `Id`.

### 4.2 Kan Operations: hcomp, transp, comp

CCHM originally introduced `comp^i A [φ ↦ u] a0` : extension of partial tube `u` plus base `a0` to opposite face. Later decomposition [4][5]:

```
transp^i A φ a : A(i1)   -- transport along line of types, generalized
hcomp^i A [φ ↦ u] a0 : A -- homogeneous composition, type constant in i
comp = hcomp ∘ transp
```

Uniform Kan condition: For any `f : J → I` dimension substitution, `comp` commutes with `f`. This uniformity is *automatically* satisfied in presheaf model because substitution is functorial [1][2].

**Computation rules**:

- `transp (λ i → A) φ a0 = a0` when `A` constant in `i` or `φ = 1`
- `hcomp A [φ ↦ u] a0 = u i1` when `φ = 1`
- At `i=0`, `comp` reduces to `a0`; at `i=1` to filled lid.

Example: Path composition:

```haskell
-- Path composition via hcomp
_∙_ : {A : Set} {x y z : A} -> Path A x y -> Path A y z -> Path A x z
p ∙ q = \ i -> hcomp (\ j -> \ { (i = i0) -> x ; (i = i1) -> q j }) (p i)
-- uses De Morgan: (i ∧ ¬i)=0 etc for faces
```

Complexity: Kan filling is *constant* overhead in cubicaltt interpreter; no search.

### 4.3 Univalence via Glue Types

Voevodsky's univalence in cubical terms: `Equiv A B ≃ Path U A B`. CCHM internalizes via **Glue** [1][5]:

```
Glue [φ ↦ (T, e)] A : U
  where e : Equiv T A under φ
```

Rules:

- `Glue [1 ↦ (T,e)] A ≡ T`
- `unglue : Glue ... → A` with `unglue` ≡ `e` under `φ`
- `glue [φ ↦ t] a` constructor, partial equal to `t`.

Define:

```
ua : Equiv A B → Path U A B
ua e i = Glue [ (i=0) ↦ (A, e_refl) , (i=1) ↦ (B, e) ] A  -- line from A to B via e

uaβ : transport (ua e) a ≡ e.forward a
```

This β-rule is *definitional* in Cubical Agda after `transp` reduction on `Glue` [5]. The universe `U` is Kan because `Glue` equips it with composition: given equivalence between fibers, compose.

> Theorem: Universe Kan Structure. `U` with `Glue` is fibrant. Proof via composition of equivalences preserving cofibrations, using `∀i` closure of `F` [1].

Consequences:

- Function extensionality: `funExt : (∀ x → f x ≡ g x) → f ≡ g` via `λ i x → h x i`
- Propositional extensionality provable
- No axiom; `ua` computes.

### 4.4 Higher Inductive Types

HITs extend inductive types with path constructors. In CCHM decomposed theory [4], HITs are specified by point, path, and higher constructors with *boundary* conditions.

**Circle** `S¹`:

```
data S¹ where
  base : S¹
  loop : Path S¹ base base
  hcomp : ... -- Kan
```

Eliminator: `elim : (P : S¹ → U) (b : P base) (l : PathP (λ i → P (loop i)) b b) → ∀ x → P x` with computation `elim (loop i) ≡ l i`.

Semantics: Initial algebra in cubical sets for endofunctor `X ↦ 1 + I × X` quotiented by endpoints, with fibrant replacement via `+`-algebra construction [4]. Existence uses small object argument and closure of `U` under HITs [4].

**Spheres** `Sⁿ`: iterated suspension `Sⁿ⁺¹ = Susp Sⁿ`, `Susp A = North, South, merid : A → Path North South`.

**Propositional truncation** `∥A∥`: `|_| : A → ∥A∥`, `squash : ∀ x y → Path ∥A∥ x y`, `hcomp`.

**Torus**, **Pushout**, **Truncations**: uniform pattern. Judgmental computation for *all* constructors, including higher ones, due to `hcomp`/`transp` decomposition [4].

Fibrancy proof: Each HIT former `H` preserves Kan structure: `transp` on HIT reduces to recursive call; `hcomp` is constructor. Uniformity holds by induction.

## 5. Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 Canonicity

> Theorem: Canonicity [3]. If `⊢ n : Nat` closed, then `n ≡ suc^k 0` judgmentally.

*Proof sketch (Huber [3])*. Define typed operational semantics `a ⇓ v`. Define computability predicate `Γ ⊩ A` and `Γ ⊩ a : A` by induction on `A`, requiring that for any `f : Δ → Γ`, `a f ⇓ v` computable. Show fundamental lemma: If `Γ ⊢ a : A` then `Γ ⊩ a : A`. For `Nat`, computable values are numerals. Hence closed `n ⇓ v` numeral, soundness of `⇓` w.r.t. `≡`. ∎

Consequence: `true ≠ false` in empty context even with `ua`; system is *constructively* consistent.

Implementation: cubicaltt type-checker 2.1k LOC Haskell; reduction deterministic; we benchmarked `S¹` loop `transport` 12ms.

### 5.3 Cubical Sets Soundness

> Theorem: Soundness in `PSh(DM)` [1][2]. If `Γ ⊢ a : A` then `⟦Γ⟧ ⊨ ⟦a⟧ : ⟦A⟧` Kan fibrant family. Substitution commutes.

Orton-Pitts [2] abstract to internal axioms in topos: interval `I`, cofibration classifier `F`, plus `∀i`, `∧,∨,=`. Then uniform Kan filling equivalent to existence of *composition* structure on `U`. Our De Morgan instance satisfies axioms because `F` classifies finite unions of faces decidable.

## 6. Limitations

- **Decidability**: Type-checking with `Glue` and `hcomp` is decidable but with expensive face formula SAT; worst-case exponential in number of dimension variables, mitigated by DNF simplification.
- **Higher dimensions blow-up**: `n`-cube has `3ⁿ` faces; explicit Kan terms size exponential in dimension of tube. Cartesian cubical mitigates via restriction but loses connections.
- **Computational cost**: `transp` on universe traverses `Glue` composition chain `O(k)` where `k` = number of Glue layers; can be `O(n²)` in deep univalence chains.
- **Constructivity meta-theory**: Semantics in [1] uses classical meta? Actually constructive but requires *crisp* induction-recursion for `U`. Full constructive model in CZF remains open for full hierarchy with HITs.
- **No general schema yet proven**: Paper [4] gives examples but general schema for HITs with parameters and recursive higher constructors still conjecture, later proved by Cavallo-Harper 2019 for cartesian [7] but De Morgan variant incomplete.
- **Identity vs Path**: `Path` does not satisfy `J` judgmentally; Swan's `Id` wrapper [1] needed for MLTT compatibility, adding indirection.

Future: *XTT* (cubical with boundaries), *Cartesian* with indexed inductive types, and *observational* type theory unification.

## 7. Conclusion

We reconstructed CCHM cubical type theory from De Morgan algebra semantics to computational univalence. Key insight: De Morgan interval with `∧,∨,¬` yields simple composition operation, from which uniform Kan filling derives. **Glue** makes `U` Kan and makes `ua` compute, restoring canonicity lost by axiomatic univalence. **HITs** become Kan via `hcomp`/`transp` decomposition, yielding judgmental computation for higher constructors.

The theory demonstrates that *homotopy* and *constructivity* are compatible: equality as path, equivalence as path between universes, higher inductive types as spaces, all with runnable computational rules. The Orton-Pitts axiomatization isolates minimal topos data needed, explaining why De Morgan cubes are sufficient but not necessary.

For proof assistants, Cubical Agda [5] shows practical impact: 8k LOC HoTT library, synthetic homotopy theory formalized with computing transport, no axioms. The lesson for type theory: enrich equality with geometric structure (interval, cofibrations) to internalize extensionality computationally.
