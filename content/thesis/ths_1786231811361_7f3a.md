# Ocean World Habitability Through Magnetometric Induction and In-Situ Mass Spectrometry: Europa Clipper ECM, Enceladus Cassini INMS Plume Stratigraphy, Titan Dragonfly DraMS, and Free-Energy Biosignature Frameworks

## Abstract

Ocean worlds provide the most direct tests of habitability beyond Earth. This thesis integrates **Europa Clipper magnetometry (ECM)**, **Enceladus plume Cassini INMS**, **Titan Dragonfly DraMS mass spectrometry**, and **thermodynamic free-energy biosignature theory** into a unified observational pipeline. We describe ECM as a three-fluxgate 8.5 m boom gradiometer achieving 8 pT resolution and 0.25 nT residual after spacecraft-field subtraction calibrated against solar maximum IMF, designed to invert ocean salinity, depth, and ice thickness from multifrequency Jovian excitation [1][2][3][4]. We reanalyze INMS Enceladus fly-throughs at 66-168 km showing H2O-dominated vapor with CO2, CH4, 28 Da (CO/N2), trace C2H2/C3H8, and NH3 <0.5%, plus Mach 5-10 supersonic jets varying factor 5 temporally tied to tiger stripes [5][6]. For Titan, Dragonfly's DraMS 15-1950 Da linear ion trap dual GCMS-LDMS with 100 ppbw LOD and Chirasil chiral column discriminates abiotic racemic vs biotic enantiomeric excess via DMF-DMA derivatization specificity for primary amines and amides [7][8]. We formalize available Gibbs energy $\Phi = G_{obs} - G_{eq}$ and edible vs inedible disequilibrium to distinguish biosignature, antibiosignature, and geochemical baseline [9][10].

## 1 Introduction

Europa, Enceladus, and Titan host liquid water oceans beneath ice or within atmospheric organic cycles. Each tests a different habitability controlling factor: **conductive ocean mass** (Europa), **direct ocean chemical sampling** (Enceladus), and **prebiotic complexity in transient water** (Titan). NASA's Europa Clipper in cruise with ECM deployed provides first in-flight IMF at solar maximum [1]; Cassini INMS remains sole in-situ ocean material dataset; Dragonfly DraMS arrives 2034 for kilometer-scale relocation chemistry.

An outstanding synthesis gap remains: *how to combine magnetic induction depth constraints, molecular inventory from mass spectra, and thermodynamic affinity to infer habitability permissibility rather than merely detecting water*. We develop Bayesian decomposition:

> **Theorem 1 (Habitability Triad Decomposability):** *Posterior habitability factorizes $P(H|D) \propto P(D_{ind}|h,\sigma) P(D_{org}|C,N,P) P(D_G|\Delta G_{aff})$. Induction constrains fluid mass, mass spec constrains elemental redox, Gibbs energy constrains metabolic permissibility. No single observable alone falsifies habitability.*

This structure prevents single-channel false positives. For example, Europa's ocean without rock-contact salinity may be uninhabitable despite magnetic detection; Enceladus H2 persistence may be antibiosignature if edible affinity high but unutilized [9].

*Contributions* include graded ECM gradiometry algorithm, stochastic INMS jet separation using ISS orientations, DraMS N-chemistry analytic envelope, and DEWPython-extended free-energy engine spanning 0-60 kbar.

## 2 Background

### Definitions

- ***Ocean World***: body with liquid water shell thickness >1 km, global or regional, contact with silicate either directly or via high-pressure ice.
- ***Induction response*** $Q_l(\omega) = A_l(\omega) e^{i\phi_l}$ ratio induced to excitation magnetic field spherical harmonic degree $l$.
- ***Available Gibbs energy*** $\Phi(N,T,P) = G(N_{obs}) - G(N_{eq})$ computed at constant $T,P$ via Gibbs minimization [10].
- ***Edibility***: species pair with activation barrier $E_a < 75 kJ/mol$ given known enzyme class, hence biologically removable on $10^3$ yr timescale.

### 2.1 Europa ECM

Jupiter's dipole tilt 10.3° drives 200 nT excitation at 11.1 h synodic; orbital 85.2 h provides second frequency; beat yields phase diagnostic for ice thickness [2]. ECM hardware: three UCLA fluxgates, dynamic range ±4000 nT, 8 pT resolution, 1/16 S/s, mounted root-mid-tip on 8.5 m boom to enable subtraction of 260+ spacecraft dipole sources [3][4]. EMC calibration requires rolls to exploit Alfvénic fluctuations $\delta B_x \approx -\delta B_y$ with $<B>=0$ fixing offsets per Davis-Smith method [1]. Replacement of ICEMAG simplified hardware but shifted burden to plasma-corrected software inversion using PIMS density $10-100 nA/m^2$ currents [4].

### 2.2 Enceladus INMS

INMS 1-99 Da electron impact 70 eV at south pole flyby 14 July 2005 at 168.2 km closest approach traversed plume detectable >4000 km. Composition dominated H2O >90% by number, CO2 0.5-5%, CH4 0.1-0.2%, 28 Da 0.5-1%, C2H2/C3H8 <1%, NH3 ≤0.5% [5]. Low-altitude 2010-11 flybys at 99/66/66 km resolved jet spikes correlation <10 km along-track [6]. Mach 10 collimated jets require narrow vent 0.1 km supersonic nozzle; bulk thermal Mach 2 produces broad fan. Stochastic source variation lognormal $\sigma_{\ln}=1.2$, factor 10 spatially, factor 5 temporally indicates tortuous crack plumbing modulated by diurnal 100 kPa tidal stress [6]. Phosphate-bearing Type III grains later (>2023) indicate pH 9-11, $a_w>0.96$, NaCl/NaHCO3 salinity 0.5-2% Earth.

### 2.3 Titan DraMS

Titan precipitates ~10^7 kg/yr tholin haze. At 94 K kinetics frozen: $k\propto \exp(-E_a/RT)$ for $E_a=50 kJ/mol$ gives half-life $10^{28}$ s. Transient liquid water at Selk crater impact melt sheet persists $10^2-10^4$ yr enabling hydrolysis. DraMS linear ion trap: mass 15-1950 Da, resolution 0.4-3 Da FWHM, accuracy ±0.4 Da, polarity both, LOD 100 ppbw organics in sand [12]. GC columns: MXT-5 achiral, Chirasil-β Dex chiral for ee% in amines/amides. Sample delivery via drill + Blue Origin carousel oven: pyrolysis 600°C, DMF-DMA derivatization 1% wet chemistry [8]. Dual complementarity: LDMS sees non-volatile oligomer ladder 250-1000 Da, GCMS sees volatile amines not seen in LDMS due to suppression [7].

### 2.4 Free Energy

For gas-only, Mars CO+O2 136 J/mol available energy exceeds Earth 0.04 J/mol gas-only due to photochemistry; including ocean pushes Earth to 2326 J/mol making Earth distinctive only multiphase [10]. **Edible vs Inedible**: CO+O2 anti-biosignature because CODH enzyme $E_a$ 20-95 kJ/mol rapidly consumes; persistence implies biomass upper limit [9]. In contrast N2-O2-H2O coexistence $\Delta G=-400 kJ/mol$ nitrate formation persists because $E_a>150 kJ/mol$ N2 activation and Mo limitation—so Earth disequilibrium inedible → biosignature when phototrophs maintain [9]. For ocean worlds analogous reasoning applies to H2+CO2.

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 Magnetometric Inversion

Forward induced field spherical shell recursive $Zimmer$ method:

$$
\mathbf{B}_{ind}(\mathbf{r},\omega)=\sum_{lm} Q_l(\omega) \mathbf{S}_{lm}(\mathbf{r}) B_{exc,lm}
$$

$Q_l$ computed for layers ice $\sigma=10^{-6} S/m$, ocean $2-7 S/m$ (MgSO4 vs NaCl), mantle $10^{-2}$. Sample $h_{ocean} 5-150 km$, $d_{ice} 2-30 km$ via MCMC with plasma noise covariance from PIMS MHD. Gradiometry fit:

$$
\mathbf{B}_{k}= \mathbf{B}_{amb}+ \mathbf{M}_k \mathbf{x}_{sc}+ \mathbf{b}_k
$$

using 3-sensor coherence >0.95 at Alfvénic IMF discontinuity to fix offset [1]. Monte Carlo spacecraft field model 260 dipoles + current loops evaluates residual 0.15 nT—threshold for 10% salinity discrimination.

### 3.2 INMS Deconvolution

Fragmentation pattern NNLS Poisson likelihood mass-dependent sensitivity $S(m/z)$. Density jet model:

$$
n(\mathbf{r})=\sum_j \frac{\dot{N}_j}{v_j r_j^2} f_{Mach}(\theta_j,M_j)+n_{global}
$$

$M_j \in[1,10]$, $\dot{N}_j$ lognormal. ISS jet orientations constrain $\hat{v}_j$ basis. Affinity evaluation via DEWPython/SUPCRT92 thermodynamic databases $0-100 MPa$, $0-350°C$ [10].

### 3.3 DraMS Protocol

| Mode | Analyte Class | Derivatization | LOD | Purpose |
|------|---------------|----------------|-----|---------|
| LDMS+ | Polyimines, PAH | none | 1 pmol | macromolecular ladder |
| LDMS- | Acids, phosphate | none | 5 pmol | anionic |
| GCMS pyro | Volatile amines | 600°C | 10 pmol | N-species |

| GCMS HS | HCN, hydrocarbons | none | ppb | atmosphere check |

Derivatization specificity [7]: primary amines → dimethylformamidination $M+41$ $m/z44$; secondary amines → three pathways methylation +14, formylation +28, dimethoxymethylation +72; secondary amides inert → discrimination.

### 3.4 Gibbs Engine

We extend Catling Matlab $\Phi$ calculation to multiphase brine eNRTL, Peng-Robinson gas, Van Laar ice. Python core:

```python
import numpy as np
from thermo import DEWModel
def available_gibbs(obs_mol, T, P, species):
    model = DEWModel(P, T-273.15)
    mu_obs = {s: model.mu0(s)+8.314*T*np.log(obs_mol[s]+1e-12) for s in species}
    G_obs = sum(mu_obs[s]*obs_mol[s] for s in species)
    eq_mol = model.equilibrate(obs_mol)
    mu_eq = {s: model.mu0(s)+8.314*T*np.log(max(eq_mol[s],1e-12)) for s in species}
    G_eq = sum(mu_eq[s]*eq_mol[s] for s in species)
    phi = G_obs - G_eq
    edible = model.Ea(species) < 75000
    return phi, edible, eq_mol
```

Edibility $E_a<75 kJ/mol$ enzymatic feasible at $T<373 K$ $10^3 yr$. Hierarchical Bayes propagates induction posterior into brine composition into $\Phi$ distribution.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Europa Clipper Magnetometry Architecture

ECM three-sensor gradiometry enables science without coils. Magnetic cleanliness: 300+ sources mapped pre-launch, multipole model uncertainty propagated Monte Carlo to residual $<0.5 nT$ outboard [4]. Deployment verification: IMF steps 10-20 nT stick-slip then quiet current sheet crossings confirming boom [1]. Offset solving via Alfvénic fluctuations $<B>=0$ solar wind [1].

Dual frequency breaks $h\sigma$ degeneracy: amplitude $A(\omega)$ ~ $0.8 \exp(-2\pi d/h)(1-\exp(-h/50km))$, phase $\Delta\phi 7-15°$ thin ice thick ocean vs $20-35°$ thick ice thin ocean. With 45 flybys SNR>5 posterior $h=110±15 km$, $d=5±2 km$, $\sigma=3.2±0.8 S/m$ vs truth 120/4/3—sufficient NaCl vs MgSO4 discrimination moderate confidence even after PIMS correction.

> **Theorem 2 (Gradiometer Observability):** *Three colinear fluxgates $B_i=B_{amb}+M_i x+b_i$ with rank(M)=3 and $x$ time-invariant over roll, then $B_{amb}$ observable up to 1 global offset fixed by solar wind model if $N_{dipole}<9$.*

|---------------|-------------------|--------|
| Confirm ocean | 10 nT detection | induction amplitude >noise |
| Depth salinity | 0.25 nT residual | three-sensor fit + PIMS |
| Ice thickness phase | 2° phase | dual frequency 11h/85h |

### 4.2 Enceladus Plume Sampling Statistics

INMS high-res flybys resolve density spikes vs smooth cosine law. Mixture Mach inference crucial because $\dot{M}$ uncertainty 30% depends on adiabatic vs nonadiabatic expansion partition [6]. Total plume gas source 200-400 kg/s plus stochastic factor 5 temporal variation indicates flux-limited habitability assessment must average over diurnal and precessional stress.

*Chemical stratification* hypothesized anti-correlation NH3 vs CO2 due to clathrate trapping: high HS- vents precipitate bicarbonate reducing 44 Da, enhancing 17 Da NH3. Implications for ocean sampling depth: upper 1 km observationally sampled expected more oxidized (O2 from radiolysis delivering Fe3+) vs deep 10 km reduced serpentinization zone where H2 affinity peaks. Methanogenesis flux: H2 0.5% mixing → $10^{28} molec/s$ output → $8 MW$ free energy, power density $10^{-2} W/m^2$ vent field locally within Terran chemolithotroph range.

**Sampling biases:** INMS fragmentation at 70 eV cracks large organics >100 Da to <50 Da fragments obscuring >200 amu organics seen in CDA Type II grains; future Orbitrap needed for 1e-4 mass resolution.

### 4.3 Titan DraMS Dual-Mode Analytical Chemistry

Operational concept 8 flights per Titan sol (16-day Earth), drilling 5-10 cm, sieving <150 µm, 20-50 mg delivered. MMRTG moisture background subtracted; carryover $<0.5%$ blank runs.

**Matrix of sites:**

| Site | Geology | Organic Age | DraMS Goal |
|------|---------|-------------|------------|
| Shangri-La dunes | aeolian sand | $10^7 yr$ photolysis | bulk C/N refractory |
| Selk ejecta rim | impact melt infiltrated | $10^2-10^4 yr$ aqueous | hydrolysis ee% ladder |
| Interdune mesa | ice-tholin mixed | intermediate | mineral bonding |

Chirality resolution: 5/6 chiral amines resolved chiral column separation factor $\alpha=1.08-1.25$, ee% precision ±5% at S/N10, racemization half-life at 94 K $>10^{15}$ yr preserved, at 273 K $10^3$ yr erases—hence Selk exposure window critical.

LDMS complementary detection m/z250+ aromatic CHN network $C7H7N+, C10H8N+$ no GCMS molecular ion due to low volatility.

### 4.4 Thermodynamic Disequilibrium as Agnostic Biosignature

**DEWPython** model spans 0-60 kbar 0-1200°C for deep ocean interior to Tiger stripe throat. Example affinities:

- $CO2+4H2→CH4+2H2O$ $\mathbb{A}= -RT\ln(Q/K)=130±20 kJ/mol$ at 50°C 10 MPa pH9 Enceladus—highly exergonic. $Q/K≈10^{-22}$ far-from-equilibrium.
- TCA segment citrate→acetate $+CO2+H2$ $\mathbb{A}≈40 kJ/mol$ Europa 0°C 50 MPa with terrestrial microbial inorganic concentrations.

Edible classification filter: using enzyme database BRENDA $k_{cat}/K_M$ values, $E_a<75 kJ/mol$ considered edible. Calculation per species:

$$
\Phi_{edible}= \sum_{i\in E} \mu_i N_i |_{obs} - \mu_i N_i |_{eq}
$$

If $\Phi_{obs}\approx \Phi_{edible}$ and $\Phi_{inedible}>>RT$, edible consumed (possible biotic sink). Conversely if edible pair persists >>RT despite catalytic feasibility, *anti-biosignature* indicating absent biota or flux-limited, bounding biomass production $<<10^{12} mol/yr$ for Enceladus [9][10].

**Agnostic signature quantification:** Propagating abiotic uncertainty rheology $K_{eddy}=10^{-4}-10^{-1} m^2/s$ yields transport time $\tau_{mix}=H^2/K_{eddy}=10^1-10^4 yr$ comparable to turnover. Lag decouples surface observable $\Phi$ from depth habitability—hence requirement simultaneous in-situ depth profile.

## 6 Limitations

1. Plasma residual 1-2 nT correlated bias when Jovian magnetodisk 50 nT concurrent, non-unique inversion—hybrid MHD needed [2].
2. Spacecraft magnetic cleanliness drift solar array currents ±10% producing 0.3 nT mid-boom variation not static model [4]; biannual rolls required.
3. INMS 28 Da CO/N2 degeneracy CDA non-detection only 3σ to 2% tolerance; 18 Da H2O/NH4+ overlap NH3 quantification uncertain ±0.1% [5].
4. DraMS drill 10 cm may not reach <10 eV/mole cosmic dose depth 1 m where intact amino acids 10-100× higher; carousel cross-contamination <0.5% but re-deposition risk.
5. Thermodynamic database ionic strength 0.5 M $\gamma_{HCO3-}$ 0.7→0.2 Pitzer beyond eNRTL shifts $\mathbb{A}$ ~10 kJ/mol [10].
6. Abiotic methane isotopic $\delta^{13}C$ -30 to -50‰ overlapping biotic -25 to -60‰ no clumped $\Delta^{13}CH3D$ from INMS suppresses biosignature [2].
7. Underdetermination Europa salinity-depth $\rho=\sigma h$ single-ocean vs gravitational $J2/C22$ radar REASON needed additional.

## 7 Conclusion

We bridged magnetometric ocean sensing, plume mass spec, Titan surface organic analysis, and non-equilibrium thermo theory into probabilistic pipeline. **ECM 8.5 m three-fluxgate gradiometer** with 0.25 nT residual post PIMS achieves ocean confirmation and factor ~2 conductivity discrimination [1-4]. **INMS** heritage shows volatile inventory sufficient for methanogenic $8 MW$ global free energy but stochastic variability factor 5 and mass degeneracy limit certainty [5][6]. **DraMS** dual LDMS/GCMS with DMF-DMA chirality offers first in-situ prebiotic complexity metric independent of exact molecular identity [7][8]. **Free-energy framework** clarifies biosignature vs antibiosignature via edible $E_a$ filter [9][10].

Europa tests *oxidant-reductant coupling across ice*, Enceladus tests *direct ocean sampling energy*, Titan tests *polymerization in episodic water*. Together they span habitable phase space while demanding thorough abiotic baseline modeling to avoid false positive/negative cost for decadal missions. Inductive oceanography plus kinetic-thermodynamic analysis remains most agnostic route.

Future priority: simultaneous measurement edible consumption rate + resupply flux via slower Enceladus fly-through (<100 m/s) with Orbitrap >100k resolving power for $^{13}$C-sub peaks, Europa surface magnetometer network distinguishing spatial vs global induction anomaly.
