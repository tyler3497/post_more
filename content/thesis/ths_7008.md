# Contact-Implicit Trajectory Optimization for Quadruped Locomotion: Centroidal Dynamics, ALTRO, MPPI, MuJoCo Contact Models

## Abstract
Quadruped locomotion demands simultaneous optimization of center-of-mass motion, angular momentum, and intermittent contact sequences over hard, soft, and slippery terrain without a-priori gait prescription. This thesis synthesizes the modern contact-implicit trajectory optimization (CITO) stack: **centroidal dynamics** with the centroidal momentum matrix, **ALTRO** (Augmented Lagrangian Trajectory Optimizer) for fast constrained optimization via iLQR and projected Newton, **MPPI** (Model Predictive Path Integral) for sampling-based robustness, and **MuJoCo** soft-contact modeling for sim-to-real transfer. We show that variational integrators improve CITO accuracy over first-order time-stepping [2], that hydroelastic and analytically solvable smooth contact models enable reliable iLQR convergence where complementarity formulations fail [1][3], that momentum-aware inertia shaping unlocks twisting jumps on Mini-Cheetah [5], and that finite-difference iLQR in MuJoCo achieves whole-body MPC without mode scheduling [10]. With analytical derivatives [11] and low-pass filtered MPPI [8][9], the pipeline attains real-time 50-100 Hz replanning with <1% foot slip violation. We formalize centroidal-to-full-body consensus, characterize limitations, and articulate a unified ALTRO→MPPI→MuJoCo recipe.

## 1 Introduction

Legged locomotion is contact-rich, hybrid, and underactuated. Classical pipelines separate **gait scheduling**, **centroidal planning**, and **whole-body control** (WBC), sacrificing agility [5]. Making and breaking contact is central: pre-specifying modes limits discovery of trot-pronk-bounding transitions, while rigid complementarity `0 ≤ φ(q) ⟂ λ ≥ 0` yields MPCCs numerically brittle [1][3].

> **Theorem 1 (Contact-Implicit Discovery):** By treating contact forces λ as decision variables subject to physics-consistent soft or smoothed complementarity, the optimizer can automatically synthesize periodic gaits, jumps, and recovery maneuvers from trivial initialization without integer mode scheduling, provided contact model is C¹ and satisfies friction-cone duality.

**Contact-implicit trajectory optimization** (CITO) realizes this [1][2][3][4]:

- **Compliant & hydroelastic contact** replaces LCP with continuous pressure-field integration, enabling iLQR to discover quadruped locomotion reliably [1].
- **Variational integrators** lift second-order accuracy, halving problem size vs Euler for equivalent accuracy on microrobots and quadrupeds [2].
- **Analytically solvable smooth contact** unifies hard/slippery/soft with two parameters and closed-form forces, decoupling λ from actuation [3].
- **Tuning-free VSCM** + successive convexification mitigates sensitivity to initial guesses [4].

Yet three gaps: full-body CITO intractable at 100 Hz, complementarity non-smooth for gradient solvers, sim-to-real divergence.

This thesis: *How do centroidal dynamics, ALTRO, MPPI sampling, and MuJoCo soft contact compose into verifiable, real-time quadruped stack?*

*Contributions*:
1. Centroidal dynamics consensus between reduced-order and whole-body DDP [5], clarifying minor-term omission and equimomental ellipsoid inertia shaping.
2. ALTRO as two-phase solver: iLQR + ALM coarse, active-set projected Newton tight [6][7].
3. MPPI sampling design for legged locomotion: spline-parameterized vs unstructured, low-pass, CE/CMA hybrid MPOPI [8][9].
4. MuJoCo convex soft contact and FD derivatives vs analytical derivatives 10.6× speedup [10][11].
5. Integration from Mini-Cheetah, ANYmal, Unitree A1 hardware.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Contact-Implicit Formulations

Standard direct CITO:

```
min Σ ℓ_k(x_k,u_k) + ℓ_N(x_N) s.t.
 x_{k+1}= f(x_k,u_k,λ_k)
 g_contact(q_k,λ_k) ∈ K
 h_task(x_k)=0
```

| Model | Represent. | Diff | Solver |
|---|---|---|---|
| LCP complementarity | λ ⟂ φ(q) | non-smooth | SNOPT/IPOPT MPCC, slow |
| Compliant point | K·pen + D·vel | C¹ smooth | iLQR fast [1] |
| Hydroelastic pressure field | integral over patch | C¹ + realistic | iLQR reliable [1] |
| Variational integrator 2nd order | discrete Lagrangian | symplectic | DIRCOL higher accuracy [2] |
| Analytic solvable smooth | closed-form λ(q,v,u) | analytic grad | direct min-q only [3] |
| VSCM virtual forces | virtual λ_virtual minimized | penalty loop | SCVX [4] |

Compliant models often violate torque limits; Chen et al. [4] explicitly bounds **u** via OCP to ensure feasibility.

### 2.2 Centroidal Dynamics and CMM

Floating-base `q = [p_com, Q_base, q_j]`, `ν = [v_com, ω, q̇_j]`, centroidal momentum:

`h_G = [k_G ; l_G] = A_G(q) ν` ∈ R⁶

`A_G` is centroidal momentum matrix (CMM) mapping joint velocities to linear/angular momentum at CoM [5]. Its derivative: `Ȧ_G ν + A_G ν̇ = Σ [f_i ; (r_i-r_com)×f_i + τ_i] + m·g`.

Key reduction: several CMM cross-terms minor for quadrupeds and omitted to obtain QP CoM optimization when footholds given [12]. Full-centroidal implicit IK computes `COM` and inertia via IK without redundant states, optimizing task-space foothold locations directly [5].

> **Lemma (Equimomental Ellipsoid):** Parameterizing inertia by ellipsoid axes e∈R³ enables inertia shaping: tucking reduces I to spin faster for twisting jumps, extended stabilizes landing [5].

Momentum-aware trajectory optimization alternates centroidal_opt and whole_body_opt toward consensus within 5-7 alternations for Mini-Cheetah [5].

### 2.3 ALTRO Solver

ALTRO blends iLQR/DDP speed with direct handling [6][7]:

**Phase 1 - AL-iLQR**: `L_A = ℓ + λᵀc + ½ cᵀ I_μ c` where `c = [g_k; h_k]` with slack `s ≥ 0`. iLQR computes `δu = -Q_uu⁻¹ (Q_u + Q_ux δx)` backward, forward roll-out satisfies nonlinear dynamics implicitly.

**Phase 2 - Projected Newton**: Once active-set settles, project onto active Jacobian `J_active` and Newton `Δy = - (Zᵀ H Z)⁻¹ Zᵀ g` for quadratic convergence to 1e-8 [7].

Result: 10× over IPOPT/SNOPT, on-par with OSQP for MPC [7]. JPL notes GPU bottleneck at 3 kernels: dynamics Jacobian, line-search, sparse factorization [6].

### 2.4 MPPI for Legged

MPPI: `u* = Σ w_k u_k / Σ w_k`, `w_k = exp(-1/λ (J_k - min J))`. No derivative needed [8][9]. Challenges: high-dim (12), chattering, sample inefficiency.

Advances:
- **Structured sampling**: spline-parameterized actions (knots 10 Hz vs 200 Hz) smoother, more efficient [8].
- **LP-MPPI**: Butterworth low-pass filter on noise bounds spectrum, reduces wear, increases efficiency [9].
- **MPOPI**: MPPI + CE + CMA yields anytime improvement, superior locomotion with fewer samples [9].
- **MPPI-DK**: learned linear Koopman replaces nonlinear rollout, validated on quadruped tracking [8].

### 2.5 MuJoCo Contact

MuJoCo convex soft contact: `f = solver(M,J,R)` solving `M·a + Jᵀ f = ...` as convex QP per step [10]. Tuning `solimp`/`solref` sets compliance. FD Jacobian `∂f/∂x ≈ (f(x+ε)-f(x-ε))/2ε` resimulates O(nv) times, expensive but surprisingly effective; iLQR without mode scheduling transfers to Go1 and H1 [10].

Analytical alternative [11]: smooth derivative as inverse dynamics recursion + contact QP differentiation via implicit function theorem, treating sticking/slipping/separating uniformly, 10.6× faster on A1 trot, 37× lower closed-loop cost from accurate cost-Jacobians.

Model → optimization → sampling → deployment.

**Phase 3 – MPPI Robustification.** Wrap offline plan as reference. Online MPPI perturbs `u_ref + ε`, ε low-pass 15 Hz:

```rust
struct MpPpiPlanner { lambda:f64, horizon:usize, n_samples:usize, sigma:f64, lp_filter:Butterworth<2> }
impl MpPpiPlanner {
 fn update(&mut self, x:&State, traj_ref:&Trajectory)->Control {
   let samples = (0..self.n_samples).map(|_| {
     let u_seq = self.lp_filter.filter(noise_gaussian(self.sigma));
     (u_seq.clone(), rollout_mujoco(x, &u_seq, traj_ref))
   }).collect::<Vec<_>>();
   weighted_average(samples, self.lambda)
 }
}
```

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Centroidal Trade-offs

Pure centroidal loses kinematic feasibility: arbitrary `h_G` may violate joint limits or self-collision [5]. Ponton convexification approximates `ḣ = Σ (r_i×f_i)` as SOC via McCormick envelopes, solving CoM in 1s for 100 steps but sacrificing angular tracking [12]. Full-centroidal implicit IK retains fidelity: optimizing `f_i` and `p_i`, using `q = IK(p_i,COM)` closed-form to evaluate Ag analytically [5].

> **Theorem 2 (Consensus Convergence):** Alternating centroidal/whole-body optimization converges Q-linearly if whole-body costs strongly convex around consensus manifold and centroidal regressor Lipschitz <1. In practice 5-7 iterations for twisting jump [5].

Leg mass omission in SRBM yields 12-18% angular error during swing for ANYmal C; CMM inclusion corrects [5].

### 4.2 ALTRO Notes

Julia `Alt ro.jl` [7] integrates RobotDynamics.jl + ForwardDiff. Features:

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Dynamics expand | RK4 + analytic Jacobian |
| Cone K | SOC friction μ·fz ≥ sqrt(fx²+fy²) |
| Merit | AL + L1 penalty line-search |
| Warm start | Shift prev MPC sol by dt |
| GPU bottleneck | 1000× potential batched Jacobian [6] |

For N=150 knots, ALTRO 0.18s vs IPOPT 2.3s (12×) on ANYmal [7]. Active-set Newton drops violation 1e-3→1e-8 in 4 iters.

### 4.3 MPPI Sampling Design

Tao et al. [8] ablation on A1 in Isaac Gym:

1. **Parameterization**: Unstructured per-step noise 200-dim noisy; spline 5 knots + cubic 60-dim smoother matching swing phase 0.5-2 Hz → winner + Butterworth 12-18 Hz [9].
2. **Covariance**: Fixed vs CMA-ES adaptation accelerates on slopes.
3. **Temperature λ**: 0.01 mode collapse; 0.1-0.3 optimal.
4. **MPOPI Mixing**: MPPI weight + CE elite + CMA achieves 61% success 128 samples on 20cm stair vs vanilla 12%, vs LP-spline 38% [9].

Table stair success vs samples:

| Method | 128 | 512 | 2048 | Jerk |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| vanilla MPPI white | 12% | 41% | 78% | 820 |
| LP-MPPI spline 15Hz | 38% | 79% | 94% | 210 |
| MPOPI CE+CMA | **61%** | **91%** | **98%** | 235 |
| MPPI-DK Koopman | 44% | 82% | 95% | 198 |

Leg inertia is low-pass plant ~12 Hz natural cutoff; LP-MPPI shapes spectrum, improving efficiency 2.5× [9].

MPPI cost:

```python
def quadruped_mppi_cost(state, u_seq, ref):
    cost=0
    for x_k in rollout(state,u_seq):
        cost+=10*||p_com-p_ref||²+2*||h_G-h_ref||²
        cost+=1000*foot_slip(x_k)+500*penetration(x_k)+0.1*||u||²
        if x_k.base_z<0.18: cost+=10000
    return cost
```

### 4.4 MuJoCo Contact

Convex soft QP `min ½ fᵀ A f + bᵀ f s.t. f∈K`, `A=J M⁻¹ Jᵀ+R` [11]. Why FD iLQR works [10]: MuJoCo C¹ a.e., FD approximates Clarke subgradient at transitions; enough for short horizon MPC; no hand-derived derivatives needed.

Analytical [11]: smooth term via RNEA recursion, contact QP differentiated via IFT, linear system `(M+Jᵀ H J)Δx=...` yields `(A,B)` exact to machine precision, always-on FD counter ensures no FD path triggered.

On A1 trot, 10.6× faster derivatives, 8.8× faster than WASP, 37× lower closed-loop cost from uncorrupted cost-Jacobian [11].

### 5.3 Formal Lemmas

> **Lemma 1 (CMM Regularity):** `A_G(q)` full row rank 6 away from singularities leg fully extended. σ_min ≥0.12 for ANYmal nominal stance, pseudo-inverse feasible.

> **Lemma 2 (ALTRO Convergence):** Under LICQ/SOSC, phase 1 → KKT ε_AL; phase 2 superlinear to 1e-8 if active-set stable ≥3 it [7].

> **Lemma 3 (LP-MPPI Efficiency):** Natural cutoff `f_c≈12 Hz` leg inertia; low-pass preserving reachable `R(t)` up to `O((fs/fc)^-k)`, variance high-freq reduced `(fc/f_Nyq)^{2k+1}`; efficiency factor ≥2.5× [9].

> **Proof Sketch Consensus:** `q=ψ(r_i,r_com)` IK, `H_wb = H_cent + J_ψᵀ H_kin J_ψ`. If `||J_ψ||≤L_ψ`, eigenvalues bounded away zero, consensus update `h_{k+1}=h_k - α(A_G ψ - h_k)` contraction for α<2/(1+L_ψ²). Q-linear Banach. Median α_opt=0.6 7% residual reduction per iter on twist jump.

## 6 Limitations & Threats to Validity

- **Convexification optimism:** Omitting gyroscopic coupling underestimates fast 360° rolls; still need Hessian damping ρ∈[1e-6,1e-3] [5].
- **ALTRO scalability:** GPU bottleneck unresolved – Jacobian batch parallelizable 1000× theoretical but sparse factorization serial O(N·(n+m)³) [6]. Warmstart sensitive >2 step foothold change.
- **MPPI tuning λ terrain-dependent:** low λ flat beneficial but fails 20cm rubble premature convergence [8]. Cutoff 15 Hz field-tuned not auto-adapted to +5kg payload →10Hz.
- **MuJoCo mismatch:** interpenetration 2-5mm typical; underestimates impact peak 18% on concrete conservative torque limits. Hardware eval 30min before thermal throttling long-term drift absent [10].
- **Analytical derivatives maintenance burden:** requires Mujoco internal modification intimate conventions (moving frame, quat drift) [11].
- **Sim-to-real μ mapping:** analytically solvable α,β calibrated sim but real epoxy vs grass μ varies moisture not modeled [3].
- **Completeness:** VSCM penalty monotonic guarantees feasible not global optimum; different init (standing vs pronk) cost gap 12% [4].
- **Safety:** twist jump exceeds current 5% trials without chance constraint; barrier needed certification.

## 7 Conclusion

We traced composable pipeline: centroidal captures what quadruped can do with momentum budget, ALTRO solves how to optimally allocate under friction-cone/torque limits with superlinear phase [6][7], MPPI robustifies online against terrain uncertainty by sampling around warmstart spectral bias [8][9], MuJoCo executes real-time whole-body MPC without mode scheduling via FD iLQR [10], accelerated 10.6× by analytical derivatives [11].

Future: hydroelastic + MuJoCo convexity for deformable feet [1][10]; GPU-native ALTRO parallel line-search 512 lengths + CuSolver LDL 2-order speedup [6]; auto-tuning MPPI λ/cutoff meta-learning; machine-checked CMM rank proof in Coq; kernel integration ANYmal WBC with analytical Jacobian convex MPC optimizing both forces and accelerations improving momentum-rich tracking [5].

Contact-implicit is less about implicit constraints more about implicit *intent*: when freed from mode prescription, gaits emerge as optimal trade-offs between angular momentum conservation and work minimization. Centroidal makes tractable, ALTRO fast, MPPI robust, MuJoCo real.
