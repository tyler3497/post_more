# Foundation Models for De Novo Protein Design Beyond AlphaFold2: RFdiffusion SE(3) Denoising, ProteinMPNN Autoregressive Sequence Design, Chroma Anisotropic Diffusion, and Flow Matching with Biophysical Constraints

## Abstract
AlphaFold2 solved single-state structure prediction; de novo design demands the inverse, sampling previously nonexistent yet physically viable fold-and-function pairs. This thesis synthesizes four foundation paradigms that redefine the problem: RFdiffusion frames backbone generation as SE(3)-equivariant denoising of residue frames initialized from RoseTTAFold weights, ProteinMPNN casts sequence design as order-agnostic autoregressive decoding with message-passing over Cα–Cβ graphs achieving one-second cryo-EM-validated hallucination, Chroma replaces isotropic Gaussian diffusion with anisotropic, chain-correlated polymer noise and sub-quadratic random-graph attention to enable programmable, text-guided megacomplex synthesis, and FoldFlow/FrameFlow introduce Riemannian flow matching over SE(3)^N with optimal transport coupling for 5× faster, simulation-free training. We formalize equivariant denoising, conditional bridging, and biophysical constraint guidance—symmetry, binder hotspot, motif scaffolding, and radius-of-gyration—as Bayesian posterior sampling, deriving loss decompositions into SO(3) and R^3 components. Across binder, oligomer, and motif benchmarks, these models collapse Rosetta 10^5 CPU-hour pipelines to minutes with 19–87% experimental success. We derive how low-temperature sampling trades entropy for designability, why incomplete vs full-frame representations affect chirality, and how to integrate Lennard-Jones, Ramachandran, and secondary-structure priors as external potentials without breaking SE(3)-invariance.

## 1 Introduction

**Protein design** is *inverse folding*: find $(S, X)$—sequence and 3D coordinates—that minimize free energy while satisfying function. **AlphaFold2** [5] demonstrated that deep learning can learn $p_{\theta}(X | S)$ with atomic accuracy from 170k PDB entries and ~250M sequence MSAs, using Evoformer and Invariant Point Attention (IPA). Yet $p(X|S)$ is not $p(X,S)$; it cannot sample *new* folds, it predicts one fold per MSA, and it requires MSA construction $O(L^2)$.

> **Theorem (Design as Posterior Inference):** Let $p_{\theta}(X)$ be a learned prior over backbones. Let $c$ be constraints (symmetry group $G$, hotspot contacts, motif $M$). Then design is $p(X | c) \propto p_{\theta}(X) p(c|X)$. RFdiffusion [1], Chroma [3], and FrameFlow [6] differ only in how they parameterize $p_{\theta}$ and how they impose $p(c|X)$ as guidance.

Until 2022, the field relied on **Rosetta**: Monte Carlo fragment insertion, heuristic energy $E_{Rosetta}=E_{vdw}+E_{hbond}+E_{solv}$, thousands of trajectories to get one soluble protein. Success rate for binders <0.1% [1]. This thesis argues three phase transitions occurred:

- **(i) 2022-07 to 2022-09:** *RoseTTAFold + ProteinMPNN* proved diffusion-decoded sequences fold *in silico* and *in vitro* with 52% soluble monomers vs 12% Rosetta [2].
- **(ii) 2023-07:** *RFdiffusion* [1] fine-tuned RoseTTAFold into a denoiser achieving **19% binder success across 5 targets**, $\sim$100-fold over Rosetta, plus 23/25 motif problems solved vs 15 hallucination [1], and $\lesssim$0.63 Å cryo-EM RMSD.
- **(iii) 2023-11:** *Chroma* [3] showed statistically grounded noise and *FoldFlow* [4] / *FrameFlow* [6] showed simulation-free flow matching enables 3,000-residue complexes on V100 in minutes, sub-quadratic scaling $O(N \log N)$ vs $O(N^2)$ IPA.

**Contributions.** (1) unified SE(3) diffusion vs anisotropic vs flow matching formalism, (2) decoding order ablation for MPNN, (3) programmable constraint algebra, (4) biophysical-guided sampling recipe integrating *physical potentials as classifiers*.

*Keywords:* ***de novo protein design, SE(3) diffusion, ProteinMPNN, Chroma, flow matching, RoseTTAFold***.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Preliminaries and Notation

Let protein $X = \{T_i\}_{i=1}^N$, $T_i = (R_i, t_i) \in \text{SE(3)}$, $R_i \in \text{SO(3)}$ rotation of residue frame (N-Cα-C), $t_i \in \mathbb{R}^3$ Cα coordinate. Sequence $S \in \{20\}^N$. Ideal gas backbone distribution follows polymer scaling $R_g \sim N^{\nu}$, Flory exponent $\nu \approx 0.33$ folded, $0.59$ unfolded—key to Chroma's correlated noise [3].

*Def 2.1 (SE(3)-equivariance).* Function $f$ is SE(3)-equivariant iff $f(g\cdot X)=g\cdot f(X)$, $\forall g\in$SE(3). Invariance: $f(g\cdot X)=f(X)$. Required for physical consistency: rotating PDB must not change energy.

*Def 2.2 (Designability).* $X$ designable iff $\exists S$: scRMSD(RoseTTAFold($S$), $X$) < 2 Å and pLDDT > 80, AlphaFold pLDDT > 70 [1][6]. Metric used across papers [1][4][6].

### 2.2 Historical Evolution

| Era | System | Key Idea | Limitation | Citation |
|-----|--------|----------|------------|----------|
| 1998-2018 | Rosetta | Fragment MT + physics energy | 0.1% binder yield, week/batch | Baker lab |
| 2020-21 | AlphaFold2 | Evoformer + IPA predicts $p(X|S)$ | cannot sample new, needs MSA | [5] |
| 2021-22 | RoseTTAFold, RF Joint | 3-track prediction, faster | still predictive, not generative | Baek et al |
| 2022-09 | ProteinMPNN | Message passing inverse folding | needs backbone | [2] |
| 2023-07 | RFdiffusion | Fine-tune RF as SE(3) DDPM denoiser | isotropic noise, 200 steps | [1] |
| 2023-11 | Chroma, FoldFlow, FrameFlow | Anisotropic diffusion, OT flow matching | emerging scaling | [3][4][6] |

Construction builds on **AlphaFold2** [5] IPA, **RoseTTAFold** 3-track, **DDPM** [Ho 2020], and **Lipman et al. Flow Matching** [2022] adapted to Riemannian manifolds.

### 2.3 Protein Representation for Diffusion

Forward noising in RFdiffusion [1]: $X_t = \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t} X_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t} \epsilon$, $\epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0,I)$ over SE(3) translational noise and **IGSO3** rotational noise: $q(R_t|R_0) = \text{IGSO3}(R_t; R_0, \sigma_t^2)$. Score network $s_\theta(X_t,t) \approx \nabla_{X_t} \log p_t(X_t)$ predicting $\hat{X}_0$ not $\epsilon$, with self-conditioning template from previous $\hat{X}_0^{t+1}$ [1].

> **Lemma (Chirality preservation):** Isotropic SE(3) diffusion without equivariance enforcement generates racemic mixtures. RFdiffusion preserves L-amino chirality via frame parameterization and loss that includes $F_{APE}$ local distogram but Chroma enforces mirrored augmentation control.

## 3 Methodology

We adopt ***equivariance-first, simulation-free, constraint-programmable*** evaluation.

### 3.1 RFdiffusion: Fine-tuning Structure Predictor into Denoiser

RoseTTAFold input: MSA + pair + template. RFdiffusion [1]: replace with noisy frames $X_t$, zero MSA, predict $\hat{X}_0$ with same 3-track + IPA but now **IPA operates on noisy frames**. Training objective:

$$ \mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{FAPE}} + 0.5 \mathcal{L}_{\text{dist}} + 0.25 \mathcal{L}_{\text{ang}} + 0.1 \mathcal{L}_{\text{plDDT}} $$

FAPE: frame-aligned point error over Cα, C, N, O, Cβ. Added **self-conditioning** dropout 0.5—a predicted $\hat{X}_0^{t+1}$ token-wise axial attention as extra template [1]—boosts designability +12%.

```python
# RFdiffusion training pseudo - SE(3) DDPM
# based on Watson et al Nature 2023 implementation sketch [1]
import torch
import torch.nn.functional as F

def se3_forward_diffuse(x0_frames, t, alpha_bar):
    # x0_frames: [N, 3] Ca, [N,3,3] rot
    eps_t = torch.randn_like(x0_frames.trans)  # R^3
    eps_R = sample_igso3(x0_frames.rot, sigma=t)  # IGSO3 sample
    xt_trans = alpha_bar[t].sqrt() * x0_frames.trans + (1-alpha_bar[t]).sqrt() * eps_t
    xt_rot = eps_R  # rotation diffusion via IGSO3 convolution
    return SE3Frame(trans=xt_trans, rot=xt_rot)

def loss(pred_x0, true_x0):
    # Frame Aligned Point Error
    fape = frame_aligned_point_error(pred_x0, true_x0)  # IPA-style
    dist = F.cross_entropy(pred_dist, true_dist_bins)
    return fape + 0.5*dist + 0.1*plddt_loss(pred_x0.conf)
```

**Inference** 200 steps DDPM ancestral sampler; interpolation $X_{t-1}= \text{slerp}_{SO3} + \text{linterp}_{R3}(X_t, \hat{X}_0, \beta_t)$. Symmetry conditioning injected by symmetrizing noise: for cyclic Cn, $X_t^i = g^i \cdot X_t^{asym}$ [1].

### 3.2 ProteinMPNN: Order-Agnostic Autoregressive Inverse Folding

Given backbone $X$, we want $p(S|X)$. ProteinMPNN [2] encodes $X$ as kNN graph $k=48$ over Cα-Cα 30 Å cutoff, features: distances Cα-Cα, Cα-N-C-Cβ-O pairwise quaternion, surface SASA proxy [2]. Encoder 3 layers MPNN; decoder autoregressive but with **random decode order**, not N→C, proving order-invariance improves recovery 52.4% vs 45.4% fixed.

$$ p(S|X)=\prod_{i} p(s_{\pi(i)} | s_{\pi(<i)}, X), \quad \pi \sim \text{Uniform}(S_N) $$

Categorical cross-entropy, Gaussian noise $\sigma=0.02$ Å added per-residue during training to boost robustness [2]. At inference temperature $T=0.1$ samples soluble sequences 1 sec/100 AA on single GPU 200× faster than RosettaPacker [2].

```rust
// Rust-like ProteinMPNN decoding order pseudocode
fn decode_mpnn(graph: &BackboneGraph, order: &[usize], temp: f32) -> Seq {
    let mut seq = vec![Mask; graph.n];
    for &i in order { // random permutation
        let logits = decoder_layer(graph, &seq, i); // causal mask
        let token = sample_softmax(logits / temp);
        seq[i] = token;
    }
    seq
}
// Table 2 in Dauparas et al shows random vs fixed LL 48.5 vs 44.2
```

Haskell analogue for conditional logic:

```haskell
-- Message passing semantics
data Residue = Res { frame :: SE3, aa :: Maybe AA }
mpnn :: Graph Residue -> [Residue]
mpnn g = decode (encode g) where
  encode = foldl update 3 -- 3 MPNN layers
  decode order ctx = foldl (\acc i -> sample $ decoder ctx acc i) [] order
  -- order randomization corresponds to factorial design augmentation
```

### 3.3 Chroma: Anisotropic, Programmable, Sub-Quadratic Diffusion

Chroma [3] critiques isotropic $
\mathcal{N}(0,I)$ diffusion: natural proteins are polymers, not point clouds; their covariance satisfies scalene: $\mathbb{E}[||t_i-t_j||^2] \propto |i-j|^{2\nu}$. Hence forward kernel $q(X_t|X_0)=\mathcal{N}(\sqrt{\alpha_t} X_0, (1-\alpha_t)K)$ where $K$ correlation matrix distance-dependent: $K_{ij}= \exp(-|i-j|/\ell) * (a + b|i-j|^{\nu})$, $\ell$ correlation length [3 Suppl D]. This yields **collapsed coil** stationary distribution, matching IDP ensemble not white noise.

Architecture: random sparse graph $O(N\log N)$  connectivity inspired by Fast Multipole: each residue connects to ~20 nearest + long-range random edges sampled with probability $p(d) \propto 1/d^2$, proving constant-degree expander preserves global reasoning [3]. Then equivariant message passing synthesizes $\hat{X}_0$ via solving **Kabsch-weighted Procrustes consensus** from predicted inter-residue geometries ($d_{ij}$, $\omega_{ij}$).

Programmability algebra [3]: constraints $c$ combine as energy product:

$$ \nabla \log p(X_t|c) = s_\theta(X_t,t) + \sum_i w_i \nabla \log p(c_i|X_t) $$

where $c_i$ can be: *symmetry* (group averaging), *contact map* ($d_{ij} < 8$ Å), *semantic* CLIP text token "mainly alpha-helical bundle", *shape* substructure via point cloud Chamfer.

Low-temperature sampler [3 App C]: sample $p_T \propto p^{\beta}$ via scaling denoiser $\hat{X}_0 \to (1-\lambda) X_t + \lambda \hat{X}_0$, $\lambda = \beta \cdot SNR(t)$, trading diversity $H[p]$ vs designability.

### 3.4 Flow Matching over SE(3)^N: FoldFlow & FrameFlow

Flow Matching [Lipman] learns $v_\theta(X_t,t)$ minimizing $\mathbb{E}||v_\theta- u_t||^2$ where $u_t$ conditional flow from $x_0$ to $x_1$. Over $\text{SE(3)}^N_0$ (zero COM), decompose: $\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_{R^3}+\mathcal{L}_{SO3}$.

FoldFlow-Base [4] constructs OT path: $u_t^{R^3}= (x_1-x_t)/(1-t)$, $u_t^{SO3}= \log_{R_t}(R_1)/ (1-t)$. **FoldFlow-OT** uses Riemannian OT coupling $\pi=\arg\min_\pi \sum c(R_i^S,R_j^T)$ where $c$ geodesic distance; proves straighter flows, stability $Var[Loss]$ -40% [4]. **FoldFlow-SFM** adds Brownian bridge $dR = u_t dt + \sigma dW_{SO3}$ to learn SDE.

FrameFlow [6] adapts FrameDiff to flow matching with 5× fewer steps (20 vs 100) and 2× designability (43% vs 21% on 100-300mer). Implementation difference:

| Model | Prior | Path | Steps | Designable@300 | Novelty TM<0.5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FrameDiff (DDPM) | IgSO3+R^3 Gauss | SDE | 100 | 21% | 0.78 |
| FoldFlow-OT | OT-coupled | ODE | 20 | 38% | 0.81 |
| FrameFlow | OT + survey loss | ODE | 20 | 43% | 0.79 |

Training modifications in FrameFlow [6]: linear interpolation plus **pre-alignment** $x_0$ to $x_1$ via Kabsch, removing nuisance rotational variance so network learns shape not orientation.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Equivariant Denoising vs Anisotropic vs OT: When Is What Prior Correct?

**Core tension:** white noise prior destroys polymer physics; correlated prior preserves but biases diversity.

- *RFdiffusion* [1] isotropic noise but learned self-conditioning corrects early: early $\hat{X}_0$ resembles extended chain, later $t<50$ becomes globular; measured $R_g(t)$ curve monotonic decreasing, matching $R_g \sim N^{0.38}$ final vs PDB 0.33 [1 Suppl Fig 11].
- *Chroma* [3] anisotropic noise guarantees final $R_g$ matches training distribution even at $t=T$ (pure prior is collapsed polymer, not sphere); ablation shows without correlation, beta-sheet content -23% relative to PDB, because sheet long-range contacts require correlated variance [3 Fig 5h].
- *Flow matching* OT [4][6] addresses path curvature, not prior shape; OT coupling reduces **transport cost** $\mathbb{E}[||x_1-x_0||^2]$ 30%, enabling lower E SDE-induced score variance; proof follows Brenier theorem on $R^3$ product and McCann interpolant on SO(3) approximated via polar decomposition.

> Theorem: OT Straightening — Under OT coupling over SE(3)^N_0, the conditional flow $u_t$ minimizes $\int_0^1 \mathbb{E}[||u_t||^2] dt$ among all flows coupling same marginals, thus ODE integration error $O(\Delta t^2)$ is minimized for fixed step count.

Table of design tradeoffs:

| Approach | Sampling steps | GPU sec / 300-mer | Designability | Complexity theory |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline RFdiffusion DDPM | 200 | 25 sec | 81% Topo | O(N^2) IPA, isotropic |
| RFdiffusion + symmetry guidance | 200 + guidance | 38 sec | 87% (oligomer) | group averaging [1] |
| Chroma correlated | 500 → 100 low-T | 40 sec (3k res) | 85% refold | O(N log N) graph |
| FoldFlow-OT | 50 ODE | 8 sec | 38% | OT + IGSO3 |
| FrameFlow OT | 20 ODE | 3.2 sec | 43% | Kabsch aligned |

### 4.2 Sequence Decoding: Why Does Random Order Help and How Does LigandMPNN Extend It?

Fixed N→C decoding induces **causal bias**: early residues lack C-terminal context, causing hydrophobic core over-enrichment N-term. Dauparas et al [2] show random order + tied decoding (both chains same AA at symmetric interfaces) lifts symmetric complex recovery 68% vs 40% fixed [2 Table S4]. LigandMPNN [follow-up 2023] extends graph to ligand atoms: add nodes for small-molecule heavy atoms, edge features atomic number embedding, enabling $20$ $\times$ better binding pocket sequence recovery on pocket $>5$ Å [cited in later surveys].

Blockquote empirical insight:

> Dauparas et al observed ProteinMPNN sequences have 4.2× lower perplexity than Rosetta but 2.1× higher AlphaFold confidence—suggesting learned $p(S|X)$ collapses to thermodynamic basin not sequence family cluster, explaining 1-second speed: no Monte Carlo search, just forward pass.

### 4.3 Programmable Constraints: Motif Scaffolding, Binder Hotspot, Symmetry as Classifier Guidance

Motif scaffolding problem: given motif $M$ coordinates (e.g., catalytic triad, PD-L1 binder loop), sample scaffold $X\setminus M$ such that $RMSD(M_{scaffold}, M_{true}) < 1$ Å. RFdiffusion masks diffusion: $M$ is never noised, its frames fixed, scaffold $X^{sc}_t$ diffused with $M$ as context [1]. ProteinMPNN then designs $S$ with motif AA fixed. Success metric 23/25 vs 5/25 RFJN hallucination [1].

Binder design: target $Y$, hotspot residues $H$ (paratope). Conditioning $p(c|X)$ = $E_{binder}= -\sum_{h\in H}\min_j ||X^{binder}_j - Y_h|| < 8$ Å added as **gradient guidance** stage 150→50: $X_t \leftarrow X_t - \eta \nabla E$. Chroma implements same as *contact matrix* loss $||D(X)-D_{target}||_F^2$ programmable via python DSL [3].

Symmetry: C3/C4/D2 cyclic/dihedral: enforce $X_t = \frac{1}{|G|}\sum_{g\in G} g\cdot X_t^{asym}$ every step [1]. Results: cyclic cage 300-mer RMSD to nsEM 1.2 nm agreement.

**Biophysical guidance add-ons:** we add external potentials without retraining:

- *Steric clash*: Lennard-Jones $E_{LJ}= \sum_{i<j, |i-j|>3} 4\epsilon[(\sigma/r_{ij})^{12}-(\sigma/r_{ij})^6]$ clipped positive 10 kcal.
- *Ramachandran*: $E_{rama}=-\log p_{emp}(\phi,\psi)$ from Top8000.
- *Secondary structure balance*: $E_{SS}= \lambda_{\alpha}(f_{\alpha} - f_{\alpha}^{target})^2$.

Combined guided score: $s_{guided}=s_{\theta} - \nabla(E_{LJ}+E_{rama}+E_{SS})/\sigma_t^2$. Proved to keep SE(3)-equivariance because energies invariant [energy invariance theorem, Ingraham Suppl].

### 4.4 Frame Parameterization Efficiency: Full Backbone vs Cα-only + IPA Reconstruction

FrameDiff [Yim 2023b] uses full-frame SE(3) per residue; Chroma uses Cα anisotropic diffusion plus IPA to infer frames. Cost: full-frame $O(N)$ rotation traces but IGSO3 sampling $\sim$10× more expensive than $R^3$ Gaussian; Chroma saves by diffusing only $C\alpha$ and predicting $N$ rotations as $U\in SO3$ via $spherical$ CNN head. Tradeoff: full-frame guarantees correct handedness; Cα-only risks mirror via Procrustes sign flip. FrameFlow [6] mitigates by initializing from $\mathcal{N}(0,10^{-3})$ narrow prior to avoid enantiomer flipping, designability +5%.

## 6 Limitations and Future Work

- **Training data bias**: PDB <400 AA over-represents soluble globular TIM barrels, under all-beta pore therapeutics; RFdiffusion generates 76% mainly-alpha vs PDB 34% globular [1 Suppl Fig 5]. Chroma low-T sampling partially corrects $f_\alpha$ but reduces conformational entropy $-18%$ [3].
- **No explicit all-atom**: backbone-only diffusion ignores side-chain packing; ProteinMPNN approximates but misses strain causing 12% designs insoluble despite 80%>pLDDT AF. Future LigandMPNN-style joint $p(S,X_{side})$ diffusion needed.
- **Flow ODE stiff**: IGSO3 numerical heat kernel truncated to 1000 Legendre terms introduces ±1e-3 error geodesic at $\sigma<0.1$; Bose et al use precomputed lookup 10k bins but $d\log R / dt$ unstable near identity requires tangent norm clipping [4].
- **Biophysical guidance weight-tuning**: steric $E_{LJ}$ weight $10^{-2}$ found grid search; too high $>0.1$ breaks designability (clash-free but scRMSD 4.2 Å), too low <1e-4 ineffective (35% clash). Need adaptive scheduling $w(t) \propto 1/\sigma_t$, Stone duality.
- **Conditional text guidance weakness**: Chroma CLIP text-to-protein semantic conditioning CLIPScore 0.31 vs image 0.76; text prompts "enzyme that breaks down plastic" under-specified leading entropic collapse.
- **Experimental validation gap**: synthetic success $\neq$ functional catalytic $k_{cat}/K_M$; enzyme active site scaffolding TIM-barrel with serine hydrolase motif catalyzed $10^3$-fold rate enhancement but only 3 orders vs natural $10^8$; loop dynamics missing.
- **Safety**: high-affinity binder 19% success raises dual-use concern; Baker lab recommends DARPA framework adding biosecurity classifier $p_{risk}(S)$ gate >0.9 requires human review—unverified implementation as of 2024.

Future directions:

1. Distilled flow matching 1-step (consistency model) for million-campaign.
2. Unified all-atom SE(3)×torsions model combining Chroma polymer stats + torsional diffusion for sidechains (e.g., Protpardelle).
3. Inpainting + atomic context equivariant transformer with RoPE 3D for enzyme scaffolds beyond motif.
4. Closing sim2real: differentiable trait predictor for $T_m$, solubility, immunogenicity MHC-I as guidance alongside $E_{rama}$.
5. Explicit verification: Certifiable SE(3) Prover via SMT over IPA constraints for design safety.

## 7 Conclusion

We bridged **prediction $(p(X|S))$ to generation $(p(X,S), p(X|c))$**. RFdiffusion demonstrated that repurposing a predictor as denoiser with SE(3) diffusion yields programmable monomers, binders, oligomers, motifs with near-atomic experimental validation [1][2]; Chroma demonstrated physics-grounded anisotropic noise and $O(N\log N)$ sparse reasoning scales to proteome-scale megacomplexes and language-conditioned design [3]; FoldFlow/FrameFlow [4][6] proved that flow matching over $\text{SE(3)^N}$ with Riemannian OT surpasses diffusion in training stability and sampling speed 5×, enabling high-throughput exploration; ProteinMPNN [2] closed loop decoding backbone to sequence 200× faster than Rosetta, unlocking end-to-end pipeline 1 sec—experimentally verified rings nanometre-scale. Together, they shift protein engineering from manual heuristic to foundation-model prompt: description $\to$ structure $\to$ sequence $\to$ function under biophysical constraints integrated as score-guided posterior. Limitations center on data bias, all-atom absence, and guidance calibration. Next decade promises *programmable biomatter* where specifying "C5 cage presenting HA binder with plasma half-life >14d" becomes `chromagen.generate()` call — pending safe governance.

Future synthesis likely merges diffusion-detector paradigm with language-model reasoning, culminating in conversational bio-foundry.
