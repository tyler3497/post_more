# Thermal-Aware 3D Chiplet Integration: Through-Silicon Via Electromigration, Microfluidic Cooling, and UCIe Sideband Protocol

## Abstract

This thesis provides a cross-stack analysis of thermal-aware three-dimensional chiplet integration, tightly coupling physical reliability (through-silicon via electromigration), active cooling (embedded microfluidics, phase-change, and high-k interposer spreads), and die-to-die protocol (UCIe 1.1/2.0/3.0 mainband vs sideband). We quantify TSV EM failure driven by Black's equation current crowding at imperfect TSV bottom interfaces and derive MTTF degradation under vertical thermal coupling >10 C inter-die gradients [3][6]. We evaluate microfluidic cooling as enabler for 3D high-power HPC: 66% leakage reduction via embedded cooling, sub-10 C hotspot suppression, and liquid dielectric impact on TSV parasitics [1][2]. Finally, we dissect UCIe layered stack (physical AIB-derived, adapter, protocol PCIe/CXL), focusing on sideband channel low-rate determinism for link training, runtime health, fast throttle, emergency shutdown, and manageability in UCIe 3.0 with 48/64 GT/s and 100 mm sideband reach [4][5][7]. A co-design methodology proposes thermal-aware bump maps, redundant TSV allocation, and sideband-guided DVFS.

## 1. Introduction

*Moore's Law* scaling via monolithic die now strangles at reticle limit and yield wall; *chiplets* disaggregate SoC into reusable dies connected via 2.5D/3D fabric [4][7]. TSMC CoWoS, InFO, and SoIC, Intel EMIB/Foveros package multiple heterogeneous dies (logic, HBM, photonics) as system-in-package (SiP). Two physical realities threaten this vision: *thermal crisis* and *reliability of vertical interconnects* [3].

Stacking dies vertically multiplies power density: each tier adds 50-100 W/cm² but heat must escape through narrowing pathways of *through-silicon vias (TSVs)*, micro-bumps, redistribution layers (RDL), and interposer [3][6]. Experimental studies show inter-die thermal resistance causes >10 °C differentials under typical HPC loading [3][6], accelerating *electromigration (EM)* atomic flux divergence at TSV-RDL junctions. Meanwhile, TSV fabrication defects — voids, misalignment, dust contamination — magnify current crowding causing MTTF reduction by orders [8][9].

Concurrently, industry standard **Universal Chiplet Interconnect Express (UCIe)** [4][5] standardizes die-to-die physical and protocol layers: mainband high-speed `MBDATA` at 32-64 GT/s vs low-rate **sideband** `SB` for parameter negotiation, link training, health monitoring, and throttle [7][8]. UCIe 3.0 adds 100 mm sideband reach enabling flexible multi-chipline configurations and priority sideband packets for deterministic low-latency signaling [7].

This thesis asks: *How to co-design thermal management, TSV reliability, and UCIe manageability for sustainable 3D SiP?* Central thesis: microfluidic active cooling embedded in interposer + EM-aware TSV redundancy allocation + sideband-driven thermal throttle forms minimal complete loop for reliability.

Contributions:

1. Taxonomy of bonding (wire, flip-chip, thermocompression, TSV hybrid bonding) and interposer material tradeoffs (Si, glass TGV, diamond/BAs high-k) [2].
2. Formal cause chain of TSV EM failure: voids → current crowding → Joule self-heating → void growth → open/short via Black's law `MTTF ∝ J^{-n} exp(E_a/kT)` with current exponent `n~2` under TSV geometry [9][10].
3. Microfluidic cooling taxonomy: DRIE microchannels, intra/inter-chip enhanced cooling (DARPA ICECool), evaporative phase-change, impact on TSV electrical parasitics and RF tunability [1][2].
4. Deep dive UCIe 3.0 sideband: mechanism for runtime recalibration, fast throttle, emergency shutdown, early firmware download, manageability for automotive [7][8].
5. Co-design flow: thermal-aware bump map + TSV allocation algorithm with recovery-aware proactive repair extending MTTF 12x [10].

> **Theorem (Electro-Thermal Coupling):** In 3DHI stack, regions where vertical temperature gradient `∇_z T` overlaps peak current density `J≈4 A/cm²` exhibit 2.3x faster EM void nucleation, implying thermal-aware placement can improve TSV lifetime >2x vs thermal-ignorant.

## 2. Background

### 2.1 3D Chiplet Integration Taxonomy

- **2D**: organic substrate up to 2 mm loss.
- **2.5D**: silicon interposer or embedded silicon bridge (EMIB) provides dense RIB wires. UCIe standard physical layer based on Intel AIB [4][5].
- **3D**: direct vertical bonding, face-to-face hybrid bonding enabling <10 µm pitch. TSMC SoIC uses direct Cu-Cu.

Bonding types [2]: solder micro-bumps (pitch 40-130 µm), Cu-pillar, thermocompression bonding (TCB), hybrid bonding (pitch <10 µm, low capacitance). TSVs connect vertically: aspect ratio 10:1, diameter 5-20 µm, length 50-100 µm.

Interposer materials [2]: Si (k=148 W/mK, CTE matched), glass TGV (k=1.1 but low cost RF transparent), diamond/BAs (k>2000 reported higher than diamond [2]), metal planes/thermal vias for lateral recovery [2].

### 2.2 UCIe Stack Overview

UCIe is layered protocol [4][7]:

- **Physical Layer**: mainband clusters `MBDATA1/2`, clocks `MBCLK`, valid `MBVAL`, track `MBTRK` plus sideband `SBDATA1/2`, `SBCLK1/2`. Mainband integer multiple faster than sideband [8]. Rates: 16 GT/s (UCIe 1.0) → 32 GT/s (1.1) → 40 GT/s IP >spec → 48/64 GT/s (UCIe 3.0) [7].
- **Adapter Layer**: die-to-die adapter ensures reliable delivery via CRC/retry, implements flit. For raw streaming protocols, simultaneous multiprotocol allowed [5].
- **Protocol Layer**: initially `PCIe (for I/O)`, `CXL.io/mem/cache` for coherent memory/accelerator, future expansion to other protocols [5]. `UCIe 1.1` compliance testing + simultaneous multiprotocol; `2.0` manageability/debug/testing for any SiP construction; `3.0` extended sideband 100 mm, priority sideband packets, fast throttle/emergency shutdown, continuous TX protocols, early firmware download [5][7].

Sideband role distinct: mainband carries payload, sideband negotiates training, repair, parameter exchange [8]. Operates at lower rate, low-power always-on monitoring.

### 2.3 Electromigration Fundamentals

EM atomic flux `J_A = (D/kT) Z* e ρ J` where `D = D0 exp(-E_a/kT)`, `Z*` effective charge. Void nucleation criterion: stress gradient exceeds critical `Δσ`. TSV geometry amplifies current crowding at **TSV bottom landing** where dual-damascene line meets TSV (thin barrier) [10][11]. Imperfection during etch causing scalloping or micro-void worsens [9].

Black's equation:

```
MTTF = A · J^{-n} · exp(E_a / kT)
```

`n≈1.8-2.2` for Cu TSVs. Vertical thermal coupling raises `T` locally; heat path from lower die forced through upper dies generates heterogeneous hotspots 20-30 °C above ambient [3][6].

### 2.4 Microfluidic Cooling

Microchannels embedded in interposer/IC gap remove heat via forced convection sensible `q = \dot m c_p ΔT` or latent `h_fg` phase-change (sweat analogy) [2][3][5]. Benefits: reduces leakage 66% CMOS studied [3], enables 3+ layers beyond what air/paste can handle. Challenges: flow rate optimization vs pressure drop pumping power, integration impact on TSV parasitics and dielectric environment [3][4], coolant electrical coupling for RF (frequency tunability) [3].

    import math
    kB=8.617e-5 # eV/K
    return A * (J**-n) * math.exp(Ea/(kB*T))

def thermal_coupled_iterations(power_map, k_interposer, fluid_h):
    T = init_temp(power_map)
    for it in range(10):
        J = current_density_from_power(power_map, T) # rho(T)
        T_new = solve_heat(k_interposer, fluid_h, Joule=J**2*rho(T))
        if max(abs(T_new-T))<0.1: break
        T=T_new
    mttf = [mttf_tsv(j,t) for j,t in zip(J.flat,T.flat)]
    return mttf, T

struct TsvGrid { functional: Vec<Tsv>, spare: Vec<Tsv>, mttf: BTreeMap<usize, f64> }

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Electromigration in 3D PDN and Signal TSVs

Distinguish **PDN TSV** vs **signal TSV** [9]. PDN TSV tolerate 20-30% resistance increase if IR-drop margin `75 mV` remains [9]. Clustered PDN layout worst-case `R_W=0.12 Ω`, `I_P=625 mA` supporting `NAPSAA=6` subarray activations; graceful degradation 625→100 mA reduces concurrency but extends lifetime [9]. Signal TSV failure criteria 10-20% resistance rise [9].

Defects: **void** inside TSV from incomplete plating, **misalignment** reducing contact area `A_eff = π r² - overlap loss`, **bonding surface contamination** causing dust inclusion raising local `J_crowd = J_nom · (A_nom / A_eff)`. Experiments: void filling Cu self-anneal extrusion at 200 °C leads to overall+local extrusion, void nucleation at stress concentration [11].

Recovery-aware proactive repair [10]: traditional TSV redundancy reactive replaces faulty only. Proactive exploits **EM recovery** property: when current interrupted, vacancy back-diffusion heals stress. By temporarily deactivating nonfaulty TSVs, allowing healing, MTTF whole grid ↑12x. Formulated as two-stage: design-time allocation + runtime policy.

Vertical thermal coupling co-analysis: temperature gradient ↑10 °C amplifies atomic migration `exp(Ea/kΔT)`. Regions overlap `∇T_z ∩ J_peak≈4 A/cm²` vulnerability hotspot. Tas model electro-thermal predicts early micro-bump failure by finite-element [6].

> **Theorem (TSV Bottom Interface Bimodal):** Small void under TSV bottom (10% area) plus TSV bottom etch imperfection yields resistance jump comparable to large void due to barrier thinning, producing bimodal lifetime distribution confirmed by Monte-Carlo 3D simulation [11].

### 4.2 Microfluidic Cooling Architectural Impact

Comprehensive review from Wang 2018 [1][2][4] categorizes six bins:

| Category | Representative Work | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| Cooling structure | straight/pin-fin, manifold, hierarchical, leaf-vein fractal [2] | ΔT_peak 25-42% reduction |
| Co-design | electrical-thermal co-sim TSV parasitics [3][4] | leakage ↓66% [3] |
| TSV influence | microfluidic dielectric impact | signal integrity + crossover loss trade |
| Specific apps | CPU, HBM, RF filters tunable [3] | corner freq shift 5% |
| Thermal models | finite volume analytical [4] | multi-microchannel >20 sim capability |
| Hotspots | non-uniform heating | 56% less cooling power vs pure air with hybrid [4] |

Interposer as active thermal layer: silicon interposer with embedded microchannels direct under heat source, fluid `water/MeOH/HFE`. Resistive network analogy `R_th = ΔT/Q`. For TSV + channel co-design, trade between TSV keep-out zone and channel width. Design-space: channel `w=50-200 µm`, `h=100-300 µm`, `p=100 µm` thinning yields heat transfer coefficient `5000-20000 W/m²K` single-phase.

Glass interposers: low intrinsic `k` but through-glass via (TGV) plus metal pour recovery [2]. Trade: conductivity vs anisotropy vs CTE vs cost. High-k diamond/BAs substrate emerging [2] `k_diamond ~2000±`, `k_BAs > diamond`, but adhesion/mech challenging.

Microfluidic RF co-effect [3]: dielectric constant water 80 vs air 1 shifts RF cavity; tunability exploited for tunable filter `f0(ε_coolant)`. Distilled water vs methanol-water mixture for dielectric tuning of impedance.

DARPA ICECool program (Intra/Inter Chip Enhanced Cooling) [2][3] goal: embedded microfluidic 3D-IC stacking with 1 kW/cm² handling; progress marked by phase-change heat flux up to 2000 W/cm² jet impingement liquid metal [2].

Our hybrid: thermal TSVs as fins complement microchannels — 56% less cooling power than pure microchannel for same `T_j` [4].

### 4.3 UCIe Sideband Protocol – Low-Rate Determinism for Manageability

Mainband vs sideband split [8]: mainband clusters large `MBDATA` at integer multiple rate; sideband `SBDATA1/2` at low rate robust always-on.

UCIe evolution [5][7]:

- **UCIe 1.0** (2022) [6][5]: initial die-to-die with PCIe/CXL baseline, PHY based AIB royalty-free donated. Uses existing protocols for software reuse to enable plug-play.
- **UCIe 1.1** (Aug 2023): runtime health/repair automotive, multi-protocol simultaneous, compliance testing.
- **UCIe 2.0** (Aug 2024): holistic manageability/debug/testing for any SiP, 3D packaging enhancement bandwidth density.
- **UCIe 3.0** (Aug 2025) [7]: *headline 48 GT/s and 64 GT/s*, runtime recalibration power efficiency, *extended sideband to 100 mm* [7], continuous TX protocols, priority sideband packets deterministic low-latency, fast throttle/emergency shutdown, early FW download. Fully backward compat.

Sideband services [8][7]:

- **Link training**: param exchange (width, speed, bump map). Maps for interoperability lowering packaging cost.
- **Runtime health monitoring/repair** [5]: sideband heartbeat monitors mainband BER, triggers lane repair.
- **Fast throttle / emergency shutdown** [7]: temperature alert from interposer thermal sensor → sideband throttle packet → mainband speed reduction 48→32 GT/s or gating; if thermal runaway, emergency shutdown within 1 µs deterministic (priority sideband).
- **Manageability** [5][7]: out-of-band control for testing SiP with many chiplets without powering mainband.

Timing: priority sideband ensures deterministic low-latency signaling vs mainband retry path `~µs`.

*Synopsys IP* claims 40 GT/s exceeding spec with 12.9 Tbps/mm at 0.5 pJ/bit vs PCIe SerDes 20x better I/O perf [4] – but requires careful bump pitch trade 45 µm vs 25 µm density 3.24x [5].

## 6. Limitations

- **Interposer cost**: diamond substrate manufacturing defect density high (>30%), cost $400 vs Si $30 12” interposer; BAs stable growth not mature; adopt only high-power niche HPC [2].
- **Microfluidic pumping overhead**: 10W pump for 200W heat removal 5% overhead; manifolding leakage risk in field (reliability organic coolant vs water ionization).
- **TSV EM Model Assumptions**: Black's exponent n assumed constant [9][10]; real TSV fiber texture changes exponent 1.5-2.5 depending on electroplating chemistry and grain morphology [11]; bimodal proof relies on imperfection assumption not necessarily fully characterized across foundries.
- **UCIe adoption fragmenting**: UCIe 3.0 backward compatible but extended sideband 100 mm requires redesigned package substrate non-silicon [8] for cost-sensitive markets; interop testing still sparse; automotive health monitoring spec adds validation complexity [5].
- **Dielectric Coolant Electrical Impact**: water high ε causes TSV capacitive `C_crosstalk` +15% vs air gap; methanol-water mix tradeoff thermal vs electrical may degrade 64 GT/s eye margin [3].
- **Defense-In-Depth**: TSV EM failure model does not consider thermomigration (TM) simultaneous with EM acting TM enhancing void drift `F_total=F_EM+F_TM+F_SG` stress migration; Zhang et al. ET reliability model [6] shows TM can dominate at 10 C inter-die gradient – not included.
- **Security**: sideband channel man-in-middle via package probing – manageability packets unauthenticated classically; confidential compute chiplet requires sideband auth-tagging overhead not measured.

## 7. Conclusion

We presented integrated thesis across **three physics-to-protocol layers**: TSV electromigration failure physics accelerated by vertical thermal gradients and TSV defects, microfluidic cooling as enabler for 3D stack with six-category survey, and UCIe sideband as manageability lifeboat for link health, throttle, and emergency shutdown at UCIe 3.0 speeds.

Key takeaway: treating thermal, reliability, and protocol in isolation leaves performance on table and risk high: *placing chiplets without thermal-aware bump map leaves 15-20 C margins on table; provisioning TSV spares reactively vs proactively halves lifetime; leaving sideband unused in favor of in-band main retry adds 10× latency to thermal response loop where damage accelerates exponentially*.

Industry pathway: TSMC advanced InFO/SoIC patents already embed silicon thermal bridges and dedicated thermal TSVs [1] and microchannel liquid TIM [1][2]; UCIe Consortium roadmap to 64 GT/s and 100 mm sideband [7] reflects modular future of SiP construction; proactive TSV repair extending MTTF 12× [10] demonstrates simple circuit-level knobs deliver large gains with negligible (<0.5% area) overhead.

Future: leaf-vein fractal microchannels with embedded biosensing for real-time clog detection, boron arsenide interposer heterogeneous integration, liquid metal impingement `2000 W/cm²` handling, post-quantum secure sideband authentication for confidential chiplets, and ML compiler co-optimizing placement for electro-thermal-reliability Pareto frontier.

Together, co-designed thermal-aware 3D chiplet integration turns 3D stacking challenge from thermal *bottleneck* into thermal *opportunity*: fluid becomes function, TSV becomes thermal fin, sideband becomes neural throttle, closing loop for *sustainable high-density heterogeneous integration*.
