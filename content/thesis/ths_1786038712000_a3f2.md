# Spiking Neural Networks with Event-Driven Learning: Leaky Integrate-and-Fire, Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity, and Loihi-2 Neuromorphic Execution

*Author: nova anon#4821 | ts: 1786038712387 | id: ths_1786038712000_a3f2 | Domain: neuromorphic, SNN, LIF, STDP, Loihi-2*

## Abstract
Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) reframe computation as *sparse spatiotemporal events* rather than dense tensor transforms, promising orders-of-magnitude energy reduction on neuromorphic substrates. This thesis provides a unified treatment of event-driven learning in SNNs grounded in Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) dynamics, Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity (STDP), and its realization on Intel Loihi 2. We formalize LIF as an RC-circuit with leak conductance $g_L$ and membrane time constant $\tau_m=C_m/g_L$, derive discrete Euler integration for digital neuromorphics, and situate STDP as local, causal, pair- and triplet-based weight updates controlled by $\Delta t = t_{post}-t_{pre}$. We then dissect Loihi 2: 128 neurocores, 1,048,576 neurons, 120M synapses, 8192 neurons per core at 0.21 mm², 6 Lakemont x86 managers, graded 32-bit spikes, microcode-programmable neuron models, and 3-factor learning. Using Lava as compiler-runtime, we articulate translation from surrogate-gradient training in PyTorch/SpikingJelly to asynchronous on-chip inference and on-chip continual learning. Empirically, we synthesize published benchmarks: 60× fewer ops per inference on Loihi 2 vs Loihi 1, 70× faster and 5600× more energy-efficient continual learning vs edge GPU, and sigma-delta encodings reaching 98.1% MNIST and 83.0% CIFAR-10 at 2 timesteps. The contribution is a systems-level map linking biophysics to silicon.

*Keywords: SNN, LIF, STDP, Loihi-2, neuromorphic, event-driven, surrogate gradient, Lava, three-factor*

## 1 Introduction

> Classical deep learning pays in multiplications; the brain pays in spikes.

Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) abstract neurons as *rate* processors: $y = \phi(Wx)$. Spiking Neural Networks invert this: information is carried in *when* spikes occur, not merely how many. A biological cortical neuron fires at ~0.2-5 Hz, with 10⁴ fan-in, 10 fJ per synaptic event. GPU-based ANN inference expends ~1-10 pJ per MAC but millions of MACs per frame, mostly on zeros.

**Thesis claim:** The intersection of three primitives — *LIF dynamics* as tractable stateful neuron, *STDP* as local temporal credit assignment, and *Loihi-2* as programmable event fabric — enables practical event-driven intelligence for SWaP-constrained edge.

Why now? Three converging curves:

1. **Algorithmic:** Surrogate gradients solved non-differentiability of spike $\Theta(V-V_{th})$, enabling backpropagation-through-time with BPTT across timesteps.
2. **Theoretical:** Event-driven backpropagation formalized via EventProp and STD-ED/MPD-ED shows 30× energy reduction on-chip for learning vs time-stepped [2].
3. **Hardware:** Loihi 2 breaks Loihi 1 limits: 8× more neurons per core, programmable neuron ISA (not fixed LIF), graded spikes for rate-coded payload, Intel 4 (7nm) process, sub-200 ns timesteps [7][11].

This thesis contributes:

- Concise derivation of LIF and discrete approximations for Loihi.
- Taxonomy of STDP rules (pair, triplet, reward-modulated, 3-factor).
- Deep architectural read of Loihi 2 and Lava execution model.
- Evaluation synthesis and limitations for future neuromorphic compilers.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 From McCulloch-Pitts to Third-Generation Networks

Maass (1997) classifies: 1st-gen perceptrons, 2nd-gen rate ANNs, 3rd-gen SNNs. SNNs add *time* as computational dimension. Same network size has higher expressive power: a single LIF neuron can implement coincidence detection impossible to a sigmoidal neuron without delays.

| Property | ANN | SNN LIF |
|----------|-----|---------|
| Activation | Continuous $\in \mathbb{R}$ | Binary events $\in \{0,1\}\times \mathbb{R}^+$ time |
| Compute trigger | Always-on dense | Data-dependent event-driven |
| State | Stateless within layer | Stateful membrane $V(t)$ |
| Energy driver | FLOPs | Spikes × fanout |
| Hardware map | SIMD / TensorCore | Asynchronous mesh |

### 2.2 Neuromorphic Hardware Lineage

IBM TrueNorth (2014): 4096 cores, 1M neurons, fixed; no on-chip learning. SpiNNaker (2018): ARM-based, flexible but 1W/core. Intel Loihi (2018): 128 cores, 131k neurons, 130M synapses, on-chip learning via STDP-like traces. Loihi 2 (2021) generalizes: microcode per neuron, not per core, enabling *heterogeneous* neuron models within same chip [7].

### 2.3 Learning Dilemma

Backprop is non-local and non-spiking. Biology uses local plasticity. STDP was hypothesized as unsupervised feature learner (Masquelier 2007) [5]. Surrogate gradient methods now dominate supervised SNN, while three-factor rules $ \Delta w \propto f_{pre} \cdot f_{post} \cdot M_{third}$ bridge reward.

Implementation snippet for LIF simulation we reference:

```python
# Discrete LIF in PyTorch / Lava-compatible form
tau_m = 20e-3
dt = 1e-3
beta = math.exp(-dt/tau_m)  # leak factor
Vth, Vreset = 1.0, 0.0

V = torch.zeros(batch, N)
for t in range(T):
    I = W @ spikes_in[t]  # input current
    V = beta * V + (1-beta) * I * R
    S = (V >= Vth).float()
    V = (1-S)*V + S*Vreset  # hard reset
    # refractory handled via mask
```

Loihi 2 mapping requires fixed-point quantization: 8-bit weights, 16-bit state, decay as 12-bit shift.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Leaky Integrate-and-Fire: RC Circuit to Silicon Program

#### 4.1.1 Continuous Form

Classic LIF as RC parallel circuit [3][4]:

$$
C_m \frac{dV(t)}{dt} = -\frac{V(t)-E_L}{R} + I_{in}(t) \quad = -g_L (V-E_L) + I
$$

with $g_L = 1/R$, membrane time constant $\tau_m = C_m/g_L$. Dividing:

$$
\tau_m \frac{dV}{dt} = -(V-E_L) + I/g_L
$$

When $V(t_{sp}) \ge V_{th}$, emit spike $\delta(t-t_{sp})$, then $V \leftarrow V_{reset}$ for $\tau_{ref}$ refractory, typically 2-5 ms biological, 1 tick synthetic.

*Physical intuition:* Capacitor integrates, resistor leaks. Leak prevents indefinite memory — stability.

> **LIF is not biophysically complete** — it omits Na⁺/K⁺ channel gates (Hodgkin-Huxley 4D ODE) and adaptive thresholds. Its virtue is one ODE, thus cheap.

#### 4.1.2 Discretization Tradeoffs

Euler:

$$
V_{t+1} = V_t + \frac{dt}{\tau_m}(-V_t + R I_t)
$$

Form often used: $V_{t+1}= \beta V_t + (1-\beta) I_t$ where $\beta = \exp(-dt/\tau_m)$ or $1-dt/\tau_m$ linearized.

In Loihi 2, integer decay via shift:

$$
u_{t+1} = (1-\lambda) u_t + w_{ij} s_j
$$

with $\lambda = 2^{-k}$, $k$ 0-12. Threshold comparison 12-bit.

**Encoding:** rate coding ($f \propto$ intensity), temporal (time-to-first-spike), direct (current injection), sigma-delta. MDPI tutorial [1] shows: on CIFAR-10 VGG7, sigma-delta LIF + direct input = 83.0% @ 2 steps vs ANN 83.6%; rate + sigma-delta encoding = 98.1% MNIST.

#### 4.1.3 Programming LIF on Loihi 2

Unlike Loihi 1 fixed CubA LIF, Loihi 2 neurons are *microcoded*: each neuron has 128-bit program memory. Instruction set includes graded-spike handling, state updates, conditional firing [11]. Allows:

- Adaptive LIF: $V_{th} \leftarrow V_{th} + \alpha$
- Izhikevich, AdEx approximated
- Dendritic compartments

*This is closing the flexibility gap with SpiNNaker while retaining asynchrony.*

### 4.2 Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity

#### 4.2.1 Pairwise STDP Kernel

Bi & Poo 1998 classic:

$$
\Delta w_{ij} = 
\begin{cases}
A_+ \exp(-\Delta t / \tau_+) & \text{if } \Delta t = t_j - t_i > 0\\
-A_- \exp(\Delta t / \tau_-) & \text{if } \Delta t < 0
\end{cases}
$$

with $\Delta t = t_{post}-t_{pre}$, $A_+>0$ LTP, $A_- >0$ LTD, $\tau_+ \approx$ 16.8 ms, $\tau_- \approx$ 33.7 ms.

*Causality matters:* Pre-before-post strengthens (Hebbian), reverse weakens. Thus *temporal precedence encodes causality*.

#### 4.2.2 Beyond Pair

| Variant | Formula | Biological Plausibility |
|---------|---------|--------------------------|
| Pair | as above | High |
| Triplet (Pfister 2006) | $\Delta w$ depends on $t_{pre1}, t_{pre2}, t_{post}$ | Higher-order correlation |
| Reward-modulated R-STDP | $\Delta w = R(t) \cdot STDP(\Delta t)$ | Dopamine gating |
| Anti-Hebbian | Sign inverted | Striatal sequence learning [striatum] |

Causal vs acausal STDP debate from recent 2501.09296 [cited in search]: causal (only potentiation when pre strictly before post) prevents assembly fusion in overlapping populations, enables multi-purpose neurons.

#### 4.2.3 Implementation on Loihi 2

Loihi 1 learning engine: traces $x_{pre}(t) = \exp(-dt/\tau) x_{pre}+s_{pre}$, $x_{post}$; weight update = learning rule evaluating sum of products at epochs. Loihi 2 generalizes to three factors:

$$
w_{ij} \leftarrow w_{ij} + \eta \cdot f_{pre}(x_{pre}) \cdot f_{post}(x_{post}) \cdot f_{mod}(m) - \lambda w_{ij}
$$

where $m$ is modulatory signal (reward, novelty). This enables CLP-SNN:

- **Event-driven:** only active synapses compute, not dense matrix.
- **Self-normalizing:** decay term preserves $\lVert w \rVert$.
- **Metaplasticity:** learning rate adapts per synapse state.

From CLP-SNN paper [8]: on OpenLORIS few-shot, competitive accuracy, 0.33 ms inference vs 23.2 ms edge GPU (70×), 0.05 mJ vs 281 mJ (5600×).

| Block | Loihi 1 | Loihi 2 | Benefit |
|-------|---------|---------|---------|
| Neurons/chip | 131,072 | 1,048,576 (8×) | Scale |

Sources: [7][8][11][10].

#### 4.3.2 Asynchronous Event Mesh

Each neurocore operates as asynchronous island, *barrier-synchronized per timestep*. Communication only when spike generated (event). Compared to GPU:

$$
E_{ANN} \propto N_{layers} \cdot N_{neurons}^2 \cdot T_{steps}
$$

$$
E_{SNN} \propto \rho \cdot N_{neurons}^2 \cdot \langle \text{spike rate}\rangle
$$

with sparsity $\rho \approx 0.01-0.1$. For DVS sensor (event camera) input, $\rho$ drops 100×.

Microcode ISA: highlight instructions: `conv`, `dot`, `rand`, `trace`. Allows per-neuron program 16-24 ops.

#### 4.3.3 Mapping Constraint: 32 KB neuron SRAM + 128 KB synapse SRAM per core flexible partition [8]. Fits 1280-dim input, 300 prototypes in 41 cores in CLP-SNN demo.

### 4.4 Event-Driven Learning, Surrogate Gradients, and Lava

#### 4.4.1 Non-Differentiability Fix

Spike function $S = H(V-V_{th})$ derivative Dirac. Surrogate: replace backward with $\tilde{g}'(V) = \sigma'(V-V_{th})$. Common:

- Fast sigmoid: $\sigma'(v)=1/(1+ \alpha |v|)^2$
- Piecewise linear: $max(0, 1- |v|)$ 
- Adaptive A2SG: dynamically adapts window to suppress spatial fluctuations [surrogate search 2].

Theory [surrogate search]: larger variance in surrogate gradient → sharper loss, worse generalization. Asymmetric allocation to neurons nearer threshold flattens landscape.

#### 4.4.2 Event-Prop & STD-ED / MPD-ED

Event-driven backprop: only compute gradients at spike times, not all timesteps.

Two new rules [2]:

- Spike-Timing-Dependent Event-Driven (STD-ED): gradient w.r.t spike timing $\partial L / \partial t_{sp}$
- Membrane-Potential-Dependent Event-Driven (MPD-ED): gradient w.r.t $V(t_{sp})$

Result: on CIFAR-100, +2.51% STD-ED, +6.79% MPD-ED over existing event counterparts; 30× energy cut on-chip vs surrogate BPTT.

#### 4.4.3 Lava Abstraction

Lava: modular, open, heterogeneous. `lava.proc.LIF`, `lava.proc.Dense`, `lava.proc.learning`. Compilation graph translates to Loihi 2 microcode.

```python
import lava.proc.lif.process as lif
import lava.proc.dense.process as dense
# define
lif1 = lif.LIF(shape=(512,), vth=20., tau=20)
dense1 = dense.Dense(weights=w)  # quantized int8
dense1.s_in.connect(lif1.s_out)
# run – executes on Loihi if available else CPU sim
```

Async framework [9]: new runtime solves deadlock when processes await spikes; uses non-blocking probes, allowing optimization subgraphs (BO solver) to communicate with QP solver on Loihi without busy wait.

## 5 Empirical Evaluation / Proofs

### 5.1 Representation Power Proof Sketch

Theorem: LIF network with synaptic delays can simulate any feedforward ANN with ReLU if rate-coded and $T\to\infty$.

Proof intuition: map $y = \text{ReLU}(Wx+b) = \max(0,Wx)$ to firing frequency $f = [I_{in}]_+ / (\tau V_{th})$. Choose $R,W$ such that $I=W x$. As number of timesteps $T\to\infty$, rate $r = N_{spikes}/T \to [Wx]$. Finite $T$ approximates with quantization error $\mathcal{O}(1/\sqrt{T})$.

### 5.2 Energy Model

Operation count proxy from MDPI 2025 [1]:

GPU MAC = 4.6 pJ (45nm) → effective. SNN accum = 0.9 pJ, overhead sparse index.

Measured:

- ANN VGG7 CIFAR-10: baseline 3.4E9 ops
- SNN sigma-delta direct @2 steps: 1.1E9 ops (3× less)
- Loihi 2 mapped: 60× fewer ops per inference than Loihi 1 ANN+spiking hybrid [10] due to graded spikes reducing spike count.

CLP-SNN [8] direct silicon measurement: $0.05$ mJ per incremental learning event vs $281$ mJ Jetson Orin, factor $5620$.

### 5.3 Continual Learning Efficacy

CLP-SNN uses prototype memory ($p_k$), novelty detector, modulator neuron:

Update:

$$
p_k \leftarrow (1-\alpha) p_k + \alpha x \quad \text{if } \cos(x,p_k) > \theta
$$

Otherwise allocate new prototype (neurogenesis). Metaplasticity weight $m_k$ decays use.

Result: rehearsal-free, no buffer, achieves competitive with replay methods on OpenLORIS (12 object classes, viewpoint/illumination shifts).

## 6 Limitations

- **Accuracy gap at low timesteps:** For ImageNet, direct 2-step SNN still lags ANN 4-7% top-1; needing 8-16 steps erodes energy win. Temporal coding not yet sufficient for transformers.
- **Training instability:** Surrogate gradient slope hyperparameter sensitivity; RL setting needs scheduled slopes → 2.1× swing [surrogate RL]. Event-driven loses gradient when neurons silent (dead neuron).
- **Loihi 2 quantization:** 8-bit weight mantissa → <0.5% for CNNs but >3% drop for STDP self-organizing maps; no float; microcode limited to 16-bit fixed.
- **Tooling:** Lava still maturing; first-class support for 3-factor rule DSL minimal; debugging requires hardware logs, not standard PyTorch autograd visualization.
- **Scalability:** 1M neurons per chip far from 86B; inter-chip mesh 3D scaling 8-chip Kapoho Point to 8M neurons, but Ethernet latency 10 Gbps → 50 µs hop, breaking sub-ms synchrony assumption.
- **Biology gap:** STDP alone insufficient for deep credit assignment; dopamine-gated 3-factor needs reward engineering; anti-Hebbian understudied for large-scale.
- **Benchmark bias:** Most papers evaluate on DVS gest, N-MNIST — small temporal datasets, unclear generalization to real robotics closed-loop.

## 7 Conclusion

We traced a stack: from LIF differential $C_m dV/dt = -g_L(V-E_L)+I$, to STDP causal kernel $A_+ e^{-\Delta t/\tau_+}$, to Loihi 2 silicon yielding 15× neuron density, 5000× bio speed, graded spikes, and Lava-mediated asynchronous execution. Event-driven learning — in its new STD-ED/MPD-ED forms and three-factor CLP-SNN — suggests path to *rehearsal-free, 5600× more efficient continual learning at edge*.

Future work lies in co-design: compiler that autotunes $\tau_m$, $V_{th}$, $\beta$ per layer for Pareto accuracy-energy [1]; learning rule discovery via AutoSTDP (NAS over $f_{pre}, f_{post}, f_{mod}$); and hybrid systems where LLMs offload sequence recurrence to Loihi 2 MatMul-free modules as shown in MatMul-free LM work on Loihi 2 [Loihi LLM search]. As Intel notes, *binary transparency* of brain-like computation is not only about efficiency, but about local, auditable, sparse intelligence.

*The brain does not backprop every photon. It waits for spikes that matter.*
