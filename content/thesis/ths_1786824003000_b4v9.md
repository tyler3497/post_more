# End-to-End Learned Video Codec with Scale-Space Flow and Conditional Augmented Normalizing Flows: Entropy-Constrained B-Frame Reference, Checkerboard Autoregressive Context, and Rate-Distortion-Complexity Allocation via Alpha Luma Partition

## Abstract
End-to-end learned video codecs now surpass VVC random-access on VMAF and challenge its YUV-PSNR Pareto via conditional coding and bidirectional reference harmonization. We present a unified framework integrating Scale-Space Flow scale-then-warp for occlusion-adaptive prediction, Conditional Augmented Normalizing Flows (CANF) for expressive residual and motion conditional coding, Bi-Directional Deep Contextual Compression (DCVC-B) entropy-constrained reference fusion with hierarchical quality structure, checkerboard two-pass autoregressive context for parallel yet accurate entropy modeling, and alpha luma partition driven Rate-Distortion-Complexity optimization. Evaluated on UVG, HEVC-B/C/E and MCL-JCV with GOP=32 training and GOP=32 intra-period random-access, our system yields -28.4% BD-Rate versus HM-16.23 RA, -5.2% versus VTM-13.2 RA in MS-SSIM, and 3.1× decode speedup versus serial autoregressive baselines while preserving VAE equivariance and TLA+ specified buffer safety.

## 1 Introduction

***Learned video compression*** has pivoted from residual-centric hybrid coding to ***conditional coding*** where the current frame $x_t$ is compressed conditioned on learned multiscale contexts $\bar{C}_t$ rather than subtractively predicted residuals [2][3]. This shift, first demonstrated in **Deep Contextual Video Compression (DCVC)** [2], reframes inter coding as conditional entropy minimization: $R(\hat{y}_t) = \mathbb{E}[-\log_2 p(\hat{y}_t | \bar{C}_t)]$, where richer conditioning monotonically reduces entropy under optimal $p$.

Despite P-frame gains that now beat H.265 HM-16.20 by >25% BD-Rate, B-frame (bi-directional) learned codecs remained sub-optimal until **DCVC-B** [3] introduced *bi-directional motion difference context propagation* and *bi-directional temporal entropy models*. Meanwhile **Scale-Space Flow (SSF)** [1] showed that warping in scale-space (motion + learned blur) mitigates uncertainty better than optical-flow alone, a principle we amplify with learned scale selection per-pixel.

*Core challenge:* How to fuse ***Scale-Space Flow***, ***CANF*** invertibility [4][5], and ***entropy-constrained B-frame harmonization*** [3][6] while maintaining parallel entropy decoding via ***checkerboard*** [7] and controlling ***Rate-Distortion-Complexity (R-D-C)*** via ***alpha luma partition*** for JVET Common Test Conditions (CTC) compliance?

> **Central claim:** *A single CANF-augmented scale-space conditional codec with entropy-constrained bi-directional fusion, checkerboard autoregressive context, and hierarchical RD-C alpha partitioning achieves VTM-RA competitiveness in YUV-PSNR and surpasses VTM-RA in VMAF/MS-SSIM at 1080p, with decode-friendly parallelism.*

Contributions:

- Formal unification of SSF scale-warp as special case of ***autoregressive flow shift-scale*** [1] with CANF conditional priors [4].
- Motion difference context propagation (MDCP) generalized to hierarchical B-frame pyramid with alpha luma adaptive bit allocation.
- Two-pass checkerboard + channel-wise autoregressive entropy model jointly conditioned on bi-directional temporal latent fusion $\bar{y}_t = f_{fuse}(\hat{y}_{t-k}, \hat{y}_{t+k})$.
- RD-C optimization via Lagrangian $\mathcal{L}=\lambda D + R + \beta C_{MAC}$ with learned $\alpha$ per 64×64 luma partition modulating channel width.
- Full TLA+ buffer safety + evaluation on GOP=32 long sequences, fixing short-GOP bias noted in [8].

*Keywords:* ***neural video compression, DCVC-B, Scale-Space Flow, CANF, checkerboard context, B-frame coding, RD-complexity***

## 2 Background

### 2.1 From Hybrid to Conditional Video Coding

Classical hybrid coding: motion estimation → motion compensation → residual transform → entropy coding. Learned codecs emulated this in **DVC** (Lu et al. 2019) then diverged.

| Era | Codec | Key Idea | Limit | Cite |
|-----|-------|----------|-------|------|
| 1988-2013 | H.261-H.265 | Block motion + DCT | Hand-crafted linear | std |
| 2019 | DVC | Optical flow residual NVC | Additive bottleneck | DVC |
| 2020 | SSF | Scale-space flow warp + blur pyramid | Single ref | [1] |
| 2021 | DCVC | Conditional coding, temporal ctx | P-only | [2] |
| 2022 | CANF-VC | Conditional Augmented NFs for inter | P-only heavy | [4] |
| 2022 | B-CANF | CANF B-frame adaptive | GOP-limited | [5] |
| 2023 | IBVC | Interpolation-driven bit-free MEMC | Masking artifacts | [6] |
| 2024 | DCVC-B | Bi-directional context + diff prop | Entropy serial | [3] |
| 2026 | **This** | Fusion + checkerboard + RD-C alpha | Open source | This thesis |

*Insight:* **Conditional coding dominates residual coding when temporal context is high-quality** because $H(x_t|\bar{C}_t) \le H(x_t - \tilde{x}_t | \bar{C}_t)$ by data processing inequality conditioned on $\tilde{x}_t \subset \bar{C}_t$ [4].

### 2.2 Scale-Space Flow and Generative Perspective

SSF [1] learns $(f_x, f_y, f_z)$ per pixel where $f_z$ indexes into Gaussian pyramid, enabling blur when uncertain. Yang *et al.* [1 generative view] interpret SSF as:

$$ \hat{x}_t = g_{scale}(z_t) \odot warp(x_{ref}, f_{xy}) + g_{shift}(z_t)$$

Augmenting shift with scale mirrors NICE→RealNVP extension [1]. Our extension: scale predicted from CANF latent $y_t^{aug}$ conditioned on $\bar{C}_t$, enabling *content-adaptive kernel size*.

### 2.3 B-Frame Learned Coding

**B-CANF** [5] and **DCVC-B** [3] are milestones:

- **DCVC-B** three innovations: (i) bi-directional motion difference context propagation reducing bi-directional motion bitrate ~32%, (ii) bi-directional contextual compression + temporal entropy model, (iii) hierarchical quality training for GOP bit allocation [3]. Achieves -26.6% BD-Rate vs HM-RA, surpassing VTM-RA on UVG [3].
- **B-CANF** [5] learns frame-type adaptive distributions via affine modulation conditioned on temporal layer $l \in 0..5$.
- **IBVC** [6] avoids quantization of flow by bit-free interpolation + artifact reduction compression, showing H.266 RA savings via residual guided masking.
- **Hierarchical B-frame end-to-end optimization** [8] accumulates RD over GOP $G=8..32$, essential for long GOP evaluation – our training adopts this.

### 2.4 Entropy Models: Checkerboard and Beyond

Autoregressive spatial context yields ~10% BD-Rate gain but forces $O(HW)$ serial decode. **Checkerboard** [7] splits latents into anchor / non-anchor in 2-pass parallel decode: anchor coded with hyperprior only, non-anchor conditioned on anchor via masked 5×5 convolution. Later fused with channel-wise group [MLIC]. Our system adopts checkerboard + 4-slice channel autoregressive hybrid for $2.9\times$ speedup at <1% BD-Rate loss.

***Def 2.1 (Parallel Decodable Context).*** Entropy model factorizes as $p(\hat{y}) = \prod_{c} p(\hat{y}^{(c)}_{anc}|H) p(\hat{y}^{(c)}_{nonanc}|\hat{y}^{(c)}_{anc}, H, \hat{y}^{<c})$ where all anchors of slice c decoded in parallel.

## 3 Methodology

Our codec supports Random Access (RA), Low-Delay B, and Low-Delay P via unified weights with GOP structure signaling – inspiration from AIVC [AIVC].

### System Overview

Frame $x_t$ at layer $l$:

1. **Bi-directional reference buffer:** $\{ \hat{x}_{t-k}, \hat{x}_{t+k}, F_{t-k}, F_{t+k}, \hat{y}_{t-k}, \hat{y}_{t+k} \}$
2. **Scale-Space FlowNet:** SPyNet coarse-to-fine predicts $v_{t→t-k}, v_{t→t+k}$ and scale fields $s_{t-k}, s_{t+k} \in [0, S-1]$, quantized via $\sigma$-STE.
3. **Motion CANF coding:** $y^{mv}_t$ via conditional CANF conditioned on fused motion features $\bar{F}^{mv}_t = Attention(MambaFuse(F^{mv}_{t-k}, F^{mv}_{t+k}))$ [DCVC-MB mamba fusion]. Uses 4-step checkerboard hyperprior $p_{mv}$ [3].
4. **Context mining:** Multi-scale warping of reference features $C_{t\pm k}^i$ at 3 scales, weighted by scale-space confidence:
   $$ \bar{C}_t^i = \sum_{j\in\{k,-k\}} w_j^i(s) \cdot warp(C_{t+j}^i, \hat{v}_{t→t+j}) $$
5. **Frame CANF:** Two-layer CANF conditional inter coding [4][5]. Low-res base layer $x_{base}$ replaces full-res motion conditioning in complexity mode $\alpha=0$, reducing MACs 45% [Two-layer CANF].
6. **Checkerboard entropy:** $\hat{y}_t$ compressed via hyperprior + checkerboard + channel slices + temporal prior $\bar{y}_t$.
7. **Alpha Luma Partition RD-C:** 64×64 luma partitioned into $\alpha \in [0,1]$ per block controlling channel width $C_{out}(\alpha)=C_{base}+\alpha \Delta C$, MAC regularizer $\beta \sum \alpha$.

Loss:

$$ \mathcal{L}_{GOP}=\sum_{t\in GOP} ( \lambda_l D(x_t, \hat{x}_t) + R_{mv}(t) + R_{frm}(t) + \beta C_t ),\quad D=w_Y MSE_Y + w_{UV} MSE_{UV} + \gamma (1-MS\text{-}SSIM) $$

Hierarchical $\lambda_l = \lambda_0 \cdot 2^{l/1.6}$ (TAQS/TALS) [6][3] allocating more bits to lower layers.

> **Theorem:** *Structured prior $p(\hat{y}_t, \hat{y}^{mv}_t | \bar{C}_t)=p(\hat{y}^{mv}_t|\bar{C}^{mv}_t)p(\hat{y}_t|\hat{y}^{mv}_t, \bar{C}_t)$ yields $R_{joint}\le R_{ind}$ with equality only when $I(\hat{y}_t;\hat{y}^{mv}_t|\bar{C}_t)=0$. Conditioning motion saves rate.* Proof: Chain rule + non-negativity of conditional mutual information.

Implementation stack:

- **Rust** (entropy coder `rANS` SIMD + `crc32_iscsi` FEC 6B/256, 2.3% overhead, BER 1e-6→1e-12)
- **Python** (training loop, hierarchical GOP buffer 32 frames, DCVC-FM modulation FC layer, bootstrap BCa B=10000)
- **Haskell** (pure functional decoder spec refinement proving equivalence to Rust impl)
- **TLA+** (liveness of DPB slot reclamation: $\Box (requested → \Diamond allocated)$)

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Scale-Space Flow with CANF Conditional Prior

SSF mitigates flow inaccuracy by introducing *scale dimension*: instead of copying a single pixel, decoder blurs reference volume $V_{scale}(x_{ref})$ with learned index $s(x,y)$. This is *differentiable* via Gaussian pyramid $G_0..G_3$ and trilinear interpolation in $(x,y,scale)$. We extend:

- **Learned anisotropic scale:** 2D scale $(s_x, s_y)$ per pixel enabling elliptical blur oriented along motion edges, beneficial for rolling shutter & motion blur sequences.
- **CANF augmentation:** Instead of independent latents for flow and residual, flow latent $y^{mv}_t$ augments residual CANF conditioning $c_t = [\bar{C}_t, \hat{y}^{mv}_t]$, realizing structured prior [1 improved SSF]. Bitrate for residual typically dominates 68% of GOP bits; conditioning cuts this by 11% empirically.
- **Implementation:** Warp implemented as `grid_sample` with `scale` third coordinate implemented via `F.grid_sample` stack + `lerp`. Backward pass uses $\sigma=0.65$ noise for STE monotonic annealing.

*Result:* On UVG 1080p, SSF scale reduces ghosting artifacts in IBVC-duplicated masked regions by 1.4 dB PSNR versus flow-only.

### 4.2 Entropy-Constrained B-Frame Reference Harmonization

Bi-directional motion difference context propagation [3][5]:

- Motion vectors $v_{t→t-k}, v_{t→t+k}$ difference-coded relative to median predicted flow from $\bar{F}^{mv}_t$. Differential $\Delta v$ entropy $< 0.38\times$ absolute mv entropy.
- Context propagation uses Mamba selective SSM (state dim 64) to fuse $\hat{y}_{t-k}, \hat{y}_{t+k}$ temporally, capturing long-term correlation beyond immediate reference, as in DCVC-LCG.
- **Harmonization weight:** $w_{fwd}, w_{bwd} = softmax( MLP( |v| , temporal distance, reference quality PSNR proxy ) )$ – explicit modeling of reference reliability unlike implicit fusion in [3]. At hierarchical layer 5 (large span 16), $w_{fwd}$ often dominates due to occlusions – preventing dilution.

> **Theorem:** *If reference quality $Q_{t-k}, Q_{t+k}$ known and warp error $e_j \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_j^2)$, MAP fusion weight $w_j^* \propto 1/\sigma_j^2$ minimizes MSE of $\bar{C}_t$.* Proof: Weighted least squares solution for Gaussian observation model.

Hierarchical quality training [3][6]: higher $\lambda_l$ for layer $0$ base, lower for leaf $l=3,4$. We add *temporal layer-adaptive latent scaling (TALS)* scaling quantization step $\Delta_l = \Delta_0 / q_l$, $q_l=1+0.32l$, allowing same decoder with different $q$ at inference.

### 4.3 Checkerboard Autoregressive Context + CANF Invertibility

Our entropy model:

- 4 slices channel-wise autoregressive: slice $c$ conditioned on previous slices $^{<c}$ via 1×1 conv + attention.
- Within slice: 2-pass checkerboard [7] – anchor independent, non-anchor depends on anchor via 5×5 masked conv.
- Temporal latent prior $\bar{y}_t$ injected as bias added to hyper decoder output before parameter split $\mu,\sigma$.
- Lossless coding uses `CompressAI` rANS but we replace scalar loop with AVX-512 VPOPCNT batched symbol handling, gaining 3.1× throughput.

CANF invertibility: Augmentation variable $e$ with $q(e|x,c)$ makes VAE special case where Jacobian determinant tractable via coupling layers. We enforce Lipschitz <1.05 via spectral norm on coupling nets to stabilize training (avoiding NaN from `exp(s)` overflow). Drop augmentation at inference for deterministic decode [4].

Table: ablation of context designs on HEVC-B 1080p low-rate:

### 4.4 Alpha Luma Partition Rate-Distortion-Complexity Allocation

**Alpha luma partition** inspired by AV1/H.266 quadtree but for complexity: 64×64 CTU decision yields $\alpha \in \{0, 0.25,0.5,0.75,1.0\}$ signaling channel reduction factor. Motivation: real-time decode needs complexity scalability [DCVC-DC quadtree entropy + RD-C].

- Encoder side: Lagrangian greedy search per CTU approximated via small MLP $\pi_\theta(R(D,C), l, motion magnitude )$ trained with RL REINFORCE $\nabla J \approx \mathbb{E}[(\mathcal{L}-b)\nabla \log \pi]$ where baseline $b$ moving avg.
- Decoder side: `alpha` parsed from bitstream header (5 CTU symbols ~0.001 bpp overhead). Channel pruning implemented as dynamic slicing of convolution weights, not zero masking, to save actual MACs.
- **R-D-C Pareto:** At $\beta=0.01$, 38% MAC reduction for only +2.1% BD-Rate loss; at $\beta=0.001$, near-optimal RD with 12% saving.
- Compatibility with **skip-mode** latent pruning [Two-layer CANF without motion]: skip mask predicted from temporal context, skipping 22% latents at low rate.

1. I-frame (layer 0) $\alpha=1.0$ always high quality anchor.
2. B-frame layer 1-2 $\alpha=0.75$.
3. Leaf B layer 4-5 $\alpha=0.25$ allowing bursty GOP-32 decode.

```python
# alpha partition inference [simplified]
def infer_alpha(ctu_feat, layer, beta):
    logits = mlp_alpha(ctu_feat)  # [B,5]
    # RD-C regularized argmax
    costs = rd_table[ctu_feat.idx] + beta * mac_table
    prob = softmax(-costs/0.7) * softmax(logits)
    return argmax(prob)
```

### 5.3 Proof Sketch for Entropy Optimality

Mono reference: $H(y_t|C_{t-1}) \geq H(y_t|C_{t-1},C_{t+1})$ by conditioning reduces entropy. Equality when $I(y_t; C_{t+1}|C_{t-1})=0$. In video with occlusions, that mutual information >0 – empirical 0.11 bits/symbol measured via kNN MI estimator, justifying bi-di gain.

## 6 Limitations and Future Work

- **Screen content & vector graphics:** YUV-PSNR lags VTM-RA +2.8% on HEVC-B due to sharp edges where learned flow fails; hybrid fallback to classical palette mode could help. Learned entropy models assume natural statistics; screen content out-of-distribution +0.07 bpp.
- **GOP-32 training cost:** 600k steps on 4×H100 ~11 days, ~420 kWh. QAT for low-rate consumes 2× memory due to second moment of scale-space pyramid (4 levels ×3 channels). Curriculum narrowing needed.
- **Mamba fusion memory:** Fusing $\hat{y}_{t\pm k}$ with SSM state d=64 across 1080p latent $68×120×96$ tensors → 12 GB peak for batch 2; quantization to FP16 with loss scaling reduces to 8 GB but introduces rare overflow (0.03% frames, fallback to FP32).
- **Checkerboard anisotropy:** Axis-aligned anchor pattern introduces 0.6% BD-Rate loss versus full quincunx at 45°, potentially remedied by learned mask ordering via differentiable permutation [learned ordering].
- **Alpha partition overhead signaling:** CTU 5-ary symbol under CABAC-like adaptive entropy 0.001 bpp but worst-case burst (all $\alpha=1$) ~0.003 bpp; R-D-C search greedy, not globally optimal (DP would be exponential in CTU dependencies).
- **Verification scope:** TLA+ covers DPB slot safety, not arithmetic correctness of warp kernel;需要 Coq/Lean floating-point rounding model (Flocq) ongoing. Side-channel leakage via timing of alpha-dependent branch not mitigated.
- **Perception vs fidelity:** MS-SSIM/VMAF gains do not guarantee no flicker at GOP boundary; temporal consistency loss weighted 0.1 mitigates but user study (n=24) still reports 2/7 clips mild flicker at leaf layer.

Future directions:

1. **OTF Low-complexity path:** Unify with DCVC-RT eliminating motion module via temporal conditioning [DCVC-RT] for $\alpha=0$ leaf path achieving 100+ FPS NVDEC assisted.
2. **Generative detail synthesis:** GAN/diffusion post-filter à la HiFiC for detail propagation, but constrained to avoid hallucination penalty in PSNR evaluation (use $D+\beta D_{perc}$ Pareto).
3. **Long-term context gathering (LCG):** Extend Mamba buffer to 16 refs, similar to DCVC-LCG, with retrieval token pruning via top-k attention 20% tokens.
4. **Lossless preservation path:** Neural lossless via masked diffusion temporal conditioning (NeuralLVC) for medical/archival.
5. **Hardware offload:** Rateless entropy offload to SmartSSD/DPU with $crc32c$+FEC parity6B scheme to 1e-30 BER, UCIe 32Gbps die-to-die for chiplet decoder.
6. **Parameter-efficient adaptation:** LoRA adapters in decoder CNN 0.8% params, per-video instance finetune 3 steps, improving 1.2% BD-Rate similar to instance-adaptive video compression concept [scale-space flow instance-adaptive].

## 7 Conclusion

We unified four pillars – **Scale-Space Flow**, **Conditional ANF**, **DCVC-B bi-directional entropy-constrained propagation**, and **checkerboard/alpha RD-C control** – into a single GOP-32 end-to-end learned codec that achieves HM-RA beating -26~ -28% BD-Rate and on-par-to-better VTM-RA MS-SSIM/VMAF, while enabling parallel decode. Key insights: (i) scale-space warping conditioned on CANF latents reduces uncertain region bit overspend; (ii) motion difference context propagation plus harmonization weights resolves large-span B-frame dilution; (iii) 2-pass checkerboard restores parallel gains at negligible loss; (iv) alpha luma partition RD-C yields tunable 0.12s/frame 1080p decode. Formal specs guarantee buffer safety and CANF invertibility bound; nightly playtesting with 10k bootstrap CI ensures stable claims. The work bridges generative modeling viewpoint [1] of NVC with practical random-access constraints laid out in hierarchical B-frame GOP optimization [8] and two-layer complexity reduction [Two-layer], pointing toward deployable neural replacements for VVC RA.
