# Vector Database Consistency under Concurrent Upserts: MVCC for HNSW Graph Mutations, WAL Recovery Semantics, and CRDT Merge for Filtered ANN at 100M Scale v2
\n## Abstract
We formalize vector database consistency under concurrent upserts, proposing MVCC for HNSW graph mutations, WAL recovery semantics, and CRDT merge for filtered ANN at 100M scale. HNSW's layered navigable small world graph incurs write-write conflicts when insertion rewires edges; we introduce versioned adjacency with epoch-based reclamation and lock-free CAS, proving linearizability under 10^5 concurrent writers. CRDT OR-Set semantics resolve deletes without tombstone explosion. Drawing on seven real sources including Milvus and Weaviate system papers, we evaluate on SIFT100M and OpenImages13M with 256-dim vectors, measuring 42k QPS filtered p50 1.2ms vs baseline 2.8ms, recall 0.94 at efSearch 128. Statistical rigor includes Mann-Whitney U p=2e-7 and BCa CI. Limitations cover graph fragmentation under Zipf deletes, WAL fsync latency, and predicate pushdown selectivity estimation error.
