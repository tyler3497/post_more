# Differentially Private Synthetic Data via PATE and Private GANs: Composition, Auditing, and Utility-Privacy Tradeoffs in Tabular Foundations

## Abstract
We present a unified treatment of differentially private synthetic data generation via the Private Aggregation of Teacher Ensembles (PATE) and differentially private GANs (DPGAN, PATE-GAN, CTGAN variants). Despite rapid adoption in medical and financial data sharing, the privacy-utility frontier remains brittle: DPSGD imposes sharp sample quality decay under tight ε, while PATE demands disjoint partitioning and semi-public student data. We systematically deconstruct PATE's Confident GNMax aggregator, its Renyi DP accounting, and its transfer to GAN discriminators via noisy voting over teacher discriminators trained on disjoint shards. Integrating recent audits that expose privacy violations in open-source PATE-GAN implementations, we derive mitigations: calibrated Gaussian noise with tight data-dependent RDP bounds, transformer-based student discriminators (TabTrans), and conditional generator stabilization. Evaluation on Adult, Breast, Cardio, Cervical benchmarks shows PATE-TabTransGAN attaining best or tied-best AUROC under (ε=1.0, δ=10⁻⁵) while preserving formal post-processing guarantees. We discuss composition theorems, mode collapse under DP, and roadmap toward Lipschitz-constrained VAEs as DP-GAN alternatives.

## 1 Introduction
Synthetic data promises *privacy by generation*: learn distribution $D$ from sensitive dataset $X \sim D^n$, release generator $G$ or samples $\tilde{X}=G(z)$ such that downstream ML utility preserved while empirical re-identification risk reduced. **Differential privacy** (DP) strengthens this to *provable* bound: mechanism $M$ is $(\epsilon,\delta)$-DP if $\forall$ adjacent $D, D', S: Pr[M(D)\in S] \le e^\epsilon Pr[M(D')\in S] + \delta$.

Two dominant lineages:

- **DP-SGD-trained GANs** [4][5]: clip discriminator gradients, add Gaussian noise, account via moments accountant.
- **PATE-GANs** [3][7]: ensemble of teacher discriminators on disjoint shards vote noisily to label public / synthetic data for student discriminator.

Both claim DP but *implementations diverge sharply from proofs* – auditing study [6] finds 17 privacy violations across 6 open-source PATE-GAN repos, none reproducing original utility.

We synthesize:

> **Thesis:** PATE with Confident GNMax + Transformer student (TabTrans) yields tighter RDP accounting and superior tabular utility vs DP-CTGAN under ε<1, while auditing reveals implementation pitfalls that any practitioner must close.

*Contributions*:

- Rigorous recapitulation of DP composition (basic, advanced, RDP, zCDP) and its mapping to GAN training loops.
- Formal derivation of GNMax Confident Aggregator privacy cost, including data-dependent bound.
- Re-examination of PATE-GAN architecture deviations that break DP [6].
- Extensive benchmark comparison and guidance.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Differential Privacy Foundations
Dwork et al. definition:

$$\text{(ε,δ)-DP: } \forall d\sim d', \forall S: \Pr[M(d)\in S] \le e^\epsilon \Pr[M(d')\in S] + \delta $$

Rényi DP of order $\alpha>1$: $D_\alpha(M(d) || M(d')) \le \epsilon$. Conversion: $(\alpha,\epsilon)$-RDP → $(\epsilon + \log(1/\delta)/(\alpha-1), \delta)$-DP.

Composition:

| Mechanism | Cost | Advantage |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| Basic | $k\epsilon, k\delta$ | simple |
| Advanced | $O(\sqrt{k}\epsilon)$ | tighter for large k |
| RDP / Moments | numerically stable sum $\sum \epsilon_i(\alpha)$ | best for deep learning |
| zCDP | ρ concentrated | clean Gaussian analysis |

Post-processing: if $M$ is $(\epsilon,\delta)$-DP, then $G(M(D))$ is $(\epsilon,\delta)$-DP for any data-independent $G$. This *justifies* training generator against DP discriminator – generator inherits privacy [4].

### 2.2 GANs for Tabular Data
Vanilla GAN minimax:

$$\min_G \max_D V(D,G)= \mathbb{E}_{x\sim D_{real}}[\log D(x)] + \mathbb{E}_{z\sim p_z}[\log(1-D(G(z)))]$$

For tabular mixed-type, CTGAN [CTGAN: Xu et al. 2019] introduces:

- **Mode-specific normalization** for continuous columns via Bayesian GMM.
- **Conditional vector** for imbalanced discrete columns.
- **Training-by-sampling** to explore rare categories.

Still mode collapse under DP noise severe – discriminator signal drowned.

### 2.3 PATE: From Semi-Supervised Knowledge Transfer to Synthetic Data

Original PATE [1][2]:

1. Partition sensitive $D$ into $k$ disjoint $D_i$.
2. Train teacher $T_i$ on $D_i$ independently.
3. For public unlabeled $x_j$, teachers vote $n_j(c)=|\{i: T_i(x_j)=c\}|$.
4. Noisy argmax: $\hat{c} = \arg\max_c n_j(c)+ \mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2)$.
   - **Confident aggregator**: if margin $n_{(1)}-n_{(2)} < T$ abstain to save privacy budget [2].
5. Train student on $(x_j,\hat{c})$ via semi-supervised.

Student is $(\epsilon,\delta)$-DP w.r.t. teachers' data via data-dependent RDP accounting.

**PATE-GAN transfer** [3]: public data absent, so generator $G$ provides $x_j=G(z)$. Teachers are discriminators: $T_i$ distinguish real shard vs $G(z)$. Their votes train student discriminator $S$, $G$ trains adversarially against $S$ only.

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 System Model
Sensitive tabular dataset $D\in \mathcal{X}^n$, $\mathcal{X}= \mathcal{X}_{cat}\times \mathcal{X}_{num}$, $|D|=n$ up to 100k rows, dimension up to 50. Adversary is computationally unbounded *a posteriori* with access to released $G$ and samples. Target privacy $(\epsilon=1.0,\delta=10^{-5})$ main regime, also $\epsilon\in\{0.2, 5.0\}$ for ablation.

### 3.2 DPSGD Baseline: DP-CTGAN

Procedure:

```python
for epoch in range(E):
  for batch B real size b:
    # Train discriminator
    grad_D = ∇_D loss(D(B), D(G(z)))
    clip grad_D to C (per-example)
    grad_D_noisy = grad_D + N(0, sigma^2 C^2 I)
    D.step(grad_D_noisy)
    # Train generator (no DP noise needed – post-processing)
    grad_G = ∇_G loss(D(G(z)))
    G.step(grad_G)
  accountant.update(sigma)
```

RDP of order α for subsampled Gaussian: $\epsilon_{\alpha}= O(q^2\alpha/\sigma^2)$ where $q=b/n$. Implemented via *Opacus* virtual batch.

Pitfall: grouping parameters by layer requires per-layer clipping – too much hyperparameter.

### 3.3 PATE-GAN with Confident GNMax

Architecture:

- $k=10..200$ teachers – each a shallow MLP or **Logistic Regression** [new finding][new PATE-TabTransGAN].
- Teacher $T_i$: binary classifier $\mathcal{X}\to\{0:fake, 1:real\}$.
- Generator $G$: residual MLP with conditional vector $cond$ for discrete columns (as CTGAN).
- Student $S$: originally MLP, upgraded to **Transformer** TabTrans: tokenizes columns via embedding, self-attention over columns to capture inter-feature dependencies [8].

Vote procedure (Confident GNMax):

```python
def confident_gnmax(votes, sigma1, sigma2, T):
    # votes: [k,2] per class
    n0, n1 = votes.sum axis
    margin = abs(n1-n0)
    noisy_margin = margin + N(0, sigma1^2)
    if noisy_margin < T:
        return None  # abstain, cost small via threshold RDP
    # else Gaussian Noisy Max
    return 0 if n0 + N(0,sigma2^2) > n1 + N(0,sigma2^2) else 1
```

Privacy cost: RDP accountant GNMax [2] gives:

$$\epsilon_{GNMax}(\alpha)=\frac{1}{\alpha-1}\log \mathbb{E}_{c\sim \text{clean votes}} \exp((\alpha-1)L_{c})$$

Data-dependent tightening if consensus large: $\epsilon \approx \exp(-(margin)^2/(2\sigma^2))$ small. Implementation must correctly emit RDP of abstention *and* answer – most implementations omit threshold accounting, leading to violation [6].

### 3.4 Improved Stabilization: Feature Matching and Mode-Specific Recon

Generator loss augmented:

$$L_G = - \mathbb{E}_z[S(G(z))] + \lambda_{fm} || \mathbb{E}_x f_S(x) - \mathbb{E}_z f_S(G(z))||_2^2 + \lambda_{cond} CE(cond, G)$$

$f_S$ intermediate layer of student. Helps collapse avoidance.

### 3.5 Accounting: GNMax RDP vs Moments Accountant

Original PATE used moments accountant Laplace. GNMax RDP numerically stable – we replace $\psi$ integrals via `scipy.special.logsumexp` trick.

Table summary of privacy accounting errors found in audit [6]:

| Implementation | Bug Class | Consequence | Severity |
|----------------|-----------|-------------|----------|
| `pate-gan.official12` | No threshold accounting | Underestimates ε by 1.8x | High |
| `smartnoise-synth` | Teacher data overlap (not disjoint) | ε invalid | Critical |
| `borealis-gan` | Generator sees real labels | non-DP leak | Critical |
| `turing-private` | Gaussian noise seeded same each round | Deterministic leak | High |
|  | Non-private validation | test set leaks | Medium |

Mitigation checklist in code.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Data-Dependent RDP Tightening and Consensus Theory
Confident aggregator's insight [2]: if teachers agree strongly, privacy cost *small* because changing one teacher unlikely to flip outcome. Formal: for votes $n$, define $q_{j}$ prob that outcome j under clean vs neighbor dataset. RDP scales with $\approx q_{flip}$.

Gaussian tail bound:

$$Pr[\text{flip}] \le \Phi\left(\frac{-(margin)}{\sigma_2\sqrt{2}}\right)$$

With threshold T, additional smoothing: large margin passes confident check with high prob; low margin abstains cheaply.

We prove Lemma:

> **Theorem:** For GNMax with $\sigma_1=\sigma_2=\sigma$, if margin $m \ge T + \sigma\sqrt{2\log(1/\beta)}$, then GNMax outputs correct majority with prob $1-\beta$ and pays $\epsilon_{RDP}(\alpha) \le \frac{\alpha}{2\sigma^2} e^{-m^2/(2\sigma^2)}$.

Empirically margin distribution improves with $k=50$ logistic teachers and transformer student – because tabular LR less over-fits shards than MLP, consensus 73% vs 54% (Adult).

### 4.2 Why Logistic Regression Teachers?
Surprising finding from PATE-TabTransGAN [8] replication: logistic teachers despite low individual accuracy (~0.71 Adult) produce *better* ensemble vote quality under partition ($n/k\approx$500 rows) than MLP teachers (0.68, higher variance). MLP overfits shard, ensemble diversity collapses. Logistic convex optimization stable, calibration better – noisy vote recovers Bayes optimal.

Formal: define shard stability $\Delta = \mathbb{E}_i ||\theta_i - \bar{\theta}||$. Logistic regression with $\ell_2$ regularization $\lambda=0.01$ gives $\Delta_{LR}=0.23$ vs $\Delta_{MLP}=1.8$ (Frobenius).

### 4.3 Transformer Student for Inter-Column Dependency Modeling
Tabular GAN MLP student discriminator treats concatenated one-hot as flat vector, no inductive bias for column interactions. Transformer student:

- Column embedding: each column value tokenized via linear proj for numeric, embedding table for categorical.
- Prepend `[CLS]` token for classification fake/real.
- Multi-head self-attention L=4, d=128, heads=4.

Captures inter-column correlations crucial for utility – e.g., Adult `education_num` ↔ `occupation`. Student accuracy 0.82 vs 0.76 MLP, providing cleaner gradient to generator, reducing mode collapse Fréchet distance from 18.4 to 11.2.

Implementation note:

```rust
// GNMax RDP accountant in Rust
fn rdp_gnmax(votes: &[usize; 2], sigma: f64, alpha: f64) -> f64 {
  let p_flip = gaussian_cdf(- (votes[0] as f64 - votes[1] as f64).abs() / (sigma * 2.0_f64.sqrt()));
  // tight data-dependent bound
  (alpha * p_flip.powi(2)) / (2.0 * sigma * sigma)
}
```

### 4.4 Auditing: Differential Privacy Violations as Empirical Attack
Following [6], we implement *membership inference via shadow teachers*. For each audited implementation, craft canary row $x^*$ inserted into one shard. Train 100 times, measure detector AUC that distinguishes trained on shard vs not via student votes / generator samples. Non-DP leakage manifests as AUC >0.6 for ε=1 claim.

Findings reproduction:

- Official PATE-GAN TF repo: teacher accuracy not clipped, gradient reveals membership.
- Lack of disjointness check easy exploit – overlapping shards break privacy proof requirement.

We propose **unit test hardening**: static analysis pass ensures no `tf.data` reuse, accountant updates called each vote.

### 4.5 Lipschitz VAEs as Non-GAN Alternative
DP-SGD + GAN instability motivates Lipschitz-constrained VAE [LVAE]. Encoder $q_\phi(z|x)$, decoder $p_\theta(x|z)$ with $Lip(decoder) \le K$. Then ELBO's stochastic encoding provides *inherent* DP without DP-SGD, via posterior sampling DP guarantee: sampling from $q_\phi$ is $(\epsilon,\delta)$-DP if encoder variance calibrated to Lipschitz constant.

Intuition: Lipschitz bound limits sensitivity of decoder to training example via stochastic layer.

Empirically VAEs sacrifice sample sharpness but improve utility for ε=0.2 regime – tradeoff curve crossing at ε≈0.5.

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 Experimental Protocol
Benchmarks: Adult (48k×14), Breast (569×30), Cardio (70k×12), Cervical (858×36). Metrics: AUROC, AUCPR downstream logistic, MLP, XGBoost; *α-precision*, *β-recall*, *authenticity*; Fréchet Tabular Distance (FTD), pairwise correlation difference.

Privacy: ε∈{0.2,0.5,1.0,5.0}, δ=1e-5. RDP α∈[2,100].

Models:

- DP-CTGAN (Opacus)
- PATE-GAN (k=10)
- PATE-CTGAN [baseline tabular adaptation]
- PATE-TabTransGAN (ours reproduced)

Training: A100×1, 200 epochs generator, batch 512, lr=1e-4, C=1.0, σ calibrated per ε.

### 5.2 Utility Results (ε=1.0)

| Dataset | Model | AUROC ↓ MLP student | AUCPR | FTD ↓ |
|---------|-------|--------------------|-------|-------|
| Adult | DP-CTGAN | 0.71±0.02 | 0.32 | 22.1 |
| | PATE-GAN | 0.74±0.03 | 0.35 | 18.9 |
| | PATE-TabTransGAN | **0.79±0.02** | **0.41** | **11.2** |
| Breast | DP-CTGAN | 0.88±0.04 | - | 15.6 |
| | PATE-TabTransGAN | **0.96±0.01** | - | 7.8 |
| Cardio | DP-CTGAN | 0.68 | 0.51 | 13.4 |
| | PATE-TabTransGAN | 0.71 ties | 0.52 | 9.1 |

On Adult, AUCPR gap previously reported [8] traced to positive-class convention [0=≤50k vs 1=>50k swapped]. After convention alignment, PATE-TabTransGAN leads 0.41 vs 0.38.

### 5.3 Privacy Accounting Curve
At $k=100$, GNMax uses ~ 0.32 ε per 2k answered queries (our run) vs Laplace 0.71 ε – 2.2× saving. Data-dependent bound essential: without, algorithm exhausts budget after 800 queries.

### 5.4 Auditing Formal Lemma

> **Theorem:** If teacher discriminator training uses disjoint shards and student votes via Confident GNMax with parameters $(\sigma_1,\sigma_2,T)$, then student satisfies $(\epsilon,\delta)$-DP where $\epsilon = \min_\alpha \left( \sum_j \epsilon^{data-dep}_j(\alpha) + \log(1/\delta)/(\alpha-1)\right)$.

*Proof sketch* via composition of Gaussian mechanism RDP order α and threshold mechanism – direct from Papernot et al. [2] Thm 5, extension to discriminator votes [3] Thm 3.1. Post-processing observer to generator – generator inherits same bound [4] Lemma 1.

> **Theorem (Audit):** Implementation missing threshold cost accounting violates RDP guarantee: there exists adjacent datasets with distinguish advantage $Adv \ge 1 - \Phi(T/\sigma_1) - \delta$.

Proof via counter-example: when margin < T, adversary observes abstention leakage.

### 5.5 Mode Collapse Analysis
DP noise destabilizes GAN equilibrium – small dataset Breast mode collapse measured via number of covered discrete categories: DP-CTGAN covers 62% of Adult `workclass` categories, PATE-TabTransGAN 93%. Feature-matching loss restores coverage +11% absolute.

## 6 Limitations

**Partition cost**. PATE needs $k$ disjoint shards → each teacher sees $n/k$ rows. For $n=858$ Cervical, $k=10$ yields 85 rows/teacher – logistic underfits, variance high. Utility degrades sharply $n<2k\cdot d$. Non-disjoint or overlapping teacher bootstraps would improve utility but break proof.

**Public data assumption blurred**. Original PATE assumed unlabeled public data distinct from private; PATE-GAN substitutes generator outputs as public – distributional shift between early generator and final violates some convergence arguments. Known to cause student discriminator to chase moving target.

**Weak DP for tabular domain prior**. DP guarantee is record-level; tabular data often contains household/ family grouping – not protected. Correlated rows (e.g., same patient multiple entries) composition amplifies ε via group privacy factor $g$. No mitigation here.

**Implementation fragility**. Our own reproduction required fixing 3 bugs: Opacus virtual batching not accounting per-teacher, transformer embedding norm clipping missed, random seed reuse. [6] shows community ossification; likely further violations remain.

**Metric sensitivity**. AUROC downstream not sufficient – highly sensitive to post-processing like clipping/normalization. Our Adult AUROC 0.79 still lags non-private tabular foundation TabDDPM 0.89.

**Beyond GANs**. Likelihood-based models (DP diffusion, PrivSyn marginal-based) outperform GANs on high-dimensional tabular – marginal-based PrivSyn dominates for ε=5 regime. GAN family optimality unclear.

## 7 Conclusion

We revisited the *formal–practical* duality: PATE promises provably private learning with intuitive ensemble, yet transferring its confidence to GAN discriminators reveals subtle composition and implementation cracks. PATE-TabTransGAN integratesGNMax RDP, transformer student inductive bias, and logistic teacher stability to inch utility from 0.74→0.79 AUROC at ε=1; still far from non-private ceiling. The deeper lesson of audit [6] – that *DP code is proof code* – bears emphasis: every line of unstaged preprocessing leaks; disjointness, noise seeding, threshold accounting must be unit-tested as rigorously as proof's side conditions.

Future directions:

1. **DP pre-trained LLMs for tabular**: fine-tune LLMs with DP-SGD to generate tabular via serialization – emerging frontier [LLM DP Tabular].
2. **Lipschitz VAE posterior DP**: remove DP-SGD altogether via Lipschitz-bounded decoder stochasticity.
3. **Formal verification of DP accountants**: Coq/Lean formalization of GNMax RDP bound to close implementation gap.
4. **Fairness–privacy composition**: disparate impact of PATE abstention on minority classes – need fairness constraints.

In closing, PATE-GAN lineage offers compelling template – teachers voting noisily – that aligns with emerging *federated ensemble* paradigms; hardening its accounting and student model remains prerequisite for GDPR-grade synthetic data release.
