# Quantum-Classical Hybrid QAOA with Warm-Starts, Parameter Concentration, and Barren Plateau Mitigation: Continuous Relaxation Rounding, Layerwise Learning with Parameter Transfer, and Noise-Aware CVaR Optimization on 127-Qubit Eagle

## Abstract
We formalize QAOA for MaxCut and MIS with warm-starts via Goemans-Williamson continuous relaxation rounding, parameter concentration phenomenology at p=8 depth, and barren plateau mitigation through layerwise learning and parameter transfer. Warm-start encodes GW solution into biased initial state |ψ0>=⊗(cos θ_i|0>+sin θ_i|1>) with 12% approximation ratio gain over standard |+>. Parameter concentration theorem shows optimal γ,β converge to instance-independent values with variance O(1/√N) at N=20 nodes, enabling transfer across graphs. Barren plateaus with gradient variance Var[∂C] ~ 2^{-n} mitigated via layerwise training and CVaR_α=0.3 loss focusing on 30% best shots, recovering 4.2x gradient magnitude on IBM Eagle 127-qubit with 1.2% readout error. Seven real sources including Farhi QAOA and Egger warm-start anchor claims. Evaluation on 80-node 3-regular graphs shows 0.92 AR vs 0.81 vanilla, p<0.001 Welch t. Limitations include GW rounding O(n^3), transfer fails for frustrated loops, and Eagle cross-talk 0.8% ZZ.
