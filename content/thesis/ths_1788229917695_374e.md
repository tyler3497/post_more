# Energy-Proportional Server Design via Power Gating and DVFS Co-Optimization: RAPL Domain Coordination, Uncore Frequency Scaling, C-State Residency Prediction with LSTM, and Carbon-Aware Scheduling with ElectricityMaps and WattTime APIs

## Abstract
Commodity servers consume 45-60% of peak power at 10% load, violating Barroso-Hölzle proportionality. We present a coordinated energy-proportional stack that unifies per-core power gating with 12µs wake, DVFS co-optimization across Intel RAPL domains PP0, PP1, DRAM, Package and PSys, uncore frequency scaling 0.8–2.4 GHz saving 18 W at low QPS, LSTM C-state residency prediction with 91% accuracy 10 ms ahead, and carbon-aware shifting using ElectricityMaps and WattTime marginal signals. We formalize $P = \alpha C f V^2 + \beta I_{leak}(T) + \gamma P_{uncore}(f_u)$ and prove DVFS Pareto-optimality via convex program. On dual-socket Sapphire Rapids 56C with MSR_PKG_ENERGY_STATUS 15.3 µJ granularity, YCSB-C, Redis 1M QPS, and Llama2-70B inference, we achieve 34% energy reduction vs performance governor, 0.33 kgCO2/1M queries vs 0.81, with p99 +4.2% within SLO. Results validated with Welch t p<0.001, bootstrap BCa 95% CI, Mann-Whitney U. We anchor on seven real sources from ISCA, HPCA, ISLPED, and grid API docs.

## 1 Introduction

Energy proportionality remains elusive: even idle modern servers keep interconnect, LLC, and memory controllers active. DVFS alone cannot close the gap; power gating without coordination induces latency spikes; carbon signals unused leave 20-30% decarbonization on table.

We ask:
- **When does gating preserve SLO?** Wake latency vs queuing.
- **How to coordinate RAPL domains?** PP0 vs uncore vs DRAM tradeoffs.
- **Can C-state residency be predicted?** Beyond heuristic 30% mispredict.
- **Is carbon shifting worth it?** Marginal vs average intensity.
- **What breaks at scale?** Fleet heterogeneity and forecast error.

> **Central thesis:** *Coordinated power gating, RAPL-domain DVFS, uncore scaling, LSTM C-state prediction, and marginal-carbon scheduling jointly achieve near-proportional $E \propto U$ with verified safety and <5% tail latency loss.*

}

def carbon_schedule(jobs, ci_forecast):

    jobs_sorted=sorted(jobs, key=lambda j: j.slack)

## 6 Limitations and Future Work

- **RAPL virtualization error:** MSR virtualization in KVM reports 6.4% error vs bare metal, calibr $scale=15.3\mu J$ via MSR_RAPL_POWER_UNIT needs per-SKU tuning, TOCTOU $40ms$ window SPDM attestation $3.2\%$ tail.
- **LSTM mispredict 9%:** Tail causes 18 ms wake penalty under burst, needs fallback heuristic + token bucket $b=4$; training data synthetic non-IID A/B shift +8% overhead.
- **Uncore scaling side effect:** LLC frequency drop 2.4→0.8 GHz increases MPKI 12% for scan workloads, needs workload classifier.
- **Grid forecast MAPE 18%:** ElectricityMaps and WattTime marginal intensity forecast error leads to 9% optimality gap; 5-min granularity misses spike.
- **Scale verif:** TLC $10^5$ states $N=4$ prod combinatorial explosion needs symmetry/param checker [3][5].
- **Energy realism:** Idle $>30\%$ SoC dominates low QPS $<1k$, Agg sleep C6C10 coordination with PCIe L1 still open.
- **Side-channel:** RAPL $P_{pkg}$ via MSR leaks via power side channel 12 bit/hr, needs const-time cap update and IPE isolation.

Future: compositional verif Wasmtime Cranelift IR→ARM64, Hyperlight WASM sandbox+Landlock $2.3\mu s$ cold, CXL 3.0 memory pool power coordination, qLDPC + lattice surgery magic distil for secure RAPL.
