# Log-Structured Merge Tree Compaction Strategies for NVMe ZNS SSDs: RocksDB Tiered-Plus-Leveled Hybrid Compaction, Pebble Leveled-Delete with Zone-Aware Placement, and WiscKey Value Separation for Write Amplification Mitigation at 1.6TB/s

## Abstract
We present a unified analysis of LSM-tree compaction on NVMe Zoned Namespace (ZNS) SSDs at 1.6 TB/s device bandwidth, integrating RocksDB tiered-plus-leveled hybrid compaction, Pebble's delete-only leveled compaction with range tombstone elision, and WiscKey-style value separation via BlobDB/vLog. ZNS exposes append-only zones requiring explicit RESET, invalidating conventional over-write and random GC. We formalize write amplification (WA), space amplification (SA), and delete amplification (DA) under zone capacity Z=1077 MiB and 3,688 zones on a Micron 7450 1.6TB device. Our methodology models lifetime-aware placement, same-zone compaction, and value-pointer indirection with 16B key / 1KB value to reduce LSM size 98%. Empirical synthesis from HotStorage'22, MSST'24, and FAST'16 shows 38.1% write throughput gain, 56.8% WA reduction, 75% space efficiency improvement, and 1.58x SA spike mitigation via proactive tombstone compaction. We provide TLA+ safety, Rust compaction scheduler, and Haskell cost model proofs.

> **Keywords:** LSM-tree, ZNS SSD, RocksDB, Pebble, WiscKey, write amplification, tiered compaction, zone-aware placement

## 1 Introduction

**Log-Structured Merge Trees (LSM-trees)** dominate *write-optimized* key-value storage, converting random writes to sequential appends, yet their compaction tax fundamentally collides with **Zoned Namespace (ZNS)** semantics [1][5]. Traditional SSDs hide garbage collection (GC) inside an opaque Flash Translation Layer (FTL); ZNS SSDs such as the Micron 7450 1.6TB expose *append-only zones* with explicit `ZONE RESET`, shifting GC to the host and demanding *sequential write constraints* and *zone capacity alignment* [6][8].

*Contemporary engines* – **RocksDB**, **Pebble**, and **BlobDB/WiscKey** – evolved for block devices. RocksDB's universal, leveled, and FIFO compactions assume mutable files; Pebble's production evolution in CockroachDB since v20.2 introduces **L0 sublevels**, **flush splitting**, and **delete-only compactions** that drop whole sstables falling within range deletion bounds [3]. WiscKey [2] first *decoupled keys and values*, storing keys plus a 12-24B value reference in LSM while full values reside in a separate **vLog**, reducing effective WA from *10-30x to ~1.14* for 16B keys / 1KB values [1][2].

This thesis argues:

- **ZNS is not a drop-in**: zone lifetime heterogeneity induces *Delete-Induced Write Amplification (DIWA)* and *Device-Level WA (DLWA)* if files with disparate lifetimes share zones [5].
- **Tiered-plus-leveled hybrid** is necessary: HotStorage'22 Compaction-Aware Zone Allocation shows *up to 10% performance* and *75% space efficiency* via hotness-aware allocation [5].
- **Delete-only compaction** must be zone-aware: range tombstones must trigger zone-scale RESET eligibility, not just sstable dropping [3][4].
- **Value separation** is *required* for 1.6 TB/s: 100 GB dataset -> ~2 GB LSM after separation, enabling metadata to reside in DRAM, reducing memory from 120 GB to 2.5 GB under 100K QPS OLTP [1].

> **Theorem: Zone-Lifetime Alignment Bound.** For zone count Q, file lifetimes lambda_i, optimal placement minimizes max zone invalid ratio variance, achieving space amplification SA <= 1 + (max lambda / min lambda)*epsilon.

> **Theorem: WiscKey WA Reduction.** With key size K=16B, value V=1KB, reference R=12B, WA_LSM=10, WA_VLog=1, effective WA_eff = (WA_LSM*K + V)/(K+V) approx 1.14, a 8.7x reduction over pure LSM.

> **Theorem: Tiered-Plus-Leveled Tradeoff.** Tiered compaction WA_T approx T, SA_T approx T; leveled WA_L approx T*L, SA_L approx 1.11; hybrid achieves WA_H = O(T + L) with tail latency P99 reduced 38.1% via ZQuery two-level bitmap [7].

> **Theorem: Delete Amplification Collapse.** Deleting 50% keys increases disk usage 1.04x -> 1.58x transiently; delete-only compaction restores 1.04x after forced compaction, preventing resurrection [4].

> **Theorem: ZNS Parallelism Constraint.** For zone append limit M=14 open zones per die, L0 flush concurrency C <= M avoids zone interference, sustaining 1.6 TB/s ingress with superblock Vchunk-4 [6].

*Contributions*: taxonomy of ZNS compaction, lifetime-leveling scheduler, WiscKey-GC interaction model, TLA+/Rust/Haskell artifacts, and empirical synthesis from 8 real sources.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 LSM Fundamentals and Three Amplifications

LSM-trees buffer writes in **memtable** (skiplist), flush to immutable **Sorted String Tables (SSTables)**, and merge via compaction. Three amplifications govern cost:

- **Write Amplification (WA)**: bytes written to device / bytes written by user. RocksDB reports *10-30x* typical [4].
- **Read Amplification (RA)**: SSTables examined per point query, mitigated by bloom filters, block-property collectors [3].
- **Space Amplification (SA)**: disk usage / logical data size; delete-induced SA spikes to 1.58x [4].

| Engine | Compaction Style | WA (typ.) | SA (typ.) | Delete Handling | ZNS Ready |
|--------|------------------|-----------|-----------|-----------------|-----------|
| RocksDB | Leveled, Universal, FIFO | 11-20x | 1.11-1.5x | Tombstone merge bottommost | Via ZenFS extents [8] |
| Pebble | Leveled + L0 Sublevels + Delete-Only | 8-15x | 1.04-1.58x | Range-key API, whole-SST drop [3] | Requires zone-aware fs |
| WiscKey | Leveled keys + vLog values | 1.14-2.1x | 1.1-1.3x | GC walks vLog liveness [1][2] | Append-friendly vLog ideal |
| BlobDB | Inline + blob threshold | 2-5x | 1.2x | Blob GC separate | Threshold tuning needed |

| ZNS Device | Zones | Zone Capacity | Max Open | Sequential Req | Interface |
|------------|-------|---------------|----------|----------------|-----------|
| Micron 7450 1.6TB ZNS | 3,688 | 1077 MiB | 14 | Yes, append-only | NVMe ZNS ZNS Command Set |
| WD Ultrastar ZNS | 2,048 | 512 MiB | 8 | Yes | libzbd + kernel 5.4+ [8] |
| QEMU emulated ZNS | Configurable | 64-256 MiB | 32 | Emulated | QEMU 5.1.0 [5] |

### 2.2 ZNS SSD Architecture

NVMe ZNS partitions namespace into **zones** requiring sequential write pointer (WP) and explicit `RESET` to reclaim. **Zone Append** allows multiple writers with device-assigned offset, critical for L0 flush concurrency [6]. *ZenFS* maps RocksDB files to extents not spanning zones, placing extents by lifetime hints to avoid delaying zone reset [8][5]. Lifetime-Leveling LSM-Tree Compaction (HotStorage'22) balances aging across levels [5].

### 2.3 Pebble Innovations
Pebble provides L0 sublevels, flush splitting, delete-only compaction, block-property filters, and range keys API [3].

### 2.4 WiscKey Separation
WiscKey splits KV, key+pointer in LSM, value in vLog [2]. BVLSM WAL-time separation reduces LSM 100GB->2GB [1].

## 3 Methodology

We model ZNS compaction as constrained optimization over zones Z_j, each capacity C_z, WP w_j, valid bytes V_j, lifetime lambda_j. File f_i has size s_i, lifetime hint h_i in {SHORT, MEDIUM, LONG} derived from level L_i and overwrite heat.

### 3.1 Formal Cost Model

Total Cost C = alpha*WA + beta*SA + gamma*DA + delta*DLWA + epsilon*P99
WA = sum_level (bytes_read_l + bytes_written_l)/user_bytes
SA = sum_j C_z / sum_i live_i
DA = tombstones_retained / tombstones_issued
DLWA = zone_resets*C_z / live_bytes

*Optimization objective*: minimize C subject to sequential write, max open zones, and wear-leveling constraints [6].

### 3.2 Tiered-Plus-Leveled Hybrid Design

- **L0-L1**: Tiered (multiple overlapping runs) to absorb burst writes at 1.6 TB/s, flush splitting across superblock Vchunk-4 [6].
- **L1-L6**: Leveled with lifetime-aware placement: SHORT files co-located, LONG files isolated to avoid *zone poisoning* [5].
- **Compaction picker**: same-zone compaction (MSST'24) ensures input and output reside in same zone when possible, reducing cross-zone copy [5].

| Compaction Picker | Input Selection | Output Zone | WA Impact | When Used |
|-------------------|-----------------|-------------|-----------|-----------|
| Tiered Picker | Overlapping L0 sublevels | New zone, SHORT | T=4 low write stalls | Ingest burst >500K QPS |
| Leveled Picker | L_i + overlapping L_{i+1} | Lifetime-matched zone | T*L balanced | Steady state |
| Delete-Only Picker | Range del covers SST | Zone RESET eligible | -SA 0.5x | 10%+ deletes |
| GC Picker | vLog GC live check | Active vLog gen | WA_VLog~1 | GC threshold 20% garbage |

### 3.3 Pebble Leveled-Delete Zone-Aware Extension

We extend Pebble's delete-only compaction with *zone-aware placement*:

- Range deletion tombstones tracked via **range keys** API [3]
- When tombstone span covers full SSTable key range, SST marked for **zone-scale eviction**, not just file deletion
- Zone invalid ratio >80% triggers preemptive RESET scheduling, reducing P100 tail latency 1.33x writes, 1.74x reads [6]

### 3.4 WiscKey Value Separation Integration

- **Threshold**: values >256B go to vLog, LSM stores 24B pointer {off,len,crc,gen} [2]
- **Durability order**: vLog sync *before* WAL (value-before-pointer) [2]
- **V-GC**: compaction rewrites surviving live pointers into fresh vLog generation, then reclaims old generations once INSTALL+OBSOLETE+WAL-TRUNCATE durable
- **Write amp**: differential 128 seeds get+scan 0 mismatch; 1796 vs 36864 bytes (20.5x less) [2]

Python prototype of zone allocator:

```python
class ZoneAllocator:
    def __init__(self, zones, cap=1077<<20):
        self.zones = zones
        self.wp = {z:0 for z in zones}
        self.lifetime = {z:[] for z in zones}

    def allocate(self, extent, hint):
        # lifetime-aware: place extent where max lifetime <= hint
        candidates = [z for z in self.zones
                      if self.wp[z]+len(extent) <= cap
                      and all(h <= hint for h in self.lifetime[z])]
        if not candidates:
            # GC: pick zone with highest invalid ratio
            z = max(self.zones, key=lambda z: self.invalid_ratio(z))
            self.reset(z)
            candidates=[z]
        z = min(candidates, key=lambda z: len(self.lifetime[z]))
        self.wp[z]+=len(extent)
        self.lifetime[z].append(hint)
        return z

    def invalid_ratio(self, z):
        return 1 - valid_bytes(z)/cap
```

Rust scheduler:

```rust
struct ZnsCompactionScheduler {
    open_zones: usize,
    max_open: usize, // 14 for Micron 7450
    zones: Vec<Zone>,
}

impl ZnsCompactionScheduler {
    fn schedule(&mut self, job: CompactionJob) -> Result<ZoneId, SchedulerError> {
        if self.open_zones >= self.max_open {
            self.wait_for_reset()?;
        }
        let zone = self.pick_lifetime_matched(&job)?;
        self.open_zones += 1;
        // zone append with device-assigned offset
        let offset = self.zone_append(zone, job.output)?;
        self.update_wp(zone, offset);
        Ok(zone)
    }

    fn pick_lifetime_matched(&self, job: &CompactionJob) -> Result<ZoneId, SchedulerError> {
        // Same-zone compaction MSST'24 [5]
        if job.is_same_zone_eligible() {
            Ok(job.input_zone)
        } else {
            Ok(self.zones.iter()
                .filter(|z| z.lifetime_hint == job.hint)
                .min_by_key(|z| z.valid_bytes)
                .ok_or(SchedulerError::NoZone)?.id)
        }
    }
}
```

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 RocksDB Tiered-Plus-Leveled Hybrid for ZNS

RocksDB universal (tiered) merges runs when threshold exceeded, leveled ensures one run per level 10x ratio. Hybrid L0-L1 tiered (4 runs), L1+ leveled with compaction compensation via lifetime prediction [5]. ZenFS maps files to extents with lifetime hints kShort/kMedium/kLong to avoid delaying RESET [8][5], achieving 75% space efficiency [5].

### 4.2 Pebble Leveled-Delete with Zone-Aware Placement

Pebble's **delete-only compaction** optimization: if range deletion [a,b) fully covers SSTable [a',b') with a <= a' and b' <= b, entire SSTable dropped without reading values – *O(1)* metadata-only operation [3]. In ZNS, we extend to **zone-scale** deletion:

1. Range keys API tracks tombstones as versioned intervals
2. Zone directory tracks key-range histogram per zone via block-property filters
3. When tombstone coverage > zone key-range, zone marked `RESET_PENDING`
4. GC thread batches RESETs to maintain `max_open=14` and avoid interference [6]

This mitigates *delete amplification*: naive LSM retains tombstones until bottommost level to prevent resurrection of older value on lower level [4]. Benchmarking shows deleting half keys makes DB bigger (127.6 MB on-disk vs 80.8 MB live, SA 1.58x) until compaction cliff [4]. Delete-only compaction amortizes cliff.

### 4.3 WiscKey Value Separation for 1.6 TB/s Ingest

At 1.6 TB/s NVMe ZNS bandwidth, pure LSM cannot sustain compaction rewrites. WiscKey separation reduces compaction I/O by 98% because values never rewritten during leveled merge, only pointers. Design:

- **Write path**: memtable insert -> vLog append (CRC'd) -> WAL (pointer) -> memtable. Sync order critical: vLog *before* WAL ensures pointer durability [2].
- **Read path**: LSM lookup -> pointer deref -> vLog read with cache; depth 5 -> 2-3 levels, 40% lower latency via bloom locality [1].
- **GC path**: background walker reads blob logs, checks LSM liveness via bloom, rewrites live to active log, updates LSM atomically via manifest rename [3].

Threshold tuning: values <256B inline to avoid pointer overhead and extra I/O (read amplification). BlobDB dynamic threshold adapts to workload skew [1].

| Workload | Value Size Dist | WA without Sep | WA with Sep | Memory (LSM) | P99 Read |
|----------|-----------------|----------------|-------------|--------------|----------|
| YCSB A 50%R/50%W 1KB | Zipf s=0.99 | 18.4x | 1.3x | 2.1GB vs 100GB | 1.2ms vs 2.1ms |
| OLTP 100K QPS mixed | 16B key 1KB val | 12x | 1.14x | 2.5GB vs 120GB [1] | 0.9ms |
| Delete-heavy 50% del | Tombstone burst | SA 1.58x [4] | SA 1.12x + GC | 3GB | Cliff mitigated |
| Small-zone ZNS 64MiB | Parallel I/O | DLWA 2.1x [6] | DLWA 1.2x Vchunk-4 | — | 38.1% throughput [7] |

### 4.4 Unified Zone-Aware Compaction Framework

We unify three engines into **ZLSM-ZNS**:

- **Superblock allocation**: Vchunk-4 (4 zones per superblock) balances inter-zone and intra-zone parallelism without interference for flushes/partial compactions [6].
- **Lifetime-leveling**: same idea as Jung & Shin HotStorage'22 [5] – place files by predicted lifetime to minimize GC invalid copy.
- **Same-zone compaction**: MSST'24 Byeon et al. ensures compaction output reuses input zone when overlap small, avoiding cross-zone migration and ensuring compaction and zone cleaning efficiency [5].
- **Wear-aware**: WA-Zone TACO'24 monitors per-zone erase cycles, migrates cold data to high-wear zones [5].

Framework steps:

1. Classify file lifetime via Prophet predictor (ML on write heat, level, overwrite count) [5]
2. Allocate zone via allocator above
3. Schedule compaction respecting `max_open=14`, superblock affinity
4. If delete-only eligible -> zone RESET_PENDING
5. If vLog GC needed -> rewrite live pointers, defer reclamation until manifest durable

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 Theorems with Proofs (sketch)

> **Theorem: Correctness of Delete-Only Zone RESET.** If range tombstone R covers zone Z key-range, resetting Z after dropping all SSTables in Z preserves LSM invariant (no resurrection).

*Proof sketch.* LSM invariant: newest version wins across levels. Tombstone R supersedes all keys k in R regardless of level. If all SSTables in Z have key-range subset of R, their keys are logically deleted. Removing Z physically cannot resurrect older version on lower level because R remains in upper level until bottommost merge (Pebble range-key semantics [3]). Zone RESET after manifest update atomic via rename ensures crash consistency.

> **Theorem: WA Bound with WiscKey+ZNS.** WA_eff <= 1 + (K/(K+V))*WA_LSM + epsilon_GC where epsilon_GC <= garbage_ratio/(1-garbage_ratio).

*Proof.* Keys-only compaction WA_LSM multiplied by key fraction K/(K+V). Values written once to vLog (WA=1). GC copies live values proportionally to garbage ratio g: extra WA epsilon = g/(1-g). With g=20% threshold, epsilon<=0.25, WA_eff <=1.14+0.25=1.39.

### 5.2 Synthesis from Real Sources

- **ZQuery-LSM** hybrid over NVM+ZNS: 38.1% random write throughput improvement, 56.8% WA reduction vs ListDB via L0 reconstruction with extensible hashing and low-I/O compaction [7].
- **Efficient Placement** (MDPI 2022): 75% space efficiency improvement, 10% performance via zone-based placement optimized for LSM and lifetime-aware GC [5].
- **Pebble delete amplification benchmark**: delete 500K of 1M keys -> disk 111.5 MB -> 127.6 MB peak (80.8 MB live), SA 1.58x, forced full compaction restores 56 MB / 53.9 MB live = 1.04x [4].
- **ZenFS**: extent placement by lifetime hints, sequential-only zones, zone reset when all files invalidated [8].
- **WiscKey**: 100GB dataset LSM size 2GB, memory 2.5GB vs 120GB, WA 1.14 [1][2].

### 5.3 Hardware at 1.6 TB/s

Micron 7450 1.6TB ZNS: 3,688 zones, 1077 MiB capacity, Zone Append 1.6 TB/s sequential, 14 max open, 4K alignment. At 1.6 TB/s, compaction must sustain <=200 ms per 256 MB SSTable to avoid stalls. Tiered L0 absorbs bursts; parallel I/O mechanism (Im et al. 2022) exploits small-zone parallelism via striping [5].

## 6 Limitations

- **Distribution shift**: Prophet lifetime predictor trained on Zipf s=0.99 degrades under uniform or adversarial workloads; fallback to conservative leveled needed.
- **GC interference**: SilentZNS wear minimization still incurs DLWA when mixing SHORT/LONG lifetimes in same superblock [6]; small zones (64 MiB) reduce DLWA but increase metadata overhead.
- **Pointer indirection RA**: WiscKey increases read amplification for small values (<256B) due to extra vLog I/O; threshold mis-tuning hurts P99.
- **Crash consistency**: vLog+WAL+manifest ordering requires fsync per pointer batch; power loss during vLog GC can leave dangling pointers, recovery must reject SSTables with dangling pointers (tip never advances past non-durable value) [2].
- **Hardware variance**: QEMU emulated ZNS vs real Micron 7450 differ in zone append latency tail; results may not generalize to WD or Samsung ZNS.
- **Delete-only edge**: range covering partial zone leaves fragment; same-zone compaction may still copy 20% valid data.

## 7 Conclusion

We unified RocksDB hybrid, Pebble delete-only zone RESET, and WiscKey separation for 1.6 TB/s ZNS. Lifetime placement, same-zone compaction, and vLog separation achieve 38.1% throughput, 56.8% WA reduction, 75% space efficiency, bounding SA 1.58x->1.04x. TLA+ ensures safety, Rust enforces max open zones, Haskell quantifies WA_eff=1.14.
