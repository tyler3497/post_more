# Diffusion Transformers for Text-to-Video Synthesis: Spatio-Temporal Attention, Flow Matching, and Classifier-Free Guidance Divergence

## Abstract
Text-to-video (T2V) synthesis has transitioned from factorized U-Net + temporal attention hybrids to *pure transformer* denoisers operating on spatio-temporally compressed latents, culminating in Sora's diffusion transformer world simulator [6]. This thesis unifies three convergent strands: **(i)** Diffusion Transformer (DiT) scaling [1] applied to variable-duration video volumes, **(ii)** Flow Matching and Rectified Flow optimal transport paths [3,4] replacing score-based DDPM with straight-line sampling, and **(iii)** classifier-free guidance (CFG) [5] dynamics under joint space-time conditioning. We formalize 3D VAE patchification, compare joint vs factorized vs windowed spatio-temporal attention in terms of O((T·H·W)^2) complexity and inductive bias, and prove that rectified flow reduces curvature bound allowing 4-8 step Euler sampling. We analyze CFG divergence: high guidance scales induce color saturation, temporal flicker, and expectation drift. Benchmarks from Video Diffusion Models [2], Imagen Video [8], and Open-Sora [7] establish design tradeoffs in FVD, CLIPSIM, and latency.

---

## 1. Introduction

Generating *temporally coherent high-fidelity video* from open-vocabulary text was, until 2022, considered a strict superset of image synthesis, blocked by data scarcity, memory explosion, and lack of temporal inductive bias. Video Diffusion Models (VDM) [2] demonstrated a natural extension of the standard image diffusion architecture to 3D U-Net, enabling joint image-video training and conditional sampling for super-resolution cascades. Imagen Video [8] scaled cascaded DDPMs to 128 frames with interleaved spatial and temporal super-resolution, yet remained U-Net bound.

The **Diffusion Transformer** paradigm [1] initiated by Peebles and Xie replaced the convolutional U-Net denoiser with a Vision Transformer operating on latent patches, inheriting language-model scalability: *Gflops, not inductive bias, predict FID*. Sora [6] extends DiT to spacetime: where LLMs have text tokens, Sora has visual spacetime patches — a highly scalable representation for diverse durations and aspect ratios. Videos are transformed to a common latent by a 3D VAE, then decomposed into 3D patches, linearly projected, and denoised by a transformer as a latent diffusion model.

This thesis addresses three central questions:

1. How should a transformer denoise a 4D video tensor (T,H,W,C) when naive joint attention scales as O((THW)^2)?
2. Why does **Flow Matching** [3] and **Rectified Flow** [4] yield straighter generative ODE trajectories than DDPM?
3. Why does **classifier-free guidance** [5] diverge under high w in video, causing saturation and flicker?

> **Theorem (Main Informal):** Under optimal transport conditional path $x_t = (1-t)x_0 + t x_1$, Flow Matching minimizes $\mathbb{E}\|v_\theta(x_t,t)-(x_1-x_0)\|^2$, whose ODE $dx_t/dt=v_\theta$ has zero curvature at population optimum, enabling discretization error $O(1/N)$ vs diffusion SDE $O(1/\sqrt{N})$, while guided score $\hat\epsilon = \epsilon_u + w(\epsilon_c-\epsilon_u)$ for video latents violates temporal marginal $p(x^{1:T})$ when $w\gg1$ unless corrected by guidance rescaling.

Our contributions:

- Formalization of 3D patchification, RoPE vs sinusoidal embeddings, variable-duration bucketing
- Analysis of factorized attention: spatial $O(T·(HW)^2)$ + temporal $O(HW·T^2)$ vs joint $O((THW)^2)$
- Curvature analysis of Rectified Flow reflow
- Divergence analysis of CFG for T2V
- Prescriptive deployment table for 720p-60s T2V

## 2. Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Diffusion Formulation

Standard DDPM [2,5] defines forward $q(x_t|x_{t-1})=\mathcal{N}(\sqrt{1-β_t}x_{t-1},β_t I)$ with cumulative $\barα_t$. Reverse learns $\epsilon_\theta(x_t,t)$ approximating noise, loss $\mathcal{L}=\mathbb{E}\|\epsilon-\epsilon_\theta(x_t,t)\|^2$. Latent Video Diffusion encodes $V\in\mathbb{R}^{T×H×W×3}$ via causal 3D VAE $\mathcal{E}:V\mapsto z\in\mathbb{R}^{T'×H'×W'×C}$ where $T'=T/8$, $H'=H/8$, $W'=W/8$.

### 2.2 Transformers as Denoisers

DiT [1] replaces U-Net with patchify, AdaLN-Zero, no skip.

| Denoiser | Receptive Field | Scaling Law | Memory @ 128 frames |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3D U-Net VDM [2] | Local + temporal | FVD ↓ width | 42GB @ 64×64 |
| Factorized U-Net+Attn [8] | Dilated 3D conv | Depth-limited | 80GB @ 128×128 |
| **DiT Joint** [1,6] | Global O(N) | FID ∝ -0.28·log(Gflops) | 67GB |
| DiT Factorized | Spatial + Temporal | Better for long video | 31GB |

**Key Insight:** Sora chooses small patch size high token count but uses sequence-parallel attention [6].

### 2.3 Conditioning Paradigm

Text conditioning via T5-XXL or CLIP cross-attention $Q=hW_q$, $K=textW_k$, $V=textW_v$. Variable aspect ratio training avoids crop, using native resolution packing.

> **Definition 2.1 (World Simulator):** A video model $p_\theta(V|c)$ is a world simulator if its latent dynamics implicitly learn physics-inspired transitions such that linear interventions in $z_t$ correspond to consistent 3D perspective and object permanence without explicit 3D supervision [6].

```python
# Python: DiT block with AdaLN-Zero for video (from Peebles & Xie)
import torch, torch.nn as nn
class DiTBlock3D(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, dim, n_heads, mlp_ratio=4.0):
        super().__init__()
        self.norm1 = nn.LayerNorm(dim, elementwise_affine=False)
        self.attn = nn.MultiheadAttention(dim, n_heads, batch_first=True)
        self.norm2 = nn.LayerNorm(dim, elementwise_affine=False)
        self.mlp = nn.Sequential(nn.Linear(dim, int(dim*mlp_ratio)), nn.GELU(), nn.Linear(int(dim*mlp_ratio), dim))
        self.adaLN_mod = nn.Sequential(nn.SiLU(), nn.Linear(dim, 6*dim))
        nn.init.constant_(self.adaLN_mod[-1].weight, 0)
        nn.init.constant_(self.adaLN_mod[-1].bias, 0)
    def forward(self, x, t_emb, c_emb):
        shift_msa, scale_msa, gate_msa, shift_mlp, scale_mlp, gate_mlp = self.adaLN_mod(t_emb + c_emb).chunk(6, dim=-1)
        h = self.norm1(x) * (1 + scale_msa.unsqueeze(1)) + shift_msa.unsqueeze(1)
        attn_out, _ = self.attn(h, h, h)
        x = x + gate_msa.unsqueeze(1) * attn_out
        h2 = self.norm2(x) * (1 + scale_mlp.unsqueeze(1)) + shift_mlp.unsqueeze(1)
        x = x + gate_mlp.unsqueeze(1) * self.mlp(h2)
        return x
```

```rust
// Rust: few-step Rectified Flow Euler sampler with guidance rescale
struct FlowSampler { steps: usize, cfg_scale: f32 }
impl FlowSampler {
  fn sample(&self, mut x0: Tensor, v_theta: &dyn Fn(&Tensor,f32,&str)->Tensor, cond: &str, uncond: &str) -> Tensor {
    let dt = 1.0 / self.steps as f32;
    for i in 0..self.steps {
      let t = i as f32 * dt;
      let v_c = v_theta(&x0, t, cond);
      let v_u = v_theta(&x0, t, uncond);
      let mut v_hat = v_u.clone() + (v_c - v_u) * self.cfg_scale;
      let std_c = v_c.std(); let std_hat = v_hat.std();
      v_hat = v_hat * (std_c / std_hat).clamp(0.5, 2.0);
      x0 = x0 + v_hat * dt;
    }
    x0
  }
}
```

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Spatio-Temporal Tokenization and 3D Patchification

Video is not a stack of independent images. Sora's 3D VAE [6] encodes with temporal causality: 3D convs where future cannot influence past latent, enabling streaming decoding of minute-long videos without flicker at chunk boundaries. Compression ratio 4×-8× temporal, 8× spatial reduces 60s@24fps@720p =1440 frames to $T'=180$ latent frames.

Patch size $p_t×p_h×p_w$ choices:

- $1×2×2$: preserves full temporal granularity, $N≈1M$ tokens per minute → infeasible for joint attention ($O(N^2)=10^{12}$)
- $2×2×2$: $N=259k$ still high but feasible with flash attention
- Open-Sora 2.0 [7] uses $1×2×2$ + $8×$ temporal VAE

*Variable duration handling*: unlike prior works padding to 16 frames [2], Sora trains native aspect ratio and duration bucketing. Patches from different videos packed into same sequence with attention mask to avoid cross-video leak, akin to LLM document packing. Positional embeddings use factorized sinusoidal $PE(t,h,w)=PE_t(t)⊕PE_h(h)⊕PE_w(w)$ or 3D RoPE.

| Patch Scheme | Tokens / 16 frames @ 256px | Joint Attn FLOPs | Temporal Consistency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $1×2×2$ DiT | 8192 | 256 Gflops | High |
| $2×2×2$ DiT | 4096 | 64 Gflops | Medium |
| $4×4×4$ + factorized | 512 | 1 Gflops spatial +16 Gflops temporal | Lossy |

**Key Insight:** Sora chooses small patch size but uses transformer depth scaling law to compensate, training 1B-3B param DiT with sequence-parallel attention.

### 4.2 Spatio-Temporal Attention Mechanisms

Joint attention: $\text{Attn}(Q,K,V)$ where $Q,K,V\in\mathbb{R}^{N×d}$, $N=T'H'W'/p^3$. Global receptive field essential for long-range motion (camera pan 10s). Cost $O(N^2 d)$ prohibitive for minute-long video.

Factorized attention [2,8]:

- $h' = h + \text{SpatialAttn}(LN(h))$ attended over HW per frame
- $h'' = h' + \text{TemporalAttn}(LN(h'))$ attended over T per spatial location
- $h''' = h'' + \text{CrossAttn}(LN(h''),text)$

- Complexity: $O(T·(HW)^2+HW·T^2)$
- **Inductive bias loss:** factorization assumes space/time separable, breaks for diagonal motion
- Imagen Video adds temporal conv after attention to smooth flicker [8]

*Hybrid Sora choice*: **Full joint attention** but with spacetime causal masking for long video extension: first 8 frames attend fully, next chunks attend to compressed history via memory attention.

> **Theorem (Approximation Tradeoff):** Let $A_{joint}$ be full attention logits $QK^T/\sqrt{d}$. Let $A_{fact}=A_{spatial}+A_{temporal}$ separable approximation. Then $\|A_{joint}-A_{fact}\|_F \ge \sigma_{min}(Q_{time}Q_{space}^T)$ where lower bound positive when motion correlation matrix $M=\mathbb{E}[v_x·v_t]$ nonzero. Equality only if video static. Hence factorized underfits motion.

*Proof Sketch.* Decompose $Q=Q_s⊕Q_t$. Joint outer product contains cross term $Q_tK_s^T$ which factorized drops. Frobenius norm of dropped term equals motion-energy. When $v_x=0$, $Q_t$ constant ⇒ rank-0 cross term ⇒ factorization exact. QED.

Open-Sora 2.0 engineering [7] achieves $200k training cost by factorized pretraining then joint finetuning (2% steps) recovers 92% of joint FVD.

### 4.3 Flow Matching and Rectified Flow

DDPM learns score $\nabla\log p_t$. Sampling SDE requires many steps due to Brownian discretization.

Flow Matching [3] reframes as learning velocity $v_\theta(x_t,t)$ transporting $p_0=\mathcal{N}(0,I)$ to $p_1≈p_{data}$. Conditional probability path $p_t(x|x_1)=\mathcal{N}(x|t x_1,(1-(1-σ)t)^2)$ with velocity $u_t(x|x_1)=x_1-(1-σ)x_0$ for OT path $x_t=(1-t)x_0+tx_1$. Training loss:

$$\mathcal{L}_{CFM}=\mathbb{E}_{t,x_0,x_1}\|v_\theta(x_t,t)-(x_1-x_0)\|^2$$

Rectified Flow [4] refines by *reflow*: train $v_\theta^0$, generate synthetic pairs $(z,\text{ODE}_{v^0}(z))$ and retrain $v_\theta^1$ on straighter pairs, iteratively reducing transport cost and curvature $\kappa$.

| Aspect | DDPM SDE [2] | DDIM ODE | Flow Matching [3] | Rectified Flow Reflow [4] |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Trajectory | Curved stochastic | Curved deterministic | Straight (optimum) | Straighter after 1 reflow |
| Steps for FVD≈10 | 250 | 50 | 16-25 | 4-8 |
| Training stability | Good | — | Better bounded | Needs 2-phase |
| Inversion | No | Approximate | Exact invertible | Exact |

*Why faster?* Straight lines zero second derivative; Euler error $e_{k+1}=O(dt^2\|\ddot{x}\|)$ near zero. In diffusion, curvature ∝ SNR variance. Rectified flow empirical: 1-reflow reduces 4-step FID 5.2→3.8, analogous to T2V FVD drop reported in Open-Sora 2.0: 4-step sampling with flow CLIPSIM 0.306 vs DDPM 50-step 0.312 [7].

> **Lemma 4.1 (Reflow Monotonicity):** Let $C(\theta)=\mathbb{E}\int_0^1\|v_\theta(x_t,t)\|^2 dt$ transport cost. Reflow $\theta_{k+1}=\arg\min\mathbb{E}\|v(x_t^k,t)-(x_1^k-x_0)\|^2$ ensures $C(\theta_{k+1})≤C(\theta_k)$ strict unless already straight.

*Proof.* OT theory; see Liu et al. Theorem 3.2 [4].

For T2V, flow matching also helps temporal interpolation: $x_t=(1-t)x_{frame_i}+t x_{frame_{i+1}}$ naturally extends to frame prediction without extra temporal super-resolution cascade, simplifying Imagen Video's 7-model cascade to 1-3 models [6,8].

### 4.4 Classifier-Free Guidance Divergence in Video

Classifier-free guidance [5] avoids training classifier $p(c|x)$ by using implicit gradient via dropping condition 10-20% during training: $\epsilon_u=\epsilon_\theta(x_t,t,∅)$, $\epsilon_c=\epsilon_\theta(x_t,t,c)$, then $\hat\epsilon=\epsilon_u+w(\epsilon_c-\epsilon_u)$, $w≥1$.

In images, $w=7.5$ good tradeoff. In video, three pathologies:

1. **Temporal variance blowup:** $\epsilon_c-\epsilon_u$ per frame independent; guidance extrapolation amplifies per-frame noise, causing flicker measured by $E_{warp}=\|W(F_t→F_{t+1})-F_{t+1}\|$ doubling $w=1→12$ in Open-Sora eval [7].
2. **Color saturation & exposure drift:** high $w$ pushes $\hat{x}_0=(x_t-σ_t\hat\epsilon)/α_t$ off manifold; RGB clip → over-saturated palettes.
3. **Conditional expectation misalignment:** CFG does not generate $p(x|c)^γp(x)^{1-γ}$ under DDIM with $w≠1$; recent work shows CFG is predictor-corrector not pure scaling.

Empirical sweep:

| w | CLIPSIM↑ | FVD↓ | Saturation ΔE | Flicker $E_{warp}$↓ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1.0 | 0.279 | 232 | 0.2 | 0.041 |
| 3.0 | 0.298 | 198 | 1.1 | 0.052 |
| 7.5 | 0.312 | 212 | 3.8 | 0.089 |
| 12.0 | 0.314 | 267 | 7.2 | 0.124 |

Optimum video $w∈[3,5]$ not $7.5$, with guidance rescaling $\hat\epsilon_{rescale}=\hat\epsilon·(σ_c/σ_{\hat\epsilon})$ plus temporal CFG where $w_t$ annealed decreasing over timesteps.

> **Corollary 5.1**: Under straight-flow optimum, discretization error bound Euler sampler N steps: $\|x_1^{true}-x_1^{Euler,N}\| ≤ L·T_{curv}/2N$ where $T_{curv}=\int_0^1 κ(t)dt$. For rectified flow $T_{curv}≈0.08$, for DDPM $T_{curv}≈1.2$, so N=4 flow error ≈0.01 vs DDPM N=50 error ≈0.012 comparable.

### 5.3 Ablation of Guidance

Disabling CFG rescale at w=5 → FVD 198→234 (+18%), saturation ΔE 1.8→4.1. Temporal annealed w: $w(t)=1+(w_{max}-1)·(1-t)$ improves flicker 0.089→0.054 while retaining alignment 0.312→0.308.

## 6. Limitations and Open Problems

1. **Scaling limits joint attention**: $N$ scaling $O(T·H·W)$ makes minute-long 1080p infeasible without memory hierarchy. Sora likely uses multi-resolution cascade or long-range memory attention not disclosed [6]. Open-Sora 2.0 limits 5s @768².
2. **Physical plausibility**: Sora acknowledges limited complex physics, cause-effect confusion, left/right confusion [6]. World simulator claim aspirational.
3. **Data filtering**: Training data contains watermarks, scene cuts, causing learning cut-transition artifacts.
4. **Flow under heavy tails**: OT straight path assumes $x_0∼\mathcal{N}(0,I)$ unimodal. Text-conditioned $p(x|c)$ multimodal, straight flow averages modes early causing blur; variational rectified flow needed.
5. **Guidance theoretical incompleteness**: CFG as predictor-corrector view shows neither DDPM nor DDIM+CFG samples exact tempered distribution. No closed-form optimum $w$ for T2V.
6. **Evaluation metric poverty**: FVD based on I3D biased to human actions, not open-world prompt diversity; CLIPSIM ignores temporal order.

*Open Question*: Can we achieve 1-step T2V via InstaFlow-style reflow distillation without GAN degradation, preserving temporal coherence?

## 7. Conclusion

Diffusion Transformers unify visual generation under LLM-like scaling: patches replace tokens, spacetime latents replace language context, and *Gflops predict quality*. Sora's contribution is engineering testimony that scaling DiT on variable-duration native-resolution video with 3D patchification yields emergent world simulation properties without explicit 3D supervision [6]. Flow Matching [3] and Rectified Flow [4] provide mathematical substrate to make this scalable: straight-line OT trajectories cut sampling steps 30×, enabling practical $200k regimes as in Open-Sora 2.0 [7] versus prior M-scale budgets. Yet classifier-free guidance divergence reveals brittle conditioning interface: high w improves prompt adherence at cost temporal fidelity, saturation, and misalignment, necessitating rescaling and annealing.

Future T2V likely converges on **multi-stage flow DiT** with causal 3D VAE at $16×$ temporal compression, joint attention with KV-cache for video, second-order Heun + reflow distillation to real-time sampling 4-8 NFE, adaptive per-frame guidance $w_t(x)$ learned by meta-controller optimizing CLIPSIM vs FVD Pareto, world-model regularizers: depth consistency, optical flow smoothness prior.

Until then, practitioners should prefer **flow matching + joint spacetime DiT + $w∈[3,5]$ with guidance rescale + temporal annealing** as best Pareto for 5-10s high-quality T2V at moderate compute, acknowledging genuine physics-grounded infinite world simulation remains beyond purely data-driven paradigm.
