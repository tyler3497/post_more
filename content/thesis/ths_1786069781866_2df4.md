# Neural Implicit Surfaces and Radiance Fields: NeRF Volume Rendering Integral Quadrature, Signed Distance Eikonal Regularization, NeuS Density Conversion, and 3D Gaussian Splatting Spherical Harmonics

## Abstract
We present a unified PhD-level synthesis of neural scene representations spanning volumetric radiance fields, implicit signed distance surfaces, and explicit Gaussian primitives. Neural Radiance Fields [1] formulated novel view synthesis as optimizing a 5D MLP $(x,d)\rightarrow(c,\sigma)$ supervised only by 2D images via differentiable volume rendering integral discretization; however, density is under-constrained leading to floating geometry. Signed distance functions with **Eikonal regularization** [4] enforce $||\nabla f||=1$ almost everywhere, yielding valid SDFs and watertight zero-level sets, yet require unbiased conversion from SDF to opacity for volume rendering training. NeuS [2] introduces an S-density $\phi_s$ logistic derivation proving first-order bias-free surface reconstruction and bridging VolSDF [6] exponential conversion. Instant-NGP [5] accelerates both via multi-resolution hash encoding $O(1)$ lookup enabling seconds-scale training. 3D Gaussian Splatting [3] then *abandons* MLPs, representing scenes as anisotropic ellipsoids optimized by tile-based differentiable rasterization with spherical harmonics view dependence, achieving $\ge 135$ FPS at 1080p. This thesis formalizes the volume integral, its quadrature alpha, NeuS weighting, Eikonal PDE theory, and Gaussian EWA projection $\Sigma'=J W \Sigma W^T J^T$ with proofs and empirical trade-offs.

## 1. Introduction

The transition from explicit meshes to *implicit neural fields* marks a methodological rupture in 3D vision. Classical multi-view stereo required depth maps, Poisson surface reconstruction, and texture baking – a pipeline where errors compound. *NeRF* [1] in ECCV 2020 proposed something radical: represent the entire scene as weights of a multilayer perceptron, and supervise only with RGB reprojection error.

> **Core Insight:** *If volume rendering is differentiable, 3D geometry can emerge from 2D photometric loss alone.*

However, NeRF's $\sigma$-field is **not a surface**: it is a fuzzy density lacking level-set semantics, making mesh extraction via marching cubes threshold-dependent and noisy. SDF-based methods [4][6][2] answer by re-parameterizing $f(x): \mathbb{R}^3\rightarrow\mathbb{R}$ such that $S= \{ x | f(x)=0\}$ is the surface, with $f<0$ interior. To train this SDF with the same robust volume rendering machinery, we must answer:

1. How to convert $f$ to $\sigma$ or $\alpha$ so that rendering weights peak at $f=0$ and integrate to 1 occlusion?
2. What regularization ensures $f$ remains a valid signed distance and does not collapse to degenerate solutions $f\equiv0$?

*NeuS's answer* [2] – the *S-density* $\phi_s(f(x)) = s e^{-s f(x)}/(1+e^{-s f(x)})^2$ – yields provably unbiased weight in first-order approximation, unlike naive $\sigma = ReLU(-f)$. Combined with Eikonal loss $\mathbb{E}_x (||\nabla f||-1)^2$ from IGR [4], it achieves state-of-the-art DTU Chamfer $<0.35$ mm without masks.

In parallel, *explicit* primitives re-emerged. 3D Gaussian Splatting [3] argues MLPs are bottleneck: rasterizing 3 million Gaussians sorted via GPU radix sort yields real-time 4K rendering, training in 30-50 minutes versus NeRF's hours, and edited easily unlike implicit. Yet both share the same **emission-absorption** image formation model derived from Kajiya [1 citation classical].

This thesis's contributions:

- Formal derivation of NeRF quadrature $C(r)=\sum T_i \alpha_i c_i$ from continuous $C(r)=\int T(t)\sigma(r(t)) c(r(t),d) dt$
- Proof of NeuS unbiased property and comparison to VolSDF [6] Laplace conversion
- PDE analysis of Eikonal equation $|\nabla f|=1$ viscosity solution and its discrete expectation
- Complete Gaussian parametrization $\Sigma=R S S^T R^T$, EWA projection, SH evaluation $k=3$ band $16$ coefficients
- *Code-level* Python implementations verifying theory

## 2. Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Volume Rendering Integral

NeRF [1] inherits from classical volume rendering (Max 1995, Kajiya & Von Herzen 1984). For ray $r(t)=o+t d$, $t\in[t_n,t_f]$:

$$ C(r)=\int_{t_n}^{t_f} T(t) \sigma(r(t)) c(r(t),d) dt, \quad T(t) = \exp(-\int_{t_n}^t \sigma(r(s)) ds) $$

where $T(t)$ is accumulated transmittance, *probability ray hasn't terminated before $t$*. Discretized with $N$ samples $t_i$ sorted, $\delta_i = t_{i+1}-t_i$:

$$ \hat{C}(r) = \sum_{i=1}^N T_i (1-\exp(-\sigma_i \delta_i)) c_i, \quad T_i = \exp(-\sum_{j< i}\sigma_j \delta_j) $$

This is *alpha compositing*: $\alpha_i = 1-\exp(-\sigma_i\delta_i)$ is opacity of segment $i$.

Positional encoding $\gamma(p) = (\sin(2^0\pi p), \cos(2^0\pi p), ..., \sin(2^{L-1}\pi p), \cos(2^{L-1}\pi p))$ with $L=10$ for $x$, $L=4$ for $d$ mitigates spectral bias of MLPs (SIREN [8] alternative uses $\sin(\omega_0 Wx+b)$).

### 2.2 Signed Distance Functions and Level Sets

A function $f:\mathbb{R}^3\rightarrow\mathbb{R}$ is SDF if:

- $f(x)=0$ on surface
- $|f(x)|= \min_{y\in S}||x-y||$
- $||\nabla f||=1$ a.e. (Eikonal equation)

SDF implies zero divergence of gradient flow outside medial axis. Unlike occupancy $o\in\{0,1\}$, SDF provides gradient direction for sphere tracing and distance-aware regularization.

IGR [4] proved that over-param MLPs with $(||\nabla f||-1)^2$ converge to SDF of plane for linear case, avoiding bad critical points $f\equiv0$.

### 2.3 Gaussians as Radiance Primitives

3DGS [3] defines scene as set $\{ (\mu_i, \Sigma_i, \alpha_i, c_i^{SH}) \}_{i=1}^M$ where:

$$ G(x)=\exp(-\frac12 (x-\mu)^T \Sigma^{-1}(x-\mu)) $$

For optimization stability, covariance factorized:

$$\Sigma = R S S^T R^T$$

where $S=diag(s)$, $s\in\mathbb{R}^3_{+}$, $R$ from quaternion $q\in\mathbb{R}^4$, $||q||=1$. This ensures positive semi-definite without explicit constraint.

Rendering evaluates EWA (Zwicker 2001) projection to 2D, then sorts Gaussians front-to-back per $16\times16$ tile.

## 3. Methodology / Formalism

### Volume to Surface Bridge

We define ray SDF $f(t)=f(r(t))$. Sphere tracing would find zero-crossing, but volume approach creates *weight* $w(t)=T(t)\rho(t)$ with $\rho$ opaque density. Requirement: $w(t)$ must be **unimodal** and maximal at $t^*$, $f(t^*)=0$, and normalize $\int w=1$ if ray hits surface.

NeuS [2] formalizes bias as distance $t^* - \arg\max w(t)$. Naive $\sigma= \text{ReLU}(-f)$ yields bias dependent on discretization.

Our formalism: let $\Phi_s(x) = (1+e^{-s x})^{-1}$ sigmoid with learnable sharpness $s$ increasing during training (inverse of temperature). Define *S-density*:

$$ \phi_s(f) = s e^{-s f} / (1+e^{-s f})^2 = \Phi_s'(f) $$

Note derivative of logistic – this is **logistic distribution** PDF, peak at $f=0$, variance $\propto 1/s^2$. As $s\rightarrow\infty$, $\phi_s\rightarrow\delta(f)$ Dirac concentrated on surface.

Define opaque density:

$$ \rho(t)=\max\left(\frac{-\frac{d}{dt}\Phi_s(f(t))}{\Phi_s(f(t))},0\right) $$

Then $T(t)=\Phi_s(f(t_{n}))/\Phi_s(f(t))$ telescoping, leading to analytic $T\rho = -\Phi_s'(f(t)) f'(t)/\Phi_s(whatever)$ weight that is exactly occlusion-aware and simplifies for discrete quadrature.

> **Theorem 1 (NeuS Unbiasedness):** For planar surface $f(t)= - \cos(\theta) (t-t^*)$ locally linear approaching zero-crossing $t^*$, the weight $w(t)=T(t)\rho(t)$ attains maximum at $t=t^*$ irrespective of sampling $\delta$, with first-order $O(\delta)$ error zero. VolSDF Laplace conversion has bias $O(1/s)$ but NeuS eliminates it via derived $\rho$.

*Proof sketch:* Take derivative of $w$, set $=0$, using $s$-sigmoid log derivative leads to condition $f''(t)=0$, true for linear. Full proof in [2] App A. $\blacksquare$

### Eikonal Variational Principle

We minimize:

$$ \mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{rgb} + \lambda_{eik}\mathcal{L}_{eik} + \lambda_{mask}\mathcal{L}_{mask}$$

where

$$ \mathcal{L}_{eik}=\mathbb{E}_{x\sim \mathcal{U}\cup \mathcal{N}(S)} (||\nabla f_\theta(x)||_2-1)^2 $$

Sampling combines uniform volume $\mathcal{U}$ and near-surface Gaussian $\mathcal{N}$ from current SDF zero set via perturbation. This is collocation of PDE $|\nabla f|=1$.

Integration by parts insight: Eikonal is Hamilton-Jacobi PDE $H(x,\nabla f)=0$, viscosity solution unique when Dirichlet $f=0$ on $S$ given. DeepSDF [Park 2019] without it produced non-metric $f$ where $|\nabla f|\approx0$ interior causing slow sphere tracing.

Our method samples 1024 rays/batch, 64 + 64 coarse+fine samples along ray as NeRF hierarchical but importance biased toward surface via $w(t)$ distribution – reuses NeRF two-stage but replaces density proposal with SDF-derived weight.

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 NeRF MLP Ray Marching and Alpha Compositing

NeRF MLP architecture in [1] is 8 layers $\times$ 256 width ReLU, with skip connection at layer 4 input $x$ concatenation, outputting density $\sigma$ + 256 feature, then concatenated with encoded view direction $d$ and decoded to RGB via 128-width head. This *bottleneck* enforces view-dependence only via $d$, ensuring multi-view consistency for $\sigma$.

**Hierarchical sampling:**

- Coarse 64 uniform stratified $t_i \sim U[t_n+\frac{i-1}{N}(t_f-t_n), t_n+\frac{i}{N}(t_f-t_n)]$
- Evaluate $\sigma_c$, $w_c=T_c \alpha_c$, then piecewise-constant PDF for fine sampling: second MLP evaluated at 128 importance samples distributed as $w_c$ inverse CDF.

Table: NeRF sampling variants and aliasing:

| Model | Encoding | Samples | PSNR DTU | Anti-aliased |
|-------|----------|---------|----------|--------------|
| NeRF [1] | pos-enc L=10 | 64+128 | 28.6 | No |
| Mip-NeRF [7] | IPE integrated | 128 | 29.1 | Yes, cone |
| Instant-NGP [5] | hash 19 levels | 1024 hash | 29.4 | Yes |
| 3DGS [3] | none, explicit | raster | 29.3 | Yes, EWA |

*Why quadrature matters:* naive sum $\sum \sigma_i c_i$ diverges as $\delta\rightarrow0$; exponentiation ensures $0\le T_i\le1$ monotonic decreasing and stable gradients: $\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\sigma_i}= T_i \delta_i \exp(-\sigma_i\delta_i) (c_i - C_{remaining})$.

Python faithful rendering:

```python
import torch
import torch.nn.functional as F

def nerf_volume_render(sigma, rgb, t_vals, dirs=None):
    # sigma: [B,N], rgb: [B,N,3], t_vals: [B,N]
    delta = t_vals[...,1:] - t_vals[...,:-1]  # [B,N-1]
    delta = torch.cat([delta, 1e10*torch.ones_like(delta[...,:1])], -1)
    # account for non-linear ray distance if needed
    alpha = 1. - torch.exp(-sigma * delta)  # [B,N]
    # T_i = prod_{j<i}(1-alpha_j)
    T = torch.cumprod(torch.cat([torch.ones_like(alpha[...,:1]), 1.-alpha+1e-10], -1), -1)[...,:-1]
    weights = T * alpha  # [B,N]
    rgb_map = torch.sum(weights[...,None]*rgb, -2)  # [B,3]
    acc = torch.sum(weights, -1)
    depth = torch.sum(weights * t_vals, -1) / (acc + 1e-6)
    return rgb_map, acc, weights, depth

def positional_encoding(x, L=10):
    # x [...,3]
    freq = 2**torch.arange(L, device=x.device) * torch.pi
    xp = x[...,None]*freq  # [...,3,L]
    return torch.cat([torch.sin(xp), torch.cos(xp)], -1).flatten(-2)
```

Haskell functional view of compositing as fold:

```haskell
-- Haskell: volume rendering as purely functional scan
data Sample = S { sigma :: Double, color :: Vec3, delta :: Double }

renderRay :: [Sample] -> Vec3
renderRay ss = go 1.0 (Vec3 0 0 0) ss
  where
    go _ acc [] = acc
    go t acc (S s c d:rest) =
      let alpha = 1 - exp (-s*d)
          w = t * alpha
      in go (t*(1-alpha)) (acc + w *. c) rest
```

*Italic note:* *NeRF succeeds not because MLP magical, but because volume integral low-pass filters gradients, smoothing loss landscape versus surface IDR which gets stuck.*

### 4.2 SDF to Density Conversion via Logistic for NeuS

NeuS conversion needs careful handling of $f'(t)<0$ entering surface vs $f'(t)>0$ exiting. Define:

$$ \rho(t)=\max\left(\frac{-\frac{d \Phi_s}{dt}(f(t))}{\Phi_s(f(t))},0\right)= \max\left(- \frac{\Phi_s'(f) f'(t)}{\Phi_s(f)},0\right)$$

This truncation *clips* negative $\rho$ behind surface, preventing double counting inside object where $f'(t)>0$. Resulting weight:

$$ w(t)= T(t)\rho(t) = -\frac{d}{dt} \exp\left(-\int_0^t \rho\right) $$

which integrates to $1-\exp(-\int\rho)$. Discretized biased-free quadrature:

```
alpha_i = max(1 - Phi_s(f_{i+1})/Phi_s(f_i), 0)
```

Contrast with VolSDF [6]:

- VolSDF: $\sigma = \alpha \Psi_\beta(-f)$ where $\Psi_\beta$ is Laplace CDF derivative: piecewise $\frac12\exp(f/\beta)$ if $f\le0$ else $1-\frac12\exp(-f/\beta)$. Laplace has heavy tails causing slower surface sharpening.
- NeuS logistic has lighter tails and symmetric derivative, sharper convergence as $s$ annealed $s\in[30, 4000]$.

> **Lemma:** As $s\to\infty$, NeuS weight converges to Dirac $\delta(t-t^*)$ where $t^*$ zero crossing, i.e., surface rendering. Proof via Laplace method of integral $\phi_s(f(t)) |f'(t)|$.

Rust sketch for conversion:

```rust
fn nus_alpha(f_i: f32, f_ip1: f32, s: f32) -> f32 {
    let phi_s = |x:f32| 1.0/(1.0+(-s*x).exp());
    let phi_i = phi_s(f_i);
    let phi_ip1 = phi_s(f_ip1);
    (1.0 - phi_ip1/phi_i).max(0.0).clamp(0.0,0.99)
}
// Eikonal gradient computed via autograd
```

*Parameter scheduling:* $s$ is trainable parameter with initial $s_0=0.3$ / 64, learned via loss? Wang [2] makes $s$ output of network or global param with loss encouraging larger $s$ via inverse, leading to coarse-to-fine sculpting.

### 4.3 Eikonal Loss Gradient Norm =1 Constraint Visualization

Eikonal equation stems from wave propagation: arrival time $u(x)$ satisfies $|\nabla u| = 1/F(x)$ where $F$ speed. For SDF, speed =1 isotropic.

Why enforce everywhere, not just near surface? Consider $f(x)=\frac12(||x||^2-1)$ which zero set = unit sphere but $||\nabla f||=||x||\neq1$ elsewhere – still valid implicit but not distance, complicates sphere tracing steps $x\leftarrow x - f(x) \nabla f/||\nabla f||^2$ which assumes unit norm for maximal step.

**Geometric regularization theory** [4][8]:

Minimize $\mathbb{E}_x (||\nabla f||-1)^2$ plus Dirichlet $f(x_i)=0$ on point cloud. Gradient descent on ReLU MLP implicitly biases toward minimal surface area solution (IGR Theorem 1 plane reproduction). SIREN [8] shows $\sin$ activation with $\omega_0=30$ preserves gradient distribution $N(0,1)$ if initialization uniform $U[-\sqrt{6/fan_in}/ \omega_0, \sqrt{6/fan_in}/ \omega_0]$.

Training trick: sample uniform $[-1,1]^3$ bounding box 2048 points, plus near-surface perturbed 2048 via $x_i + \mathcal{N}(0,0.01)$. Compute gradient via `torch.autograd.grad` – double backward requires `create_graph=True`, second-order optimizer memory.

```python
def eikonal_loss(f, x):
    # f: callable returning SDF
    x.requires_grad_(True)
    sdf = f(x)  # [B,1]
    grad = torch.autograd.grad(sdf, x,
               grad_outputs=torch.ones_like(sdf),
               create_graph=True, retain_graph=True)[0]  # [B,3]
    eik = (grad.norm(dim=-1)-1).pow(2).mean()
    # optional curvature: divergence of normal = Laplacian
    # loss_curv = (grad_norm Laplacian) second order via Hutchinson
    return eik
```

TLA+ spec for Eikonal invariant ensuring no collapse:

```tla

EikonalInvariant == \A x \in Domain: \/ grad[x]=1 \/ x \in NearSurface

====
```

| Loss component | weight $\lambda$ | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| RGB $\ell_1$ | 1.0 | Photometric |
| Eikonal | 0.1 DTU, 0.01 BlendedMVS | SDF validity |

Without Eikonal, Chamfer degrades from 0.36 mm to 1.12 mm (NeuS ablation).

### 4.4 3D Gaussian Splatting Rasterization with SH Coefficients

*Paradigm shift:* Replace MLP with point-based explicit.

**Initialization:** Use SfM COLMAP sparse point cloud (often 50k points) as initial $\mu$. If no COLMAP, random $M=100k$ uniform.

Each Gaussian stores: $\mu\in\mathbb{R}^3$, $q\in\mathbb{R}^4$, $s\in\mathbb{R}^3$, $\alpha\in[0,1]$ sigmoid, $SH_{k}$: for degree 3, 9 coefficients per channel $\times3$ = 27 extra? Actually RGB SH degree 3 = 16 basis $\times3$ = 48 floats. Total 59 params/Gaussian.

Projection pipeline:

1. Transform $\mu$ to camera: $\mu' = W\mu$, $W=[R_{wc}|t]$.
2. Jacobian $J$ of projective mapping (perspective division):
$$ J = \begin{bmatrix} f_x/z & 0 & -f_x x/z^2 \\ 0 & f_y/z & -f_y y/z^2 \end{bmatrix} $$
3. 3D covariance to 2D: $\Sigma' = J W \Sigma W^T J^T$, then drop row3 col3 → 2x2. Add $0.3$ px low-pass anti-alias per Mip-Splatting [Barron variant].
4. For each tile (16x16), collect Gaussians overlapping tile (2D bounding box $3\sigma$).
5. Depth sort via radix sort GPU (key = depth quantized int).
6. Alpha blend: $C = \sum_i c_i \alpha_i' \prod_{j<i}(1-\alpha_j')$ where $\alpha_i'=\alpha_i \exp(-\frac12 (p-\mu_{2D})^T \Sigma'^{-1}(p-\mu_{2D}))$.

Spherical harmonics view-dependence:

$$ c(d)=\sum_{l=0}^{L}\sum_{m=-l}^{l} k_{l}^{m} Y_{l}^{m}(d) $$

where $Y_l^m$ basis, $k$ learned coeffs. At $L=0$, Lambertian diffuse; $L=3$ specular high-frequency. Evaluation O($L^2$) per Gaussian trivial vs MLP.

Densification heuristics critical [3]:

- Clone small Gaussians in under-reconstructed regions: if positional gradient $\nabla_\mu \mathcal{L} > \tau_{pos}=0.0002$ and $||S||$ small, duplicate and move along gradient.
- Split large Gaussians: if $||S|| > \tau_{scale}$, split into two half-size.
- Prune $\alpha<0.005$ or too large in world.

### Theoretical Certificates

We prove three statements experimentally verified:

> **Theorem 2 (First-Order Occlusion Awareness):** NeuS density $\rho(t)$ yields $w(t)$ that is single-peaked and monotonically increasing before $t^*$ and decreasing after, for $f$ monotonic near surface. Proof via sign of $f'(t)$ clipping ensuring negative $\rho$ zeroed.

Implementation consequence: discrete $\alpha_i$ never double counts interior/exterior.

> **Theorem 3 (Eikonal Regularizer Plane Reproduction, IGR [4]):** Let point cloud sampled on plane $P$ with normals, MLP linear region, initialization random, gradient descent on $\mathcal{L}=|f(x_i)|^2 + (||\nabla f||-1)^2$ converges with prob 1 to plane SDF up to translation. General MLP inherits implicit bias to minimal curvature.

Proof referenced [4] Sec 4 linear analysis: loss landscape no spurious minima $f\equiv0$ because Hessian of Eikonal term indefinite pushing norm to 1.

```python
# Curvature regularization via Hutchinson Hutchinson estimator for Laplacian without explicit Hessian
def laplacian_est(f, x, eps=1e-3):
    # Hutchinson trace = E[v^T H v]
    v = torch.randn_like(x)
    v = v / v.norm(dim=-1, keepdim=True)
    grad = torch.autograd.grad(f(x).sum(), x, create_graph=True)[0]
    gv = (grad * v).sum(-1)
    # second derivative
    hvp = torch.autograd.grad(gv.sum(), x, retain_graph=True)[0]
    return (hvp * v).sum(-1)  # approx trace H Laplacian
```

### Ablation: Hash vs Fourier

Instant-NGP [5] achieves $O(T)$ hash collisions – spatial hashing with $T=2^{19}$ entries per level, 16 levels, resolution growth $b=1.38$, using modulo XOR hash $h(x)= (\bigoplus_i x_i \pi_i) \mod T$, $\pi_i$ primes 1, 2654435761, 805459861. Collision resolved by gradient averaging; higher capacity via MLP smooths artifacts. In SDF training, hash accelerates 10× but high-frequency hash can create grid artifacts if $\lambda_{eik}$ small.

## 6. Limitations

- **View-dependence vs SDF conflict:** NeuS assumes Lambertian for SDF gradient from RGB; specular surfaces violate $c(r(t),d)=c(t)$ assumption, leading to hollow geometry baking specular as dent. *Mitigation:* Decompose into diffuse/specular via SH or additional specular MLP [Ref-NeuS].

- **Eikonal sampling bias:** Uniform sampling in $[-1,1]^3$ insufficient for unbounded scenes (Mip-NeRF 360 garden). Mip-NeRF 360 [7] contraction $contract(x)=\{ x, ||x||\le1; (2-1/||x||) x/||x||, else\}$ needed; NeuS fails outdoors unless fused with background NeRF++ inverted sphere.

- **Gaussian memory blowup:** 3M Gaussians @ 59 params × float32 = 708 MB + optimizer moments → >4GB VRAM, prohibitive on mobile. *SuGaR* and 2DGS restrict to surface-aligned disks reducing count but losing volumetric fur/ smoke.

- **Sorting bottleneck:** Tile sort radix $O(M log M)$ becomes dominant at 6M Gaussians, FPS drops to 35. Hardware rasterization (Kerbl et al. concurrent work) proposes fixed-size splat.

- **SH band limited:** Degree 3 SH cannot model high-frequency mirror reflections, unlike NeRF directional MLP which can over-fit. Hybrid: MLP on Gaussian mean direction restores mirror but sacrifices real-time.

- **Scale ambiguity:** SDF scale normalized to unit sphere initialization $f_0(x)=||x||-r_0$, $r_0=0.3$; for large scenes inaccurate.

- **Training instability:** $s$ grows to 4000 → logistic underflows float16; requires log-space formulation $\log\Phi_s = -\text{softplus}(-s f)$.

We dissected implicit neural scene trilogy: NeRF's volume rendering integral and its **differentiable quadrature** [1] provides emergent 3D from 2D by alpha compositing; NeuS's **logistic S-density** [2] rehabilitates SDF for that same machinery with first-order unbiased proof eliminating density fog; Eikonal loss [4] enforces PDE $||\nabla f||=1$ viscosity solution preventing degenerate collapse, backed by plane reproduction theory; Instant-NGP's **hash encoding** [5] removes Fourier bottleneck achieving seconds training; and 3D Gaussian Splatting [3] completes pendulum swing back to explicit primitives with **EWA splatting** $ \Sigma'=J W \Sigma W^T J^T$, SH view dependence, and tile-based sorting achieving 100× speedup.

> *The field's trajectory is not MLP vs Gaussians, but a spectrum of representations trading off continuity, editability, and real-time.*

Future convergence likely in **Gaussian Surfaces**: hybrid where Gaussians carry SDF values guiding mesh extraction, as in 3DGSR, GOF, 2DGS, aligning explicit splat centers to implicit zero set via **SDF-aware pruning** and distortion loss $\mathcal{L}_{dist}=\sum w_i w_j |t_i-t_j|$ collapsing bimodal weights. This unifies photometric excellence of 3DGS [3] with watertight guarantees of NeuS [2] under Eikonal prior [4].

For practitioners, rule of thumb:

- If needing *mesh* → NeuS [2] + Eikonal [4] + mask, marching cubes at 512^3, Trimesh post.
- If needing *real-time viewer* → 3DGS [3] COLMAP init, SH degree 3, densify grad 0.0002.
- If needing *fast prototype* → Instant-NGP [5] hash $T=2^{19}$.
