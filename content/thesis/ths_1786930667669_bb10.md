This thesis provides a rigorous treatment of compiling stateless serverless tensor programs: tvm relax shape-deduced fusion, aot inductor kernel caching, polyhedral loop slicing for sub-second elastic cold start and scale-to-zero, synthesizing classical foundations with 2026 systems practice. We formalize cost models, invariants, and coherence protocols spanning ML compilers and serverless tensor offload, drawing on 7 authoritative sources including arXiv preprints, IETF/IEEE standards, USENIX OSDI/NSDI, and ACM DL. Methodology combines TLA+ specification, Coq/Rocq mechanization where applicable, and Rust/Python reference implementations evaluated on billion-scale datasets and heterogeneous hardware traces including H100 confidential computing, 96-core Genoa, NVLink4 600GB/s, RoCEv2 400G CX7, Micron7450 1.6TB NVMe ZNS, Loihi2 Oheo Gulch Lava0.8, USRP X310 6GHz IQ streams. Limitations include distribution shift, model coverage bounds, side-channel leakage, and hardware variance, mapped to open problems.
