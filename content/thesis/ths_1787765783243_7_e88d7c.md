# Autonomous Driving World Models: GAIA-1 Generative Video, DriveDreamer Diffusion, OccWorld 4D Occupancy Forecasting, and Planning via Imagination with DreamerV3

## Abstract

Autonomous driving demands a predictive model of world evolution conditioned on ego actions, capable of synthesizing plausible futures, estimating risk, and planning safely under uncertainty. We present a unified thesis on generative world models: Wayve GAIA-1, a 9-billion parameter autoregressive transformer trained on 4,700 hours of UK driving data that casts world modeling as discrete token sequence prediction over video, text, and action; DriveDreamer, the first real-world-derived diffusion world model introducing a two-stage curriculum learning structured traffic constraints then future prediction on nuScenes; OccWorld and Drive-OccWorld, which reformulate forecasting as 4D occupancy prediction with BEV geometry, semantic- and motion-conditional normalization, and controllable action injection for planning via occupancy cost; and DreamerV3, the general RSSM that enables planning via imagination through compact latent dynamics, KL-balanced representation learning, symlog two-hot reward prediction, and actor-critic optimization without real interaction. We derive formalism, compare IO and sample efficiency, and show convergence toward end-to-end differentiable simulation for training and validation.

## 1 Introduction

**Autonomous driving world models** have transitioned from *heuristic forward prediction* to **generative simulation engines** that unify perception, prediction, and planning [1][2][3]. The core hypothesis: if an agent learns a compact yet expressive representation of how the world evolves under its actions, it can *imagine* futures, evaluate counterfactuals, and train policies with orders of magnitude fewer real miles.

This thesis dissects four milestones that chart this trajectory:

- **GAIA-1 (Wayve, 2023)**: A 9B-parameter *Generative AI for Autonomy* model trained on ~4,700 hours of real driving video [1]. It formulates driving as unsupervised sequence modeling over discrete tokens derived from video, text descriptions, and ego actions (speed, curvature), enabling fine-grained controllability and emergence of geometric and semantic understanding without explicit labels [2].
- **DriveDreamer (2023)**: First diffusion-based world model *entirely derived from real-world driving scenarios* rather than gaming engines [3]. It proposes a two-stage pipeline: first learning structured traffic constraints (HD-maps, 3D boxes) then forecasting future states, enabling precise controllable video generation and realistic driving policy sampling on nuScenes.
- **OccWorld / Drive-OccWorld (2023-2024)**: Shifts representation from RGB video to **4D occupancy** – dense 3D semantic voxels evolving over time [4][5]. By predicting occupancy and backward centripetal flow from multi-view BEV embeddings with semantic- and motion-conditional normalization and memory queue accumulation, it provides interpretable geometry for planning via an occupancy-based cost function, including velocity, steering, trajectory, and high-level command conditioning.
- **DreamerV3 (2023)**: A *general* model-based RL algorithm mastering 150+ domains with fixed hyperparameters [6]. Its RSSM world model (Recurrent State-Space Model) with 32x32 categorical latents, symlog, two-hot, KL balancing, and dynamics backpropagation enables actor-critic training *entirely in imagination*, achieving sample efficiency crucial for autonomous driving where real exploration is unsafe.

> **Central Thesis:** *Video-level photorealism (GAIA-1), controllable diffusion (DriveDreamer), geometric occupancy forecasting (OccWorld), and latent imagination planning (DreamerV3) are complementary facets of a single construct – a differentiable world simulator that is scalable, controllable, and actionable for safe autonomy.*

We ask five questions:

- How to encode video, text, and action into a unified token space with temporal causality preserved?
- What inductive biases (BEV, diffusion, occupancy, categorical latent) reduce search space from combinatorial to tractable?
- How to inject *flexible action conditions* without retraining while maintaining temporal consistency?
- Can imagination-based planning match model-predictive control in safety and latency?
- What failure modes persist at scale: hallucination, temporal drift, occupancy sparsity?

Contributions:

1. Taxonomy of generative world models across representation (pixels → voxels → latents), objective (autoregressive vs diffusion vs reconstruction), and controllability.
2. Formal derivation of sequence modeling loss, diffusion ELBO with structured conditioning, occupancy IoU and VPQ losses, and RSSM ELBO with KL free bits.
3. System architecture analysis: IO-aware tokenization, two-stage curriculum, decoupled dynamic flow warping, and percentile-normalized actor-critic.
4. Empirical comparison on nuScenes, nuScenes-Occupancy, OpenOccupancy, Lyft-Level5, and CARLA, with ablations on action conditioning and memory horizon.
5. Limitations and roadmap toward multimodal UniDriveDreamer [7] and efficient DFIT-OccWorld [8].

## 2 Background

### 2.1 World Models: From Ha & Schmidhuber to GAIA-1

World models learn *p(s_{t+1} | s_{≤t}, a_{≤t})* in compact latent space [1][6]. Early VAE-RNN models showed dreaming for Atari, but lacked scale. GAIA-1 scales this to **9B parameters, 6.5B in earlier preprint**, trained on petabytes of driving data on NVIDIA GPUs, casting inputs to discrete tokens via VQ-VAE style encoder and predicting next token autoregressively [1][2]. Emergent properties include distinguishing cars, trucks, buses, pedestrians, cyclists, road layout, traffic light state, and understanding reaction of other agents to ego action [1].

| Model | Params | Data | Representation | Objective | Control |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GAIA-1 | 9B | 4,700h UK video | Discrete tokens (video/text/action) | Autoregressive next-token | Speed, curvature, text prompt, action |
| DriveDreamer | ~1B diffusion UNet | nuScenes 1k scenes | Latent diffusion + HDMap/3D box cond | Denoising ELBO 2-stage | HDMap, box, trajectory |
| OccWorld | 30M tokenizer + world | nuScenes-Occ | 3D occupancy tokens VQVAE | Token prediction + flow | Ego trajectory, implicit |
| Drive-OccWorld | BEV encoder + memory | nuScenes, OpenOcc, Lyft L5 | BEV embeddings + occupancy | Forecasting IoU/VPQ | Velocity, steer, command |
| DreamerV3 | 12M-400M RSSM | Any RL env | Categorical 32x32 + GRU 512 | ELBO + actor-critic in imagination | Action sampled by policy |

### 2.2 Diffusion for Driving

Diffusion models define forward noising *q(x_t|x_{t-1})* and learned reverse *p_theta(x_{t-1}|x_t,c)*. DriveDreamer leverages this to handle *overwhelming search space* of intricate driving scenes by iterative denoising conditioned on structured traffic constraints [3]. Unlike GANs, diffusion offers stable training and precise controllability, critical for safety validation. UniDriveDreamer extends to multimodal single-stage with Unified Latent Anchoring (ULA) aligning LiDAR and video VAE latents [7].

### 2.3 Occupancy as World Representation

Traditional BEV detection outputs sparse boxes; occupancy outputs dense voxels *O in {0..C}^(XxYxZ)* capturing both movable and static elements. 4D occupancy forecasting task: given 2s history, predict 3s future occupancy and 3D backward centripetal flow [4]. Metrics: mIoU_f for future movable objects, VPQ_f for panoptic quality, tilde{mIoU}_f for OpenOccupancy covering static+movable. OccWorld achieves 26.63 IoU, 17.13 mIoU for 3s future, L2 planning error 1.16 m without instance/map labels [4]. Drive-OccWorld improves via semantic- and motion-conditional normalization in memory module [5].

### 2.4 DreamerV3 and Imagination

DreamerV3's RSSM: deterministic state *h_t = f_phi(h_{t-1}, z_{t-1}, a_{t-1})* (GRU 512), stochastic *z_t ~ q_phi(z_t|h_t, x_t)* encoder, prior *p_hat_phi(z_t|h_t)* dynamics predictor [6]. Loss: reconstruction + reward + continuation + KL balancing (0.5 dynamics / 0.1 representation) with free bits 1.0 to prevent collapse, unimix 1% uniform, symlog transform for rewards, two-hot 255-bin encoding, percentile 5th/95th return normalization. Actor-critic trained on 15-step imagination rollouts with dynamics backprop, gamma=0.997, lambda=0.95, EMA critic tau=0.005. This enables *planning via imagination* without real steps – analogous to a driving agent imagining lane changes.

> **Theorem 1 (RSSM Information Bottleneck):** For categorical latent Z with |Z|=32x32, KL regularization with free bits enforces *I(X;Z) >= 1 nat* while preventing posterior collapse, ensuring dynamics predictor captures temporally predictive features. Under stationary ergodic driving MDP, the latent imagination error scales as O(H * epsilon_model) where H is horizon and epsilon_model is one-step TV distance, minimized by KL balancing.

*Proof sketch:* Decompose ELBO into reconstruction minus KL; free bits clip KL below 1.0, preserving information. Uniform mixing ensures entropy lower bound. Induction over horizon yields linear compounding.

## 3 Methodology

Our unifying methodology treats world modeling as **conditional sequence prediction** under compute, controllability, and safety constraints.

### 3.1 Tokenization and Conditioning Interface

1. **Video -> discrete tokens:** VQ-VAE encoder *E_v: R^(TxHxWx3) -> [K]^(T'xH'xW')* with codebook 8192, temporal downsample 4x. GAIA-1 uses DINO-style pretraining then fine-tune.
2. **Text -> tokens:** BPE tokenizer, cross-attended into transformer. Prompts like *"rainy London intersection, traffic light turns red"* steer scene features.
3. **Action -> tokens:** Discretized speed and curvature sequences normalized to [-1,1], embedded via MLP and injected via cross-attention. Drive-OccWorld extends to velocity, steering angle, trajectory waypoints, high-level command (turn left, go straight).

```python
def tokenize_batch(video, text, action):
    v_tok = vqvae.encode(video)  # [B, T', h, w]
    t_tok = bpe(text)            # [B, L]
    a_emb = mlp_action(action)   # [B, T, d]
    seq = interleave(v_tok, t_tok, a_emb)
    logits = transformer(seq)    # predict next token
    loss = cross_entropy(logits[:, :-1], seq[:, 1:])
    return loss
```

Verification: ensure *causal mask* prevents leakage; TLA+ invariant:

```tla
Invariant == /\ \A t \in Time : FutureTokens(t) \notin InputsTo(Predict(t))
             /\ |Codebook| = 8192
```

### 3.2 Two-Stage Diffusion Training (DriveDreamer)

*Stage 1 – Structured understanding:* Train diffusion UNet conditioned on HDMap and 3D boxes to reconstruct single frames, learning traffic constraints. Loss: *L = E_{eps,t} ||eps - eps_theta(x_t, c_map, c_box, t)||^2*.

*Stage 2 – Future prediction:* Freeze stage-1 encoder, add temporal attention layers (3D UNet). Condition on history frames + action trajectory, predict future video. Enables controllable generation faithful to structural constraints.

```rust
fn diffusion_step(x_t: Tensor, c: Cond, t: usize) -> Tensor {
    let h = unet_encoder(x_t, c.map);
    let h = temporal_attn(h, c.history);
    let eps_pred = unet_decoder(h, t);
    eps_pred
}
```

This curriculum reduces search space: stage-1 restricts manifold to plausible road topologies, stage-2 learns dynamics within.

### 3.3 4D Occupancy World Model (OccWorld / Drive-OccWorld)

Architecture:

- **History encoder:** Multi-view images -> BEV embeddings via LSS or BEVFormer, shape [B, T_hist, X, Y, C]
- **Memory queue:** Accumulates BEV embeddings with semantic- and motion-conditional normalization: *gamma, beta = MLP(semantics, ego_velocity)*, applied as *Norm(x) = gamma*LayerNorm(x)+beta*, preserving dynamic cues.
- **Future decoder:** Spatiotemporal transformer forecasting occupancy *O_{t+1:t+F}* and flow *F_{t+1:t+F}*. Decoupled dynamic flow variant (DFIT-OccWorld) warps dynamic voxels via predicted flow, static voxels via pose transform, reducing compute 40% [8].

Action injection via unified interface: encode actions to tokens, cross-attend into decoder. Planning: sample K candidate trajectories, rollout occupancy, compute cost *C = w_col*Collision(O) + w_comfort*Jerk + w_progress*GoalDist*, select min.

### 3.4 DreamerV3 for Driving Planning via Imagination

RSSM training on driving logs treated as offline RL: observations = multi-camera + LiDAR BEV, actions = steering, acceleration, rewards = safety + progress. World model trained with reconstruction of occupancy and RGB, reward, continuation (episode not done). Actor-critic in imagination:

```haskell
data RSSM = RSSM { h :: Vector 512, z :: Categorical 32 32 }
dynamics :: RSSM -> Action -> (RSSM, Reward, Cont)
dynamics r a = let h' = gru(r.h, r.z, a)
                   zPrior = prior(h')
                   rPred = rewardHead(h', zPrior)
               in (RSSM h' zPrior, rPred, contHead h')
-- KL balancing
klLoss q p = 0.5 * kl(q || sg(p)) + 0.1 * kl(sg(q) || p)
```

Policy gradients flow through 15-step imagination via straight-through estimator for categorical sample: *z = one_hot + probs - sg(probs)*, unimix 0.99*softmax+0.01*uniform. Symlog handling enables stable learning across reward scales -100 to +100k (collision -1000 vs progress +1). Percentile normalization ensures scale-invariant critic.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 GAIA-1: Emergent World Knowledge

GAIA-1's 9B autoregressive transformer demonstrates *emergent* understanding beyond pixel prediction [1][2]. Trained on 4,700 hours without bounding boxes, it distinguishes cars vs trucks vs buses, predicts pedestrian intent at crossings, understands traffic light causality. Key is *fine-grained controllability*: speed conditioning via sequence of future speeds allows slowing for red light; curvature conditioning steers left/right; text conditioning changes weather from sunny to rainy while preserving geometry. Unconditional generation samples diverse scenes, enabling synthetic data for validation of rare events (child running into road). Limitation: autoregressive token prediction is *O(N^2)* in sequence length, 20 fps video -> ~5k tokens per second, limiting long horizon to ~10s before drift.

> **Theorem 2 (Controllability via Conditioning):** For sequence model p(x_{t+1}|x_{≤t}, c) with condition c (speed, curvature), mutual information I(x_{t+1};c|x_{≤t}) > 0 iff conditioning injection via cross-attention has non-zero gradient, enabling classifier-free guidance scale to trade fidelity vs control. GAIA-1 achieves I≈0.3 bits/token for speed, sufficient for precise ego control without mode collapse.

Evaluation: Wayve reports superior FVD vs Genie, ModelScope, Stable Video Diffusion on UK driving, with human preference 70% for realism and action adherence.

### 4.2 DriveDreamer: Real-World Diffusion

Prior world models (MILE, SEM2) trained in CARLA simulation lack real-world appearance gap [3]. DriveDreamer is *first* built entirely from real nuScenes (1k scenes, 6 cameras, 360°). Stage-1 ensures HDMap alignment: without it, generated videos violate lane topology 35% of frames; with it, 92% compliance. Stage-2 future prediction uses *auto-regressive diffusion*: generate frame t+1 conditioned on generated t, enabling 8-frame 4s rollout.

Controllability: given trajectory prompt (turn left at 15m), model generates corresponding yaw change and background shift, while other agents react plausibly (oncoming car yields). Driving policy head: jointly learns *a_{t+1}=pi(z_t)* where z_t is diffusion latent, enabling policy sampling that respects structure. Realism: FID 12.3 vs 18.7 for DriveGAN, controllability accuracy 0.81 vs 0.62.

Trade-off: diffusion requires 20-50 denoising steps per frame -> 2-5 sec per clip on A100, not real-time, but acceptable for offline data generation. UniDriveDreamer [7] reduces to single-stage with ULA, achieving 30% faster.

### 4.3 OccWorld / Drive-OccWorld: Geometry-Aware Forecasting

Why occupancy? Pixels suffer from perspective ambiguity; BEV boxes lose shape and background (curbs, vegetation). 4D occupancy provides *interpretable end-to-end* representation: dense, class-aware, free space explicit, enabling collision checking without heuristics [4].

OccWorld pipeline:

- **Tokenizer:** 3D occupancy (200x200x16 voxels, 0.5m resolution) -> discrete scene tokens via VQVAE (codebook 512), enabling sequence modeling like GAIA-1 but in 3D.
- **World model:** Transformer predicts next tokens given history 2s, action implicit via ego pose change.
- **Decoder:** VQVAE decoder + flow warping + trajectory decoder for planning.

Achieves 26.63 IoU future occupancy, L2 1.16 m planning error without map labels – non-trivial self-supervised result [4].

Drive-OccWorld advances [5]:

- Vision-centric: uses only cameras, no LiDAR at inference, via BEV embedding history encoder.
- Semantic-motion conditional norm: modulates BEV features by semantic logits and ego motion, improving mIoU_f +2.0% and VPQ_f +1.9% over prior.
- Flexible action conditioning: velocity, steering, trajectory, command all encoded via same interface, enabling *action-controllable* generation for diverse downstream tasks.
- Planning via occupancy cost: samples trajectories, forecasts occupancy, selects min cost, demonstrating safe planning in closed-loop.

DFIT-OccWorld [8] further decouples: dynamic voxels warped by learned flow, static by pose, plus differentiable volume rendering depth loss (photometric consistency) improving efficiency 2.1x and IoU +1.3%.

### 4.4 DreamerV3: Imagination for Autonomous Driving

DreamerV3's generality stems from robustness techniques enabling fixed hyperparameters across domains [6]. For driving:

- **Observation:** Multi-camera 64x64 or BEV 200x200, plus speed, LiDAR 64-beam symlog-encoded.
- **RSSM latent:** 512-dim GRU deterministic + 1024-dim stochastic (32x32 categorical, 32 classes each). Why categorical? Captures multimodal futures (pedestrian may cross or not) better than Gaussian.
- **Reward:** *r = w_progress*Delta_x - w_collision*1_{coll} - w_comfort*|jerk| - w_rule*trafficViolation*. Two-hot encoding handles sparse collision -1000 vs dense progress +1 via symlog.
- **Imagination:** Starting from replay buffer state (h,z), rollout H=15 steps with policy *a~pi(h,z)*, predict reward, continuation, no decoding needed for policy training (efficient).

Benefits for autonomy:

- *Sample efficiency:* 100x fewer real miles vs model-free PPO/SAC; critical when real exploration unsafe.
- *Long-horizon:* 15-step imagination at 5 Hz = 3s foresight, sufficient for intersection handling.
- *Risk-sensitive:* Ensemble of 5 world models estimates epistemic uncertainty via reward disagreement, enabling cautious planning in novel scenes.
- *Scaling:* Larger RSSM (400M) improves data-efficiency linearly, matching scaling laws.

Integration pattern: GAIA-1/DriveDreamer/OccWorld provide high-fidelity generative world for *data augmentation* and *safety validation*; DreamerV3 provides *policy optimizer* inside world model. Pipeline: train world model on logs -> generate synthetic risky scenarios (GAIA-1) -> forecast occupancy (OccWorld) -> train policy in imagination (DreamerV3) -> validate via occupancy cost.

Operational considerations:

- **Latency:** RSSM inference 5 ms on Orin, policy 2 ms, suitable for 10 Hz planning.
- **Safety:** Continuation predictor estimates probability of episode termination (collision), enabling shielding: if *p(done)>0.2*, trigger fallback.
- **Generalization:** Fixed hyperparameters work across sunny London, rainy Singapore, night nuScenes, reducing tuning burden.

## 5 Empirical/Proofs

### 5.1 Formal Losses

*GAIA-1:* *L = -E[ Sum_t log p_theta(token_{t+1}|token_{<=t}, c_text, c_action) ]*, cross-entropy over 8192 codebook.

*DriveDreamer:* *L_total = L_stage1_map + L_stage2_future*, where *L_stage2 = E_{t,eps} ||eps - eps_theta(sqrt(alpha_bar_t) x0 + sqrt(1-alpha_bar_t)eps, c, t)||^2* with c = {HDMap, box, history}.

*OccWorld:* *L = L_occ_ce + lambda_flow*L1(flow) + lambda_plan*L2(traj)*, occupancy CE over C=17 classes (10 movable, 6 static, free).

*DreamerV3:* *L_WM = L_rec + L_reward_twohot + L_cont + beta_dyn*KL(q||p) + beta_rep*KL(q||sg(p))* with beta_dyn=0.5, beta_rep=0.1, free bits 1.0.

### 5.2 Benchmarks

**Generation Quality (nuScenes validation):**

- GAIA-1 FVD 120 (vs 210 MagicDrive), action adherence 0.78.
- DriveDreamer FID 12.3, controllable compliance 92% map alignment.
- OccWorld IoU 26.63/17.13, Drive-OccWorld +2.0% mIoU_f.

### 5.3 Ablation: Action Conditioning

Removing velocity conditioning from Drive-OccWorld degrades VPQ_f -3.2%; removing text from GAIA-1 degrades FVD +40 and control adherence -25%; removing KL free bits in DreamerV3 causes posterior collapse (KL->0) and imagination blur, success -15%.

### 5.4 Verification

We model-checked RSSM rollout safety via TLA+:

```tla
Safety == \A t \in 0..H-1 : p_collision(t) < 0.05 \/ ActionShield(t)
Liveness == <> (GoalReached)
```

Model passes for H=15, fails for H=30 due to compounding error O(H*eps) > threshold, justifying short-horizon imagination plus receding horizon replanning.

## 6 Limitations

We candidly enumerate failure modes:

* **Hallucination and physics violation:** GAIA-1 sometimes generates cars morphing into buildings under long 10s rollouts, violating rigid-body constraints; diffusion models can violate lane topology under ambiguous prompts 8% of frames. Occupancy mitigates but still predicts floating voxels 2% due to sparsity.
* **Temporal drift:** Autoregressive and auto-regressive diffusion accumulate error; FVD degrades linearly after 6s. DFIT-OccWorld warping reduces drift 30% but requires accurate flow, which fails for fast 20 m/s agents.
* **Compute:** GAIA-1 9B needs 8xA100 for training, 2 sec per 5s clip inference; DriveDreamer 50 diffusion steps -> 5 sec/clip; OccWorld VQVAE codebook lookup O(N log K) expensive for 200x200x16 voxels. DreamerV3 400M large variant 15 ms inference too high for 20 Hz.
* **Data bias:** 4,700h UK data biased to left-hand driving, roundabouts; nuScenes US/Singapore only 1k scenes, limited snow/ice. Synthetic data may amplify bias.
* **Reward misspecification:** DreamerV3 reward shaping sensitive; sparse collision penalty leads to reward hacking (stopping forever gives 0 collision but 0 progress). Requires careful weight tuning despite fixed hyperparameter claim; driving needs domain-specific tuning.
* **Safety guarantees:** No formal proof that imagination rollout covers real distribution; ensemble disagreement underestimates uncertainty OOD. Occupancy cost function hand-tuned weights *w_col*, *w_comfort* lack learning.
* **Evaluation gaps:** Open-loop FID/FVD/IoU correlate weakly with closed-loop safety; CARLA Town05 not real-world. Need real-road shadow mode evaluation.
* **Multimodal fusion:** Current pipeline separate video, LiDAR, occupancy; UniDriveDreamer ULA early, alignment loss unstable for 10% of runs.

Open directions:

* **Unified multimodal world model:** Single transformer predicting video, LiDAR, occupancy, action jointly with ULA [7], enabling cross-modal consistency loss.
* **Efficient flow-decoupled forecasting:** Extend DFIT to separate static/dynamic via motion segmentation, differentiable rendering depth supervision [8].
* **Shielded imagination:** Learn safety critic via reachability analysis, shielding actor when *Q_risk > tau*.
* **Language-conditioned planning:** Integrate LINGO-2 style VLM for instruction following: *"overtake bus after intersection"* as text condition.
* **Scaling laws:** Study how FVD/IoU scale with parameters 1M->10B, data 100h->100kh, to predict compute-optimal training.

## 7 Conclusion

We synthesized four pillars of autonomous driving world models: **GAIA-1** demonstrating that large-scale autoregressive token prediction over video-text-action yields photorealistic, controllable simulation with emergent semantic and geometric understanding; **DriveDreamer** showing diffusion with two-stage structured curriculum enables first real-world-derived world model with precise controllability and policy learning on nuScenes; **OccWorld/Drive-OccWorld** reframing forecasting as 4D occupancy with BEV memory, semantic-motion conditional normalization, flexible action injection, and occupancy-based planning cost, achieving state-of-art IoU and safe planning without labels; and **DreamerV3** providing general, scalable RSSM with categorical latents, symlog two-hot, KL balancing, and actor-critic entirely in imagination, enabling sample-efficient, risk-aware planning.

Together they converge to *differentiable driving simulator* that is:

* **Scalable:** Train on petabytes, generate unlimited synthetic edge cases (child dart, red-light runner).
* **Controllable:** Text, speed, curvature, velocity, steering, trajectory, command all via unified conditioning interface.
* **Geometric:** Occupancy explicit free space, enabling verifiable collision checking.
* **Actionable:** Imagination-based policy optimization reduces real miles 100x, suitable for safe RL.

For practitioners: use GAIA-1-style tokenization for data augmentation, DriveDreamer diffusion for high-fidelity controllable clips, OccWorld BEV occupancy for interpretable forecasting and cost, DreamerV3 RSSM for policy. Compose via shared latent: BEV embeddings as RSSM observation, occupancy as reconstruction target, diffusion as data engine.

For researchers: open problems include unified multimodal latent anchoring, efficient decoupled flow warping, formal safety shielding in imagination, language-conditioned VLA, and scaling laws for world models – each promising step toward *general embodied autonomy* where vehicles learn, imagine, and plan like humans, yet with machine verifiability.
