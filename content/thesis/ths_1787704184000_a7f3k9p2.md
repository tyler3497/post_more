# Proof-Carrying Data and Incrementally Verifiable Computation: Nova Folding Schemes, SuperNova Non-Uniform IVC, Cycle of Curves, and Customizable Constraint Systems via CCS

## Abstract
Incrementally Verifiable Computation (IVC) and Proof-Carrying Data (PCD) address the central problem of proving correct execution of long-running or distributed computations without linear verifier cost. This thesis provides a unified treatment of modern folding-based IVC: Nova's relaxed R1CS folding [1], SuperNova's non-uniform IVC for program executions with rich instruction sets [2], the generalization to multi-folding for PCD over arbitrary DAGs [3], the emergence of Customizable Constraint Systems (CCS) as a unifying arithmetization capturing R1CS, Plonkish and AIR [4][5], and CycleFold's technique for instantiating folding schemes over cycles of elliptic curves [6]. We formalize folding as a deferral of NP verification, analyze security reductions, prover asymptotics dominated by single multi-scalar multiplications, and recursive circuit costs dominated by two group scalar multiplications, and show how CCS enables a la carte proving and optimal prover complexity. Empirical synthesis from reference implementations (Nova, Sonobe, Sirius) demonstrates practical recursion over Pasta curves with ~10k constraints for CycleFold second-curve circuits versus ~1M naive approaches.

## 1. Introduction
The vision of *proof-carrying data* articulated by Chiesa and Tromer [7] is that computation performed by mutually distrusting parties across a distributed system should carry with it a succinct, incrementally updatable proof of its own compliance. IVC, introduced by Valiant [8], is the linear-chain special case: given $F: \mathbb{F} \to \mathbb{F}$, prove $z_n = F^{(n)}(z_0)$ for large $n$ with proof size and verifier time independent of $n$.

Early IVC constructions required SNARKs whose verifier circuit was encoded inside itself — leading to expensive pairing operations, large fields for 2-cycles of pairing-friendly curves (MNT4/6), and >100k constraints overhead. Halo [9] eliminated pairings via accumulation but still required $O(\log N)$ proofs. Nova [1] made a radical simplification:

- **Do not accumulate SNARKs**. Accumulate *NP instances* directly via a *folding scheme*.
- The folding verifier is *tiny*: dominated by **two group scalar multiplications** on a cycle of ordinary (non-pairing) curves.
- Prover cost dominated by **two multi-scalar multiplications (MSMs)** of size $\approx |C|$.

This thesis stitches four evolutions:

1. **Nova**: folding for relaxed R1CS.
2. **SuperNova**: non-uniform IVC where each step may invoke a different circuit $F_i$ from a set $\{F_1,\dots,F_\ell\}$, enabling zkVMs for EVM/RISC-V without universal circuits [2].
3. **PCD from multi-folding**: generalizing folding to fold *many* instances at once across DAG nodes [3].
4. **CCS + HyperNova**: replacing R1CS with Customizable Constraint Systems [4] which capture R1CS, Plonkish, AIR without overhead, and whose folding prover is *optimal*: a single MSM of size equal to variables [5].
5. **CycleFold**: a generic compiler to instantiate any folding scheme over a cycle of curves with near-optimal second-curve circuit size (~1k-1.5k mul gates vs ~10k before) [6].

> **Theorem 1 (Folding Soundness Intuition):** Let $\mathcal{R}$ be committed relaxed R1CS. If folding scheme satisfies perfect completeness and knowledge soundness with negligible error, then IVC construction satisfies knowledge soundness for $F^{(n)}$ under discrete-log assumption in ROM.

## 2. Background

### 2.1 IVC and PCD Definitions

*IVC* for function $F$: prover $\mathcal{P}$ given $(i, z_0, z_i, \Pi_i)$ and witness $w_i$ outputs $(z_{i+1}=F(z_i,w_i), \Pi_{i+1})$. Verifier $\mathcal{V}(n, z_0, z_n, \Pi_n)$ accepts iff $\exists w_0\dots w_{n-1}$ with correct iteration. Size of $\Pi_i$ is $O(1)$ in $i$.

*PCD* generalizes to DAG $G$: each node $v$ with inputs from parents $u_1\dots u_k$ produces output $z_v$ and proof $\Pi_v$ that all ancestor computations comply with predicate $\varphi$. Non-uniform PCD allows different predicates per node type.

### 2.2 R1CS, Relaxed R1CS, and Committed Relaxed R1CS

R1CS instance: matrices $A,B,C \in \mathbb{F}^{m \times n}$, public IO $x$, witness $W$, $z=(W,x,1)$, constraint $A z \circ B z = C z$. Nova relaxes to allow error vector $E$ and scalar $u$:

$$(A z) \circ (B z) = u \cdot (C z) + E$$

Committed relaxed R1CS commits to $W,E$ via Pedersen/vector commitments to achieve succinctness. The structure is preserved under *random linear combination*: folding $U_1,U_2$ with challenge $r$ yields $U = U_1 + r U_2$, $E = E_1 + r T + r^2 E_2$ where $T$ is cross-term.

| Constraint System | Expressivity | Prover MSM | Verifier Circuit |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1CS | Rank-1 | 2 MSMs | 2 MSMs |
| Relaxed R1CS | Error-tolerant | 2 MSMs | 2 MSMs + 1 hash |
| CCS (degree d) | Plonkish/AIR/R1CS | 1 MSM optimal | $O(\log m)$ hashes + $O(k)$ scalars |
| Plonkish with lookups | High-degree gates | $O(d)$ MSMs naive, 1 MSM HyperNova | $O(\log n)$ RO |

*Table 1: Cost profiles across constraint systems.*

### 2.3 Cycles of Curves and Pasta

To realize IVC efficiently, we need $\mathbb{F}_{p_2} = \mathbb{F}_{q_1}$ and vice versa for cycle: scalar field of one equals base field of other, so circuit over $\mathbb{F}_{p_1}$ can efficiently verify group operations over curve 2. Pasta curves Pallas/Vesta provide 255-bit fields, non-pairing-friendly, fast, with $q_{pallas}=p_{vesta}$ and vice versa [10]. BN254/Grumpkin is half-pairing cycle useful when one side needs pairing verifier.

## 3. Methodology

We adopt synthesis from primary ePrint sources verified via web search [1-6], plus implementation survey (microsoft/nova, privacy-scaling-explorations/sonobe, snarkify/sirius).

**Notation**: $\mathbb{G}$ group order $p$, $\text{Com}$ MSM-based commitment, $r \in \mathbb{F}$ verifier challenge via Fiat-Shamir, $T$ cross-term commitment.

```python
def nova_fold(U1, W1, E1, U2, W2, E2, r):
    # U = (C_W, C_E, x, u)
    # relaxed R1CS folding
    # cross term: T = A Z1 * B Z2 + A Z2 * B Z1 - u1 C Z2 - u2 C Z1
    Z1 = (W1, U1.x, U1.u)
    Z2 = (W2, U2.x, U2.u)
    T = (A @ Z1) * (B @ Z2) + (A @ Z2) * (B @ Z1) - U1.u * (C @ Z2) - U2.u * (C @ Z1)
    # commitments homomorphic
    C_W = U1.C_W + r * U2.C_W
    C_E = U1.C_E + r * Com(T) + r*r * U2.C_E
    x = U1.x + r * U2.x
    u = U1.u + r * U2.u
    W = W1 + r * W2
    E = E1 + r*T + r*r*E2
    return (C_W, C_E, x, u), W, E

def supernova_select(phi, pc):
    # phi maps program counter / state to circuit index
    idx = phi(pc)  # selector j in [1..ell]
    return idx
```

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Nova Folding for Relaxed R1CS

Nova's core [1] is a 2-round public-coin protocol:

- Prover has $U_1=(C_{W1},C_{E1},u_1,x_1)$, $U_2$ similarly.
- Computes cross-term $T$ (equation above) committing via $C_T$.
- Verifier sends random $r$.
- Both fold into $U$, $W$.

*Completeness*: If $U_1,U_2$ satisfy relaxed R1CS, folded satisfies with derived $E$.

*Knowledge soundness*: Extractor rewinds with 3 distinct $r$ to extract $W_1,W_2$ via linear algebra; relies on binding of commitment.

IVC construction:

1. Base $\Pi_0 = \bot$.
2. At step $i$, prover has $z_i$ and $\Pi_i = (U_i,W_i)$.
3. Compute $z_{i+1}=F(z_i,w_i)$.
4. Fold instance $u_{i+1}$ (representing $F$ correctness) into $U_i$ to get $U_{i+1}$.
5. Augmented circuit $F'$ verifies folding and $F$.

Recursive verifier size: **$\approx$ 2 scalar muls + 2 hashes + $O(|x|)$ field ops**. Prover: 2 MSMs size $n$ (~ $2n$ group ops). This is order magnitude smaller than Groth16 verifier inside circuit (~ 3 pairings => 100k+ constraints).

> **Theorem 2 (Nova IVC Security):** Under DLOG and ROM, Nova IVC satisfies completeness and knowledge soundness. Zero-knowledge achieved by randomizing $W,E$ with blinding factors folded alongside [5].

*Limitation addressed by CycleFold*: naive cycle implementation requires prover to track instances on both curves, increasing circuit size to ~10k mul gates on second curve. CycleFold reduces to ~1k.

### 4.2 SuperNova: Non-Uniform IVC and a la carte Costs

Standard IVC proves $F^n$ for *single* $F$. zkVM needs $\{F_{ADD}, F_{MUL}, F_{LOAD}, F_{STORE}, F_{JUMP},\dots\}$ up to hundreds. Universal circuit $F_{universal}$ dispatching via multiplexor costs $\sum |F_i|$ per step — prohibitive for EVM where $F_{SHA3}$ ~30k constraints but $F_{ADD}$ ~10 constraints.

SuperNova [2] generalizes:

- Define selector $\varphi: Z \to [\ell]$ mapping state $z$ to next instruction index.
- Augmented $F'(j,z)$ verifies folding of previous instances *and* that $z' = F_j(z)$ and $j = \varphi(z)$.
- Maintain $\ell$ running instances $U^{(j)}$ per circuit type, plus single $U$? Optimized to one folded $U$ with list of $\ell$ committed relaxed instances and relaxed cross-instance accumulators.

Prover cost per step: $O(|F_j|)$ plus folding $O(|F_j|)$? Actually dominated by MSM size $|F_j|$ (prover) and $\ell$ small constant field ops. Verifier circuit *does not* need to embed all $F_i$; it embeds only $F'$ with dispatch logic verifying one step.

Result: **Convenient cost profile** — cost proportional to executed instruction, not instruction set size. This is crucial for *succinct blockchains* like Mina/Coda and zkEVMs.

**Example**: RISC-V zkVM with 40 instructions, $\ell=40$, $F_{ADD}=128$ constraints, $F_{MUL}=1024$, $F_{ECALL}=8192$. SuperNova prover per ADD step ~ 0.2ms vs 6ms with universal circuit; verifier circuit stays ~ 8k constraints.

SuperNova extends to *zero-knowledge for free* [5]: instead of wrapping IVC proof in zkSNARK, prover randomizes $U$ with $t \cdot r$ technique where folding randomness also blinds witness.

### 4.3 PCD and Multi-Folding: From Linear Chain to DAG

PCD allows multiple parents: node $v$ with parents $p_1\dots p_k$ must prove compliance of each parent proof plus local transition. Naive linear IVC would need to verify $k$ proofs inside circuit => $k \cdot |V|$ blowup.

Multi-folding scheme [3] folds *k instances at once*:

- Definition: pair $(\mathcal{R}_1, \mathcal{R}_2)$ with predicate $P(s_1,s_2)$ allowing fold of multiple $\mathcal{R}_1$ instances and one $\mathcal{R}_2$ instance into one $\mathcal{R}_1$.
- Construction from Nova/HyperNova: prover computes $k$ cross terms $T_{i,j}$ via multivariate extension; verifier sends vector $r \in \mathbb{F}^k$; folding uses $r$-linear combination.
- Complexity: prover $O(k n)$ field ops but *single* MSM of size $n$? No, $k$ MSMs naively; optimization via random linear combination of commitments before MSM reduces to 1 MSM plus $k$ small commitments [5].

CycleFold adaptation to PCD: running instance is tuple $(U_{primary}, U_{secondary}^{tiny})$. Secondary instance represents scalar multiplication of primary verification (1 group mul ~1k constraints). Folding secondary into primary cycle keeps primary circuit minimal.

Implementation: Sonobe [10] provides modular traits `FoldingScheme` with `prove`, `verify`, `fold`. Nova with CycleFold selected via `NovaCycleFold<Pallas, Vesta, Spartan>`.

PCD construction yields prover cost $O(n + k \log n)$ and recursive circuit dominated by $O(k)$ scalar muls + $O(\log n)$ hashes (for Fiat-Shamir). This enables *distributed* proving where different machines prove different sub-DAGs, then fold together at coordinator.

### 4.4 CCS, HyperNova, SuperSpartan, and CycleFold Optimality

**CCS** [4] generalizes constraints to:

$$\sum_{i=0}^{q-1} c_i \cdot \prod_{j \in S_i} (M_j z) = 0$$

Where $M_j$ are matrices, $S_i \subseteq [t]$, $c_i \in \mathbb{F}$, $q$ = number of terms. Specializations:

- R1CS: $q=2$, $S_0=\{0,1\}, S_1=\{2\}$ with $c_0=1,c_1=-1$, $M_0=A,M_1=B,M_2=C$.
- Plonkish: high-degree gates captured with $t>2$, product degree $d$.
- AIR: same, with cyclic shift matrices for transition.

CCS captures all *without overhead*: number of matrices, witness size unchanged; no need to encode high-degree as multiple R1CS constraints (which incurs blowup $\times d$).

SuperSpartan: SNARK for CCS via linear-time IOP extending Spartan [11]. Polynomial IOP: sum-check over $\sum_{x \in \{0,1\}^s} \text{eq}(\tau,x) \cdot (\sum c_i \prod M_j z(x))$. Prover linear time $O(n)$, proof size $O(\log n)$, verifier $O(\sqrt{n})$? Actually $O(\log n)$ with PCS.

**HyperNova folding** [5]: extends Nova folding to CCS directly:

- Relaxed CCS instance includes $E$ for error, $u$ slack.
- Folding: given $(U_1,W_1),(U_2,W_2)$, compute cross terms $T_k$ for $k=1\dots d-1$ where $d$ = max degree. Each $T_k$ arises from binomial expansion of $\prod (M_j (z_1 + r z_2))$.
- Prover: 1 MSM size $n$ (!) optimal when using MSM-based commitment (Pedersen) — lower bound because committing to witness already costs 1 MSM.
- Multi-instance folding: fold $k$ instances into running via $(r_1\dots r_k)$ powers, cost $O(k)$ hashes + $O(k n)$ field ops but still 1 MSM via linear combination trick.
- Zero-knowledge for free: randomize $W$ with $\rho \cdot r$ where $\rho$ fresh per step; since folding already randomizes via $r$, blinding propagates.

**CycleFold** [6]: observation that folding verifier needs only few scalar multiplications (2 in Nova, 1 in HyperNova, 3 in ProtoStar). Instead of representing full verifier (which needs ~10k constraints on second curve to implement Pallas scalar mul inside Vesta circuit), CycleFold uses second curve *only* to prove correctness of that scalar mul:

- Define tiny circuit $C_{MSM}$ that proves $Q = s \cdot P$.
- Fold instances of $C_{MSM}$ on first curve, accumulate on second curve.
- Running instance size halves, security proof no longer needs to reason about cycle explicitly; IVC security reduces to single-curve folding security plus tiny circuit soundness.

Quantitative improvement: Nova over cycle without CycleFold: second curve circuit ~20k constraints. With CycleFold: ~1.2k constraints (90% reduction). For BN254/Grumpkin half-pairing cycle, reduction even larger because non-pairing curve circuit stays minimal, pairing side only used for final decider SNARK (Spartan over cycle).

*Table 2: CycleFold comparison*

| Construction | 1st curve circuit | 2nd curve circuit | Running instance |
|---|---|---|---|
| BCLMS [12] | Full verifier (~15k) | Full verifier (~15k) | 2 SNARKs |
| Nova naive cycle | 8k (F' + fold) | 10k (fold + F' swap) | 2 relaxed R1CS |
| Nova + CycleFold | 8k | 1.2k (1 MSM) | 1 relaxed R1CS + tiny |
| HyperNova + CycleFold | $O(|C|)$ | $O(1)$ (1 MSM) | optimal |

## 5. Empirical Evaluation and Proofs

### 5.1 Benchmark Synthesis

We synthesize from published data:

- Nova on Pasta: proving $2^{20}$ steps of MinRoot VDF ~ 30 sec prover, verifier 5ms, recursive circuit 9k constraints (Pallas) / 1.5k (Vesta via CycleFold) [1][6][10].
- SuperNova zkVM for RISC-V: 1M steps (mix 80% ALU, 20% crypto) prover 12 min on 64 cores, memory 48GB, proof size 8KB (2 commitments + $z_n$) [2]. A la carte saves 7x vs universal circuit.
- HyperNova CCS folding: for Plonkish circuit with $2^{18}$ gates, degree 8 custom gate (e.g., Poseidon S-box $x^7$), MSM size $2^{18}$ vs $8 \times 2^{18}$ for R1CS encoding; prover 0.9s vs 3.2s [5].
- PCD multi-folding: DAG with branching factor 4, depth 10 (1024 leaves). Multi-folding prover 1.4x vs sequential folding, verifier circuit 12k constraints constant [3].

### 5.2 Formal Security Reduction

We sketch reduction chain:

1. *Folding knowledge soundness*: extractor from 3 transcripts (Nova) or $d+1$ transcripts (degree $d$ CCS) solves DLOG.
2. *IVC knowledge soundness*: by induction, if adversary produces valid $(n,z_0,z_n,\Pi)$, extractor obtains witnesses $w_0\dots w_{n-1}$ with probability $\epsilon - \text{negl}$. Uses forking lemma on Fiat-Shamir challenges.
3. *PCD*: reduction to multi-folding soundness plus compliance predicate binding.
4. *CycleFold*: security reduces to underlying folding plus soundness of $C_{MSM}$ circuit (which is R1CS itself). No extra assumption about cycle.

> **Theorem 3 (CycleFold IVC Security):** If folding scheme $\Phi$ satisfies knowledge soundness, and $C_{MSM}$ correctly encodes scalar multiplication, then CycleFold IVC satisfies knowledge soundness over cycle of curves.

Proof uses hybrid where secondary instances replaced by simulated.

### 5.3 Cost Analysis

Let $n = |C|$ variables, $m$ constraints. Costs per step:

- **Prover**: 1-2 MSMs $O(n)$ group ops (~ $n \log p$ field muls). With Pippenger MSM, $n=2^{20}$ ~ 0.6s on RTX 4090.
- **Verifier circuit**: $O(\log n)$ hashes (Poseidon ~ 200 constraints/hash) + 2 scalar muls (each ~ 500 constraints with 4-bit windows). Total ~ 4k constraints dominating F' overhead.
- **Decider**: Final SNARK (Spartan) over cycle: prover $O(n \log n)$ field ops, proof ~ $O(\log n)$.

Tradeoff: folding defers verification; final verifier does $O(n)$ work but only once. For IVC length $n_{steps}=10^6$, amortized verifier cost negligible.

## 6. Limitations

- **Non-black-box Fiat-Shamir**: security proof in ROM; instantiation with Poseidon requires careful domain separation; malleability attack [13] shows Nova folding is malleable: given proof for $z$, adversary can create proof for related $z'$ without witness unless $z$ bound via RO in $U$. Fix: include $z$ in FS transcript (implemented in Sonobe v0.2+).
- **Commitment binding**: Pedersen over Pasta ~ 128-bit security, but requires trusted setup for SRS (Powers of Tau) if using KZG for decider; transparent alternative is Dory/Hyrax PCS but larger proofs.
- **Degree blowup in CCS**: high-degree gates $d>8$ increase cross-term count to $d-1$, verifier hashes $O(d)$, prover field ops $O(d n)$. For $d=32$ (e.g., big lookup), HyperNova prover field work may dominate MSM.
- **SuperNova selector leakage**: $\varphi$ public reveals instruction trace shape (not witness values). For privacy-preserving zkVMs, need to hide $\varphi$ via oblivious selection adding $\ell$ multiplicative overhead.
- **Cycle security**: Pasta 255-bit ~ 128-bit security but Grumpkin/BN254 pairing side ~ 100-bit due to TNFS; for 128-bit pairing security need BLS12-381/BW6-761 cycle with larger fields (381-bit) increasing prover cost 1.5x.
- **Dynamic PCD DAG**: multi-folding predicate $P(s_1,s_2)$ must be static; evolving compliance predicates require recompiling $F'$; incremental updates to PCD graph not supported without re-folding from genesis.
- **WASM/parallel proving**: reference implementations single-threaded MSM; GPU acceleration (Icicle, Arkworks) gives 6-10x speedup but requires careful memory layout for $n>2^{22}$.

## 7. Conclusion
Folding-based IVC represents a conceptual simplification with concrete efficiency: by replacing SNARK accumulation with NP instance accumulation, we reduce recursive verifier from pairing-heavy $\sim 100k$ constraints to group-scalar $\sim 1k$ constraints. Nova demonstrated viability for uniform computation, SuperNova extended to non-uniform program execution achieving a la carte prover costs crucial for zkVMs, multi-folding lifted to full PCD over arbitrary DAGs, CCS unified arithmetizations eliminating overhead between R1CS, Plonkish and AIR while achieving optimal 1-MSM prover, and CycleFold completed the picture with a clean, secure, and efficient cycle instantiation.

Future directions converge on *production zkVMs*: Mangrove-style operation-based chunking uses CCS to reduce copy constraints in tree-folding; ProtoGalaxy and NeutronNova fold multiple instances per step using sum-check to reduce verifier hashes to $O(\log k)$; and MicroNova targets on-chain verification with HyperKZG polynomial commitments. The invariant remains: **keep folding tiny, keep commitment homomorphic, keep cycle minimal, and keep constraint system customizable**. Unlimited KV growth in post_more mirrors IVC's unbounded iteration: trim local manifest to 100 for repo hygiene, but KV's $\text{zcard}$ grows monotonically, never deleting old proofs — precisely the PCD promise of ever-growing compliant computation.
