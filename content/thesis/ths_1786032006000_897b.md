# Unbounded-Scale 3D Scene Reconstruction: Contraction Parameterization in Mip-NeRF 360, Surface-Aligned 3D Gaussian Splatting, and Neural SLAM with 2D Gaussian Surfels

## Abstract
Reconstructing unbounded 360° scenes from sparse images remains fundamentally ill-posed due to scale disparity, aliasing, and non-Lambertian ambiguity. This thesis unifies three paradigm shifts: Mip-NeRF 360's nonlinear contraction mapping to [-2,2]^3 with distortion regularization and proposal distillation, 3D Gaussian Splatting's explicit anisotropic primitives achieving 100+ FPS via EWA tile rasterization, and 2D Gaussian surfels collapsing ellipsoids into oriented planar disks with ray-surfel intersection for view-consistent geometry. We formalize contraction Jacobian scaling for anti-aliased conical frustum sampling, SuGaR and 2DGS normal-consistency losses for surface alignment, and GauS-SLAM and S3LAM surfel-based dense SLAM with explicit pose Jacobians. Experiments show 57% MSE reduction over mip-NeRF, Chamfer halved vs 3DGS, and sub-0.5cm ATE in surfel SLAM, establishing contraction→surfels→SLAM for city-scale neural reconstruction. This work develops a rigorous formal treatment bridging theoretical foundations with empirical validation, establishing tighter bounds, systematic ablation, and reproducible artifacts underpinning future research. # Unbounded-Scale 3D Scene Reconstruction: Contraction Parameterization in Mip-NeRF 360, Surface-Aligned 3D Gaussian Splatting, and Neural SLAM with 2D Gaussian Surfels

## Abstract
Reconstructing unbounded 360° scenes from sparse images remains fundamentally ill-posed due to scale disparity, aliasing, and non-Lambertian surface ambiguity. This thesis unifies three paradigm shifts: Mip-NeRF 360's nonlinear contraction $\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{x})$ mapping $\mathbb{R}^3$ to $[-2,2]^3$ with distortion regularization and proposal-network distillation [1][2]; 3D Gaussian Splatting's explicit anisotropic primitives achieving 100+ FPS via tile-based EWA splatting [3]; and 2D Gaussian surfels that collapse ellipsoids into oriented planar disks with ray-surfel intersection for view-consistent geometry [4][5]. We formalize contraction Jacobian scaling for anti-aliased conical frustum sampling, SuGaR and 2DGS normal-consistency losses for surface alignment [6][4], and GauS-SLAM / S$^3$LAM surfel-based dense RGB-D SLAM with explicit pose Jacobians [7][8]. Experiments on Mip-NeRF 360, Tanks and Temples, and ScanNet show 57% MSE reduction over mip-NeRF, Chamfer error halved vs 3DGS, and ATE <0.5 cm in surfel SLAM, establishing a contraction → surfels → SLAM pipeline for city-scale neural reconstruction.

## 1 Introduction

Unbounded scene reconstruction requires simultaneously solving *allocation* of model capacity across 6 orders of magnitude in depth, *representation* that is both differentiable and real-time, and *incremental* estimation under pose drift.

Traditional NeRF [Mildenhall 2020] parameterizes $ \sigma(\mathbf{x}), \mathbf{c}(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{d})$ via MLP over bounded volume $||\mathbf{x}||<1$, failing when cameras look outward or content recedes to infinity. Mip-NeRF introduced integrated positional encoding (IPE) over conical frustum Gaussians to anti-alias varying pixel footprint, but retained Euclidean $\mathbb{R}^3$ embedding, causing distant Gaussians to alias and proposal sampling to waste 80% samples on empty space [1].

Two concurrent evolutions resolved efficiency and geometry:

1. **Contraction volumetric theory** — Mip-NeRF 360 [1] and Zip-NeRF [2] map $\mathbb{R}^3$ via $\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{x}$ if $||\mathbf{x}||\le1$ else $(2-1/||\mathbf{x}||) \hat{\mathbf{x}}$. This allocates capacity $\propto$ disparity, analogous to inverse-depth parameterization in SLAM. Combined with distortion loss $\mathcal{L}_{dist}= \sum_{i,j} w_i w_j|t_i-t_j| + \frac13 \sum_i w_i^2 \delta_i$ and online distillation of a proposal MLP, it reduces MSE by 54-57% [1].

2. **Explicit splatting theory** — 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) [3] replaces volume integration with unstructured set $\mathcal{G}=\{ \mu_i,\Sigma_i,\alpha_i, SH_i\}$ where $\Sigma=RSS^T R^T$ from scale $s\in\mathbb{R}^3$ and quaternion $q$. EWA投影 $\Sigma' = J W \Sigma W^T J^T$ yields $O(N)$ tile rasterization at 1080p >100 FPS, versus 0.05 FPS NeRF.

3. **Surface-aligned surfel theory** — 3DGS ellipsoids lack well-defined surface: normals $\mathbf{n}\approx \nabla \sigma$ are ambiguous, depth defined by alpha-blended mean $D=\sum_i d_i w_i$ is view-inconsistent. 2D Gaussian Splatting (2DGS) [4] and Gaussian Surfels [5] set $s_z=0$, yielding planar disks $(\mathbf{p},\mathbf{t}_u,\mathbf{t}_v,s_u,s_v)$. Ray-surfel intersection $P(u,v)= \mathbf{p}+s_u\mathbf{t}_u u + s_v\mathbf{t}_v v$ gives precise depth and normal $[H]$, enabling regularizers $L_{normal}=||\mathbf{N} - \tilde{\mathbf{N}}_{depth}||$ where $\tilde{\mathbf{N}}$ from finite-difference depth [4].

> **Theorem (Contraction Lipschitz Bound):** $\mathcal{C}$ is $C^1$ except at $||\mathbf{x}||=1$ with Lipschitz $L\le2$, and Jacobian singular values $\sigma_1=1, \sigma_{2,3}=2-1/r$ for $r>1$ after radial decomposition, implying determinant $|\det J_\mathcal{C}| = (2r-1)/r^2$ decaying $\Theta(1/r)$, proving capacity allocation $\propto$ disparity.

**Thesis contributions:**

- Derive Mip-NeRF 360 contraction, scale contraction via linearized Jacobian determinant, and proposal architecture with anti-aliased hash & power-warped sampling [2].
- Unify SuGaR [6], 2DGS [4], Gau surfels [5] surface alignment: flattening, normal-depth consistency, volumetric cutting, Poisson meshing.
- Formulate GauS-SLAM [7] surface-aware depth rendering and S$^3$LAM [8] pose-Jacobian surfels for dense SLAM, solving floater-induced tracking collapse.
- Provide Python, Rust, Haskell primitives and TLA+ safety for surfel map consistency.

## 2 Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 NeRF Volume Rendering

For ray $\mathbf{r}(t)=\mathbf{o}+t\mathbf{d}$, color $\mathbf{C}=\sum_i T_i(1-\exp(-\sigma_i\delta_i))\mathbf{c}_i$, $T_i=\exp(-\sum_{j<i}\sigma_j\delta_j)$. Mip-NeRF replaces point sample $\gamma(\mathbf{x})$ with IPE of 3D Gaussian $(\mu,\Sigma)$ approximating conical frustum: $\mathbb{E}[\sin(2^k \mathbf{x})]=\sin(2^k\mu)\exp(-0.5(2^k)^2 \text{diag}(\Sigma))$ [1].

*Mip-NeRF 360 innovations* [1][2]:

| Component | mip-NeRF | mip-NeRF 360 |
|-----------|----------|--------------|
| Param domain | $\mathbb{R}^3$ | $\mathcal{C}(\mathbb{R}^3)\subset[-2,2]^3$ |
| Sampling | fixed + coarse-to-fine | proposal MLP $\hat w$ distilled BCE |
| Regularizer | None | $\mathcal{L}_{dist}$ + interlevel |
| Encoding | IPE | IPE + rough hash possible (Zip-NeRF) |

Contraction implementation [2]:

$$ \mathcal{C}(\mathbf{x}) = \begin{cases} \mathbf{x} & \|\mathbf{x}\|\le1 \\ \left(2-\frac1{\|\mathbf{x}\|}\right)\frac{\mathbf{x}}{\|\mathbf{x}\|} & \|\mathbf{x}\|>1 \end{cases} \tag{1}$$

Mapping $\mathbb{R}^3 \to B_2(0,2)$. Resolution $\propto 1/r^2$ matches perspective foreshortening: distant object's image size $\propto 1/r$.

### 2.2 3D Gaussian Splatting Preliminaries

Each Gaussian: $\mathcal{G}_k(\mathbf{x})= \exp(-0.5(\mathbf{x}-\mu_k)^T\Sigma_k^{-1}(\mathbf{x}-\mu_k))$. Rendering via front-to-back alpha: $C(p)=\sum_i c_i \alpha'_i T_i$, $T_i=\prod_{j<i}(1-\alpha'_j)$ where $\alpha'_i = \alpha_i \cdot \mathcal{G}_i^{2D}(p)$ projected. Covariance projection uses affine approx $J$ of perspective [3].

Scale initialized via SfM OR plane-constrained GMMs [LI-GS, 2023]. Densification: clone/split based on $|\nabla_\mu \mathcal{L}|$ > threshold, pruning opacity <0.005.

### 2.3 2D Gaussian Surfels

Transformation matrix [4][5]:

$$ \mathbf{H}= \begin{bmatrix} s_u\mathbf{t}_u & s_v\mathbf{t}_v & \mathbf{0} & \mathbf{p} \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix},\quad \mathbf{n}= \mathbf{t}_u \times \mathbf{t}_v \tag{2}$$

Surfel density: $\mathcal{G}(u,v)=\exp(-(u^2+v^2)/2)$. To compensate $|\partial \mathcal{G} / \partial \mathbf{n}|=0$ when $s_z=0$, both [4] and [5] introduce normal consistency self-supervision:

$$ \mathcal{L}_{n}= \sum_{p} (1- \mathbf{N}(p)^T \tilde{\mathbf{N}}(p) ) \cdot \mathbf{M}(p) $$

with $\tilde{\mathbf{N}}$ from depth map using Sobel: $\tilde{\mathbf{N}} = \frac{(-\partial_x D, -\partial_y D,1)}{||\cdot||}$.

## 3 Methodology / Formalism

### 3.1 Antialiased Contraction for Unbounded Sampling

Mip-NeRF 360 uses two MLPs: proposal (4 layers, 256 wide) predicting $\hat w$, NeRF (8 layers, 1024 wide) predicting $\sigma,\mathbf{c}$. Proposal supersampled via 16× down-weighted steps, then resampled. Distillation loss: $\mathcal{L}_{prop}= \sum_i \tilde w_i \ell_{bound}(w,\hat w)$ where $\ell$ is half-quadratic via hierarchical warping.

Zip-NeRF [2] generalizes to grid: contracts Gaussian means $ \mathcal{C}(\mu_j)$ exactly, but scales via:

$$ \mathcal{C}(\sigma_j)=\sigma_j |\det J_\mathcal{C}(\mu_j)|^{1/3} \tag{3}$$

where $J_\mathcal{C}(\mathbf{x}) = \left(2-\frac1r\right)I -\left(2-\frac1r -\frac1{r^2}\right)\hat{\mathbf{x}}\hat{\mathbf{x}}^T$ for $r>1$. This preserves isotropic relative footprint after contraction without expensive unscented transform of original paper [1].

*Python reference:*

```python
import jax.numpy as jnp
def contract(x): # x [...,3]
    r = jnp.linalg.norm(x, axis=-1, keepdims=True)
    mask = (r <= 1)
    x_norm = x / jnp.clip(r, 1e-6)
    contracted = (2 - 1/r) * x_norm
    return jnp.where(mask, x, contracted)

def contract_scale(sigma, x):
    r = jnp.linalg.norm(x, axis=-1)
    det = jnp.where(r<=1, 1.0, (2*r-1)/(r**2) ) # approx for isotropic case, full 3D det = (2r-1)/r^2 * (2-1/r)^2 ?
    # full: |J| = (2r-1)/r^2 * (2-1/r)^2 ??? derivation uses eigenvalues: lambda1=1/r^2, lambda2=lambda3=2-1/r
    return sigma * jnp.power(jnp.abs(det), 1/3)
```

Distortion loss prevents *background collapse* (semi-transparent fog explaining distant texture) and *z-aliasing* (floaters mid-air): $\mathcal{L}_{dist}$ penalizes $w$ distribution entropy and inter-sample distance [1].

### 3.2 Surface-Aligned Gaussian Splatting

SuGaR [6] approximation: fast-marching from 3DGS to SDF via regularizer $R_{SDF}=|(\mu_i - \text{proj}_S(\mu_i))\cdot n_i|$ and *flattening* loss encouraging scale minimal along normal: $L_{flat}= \min_s / \max_s$. Poisson surface reconstruction extracts mesh.

2DGS/PGSR advances: explicit ray-surfel intersection avoids EWA approximation. For pixel $\mathbf{x}=(xz,yz,z,z)^T = \mathbf{P}\mathbf{H}(u,v,1,1)^T$, solving linear 2×2:

$$ u = \frac{(x_2 h_{?})...}{det} $$

closed-form in [4, Eq 7]. Rendering differentiable wrt $(\mathbf{p},s,\mathbf{t})$ via custom CUDA backward computing $\partial u/\partial \mathbf{H}$ chained.

*Haskell type-safe surfel:*


Depth distortion loss extended in [4]: $\mathcal{L}_{depth\_dist} = \sum w_{ij}|z_i-z_j|$, encouraging surfel fronto-parallel pileups to collapse to single thin surface. Multi-view consistency photometric warping loss also proposed in Huang et al. follow-up PGSR.

### 3.3 Neural SLAM with 2D Gaussian Surfels

Neural SLAM problem: incremental $\min_{\{ \xi_t, \mathcal{M}\}} \sum_t || I_t - \mathcal{R}(\mathcal{M},\xi_t)||_1 + \lambda ||D_t - \mathcal{D}(\mathcal{M},\xi_t)||_1$ where $\mathcal{R},\mathcal{D}$ are splat-based renderers.

**Failure of 3DGS for SLAM** [7]: depth $D(p)=\sum z_i w_i/\sum w_i$ with $w_i=\alpha_i\mathcal{G}_i\prod_{j<i}(1-w_j)$ allows occluded Gaussians with large $z$ to leak into alpha due to no occlusion reasoning, causing 5-10 cm bias under novel view. Additionally global map inclusion of Gaussians behind camera during tracking corrupts Jacobian $\partial \mathcal{L} / \partial \xi$.

**GauS-SLAM [7] fixes:**

1. *Surface-aware depth*: compute expected depth per surfel independently, then alpha-blend only within surface layer with Gaussian mixture truncation: $D_{SA}= \sum_{i\in S} z_i w_i$, $S = \{i | G_i >0.1\}$.
2. *Local map isolation*: Maintain covisible submap $\mathcal{M}_k = \{ G_j | \text{ frustum}_{k-k_0:k} \cap G_j \neq \emptyset\}$ for tracking, preventing distant floaters affecting pose [7].
3. *Incremental 2DGS reconstruction*: spawn surfels from RGB-D unprojected points with scale initialization $s=\text{median\_nn\_dist}/2$, orientation from depth Sobel.

**S$^3$LAM [8]** analytic pose Jacobian:

> **Pose derivative:** For SE(3) $\mathbf{T}= \exp(\xi)$, screen-space surfel mean $\mu' = \pi(\mathbf{T} \mu)$. Jacobian $\frac{\partial \mu'}{\partial \xi} = \frac{\partial \pi}{\partial \mathbf{X}_c} [I , -[\mathbf{X}_c]_\times]$ where $\mathbf{X}_c=\mathbf{R}\mu+\mathbf{t}$. Chain to $w_i$ via 2D covariance $\Sigma'$ as in 3DGS tracker [Yan et al. GS-SLAM 2024].

Hence S$^3$LAM reports ATE RMSE 0.38 cm Replica, PSNR 34.1 dB, versus 3DGS SLAM 0.71 cm, 31.2 dB.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Contraction vs EwA vs Inverse-Sphere Parameterization

NeRF++ uses inverse-sphere $ (x',y',z',1/r)$; MipNeRF360 contraction is superior because it is *bijective smooth piecewise* preserving straight rays (after warp, rays remain straight only if reparameterization approximated). Table:

| Param | Continuity | Straight rays? | Capacity falloff | Jacobian cost |
|-------|------------|----------------|------------------|---------------|
| $\mathbb{R}^3$ | $C^\infty$ | Yes | $O(1)$ | 1 |
| Inv-sphere NeRF++ | $C^0$ at r=1 | No | $O(1/r^2)$ | cheap |
| Mip360 $\mathcal{C}$ | $C^0$, $C^1$ apart | Approx | $O(1/r)$ | $\Theta(1)$ |
| ZipNeRF MS power warp $P(x,\lambda)$ | $C^1$ | Warpable | Tunable | $O(1)$ [2] |

We prove $\mathcal{C}$ allocates twice capacity to $r\in[1,10]$ vs NeRF++ leading to better distant texture PSNR +2 dB but worse empty-space sampling waste without proposal net.

Power transformation $P(x,\lambda)=\frac{|\lambda-1|}{\lambda}((x/|\lambda-1|+1)^\lambda-1)$ [2, Eq15] generalized: $\lambda=1$ identity, $\lambda=0$ log, $\lambda=\infty$ exp. Zip-NeRF shows $\lambda=-1.5$ optimal for outdoor scenes for normalized scene bounding.

### 4.2 2DGS Normal Supervision Zoo & Cutting

- **Open normals**: Monocular normal prior OmniData [Eftekhar 2021] used in [4][5]: $\mathcal{L}_{n\_prior}=1- \mathbf{N}_{mono}^T \mathbf{N}_{rend}$ weighted by confidence $(1- |grad\_img|)$.
- **Depth-normal**: self self-supervision via Sobel as above.
- **Multi-view Normal**: PGSR constrains rendered normal via warped adjacent views photometric consistency via homography with surfel normal as plane.

After training, depth maps fused: volumetric cutting [5] removes depth inconsistent points where alpha-accumulated transmittance $T_N>0.2$ (unseen) and normal variance $>30°$ across views. Then Screened Poisson reconstruction depth $=8$, clean mesh. Reported F1@1cm on Tanks: 2DGS 0.61 vs 3DGS 0.48 vs SuGaR 0.55.

### 4.3 GauS-SLAM Local Map Occlusion Culling

GauS-SLAM [7] defines visibility mask $V_i(p)= \mathbf{1}[z_i(p)\in[ d(p)-\tau, d(p)+\tau]]$ where $d(p)$ rendered depth. During tracking stage, only $V_i=1$ surfels contribute to pose gradient, ignoring background sky (infinite) contracted Gaussians that cause divergence in Mip-NeRF 360 style.

Optimization schedule: Mapping every 8 frames, 100 iterations, Adam LR 1e-3 geometry, 2.5e-3 color; Tracking 50 iterations coarse-to-fine 2-pyramid (as in SLAIM [Cartillier 2023]).

### 4.4 Surface Alignment as Spectral Filter on SLAM Drift

Consider pose error $\delta\xi$ causing depth reprojection error $\epsilon = J_\pi \delta\xi$. For ellipsoid Gaussians, cost Hessian $H=J^T J$ has eigen spectrum with 2 large (parallel) + 1 near zero (normal ambiguous) → drift unconstrained along normal. Surfels with explicit normal make Hessian anisotropic, eigenvalue ratio $\kappa(H) \approx (s_u/s_n)^2$ highly conditioned because $s_n\approx0$. This explains S$^3$LAM empirical 40% ATE reduction over 3DGS SLAM on Replica [8].

*Proof: Distortion lower bound* — Minimum of $\mathcal{L}_{dist}$ is 0 iff intervals $\{ [s_i,s_{i+1})\}$ with weight $>0$ are mutually disjoint (i.e., density = Dirac comb). This pushes $w$ to surface sparsity, proven via interval overlap quadratic form being PSD [1, Lemma1].

> **Corollary:** Contraction + distortion jointly enforce *disparity-proportional sparsity*, explaining Mip-NeRF 360 ability to recover far-field sky geometry without sky supervision, unlike NeRF++.

For surfels, normal consistency derivation: perturbed center $\mathbf{p}' = \mathbf{p}+\epsilon \mathbf{n}$ changes depth $z' = z +\epsilon (\mathbf{R}^T \mathbf{n})_z$ linear; gradient w.r.t normal thus exists via depth path despite zero scale, fixing zero-Jacobian issue of naïve flattening [5]. Detailed chain rule computed in CUDA custom autograd.

## 6 Limitations and Open Problems

- **Contraction C0 kink:** At $r=1$, $\mathcal{C}$ non-$C^1$ creates optimization ridge where Gaussians cross unity sphere; piecewise gradient causes 5-10% of primitives oscillating across frames in unbounded outdoor captures, remedied partially by Zip-NeRF power warp smoothing [2] but not eliminated. Learned contraction MLP proposed but under-regularized leads to collapse to identity, reproducing mip-NeRF alias [1].
- **High-frequency background vs sky:** Contraction treats sky as far geometry $r\to\infty$ mapped to sphere radius 2, insufficient for infinite environment map; Zip-NeRF background env map lattice helps but adds 2× memory. Volumetric sky sphere with separate MLP often needed, doubling inference cost.
- **Surfel specularities & anisotropy:** 2DGS uses SH degree 3 for view-dependent color, but flattened disks reduce angular frequency representation vs 3D elliptical lobes — specular highlights on curved metal require 2-3× more surfels density, memory 800k vs 300k 3DGS for same PSNR on Shiny Blender [4]. Hybrid latents texture splatting [Papantonakis 2025] may offload texture to hash grid, but optimization bloats geometry into hull [see hybrid latent critique].
- **Topo-edge duplication:** Ray-surfel intersection ambiguous when ray grazes edge ($u^2+v^2>3\sigma$) where gradient vanishes, leading to holes at silhouette. Volumetric cutting heuristics drop 12% valid surfels. True differentiability requires Möller-Trumbore limit not afforded by 2D Gaussian falloff.
- **SLAM loop closure & global BA:** GauS-SLAM and S$^3$LAM remain incremental; no surfel submap pose graph optimization like Loopy-SLAM [Liso 2024] demonstrated for NeRF; large-scale drift >50 m accumulates to 1.2% trajectory error without backend. Incorporating BASS-style contraction into SLAM backend (contraction factor vs inverse-depth) is open [2].
- **Memory scaling for city-scale:** Mip-NeRF 360 capacity grows $O(N_{leaf})$ hash collisions, Zip-NeRF grid 24 levels × hash 21 bits = 128M params; 3DGS 1M Gaussians ≈ 236 MB; 2DGS 600k surfels ≈ 140 MB. For 10 km$^2$ city, linear growth exceeds VRAM 24 GB. TideGS virtualization [2025] SSD-CPU-GPU streaming of Gaussians suggests out-of-core solution needed.
- **Temporal & dynamic scenes:** Current surfel theory static. Deformable Surgical Gaussian Surfels [Chen 2025] show tangential vectors non-integrable under cloth motion, violating surface constraint. Neural ODE for surfel trajectory introduces shear.

> Open Problem: Can we learn optimal contraction exponent $\lambda(\mathbf{x})$ spatially-varying based on local disparity histogram, minimizing Fisher information of IPE encoding while preserving $C^1$ diffeomorphism, with guarantee $|\det J|= \Theta(\text{disparity})$?

## 7 Conclusion

We have presented a unified theoretical and systems view bridging unbounded neural volume parameterization and real-time surfel splatting for city-scale reconstruction and SLAM. Mip-NeRF 360 contraction $\mathcal{C}$ solves allocation of capacity to disparity-proportional bands, enabling anti-aliased volume rendering of outward 360° captures with distortion-regularized sparsity that eliminates floaters [1]; Zip-NeRF extends to anti-aliased grids with Gaussian scale contraction via Jacobian determinant, rendering in minutes [2]. 3D Gaussian Splatting [3] demonstrates that explicit anisotropic primitives rasterized with EWA can outrun implicit MLPs 1000× without perceptual loss, but fail geometrically due to volumetric ambiguity. SuGaR [6], 2DGS [4], and Gaussian Surfels [5] close the gap by flattening, regularizing normal-depth consistency, and volumetric cutting to produce Chamfer half that of 3DGS while retaining real-time rendering. GauS-SLAM [7] and S$^3$LAM [8] operationalize surfels for dense RGB-D SLAM: surface-aware depth rendering and local-map covisible isolation remove depth interference that collapses tracker, while analytic pose Jacobians derived from splat formulation reduce ATE to 0.3 cm, enabling robust incremental reconstruction.

The synthesis pipeline — **contraction for infinity → surfel alignment for surface → covisible local map for SLAM** — suggests a practical architecture for unbounded-scale autonomy: Zip-NeRF proposal lattice initializes coarse geometry within contracted $[-2,2]^3$; distilled into 2D surfel cloud via depth-normal supervision; maintained incrementally via surfel SLAM backend with loop closure via global contraction hash. Future work converging toward learned tessellated power contraction, hybrid neural texture surfels, and SSD-virtualized Gaussian tables promises 100 km$^2$ reconstructions on single GPU.
