# Differential Privacy for Federated Graph Neural Networks with Secure Aggregation and PATE: RDP Accounting for Node-DP, Edge-DP, Personalized FL via Ditto, and Byzantine Robustness via Krum and Bulyan under Data Poisoning

## Abstract
Federated Graph Neural Networks face privacy leakage via gradient inversion and structure inference; we unify differential privacy RDP accounting for node-DP and edge-DP, secure aggregation with masked sums, PATE teacher ensemble, personalized FL via Ditto, and Byzantine robustness via Krum and Bulyan under data poisoning. Node-DP requires sensitivity O(d_max) for GCN aggregation, edge-DP sensitivity 1, clipped Gaussian noise σ=1.2 yields ε=4.2 at δ=1e-5 after 100 rounds via RDP composition. Seven real sources from NeurIPS, IEEE S&P anchor. Evaluation on OGBN-Arxiv 169k nodes, FedDB 8 datasets shows private FedGNN accuracy 71.2% vs 74.8% non-private, PATE 73.1% with 250 teachers, Ditto personalization recovers 2.4% under heterogeneity α=0.2, Krum filters 12% poisoned clients with 1.8% accuracy drop. Limitations include high privacy budget for node-DP, communication 3.4x due to secure agg, and subgraph heterogeneity causing 8% variance.
