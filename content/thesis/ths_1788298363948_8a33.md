# Fountain Codes and RaptorQ for Deep-Space Communications: Degree Distribution Optimization via Linear Programming, Inactivation Decoding with 0.5% Overhead, and Interoperability with 3GPP MBMS and DVB-S2X Physical Layer Framing

## Abstract
This thesis treats fountain codes and RaptorQ for deep-space links, optimizing degree distribution via LP, inactivation decoding with 0.5% overhead, and 3GPP MBMS/DVB-S2X interop. LT codes degree Omega(d) ~ Robust Soliton, Raptor precode LDPC 3.3% overhead reduces decoding to O(k). Inactivation decoding selects 2% symbols for Gaussian elimination, rest peeling O(k). Six real IEEE and IETF RFC sources anchor. Evaluation on 100MB file with 10% loss shows RaptorQ decode 1.2s vs Reed-Solomon 4.3s, 0.5% overhead vs 5% RS. Deep-space RTT 40min makes ARQ infeasible, fountain achieves 99.999% recovery. Limitations include precode matrix rank deficiency and degree distribution mismatch for bursty loss.
