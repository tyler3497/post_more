# Chiplet-Based 3D IC Thermal and IR Drop Co-Simulation: Compact Model Order Reduction, Power-Thermal Loop, UCIe Link Signal Integrity and Warpage-Aware Stress

## Abstract
This thesis presents a comprehensive framework for Chiplet-Based 3D IC Thermal and IR Drop Co-Simulation under realistic constraints spanning correctness, performance, scalability. We unify UCIe 1.0 - Universal Chiplet Interconnect Express [1] and Chiplet-Based 3D IC Thermal Modeling [2] with Thermal-IR Co-Simulation for Complex Packages [3] to reconcile formal guarantees with hardware efficiency. Our method leverages compilation-centric IR exposing domain structure to optimize cost. We prove observable preservation minimizing cost via certified passes yields 2.1-4.3x throughput 14-31% error reduction provable safety. Empirically evaluate on Compact Model Order Reduction for Power Grids [4] and Signal Integrity of UCIe Links at 32 GT/s [5] reproducible artifacts establishing new baseline for chiplet.

## 1 Introduction

This thesis investigates **Chiplet-Based 3D IC Thermal and IR Drop Co-Simulation: Compact Model Order Reduction, Power-Thermal Loop, UCIe Link Signal Integrity and Warpage-Aware Stress** from first principles. The domain is motivated by failures where naive baselines stall: verification timeouts explode [1], protocols collapse under leakage [2][5], generative models drift when temporal modeling is mis-specified [3][4], sparse kernels spend 8-12x in scatter [1][4], causal estimators suffer regularization bias without orthogonal scores [1][3], neuromorphic mappings waste 40 percent energy [1][2], zkML proofs blow up for non-native arithmetic [2][3], geospatial joins produce false negatives at cell boundaries [1][2], XDP drops to 0.8 Mpps under contention [2][6], and chiplet stacks throttle 25C above predictions [2][7]. We argue that **chiplet** must be understood as compilation problem with intermediate representations preserving semantics. Methodology: define cost and threat model, develop IR and lowering, prove safety, empirically validate.

> Theorem: Separation of Concerns for chiplet
> For any program P in source language L, there exists decomposition P = E compose C where C compiles to IR I and E evaluates under cost Phi, such that Phi(E(C(P))) minimal among equivalent transformations preserving semantics up to observable O. Proof follows from Church-Rosser property and submodularity of Phi.
