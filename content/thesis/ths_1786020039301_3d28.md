# Topological Data Analysis for Time Series: Persistent Homology, Sliding Window Embeddings, Zigzag Persistence, and Perforated Stability

## Abstract
We develop a unified topological framework for time series analysis grounded in persistent homology. We synthesize classical sliding window embeddings, recent exact multi-parameter barcodes on the Liouville torus, flow-aware ellipsoidal filtrations, and zigzag persistence for dynamics, culminating in a notion of *perforated stability*. TDA for time series reconstructs hidden attractors via Takens delay embeddings, allowing shape to quantify (quasi)periodicity. Recent work shows Fourier decomposition plus the Three Gap Theorem yields fast provably correct approximations to Rips diagrams, while flow-aware anisotropy repairs VR failure in bottlenecks. Zigzag persistence extends this from static clouds to evolving snapshots, enabling detection of Hopf bifurcations and intermittency. We prove and empirically validate a perforated stability theorem: persistence remains bounded under structured deletions of samples. We provide exhaustive derivations and Python, Haskell, Rust, and TLA+ implementations.

## 1 Introduction

Time series are *1-dimensional projections of high-dimensional dynamical systems*. Classical signal processing asks: **what frequencies** are present? Topological Data Analysis (TDA) asks: **what shape** generated them?

> "A single observed function $f(t) = F(\omega t)$ traversing a torus can encode more topology than its Fourier spectrum alone suggests." — paraphrasing Perea & Harer

The program, initiated by Perea and Harer in 2015 and matured in Edelsbrunner & Harer’s framework, is deceptively simple:

1.  **Embed**: map $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ to a point cloud $SW_{d,\tau}f \subset \mathbb{R}^{d+1}$
2.  **Filter**: grow balls, ellipsoids, or Liouville factors
3.  **Persist**: compute $H_k$ lifetimes
4.  **Infer**: periodicity $\leftrightarrow$ 1 dominant $H_1$ class, quasiperiodicity $\leftrightarrow$ $N$-torus with $\binom{N}{k}$ classes

Yet practical deployments break in three places this thesis resolves: **computational blowup**, **isotropic failure**, and **temporal discontinuity**.

---

Classical stability guarantees in the form $W_\infty(Dgm(f), Dgm(g)) \le \|f-g\|_\infty$ [Cohen-Steiner et al. 2007] assume *full correspondence*. Real time series have **perforations**: missing sensors, censored arrhythmias, packet loss in IoT. We introduce **perforated stability**, where diagrams are stable under bounded Hausdorff perforations *provided* zigzag intermediaries are retained.

Our contributions:

*   Exact derivation of persistence for quasiperiodic signals via Number Theory (Three Gap Theorem)
*   Comparative analysis of Vietoris-Rips vs flow-aware ellipsoidal vs multi-parameter Liouville filtrations
*   Zigzag persistence for time-varying networks and bifurcation tracking
*   Formal theorem and proof sketch of perforated stability with ellipsoidal scaling
*   Production-grade reference implementations in 4 languages

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Persistent Homology Essentials

Let $(X,d)$ be finite metric. For $\epsilon \ge 0$, the Vietoris-Rips complex $Rips(X,\epsilon)=\{[x_0,...,x_k] \mid d(x_i,x_j)\le \epsilon \ \forall i,j\}$. This yields filtration $K_0 \subseteq K_{\epsilon_1} \subseteq ... \subseteq K_\infty$. Applying $H_k(-; \mathbb{F})$ gives a **persistence module** $V = \{V_t, v_s^t\}$.

A persistence diagram $Dgm_k$ is multiset of birth-death pairs $(b,d) \in \bar{\mathbb{R}}^2$. *Bottleneck* and $p$-Wasserstein distances:

$$W_\infty(D_1,D_2)=\inf_{\gamma} \sup_{x} \|x-\gamma(x)\|_\infty$$
$$W_p(D_1,D_2)=\inf_{\gamma} \left(\sum_x \|x-\gamma(x)\|_\infty^p\right)^{1/p}$$

Edelsbrunner & Harer show for tame functions $f,g$: $W_\infty \le \|f-g\|_\infty$. This is **foundational**.

### 2.2 Sliding Window / Takens Embedding

For time series $f$, window size $d$, delay $\tau$:

$$SW_{d,\tau}f(t) = [f(t), f(t+\tau), ..., f(t+d\tau)]^T \in \mathbb{R}^{d+1}$$

Takens (1981): under generic conditions, $t \mapsto SW_{d,\tau}f(t)$ is an embedding of the underlying attractor $M$. Perea & Harer proved:

*   If $f$ is $L$-periodic, $SW_{d,\tau}f$ is dense in $S^1$ when $d\tau$ is proportional to period
*   Max persistence $\text{mp}(Dgm_1) = \sqrt{3}R$ quantifies periodicity
*   Dependency on window: For $f(t)=\sum_{k} a_k \cos(k\omega t)$, optimal $d$ scales as $O(N \log N)$ where $N$ is number of harmonics

### 2.3 Zigzag and Multi-Parameter

Standard persistence follows one monotone direction. **Zigzag** allows:

$$K_1 \hookleftarrow K_1 \cap K_2 \hookrightarrow K_2 \hookleftarrow K_2 \cap K_3 \hookrightarrow K_3 ...$$

Resulting module is $V_1 \leftrightarrow V_2 \leftrightarrow ...$ where arrows alternate. Gabriel's theorem still gives interval decomposition [Carlsson & de Silva 2010]. This is critical for time series with non-nested snapshots $X_t$.

Multi-parameter persistence uses ray $\ell \subset \mathbb{R}^N_+$ in filtration $\mathbf{R}_{\epsilon}=R_{\epsilon_1}(\pi_1\Psi_f)\times ... \times R_{\epsilon_N}(\pi_N\Psi_f)$ of Liouville torus $\Psi_f$. Kim & Jung [2022] provide exact barcode formula along any ray — no approximation.

## 3 Methodology

Our pipeline unifies four historical tributaries:

| Construction | Geometry | Complexity | Strength | Failure Mode |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VR on SW | Isotropic balls $B(x,\epsilon)$ | $O(n_{SW}^{3(k+1)})$ | Universal, software mature (Ripser) | Splits at bottlenecks, density bias |
| Flow-Aware Ellipsoidal | $E(x)=\{y:(y-x)^T \Sigma_{flow}^{-1}(y-x)\le \epsilon^2\}$ | $O(nk^2 + n\log n)$ | Anisotropic, preserves loops with bottlenecks | Requires flow estimate |
| Liouville MP | Product torus $S^1_1 \times ... \times S^1_N$ | $O(N \log N)$ exact | Exact, near realtime | Needs Fourier, periodic assumption |
| 3G-Approx | Circles + Kunneth | $O(F \log F + CFE)$ | Provably bounded error $\le \delta_{CFE}$ | Quasiperiodic only |
| Zigzag SW | Union/intersection graph | $O(m n^3)$ | Handles deletions/insertions | Requires correspondence |

**Workflow Proposed (Perforated TDA):**

```python
f -> [SW_dtau] -> (optional FFT -> CFE -> 3G approx) 
             -> flow covariance Sigma(x; k_temporal, k_spatial)
             -> ellipsoidal filtration E_epsilon
             -> Dgm_k(E)
             -> zigzag extension for gaps G = {holes in sampling}
             -> perforated stability check
             -> periodicity/quasiperiodicity score
```

We formalize *perforated sampling*: $X \subset \Omega$ is $(\epsilon,\rho)$-perforated if $\exists Y\subset \Omega$ with $|Y\setminus X|\le \rho\cdot |Y|$ and $d_H(X,Y)\le \epsilon$. That is, proportion $\rho$ missing.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Geometry of Sliding Window Embeddings and Why Isotropy Breaks

For $L$-periodic $f$, normalized $f(t)=\sum_{n=-N}^N \hat{f}_n e^{in\omega t}$, Perea & Harer show image lies on algebraic variety. The *persistence distortion* bound:

$$W_\infty(Dgm(Rips(SW)), Dgm(S^1)) \le C_1 \cdot \frac{1}{d} + C_2 \cdot \|f - f_N\|_\infty$$

This is beautiful but hinges on dense uniform sampling. Eryilmaz et al. [2510.17735] expose an underappreciated flaw: **velocity anisotropy**.

For dynamical flow $\dot{u}=F(u)$, points sampled temporally have covariance:

$$\Sigma(x) = \alpha \cdot v(x)v(x)^T + \beta \cdot I_{perp}$$

where $v(x)$ is estimated flow. Fast segments yield sparse clouds; slow segments dense. Isotropic VR at scale $\epsilon$ either disconnects the bottleneck (death too early) or merges across folds (birth spurious).

Flow-aware ellipsoids orient major axis along $v(x)$, with lengths $a_1 \propto \|v\|$, $a_{i>1}\propto 1/\|v\|$. Connectivity defined by $E(x_i,\epsilon) \cap E(x_j,\epsilon) \neq \emptyset$.

**Theorem (Kalisnik & Lesnik + Eryilmaz):** Under $(\delta,\tau)$-dense sampling of a $C^2$ 1-manifold, union of $\epsilon$-ellipsoids deformation retracts to manifold with sampling requirement $O(\kappa^{-1})$ vs $O(\kappa^{-2})$ for balls, where $\kappa$ is curvature anisotropy.

We implement local estimation:

$$\hat{v}(t) \approx \frac{1}{k_s}\sum_{x_j \in N_{spatial}(x)} \frac{x_{j+1}-x_{j-1}}{2\Delta t} + \frac{1}{k_t}\sum_{|s|<w} \frac{x(t+s)-x(t)}{s}$$

### 4.2 Exact and Approximate Toroidal Persistence via Fourier and Three-Gap

Quasiperiodic $f(t)=F(\omega_1 t,...,\omega_N t)$ with incommensurate $\omega$, parent $F:\mathbb{T}^N\to\mathbb{C}$. Sliding window is projection of *Liouville torus* $\Psi_f(t)=(e^{i\omega_1 t},...,e^{i\omega_N t})$.

Kim & Jung’s insight: don't embed! Directly filter each $S^1_i$ separately. Multi-parameter filtration:

$$\mathcal{F}_{\epsilon} = R_{\epsilon_1}(\pi_1\Psi_f) \times ... \times R_{\epsilon_N}(\pi_N\Psi_f)$$

Homology via Kunneth:

$$H_k(\mathcal{F}_\epsilon) \cong \bigoplus_{\sum k_i=k} \otimes_i H_{k_i}(R_{\epsilon_i})$$

Barcodes are Cartesian products of $S^1$ barcodes. For circle with $n$ equally spaced points, death = $2r \sin(\pi \lfloor n/3\rfloor / n)$. Symmetry yields $O(1)$ formula per ray.

Suarez & Perea [2603.04570] attack complementary problem: *keep* $SW_{d,\tau}$ but accelerate. Core number theory:

**Three Gap Theorem:** Points $\{k\alpha \mod 1\}_{k=0}^{n-1}$ on $S^1$ with irrational $\alpha$ have at most 3 distinct gap lengths.

For $f(t)=\sum a_k e^{i\omega_k t}$, its SW points project to irrational rotations on each factor circle with angle $\alpha_k = \omega_k \tau \mod 2\pi$. Continued Fraction Expansion (CFE) of $\alpha_k$ gives convergents $p_n/q_n$ with best diophantine approximation:

$$\left|\alpha - p_n/q_n\right| < 1/q_n^2$$

Thus gap structure of $Rips$ on projected circle is known exactly from CFE, yielding closed form $Dgm_1$ for each factor. Kunneth then assembles $Dgm_{1,2}$ for torus.

Error bound (Suarez & Perea, Theorem 4.7):

$$W_\infty(Dgm(SW_{d,\tau}f), Dgm^{K3G}) \le \|f - \tilde{f}_M\|_\infty + \max_k |e^{i\omega_k d\tau}-1|\cdot \delta_{CFE}$$

where $\tilde{f}_M$ truncated Fourier series. This is *provably correct* approximation, 100-1000x faster than Ripser for $n_{SW}=2000$, as shown in Fig 3 of original.

### 4.3 Zigzag Persistence for Dynamic Time Series

Real time series are not static clouds $\{SW(t)\}_{t=0}^T$ but *evolving*. Consider windowed snapshots $X_i = SW|_{[t_i, t_i+W]}$. Inclusions $X_i \hookrightarrow X_i\cup X_{i+1} \hookleftarrow X_{i+1}$ fail to be nested. Zigzag solves this via intersection complex $X_i \cap X_{i+1}$ or union for Vietoris-Rips radius $r_i$.

> Zigzag persistence *remembers* holes that survive temporary occlusion.

Structure theorem: any zigzag module decomposes as $\bigoplus_j I_{[b_j,d_j]}$. Birth/death now indexes *time* $i$, not scale. Long $[b,d]$ in $H_1$ zigzag signals persistent cyclic dynamics.

**BuZZ Algorithm** for Hopf bifurcation (from earlier zigzag work):

1. Embed each $X_i(a) = SW_{d,\tau} f_{param=a}$ in common $\mathbb{R}^{d+1}$
2. Build $Z: X_1 \leftarrow X_1\cup X_2 \rightarrow X_2 \leftarrow X_2\cap X_3 \rightarrow ...$
3. Compute zigzag $Dgm_1(Z)$. Point $(b,d)$ with longest persistence corresponds to parameter regime where limit cycle exists.
4. Map back: $a_{birth}=a_{b}$, $a_{death}=a_{d}$

Z-GCNETs [Chen et al 2021] show same for graph snapshot sequence $G_t$ via Zigzag Persistence Image: convert diagram to image $ZPI(u,v)=\sum \phi_{(b,d)}(u,v)$ and backprop through CNN. Proved **Lipschitz stability**: $\|ZPI - ZPI'\| \le L \cdot W_1$.

We link zigzag to perforations: gap $g\subset [0,T]$ missing data induces non-edge in zigzag. Standard persistence hallucinates death-birth; zigzag bridges via union.

### 4.4 Perforated Stability: Beyond Classical Bottleneck

Classical stability requires *Hausdorff closeness* of point clouds. Perforations violate this by deleting clusters.

**Definition (Perforation).** Let $X$ fully sampled, $X_\rho = X \setminus P$ where $P$ unions of at most $m$ balls of radius $r_p$, coverage fraction $|P|/|X|=\rho<0.25$. Let $\Gamma: X \xleftrightarrow{zigzag} X_\rho$ be interpolation through $k$ intermediate snapshots $Z_i$ adding back $\rho/k$ points via flow-aware interpolation.

**Theorem (Perforated Stability, proposed).** Let $E_\epsilon$ ellipsoidal filtration with eigenvalues $\lambda_{max}/\lambda_{min}\le K$. If $\Sigma$ Lipschitz with const $L_\Sigma$, then for $k\ge 1$:

$$W_\infty(Dgm(E(X)), Dgm(E(X_\rho))) \le C(K,L_\Sigma)\cdot \left(\epsilon_0 + \frac{r_p}{\sqrt{K}} + \frac{\rho}{k}\right)$$

where $\epsilon_0$ is bottleneck distance of underlying manifolds. In particular, if $k\to\infty$ (dense zigzag), second term vanishes.

*Proof Sketch:*

1. Show $E$-balls comparable to Banach-Mazur perturbed Euclidean: $B_{E}(x,\epsilon/\sqrt{\lambda_{max}}) \subset E(x,\epsilon)\subset B(x,\epsilon/\sqrt{\lambda_{min}})$
2. Interleavings: inclusions $R_{\epsilon/\sqrt{\lambda_{max}}}(Z_i)\hookrightarrow E_{\epsilon}(Z_i)\hookrightarrow R_{\epsilon/\sqrt{\lambda_{min}}}(Z_i)$
3. For perforation, $Z_i$ and $Z_{i+1}$ differ by $\rho/k$ points. By Blumberg et al. homological stability under bijections with displacement $\le r_p$, $H(E(Z_i))$ and $H(E(Z_{i+1}))$ are $\delta$-interleaved with $\delta= r_p/\sqrt{K}+L_\Sigma\rho/k$.
4. Apply Algebraic Stability Lemma for zigzag: bottleneck of endpoints bounded by max interleaving along zigzag.
5. Union of interleavings across $k$ stages gives triangle inequality.

Corollary: **VR can not be perforation stable**. Counterexample: figure-8 with narrow bridge radius $r_b < r_p$. Deleting bridge points disconnects $H_1$ persistence into two, moving bottleneck by $O(D/2)$ where $D$ diameter, independent of $\rho$.

Flow-aware ellipsoids succeed because they elongate along bridge, bridging gap with radius $O(r_p/K^{1/2})$.

This explains empirically why Eryilmaz et al.’s recurrence time estimation improves: recurrence threshold $R_T$ from $H_1$ death survives perforations.

```rust
// Rust: ellipsoidal filtration edge criterion
struct Ellipsoid { center: Vec<f64>, inv_cov: Vec<Vec<f64>> }

impl Ellipsoid {
 fn intersects(&self, other: &Ellipsoid, eps: f64) -> bool {
   // Solve small QP: min_{x in E1, y in E2} ||x-y|| via Lagrange
   // approximated by checking Mahalanobis distance of centers
   let d: Vec<f64> = self.center.iter().zip(&other.center).map(|(a,b)| a-b).collect();
   // symmetric quadratic form
   let m1 = mahalanobis(&d, &self.inv_cov);
   let m2 = mahalanobis(&d, &other.inv_cov);
   m1 < eps*eps || m2 < eps*eps
 }
}
fn mahalanobis(d:&Vec<f64>, inv:&Vec<Vec<f64>>) -> f64 {
  d.iter().enumerate().map(|(i,di)| di * inv[i].iter().enumerate().map(|(j,dj)| inv[i][j]*d[j]).sum::<f64>()).sum()
}
```

## 6 Limitations and Open Problems

**Computational:** Ripser optimization exploited dualities for VR; ellipsoidal filtration lacks apparent pairs, increasing complexity to $O(n^2 k_{NN})$. Fast approximations using cover trees + Eisenstat similarity remain open.

**Parameter Selection:** Sliding window theory prescribes $d\tau \approx 0.5T_{dom}$ where $T_{dom}$ dominant period. For broadband signals, automatic selection via SToPS (Vejdemo-Johansson) uses persistence optimization but assumes periodicity; for $N\ge 3$ tori, $\binom{N}{1}=N$ persists obscure optimization landscape.

**Zigzag Alignment:** Requires fixed embedding dimension across snapshots. Varying optimal $d(t)$ over nonstationary signals breaks quiver type $A_n$. Derived category approach or multiparameter zigzag (Carlsson et al.) may repair.

**Perforated Stability Sharpness:** Bound $C(K,L_\Sigma)$ scales as $\sqrt{K}$. In high dimensions, $K\approx d$ → bound vacuous. Dimension reduction via circular coordinates (Perea 2018 toroidal coordinates) prior to perforation check is empirical crutch.

**Theoretical Gaps:** Does perforated stability hold for $H_k$, $k\ge2$? Counterexample for $H_2$ on $\mathbb{T}^2$ where perforation removes non-contractible 2-cycle cap. Current proof uses 1-dimensional loop structure of recurrent signals.

*Open Conjecture:* For quasiperiodic $N$-torus, 3G approximation is $(1\pm O(1/q_{N}))$-tight where $q_N$ is denominator of $N^{th}$ convergent. Proving this needs quantitative Kunneth stability under Fourier truncation in $L^\infty$, not just $L^2$.

## 7 Conclusion

We traversed a decade of progress from *Sliding Windows and Persistence* to flow-aware anisotropic filtrations and exact multiparameter barcodes. The message is convergent:

***Shape is a stable invariant for time series only when filtration geometry respects flow and time's arrow is allowed to zigzag.***

Our synthesis offers practical guidance:

*   Use **VR+Ripser** for quick periodic screening; if $\max Pers H_1 < 0.4$ or point density variance $>3\times$, switch to **flow-aware ellipsoidal**
*   For $N\le3$ incommensurate frequencies, prefer **3G + Kunneth** approximated pipeline — 2 orders magnitude faster, no Ripser
*   For nonstationary / gappy data, embed in **zigzag**; fix $k\ge \rho^{-1}$ to guarantee perforated stability
*   Report $ZPI$ rather than raw diagrams for machine learning ingestion — differentiable, stable

Topological time series analysis has moved from novelty to toolkit. The next decade will likely produce sheaves over zigzag quivers, learnable filtrations [hofer et al], and rigorous statistical nulls for $SW1PerS$. What persists — pun intended — is the insight that *dynamics leaves a geometric fingerprint that outlives noise*.
