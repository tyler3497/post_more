# Silicon Photonic Interconnects for Scale-Out LLM Inference: Microring Modulator Weight Banks, AWGR Wavelength Routing, Co-Packaged Optics, and Broadcast-and-Weight Architectures

## Abstract
Scale-out LLM inference is bottlenecked by interconnect bandwidth, power, and latency, not FLOPs. Electrical SerDes and pluggable optics impose 5-10 pJ/bit and limited beachfront density, throttling expert-parallel and KV-cache traffic across thousands of GPUs. This thesis analyzes silicon-photonic scale-out using microring resonator (MRR) weight banks, arrayed waveguide grating router (AWGR) wavelength routing, and co-packaged optics (CPO) as a unified substrate for both communication and wavelength-domain compute. We formalize the broadcast-and-weight protocol where each neuron's output is assigned a unique WDM carrier, weighted by tunable MRRs, and summed by photodetection, enabling O(1) matrix-vector products in propagation delay. We evaluate CPO architectures achieving 3.5× power efficiency and 80× bandwidth density uplift over electrical interconnects, AWGR-based all-to-all fabrics enabling 160+ TOPS tensor acceleration, and non-volatile MRR weight banks for KV-cache block selection in long-context inference. Empirical models show 294× CTRNN acceleration and sub-5 pJ/bit at 25 Gb/s with automated thermal stabilization over 9°C.

---

## 1 Introduction

Large language model inference at *scale-out* — 8 to 1000+ accelerators serving a single request via tensor parallelism, expert parallelism (MoE), and disaggregated KV-cache — has inverted the dominant cost: **data movement now dominates compute**. Training a 70B parameter model on conventional electrical wiring consumes months and gigawatt-hours; IBM Research projects that co-packaged optics (CPO) can reduce training time from three months to three weeks and cut energy by >5× versus mid-range electrical interconnects [1][2]. NVIDIA's Spectrum-X Photonics CPO switches demonstrate 102.4 Tb/s to 409.6 Tb/s in a single chassis with 3.5× power efficiency and 10× resiliency improvement by eliminating pluggable transceivers [3].

In parallel, silicon photonics offers more than communication: it offers *wavelength-domain compute*. Microring resonator (MRR) weight banks implement continuous-valued weighting of WDM signals, summed by total power detection — the **broadcast-and-weight** protocol [4]. A 4-node recurrent network with 16 tunable MRR weights has been shown isomorphic to a continuous-time recurrent neural network (CTRNN), with predicted 294× acceleration over CPU for ODE solving [4]. Recent extensions propose photonic similarity engines for KV-cache block selection in long-context LLM inference, converting O(N) memory-bandwidth-bound search into O(1) optical inner products via WDM broadcast [5][6].

This thesis synthesizes these threads:

* **Communication**: How CPO and AWGR-based wavelength routing break radix and power limits of electrical multi-socket boards (MSB) [7][8]
* **Compute**: How MRR weight banks and AWGR tensor cores achieve 7.3 TOPS mm⁻² and 163.8 TOPS experimental throughput [8][9]
* **System**: How they combine into a scale-out LLM inference fabric where interconnect *is* the accelerator

> **Theorem 1 (Broadcast-and-Weight Isomorphism):** A silicon photonic network consisting of N laser diodes assigned unique wavelengths λᵢ, a 1×N broadcast, M MRR weight banks with tunable transmission wᵢⱼ, balanced photodetectors, and electro-optic nonlinear modulators is dynamically isomorphic to a CTRNN of N nodes under appropriate low-pass filtering and gain conditions [4].

The remainder of this work formalizes devices, architectures, and empirical limits.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Silicon Photonic Device Primer

* **Microring Resonators (MRR):** A closed-loop waveguide of radius 5 μm yields FSR ≈ 2.4 THz near 1550 nm, accommodating 32-48 channels at 50 GHz spacing [7]. Resonance wavelength λ_res = n_eff * L / m shifts via thermo-optic (TO) heaters or carrier depletion (MOSCAP). Q-factor 5k-15k balances narrow filtering (low Vpp) against photon-lifetime bandwidth limit [10].
* **Microring Modulators (MRM):** MOSCAP MRMs gated with high-mobility conductive oxide (ITiO) achieve 0.8 Vpp drive, 11 GHz EO bandwidth, 53 fJ/bit, and clear eyes at 25 Gb/s [10]. Intel's ring-based transmitter demonstrates 8λ × 32 Gb/s/λ = 256 Gb/s/fiber with integrated DFB array, comb combiner, heater control loop, and SOA [11].
* **AWG / AWGR:** Arrayed waveguide grating routers provide cyclic wavelength routing: λₖ at input port i routes to output port (i+k) mod N. An N×N AWGR thus provides all-to-all connectivity with N wavelengths [7]. Silicon LIONS chips implement 8×8 AWGR in 1.2 mm × 3.6 mm footprint [7].

### 2.2 Scale-Out LLM Inference Bottlenecks

Contemporary LLM serving partitions work as:

1. **Tensor Parallel (TP):** All-reduce of activations every layer, latency-sensitive <20 μs
2. **Expert Parallel (MoE):** All-to-all dispatch of tokens to experts, bandwidth-heavy and sparse
3. **KV-Cache Disaggregation:** Prefill nodes stream KV blocks to decode nodes, memory-bandwidth bound
4. **Sequence Parallel:** Ring attention requires bidirectional streaming

Electrical interconnects hit two walls:

- **Beachfront density:** Electrical SerDes shoreline limited to ~50 GB/s/mm
- **Power:** Pluggable optics >10 pJ/bit, >50% of system power at scale

CPO attacks both by moving optics onto package, increasing fiber density 6× and reducing optical path loss [1].

### 2.3 Related Work

Broadcast-and-weight was proposed as a standard protocol for neuromorphic photonics compatible with mainstream silicon photonics, unlike free-space networks [4]. Subsequent work demonstrated thermally tunable MRR weight banks for recognition, in-memory photonic dot-product engines with electrically programmable weight banks [9], and heterogeneous III-V-on-Si memresonators with 12-hour retention, 300 ps SET, 0.15 pJ switching [12].

## 3 Methodology

We adopt a three-pillar methodology: device modeling, fabric modeling, and workload mapping.

### 3.1 Device-Level Modeling

We model MRR transmission using coupled-mode theory:

```python
import numpy as np
def mrr_transmission(wavelength, lambda_res, kappa, alpha=0.98):
    """Through-port transmission of all-pass MRR"""
    phi = 2*np.pi*3.48*(2*np.pi*5e-6)/wavelength  # neff*L/wl approx
    # Lorentzian near resonance
    detune = wavelength - lambda_res
    FWHM = 0.08  # nm for Q~19000 @1550nm
    t = (detune**2 + (FWHM/2 * (1-kappa))**2) / (detune**2 + (FWHM/2)**2)
    return alpha * t

# Sweep weights for 8-channel WDM bank
lambdas = np.linspace(1549, 1551, 8)
weights = [mrr_transmission(1550.12+i*0.4, 1550.12+i*0.4+dw, kappa=0.3) 
           for i,dw in enumerate(np.linspace(-0.05,0.05,8))]
```

Thermal tuning is modeled as Δλ = 70 pm/K × ΔT with closed-loop control sensing photocurrent to lock insertion loss [11].

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Microring Modulator Weight Banks — The Photonic Synapse

Weight banks are the core compute element. Each bank consists of *N* cascaded MRRs coupled to a bus waveguide, each tuned to a distinct λᵢ [4][9]. Transmission wᵢ ∈ [0,1] is continuously tunable via heater current or MOSCAP bias. Balanced photodetection (BPD) computes Σ wᵢ·Pᵢ - Σ (1-wᵢ)·Pᵢ, implementing signed weights [-1,1].

Key advances:

* **Sub-volt operation:** ITiO-gated MOSCAP MRMs achieve 0.8 Vpp, 11 GHz, 53 fJ/bit, critical for direct CMOS drive without high-swing drivers [10].
* **Non-volatile weights:** Memresonator (III-V/Si MRR + HfO₂ memristor) stores phase via resistive state, 0.15 pJ SET, 300 ps switching, 12 h retention, 1000 cycles [12]. This eliminates static heater power (~10 mW/ring) which dominates large banks.
* **Scalability metrics:** Prior work defined MRR weight bank scalability as number of channels before thermal crosstalk > -20 dB and accuracy as effective bits given crosstalk and shot noise [4]. 8-λ banks at 200 GHz spacing with thermal control unit (TCU) stabilizing IL against process/temp variation are production-ready [11].
* **In-memory variant:** Electrically programmable weight banks using phase-change material (PCM) achieve intensity weighting without resonance sensitivity, enabling stable multi-wavelength operation under drift [13].

> **Theorem 2 (Weight Bank Precision Bound):** For a microring weight bank with channel spacing Δλ, Q-factor Q, and thermal crosstalk coefficient κ_th, achievable weight precision in bits B ≈ log₂( Δλ / (FWHM·√(SNR⁻¹ + κ_th²)) ). For Δλ=200 GHz (1.6 nm), Q=8000 (FWHM≈0.19 nm), κ_th=-25 dB, SNR=30 dB, B≈5.2 bits — sufficient for rank-order in PRISM top-k [5].

*Rust driver for bank calibration:*

```rust
pub struct MRRBank { rings: Vec<Ring>, pd: BalancedPD }
impl MRRBank {
    pub fn calibrate(&mut self, target: &[f32]) -> Result<(), CalibError> {
        for (ring, &w) in self.rings.iter_mut().zip(target) {
            let lambda_err = ring.sense_photocurrent();
            ring.heater.set_pid(lambda_err, w)?;
            // lock to insertion loss per Intel TCU [11]
        }
        self.pd.auto_zero();
        Ok(())
    }
    pub fn set_weights(&mut self, w: &[f32]) { /* DAC -> MOSCAP */ }
}
```

### 4.2 AWGR Wavelength Routing — The Scale-Out Fabric

AWGR-based interconnects break the electronic radix limit. An N×N AWGR provides contention-free all-to-all with N wavelengths per node [7]. Theory allows large port counts, but SOI tolerance, wavelength registration errors, and crosstalk limit single-AWGR scale. Practical workaround: hierarchical construction using M² W-port AWGRs where N=M×W achieves same all-to-all with smaller AWGRs [7].

For example, N=32 can be built from 16× 8×8 AWGRs (M=4, W=8) with 32 TRXs per node grouped as 4×8 λ.

**Multi-dimensional AWGR Tensor Core:** Recent work extends AWGR from routing to compute by exploiting cyclic routing + T-W-SDM (Time-Wavelength-Space Division Multiplexing) [8]. A 16×16 AWGR module with broadband intensity modulators at 20 Gbaud yields 163.8 TOPS for matrix-by-tensor multiplication (MbTM), breaking 100 TOPS barrier for waveguide-based accelerators. Prior WG accelerators capped at ~11 TOPS [8].

**Temperature tolerance:** On-chip WDM transmitter + AWGR interconnect demonstrated automated thermal drift compensation over 9°C with Q=5.8 at 25 Gb/s, critical for data-center MSB deployment [14].

**Haskell model of routing:**

```haskell
-- Cyclic AWGR routing function
awgrRoute :: Int -> Int -> Int -> Int -> Int
awgrRoute n inputPort lambdaIdx = (inputPort + lambdaIdx) `mod` n

-- All-to-all schedule for N=8
allToAll :: Int -> [(Int,Int,Int)] -- (src,dst,wl)
allToAll n = [(src, awgrRoute n src wl, wl) | src <- [0..n-1], wl <- [0..n-1]]

contentionFree :: Int -> Bool
contentionFree n = let routes = allToAll n
                       destWl = [(dst,wl) | (_,dst,wl) <- routes]
                   in length destWl == length (nub destWl)
```

### 4.3 Co-Packaged Optics — Breaking the Beachfront Wall

CPO moves optical engines onto ASIC package, eliminating long electrical traces and pluggable cages.

* **IBM CPO breakthrough (2024):** 50 μm pitch polymer waveguide (PWG), adiabatic coupling to SiPh, 6× beachfront density increase, terabit-per-second per fiber, 80× bandwidth vs electrical, 5× power reduction vs mid-range electrical, enabling LLM training 5× faster (3 months → 3 weeks for 70B) [1][2].
* **NVIDIA Spectrum-X Photonics:** Liquid-cooled chasses SN6810 (102.4 Tb/s, 128×800G) and SN6800 (409.6 Tb/s, 512×800G) powered by silicon photonics, 3.5× power efficiency, 10× resiliency, 1.3× faster turn-on [3].
* **Broadcom TH6-Davisson:** 102.4 Tbps Ethernet switch with TSMC COUPE co-packaged optics, 70% reduction in optical interconnect power, improved link flap stability via heterogeneous integration [15].

### 4.4 Broadcast-and-Weight for LLM Inference — The Unifying Architecture

The broadcast-and-weight protocol [4] is remarkably matched to LLM inference bottlenecks:

1. **Query Broadcast:** Encode d-dimensional query sketch onto d WDM channels (e.g., comb laser), split via 1×N passive splitter to N channels at no additional energy beyond splitting loss [5].
2. **Weighting:** N MRR weight banks store quasi-static signatures (updated every 64-512 tokens) via electro-optic tuning (Pockels in TFLN) [5][6]. Each computes inner product Iₙ ∝ Σⱼ wₙ,ⱼ sⱼ as analog optical dot product in ~10 ps/mm propagation [6].
3. **Top-k Selection:** Electronic comparator selects highest-scoring block indices, only those KV blocks fetched from memory, breaking O(N) memory-wall [5].
4. **MoE Dispatch:** AWGR routes tokens to experts by wavelength; ring filter selects expert.

PRISM [5][6] proposes Photonic Ranking via Inner-product Similarity with Microring weights: O(1) photonic block selection vs electronic full-scan which dominates KV retrieval latency [5]. Since only rank order matters (top-k), 4-6 bit precision suffices, relaxing MRR control requirements [6].

Full LLM transformer mapping [9][10]: >95% of time/energy in dense matmuls of width 1k-16k; photonic tensor cores <1 pJ/MAC, >10 TOPS mm⁻²; median MZI mesh 0.3-3 pJ/MAC at 6-bit linear precision [10]; heterogeneous pipelines where photonic cores accelerate linear subgraphs, GPUs/ASICs handle non-linear/control/logic — adopted by Albireo light-offloading prototype [10].

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 Throughput and Energy Analysis

Consider a 32×32 AWGR fabric, 8 λ/node, 25 Gb/s/λ, 4 groups [7]:

* **Bisection bandwidth:** 32 nodes × 8 λ × 25 Gb/s = 6.4 Tb/s per direction, 12.8 Tb/s bisection.
* **Power:** 256 MRRs × 10 mW heater = 2.56 W (volatile) → 0.04 W with memresonator non-volatile [12]. Transceivers: 256 × 53 fJ/bit × 25 Gb/s = 0.34 W modulator dynamic.
* **Latency:** Propagation 10 ps/mm × 10 mm ≈ 100 ps + EO/OE 50 ps + arbitration 5 ns (electronic) ≈ 5.15 ns vs 80 ns electrical switch.

TOPS calculation for AWGR tensor core [8]: 16×16 AWGR, 20 Gbaud, 16 λ, 16 spatial paths, 2 ops/MAC = 16×16×20e9×2 = 10.24 TOPS per dimension × 16 (tensor) = 163.8 TOPS measured.

### 5.2 CTRNN Acceleration Proof Sketch

Tait et al. proved neuromorphism by observing bifurcations [4]: single-node bistability across cusp bifurcation, two-node oscillation across Hopf bifurcation, matching CTRNN model. Simulation of 24-modulator CTRNN solving Lorenz ODE yielded 294× speedup vs CPU [4]. For LLM, analogous ODE is continuous-time token dynamics in recurrent refinement (e.g., diffusion LM).

> **Theorem 3 (Energy Dominance of Converters):** In full-system photonic LLM inference, data-converter overhead (6 GS/s 8-bit ADC/DAC 25-80 fJ/sample) and DRAM traffic dominate photonic compute energy by 10× [10]. Therefore CPO must co-package converters and memory to realize gains.

### 5.3 KV-Cache Photonic Selection Validation

PRISM simulation: N=4096 blocks, d=64, 4-bit weights, optical SNR 28 dB → Recall@16 = 0.94 vs full-scan 1.0, while reducing memory fetches by 256× (4096→16). Latency: photonic 1.2 ns vs electronic 180 ns (DRAM-bound) [5]. Cohen's kappa 0.799 for DDoS detection workload using AWGR accelerator executing 131,072 NN ops [8].

## 6 Limitations

* **Thermal Sensitivity:** MRR resonance drifts 70 pm/K; requires TCU locking photocurrent to heater [11]. Automated compensation demonstrated over 9°C but data-center temp swing 15-20°C may exceed range without active cooling [14]. Non-volatile PCM weights [13] or memresonators [12] reduce static power but add write endurance limits (1000 cycles) and 300-900 ps write latency.
* **Precision Ceiling:** Fabrication variability and shot-noise limit practical dynamic range to ~6 ENOB [10]; experimental studies report up to 15% accuracy loss when non-idealities not compensated [10]. For LLM, 4-6 bits sufficient for retrieval ranking [6] but insufficient for high-precision softmax without digital correction.
* **Converter Overhead:** As noted, ADC/DAC and DRAM traffic can dominate 10× over photonic MAC [10]. CPO reduces but does not eliminate this; co-packaged HBM and analog-to-digital conversion near photodiode required.
* **AWGR Crosstalk & FSR:** Ring FSR 2.4 THz limits 32-48 channels at 50 GHz spacing [7]; larger N requires larger radius (lower FSR) tradeoff vs footprint. Crosstalk dense spacing limits usable SNR.
* **Manufacturability:** 50 μm pitch PWG assembly uses standard packaging processes [1] but yield at 6× density unproven at hyperscaler volumes; TSMC COUPE still early production [15].
* **Software Stack:** Mapping dynamic LLM control flow (top-k routing, speculative decoding) to wavelength allocation needs new compiler (LightCode) [10]; lack of mature photonic-aware autograd.

## 7 Conclusion

Silicon photonic interconnects unify communication and compute for scale-out LLM inference. Microring modulator weight banks provide programmable, non-volatile, sub-volt weighting at 53 fJ/bit and 25 Gb/s [10][12]; AWGR wavelength routing provides contention-free all-to-all and 163.8 TOPS tensor acceleration in 16×16 module [8]; CPO provides 6× beachfront density, 80× bandwidth, 5× power reduction, and 3.5× system efficiency enabling 409.6 Tb/s switches [1][3]. The broadcast-and-weight protocol [4][5] maps naturally to MoE all-to-all, KV-cache block selection, and attention scoring, converting memory-bandwidth-bound problems into O(1) optical fan-out.

Roadmap:

1. **Near-term (2025-27):** CPO switches for MoE scale-out, PRISM-style photonic KV-cache filters in disaggregated inference, thermal-tolerant 32×32 AWGR MSB [14]
2. **Mid-term:** Heterogeneous III-V-on-Si memresonator banks for zero-static-power weight storage, TFLN Pockels tuning for ns reconfiguration [5]
3. **Long-term:** Fully co-packaged photonic-electronic inference tile with on-package HBM, analog accumulation, and LightCode compiler [10] — a photonic super-node where *the network is the computer*

Open problems include 6+ bit precision with closed-loop calibration, 1024-port AWGR with < -20 dB crosstalk, and wafer-scale integration of DFB comb arrays with < 100 kHz linewidth for coherent detection.
