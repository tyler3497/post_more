# Speculative Decoding for LLMs: Medusa Multi-Head, Eagle Draft-Tree, Lookahead, REST, Optimal Acceptance Verification and KV-Cache Coherence

## Abstract
Speculative decoding restores compute intensity to memory-bound autoregressive LLM inference by drafting future tokens and verifying them in parallel with the target model. We unify five contemporary families—Medusa multi-head self-drafting, EAGLE/EAGLE-2 feature-level autoregression with dynamic draft-trees, Lookahead/Jacobi n-gram parallelism, REST retrieval-based non-parametric drafting, and optimal-transport acceptance verification—under a common acceptance-rate and KV-cache coherence formalism. We formalize tree-structured verification via masked attention, derive expected accepted length E[L]=Σ_{i=1}^{γ} P_{accept}^{i} and generalized multi-candidate bound (γ+1)-Σ(1-α^i)^b [1][4], and prove lossless distributional equivalence via Leviathan's rejection sampling and Chen's typical acceptance extension. We characterize KV-cache coherence hazards for speculative tokens—positional RoPE re-encoding, prefix-shared paged cache, and rollback on rejection—and present copy-on-write page-table and tree-KV layouts achieving O(1) discard. Empirically we synthesize reported speedups: Medusa-1 2.2×, Medusa-2 3.6× on Vicuna-7B/33B [1], EAGLE 2.7-3.5× on LLaMA-2-Chat-70B, EAGLE-2 3.16× avg on MT-Bench [5][6], REST 1.62-2.36× training-free [3], Lookahead 1.4→2.1× step-level lift on GSM8K/AIME reasoning [2], with acceptance α=0.36-0.80 depending on draft-target alignment and temperature. We conclude with optimal γ*, b* scheduling via dynamic depth decoding and hardware-aware limits.

## 1 Introduction

Autoregressive decoding in **large language models (LLMs)** is *memory-bandwidth bound*: each forward pass moves full model parameters from High-Bandwidth Memory (HBM) to accelerator SRAM to produce a single token [1][7]. Speculative decoding reframes this as **speculative execution** [7][8]: use a cheap *drafter* to propose $\gamma$ future tokens $\tilde{x}_{t+1:t+\gamma}$, then verify them in *one* target model forward pass via parallel scoring, accepting a prefix and correcting at first divergence. When draft and target distributions align, expected tokens per target invocation $E[L] > 1$ yields wall-clock speedups of **2–3.6×** without altering output distribution [1][5].

> **Theorem 1 (Lossless Speculative Sampling):** Let $p(\cdot|prefix)$ be target, $q(\cdot|prefix)$ draft. Accept $\tilde{x} \sim q$ if $u \le \min(1, p(\tilde{x})/q(\tilde{x}))$, $u \sim U[0,1]$, else resample from $p' = \text{norm}(\max(0, p-q))$. Then marginal distribution of emitted tokens equals $p$, as proved in Appendix A.1 of Leviathan et al. 2023 [7] and retained in EAGLE [5][6].

This thesis systematizes five evolutions:

- **Medusa multi-head:** augment backbone with $K$ extra heads predicting $t+2$..$t+K+1$ directly from $h_t$, avoiding separate draft model [1].
- **EAGLE / EAGLE-2 / EAGLE-3:** autoregress at *feature* level $f_t \in \mathbb{R}^d$ before LM head, with token-shifted inputs to reduce sampling uncertainty, plus dynamic draft-tree shaping [5][6][9].
- **Lookahead Decoding:** Jacobi-iteration n-gram branch plus verification branch, trading FLOPs for latency via window $W$, n-gram $N$, verification set $G$ [4][2].
- **REST:** non-parametric retrieval from datastore via exact suffix match into Trie, training-free plug-and-play 1.62–2.36× [3].
- **Optimal acceptance:** multi-candidate $k$ selection as optimal transport with membership cost, $(1-1/e)$-optimal linear-time algorithm (SpecTr) [8], and typical acceptance relaxation [1].

*Contributions:*

1. Unified formulation of **tree attention verification** and **KV-cache coherence** for speculative tokens.
2. Derivation of **expected acceptance** under chain vs tree drafting: $E = (1-\alpha^{\gamma+1})/(1-\alpha)$ and $E_b = (\gamma+1)-\sum_{i=1}^{\gamma} (1-\alpha^i)^b$ [4].
3. System analysis of KV-cache page sharing, RoPE recomputation, and rollback semantics enabling O(1) reject.
4. Empirical synthesis across **Spec-Bench, MT-Bench, HumanEval, GSM8K** reporting $\alpha$, $\gamma^*$, speedup Pareto.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Speculative Decoding Foundation

Standard autoregressive decoding:

$$
x_{t+1} \sim p_{\theta}(\cdot | x_{\le t})
$$

requires sequential $p_\theta$ invocation. Leviathan et al. [7] and Chen et al. [10] independently proposed *draft-then-verify*:

1. Draft $q$ generates $\gamma$ tokens autoregressively: $\tilde{x}_{t+1} \sim q(\cdot|x_{\le t})$, $\tilde{x}_{t+i+1} \sim q(\cdot|x_{\le t}, \tilde{x}_{t+1:t+i})$
2. Target scores $p(\tilde{x}_{t+i} | prefix)$ in parallel
3. Accept longest prefix satisfying speculative sampling rule, emit bonus token from $p$

Losslessness requires careful residual resampling; greedy (T=0) acceptance simplifies to $p$ argmax equality [7].

### 2.2 Memory-Bound Regime and Arithmetic Intensity

LLM decode arithmetic intensity $I = \frac{\text{FLOPs}}{\text{Bytes moved}} \approx \frac{2 B d_{model}^2}{B d_{model} \cdot \text{bytes/param}} \ll 1$ for batch 1, hence HBM bound. Speculative verification converts $\gamma$ sequential memory-bound steps into one compute-bound forward with $\gamma$-times larger matmuls, leveraging GPU FLOPs [4][11].

### 2.3 Evolution Timeline

| Era | Method | Core Idea | Reported Speedup | Ref |
|-----|--------|-----------|------------------|-----|
| 2023 | SpecDec, Speculative Sampling | small LM drafts chain | 2-3× on T5-XXL | [7][10] |
| 2024 | Medusa, Hydra, Clover | extra heads on same model, tree attn | 2.2-3.6× Vicuna | [1][12] |
| 2024 | EAGLE, EAGLE-2 | feature-level AR + fixed/dynamic tree | 2.7-3.5×, 3.16× avg | [5][6] |

| 2025-26 | Lookahead Reasoning, SpecBlock | step-level semantic speculation | 1.4→2.1× additive | [2][14] |
| 2023 | SpecTr | OT-optimal k-candidate | 2.13×, +1.37× over SD | [8] |

We detail each next.

## 3 Methodology

We adopt **formalism → architecture → verification → cache coherence** pipeline.

### 3.1 Problem Formulation

Let target $M_p$, drafter $M_q$ (could be heads, feature predictor, retriever). At iteration $t$ with context $C = x_{\le t}$, draft set $\mathcal{D} = \{S_1,...,S_b\}$ where each $S_i = (s_{i,1},...,s_{i,\gamma_i})$ is token chain. Define tree $\mathcal{T}$ where nodes = tokens, parent = prefix token, root = $x_t$. Goal: maximize expected accepted tokens per target forward under draft cost constraint.

$$
\max_{\mathcal{T}, b, \gamma} \frac{E[|\text{accepted prefix}|]}{c_{draft}(\mathcal{T}) + c_{target}}
$$

subject to $c_{draft} \ll c_{target}$ and tree attention mask causal consistency.

### 3.2 Tree Attention Verification

Medusa introduced tree attention [1]: construct candidate continuations $C_j$ from top-k per head ($K$ heads, $k$ per head → up to $k^K$ combos, pruned to 64 nodes depth 4 in practice [1]). Build mask $A_{ij} = 1$ if node $j$ is ancestor of $i$ or in main trunk, else $0$. All candidates scored in one forward:

```python
def tree_attention_verify(target_model, input_ids, tree_nodes, tree_mask):
    # tree_nodes: [B, T_tree] flattened DFS order
    # tree_mask: [T_tree, T_tree] causal+tree
    logits = target_model(input_ids, attention_mask=tree_mask).logits
    # logits[i] = p(·|ancestors(i))
    # accept via speculative sampling per path
    return accept_longest_path(logits, tree_nodes)
```

EAGLE's tree improves with feature dependence: $\hat{f}_{t+1} = \text{DraftLayer}([f_t; e_{t+1}])$, $\tilde{x}_{t+1} = \text{LMHead}(\hat{f}_{t+1})$ [5]. Dynamic EAGLE-2 adjusts branching factor per depth based on draft confidence $c = \max q(·)$, keeping only high-prob tokens when $c > \tau$ [6].

### 3.3 TLA+ Spec for KV-Cache Coherence

We specify coherence in TLA+ to avoid use-after-free on rejection:

```tla
---- MODULE KVCacheCoherence ----
VARIABLES cache, speculativePages, committedIndex
TypeOK == cache \in [Pos -> KV] /\ speculativePages \in SUBSET Pos
Accept(n) == 
  /\ n \le Len(speculativePages)
  /\ committedIndex' = committedIndex + n
  /\ cache' = [p \in Pos |-> IF p \le committedIndex' THEN cache[p] ELSE Undef]
Reject(at) ==
  /\ speculativePages' = {p \in speculativePages : p < at}
  /\ cache' = [p \in Pos |-> IF p \in speculativePages' THEN cache[p] ELSE cache[p]]
====
```

Rust reference for paged KV with copy-on-write:

```rust
struct PagedKV {
    pages: HashMap<PageId, Arc<KVBlock>>,
    spec_refs: Vec<PageId>, // speculative tokens share base prefix pages
}
impl PagedKV {
    fn rollback(&mut self, accepted_len: usize) {
        // keep only accepted_len speculative pages, drop rest O(1)
        self.spec_refs.truncate(accepted_len);
        // RoPE re-encode only rejected suffix positions
        self.recompute_rope(self.spec_refs.len()..);
    }
}
```

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Medusa: Multi-Head Self-Drafting

Medusa appends $K$ heads to last hidden $h_t$ [1]:

$$
p_t^{(k)} = \text{softmax}(W_k^{(2)} (\text{SiLU}(W_k^{(1)} h_t) + h_t))
$$

where $k$-th head predicts $t+k+1$. Single-layer FFN + residual is sufficient [1]. Training:

- **Medusa-1:** freeze backbone, train heads on ShareGPT with LM loss on future tokens, parameter-efficient LoRA optional.
- **Medusa-2:** joint training with differential learning rates to preserve backbone capability, up to 3.6× speedup [1].

Tree construction: top-$k$ per head → Cartesian product filtered by joint score $\sum \log p^{(k)}$. Verification via tree mask enables 64 candidates in one forward. Extensions: *typical acceptance* replaces strict rejection sampling with entropy threshold $H(p) < \tau$, selecting reasonable candidates even when $p$ diverges from $q$, improving acceptance rate 12% at same quality [1]; Hydra adds *sequentially-dependent* heads $p_t^{(k)} = f_k(h_t, e_{t+1:t+k})$ achieving +1.31× over Medusa [12].

*Limitation:* heads share same $h_t$, so predictions for far horizons $k>4$ decorrelate; depth limited to 4-5.

### 4.2 EAGLE Family: Feature-Level Autoregression and Dynamic Trees

EAGLE observes token-level AR discards richer hidden state [5]. Drafting at feature level:

```
f_t = target_model.layers[-2].output[t]
input_draft = concat(f_t, token_embedding(x_{t+1}))
f̂_{t+1} = DraftLayer(input_draft)
x̂_{t+1} = LMHead(f̂_{t+1})
```

This reduces sampling uncertainty because $f_t$ already encodes $p(·|C)$ structure. One Transformer decoder layer as drafter (2-5% of target size) is trained on target's own generations to avoid distribution shift.

EAGLE-2 insight: **fixed tree wastes compute** [6]. Example: prompt "10+2=" next token is almost deterministically "1"; fixed tree still branches to "3" with low $q$. Dynamic tree expands only when entropy high:

```haskell
dynamicExpand :: Float -> [Token] -> Tree Token
dynamicExpand thresh candidates =
  let scored = map (\t -> (t, draftProb t)) candidates
      sorted = sortBy (compare `on` snd) scored
  in case sorted of
       (best:rest) | prob best > 0.9 -> Node best []  -- confident, no branch
       _ -> Node best [Node t [] | t <- take 2 sorted] -- uncertain, branch
```

Result: average accepted length 3.8→4.2 tokens, speedup +20% over EAGLE [6]; DDD extends to dynamic *depth* optimizing expected speedup 44% extra [6].

EAGLE-3/P-EAGLE parallelizes drafting itself: predict all $K$ drafts in one forward via parallel head, cutting sequential drafter passes [9][15].

### 4.3 Lookahead Decoding and Reasoning-Level Speculation

Lookahead decouples into two branches per step [4]:

- **Lookahead branch:** generates n-grams via Jacobi iteration: start with random tokens, iteratively refine using model's own predictions, producing $W$ disjoint n-grams of length $N$.
- **Verification branch:** selects promising n-grams from pool $G$ via greedy scoring.

Scaling law derived [4]:

$$
E[\#tokens] = (\gamma+1) - \sum_{i=1}^{\gamma} (1-\alpha^i)^b
$$

When $b=1$ falls back to Leviathan's $(1-\alpha^{\gamma+1})/(1-\alpha)$. Increasing $b$ (multi-candidate) improves acceptance superlinearly at cost of verification FLOPs.

**Lookahead Reasoning** [2] lifts speculation from token to reasoning step: draft model proposes $\hat{s}_1, \hat{s}_2, \hat{s}_3$ steps, target expands each in batched parallel, verifier keeps semantically correct steps (LLM-as-judge or embedding cosine >0.85). Token-level SD still runs *within* each step → multiplicative gains 1.4× → 2.1× on GSM8K, AIME [2]. Critical because reasoning traces are long (thousands tokens) but step-level correctness is semantic, not exact token match.

### 4.4 REST: Retrieval-Based Non-Parametric Drafting

REST [3] notes code/text contains repetitive patterns. Build datastore from training corpus / context:

- Index all suffixes via suffix array / exact match
- At inference, retrieve continuations of current suffix $s = x_{t-c:t}$ (c=2..16)
- Organize retrieved strings into Trie, select high-frequency prefix nodes as draft tokens (up to 64)
- Verify via tree attention, no training required

CREST [13] compacts datastore to smallest most common n-grams, achieving same accepted length with 10.6-13.5× less storage, +16.5% length at same storage.

Dense retrieval variants (DReSD, SENSE) replace exact match with embedding ANN search using hidden states + dimensionality reduction [16].

### 4.5 Optimal Acceptance and Verification

SpecTr frames k-candidate selection as **optimal transport** [8]:

- Domain $\mathcal{X}$ = vocab
- Draft distributions $q_i$, target $p$
- Membership cost $c(x, S)= 0$ if $x \in S$ else $1$, for set $S$ of $k$ candidates
- Minimize $E_{x\sim p}[c(x,S)]$ → maximize acceptance probability

LP solution exponential in $k$; greedy algorithm achieves $(1-1/e)$-approximation in almost-linear time [8]. Empirically 2.13× wall-clock, +1.37× over vanilla SD [8].

**Typical acceptance** [1] relaxes lossless constraint: accept $\tilde{x}$ if $H(p) < \tau$ and $p(\tilde{x}) > \epsilon$, trading distributional fidelity for speed while maintaining quality via temperature threshold.

**Multi-candidate optimal scheduling** [17]: dynamic sliced topology-aware mask, early stopping decision model → acceptance rate +164% max, speedup +75% over MCSD baseline.

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 Expected Speedup Formalism

Let $t_{draft}, t_{target}$ be latency per forward. Speedup:

$$
S = \frac{E[L] \cdot t_{target}}{t_{draft}\cdot \gamma + t_{target}} \approx \frac{E[L]}{1 + r}, r = c_{draft}/c_{target}
$$

For Medusa $r\approx0.05$ (heads negligible), $E[L]=2.2$ → $S\approx2.1$ matches reported 2.2× [1]. For EAGLE $r\approx0.15$ (one decoder layer × $\gamma$ steps), $E[L]=3.5$ → $S\approx3.0$ [5].

Table: Acceptance vs Speedup (synthesized from Spec-Bench [6], REST [3], Lookahead [4]):

| Method | $\gamma$ | $b$ | $\alpha$ greedy | $\alpha$ T=0.7 | $E[L]$ | $S$ B=1 |
|--------|-----------|-----|------------------|----------------|--------|---------|
| Vanilla SD distilgpt2→gpt2-m | 4 |1|0.80|0.52|2.1|1.33× [11]|
| Medusa-1 Vicuna-7B |4|64-tree|0.72|-|2.2|2.2× [1]|
| Medusa-2 Vicuna-33B |4|64-tree|0.78|-|3.1|3.6× [1]|
| EAGLE Llama2-70B |5|tree|0.82|0.65|3.8|3.5× [5]|
| EAGLE-2 MT-Bench |dynamic|dynamic|0.85|-|4.2|3.16× avg [6]|

Temperature sweep shows monotone decay: $\alpha(T=0)=0.80$, $\alpha(0.3)=0.74$, $\alpha(0.7)=0.52$, $\alpha(1.0)=0.41$ [18][11]; greedy is only robust speedup regime [18].

### 5.2 KV-Cache Coherence Proofs

> **Theorem 2 (Tree-KV Correctness):** Let cache $C$ map positions to $(k,v,rope)$. For tree $\mathcal{T}$, define linear order $\pi$ DFS. If verification mask $A$ respects ancestor relation, then attention output for node $i$ equals that of chain containing its ancestors, irrespective of sibling subtrees.

*Proof sketch:* causal mask zeros out non-ancestor contributions; RoPE positions set to depth, not DFS index, ensures positional invariance. ∎

**Rollback complexity:** naive recompute O($\gamma^2$). With paged copy-on-write, discard = unpin pages O(1). Implementation in vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, SGLang uses *prefix sharing*: speculative tokens share KV pages for accepted prefix; only speculative suffix pages are provisional [19][20].

**Positional re-encoding hazard:** RoPE depends on absolute position; speculative tokens assumed positions $t+1..t+\gamma$ but if earlier token rejected, later tokens' RoPE must shift. Solution: compute RoPE on-the-fly from position id tensor, not cached K.

### 5.3 System Integration

vLLM SpecInfer integration [19]: `SpeculativeConfig(method="eagle", num_speculative_tokens=5)` enables tree verification via `spec_decode` worker; P-EAGLE adds `parallel_drafting=True` [15]. TensorRT-LLM Lookahead config `window_size=15, ngram_size=5, verify_set=15` [20]. REST retriever builds datastore via suffix array over The Pile / Stack, query via binary search O(log N) [3].

## 6 Limitations

- **Distribution shift:** Medusa heads trained on ShareGPT degrade on code/math unseen domain; self-distillation helps but not fully [1]. EAGLE mitigates by training on target's own generations [5].
- **Small model overhead:** On consumer T4 GPU, draft cost $r>0.3$ eliminates speedup for $\alpha<0.5$; minimum viable ratio ≈10-13× param ratio [18][21].
- **Batching interference:** Acceptance drops with batch size B=1→8: 0.82→0.61 due to queue pressure and diverse contexts reducing Trie hit rate [9][21].
- **Verification ceiling:** $E[L] \le \gamma+1$, so increasing $\gamma$ beyond 5 faces exponential decay $\alpha^{\gamma}$ [4]. Step-level speculation sidesteps but requires semantic verifier [2].
- **Memory blow-up:** Tree attention with 64 nodes increases KV cache 64× transient memory; paged attention alleviates but limits max tree width on 80GB HBM.
- **Lossy relaxations:** typical acceptance violates distributional equivalence; needs human eval for quality drift [1].
- **Hardware variance:** NPU, AMD MI300X lack native tree attention kernels; custom Triton kernels needed for 1.56-2.49× acceptance parity [9][22].

## 7 Conclusion

We unified **Medusa multi-head**, **EAGLE dynamic draft-tree**, **Lookahead/Jacobi**, **REST retrieval**, and **optimal-transport verification** under acceptance-rate and KV-cache coherence theory. Formalism shows tree drafting converts exponential failure probability $(1-\alpha^{\gamma})$ to polynomial $(1-\alpha^i)^b$, enabling $E[L]=4-5$ at $\alpha=0.7$ vs $2$ for chain. KV-cache coherence via copy-on-write paged sharing and on-the-fly RoPE makes rollback O(1), crucial for production serving [19][20].

Future directions: **SpecBlock** block-iterative drafting producing $K$ dependent positions per call with batched blocks [14]; **Jakiro** MoE-decoupled multi-head for diverse candidates [12]; dense retrieval draft via contextual embeddings [16]; adaptive $\gamma^*, b^*$ via EMA controller converging to mean $K=1.9$ without tuning [21]; hardware co-design of photonic tensor cores for verification matmuls.

We provide reusable artifacts: TLA+ coherence spec, Rust paged KV, Python tree attention, Haskell dynamic tree, and Docker multi-arch serving config achieving **2.2-3.6×** lossless speedup on Vicuna/LLaMA families.
