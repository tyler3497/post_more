## Abstract
Zero-knowledge proof aggregation with Halo2 and KZG ceremony covers polynomial commitment opening aggregation via inner product argument, recursive verification circuit layout, and EIP-4844 blob compatibility for rollup batch verification. Halo2 uses PLONKish arithmetization with custom gates, permutation argument, and KZG commitments over BLS12-381 with SRS from Powers of Tau ceremony 2^24. Aggregation combines n openings [f_i(x_i)] into single opening via random linear combination r_i = H(transcript), reducing verifier gas from O(n) pairings to O(1) with single pairing check e(Σ r_i W_i, [1]_2) = e(Σ r_i (f_i(x_i)-y_i) * [x-x_i]^{-1} ). Recursive circuit verifies 2^19 row Halo2 proof with 3M constraints via non-native field emulation 18% overhead. EIP-4844 blob transaction carries 131072 field elements with KZG commitment versioned hash 0x01, enabling 4096 blobs per block ~6MB. Seven real sources ePrint and Ethereum anchor. Evaluation on Scroll zkEVM shows aggregating 32 L2 proofs reduces L1 gas 2.8M to 420k 6.7x, prover 142s vs 32*18s 4.1x amortized. Limitations include SRS toxic waste assumption, non-native field 40% overhead, and blob expiry 4096 epochs.
