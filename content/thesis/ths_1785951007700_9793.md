# Factor Graph SLAM, iSAM2 and Lie Groups: Incremental Smoothing for LiDAR-Inertial Odometry on SE(3)

## Abstract

We formalize LiDAR-inertial odometry as incremental smoothing on factor graphs over the Lie group SE(3) and its extended pose form SE2(3). The Bayes tree data structure central to iSAM2 converts the square-root information matrix into a directed clique tree of conditional densities, enabling fluid relinearization, incremental variable reordering via COLAMD/CAMD, and partial state updates with O(1) complexity for exploration trajectories. We derive on-manifold Gauss-Newton optimization with exponential maps, logarithmic errors, retractions, and adjoint adjoints for IMU preintegration factors à la Forster et al., LiDAR point-to-plane odometry factors, and loop closure constraints. Through LIO-SAM as canonical implementation, we show how marginalization, fixed-lag smoothing, and sub-keyframe sliding windows preserve sparsity while fusing 200Hz IMU and 10Hz LiDAR. Analysis extends to gauge freedom, observability, and degeneracy handling in structured environments.

## 1. Introduction

**Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM)** is rarely a filtering problem. While *Extended Kalman Filters (EKFs)* propagate mean and covariance forward, discarding history, modern robotic navigation requires *smoothing*: jointly optimizing the entire trajectory posterior $p(\mathcal{X}_{0:k} | \mathcal{Z}_{0:k})$ under nonlinear, non-Gaussian measurements with loop closures arriving arbitrarily late. The **factor graph** has emerged as the lingua franca for this problem, offering a bipartite representation $G=(\mathcal{F},\Theta,\mathcal{E})$ where variable nodes $\Theta$ encode states in $SE(3)$ and factor nodes $\mathcal{F}$ encode measurement likelihoods $\phi_j(\Theta_j) \propto \exp(-\frac{1}{2} \| h_j(\Theta_j)-z_j\|^2_{\Sigma_j})$ [1][2][3].

The core contribution of **iSAM2** [1][2] is an *incremental* exact solution to $\arg\max_\Theta \prod_j \phi_j(\Theta_j)$ without periodic batch re-linearization. Traditional *iSAM1* maintains a square-root information matrix $R$ from QR factorization $A = Q [R;0]$ where $A$ is Jacobian of all factors, updating $R$ via Givens rotations when new rows are appended. This suffers from forced variable ordering and fill-in growth. iSAM2 replaces matrix surgery with graphical editing on the **Bayes tree** — a directed chordal clique tree where each node $C_k = F_k : S_k$ stores frontal variables $F_k$ and separator $S_k$, conditional $p(F_k|S_k)$, and cached linearized factors.

*Why LiDAR-inertial fusion exposes the need for Lie groups?* Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) integration introduces state on $SO(3)\times \mathbb{R}^9$ ( rotation, position, velocity, biases). Naïve quaternion $+\mathbb{R}^3$ Euclidean parameterization violates orthogonality constraints, requiring reprojection. **Lie theory** provides manifold-aware increments: optimization in $\mathfrak{se}(3)$ tangent space with retraction ${\bf T} \leftarrow {\bf T}\cdot \exp(\xi^\wedge)$ where $\xi\in\mathbb{R}^6$, preserving group structure and correct uncertainty propagation via Adjoint.

**Motivation for LIO-SAM** [4]: Tightly-coupled LiDAR-inertial odometry via smoothing and mapping demonstrates factor graph practicality: IMU preintegration de-skews LiDAR sweeps, LiDAR odometry corrects bias drift, GPS/loop factors added opportunistically, and iSAM2 optimizes up to $5,000$ keyframes at 10Hz on ARM.

> **Theorem 1 (MAP equivalence):** For Gaussian factors, MAP inference over factor graph $G$ equals nonlinear least squares $\Theta^* = \arg\min \sum_j \| r_j(\Theta_j)\|^2_{\Sigma_j}$ where $r_j$ are residuals defined on manifold. The Bayes tree encodes $R^T R = J^T J$.

This thesis synthesizes four pillars: (i) factor graph representation and MAP, (ii) Bayes tree and iSAM2 operations, (iii) Lie group optimization on $SE(3)$ and $SE_2(3)$, (iv) LiDAR-inertial factor design.

## 2. Background

### 2.1 Factor Graphs for Smoothing

Following Dellaert & Kaess [3], a factor graph $f(\Theta)=\prod_i f_i(\Theta_i)$ factorizes posterior. MAP solves *nonlinear* least squares via iterated **Gauss-Newton / Levenberg-Marquardt**:

$$
(J^T J + \lambda I) \Delta = -J^T r
$$

where $J = \partial r / \partial \delta\xi$ is Jacobian w.r.t. local coordinates $\delta\xi$ in $\mathfrak{g}$.

Key sparsity: $J^T J$ is block-sparse, chordal completion after variable elimination yields $R$. Fill-in minimized by **COLAMD** [3]. Without clever ordering, $J^T J$ density explodes for loop closures.

*Variables in LIO* [4][5]:

- ${}^W{\bf T}_{B_i}\in SE(3)$: body pose at keyframe $i$
- ${}^W{\bf v}_i\in\mathbb{R}^3$, ${\bf b}^a_i, {\bf b}^g_i$: velocity, accel/gyro bias
- Extrinsics ${}^B{\bf T}_L$.

Continuous-time LiDAR distortion corrected using IMU forward propagation.

### 2.2 IMU Preintegration on Manifold — Forster et al.

Forster et al. [5][6] introduced **on-manifold preintegration** that avoids re-integrating high-rate IMU when linearization point changes. Core idea: *relative motion constraints* between consecutive keyframes $i,j$:

$$
\Delta {\tilde R}_{ij} = \prod_{k=i}^{j-1} \exp((\tilde\omega_k-{\bf b}^g_i - \eta^g_k)\Delta t)
$$

with first-order bias Jacobians $\partial \Delta / \partial {\bf b}$. The preintegrated measurement becomes factor:

$$
r_{{\cal I}_{ij}} = \begin{bmatrix} \log(\Delta \tilde R^T_{ij} R_i^T R_j)^\vee \\ R_i^T(v_j-v_i- g \Delta t)-\Delta \tilde v_{ij} \\ R_i^T(p_j-p_i-v_i\Delta t-\tfrac12 g \Delta t^2)-\Delta \tilde p_{ij} \\ b^a_j-b^a_i \\ b^g_j-b^g_i \end{bmatrix}
$$

Noise propagation in tangent space yields covariance $\Sigma_{{\cal I}}$. Using $SE_2(3)$ group [7] gives more accurate structure.

$$
{\bf T}= \begin{bmatrix} R & v & p \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\0&0&1 \end{bmatrix} \in SE_2(3)
$$

with unified exponential.

### 2.3 Lie Groups — SE(3), SO(3), Adjoint

*Lie group* $G$ is smooth manifold with group operation. Its **Lie algebra** $\mathfrak{g}=T_{{\cal E}}G$ is vector space. Maps $\exp: \mathfrak{g}\to G$, $\log: G\to\mathfrak{g}$.

For ${\bf \phi}\in\mathbb{R}^3$, $\phi^\wedge \in \mathfrak{so}(3)$ is skew-symmetric:

$$
\phi^\wedge = \begin{bmatrix}0&-\phi_z&\phi_y \\ \phi_z&0&-\phi_x \\ -\phi_y&\phi_x&0\end{bmatrix},\quad \exp(\phi^\wedge)=I + \frac{\sin\|\phi\|}{\|\phi\|}\phi^\wedge+\frac{1-\cos\|\phi\|}{\|\phi\|^2}(\phi^\wedge)^2
$$

Rodrigues formula [8]. Left Jacobian $J_l(\phi)$ couples translation:

$$
J_l(\phi)=I+\frac{1-\cos\|\phi\|}{\|\phi\|^2}\phi^\wedge + \frac{\|\phi\|-\sin\|\phi\|}{\|\phi\|^3}(\phi^\wedge)^2
$$

$SE(3)$ element:

$$
{\bf T}= \begin{bmatrix} R & t \\ 0&1\end{bmatrix},\; \xi=[\rho,\phi]^T,\; \xi^\wedge= \begin{bmatrix}\phi^\wedge&\rho\\0&0\end{bmatrix},\; \exp(\xi^\wedge)=\begin{bmatrix}\exp(\phi^\wedge)&J_l(\phi)\rho\\0&1\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Adjoint** $\operatorname{Ad}_{{\bf T}}(\xi)= ({\bf T}\xi^\wedge {\bf T}^{-1})^\vee$ transforms tangent vectors between frames:

$$
\operatorname{Ad}_{{\bf T}}= \begin{bmatrix} R & t^\wedge R \\ 0 & R \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^{6\times6}
$$

Critical for Jacobian chaining, uncertainty propagation $\Sigma' = Ad_T \Sigma Ad_T^T$.

*Right vs Left* perturbations: GTSAM uses ${\bf T}\leftarrow \exp(\delta\xi^\wedge){\bf T}$ (local) vs $\exp$ right composition; choice changes Jacobian sign.

## 3. Methodology

### 3.1 Factor Graph Construction for LiDAR-Inertial SLAM

We build time-indexed graph $\mathcal{X} = \{{\bf T}_i,{\bf v}_i,{\bf b}_i\}_{i=0}^N$.

**Factor types**:

| Factor | Residual Type | Manifold | Freq |
|--------|---------------|----------|------|
| IMU Preintegration | 9-dim + 6 bias | $SE_2(3) \times \mathbb{R}^6$ | 200Hz aggregated |
| LiDAR Odometry | point-to-edge/plane | $SE(3)$ relative | 10Hz keyframe |
| Loop Closure | $\log(T_i^{-1} T_j \Delta \tilde T_{ij}^{loop})$ | $SE(3)$ | opportunistic |
| GNSS / Prior | absolute pose | $SE(3)$ | 1Hz |

Edge generation pipeline: de-skew point cloud using IMU forward integration to $t_{keyframe}$, extract edge/planar features via curvature, build voxel map of sub-keyframes $\mathcal{M} = \cup_{k=N-M}^{N-1} {}^W{\cal F}_k$ transformed by current poses, ICP Gauss-Newton optimization for $T_{i}$ estimate, provide as factor target.

Marginalization strategy: LIO-SAM selective keyframe $>1m$ or $>10^\circ$; old non-loop frames marginalized via optional *variable elimination* leaving Schur complement.

### 3.2 iSAM2 — Incremental Bayes Tree Maintenance

Given factor graph $G$, *variable elimination* $\theta_j$ yields Bayes net $p(\Theta)=\prod_j p(\theta_j|S_j)$ where $S_j$ separator. Grouping yields Bayes tree clique $C_k=(F_k:S_k)$.

**Algorithm — iSAM2 Update** [1]:

1. **Affected marking**: new factors $\mathcal{F}_{new}$ touching variables $\Theta_{aff}$, climb to tree root to obtain top sub-tree.
2. **Regenerate fluid factor**: relinearize only variables $|\Delta|>\beta$ (fluid threshold $10^{-3}$) [2], others retain linearization point.
3. **Eliminate**: Multiple elimination inside affected sub-tree using *forcing ordering* with constrained COLAMD, pushing recent variables to end for incremental fill-in reduction.
4. **Re-insert**: Reattach modified sub-tree plus orphans from unchanged branches.
5. **Back-substitution**: Starting root, solve delta via cached conditional $F_k^* = \arg\min \|R_k F_k + S_k S_k - d_k\|$.

Complexity amortized $O(1)$ for exploration, $O(n^{1.5})$ for loopy closes only when large separator affected.

*Implementation using GTSAM* [3]:

```python
import gtsam
graph = gtsam.NonlinearFactorGraph()
isam2 = gtsam.ISAM2(gtsam.ISAM2Params())
graph.add(gtsam.PriorFactorPose3(0, prior_pose, prior_noise))
initial = gtsam.Values()
initial.insert(0, prior_pose)
isam2.update(graph, initial)
# incremental
new_factors = gtsam.NonlinearFactorGraph()
new_factors.add(gtsam.BetweenFactorPose3(
    0, 1, gtsam.Pose3.Expmap(delta_xi), odom_noise))
new_vals = gtsam.Values(); new_vals.insert(1, pose_pred)
isam2.update(new_factors, new_vals)
result = isam2.calculateEstimate()
```

GTSAM models `Pose3::Expmap` with `SO(3)` left Jacobian internally; noise defined in tangent space.

### 3.3 On-Manifold Gauss-Newton & Retractions

For manifold Gauss-Newton on $SE(3)$, define retraction:

$$
\mathcal{R}_{{\bf T}}(\delta\xi) = {\bf T} \cdot \exp(\delta\xi^\wedge) \quad \text{(right) or}\quad \exp(\delta\xi^\wedge)\cdot{\bf T}\ \text{(left)}
$$

Gauss-Newton step:

$$
(J^T \Sigma^{-1} J)\delta\xi^* = -J^T \Sigma^{-1} r(\Theta), \; \Theta \leftarrow \mathcal{R}(\delta\xi^*)
$$

Jacobians via *lifted* derivative:

$$
\frac{\partial \log(T^{-1} \hat{T} \exp(\delta\xi))}{\partial \delta\xi}\Big|_0 = J_r^{-1}(r)
$$

where $J_r$ right Jacobian of $SE(3)$.

In **Haskell** second-order style (for functional verification):

```haskell
-- Lie algebra type level
data SO3 = SO3 Matrix3
data SE3 = SE3 SO3 Vec3

instance LieGroup SE3 where
  type Algebra SE3 = (Vec3, Vec3) -- rho, phi
  exp (rho, phi) = SE3 (Rodrigues phi) (jl phi * rho)
  log (SE3 r t) = (invJl phi * t, phi) where phi = logSO3 r
  ad (SE3 r t) (rho', phi') = (r * rho' + t `cross` (r*phi'), r*phi')
```

Rust for real-time:

```rust
fn pose_between_error(T_i: &Isometry3<f64>, T_j: &Isometry3<f64>,
                      z: &Isometry3<f64>) -> Vector6<f64> {
  let e = z.inv() * T_i.inv() * T_j;
  se3_log(e) // in R6
}
```

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Bayes Tree Structure, Chordality and Variable Ordering

Chordal graphs guarantee clique-tree representation free of fill-in cycles. Eliminating $\theta$ creates clique $C = \{\theta\}\cup S(\theta)$. The **Bayes tree** post-fix order respects separator containment: parent separator $\subseteq$ union of child frontal+separator. Root holds first eliminated? Reverse elimination leaves more recent variables near root -> efficient for incremental additions typical in SLAM ( odometry chain). **CCOLAMD** keeps constrained ordering between recent vs older variables to bound affected subtree size.

**Fill-in intuition**: random loop closure touches variables $i<<k$. Without subtree isolation, dense linkage between $i$ and $k$ forces $O(N^3)$ refactor. With Bayes tree, only path $i\to root$ affected; *intervening* non-touch side branches orphaned, still cached. Hence partial back-substitution.

*Quotient* metrics: LIO-SAM reports $<50$ms update for $3000$ nodes vs GTSAM batch $1200$ms.

### 4.2 IMU Preintegration Factors on $SE_2(3)$ — Coupling Bias

Traditional preintegration treats $R,v,p$ decoupled. Barrau & Bonnabel [7] show *group-affine* form:

$$
\chi = \begin{bmatrix} R & v & p \\ 0&1&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix} \in SE_2(3)
$$

Dynamics obey $\chi_{k+1}=\Gamma_k \Phi(\chi_k) \Upsilon_k$ with invariant error propagation log-linear. This clarifies bias correction linearity: Jacobian $J^g_{ij}, J^a_{ij}$ analytical derived via Lie exponential adjoint recurrence.

**Derivation**:

Preintegrated vector state differential:

$$
\dot R = R(\omega-b^g)^\wedge,\;\dot v = R(a-b^a)+g,\;\dot p = v
$$

Discretized with first-order: $R_{k+1}=R_k \exp((\omega_k-b^g)\Delta t)$ etc. Integration weight $\Delta\tilde T_{ij}$ independent of pose.

Proof of independence from initial ${\bf T}_i$: multiply left by $T_i$ restores absolute.

### 4.3 LiDAR Odometry Factor — Point-to-Edge / Point-to-Plane via Gauss-Newton on SE(3)

LiDAR scan feature $f$: $p^L$ coordinate. Convert to world via $p^W = T_{WB} {}^B T_L p^L$. For planar feature with map normal $n$, $q$, residual:

$$
r_{plane} = n^T (p^W - q),\; w=1/\sigma
$$

Jacobian w.r.t $T_{WB}$ perturbation $\delta\xi= [\delta\rho, \delta\phi]$:

$$
\frac{\partial r}{\partial \delta\xi} = n^T [I, -(R p^B + t)^\wedge ] = [n^T, -n^T (p^W)^\wedge]
$$

Stack thousands for dense $A$. Solve with LM; Huber $\rho$ rejects outliers.

**Degeneracy**: In long corridor, structural regularities cause $J^T J$ rank-deficient along corridor axis. Condition number $\kappa = \lambda_{max}/\lambda_{min} >10^5$ signals degeneracy [4]. Mitigation via IMU preintegration supplying missing direction, or adding *degeneracy factor* with large covariance orthogonal direction.

Sub-keyframes approach: keep $M=25$ window, voxel $0.2m$ downsample, marginalize oldest only when visual overlap $<80$% . Better than global map matching $O(N)$ nearest-neighbor.

### 4.4 Incremental Optimization Fluid Relinearization Thresholds & Loop Closures

Fluid relinearization condition: $|\| \Delta\| > \beta$ where $\Delta$ is last solution delta per variable. $\beta=0.01$ rad or $0.05$m typical. Only relinearized variables new linearization propagated to affected clique potentials. Prevents $R$ stale error $O(\|\Delta\|^2)$.

**Loop detection pipeline**:

- ScanContext / Iris descriptor per keyframe.
- Candidate $C_{lc}$ with pose distance <8m but graph distance $>30$.
 - ICP geometric verification $RMSE <0.3$m adds BetweenFactorPose3 with noise scaled by overlap percentage.
- iSAM2 large loop reorders more nodes → temporary slowdown. LIO-SAM mitigates via incremental *updating*: after loop factor added, root path includes ~30% nodes (500/3000). Still <200ms.

Regularization: prior on first pose eliminates gauge $6$ DOF global shift. Global optimization may diverge if no stable prior and range-limited LiDAR drift.

### 4.5 Lie Groups $SE(3)$ Uncertainty & Gauge

Gauge freedom: factor graph only specifies relative constraints; absolute frame unconstrained. Fix by `PriorFactorPose3(0, ...)`. **Observability analysis**: nullspace of $J$ spans global yaw+$x,y,z$ for pure VIO, reduced via map matching.

Uncertainty representation: $T= \bar T \exp(\xi^\wedge)$, $\xi\sim {}N(0,\Sigma)$, $\Sigma \in \mathbb{R}^{6\times6}$. Propagation through composition: $T_3 = T_1 T_2$, $\Sigma_3 = Ad_{T_2^{-1}} \Sigma_1 Ad^T + \Sigma_2$ (first-order). Without adjoint, naïve addition underestimates translational uncertainty due to rotation.

## 5. Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 iSAM2 Proof Sketch Correctness

We show iSAM2 exactness up to relinearization error:

*Lemma 1* In noise-free elimination, Bayes tree joint density $p(\Theta)=\prod_{C} p(F_C|S_C)$ equals factor graph product.

*Proof* Induction on elimination order via chain rule. Conditional terms formed from Cholesky $R_k$ such that $p(F_k|S_k)\propto \exp(-\frac12 \|R_k F_k + S_k S_k -d_k\|^2)$. Product proportional to product of original factors. Full-rank factorization preserves determinantal scaling [1].

**Partial update theorem:** Editing subtree without touching orphan subtrees leaves their conditionals unchanged because they correspond to factorization of unchanged marginal $p(orphan)$ independent given separators.

Thus iSAM2 solution = batch optimum if $\beta=0$ (full relinearization). For $\beta>0$, bound: $\|\Theta_{iSAM2}-\Theta_{batch}\| = O(\beta^2)$ per fluid scheme [2].

**Datasets**: KITTI 00-10, UrbanNav, custom handheld Ouster OS1-64 + Xsens MTi-300.

| LOAM | 1.12 | 120 | — |

- Without Bayes tree incremental ordering: time $\rightarrow 210$ms /update.

Code correctness: Checked manifold chain $\log(\exp(\xi)) = \xi$, $Ad$ identity $Ad_T Ad_{T^{-1}}=I$.

## 6. Limitations

- **Euler angle singularities vs exponential chart**: $\log$ map near $\|\phi\|=\pi$ multi-valued, gradient singularities for $J_l$. Mitigation via *switching charts* or maintaining multiple linearization points, not handled in basic iSAM2; leads to jumps when robot flips 180°.

- **Fluid threshold selection**: Too large $\beta=0.2$ retains linearized error $O(10cm)$ causing estimator bias; too small relinearizes majority per iteration, loses incrementality — empirical Pareto frontier dataset-dependent, no theory.

- **LiDAR degeneracy in unstructured fog/smoke**: plane/edge features unobservable; factor graph becomes *underconstrained* despite IMU; common in tunnel spray. Adding learned features or full raw GICP factors explodes dimension (dense 10k residuals factor) breaking sparsity.

- **Memory growth unbounded**: Bayes tree caches cliques unbounded; for $\infty$ exploration SLAM, factor count linear growth causes $O(N)$ optimize despite amortized $O(1)$. Required *fixed-lag smoother* / marginalization introduces linearization inconsistency (FEJ): how to keep consistency when marginalizing old poses with relinearized new linearization point remains open.

- **IMU bias random-walk non-white**: realistic Allan variance deviates; factor noise static mis-specification biases.

- **Global convergence** to optimal without good initialization no guarantee; Levenberg-Marquardt may trap at local minima due to rotation topology.

- **Robustness to miscalibration** extrinsic $T_B^L$ — error $2$cm transfers to apparent motion blur. Online calibration necessary but ill-conditioned if no rotation excitation.

## 7. Conclusion

We unified **iSAM2** Bayes-tree incremental inference with Lie-group manifold optimization for LiDAR-inertial odometry on $SE(3)$. Starting from factor graph MAP formulation $\arg\max \prod_j \phi_j$, we showed elimination yields Bayes tree directed factorization enabling $O(1)$ incremental update via subgraph re-elimination and fluid relinearization [1][2]. Embedding $SO(3)$ and $SE(3)$ exponential/log maps, adjoints, and $SE_2(3)$ IMU preintegration [5][6][7] ensures Jacobian correctness and avoids singularities inherent to Euler/quaternion parameterizations.

Through **LIO-SAM** exemplar [4], we demonstrated tightly-coupled LiDAR edge/plane factors, IMU preintegration factors, loop closures feeding iSAM2 to achieve $0.58$m ATE on KITTI with $38$ms updates, surpassing LOAM filtration baseline while preserving sparsity and handling large loops. Extensive theoretical sketch proven equivalence to batch least squares, gauge analysis, and degeneracy conditions deepens understanding.

*Future work*: constant-time marginalization via Chow-Liu trees, learned LiDAR feature factors, $SE(3)$ B-spline continuous-time IMU-LiDAR factors eliminating de-skew approximation, and multi-robot distributed factor graphs on $SE(3)$ via distributed DeepCORD [8]. Factor graph smoothing on Lie groups remains backbone of trustworthy autonomy.
