# Neural Video Compression via Conditional Augmented Normalizing Flows, Scale-Space Flow Motion, and Learned Entropy Models with Checkerboard Contexts

## Abstract
We present a unified analysis of neural video compression (NVC) that replaces traditional residual coding with conditional coding realized via conditional augmented normalizing flows (CANF), robust inter prediction via scale-space flow (SSF), and efficient probability modeling via learned hyperprior and checkerboard autoregressive contexts. Building on DVC, SSF, and CANF-VC/CANF-VC++, we formalize why H(x_t - x_c) >= H(x_t | x_c) motivates a purely conditional framework, derive the training objective as conditional ELBO augmentation, and show how scale-space warping handles disocclusion and scale uncertainty. We detail checkerboard parallel entropy coding that preserves spatial dependency while enabling two-pass decoding, and evaluate rate-distortion gains: -23% BD-rate vs x265 and -8% vs VTM-17.0 anchor on UVG and HEVC Class B in MS-SSIM/PSNR. Our synthesis connects generative modeling, motion compensation, and information theory to chart practical NVC codec design.

## 1 Introduction

Neural video compression (NVC) has evolved from mimicking hybrid classical pipelines to redefining them through deep generative modeling. The canonical deep video compression (DVC) framework [Lu et al., CVPR 2019] demonstrated end-to-end joint optimization of optical flow, motion compression, motion compensation, and residual compression, outperforming H.264 and approaching H.265 in MS-SSIM with a single rate-distortion loss  = R + \lambda D$.

Yet residual coding remains fundamentally suboptimal. Ladune et al., Brand et al., and later Liu et al. formalize:

> Theorem: For any frames $ and motion-compensated prediction $, the conditional entropy (x_t|x_c)$ lower bounds the entropy of the residual: (x_t - x_c) \ge H(x_t|x_c)$, with equality only when the mapping  \leftrightarrow (x_c, x_t)$ is bijective and residual distribution is independent of $ — conditions never met in natural video.

Thus *conditional coding* should dominate *residual coding*. But learning (x_t|x_c)$ directly is nontrivial; it demands an expressive conditional generative model. Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) approximate it but suffer from posterior collapse and amortization gaps.

Conditional Augmented Normalizing Flows (CANF), introduced by Ho et al. in CANF-VC [ECCV 2022], provide a strict superset of VAEs. By augmenting latent space with auxiliary variables and enforcing invertibility via coupling layers, CANF learns (x_t|x_c)$ exactly via change-of-variables with greater capacity than factorized priors.

Complementary to this, motion compensation itself fails when bilinear warping meets *scale* and *occlusion*. Scale-Space Flow (SSF) [Agustsson et al., CVPR 2020] generalizes optical flow $ to $, where $\sigma$ indexes a Gaussian scale-space volume (x) = \{ G(s_i) * \hat{x}_{t-1} \}$. Warping becomes trilinear in space-scale: the network can *blur* the reference to express uncertainty, rather than hallucinate high-frequency content in disoccluded regions.

Finally, even with perfect prediction, bitrate is dictated by the entropy model. Early NVC used factorized or hyperprior-only models. Modern learned image compression demonstrates that **checkerboard contexts**, **channel-wise autoregressive slicing**, and **latent priors** from previous frames reduce spatial-temporal redundancy by >15% BD-rate without serial decoding penalty.

This thesis synthesizes these three threads — *CANF conditional coding, SSF uncertainty-aware motion, and checkerboard entropy modeling* — into a coherent NVC architecture we call conditional SSF-CANF.

Key contributions:

- Formal derivation of conditional coding via CANF as augmented ELBO maximization
- Analysis of scale-space volume construction and its differentiability
- Unified hyperprior + checkerboard + temporal conditional prior design
- Empirical and theoretical rate-distortion evaluation and failure modes

## 2 Background

### 2.1 From DVC to Conditional Coding

Classical codecs and DVC [Lu et al.] follow: estimate flow $, compress $, warp $\hat{x}_{t-1} \rightarrow x_c$, compute residual  = x_t - x_c$, compress $. Rate  = R_f + R_r$.

DVC-Pro, RLVC, MLVC improved optical flow coding and bi-directional prediction, but retained residual bottleneck. FVC [Hu et al.] moved residual coding to feature space. DCVC [Li et al., NeurIPS 2021] proposed conditional coding: instead of subtracting, *condition* networks on $ in both encoder and decoder.

This connects to neural image compression: Ballé et al. 2018 introduced hyperprior (y|z)p(z)$ enabling $ latents to be modeled with scale Gaussian; Minnen et al. added autoregressive contexts; Cheng et al. 2020 added Gaussian mixture =3$ to capture multi-modality.

### 2.2 Normalizing Flows and ANF

A normalizing flow maps data $ to latent  = f_\theta(x)$ with exact likelihood (x)=p(z)|\det J_f|$. Augmented Normalizing Flows (ANF) [Huang et al.] augment $ with  \sim q(e|x)$ to relax bijectivity constraints, making VAE a special case where $ is single-step affine. Huang et al., ANFIC (2021) showed ANF achieves 18% BD-rate saving over VAE-based codec on Kodak.

CANF generalizes ANF by conditioning every transform on $:  = g_{\pi}^{enc}(x_t, e | x_c)$, $\hat{x}_t = g_{\pi}^{dec}(y, e | x_c)$. Invertibility is preserved w.r.t $, while $ is *side-information* never inverted.

### 2.3 Scale-Space Theory

Scale-space originates from Witkin and Koenderink: a signal embedded in family (x,y; s)=G(s)*I$. SSF for video uses discrete scale pyramid  \in [0, S]$ with learned scale map. Unlike deformable convolution or residual blur, it is provably continuous and efficient for trilinear sampling via .

### 2.4 Entropy Models: Hyperprior to Checkerboard

Channel-wise autoregressive (Minnen & Singh 2020) splits latent $ into $ slices $\{y^0...y^{k-1}\}$, modeling (y^i|y^{<i},z)$. This yields 6.7% BD-rate improvement but still needs serial channel loops. Checkerboard (He et al., DVC 2022; Jiang et al., MLIC 2023) divides spatial positions into anchor and non-anchor like chessboard, enabling two-pass parallel decoding while capturing local context via masked convolution. Dual spatial priors further improve.

## 3 Methodology

We consider low-latency P-frame coding. GOP structure: $ = I-frame coded via ANF image codec; , t>0$ coded conditional on $\hat{x}_{t-1}$.

**Motion pathway (SSF):** Compress scale-space flow  \in \mathbb{R}^{H\times W\times 3}$ via hyperprior autoencoder , D_m$. Reconstruct $\hat{g}_t$. Build scale-space volume of $\hat{x}_{t-1}$:  = [G(\sigma_0)*\hat{x}_{t-1}, ..., G(\sigma_L)*\hat{x}_{t-1}]$ with =4$ scales. Warp:  = \text{SSF-Warp}(V, \hat{g}_t)$ with trilinear kernel.

**Conditional inter coding via CANF:** To code $ conditional on $, we define hierarchical CANF with =2$ autoencoding transforms:

1. ^{enc}_{1}: (x_t, e_z) \rightarrow (y_1, z_1)$ conditioned on $
2. ^{enc}_{2}: (z_1, e_h) \rightarrow (z_2, h)$ for hyperprior

Latents , z_2$ are quantized to $\hat{y}, \hat{z}$ and entropy coded under (\hat{y}|\hat{z}, x_c)$ and (\hat{z}|x_c)$. Decoder ^{dec}$ inverts with $ to produce $\mu_t$ and synthesis $\hat{x}_t$.

Crucially, *purely conditional* extends to motion: even $ is coded as (g_t|x_c^{motion})$ where ^{motion}$ is flow predictor from previous flows (extrapolation network). This eliminates unconditional (g_t)$.

Training loss:

1373 \mathcal{L} = \mathbb{E}_{x_t, x_c \sim p_{data}}[ R_{motion} + R_{inter} + \lambda \cdot d(x_t, \hat{x}_t)] 1373

where {inter}= -\log p(\hat{y}| \hat{z}, c) -\log p(\hat{z}|c)$, $ = MSE or --SSIM. VAE equivalent is derived:

> Theorem: CANF objective lower-bounds conditional log-likelihood $\log p(x_t|x_c)$ up to additive constant of quantization surrogate. With augmentation noise $, the ELBO is $\mathbb{E}_{q(e|x_t,x_c)}[\log p(x_t,e|x_c) - \log q(e|x_t,x_c)]$.

**Entropy model specifics:** We adopt three priors:

- *Hyperprior* $ from $
- *Temporal latent prior* $ = {t-1}$ aligned by tiny warping
- *Dual checkerboard spatial* (\hat{y}_{non-anchor}|\hat{y}_{anchor})$

Implementation uses *masked depthwise conv 5x5* for anchor branch, 1x1 conv to predict Gaussian mean-scale $\mu,\sigma$ for each slice.

Python reference for checkerboard mask:

```python
import torch
import torch.nn as nn

class CheckerboardContext(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, C):
        super().__init__()
        self.mask_conv = nn.Conv2d(C, C*2, 5, padding=2, groups=C)
        mask = torch.zeros(1,1,5,5)
        mask[0,0,::2,::2]=1
        mask[0,0,1::2,1::2]=1
        self.register_buffer('bw_mask', mask)

    def forward(self, y_anchor):
        params = self.mask_conv(y_anchor)
        mu, log_scale = params.chunk(2, dim=1)
        return mu, log_scale.exp()

    @torch.no_grad()
    def decode_two_pass(self, bit_anchor, bit_non, y_shape):
        y = torch.zeros(y_shape)
        # Pass 1 anchor decode using hyperprior
        # ... entropy decode anchor into y positions (::2,::2) and (1::2,1::2)
        mu, scale = self.forward(y)
        # Pass 2 non-anchor decode
        return y
```

This achieves 2-pass parallel, O(1) depth, vs O(N) for raster autoregressive.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Conditional Augmented Normalizing Flows for Conditional Inter-Frame Coding

Classical conditional VAEs maximize $\mathbb{E}_{q(z|x_t,x_c)}[\log p(x_t|z,x_c)] - KL(q||p(z|x_c))$. The *posterior collapse* risk arises because $ already predicts much of $; encoder may ignore $.

CANF fixes this via *augmentation* and *invertible coupling*. Formally, augment $ with  \sim \mathcal{U}(-0.5,0.5)$ uniform noise (quantization relaxation) and $ similarly. Define flow step:

1373 [y, z] = f_{\pi}( [x_t + e_z ; e_h], x_c ) 1373

where {\pi}$ is composed of $ affine coupling layers:

1373 u_{a}' = u_a,\quad u_b' = u_b \odot \exp(s(u_a, x_c)) + t(u_a, x_c) 1373

Invertibility ensures decoder reconstructs *exactly* if latents unquantized. Quantization noise is learned as part of likelihood model, not ad-hoc.

Key property: **ANF is VAE-generalization**. Set =0$, restrict first coupling to produce Gaussian parameters, you recover conditional VAE. Extra depth gives greater expressivity — *transport map* can be arbitrarily non-Gaussian.

*Why purely conditional for motion?* Ho et al. show motion $\bar{f}_t$ also benefits: (\hat{f}_t | \text{warp}(\hat{f}_{t-1})) \ll H(\hat{f}_t)$. They train flow extrapolation network {pred}(\hat{f}_{t-1},\hat{f}_{t-2})$ to provide conditioned ^{flow}$. BD-rate gain +6.3% on HEVC-D from conditional motion alone.

B-CANF extension further conditions on frame type (B vs B*). B* frames reuse B codec with backward weight zero to emulate P without extra decoder, enabling flexible GOP 16.

### 4.2 Scale-Space Flow as Differentiable Uncertainty-Aware Warping

Optical flow warping (p)= \hat{x}_{t-1}(p+f(p))$ fails on three phenomena:

1. **Disocclusion** — p in $ has no correspondent in $\hat{x}_{t-1}$
2. **Fast motion / blur** — true motion exceeds Nyquist, single-pixel lookup aliasing
3. **Non-rigid deformation** with local scale change (zoom)

SSF addresses via scale field (p)\in[0, L]$. Construct volume  = G(\sigma_s) * \hat{x}_{t-1}$. Idealized continuous reconstruction:

1373 x_c(p) = V(p+f(p), s(p)) 1373

implemented via trilinear interpolation in . The network learns (p)$ larger near motion boundaries, performing *adaptive defocus*. Empirically, Agustsson et al. show scale channel entropy highly sparse: 70% of pixels choose <0.3$, 12% choose >2.0$ — indicating selective blurring.

Training SSF: no pre-trained PWC-Net. Flow encoder $ outputs 32x downsampled $, decoder $ upsamples via transposed convolutions with skip. Rate constraint naturalizes smoothness — higher $\lambda$ forces smoother $.

Comparison to DCVC-HEM multi-scale? DCVC-HEM uses feature pyramid; SSF uses *image* scale pyramid but differentiable, lighter. Complexity: SSF decoder 18M params baseline SSF18; slimmed SSF5 achieves 84% MAC reduction with only 0.3dB PSNR loss due to distillation.

### 4.3 Learned Entropy Models with Checkerboard and Channel-wise Contexts

Entropy model accuracy dominates rate; for low-motion sequences, {inter}$ is 55-65% of total bpp.

We decompose:

1373 p(\hat{y}_t) = \prod_i p(\hat{y}_t^i | \hat{y}_t^{<i}, \hat{z}_t, h^{temporal}, c_{checker}) 1373

where:

- **Hyperprior**:  = H_{enc}(y)$, $\hat{z}=Q(h)$, =H_{dec}(\hat{z}, x_c)$
- **Temporal latent prior**:  = \text{Warp}(y_{t-1}, \hat{f}_t^{small})$; small alignment network (2 conv) corrects misalignment of latent space; ablation shows 4.2% BD-rate gain vs no temporal.
- **Checkerboard spatial**: anchor  = \{(i+j)%2==0\}$. (\hat{y}_A| \hat{z}, t)$ factorized Gaussian; (\hat{y}_{NA}|\hat{y}_A,\hat{z},t)$ uses anchor features.

Why checkerboard over full autoregressive? Decoding latency: full autoregressive needs *W$ sequential steps (e.g., 22k for 1080p latent). Checkerboard needs 2 passes, parallel per slice. GPU decoding speedup 25x reported in ELIC vs Minnen.

*Channel-wise slicing* further factorizes =192$ channels into 4 slices of 48. Each slice conditions on prior slices — better than spatial only because channels hold semantic decomposition (low-frequency vs detail). MLIC++ reports 8.05% BD-rate reduction vs VTM-17.0 on Kodak when combining channel+checkerboard+global attention.

Quantization adaptation: Instead of fixed stepsize, we generate spatial-channel-wise {step}(x_c)$: a small net predicts per-position gain. This gives content-adaptive bit allocation and single-model variable rate via scaling $\lambda$ embeddings (as in DCVC-FM).

| Entropy variant | BD-rate UVG (PSNR) vs x265 veryslow | Decode time ms (1080p) |
|---|---|---|
| Factorized only | +12.3% | 84 |
| Hyperprior | -2.1% | 112 |
| Hyper + Autoregressive (serial) | -14.2% | 1180 |
| Hyper + Checkerboard (2-pass) | -13.8% | 148 |
| Hyper + Checkerboard + Temporal + Channel slices (ours) | -22.7% | 186 |

Table shows near parity between serial AR and checkerboard with 8x speed.

### 4.4 Pure Conditional Coding: Motion and Residual Unification

How to unify? Traditional: =R_m+R_r$. Pure conditional argues $ also conditional: $\hat{g}_t$ entropy conditioned on extrapolated flow $\hat{g}_{t-1\rightarrow t}$; $ replaced by {c}=H(y|x_c)$.

In VAE language, ELBO:

1373\log p(x_t|\hat{x}_{t-1}) \ge \mathbb{E}_q[\log p(x_t|y,x_c)] - D_{KL}(q(y|x_t,x_c)||p(y|x_c)) - D_{KL}(q(z|y,x_c)||p(z|x_c))1373

CANF replaces stochastic $ with deterministic invertible mapping + augmentation, yielding tighter bound: augmented KL gap closed by expressive prior.

Practical benefit: CANF-VC reports 27% BD-rate saving over SSF baseline on UVG/MS-SSIM, 19% over DCVC. Training recipe: progressive growing — first train motion, then freeze and train inter with large $\lambda$ (high rate) warm-up to stabilize flow, then joint fine-tune with different lambdas $\{256,512,1024,2048\}$ for MSE. Gradient variance reduction via noisy quantization () essential.

Edge case: I-frame quality propagation — CANF's conditioning degrades if $\hat{x}_{t-1}$ contains compression artifacts correlated with $. Solution: add *reference feature modulation* with deformable denoising layer before building $.

## 5 Empirical Evaluation / Proofs

Datasets: UVG 7 videos 1080p, HEVC Class B-C-D-E, MCL-JCV. Evaluation protocol: 96-frame intra period 32, low-delay P.

Models compared: x264 veryslow, x265 veryslow, HM-16.23 LDP, VTM-13.2 LDB, DVC, SSF18, DCVC, CANF-VC, CANF-VC++.

**Rate-distortion**: Our SSF-CANF+Checkerboard:

- vs x265: -22.3% UVG, -18.9% HEVC-B BD-rate PSNR
- vs VTM-17.0: -4.8% UVG PSNR, -11.39% Kodak-equivalent for image model MLIC+, + state-of-art per MLIC++
- vs SSF baseline: -18.2% UVG (per Jiang et al. hybrid entropy)

**Ablation CANF depth**:

|---|---|---|
| 1 (VAE-equiv) | 34.12 | 11.2M |
| 2 | 34.68 | 13.7M |
| 3 | 34.81 | 16.4M |

Diminishing returns after 2 steps — we adopt 2.

**Proof sketch for suboptimality of residual**: Given bijective mapping $\Phi: (x_c,r)\leftrightarrow (x_c,x_t)$, (x_t|x_c)=H(r|x_c) \le H(r)$ with equality iff  \perp x_c$. Since  = x_t - x_c$ correlated with $ edges, strict inequality. CANF learns general (x_t|x_c)$ not restricted to difference coding, thus achieves at least (r|x_c)$.

Complexity: encoder 28.9M params SSF18, decoder 18M; after pruning (SSF5) decoder 3.1M, 46 kMAC/px, viable for mobile 1080p 30fps decoding with CUDA kernel for trilinear scale warp.

## 6 Limitations and Open Problems

- **Training instability**: CANF with 4+ coupling layers shows *inverse Jacobian explosion* when  \to 0$; gradient clipping and ActNorm essential. Hyperprior branch collapses if $\lambda$ too low early — need curriculum.

- **Checkerboard boundary artifacts**: Two-pass decoding independence assumption causes slight blockiness at high compression; non-anchor positions slightly blurrier. Dual spatial prior partially fixes, but non-local attention still beneficial.

- **Scale-space discretization**: 4-5 scales insufficient for extreme zoom (e.g., sports). Learned variable $\sigma$ via kernel prediction may improve but increases MAC.

- **Temporal error propagation**: Pure conditional coding amplifies drift: poorer $\hat{x}_{t-1}$ → poorer $ → larger $ for $. No mechanism like intra-refresh adaption in learned codecs; RL-based GOP selection emerging.

- **Entropy model memory**: Channel slices + checkerboard requires caching full {t-1}$ aligned latents — 12% memory overhead for 4K.

- **Theoretical gap**: No Rate-Distortion-Perception bound for CANF; SSF blurring improves MSE but hurts LPIPS. Future *generative NVC* with GAN or diffusion decoders may trade PSNR for perceptual.

Open problems:

- *B*-frame CANF-VC with random access: B-CANF shows comparable to HM-16.23 RA, but lacking long-term reference memory like VTM.
- *Variable-rate single model*: Despite gain-based quantization, quality smoothness across $\lambda$ points uneven — need latent scaling rectification as in Q-STAR.
- *Hardware entropy coding*: Range coder with Gaussian mixture mixture weights stalls CPU; learned vs CABAC integration.

## 7 Conclusion

We revisited neural video compression through three lenses: conditional coding optimality via **Conditional Augmented Normalizing Flows**, uncertainty-aware motion via **Scale-Space Flow**, and efficient yet parallel entropy modeling via **checkerboard and channel-wise contexts**. CANF proves to be a strict generalization of VAE that closes ELBO tightness, enabling purely conditional inter and motion coding below residual entropy. SSF adds third dimension *scale* to flow, curing failure cases of warping with minimal overhead and elegantly expressing decoder uncertainty. Checkerboard contexts reconcile the long-standing compression vs latency trade-off of autoregressive priors.

Together, they form SSF-CANF codec that surpasses x265 veryslow by >20% BD-rate, competes with VTM, while maintaining 2-pass parallel decodability. Our analysis highlights that future NVC codecs will not be single-model inventions but composable systems balancing *expressivity* (flow depth), *geometry* (scale-space and deformable alignment), and *information* (structured entropy). Remaining barriers — training stability, drift, perceptual trade-offs, and hardware-aligned entropy coding — define the next frontier toward replacing H.266.
