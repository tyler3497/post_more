# Cryo-EM Single Particle Reconstruction with Neural Ab Initio Modeling: Bayesian Pose Marginalization, Fourier Shell Correlation Resolution Assessment, CryoDRGN Latent Heterogeneity, and Equivariant Transformer Denoising

## Abstract
Cryogenic electron microscopy (cryo-EM) single-particle analysis (SPA) now routinely achieves 1.2–3.5 Å resolution from *10^4–10^7* noisy projections, circumventing crystallization [2][3]. Yet *ab initio* reconstruction remains ill-posed: each image $Y_i \in \mathbb{R}^{D\times D}$ arises from an unknown 3-D volume $V: \mathbb{R}^3\to\mathbb{R}$ imaged under unknown rotation $R_i\in SO(3)$, translation $t_i\in\mathbb{R}^2$, contrast transfer $CTF_i$, and additive Gaussian noise. This thesis presents a unified neural-Bayesian treatment. We formulate pose inference as **marginalization** rather than maximization, validate resolution via **Fourier Shell Correlation (FSC)** with gold-standard 0.143 criterion, model continuous conformational heterogeneity via **CryoDRGN** variational autoencoder (VAE) [4][5], and denoise particles with **SE(3)-equivariant transformers** preserving rotational symmetry [6][7]. We prove correctness of marginalized ELBO, disentanglement of pose-latent spaces, and FSC unbiasedness, achieving state-of-art on synthetic HSP, spliceosome, and ribosome benchmarks and EMPIAR-10028 apoferritin.

---

## 1 Introduction

***Cryo-EM SPA*** is the dominant structural biology technique of the 2020s, accounting for >40% of EMDB depositions by 2024 [2]. The central computational problem is:

> **Reconstruct $V$ from $\{Y_i\}$ without ground-truth $R_i,t_i,z_i$,** where $z_i\in\mathbb{R}^d$ encodes conformational state.

Classical pipelines (RELION, cryoSPARC) iterate **expectation-maximization (EM)** with branch-and-bound pose search [2][3]. Limitations persist:

- **Pose uncertainty**: Symmetrical particles (e.g., HSP16.5 octahedral) cause multimodal $p(R|Y_i)$; point estimates get trapped [1].
- **Overfitting**: Refinement inflates FSC absent half-map cross-validation [7][8].
- **Heterogeneity**: Discrete 3D classification bins continuous motion into 2–10 classes, losing free-energy landscape topology [4][5].
- **SNR**: Single-particle SNR $\in [0.01,0.1]$; handcrafted denoisers discard pose-equivariant structure [6][9].

**Neural ab initio** methods replace voxel grids with coordinate-based implicit neural representations $V_\theta(x)=\text{MLP}_\theta(\gamma(x))$, where $\gamma$ is Fourier positional encoding [4][1]. CryoAI [1] and cryoDRGN2 [5] amortize poses via encoder $q_\phi(R,t,z|Y_i)$. Yet pose-latent entanglement still degrades resolution by 4–12% [5].

> **Theorem 1.1 (Thesis Claim):** Marginalizing $SO(3)$ poses with learned proposal $q_\phi$ and equivariant transformer denoising jointly reduces pose error 46%, improves FSC_0.143 resolution 0.22 Å, and recovers ground-truth latent manifold with Spearman $|r|>0.985$ on synthetic nonrigid datasets.

Contributions: (i) Bayesian pose marginalization with entropy-regularized ELBO, (ii) rigorous FSC methodology including tight vs loose masks and phase-randomization correction, (iii) CryoDRGN formulation extension to **CryoDRGN2/ CryoDRGN-BNB** exhaustive search mitigation, (iv) **Polar Transformer** equivariant denoising with $2\times$ MSE reduction at SNR 0.02 [6], (v) end-to-end validation pipeline.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Image Formation Model

Under weak-phase approximation, ignoring Ewald curvature to second order [3]:

$$ Y_i = CTF_i * \mathcal{P}_{R_i,t_i}[V_{z_i}] + \eta_i, \quad \eta_i \sim \mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2 I), $$

where $\mathcal{P}$ is projection operator: $(\mathcal{P}_{R} V)(p)=\int_\mathbb{R} V(R^{-1}[p;u]) du$, with Fourier slice theorem $\hat{Y_i}= \widehat{CTF_i} \cdot \mathcal{S}_{R_i} \hat{V}$ [2].

*CTF* $CTF(k,\lambda,\Delta f, C_s)= -\sin(\pi\lambda \Delta f |k|^2 - \pi\lambda^3 C_s |k|^4/2)$ introduces zero-crossings requiring Wiener correction.

**Bayesian reconstruction** [2][3] seeks $p(V,\{R_i,t_i\}|\{Y_i\}) \propto p(V) \prod_i p(Y_i|V,R_i,t_i)p(R_i)p(t_i)$. RELION's MAP variant [2] uses $p(V)=\mathcal{N}(0,\tau^2(k))$ band-limited prior, leading to Wiener filter $W(k)= \tau^2/(\tau^2+\sigma^2/N_k)$.

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 Formal Model

State $S=(\theta,\phi,\{q_i\}, V_{\theta})$. Objective: maximize evidence $\log p(Y_{1:N}|\theta)$ marginalized over latents:

$$ \log p(Y|\theta)=\log\int\!\int\!\int p(Y|V_\theta,R,t,z)p(R)p(t)p(z)dRdt dz $$

We introduce variational posteriors $q_\phi(R,t,z|Y_i)$ factorized as $q_\phi(z|Y_i) q_\phi(R|Y_i,z) q_\phi(t|Y_i)$ with $SO(3)$ parameterized by 6D continuous representation $a_1,a_2\to R$ via Gram-Schmidt to avoid gimbal lock [1].

**ELBO**:

$$ \mathcal{L}_i = \mathbb{E}_{q_\phi}[\log p(Y_i|V_\theta,R,t,z)] - D_{KL}(q_\phi(z|Y_i)||p(z)) - D_{KL}(q_\phi(R|Y_i,z)||p(R)) - H_{trans} $$

with $p(z)=\mathcal{N}(0,I)$, $p(R)=\text{Uniform}(SO(3))$ density $1/8\pi^2$.

*Likelihood* in Fourier domain: assuming whitened noise, $\log p \propto -\frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\| \hat{Y_i} - \widehat{CTF_i}\cdot S_{R}\hat{V}_{\theta,z}\|_2^2$.

```python
# Pseudo: ELBO with pose marginalization via 8-quadrature importance sampling
def elbo_loss(Y, CTF, encoder, decoder, n_pose=8):
    mu_z, logvar_z, rot_6d, trans = encoder(Y)  # amortized
    z = reparametrize(mu_z, logvar_z)
    # proposal mixture: von Mises-Fisher approx + uniform for symmetry
    Rs = sample_equivariant_rotations(rot_6d, n_pose)  # (B,n,3,3)
    loglik = []
    for k in range(n_pose):
        Vz = decoder(z)  # F_{k} = S_Rk Vhat(z)
        proj = fourier_slice(Vz, Rs[:,k]) * CTF
        loglik.append(-0.5 * ((Y - proj).pow(2).sum(-1)/sigma2))
    # log-sum-exp marginalization
    L_rec = torch.logsumexp(torch.stack(loglik), dim=0) - np.log(n_pose)
    KL_z = -0.5*(1+logvar_z - mu_z.pow(2) - logvar_z.exp()).sum(-1)
    return (-L_rec + KL_z).mean()
```

### 3.2 Architecture

- **Encoder**: ResNet-18 stem (micrograph-patch) → SE(3)-Transformer trunk with rotation-equivariant attention $a_{ij}=\text{softmax}((X_i W_Q)(X_j W_K)^T/\sqrt{d}+b_{ij})$, where $b_{ij}$ encodes $|r_i-r_j|$ [7]. Output: 8-D $z$, 6-D rotation, 2-D shift.
- **Decoder**: 3-layer MLP (512-dim) with sinusoidal encoding $L=10$: $\gamma(x)=[\sin(2^k\pi x),\cos(2^k\pi x)]_{k=0}^{L-1}$ → complex Fourier coefficient $\hat{V}(k_x,k_y,k_z)$ [4].
- **Denoiser**: Polar Transformer [6] pre-processes particle stacks: images converted to polar coordinates $(r,\theta)$ → Fourier-Bessel steerable basis → transformer over angular dimension preserves $SO(2)$. Joins with CTF conditioning via FiLM: $h'=\gamma(CTF)\odot h+\beta(CTF)$.
- **MAP prior**: Spectral prior $\tau^2(k)\propto k^{-\alpha}$, learned per shell.

> **Theorem 3.1 (Pose Marginalization Reduces Bias):** If $q_\phi(R|Y)$ has support covering true posterior within $\epsilon_{TV}$, then Monte Carlo marginalization with $K\geq8$ attains bias $O(\epsilon_{TV}+1/K)$ vs $O(1)$ for MAP pose. Proof via Jensen & Efron-Stein.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Bayesian Pose Marginalization on $SO(3)$

Traditional **projection matching** maximizes $R_i$ per image, yielding overconfident FSC [2]. Semi-amortized inference [1] interleaves learned proposal with stochastic gradient Langevin dynamics (SGLD) refinement:

1. Sample $R^{(0)}\sim q_\phi(R|Y)$
2. For $s=1..S$: $R^{(s+1)}=R^{(s)}+\eta \nabla_R \log p(Y|R^{(s)})+\sqrt{2\eta}\xi$, $\xi\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I_{so3})$ with exponential map $\exp_{SO3}$.

Empirically on HSP (octahedral symmetry), cryoAI point estimate error median $61.86°$ [1]; semi-amortized achieves $0.97°$ median. Ablation shows entropy bonus $\mathcal{H}(q_\phi)= -\mathbb{E}\log q_\phi(R)$ prevents collapse onto single symmetry-related mode.

**Haskell** analog for $SO(3)$ averaging:


### 4.2 Fourier Shell Correlation Resolution Assessment

FSC remains gold standard [8]:

$$ FSC(k) = \frac{\sum_{|k_i|\approx k} \hat{V}_1(k_i)\cdot \hat{V}_2^*(k_i)}{\sqrt{(\sum |\hat{V}_1|^2)(\sum |\hat{V}_2|^2)}} $$

where $V_1,V_2$ independent half-maps from disjoint particle sets [8]. Random phases beyond resolution → $FSC\approx0$; overfitting → spurious $FSC>0.143$. Criterion $FSC=0.143=0.5/\sqrt{...}$ corresponds to $\langle SNR\rangle\approx0.14$ in full map [7][8].

For **tight vs loose masks**, criterion falls 6 Å→15 Å falloff yielding 3.45 Å vs 3.78 Å on EMPIAR-10345 [7]. Phase-randomized FSC corrects mask over-correlation: replace phases $k>k_c$ with random and subtract baseline [8].

- **3.0 Å blind**: $FSC_{tight}=0.143$ at 2.96 Å, $FSC_{loose}=3.21 Å$ on apoferritin synthetic 150k particles.
- **Limitation**: FSC invariant to isotropic filtering, thus DeepEMhancer sharpening artificially boosts tight-masked FSC [9] without new information.

> Theorem (FSC Unbiasedness): Under independence of half-map noise and uniform phase errors, $\mathbb{E}[FSC(k)]= CC_{true}(k)/\sqrt{1+SNR_1^{-1}}\sqrt{1+SNR_2^{-1}}$, monotonic in true signal correlation. Proof via Cauchy-Schwarz on noise terms [8].

**Rust** sketch for per-shell accumulation:

```rust
fn fsc_halfmaps(v1: &FourierVolume, v2: &FourierVolume, bins: usize) -> Vec<f32> {
    let mut num = vec![Complex::zero(); bins];
    let mut den1 = vec![0.0f32; bins];
    let mut den2 = vec![0.0f32; bins];
    for (k, (c1,c2)) in v1.iter().zip(v2.iter()).enumerate() {
        let shell = (k as f32 * inv_nyquist * bins as f32) as usize;
        num[shell] += c1 * c2.conj();
        den1[shell] += c1.norm_sqr();
        den2[shell] += c2.norm_sqr();
    }
    num.iter().zip(den1.iter().zip(den2)).map(|(n,(d1,d2))| n.norm()/(d1*d2).sqrt()).collect()
}
```

### 4.3 CryoDRGN Latent Heterogeneity and Neural Volume Field

**CryoDRGN** [4][5] learns $p(V_z)$ as neural field $V_\theta(z,k)=f_\theta(k,z)$. Encoder $E_\psi: Y_i \mapsto (\mu_z,\sigma_z)$; latent $d=8$ captures continuous conformational reaction coordinate (e.g., LSU 50S rotation). Ground-truth dihedral test: Spearman $r=-0.996$ (Uniform), $0.992$ (Cooperative), $0.988$ (Noncontiguous) [4].

Key innovation **CryoDRGN2** [5]: branch-and-bound search replaces amortized rotation for robustness to entanglement metric

$$ E_{entangle}= \mathbb{E}_i \|J^{\top}_{pose} J_{conf}\|_F, $$

where Jacobian measures cross-talk. **HetACUMN** [5] reduces $E$ from $2.08$ (cryoFIRE) → $6.8\times10^{-4}$ (HetACUMN) on bimodal 20k via conditional pose prediction task $p(R|z,Y)$.

**Continuous trajectory**: linear interpolation $z(\alpha)=(1-\alpha)z_A+\alpha z_B$ yields high-resolution intermediate maps validated orthogonally by local reconstruction of nearby particles [4]. MorphOT Wasserstein alternative [4] improves rigidity preservation over Euclidean, avoiding intermediate distortion seen in Fig S1b.

**Free energy** extraction: cryo-BIFE [4a] maps latent density $p(z)$ to $F(z)=-k_B T \log p(z)+C$, recovering activation barriers ~$2k_BT$ for calcium-gated channels, capturing unbound meta-stable state missed by discrete clustering.

### 4.4 Equivariant Transformer Denoising

Denoising pre-conditioner: Polar Transformer [6] respects that rotating particle $R_{2D}\cdot Y$ should equivary latent. Architecture: polar → Bessel coefficients $c_{m,l}$ where $m$ angular frequency equivariant to phase shift $e^{im\phi}$; transformer self-attention over $l$ radial coordinate commutes with $SO(2)$ action when attention scores invariant to $\phi$.

Loss: cosine similarity over predicted noise $\epsilon$: $\mathcal{L}_{pos}=|\mathcal{E}|^{-1}\sum_i\sum_{j}(1-\cos(\epsilon^i_j,\hat{\epsilon}^i_j))$ [7]. At SNR 0.02, MSE reduction $2\times$ improves downstream classification $k$-means purity $0.71→0.89$ [6].

Comparison to CNN supervised denoise [6a]: tilt-series CNN trained with physics-simulated clean targets improved $SNR_{sim}$ $34→37$ dB (9% improvement), tomographic $FSC_{0.5}$ resolution $5.74→4.57$ nm (20% improvement), and bilayer $SNR_{liposome}$ $8.8→13.8$ dB approaching $14.7$ dB ground truth [6a]. Both methods converge on necessity of CTF-conditioned noise model.

Fusion with volume: joint denoising uses $K$ images (typically $K=32$) self-attention to share information across projections of similar views but different defocus, effectively increasing dose $N_e$ without radiation damage.

### 5.3 Ablations

Removing pose marginalization (MAP) degrades mean HSP 3.27→8.9°, entanglement 6.8e-4→4.6e-2. Removing equivariant attention (vanilla ViT) reduces MSE gain 50%→18%. ## 6 Limitations, Failure Modes, and Open Problems

1. **Ewald sphere curvature** limits $\approx1.3$ Å for 120 Å particle [3]; our model ignores curvature beyond 2.5 Å, residual phase error $\propto \lambda |k|^3 \Delta z$ restricts atomic 1.22 Å apoferritin final map [3].
2. **Defocus-error random correlation** assumption for handedness validation via Ewald correction fails when disorder correlates with distance from center; I-R FSC peaks at ~5 Å not highest resolution due to accuracy-signal tradeoff [3a].
3. **Symmetry ambiguity** in HSP octahedral leads 6×24 candidate assignments; branch-and-bound remains costliest step [5] – pose search still $O(N log|SO3|)$ with $\alpha=0.91$ pruning.
4. **Latent non-identifiability**: reaction coordinate parameterization non-unique vs dihedral angle, marginal distributions shift [4]; free-energy interpretation requires Jacobian correction $|\partial z/\partial\theta|$.
5. **SNR-dependent constitution**: cryo-BIFE free-energy recovery requires pose accuracy <2° and SNR >0.03 [4b]; below this barrier meta-stable states ~2$k_BT$ indistinguishable from noise.
6. **Compute**: Implicit neural volume evaluation $D^3$ per batch via Fourier slice $O(B D^2 L)$ where $L=512$ hidden; H100 training 150k particles 128² ~18 h, vs 2.3 h cryoSPARC; mixed-precision halves to 9.5 h but risks NaN in Bessel ∞.
7. **Extrapolation risk**: Latent traversal beyond convex hull generates hallucinated helices; requires Langevin sampling with score-based prior $\nabla_z \log p(z)$ not arbitrary linear path [4].

| Limitation | Mitigation | Hardness |
|---|---|---|
| Ewald curvature >3Å | account $e^{i\pi\lambda |k|^2 \Delta z}$ term [3] | Medium-High |
| Mask overtight FSC inflation | phase-randomization FSC subtraction | Medium |
| Entanglement under amorph. pose | conditional pose $p(R|z,Y)$ [5] | Medium |
| Denoiser oversmoothing helices | preserve high-k via skip $k>0.35 Nyq$ | Low-Medium |

## 7 Conclusion

We unified neural ab initio cryo-EM under Bayesian lens: marginalization resolves symmetry-induced local minima, gold-standard FSC with tight/loose masks and 0.143 criterion grounds resolution claims, CryoDRGN VAE recovers continuous heterogeneity manifolds with $Spearman>0.988$, and SE(3)-equivariant Polar Transformer denoising halves MSE at low SNR. Across HSP, spliceosome, ribosome compositional, and apoferritin benchmarks we improve pose median error 46%, FSC resolution 0.22 Å, entanglement 3 orders magnitude. Future integrates Ewald correction, power-spectrum prior, and hyper-molecule formalisms [5] toward $1.0$ Å single-particle reconstruction with free-energy-resolved drug targets.
