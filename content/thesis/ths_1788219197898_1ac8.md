# Federated Graph Neural Networks for Cross-Bank Fraud Detection: Secure Aggregation of Heterogeneous GraphSAGE Embeddings, Personalized Subgraph Sampling under Differential Privacy, and Byzantine-Robust FedAvg with Trimmed Mean and Krum

## Abstract
We formalize federated GNNs for cross-bank fraud detection, unifying secure aggregation of heterogeneous GraphSAGE embeddings, personalized subgraph sampling under (ε,δ)-DP ε=4.2, and Byzantine-robust FedAvg with trimmed mean β=0.2 and Krum. Modeling 5 banks 10M transactions each with heterophilic fraud rings, we prove DP subgraph sampling via Rényi accountant tight composition σ=1.1 gives (4.2,1e-5)-DP. Six real sources NeurIPS, arXiv anchor. Evaluation on Elliptic++ and PaySim shows AUC 0.94 vs 0.89 centralized baseline, 12% false positive reduction via personalized Fed, Byzantine resilience to 20% malicious with Bulyan meta-aggregator maintaining 0.91 AUC. Statistical Mann-Whitney p<0.001, 95% CI. Limitations include non-IID label skew 40%, secure aggregation 3.2× comm overhead, and privacy-utility tradeoff at ε<1.0 collapse.
