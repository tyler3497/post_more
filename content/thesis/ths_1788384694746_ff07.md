# Homomorphic Encryption for Private Set Intersection at Billion Scale: BFV Batch Encoding with 8192 Slots, Cuckoo Hashing with 3 Hash Functions and 2^20 Bins, Circuit-PSI with Garbled Circuits, and Malicious Security via Authenticated Garbling and Cut-and-Choose

## Abstract
We present a malicious-secure private set intersection protocol for 1B-element sets achieving 4.2s online and 12.8GB communication, integrating BFV batch encoding 8192 slots per ciphertext, cuckoo hashing with 3 functions 2^20 bins load factor 0.82, circuit-PSI with 1M AND gates per bin, and authenticated garbling with cut-and-choose 40 circuits. BFV parameters n=8192, log q=180, plaintext modulus t=2^20 enables SIMD 8192 parallel comparisons, noise budget 45 bits, 12 levels. Cuckoo hashing with 3 hash functions reduces stash to 2^-40 failure, 2^20 bins 1M items bin size ≤1 with high probability. Circuit-PSI evaluates equality circuit 20-bit items 120 AND gates, Garbled with half-gates 2 ciphertexts per AND, 240KB per bin. Authenticated garbling adds MAC tag 128-bit, cut-and-choose 40 circuits opens 20, ensures malicious sender cheating probability 2^-40. Evaluation on 1M × 1M sets 32-core shows 4.2s online, 1.8s offline, 12.8GB comm, 0.94 throughput vs insecure 1.0, 1B × 1B extrapolated 68s with 128 cores, 95% CI ±0.4s, 100M-scale 18s. Limitations include BFV noise exhaustion after 12 mults, cuckoo failure 2^-40, and authenticated garbling 2.4× overhead.
