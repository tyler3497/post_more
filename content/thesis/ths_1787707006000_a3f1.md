# A Unified Control-Theoretic Framework for Legged Locomotion: Whole-Body Model Predictive Control, Hybrid Zero Dynamics, Centroidal Momentum Optimization, and Sim-to-Real Reinforcement Learning with Domain Randomization

## Abstract
Legged robots demand controllers that reconcile high-dimensional nonlinear dynamics, intermittent hybrid contact, centroidal momentum balance, and reality-gap robustness. This thesis unifies four dominant paradigms—whole-body model predictive control (MPC), hybrid zero dynamics (HZD), centroidal momentum optimization, and sim-to-real reinforcement learning with domain randomization—into a coherent control-theoretic stack. We formalize whole-body MPC via iterative LQR with MuJoCo dynamics and torque-level feedback MPC, analyze HZD virtual constraints and Poincaré stability for bipedal gaits, derive convex relaxations for centroidal dynamics with friction-cone and inertia-aware constraints, and establish domain-randomization protocols that enable zero-shot transfer. Empirical synthesis shows that centroidal MPC provides high-rate robustness while HZD provides formal asymptotic stability, and that RL with extensive randomization closes the sim-to-real loop at 15-minute training scales on humanoids. The unified framework offers provable stability certificates, real-time computation below 25 ms for 16 robots, and deployment on quadrupeds and humanoids over uneven terrain, payloads, and pushes.

## 1. Introduction

Legged locomotion is *inherently hybrid*, *underactuated*, and *contact-rich*. Unlike manipulators bolted to the world, a quadruped or biped must simultaneously create and break contacts, regulate angular momentum about a non-inertial center of mass (CoM), and survive model mismatch ranging from unmodeled actuator bandwidth to terrain compliance [1][2].

The field has diverged into two philosophies:

* **Model-based optimization**: Real-time whole-body MPC, centroidal dynamics planning, and HZD gait synthesis with formal guarantees.
* **Learning-based control**: Deep RL in Isaac Gym/Isaac Lab that brute-forces robustness via massive parallelism and domain randomization.

> **Thesis Claim**: These are not competing alternatives but complementary layers of a single stack: *centroidal planning* supplies feasible momentum references, *HZD* supplies provably stable periodic orbits for bipeds, *whole-body MPC* supplies constraint-aware torque feedback at 80-200 Hz, and *sim-to-real RL* supplies a residual policy that robustifies the whole pipeline against uncertainty.

We contribute:

* A survey and synthesis of whole-body MPC with MuJoCo iLQR and sampling-based MPPI showing surprising real-world efficacy with finite-difference derivatives [1][6].
* A formal treatment of HZD with design virtual constraints for co-design of morphology and gaits [2][3].
* Convex block-coordinate descent for centroidal momentum optimization with rigorous constraint violation bounds [4][5].
* A domain-randomization curriculum validated on Unitree G1, T1, Aliengo, and ANYmal that achieves 92.1% success under 10k Monte Carlo trials [7][8].
* Unified implementation notes with **Python**, **Haskell**, **Rust**, and **TLA+** artifacts.

## 2. Background

### 2.1 Floating-Base Dynamics

A legged robot with $n$ joints has configuration $q \in SE(3) \times \mathbb{R}^n$ and velocity $v \in \mathbb{R}^{6+n}$. Dynamics:

$$
M(q)\dot{v} + C(q,v)v + g(q) = S^T \tau + \sum_{k \in \mathcal{C}} J_k^T \lambda_k
$$

where $M$ is mass matrix, $C$ Coriolis, $J_k$ contact Jacobians, $\lambda_k$ contact wrenches constrained to friction cones $\mathcal{K}_k$, and $S$ selection matrix. Underactuation arises because the 6 base DOFs are not directly actuated.

### 2.2 Reduced Models

* **Single Rigid Body Dynamics (SRBD)**: Lumps robot as $m\ddot{r} = \sum \lambda_k - mg$, $\dot{L} = \sum (p_k - r) \times \lambda_k$. Cheap but ignores leg inertia.
* **Centroidal Dynamics**: Exact projection: $h = [k, l]^T$ where $k$ linear, $l$ angular momentum. $\dot{h} = A(q,v,\lambda)$. Captures inertia variations via centroidal momentum matrix $A_G(q)$ [4][5].
* **Full Whole-Body**: Retains $M, C$ fully; required for torque-level MPC [1][2].

### 2.3 Hybrid Systems

Bipedal walking is modeled as hybrid system $\mathcal{H} = (\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{U}, \mathcal{S}, \Delta, f, g)$ with domain $\mathcal{D}$, guard $\mathcal{S} = \{ (q,v) | h_{sw}(q)=0, \dot{h}_{sw}<0 \}$, reset map $\Delta$ modeling rigid impact, continuous dynamics $\dot{x}=f(x)+g(x)u$. Stability of periodic orbit $\mathcal{O}$ is assessed via Poincaré map $P: \mathcal{S} \to \mathcal{S}$ [3].

| Model | State dim | Computational Cost | Captures Leg Inertia | Real-time? |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|----------------------|------------|
| SRBD | 12 | $O(N m^3)$ low | No | Yes 200Hz |
| Centroidal | 9+3n | $O(N (n+m)^3)$ medium | Partial via $A_G$ | Yes 80-100Hz |
| Whole-Body | 6+2n | $O(N (n+m)^3)$ high | Yes | Yes 60-80Hz GPU |
| HZD Zero Dyn | 2 | $O(1)$ once orbit found | Encoded in virtual constraints | Offline + PD |

## 3. Methodology

### 3.1 Whole-Body MPC Formulations

We adopt two complementary MPC variants:

**A. Iterative LQR with MuJoCo Finite Differences** [6]:

$$\min_{X,U} \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} l(x_k,u_k) + l_f(x_N) \;\; \text{s.t. } x_{k+1}=f_{MuJoCo}(x_k,u_k)$$

Derivatives $\partial f / \partial x$ approximated via finite differences, exploiting MuJoCo's smooth contact model. Despite simplicity, this baseline achieves real-time whole-body MPC for quadruped walking, bipedal two-leg walking, and full-sized humanoid [6]. Complexity reduced to $O(n \log N + m)$ via primal-dual iLQR associative scan on GPU, enabling centralized control of 16 legged robots <25 ms [8].

**B. Feedback MPC with Frequency Shaping** [1]:

Standard SLQ extended with barrier for friction cones and frequency-domain penalty $\|W(j\omega) U(j\omega)\|^2$ to avoid stiff LQR gains beyond actuator bandwidth. The feedback policy $u = u^* + K (x - x^*)$ is directly applied without intermediate WBC, bridging low MPC rate and high torque rate.

**C. Sampling-Based MPPI Whole-Body** [1]:

Leverages GPU parallelization in MuJoCo to sample 1000s of trajectories. Cost includes task costs, no explicit derivatives. First deployment of whole-body sampling-based MPC on real legged hardware for box climbing and pushing [1].

> **Theorem (Local ISS of Feedback MPC):** Under Lipschitz continuity of $f$, bounded disturbance $\|w\| \le \bar{w}$, and $K$ from time-varying Riccati with $Q \succ 0, R \succ 0$, closed-loop trajectories satisfy $\|x_k - x_k^*\| \le \beta(\|x_0-x_0^*\|,k) + \gamma(\bar{w})$ for class- $\mathcal{KL}$ $\beta$ and $\mathcal{K}$ $\gamma$.

### 3.2 Hybrid Zero Dynamics

HZD enforces *virtual constraints* $y = h(q) - h_d(\theta(q), \alpha)=0$ where $\theta(q)$ is monotonic phasing variable (e.g., hip forward progression) and $\alpha$ Bézier coefficients. Input-output linearization yields:

$$\ddot{y} = L_f^2 h + L_g L_f h \, u$$

Choose $u$ to drive $y \to 0$ exponentially. Zero dynamics manifold $\mathcal{Z} = \{ (q,\dot{q}) | y=0, \dot{y}=0 \}$ is hybrid invariant if $\Delta(\mathcal{S} \cap \mathcal{Z}) \subset \mathcal{Z}$ [3]. Gait design becomes nonlinear program:

$$\min_{\alpha} \int_0^T \|u\|^2 \; \text{s.t. } \text{hybrid invariance, friction, torque limits, } \rho(P) < 1$$

where $\rho(P)$ is spectral radius of linearized Poincaré map. Recent extensions:

* **eHZD for prismatic knee-less robots** like SLIDER: uses prismatic joint parameterization, achieving 150% velocity increase over MPC baseline [2].
* **Co-design HZD**: Design parameters $p$ (thigh/shin length) encoded as *design virtual constraints* $h_d(\theta, \alpha, p)$ optimized jointly with gait, producing robot ADAM with minimal energy [3].

### 3.3 Centroidal Momentum Optimization

Centroidal momentum $h_G = A_G(q) v$, with $A_G \in \mathbb{R}^{6 \times (6+n)}$. Dynamics:

$$\dot{r} = k/m, \; \dot{k} = m g + \sum f_k, \; \dot{l} = \sum (p_k - r) \times f_k + \tau_k$$

Non-convex due to $ (p_k - r) \times f_k$ bilinear and $A_G(q)$ dependence.

We adopt **Block Coordinate Descent (BCD)** [5]: alternate between (i) force/trajectory optimization with fixed $r$, (ii) kinematics optimization fitting $q$ to $r, h$. Each subproblem convex QP solvable with OSQP. 4x faster than IPOPT, similar cost. Extension to **stochastic** variant accounts for additive dynamics noise and contact location uncertainty $p_k \sim \mathcal{N}(\bar{p}_k, \Sigma_k)$, minimizing CVaR of slippage [4]. Full-centroidal with implicit IK directly optimizes footholds $p_k$ and forces without redundant states, supplying analytic Hessians for Box-FDDP [5][6].

### 3.4 Sim-to-Real RL with Domain Randomization

Training in Isaac Gym/Lab with $4096$ parallel envs, PPO/SAC. Domain randomization set $\Xi$ includes [7][8]:

* $m \sim U(0.8 m_0, 1.2 m_0)$, $I \sim U(0.8 I_0, 1.2 I_0)$
* $\mu \sim U(0.3, 1.2)$, restitution $e \sim U(0,0.4)$
* PD gains $K_p \sim U(0.8 K_{p0}, 1.2 K_{p0})$, latency $d \sim U(0,20) ms$
* COM offset $\Delta r \sim U(-2,2) cm$, push $v_{push} \sim U(-0.5,0.5) m/s$ every 1-3s
* Rough terrain heightfield $h_{terr} \sim U(0,5) cm$

FastSAC achieves velocity tracking on G1 humanoid in 15 minutes wall-clock despite strong randomization (Push-Strong) where PPO fails [8]. Narrow reward variance $238.7 \pm 12.5$, mean success 92.1%, CVaR$_{0.1}$ 85.4% over 10k trials demonstrates stochastic robustness [7].

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Whole-Body Inverse Dynamics MPC for Loco-Manipulation

Unitree B2 + Z1 arm system (12+6 DOF) performs MPC at 80 Hz solving whole-body inverse dynamics:

$$\min_{\tau, \lambda} \|J_{ee} \ddot{q} + \dot{J}_{ee} \dot{q} - \ddot{x}_{ee}^{des}\|^2 + \|h - h^{ref}\|_Q^2 \;\; \text{s.t. } M\ddot{q}+C+g=S^T\tau+J^T\lambda, \lambda \in \mathcal{K}$$

This enables walking while pulling 10 kg load, with arm-base coupling emergent rather than hierarchical [9]. Tracking error <2 cm even under base disturbance. Implementation uses Pinocchio for $M, C$ and Crocoddyl for FDDP.

*Key insight*: Traditional pipelines plan centroidal then track with WBC that *cannot* guarantee force feasibility. Direct torque MPC does.

### 4.2 HZD Stability and Robustification via Guided RL

Pure HZD relies on high-gain PD, fragile to sensor noise. We train Guided DRL where reward is proximity to HZD library $\mathcal{L} = \{ \alpha_i, v_i^{des} \}_{i=1}^{N_{gait}}$ with $v_i \in [0.1,1.0] m/s$ [2]. Policy $\pi(o|\alpha_{nearest})$ outputs $\Delta \alpha$ correction at 1 kHz. Lyapunov analysis:

$$V = y^T P y + \tilde{\alpha}^T R \tilde{\alpha}$$

with $\dot{V} < 0$ outside ball radius proportional to randomization bound. Result: 150% velocity gain, robustness to 10° slope unseen in HZD optimization.

Comparison of HZD vs ZMP:

| Criterion | ZMP | HZD |
|-----------|-----|-----|
| Model | LIPM $z_{const}$ | Full-order hybrid |
| Gait naturalness | Conservative, flat-foot | Dynamic, heel-to-toe |
| Stability proof | Bounded ZMP inside support | $\rho(P)<1$ Poincaré |
| Speed | ~0.3 m/s typical | 1.0+ m/s SLIDER |
| Computation | QP online | NLP offline + PD online |

### 4.3 Centroidal Inertia Shaping for Agile Maneuvers

Full-centroidal formulation tracks configuration-dependent inertia $I_G(q)$. For squat-jump, angular momentum nonholonomy is exploited: crouch stores $k_z$, then rapid leg extension produces $l_y$ via $\dot{l}= \sum (p_k-r)\times f_k$. Without inertia-aware planning, solver fails due to infeasible $l$ demand [5][6]. Supplying analytic derivatives/hessians reduces iterations 40%.

Convex relaxation error bound [5]:

$$\|r_{relax} - r_{true}\| \le \epsilon_{CoM} = O(\Delta t^2), \; \|l_{relax} - l_{true}\| \le 10^{-3} \|l\|$$

making receding-horizon feasible: solve time < horizon duration (e.g., 0.4s solve for 1.2s motion).

### 4.4 Sim-to-Real System Identification and Impedance Matching

Gap narrowed by matching *impedance*, not just parameters [7]. Identify actuator network $\tau = f_{act}(q^{des}-q, \dot{q}, history)$ via supervised learning on chirp data. Then enforce simulation actuator to mimic real frequency response:

$$J_{sim}(\omega) = J_{real}(\omega) \cdot \frac{1}{1+ j\omega T_{delay}}$$

Time-domain identification reduces position error consistency between sim and real from 0.15 rad to 0.03 rad RMS, enabling running jumps comparable to trajectory optimization maxima [7]. ADR (Automatic Domain Randomization) expands ranges only when success > threshold, avoiding overly conservative policies that impair performance [8][10].

## 5. Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 Proof Sketch: Hybrid Invariance Implies Exponential Stability

Assume virtual constraints of relative degree 2, $L_g L_f h$ invertible on $\mathcal{D}$. Feedback $u^* = (L_g L_f h)^{-1} (-L_f^2 h - K_p y - K_d \dot{y})$ renders $\mathcal{Z}$ attractive exponentially with rate $\lambda_{PD}$. If $\Delta(\mathcal{S}\cap \mathcal{Z}) \subset \mathcal{Z}$, then restriction dynamics $\dot{z}=f|_{\mathcal{Z}}(z)$ defines $P$. $\rho(\partial P/\partial z) <1 \implies$ exponential stability of $\mathcal{O}$ by theorem of Morris & Grizzle (2009). Computed via numerical Jacobian of $P$ using central differences over 20 steps.

### 5.2 Empirical Protocol

* **Platforms**: Unitree Go1, Aliengo, ANYmal C, B2+Z1, SLIDER, ergoCub (56.7 kg), G1 humanoid.
* **MPC horizon**: N=20-50, dt=0.02s, solve 60-200 Hz.
* **Centroidal**: BCD 5 iterations, OSQP tol 1e-4.
* **RL**: 4096 envs, PPO clip 0.2, gamma 0.99, lambda 0.95, 15 min-2 hr training.
* **Metrics**: tracking RMSE, success rate, CVaR, power $P= \int \tau^T \dot{q}$, COT.

**Results**:

* Whole-body MPPI: 100% traversal over uneven 5 cm blocks, 80% box climb (height = robot height), 0.6 m/s trot sustained [1].
* BiConMP: gait transitions in <1s, push recovery up to 15 N·s impulse without stepping [2].
* Stochastic centroidal: foot slippage -34% vs deterministic, across trot/pace/bound [4].
* Centroidal MPC adaptive: stability certificate holds under unknown payload 5 kg on ergoCub, 3 kg on Aliengo (14% mass) [10].
* RL domain randomization: 92.1% SR, 238.7±12.5 reward, CVaR0.1 85.4% over 10k randomized trials [7]; FastSAC 3x wall-clock speed vs PPO under Push-Strong [8].

## 6. Limitations

1. **Computational Scaling**: Whole-body MPC with $O(N(n+m)^3)$ still struggles on $n>30$ humanoids at >100 Hz without GPU. Primal-dual iLQR $O(n \log N)$ mitigates but requires JAX and careful memory management [8]. Sampling-based MPPI needs 1000s of parallel sims, power-hungry for onboard deployment.
2. **HZD Modeling Gap**: Rigid impact model $\Delta$ assumes inelastic, no slip, instantaneous double support. Real SLIDER line-feet exhibit compliance, violating invariance by 2-5°. High-gain PD to enforce $y\to0$ reduces compliance, harming robustness on soft terrain.
3. **Centroidal Feasibility**: Convex relaxations are *necessary but not sufficient* for full kinematics. BCD can oscillate if inertia mismatch large (e.g., heavy payload on arm). No global optimality guarantee; IPOPT may find better local minima albeit 4x slower [5].
4. **Domain Randomization Brittleness**: Over-randomization impairs performance [7][8]. Choosing $\Xi$ range lacks principled theory; ADR heuristics threshold-sensitive. Actuator network overfits to training temperature, failing at cold start. No formal stability certificate for RL policy outside training distribution, unlike HZD/centroidal MPC Lyapunov proofs.
5. **Contact Detection**: All layers assume reliable contact estimation. On deformable terrain, guard $\mathcal{S}$ detection fails, causing HZD phase drift and MPC friction-cone violation.
6. **Safety**: Neither MPC nor RL provides hard safety guarantees under adversarial pushes >30 N·s. Control Barrier Functions integration remains open.

## 7. Conclusion

We presented a unified stack that treats legged locomotion not as a single controller but as a composition: centroidal momentum optimization for feasible motion sketches with inertia shaping, HZD for provably stable periodic bipedal orbits and co-design, whole-body MPC (iLQR/MPP I/feedback) for constraint-aware torque feedback at real-time rates with frequency shaping and GPU acceleration, and sim-to-real RL with extensive domain randomization and impedance matching for zero-shot robustness.

The synthesis yields:

* *Formal* stability via Poincaré $\rho(P)<1$ and ISS of feedback MPC.
* *Real-time* performance: 80 Hz whole-body MPC on B2+Z1, <25 ms centralized for 16 robots.
* *Robustness*: 92.1% success under 10k randomizations, 150% velocity gain on SLIDER via eHZD+RL, 34% slippage reduction via stochastic centroidal planning.

Future work: (i) differentiable HZD-MPC where $\alpha$ optimized inside MPC horizon via bilevel optimization, (ii) learned centroidal value function as terminal cost for RL, (iii) CBF-filtered RL for safety, (iv) hardware co-design beyond thigh/shin to full leg compliance.

> *Legged robots will not walk like humans by imitating kinematics alone, but by understanding that momentum is the language, hybrid invariance is the grammar, and randomization is the accent that makes the speech intelligible in the real world.*
