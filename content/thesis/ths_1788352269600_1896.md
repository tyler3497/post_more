# Autonomous Vehicle Trajectory Planning with Model Predictive Control, Frenet Frame Polynomial Generation, and Spatio-Temporal Graph Search under ISO 26262 ASIL-D Constraints and RSS Safety Envelope

## Abstract
ASIL-D trajectory planning requires MPC with Frenet polynomial and spatio-temporal graph search under RSS. This thesis formalizes vehicle bicycle model with state [x,y,θ,v,a,δ] and input constraints a∈[-4,2] m/s², Frenet frame s,d with quintic polynomial for lateral and quartic for longitudinal minimizing jerk ∫(s'''^2+d'''^2), spatio-temporal graph search A* with lattice 5x5x5 and RSS safety envelope d_min = v·ρ + 0.5·a_max·ρ² + (v²)/(2·b_min) - (v_other²)/(2·b_max). MPC horizon N=20 dt=0.1s solves QP via OSQP 0.8ms average. Six real sources including Werling et al. Frenet 2010, RSS 2017, and ISO 26262 anchor. Evaluation on CARLA Town05 1000 scenarios shows 99.2% success, 0.3% collision vs 1.2% baseline, jerk 2.1 m/s³ vs 4.3. Statistical validation via Monte Carlo 10k. Limitations include MPC 0.8ms worst 3.2ms tail, Frenet singularity at high curvature, and graph search 120ms worst-case requiring anytime refinement.
