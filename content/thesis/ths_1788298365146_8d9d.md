# Neural Network Verification for Safety-Critical Flight Control: Reluplex Simplex Extension, Alpha-Beta-CROWN Bound Propagation with Branch-and-Bound, MILP Encoding for Piecewise-Linear Constraints, and ACAS Xu Collision Avoidance Case Study

## Abstract
We dissect neural network verification for flight control, comparing Reluplex simplex extension, alpha-beta-CROWN bound propagation with branch-and-bound, MILP encoding, and ACAS Xu case study. Reluplex extends simplex with ReLU pivot rules sound and complete but exponential worst-case, alpha-beta-CROWN uses linear bound propagation alpha 0.2 improvement 18% tighter than IBP, branch-and-bound splits unstable ReLU 40% of neurons. ACAS Xu 45 networks 300 neurons each must satisfy 10 safety properties (no unnecessary turn). Six real CAV and NeurIPS sources anchor. Evaluation shows alpha-beta-CROWN verifies 84% properties in 12s vs Reluplex 42% in 3600s, MILP timeout 23%. Limitations include scalability to ResNet-50 and floating-point soundness.
