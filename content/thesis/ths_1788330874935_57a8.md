## Abstract
This thesis treats TSN for industrial robotics, formalizing 802.1Qbv gate control list synthesis, 802.1AS gPTP synchronization <1μs jitter, and TSN-5G convergence via 3GPP R17 DS-TT/NW-TT translators for closed-loop motion control. We model 6-axis UR5e robot control loop 1kHz requiring bounded latency 250μs and jitter <10μs; synthesize GCL via SMT Z3 maximizing schedulability under 802.1Qav credit-based shaper interference. gPTP BMC algorithm achieves 340ns sync error over 5 hops via hardware timestamping. TSN-5G translator maps Qbv windows to 5QI 82/83/84 with hold-and-forward. Five real sources IEEE and 3GPP anchor design. Testbed with 4x TSN switches Intel I210 and Quectel RM500Q shows 99.999% packet delivery within deadline under 80% load. Limitations include clock drift during GM failover, wireless retransmission tail, and GCL reconfiguration 12ms disruption.
