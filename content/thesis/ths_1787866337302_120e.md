This thesis provides a rigorous treatment of planetary-scale time-series forecasting with state-space models: mamba selective ssm, s4 structured state, deepar probabilistic decoding, and temporal fusion transformers for 10b-point horizons, synthesizing classical foundations with 2026 systems practice.
