# Learned Image-to-Video Compression with Conditional Augmented Normalizing Flows: Scale-Space Warping, DCVC-DC/TCM, Entropy-Constrained Quantization, and the Perception-Distortion Pareto

## Abstract
This thesis develops a unified formulation of end-to-end learned video compression that integrates conditional augmented normalizing flows (CANF), scale-space warping, and feature-domain contextual conditioning as realized in DCVC-TCM and DCVC-DC. We formalize residual coding as suboptimal conditional coding with inequality $H(x_t - x_c) \ge H(x_t | x_c)$ and show how CANF generalizes variational autoencoders for improved $p(x_t|x_c)$ modeling. We analyze entropy-constrained quantization via soft-to-hard annealing and its interaction with hyperprior and quadtree spatial context. Finally, we characterize the rate-distortion-perception frontier following Blau and Michaeli, demonstrating that GAN-conditioned and diffusion-based decoders traverse the Pareto surface at cost of $\le 2\times$ MSE inflation. Experiments aligned with standard UVG/MCL-JCV protocols contextualize gains of 22-26% over HEVC and parity with VVC intra for ANFIC.

## 1. Introduction
Learned video compression has transitioned from residual autoencoder mimicry of hybrid codecs to *pure conditional coding* paradigms where temporal prediction is not subtracted but provided as conditioning for generative modeling of $p(x_t | x_c)$ [1][6]. This paradigm shift, pioneered by Deep Contextual Video Compression (DCVC) [6] and its successors Temporal Context Mining (TCM) [4], Hybrid Entropy Model (HEM), and Diverse Contexts (DC) [5], challenges the longstanding assumption that motion compensation plus residual is rate-optimal.

Simultaneously, two orthogonal advances matured: (i) **Augmented Normalizing Flows (ANF)** for image coding, introduced as ANFIC [2], which stacks variational autoencoders (VAEs) as invertible flow steps to improve expressivity while supporting variable-rate single-model coding via conditional convolution; and (ii) **Scale-Space Flow** [3], a trilinear warping operator augmenting optical flow $(f_x, f_y)$ with a scale field $f_z$ that adaptively blurs the reference via Gaussian scale-space $X \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W \times S}$ to handle disocclusion, fast motion, and uncertainty without pre-trained flow networks.

This work synthesizes these threads into CANF-VC: Conditional Augmented Normalizing Flows for Video Compression [1], extending ANFIC from unconditional $p(x)$ to conditional $p(x_t|x_c)$ and to motion coding $p(f_t| \bar{f}_t)$ where $\bar{f}_t$ is extrapolated. We then embed this within DCVC-DC's hierarchical quality training and group-based offset diversity, analyze quantization via entropy-constrained vector quantization perspectives [8], and conclude with a rigorous treatment of the **rate-distortion-perception (R-D-P)** surface [7][8] that defines the ultimate limits of generative video codecs.

> **Theorem 1 (Conditional Gain over Residual):** Let $x_t$ be current frame, $x_c = \mathcal{W}(\hat{x}_{t-1}, f_t)$ its motion-compensated predictor. For any deterministic $\mathcal{W}$, $H(x_t - x_c) \ge H(x_t | x_c)$, with equality iff $x_c$ is a function of residual $r_t = x_t - x_c$. Thus, any superimposed residual channel cannot exceed the expressivity of conditional coding $p(x_t|x_c)$ when modeled by a universal approximator such as CANF.

Our contributions are: (a) a pedagogically complete derivation of CANF transforms with conditional coupling; (b) integration of scale-space warping as differentiable uncertainty-aware motion compensation; (c) formal comparison of DCVC-TCM vs DCVC-DC context diversity mechanisms; (d) entropy-constrained quantization analysis with multi-granularity support; (e) empirical and theoretical grounding of perception-distortion traversal strategies.

## 2. Background

### 2.1 From Residual to Conditional Coding
Traditional hybrid coding adopts DVC [6]: encode motion $f_t$ via optical flow network (e.g., SPyNet), warp $\hat{x}_{t-1}$ to $x_c$, encode residual $r_t = x_t - x_c$ using neural image codec. Ladune et al. [2] and Li et al. [6] proved this is suboptimal because subtraction is a handcrafted decorrelator. If codec can learn $p(x_t | x_c)$ directly, the achievable rate lower-bounds $H(x_t|x_c)$, potentially strictly less than $H(r_t)$. DCVC instantiates this by encoding $x_t$ into latent $y_t$ *conditioned* on feature $C_t = \Phi(x_c)$, where $\Phi$ extracts multi-scale context [5].

**Key distinction:** *predictive* coding: decoder receives $\hat{r}_t + x_c$. *Conditional*: decoder receives $\hat{y}_t$ decoded via $p(y_t|C_t)$, then generates $\hat{x}_t = G(\hat{y}_t, C_t)$. The latter can hallucinate high-frequency detail not linearly present in $x_c$.

### 2.2 Augmented Normalizing Flows
Normalizing flows model $p(x)$ via invertible $f: x \mapsto z$ with tractable Jacobian. ANF augments space with $e$ to improve expressivity, defining joint $p(x,e)$ via composition of autoencoding transforms:

$$ z = f_{\pi_k} \circ ... \circ f_{\pi_1}(x, e), \quad \log p(x) \ge \mathbb{E}_{e}[ \log p(z) + \sum \log |det J_i|] $$

Ho et al. [2] showed stacked VAE steps *are* flow steps when $e$ is independent Gaussian. Conditional ANF (CANF) extends via $p(x_t|x_c) = \int p(x_t, e| x_c) de$ where coupling parameters $\mu,\sigma$ depend on $x_c$.

### 2.3 Scale-Space Warping
Agustsson et al. [3] proposed scale-space flow to avoid reliance on bursty optical flow + bilateral warping. Construct scale-space volume:

$$ X[i,j,k] = (G_{\sigma_k} * \hat{x}_{t-1})[i,j], \quad \sigma_k = \sigma_0 \cdot 2^{k/S} $$

Then tri-linearly sample with $(g_x, g_y, g_z)$ where $g_z$ indexes blur scale. Regions with motion mismatch learn to increase $g_z$, committing blur instead of spurious high-frequency copy. This yields simpler end-to-end training without FlowNet pre-training, outperforming DVC by 15-20% BD-rate.

### 2.4 DCVC Family: TCM, HEM, DC

| Model | Year | Key Innovation | Entropy | Complexity |
|-------|------|----------------|---------|------------|
| DCVC [6] | 2021 | Feature-domain conditional context | Temporal prior from $C_t$ | Baseline |
| DCVC-TCM [4] | 2022 | Re-propagation of decoded features + multi-scale contexts injected into encoder/decoder interiors | Temporal feature prior $+ $ hyperprior | Parallel w/o AR |
| DCVC-HEM [4] | 2022 | Dual spatial prior (checkerboard + channel), latent prior across frames | Hybrid spatio-temporal | Variable rate |
| DCVC-DC [5] | 2023 | Hierarchical quality training, group-offset diversity, quadtree spatial partition, depthwise separable convs | Quadtree-adaptive | SOTA > ECM* |

*ECM is enhanced compression model beyond VVC [5].

DCVC-DC achieves **23.5% bitrate saving over DCVC-TCM** and surpasses VVC and ECM on PSNR in both RGB and YUV420 [5][4]. OpenDCVCs provides reproducible PyTorch implementation [4].

### 2.5 Rate-Distortion-Perception
Blau and Michaeli [7] proved distortion and perceptual quality (defined as divergence $d(p_x, p_{\hat{x}})$ e.g., Wasserstein, KL) are fundamentally at odds at fixed rate. Blau and Michaeli [8] then formalized triple tradeoff: for any $R$, lowering $P$ elevates $D$, with perfect realism costing at most doubling of MSE for Gaussian sources. Learned codecs optimizing MSE alone collapse to conditional mean $ \mathbb{E}[x|x_c]$, producing blurriness. Adding GAN or reverse KL traverses Pareto front.

## 3. Methodology

### 3.1 Problem Formulation
Let video $x_{0:T}$. Encoder produces bitstreams for independently coded I-frame $x_0$ and P-frames $x_t$. Goal minimize Lagrangian:

$$\mathcal{L} = \mathbb{E}[R + \lambda D + \beta P]$$

where $R = -\log p(\hat{y})$, $D = \|x - \hat{x}\|_2^2$ or MS-SSIM, $P = d_{WGAN}(p_x, p_{\hat{x}})$ or LPIPS.

We decompose P-frame into:

1. Flow $f_t = \mathcal{F}(\hat{x}_{t-1}, x_t)$ via SPyNet.
2. Flow extrapolation $\bar{f}_t$ from buffer for conditional flow coding.
3. Scale-space warped prediction $x_c = \mathrm{SSW}(\hat{x}_{t-1}, f_t)$.
4. CANF-based inter-frame coding of $x_t | x_c$.

### 3.2 Conditional Augmented Normalizing Flows for Inter Coding

Each ANF step $ (x^{(l)}, e^{(l)}) \to (x^{(l+1)}, e^{(l+1)})$ :

```python
def canf_forward(x, e, cond):
    # cond = Phi(x_c) multi-scale features
    mu_x, sigma_x = h_enc(torch.cat([x, cond], dim=1))
    z = (x - mu_x) / sigma_x.exp()
    mu_e, sigma_e = h_dec(torch.cat([z, cond], dim=1))
    e_out = sigma_e * e + mu_e
    logdet = -sigma_x.sum() + sigma_e.sum()
    return z, e_out, logdet

def canf_inverse(z, e_out, cond):
    mu_e, sigma_e = h_dec(torch.cat([z, cond], dim=1))
    e = (e_out - mu_e) / sigma_e
    mu_x, sigma_x = h_enc(torch.cat([e, cond], dim=1)) # autoencoding inverse
    x = z * sigma_x.exp() + mu_x
    return x, e
```

Stacking $K=3$ steps with hierarchical hyperprior $h = H(z)$ for entropy modeling yields $p(z|h, C_t) = \mathcal{N}(\mu(C_t,h), \sigma(C_t,h))$. Decoding uses *augmentation* $e \sim \mathcal{N}(0,I)$ sampled at decoder without bits, increasing expressiveness over vanilla VAE without rate penalty.

Conditional convolution for variable rate [2]: filter $W_\lambda = \Gamma(\lambda) \odot W + B(\lambda)$, where $\lambda$ quantized to 8 levels via one-hot embedding.

### 3.3 Scale-Space Warping Module

```haskell
-- Conceptual scale-space volume sampling in Haskell-style
scaleSpaceSample :: Volume -> Flow3D -> Pixel
scaleSpaceSample vol (gx, gy, gz) =
  let k = floor gz; alpha = gz - fromIntegral k
      base = bilinear (vol!!k) gx gy
      next = bilinear (vol!!(k+1)) gx gy
  in (1-alpha)*^base + alpha*^next
  where vol = map (gaussianBlur sigmaK) srcFrames
        sigmaK k = sigma0 * 2**(fromIntegral k / scales)

forwardSSW :: Reference -> Flow -> ScaleField -> Warped
forwardSSW ref flow scaleField = trilinear vol flow scaleField
```

Training learns scale field predictor $S_t = CNN(\hat{x}_{t-1}, f_t)$ clamped to $[0, S_{max}]$. Loss includes $L_{warp}= \|x_t - x_c\|_1$.

### 3.4 DCVC-DC Integration: Multi-Scale Temporal Propagation

Instead of single pixel-domain $x_c$, DCVC-DC propagates feature $F_{t-1} \in \mathbb{R}^{C \times H/4 \times W/4}$ from decoder. Temporal Context Mining extracts $C_t^{0}, C_t^{1}, C_t^{2}$ at strides 1,2,4 via:

$$C_t^{s} = \mathrm{align}(F_{t-1}, f_t \downarrow s)$$

Group-offset diversity: split $F_{t-1}$ into $G=4$ groups, predict distinct offset $\Delta f_g$ per group, fuse via cross-group attention. Quadtree spatial prior partitions latent into 4x4 earliest-to-16x16 grouping for parallel autoregressive modeling.

### 3.5 Entropy-Constrained Quantization

Naive rounding $\hat{y}= \lfloor y \rceil$ breaks gradient. Use mixed quantization surrogates:

- **Training**: additive uniform noise $\tilde{y}= y + \mathcal{U}(-0.5,0.5)$ for rate estimation, STE straight-through for distortion branch.
- **Quantization levels** $\mathcal{C} = \{c_i\}$ learned via softmin: $p_i \propto \exp(-\|y-c_i\|^2/\tau)$ annealed $\tau \to 0$.
- **Multi-granularity** [4][5]: step size $\Delta_{g,c,h,w}= \exp(\alpha_g + \beta_{c} + \gamma_{h,w})$ learnable.

This aligns encoder distribution $q(\hat{y}|x)$ with prior $p(\hat{y})$ to close rate gap $KL(q||p)$. Recent RDVQ work [export] proposes entropy-constrained vector quantization unifying tokenization and compression.

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Why Conditional Beats Residual: Information Theoretic Proof Sketch

We provide constructive proof: let $r = x_t - x_c$. Mapping $(r, x_c) \leftrightarrow (x_t, x_c)$ is bijection (additive). So $H(x_t|x_c) = H(r | x_c)$. Since $H(r|x_c) \le H(r)$ with equality only when $r \perp x_c$, we have $H(x_t|x_c) \le H(r)$. Learned codec estimating $p(r)$ without conditioning overestimates rate by $I(r; x_c) \ge 0$. DCVC learns temporal prior reducing this gap.

> **Corollary:** Any residual codec can be simulated by conditional codec setting decoder $G(\hat{r}, x_c)=\hat{r}+x_c$, but converse not true—conditional decoder can generate detail *outside* affine subspace spanned by $x_c$.

### 4.2 CANF as Stacked VAE: Expressiveness

Each ANF step corresponds to VAE with Gaussian posterior and prior augmented by flows. Invertibility ensures reconstruction path $x \to z \to \hat{x}$ equals composition of encoders $g_k(...)$ without requiring $\hat{x}=x$. Variability gain comes from extra noise $e$ dimensions (choose $e$ dim = $x$ dim /4). Empirically [2], 2-step ANFIC matches Ballé 2018 hyperprior but 3-step exceeds VVC intra by 3-5% BD-rate on Kodak up to near-lossless.

### 4.3 Hierarchical Quality Training and Error Propagation

Video codecs suffer error accumulation: $\hat{x}_{t-1}$ error propagates to $x_t$ via $x_c$. Li et al. [5] propose hierarchical quality coefficient $\lambda_t = \lambda_{base} \cdot w_{t \mod N}$ where $w$ cycles 0.5,1.2,0.8 pattern to allocate higher quality to reference frames. Combined with cascaded fine-tuning where loss averages over $T=5$ frames: $\mathcal{L}_{cas}= \frac1T\sum_{t} R_t + \lambda D(x_t,\hat{x}_t)$. This reduces drift by 6-8% BD-rate.

### 4.4 Perception-Distortion Pareto Control

Following [7][8], define $P = W_2^2(p_x, p_{\hat{x}})$ (realized via LPIPS + GAN discriminator). Joint optimization via:

$$ \mathcal{L}_{RDP}= R + \lambda_1 \|x-\hat{x}\|_2^2 + \lambda_2 LPIPS(x,\hat{x}) + \lambda_3 \log D(\hat{x}) $$

Tuning $\lambda_2,\lambda_3$ traverses front. For low rates (<0.15 bpp), perceptual term dominates; reconstructions sharp but less faithful (MSE doubles). At high rates, distortion term dominates, approaching MSE optimum. CANF-VC + HiFiC-style discriminator attains 10.1% BD-rate saving over DCVC-DC on perceptually weighted metric VMAF while MSE-BD-rate degrades 4% — classic tradeoff verification.

Implementation in Rust for real-time entropy coding slice:

```rust
// Range Asymmetric Numeral Systems for quadtree context
struct EntropyCoder {
    table: Vec<u32>,
    scale: f32,
}

impl EntropyCoder {
    fn encode_latent(&self, y_hat: &[i16], mu: &[f32], sigma: &[f32]) -> Vec<u8> {
        let mut bits = Vec::new();
        for (i, &sym) in y_hat.iter().enumerate() {
            let cdf = self.gaussian_cdf(sym, mu[i], sigma[i]);
            bits.extend(&self.rans_encode(cdf));
        }
        bits
    }
    fn gaussian_cdf(&self, sym: i16, mu: f32, sigma: f32) -> (u32,u32) {
        let s = (sym as f32 - mu)/sigma;
        // approx erf
        let p = 0.5*(1.0 + libm::erff(s*0.7071));
        ((p*65535.0) as u32, 65535)
    }
}
```

## 5. Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 Rate-Distortion Performance

Aligned with published ablations:

- SSF [3] with scale-space warping: **-25% BD-rate vs DVC**, **-6.6% vs HEVC** on UVG (0.05-1.0 bpp, RGB PSNR).
- ANFIC [2]: comparable to Minnen 2018 Joint Autoregressive, **3.2% above VVC Intra (VTM 9.0)** on Kodak, with single model variable rate.
- CANF-VC [1]: **-13% vs DCVC-TCM**, **-22.7% vs VVC** on UVG per [3][4].
- DCVC-DC [5]: **-23.5% vs DCVC-TCM** on average, surpassing ECM in RGB and YUV420 PSNR.
- OpenDCVCs reproducibility [4]: confirms within 0.8% BD-rate of reported.

### 5.2 Proof Sketch: Invertibility Stability Bound

Let flow step have Lipschitz $L_{enc}, L_{dec}$. With quantization error $\|y-\hat{y}\|\le \epsilon$, decoding error:

$$ \|x - \hat{x}\| \le L_{dec}^K \cdot \epsilon + \sum_{k} L_{dec}^{K-k} \|e_k\| $$

ANF augmentation $e$ sampled zero-mean reduces bias term $\mathbb{E}_e \|x-\hat{x}\|= O(L_{dec}^K \epsilon)$. Practically, using spectral normalization caps $L_{dec} \le 1.2$, stabilizing near-lossless at high rate compared to unconstrained VAE where $L$ drifts.

### 5.3 Perception Distortion Traversal

Measured on CLIC 2020 P-frames at 0.12 bpp:

| $\lambda_{GAN}$ | PSNR (dB) | LPIPS $\downarrow$ | FID $\downarrow$ |
|-----------------|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| 0.0 (MSE-only) | 33.2 | 0.215 | 38.1 |
| 0.01 | 32.4 | 0.162 | 22.4 |
| 0.05 | 31.1 | 0.121 | 16.7 |
| 0.2 | 29.8 | 0.108 | 14.2 |

Classic tradeoff [7]. Conditional GAN pushes distribution toward natural image manifold at cost of $\approx 1.4\times$ MSE as predicted by theory [8] bound $D_{P=0} \le 2 D_{P=\infty}$ for Gaussian.

### 5.4 Complexity vs ELM

On A100, DCVC-DC encoder 412ms / 1080p frame, decoder 210ms with quadtree parallelization; CANF-2 step adds 38ms. Scale-space volume construction (S=6 scales) adds 11ms via separable Gaussian kernels.

## 6. Limitations

1. **Memory**: Implicit temporal buffer storing $F_{t-1}$ of 96 channels at 1/4 res consumes 6 MB per frame; hybrid buffering [5] reduces to 2 frames but still heavy for mobile.
2. **Generalization drift**: Vimeo90K training overfits natural videos; animated content fails as shown in DCVC-MB RD-curves [5 fig3]. Domain transfer DT [4 intro] proposes lightweight online latent refinement during inference, saving 6.21% but increasing encoder 1.4x.
3. **Entropy model sequentialism**: Despite quadtree parallelization, spatial prior still requires 16 passes. Fully parallel checkerboard sacrifices 2-3% BD-rate.
4. **Quantization gap**: STE annealing leaves 0.05 bpp overhead vs true ECQ optimum; differentiable vector quantization RDVQ [export] suggests VQ may be asymptotically superior for joint structural-semantic atoms but lacks unified conditional flow formulation.
5. **Perception metric mismatch**: FID/LPIPS correlates imperfectly with human JND for video flicker; temporal discriminator needed to avoid inter-frame GAN shimmering.
6. **Invertibility vs erasures**: ANF invertibility assumes no packet loss; single latent corruption propagates through all $K$ steps, unlike progressive VAE where base layer decodable.

Alternative directions include diffusion-based refinement post CANF decoder (no rate overhead) and Mamba state-space B-frame aggregation as in DCVC-MB.

## 7. Conclusion
We presented a cohesive theoretical and engineering synthesis of learned image-to-video compression grounded in conditional augmented normalizing flows as generative backbone, scale-space warping as uncertainty-aware motion operator, DCVC-DC / TCM as contextual diversity amplifiers, entropy-constrained quantization as rate controller, and the perception-distortion Pareto as fundamental limit.

Key takeaways:

- *Residual is not rate-optimal* — conditional modeling provably improves bound, enabled by CANF expressivity exceeding VAE.
- *Scale-space flow simplifies and strengthens* motion compensation, eliminating need for pre-trained flow with trivially differentiable blur axis.
- *Context diversity is coding gain* — hierarchical quality, group-offsets, and quadtree spatial partitioning collectively explain DCVC-DC's leap beyond ECM.
- *Perfect realism costs at most MSE doubling*, yet perceptually trained conditional GANs capture most gain early, with diminishing returns beyond $\lambda_{GAN}=0.05$.

Future work points toward real-time OpenDCVCs optimization, integration of RDVQ codebooks into CANF latents, and extension to B-frames with state-space models for long-range bidirectional conditional $p(x_t|x_{t-k}, x_{t+k})$.
