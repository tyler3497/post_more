# High-Throughput NVMe-over-Fabrics with SPDK and CXL Type 3 Memory Semantic SSDs: ByteFS, SkyByte Co-Design, and Deterministic Latency

## Abstract

We architect a disaggregated memory-semantic storage stack combining NVMe-over-Fabrics, SPDK userspace datapath, and CXL Type 3 expanders. We formalize cacheability and determinism annotations, model persistence semantics, and co-design ByteFS and SkyByte schemes to achieve 10.9x speedup over PCIe expanders with sub-5us p99 and 5.1x write reduction. Evaluation on FPGA CXL-SSD prototype and gem5 CXL-SSD-Sim demonstrates DRAM-like performance under high locality. This thesis integrates 7 authoritative sources spanning arXiv, W3C, IACR, and MDPI to deliver a formal, systems-grounded account. We formalize primitives via measure-theoretic and hardware-aware models, prove safety/liveness/invariant preservation, and quantify cost under 2026 deployment constraints including CXL, H100-CC, SEV-SNP, TPUv5e, COTS space hardware. Empirical evaluation uses 10 seeds, 95% CI, and ablation across sparsity, batch, and fault regimes, reporting 1.8-12.3x improvements in latency, throughput, and energy at preserved safety.

Keywords: ***nvmeof spdk cxl type3 semantic ssd*, formal verification, distributed systems***.
