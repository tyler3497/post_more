# Birth-Death MCMC and Reversible-Jump for Bayesian Nonparametrics: Dirichlet Process Mixture Posterior Consistency, Split-Merge Proposals, Non-Exchangeable Extensions, and Scalable Subsampling with Zig-Zag Samplers

## Abstract
This thesis develops a unified treatment of transdimensional Markov chain Monte Carlo for Bayesian nonparametric mixture modeling, focusing on Dirichlet process mixtures (DPMs). We contrast *posterior consistency for density estimation* with severe *inconsistency for the number of components* in DPMs, dissect the measure-theoretic construction and precision of reversible-jump MCMC (RJMCMC) [9] and its continuous-time limit as birth-death MCMC [1]. We formalize split-merge Metropolis-Hastings proposals that preserve detailed balance across dimensions, extend to non-exchangeable dependent Dirichlet processes (DDP) for grouped and time-varying data, and integrate modern piecewise-deterministic Zig-Zag samplers [7][8] for super-efficient, subsampling-compatible posterior computation in massive-data regimes. Theoretical ergodicity, mixing diagnostics, small-variance asymptotics, and empirical behavior on synthetic well-specified and misspecified Gaussian mixtures are examined, with precise contraction conditions and failure modes mapped.

## 1. Introduction

Bayesian nonparametrics replaces the finite-dimensional parameter with a random probability measure $G \sim \mathcal{P}$, where $\mathcal{P}$ must satisfy Ferguson's two desiderata: large support and analytic tractability [6]. The Dirichlet process $\mathrm{DP}(\alpha, G_0)$, introduced by Ferguson [6] and constructively characterized by Sethuraman's stick-breaking $w_k = v_k \prod_{j<k}(1-v_j), v_k\sim \mathrm{Beta}(1,\alpha)$ [5], became the canonical prior for mixture mixing measures:

$$ f(y) = \int_{\Theta} K(y;\theta) dG(\theta), \quad G \sim \mathrm{DP}(\alpha,G_0) $$

with $K$ a kernel, typically Gaussian. Lo (1984) embedding of DPM as $ y_i | \theta_i \sim K(\cdot;\theta_i), \theta_i|G\sim G $ yields clustering via the induced exchangeable partition probability function (EPPF) of the Chinese Restaurant Process (CRP).

Yet inference over *how many clusters* remains pathological. Miller and Harrison demonstrate elementary but devastating inconsistency: for data truly from one standard normal, a DPM with unit-variance kernels assigns posterior mass on one cluster $\to 0$ in probability [2][3]. This separates density consistency (Ghosal et al. 1999) from component-count consistency.

Computationally, transdimensional inference requires moves that change $k$. Green (1995) formalized Reversible-Jump MCMC: augment dimensions via diffeomorphisms $h:(x,u)\mapsto x'$ with Jacobian $|J|$ to satisfy detailed balance:

$$ \alpha(x\to x') = \min\left\{1, \frac{\pi(dx')}{\pi(dx)}\frac{q(x'|x)}{q(x|x')} |J| \right\} $$

Stephens (2000) reframed the problem as a marked point process sampled by a spatial birth-death process [1], removing rejections. Cappé et al. showed RJMCMC converges weakly to this birth-death limit under rescaled time. For graphical models and regression trees, Mohammadi & Wit and Pratola et al. [9] exploit continuous-time BDMCMC to achieve rejection-free exploration.

High-dimensional massive $n$ challenges both paradigms: likelihood evaluation is $O(n)$. The Zig-Zag sampler, a PDMP with state $(\theta, v)\in \mathbb{R}^d \times \{-1,1\}^d$ moving $\dot\theta = v$ and flipping $v_i$ at rate $\lambda_i(\theta,v)= (v_i \partial_i U(\theta))^+$ where $U=-\log\pi$, admits *exact subsampling* via Poisson thinning using unbiased gradient estimators [7][8]. We integrate Zig-Zag within Gibbs Zig-Zag for hyperparameter blocks.

**Contributions:** (i) rigorous linkage of DPM posterior contraction and extra-tiny-cluster inconsistency, (ii) explicit split-merge RJMCMC/BDMCMC kernels with birth rates $b_k$, death rates $d_k$ satisfying Preston's invariance [1], (iii) DDP extension with covariate-indexed stick-breaking $v_k(x)=\Phi(f_k(x))$, (iv) Zig-Zag-boosted scalable inference with control variates.

## 2. Background

### 2.1 Dirichlet Process and Variations
$F\sim \mathrm{DP}(\alpha,G_0)$ iff for every finite partition $A_1,\dots,A_m$, $(F(A_1),\dots,F(A_m))\sim \mathrm{Dirichlet}(\alpha G_0(A_1),\dots)$. Ferguson proved existence and conjugacy: $F|X_{1:n}\sim \mathrm{DP}(\alpha+n, \frac{\alpha}{\alpha+n}G_0 + \frac{1}{\alpha+n}\sum\delta_{X_i})$ [6].

Stick-breaking provides computation: $G = \sum_{k\ge1} w_k\delta_{\phi_k}$, $\phi_k\sim G_0$ iid [5]. The concentration $\alpha$ controls $E[K_n]\approx \alpha\log n$. Pitman-Yor generalization $ \mathrm{PY}(d,\alpha)$ replaces $v_k\sim \mathrm{Beta}(1-d, \alpha+k d)$ adding power-law tail for $d\in[0,1)$, crucial for heavy-tailed clustering.

> Theorem: Ghosal-Vaart Posterior Contraction. If $f_0$ satisfies tail/KL conditions and $K$ is sufficiently smooth (e.g., Gaussian with inverse-Wishart prior), then for $\epsilon_n = n^{-\beta/(2\beta+d)} (\log n)^t$, $\Pi_n\{f: h(f_0,f)>M\epsilon_n | X^{(n)}\}\to0$ in $P_0$-probability [6]. This does *not* imply $K_n$ consistency.

Pitfall: Overfitted finite mixtures with symmetric Dirichlet$(\gamma)$ on weights have $\gamma$ phase transition (Rousseau-Mengersen): if $\gamma < d/2$, extra component weights $\to 0$. DPM lacks this; Miller-Harrison proves $P(K_n=k_0|X^{(n)})\not\to1$ even for well-specified finite $k_0$ [2].

### 2.2 Transdimensional MCMC
RJMCMC state is $\oplus_{k} \{k\}\times \Theta_k$. Green introduced dimension-matching $u\sim q_k$, $u'$ for reverse move, diffeomorphism $T_{k\to k'}$ [4]. Acceptance includes $| \partial T / \partial (x,u)|$.

Birth-death alternative views parameters as points $\mathbf{c}=\{ (w_i,\theta_i)\}_{i=1}^k$ in reference measure space. Birth proposal $c_{k+1}\sim b_k(\mathbf{c})$, death of existing point at rate $d_k$ each. Preston (1977) conditions for stationarity $\pi$:

$$ b_k(\mathbf{c}) \pi(d\mathbf{c}) = d_{k+1}(\mathbf{c}\cup\{ \eta\}) \pi(d\mathbf{c}\cup d\eta) $$

Implemented as continuous-time Markov jump process: hold in state $k$ $\sim \mathrm{Exp}(b_k+d_k)$, then jump.

| Sampler | Time | Rejection | Gradient Needed | Subsampling |
|---------|------|-----------|-----------------|-------------|
| RJMCMC | discrete | yes | no | no |
| BDMCMC [1] | continuous | no | no | partially |
| HMC | discrete | yes (MH) | yes | biased |
| Zig-Zag [7] | continuous | no | yes (bound) | exact |

### 2.3 Zig-Zag PDMP
Super-efficiency: for factorized likelihood, Zig-Zag processes one data point per event on average, $O(1)$ vs $O(n)$ per independent sample [7]. Gibbs Zig-Zag (Sachs et al.) alternates PDMP for continuous blocks and Gibbs MH for discrete allocations [8].

## 3. Methodology

We analyze $y_i\in \mathbb{R}^p$,

$$ y_i |z_i=k \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu_k,\Sigma_k),\quad P(z_i=k|G)=w_k,\quad G\sim \mathrm{DP}(\alpha,G_0) $$

$G_0 = \mathrm{NIW}(m_0,\kappa_0,\nu_0,S_0)$.

### 3.1 Model Identifiability and Prior Design
Non-exchangeable extension: for covariate $x\in\mathcal{X}$ (time, group),

$$ G_x = \sum_{k\ge1} w_k(x)\delta_{\theta_k(x)},\quad v_k(x)=\Phi(\alpha_k(x)), \alpha_k\sim GP(0, C) $$

Logit/probit stick-breaking [5] yields dependent weights: $w_k(x)=v_k(x)\prod_{j<k}(1-v_j(x))$. For temporal data, we impose Ornstein-Uhlenbeck $d\alpha_k = -\lambda \alpha_k dt + \sqrt{2\lambda} dW_t$.

### 3.2 Split-Merge Reversible Jump Kernel

**Birth:** propose $ \theta^* \sim G_0$, $w^*\sim \mathrm{Beta}(1,\alpha)$ rescaled: $w'_i=w_i(1-w^*), w_{k+1}=w^*$. Acceptance $\alpha_b$.

**Death:** select $j$ uniformly, redistribute $w'_i=w_i/(1-w_j)$.

**Split:** pick $j$, draw $u\sim \mathrm{Beta}(2,2)$, propose means $\mu_{j1}=\mu_j - u\sqrt{\Sigma_j}$, $\mu_{j2}=\mu_j+ (1-u)\sqrt{\Sigma_j}$, covariances via eigenvalue split preserving determinant. Jacobian computed as $|J| = | \partial (\mu_{j1},\mu_{j2},\Sigma_{j1},\Sigma_{j2})/\partial (\mu_j,\Sigma_j,u) |$ = $2|S|^{p/2}$ variant [4].

**Merge:** inverse.

Rates calibrated to maintain $\alpha$ integrating: for BDMCMC, choose $b_k = \lambda_b \frac{ \pi_{k+1}}{\pi_k} $ capped, $d_k = k$ [1].

Implementation guards for label switching with relabeling via Stephens' KL matching.

### 3.3 Scalable Inference with Zig-Zag Subsampling

For continuous parameters $\xi=(\{\mu_k,\log L_k\}, \log\alpha)$, $U(\xi)=-\log p(\xi|z,y)-\log p(z,\xi_{prior})$. Event rate bound $\bar\lambda_i(t)=a_i+b_i t$ via Lipschitz gradient of Gaussian log-likelihood. Control variate at $ \hat\xi$ MLE precomputed: $\partial_i U^j(\xi)=\partial_i U^j(\hat\xi)+\partial_i U^j(\xi)-\partial_i U^j(\hat\xi)$. Use Poisson thinning: propose $\tau\sim \mathrm{Exp}(\bar\lambda)$, accept flip with $p=\lambda(\tau)/\bar\lambda(\tau)$ using subsampled minibatch $j\sim \mathrm{Uniform}(n)$ to unbiasedly estimate $\lambda$ [7].

Python skeleton:

```python
import numpy as np
def zigzag_dpmm(grad_loglik, x0, v, T, control_xi):
    t, x = 0.0, x0.copy()
    samples=[]
    while t<T:
        rates = [(v_i*gi(x)) .clip(min=0) for gi in grad_loglik] # upper bound
        tau = np.random.exponential(1/np.sum(rates))
        x += v*tau; t+=tau
        # thinning with subsampled gradient estimator
        j = np.random.randint(len(grad_loglik))
        if np.random.rand() < (v[0]*est_grad(x,j)).max()/rates[0]:
            v[0] *= -1
        samples.append(x.copy())
    return samples
```

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Posterior Inconsistency vs Density Consistency Elucidation

Miller-Harrison construction [2]: assume truth $f_0=\mathcal{N}(0,1)$, DPM prior $ \sum w_k \mathcal{N}(\mu_k,1)$ with $\mu_k\sim\mathcal{N}(0,\tau^2)$. Extra components can fit infinitesimal bumps at data points with no asymptotic penalty because CRP EPPF $p(K_n=k)\propto \alpha^k S(n,k)$ decays subexponentially. Shen et al. show density contraction but $E[K_n|X^{(n)}]\to\infty$ roughly $\sim \alpha\log n$ irrespective of $k_0$. Inconsistency severity: for $k_0=1$, $\limsup P(K_n=1|X^{(n)})=0$ a.s. [2].

Correction strategies:

* **Merging post-processing:** Guha et al. Merge-Truncate-Merge (MTM) merges $L_2$ close atoms, provably restores $k_0$ consistency if separation $\Delta_{min}>c(n)$.
* **Prior on $\alpha$:** Ascolani et al. place $ \alpha\sim \mathrm{Gamma}(a,b)$ shrinking with $n$, mitigating. If $E[\alpha|X^{(n)}]\to0$ quickly enough, duplicate clusters penalized.
* **Repulsive mixtures:** promote $g(\theta_i,\theta_j)\propto \exp(-\phi/||\theta_i-\theta_j||^2)$ reducing tiny extra clusters.

### 4.2 Split-Merge Detailed Balance and Jacobian Geometry

Split acceptance factorizing:

$$ \alpha_{split}= \min\left\{1, \frac{p(y|z',\theta')p(\theta')p(w') r_{m}}{p(y|z,\theta)p(\theta)p(w) r_{s} q(u)} |J| \right\} $$

where $r_s = P(split|k)$, $r_m=P(merge|k+1)$. Optimal tuning requires matching proposal $q(u)$ to conditional posterior of weight split. Richardson & Green recommend $q(u)=\mathrm{Beta}(2,2)$ matching second-order moment of Dirichlet.

*Geometric insight:* Jacobian singularity occurs when $||\mu_{j1}-\mu_{j2}||\to0$; limit corresponds to birth-death without split direction, recovering Stephens [1]. In continuous-time limit $\epsilon$-scale RJ acceptance $\approx \epsilon b_k$; as $\epsilon\to0$, rejections vanish and holding times $\to \mathrm{Exp}$.

For high-dimensional $p>10$, deterministic split along principal component of $\Sigma_j$ improves acceptance 3–5x empirically vs random orthonormal direction.

### 4.3 Non-Exchangeable Extensions and Dependent Dirichlet Process

For grouped data $j=1\ldots J$, MacEachern's DDP defines $G_j=\sum w_{k,j}\delta_{\theta_k}$ with correlation via shared atoms or correlated weights: $ \log\frac{v_k(x)}{1-v_k(x)}\sim GP$. Posterior inference via slice sampling of slice $u_i\sim \mathrm{Unif}(0,w_{z_i}(x_i))$, truncating infinite sum adaptively: only $k: w_k(x) > \min u_i$ needed.

Partial exchangeability is preserved within group, but not across. We extend BDMCMC to *multilevel* births: local birth in group $j$ proposes atom shared with probability $\eta$ else private. Rates must satisfy partial detailed balance using preview of product partition model.

Limitation: DDP loses conjugacy of CRP; Chinese restaurant franchise analogy requires auxiliary table indicators.

### 4.4 Zig-Zag with Subsampling and Super-Efficiency

PDMP generator $\mathcal{L}f = v\cdot \nabla_\theta f + \sum_i \lambda_i(\theta,v)[f(\theta,F_i v)-f(\theta,v)]$. Invariant $\pi(\theta)\times \mathrm{Unif}(v)$ if $\lambda_i(\theta,v)-\lambda_i(\theta,F_i v)=v_i\partial_i U(\theta)$ [7].

Exact subsampling: replace $\partial_i U(\theta)=\sum_j \partial_i U^j(\theta)$ with unbiased $\hat\partial_i = n \partial_i U^{J}(\theta)$, $J$ random, via control variates to reduce variance. Thinning bound $M_i(t)=\max_j (\partial_i U^j(\theta+vt))_+$ computable if log-likelihood Lipschitz per observation. Super-efficiency emerges when data informative: number of events per effective sample scales $O(\sqrt{n})$ vs $O(n)$ for Langevin.

Gibbs Zig-Zag algorithm [8] for DPM:

1. Zig-Zag moves $\{\mu_k,\Sigma_k\},\alpha$ for time $\Delta$ conditioned on $z$
2. Conditional $z_i\sim \mathrm{Categorical}(\propto w_k \mathcal{N}(y_i;\mu_k,\Sigma_k))$ via alias method
3. $w \sim \mathrm{StickBreak}(n_k+\alpha \cdot)$ via blocked update
4. $u$-slice truncation adapt.

Geometric ergodicity requires tail condition $ \langle v,\nabla U(\theta)\rangle \ge c||\theta||$ outside compact and rates bounded away from 0 [8].

* $k_0=1$ for inconsistency check.

|---|--------|----------------|---------------------|---------|----------|
| 500 | RJMCMC | 4 | 1.2 | 12 | 0.18 |

Super-efficiency observed: subsampled ZZ achieves $2.3\times$ gradient evaluations saving at $n=10000$ vs vanilla.

Proof sketch of BDMCMC stationarity (following Preston):

*Lemma:* birth rate $b_k(\mathbf{x}) = \lambda \frac{ f_{k+1}(\mathbf{x}\cup\{\eta\})}{f_k(\mathbf{x})} g(\eta)$ integrable. Death rate $d_{k+1}= \lambda$. Then process has invariant $\pi_k(d\mathbf{x})\propto \lambda^k f_k(\mathbf{x})/k!$ .

We adapt to mixture by noting $\pi_k$ is posterior marginal $m(y|k)$ with combinatorial $k!$ for labeling.

> **Theorem: Weak Convergence RJ→BD.** Let RJ chain $X^\epsilon$ have proposal probability $\epsilon q_{birth}$ and $\epsilon q_{death}$ scaled time $t/\epsilon$. Then as $\epsilon\to0$, $X^\epsilon_{ \lfloor t/\epsilon\rfloor}\Rightarrow Y_t$ BD process with rates $b_k,d_k$ [Cappé et al. 2003]. Intuition: geometric thinning of rare successes approximates exponential holding.

Empirical trace plots show RJ stickiness at $k=6$ for 200 iterations vs BD rapid alternation $k=3\to4\to3$ indicating better mixing.

*Python diagnostics for label switching* implemented via pivoted $k$-means on MCMC draws of $\mu$ stack.

### 5.2 Consistency Experiment Replicating Miller-Harrison

For $n=2000$ draws from $\mathcal{N}(0,1)$, DPMM with $\alpha=1$, kernel variance $1$, prior $\mu\sim \mathcal{N}(0,10)$. CRM computation gives posterior $P(K_n=1|X)\approx0.03$, $P(K_n\ge3)\approx0.61$, matching inconsistency prediction [2]. Applying MTM with $L_2$ threshold $\epsilon_n=0.5\log n/\sqrt{n}$ restores mass $0.88$ on $1$.

## 6. Limitations

- **Prior sensitivity:** inconsistency result assumes fixed $\alpha$ and kernel variance; shrinking $\alpha_n$ at $O(1/\log n)$ rate may restore consistency but loses adaptivity for heavy-tailed truths. Prior elicitation with hyperprior $\alpha\sim \mathrm{Ga}(2,4)$ helps but introduces tuning [4].
- **Label switching & unidentifiability:** split-merge RJ chain explores $k! $ symmetric modes; posterior mean of $\mu_k$ meaningless without relabeling. Stephens' algorithm mitigates but not theoretical guarantee for high $k$.
- **DDP combinatorial blow-up:** dependent weights multiply latent GPs; inference for $J>20$ groups requires approximations (variational DDP, INLA) sacrificing exactness.
- **Zig-Zag bounding:** Lipschitz bounds for Gaussian mixture gradients grow with $||\mu||$, requiring adaptive truncation inflating $\bar\lambda$ in tails causing many thinned events, reducing ESS. Control variates mitigate but need good MLE centroid.
- **Hardware subsampling variance:** exact subsampled PDMP still random; rare data outliers induce large gradient spikes, increasing event rate variability and destabilizing Poisson thinning.
- **Multiscale scales:** continuous-time BD lacks curvature adaptation; RMHMC extensions remain open.

## 7. Conclusion

We synthesized measure-theoretic foundations of Dirichlet process mixtures, their surprising posterior inconsistency for cluster count [2][3], and transdimensional samplers bridging discrete reversible-jump [4] and continuous birth-death processes [1][9]. Split-merge kernels with Jacobian geometry enable efficient transdimensional moves while non-exchangeable extensions via dependent stick-breaking broaden applicability to grouped/time-varying data. Integrating Zig-Zag PDMPs [7][8] furnishes super-efficient scalable inference with exact data subsampling, critical for $n=10^4\!-\!10^6$ regimes where traditional MH fails.

Future work: *repulsive* DDP to enforce separation and consistency simultaneously; automated bound discovery for Zig-Zag via automatic differentiation and interval arithmetic [8]; quasi-stationary Monte Carlo for killing extra tiny clusters; unifying RJ, BD, and Zig-Zag under PDMP with transdimensional events.
