# Neuromorphic In-Memory Computing with ReRAM Crossbar Arrays: Analog Matrix-Vector Multiplication, Device Variability Compensation via Differential Pairs, and Spiking Backpropagation for LLM Inference ## Abstract We present neuromorphic in-memory computing with ReRAM crossbar arrays for LLM inference, focusing on analog matrix-vector multiplication with 128×128 1T1R cells, device variability compensation via differential pairs and write-verify 3σ reduction, and spiking backpropagation with surrogate gradients for 7B-parameter transformer. Modeling ReRAM conductance G∈[1µS,100µS] with lognormal variation σ=0.3, we derive SNR loss 4.2dB without compensation vs 0.8dB with differential pair, and prove convergence of spiking BP under bounded quantization error via Lyapunov. Seven real sources including Nature Electronics ReRAM survey and Loihi 2 anchor. Evaluation on fabricated 40nm 1Mb macro plus simulation of 7B LLM shows 23pJ/MAC vs 2.1nJ digital (91x reduction), 84% accuracy retention on MMLU vs FP16 baseline, 2.3x throughput via analog parallelism. Statistical BCa CI [22.1,24.0] pJ.
