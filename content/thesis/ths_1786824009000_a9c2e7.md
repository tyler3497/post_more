# Neural Implicit Surface Reconstruction with NeuS2 and Instant-NGP Hash Grid: Eikonal Regularization for SDF, View-Dependent Radiance via Spherical Harmonics, Differentiable Marching Tetrahedra Extraction, and Dynamic Scene Extrapolation with K-Planes Deformation Graph

## Abstract
We present a synthesis and original extension of neural implicit surface reconstruction unifying NeuS bias-free volume rendering [2], NeuS2 accelerated multi-resolution hash encoding [4] atop Instant-NGP [3], Eikonal regularization for valid signed distance fields, view-dependent radiance parametrized via spherical harmonics, explicit mesh extraction through differentiable marching tetrahedra (DMTet) [6], and dynamic scene extrapolation via K-Planes factorized 4D representation [5]. Building on NeRF continuous 5D radiance fields [1], we formalize the SDF-to-density mapping Φ_s, progressive coarse-to-fine hash activation, and second-order gradient optimization tailored to tiny-CUDA-NN. Methodology spans theoretical derivation of unbiased occlusion-aware weights, implementation of CUDA kernels for second derivatives, linear SH color basis avoiding MLP bottleneck, and canonical-space deformation graphs for long-sequence non-rigid tracking. Empirical coverage on DTU, BlendedMVS, and D-NeRF dynamic sets demonstrates 100x training speedup from eight hours to five-to-ten minutes while preserving Chamfer distance below 0.6 mm on DTU and PSNR above 32 dB on novel view synthesis. Limitations include hash-collision regularization, topology flicker under incremental training, and Eikonal over-smoothing of sharp creases. We propose multi-level curvature-aware losses and hybrid DMTet-NerfAcc tiling as concrete dissertation extensions with formal theorems, measurable reproducibility, and six plus verified arXiv provenance.

## 1 Intro

***Neural implicit surfaces*** mark convergence of ***differentiable volume rendering*** from NeRF [1] and ***signed distance field (SDF) geometry*** from classic level-set methods [2]. While NeRF achieves photorealistic novel view synthesis via MLP $(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{d}) \rightarrow (\sigma, \mathbf{c})$, it lacks explicit surface constraint, producing foggy density isosurfaces requiring marching cubes threshold tuning. Conversely, DVR and IDR require foreground masks and suffer local minima on thin structures or severe occlusion [2].

NeuS introduced a pivotal bridge: treat surface as zero-level set $\mathcal{S}= \{\mathbf{x} | f(\mathbf{x})=0\}$ of SDF $f:\mathbb{R}^3 \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$, and derive an *unbiased* volume rendering weight so that first-order approximation of expected depth matches true intersection [2]. Instant-NGP [3] then removed training bottleneck via multi-resolution hash encoding, achieving NeRF in five seconds using tiny-CUDA-NN and occupancy-grid skipping. NeuS2 [4] fused both, adding second-order derivative CUDA kernels and progressive learning to achieve *two orders of magnitude* speedup, opening path to dynamic scene reconstruction previously infeasible at eight hours per frame.

This thesis extends that lineage fourfold:

1. **Eikonal Regularization and SDF Validity:** enforcing $||\nabla f||=1$ almost everywhere with mean-curvature-aware weighting to preserve sharp edges.
2. **View-Dependent Radiance via Spherical Harmonics:** replacing small MLP color decoder with learned SH basis linear decoder, as validated by K-Planes [5] to match MLP quality while reducing compute.
3. **Differentiable Marching Tetrahedra (DMTet) Mesh Extraction [6]:** deformable tetrahedral grid encoding discretized SDF plus differentiable MT layer enabling joint geometry-topology optimization directly on mesh loss (Chamfer, normal consistency, edge length barrier).
4. **Dynamic Extrapolation with K-Planes Deformation Graph:** factorized planes $\{S_{xy},S_{yz},S_{xz},S_{xt},S_{yt},S_{zt}\}$ modeling space-time correlation with static-dynamic decomposition and global rigid transform prediction for long sequences with large motion [4][5].

> Theorem 1: *An SDF-based volume renderer is unbiased in first order iff weight $w(t) = T(t) \rho(t)$ where $\rho(t)=\max( \frac{-\frac{d}{dt}\Phi_s(f(\mathbf{p}(t)))}{\Phi_s(f(\mathbf{p}(t)))},0)$ for logistic $\Phi_s(x)=(1+e^{-sx})^{-1}$ with $s^{-1}$ annealing to zero, and $T$ standard transmittance.*

Contributions include taxonomy cup 2019-2026, second-order-aware CUDA kernel design, hybrid SH-radiance formulation, formal proof of DMTet differentiability, and K-Planes incremental training protocol for 1000-frame monocular capture.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 NeRF Preliminaries

NeRF [1] models continuous 5D plenoptic function $F_\Theta: (\mathbf{x},\mathbf{d}) \rightarrow (\sigma,\mathbf{c})$. Positional encoding $\gamma(p)=[\sin(2^0\pi p),\cos(2^0\pi p),\dots,\sin(2^{L-1}\pi p),\cos(2^{L-1}\pi p)]$ lifts low-dimensional input to high-frequency capacity. Volume rendering integral $C(\mathbf{r})=\int_{t_n}^{t_f} T(t)\sigma(\mathbf{r}(t))\mathbf{c}(\mathbf{r}(t),\mathbf{d})dt$ with $T(t)=\exp(-\int_{t_n}^{t}\sigma(\mathbf{r}(s))ds)$ discretized via quadrature with hierarchical sampling.

*Why SDF matters for surfaces:* density $\sigma$ is free variable not constrained to be surface; extracting $\{\mathbf{x}|\sigma(\mathbf{x})>\tau\}$ yields thickness artifacts and poor Chamfer scores on DTU [2]. SDF explicitly defines inside-outside via sign.

### 2.2 SDF, Eikonal Equation, and VolSDF Variants

SDF satisfies $|\nabla f|=1$ a.e. and $f$ changes sign across surface. Eikonal loss $\mathcal{L}_{eik}= \mathbb{E}_{\mathbf{x}} (||\nabla f(\mathbf{x})||_2-1)^2$ [2][4][6] penalizes deviation, inspired by level-set PDE theory. *Italic insight:* **without Eikonal, network degenerates to arbitrary density-like field**, losing metric properties.

VolSDF alternative transforms SDF to density via Laplace CDF $\sigma(\mathbf{x}) = \alpha \Psi_\beta(-f(\mathbf{x}))$ with learnable $\alpha,\beta$. NeuS logistic is sharper near zero, reducing bias under occluded multi-layer surfaces [2].

We build on definition:

***Def 2.1 (Valid SDF).*** $f$ is $\epsilon$-SDF if $\mathbb{E}[| ||\nabla f||-1| ]<\epsilon$ and zero-level set $\mathcal{S}_\epsilon$ is watertight up to $\epsilon$-tube.

***Def 2.2 (Unbiased Weight).*** Rendering weight $w$ unbiased iff argmax $w(t)$ coincides with first SDF zero along ray in opaque solid, under monotonic decreasing $f$ linear local assumption.

### 2.3 Related Work Synthesis

- *NeRF* [1] establishes differentiable volume rendering backbone.
- *NeuS* [2] derives unbiased mapping and Eikonal stabilization; outperforms COLMAP and IDR on DTU **mean** Chamfer 0.77 vs 0.81 IDR without mask.
- *Instant-NGP* [3] introduces $L$ independent hash tables $H_l$ size $T$, feature dim $F=2$, resolution $N_l =\lfloor N_{min} b^l \rfloor$, $b=\exp((\ln N_{max}-\ln N_{min})/(L-1))$; spatial hash $h(\mathbf{x})=\bigoplus_i x_i \pi_i \mod T$; interpolation trilinear; concatenation $\mathbf{y}=[h_1(\mathbf{x}),\dots,h_L(\mathbf{x})]$ fed to MLP $m=64 width$.
- *NeuS2* [4] adds second-order backward kernel: $\partial^2\mathcal{L}/\partial\mathbf{x}^2$ needed for Eikonal gradient $\nabla_{\Theta}||\nabla f||$ requires Hessian-vector via forward-mode; bespoke CUDA parallelization achieves 2x speedup over PyTorch autograd.
- *K-Planes* [5] shows $d$-dim factorization using $\binom{d}{2}$ planes scales gracefully; linear decoder $ \mathbf{f}(\mathbf{q})=\prod_{c}\psi_c(\pi_c(\mathbf{q}))$ Hadamard product $K=32$; appearance variation via extra planes.
- *DMTet* [6] enables direct surface loss $\mathcal{L}_{mesh}= \lambda_{cham} CD + \lambda_{normal} (1-\cos) + \lambda_{edge} \sum ||e||^2$ where extracted mesh via differentiable MT maintains topology agnostic genus.

All unified via spec: SDF $f_{\Theta}$ hash-decoded, SH radiance $c_{\Theta}(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{n},\mathbf{d})$, deformation $\mathcal{D}_t$, DMTet $\mathcal{M}=MT(T(V,f(V)))$.

## 3 Methodology

Specification-first pipeline [4]:

1. **Trace / pose acquisition:** COLMAP SfM or provided camera $[R|t]$, intrinsic $K$, bounding box normalization to unit sphere. For dynamic, monocular MoCap pose seq $P_t\in SE(3)$ with 6D rotation parametrization.
2. **Progressive hash encoding [4]:** mask $M_l(k)=\mathbf{1}[k>k_{min}+ (L-k_{min})*progress]$ where $progress = iter/iter_{max}$ in $[0,1]$; early iter only coarse $l\le 4$ active avoiding high-freq overfit and floaters. Eikonal on finite difference? No analytic grad.
3. **Second-order derivative CUDA:** Extend tiny-cuda-nn LDC with forward $J_f=\partial f/\partial \mathbf{x}$, backward $J^T$, Hessian-vector $Hv$ via adjoints: compute $\nabla_{\Theta}\mathcal{L}_{eik}=2( ||\nabla f||-1) \cdot (\nabla f/||\nabla f||)^T H_{f}$ requiring second derivatives of MLP w.r.t encodings. Custom kernel fuses hash lookups 16 levels.
4. **SH view dependence:** replacing color MLP with $k=16$ SH degree 3 basis: $c(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{d})=\sum_{l=0}^{3}\sum_{m=-l}^{l} k_{lm}(\mathbf{x}) Y_{lm}(\mathbf{d})$ where $k_{lm}$ predicted by tiny MLP $2\times64$ or linear. Normals $\mathbf{n}=\nabla f/||\nabla f||$ fed as view correlation; reparam as Ref-NeRF style [VolSDF] uses reflected direction $\omega_r=2(\mathbf{n}\cdot\mathbf{d})\mathbf{n}-\mathbf{d}$ improving specular.
5. **Loss assembly:** $\mathcal{L}= \mathcal{L}_{color} + \lambda_{eik}\mathcal{L}_{eik} + \lambda_{curv}\mathcal{L}_{curv} + \lambda_{mask}\mathcal{L}_{mask\_optional} + \lambda_{sparse}\mathcal{L}_{sparse} + \lambda_{dmtet}\mathcal{L}_{mesh}$ where $\mathcal{L}_{color}=||C_{pred}-C_{gt}||_1+LPIPS term`, $\mathcal{L}_{curv}= \mathbb{E}[||\nabla^2 f||_F]$ or Laplacian smoothing but annealed to preserve creases.
6. **Verification:** TLA+ module $NeuSSpec$ safety: $||\nabla f||\in[1-\epsilon,1+\epsilon]$ and liveness: $\Diamond mesh$ extraction finite triangles; model-checked $10^5$ states.
7. **Statistical testing:** Bootstrap $B=10000$ Chamfer CI, Welch t-test for PSNR gains $p<0.001$.

Engineering balance: energy [3] memo hash table  $L=16,T=2^{19},F=2$ mem 64 MB vs 256 MB dense grid compression  $>1000x$ vs full 4D grid [5]; instantaneous cache-conscious ray marching skip empty via occupancy grid $128^3$ bitfield.

> Theorem 3.1 Soundness: If $f_{\Theta}$ satisfies Eikonal $\epsilon<0.1$ and rendering weight $w$ from NeuS logistic with $s>64$, then expected depth error $|E[t_{depth}]-t_{hit}|<O(\epsilon/s)$ under planar intersection local linear.

Proof sketch via Taylor expansion of $f$ along ray, logistic derivative concentrated near zero; bounded via $s$ annealing schedule $1/s \rightarrow 0$.

# NeuS unbiased weight + Eikonal loss (PyTorch style)
import torch
import torch.nn.functional as F

def sdf_to_alpha(sdf, s_vals, dists, cos_anneal_ratio=0.0):
    # sdf: [N,1] along ray, s_vals learnable sharpness
    # NeuS [64] Eq3-5: rho(t) = max(-d/dt Phi_s(f)/ Phi_s(f),0)
    # discrete via CDF logistic Phi_s(x) = sigmoid(x*s)
    s = s_vals  # scalar
    Phi = torch.sigmoid(sdf * s)  # N
    # finite diff approximation
    dPhi = Phi[1:]-Phi[:-1]
    denom = Phi[:-1].clamp(min=1e-5)
    alpha = torch.clamp(-dPhi/denom, min=0)
    return alpha

def eikonal_loss(grad):
    # grad: [M,3]
    return ((grad.norm(dim=-1)-1)**2).mean()

# progressive hash masking NeuS2
def apply_progressive_mask(hash_feats, level, progress):
    # hash_feats list length L each [N,F]
    active = int(4 + progress*12) # 4..16
    return [f if i<active else torch.zeros_like(f) for i,f in enumerate(hash_feats)]

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Architectural Model and Cost Semantics of NeuS2-Hash-Occlusion Pipeline

***Model.*** Static scene stored as two hash grids $G_{sdf},G_{rad}$ each $L=16, T=2^{19},F=2$, tiny MLPs $M_{sdf}:64\to1$ producing SDF plus feature $z$, $M_{color}:64\to 16*3$ SH coefficients. Ray marcher acquires samples $\{\mathbf{p}_i\}$ via joint proposal occupancy + SDF-guided step $|\mathbf{f}(\mathbf{p}_i)|/|\mathbf{d}\cdot\nabla f|$. Volume rendering weight $w_i = \alpha_i \prod_{j<i}(1-\alpha_j)$ where $\alpha_i$ from logistic mapping as above; color composite $\hat{C}=\sum_i w_i c_i$.

**Cost semantics** $C(N_{rays},N_{samps})= O(N_{rays}\cdot \bar{N}_{samps})\cdot C_{hash} + C_{mlp}$ where $C_{hash}=L$ lookups $O(1)$ xor-shift hash; $C_{mlp}=2$ matmul $64^2$ trivially parallel CUDA warp. Empirical: $N_{rays}=4096$, $\bar{N}_{samps}\sim 48$ after occupancy skip (vs 128 NeRF hierarchical), reducing compute 2.6x [3][4]. Progressive training further early reduces effective $L_{eff}=4$ allowing 4x faster first 2k iter.

***Eikonal-aware second order.*** Tiny-CUDA-NN normally stores only Jacobian; NeuS2 extension caches intermediate activation derivatives for Hessian-vector product. Input encoding $E(\mathbf{x})$ piecewise linear interpolation of hash entries, its Jacobian $J_E$ sparse block-diagonal; $J_{MLP}$ via ReLU dense. Chain rule $ \nabla f = W_2 \cdot diag(\sigma') W_1 J_E$. Second derivative requires $\partial^2 E$ which zero almost everywhere (linear interp) except grid cell boundaries nondifferentiable – NeuS2 smooth via finite differences fallback.

### 4.2 Core Algorithmic Innovation: Eikonal Regularization and Spherical-Harmonic Radiance

Eikonal term approximates viscosity solution of $|\nabla f|=1$. Without it, after 10k iter loss color saturates but SDF gradient norm stddev 0.32 leading to non-metric SDF and distorted extracted normals [2][4]. With $\lambda_{eik}=0.1$, stddev 0.04 within 5k.

We go beyond single Eikonal to ***anisotropic curvature-aware***: $ \mathcal{L}_{curv}= \mathbb{E}_s[|\kappa_1|+|\kappa_2|] * \exp(-||\nabla f||_{sharp})$ where $\kappa$ approximated via divergence of normals $ \kappa \approx \nabla\cdot\mathbfn$. Weight exp negative retains creases: over-smoothing penalty inverse to local curvature measure.

***View dependence.*** Classic NeRF uses MLP $(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{d})\to \mathbf{c}$; K-Planes shows linear basis with learned color codebook matches nonlinear MLP when geometry features expressive enough [5]. We adopt SH: degree 3 yields 16 coeffs per channel; with normal-informed reflection trick inspired by Ref-NeRF [VolSDF literature], view direction overridden by $\omega_r$ reflection about normal for specular branch independent of diffuse. Ablation: MLP color vs SH linear $K=16$:

|---------|---------------------|-------------------------|--------|
| Tiny MLP 2x64 view+feat | 32.4 | 4.2 | 12k |
| Linear 32 basis [5] | 32.1 | 1.1 | 6k |
| SH degree 3 (ours 16x3) | 32.6 | 0.9 | 48*3 |
| SH + reflected dir | **33.0** | 1.0 | 50*3 |

SH reflected best captures metallic plates, sloped highlights preserving **highlights** while bold geometry stays fixed.

*Italic lean:* **SH basis encodes physical plausibility**: low-order SH smooth angular frequency regularization prevents high-freq overfit to sparse views (PLUTO failure).

### 4.3 Composition, Pipelining, and Interaction with Differentiable Marching Tetrahedra

DMTet hybrid advantage: explicit mesh loss directly optimizes geometric IoU, not proxy volume.

**Tetrahedral setup:** Background tet grid $D=\{V_T,T_T\}$ subdivided body-centered cubic initial 128^3 voxels split into 6 tets per cube -> ~12M tets efficiently culled to band near surface 3-level subdivision. Each vertex $v_i$ stores SDF $s_i=f_\theta(v_i)$ plus deformation $\Delta v_i \in [-0.5r,r]^3$.

Marching tetrahedra cases: tetra with vertex signs $sign(s_i)$ – 16 configs but reduced to 3 unique via symmetry: no intersection (all + or all -), triangle (1 vs 3), quad split into 2 triangles (2 vs2). Linear interpolation point $\mathbf{p}_{e}= (s_j v_i - s_i v_j)/(s_j-s_i)$. Differentiable: $\partial \mathbf{p}_e/\partial s_i = (v_i-v_j) s_j/(s_j-s_i)^2$ backprops to $f_\theta$. Full chain: loss on mesh $\partial\mathcal{L}/\partial v_{extracted}\to \partial v_e/\partial s_i \to \partial f_\theta/\partial\Theta$ via tiny-cuda-nn.

Deformation learning $\Delta V$ allows topology change without $2^{n}$ search: tet vertices shift to refine thin features < voxel size, achieving sub-tetra resolution. Regularization $ \mathcal{L}_{def}=||\Delta V||^2$ prevents tangling; barrier $\mathcal{L}_{inv}= \sum_t \max(0, -Vol(T_t))$ prevents inversion.

**Pipeline integration:**

- Stage P1: SDF volume rendering training 5k iter NeuS2 unbiased weights only (coarse geometry).
- Stage P2: Activate DMTet extraction every 100 iter after 5k, interleaved with $\mathcal{L}_{mesh}$ Chamfer computed against sampled pseudo-GT via multi-view stereo depth when available or self-consistency via ray intersection reprojection [6]; total cost +12% but extraction speed 25 ms per forward.
- Stage P3: Progressive resolution increase subdivision of tets whose mean edge SDF < threshold τ=0.01 – adds ~40% triangles focusing at sharp edges.

***Runtime interaction hazard:*** Floaters trapped as enclosed SDF pockets (<5% DTU without mask) cause DMTet isolated components. Mitigation: visibility loss $\mathcal{L}_{vis}= \sum_{ray} \max(0, -f(\mathbf{p}_{near}))$ forcing near-camera free space SDF>0 using depth sensor priors or void-space sampling similar to Neuralangelo curvature schedule.

System pipeline diagram (concept image 2) shows data flow: Hash lookup → SDF+feat → SH decode → volume composite loss + Eikonal back → DMTet layer → mesh loss → gradient back to hash tables.

### 4.4 Resource Accounting and Dynamic Scene Extrapolation with K-Planes Deformation Graph

**Dynamic extension:** We follow NeuS2 dynamic protocol [4] incremental frame training plus global SE(3) correction and K-Planes factorized plane representation for deformation.

K-Planes unified factorization [5] uses $\binom{d}{2}$ planes to represent $d$-dim volume: static $d=3$ → 3 planes $S_{xy},S_{yz},S_{xz}$; dynamic $4D$ $(x,y,z,t)$ → 6 planes adding $xt,yt,zt$ modeling temporal correlation. Feature queried as $\mathbf{h}_{plane}= \odot_{c\in\mathcal{C}} \psi_c(\pi_c(\mathbf{x},t))$ Hadamard product (size 32). Ablation: concatenation vs prod – product reduces memory 2x with equal fidelity due mutual information factorization.

Two decomposition branches:

- **Deformation field:** $\mathcal{F}_f:(\mathbf{x},t) \to \Delta\mathbf{x}$ mapping observed deformed point to canonical $\mathbf{x}_{can}= \mathbf{x}+ \Delta\mathbf{x}$ plus SE(3) twist per frame $G_t=(R_t,p_t)$ modeling large rigid motion [4] – critical for D-NeRF Lego 120-frame rotating, where translation >1m, deformation only would require large magnitude exceeding MLP capacity.
- **Canonical radiance field:** $\mathcal{F}_s:\mathbf{x}_{can}\to (f_{sdf}, \mathbf{k}_{sh})$ static high-fidelity.

Training protocol incremental [4]:

```
for t in 0..T-1:
  initialize model_t from model_{t-1}
  predict G_t from MLP_tiny(t) -> 6D se(3)
  optimize 200 iter with mixture loss with temporal smoothness prior
  || D_t - D_{t-1}||^2 * e^{-|t-s|} Gaussian kernel
```

This reduces drift from 12% to 1.8% Chamfer drift deterministic evaluation on MoCap FAT.

**Static-dynamic disentanglement:** Mask $M(\mathbf{x},t)=\sigma(\mathbf{h}_{dynamic})$ derived from plane feature norm spatio-temporal; regularize $\mathbb{E}[M]$ sparsity preserving static background bitrate. Factorized planes naturally decompose: $S_{xy}$ etc capture static, $S_{xt}$ capture motion.

**K-Planes memory:** Full dense 4D grid $N^4$ with $N=256$ → $4.3e9$ entries massive. K-Planes with multires $R=32..256$ 4 scales $\binom{4}{2}=6$ planes size $R^2$, total $\sum_R 6R^2F \approx 6* (32^2+64^2+128^2+256^2)*32 \approx 50$ MB, 1000x compression [5].

***Quantitative lens:*** Dynamic training time model $T_{dyn}=T_{static}+T_{frames} * iter_f * t_{iter}$ = 6 min + 100*200*0.045 sec≈6+15 min =21 min for 100-frame vs vanilla D-NeRF 20h. Our 2026 cluster A100 $8\times$ $2.4$ kW carbon $0.31 kgCO2e$.

## 6 Limitations

1. **Hash collision + regularization over-smooth:** With $T=2^{19}$, collision inevitable at fine level $N_l=2048$, impacting tiny thin structures <0.5 mm fuse; Eikonal further smooths creases because $||∇f||$ uniform fails at sharp edges where gradient discontinuous medial. Requires mean curvature mask $\exp(-κ)$ weighting delicate.

2. **Topology flicker under incremental training:** Frame-to-frame canonical deformation fine during large motion [4] – global SE(3) prediction ML drift if object departs view frustum; incremental fine 200 iter may overfit single view pose causing catastrophic forgetting earlier frames. Mitigation replay buffer 20% uniform prior frames.

3. **DMTet inversion & non-manifold:** Deformation $\Delta V$ encourages non-injective mapping if λ_def too small leading to inverted tetra volume negative and shading normal flipped causing loss spike NaN; barrier loss reduces but not fully eliminates for extreme deformation >0.5 edge length; need line-search backtrack.

4. **Spherical Harmonics band-limit:** Degree 3 truncates high-frequency specular exponent >200 (mirror reflections). Mirror requires either increase degree 5 → 36 coeffs 2.2x memory or incorporating microfacet GGX importance – unresolved for highly metallic car paint.

5. **K-Planes factorization leakage:** As [5] notes temporal planes $S_{xt}$ capture static content if temporal smoothness prior λ_temp weight too low leading to static-dynamic entanglement visible as ghosting of background under moving object – requires disentanglement orthogonality loss $⟨h_{static},h_{dynamic}⟩$.

6. **Formal verification scale:** TLA+ checked $N=4$ hosts only; parameterized induction to arbitrary tetrahedral subdivision not machine-checked Coq; second-order CUDA kernel not verified Wasm sandbox – race possible under sparse hash atomic add if two threads write same hash cell with differing grad sync – leads 0.3% divergent PSNR across seeds.

7. **Energy and carbon variance:** Tiny-CUDA-NN relies on RTX TF32, portability to AMD ROCm HCC not trivial; Instant-NGP dynamic sparsity occupancy grid atomic contention under high res 128³ slows under heavy DLSS upstream 2.3% beam.

Future roadmap mapped to dissertation chapters:

- ***Hybrid tiling*** merging NerfAcc [7? K-Planes nerfacc integration] with NeuS2 hash to cap samples 48 average with early-termination transmittance >0.999.
- ***Single-shot segmentation aware*** DMTet extended texturing via MaterialSH disentangling albedo vs irradiance.
