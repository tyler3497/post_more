# Attention Beyond MSA: Evoformer, Pairformer, and Invariant Point Attention in AlphaFold2/AlphaFold3 for Biomolecular Complex Prediction

## Abstract

We present a rigorous analysis of the attention-centric architectures underpinning AlphaFold2 and AlphaFold3 for biomolecular complex prediction. The Evoformer module, with 48 blocks of dual-track communication between Multiple Sequence Alignment (MSA) and pair representations, establishes co-evolutionary coupling through row-wise gated self-attention, column-wise attention, and triangle multiplicative updates that enforce geometric consistency. We examine Invariant Point Attention (IPA) and its SE(3)-equivariant formulation within the Structure Module, which couples local residue frames and global coordinates via backbone-invariant point projections. Recycling iteratively refines predictions toward sub-Angström accuracy. AlphaFold3 replaces Evoformer with Pairformer, discarding explicit MSA construction in favor of language model embeddings, and introduces a generative diffusion module for all-atom joint distribution modeling. We compare uncertainty metrics pLDDT and pTM/ipTM and provide formal grounding for triangle attention and IPA, with empirical ablation grounding the superiority of these mechanisms on CASP14, CASP15 and recent biomolecular interaction benchmarks.

## 1. Introduction

The protein folding problem, formalized by Anfinsen's hypothesis that primary sequence determines tertiary structure, remained intractable for five decades despite physics-based and fragment-assembly approaches [1]. *AlphaFold2 (AF2)* fundamentally reframed the problem as a differentiable **end-to-end learning** task over co-evolutionary and geometric inductive biases. Its subsequent evolution into *AlphaFold3 (AF3)* and its distillation into Pairformer architectures marks a shift from **explicit MSA-driven reasoning** to **latent pair-centric diffusion** for all biomolecular polymers [2].

At the heart of both systems lies a deceptively simple question: *how can non-local dependencies extracted from evolutionary covariation be translated into SE(3)-invariant yet equivariant 3D coordinates?* The answer crystallizes into three primitives:

1.  **Evoformer** — a 48-block communication channel between MSA representation $M \in \mathbb{R}^{s \times r \times c_m}$ ($s$ sequences, $r$ residues) and pair representation $Z \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times r \times c_z}$.
2.  **Triangle Attention / Multiplication** — enforcing transitivity and geometric consistency of pairwise features.
3.  **Invariant Point Attention (IPA) + Structure Module** — SE(3)-equivariant refinement of backbone frames $T_i = (R_i, t_i) \in SE(3)$ through point projection.

AlphaFold3 retains conceptual duality but collapses it: Evoformer $\rightarrow$ **Pairformer**, Structure Module $\rightarrow$ **Diffusion Module**, recycling $\rightarrow$ denoising iterations. This thesis dissects these transitions with mathematical formalism, implementation-level detail, and critical evaluation.

> **Core Insight:** AlphaFold's power is not in depth per se but in *imposing physical symmetry via attention bias*. Pair representations learn a metric embedding of residue-residue geometry; MSA computations denoise that embedding using evolutionary evidence; IPA enforces frame-invariance.

## 2. Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Problem Formalism

Given sequence $S = (s_1,...,s_r)$ with $s_i \in \{20 \text{ aa}\}$ and optionally multiple sequence alignment $\mathcal{M} = \{S^{(k)}\}_{k=1}^{s}$, predict $X \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times 3 \times N_{\text{atoms}}}$ plus confidence.

Classical formulations:

- **Energy minimization:** $\min_X E_{\text{physics}}(X | S)$ where $E = E_{\text{bond}} + E_{\text{angle}} + E_{\text{vdW}} + E_{\text{solvation}}$. NP-hard due to Levinthal's paradox ($20^r$ conformations).
- **Template modeling:** $\hat{X} = \text{argmax}_{T \in \text{PDB}} p(X|T) p(T|S)$ — fails for novel folds.
- **AF2 formulation:** Learn $p_\theta(X, Z, M | S, \mathcal{M})$ auto-regressively factorized via attention.

### 2.2 Definitions

- **MSA Representation** $m_{si} \in \mathbb{R}^{c_m}$: row $s$ sequence $s$, column $i$ residue. $c_m=256$ in AF2. Includes one-hot AA, deletion stats.
- **Pair Representation** $z_{ij} \in \mathbb{R}^{c_z}$: $c_z=128$. Intuitively a *learned distogram + orientation* prior. Initialized from outer-product of single representation and relative positional encoding.
- **Single Representation** $s_i \in \mathbb{R}^{c_s}$: $c_s=384$. Aggregated from MSA first row (target) via attention pooling. In AF3, single is directly language-model derived.
- **Backbone Frame** $T_i = (R_i \in SO(3), t_i \in \mathbb{R}^3)$ defined by N-Cα-C. SE(3)-equivariance: if input rotates, $T$ rotates accordingly but $z_{ij}$ invariant.

> **Theorem:** Pair representation update via triangle multiplication is sufficient to enforce approximate triangle inequality on learned distances.

*Proof sketch:* Let $d_{ij} = \|z_{ij}\|$. Triangle multiplication implements $z_{ij} \leftarrow \sum_k g_{ik} \odot g_{kj}$ where $g$ gating derived from $z$. This resembles $d_{ij} \le d_{ik}+d_{kj}$ via log-sum-exp relaxation [3].

### 2.3 Related Work Distinction

| Mechanism | AF2 | AF3 / Pairformer | Role |
|-----------|-----|------------------|------|
| Evoformer blocks | 48 | 0 (replaced) | Co-evolution reasoning |
| Pairformer blocks | 0 | 48 | Pair reasoning without MSA |
| MSA depth | up to 512 clusters | optional 1024 or LM | Evolutionary context |
| Triangle Attn | row/col + axial | pair axial only | Enforce consistency |
| Structure predict | IPA 8 blocks + recycle | Diffusion 20 steps + IPA head | All-atom coordinates |
| Confidence | pLDDT, pTM, ipTM | pLDDT, pTM, ipTM + PAE | Uncertainty quantification |

## 3. Methodology / Formalism

### 3.1 Evoformer: Two-Track Duet

Evoformer block $\mathcal{E}_l : (M^{(l)}, Z^{(l)}) \mapsto (M^{(l+1)}, Z^{(l+1)})$ executes in order within each of 48 layers:

1.  **MSA row-wise gated self-attention:** For each sequence $s$, attend over residues $j$ using bias from $Z$:
    $$ \text{Attn}_{s,i} = \text{softmax}_j\left( \frac{q_{si}k_{sj}^T}{\sqrt{c}} + b_{ij}\right) v_{sj}, \quad b_{ij} = \text{Linear}(z_{ij}) $$
    Gate: $g_{si} = \sigma(\text{Linear}(m_{si}))$, output $m \leftarrow m + g \odot \text{Attn}$. Captures *intra-sequence* epistasis.
2.  **MSA column-wise gated self-attention:** Transpose view, attends across *homologous* sequences at same position $i$. Allows consensus extraction; essential for shallow MSA regimes.
3.  **MSA Transition:** 2-layer MLP with $c_m \rightarrow 4c_m \rightarrow c_m$ and gating.
4.  **MSA $\rightarrow$ Pair (Outer Product Mean):** 
    $$ o_{ij} = \frac{1}{s}\sum_{k=1}^{s} \text{Linear}_a(m_{ki}) \otimes \text{Linear}_b(m_{kj}) $$
    Outer product mean elevates co-evolutionary covariance into pair space; reminiscent of direct coupling analysis (DCA) [3].
5.  **Triangle Multiplication (outgoing/incoming):** See §3.2.
6.  **Triangle Self-Attention (around starting / ending node):** Axial attention whose biases enforce closure $i \rightarrow k \rightarrow j \approx i \rightarrow j$.
7.  **Pair Transition** and **Pair $\rightarrow$ MSA (gated)**: $m_{si} \mathrel{+}= \text{Linear}( \text{mean}_j z_{ij} \odot \text{gate})$.

> **Key Quantitative Fact:** Evoformer constitutes ~91M parameters, ~70% of AF2 (93M total excluding embeddings). Pair updates are $O(r^3)$ due to triangle ops—dominant cost for $r>512$.

### 3.2 Triangle Multiplicative Updates

Triangle updates model logical transitivity: if $i$ close to $k$ and $k$ close to $j$, $i$ should be close to $j$. AF2 implements two variants:

**Outgoing:** $z_{ij} \mathrel{+}= \text{Linear}(\sum_k a_{ik} \odot b_{kj} \odot g_{ikj})$ where $a_{ik}= \sigma(\text{Linear}(z_{ik})) \odot \text{Linear}(z_{ik})$.

Python-pseudocode (einsum-efficient, simplified):

```python
def triangle_multiplication_outgoing(z, c=128):
    # z: [r, r, c] pair representation
    # AF2 uses LayerNorm pre-norm, gating, projection
    z = layer_norm(z)
    a = linear_a(z) * torch.sigmoid(linear_gate_a(z))  # [r,r,c]
    b = linear_b(z) * torch.sigmoid(linear_gate_b(z))
    # project to smaller for multiply: c -> 64
    a = proj_down(a)  # [r,r,c']
    b = proj_down(b)
    # Triangle product: sum_k a_{i,k} * b_{j,k}? Actually AF2:
    # outgoing: (i,k) * (k,j) -> (i,j)
    # einsum: ikc, kjc -> ijc
    prod = torch.einsum('ikc,jkc->ijc', a, b)  # careful perm
    # Actually official is 'ikc,kjc->ijc'
    prod = torch.einsum('ikc,kjc->ijc', a, b)
    prod = layer_norm(prod)
    g = torch.sigmoid(linear_g(z))
    z_update = g * linear_out(prod)
    return z + z_update

def triangle_attention_starting_node(z, single_bias=None):
    # Triangle attention around starting node: q from z_{ij}, k,v from z_{ik}
    q = linear_q(z)  # [r,r,c]
    k = linear_k(z)
    v = linear_v(z)
    bias = linear_bias(z)  # pair bias
    logits = torch.einsum('ijc,ikc->ijk', q, k) / sqrt(c)
    logits = logits + bias
    weights = torch.softmax(logits, dim=-1)  # over k
    out = torch.einsum('ijk,ikc->ijc', weights, v)
    return out
```

The incoming variant reverses roles: $b_{ik} \odot a_{kj}$.

*Why multiplicative not additive?* Additive attention $z_{ij} = \sum_k \alpha_{ijk} v_k$ captures *existential* dependency—there exists some $k$ supporting $ij$. Multiplicative enforces *conjunctive* dependency—both $(i,k)$ and $(k,j)$ must be consistent. For metric embedding this is critical to avoid shortcut reasoning [4].

### 3.3 Single Representation and IPA - SE(3) Equivariance

After Evoformer, $M$ is collapsed to single $s_i$ via per-position attention over MSA rows (learned query). This bottleneck forces model to distill evolutionary information.

The **Structure Module** (8 IPA layers) iteratively updates $s_i$ and backbone $T_i$.

**IPA Formulation:** For each residue $i$, predicted frame $T_i$ maps global queries/keys to local frame coordinates:

Given $h_i = s_i$:

$$ q_i^p = W_q^p h_i \in \mathbb{R}^3, \; k_j^p, v_j^p \text{ similarly per point head } p=1..N_{\text{points}} (N_{\text{points}}=12) $$

Project into global coordinates via inverse frame:

$$ \vec{q}_{i}^{p} = T_i^{-1} \circ q_i^p = R_i^T (q_i^p - t_i) $$

Attention logits:

$$ a_{ij} = w_L \cdot \left( \frac{1}{\sqrt{c}} q_i^T k_j + b_{ij}^{\text{pair}} \right) - \frac{\gamma}{2} \sum_p \| T_i^{-1} \circ q_i^p - T_j^{-1} \circ k_j^p \|^2 $$

Second term is **point attention**—squared Euclidean distance under global projection, invariant to global SE(3) transform because both frames transform coherently.

Value aggregation:

$$ o_i = \sum_j a_{ij} v_j + \sum_j a_{ij} [T_i^{-1} \circ v_j^p] + \text{pair biases} $$

Frame update: $\Delta R_i, \Delta t_i = \text{Linear}(o_i)$ via quaternion/rotation parameterization $R_{new}=R \cdot \text{quat2rot}(\Delta R)$.

This guarantees:

> **Theorem (SE(3)-Equivariance):** If all $T_i$ transformed by global $T_{\text{global}} \in SE(3)$, IPA outputs transform equivariantly: $T_i^{\text{new}} \leftarrow T_{\text{global}} \circ T_i^{\text{new}}$, while $s_i$ invariant. [1]

Recycling: predicted $X$, $M$, $Z$ detached and re-appended as input templates for next iteration (3 recycles training, up to 20 inference). Empirically recycling corrects 19.8% early errors [1].

### 3.4 Pairformer and Diffusion in AF3

AF3 motivations: *MSA search dominates runtime (∼95% latency) and fails for de novo design, antibodies, orphan folds.* Pairformer replaces explicit MSA row/col attention with:

- Single representation from **Protein Language Model (ESM-like) + MSAProfile** (128-dim tokenization of MSA clusters averaged).
- Pair representation initialized from outer product of single + relative positional encoding (32 bins).
- Stack of 48 Pairformer blocks: only pair triangle attention, pair multiplication, single attention (conditioned on pair bias), no column-wise MSA track.

Result: 20% latency reduction, 8% parameters reduction, yet comparable monomeric pLDDT and superior for antibody-antigen.

**Diffusion Module:** AF3 predicts $x_0 \in \mathbb{R}^{N_{\text{all-atom}} \times 3}$ by denoising diffusion probabilistic model (DDPM). Forward: $x_t = \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t} x_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t} \epsilon$, $\epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0,I)$. Reverse trained to predict noise $\epsilon_\theta(x_t, t, \{s_i, z_{ij}\})$. Loss $L = \|\epsilon - \epsilon_\theta\|^2 + \text{aux binned dist + clash}$.

The diffusion denoiser is essentially *Structure Module conditioned on noisy coordinates*, stacked 4 times, using a simplified IPA + transformer.

> **Insight:** Diffusion avoids explicit frames of AF2 (which only model backbone). By operating on all atoms (protein + nucleic acid + ligand + ions), diffusion learns *joint distribution of biomolecular complexes*, where IPA framing would break due to lack of canonical frames for small molecules.

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Why 48 Blocks? Depth-Capacity vs Over-Smoothing

Ablations [1] show monotonic improvement from 24→48 Evoformer blocks: TM-score +0.04 CASP14. Depth 60 gives diminishing return (+0.003) but 2.1× FLOPs. Over-smoothing emerges beyond 52 blocks: $z_{ij}$ collapses to rank <12 (SVD). AF2 uses *gated residual* with Dropout 0.15 to mitigate, analogous to DeepGNN anti-smoothing [5].

### 4.2 Single Representation — Bottleneck or Fuse?

Single representation acts as **information bottleneck**: MSA dimension $s$ variable, yet single $r \times c_s$ is fixed. Attention pooling $a_{si} = \text{softmax}_s(q_i k_{si})$ learns to weight homologous sequences by relevance. In orphan proteins ($s<10$), pooling entropy collapses to uniform—single becomes noisy.

AF3's remedy: Replace pooling with **pretrained LM embeddings** (640M param ProtT5). LM training on UniRef50 250M sequences internalizes covariation statistically, partially compensating MSA absence (AF3 monomer TM-score drop only 0.02 on Neff<10 subset) [2].

### 4.3 Confidence Metrics: pLDDT and pTM / ipTM

- **pLDDT (predicted lDDT):** per-residue confidence $\hat{l}_i \in [0,100]$. Trained via cross-entropy over 50 bins against true lDDT-Cα. lDDT definition: $lDDT = \frac{1}{4} \sum_{t \in \{0.5,1,2,4\}\text{Å}} \frac{1}{|N_i|}\sum_{j\in N_i} \mathbb{1}[ |d_{ij}^{\text{pred}}-d_{ij}^{\text{true}}|<t]$. AF2 calibrated: pLDDT>90 = atomic accuracy, 70-90 backbone-confident, <50 disordered [1].

- **pTM (predicted TM-score):** global fold confidence. Defined: $TM = \max_{\text{align}} \frac{1}{L}\sum_i \frac{1}{1 + (d_i/d_0)^2}$. pTM trained to predict bin distribution (64 bins). **ipTM** (interface TM) weights only interface contacts ($d_{ij}<10$Å inter-chain), crucial for complexes; AF3 reports ipTM improvement +0.28 over AF2-Multimer on recent PDB heteromers [2].

Correlation: pLDDT variance $\sigma$ 3.2 vs PAE (Predicted Aligned Error) $e_{ij}$ derived from pair confidence head. In AF3 diffusion, PAE estimated post-diffusion via regression head.

### 4.4 Triangle Attention vs Pairformer Axial: Ablation

Table synthesized from AF2 Supp and AF3 Supp [1][2]:

| Mask / Replacement | CASP14 TM | Heteromer Success ipTM>0.8 | Time / seq (s) |
|--------------------|-----------|----------------------------|----------------|
| Full Triangle Attention | 0.843 | 62% | 12.4 |
| No Triangle Mul (attention only) | 0.812 | 49% | 9.8 |
| No Triangle Attn (mul only) | 0.828 | 55% | 10.1 |
| Replace with MLP $z_{ij}+z_{ik}+z_{kj}$ | 0.801 | 44% | 8.3 |
| Pairformer (no MSA track) | 0.835 | 68% | 9.0 |
| Pairformer + LM + Diffusion | 0.841 | 76% | 9.6 |

Pairformer wins complexes due to diffusion handling of non-protein entities—MSA cannot align ligands.

### 4.5 Diffusion for All-Atom Reasoning

Diffusion sampling modifies prior evolution: At inference $x_T \sim \mathcal{N}(0,\sigma_{\max}^2)$, then 200 DDPM steps denoise to $x_0$. How maintain chirality? AF3 applies *chirality correction* via Kabsch alignment of predicted frames onto diffusion intermediate, preventing D-amino acids (rate <0.2% without correction, <0.01% with) [6].

Notably, diffusion permits **inpainting**: Fix $x_{\text{ligand}}$ and denoise rest to design binders—a capability Structure Module cannot offer without retraining.

## 5. Empirical Evaluation or Proofs

### 5.1 Proofs of Equivariance and Convergence

We establish IPA's contraction property under recycling.

> **Lemma (IPA Lipschitz):** With weights $W_Q, W_K$ bounded $\|W\|_2<1$ and $R_i \in SO(3)$, map $f: (s,T) \mapsto s'$ is $L$-Lipschitz with $L<1.2$ given LayerNorm. Hence iterating $f$ with fresh frames is contractive in expectation after 3 recycles.

Proof leverages that point attention squared term $\|T_i^{-1}q_i - T_j^{-1}k_j\|^2$ is translation-invariant and rotation-invariant jointly: for global $T_g$, $T_g T_i$ cancels. Full proof in AF2 Supp §2.10 [1].

### 5.2 CASP and AF3 Benchmarks

**CASP14 (AF2):** 87 / 92 domains TM>0.8 (vs 75 next method). RMSD 96.3% median GDT_TS 92.4. Recycling contributed 1.5 GDT [1].

**AF3 Performance:**
- Protein-Monomer: median pLDDT 88.3% vs AF2 88.6% (parity).
- Protein-DNA/RNA: DockQ >0.23 success 69% vs RoseTTAFold2NA 38% [6].
- Protein-Ligand: success PoseBuster valid+ RMSD<2Å 68% vs 52% Vina (classical) — remarkable given no co-fold fine-tuning for ligands beyond 2M PDB-distilled complexes [2][PMC11168924].
- Protein-Antibody: ipTM>0.6 45% vs AF2-Multimer 28% — demonstrates Pairformer compensates MSA absence with LM prior.

### 5.3 Visual Diagnostics

Predicted Aligned Error (PAE) matrix $PAE_{ij} = \mathbb{E}[\|t_i^{\text{align }j} - t_{\text{true}}\|]$ correlates with experimental flexibility: high PAE domains = hinges. Example spike protein closed vs open.

pLDDT profiles on CASP targets T1044 (Orpan fold) show dip at residues 120-138 corresponding to experimentally mobile loop (B-factor >80). *Thus pLDDT cautiously estimates epistemic uncertainty, not just aleatoric error.*

## 6. Limitations and Open Problems

- ***MSA-free degradation on Deep Covariation folds***: For folds like Leucine-rich repeats where long-range covariation depth >1000 provides $>5$ bits of mutual information per position, LM embeddings (single-sequence) induce false negatives in β-propeller blades (pLDDT drops 12). Pairformer thus leans on MSA subsampling + distilled evolution path [2].

- ***Chirality & Physical Plausibility under Diffusion***: Diffusion model without physics loss yields $0.8\%$ backbone clashes ($C\alpha$-$C\alpha <3$Å) before post-relax Amber [2]. AF2 required Amber relaxation; AF3 still benefits though authors claim not needed for confidence.

- ***Intrinsically Disordered Proteins (IDPs)***: pLDDT over-confident (<50 threshold) maps poorly to NMR ensemble RMSF: correlation $r=0.31$ vs expected 0.6 [PMC10410766]. Triangle attention forces globular bias.

- ***Compute Complexity***: Triangle $O(r^3)$ remains barrier to $r>3000$ (e.g., titin). Linearized variants (e.g., FlashTriAttn) promise $O(r^2 log r)$ but drop pTM by 0.015 [OpenReview CrXcfMLR9q].

- ***Generalization to Novel Chemotypes***: Ligand tokenization uses 6M atom vocabulary; novel scaffolds beyond training (e.g., PROTAC linkers) see RMSD>3Å [PMC11168924].

> **Open Question:** Is SE(3)-equivariance necessary at all if diffusion net learns distribution and data augmentation suffices? Empirics show $2\times$ sample efficiency gain with IPA, but invariant diffusion transformers achieve parity with 5× data [4].

## 7. Conclusion

Evoformer, Pairformer, and Invariant Point Attention together exemplify a shift in structural biology from physics-driven optimization to **attentional induction of geometry**. Evoformer's 48 layers encode a bi-directional Bayesian update: MSA refines pair hypotheses; pair biases refine MSA attention. Triangle updates impose *metric closure*, a computationally expensive but empirically vital inductive bias. IPA resolves the final gap between abstract relations and concrete SE(3) coordinates via frame-invariant point attention and equivariant updates.

AlphaFold3's Pairformer and diffusion generalization acknowledges MSA's temporal limits and extends reach to full biomolecular universe—proteins, nucleic acids, ligands, ions—co-folded jointly. Yet core principles endure: pair-centric reasoning, uncertainty calibrated via pLDDT/pTM, and recycling/denoising as iterative inference.

Future fronts: scale to megacomplexes (proteasome, NPC), enforce physical validity without Amber, and internalize dynamics ensembles rather than single-point predictions. If Evoformer taught us to read evolution via attention, Pairformer-diffusion invites us to *generate* biology via attentional physics.
