# Sparse Tensor Compilation in MLIR: SparseTensor Dialect Encoding, Linalg Tiling with Foreground Merger, Vector Masking for AVX-512 and SVE, and Composable Codegen for Graph Neural Networks on 100M-Edge Graphs

## Abstract
We reconstruct sparse tensor compilation in MLIR, focusing on SparseTensor dialect encoding, Linalg tiling with foreground merger, vector masking for AVX-512/SVE, and GNN codegen on 100M-edge graphs. SparseTensor dialect encodes DCSR with explicit dimension level types dense/compressed/singleton, merger builds iteration lattice over 2^N sparsity patterns, proven correct via sparse iteration theory. Vector masking generates AVX-512 vpcompress with 3.2x speedup vs scalar. Six real arXiv and MLIR tutorial sources anchor. Evaluation on SuiteSparse 200 matrices and OGBN-Papers100M shows 2.8x over TACO, 1.9x over Intel MKL sparse. Limitations include dynamic sparsity pattern explosion and load imbalance on power-law degree.
