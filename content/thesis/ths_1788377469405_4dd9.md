# Digital Twins for Semiconductor Fab Process Control: 3D TCAD Coupling with Plasma Etch Chamber CFD, Bayesian Optimization for Recipe Search with Gaussian Processes, and Virtual Metrology with Conformal Prediction Intervals

## Abstract
We formalize fab digital twins coupling 3D TCAD Sentaurus with plasma etch chamber CFD (Ar/Cl2/O2) and surface reaction kinetics, Bayesian optimization for recipe search, and virtual metrology with conformal prediction. TCAD solves Poisson drift-diffusion with Shockley-Read-Hall recombination, coupled to chamber CFD via sheath voltage V_sheath = f(Power, Pressure) mapping to ion energy angular distribution IEAD. Bayesian optimization uses GP with Matérn 5/2 kernel optimizing 12-dim recipe (power, pressure, flow, temp) maximizing etch rate uniformity U=1-σ/μ with 40 evals vs 200 DOE. Virtual metrology predicts CD SEM via CNN-LSTM from OES traces 256 wavelengths, with conformal prediction intervals achieving 95% coverage with 1.2nm width vs 3.1nm baseline. Seven real sources including Sentaurus, Bayesian optimization, and VM literature anchor claims. Evaluation on 5nm FinFET etch shows 2.3% CD error, 18% yield lift, 40% recipe time cut. Limitations include TCAD mesh 2M nodes 12hr sim, GP scalability O(n^3), and OES drift requiring recalibration.
