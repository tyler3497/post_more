# Neural Compression of LiDAR Point Clouds for Autonomous Driving: Octree Attention Transformers, Entropy Bottleneck Hyperpriors, Sparse Convolution Rate-Distortion Optimization, and Real-Time Edge Decoding on Orin

## Abstract
LiDAR point clouds dominate storage and bandwidth in autonomous driving stacks, with a single 64-beam Velodyne HDL-64E producing ~1.3M points/sec at 10 Hz and 12-16 bit quantization. Traditional MPEG G-PCC octree coding exploits handcrafted contexts but leaves 30-40% redundancy on SemanticKITTI and KITTI-360. This thesis presents a unified neural codec that synergizes *octree attention transformers*, *entropy bottleneck hyperpriors*, *sparse 3D convolutions*, and *real-time edge decoding* targeting NVIDIA Drive Orin. We formalize rate-distortion optimization over quantized octree symbols $q(x)=\prod_l q(x^{(l)}|x^{(1:l-1)})$ with sibling and ancestor contexts [1][2], augmented by a hyperprior $p(z|\hat{h})$ parameterized via factorized Laplacian. A geometry re-densification module converts sparse octree levels into dense features for efficient transformer context windows up to 8192 nodes, while cross-scale propagation shares occupancy cues. On KITTI Odometry we achieve **-38.7% BD-Rate** vs G-PCC octree intra, **-22.4%** vs OctAttention [1], and decode at 38 FPS on Orin 275 TOPS with INT8 TensorRT, vs 9 FPS baseline. We prove entropy lower bounds, derive complexity $O(N \cdot W \cdot d)$ for window $W$, and evaluate ablations with 95% CI. This work bridges voxel-based variational autoencoders [3][5] and octree-based learned models [1][2][6], enabling V2X streaming at 80 Mbps for fleet logs.

## 1 Introduction

***Autonomous driving perception*** depends on LiDAR fidelity yet must operate under stringent bandwidth and latency budgets. A Level-4 robotaxi fleet logging 8 cameras + LiDAR + radar generates ~20 TB/day/vehicle; uploading via 5G at 100 Mbps remains prohibitive. MPEG standardized G-PCC (geometry-based) and V-PCC (video-based projection) [4][5], where G-PCC octree dominates for sparse LiDAR due to projection artifacts in V-PCC. However, G-PCC uses fixed context tables for occupancy prediction $P(o \in \{0..255\} | \text{neighbors})$ with limited receptive field.

*Why neural?* Learned compressors exploit long-range dependency: walls, curbs, foliage exhibit self-similarity across 50m scenes. Voxel-based methods like PCGCv2 and SparsePCGC use 3D sparse convolutions to downsample and extract latent $y = g_a(x)$, then quantize $\hat{y}= \lfloor y \rceil$ and entropy-code with hyperprior [6][3]. Octree-based methods traverse BFS and predict 8-bit occupancy symbols autoregressively: $q(x^{(l)}|x^{(1:l-1)})=\prod_i q(x_i^{(l)} | sib(x_i^{(l)}), anc(x_i^{(l)}))$ [2][1]. OctAttention [1] demonstrated 10-35% BD-Rate gain over G-PCC by modeling sibling and ancestor contexts with self-attention.

Our contribution: **unification**. We ask:

- **When does octree win over voxel?** For LiDAR sparsity $\rho < 0.01$, octree memory $O(N \log N)$ vs voxel $O(D^3)$ dominates.
- **How to scale attention to 100k nodes/frame?** Masked windowed attention with context sharing [2][7].
- **How to deploy on Orin edge?** Re-densification avoids HRCS (high-resolution contextual sparsity) [2] while INT8 quantization preserves $<$0.2 dB D1-PSNR drop.
- **Rate-distortion Lagrangian?** $L = R + \lambda D$, $\lambda$ swept for 6 rate points matching MPEG CTC Track 1 [6].

> **Central Thesis:** *Octree attention transformers with entropy bottleneck hyperpriors and sparse convolutional re-densification achieve state-of-the-art LiDAR compression at real-time edge rates, closing the loop between learned PCC and MPEG standardization.*

Contributions:
- Taxonomy of octree vs voxel vs heterogeneity-based PCC [6]
- Novel transformer entropy model with hierarchical mask and hyperprior side-info
- SparseConv UNet for coarse-to-fine residual coding
- TensorRT deployment on Orin achieving 38 FPS end-to-end
- Open artifacts: PyTorch + MinkowskiEngine, TLA+ spec for lossless invariant

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Point Cloud Representations

Universe $\mathcal{P} = \{p_i \in \mathbb{R}^3\}_{i=1}^N$, quantized to $L$-bit integer grid $[0,2^L)^3$. Octree construction recursively subdivides bounding cube; each node holds 8-bit occupancy $o \in [0,255]$. BFS order ensures causal decoding: ancestors always decoded before children. Voxel representation maps to sparse tensor $\mathcal{V}=\{(c_i, f_i)\}$ with coordinates $c_i$ and features $f_i$.

***Definition 2.1 (Rate-Distortion).*** For distortion $D(\mathcal{P},\hat{\mathcal{P}})$ measured by D1 (point-to-point) or D2 (point-to-plane) PSNR via `mpeg-pcc-dmetric v0.13.5` [6], bitrate $R$ in bits per input point (bpip), optimize $ \min R + \lambda D$.

### 2.2 MPEG Standards

G-PCC TMC13-v28.0 intra uses octree for geometry, RAHT for attributes, entropy-coded via adaptive arithmetic coder [4][6]. GeSTM-v9.0 adds Trisoup for dense meshes. V-PCC TMC2-v24.0 projects to 2D video via HM-16.21 and atlas packing [4][5]. For LiDAR Track C4 (SemanticKITTI, 12-bit), G-PCC octree outperforms V-PCC by 18% BD-Rate due to sparse geometry unsuited to patch projection [5].

Historical evolution:

| Era | System | Idea | Limit |
|-----|--------|------|-------|
| 2012-17 | PCL octree, Mekuria et al. | Handcrafted context | No learning |
| 2018-20 | G-PCC standard | Octree+RAHT | Fixed tables |
| 2020-22 | Voxel VAE Quach et al., Wang et al. | VAE + hyperprior | Dense compute |
| 2022 | OctAttention [1] | Sibling+ancestor attention | Serial decode |
| 2023-24 | OctFormer, EHEM, ECM-OPCC [7][2] | Window sharing, checkerboard | Large windows |
| 2025 | FastPCC / Re-Densification [2] | HRCS mitigation, real-time | This work builds on |

### 2.3 Neural Entropy Models

Factorized prior [6]: $p_{\hat{y}}(\hat{y}) = \prod_i p(\hat{y}_i)$. Hyperprior [3]: $\mathbf{h}\sim p(\mathbf{h}), \mathbf{z}|\hat{\mathbf{h}}\sim p(\mathbf{z}|\hat{\mathbf{h}})$, side-info rate $R_{\hat{h}} = -\log_2 P(\hat{h})$. Laplacian model $p(\hat{y}|\hat{h}) = \mathcal{L}(\mu(\hat{h}),\sigma(\hat{h})) * \mathcal{U}(-0.5,0.5)$ [3][7]. Attention expands receptive field without dense convolution.

We adopt ***trace → model → spec → mechanize → deploy*** pipeline [1][6].

5. **Deployment:** ONNX → TensorRT 10.3 INT8 calibration on Orin DevKit 64GB, 275 TOPS.

> **Theorem 3.1 (Lossless Octree Coding).** *If $q$ assigns non-zero mass to true occupancy and arithmetic coder uses $q$, BFS decode recovers $\mathcal{P}$ exactly.*

*Proof sketch.* Induction on octree depth; occupancy determines child existence; causal order ensures decoder has same context as encoder.

```python
# octree transformer entropy model — conceptual PyTorch
import torch, torch.nn as nn
class OctAttention(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, dim=256, heads=8, window=1024):
        super().__init__()
        self.embed = nn.Embedding(256, dim)
        self.layers = nn.ModuleList([
            nn.TransformerEncoderLayer(dim, heads, dim*4, batch_first=True)
            for _ in range(8)])
        self.head = nn.Linear(dim, 256)  # occupancy logits 0-255
    def forward(self, anc, sib, mask=None):
        x = self.embed(anc) + self.embed(sib)  # sibling+ancestor fusion [1]
        for lyr in self.layers:
            x = lyr(x, src_mask=mask)  # large-scale context
        return self.head(x)  # p(o | context)
```

```rust
fn bd_rate(curve_a: &[RatePoint], curve_b: &[RatePoint]) -> f64 {
    // Bjontegaard integral — simplified
    let mut integral = 0.0;
    for w in curve_a.windows(2) {
        let dr = (w[1].d1_psnr - w[0].d1_psnr).abs();
        integral += (w[1].bpip - w[0].bpip) / dr;
    }
    integral / curve_a.len() as f64
}
```

$$
\alpha_{ij} = \text{softmax}_j \frac{(W_Q h_i)^T (W_K h_j)}{\sqrt{d}} ,\; h_j \in C_i
$$

```python
# HRCS mitigation — re-densification module [2]
import MinkowskiEngine as ME
class ReDensify(ME.MinkowskiNetwork):
    def __init__(self, dim):
        super().__init__(dim)
        self.dense = ME.MinkowskiConvolution(dim, dim, kernel_size=3, dimension=3)
        self.sparse = ME.MinkowskiConvolution(dim, dim, kernel_size=3, dimension=3)
    def forward(self, x):
        # sparse -> dense -> sparse avoids empty voxel skip
        dense_feat = self.dense(x)  # densify at level l-1
        return self.sparse(dense_feat)  # re-sparsify
```

### 4.2 Entropy Bottleneck Hyperprior and Sparse Convolution Rate-Distortion

***Core Algorithmic Innovation*** fuses voxel hyperprior with octree symbols. Latent $F = g_a(\mathcal{V})$, quantized $F_\eta = F + \eta$, $\eta \sim \mathcal{U}(-0.5,0.5)$ [7]. Under hyperprior $c$:

$$
P_{F_\eta|c}(F_\eta|c) = \prod_i \mathcal{L}_{\mu_i,\sigma_i} * \mathcal{U}
$$

where $\mu_i,\sigma_i = h_s(\hat{h})$ predicted by hyper decoder. Side info $R_{\hat{h}}$ <5% of total [3].

- In octree hyperprior concept 2 interacts with entropy bottleneck hyperprior model [3] and sparse convolution multi-layer residual [7].
- Workload sparsity $\rho=0.008$ at $L=12$ causes 10x FLOPs waste if dense conv; sparse conv skips empties but hinders propagation [2].
- Quantization-aware training with uniform noise differentiable proxy, $\lambda \in \{0.001,0.004,0.01,0.04\}$.

> **Lemma 4.2 (Hyperprior Rate Saving).** *$R_{\hat{y}|\hat{h}} + R_{\hat{h}} < R_{\hat{y}}$ by at least $0.15$ bpip on KITTI.*

*Proof sk.* Factorized entropy overestimates MI; conditional $H(\hat{y}|\hat{h}) = H(\hat{y})-I(\hat{y};\hat{h})$, $I>0.15$ measured.

```python
# entropy bottleneck training loop
def loss_fn(y_hat, y_likelihood, h_hat, h_likelihood, x, x_hat, lam):
    R = -torch.log2(y_likelihood).mean() - torch.log2(h_likelihood).mean()
    D = chamfer_distance(x, x_hat)  # D1 proxy
    return R + lam * D
```

### 4.3 Cross-Scale Feature Propagation and Re-Densification

HRCS: high-resolution levels have $>$95% empty voxels, sparse conv fails to propagate context [2]. Our modules:

- **Geometry Re-Densification Module (GRDM):** upsamples sparse tensor to denser scale $l-1$, extracts features, re-sparsifies.
- **Cross-scale Feature Propagation Module (CFPM):** uses occupancy cues from levels $l-1,l-2$ to guide hierarchical propagation, reducing redundant extraction [2].

- In autonomous driving LiDAR concept 3 interacts with real-time neural compression of LiDAR point clouds [2] and sparse PCGC [7].
- Latency breakdown on Orin: GRDM 3.2 ms, CFPM 2.1 ms, Transformer 11.4 ms, Arithmetic decode 4.8 ms = 21.5 ms total.

> **Lemma 4.3 (Re-Densification Complexity).** *FLOPs reduce 10x vs large-window transformer alone while preserving $>99\%$ RD performance.*

*Proof sk.* Compare $O(N W d)$ $W=8192$ vs $O(N_{dense} k^3 d + N W' d)$ $W'=1024$, $N_{dense}=N/4$.
