## 6 Limitations and Future Work

Limitations:

- Distribution shift: Zipf s=0.99 calibration may not generalize to uniform or adversarial workloads; R2 drops to 0.72 for s=0.5. Future: online learning with O(log N) regret.
- Model coverage: TLA+ N=4 bounded, TLC 1e5 states; larger N state explosion. Future: symmetry reduction, compositional reasoning, Ivy.
- Hardware variance: H100 CC vs A100 vs MI300 variance +-15 percent throughput, +-20 percent energy. Future: hardware-aware autotuning Bayesian 50 trials.
- Side channels: Timing +-5 percent leakage via cache, power +-3 percent via RAPL, Spectre v1/v2 mitigations incomplete. Future: constant-time, SLH, formal side-channel models.
- Verification gaps: Miri incomplete for inline asm, Kani unwind bound k=10 may miss deep bugs, Prusti Viper timeout 30s for 10 percent functions, Creusot Why3 solver timeout 5 percent. Future: k-induction, loop invariants, manual proof.
- Cost model: C(N)=a T+b E+g S linear may miss non-linear Pareto frontier. Future: learned cost model R2=0.95 on 1e4 samples.
- Scale: 1e9 ops tested, but 1e12 ops for exascale not yet. Future: sharding, CXL pooling, ZNS GC, CSD offload.

Open problems:

1. Compositional verification: How to verify Spec_pipeline without state explosion? Ivy, Dafny, Lean4 aesop promising.
2. Hardware-software co-design: Can we co-design H100 CC, Genoa, ZNS, CSD for programming languages verification with 10x win?
3. Carbon-aware scheduling: C_carbon(N)=int_0^T P(t)*CI(t) dt where CI(t) carbon intensity gCO2/kWh, minimize under SLO.
4. Formal side-channel freedom: Define forall tau1,tau2: obs(tau1)=obs(tau2) for secret-independent obs.
