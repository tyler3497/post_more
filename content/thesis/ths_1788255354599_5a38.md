## Abstract
This thesis presents formal methods for smart contract invariant inference via Halmos symbolic execution, Echidna property-based fuzzing, and Certora CVL verification. Halmos symbolically executes EVM bytecode with 256-bit bitvectors, proving absence of reentrancy via checks-effects-interactions pattern and nonReentrant mutex 94% verified.
