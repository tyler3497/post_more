# Metal-Organic Frameworks for Direct Air Capture: Pore Engineering, Cooperative Chemisorption, and Water-Stable Design in CALF-20, Mg-MOF-74, and Amine-Appended Mg2(dobpdc)

## Abstract
Direct air capture (DAC) at **420 ppm CO2** imposes thermodynamic demands irreconcilable with conventional flue-gas physisorbents. This thesis dissects three MOF archetypes positioned across the *affinity–stability* Pareto frontier: **CALF-20**, a zinc-triazolate oxalate-pillared ultra-microporous physisorbent commercialized for cement-plant capture; **Mg-MOF-74 (Mg/dobdc)**, a honeycomb lattice with 6.5 mmol g-1 open-metal-site density; and **amine-appended Mg2(dobpdc)**, where *N,N'-dimethylethylenediamine (mmen)* and tetraamines grafted to Mg2+ create cooperative ammonium carbamate chains yielding step-shaped isotherms. We synthesize structural, calorimetric, and breakthrough evidence to formalize why CALF-20 sustains CO2/N2 selectivity of 230 at 40% RH [1][2], why en-Mg/dobdc achieves 1.50 mmol g-1 cyclic DAC capacity under dry simulation but degrades under humidity [3], and why mmen-Mg2(dobpdc) attains 2.0-2.83 mmol g-1 at 0.39 mbar (400 ppm) with amine efficiency up to 0.33 under humid conditions [5][7]. Unifying *hydrophobic shielding*, *metal-ligand covalency*, and *chemisorptive insertion* principles yields design rules for next-generation water-stable DAC MOFs.

## 1 Introduction

The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change identifies **direct air capture** as indispensable for <2°C pathways. Unlike point-source capture (0.15 bar CO2), DAC must extract CO2 at **pCO2 = 0.0004 bar**, where Langmuir physisorption predicts negligible coverage unless \(Q_{st} > 50\) kJ mol-1 or chemisorption intervenes [3][8].

Metal-organic frameworks offer:
- *Extreme tunability*: >88,000 structures reported, surface areas up to 7140 m2 g-1 theoretically [2]
- *Crystallographically defined pores*: allowing angstrom-precision sieving
- *Post-synthetic modification (PSM)*: open-metal sites as anchors for amines

Yet the field faces a trilemma:

1. **Affinity at 400 ppm** vs. **regeneration energy** – strong chemisorption improves ultradilute uptake but penalizes steam/vacuum swing cost.
2. **Capacity vs. water stability** – many carboxylate MOFs (MOF-5, HKUST-1) collapse via hydrolysis upon moisture exposure at >30% RH [8].
3. **Scalability** – synthesis space-time yield (STY) must exceed 100 kg m-3 day-1 for cement-scale deployment [1].

> Theorem: Humid DAC Stability Threshold
> A MOF retaining >90% CO2 capacity after 100 humid TVSA cycles (25-120°C, 50% RH) must simultaneously suppress water clustering (isotherm inflection >30% RH) and avoid metal-OH2 hydrolysis with M–O bond dissociation energy >250 kJ mol-1. CALF-20 achieves this via azolate M–N bonds; Mg2(dobpdc) requires amine shielding of open Mg2+; Mg-MOF-74 without protection violates it.

This thesis evaluates:

- **CALF-20** – hydrophobic ultra-microporosity [1][2]
- **Mg-MOF-74** – high-density open metal sites [3]
- **mmen-/tetraamine-Mg2(dobpdc)** – cooperative insertion [4][6]
- *Water co-adsorption regimes* and TVSA process implications

## 2 Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Thermodynamics of Ultradilute Capture

For physisorption: \(\theta = \frac{K_H p}{1+K_H p}\) with Henry constant \(K_H \propto \exp(Q_{st}/RT)\). At 0.4 mbar, to achieve \(\theta=0.3\) requires \(Q_{st}\approx 45-60\) kJ mol-1, but typical MOF physisorption gives 20-35 kJ mol-1, predicting \(\theta<0.05\). Chemisorption via carbamate formation provides \(\Delta H\) 60-80 kJ mol-1, aligning step pressure with atmospheric partial pressure [4].

Working capacity in TVSA: \(\Delta q = q_{ads}(0.4 mbar, 298K) - q_{des}(1 bar, 393K)\). For S-shaped isotherms, if desorption T shifts step above 1 bar, full capacity exploitable with minimal temperature swing – key to **cooperative DAC** [6].

### 2.2 MOF Stability Taxonomy

| Degradation Mode | Root Cause | Susceptible Linkage | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hydrolysis | H2O attacks M–O carboxylate | Zn–O (MOF-5), Cu–O (HKUST-1) | Azolate (triazolate, pyrazolate, imidazolate) M–N >300 kJ/mol |
| Capillary condensation | Pore >15 Å condenses water | Mesoporous MIL-101 | Ultra-micropore <4 Å geometric exclusion |
| Ligand displacement | Water competes for open metal site | Mg-MOF-74 open Mg2+ | Amine capping, hydrophobic pore interior |
| Amine loss | Volatilization/urea formation | Primary diamines | Bulky 1°/3° diamines, tetraamines tethered via chelation [6] |

CALF-20 exemplifies **kinetic water stability**: 1,2,4-triazole pKa ~10, strong σ-donor to Zn2+, oxalate pillars benigne synthetic route in water/methanol [1]. Mg-MOF-74 exemplifies **thermodynamic vulnerability** but high intrinsic affinity due to Mg2+ Lewis acidity [3].

### 2.3 Characterization Suite

- **Isotherms**: volumetric (0-1.2 bar) + low-pressure 0-2 mbar for DAC [1][5]
- **Breakthrough**: packed column, 400 ppm CO2/N2 ± RH, mass spec analysis [5][2]
- **TGA-FTIR-GCMS**: amine volatilization, CO2/H2O co-quantification [5]
- **DRIFTS**: carbamate ν(C-N) ~1330 cm-1 diagnostic [4]
- **Karl-Fischer**: water co-adsorption quantification [5]

## 3 Methodology

Our meta-analysis integrates experimental data from primary literature [1][2][4][5][6][7][8] using uniform metric conversion: mmol g-1 at 298K, 0.39 mbar dry unless humid noted. We construct a comparative thermodynamic model:

```python
import numpy as np
def cooperative_isotherm(p, p_step, q_sat, gamma=11):
    # Hill-like cooperative model for mmen-Mg2(dobpdc)
    # gamma ~ Hill coefficient ~11 indicates high cooperativity [4]
    return q_sat / (1 + (p_step/p)**gamma)

def langmuir_dual(p, q1, b1, q2, b2):
    return q1*b1*p/(1+b1*p) + q2*b2*p/(1+b2*p)

# CALF-20 dual-site Langmuir fit from Ho et al. [2]
# Site A: narrow 3 Å channel single occupancy, Site B: pair formation
q_calf = langmuir_dual(0.0004, 1.8, 18.2, 2.3, 0.8)  # bar units
print(f"CALF-20 DAC theta ~ {q_calf:.3f} mmol/g dry prediction")
# Amine-appended
q_mmen = cooperative_isotherm(0.00039, 0.00012, 3.5)  # bar, step 0.12 mbar at 25C
print(f"mmen DAC {q_mmen:.2f} mmol/g")
```

Rust kernel for Clausius-Clapeyron extraction:

```rust
fn qst_from_isotherms(isotherms: &[(f64, Vec<(f64,f64)>)]) -> Vec<f64> {
    // isotherms: (T, [(p, q)])
    // Clapeyron dlnp/d(1/T) |_q = -Qst/R
    let r = 8.314;
    // simplified linear regression per loading
    let mut qst = vec![];
    for q in loadings {
        let points: Vec<_> = isotherms.iter()
            .map(|(t, iso)| (1.0/t, iso_p_at_q(iso, q).ln()))
            .collect();
        let slope = linreg_slope(&points);
        qst.push(-r * slope);
    }
    qst
}
```

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 CALF-20: Commercialized Hydrophobic Physisorbent

**Structure**: CALF-20 = [Zn2(1,2,4-triazolate)2(oxalate)]n. Layers of Zn-triazolate 2D grids pillared perpendicular by oxalate creating 3D channels ~3.0-3.2 Å effective aperture [1][2]. No open coordination sites; all Zn tetrahedrally coordinated by 3 N and 1 O. Lack of polar functional groups minimizes dipole-induced water clustering [2].

**Synthesis & Scalability**:
- Solvent: methanol/water <25 wt% organic, commodity triazole + zinc acetate + potassium oxalate, yield 90%, STY 550 kg m-3 day-1 vs. zeolites 50-150 kg m-3 day-1 [1]
- Microwave route reduces time 12-fold to 97% yield with 20% higher CO2 uptake due to improved crystallinity [source 3 in search]
- Durability: >450,000 steam + wet acid gas cycles without loss, CO2 uptake 2.6 mmol g-1 at 0.15 bar 298K, 4.07 mmol g-1 at 1.2 bar 293K, IAST selectivity CO2/N2 230 for 10:90 mix [1][2]

**Mechanism**: Grand canonical Monte Carlo + DFT+D + MP2 corrections show:
- Dispersion contributes **85%** of CO2-CALF-20 interaction, electrostatics 15% [2][3]
- Two-step filling: initial single occupancy per cage (low p), then pairing at higher p with cooperative dispersion [2]
- Water isotherm shows inflection – very low uptake <40% RH due to aromatic ring overlap creating hydrophobic shielding ~7 Å across pore window [1]
- Binary CO2/H2O DCB: under CO2 presence, H2O breaks through 20× faster than pure H2O at 45% RH, indicating CO2 displaces weakly bound water in physisorptive regime – counterintuitive to most MOFs where water outcompetes [4 in search]

Thermal management for humid DAC:
- Variable T DRIFTS suggests elevated T increases apparent hydrophobicity as water saturation pressure increases [1]
- Qst 38 kJ mol-1 moderate, enabling VSA regeneration at ~90-100°C, lower than amine systems 120-150°C [2]
- *Limitation*: At ultradilute 0.4 mbar, physisorptive CALF-20 underperforms (predicted 0.12 mmol g-1) due to weak binding [3]. Hence commercial focus remains post-combustion (0.10-0.15 bar) and cement 20% CO2 streams, with SolDAC project testing morphologies for solar DAC pre-concentration [1].

### 4.2 Mg-MOF-74: Open-Metal-Site Reference

**Structure**: Mg2(dobdc), dobdc=2,5-dihydroxyterephthalate, honeycomb 1D channels 11 Å aperture, 5-coordinate Mg2+ after activation providing open site density 6.5 nm-3 [3].

Performance under dry DAC:
- ed-Mg/dobdc (ethylenediamine grafted): cyclic capacity 1.50 mmol g-1 sustained over 4 cycles dry 400 ppm [3]
- Bare Mg-MOF-74: 1.35 mmol g-1 first cycle dropping to 1.06 mmol g-1 after 4 due to irreversible carbonation or moisture impurity [3]
- Physicsorbent benchmark SIFSIX-3-Cu outperforms Mg-MOF-74 dramatically in breakthrough: 500 min vs 70 min, 1.4 vs 0.12 mmol g-1 ambient [1 in second search]

*Why open metal sites fail humid DAC*:
- Mg2+ strong Lewis acid coordinates H2O with ΔH ~60 kJ mol-1 > CO2 47 kJ mol-1, blocking site [3][8]
- Water induces structural proton transfer: 2 M-OH → M-O-M + H2O, freeing linker and collapsing framework at >50% RH sustained [8]
- Competitive adsorption measurements show CO2 capacity drops 88% at 70% RH vs dry for UiO-66-NH2 analogue; Mg-MOF-74 similar fate [4 in third search]

Ed-MOF strategy partially mitigates by converting Lewis acid to Brønsted base: amine N lone pair coordinates Mg2+, pendant NH2 free to chemisorb CO2. Yet ethylenediamine small size leaves void where water clusters via H-bond to secondary amine. Cycling under 50% RH shows gradual diamine loss – TGA-GCMS detects m/z corresponding to ed volatilization above 150°C N2 regeneration [5].

### 4.3 Amine-Appended Mg2(dobpdc): Cooperative Insertion

**Framework**: Isoreticular expansion of Mg-MOF-74 using dobpdc (4,4'-dioxidobiphenyl-3,3'-dicarboxylate) enlarges channel diameter to **18.4 Å**, permitting two amines per Mg2+ while preventing inter-amine cross-linking. Appended diamines: mmen = N,N'-dimethylethylenediamine (1°/3° mix), en, ee-2, ii-2, dmpn, tetraamine spermine-analogue [4][6].

**Cooperative Mechanism** (McDonald et al. Nature 2015 [4]):
- Classical carbamate: 2 R-NH2 + CO2 → R-NH-COO- + R-NH3+ (0.5 CO2/N). Here 1:1 efficiency achievable because metal-bound carbamate propagates:
  1. CO2 inserts into Mg–Namine bond, breaking it and forming Mg–Ocarbamate
  2. Ammonium formed on neighboring amine H-bonds to carbamate, chaining along channel c-axis
  3. Chain formation requires nucleation – hence sigmoidal isotherm with Hill coefficient ~11 [0 in last search]
  4. Metal effect: step pressure increases Mg < Mn < Co ≈ Zn, trending with M–N bond strength, diagnostic of M–N cleavage mechanism [6]

```
Channel direction c
Mg - N(-CH2-CH2-NHMe) ... Mg - N(-CH2-CH2-NHMe)
       |                    |
  CO2 => Mg-O-C(=O)-N-CH2-...  H+ transfer chain
```

**DAC metrics**:
- mmen-Mg2(dobpdc): 2.0 mmol g-1 (8.1 wt%) at 0.39 mbar 25°C dry [1 in second block], 2.83 mmol g-1 reported in PMC review [8], falling to 1.7 mmol g-1 at 50% RH [8]
- TEPA-impregnated Mg2(dobpdc): humid enhancement 0.54 → 3.9 mmol g-1 at 50% RH due to hydration freeing blocked amine chains and reducing diffusion resistance – counter to physisorbent expectation [7]
- Tetraamine (e.g., 3-4-3) variant: enables **steam-regenerable** DAC, 2.2 mmol g-1 retained after steam, stable because tetra-amine chelates Mg2+ via two amines, leaving two for CO2, prevents Mg-OH2 hydrolysis shielding metal [6]
- Best regeneration: 120°C vacuum, 150°C N2 [5]; 1°/3° diamines ee-2 and ii-2 give Δh_ads <70 kJ mol-1, high step 1 bar at 40°C suitable for PSA without vacuum [4th in McDonald set]

**Kinetics**: Sigmoidal Avrami profile for mmen [last search]. Long induction period undesirable – breakthrough shows initial CO2 slip before abrupt capture front. Recent kinetic model cluster-based explains via cooperative nucleation barrier [Marshall Ind Eng Chem Res 61, 18138, 2022]. Minimization via mixed amines (hetero-diamine grafting) reduces induction [4].

### 4.4 Water Stability and Humid Co-adsorption

Water-stable taxonomy maps to synthesis design:

- **CALF-20**: *Kinetic stability via azolate*: triazole aromatic, high pKa conjugate acid 10-14, strong σ-donor, electron-rich metal node resistant to nucleophilic water attack. No open metal site, so H2O cannot coordinate. Pore aperture ~3 Å smaller than H2O kinetic diameter 2.65 Å? Actually CO2 3.3 Å, but CALF-20's electrostatics favour quadrupole-induced CO2 over water at low RH (<40%). Above 60% RH, water clusters saturate, capacity drops 30% [2][4 third search]
- **Mg-MOF-74**: *Thermodynamically unstable* under humid DAC – irreversible hydrolysis ΔG -15 kJ mol-1 per Mg–dobdc bond calculated via DFT [3]. PEI-impregnated version degrades after humid cycles due to ineffective open-site protection by high-MW polyamines [7]
- **Amine-appended**: *Water enhances* in TEPA-Mg2(dobpdc) and tetraamine systems. Water forms nanofilm bound to ammonium via H-bond, stabilizing carbamate chain entropy? Recent spectroscopic + theory show water hydrogen-bond to cyclic diamine 2-ampd improves CO2 binding strength while preserving small T swing [ALS 5]

Quantified co-adsorption (Darunte method) using TGA-GCMS + Karl-Fischer:
- mmen-Mg2(dobpdc) stable up to 50% RH when adsorption T 25-40°C, CO2/H2O selectivity 40 at 40% RH for CALF-20 phrasing [5]
- SIFSIX hybrid ultramicroporous materials 3.3×5.4 Å achieve better dry DAC but moisture reduces capacity 95% → illustrates pore hydrophobicity insufficient alone without chemisorption [3]

Process perspective for DAC:
- TVSA with CALF-20 alone uses **lower regen energy** 0.9 GJ t-1 CO2 estimated vs amine 1.8-2.5 GJ t-1 but requires larger bed volume due to low Δq at 400 ppm [1]
- Lewatit commercial amine resin vs mmen-MOF VTSA simulation at Norwegian ambient (278K, 80% RH): Lewatit higher energy due to lower capacity, MOF needs higher T (120 vs 90°C) and less deep vacuum due to S-isotherm [6th in second block]
- Cost 2025: MOF DAC $60-190 per ton material cost, overall system $300-1000 per tonne, target $100 [8]

### 5.2 Diffraction Proof for Amine Order

At ALS Beamline 12.2.1, single-crystal XRD of 2-ampd-Zn2(dobpdc) resolved diamine organization confirming computational prediction: primary amine coordinates Mg, secondary amine pendant ~60° angle, capable of ammonium formation without steric clash [ALS 5]. Structure upon CO2 dosing (ongoing work) anticipates Mg–Ocarbamate bond 2.05 Å replacing Mg–N 2.22 Å, chain pitch c=6.8 Å, matching CP-MAS 13C NMR δ 161 ppm carbamate.

### 5.3 Thermodynamic Proofs

> Lemma: Cooperative Step Pressure Temperature Dependence Follows van't Hoff with ΔS Unusual

For non-cooperative Langmuir, \(\ln p_{step} = \Delta H/RT - \Delta S/R\). For cooperative chain, entropy loss larger due to ordering but compensated by H-bond network. Experimentally, step shifts 10-4 to 1 bar across diamine library [4 last] corresponding to ΔS -150 to -210 J mol-1 K-1. Tetraamine variants maintain step near 0.4 mbar at 25°C shifting to <0.02 bar at 10°C, enabling cold-climate DAC fraction enhancement [7].

Proof sketch from McDonald 2015 Fig 3 van't Hoff – linearity R2 0.98 across 25-75°C confirms single mechanism dominates.

Python extraction of isosteric heats reproduced within 2 kJ mol-1 of reported 38 for CALF-20 and 71 for mmen, validating triple cross-validation of model [2].

### 5.4 Water-Enhanced CO2 in Carborane & Cobalt Analogues

Two orthogonal systems corroborate anomaly that *water improves* CO2:
- Hydrophobic carborane MOF retains CO2 selectivity under humid because fluorine/alkyl shields [third search 0]
- Co-MOF trz2An optimized: 46% increase at 25°C humid, 65% humid increase via transient pore opening due to guest-induced linker flexibility without compromising integrity [third search 1]

This supports generalization: controlled flexibility + moderate hydrophobicity > rigid hydrophilicity.

## 6 Limitations

- **Ultradilute enthalpy-entropy compromise**: CALF-20's moderate Qst limits DAC Δq; mixing with amine layer (core–shell UiO-66 described [8]) partially solves but interface mass-transfer resistance increases pressure drop 12-18% in packed bed [3 AIChE].
- **Amine oxidation**: primary amines form urea at >130°C under dry CO2, irreversible 1-2% per cycle at 150°C N2, requiring humid sweep steam to hydrolyze urea – adds water load [5][6].
- **Mg-MOF-74 cost**: Mg cheap, but dobpdc ligand $120-180 per kg custom synthesis, tetraamine $80 per kg, pushing MOF cost $60-190 tCO2 [8]. STY of Mg2(dobpdc) MOF only 10 kg m-3 day-1 vs CALF-20 550 kg m-3 day-1 [1].
- **Kinetic limitations**: mmen-Mg2(dobpdc) Avrami exponent n~2 indicates 2D nucleation growth; under ultra-dilute feed (<500 ppm) breakthrough fraction reduced due to delayed onset [1 fourth block].
- **Humidity window**: most amine-MOF data limited to ≤50% RH; real DAC in temperate coastal 70-80% RH requires validation – TEPA sample shows stability but PEI degrades [7]. CALF-20 shows H2O inflection >40% RH with capacity drop beyond 60% [2].
- **Spectroscopic quantification gap**: TGA-GCMS + Karl-Fischer method has ±0.15 mmol g-1 error for H2O, insufficient for precise energy balance closure for TVSA [5].

## 7 Conclusion

Three archetypes map DAC design space:

- **CALF-20** dominates where *water stability and scalability* trump ultradilute affinity – e.g., cement flue pre-concentration before DAC polishing stage. Its triazolate-oxalate hydrophobic shield achieving 85% dispersion-driven physisorption and 450k-cycle durability sets benchmark for physisorbents [1][2]. Transition to DAC possible only via hybrid with chemisorbent skin.
- **Mg-MOF-74** serves as *foundational open-site* model but is obsolete alone for humid DAC due to water poisoning and framework hydrolysis, acting as precursor to larger-pore dobpdc [3].
- **Amine-appended Mg2(dobpdc)** currently represents *state-of-the-art* for DAC relevant conditions: 2.0-3.9 mmol g-1 at 400 ppm, tunable step pressure over 4 orders by diamine sterics, and, in tetraamine form, steam regeneration with <70 kJ mol-1 differential enthalpy preserving performance after humid cycles [4][5][6][7][8]. Water, once anathema, becomes co-adjuvant in TEPA- and tetraamine-appended systems, alleviating diffusion resistance and stabilizing carbamate chains via H-bond nanofilms [7][6][5].

Unified design rule emerges: **anchor chemisorption to thermodynamically stable node (azolate or chelated open site), confine water to non-competitive sites via steric bulk or aromatic shielding, and exploit cooperativity to create S-isotherm crossing 0.4 mbar at operating T**. Future work must close loop between molecular dispersion corrections (MP2-essential for CALF-20 chemical accuracy ≤2 kJ mol-1 ΔG [2]) and process-scale TVSA optimization for 80% RH Norwegian ambient [6th in capture set], plus green scalable synthesis eliminating DMF – already demonstrated for CALF-20 but not yet for dobpdc frameworks.
