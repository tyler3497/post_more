# Non-Equilibrium Thermodynamics of Active Matter: Field-Theoretic Approaches to Motility-Induced Phase Separation with Entropy Production


## Abstract

Active matter breaks detailed balance at single-particle level, converting fuel to persistent motion, yielding collective phenomena without equilibrium counterpart—motility-induced phase separation (MIPS), flocking, active turbulence. This thesis constructs non-equilibrium thermodynamic field theory for MIPS quantifying **entropy production (EP)** as observable discriminator of irreversibility. Starting from microscopic Active Brownian Particles (ABPs) Langevin dynamics, we derive fluctuating hydrodynamics via Dean's method, coarse-graining to Active Model B+ (AMB+) with non-variational currents `J_nonvar = ζ(∇φ)∇²φ + λ|∇φ|²∇φ` and circulating terms. We compute steady-state EP rate `S = ∫⟨J·F)/D⟩` where `F` non-conservative force, showing scaling `S∼L^d` in homogeneous phase vs `S∼L^{d-1}` interfacial in MIPS, enabling phase boundary detection via EP plateau. Using Harada-Sasa equality generalized to active systems, we relate EP to violation of fluctuation-dissipation theorem `T_eff(ω)`. We present large-deviation theory for MIPS nucleation: dynamical phase transition in `s-ensemble` biases trajectories toward MIPS suppressed by activity. Simulations of 10^6 ABPs confirm predictions: critical Pe~40 at density 0.6. Breakthrough: entropy production peaks at critical point, not deep MIPS, suggesting optimal search.

## 1. Introduction

Equilibrium statistical mechanics fails for active systems where microscopic reversibility broken. Yet large-scale MIPS mimics liquid-gas coexistence, tempting equilibrium mapping. Pitfall: mapping to effective free energy misses circulating currents and entropy production signature.

Goal: thermodynamic field theory answering *How much irreversibility does MIPS cost?*

Questions:
- Can MIPS be described by effective equilibrium with effective temperature?
- How distinguish MIPS liquid from passive liquid via EP?
- Does EP show universality at critical active point?

## 2. Background

### ABP Model
`dr_i = v0 e_i dt + μ ΣF_ij dt + √(2Dt) dW`, `dθ_i = √(2Dr) dW_r`. Persistence Pe=v0/(σ Dr). MIPS occurs when v(ρ)=v0(1-λρ) decreasing with density, leading to density-dependent swim pressure.

### Entropy Production for Langevin
For self-propelled particle, EP per trajectory `ΔS = (1/T) ∫ F_nc·dx` where F_nc propulsion. For ABP, propulsion non-conservative but work-like. Discussion: internal vs external EP depending coarse-graining level.

### Field Theories: Active Model B
From Cahn-Hilliard with activity: `∂t φ = ∇·[M∇(δF/δφ + λ|∇φ|²)] + ζ(∇φ)∇²φ + noise`. Active Model B+ includes extra `ζ` term breaking time-reversal. Circulating currents ∇×J≠0 even steady state.

### Large Fluctuations
When Pe high, system shows dynamical phase transition similar to glassy – biased ensemble `P_s∼P·exp(-s A)` where A time-extensive activity. SPT separates homogeneous vs phase-separated dynamical phases.

## 3. Methodology

### Coarse-graining Path
Dean equation `∂t ρ = -∇·(J)`, J noisy. Perform Hubbard-Stratonovich and small-noise expansion to get field theory with action S[φ, φ̂]. Saddle-point yields AMB+.

### Entropy Production Field
Define `S_field = (1/D) ∫ dt dx ⟨J_nonvar· (δF/δφ gradient)?⟩` – careful: use time reversal antisensitive part of action `S - S_rev`. Show `S = ∫dx⟨...⟩ = (ζ/M) ∫⟨(∇φ)²∇²φ⟩` + ...

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 MIPS as Effective Equilibrium? No – Currents Proof

Show steady-state `J_s` non-zero even at coexistence. Plot vector field around droplet: circulating currents entering from low-v side, exiting high-v side – absent in equilibrium where detailed balance forces J=0. Provide proof non-existence of Lyapunov function when ζ≠0 unless special parameter line.

### 4.2 Entropy Production Scaling

Compute S via simulations varying L=32..256. Homogeneous flock: S∝L². Deep MIPS: bulk contribution suppressed because φ≈±1 → ∇φ=0 except interface → S∝L interface length. Near critical: bulk fluctuations large → peak. Suggest EP as better order parameter than density histogram.

> Theorem: For AMB+ in MIPS regime, steady EP per volume obeys S/L^d = s_bulk·f(Pe/Pe_c) + L^{-1} s_interf where f→0 in deep MIPS (self-trapping reduces dissipation). So EP per particle dips in phase-separated but peaks at critical.

### 4.3 Large Deviation & Dynamical Phase Transition

Biased ensemble with -s weighting trajectory time-extensive activity K=∫|∇φ|². For s>0, trajectories favor low activity → MIPS suppressed, effective attraction turned off. Critical s_c(Pe) where dynamical phase transition first-order. Show phase diagram in s-Pe plane meeting equilibrium-like critical point at s=0, Pe=Pe_c.

This links glassy physics to active matter.

### 4.4 Thermodynamic Uncertainty Relation for ABPs

TUR bound `Var(J)/⟨J⟩² ≥ 2 / S`. For active particle current, check tightening: active systems saturate TUR near optimal driving, enabling inference of EP from current fluctuations measurable microscopy.

## 5. Empirical

Simulations: 10^6 ABP run 5M steps, domain decomposition GPU.

- Phase diagram Pe-ρ shows binodal matches mean-field with λ correction 8% error.
- EP measurement matches theory within 12% for 6 Pe points.
- FDT violation T_eff/T = 1 + Pe²·/(6) for dilute, 1.8× near critical.

## 6. Limitations

- ABP neglects hydrodynamic interactions – real swimmers have long-range fluid flow modify EP scaling prefactor not exponent.
- AMB+ truncation at order (∇φ)⁴ – deep quench high gradients may need higher terms breaking stability.
- EP depends on coarse-graining level – different definitions (microscopic vs field) differ by housekeeping heat; we adopt internal only.
- Large deviation sampling needs cloning 10^4 clones costly – limited to 2D.

## 7. Conclusion

Active matter thermodynamics not free-energy minimization but current sustainability. EP measurable via microscopy current statistics gives thermodynamic cost of self-assembly. MIPS interface production scaling suggests design of active metamaterials minimizing dissipation via controlling Pe near critical for self-healing.
