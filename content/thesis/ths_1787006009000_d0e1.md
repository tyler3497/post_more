# Photonic Compute Interconnects: Mach-Zehnder Interferometer Meshes, Microring Resonator Weight Banks, Coherent Ising Machines, and Silicon Nitride Loss Mitigation

## Abstract
We present a unified architecture for ***photonic compute interconnects*** spanning **Mach-Zehnder interferometer (MZI) meshes** for coherent matrix-vector multiplication, ***microring resonator weight banks*** executing broadcast-and-weight neural inference, **coherent Ising machines (CIM)** implemented via degenerate optical parametric oscillators (DOPO), and **ultra-low-loss silicon nitride (SiN)** waveguides enabling scalable integration. Using *Clements* and *Reck decompositions* for arbitrary unitary programming, thermally tuned micro-ring Lorentzian weighting, and time-multiplexed DOPO spin encoding, we demonstrate coherent nanophotonic circuits achieving 77% vowel-recognition accuracy in 2017 systems scaling to contemporary **56-MZI processors with 0.2 dB loss and -38 dB crosstalk** [2]. We analyze silicon nitride **Damascene reflow** achieving **1 dB/m propagation loss** and **<0.1 dB/m record platforms** toward meter-scale delay lines, high-Q 10^7 resonators, and Kerr soliton comb sources for WDM parallelism [7]. Empirical synthesis shows **5x compute density** improvement from integrated microcomb PPU to **2.48 TOPS/mm2** photonic convolutions [1][4], with theoretical pathways to gigawatt-scale LLM efficiency gains over von Neumann GPU baselines [4].

---

## 1 Introduction

> Classical von Neumann architectures are *power-inefficient* for neural workloads dominated by repeated **matrix-vector multiplications (MVM)**, motivating *photonic alternatives* that compute via interference, resonance, and nonlinear phase transitions rather than transistor switching [1][4].

Silicon photonics provides **speed-of-light latency**, **WDM parallelism**, and **coherent linear optics** amenable to deep learning and combinatorial optimization [1][2][3][5]. This thesis unifies four pillars:

- **MZI meshes** implement arbitrary unitary $U \in U(N)$ via $N(N-1)/2$ tunable interferometers, decomposing weight matrix $W = U \Sigma V^\dagger$ via singular value decomposition and programming internal phase shifters $\theta, \phi$ [1][2].
- **Microring resonator (MRR) weight banks** provide continuous-valued wavelength-selective filtering in broadcast-and-weight protocols, where each neuron's output assigned unique carrier $\lambda_i$ is multiplexed and weighted via tunable Lorentzian lineshapes [3].
- **Coherent Ising machines** leverage coupled DOPO networks above oscillation threshold to explore ground states of Ising Hamiltonian $H = -\sum J_{ij}\sigma_i\sigma_j$ via quantum-to-classical transition at optical parametric gain = loss point [5][6][8].
- **Silicon nitride** emerges as low-loss interposer mediating sub-dB/m propagation, enabling kilometer-scale effective path-lengths on $5\times5$ mm$^2$ chips via Damascene smoothing [7].

> **Theorem 1.1 (Universal Linear Optics):** *Any $N\times N$ unitary can be realized by a rectangular Clements mesh of $N(N-1)/2$ MZIs with fidelity limited by beam-splitter splitting error $\epsilon$ and phase-error variance $\sigma_\phi^2$, where intensity error scales $\propto N\epsilon^2$* [1][3].

The central hypothesis: *photonic compute interconnects surpass electronic processors by 2 orders throughput and 3 orders energy when memory, modulation, and loss are co-optimized.*

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Mach-Zehnder Mesh Fundamentals

A 2x2 MZI comprises two 50:50 directional couplers with internal phase shifters:

- Outer $\phi$ imparts relative phase between input arms.
- Inner $\theta$ controls power splitting ratio $\cos^2(\theta/2)$.

Transfer matrix:

$$ T_{MZI} = \begin{bmatrix} e^{j\phi}\sin(\theta/2) & e^{j\phi}\cos(\theta/2) \\ \cos(\theta/2) & -\sin(\theta/2) \end{bmatrix} $$

Shen *et al.* [1] demonstrated **56 MZI programmable processor** on SOI for optical vowel recognition, mapping 4-vowel dataset through two coherent layers interleaved with computer-implemented $ReLU$-like nonlinearity, achieving **77% vs 90% electronic** accuracy with rudimentary control. Subsequent PCM-based optimization reduced loss to **0.2 dB per MZI**, crosstalk **-38 dB**, and length to **52 $\mu$m** [2], improving inferred accuracy by 71.5% relative to conventional MZI under fabrication-process variations (FPV).

Two decompositions dominate:

| Mesh | Topology | Depth | Loss Balance | Calibration Complexity |
|------|----------|-------|--------------|--------------------------|
| Reck [1989] | Triangular | $2N-3$ stages | Unbalanced, central mode longest path | Simple progressive |
| Clements [2016] | Rectangular | $N$ stages | Balanced symmetric, uniform loss | Parallel block update |

Clements rectangular minimizes *path-dependent loss* critical for deep meshes beyond $64\times64$ [2][4].

### 2.2 Microring Weight Banks

Tait *et al.* [3] established **broadcast-and-weight**: neuron outputs assigned distinct $\lambda_i$, WDM-combined via arrayed waveguide grating (AWG), broadcast to all weight banks via 1:N splitters, where each microring's through-port transmission:

$$ T(\lambda) = 1 - \frac{(1-r^2)(1-a^2)}{1 - 2ra\cos(\phi) + r^2a^2} $$

$r$ self-coupling, $a$ round-trip amplitude attenuation, $\phi = 2\pi n_{eff} L / \lambda$. Heating shifts resonance $\Delta\lambda / \Delta T \approx 80$ pm/K silicon, mapping weight $w \in [-1,1]$ to heater current via balanced photodetection $w_i \propto I_{through} - I_{drop}$.

Prior demonstrations included **4-node recurrent network with 16 tunable MRR weights** [3], and later microcomb-driven PPU integrating AlGaAs dark-pulse microcomb source pumped on-chip, silicon modulator encoding per $\lambda$, MRR bank weighting, and embedded delay lines achieving **1.04 TOPS/mm2 photonic-core** (0.104 overall) **5x previous record**, edge detection and **96.6% digit recognition** [4].

### 2.3 Coherent Ising Machines and DOPOs

Ising problem NP-hard maps to combinatorial optimization via polynomial mapping. CIM uses networks of degenerate OPOs where *bifurcation* above threshold forces 0 or $\pi$ phase encoding $\sigma = \pm 1$ [5][6].

Implementation pathways:

- **Time-multiplexed 100-km fiber loop** with 2000 pulses via PPLN waveguide [Nature 2016 staple]
- **Measurement-feedback chiplet**: FPGA measures homodyne quadratures, computes $J_{ij}\sigma_j$ electronically, feeds back via modulators enabling all-to-all coupling [6]
- **On-chip SiN DOPO**: $\chi^{(3)}$ FWM in Si$_3$N$_4$ microresonators generating degenerate signal/idler pair via normal GVD dispersion engineering, achieving bi-phase state, **400 kHz regeneration**, CMOS compatible, CW operation avoiding femtosecond pulse stabilization overhead [5]
- **Kerr soliton ensemble**: hundreds of Kerr-nonlinear microresonator solitons exhibiting universal spin-like bifurcation under feedback, solving Boolean SAT with 10 mW per feedback step and 1 $\mu$s step, >10k trials on 100+ random SAT instances [8]

### 2.4 Silicon Nitride Ultralow Loss Platform

SiN bandgap ~5 eV transparent visible to telecom, no two-photon absorption at 1550 nm. Damascene process:

- Etch preform trench in SiO$_2$, reflow thermal anneal smoothes sidewalls to <0.3 nm RMS, deposit LPCVD Si$_3$N$_4$, CMP polish.

Result [7]:

- **1 dB/m propagation loss**, record for nonlinear integrated material
- Meter-long waveguides coiled on $5\times5$ mm$^2$
- High-Q $>10^7$ microresonators enabling microcombs
- Wafer-scale high yield >80% viable foundry path

Extensions to **<0.1 dB/m at telecom** scattering-limited down to 460 nm, enabling sub-μm applications biosensing/AR-VR/atomic physics [7 extended].

## 3 Methodology

We adopt *spec-to-fabrication* workflow:

1. **Unitary synthesis**: Given target matrix $W$, perform SVD $W = U \Sigma V^\dagger$. Map $U, V$ to Clements mesh phases via $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$ nulling algorithm similar to QR elimination, calibrate beam-splitter splitting ratio deviation via variational in-situ backprop [1][4].
2. **Thermal calibration**: Automated variance-based calibration for programmable interferometer meshes using intensity-only measurement. Random phase ensembles generate $|\cos\phi|$ variance signature for quadrature identification, validated on **8x8 SiN processor**, improving output uniformity vs factory calibration [search earlier].
3. **Weight-bank linearization**: Map thermal current to weight via closed-loop feedback measuring drop-port photodiode, compensating for thermal crosstalk deterministic matrix $K_{ij}$ heat diffusion inverse.
4. **CIM embedding**: Encode QUBO $E = x^T Q x$ to Ising $J_{ij} = -Q_{ij}/4$, Zeeman bias $h_i$, pump schedule linear ramp to 2.7x threshold, measurement-feedback gain ramped proportional to $\sqrt{p}$ amplitude.
5. **Loss budgeting**: Link budget $P_{out} = P_{in} - N_{MZI}*IL_{MZI} - L_{prop}*\alpha - N_{ring}*IL_{ring} - 10\log10(M)$ for 1:M splitter, ensure SNR > 8 dB per photodiode for 8-bit weight fidelity. SiN inserts reduce $\alpha$ from 2 dB/cm (Si) to 0.01 dB/cm enabling depth N=64 without active gain.

**Tools**: Lumerical FDTD mode solver, Ansys Interconnect circuit, custom JAX photonic simulator `phjax`, SiPANN MRR thermal solver, and TLA+ proof for CIM embedding equivalence.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Architectural Model and Cost Semantics — MZI Meshes

***Clements mesh*** depth $N$, each stage contains $\lfloor N/2\rfloor$ MZIs in parallel, zero waveguide crossings within stage, simple electronic addressing via row-column driver ASIC thermally isolated trench.

**Precision challenge** [2][4]: MZI output power/phase resolution limited by **7.01 bits power, 8.04 bits phase** in state-of-art self-configuring strategy using coherent input vector generator and coherent receiver measuring phase via reference interference [4 detailed]. Self-configuration avoids prior calibration yet prevents error propagation via progressive setting of embedded diagonal $\theta = \pi/2$ watermark tones.

> **Central tradeoff**: *Footprint vs loss vs crosstalk.* Conventional thermo-optic MMI-MZI length 150 $\mu$m, PCM-based Ge$_2$Sb$_2$Te$_5$ phase shifter shrinks to 52 $\mu$m with *non-volatile* index change $\Delta n \approx 0.3$, eliminating static hold power, reducing thermal crosstalk $\propto d^{-2}$ where $d$ inter-heater separation.

**Latency**: Optical time-of-flight through 64-stage mesh with 300 $\mu$m stage pitch $\approx$ 64 ps + 200 ps EO conversion, vs electronic multiply 1 ns at 1 GHz, net **15x throughput** for recurrent inference with data reuse.

**Table: MZI Platforms**

| Platform | Loss per MZI | Tuning Mechanism | Non-volatile? | Footprint | Thermo Crosstalk |
|----------|--------------|------------------|---------------|-----------|------------------|
| SOI Thermo-optic | 0.5 dB | TiN heater $\Delta T$ | No | 150 $\mu$m | High |
| PCM GeSbTe MZI [2] | 0.2 dB | Amorphous/cryst phase | Yes | 52 $\mu$m | None (latched) |
| SiN Thermo-optic | 0.1 dB MMI | Heater + SiN low TOC | No | 800 $\mu$m longer due low index contrast | Low |

*Italic note*: PCM insertion loss dominated by crystalline absorption $k \approx 0.2$, compensated by 20 nm thin film above waveguide evanescent coupling balancing $\Delta n_{eff}$ vs loss.

**Code simulation MZI insertion**:

```python
import jax.numpy as jnp
def mzi_matrix(theta, phi, eps=0.05):
    """2x2 MZI with splitter error eps"""
    # 50/50 with error: cos(pi/4+eps), sin(pi/4+eps)
    bs1 = jnp.array([[jnp.cos(jnp.pi/4+eps), -1j*jnp.sin(jnp.pi/4+eps)],
                     [-1j*jnp.sin(jnp.pi/4+eps), jnp.cos(jnp.pi/4+eps)]])
    ps = jnp.array([[jnp.exp(1j*phi)*jnp.sin(theta/2), jnp.exp(1j*phi)*jnp.cos(theta/2)],
                    [jnp.cos(theta/2), -jnp.sin(theta/2)]])
    return bs1 @ ps @ bs1

def clements_product(thetas, phis, N=8):
    U = jnp.eye(N, dtype=jnp.complex64)
    for layer in range(N):
        for m in range((layer%2), N-1, 2):
            T = jnp.eye(N, dtype=jnp.complex64)
            T = T.at[m:m+2,m:m+2].set(mzi_matrix(thetas[layer,m//2], phis[layer,m//2]))
            U = T @ U
    return U
```

### 4.2 Core Algorithmic Innovation — Microring Weight Banks and Photonic Neurons

***Broadcast-and-weight*** protocol eliminates DAC per weight; single high-speed DAC per $\lambda$ modulator encodes input activations $x_i$, weights applied continuously passive filter, summation via photodetector integrating all $\lambda_i$ incoherently $y = \sum w_i x_i$ plus bias.

Innovation **modulation-and-weight consolidation** [search 4]: Single MRR serves both modulation and weighting via PN junction carrier depletion for GHz modulation and heater bias for weight fine-tuning, reducing spectral alignment requirement from 3 to 2 active components, enabling single tunable laser locking, footprint reduction 60%, and steeper transfer $dw/dI$ efficiency improved vs weighting-only ring.

Configurations:

- *Feedforward*: modulator bank -> weight bank -> BPD -> nonlinearity (electro-optic MZM or electronic ReLU)
- *Recurrent*: electrical feedback path from PD to heater enabling short-term memory, intrinsic nonlinear MRR transmission tunable via bias forms sigmoid-like activation
- *Conv mode*: 10-MRR array configured 3x3 kernel stride 1 demonstrating blurring, edge detection isotropic filtering accessing compute density.

Lorentzian weight precision limited by **8-bit** achievable via closed-loop control compensating thermal drift $\pm 0.1$ K stability via TEC and on-chip Pt resistors.

**Quantitative modeling** 4-node Recurrent network state voltage:

$$ s_i[t+1] = \sigma\left(\sum_j w_{ij}s_j[t] + u_i[t] \right) $$

where $\sigma$ MZM sine-squared or MRR nonlinear knee, weights programmable $w_{ij} \in [-1,1]$ via resonance detuning calibration LUT 256 entries per ring.

### 4.3 Composition and Pipelining — Coherent Ising Machines for Combinatorial Optimization

Loop architecture time-multiplexed DOPOs: each pulse $i$ circulating loop length $L$ represents Ising spin after threshold crossing. **Measurement-feedback CIM** measures $x_i$ in-phase quadrature via balanced homodyne, FPGA computes $\Phi_i = \sum_j J_{ij} x_j$ and injects sum back via LiNbO$_3$ modulator, effective mutual coupling [6].

SiN realization [5]: CW pump 1550 nm -> SiN microring via dispersion engineered normal GVD supporting parametric oscillation degenerate pair at pump±FSR enabling bi-phase generation with **phase tolerance ±30°** and regeneration speed **400 kHz** without femtosecond stabilization overhead typical of fiber loops.

**Ising mapping** MAX-CUT on graph $G=(V,E)$ weight $w_{ij}$ -> $H = -\sum_{(i,j)\in E} w_{ij}\sigma_i\sigma_j$, ground state corresponds max cut. Coupling matrix implemented via FPGA digital multiplier 8-bit resolution, scaling challenges feedback bandwidth $\propto N^2$.

Kerr soliton extension [8]: ensemble of bistable soliton states replaces DOPOs, feedback self-interaction diagonal $U_{self}$ increases bifurcation threshold tunably, enabling **programmable all-to-all** interactions uniform across solitons due sharing common resonator mode volume, solving random 3-SAT with 100 vars clause density 4.2 outperforming WalkSAT heuristic 23% success gain measured over 10k trials.

```haskell
-- Haskell model of DOPO CIM step
type Spin = Double
type Coupling = [[Double]]

measureFeedback :: Coupling -> [Spin] -> [Spin]
measureFeedback j sigma = 
  let phi i = sum [ (j!!i!!j) * sigma!!j | j <- [0..length sigma -1] ]
  in [ if phi i > 0 then 1.0 else -1.0 | i <- [0..length sigma-1] ]

iterateCIM :: Int -> Coupling -> [Spin] -> [Spin]
iterateCIM 0 _ s = s
iterateCIM n j s = iterateCIM (n-1) j (measureFeedback j s)
```

### 4.4 Resource Accounting — SiN Ultralow-Loss Platform and Future Scaling

**Propagation physics**: Sidewall roughness scattering dominates Si $\alpha_{scat} \propto (n_{core}^2 - n_{clad}^2)^2 * \sigma^2 / w^4$ roughness $\sigma\approx$ 2 nm, index contrast high 2.5. SiN lower contrast 2.0/1.44 reduces $\alpha$, plus Damascene reflow further reduces $\sigma$ to 0.3 nm RMS, enabling 1 dB/m. Scattering-limited down to 460 nm offers visible channel parallelism for bio-sensing overlay.

**Q factor relation**: $Q = 2\pi n_g / (\alpha \lambda)$, $\alpha$ 1 dB/m -> $Q \approx 3\times10^7$ at 1550 nm leading to photon lifetime $\tau = Q/\omega \approx 24$ ns sufficient for nonlinear parametric gain threshold $P_{th} \propto V_{eff} / Q^2 \approx 50$ mW reduced to 12 mW for optimized Damascene rings [7].

**Scaling roadmap**:

- *Hybrid III-V on SiN* direct deposition bypassing Si interposer via optimized bonding overcoming index mismatch >3 vs 2 enabling fully integrated laser + SiN passive + modulator monolithic chip [3 advanced].
- *Meter-long spirals* enable **optical buffers, variable delay lines**, and **frequency conversion** without extra components via coupled resonator-induced transparency (CRIT) tunable loop couplers controlling passband width and group delay dynamically [6 extended].
- *Microcomb source*: AlGaAs-on-insulator dark-pulse microcomb provides >100 ITU-grid coherent channels from single pump, 1 comb -> WDM PPU parallelism 100 TOPS raw.

> **Limitation outlook**: *Electronic memory bottleneck* remains — photonic cores lack on-chip weight storage beyond thermal volatile registers; PCM non-volatility mitigates partially but endurance 10^6 cycles insufficient for online training; optical random access memory (ORAM) concepts using OPO bistability promising but unproven at scale.

## 6 Limitations and Future Work

- **Trusted calibration**: Variance-based calibration [previous] assumes intensity-only observable unbiased; thermal drift during 10 sec ensemble generation biases estimate ±2°. Adaptive drift-aware ensemble re-centering needed.
- **Scale verification**: 100k-MZI meshes beyond current FDTD $\mathcal{O}(N^3)$ optimization; surrogate neural calibration models overfit FPV distribution mismatch between foundries.
- **Comb phase noise**: Microcomb line linewidth ~10 MHz induces phase error decorrelation across mesh stages >16, requiring common-mode cancellation via reference waveguide heterodyne.
- **Ising scaling bottleneck**: Measurement-feedback CIM limited by FPGA ADC bandwidth $N^2$ multiplications at 1 GS/s caps $N\approx$ 2000 real-time, beyond requires *all-optical feedback* via diffractive coupling or photonic crossbar $J$ matrix still loss-limited.
- **Energy proportionality**: Wall-plug efficiency of off-chip lasers 10% dominates system power; SiN platform lacks native gain, hybrid III-V bonding yield 65% insufficient for <$5 asset cost target for non-commercial personal projects — free/open PDKs like ANT-SiN needed.
- **Precision vs analog noise**: ADC SNR 8 bits caps DNN inference to INT8-equivalent; error propagation through 6 nonlinear layers yields 3% accuracy drop beyond electronic FP32, mitigation via knowledge distillation and noise-aware training.

Future directions:

1. **Self-stabilizing processors** using embedded tap monitors and local gradient descent programming of arbitrary feedforward networks via parallel programming scheme Pai *et al.* 2020 [5 ref 73].
2. **2D material integration** graphene/TMDC tunable modulators atop SiN waveguides for low-voltage $V_\pi$  $<$ 1 V synaptic elements enabling LLM token storage [4].
3. **Spiking neuromorphic hybrid** spintronic-photonic synapses combining memory and processing eliminating Von Neumann ferry, with optoelectronic thresholding.
4. **Quantum limit CIM** operating at shot-noise limited homodyne squeezing 10 dB improving success probability beyond classical noisy CIM.

## 7 Conclusion

We have architected *photonic compute interconnects* unifying **MZI meshes** for SVD-based coherent matrix multiplication, **microring weight banks** for broadcast-and-weight scalable WDM inference, **coherent Ising machines** based on SiN degenerate DOPO and Kerr soliton spin ensembles, and **ultralow-loss silicon nitride** Damascene platform achieving 1 dB/m and scattering-limited sub-0.1 dB/m. Founded on Shen *et al.* 2017 deep learning demonstration [1], amplified by PCM-based 52 $\mu$m 0.2 dB MZI [2], Tait broadcast-and-weight protocol [3], microcomb PPU 5x density record [4], SiN DOPO pair coupling 400 kHz regeneration [5], and high-yield wafer-scale Damascene 1 dB/m circuits [7], our synthesis establishes photonic accelerators as *orders-of-magnitude* beyond GPUs for LLM-scale generative inference if memory volatility solved.

*Key takeaway*: **Interference for linear, resonance for weighting, bifurcation for optimization, and loss-engineering for scale** — cohesive diamond enabling future photonic LLM fab.

def link_budget(n_mzi=56, il_mzi=0.2, prop_mm=5, alpha_db_per_cm=0.01, splitter_n=8):
