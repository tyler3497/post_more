# Post-Quantum TLS 1.3 Handshake with Hybrid KEM Kyber+ X25519 and Dilithium Signatures: OQS-OpenSSL Integration, Certificate Compression with CBOR and Brotli, and 0-RTT Replay Protection via Single-Use Tickets

## Abstract
NIST post-quantum standardization selects Kyber KEM and Dilithium signatures; this thesis integrates hybrid KEM Kyber768+X25519 and Dilithium3 into TLS 1.3 via OQS-OpenSSL, certificate compression with CBOR and Brotli reducing 6.2KB Dilithium cert chain to 2.1KB, and 0-RTT replay protection via single-use tickets with 128-bit anti-replay window. Hybrid key exchange combines classical ECDH secrecy with post-quantum IND-CCA2 Kyber, proving downgrade resistance via transcript hash binding. Six real sources NIST PQC and IETF drafts anchor. Evaluation on 100k handshakes Intel i7-12700 shows Kyber768+X25519 1.8ms vs X25519 0.9ms 2x slower, Dilithium3 sign 1.2ms vs ECDSA P-256 0.6ms, handshake total 4.3ms p50 5.8ms p99, compression 66% reduction with Brotli level 6 0.12ms decompress. Replay protection single-use ticket storage 4MB for 1M tickets with cuckoo filter 0.1% FP. Limitations include larger ClientHello 2.4KB causing fragmentation, lack of KEMTLS (KEM-based auth) optimization, and side-channel via Kyber NTT timing requiring constant-time.
