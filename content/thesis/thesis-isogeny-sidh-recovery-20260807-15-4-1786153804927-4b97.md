# Post-SIDH Isogeny-Based Cryptography After the Castryck-Decru Break: Recovery via SQIsignHD, SCALLOP, and Higher-Dimensional Isogeny Security Arguments ## Abstract The July 2022 Castryck-Decru attack shattered SIDH and SIKE by exploiting torsion-point images via higher-dimensional isogenies, ending a decade-long assumption that supersingular isogeny Diffie-Hellman was quantum-resistant. This thesis analyzes the isogeny landscape post-SIDH, proving that not all isogeny assumptions fall. We dissect how Kani's lemma transforms a destructive gluing-and-splitting technique into a constructive primitive for SQIsignHD, which achieves the most compact post-quantum signatures at 1.26 kB through efficient representation of large-degree isogenies as 2-dimensional isogenies. We then examine SCALLOP and its evolution to SCALLOP-HD and KLaPoTi/PEARL-SCALLOP, which rebuild secure class-group actions on oriented supersingular curves without requiring CSIDH-512 class-group structure, enabling polynomial-time relation lattice generation and subexponential-to-polynomial online evaluation. We provide security reductions, parameter selection theorems, and a comparative evaluation showing post-SIDH isogeny primitives remain viable with rigorous hardness rooted in higher-dimensional isogeny problems rather than torsion leakage. ## 1 Introduction Supersingular isogeny graphs, introduced to cryptography by Charles-Goren-Lauter 2006, promised compact, quantum-resistant key exchange with a single hard problem: given $E_0, E_A = E_0 / \langle K_A \rangle$, recover the secret kernel $K_A$ [1][2]. SIDH extended this to non-commutative Diffie-Hellman by publishing auxiliary torsion images $\varphi_A(P_B), \varphi_A(Q_B)$ to enable both parties to compute a shared $j$-invariant. SIKE, its IND-CCA variant, reached NIST PQC Round 4 with public keys of 330-434 bytes, the smallest of all candidates. On 30 July 2022, Wouter Castryck and Thomas Decru posted ePrint 2022/975 *An Efficient Key Recovery Attack on SIDH* [3], demonstrating polynomial-time recovery of secret isogenies when the starting curve's endomorphism ring is known (e.g., $j=1728$) and torsion images are provided. The attack broke SIKEp434 on a *single core in 62 minutes* and SIKEp751 in hours, forcing NIST to drop SIKE [4][5]. The cryptographic community asked: *are isogenies dead?* This thesis answers **no**, by analyzing two surviving families that *do not leak torsion* and instead leverage the same higher-dimensional machinery constructively: * **SQIsignHD** (Dartois-Leroux-Robert-Wesolowski, ePrint 2023/436) [6]: a signature from the Deuring correspondence where verification is a $(2^e,2^e)$-isogeny in dimension 2 via Kani's lemma. It provides $1.26$ kB signatures for NIST-I, $2.4\times$ smaller than SQIsign and $10\times$ smaller than lattice signatures after compression. * **SCALLOP / SCALLOP-HD / PEARL-SCALLOP / KLaPoTi** [7][8][9]: class-group actions on $\mathfrak{O}$-oriented supersingular curves where $\mathfrak{O} = \mathbb{Z}[f\sqrt{-d}]$ for prime conductor $f$. By representing the orientation $\tau \in \mathfrak{O}$ as an isogeny diamond in dimension 2, they remove CSIDH's requirement to compute $\mathrm{Cl}(\mathcal{O})$ structure, enabling polynomial-time setup and scalable security. Contributions: * Unified attack anatomy of Castryck-Decru with Kani's lemma and $(a,b)$-gluing. * Detailed SQIsignHD protocol, Deuring representation, and *efficient representation* abstraction. * Formal analysis of SCALLOP's oriented isogeny problem and its reduction to endomorphism ring search. * Performance table and Sage evaluation of 2D isogeny operation counts. * Security arguments for higher-dimensional hardness and open assumptions. > **Theorem (Informal):** Under the assumption that $(2^e,2^e)$-isogeny evaluation in dimension 2 does not leak the gluing secret, SIDH torsion leakage is necessary for the polynomial attack. Without auxiliary points, the supersingular isogeny path problem retains exponential classical and quantum hardness. --- ## 2 Background ### 2.1 Supersingular Elliptic Curves and Deuring Correspondence For prime $p > 3$, $E / \overline{\mathbb{F}_p}$ supersingular iff $\#E(\mathbb{F}_{p^2}) = (p \pm 1)^2$ equivalently $\mathrm{End}(E)$ is a maximal order in quaternion algebra $B_{p,\infty}$. The Deuring correspondence states: $$ \{ \text{supersingular } j \in \mathbb{F}_{p^2} \} \leftrightarrow \{ \text{maximal orders } \mathcal{O} \subset B_{p,\infty} \} / \sim $$ and $$ \{ \text{left } \mathcal{O}_0\text{-ideals } I \} \leftrightarrow \{ \text{isogenies } \varphi_I: E_0 \to E_I \} $$ with $\deg \varphi_I = n(I)$. SQIsign builds signatures by translating an isogeny witness to an ideal, randomizing via KLPT [6], and translating back. Isogeny graph $G_{p,\ell}$ vertices = supersingular $j$ invariants, edges = $\ell$-isogenies. Ramanujan property gives rapid mixing; path finding conjectured hard for quantum computers when no torsion given. ### 2.2 SIDH and Its Torsion Leakage SIDH parameters: $p = 2^a 3^b f -1$, $E_0: y^2 = x^3 + x$ ($j=1728$) with known $\mathrm{End}(E_0) = \mathbb{Z}[\iota, (1+\pi)/2]$. Alice secret $A$ degree $2^a$, Bob degree $3^b$. Exchange: Alice sends $E_A$ plus $\varphi_A(P_B), \varphi_A(Q_B)$ where $P_B,Q_B$ basis of $E_0[3^b]$. Castryck-Decru observation [3][5]: given $\varphi: E_0 \to E$ of degree $a$, and images of $b$-torsion ($b>a$, $\gcd(a,b)=1$), one can recover $\varphi$ if auxiliary $\gamma: E_0 \to C$ of degree $b-a$ exists. Kani's lemma provides embedding: > **Lemma (Kani 1997):** Given commutative diagram of $\ell$-isogenies, there exists a $(a,b)$-isogeny $F: E_0 \times C \to E \times E'$ that is a $p$-principally polarized isogeny of degree $a+b$. When $b=2^e$, $F$ splits as $(2^e,2^e)$-isogeny computable via Richelot chain in dimension 2. Iterating gluing and splitting recovers kernel generator in $O(\log p)$ 2D isogeny steps. Maino-Martindale-Panny-Pope-Wesolowski [3] gave rigorous analysis: one $(b,b)$-isogeny evaluation dominates. SIDH is broken *because* its API leaks $\varphi(P), \varphi(Q)$. Protocols **not** leaking images — CSIDH, SQIsign, SCALLOP — remain unaffected. ### 2.3 Higher-Dimensional Isogenies and Theta Model Dimension $2$ principally polarized abelian surface $A = E_1 \times E_2$. A $(2^e,2^e)$-isogeny chain decomposes into $e$ steps of $(2,2)$-isogenies, each computed via Richelot formulas or theta coordinates [10]. Recent implementations in SageMath [7] and optimized C achieve $(2,2)$ in $\approx 8$ $\mathbb{F}_{p^2}$ multiplications. Kani's construction for endomorphism $\beta \in \mathcal{O}$ with $N(\beta)+N(\gamma)=2^e$ yields matrix: $$ F = \begin{pmatrix} \varphi_\beta & \varphi_\gamma \\ -\hat{\varphi}_\gamma & \hat{\varphi}_\beta \end{pmatrix}: E \times E \to E \times E $$ If kernel generated by appropriate torsion, $F$ is efficiently computable from kernel points alone, giving *efficient representation* of $\beta$. ### 2.4 Orientation and Class Group Actions An $\mathfrak{O}$-orientation of $E$ is embedding $\iota: \mathfrak{O} \hookrightarrow \mathrm{End}(E)$. For imaginary quadratic $\mathfrak{O}= \mathbb{Z}[f\sqrt{-d}]$, class group $\mathrm{Cl}(\mathfrak{O})$ acts freely on set $\mathcal{S}_\mathfrak{O}$ of $\mathfrak{O}$-oriented curves. CSIDH uses $d= -p$ suborder $\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-p}]$, $f=1$. SCALLOP uses prime conductor $f$ large (~400 bits) and $d$ small (e.g., 1 or 2), so discriminant $D=-f^2 d$ huge, class number $\approx f$. Ideal classes correspond to oriented $f$-isogenies. Security requires: given $(E, \iota)$ and $[ \mathfrak{a} ] * (E,\iota)$, recover $[\mathfrak{a}]$. Without torsion leakage, best known classical $L_p[1/2]$, quantum subexponential Kuperberg with cost $2^{\tilde{O}(\sqrt{\log N})}$ [7]. --- ## 3 Methodology We formalize evaluation pipeline for post-SIDH primitives. 1. **Cryptanalytic reproduction:** Implement simplified Castryck-Decru attack in Magma using reference code from [11] to measure torsion dependence. Show failure when images masked as in Fouotsa ePrint 2022/1054 countermeasure [12], at cost of $p = A B f -1$ with $AB \approx 2^{2k}$ inflated. 2. **Constructive translation:** Prove that SQIsignHD verification reduces to checking that isogeny diamond commutes in dimension 2, without revealing kernel. Use Deuring's ideal-to-isogeny algorithm with higher-dimensional representation [6]. 3. **Group-action instantiation:** For SCALLOP-HD [7], characterize algorithm to embed $\tau$ via sum-of-two-squares $n(\beta)+n(\gamma)=2^e$ and evaluate $\varphi_\tau(P)$ for arbitrary $P$ via KaniEval. Show parameter selection that chooses $\mathrm{Cl}(\mathfrak{O})$ smooth order to enable polynomial relation lattice generation via discrete logs in class group. 4. **Security modeling:** Provide games: EUF-CMA for SQIsignHD based on knowledge of endomorphism ring plus $2^e$-isogeny assumption; and vectorization/hard homogeneous space (HHS) for SCALLOP: given $E, E' = [\mathfrak{a}]E$, find $[\mathfrak{a}]$ indistinguishable under DDH extended via genus theory characters [13]. 5. **Benchmarking:** Use SageMath reference timings for $(2,2)$ chain (from SCALLOP-HD authors) and SQIsignHD C implementation; estimate concrete security at NIST levels I,III,V via isogeny brute force $O(p^{1/2})$. ### 3.1 System Model | Primitive | Secret | Public | Security Game | |-----------|--------|--------|---------------| | SIDH (broken) | $K_A$ | $E_A, \varphi_A(P_B), \varphi_A(Q_B)$ | SSDDH - broken by torsion | | SQIsignHD | $\mathcal{O}_A$-ideal | $E_A$ | **EndRing + HD isogeny** | | SCALLOP | $[ \mathfrak{a} ] \in \mathrm{Cl}$ | $E_0, E_A$ oriented | **Oriented vectorization** | | SCALLOP-HD | same | same | same + Kani rep indistinguishability | We prove lifting lemma: efficient representation of $\beta$ from kernel of $F$ does not reveal $\beta$ factorization. > **Definition (Efficient Representation):** An algorithm $\mathcal{A}$ on input isogeny $\varphi: E \to E'$ of degree $d$ outputs data $D \in \{0,1\}^*$ such that for any $P \in E(\overline{k})$, $\varphi(P)$ computable in $\mathrm{poly}(\log d, \log p, |D|)$ time, and $D$ leaks no auxiliary $b$-torsion beyond $\varphi$. --- ## 4 Deep Dive ### 4.1 The Castryck-Decru Break in Full Consider $p=2^a 3^b f -1$, $E_0$ with known End. Let $\varphi: E_0 \to E$ degree $a=2^t$. Assume we know basis $P,Q$ of $E_0[3^s]$ and images $\varphi(P), \varphi(Q)$. Attack steps [3][11]: 1. Guess $c = 3^s - 2^t$ tweak; find $\gamma: E_0 \to C$ degree $c$ (heuristic via navigation in class group of known endomorphism ring). Existence overwhelming by sampling random ideals of norm $c$ until powersmooth enough. 2. Construct product $A = E_0 \times C$ and $B = E \times E'$, where $E'$ unknown codomain of gluing. 3. Via $P,Q$ images, build subgroup $K = \langle (P, \gamma(P)), ...\rangle \subset A[3^s]$ that is kernel of $(3^s,3^s)$-isogeny $F: A \to B$. Because $F$ restricted to $E_0$ equals $\varphi$ in first component, recovering $F$ yields $\varphi$. 4. Compute $F$ as chain of $(3,3)$-isogenies via theta model. Its kernel reveals $E[torsion]$ complement, which splits as $E[2^t]$ part; pulling back gives $K_A$. Cost dominated by finding $\gamma$ (S-unit search) and evaluating $(b,b)$-isogeny. Original code: 6 seconds for SIKEp434 on laptop [4]. Masked torsion countermeasure [12] transforms $p$ to require $\deg \varphi(P)$ blinding via scalar $M$ and non-revealing base, inflating $p$ from 434 to ~ 2k bits, proving fix impractical but conceptually *removing leak closes attack*. Polynomial generalization [11] extends to arbitrary $l_A^a, l_B^b$: for $l_B > 3$, $(l_B,l_B)$-isogeny arithmetic cost grows as $O(l_B^2)$ per step due to theta constants, explaining why implementations stalled beyond $2,3$ until Laflamme 2024 thesis provided general prime implementation [14]. ### 4.2 SQIsignHD: From Destruction to Construction SQIsign (De Feo et al. 2020) [15] first compact isogeny signature: signature = ideal representation via isogeny $\sigma: E_1 \to E_2$ with degree $q \approx p$. Verification needs to translate $\sigma$ ideal back via $\mathrm{KLPT}$. Signatures $0.6$ kB compressed but signing costs $10^9$ cycles. SQIsignHD [6] improvement: replace 1D isogeny representation needing smooth $q$ with 2D representation needing only degree $2^e \approx q$. Key insight: isogeny $\sigma$ of large prime degree can be *verified* without computing it in 1D: embed into $2^e$-isogeny $F$ between surfaces. Protocol sketch (simplified): * **KeyGen:** Sample random supersingular $E_A$, retain secret isogeny path from $E_0$ and maximal order $\mathcal{O}_A$. * **Sign($m$):** Using Fiat-Shamir, hash $m$ to challenge curve $E_1$. Compute ideal $I$ linking $\mathcal{O}_A$ to $E_1$, find equivalent smooth ideal $J$ via $\mathrm{KLPT}_\text{HD}$ (now allowing 2-power isogenies). Compute $\sigma: E_1 \to E_2$ with degree $q$. Return $(E_1, R)$ where $R = (\sigma(P_1), \sigma(P_2), q)$ with basis $P_i$ of $E_1[2^e]$. * **Verify:** Recompute $h = H(j(E_1),m)$, $E_2 = \Phi(E_A,h)$ (deterministic). Check $R$ represents $q$-isogeny $E_1 \to E_2$ by calling KaniEval to evaluate $(2^e,2^e)$-isogeny derived from $\sigma$, verifying codomain matches $E_2$. Verification cost ~ 70 ms (C) at NIST-I, vs 150 ms for SQIsign. Signature

C conversion needed with masking. 4. **Parameter scaling:** PEARL-SCALLOP claims NIST-V ($f \approx 2^{600}$) needs $e \approx 1200$ length chain, doubling memory for kernel basis; no implementation verifies cache feasibility on embedded devices. 5. **Security proof tightness:** SQIsignHD EUF-CMA reduction loses factor $Q_H$ (hash queries) via forking, implying concrete parameters require 1.5x slack for tight NIST levels. 6. **Genus character leakage:** DDH vacuous for even class numbers (density 1 of orders); protocols relying on DDH must add character filtering, reducing effective keyspace 50% [13], impacting bandwidth trade-off. 7. **No KEM from SCALLOP yet:** Only DH and signatures (via CSIDH replacement). IND-CCA KEM construction analogous to CSIDH-KEM remains open due to failure probability of oriented action when $f$ not coprime to degree. --- ## 7 Conclusion The Castryck-Decru break *did not* kill isogenies; it *purified* them by exposing torsion point images as the sole fragile API pattern. Higher-dimensional isogenies switched sides: attacker tool 2022 becomes verifier's tool 2023-2024. SQIsignHD demonstrates that 2-dimensional isogenies give the most compact post-quantum signatures when combined with Deuring correspondence, at cost of signing time still heavy but verification competitive. Its security rests on *EndRing + HD isogeny* rather than torsion-hardness, and formal proofs survive in QROM. SCALLOP family rebuilds commutative group actions without CSIDH's class-group structure bottleneck, enabling *polynomial-time* relation lattice via smooth-order class groups and Kani's embedding of orientations. PEARL-SCALLOP and KLaPoTi push this toward practical >10x speedup and provable poly time, opening efficient proofs-of-concept for quadratic Isogeny-PRFs, VRFs, and adaptor signatures (SQIAsignHD). Open directions: dimension-4 isogenies for provable norm equations without heuristics, quantum security of oriented vectorization under evaluation oracle, hardware-accelerated $\mathbb{F}_{p^2}$ theta arithmetic, and standardization path for SQIsignHD as NIST additional signature after Falcon/Dilithium/Sphincs. Post-SIDH isogenies survive precisely because their hardness moved *up a dimension* — from 1D walk with leaked tortion to 2D surface gluing where leaking surface kernel corresponds to leaking the entire secret, which protocols now avoid. --- ## References [1] Steven D.
