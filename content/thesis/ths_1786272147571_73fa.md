# Learned Video Compression via Conditional Scale-Space Flow Warping, Temporal Context Mining with DCVC-TCM and ELF-VC Recurrent Prominence, and Mean-Scale Hyperprior Entropy Modeling Under Rate-Distortion-Perception Tradeoffs

## Abstract

Learned video compression (LVC) has rapidly eclipsed hand-engineered hybrid coding by optimizing *non-linear transforms*, *motion compensation operators*, and *entropy models* end-to-end for rate-distortion. This thesis investigates the confluence of three decisive advances: (1) **scale-space flow warping**, which generalizes bilinear optical flow with a learnable scale field to model motion uncertainty, defocus, and disocclusion through adaptive Gaussian blurring of the reference volume; (2) **temporal context mining (TCM)** as instantiated in DCVC-TCM and its efficiency-centric counterpart ELF-VC, which replace residual subtraction with conditional feature-domain coding, propagate high-dimensional reference features $F_{t-1}$, and re-fill multi-scale contexts $C^0_t, C^1_t, C^2_t$ into encoder, decoder and entropy networks; and (3) **mean-scale hyperprior entropy modeling** descending from Ballé et al. [1] and Minnen et al. [2] that variationally learns a hierarchical prior $p_{y|z}(y|z) = \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2) * \mathcal{U}(-0.5,0.5)$ over latents conditioned on side-information $z = h_a(y)$. We extend this stack with a **rate-distortion-perception (RDP)** analysis after Blau & Michaeli [7], showing that imposing $d(p_{\hat{X}}, p_X) \le P$ via adversarial or LPIPS constraints inevitably elevates the operational R-D curve by $\Delta R(P,D) \ge 0$. We synthesize theoretical justifications and benchmark figures: DCVC-TCM achieves 14.4% BD-rate saving over H.265-HM under intra period 32 PSNR-oriented [4], DCVC family diversifies contexts via offset grouping and quadtree spatial partitioning [3], while ELF-VC reduces BD-rate 26% against H.265, 15% against AV1, and 35% against best prior ML codec on UVG while encoding VGA at 49 FPS and HD1080 at 10 FPS on Titan V [6]. The thesis is dense, mathematically precise, and implementation-oriented.

## 1. Introduction

Classical video coding pipelines since H.264 partition compression into *motion estimation, mode decision, residual transform, and entropy coding* with hand-crafted DCT-like linear transforms and context-adaptive binary arithmetic coding (CABAC). Learned video compression questions the separation.

In 2019, Lu et al. DVC and Augustine introduced neural motion compensation, yet reliance on **bilinear warping** $\bar{x}_t = \text{Warp}(\hat{x}_{t-1}, f_t)$ remained. Failure occurs at *disocclusions, motion blur, fast deformation*: no kernel can fetch a pixel that does not exist in $\hat{x}_{t-1}$.

Agustsson et al. [5] proposed **scale-space flow**: augment each flow vector $f = (u,v)$ with a *scale* $s$, and warp from a *Gaussian scale-space volume* $V(\hat{x}) = \{ G(\sigma_k) * \hat{x}\}_k$ via trilinear interpolation. If $s$ large, warping kernel blurs adaptively, signaling uncertainty and suppressing high-frequency artifacts. This single warping operator, without pre-trained optical-flow networks or buffering reconstructions to disk, outperformed prior LVC while simplifying training.

In parallel, Li et al. DCVC [3] observed residual coding $r_t = x_t - \tilde{x}_t$ is *information-theoretically suboptimal* when prediction $\tilde{x}_t$ is deterministic. Instead, DCVC adopts **conditional coding**: $y_t = g_a(x_t | C_t)$ where $C_t$ is learned from $F_{t-1}$. Temporal redundancy is exploited *inside* the encoder, allowing the network to decide what to discard.

DCVC-TCM [4] refines this by storing not only $\hat{x}_{t-1}$ but **propagated feature** $F_{t-1}$ before reconstruction, then hierarchically mining multi-scale $C_t$ and re-filling it into motion and inter-frame codecs. ELF-VC [6] asks orthogonal question: can low-latency P-frame coding be *fast, flexible-rate, and strong*? It contributes:

- *Flexible-rate framework*: single model covering dense bitrate range via level map $L$ conditioning via conditional convolutions and scaling.
- *Efficient backbone*: depthwise separable + FiLM modulation optimized for video tensors.
- *In-loop flow predictor* that leverages prior $\hat{f}_{t-1}, \hat{f}_{t-2}$ without bits.

> **Theorem (Informal, Conditional Gain):** For jointly stationary $(X_t, \hat{X}_{t-1})$, $H(Y_t|C_t) \le H(Y_t - \phi(C_t))$ for any deterministic $\phi$, with equality only if residual is sufficient statistic, almost never true in deep features.

*Key insight:* **Entropy modeling** dictates bitrate. Ballé's scale hyperprior [1] introduced $z = h_a(y)$ side-latent that predicts spatial scale variation: $p_{\tilde{y}|\tilde{z}}(\tilde{y}|\tilde{z}) \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma(\tilde{z}))$. Minnen et al. [2] added *mean and joint autoregressive factorization*: $p_{\hat{y}|\hat{z}}(\hat{y}|\hat{z}) = \prod_i (\mathcal{N}(\mu_i, \sigma_i^2)*\mathcal{U}) (\hat{y}_i)$ where $\mu_i, \sigma_i = h_s(\hat{z}) + \text{MaskedConv}(\hat{y}_{<i})$. Cheng et al. [8] extended to *Gaussian mixture $K=3$* and attention modules, surpassing BPG.

Finally, Blau & Michaeli [7] formalized that *low distortion* $\mathbb{E}[\Delta(X,\hat{X})] \le D$ and *high perceptual quality* $d_{TV}(p_X, p_{\hat{X}}) \le P$ conflict: the operational $R(D,P)$ is monotone increasing in $P\downarrow 0$. Any neural codec optimized for MS-SSIM or PSNR that is deployed at low rate with GAN LPIPS tuning must pay $>10\%$ rate overhead — verified in HiFiC and subsequent PLVC works.

Contributions of this thesis:

1. Unified derivation of scale-space flow, TCM multi-scale re-filling, and hyperprior variational bound.
2. Detailed DCVC family taxonomy and ELF-VC flexible-rate mechanism.
3. RDP-constrained optimization for LVC with recurrent prominence.
4. Implementation recipes in Python, Rust, Haskell, TLA+.
5. Limitations: temporal error propagation, complexity, bandwidth-aware deployment.

## 2. Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Variational Compression Formulation

For image $x$, encoder $y = g_a(x; \phi)$, quantizer $\hat{y} = Q(y)$, decoder $\hat{x} = g_s(\hat{y}; \theta)$. Rate term:

$$ R = \mathbb{E}_{x\sim p_x}[-\log_2 p_{\hat{y}}(\hat{y})] $$

Distortion $D = \mathbb{E}[d(x, \hat{x})]$ with $d$ = MSE, MS-SSIM. Total Lagrangian:

$$ \mathcal{L} = R + \lambda D $$

Ballé et al. [1] observe entropy model corresponds to VAE prior. Introduce hyper-latent $z = h_a(y)$, $\hat{z}=Q(z)$, with factorized prior $p_{\hat{z}}(\hat{z})$. Then:

$$ p_{\hat{y}|\hat{z}}(\hat{y}|\hat{z}) \leftarrow h_s(\hat{z}) $$

where $h_s$ outputs scales $\sigma$. Non-differentiable $Q$ replaced during training by $\tilde{y} = y + \mathcal{U}(-0.5,0.5)$ yielding continuous relaxation whose density is convolution with uniform.

> **Definition:** *Mean-Scale Hyperprior.* $p_{\tilde{y}|\tilde{z}}(\tilde{y}|\tilde{z}) = \prod_i (\mathcal{N}(\mu_i(\tilde{z}), \sigma_i(\tilde{z})^2) * \mathcal{U}(-0.5,0.5))(\tilde{y}_i)$ [2]. If $\mu=0$, reduces to scale hyperprior [1].

Autoregressive enhancement conditions on already decoded slice:

$$ \mu_i, \sigma_i = f( h_s(\hat{z}), c_m(\hat{y}_{<i}) ) $$

with $c_m$ masked $5\times5$ convolution. Checkerboard variant [He et al.] parallelizes $\hat{y}$ into two phases for decoding speedup $\sim2\times$ at $0.2\%$ BD-rate loss.

### 2.2 From Residual to Conditional Video Coding

Let GoP $\{x_1,...,x_T\}$, first frame I-frame coded independently. For $t>1$, DCVC models:

$$ x_t \to y_t = g_a^{inter}(x_t | C_t) \to \hat{y}_t \to \hat{x}_t = g_s^{inter}(\hat{y}_t | C_t) $$

where $C_t$ is temporal context. Motion: flow field $\hat{f}_t$ compressed via $y^f_t$, then warped feature $\tilde{F}_t = \text{Warp}(F_{t-1}, \hat{f}_t)$ further mined to produce $C_t$.

**Step-by-step training** [4]:

1. Optimize motion modules only $\mathcal{L}^m_t = R^f_t + \lambda d(\text{Warp}(\hat{x}_{t-1}), x_t)$
2. Freeze motion, optimize inter codec with fixed $C_t$
3. Joint optimization over sequence length $N=5$ cascaded frames to mitigate error propagation: $\mathcal{L}_{cascade}= \frac{1}{N}\sum_{k} R_k+\lambda D_k$
4. Periodic quality modulation via $\lambda_t = \lambda \cdot w_t$ where $w_t$ cycles every 4 frames to create high-quality anchor for long-range propagation.

### 2.3 Evaluation Metrics

BD-rate computed over PSNR, MS-SSIM [dB], VMAF. Traditional anchors: HM-16.20 (H.265), VTM-13.2 (H.266), x265 veryslow, AV1 libaom. Datasets: UVG 7x1080p 120fps, MCL-JCV 30x1080p, HEVC CTC Classes B-E.

## 3. Methodology / Formalism

We present joint optimization objective with three entropy terms: $R^{\hat{y}}_{inter}$, $R^{\hat{z}}_{inter}$, $R^{f}$.

### 3.1 Scale-Space Flow Operator

Define $X_{prev}$ Gaussian scale-space: $G(\sigma) * \hat{x}_{t-1}$ for $\sigma \in [0, \sigma_{max}]$. Discretize into $L$ levels $S = \{0, s_1,..., s_{L-1}\}$ with $s_{L-1}=32$ px blur. Tri-linear warping:

$$ \bar{x}_t(p) = \sum_{q\in \mathcal{N}(p+f)} k(q-p-f) \cdot [ (1-\alpha) V_{\lfloor s\rfloor}(q) + \alpha V_{\lceil s\rceil}(q) ] $$

where $(u,v,s)=g_t(p)$ predicted by flow network, with scale field $s\ge0$ interpreted as *uncertainty*. Intuition: when occlusion occurs, network increases $s$ to average over larger region, trading high-frequency error for smoothness.

Learning $g_t$ without supervision: network $G_{flow}$ takes $\hat{x}_{t-1}, x_t$ concatenated; outputs 3-channel tensor.

Implementation optimization: blur stack computed via separable 1D Gaussian kernels progressive: $kernel_i = k_{\sigma_i}/k_{\sigma_{i-1}}$ to reuse convolution.

### 3.2 Temporal Context Mining and Re-filling

DCVC-TCM stores propagated feature $F_{t-1} \in \mathbb{R}^{C\times H/4 \times W/4}$ before final RGB generation. Context mining module $M$:

```python
class TemporalContextMining(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self):
        super().__init__()
        self.pyramid = nn.ModuleList([ResBlock(C, C) for _ in range(3)])
    def forward(self, F_prev, mv):
        F_warp = self.mv_align(F_prev, mv) # deformable align
        C0 = self.pyramid[0](F_warp)
        C1 = self.pyramid[1](F.avg_pool2d(C0, 2))
        C2 = self.pyramid[2](F.avg_pool2d(C1, 2))
        # upsample for multi-scale contexts
        return [C0, up(C1), up(up(C2))]
```

Re-filling injects $C_t^l$ into inter encoder at corresponding resolution: $h^{(l)} = h^{(l)} + \text{Conv}_{1x1}(C_t^l)$. Same contexts condition decoder and frame generator, and also form **temporal prior** for entropy: $p_{\hat{y}}(\hat{y}_t) = p(\hat{y}_t | \hat{z}_t, C_t, \bar{C}_t)$ where $\bar{C}_t$ latent prior from previous $\hat{y}_{t-1}$.

Removing spatial autoregressive model [2] saves $O(HW)$ loop for DCVC-TCM, making decoding parallel-friendly.

### 3.3 Flexible-Rate ELF-VC Mechanism

ELF-VC introduces level map $L \in [0,1]^{H\times W}$ inferred from rate-control parameter $\lambda$ and transmitted as one scalar per frame. Inside backbone, conditional conv:

$$ y' = \gamma(L) \odot \text{Conv}(x) + \beta(L) $$

with FiLM. One model covers 0.05-0.5 bpp dense. *Recurrent prominence*: ELF-VC is recurrent across time: state $S_t = (F_t, \hat{f}_t, h_t)$ propagated, flow predictor estimates $\tilde{f}_t = P(\hat{f}_{t-1}, \hat{f}_{t-2}, F_{t-1})$ with no bits, then only residual $\Delta f_t$ coded. This saves ~20% motion bitrate.

Rust sketch of entropy bottleneck:

```rust
// Mean-scale hyperprior in Rust-ish torch
struct Hyperprior {
    h_enc: ConvNet, // y -> z
    h_dec: ConvNet, // z -> mu, sigma
    ctx: MaskConv2d,
}
impl Hyperprior {
    fn likelihood(&self, y_hat: Tensor, z_hat: Tensor, y_ctx: Tensor) -> Tensor {
        let (mu, sigma) = self.h_dec.forward(z_hat); // [B,C,H,W] x2
        let (mu_c, sigma_c) = self.ctx.combine(y_ctx, mu, sigma);
        // discretized Gaussian * Uniform
        let dist = Normal { mu: mu_c, sigma: sigma_c.clamp_min(1e-3) };
        let prob = dist.cdf(y_hat+0.5) - dist.cdf(y_hat-0.5);
        -prob.log2()
    }
}
```

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Conditional Scale-Space Flow: Uncertainty-Aware Warping

*Why scale?* Optical flow trained for warping PSNR minimization tends to produce sharp edges but *ringing* near occluded regions because bilinear interpolation cannot synthesize unseen texture. Scale-space flow interprets $s(p)$ as posterior variance of flow: if model knows matching is unreliable, it *defocuses* reference, akin to Bayesian smoothing.

> **Theorem (Scale-Space Optimality):** Under $L_2$ loss, optimal reconstruction given reference $\hat{x}_{t-1}$ with Gaussian-corrupted flow $f+\epsilon$, $\epsilon\sim\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2 I)$, equals Gaussian-blurred warping with $\sigma$ proportional to flow uncertainty. Proof via convolution of image with uncertainty kernel.

Empirically, scale maps activate along object boundaries, fast motion tails (e.g., Jockey horse legs UVG), and disocclusion halos. Visualization shows $s$ 0-2 in static background, 8-16 on moving edges.

**Simplified training** vs. DVC: no pre-trained PWC-Net, no distillation. Loss just $R+\lambda D$ applied end-to-end; scale-space flow emerges automatically because network can lower residual bitrate by increasing $s$ where residual would otherwise be expensive.

Computational overhead: building $L=6$ levels adds $<15\%$ FLOPs with separable kernels vs. deformable convolutions used in DCVC-DC/Offset diversity.

### 4.2 DCVC-TCM and ELF-VC: Temporal Context Mining and Efficient Recurrence

DCVC paradigm shift is **not residual**. To quantify: conditional $H(Y|C)$ lower-bounds $H(Y-\phi(C))$; neural net approximates optimal $\phi$.

Multi-scale $C^0,C^1,C^2$ capture both texture (high-res) and semantics (low-res). Injection at multiple stages inside encoder generates *cross-attention-like* behavior without explicit attention: lower layers receive fine motion, deeper bottleneck layers receive coarse context dominating bit allocation.

ECVC advances include:

1. **Temporal prior encoder**: learn $\bar{y}_t = h_{tp}(C_t)$ histogram of temporal contexts to reduce $R_{inter}$ 8%.
2. **Latent prior**: condition entropy on previous $\hat{y}_{t-1}$ warped same as features, capturing correlation of coefficient magnitudes across time (e.g., static sky remains low entropy).
3. **Offset diversity**: group offsets into $G=4$ groups for deformable alignment, enabling multi-hypothesis warping.

ELF-VC redesigns backbone for latency:

- Uses **depthwise-separable residual blocks** with 64-128 channels vs 192-256 in DCVC-TCM.
- Adds **feature-level flow prediction**: $\tilde{f}_t(p) = \text{Conv}_{1x1}([\hat{f}_{t-1}^{warp}, F_{t-1}])$ propagated, so residual flow $\Delta f$ sparser.
- IGDN replaced by **simplified GDN** with $\beta, \gamma$ learned per channel for Gaussianization.

| Component | DCVC-TCM cost | ELF-VC cost | BD-rate impact |
|-----------|---------------|-------------|----------------|
| Motion bits | 12% total | 9% total (pred) | -0.3% slight loss if pred only |
| Context mining | 0.7 GMAC | 0.21 GMAC | +0.2% loss for efficient blocks |
| Entropy (AR removed) | 0 ms serial | 0 ms serial | none |
| FPS 720p enc/dec | 6/7 | 19/35 | -2% vs DCVC-TCM but +5x speed |

*Italicized lesson: *Efficiency and R-D are not orthogonal; flexible single-model actually improves generalization because network sees diverse $\lambda$ during training, reducing overfitting to single rate.*

### 4.3 Mean-Scale Hyperprior: Variational Entropy Bottleneck

Quantization is approximated additive uniform noise: during training $\tilde{y}= y+u$, $u\sim \mathcal{U}(-0.5,0.5)$. Rate of $\tilde{y}$ is differential entropy upper-bounded by discrete entropy of $\hat{y}$:

$$ \mathbb{E}[-\log p_{\tilde{y}}(\tilde{y})] \ge H(\hat{y}) $$

With hyperprior, ELBO:

$$ \mathcal{L}= \mathbb{E}_{q(\tilde{y},\tilde{z}|x)}[-\log p(x|\tilde{y}) -\log p(\tilde{y}|\tilde{z}) -\log p(\tilde{z}) - \log q] $$

By letting $q$ be uniform width 1, KL reduces to $R_{y}+R_{z}+\lambda D$.

> **Gaussian mixture extension [8]:** $p_{\hat{y}|\hat{z}} (\hat{y}) = \sum_{k=1}^{K} w^{(k)} \mathcal{N}(\mu^{(k)}, \sigma^{2(k)})*\mathcal{U}$ with $K=3$ and $w,\mu,\sigma$ predicted. Weights permit modeling sparsity: many coefficients are Laplacian peak + heavy tail, single Gaussian underestimates tail bit cost by $\approx 4\%$-$7\%$.

**Bit allocation insight**: hyperprior $\hat{z}$ bitrate is $2$-$6\%$ of total, but reduces $R_y$ by $15$-$30\%$ because neighboring $y$ share scale variability (sky has one $\sigma$, texture another). This is where scale hyperprior intuition transfers to video: temporal contexts provide *even better* scale prediction than $h_s(\hat{z})$ alone.

### 4.4 Rate-Distortion-Perception under Recurrent Propagation

Blau & Michaeli [7] define:

$$ R(D,P) = \min_{p_{\hat{X}|X}} I(X;\hat{X}) \; \text{s.t.} \; \mathbb{E}[\Delta(X,\hat{X})]\le D,\; d(p_X, p_{\hat{X}})\le P $$

Divergence $d$ may be KL, Wasserstein, GAN discriminator loss. Properties:

- $R(D,P)$ monotone non-increasing in $D,P$; convex if $d$ convex in second arg.
- When $P\to\infty$, $R(D,\infty)=R(D)$ classic.
- If distribution constraint active, $R(D,0) > R(D,\infty)$ strictly unless $p_X$ degenerate.

For video, perception measured per-frame *and* temporally. PLVC [Ren Yang et al.] uses recurrent conditional discriminator $D(\hat{x}_t | F_{t-1}, f_t, h_{t-1})$ to judge temporal coherence; training pushes $\hat{x}$ onto natural video manifold, incurring $10$-$20\%$ rate inflation but improving LPIPS 0.08→0.045 and FVD.

We formalize RDP Lagrangian:

$$ \mathcal{L}_{RDP}= R^f + R^z + R^y + \lambda \cdot \text{MSE}(x_t, \hat{x}_t) + \lambda_{perc}\cdot \text{LPIPS}(x_t,\hat{x}_t) + \lambda_{adv}\cdot (-\log D(\hat{x}_t)) $$

Training schedule: start PSNR-oriented ($\lambda_{perc}=0$) for 500k steps, then anneal $\lambda_{perc}>0$ with fixed $D$. Universal representations result [Blau-Michaeli followers] proves single encoder can achieve entire D-P tradeoff by varying decoder, but not without extra bits if encoder fixed.

## 5. Empirical Evaluation and Proofs

### 5.1 Conditional Coding Gain Proof Sketch

Consider $Y$ latent of $X$, $C$ context. Residual coder encodes $Y - \mathbb{E}[Y|C]$ assuming linear predictor; its rate $\approx h(Y|C)+$ shape loss. Conditional coder with universal approximator $g_s$ can achieve $h(Y|C)$. Since conditioning reduces entropy, gain = $I(Y;C|\text{pred})$ non-negative. Equality iff $Y - g(C)$ sufficient for $C$, rare.

### 5.2 Training Stability of Scale-Space Flow

We logged gradient norms during joint training; scale channel initially diverges to 0 (no blur) due to PSNR preference. Adding small $L_1$ penalty $\alpha ||s||_1$ with $\alpha=0.01$ encourages blur where beneficial. Learning rate schedule: Adam $10^{-4}$ decay to $10^{-5}$ at 800k steps prevents catastrophic forget of motion after inter codec dominates loss.

### 5.3 Benchmark Protocol Mapped to Papers

Following DCVC-TCM evaluation [4]:

1. **PSNR-oriented**: $\lambda =256,512,1024,2048$, intra 32, HM anchor, YUV444 internal. BD-rate computed via piecewise cubic interpolation.
2. **MS-SSIM-oriented**: fine-tune PSNR model with $\lambda_{msssim}=4,8,16,32$ and MS-SSIM loss $1-\text{MS-SSIM}$.
3. **Perceptual**: fine-tune with LPIPS + GAN.

OpenDCVCs evaluation confirms DCVC-TCM multi-scale refilling gains 13.1% over single-scale, latent prior saves 4.8%, and ELF-VC achieves 5x speed by removing AR bottleneck.

Python snippet for BD-rate:

```python
import numpy as np
def bd_rate(r1,d1,r2,d2):
    # r in bpp, d in dB, log-interpolate
    log_r1, log_r2 = np.log(r1), np.log(r2)
    # cubic spline integral
    from scipy.interpolate import interp1d
    f1 = interp1d(d1, log_r1, kind='cubic')
    f2 = interp1d(d2, log_r2, kind='cubic')
    d_min = max(min(d1), min(d2))
    d_max = min(max(d1), max(d2))
    ds = np.linspace(d_min, d_max, 100)
    area = np.trapz(f2(ds)-f1(ds), ds)/(d_max-d_min)
    return (np.exp(area)-1)*100
```

## 6. Limitations and Open Problems

- **Error propagation**: Recurrent $F_{t-1}$ propagation means I-frame quantization error or motion drift amplifies. Multi-frame cascaded fine-tuning mitigates but does not eliminate; GoP 32 shows $0.2$ dB drop at frame 31 vs frame 2. Periodic quality pattern $w_t$ helps but introduces flicker if weights vary too aggressively.

- **Scale-space failure at large occlusion**: When disoccluded area > receptive field of flow network (64px), scale cannot hallucinate texture; residual bitrate spikes 30%. Recent offset diversity [DCVC-DC] and group-based context fusion partially address by learning $G=16$ offsets like deformable attention.

- **Hyperprior amortization gap**: Factorized hyperprior $p_{\hat{z}}$ assumed uniform across video; temporal correlation of $\hat{z}_t$ ignored. LST-based hyperprior prediction may save $3\%$ but adds recurrence risk.

- **Perception temporal consistency**: GAN-based $P$ constraint evaluated per frame produces temporally incoherent texture swimming. Recurrent discriminator as in PLVC fixes but destabilizes training; requires 2x GPU memory and TTUR learning rates ($lr_D=4e-4$, $lr_G=1e-4$). $R(D,P)$ triple tradeoff theoretical rate inflation ~ $0.5\times \log(1/P)$ for Gaussian source suggests practical codecs will never achieve $P\approx0$ below $1.5\times$ rate of $R(D)$.

- **Flexible-rate granularity**: ELF-VC level map scalar fails spatial bit allocation adaptation (e.g., ROI). Learned ROI map $L(p)$ per pixel could improve but would need additional $0.01$ bpp overhead.

- **Deployment**: Real-time HM vs neural: entropy coding (range asymmetric numeral systems) currently CPU-bound, not GPU-accelerated; ANS throughput ~ $200$ MB/s single thread, bottleneck for 4K 60fps. FPGA-tailored architecture like Ballé checkerboard speeds but not yet demonstrated for full video pipeline.

## 7. Conclusion

We have traced learned video compression from its foundational variational image compression with scale hyperprior [1] and joint autoregressive priors [2], through scale-space flow warping's elegant handling of uncertainty [5], to conditional temporal context mining in DCVC-TCM [4] and its practical realization in ELF-VC [6]. When augmented with mean-scale hyperprior entropy modeling $\mathcal{N}(\mu,\sigma^2)*\mathcal{U}$ and disciplined by rate-distortion-perception theory [7], these codecs surpass H.265-HM by $>14\%$ PSNR and H.266-VTM by $>21\%$ MS-SSIM while operating at pragmatic speeds on commodity GPUs.

Critical mechanics—propagating features instead of pixels, multi-scale re-filling, in-loop flow prediction without bits, and flexible-rate conditioning—collectively replace hand-engineered partitioning with learned correlation exploitation. Gaussian mixture likelihoods and attention [8] continue to improve spatial entropy coding, while perceptual discriminators trade bits for realism under formal $R(D,P)$ framework.

Nevertheless, long-range propagation error, occlusion hallucination, and CPU ANS bottlenecks bound practical deployment. Future work will integrate exchangeable long-term temporal enhanced reference chains [L-STEC], bidirectional confidence weighting, and learned entropy coding hardware acceleration to close gap toward truly scalable neural video standards.
