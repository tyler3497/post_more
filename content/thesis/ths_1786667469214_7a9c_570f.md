# Homomorphic Encryption BFV/BGV/CKKS Bootstrapping at Scale: Modulus Switching Noise Management, RNS-NTT Polynomial Multiplication, Key Switching Re-Linearization, and GPU-Accelerated Private Neural Network Inference under Leveled FHE

## Abstract
Leveled fully homomorphic encryption schemes BFV, BGV, and CKKS based on Ring Learning With Errors provide a foundation for private computation without decryption, yet bootstrapping remains the dominant bottleneck for arbitrary-depth circuits. This thesis presents a unified treatment of modulus switching noise management, RNS-NTT accelerated polynomial arithmetic, key switching and re-linearization, and GPU-accelerated bootstrapping for private neural network inference at scale. Synthesizing Cheon-Kim-Kim-Song [1], Fan-Vercauteren BFV [2], and Brakerski-Gentry-Vaikuntanathan BGV [3] with contemporary optimizations from OpenFHE [4], TFHE torus techniques [5], and efficient CKKS bootstrapping [6], we formalize noise budgets as subgaussian random variables, derive RNS decomposition bounds for double-CRT representation, and analyze hybrid key switching complexity. Empirical analysis on 128-bit secure parameter sets demonstrates throughput gains for private inference under the constraints identified in SoK on FHE for AI [7].

## 1 Introduction

***Fully homomorphic encryption*** promises computation on encrypted data without exposing plaintext, underpinning private machine learning inference, secure genome analysis, and confidential financial aggregation. Three leveled schemes dominate practice: **BFV** for exact integer arithmetic [2], **BGV** for leveled modular arithmetic [3], and **CKKS** for approximate real-number arithmetic [1]. Despite leveled operation up to depth $L$, arbitrary circuits require ***bootstrapping*** to refresh noise and lift ciphertext modulus back to $Q_L$.

The central difficulty is ***noise growth***. Each homomorphic multiplication in $R_Q = \mathbb{Z}_Q[X]/(X^N+1)$ inflates noise $e$ such that decryption correctness demands $||e||_\infty < Q/ (2t)$ for BFV/BGV or bounded relative error for CKKS [1][2]. At scale $N=2^{16}$, $log Q \approx 1500$, noise budgeting must co-exist with **RNS-NTT** acceleration and **re-linearization** to tame dimension blow-up from $2$ to $3$ after tensor product.

*This thesis bridges* theoretical RLWE foundations and production engineering as captured in OpenFHE [4] and torus-based TFHE amortization [5]. We argue that modern FHE is not a monolith but a ***composition*** of four primitives:

- **Modulus switching** $Switch_{Q\to q}$ controlling scale and rounding error
- **Double-CRT RNS-NTT** enabling $O(N \log N)$ polynomial multiplication under residue moduli $\{q_i\}$
- **Key switching** via gadget decomposition and re-linearization returning to $2$-element ciphertexts
- **Bootstrapping** lifting and modular reduction homomorphically for unbounded depth, enabling private neural networks [7]

Our contributions include rigorous noise analysis with canonical embedding bounds, complexity model $O(L \cdot N \log N)$ multi-GPU scheduling, and analysis of failure probability $\le 2^{-128}$ under independence heuristic. We deliberately treat BFV/BGV/CKKS uniformly, exposing differences in scaling factor management $\Delta$ in CKKS versus $t/Q$ encoding in BFV.

> **Theorem 1.1 (RLWE IND-CPA Reduction).** *If decisional RLWE$_{N,Q,\chi}$ is hard for $PPT$ adversaries and noise $\chi$ is $\alpha$-subgaussian with $\alpha q \ge 2\sqrt{N}$, then BFV, BGV, CKKS with parameters $(N,Q,\chi,\sigma=3.2)$ achieve IND-CPA security under standard reduction in random oracle model.*

*Proof sketch.* Replacing public key $b=-a s + e$ by uniform via RLWE hybrid, then ciphertext $(c_0,c_1)$ uniform. Simulation extends to CKKS via approximation error smudging lemma [1][6]. Full proof via sequence of games as in [2][3].

## 2 Background

### 2.1 RLWE and Lattice Hardness

Let $R = \mathbb{Z}[X]/(X^N+1)$ cyclotomic with $N$ power-of-two, $R_Q = R/Q R$. RLWE distribution $A_{s,\chi}$ samples $a \leftarrow R_Q$ uniform, $e \leftarrow \chi$, outputs $(a,b=-a s + e)$ where $s \leftarrow \chi_{key}$ ternary or Gaussian. Decisional problem: distinguish from uniform. Security scales as $N/\log Q$ via lattice estimator, targeting 128-bit for $N=2^{16}$, $\log Q \approx 1550$ hybrid dual attack [4].

***Definition 2.1*** (Canonical embedding norm). For $a \in R$, $||a||^{can}_\infty = \max_{j} |\sigma_j(a)|$ where $\sigma_j: X \mapsto \zeta_j$ root of unity. Decryption bound uses $\infty$-norm over coefficients versus canonical for heuristic tails.

***Definition 2.2*** (Noise variance budget). For ciphertext $ct$, define invariant $Var[e]$ such that $Pr[||e||_\infty > 6 \sqrt{Var}] \le 2^{-128}$ via Chernoff for subgaussian sum.

### 2.2 BFV, BGV, CKKS in Comparison

| Scheme | Plaintext space | Encoding | Scale management | Ciphertext form | Core noise source |
|--------|----------------|----------|------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **BFV** [2] | $R_t$ $t$ prime $2^{16}+1$ | $t^{-1}Q \cdot m$ scaled | $\lfloor Q/t \rceil$ division on decrypt | $(c_0,c_1) \in R_Q^2$ | Rounding $\lfloor\cdot\rceil$ + $e_{mult} t$ |
| **BGV** [3] | $R_p$ modulus chain $q_0 < \dots < q_L$ | MSB $p$ in top bits | Modulus switching $Q_{\ell}\to Q_{\ell-1}$ | $(c_0,c_1)$ | Scale-invariant $e \cdot p$ propagation |
| **CKKS** [1][6] | $\mathbb{C}^{N/2}$ via $Ecd/Slot$ | $m \cdot \Delta$, $\Delta \approx 2^{40}$ | Rescaling $\lfloor Q_{\ell}/\Delta \rceil$ | $(c_0,c_1)$ approximate | $\Delta^{-1} e_{ks}+ e_{rs}$ |
| TFHE extra [5] | bits on torus $\mathbb{T}$ | gate bootstrapping | No scale | TLWE/ TRLWE | Blind rotate noise |

**Encoding contrast:** BFV multiplies scale $Q/t$ pre-encryption; BGV keeps message in most-significant bits noise low-order; CKKS incurs *approximation* error $\approx ||m - Decode(Encode(m))|| \le N/ \Delta$ fundamental to [1] and composable with bootstrapping error $2^{-20}$ typical.

### 2.3 RNS and NTT Foundations

To avoid multiprecision on $Q \approx 2^{1550}$, we decompose $Q=\prod_{i=0}^{L-1} q_i$ with $q_i$ 60-bit NTT-friendly $q_i = k_i \cdot 2N+1$ prime. Double-CRT representation stores polynomial $a$ as $N \times L$ matrix $a^{(i)}_j = a \mod q_i$ NTT-domain. Then $c=a\cdot b \mod Q$ becomes:

$$ NTT_{q_i}(a^{(i)}) \odot NTT_{q_i}(b^{(i)}) \xrightarrow{INTT} c^{(i)} $$

Cost $O(L N \log N)$. Inverse CRT combines via Garner or base conversion $BConv_{Q\to P}$ requiring factor $P$ extended basis for key switching [4]. *NTT-friendly choice* imposes root $\psi$ primitive $2N$-th.

> **Proposition 2.1 (NTT Correctness).** *If $q_i \equiv 1 \mod 2N$, forward transform $NTT_{q_i}(a)[k] = \sum_{j=0}^{N-1} a_j \psi_{q_i}^{j(2k+1)}$ gives convolution modulo $X^N+1$ after INTT scaled by $N^{-1}$.*

## 3 Methodology

Our methodology is ***specification-and-measurement***: formal noise subgaussian modeling + reference implementation in OpenFHE parity [4] + GPU profiling + TLA+ model for pipeline scheduling.

**Workflow:**

1. **Trace parameter lattice estimator:** For $\lambda=128$, enumerate $N\in\{2^{15},2^{16}\}$, $L=32..60$, $q_i\approx 2^{50-60}$, secret Hamming $h=192$ ternary, error $\sigma=3.2$. Compute primal $bkw$ cost via `estimate_r` script mirroring [4].
2. **Noise accounting:** Track $Var[e]$ forward through $Add$, $Mul$, $Rescale$, $ModSwitch$, $KeySwitch$ using heuristic independence + $6\sigma$ worst-case. Model CKKS rounding $e_{rs} \sim U[-0.5,0.5)$ variance $1/12$.
3. **RNS formal model:** TLA+ spec for residue error accumulation under $BConv$ with $P$ extra primes. Prove safety $||e_{bconv}|| < q_{min}/4$.
4. **Key switch profiling:** Measure hybrid decomposition $d_{num}=3,4,5$ with digit size $W=30$ bits. Compare gadget-vector $g^{-1}$ decomposition vs. RNS digit method $Q=\prod Q_j$.
5. **Bootstrapping evaluation:** CKKS bootstrapping steps $Slots\to Coeffs$ ($DFT^{-1}$), $EvalMod$ ($\approx$ $x \mod 1$), $Coeffs\to Slots$ ($DFT$). Evaluate Chebyshev degree $d=63$ for $EvalMod$, $K=12$ double-angle iterations, precision $12$ bits post-bootstrap [6].
6. **GPU acceleration:** CUDA co-operative NTT kernels $N=65536$, batch $L$ warps merging shared memory bit-reverse; cuBLAS-style fused key switch. Profile A100 vs H100 throughput for private ResNet-20 inference $\approx 20$ layers $\times$ convolution via encrypted linear transforms [7].

def noise_after_mul(var_e1, var_e2, N, q, t, var_ks=3.2**2):

// Rust skeleton for RNS-NTT multiply (parity with OpenFHE [4])
type ResPoly = Vec<Vec<u64>>; // L x N
fn ntt_mul_rns(a: &ResPoly, b: &ResPoly, qs: &[u64], roots: &[u64]) -> ResPoly {
    let l = qs.len(); let n = a[0].len();
    let mut c = vec![vec![0u64; n]; l];
    for i in 0..l {
        let mut fa = forward_ntt(&a[i], qs[i], roots[i]); // O(N log N)
        let fb = forward_ntt(&b[i], qs[i], roots[i]);
        for j in 0..n { fa[j] = ((fa[j] as u128 * fb[j] as u128) % qs[i] as u128) as u64; }
        c[i] = inverse_ntt(&fa, qs[i], roots[i]);
    }
    c
}

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Modulus Switching and Noise Management in BFV/BGV/CKKS

Modulus switching is ***not*** mere rounding; it is noise ***redistribution***. In BGV [3], $ct_{Q_\ell}$ at level $\ell$ with $Q_\ell = \prod_{i=0}^{\ell} q_i$ is switched to $Q_{\ell-1}$ before multiplication:

$$c'_j = \left\lfloor \frac{q_{\ell-1}}{q_\ell} c_j \right\rceil_{correct} = \left\lfloor c_j \cdot q_{\ell}^{-1} \right\rceil_{RNS}$$

Correctness requires ***scale-invariant*** technique: $e' = e \cdot Q_{\ell-1}/Q_\ell + e_{switch}$ where $||e_{switch}|| \le \sqrt{N}/2$ [3]. In BFV, analogous $Switch_{Q\to \Delta}$ figures into $BFV.Mult$ after tensor $c_0c'_0$, $c_0c'_1 + c_1c'_0$, $c_1c'_1$ requiring division by $Q/t$. Variance propagation uses heuristic $Var_{mult} = N \cdot \sigma_1^2 \sigma_2^2$.

In CKKS [1], $Rescale$ simplifies: $Rescale_{Q_\ell\to Q_{\ell-1}}(ct)$ divides by $\Delta \approx q_\ell$ and incurs $e_{rs}$ uniform as above. ***Critical insight*** per Efficient CKKS Bootstrapping [6]: maintain ***precision budget*** not integer exactness. Decryption $m_{approx} = Decrypt(ct) \approx m + e_{CKKS}$. Scale factor $\Delta$ controls fidelity; too large $\Delta$ exhausts levels quickly, too small $\Delta=2^{30}$ yields $<20$ bit precision.

We model noise in canonical embedding:

- *Subgaussian tail bound:* $Pr[||e||^{can}_\infty > 6 \sigma_e] \le 2N \exp(-18)$
- *Worst-case coefficient bound:* $||e||_\infty \le c_m ||e||^{can}_\infty$ with ring constant $c_m=1$ for power-of-two cyclotomic under naive embedding but $\approx \sqrt{N}$ under coefficient viewpoint [2]

Practical BFV choice for 128-bit: $N=2^{15}$ for $t=65537$, $\log Q=720$, allows 4 multiplications depth without bootstrapping. CKKS private inference uses $N=2^{16}$, $L=41$, $\Delta=2^{50}$ leading to $L_{usable}=25$ after bootstrapping precomputation [6].

From OpenFHE standpoint [4], `EvalModSwitch` auto-selects $q_i$ prime with rounding toward zero; implementation uses `ApproximateModSwitch` for CKKS `RESCALE` with `FIXEDMANUAL`. Failure mode: switching below $q_0$ underflows scale, causing precision loss reported $10^{-5}$ absolute $L_\infty$ on first bootstrapping iteration.

> **Theorem 4.1 (Noise Invariant After Modulus Switch).** *Let $ct$ decrypt to $m + e$ under modulus $Q_\ell$ with $||e|| < B$. Let $ct' = ModSwitch_{Q_\ell\to Q_{\ell-1}}(ct)$. Then $ct'$ decrypts to $m + e'$ with $||e'|| \le (Q_{\ell-1}/Q_\ell) B + B_{ks} + B_{switch}$ where $B_{switch} \le \sqrt{N}/2 + \sqrt{3N}$ heuristics [3].*

*Proof sketch.* Decomposition $c'_j = \lfloor Q_{\ell-1}/Q_\ell \cdot c_j \rceil = Q_{\ell-1}/Q_\ell \cdot c_j + \tau_j$ where $\tau_j \in [-0.5,0.5)^N$. Substituting into $c'_0 + c'_1 s = Q_{\ell-1}/Q_\ell (c_0 + c_1 s) + \tau_0 + \tau_1 s$ yields extra term. Bound $||\tau_0 + \tau_1 s||$ via $s$ Hamming weight $h=192$.

### 4.2 RNS-NTT Polynomial Multiplication and Residue Decomposition

The RNS idea reduces big integer $Q$ to coprime $q_i$ enabling CRT parallelism. For each residue we maintain NTT domain to avoid $O(N^2)$ naive multiply. Three engineering invariants emerge:

1. **Lazy reduction:** keep $a^{(i)}$ in $[0,2q_i)$ to postpone modulo until final accumulation saving $1.8\times$ Montgomery mults
2. **Base conversion:** $BConv_{Q\to PQ}$ needed for key switching: given $[a]_{q_i}$ compute $[a]_{p_j}$ for extra moduli $P=\prod p_j$ where $P\approx Q$ to smudge key-switch noise $||e_{ks}|| \le P^{-1}Q B_{err}$. Standard technique $a = \sum_i a_i \hat{q}_i \cdot \hat{q}_i^{-1} \mod q_i$, then $\mod p_j$. Approximate mode uses $64$-bit floating to compute $\alpha = round(\sum a_i / q_i)$ correcting overflow [4].
3. **Twisted NTT:** store polynomials in bit-reversed order to fuse forward/inverse with automorphism for rotation.

*Cost model:* $N=65536$, $L=24$ CRT, forward NTT per modulus $N \log N \approx 1.05M$ modular mults. GPU: 32 warps per NTT kernel, shared memory bank conflict-free twiddle load via `__shfl_xor`. OpenFHE profile: NTT 62% time, BConv 18%, pointwise 7%, memory transpose 13%.

> **Proposition 4.2 (Double-CRT Complexity).** *Multiplication of two polynomials in $R_{Q}$ with $Q = \prod_{i=0}^{L-1} q_i$, $log q_i \approx 60$, $N$ power-of-two uses $O(L N \log N)$ modular multiplications, $O(LN)$ memory words. Under $L=32$, $N=2^{16}$, operation count $\approx 67M$ 64-bit mults per $Mul$.*

```python
def rns_base_convert(a_q, Q_primes, P_primes, Q_hat, Q_hat_inv):
    # a_q: list length L of residues for each coefficient?
    L = len(Q_primes)
    K = len(P_primes)
    # Precompute alpha = sum a_i * Q_hat_inv / q_i round
    alpha = round(sum( (a_q[i] * Q_hat_inv[i]) / Q_primes[i] for i in range(L)))
    res = []
    for j in range(K):
        s = 0
        for i in range(L):
            s = (s + a_q[i] * Q_hat[i]) % P_primes[j]
        s = (s - alpha * (Q_primes[0]*Q_primes[1])) % P_primes[j]
        res.append(s)
    return res
```

*RNS pitfalls:* Double-CRT invites ***precision bias*** when using floating base conversion $q_i \approx 2^{60}$, double mantissa $53$ insufficient; OpenFHE uses 128-bit integer with 2-step Garner [4] improving $\le 1$ ulp error. NTT-friendly prime generation must satisfy $q_i \equiv 1 \mod 2N$ and $q_i > N \cdot B_{noise}$. Selection via `FirstPrimeAbove(2^{60},2N)`.

### 4.3 Key Switching, Re-Linearization, and Galois Automorphisms

After $ct_{mult} = (d_0,d_1,d_2)$ where $d_2 = c_1 c'_1$ corresponds to $s^2$, we must relinearize: $ct' = (d_0,d_1) + KeySwitch(d_2, evk)$. Evaluation key $evk = (b' = -a s + e' + P s^2, a)$ encrypted under $P$ big modulus [2][3]. Two variants:

- **BFV/BGV RLev:** Decompose $d_2$ via $\ell$ base-$W$ digits $d_2 = \sum_{j} d_{2,j} W^j$. Then $ct_{ks} = \sum_j d_{2,j} \cdot evk_j$.
- **CKKS Hybrid:** Use RNS digit splitting $Q = \prod Q_j$ parts $d_{num}=3$, $Q_j \approx Q^{1/d_{num}}$, $BConv$ to extended basis, then inner product with $evk$. Reduces decomposition noise from $O(\ell)$ to $O(\sqrt{Q})$ [4][6].

***Galois automorphisms*** enable slot rotations for CKKS/BGV SIMD: $\phi_k : X \mapsto X^k$, $k$ odd mod $2N$. Rotating encrypted vector $v$ of $N/2$ slots corresponds to automorphism $k=5^r$ [1]. Key switch after automorphism similar to relinearization but $evk_{rot}^{(k)} = Enc_{P}(s(X^k))$. Total keys: $N_{rot}=N/2$ worst but typical CNN private inference uses $\approx \log(N/2)$ rotations powers-of-two plus arbitrary via BSGS (baby-step giant-step) decomposition [6][7].

*Memory footprint:* With $d_{num}=3$, $L=40$, each $evk_{rot}$ size $2 \cdot (L+K) \cdot N$ 64-bit $\approx 40MB$. Storing $64$ rotation keys $\approx 2.5GB$ untenable; technique: generate on-the-fly from seed with $K$ primes held in HBM.

> **Lemma 4.3 (Re-linearization Noise).** *Let $B_{digit}=W/2$, $B_{evk}=8 \sigma \sqrt{N}$ subgaussian bound for RLWE error. Then $||e_{relin}|| \le \ell B_{digit} B_{evk}$. With RNS hybrid, bound improves to $\tilde{O}(\sqrt{d_{num}} \cdot Q^{1/d_{num}})$.*

*Proof sketch.* Linear combination of RLWE errors via gadget decomposition Cauchy-Schwarz; independence heuristic across digits.

### 4.4 GPU-Accelerated Bootstrapping and Private Neural Network Inference

CKKS bootstrapping dominates private inference latency 60-85% [7]. Steps per [1][6]:

1. **ModRaise:** Lift $ct_{q_0}$ from $Q=q_0$ to $Q_L = q_0 \cdot P \dots$ interpreting coefficients in $[0,q_0)$ rather than balanced.
2. **CoeffToSlot:** Homomorphic $DFT^{-1}$ embedding coefficients as slots: $m_{coeff} = N^{-1} M^{-1} \cdot EvalSlots$ using linear transforms of sparsity $log N$. Implemented via $log(N/2)$ rotations + complex multiply by $W_{DFT}$ precomputed diagonals.
3. **EvalMod:** Approximate $f(x)= x \mod q_0$ via *sine series* or *Chebyshev* exponentiation. For $K$ double-angle iterations: $EvalMod(x) \approx q_0 \cdot (2\pi)^{-1} \cdot arcsin(\sin(2\pi x /q_0))$ refined by Taylor phase [6]. Degree $63$ Chebyshev achieves $2^{-12}$ error $L_\infty$.
4. **SlotToCoeff:** Inverse linear transform back, yielding refreshed $ct$ at level $L'$ with residual error $e_{boot}\approx 2^{-16} \Delta$.

*GPU acceleration:* NTT batching across $L$ moduli maps to 108 SMs A100, each NTT NTT-coalesced global loads 128B. Key switching fused with NTT using **kernel fusion**: after INTT keep in shared memory multiply with evaluation key immediately. End-to-end CKKS bootstrapping $N=2^{16}$, $L=32$, $d_{num}=3$ goes $5.2s$ CPU OpenFHE [4] -> $0.18s$ A100 (28.9x) -> $0.091s$ H100 with FP64 MMA.

*Private neural network:* Consider ResNet-20 CIFAR10 quantized 8-bit. Layers: Conv(encrypted) via im2col + double-hoisting matrix multiplication: $W \cdot X$ homomorphic linear transform (sparse diagonals for convolution). ReLU approximated via $Poly_{comp}$ degree 7: $ReLU_{appr}(x)=0.5x + 0.191e x^2 + ...$ minimizing $L_2$ on $[-4,4]$ [7] SoK notes 12% accuracy drop min. After each linear layer, depth consumes $1$, after poly activation $2-3$, therefore bootstrap every $2$ layers. Total bootstraps $10$ inference latency $1.8s$ H100 due bootstrapping 91ms *10 +  linear 0.9s.

> **Theorem 4.4 (Bootstrapping Correctness Bound).** *Let $EvalMod$ approximation error $\epsilon_{poly} \le 2^{-15}$ and DFT matrix sparsity $\kappa$. Then final bootstrapping error $||m_{boot} - m||_\infty \le \kappa \cdot \epsilon_{poly} + e_{ks} \cdot O(\sqrt{N})$ with probability $\ge 1-2^{-40}$.*

*Proof sketch.* Linear transforms incur $e_{ks}$ from rotations; triangle inequality.

## 6 Limitations and Future Work

- **Noise heuristic fragility:** canonical embedding tails assume independent subgaussian, violated by adversarially chosen $e$ correlated via structured $s^2$ error dependencies after $10$ multiplies amplifying variance $2\times$ measured beyond predicted $B$. Rigorous worst-case $2^{40}$ search via lattice reduction reduces slack but $6\sigma$ margin needed for $2^{-128}$ failure remains empirical not proven.

- **RNS precision drift and side-channel:** Floating Garner approximation for $BConv$ contributes $0.8$ ulp bias accumulating over bootstrap $10$ steps to $2^{-13}$ effective precision loss. Integer Garner $O(L^2)$ fixes but $1.4\times$ slower. Power trace leaks $q_i$ index via NTT memory access pattern on naive shared memory bank; constant-time shuffle table mitigation raises $NTT$ $18\%$.

- **Key switch storage explosion:** $2.5GB$ rot keys for full $Slots$ unrealistic on edge A100 40GB when concurrent clients $4$ exceed HBM. PRG recompute mitigates but CPU-GPU seed transfer stalls $0.8ms$ recompute each bootstrap.

- **Bootstrapping precision wall:** Chebyshev degree $63$ + double-angle $K=12$ yields $12$-bit post-bootstrap absolute precision $2^{-12}\Delta$. Higher depth neural networks $>30$ layers require $16$-bit to prevent gradient blow-up [7]; current technique needs degree $127$ $2.1\times$ slower.

- **GPU TCB and multi-tenancy:** CUDA kernels assume honest-but-curious cloud; malicious scheduler can measure NTT kernel timing to infer $s$ Hamming distribution via cache contention. Enclave GPU (H100 confidential compute) adds $23\%$ overhead attestation.

- **TFHE vs CKKS tradeoff emptiness:** TFHE gate bootstrapping per [5] excels at arbitrary non-polynomial ReLU but loses SIMD batching; CKKS loses non-polynomial exactness. Hybrid CHIMERA style switching mod $Q\to q_{LWE}$ conversion noise $2^{-8}$ needs further tuning for mixed networks [5][7].

- **Quantum estimator variance:** lattice estimator security 128-bit sensitive to BKZ $\beta$ model; update from Albrecht 2024 drops effective bits $7$ for $N=65536$, $log Q=1720$ pushing to $N=2^{17}$ unacceptable $2\times$ NTT cost. Tight estimator ongoing [4].
