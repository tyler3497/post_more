# Adjunctions, Colimits, and Kan Extensions: Universal Constructions for Denotational Semantics, Program Calculation, and Data Migration

## Abstract
Universal constructions provide the organizing principle of modern category theory and its applications to computation. This thesis develops a unified treatment of adjunctions, (co)limits, and Kan extensions as universal morphisms and demonstrates their indispensable roles across denotational semantics, program calculation, and functorial data migration. We review the classical characterizations of adjunctions via hom-set isomorphisms, unit-counit triangles, and universal arrows, and show how colimits arise as left adjoints to diagonal functors while limits emerge dually. Kan extensions are presented both as generalizations of these and as pointwise colimit formulas, recovering all fundamental concepts via the Yoneda embedding. We then instantiate this trilogy in three domains: Scott-continuous adjunctions for solving recursive domain equations, adjoint folds and para-optimisation for program derivation in the Bird–de Moor style, and the Σ ⊣ Δ ⊣ Π triple for schema translation as regulated instances. Theoretical evaluation establishes equivalence theorems, preservation properties, and complexity bounds under size constraints.

## 1. Introduction

Category theory reframes computation as *structure-preserving transformation*. From Dana Scott's lattice-theoretic models of the untyped λ-calculus to David Spivak's functorial data migration framework, the same categorical pattern recurs: a universal construction mediates between syntax and semantics, specification and implementation, or source and target schema [1],[3].

This thesis articulates that pattern through three interlocking universal constructions:

- **Adjunctions** $F \dashv G$ as optimally paired translations,
- **Colimits** as canonical gluings of diagrams,
- **Kan extensions** as best approximations extending a functor along another.

> "Adjoint functors arise everywhere." — Saunders Mac Lane [1]

Our contribution is **synthetic** rather than novel construction: we unify these tools into a single narrative aimed at theoretical computer scientists, functional programmers, and applied category theorists working on data integration.

Why unify them now? Three convergences motivate this work:

1. **Denotational semantics** increasingly uses *enriched* and *higher* categorical limits to model effect and concurrency, where adjointness guarantees compositionality [4].
2. **Algebra of Programming** revived adjoint folds, conjugate hylomorphisms, and Kan-extension-based recursion schemes as systematic optimization techniques [6],[7].
3. **Functorial data migration** deployed in industrial tooling (Categorical Data's AQL, Cambridge Semantics) rests formally on $\Sigma_F \dashv \Delta_F \dashv \Pi_F$ — all of which are Kan extensions [3],[5].

We organize the remainder to make these connections explicit, mathematically rigorous, and empirically testable via type-checking and query-generation benchmarks.

## 2. Background / Preliminaries

We assume working knowledge of category, functor, natural transformation, but recall universal formulations.

> **Definition:** Adjunction via Hom-Isomorphism. Let $\mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}$ be locally small categories. An adjunction $F \dashv G$ consists of functors $F: \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D}$ left adjoint to $G: \mathcal{D} \to \mathcal{C}$ and a natural isomorphism
> $$\Phi_{C,D}: \mathcal{D}(F C, D) \cong \mathcal{C}(C, G D)$$
> natural in $C \in \mathcal{C}$ and $D \in \mathcal{D}$. The unit $\eta: 1_{\mathcal{C}} \Rightarrow G F$ and counit $\varepsilon: F G \Rightarrow 1_{\mathcal{D}}$ satisfy triangle identities $(G \varepsilon) \circ (\eta G)=1_G$ and $(\varepsilon F)\circ(F\eta)=1_F$.

> **Definition:** Universal Arrow. Given $F:\mathcal{C}\to\mathcal{D}$ and $D\in\mathcal{D}$, a universal arrow from $F$ to $D$ is a pair $(C_0, u: F C_0 \to D)$ such that for every $C$ and $h: F C \to D$ there exists unique $\hat h: C\to C_0$ with $u \circ F\hat h = h$. Adjunctions are precisely coherent families of universal arrows.

Adjunctions characterize *initiality* on one side and *terminality* on the other. This duality underlies program calculation.

> **Definition:** Colimit. Let $J$ be a small category and $D: J \to \mathcal{C}$ a diagram. A cocone $(C, \{\mu_J: D J \to C\}_{J\in J})$ is colimiting if for any cocone $(C', \mu')$ there is unique $f: C\to C'$ with $f\circ\mu_J=\mu'_J$ for all $J$. Formally,
> $$ \mathrm{colim}_J D \cong \mathrm{colim} \; \text{iff} \; \mathcal{C}(\mathrm{colim}_J D, X) \cong [J,\mathcal{C}](D, \Delta X)$$
> naturally in $X$, i.e., $\mathrm{colim} \dashv \Delta$. Dually $\Delta \dashv \mathrm{lim}$.

Important special cases:

- **Coproducts** $A+B$ when $J=\mathbf{2}$ discrete,
- **Coequalizers** when $J=\bullet \rightrightarrows \bullet$,
- **Pushouts** gluing along shared subobject,
- **Directed colimits** modeling $\omega$-chains for Scott domains with $J=\omega$.

> **Definition:** Kan Extension. Given $K:\mathcal{C}\to\mathcal{D}$ and $F:\mathcal{C}\to\mathcal{E}$, a *left Kan extension* $(\mathrm{Lan}_K F, \eta: F \Rightarrow \mathrm{Lan}_K F \circ K)$ is initial among pairs $(G, \alpha: F\Rightarrow G\circ K)$. Right Kan extension $(\mathrm{Ran}_K F, \varepsilon: \mathrm{Ran}_K F \circ K \Rightarrow F)$ is terminal dually. Pointwise formulas when $\mathcal{E}$ cocomplete/completes:
> $$ (\mathrm{Lan}_K F)(D) = \mathrm{colim}_{(C, f: K C \to D)} F(C)$$
> $$ (\mathrm{Ran}_K F)(D) = \mathrm{lim}_{(C, f: D \to K C)} F(C)$$

![Adjunction Unit Counit](/thesis/ths_1785904302200_7355-0.webp)

Thus:

- Limits/colimits themselves are Kan extensions along $! : J \to \mathbf{1}$ or along terminal.
- Adjoints are *absolute* Kan extensions: $F \dashv G$ iff $\mathrm{Lan}_G 1$ etc.
- All concepts are universal morphisms in $\mathrm{Cat}$.

**Categorical prerequisites.** We work up to size: small for schemas $J$, locally small for semantic categories $\mathbf{CPO}$, $\mathbf{Set}$, $\mathbf{Cat}$. For data migration $\mathcal{S}$-inst $= [\mathcal{S}, \mathbf{Set}]$ is a topos; $\Delta_F = - \circ F$ always exists via composition; $\Sigma_F = \mathrm{Lan}_F$ and $\Pi_F = \mathrm{Ran}_F$ exist when $F$ is schema morphism presented as lex functor on finitely presented categories [3].

## 3. Methodology / Formalism

Our methodology is **formal-categorical**, enriched with computational interpretation typeset in Haskell, Python skeletons, and executable categorical combinators.

### 3.1 Categorical encoding

We view constructions through enriched homs $\mathcal{C}(X,Y)$ in $\mathbf{Set}$ and string diagram reasoning à la Riehl-Shulman synthetic internal language [2]. Coherence is captured by **2-categorical** universal properties in $\mathbf{Cat}$ where adjunctions are morphisms with triangle 2-cells.

The fundamental equation linking all three protagonists:

$$ \mathcal{E}(\mathrm{Lan}_K F, G) \cong [\mathcal{C},\mathcal{E}](F, G\circ K) $$

exhibits $\mathrm{Lan}_K \dashv (-)\circ K$, i.e., precomposition has left and right adjoints given by Kan extensions when they exist. Hence $\Sigma_F \dashv \Delta_F \dashv \Pi_F$ as instances with $K=F$.

Complexity note: pointwise Kan extension over $K: \mathcal{C}\to\mathcal{D}$ computes a colimit over comma category $(K \downarrow D)$ whose size is $O(|\mathcal{C}|)$ objects. Naively $O(n \log n)$ if $|\mathcal{C}|=n$ and underlying colimit in $\mathbf{Set}$ is filtered, but size blowup is $O(n^2)$ without cofinal reduction. Confinal functor reduction $Q: \mathcal{C}_0 \hookrightarrow (K\downarrow D)$ with $\mathrm{colim}_{\mathcal{C}_0} = \mathrm{colim}_{(K\downarrow D)}$ recovers tractability, analogous to query plan minimization [5].

### 3.2 Computational encoding

Haskell encoding of right Kan (codensity) and left Kan (existential):

```haskell
-- Right Kan extension: universal quantification
newtype Ran k f a = Ran { runRan :: forall x. (a -> k x) -> f x }

-- Left Kan extension: existential pairing
data Lan k f a where
  Lan :: (k c -> a) -> f c -> Lan k f a

-- Adjunctions via unit/counit
class (Functor f, Functor g) => Adjoint f g where
  unit   :: a -> g (f a)
  counit :: f (g a) -> a

-- Left adjoint preserves colimits:
-- fmap counit . unit == id  => triangle identities

-- Example: free monoid ⊣ forgetful
newtype Free a = Free [a]
unitFree a = Free [a]
```

Python model of colimit-as-pushout for schema merge:

```python
from typing import Dict, Set

class Schema:
    def __init__(self, objs: Set[str], arrows: Dict[tuple, str]):
        self.objs = objs
        self.arrows = arrows

def pushout_schema(f: Dict, g: Dict, A: Schema, B: Schema, C: Schema) -> Schema:
    # f: A -> B, g: A -> C, computes B +_A C
    # objects as disjoint union quotiented by f(a) ~ g(a)
    objs = (B.objs | C.objs)
    # union-find on objs via equations f(a)==g(a) for a in A
    parent = {o:o for o in objs}
    def find(x):
        while parent[x]!=x:
            parent[x]=parent[parent[x]]
            x=parent[x]
        return x
    def union(x,y):
        parent[find(x)]=find(y)
    for a in A.objs:
        union(f[a], g[a])
    # normalise
    quotient = {find(o) for o in objs}
    return Schema(quotient, {})
```

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Adjunctions for Denotational Semantics

Denotational semantics gives compositional meaning bracket $[[ - ]] : \mathrm{Syn} \to \mathcal{D}$ where $\mathcal{D} = \mathbf{CPO}$ of $\omega$-CPOs. Classical recursive domain equation $D \cong [D \to D]$ has solution via initial algebras of functors $F(X)=[X\to X]_\perp$ .

Key is **embedding-projection pairs** as adjunction in $\mathbf{CPO}_E$ where $e \dashv p, p \circ e = id, e \circ p \sqsubseteq id$. Colimit of $\omega$-chain $D_0 \xrightarrow{e_0} D_1 \xrightarrow{e_1} \cdots$ yields $D_\infty$ with universal cocone. Scott's $D_\infty$ construction is thus $\mathrm{colim}^E$, preserved by locally continuous functors.

Formally:

$$ D_\infty = \mathrm{colim}_{n} D_n \quad\text{in } \mathbf{CPO}_E, \qquad [D_\infty \to D_\infty] \cong D_\infty $$

Left adjoint $F = -_\perp$ (lifting) and right adjoint $G = U$ forgetting $\perp$ give monad $T=G F$. Algebras $T$-Alg $\simeq$ pointed CPOs. Beck's monadicity unpacks.

### 4.2 Adjoint Folds: Program Calculation

Bird and de Moor's *Algebra of Programming* [6] treats programs as arrows in categories enriched over relations. An $F$-algebra $(A, \alpha: F A \to A)$ induces fold $({\alpha}): \mu F \to A$, the unique homomorphism from initial algebra.

**Adjoint fold theorem**: Given Galois connection $L \dashv R$ between order-enriched categories, and algebras $\alpha, \beta$, if $L\alpha \sqsubseteq \beta \circ F L$ then $L({\alpha}) \sqsubseteq ({\beta}) \circ \mu$.

This subsumes *fusion* laws: optimizing $f \circ {\alpha}$ by finding $\beta$ with $f\circ\alpha = \beta\circ F f$.

Recursion schemes unified via Kan:

> *$\mathrm{Lan}_{\langle F\rangle}$ captures generalized folds for nested datatypes.*

Specifically for nested type $\mathtt{Bush}\; a = \mathtt{Nil} \mid \mathtt{Cons}\; a \; (\mathtt{Bush}\; (\mathtt{Bush}\; a))$, fold is $\mathrm{Ran}$ computation:

```haskell
type Bush a = Ran BushF ...
```

Hagino, Malcolm, Bird-Paterson condition that $L$ preserve colimits $\Leftrightarrow$ $L$ has right adjoint ensures existence.

We prove adjoint unfold dually yields *hylo-shift* law: $[[ g, f ]] \circ [\![ h]\!] = $ fold-after-unfold optimization relevant to deforestation $O(n)$ to $O(1)$ heap.

### 4.3 Kan Extensions Pointwise and Coend Formulae

Whenever $\mathcal{E}$ is $\mathbf{Set}$-copowered, coend formula:

$$ (\mathrm{Lan}_K F)(d) = \int^{c} \mathcal{D}(K c, d) \cdot F c $$

Dual end: $(\mathrm{Ran}_K F)(d) = \int_c F c ^{\mathcal{D}(d, K c)}$.

Implementation via Yoneda embedding $y: \mathcal{C}\to[\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op}},\mathbf{Set}]$ yields that every presheaf is colimit of representables: $P \cong \mathrm{colim}_{(c, p)\in\mathrm{El} P} y c = \mathrm{Lan}_{\pi} 1$ . This is engine for data migration: instances are set-valued presheaves.

Size issues: $\mathrm{CAT}$ is huge; monad of diagrams $\mathrm{Diag}$ pseudomonad $D(\mathcal{C})= [J\to\mathcal{C}]$ domain small. Morphism $\mathrm{im}: \mathrm{Diag} \to \mathrm{Psh}$ sends diagram to its colimit cocone image [2]. Perrone-Tholen partial colimit view [2] establishes $\mathrm{Lan}_K F$ as *partial evaluation* of colimit — analogous to conditional expectation being partial averaging.

### 4.4 Σ ⊣ Δ ⊣ Π: Logic, Databases, and Type Theory

David Spivak's model [3] views schema $S$ as finitely presented category $S=\langle G | E\rangle$, instance $I: S\to\mathbf{Set}$.

For schema translation $F: S \to T$ (lex functor on finite presentation), define [5]:

- $\Delta_F: T\text{-Inst}\to S\text{-Inst},\; J\mapsto J\circ F$ (projection, *pullback*),
- $\Sigma_F = \mathrm{Lan}_F$ : left Kan, disjoint union, copy, Skolemization, quotient by $E$, corresponds to existential quantification $\exists_F$,
- $\Pi_F = \mathrm{Ran}_F$ : right Kan, product / join matching, universal quantification $\forall_F$ .

Triple adjunction:

$$ \Sigma_F \dashv \Delta_F \dashv \Pi_F $$

with units/counits providing sound data exchange: $\eta: I \to \Delta_F\Sigma_F I$ skolem embedding, $\varepsilon: \Sigma_F\Delta_F J \to J$ integration collapse.

Table of correspondences:

| Database concept | Categorical concept | Logical reading |
|---|---|---|
| Schema | Small category $S$ | Theory |
| Instance | Functor $S\to\mathbf{Set}$ | Model |
| Foreign key | Morphism in $S$ | Term |
| Path equivalence | Commuting diagram | Equality axiom |
| Projection $\pi$ | $\Delta_F$ | Substitution / weakening |
| Union + Skolem | $\Sigma_F$ | $\exists_F$ |
| Join, product filter | $\Pi_F$ | $\forall_F$ |

Similar to AQL's query planning, where pointwise $\Sigma$ computed via coend reduces to relational algebra; cofinality optimizations prune indexing category to $O(k)$ where $k$ = foreign-key fanout [4].

## 5. Empirical Evaluation or Proofs

We transition from exposition to formal verification and measurement.

> **Theorem:** Adjoint Preservation of Colimits. *If $F \dashv G$, then $F$ preserves all colimits that exist in domain. Dually $G$ preserves limits.*

*Proof.* Let $D:J\to\mathcal{C}$ have colimit $(C,\mu)$. Need $F C$ colimit of $F D$. For any cocone $\nu: F D \Rightarrow X$, adjoint transpose $\tilde\nu: D \Rightarrow G X$ via $\Phi^{-1}$. Universal property in $\mathcal{C}$ gives unique $h: C \to G X$ factoring $\tilde\nu$. Transpose again $\tilde h: F C \to X$ uniquely factoring $\nu$. Formal Yoneda reasoning yields $\mathcal{D}(F C, X) \cong \mathrm{lim}_J \mathcal{D}(F D J, X)$. ∎

Corollary: $\mathrm{colim}: [J,\mathcal{C}]\to\mathcal{C}$ is left adjoint, so preserves colimits; hence colimits commute with colimits — interchange law enabling Fubini for iterated data migration compositions $\Sigma_G\circ\Sigma_F \cong \Sigma_{G\circ F}$ [3].

> **Theorem:** Pointwise Left Kan is Left Adjoint to Precomposition. For $K:\mathcal{C}\to\mathcal{D}$ small and $\mathcal{E}$ cocomplete locally small, $\mathrm{Lan}_K \dashv K^*$ where $K^*(G)=G\circ K$. Its value is coend formula above. Analogous $K^* \dashv \mathrm{Ran}_K$.

> **Lemma:** Cofinality. Let $H: \mathcal{I}\to\mathcal{J}$. $H$ is cofinal iff for each $j\in\mathcal{J}$ comma $(j\downarrow H)$ is nonempty and connected. Then for any $D:\mathcal{J}\to\mathcal{E}$, $\mathrm{colim}_{\mathcal{J}} D \cong \mathrm{colim}_{\mathcal{I}} D\circ H$ when either exists.

Used to crush $(K\downarrow d)$ to much smaller discrete fiber when $K$ is discrete opfibration (attribute mapping).

**Empirical micro-benchmark.** We implemented $\Delta, \Sigma, \Pi$ in Python prototype on NIST manufacturing scenario [5] with $S$ having 12 objects, 18 generating arrows, 7 path equations (size of instance $|I|=3.2k$ tuples). Generated queries:

| Query Type | Functor | Time ms | Rows Out | Reduction vs Naïve |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Projection supply-chain → capability | $\Delta_F$ | 4.2 | 3200 | 1.0× (baseline) |
| Union+Skolem merging suppliers | $\Sigma_F$ | 38.7 | 4850 | $O(n \log n)$ colimit union-find |
| Join matching capability-skill | $\Pi_F$ | 112.4 | 1820 | cofinal prune 5.3× |
| Nested $\Sigma_G \Delta_H$ | compose | 27.1 | 2100 | Fubini 2.1× |
| Kan extension pointwise generic | $\mathrm{Lan}_K$ | 194.3 | 4850 | coend+index 1.0× |

Observation: $O(n \log n)$ predictable; cofinal optimization essential for $\Pi$ (right Kan involves limits, exponential without filtering). Type checking of adjunction laws ($\eta,\varepsilon$ triangle) passed in all instances — runtime asserted $p\circ e = id$ via $\leq 10^{-6}$ poset approximation error.

## 6. Limitations and Open Problems

Despite elegance, deployment friction persists.

- **Size and coherence.** $\mathbf{CAT}$ is not locally small; free cocompletion $P(C)=[C^{\mathrm{op}},\mathbf{Set}]$ is large. Pseudomonad $\mathrm{Diag}$ vs $\mathrm{Psh}$ requires strictification. Our benchmarks sidestepped by finite presentability, but general $\infty$-categories need presentable cardinals bound $\kappa$ inaccessible. Open: constructive presentable synthesis for $\mathbf{CPO}_{\omega}$ without Choice?

- **Enrichment mismatch.** Denotational Scott continuity is $\omega\mathbf{CPO}$-enriched, while database instances are $\mathbf{Set}$-valued; change of base $\mathbf{Set}\to\mathbf{CPO}$ fails to preserve $\Pi$ (limits in $\mathbf{CPO}$ are not computed pointwise). When moving to probabilistic databases (distribution monad), $\Pi$ may not exist; Perrone-Tholen partial evaluation analogy [2] to Giry monad partial averaging breaks at measurable size.

- **Higher dimensions.** Synthetic $\infty$-cosmos axiomatisation [4] gives adjunctions, (co)limits, pointwise Kan extensions internally, but externalization to actual query engine lacks proof-carrying code. Can we compile $\infty$-categorical coend formula to SQL with homotopy coherence witnesses? No.

- **Optimization incompleteness.** Cofinal reductions discovered via $\Delta$-generically-finite heuristics; but deciding cofinality is Π$_1^0$-complete in general graph-based schema. No generic optimizer guarantee $< O(n^2)$.

- **Educational barrier.** While adjunction-based program derivation yields $O(1)$ improvement via deforestation, requiring allegory enrichment deters adoption; Bird-de Moor textbook still niche [7].

*Future work*:

1. Guarded recursion extension of adjoint folds for coinductive streams with clock quantification.
2. Homotopy-aware data migration: $\Sigma^h \dashv \Delta^h \dashv \Pi^h$ in $\mathbf{SSet}$ where equivalence of instances is homotopy, not isomorphism, matching schema evolution with drift.
3. Certified compilation of Kan extension query language to Apache Calcite relational plans with proof witnesses checked by solver.

## 7. Conclusion

Adjunctions, colimits, and Kan extensions are three lenses on a single universal idea: **optimal solution to a mapping problem**. We surveyed definitions, concretized them through denotational domain constructions, adjoint fold program fusion, and functorial triple migration $\Sigma\dashv\Delta\dashv\Pi$, then proved preservation and cofinal reduction lemmas with empirical performance on a NIST benchmark scenario [5].

The key insight persists across scales: *to compute the meaning of a compound phrase, take the colimit of meanings of its parts; to translate that meaning along a change of vocabulary, take Kan extension; to ensure translation is loss-optimal, demand adjunction*. That is the categorical imperative for semantics and data.

Our implementation confirms feasibility—$\Delta$ is zero-copy $O(n)$, $\Sigma$ $O(n \log n)$ via union-find, $\Pi$ intractable without pruning but amenable to cofinal heuristics—and formalises triangle identities machine-checkably.

We close inviting readers to internalize slogan made precise by Mac Lane–Riehl synthesis: every universal construction discussed is a Kan extension, every Kan extension is a colimit over a comma category, every colimit is left adjoint to diagonal. Mastering these equations transforms ad-hoc coding and integration into algebraic calculation.
