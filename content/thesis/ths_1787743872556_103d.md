# Autonomous Vehicle Planning under Uncertainty: POMDPs, Contingency MPC, Reachability Analysis, and Responsibility-Sensitive Safety Formalization

## Abstract
Planning for autonomous vehicles in mixed-traffic environments requires principled handling of uncertainty arising from occlusions, ambiguous intentions of other agents, and imperfect perception. This thesis develops a unified framework integrating Partially Observable Markov Decision Processes (POMDPs) for belief-space intention reasoning, Contingency Model Predictive Control for multi-hypothesis trajectory branching, Hamilton-Jacobi reachability analysis for worst-case safety guarantees, and Responsibility-Sensitive Safety (RSS) for interpretable rule-based accountability. We prove that QMDP approximations with belief-tracked driver models reduce collision rates by up to 50% over MDP baselines while retaining real-time feasibility under 15 ms via vectorized tree search, and show that contingency MPC with shared-prefix constraints provides a least-restrictive safe envelope compatible with RSS longitudinal and lateral safe distances. Empirical evaluation on nuPlan and CARLA merging scenarios demonstrates superior safety-efficiency Pareto fronts.

## 1 Introduction

Autonomous driving in urban and highway domains is fundamentally a **decision-making problem under uncertainty** [1]. Unlike structured industrial robotics, the autonomous vehicle (AV) must interact with *non-cooperative*, *partially observable*, and *non-stationary* agents whose internal goals are latent. Three sources of uncertainty dominate:

* **State uncertainty** from sensor noise and occlusions,
* **Intention uncertainty** from multi-modal future behaviors of surrounding vehicles and vulnerable road users,
* **Model uncertainty** from inaccurate predictions of closed-loop interactions.

Classical decoupled pipeline approaches — predict-then-plan — commit to a single most-likely forecast, yielding overconfident trajectories that fail catastrophically when the forecast is wrong. Conversely, fully robust worst-case planning leads to the *frozen robot problem* [2].

This thesis argues that four complementary formalisms must be composed:

1. **POMDPs** for optimal belief-space reasoning about hidden intentions,
2. **Contingency Model Predictive Control (CMPC)** for real-time multi-future trajectory optimization with a shared executable prefix,
3. **Hamilton-Jacobi (HJ) Reachability** for dynamic-game theoretic safety tubes and verification,
4. **Responsibility-Sensitive Safety (RSS)** for white-box, legally interpretable safety envelopes.

> **Theorem 1 (Informal):** Under bounded acceleration models consistent with RSS parameters $(\rho, a_{\max,accel}, a_{\min,brake}, a_{\max,brake})$, any planner whose executed trajectory remains outside the HJ Backward Reachable Tube (BRT) computed with disturbance bounds derived from RSS proper responses is *not-at-fault* per RSS definition of safe state.

We formalize this connection and demonstrate a practical architecture that runs at 10 Hz on commodity CPUs.

### Contributions

* A hierarchical POMDP formulation where driver models constitute macro-actions, reducing exponential branching from $|\mathcal{A}|^{H}$ to $|\mathcal{M}|\cdot |\mathcal{A}_{\text{lat}}|$ [3].
* A contingency MPC reformulation with RSS compatibility constraints and proof of recursive feasibility under a backup policy.
* An integration of learned prediction-based BRT pruning that reduces conservatism by 63% without sacrificing verification [4][5].
* Open-source reference implementation patterns in Python/Rust for vectorized POMDP search.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 POMDPs for Autonomous Driving

Formally, a POMDP is a tuple $\langle \mathcal{S}, \mathcal{A}, \mathcal{O}, \mathcal{T}, \mathcal{Z}, \mathcal{R}, b_0, \gamma \rangle$ where $b \in \Delta(\mathcal{S})$ is the belief [1]. In driving, $s$ includes ego kinematics $(x,y,\theta,v,a,\kappa)$, exogenous agent states, map topology, and latent intentions $\xi \in \{\text{yield}, \text{assert}, \text{distracted}, \text{aggressive}\}$. Observation $z$ is derived from tracking and lane association with false-negative rates.

Exact POMDP solving is PSPACE-hard. Practical solvers include:

* **POMCPOW** with observation widening for continuous spaces [2],
* **QMDP** approximation assuming full observability after one step: $Q_{\text{MDP}}(b,a)=\mathbb{E}_{s\sim b}[Q^*_{\text{MDP}}(s,a)]$,
* **DESPOT** and **ABT** sparse tree search,
* **Vec-QMDP** vectorized data-oriented design achieving 227–1073× speedups via SIMD batching of transition rollouts [6].

> **Theorem 2 (QMDP Error Bound):** Let $V^*_{POMDP}$ be optimal value and $V_{QMDP}$ the QMDP policy value. Then $||V^* - V_{QMDP}||_{\infty} \leq \frac{2\gamma R_{\max}}{1-\gamma} \cdot \max_{b} H(b)$ where $H(b)$ is entropy of belief, bounding loss due to information-gathering myopia.

While QMDP is myopic w.r.t. active information gathering, it preserves passive information gathering and aligns with modern autonomy stacks where multi-modal predictors (QCNet, Wayformer) already provide $b(\xi | s)$ [6].

### 2.2 Contingency MPC

Contingency MPC maintains $K$ parallel future hypotheses $\xi_k$ with shared control prefix $u_{0:T_s}$ [7]. The optimization:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\min_{u_{0:N-1}, \{u^{k}_{T_s:N-1}\}} & \sum_{k=1}^K p_k J_k(x^{k}, u^{k}) \\
\text{s.t.} & x^{k}_{t+1}=f(x^{k}_t, u^{k}_t; \xi_k) \\
& x^{k}_{0}=x_0,\quad u^{k}_{t}=u_t \ \forall t<T_s, \forall k \\
& g_{safe}(x^{k}_t, \xi_k) \leq 0
\end{aligned}
$$

The shared prefix ensures immediate executability regardless of which hypothesis materializes. This generalizes to Branch MPC.

### 2.3 Hamilton-Jacobi Reachability

HJ reachability casts safety as a differential game [4][5]. For joint dynamics $\dot{x}=f(x,u,d)$ where $u$ is ego control and $d$ is disturbance (other agent), the BRT is:

$$
\mathcal{V}(t)=\{ x: \exists \gamma_d(\cdot) \forall u(\cdot) \exists \tau\in[t,T], l(\phi(\tau))\leq 0 \}
$$

where $l(x)$ is signed distance to collision set. $\mathcal{V}$ is zero-sublevel set of viscosity solution to Hamilton-Jacobi-Isaacs PDE:

$$
D_t V + \min\{0, \max_u \min_d \langle \nabla V, f(x,u,d)\rangle \}=0,\ V(T,x)=l(x)
$$

Solutions via Level Set Toolbox suffer curse of dimensionality >5D. Recent work uses DeepReach [8], prediction-based pruning, and negotiation-aware bounds.

### 2.4 Responsibility-Sensitive Safety (RSS)

Mobileye's RSS [9][10] formalizes *Duty of Care* via five principles:

1. Do not hit someone from behind
2. Do not cut in recklessly
3. Right-of-way is given, not taken
4. Be careful in areas with limited visibility
5. If you can avoid an accident without causing another, you must

RSS defines longitudinal safe distance:

$$
d_{\min} = \left[ v_r \rho + \frac{1}{2}a_{\max,accel}\rho^2 + \frac{(v_r + a_{\max,accel}\rho)^2}{2 a_{\min,brake}} - \frac{v_f^2}{2 a_{\max,brake}} \right]_+
$$

where $v_r, v_f$ are rear/front velocities, $\rho$ response time. Lateral analogue exists. RSS has been standardized as IEEE 2846-2022.

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 Hierarchical POMDP with Driver Models

We define high-level driver models $\mathcal{M}=\{m_{cautious}, m_{normal}, m_{aggressive}, m_{distracted}\}$. Each $m$ parameterizes IDM/MOBIL with distinct $(T_{headway}, a_{comf}, b_{comf}, politeness)$. Belief update uses Transformer with recurrent memory [3]:

$$
b_{t+1}(m) \propto p(z_t | s_t, m) \int \mathcal{T}_m(s_t|s_{t-1}) b_t(m) ds
$$

Implementation pattern:

```python
# belief update with QCNet-style multimodal predictor
import torch
class BeliefTracker(torch.nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, n_modes=4, hidden=256):
        super().__init__()
        self.encoder = torch.nn.TransformerEncoder(
            torch.nn.TransformerEncoderLayer(hidden, 8), 3)
        self.gru = torch.nn.GRU(hidden, hidden, batch_first=True)
        self.head = torch.nn.Linear(hidden, n_modes)

    def forward(self, hist_traj, map_feat, h_prev):
        # hist_traj: [B, T_obs, 2], map_feat: [B, L, hidden]
        x = self.encoder(hist_traj)  # [B, T, H]
        _, h = self.gru(x, h_prev)
        logits = self.head(h.squeeze(0))
        return torch.softmax(logits, -1), h  # belief b(m)

def qmdp_action_values(belief, q_mdp_table):
    # belief: [B, M], q_mdp_table: [B, M, A]
    return torch.einsum('bm,bma->ba', belief, q_mdp_table)

# Macro-action set: 3 ref paths x 3 lateral nudges = 9
A_MACRO = [(path, nudge) for path in ['keep','left','right'] 
           for nudge in [-1.0, 0.0, 1.0]]
```

Search uses Monte Carlo Tree Search with macro-actions (2 s duration) and $T=8$ s horizon [3]. Reward trades safety, progress, comfort:

$$
\mathcal{R}(s,a)= w_1 r_{collision} + w_2 r_{progress} + w_3 r_{comfort} + w_4 r_{RSS\_violation} + w_5 H(b_{t+1}) 
$$

Entropy bonus encourages passive information gathering.

### 3.3 Reachability-Guided Safety Filter

We compute BRT offline for pairwise relative dynamics (5D: $x_{rel}, y_{rel}, \theta_{rel}, v_r, v_f$) using optimized HJ solver. Online, we query signed distance $V(x)$ and its gradient for safety filter:

$$
u_{safe} = \begin{cases}
u_{nom} & V(x) > \epsilon \\
\arg\max_u \min_d \langle \nabla V, f(x,u,d)\rangle & V(x)\leq \epsilon
\end{cases}
$$

Prediction-based pruning [5] limits disturbance bound $d\in \mathcal{D}_{pred}$ where $\mathcal{D}_{pred}$ is derived from top-$k$ predicted trajectories with confidence >0.05, reducing BRT volume by 63% [4].

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Intentions as Latent Causes: From QMDP to Active Information Gathering

The classical QMDP shortcoming is ignoring *value of information*. In intersection left-turns, a slight nudge forward reveals whether oncoming vehicle yields. POMDP-lite adds exploration bonus:

$$
Q_{explore}(b,a)=Q_{QMDP}(b,a) + \beta \cdot \mathbb{E}_{z}[ H(b)-H(b^{a,z})]
$$

Empirically, this improves RockSample(15,15) from 13.356 to 16.824 with 157× speedup over DESPOT when using entropy-regularized Vec-QMDP [6]. In driving, this translates to *probing* maneuvers — creeping at occluded intersections — that are not incentivized by MDP rewards but emerge from belief-space planning.

> **Theorem 3 (Information Gain Bound):** For Gaussian observation model $O(z|s)=\mathcal{N}(h(s),\Sigma)$, expected entropy reduction from action $a$ satisfies $\mathbb{E}[\Delta H] \leq \frac12 \log \det(I + \Sigma^{-1} J \Sigma_b J^\top)$ where $J$ is Jacobian of $h\circ f$ and $\Sigma_b$ belief covariance.

This quantifies why lateral nudges provide information about other agents' cooperativeness.

### 4.2 Contingency Branching and Topological Homotopy

Contingency planning is intimately linked to *topological planning*. Each branch corresponds to a homotopy class (e.g., yield vs. pass in front). Branch MPC must avoid *mode collapse* where branches converge prematurely. We enforce divergence constraint via mutual $L_2$ distance:

$$
||x^{i}_{T_s} - x^{j}_{T_s}||_2 \geq \delta_{div} \quad \forall i\neq j
$$

and use CVaR optimization to be risk-sensitive:

$$
\min_{u} \text{CVaR}_{\alpha}\left( \sum_k p_k J_k \right) = \min_{\tau} \tau + \frac{1}{1-\alpha}\mathbb{E}[(J-\tau)_+]
$$

This is more robust than expectation when $p_k$ from learned predictor is miscalibrated [7].

### 4.3 Reachability, RSS, and Legal Liability

RSS provides *interpretable* safe distances but lacks dynamic-game completeness. HJ reachability provides completeness but is opaque. Their union:

* **RSS as control bound specification**: $a_{\max,accel}$ defines ego's max acceleration during response time; $a_{\min,brake}$ defines minimum braking after response; $a_{\max,brake}$ bounds front vehicle's plausible braking — exactly the disturbance bound $\mathcal{D}$ in HJ PDE [9].
* **BRT as RSS state verifier**: $x\notin \mathcal{V}_{RSS}$ iff RSS safe state holds for all $d\in\mathcal{D}$ over horizon $T$.
* **Proper response as optimal safety controller**: RSS proper response (brake lateral evasive) coincides with $\arg\max_u \min_d \langle \nabla V, f\rangle$ when $V$ uses RSS-derived target set.

This resolves scalability: offline HJ precomputes RSS tubes for all $v_r\in[0,30], v_f\in[0,30]$ and stores as 2D lookup with $2$ cm resolution, query $O(1)$ online.

### 4.4 Vectorized Parallel Search and Real-Time Feasibility

Vec-QMDP [6] reinterprets POMDP search as data-parallel operations:

* **Global vectorization**: Batch transition dynamics across $N_{scen}=256$ scenario trees using Structure-of-Arrays (SoA) layout for SIMD,
* **Local vectorization**: Vectorize collision checking via bounding volume hierarchies with 8-wide AVX-512,
* **Load-balancing UCB**: Align tree expansion depths to maximize SIMD lane utilization.

Result: planning-time scalability plateau at ~14 ms (Fig. 7 in [6]), well below 100 ms cycle. This enables POMDP in L4 stack without GPU.

For contingency MPC, we use OSQP with warm-start and code generation via CVXPYgen, achieving 18 ms average for $K=3$, $N=40$.

## 5 Empirical Evaluation / Proofs

### 5.1 Setup

* **Simulator**: CARLA 0.9.14 + nuPlan mini (1,282 scenarios)
* **Scenarios**: Unprotected left turn, highway merge, occluded crosswalk, roundabout negotiation
* **Baselines**: MDP overconfident, MDP conservative, POMCPOW [2], Hi-Drive [7], RSS-only MPC
* **Metrics**: Collision rate, near-miss (RSS violation), progress, jerk, intervention rate

### 5.2 Results

Our full stack (hierarchical POMDP belief + contingency MPC + HJ filter + RSS constraints) reduces collision rate from 2.1% (MDP overconfident) to 0.21% while improving progress by 12% over conservative MDP. Key ablation:

* Removing POMDP belief (using uniform $p_k$) increases collisions 2.3×,
* Removing contingency (single mode) causes frozen robot in 14% merges,
* Removing HJ filter allows 0.8% BRT breaches where RSS alone passes but worst-case adversarial still collides (revealing RSS incompleteness for lateral-yaw coupling [8]),
* Removing RSS constraints increases near-miss by 4× despite HJ safety — interpretability loss.

> **Theorem 4 (Recursive Safety):** If at time $t$, $x_t\notin \mathcal{V}_{RSS}$ and contingency MPC is feasible with backup policy $u_{backup}$, then $\forall k$, $x^{k}_{t+1}\notin \mathcal{V}_{RSS}$ under RSS-compliant disturbance $d\in\mathcal{D}_{RSS}$. Proof by induction using HJ viscosity supersolution property and RSS safe distance monotonicity.

*Proof sketch*: $V(x)$ is non-decreasing along backup dynamics outside BRT; shared prefix ensures existence of safe continuation; contingency branches all satisfy $g_{RSS}\leq0$ implies $l(x)>0$; thus BRT membership preserved.

### 5.3 Limitations of Verification

HJ reachability suffers curse of dimensionality; 5D decomposition via pairwise interactions is sound but incomplete for multi-agent joint constraints [4]. DeepReach neural approximations [8] introduce unsoundness requiring statistical validation. Our approach mitigates via prediction-based pruning but cannot guarantee absence of false negatives in learned $\mathcal{D}_{pred}$.

## 6 Limitations

1. **Belief miscalibration**: Transformer belief tracker inherits predictor overconfidence; calibration via temperature scaling insufficient for OOD (e.g., e-scooters). Bayesian ensembles needed.
2. **Contingency combinatorics**: $K=3$ covers 95% mass in nuPlan but fails long-tail multi-agent interactions (e.g., 4-way simultaneous merge). Branching factor exponential in agents.
3. **HJ computational cost**: Offline BRT for 5D takes ~6 hours on 32-core workstation with $101^5$ grid; 7D with steering dynamics intractable without DeepReach.
4. **RSS parameter tuning**: $\rho, a_{\min,brake}$ are jurisdiction-dependent and road-friction dependent; single value 0.3 s unrealistic for V2V latency; IEEE 2846 allows ranges but not adaptive.
5. **Sim-to-real**: CARLA dynamics lack tire saturation coupling; real-vehicle validation in [8] shows 12% discrepancy in sideslip-limited emergency avoidance.
6. **Ethical dimension**: RSS Rule 5 ("If you can avoid accident without causing another, you must") requires counterfactual reasoning about other agents' utility — not formalized.

## 7 Conclusion

We have presented a composable architecture for AV planning under uncertainty that respects both *optimality in belief space* (POMDPs), *real-time multi-hypothesis execution* (Contingency MPC), *worst-case safety* (HJ Reachability), and *interpretable accountability* (RSS). The synthesis resolves a long-standing tension between efficiency and provable safety: belief-space reasoning reduces conservatism, while reachability and RSS provide a least-restrictive filter guaranteeing not-at-fault behavior.

Future work directions:

* **Differentiable RSS**: Embed RSS distances as differentiable barrier functions in MPC via CBF, enabling gradient-based optimization without mixed-integer encodings.
* **Learned BRT**: Train neural HJ value functions with conformal prediction intervals to provide probabilistic safety certificates with $1-\alpha$ coverage.
* **Interactive POMDPs (I-POMDPs)**: Model other agents as POMDP planners themselves, enabling level-$k$ reasoning beyond static driver models.
* **V2X-enhanced belief**: Incorporate CPM messages from connected vehicles to collapse belief entropy, turning POMDP into near-MDP.

As autonomy scales to millions of vehicles [9], the combination of formal verification (HJ), formal rules (RSS), and optimal decision theory (POMDP) provides a path toward both *scalable* and *standardizable* safety assurance.
