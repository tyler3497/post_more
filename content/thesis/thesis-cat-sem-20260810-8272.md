# Categorical Semantics of Dependent Type Theory with Cohesive Modalities and Synthetic Guarded Domain Theory: Carboni-Rosolini Predomains, Later Modality, Clock Quantification, and Normalization-by-Evaluation for Cubical Homotopy Type Theory

## Abstract

We develop a unified categorical account of dependent type theory extended with cohesive modalities and synthetic guarded domain theory, placing normalization-by-evaluation for cubical homotopy type theory within the same framework. Building on categories with families and natural models, we treat cohesive adjoint quadruples integral - flat - sharp as lex idempotent modalities internalized via dual-context Fitch-style rules, and interpret Carboni-Rosolini partial map classifiers as predomains in the topos of trees S = PSh(omega). The later modality as an endofunctor on S is shown to commute with clock quantification forall kappa, yielding intensional guarded recursion encapsulated by Lob induction. We prove adequacy of synthetic guarded domain constructions for PCF/FPC and relate countable products of points to clock irrelevance. Finally, we embed cartesian cubical type theory into multimodal dependent type theory MTT via synthetic Tait computability in the Artin gluing Gl(F) along the nerve of cubes, deriving a reduction-free normalization function that is stable under cubical Kan operations and univalence, with decidability of judgmental equality and injectivity of type constructors as corollaries.

## 1. Introduction

**Dependent type theory** occupies a double role as both a foundation for constructive mathematics and the core calculus of proof assistants, yet its categorical semantics remains subtle when *modal extensions* are introduced to capture spatial, cohesive, and temporal phenomena. This thesis isolates a common fibred structure underlying three seemingly disjoint advances: **(i)** cohesive homotopy type theory with shape modalities int, flat, sharp; **(ii)** synthetic guarded domain theory via the later modality later_kappa and clock quantification forall kappa; and **(iii)** cartesian cubical type theory equipped with **normalization-by-evaluation (NbE)** via synthetic Tait computability.

We argue that **natural models** in the sense of Awodey and categories with families (CwFs) supply a 2-categorical *mode theory* in the sense of Gratzer et al. [1][2] whose diagram of models M:J->Cat with Rezk completion can interpret both cohesive and guarded modalities using the same context-lock mechanism. *Cohesion* in the sense of Lawvere and Schreiber-Shulman [3] yields an adjoint string int -| flat -| sharp -| flat sharp internalized by crispness annotations; **guarded recursion** in the topos of trees PSh(omega) yields later with Lob rule Gamma, later A |- A / Gamma |- A and fixpoint fix:(later A-> A)-> A; *clock quantification* forall kappa A acts as an escape hatch from guarded world to coinductive final coalgebras nu F = forall kappa. nu^kappa F.

Our contributions are:

- A **cohesive CwF** definition with locally cartesian closed fibration p:E->M where each cohesive modality is a lex idempotent comonad interpreted by morphism of natural models [4][5].
- Identification of **Carboni-Rosolini predomains** with partial map classifiers in S via Rosolini dominance Sigma, showing L A = A +_Sigma 1 as synthetic lift monad [6].
- Proof that forall kappa commutes with finite products of later-algebras under clock irrelevance, enabling adequacy proofs for guarded PCF.
- A **synthetic gluing** construction Gl(nerve:PSh(cube)->PSh(Lambda)) whose internal lex modalities circ, bullet realize **normalization for cubical type theory** in the style of Sterling-Angiuli [7][8], closing decidability of definitional equality and injectivity.

> **Theorem 1 (Cohesive Modal Soundness).** Let C be a locally cartesian closed category with finite colimits modelling extensional MLTT. For any finite mode theory N with adjoint triple int -| flat -| sharp where flat is lex and idempotent, there exists a model of MTT over N with these modalities interpreted as morphisms of natural models preserving comprehension up to isomorphism.

> **Theorem 2 (Clock Irrelevance Commutation).** In PSh(omega^K) with K clocks, N is clock-irrelevant then forall kappa.(N x later_kappa X) iso N x forall kappa.X; the finite powerset P_f also commutes with forall kappa.

These frame our *synthetic* treatment: denotational semantics inspires new typing constructions as in prior domain theory, monads, and HoTT.

## 2. Background

### 2.1 Categories with Families and Natural Models

Categories with families **CwFs** consist of category C of contexts, presheaf Ty:C^op->Set of types, and Tm:int Ty->Set. **Natural models** [9] are representable map p:tilde U -> U in PSh(C); *morphism of natural models* preserves this structure. Weak morphisms F:C->D sending tilde U to families of D interpret modal operators as diagrams of CwFs [10].

### 2.2 Cohesion and Shape Modalities

**Cohesive infinity-topos** H admits adjoint quadruple int -| flat -| sharp -| flat sharp where flat is discrete coreflection, sharp is codiscrete reflection, int is shape/probe, with *crisp* variables flat-modal. In type theory, Shulman [11] gives *real-cohesive HoTT* with axioms for R, Brouwer fixed-point proven via sharp. Parametricity via cohesion [12] observes *modal aspect* of Reynolds parametricity internalized as cohesion.

### 2.3 Guarded Domain Theory and Clocks

The topos of trees S=PSh(omega) has objects (X_n,r_n) n in omega with restriction r_n:X_{n+1}-> X_n. The **later modality** (later X)_0=1, (later X)_{n+1}=X_n, with next:X->later X and applicative circledast:later(A->B)->later A->later B.

**Delayed substitutions** x<-a generalize circledast for dependent types: Gamma,later_kappa[x<-t]A crucial for gDTT [13]. Lob rule yields guarded recursion:

```haskell
-- Guarded fixpoint as in gDTT
fix :: (Later a -> a) -> a
fix f = f (Next (fix f))

strMap :: (a -> b) -> Str a -> Str b
strMap f s = (f (hd s) : Next (strMap f <$> tl s))
```

Clock quantification forall kappa A *quantifies away* intensional step-index nature. Naive termination in guarded logic predicts every loop terminates in <n steps for all n, i.e., progress not termination; clock quantification escapes to ordinary mathematics [14].

### 2.4 Cubical Type Theory and Synthetic Tait Computability

Cartesian cubical type theory adds interval I De Morgan algebra, cofibrations F, composition comp^i A [phi|-> u] u0. Univalence computable. Normalization open problem closed by **Sterling-Angiuli 2021** [15][16] using *synthetic Tait computability* (STC): embed model into PSh(C), glue along Gl(F:PSh(C)->PSh(D)), internal language contains lex monads circ,bullet recovering both sides. *Strict equations* disappear inside internal extensional type theory; normalization model constructed via programming in internal language, solving problem of strict equality extension from iso witness.

## 3. Methodology

### 3.1 Mode Theory as Cohesive Classifiers

We instantiate MTT [1] with mode theory M_cgc:

- modes c (cohesive), g (guarded), c x g (cubical guarded)
- modalities flat:c->c, sharp:c->c, later_kappa:g->g, forall kappa:g->., I:c x g->c x g
- 2-cells eta:id=>sharp flat, epsilon:flat sharp=>id, next:id=>later

The diagram J->Cat sends each mode to natural model of MLTT with universes. Functors interpreting modalities have left adjoint after *fattening* construction of Gratzer-Kavvos-Nuyts-Birkedal.

### 3.2 Partial Map Classifiers and Predomains

**Dominance** Sigma hook->Omega classifies propositions closed under top and wedge; for assemblies Sigma={p|p<= top}. **Carboni-Rosolini** characterization: partial maps X rightharpoonup Y correspond to total maps X->L Y where L lift monad L Y = Y+_Sigma 1. In S, Sigma = y(1)=later^infty. We show lift:

```rust
// Synthetic predomain as dominance lift in S (Rust proof sketch)
struct Lift<A> { defined: Sigma, value: Option<A> }
fn kleisli<A,B>(f: Box<dyn Fn(A)->Lift<B>>, mu: Lift<A>) -> Lift<B> {
  match mu.defined {
    true => f(mu.value.unwrap()),
    false => Lift { defined: false, value: None }
  }
}
```

Staged by clock K, countable K indexing ensures local continuity.

### 3.3 Guarded Recursion with Clock Irrelevance Proofs

We formalize clock-irrelevance by induction on inductive and higher inductive types [13][14]: N clock-irrelevant means N->forall kappa.N equivalence via force. Pf commutes: functor F(X)=P_f(A x X) preserves forall kappa. Then final coalgebra LTS=forall kappa.nu^kappa F correctly encodes finitely branching labelled transition systems.

Pipeline:

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Cohesive Modalities via Dual-Context Locks

Cohesion internalized with **crisp** @-contexts Delta;Gamma. Rule preserved dual-context Fitch-style lock Gamma,star_mu. Preservation of comprehension corresponds to **dependent right adjoint** (DRA) of Bhatt.

> **Theorem 3 (Locks admit left adjoints after fattening).** From any M-diagram D, define D^oplus_m = sum_{n,mu:m->n} D_n. Then each modality functor mu^*:D^oplus_n->D^oplus_m has left adjoint mu_! given by coproduct inclusion with unit eta. The induced comprehension p^oplus strictly preserves pullbacks.

Gives semantics without visible left adjoints internally.

### 4.4 Normalization-by-Evaluation for Cubical HoTT

Cubical type theory extends Martin-Lof with interval algebra path types Path A a b <=> i:I -> A with endpoints. Univalence ua:(A equiv B)->Path_U A B with beta strict. The cartesian Kan operation must satisfy *regularity* and *uniformity*.

Our STC proof lifts to MTTbox [17][18] where mode maps handle interval modal shifts. Steps:

- Build computability topos G = Gl(N:PSh(T)->PSh(cube)); internal language extensional.
- Define Ty record with code code:circ U and predicate P:code->U_bullet normal forms.
- Constants for Pi,Sigma,Path,Glue, S^1, U extended via realignment.

> **Theorem 4 (NbE Correctness).** There exists function nbe:Tm(A)->Nf(A) such that Gamma |- t=u:A iff nbe(t)=nbe(u). Moreover nf compose emb = id on normal forms and embedding is section of forgetful.

Corollaries: decidability of judgmental equality, injectivity of Pi and Path, homotopy canonicity |- p:N boolean evaluates to numeral in cubical setting, sharpening Huber-Sattler [19].

## 6. Limitations

- **Universe cumulativity:** We require algebraic cumulativity (Lumsdaine-Shulman style) ensuring code family injective up to cumulativity coercions; full Russell cumulativity still needs additional realignment strictifying universe hierarchy inside bullet side.
- **Clock quantification cardinality:** Our proof of P_f commuting uses finiteness of branching; infinite branching P_infty fails commutation, limiting encoding of countably non-deterministic languages.
- **Performance of gluing:** Normalization extracted via STC internal language as proof-relevant program incurs double-exponential blow-up on higher-dimensional cubes n>=5 due to De Morgan normal form enumeration of cofibrations; practical implementation in cooltt/redtt requires caching of cofibration disjunctions as BDDs.
- **Classical Sigma dominance:** In Boolean topos, Sigma boolean forces discreteness, collapsing synthetic domain to discrete sets; we restrict to realizability topos to recover Sigma with continuity conditions (L monad not idempotent there).
- **Cohesive axioms sheaves:** Lawvere's axiom flat -| sharp implies base topos satisfies continuum real-cohesion; our mode theory doesn't enforce int preserves products unless H locally connected, leaving gap for synthetic differential geometry intended extension (SDG modalities need infinitesimal shape).
- **Modal operator scope inside forall kappa:** Clock quantification fails to commute with later elimination when free variables include clocks; guarded recursive powerdomains need restriction to clock-irrelevant contexts, enforced by side-condition on delayed substitutions x<-t where t clock-irrelevant, checked syntactically but not decidable in full generality.

## 7. Conclusion

We presented a **cohesive guarded cubical** semantics synthesis that treats cohesive shape int,flat,sharp, temporal later, and clock quantification forall kappa as instances of a single *multimodal dependent type theory* over a mode theory of lex idempotent comonads realised as morphisms of natural models.

*Synthetic guarded domain theory* reconstructs Carboni-Rosolini predomains internally as Sigma-lift algebras in the topos of trees, adequate via clock-irrelevance commutation and final coalgebra encodings. *Cubical HoTT normalization* via **synthetic Tait computability** reuses same gluing pattern, embedding syntax into computability topos Gl(N) with lex modalities circ,bullet recovering syntactic and semantic hemispheres.

Result is base for three future strands: (i) **synthetic differential guarded cubical** with infinitesimal cohesion for differentiable programming correctness, (ii) **guarded parametricity** consolidating parametric logical relations inside cohesion, (iii) mechanistic analog compute lifted as synthetic modality over physical noise monad, bridging type theory and analog hardware for hybrid butterfly attention -- direction linking other theses in this batch.

Our proofs reduce normalization to programming in extensional ambient type theory with strict equations boxed behind modal circ, satisfying the *objective metatheory* goal of Sterling: metatheorems as internal programs, no free-hand strict reasoning.
