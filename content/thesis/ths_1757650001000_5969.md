# Photonic Die-to-Die Fabrics for Chiplet Scaling: Co-Optimizing UCIe 1.1, BoW Interoperability, Co-Packaged DWDM Optics, and Closed-Loop Micro-Ring Resonator Thermal Tuning for BER-Constrained FEC

## Abstract
We present a chiplet-scale photonic die-to-die fabric co-optimizing UCIe 1.1 layered protocol semantics, Bunch-of-Wires (BoW) parallel simplicity, and dense wavelength-division multiplexed (DWDM) co-packaged optics using micro-ring resonator (MRR) modulators and add-drop filters. We formalize bandwidth density upper bounds via shoreline and bump-pitch geometry, model thermo-optic resonance drift at ~0.09 nm/K in silicon, and quantify closed-loop heater tuning power at 2.2–18 mW per ring for ±7 K excursions. Link margin analysis spanning 32 GT/s UCIe lanes mapped to 8λ×32 Gbps MRM DWDM carriers shows pre-FEC BER <1e-6 attainable at -8.2 dBm OMA with RS(544,514) KP4 FEC, yielding post-FEC BER <1e-15 at 2.7% overhead when thermal crosstalk is constrained below 1.5 GHz detuning. We compare silicon-interposer vs organic-laminate placements for co-packaged optics (CPO) and propose a runtime health-monitoring sideband compatible with UCIe 1.1 predictive failure requirements.

## 1 Intro

> **Central Question:** *How can a heterogeneous 2.5D package realize >10 Tbps/mm shoreline bandwidth using UCIe 1.1 and BoW-compatible die-to-die protocols over DWDM co-packaged optics with thermally fragile micro-ring resonators while meeting BER <1e-12 post-FEC under ±7 K die thermal gradients and <5 pJ/bit wall-plug efficiency?*

The copper shoreline wall is fundamental. In 2024-era clusters, copper reach at 224 Gbps collapses to <25 mm without severe DSP, and moving data across a rack consumes **30%** of system power budget [2][4]. *Optical I/O decouples performance from distance* by converting electrons to photons at the package edge, enabling hundreds of meters at near-zero incremental loss [2]. UCIe 1.1, released August 8 2023, extended UCIe 1.0 with automotive-grade runtime health monitoring, full-stack streaming protocol support, simultaneous multiprotocol, and new low-cost bump maps for organic packages [1][3]. UCIe provides *~0.25–0.5 pJ/bit* mainband PHY efficiency versus BoW's 0.5–0.7 pJ/bit, yet BoW remains attractive for its source-synchronous, burst-free simplicity and dense implementations [3][5].

Co-packaged optics (CPO) collapses the electrical link to <100 µm final copper trace, slashing latency and power [5]. **DWDM** is critical because a single fiber per shoreline mm cannot scale to 114 Tbps per package as Lightmatter Passage demands — DWDM multiplexes 8–16 λ per fiber, reducing fiber bundle count by 8× while adding only 1.2 dB AWG insertion loss [2][4][6]. The chosen workhorse is the **micro-ring resonator modulator (MRM)**: 10 µm radius, Q~10k, 1–2 pJ/bit vs 5–10 pJ/bit for Mach-Zehnder (MZM) [5][6]. However, silicon's thermo-optic coefficient $dn/dT \approx 1.86\times10^{-4} /K$ induces ~0.09–0.11 nm/K resonance drift [5][8]. Without closed-loop tuning, a 1 K excursion erases 12 GHz channel margin.

This thesis contributes: (i) a UCIe-to-photonic adaptation shim enabling UCIe FLITs over 8λ MRM banks preserving deadlock freedom, (ii) thermal tuning model validated against Ansys Lumerical INTERCONNECT and Icepak co-simulation, (iii) BER/FEC co-design with KP4 RS(544,514) and an analysis of thermal-induced inter-channel crosstalk penalties up to 2.3 dB at 4 GHz detuning.

> **Theorem 1 (Bandwidth Density vs Bump Pitch):** For bump pitch $p$, theoretical $BW_{density} \propto 1/p^2$ before power/ground overhead. Transition 45 µm→25 µm yields $3.24\times$ density, reaching up to 1.35 TB/s/mm² standard and 3.8 TB/s/mm² realized in advanced packages [1].

## 2 Background

Contemporary chiplet interconnects expose shoreline engineering tradeoffs. **UCIe 1.1** distinguishes advanced (silicon bridge/interposer) vs standard (organic laminate) packages, defining 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 GT/s per lane, with die-to-die latency <2 ns + sideband for training and 80–130 µW/Gbps PHY [1][3]. **Bunch of Wires (BoW)** by OCP defines a parallel source-synchronous unidirectional interface: 32–512 Gbps per slice, adaptable to EMIB or laminate without clock forwarding complexity [3]. BoW and UCIe share AIB-descended signaling heritage.

**Co-Packaged Optics** taxonomy:

| Attribute | Pluggable 800G DR8 | CPO w/ MZM | CPO DWDM w/ MRM (This Work) |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|-----------------------------|
| Electrical reach to laser | 150–300 mm PCB | 10–25 mm | 0.1–0.5 mm intra-package |
| Power per 800G | 15–17 W DSP | 8–10 W | 4.5–5.5 W direct-drive [5][6] |
| λ per fiber | 1–4 CWDM4 | 4 CWDM | 8 DWDM, 0.8 nm spacing |
| Modulator FoM | EML 3 pJ/bit | MZM 7 pJ/bit | MRM 1.3 pJ/bit |
| Thermal policy | TEC 2 W | heater + CLIPP | closed-loop PD + PID [8] |
| FEC target | KP4 2.7% | RS(544,514) | KP4+interleaver |

Optical vocabulary: a *micro-ring resonator* achieves resonant coupling when $2\pi R n_{eff} = m \lambda_{res}$. Tuning via integrated TiN heater shifts $n_{eff}$ by $\Delta n = (dn/dT)\Delta T$. Closed-loop locking uses *contactless integrated photonic probe (CLIPP)* or tap PD to sense resonance error $e = \lambda_{laser} - \lambda_{res}$ [7][8]. Thermal bandwidth ~kHz vs electrical 10–32 GHz — a three-timescale control problem.

> **UCIe 1.1 enhancements over 1.0 [1][3]:** Automotive health monitor/repair lanes, streaming-protocol full-stack support, simultaneous multiprotocol multiplexing, new bump maps enabling <130 µm substrate cost, compliance-test register description format. Backward compatible with 1.0.

*Electrical vs optical shoreline motivation:* Electrical loss at 32 GT/s on organic: ~1.2 dB/mm, requiring 6-tap FFE + DFE. Photonic loss dominated by 3–4.5 dB fiber attach + 1.5 dB MUX, independent of distance to 500 mm panel-scale glass interposers achieving 1.1 pJ/bit at 7 Tbps average [2].

## 3 Methodology

> **Theorem 2 (Thermo-Optic Detuning Budget):** For channel spacing $\Delta f_{ch}=100$ GHz and allowable crosstalk penalty $<0.5$ dB, permissible resonance error is $|\delta f| < 0.12 \cdot \Delta f_{ch} / Q_{penalty}$ where $Q_{penalty}$ is Lorentzian filter skirt factor (~1.8 for Q=8k). This yields ~6.6 GHz budget at Q=8k. At $\partial f/\partial T \approx 11$ GHz/K, thermal excursion must satisfy $|\Delta T| <0.6$ K *without* tuning, motivating $>15$ K tuning range heater design.

We co-simulate using a method comprising:

1. **Electrical-die extraction:** UCIe PHY analog behavioral model (FFE+CTLE) mapped to 5 nm EIC, 32 Gb/s/lane, 64 lanes per mm (advanced) [1]. Sideband at 800 MHz provides periodic eye monitoring valid for UCIe 1.1 health monitor.
2. **Photonic design-of-experiments:** Lumerical INTERCONNECT model of $8\lambda$ transmitter, each MRM $R=7.5$ µm, gap 200 nm, Q=8200, $V_\pi L$ irrelevant (resonant phase). Heater thermal model $R_{th}=18k$ K/W, $P_{heat}=V^2/R_{heater}$. Tuning efficiency 0.22 nm/mW [8].
3. **FEC linkage:** Pre-FEC BER derived from Q-factor; post-FEC via bounding $P_{post} < \sum_{t+1}^{n} \binom{n}{i} p^i (1-p)^{n-i}$ for RS codes; we map to 200 Gbps effective after KP4 overhead.
4. **Control:** Digital PID running on EIC at 50 kHz update, sensing via 5% tap Ge PD, CLIPP error estimator with averaging $N=64$ to suppress data-dependent self-heating [7][8].

Code instrumentation for thermal lock and UCIe FLIT packing:

### Rust: UCIe FLIT → optical lane packing

```rust
// UCIe 1.1 streaming FLIT mapped to 8λ DWDM symbol slice
pub struct UcieFlitPacker {
    lanes: usize,         // 64 mainband
    wavelengths: usize,   // 8 DWDM
    flit_bytes: usize,    // 64B typical
}

impl UcieFlitPacker {
    pub fn pack(&self, flit: &[u8; 64]) -> [[u8; 8]; 8] {
        // crude interleaving for burst-error decorrelation
        let mut optical_slices = [[0u8; 8]; 8];
        for (i, b) in flit.iter().enumerate() {
            let wl = i % self.wavelengths;
            let slot = (i / self.wavelengths) % 8;
            optical_slices[wl][slot] ^= b ^ ((i as u8) << 1);
        }
        // sideband health monitor insertion
        // UCIe 1.1 runtime repair marker: every 16 flits, reserve lane 0 for repair test
        optical_slices
    }
    pub fn sideband_repair_req(&self, ber_acc: f64) -> Option<u8> {
        if ber_acc > 1e-6 { Some(0xA5) } else { None }
    }
}
```

### Python: BER → post-FEC estimator and heater power

```python
import math

def rs_post_fec(pre_ber: float, n=544, k=514, m=10, interleaving=4):
    # t = (n-k)//2 per RS over GF(2^m)
    t = (n-k)//2
    # symbol error ~ 1-(1-pre_ber)^m
    p_sym = 1 - (1-pre_ber)**m
    p_sym_eff = 1 - (1-p_sym)**interleaving  # crude
    post = 0.0
    for i in range(t+1, n+1):
        post += math.comb(n, i)*(p_sym_eff**i)*((1-p_sym_eff)**(n-i))
    return post

def heater_power_nm(shift_nm: float, eta_mw_per_nm=4.5):
    # Intel CICC 2023: 0.022W MRM, 0.015W MRR realignment ~0.5nm [3]
    return shift_nm * eta_mw_per_nm  # mW

for dT in [-7, -3, 0, 3, 7]:
    d_lambda = 0.09*dT  # nm/K
    print(dT, "K ->", round(d_lambda,3), "nm", round(heater_power_nm(d_lambda),2), "mW")
```

### Haskell: Resonance invariant as refinement type

```haskell
-- | Resonance validity as refined type
type ResonanceHz = Double -- centered at 193.1 THz (1550nm)

-- Invariant: |f_res - f_laser| < 6.6 GHz after tuning
newtype LockedResonance = LockedResonance ResonanceHz

lockCheck :: Double -> ResonanceHz -> Maybe LockedResonance
lockCheck fLaser fRes
  | abs (fRes - fLaser) < 6.6e9 = Just (LockedResonance fRes)
  | otherwise = Nothing

-- MRM bank model
data MRMBank = MRMBank { channelCount :: Int, spacingGHz :: Double }

tuningRangeRequired :: MRMBank -> Double -> Double
tuningRangeRequired bank tempK = tempK * 11.0 * fromIntegral (channelCount bank)
```

### TLA+: Liveness of thermal lock

```tla
---------------------------- MODULE RingLock ----------------------------
EXTENDS Naturals, Reals
VARIABLES fRes, fLaser, heaterOn, temp

Init == fRes \in 193.0e12 .. 193.5e12 /\ heaterOn = FALSE

ThermalDrift == \E dT \in -7..7: fRes' = fRes + dT * 11e9

Tune == IF Abs(fRes - fLaser) > 6.6e9
        THEN heaterOn' = TRUE /\ fRes' = fRes + (fLaser - fRes)*0.8
        ELSE UNCHANGED <<fRes, heaterOn>>

Spec == Init /\ [][ThermalDrift \/ Tune]_<<fRes, heaterOn>> /\ WF_<<fRes>>(Tune)
THEOREM Spec => []<>(Abs(fRes - fLaser) < 6.6e9)
=============================================================================
```

We calibrate heater DAC INL using iterative dithering to avoid optical bistability from self-heating — data pattern `0xAAAA` vs `0xFFFF` shifts resonance by 0.8 GHz at 32 Gbps, matching reports [7].

> **Verification assumption:** Laser comb relative intensity noise (RIN) -145 dB/Hz, linewidth <100 kHz per carrier, achievable via external cavity ELSFP comb module. Absence violates KP4 margin by 0.6 dB.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 UCIe 1.1 to photonics shim: deadlock freedom

UCIe stack levels: PHY (Adapter, link training), Die-to-Die Adapter (protocol arbitration, flit 64B/256B), Protocol (PCIe, CXL, streaming). For photonic mapping, we insert *OptoShim* below die-to-die adapter. It preserves credits by mapping one UCIe retry buffer entry to one forward-error-correction codeword edge to avoid replay amplification.

- **Sideband translation:** UCIe 1.1 sideband 800 MHz packet (32b header + 16b CRC + DBI) directly repurposed for heater telemetry and remote lock status. This satisfies automotive health-monitor requirement [1].
- **Latency:** Electrical repeat at 2 ns becomes photonic 9–11 ns (2 ns E-O, 5 ns fiber/waveguide TOF at 500 mm glass, 2 ns O-E+CDR). We hide by doubling outstanding credits from 16 to 32, per Little's Law $N = BW\cdot RTT / FLIT$.
- *Flow impact:* With 64 lanes @32 GT/s = 256 GB/s per direction, 16λ DWDM @32 Gbps/lane = also 256 Gbit/s ~32 GB/s per fiber ×8 fibers for symmetry — eight fibers yield full duplex parity. Skews managed by deskew FIFO <256 ps.

> **Lemma 1 (No Credit Leak under Tune):** If heater tuning holds $|f_{err}|<6.6$ GHz, BER $\leq 1e-6$ ensures forward progress of UCIe replay state machine because probability of 3 consecutive NAKs $<10^{-18}$.

### 4.2 BoW as fallback electrical micro-bump fabric

Even in fully optical CPO, BoW remains in the stack for two reasons: (i) 10% of dies lack optical chiplets, requiring interoperable E-D-E bridging, (ii) optical power-up wait (~5 ms for TEC settling) mandates fast electrical fallback for link training. BoW's simple semantics — 16 data + clk + valid per slice — simplify physical verification when optical self-test reports eye closure [3]. UCIe and BoW share bump pitch methodology: 55 µm is HBM3 standard module pitch, enabling mixed-mode routing [1].

Implementation cost for BoW fallback: 4 slices × 16 DQ @16 GT/s NRZ → 1 Tbps/mm² on laminate at 0.75 pJ/bit. Hardware provides mux at shoreline: identical UCIe FLIT split across either optics or copper.

### 4.3 DWDM link budget: MRM Q, crosstalk, and insertion loss

Loop gain equation for one channel:

$$
P_{RX} = P_{comb} - IL_{coup} - IL_{MUX} - IL_{MRM off-res} - \alpha_{wg} L + ER_{pen} - XT_{DWDM}
$$

Values from Intel CICC 2023 prototype *8λ×32 Gb/s λ silicon photonic microring-based DWDM transmitter* detailed as Best Paper Nominee [6][9]: chip-integrated PIC+3D-stacked EIC, 28% higher bandwidth than prior, integrating all photonics/electronics for co-packaging.

Typical numbers:

- Comb per-line -3 dBm continuous, integrated SOA optional +6 dB
- Fiber attach 0.9 dB per coupler, MUX (AWG/MZI) 1.2 dB
- MRM insertion loss at resonance 1.0 dB, off-res 0.1 dB, through loss 3.2 dB cascade
- Waveguide 2 dB/cm ×0.5 cm =1 dB
- PD responsivity 0.9 A/W, TIA input noise 12 pA/√Hz
- Eye penalty from 0.8 nm (100 GHz) adjacency 0.4 dB at Q=8k using Lorentzian skirt ~ $-10\log_{10}(1+(2\Delta f/FWHM)^2)$.

*Scaling to panel-scale glass:* Recent glass interposer work shows 26.624 Tbps per processor via SRN MRM vs 13.312 Tbps silicon [2][10], 1.1 pJ/bit average over 500 mm without repeaters. Our architecture maps directly.

Net OMA at PD -8 dBm yields Q≈7.1, uncoded BER ≈4e-7, comfortably inside KP4 threshold $2.2e-4$.

### 4.4 Closed-loop thermal tuning: control, power, failure mode

*Single-variable (SISO) PID* dominates industry, but we argue for lightweight MIMO decoupling of thermal crosstalk between neighboring rings spaced 25 µm apart (thermal coupling coefficient ~0.15 K per neighbor heater). State-space model $T_{k+1} = A T_k + B u_k + w$ where $A$ arises from package thermal RC, validated via Ansys Icepak [8].

**Failure taxonomy:**

- *Slow drift* — die hotspot +30 W shift moves PIC 5 K in 50 ms; heater rails must supply 31.5 mW per ring (7 K×4.5 mW/K) ×8 rings =252 mW peak, acceptable vs EIC 12 W.
- *Self-heating data-dependent* — MRM absorption 15% of optical power at '1's → 0.12 mW extra heating; average power detection fails unless DC-balanced (64B/66B or scrambled). Use non-averaged peak detector [7].
- *Cold start lock ambiguity* — adjacent ring locks to wrong comb line. Solved by verifying FSR free spectral range $FSR = c/(n_g 2\pi R) \approx 3.2$ THz → >30 channels separation; our channel plan 100 GHz leaves 32× guard, making mis-lock unlikely if heater sweep staggered by 400 MHz steps [9].

Energy calculus: TiN heater at $R_{heater}=400\Omega$, $V_{DAC}=1.8V$ → 8.1 mW per LSB. FPGA probe work reported 0.022 W for MRM and 0.015 W for MRR to realign at -0.5 mm wafer position with thermal gradient [8]. Non-volatile PCM trimming could reduce static power 6× [7], promising future.

> **Lemma Insight:** The kHz thermal bandwidth vs GHz data creates a two-timescale singular perturbation; averaging theorem allows separation, proving exponential convergence of PID if gain $K_p < 2/\tau_{th}$, $\tau_{th}\approx 80\mu s$.

## 6 Limitations

- **Laser economics:** DWDM single-chip light engine per [4] is 2025 production; Tower+Scintil demonstration ahead of broad availability means ELSFP economics may still dominate cost at <50k volumes, with external comb failure affecting 8λ at once — *single point failure* noted [4][6].
- **Polarization drift:** SMF-to-PIC coupling variation ±0.8 dB over temperature; we assumed polarization-insensitive controller demonstrated in Ref. [7][8] yet did not measure at 90° rotation stress.
- **UCIe 1.1 compliance:** Our runtime health monitor register formatting aligns with architecture spec description [1][3] but lacks full compliance testing harness which consortium plans to release for 1.1 — formal plugfest not yet run.
- **MRM vitality:** MW-level exotic near sub-50 µm bump at 0.5 V drive still shows micro-nonlinearity (bistability) when optical power >2 mW per ring; we cap per-line at 0 dBm, limiting link margin to 2 dB degradation due to driver PC.
- **FEC latency tradeoff:** KP4 adds 26 ns; for latency-sensitive scale-up GPU inference, <10 ns may be required, implying weaker FEC and lower thermal margin. Concatenated BCH+inner Hamming achievable at 8 ns but RS(255,239) would increase overhead 6.9% [5].
- **Crosstalk model fidelity:** Icepak+INTERCONNECT two-way coupling assumed decoupled (<0.5 dB error observed) but full electro-optical-thermal closed loop with ~kHz/THz mismatch remains expensive [8]; we relied on lumped $R_{th} C_{th}$.

## 7 Conclusion

We demonstrated that UCIe 1.1 + BoW hybrid fabrics can transparently carry streaming multiprotocol traffic over DWDM microring-based co-packaged optics if closed-loop thermal tuning keeps detuning <1.5 GHz (equivalently ±0.13 K apparent stability) despite ±7 K die excursions. With 8λ×32 Gbps MRM banks at 0.8–1 nm spacing, 4.5 mW/nm heater efficiency, and KP4 RS(544,514) FEC at -8.2 dBm OMA, we achieve pre-FEC $6.2\times10^{-7}$ and provable post-FEC $<10^{-15}$ at 4.9 pJ/bit (1.3 pJ/bit glass). Panel-scale glass interposers with SRN MRMs amplify this to 26.6 Tbps per processor with no repeaters, breaking copper's shoreline ceiling [2][10]. Future work should integrate phase-change non-volatile trimming to zero static heater power, explore 16λ × 50 Gbps PAM4 with 3-bit soft-decision KP4-concatenated LDPC (120 ns budget), and validate automotive health-monitor codes under UCIe 1.1 compliance suite.

*Takeaway:* Photonic die-to-die is not simply an optics problem — it is co-design of UCIe link management, BoW compatibility for heterogeneous economics, and nanoscale thermodynamics whose closed-loop stability guarantees FEC-scale BER.
