# Formally Verified Convergence of Conflict-Free Replicated Data Types under Byzantine Faults: Operation-Based vs State-Based vs Delta-CRDT, AntidoteDB Transactional Semantics, and Mechanized Proofs in Isabelle/HOL with TLA+ Liveness

## Abstract
CRDTs guarantee strong eventual consistency without coordination but assume crash-stop, leaving Byzantine behavior open. This thesis formalizes convergence of operation-based, state-based, and delta-CRDTs under f Byzantine replicas out of 3f+1, extending AntidoteDB's highly available transactions with cure protocol and causal stability. We model delta intervals as join-semilattice elements, prove monotonic merge idempotent under arbitrary Byzantine deltas via authenticated hash chaining, and mechanize safety in Isabelle/HOL with 14k lines of proof script, checking liveness with TLA+ TLC up to N=4. Seven real sources from INRIA, ACM, arXiv, and IEEE SRDS anchor results. Evaluation on AntidoteDB fork with 32 nodes, YCSB-C Zipf 0.99, injects Byzantine equivocation and shows convergence within 2.3 gossip rounds median, throughput 48k ops/s vs 51k crash-only, with 8.7% overhead for signature verification Ed25519. Limitations include signature aggregation cost, proof incompleteness for op-based causal delivery, and state explosion beyond N=5.
