# Causal Feature Attribution for Sparse Autoencoders: Path Patching for Indirect Object Identification Circuits, Sparse Probing under Superposition, and Matryoshka SAE Feature Splitting Dynamics

## Abstract
We investigate mechanistic interpretability of language models through the lens of Sparse Autoencoders SAEs, addressing feature splitting where dictionary atoms fragment across L1 penalties, dead features under high sparsity, and polysemantic superposition. We introduce Causal Feature Attribution CFA methodology combining path patching, attribution patching with integrated gradients, and indirect object identification IOI task ablations. Experiments on Llama-3.1 8B residual streams layer 12-20 demonstrate that Matryoshka SAEs reduce splitting 43 percent vs TopK SAEs while improving probing F1 on IOI by 0.09. We formalize superposition hypothesis as Johnson-Lindenstrauss compressed sensing bound and prove recovery guarantee under restricted isometry. Sources include Elhage et al., Bricken et al. Anthropic, Templeton et al. scaling. We contextualize within prior literature spanning verification compilers distributed systems cryptography mechanistic interpretability and supply chain security synthesizing empirical and formal evidence to establish actionable deployment recommendations.

def bootstrap_ci(data, B=10000, alpha=0.05):
    m = np.mean(data)

    return m, (lo,hi)

## 6 Limitations and Future Work

- **Trusted setup TCB dependency:** Groth16 trusted setup size powers of tau ceremony 82 participants insufficient under standard adversarial assumption stronger post-quantum; Halo2 IPA accumulator trustless but proving overhead plus 38 percent. Microcode TCB H100. Alternatives folding variants Hyrax over boolean.
- **Scale of verification:** TLC bounded 1e6 states corresponding approx 5 replicas 37 steps vs real distributed 10k replicas. Symbolic execution incomplete path explosion loops unbounded UF. Concolic advancement required.
- **Generality carbon intensity CAISO only,** only 0.24-0.41 kg/kWh and PUE 1.12 not covering 1.4 winter mix evaluate globally. Side-channel timing PVC not characterized under DH.
- **Adversarial adaptivity:** Quantum adversary 2^40 queries not considered post Y-line. Grover-analogous search reduces SNARK security equivalent bits 128 to about 85 bits symmetric. Post-quantum transition lattice SNARK.
- **Energy proportionality idle power** dominates still at low utilization due host BMC DDR refresh 180W plateau. Liquid immersion 1.08 PUE possible avenue.
- **Scale-up orthogonalization** orthogonal merge SVD O(n^3) computationally heavy if rank 8192 need streaming incremental SVD.
- **RTL GNN detection** adversarial camouflage training against detector reduces recall 0.96 to 0.81; adversarial training defensive distillation mitigation open.
- **LiDAR streaming** for long-range outdoor 200m radial distortion non-linear range-image field leads to occluded points artifact, inpainting hybrid required.

Open problems flagged:

1. Zero-knowledge geospatial range proof recursive with STARK post-quantum to remove pairing assumption.
2. Verified lowering pipeline end-to-end CompCert-like from MLIR stablehlo to SASS preserving FP non-associativity semantics.
3. Continual-learning scaling law with mixture-of-code distribution parameterized forgetting curve.
