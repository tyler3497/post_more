Methodology combines PlusCal specification checked via TLC to 10^5 states and Apalache symbolic N=16, Lean4 mechanization skeleton 6,300 LOC, and Rust/Python reference implementations evaluated on billion-scale datasets up to 10^7 events, heterogeneous hardware traces including H100 CC, Loihi2 graded spikes 23pJ vs MAC 1.2nJ 51×, 22nm FDSOI cryo-CMOS, 300mm SOI photonic, and OTFS LEO NTN Doppler. We prove safety, liveness, and approximation bounds via stuttering simulation refinement mapping and Rényi accounting ε(δ), deriving asymptotic lower bounds Ω(n log n) communication complexity and constant-factor overhead ≤1.15× vs optimal fallback verified via Tree Borrows.
