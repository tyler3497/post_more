## 6 Limitations

- **Assumption alpha:** i.i.d. may fail drift TV 0.12+ only. Production applied cryptography encrypted search nonstationary.
- **Assumption beta:** TLA+ small-scope N=4 1e5 states; larger 10k may expose Heisenbugs missed. Counterexample 6-depth race clique N=7.
- **Assumption gamma:** RAPL omits DRAM refresh 0.3W; cryo CMOS 4K J/query incomparable.
- **Assumption delta:** Transient exec mitigations rely on microcode 0x219; GhostWrite silicon bug feature-off 50% perf loss for RISC-V LP unacceptable.
- **External validity:** Graph factorization O(N^fhtw) only when indices hash trie / B-trees; Parquet row-groups unsupported.
- **Formal gaps:** Coq StackedBorrows->TreeBorrows refinement conjecture 7.3 POPL26; TLA+ WF liveness may hide livelock WAN variance 31ms-2190ms Janus.
- **Mitigation**: future includes adaptive clipping via private quantiles [6], DICE anti-rollback, SPDM1.2 measurement HWRoT, RATS EAT.
