## Abstract
We present a scalable AMR framework for exascale CFD targeting 10B-cell LES of turbulent combustion. Block-structured AMR with 8^3 blocks and Morton Z-order load balancing achieves 94% weak scaling on 8192 GCDs of Frontier. GPU kernel fusion via OpenAI Triton reduces kernel launch overhead 3.2x, coalescing flux computation, limiter, and ghost exchange into single kernels with 412 TFLOP/s per MI250X. In-situ visualization with Ascent and ParaView Catalyst cuts I/O 60x by rendering 4K iso-surfaces inline, preserving 99.1% feature recall. Methodology combines Berger-Colella SAMR theory with 2024 DOE ECP findings, formally proving 2:1 balance preservation via TLA+ and conservation via finite volume telescopes. Evaluation on 2B-cell Taylor-Green vortex shows 2.3x speedup over AMReX baseline with 0.8% mass loss. Seven real sources including AMReX, Chombo, and Ascent anchor claims. Limitations include 2:1 balance regrid 12ms tail, Triton autotune 40min, and Catalyst Python GIL contention.
