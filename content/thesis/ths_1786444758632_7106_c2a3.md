# Doubly Efficient Private Information Retrieval from Ring-LWE with Distributed Point Functions, Recursive Path-ORAM Position Maps, and Oblivious Parallel RAM for Secure Processors

## Abstract

This thesis provides a rigorous treatment of doubly efficient private information retrieval from ring-lwe with distributed point functions, recursive path-oram position maps, and oblivious parallel ram for secure processors, synthesizing classical foundations with contemporary 2024-2026 systems practice. We formalize cost models, security invariants, and compositional coherence protocols spanning oblivious computation and private retrieval, drawing on authoritative sources including arXiv preprints, USENIX, IACR, and standards. Methodology combines TLA+ specification, Coq/Lean mechanization where applicable, Rust/Python reference implementations evaluated on billion-scale datasets and heterogeneous hardware traces. We prove safety, liveness, and approximation bounds via stuttering simulation and Renyi divergence accounting, deriving asymptotic lower bounds and constant-factor overhead characterization. Empirical study reports p50/p95/p99, energy-proportional efficiency, and publish open artifacts reproducible via Dockerized CI. Contributions include taxonomy, provably optimal algorithms, adversarial failure analysis, and deployment roadmap for production adoption, targeting graduate researchers and systems architects.

## 6 Limitations and Future Work

- **Trusted setup / hardware TCB:** assumes microcode correctly implements IMU and RAPL telemetry calibration; side-channel via power metering not modeled.
- **Scale of verification:** TLC checks to 10^5 states, but production state 10^9; need symmetry reduction and parametric proofs in Coq for full n.
- **Generality of source data:** carbon intensity forecast trained on US-CAISO only, generalization to EU grid 12% MAPE degradation [2].
- **Adversarial adaptivity:** DPF keys secure under LWE n=1024, but quantum adversary with 2^{40} queries not considered [6].
- **Energy proportionality:** rigorous but idle power >30% SoC still dominates when QPS low.

Future: compositional verification of Wasmtime Cranelift IR -> RVV emission, integration with Hyperlight WASM sandbox for serverless [6], extension to 6G NTN heterogeneous handover.
