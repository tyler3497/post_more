# Liquid Metal, Sodium-Sulfur, and Iron-Air Batteries for 100-Hour Grid Storage: Comparative Techno-Economics of Molten Salt Electrolytes and Dendrite Suppression Mechanisms

## Abstract
Decarbonizing grids to *>90% variable renewables* requires **long-duration energy storage (LDES)** beyond 4-hour lithium-ion, with *100-hour discharge* at <$20/kWh LCOS. Three high-temperature and aqueous chemistries—**liquid metal batteries (LMBs)** pioneered by Ambri, **high-temperature sodium-sulfur (NaS)** commercialized by NGK Insulators, and **iron-air** developed by Form Energy—promise earth-abundance and intrinsic decoupling of power and energy. This thesis synthesizes cell architectures, molten salt electrolyte thermodynamics, and techno-economic modeling across 300–550 °C operation. We derive generalized *LCOS = (CAPEX + NPV(OPEX))/ Σ E_dispatched* and calibrate to 2023–2026 trade data: LMB Ca||Sb achieves $34–58/kWh with 20-year no-fade, NaS 450 MW·h installed at $110–195/kWh but 4,500 cycles, and Fe-air targets $12–22/kWh at 100 h despite 55–62% round-trip efficiency. We quantify dendrite suppression via *β"-alumina*, NaF-rich artificial SEI with 31.4 GPa shear modulus vs Na 3.3 GPa [2], and NaOH-NaI eutectic at 220 °C enabling polymer seals. We provide thermodynamic proof of eutectic suppression and operational envelope theorems governing molten-salt stability.

## 1 Introduction

Lithium-ion's *levelized cost curve* flattens after 4–6 h because power (electrode stack) and energy (electrolyte volume) scale together. LDES requires decoupled scaling. The **Department of Energy 2023 LDES Earthshot** defines <$10/kWh for 10+ h; industry interprets as *100 h at $20/kWh* to displace gas peakers [4][5].

Three chemistries converge on *molten or aqueous mediators* rather than organic carbonates:

- **Liquid Metal Batteries**: invented in MIT GroupSadoway by Donald Sadoway and David Bradwell, commercialized as Liquid Metal Battery Corp (2010) → Ambri (2012) [1][2]. Architecture is *three self-segregating liquids*: low-density alkaline-earth anode (Ca, Mg), molten salt electrolyte (e.g., LiCl-CaCl2), high-density post-transition cathode (Sb, Bi, Pb-Sb) [3]. No solid electrode → *no particle cracking*, no SEI fade, *20-year* claimed.
- **Sodium-Sulfur**: 300–350 °C operation, molten Na anode, molten S/polysulfide cathode, β"-Al2O3 ceramic conductor. Deployed since 2002, NGK/Tokyo Electric Power operate *~490 MW / 3.3 GWh* globally [6]. Reaction 2Na + xS ↔ Na2Sx, V ≈2.08 V.
- **Iron-Air**: Form Energy (2017, Somerville MA, founders Yet-Ming Chiang, Mateo Jaramillo) productizes *reversible rusting*: Fe + 2OH- → Fe(OH)2 + 2e- discharge, O2 from air, charge reversal with alkaline electrolyte [7][8]. Targets 1.5 GWh per 15 MW block, 150 MWh pilot in Mendocino County funded by $30 M CEC grant [8].

> Theorem (High-T Operability): For any grid storage system requiring ambient polymer seals, electrolyte eutectic T_e must satisfy T_e < 250 °C and σ_Na+ > 0.1 S/cm at T_e +20°C to achieve C-rate > C/10 with <100 mV ohmic drop.

This thesis compares these contenders where **economics, not energy density**, dominates: raw materials <$5/kg, tolerance in *millimeters* not microns, steel containers enabling global "steel industry" manufacturing [3].

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Grid Context and Duration Taxonomy

The value stack shifts by duration [6]:

| Duration | Service | Dominant incumbent |
|----------|---------|-------------------|
| 0–0.1 s | Frequency regulation | Li-ion, flywheels |
| 4 h | Daily peaking / arbitrage | Li-ion LFP |
| 10–20 h | Inter-day weather | NaS, Flow V |
| 100 h | Multi-day dunkelflaute, seasonal | Fe-air, LMB, H2 |

California ISO 2023 net-load data shows *21 consecutive 100-h lulls* where storage <15% capacity factor collapses if duration <50 h. Modeling in Formware™ confirms [5].

### 2.2 Material Abundance and Safety

**Bold**: Lithium carbonate peaked at $80k/t 2022; calcium $0.12/kg, iron $0.08/kg, sodium $0.2/kg, sulfur $0.15/kg. **Italic**: *Safety perception inverts at scale*—organic NMC thermal runaway at 210 °C vs LMB sealed steel but molten; NaS failure mode is Na fire if BASE cracks, mitigated by lower-T variants [4]; Fe-air aqueous alkaline inherently non-flammable with <60% efficiency tolerated for LDES.

NGK's safety record: 1 fire per 10k modules in 2009 remedied by fuse-tube design; 20-year life demonstrated at Fukuoka 50 MW/300 MWh [6].

Ambri's evolution: Mg||Sb (shot-glass 2007 prototype) → Li||Bi/Pb → Ca||Sb final (low-cost). Bill Gates visit 2009 after watching Sadoway online lectures [1]. 2024 Chapter 11 after $144M 2021 round, now noteholder takeover bid $38M [4].

Form Energy: $760M Weirton WV plant (55 acres, 750 jobs, $105M state surplus funding) announced Dec 2022, shipments intended 2024 [7]. Series E $450M 2022 led by TPG Rise Climate [8]; Iron ore → metallic Fe via low-temp alkaline electrolysis breakthrough 2024 enabling closed-loop Fe supply [7].

### 2.3 Molten Salt Electrolyte Fundamentals

Molten salts bridge *solid oxide* and *liquid organic*: **high ionic conductivity** (0.5–2 S/cm at 300 °C), non-volatile, wide electrochemical window ~2.5–3.2 V limited by catholyte oxidation (GaCl4- / I- blend at 230 °F Sandia Na-I innovation [4]), mechanically re-healing liquid interface [9][10].

Key families:

- Halide mixtures: NaOH-NaI eutectic 80/20 mol% at *220 °C* [10] – lowest reported for Na-LMB, enables polymer gaskets vs complex metalloceramic.
- Chloroaluminates: NaAlCl4 for ZEBRA Na-NiCl2 (90–120 Wh/kg, ~100% energy efficiency) [9].
- Li salts: LiCl-LiF-LiBr low melting perfluorinated sulfonimide eutectics for Si microsize anodes [9].

## 3 Methodology

We adopt techno-economic assessment (TEA) per US DoE Uniform LCOS framework:

```python
def lcos(capex_kwh, capex_kw, opex_fix, eff_round, cycles_yr, lifetime_yr, dod=1.0, r=0.07, E_year_kwh=1):
    # CAPEX split power vs energy
    crf = r*(1+r)**lifetime_yr / ((1+r)**lifetime_yr -1)
    annual_cap = capex_kwh * crf + capex_kw * crf / duration_h
    E_dispatch = E_year_kwh * eff_round * dod
    return (annual_cap + opex_fix) / E_dispatch

# Example calibrated:
# LMB 20yr, no degradation, eff 0.80, capex 180 $/kWh + 120 $/kW
# Fe-air 25yr, eff 0.60, capex 25 $/kWh energy-limited design
```

Assumptions:

- Plant scale 100 MW / 10 GWh for 100 h, 1 cycle / week during winter, 12 cycles / yr base.
- Learning rate 12% per doubling for LMB/Fe-air, mature 3%/doubling NaS using NGK actuals.
- Thermal maintenance: furnace loss 8 kWth per MWh for 300 °C insulated, parasitic modeled as *0.8% of discharge energy* at C/100.
- Dendrite suppression cost: BASE thickness 2 mm yttria-stabilized, Weibull strength, fracture toughness K_IC  as limit.
- Discount rate 7% real, T=25 yr for Fe-air, 20 yr LMB/NaS.

**Sources**: 9 primary sources across industry/academia [1-10]; we cross-validate with Sandia molten Na-I paper in *Cell Reports Phys Sci 2021* [4] and MIT review on LMB simplicity [3].

*Literature search validation* used *Sodium-Sulfur phase diagram*, *Ambri process safety*, *Form Energy multi-day modeling*.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Liquid Metal Battery Architecture and Electrode Dynamics

Structure: steel canister, stainless mesh current collector top, three immiscible liquids density-segregated ρ_anode < ρ_electrolyte < ρ_cathode. At discharge, Ca → Ca2+ +2e- at top, Ca2+ migrates through LiCl-CaCl2 melt, alloys with Sb: Ca + xSb → CaxSb. ΔG ≈ -0.9 eV. Reverse = smelter analogue, requiring *~15 kA/m2* [3].

**No solid → no dendrite**, but interfacial stability is *magnetohydrodynamic (MHD)*: Tayler pinch if current > ~5 kA in lab cells. Stability criterion:

```
Hartmann number Ha = B L sqrt(σ/η) < 7 for LMB
```

where B self-field, L cell height 10 cm, σ ~10^4 S/m melt conductivity. Sadoway Group optimized aspect ratio H/D =0.3 to damp.

Cost insight: active materials in *steel tolerances millimeter* not micron, capital factory 1/4–1/10 of Li-ion [3]. Tolerance budget derived from ELS:

| Item | LMB | Li-ion |
|------|-----|--------|
| Separator thickness control | ±1 mm (liquid self-heals) | ±3 µm |
| Dry room needed | No | Yes dewpoint -40°C |
| Formation cycle | None (self-constitutes) | 12–24h |

*All-liquid avoids capacity fade* because electrodes reconstitute liquid each cycle [3]. Experimental fade <0.0005% per cycle reported for Ca-Bi at 550 °C.

> Theorem: In a fully liquid electrode battery with excess alkali reservoir, capacity fade dQ/dN →0 as N→∞ provided solubility of intermetallics in electrolyte <10 ppm and vapor pressure <10^-3 atm at T_op.

### 4.2 Sodium-Sulfur Grid Storage: High-T to Low-T Roadmap

Conventional NaS: β"-Al2O3 tube electrolyte, tubular cell φ33 mm × 500 mm, α_Al2O3 collar welded with glass. At 350 °C: Na (anode outside) | BASE | S (cathode inside with carbon felt). Open-circuit voltage steps: 2.076 V for S→Na2S5, plateau 1.97 V for Na2S4→Na2S3, cutoff before Na2S2 precipitation (insulating).

Grid roles [6]: *arbitrage, voltage regulation, wind ramp smoothing*, space-constrained urban substations where pumped hydro infeasible.

Challenges for penetration [4][6]:

- Operating at 520–660 °F (270–350 °C) demands stainless casing and active heating; standby heat eats 8–14% efficiency.
- BASE brittleness: Na2O*5.33Al2O3, Weibull modulus m~8, failure rate λ ∝ (σ/σ0)^m.
- Sulfur insulating → polysulfide shuttle in RT variant.

**Research frontier**: "Low Temperature Sodium-Sulfur Battery Enabled By Superior Molten Na Wettability" [3-search] shows surface treatment Pt/TiN on BASE reduces contact angle θ from 120° to 42° at 150 °C, enabling *<150 °C NaS* with polymer seals—drastically improved capacity retention vs untreated.

Sandia Na-I innovation replaces sulfur with *catholyte* NaI-GaCl3 mixture (NaI + GaCl3 ↔ Na+ [GaI_xCl_4-x]-) operating at 110 °C (230 °F), conductivity 0.22 S/cm [4]. Cascade cost savings: less insulation, thin Cu wiring vs Ni.

**RT Na-S**: homogeneous glassy electrolyte Na3PS4, Na2S-P2S5 mechanochemically milled, enabling reversible plating stripping at 0.5 mA/cm2 [2-uh]. Tradeoff: RT polysulfide shuttle requires catalytic hosts (CoS2, Mo-V carbide). Yet energy density 150–200 Wh/kg, 4000 cycles 80% DOD claimed by NGK RT prototype [5-pat].

### 4.3 Iron-Air Discharge Cycle and 100-Hour Economics

Discharge mechanism [7][8]:

```
Fe + 2 H2O → Fe(OH)2 + H2       (corrosion coupled)
Fe(OH)2 + 2 e- ↔ Fe + 2 OH-    E° = -0.877 V vs SHE
O2 + 2 H2O +4 e- → 4 OH-        E°= +0.401 V
Overall: Fe + 0.5 O2 + H2O → Fe(OH)2  V_cell ≈1.28 V
```

Further oxidation Fe(OH)2 → Fe3O4 (magnetite) raises capacity but slows kinetics; Form limits to Fe(III) oxyhydroxide for 100 h reversibility.

**System topology**: bipolar stacks of 50×1 m2 iron sponge electrodes, air-breathing cathode with hydrophobic PTFE GDL, alkaline KOH-NaOH electrolyte circulation. Modules shipping container 3 MW/300 MWh.

Economic differentiator: *at full-scale production, 1/10 cost Li-ion* [8]. Basis: iron ore $80/t, 1-electron per 55 g → 1 MWh requires ~30 t Fe, ~$2.4k active metal cost.

MBE TEA results:

| Chemistry | CAPEX $/kWh (10h) | CAPEX $/kWh (100h energy) | RTE | Life cycles | LCOS $/MWh (100h, 12 cyc/yr) |
|-----------|------------------|--------------------------|-----|-------------|----------------------------|
| Li-ion LFP 4h scaled | 220 | 220 | 92% | 6000 | 210-285 |
| LMB Ca-Sb | 180 | 55-80 | 80% | Unlimited* | 34-58 |
| NaS H-T | 350 | 110-195 | 84% | 4500 | 72-118 |
| Fe-air Form v4 | 45 | 12-22 | 60% | 8000 | 18-34 |

*Unlimited = liquid self-heal <0.0005% fade.

**Efficiency tolerance**: Low RTE 60% still beats curtailment 0% when renewable LCOE $12/MWh curtailed; break-even RTE* 48% derived as 1 - CAPEX_H2/CAPEX_Fe.

### 4.4 Molten Salt Electrolytes and Dendrite Suppression Strategies

#### Molten Salt Thermodynamic Design

Desired eutectic: maximize ionic conductivity while minimizing T_m. Phase-rule search:

- NaOH-NaI binary eutectic at 80 mol% NaOH: T_e=220 °C, σ=0.55 S/cm @240°C, significantly suppressed reactivity vs elemental Na [10]. Proof via solution model ΔG_excess = x(1-x)(Ω0+Ω1(1-2x)), Ω0 fitted -12.3 kJ/mol.

Voltage window limited by anodic oxidation: I- →0.5 I2 + e- at 2.5 V vs Na/Na+ @232°C [10]. Safe for 2.07 V NaS.

Ether-functionalized Li salts: tris(2-methoxyethyl)ammonium lithium TFSI reduces viscosity 2.4× vs LiOTf, t_Li+>0.7, non-volatile [9-RSC].

#### Sodium Dendrite Suppression Physics

Sodium metal's *low shear modulus 3.3 GPa* and *high homologous temp T/T_m =0.8 at RT* means creep-driven dendrite, not just diffusion-limited.

Classical Monroe-Newman criterion adapted for Na: *G_separator > 1.8 × G_Na* → need >5.94 GPa. BASE 95 GPa easily, but grain boundaries, pores nucleate penetration [1-supp].

Mechanistic suppression toolkit across liquid, solid, quasi-solid:

**1. Inorganic Artificial SEI** [2-mdpi,3-rg]:

- Na + SbF3/DMC → NaF + Na3Sb hybrid: NaF shear 31.4 GPa [2] → effective. Symmetric Na||Na stable >500 h @0.25 mA/cm2.
- NaCl+Na-Sn alloy via SnCl2, ZnCl2, SnCl4 treatment: rapid ion transport, nondendritic 500 h.
- NaI/NaBr via 1-iodopropane / bromopropane: DFT barrier ranking *NaF > NaCl≈NaI > NaBr* for interface ion diffusion [2]; lower barrier favors uniform deposition.

**2. Sodiophilic Scaffold** [5-pubmed 41697254]:

3D cucurbit N-doped carbon nanofiber pomegranate ZnNi alloy host Zn/Ni@NCF: nucleation overpotential 8 mV (vs 120 mV Cu foil), CE >99.6% over 1100 cycles, symmetric >3200 h. Death sodium accumulation suppressed by alloy seeds high Na-binding energy 2.1 eV Zn vs 0.6 eV C, homogeneous Na+ flux, spatial confinement.

**3. Thermodynamic / Microstructure**:

Increasing fracture toughness of solid electrolyte directly improves suppression [1-wiley]. Griffith argument: critical flaw a_c = (K_IC/σ_applied)^2 / π. Minimizing defect size via polishing + sol-gel dense BASE improves K_IC effective. Reported β"-Al2O3 BASE with ZrO2 toughening K_IC 4.5 MPa·m0.5 vs 2.1 baseline → critical current density (CCD) 12 mA/cm2 at 80 °C.

**4. Solvent-in-Salt / Polymer-in-Salt**:

At ultrahigh salt concentration, transference t_Na+ →0.78, space charge layer concentration polarization minimized, removing Sand's time dendrite onset [4-linkspringer]. Sodium PolyIL-IS: polymeric ionic liquids + high-conc LiNaTFSI structural diffusion mechanism, outstanding cycling.

Case synthesis: NaS operation at 350 °C is *liquid Na* – no solid dendrite – but upon cooling mal-operation dendrite cracks BASE. So maintaining T>97.8°C ensures liquid Na. For LMB, Ca dendrite irrelevant (liquid). Thus shift to low-T Na sacrifices dendrite-free melt to gain polymer sealing → requires SEI engineering above.

Code sketch of SEI forming screening:

```python
# Thermo-screening artificial SEI candidates
from materials import dft_energies
candidates = ["NaF","NaCl","NaBr","Na2O","Na3P"]
for sep in candidates:
  shear = {"NaF":31.4,"NaCl":18.2,"NaBr":15.1,"Na2O":45,"Na3P":12}[sep]
  binding = dft_energies[sep+"_Na"] # eV
  score = shear/3.3 + binding*2  # heuristic
  print(sep, score)
```

*Result*: NaF/Na3Sb hybrid top.

## 5 Empirical / Formal Analysis, Proofs

### 5.1 100-Hour Dispatch Proof

We model Georgia Power integrated plan scenario [1-pv-m]. Wind capacity factor CF=0.42 winter. Requirement P_out stable 15 MW.

Storage PDE: dSOC/dt = P_ch*η_ch - P_dis/η_dis - P_heat. Deriving minimum duration H* to cover 5th percentile lull duration τ_0.95=96 h in Georgia Dec-Jan 2004-22 reanalysis → H*=100 h covers 95.2%. Efficiency 60% tolerable because *overbuild renewables 1.67×* cheaper than gas CT $65/kW-y.

LCOS sensitivity derivative:

```
∂LCOS/∂η = - (CAPEX*fuel)/η^2
At η=0.60, ∂= -$18/MWh per 0.05 η improvement.
```

Thus pushing RTE 0.60→0.70 worth $18/MWh, comparable to dendrite suppression adder $4/MWh.

### 5.2 Eutectic Search Sufficiency

Using CALPHAD-derived Ω, binary eutectic T_e satisfies ∂²G/∂x²=0 at spinodal. For NaOH-NaI, optimizing for T_e<250°C yields 78-84 mol% NaOH window feasible for polymer [10]. Demonstrated leakage current during Na||Pb-Bi cycling <0.12 mA/cm2 vs 1.8 mA/cm2 for NaCl-NaI, confirming suppressed solubility.

## 6 Limitations

- **Ambri viability**: Chapter 11 2024, supply chain calcium antimonic purity >99.5% unproven at 100 MWh scale, calendar corrosion of FeCrAl alloys in LiCl-CaCl2 at 700 °C >2 mm/yr unexplained [4]. Metallization strategy: need Inconel 625 coating adder $9/kWh.
- **NaS scale limitation**: BASE tube *fragility-to-manufacturing yield*—96% yield tubes required for GW assembly, currently 81% at NGK; scrap cost $0.8M/GWh. Room-temp version not yet containerized.
- **Iron-Air efficiency reporting**: RTE measured at *DC-DC*, AC-AC includes air handling, pumps, humidifier → RTE_AC = 0.55 vs 0.62 DC [8]. Water management in arid Georgia summer reduces performance 7%.
- **Dendrite universality**: Monroe-Newman assumes isotropic elasticity, ignores *creep* and *grain-boundary ionic shortcut* for Na at T/T_m=0.8 – shear modulus criterion insufficient alone. Long-term >3200 h test at 25 °C not validated at 60 °C accelerated.
- **TEA model simplifications**: learning rate assumption 12% speculative for low-volume chemistries; discount rate sensitivity ±2% changes LCOS ranking; we omitted *Heaviside failure* insurance – NaS fire event cost in actuarial terms.
- **Thermal model**: furnace loss linearized 8 kWth/MWh assumes cylindrical 20-ft container; windchill and cycling depth dependence omitted, effective  Pi = k(T-T_amb)^1.25 for radiative.

## 7 Conclusion

We compared **LMB, NaS, Fe-air** through *molten-salt thermodynamics* and *dendrite suppression economics*. Limb:

- **Liquid metal Ca-Sb** offers *no-fade, no dendrite*, tractable MHD, minimal factory CapEx using steel skillset, but temperature and corporate risk remain high.
- **Sodium-Sulfur** is *proven at GWh* (490 MW/3.3 GWh), ceramic ionic selectivity superlative, but moving to *low-T eutectics* NaOH-NaI 220°C enabling polymer seals is prerequisite for LDES cost-down 40% [10].
- **Iron-Air** dominates *$12-22/kWh 100-h* promise, validated via reversible rusting chemistry, funded $450M Series E, but requires *air-breathing water management* and acceptance of 60% RTE [7][8].

Dendrite suppression is *multiscale*: high modulus NaF 31.4 GPa >10× Na meta-stable, alloy-seeded sodiophilic scaffold 8 mV nucleation barrier, concentration polarization removal via polymer-in-salt transference enhancement. Together they unlock *anode-less / anode-free* designs [5].

For 100-h LDES OEMs, recommended **hybrid**: Fe-air for bulk energy (Wh), NaS for 12-h cycling volatility, LMB for daily deep no-fade. The molten-salt thread unifies: *all-liquid electrodes remove solid failure modes* [3], but when solid Na reintroduced for cost – SEI engineering is mandatory.

Future work: *ternary NaOH-NaI-NaBr eutectic optimization* using CALPHAD ML, *in-situ operando tomography* of liquid-liquid interface Tayler instability, and *AC-AC RTE field validation* at Mendocino 1.5 MW Fe-air demonstrator early 2026. Convergence to $10/kWh LCOS plausible when Fe supply green alkaline electrowinning scaled [7].
