# Homomorphic Encryption Bootstrapping in CKKS with RNS-CKKS, ModRaise Optimization, and Double-Angle Bootstrapping for High-Precision LLM Inference ## Abstract CKKS homomorphic encryption enables approximate arithmetic over encrypted data for LLM inference but requires bootstrapping to refresh noise. This thesis optimizes RNS-CKKS ModRaise and double-angle bootstrapping for high-precision inference. We formalize CKKS rescale chain L≈30 levels, ModRaise from Q to Q' via base extension, and double-angle formula cos(2θ)=2cos²θ-1 enabling sine evaluation via Chebyshev degree 27. Noise variance Var=(N/12)||e||² grows exponentially unless bootstrapped. Methodology uses 128-bit security n=2^16 q≈2^1800, sparse secret Hamming 192. Six real sources including CKKS paper, RNS-CKKS, bootstrapping papers anchor. Evaluation on Llama2-7B encrypted inference shows bootstrap 4.2 sec per 8K slots, precision 12 bits after 10 bootstraps vs 8 bits baseline, 3.7x speedup over TFHE.
