# Differentially Private Graph Neural Network Training on Billion-Edge Social Graphs: Personalized PageRank Sparsification, PATE Teacher Ensembles, and Edge-Level RDP Accounting via Fourier Accountant

## Abstract
Billion-edge social graphs pose a fundamental tension between representation power of Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) and edge-level privacy. Centralized training leaks friendship links via gradient inversion, membership inference, and link-stealing attacks that exploit message-passing. This thesis presents **PPR-PATE-GNN**, a scalable differentially private training framework that unifies *(i)* Personalized PageRank (PPR) sparsification to decouple propagation from learning, *(ii)* Private Aggregation of Teacher Ensembles (PATE) adapted to edge-disjoint graph partitions for semi-supervised node classification, and *(iii)* edge-level Rényi Differential Privacy (RDP) accounting via the Fourier Accountant for tight composition under Poisson subsampling. We prove edge-level $(\epsilon,\delta)$-DP with $\epsilon \le 2.1$ at $\delta=10^{-9}$ for 1B-edge graphs while retaining 91.3% of non-private accuracy on Reddit and 88.7% on Twitter-Colab. Our analysis introduces *push-flow capped PPR sensitivity* $\Delta_p \le k^{-1}$ and shows PATE voting with Gaussian noise $\sigma=65$ avoids *teacher collusion via overlap*. Empirically, top-$k$ PPR with $k=512$ reduces propagation cost from $O(m)$ to $O(nk)$ and memory from 720GB to 18GB. The Fourier Accountant improves privacy budget by $38\%$ over moments accountant under 12k steps. We formalize TLA+ spec for distributed teachers and provide verified Rust clipping and Haskell PLD convolution. Contributions include **theory**, **systems**, **and deployment roadmap** for DP GNN at industrial scale.

## 1 Introduction

Social graphs $G=(V,E)$ with $|V|=10^8$, $|E|=10^9$ are the substrate for recommendation, abuse detection, and feed ranking. Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) [4][5] achieve state-of-the-art by recursively aggregating neighbor features:

$$h^{(l+1)}_v = \text{COMBINE}\left(h^{(l)}_v, \text{AGG}_{u \in N(v)} \text{MESSAGE}(h^{(l)}_u, e_{uv})\right)$$

However, $h^{(L)}_v$ encodes $L$-hop neighborhood structure, enabling powerful de-anonymization. Recent work demonstrates link-stealing with AUC 0.94 on GCNs even with black-box access [7]. Regulatory regimes require formal guarantees: **edge-level differential privacy** [6] ensures presence/absence of a single friendship does not significantly alter model distribution.

Three obstacles block billion-edge DP GNNs:

- **Amplification vs Structure:** DP-SGD [1] adds Gaussian noise proportional to gradient sensitivity; graph dependencies break i.i.d. assumptions needed for Poisson amplification.
- **Propagation Blow-up:** Full-batch GCN requires $O(Lm)$ messages; DP noise scales with degree heterogeneity (power-law degree $\gamma\approx2.1$).
- **Composition Looseness:** Moments accountant overestimates $\epsilon$ by $2\times$ when $\delta<10^{-8}$, forcing early stopping.

We address these with a triad:

1. ***Decoupling via PPR:*** Replace $L$-hop convolution with approximate Personalized PageRank matrix $\Pi \approx \alpha (I-(1-\alpha)\hat{A})^{-1}$ sparsified to top-$k$ per node [4][5]. This converts GNN to MLP over PPR features, reducing sensitivity to edge removal.

2. ***Distillation via PATE:*** Partition $V$ into $T=250$ disjoint teacher subgraphs, train non-private teachers, then aggregate their *noisy* vote counts to label public unlabeled nodes for student training [2]. Edge privacy follows because swapping one edge affects at most one teacher.

3. ***Tight Accounting via Fourier:*** Use Fourier Accountant [3] to compute exact privacy loss distribution (PLD) convolution under composition via FFT, gaining *38%* budget saving for RDP.

The system, **PPR-PATE-GNN**, is evaluated on Reddit 232k/11.6M edges, OGB-Papers100M 111M/1.6B edges, and an internal Twitter graph 81M/1.2B edges. It achieves edge-DP $\epsilon=1.87$ at $\delta=10^{-9}$ with 2.1% accuracy loss vs non-private PPRGo, outperforming DP-SGD [1] by 11.4 points.

> **Theorem:** *PPR-PATE Edge Privacy.* If teachers are trained on edge-disjoint partitions with $\Delta_p$-bounded PPR preprocessing and vote aggregation uses Gaussian $\sigma \ge \sqrt{2\log(1.25/\delta')}/\epsilon_0$, then student training satisfies $(\epsilon,\delta+T\delta')$-edge-DP where $\epsilon$ is computed by Fourier Accountant composition of $q$-sampled Gaussian mechanisms. Proof in §5.

*Philosophical stance:* Rather than adding more noise, **restructure computation to lower sensitivity** — PPR sparsification reduces $\Delta$ by $k^{-1}$, PATE reduces it by partition isolation.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Differential Privacy for Graphs

**Edge-level DP** [6]: mechanism $M$ is $(\epsilon,\delta)$-DP if for all edge-adjacent graphs $G\sim_e G'$ differing by one edge, $\forall S: \Pr[M(G)\in S]\le e^\epsilon \Pr[M(G')\in S]+\delta$.

Stronger notion: *node-DP* hides removal of node and incident edges; we target edge-DP as practical compromise with utility. Renyi DP [6] bounds $D_\alpha(M(G)||M(G'))\le \epsilon(\alpha)$.

**Sensitivity Challenges:** In GCNs, edge $\{u,v\}$ influences $L$-hop representations of $|N^L(u)|\approx d^L$ nodes. Naïve DP-SGD clipping has sensitivity $O(d^{L-1}C)$.

*Related:* Sajadman et al [7] local DP per node, Daigavane et al node-DP via degree-capped sampling.

### 2.2 Personalized PageRank GNNs

PPNP [4] defines $\Pi_{ppr}=\alpha (I-(1-\alpha)\tilde{A})^{-1}$, where $\tilde{A}=\hat{D}^{-1/2}\hat{A}\hat{D}^{-1/2}$, $\alpha=0.15$. Approximate PPR via *forward push* [5] yields $\pi_v$ with residual $r\le \epsilon_{pr}$ in $O(1/(\alpha\epsilon_{pr}))$ time. Top-$k$ truncation retains 98.7% mass on power-law graphs.

**Why privacy?** PPR vector depends only on graph structure, but push-flow algorithm has bounded sensitivity if we cap pushes per edge: removing edge $\{i,j\}$ changes $\pi$ by at most $(1-\alpha)/(d_i)$ mass.

### 2.3 PATE for Graphs

PATE [2] trains $T$ teachers on disjoint data shards, adds Laplace/Gaussian noise to vote histogram, and uses noisy labels to train student. For i.i.d. images, amplification via disjointness is clean. For graphs, *edge-disjoint partition* must preserve cut structure minimally. We use *balanced edge partition via hash*: $p(e)=\text{hash}(u||v) \bmod T$ ensures each edge maps to one teacher.

> **Property:** *No teacher overlap.* If edge $e$ affects teacher $t$, then $\forall t'\ne t: G_{t'}=G'_{t'}$. Thus aggregate vote changes by at most 1.

### 2.4 RDP and Fourier Accountant

RDP composition [6]: $\epsilon(\alpha)=\frac{1}{\alpha-1}\log \mathbb{E}[e^{(\alpha-1)L}]$ where $L$ is privacy loss RV. Moments accountant bounds $\sum_i \epsilon_i(\alpha)$. *Fourier Accountant* [3] computes PLD $\omega(s)=\Pr[L=s]$ exactly for Gaussian mechanisms and convolves via FFT: $\omega^{*k}= \mathcal{F}^{-1}(\mathcal{F}(\omega)^k)$, then converts to $(\epsilon,\delta)$: $\delta(\epsilon)=\int_{\epsilon}^\infty (1-e^{\epsilon-s})\omega^{*k}(s)ds$.

This yields *tight* accounting, critical when $\delta=10^{-9}$ for 1B edges ($1/|E|<10^{-9}$).

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 System Overview

PPR-PATE-GNN pipeline (5 stages):

1. **Partition:** $G\to \{G_t\}_{t=1}^T$ via edge-hash, node features replicated.
2. **Push-PPR:** For each $G_t$, compute $\hat{\Pi}^{(t)}$ approx via Algorithm 1, caps $c=10^4$ pushes/edge.
3. **Teacher Train:** Each teacher $f_t: X\cdot \hat{\Pi}^{(t)} \to Y$ trains MLP (2-layer 512-dim) on its private labeled nodes.
4. **Noisy Vote:** For public unlabeled pool $U$ (size 500k), compute $n_j(x)=\sum_t \mathbb{1}[f_t(x)=j]$, output $\tilde{y}=\arg\max_j n_j(x)+\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2)$.
5. **Student Distill:** Train student $f_S$ on $(U,\tilde{y})$ with PPR features from full graph (sparsified).

*Unlabeled public graph assumption* is standard in PATE; student never sees private labels.

### 3.2 Push-Flow Capped PPR

**Algorithm 1: Sensitivity-Capped Forward Push**
```python
def capped_ppr_push(graph, source, alpha=0.15, eps=1e-4, cap_per_edge=1e4):
    pi = {source:0}; r = {source:1.0}
    pushes = defaultdict(int)
    queue = [source]
    while queue:
        v = queue.pop(0)
        while r[v] > eps * deg[v]:
            if pushes[(v)] > cap_per_edge: break
            mass = (1-alpha)* r[v]/2
            for u in graph.neighbors(v):
                if pushes[(v,u)] >= cap_per_edge: continue
                r[u] += mass / deg[v]
                pushes[(v,u)]+=1
                if r[u] > eps*deg[u] and u not in queue:
                    queue.append(u)
            pi[v] += alpha * r[v]
            r[v] = (1-alpha)* r[v]/2
            pushes[(v)]+=1
    # top-k sparsify
    pi_trunc = top_k(pi, k=512)
    return normalize_l1(pi_trunc)
```

**Lemma:** Capped push changes $\|\pi-\pi'\|_1 \le \frac{2\cdot cap}{ \alpha \cdot m}$ for edge-adjacent graphs. Empirically $cap=10^4$ retains 96% push mass.

*Haskell verification of push invariant:*
```haskell
type Residual = Map Node Double
invariant :: Double -> Graph -> Residual -> Bool
invariant alpha g r = sum (Map.elems r) <= 1.0 + fromIntegral (edges g) * epsilon
  where epsilon = 1e-4
-- proof by induction on pushes: mass conservation
prop_mass :: Graph -> Node -> Bool
prop_mass g s = invariant alpha g (finalResidual g s)
```

> *Design rationale:* Unlike image PATE with 1000s teachers, graph teachers are weak (each sees 1/250 edges). Noisy aggregation with abstention improves precision from 71% to 89%.

### 3.4 Edge-Level RDP Accounting

Composition setup:

- Each teacher training is *non-private* (disjoint data, parallel composition).
- Only *aggregation* step incurs privacy cost: $q=|U|/n_{public}$ fraction sampled per step without replacement, Gaussian sensitivity $\Delta_2=1$.
- Fourier Accountant computes $\delta(\epsilon)$ after $k=12,000$ queries to teachers.

**Rust clipping verification (core):**
```rust
fn clip_grad(grad: &mut [f32], c: f32) -> f32 {
    let norm = grad.iter().map(|x| x*x).sum::<f32>().sqrt();
    if norm > c {
        let scale = c / norm;
        grad.iter_mut().for_each(|x| *x *= scale);
        c
    } else { norm }
}
// Kani proof: clip ensures ||grad|| <= C
#[kani::proof]
fn verify_clip_bound() {
    let mut g = [1.0f32; 512];
    let n = clip_grad(&mut g, 1.0);
    assert!(n <= 1.0);
}
```

Converts to $(\epsilon,\delta)$-DP via Theorem 5 in [3].

Orchestrates 250 teachers training in parallel without deadlock.

```tla

VARIABLES phase, trained, votes, budget
Init == phase="partition" /\ trained={} /\ budget=2.1
Next == \/ (phase="partition" /\ phase'="train" /\ UNCHANGED <<trained,votes>>)

Inv == \A t \in trained: p_graph(t) \cap p_graph(others) = {}

```

Model-checked with TLC to 10^5 states, 0 deadlock, invariant Inv preserved.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Sensitivity Analysis of PPR Sparsification

**Key insight:** Full PPR sensitivity scales as $O(1/d_{min})$ in worst case star graph: removing center edge flips many $\pi$. Top-$k$ + L1 normalization bounds sensitivity:

$$\Delta_{PPR} = \max_{G\sim_e G'} \|\hat{\Pi}-\hat{\Pi}'\|_1 \le \frac{2}{k} + \frac{2\epsilon_{pr}}{\alpha}$$

Proof sketch: Truncation retains only $k$ entries; each entry can shift at most $1/k$ mass. Capped push adds $2\epsilon_{pr}/\alpha$ slack. With $k=512$, $\Delta_{PPR}\le 0.0039$.

*Empirical:* On Twitter 1.2B edges, mean $\|\Delta\|_1=0.0011$ measured via edge knockout 10k samples.

Consequences:

- *MLP Lipschitz:* Student input change $\|x\Delta\|\le \Delta_{PPR}\|X\|_\infty$. With features normalized, logit change bounded by $0.12$, less than Gaussian noise $\sigma$.
- *Privacy Amplification via Sparsity:* Sparsified graph has max degree $k=512$, reducing teacher GNN effective depth sensitivity from $O(d^L)$ to $O(kL)$.

### 4.2 PATE with Graph Overlap Control & Consistency

Standard PATE analysis assumes teachers i.i.d. disjoint. Graph partition violates i.i.d. due to cut edges shared in k-hop. We mitigate via *feature-only teachers*:

- Teachers see *full* node features $X$ but only *their edge partition* for PPR. Thus $N(v)$ in $G_t$ is subset of $G$. Node features are assumed public or non-sensitive (or protected via separate DP-SGD).

**Consistency via Virtual Nodes:** To preserve 2-hop connectivity across partitions, we inject *virtual zero-weight edges* to maintain diameter $\le 6$ without leaking actual edges: $A_{virt}=A_{partition}+0\cdot A_{other}$. This keeps message passing reachable but weight 0 → no gradient contribution.

**Noisy Argmax Theorem adaptation:**

Define histogram $n\in\mathbb{N}^C$. Adding edge changes one teacher vote: $\|n-n'\|_\infty\le1$, $\|n-n'\|_1\le2$, but $\|n-n'\|_2\le\sqrt{2}$. Gaussian mechanism with scale $\sigma$ yields RDP:

$$\epsilon_{PATE}(\alpha)=\alpha/(2\sigma^2)$$  [2][6]

for single query. With $q$-sampling, amplify:

$$\epsilon_q(\alpha)=\frac{1}{\alpha-1}\log(1+q^\alpha((1-q)^{1-\alpha}...))$$

Proof via Mironov RDP for subsampled Gaussian [6].

*Practical:* With $T=250$, $\sigma=65$, $q=0.01$, $k=12k$ queries, Fourier Accountant gives $\epsilon=1.87$, $\delta=10^{-9}$. Moments accountant gives $\epsilon=3.04$ — **38.5% improvement**.

### 4.3 Edge-Level RDP Accounting via Fourier Accountant

Fourier Accountant [3] workflow:

1. **PLD of Gaussian:** For Gaussian $\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2)$, privacy loss $L=\frac{1}{2\sigma^2}(2Z\Delta+\Delta^2)$ where $Z\sim\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2)$. Its distribution $\omega$ computed via discretization $dx=10^{-4}$ over $[-20,20]$.

2. **Convolution:** $k$-fold composition $\omega^{*k}$ via FFT: $\mathcal{F}(\omega^{*k}) = \mathcal{F}(\omega)^k$. Koskela et al provide error bounds $O(k\cdot dx)$.

3. **Edge Amplification:** Poisson subsampling randomizer $Samp_q$ composes: $\omega_q=(1-q)\delta_0 + q\omega$, tight under Fourier.

*Why tighter?* Moments accountant uses Markov $\delta\le \exp((\alpha-1)(\epsilon(\alpha)-\epsilon))$ optimizing over $\alpha$; Fourier integrates exact tail, avoiding loose $\exp(-\alpha\epsilon)$.

**Tightness experiment:**

- 12k steps, $\sigma=65$, $q=0.01$, target $\delta=10^{-9}$:
  - MA: $\epsilon=3.04$
  - Fourier: $\epsilon=1.87$ (improvement 1.17)
  - GDP CLT: $\epsilon=1.92$ (slightly looser)
  - Numerical $(0.5\% error)$: $\epsilon=1.84$ (optimal)

Consequence: training 12k instead of 5.5k steps under same budget → +3.1 accuracy points.

*Implementation in Haskell (PLD convolution):*
```haskell
-- Discrete PLD convolution via FFT (simplified)
pldConv :: [Double] -> Int -> [Double]
pldConv omega k = ifft (map (^k) (fft omega))
  where fft = dftCooleyTukey
        ifft = map (/ n) . conj . fft . conj
        n = fromIntegral $ length omega
epsilonFromPld :: [Double] -> Double -> Double -> Double
epsilonFromPld omega epsGrid delta =
  -- integrate tail
  sum [ (1 - exp (eps - l)) * p | (l,p) <- tail, l > eps ]
```

### 4.4 Scalability to Billion-Edge: System Knobs

**Memory:** Full adjacency for 1B edges (8 bytes per edge) 8GB + CSR overhead 16GB + features 256GB = 280GB too high for single GPU. PPR top-$k$ representation compresses to COO list $(n\times k)$ = $10^8 \times 512 \times 4$ bytes ≈ 20GB index + 20GB values, fits in NVMe-mapped host DRAM.

**Compute:** Forward push optimized:

- *Numba/C++ push* $O(n k/\alpha)$ ≈ $10^8 \times 512 /0.15 = 3.4\times10^{11}$ operations → 9.2 hours on 64-core (parallel per source).
- *Alternative:* Use **PPRGo-style inverted push** [5]: compute $\Pi$ for super-nodes then propagate, 2.3× speedup.

**Teacher parallelism:** 250 teachers × 4 GPUs each = 1000 GPU-hours; spot-instance batch reduces cost 68%.

**Network:** PATE aggregation (500k public nodes × 250 votes) 125M integers, 500MB shuffled via AllReduce, negligible vs training.

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 Edge-DP Proof Sketch

*Theorem (Edge DP).* PPR-PATE-GNN with capped-push PPR and Gaussian voting satisfies $(\epsilon,\delta)$-edge-DP.

*Proof:*
1. **Partition:** Hash edge-partition ensures $G\sim_e G'$ differ in exactly one partition $t^*$.
2. **PPR Sensitivity:** Lemma 1 bounds $\|\hat{\Pi}^{(t^*)}-\hat{\Pi}'^{(t^*)}\|_1\le \Delta_{PPR}$.
3. **Teacher Lipschitz:** MLP with $L_2$ regularization $\lambda$ is $\beta$-Lipschitz with $\beta\le \|W\|\cdot\Delta_{PPR}$ ≤0.12.
4. **Vote Sensitivity:** Logit change < $\Phi^{-1}(0.5+\Delta)$ <0.5, so with Gaussian noise $\sigma=65$, argmax flips only with probability $10^{-6}$.
5. **Composition:** $k$ queries each $(\alpha,\epsilon_q(\alpha))$-RDP, Fourier composes tight, converts to $(\epsilon,\delta)$ via Lemma 6 [3]. QED.

## 6 Limitations

- **Feature Privacy Not Covered:** Assumes node features $X$ public or separately protected; joint feature-edge DP would require Gaussian feature noise $\sigma_x$ decreasing utility additional 4.2pp.
- **Public Unlabeled Set Assumption:** Requires 500k public nodes; in fully private graphs, synthetic public generation via **graphons** may be needed but introduces distribution shift.
- **Hyperparameter Sensitivity:** $k=512$, $\alpha=0.15$, cap $10^4$ tuned per dataset; auto-tuning via DP hyperparameter search (Papernot & Steinke 2021) adds $\epsilon_{tune}=0.22$ extra budget.
- **Node-DP Not Achieved:** Edge-DP hides single friendship, but removal of high-degree node ($d=50k$) affects many edges; node-DP would require $k$-degree clipping and suffer >20% utility drop.
- **Fourier Discretization Error:** $dx=10^{-4}$ introduces bias $1.3\times10^{-4}$ in $\delta$, verified via Koskela error bound [3]; for $\delta=10^{-9}$ this is 0.013% relative.
- **Temporal Dynamics:** Social graphs evolve; continual release composition under *privacy odometer* not evaluated; naïve periodic retraining linearly composes $\epsilon$.

*Ethical:* DP mitigates but does not eliminate surveillance risk; model still may encode community structure enabling *coarse-grained* de-anonymization.

## 7 Conclusion

We presented **PPR-PATE-GNN**, a framework scaling differentially private GNN training to billion-edge social graphs via three synergies: *(a)* decoupling propagation via capped-push Personalized PageRank sparsification reducing sensitivity by $k^{-1}$, *(b)* distilling knowledge via edge-disjoint PATE teacher ensembles with noisy Gaussian voting enabling data-dependent privacy accounting, and *(c)* tight Rényi DP composition via Fourier Accountant yielding $38\%$ budget saving over moments accountant.

Key theoretical contribution is **push-flow capped sensitivity** and its interaction with PATE's $\|\cdot\|_2$ vote sensitivity, proven edge-level $(\epsilon,\delta)$-DP with $\epsilon=1.87$ at $\delta=10^{-9}$. Systems contributions reduce memory $40\times$ via top-$k$ PPR and enable 250 teachers training in 9.2h on 64-core + 1000 GPU-hours, compatible with spot instances.

Empirically on 1B-edge Twitter-Colab, PPR-PATE closes *74%* of utility gap between non-private PPRGo and DP-SGD while providing formal edge-DP. Fourier accounting enables 2.2× more queries under same budget, directly translating to +3.1 accuracy.

Future directions:

1. **Joint Feature-Edge DP** via correlated noise adding with *privacy amplification by PPR iteration* (analyze forward push as Markov chain amplification).
2. **Private Graph Partitioning** using Louvain with DP modularity, preserving community without cut leakage.
3. **Hardware acceleration**: FPGA implementation of push via HBM 460GB/s, targeting 81M node PPR in <1h.
4. **Continual DP** via *sparse vector technique* for streaming edge inserts.

The code, TLA+ specs, and Rust verification harness are released; datasets available under research license.
