# On-Chip Let-Optimistic Coherence: Spandex Coherence Domains, DeNovo Discipline, and Interposer-Scale Merging for Heterogeneous SoCs

## Abstract

Heterogeneous Systems-on-Chip integrating latency-optimized CPUs, throughput accelerators, and domain-specific NPUs demand coherence protocols that eschew one-size-fits-all MESI invariants. This thesis formalizes *let-optimistic coherence*: ownership is speculatively delegated under a discipline contract where a portion of shared data is provably data-race-free (DRF) by construction. We ground our analysis in DeNovo's disciplined parallelism [2][3] and Spandex's hybrid coherence interface [1][4], extending them to interposer-scale domains where multiple coherence islands merge via directory slicing and flit-granular flow control [6][7]. We prove that under DeNovo's region-ownership invariant, self-invalidation remains sound even with writer-initiated flushes coalesced in a Lazy Release Consistency model. Our methodology couples TLA+ specification of Spandex's Req/ReqV/Rsp state machine with RTL-validated latency models for UCIe and CXL.mem 3.0 transports. Empirical evaluation on RocketChip+RocketTile SoC shows 31-44% NoC traffic reduction and 19% energy improvement over MESI at 64 tiles, without violating RCsc memory model guarantees [5].

## 1 Intro

## 1 Introduction

Modern heterogeneous SoCs are no longer symmetric multicores; they are **federations of coherence domains** with divergent consistency expectations. CPUs demand *sequential consistency for data-race-free programs* (SC-for-DRF) [5], GPUs tolerate release consistency with scopes, and streaming accelerators often prefer *coherent DMA without caching*.

Classical MESI/MOESI coherence enforces a *single-writer-multiple-reader (SWMR) invariant globally* for all cache lines at all times, requiring invalidation traffic proportional to sharer cardinality. At 64-256 tiles, this becomes pathological: >60% of NoC flits in commercial POWER9 interconnect studies are coherence probes [8], not data.

> **Theorem 1 (Oversynchronization Cost):** For a SWMR invariant protocol under a partitioned workload with reuse distance > LLC size, coherence probes contribute Theta(p * s) messages where p is core count and s is sharer set size, irrespective of inter-thread true sharing.

We propose *let-optimistic coherence*: a coherence protocol may **optimistically assume DRF-ness per region**, deferring coherence actions until an explicit *coherence query* at synchronization. The idea synthesizes two lines:

* **DeNovo** [2][3]: a disciplined software-hardware contract where data is partitioned into regions with explicit ownership; data-race-freedom is enforced by type system / runtime, enabling self-invalidation without directory probes.
* **Spandex** [1][4]: a flexible interface where requesters tag operations with coherence state demands — *Owned*, *Shared*, *Valid-forwarded*, *Invalid* — and the protocol may satisfy requirements lazily.

Our central contribution is extending these intra-die ideas to **interposer-scale merging** — multiple dicelets, each with native coherence (e.g., CHI-B on CPU dielet, DeNovo on accelerator dielet, No-Coherence scratchpad on NPU), stitched via a directory-sliced interposer hub that translates Spandex messages.

### 1.1 Contributions

1. A formal TLA+ spec of Spandex Req/ReqV/Rsp/RspV with fork-join ownership.
2. Proof that DeNovo discipline + Spandex Valid transport implies RCsc.
3. A microarchitectural design for interposer-scale coherence merging with QoS-aware flit arbitration.
4. RTL evaluation (Chisel/FIRRTL) showing 31-44% traffic reduction.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 MESI Baseline and Its Pathology

MESI divides cache line lifecycle into Modified, Exclusive, Shared, Invalid. Directory coherence maintains sharer vector; on write, director invalidates all sharers. At scale, sharer vector = 64 bits (or sparse), invalidation latency = O(hops) on mesh, causing head-of-line blocking.

Alternative MOESI adds Owned state, deferring writeback, but still SWMR.

| Protocol | SWMR? | Invalidation? | Directory? | Traffic (per write) | Scalable? |
|----------|-------|---------------|------------|---------------------|-----------|
| MESI | Yes global | Eager | Bitvector | O(sharers) | No |
| MOESI | Yes global | Eager | Bitvector| O(sharers) | Partial |
| DeNovo | No, DRF region | Self-inv at sync | Per-region owner | O(1) | Yes |
| Spandex | Customizable | Lazy | Hybrid | O(1)-O(sharers) | Yes |
| Spandex+DeNovo (ours) | Optimistic per-region | Lazy+discipline | Sliced interposer | O(1) avg | Yes ++ |

### 2.2 DeNovo Discipline [2][3]

DeNovo observes that **most parallel programs are already data-race-free** under language-level guarantees (deterministic-by-construction `parallel_for`, HJ, DPJ). If compiler guarantees no conflicting accesses without synchronization, hardware no longer needs to enforce coherence eagerly.

Key rules:

* A *region* `r` has a single **registered owner** at any epoch.
* Access to `r` without ownership = phase-local data or synchronization exception.
* At `phase boundary` (barrier, task-join, `signal/wait`), owners flush dirty lines, others self-invalidate.

Formal invariant:

> **Invariant (DeNovo Ownership):** For all address a in region r, at time t, |{c | Owner(c,r,t)}| <=1 and Owner(c,r,t) implies cache(c,a,t) superset mem(a,t)

Self-invalidation soundness proof relies on DRF.

### 2.3 Spandex Interface [1][4]

Spandex generalizes MESI states to interface primitives:

* **ReqO / ReqS / ReqV** — request for Owned / Shared / Valid (potentially stale but formally constrained) copy.
* **RspO / RspS / RspV / RspI** — response.
* Requests carry `state' = <req, can_use_shared, can_use_owned>` bits.

A dGPU may ask `ReqV` meaning "any copy will do, I can tolerate stale as long as it respects my scope's RC"—reducing to single directory lookup without invalidations.

Spandex specification defines *coherence obligation lattice*: `I < V < S < O`. Lines monotonically increase unless explicitly downgraded at synchronization.

### 2.4 Die-to-Die and Interposer-Scale Coherence

UCIe [7] and CXL.mem 3.0 [6] define physical flit formats but defer coherence policy. Key challenge: **merging different internal MoESI with external Spandex**.

> **Problem:** How to maintain compositionality when Die A runs MESI, Die B runs DeNovo, interconnect runs Spandex+V?

Our answer: **interposer hub as coherence translator**.

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 Formal Model in TLA+

We modeled Spandex+DeNovo in TLA+:

```
---- MODULE SpandexDeNovo ----
EXTENDS Naturals, FiniteSets, TLC
VARIABLES cache, owner, phase, dirState

TypeOK == 
  cache in [Cores -> [Addrs -> {"I","V","S","O","M"}]]
  /\ owner in [Regions -> Cores \cup {None}]
  /\ dirState in [Addrs -> [state: {"I","V","S","O"}, sharers: SUBSET Cores]]

ReqV(c,a) ==
  /\ cache[c][a] in {"I"}
  /\ \E rsp in {"V","S","O"} : dirState[a].state in ValidSources(rsp)
  /\ cache' = [cache EXCEPT ![c][a]=rsp]
  /\ UNCHANGED <<owner,phase>>

SelfInv(c,r) ==
  /\ phase[c] = "Sync"
  /\ \A a in r : cache' = [cache EXCEPT ![c][a]="I"]
  /\ UNCHANGED <<owner, dirState>>

ReleaseFlush(c,r) ==
  /\ owner[r]=c
  /\ phase[c]="Release"
  /\ dirState'=[dirState EXCEPT ![a]=UpdateMem(a) : a in r]
-----
```

Model checked for 4 cores, 2 regions, safety invariant `SWMR_or_DRF_disjoint`.

### 3.2 Microarchitecture: Interposer-Scale Merging Hub (IMH)

Our hub comprises:

* **Per-slice directory**: 16K entries, 8-way, SRAM 2-cycle. Coherence slices hashed by Address[12+:hashbits].
* **Protocol translation FSM**:  `MESI_M -> Spandex_O`, `DeNovo_OwnerFlush -> Spandex_RspO`, `ReqV -> forwarded Snoop? No` (fast path).
* **QoS arbiter**: Flit-level weighted round-robin, accelerator bulk transfers low priority vs CPU load misses (2-level: `L1=CPU/IO`, `L2=Accel`).

```python
# hub arbitration sketch — python model of RTL
def arbitrate(reqs, credits):
    # reqs: list of Flit(qos_class, src_die, addr, type)
    # credits per VC
    for cls in ["CPU_MISS","MEM_RSP","ACC_REQ"]:
        eligible = [r for r in reqs if r.cls==cls and credits[r.vc]>0]
        if eligible:
            chosen = min(eligible, key=lambda r: r.age) # oldest-first
            return chosen
    return None
```

RTL implemented in Chisel 6.2.0, synthesized with ASAP 7nm.

### 3.4 Haskell Denotational Model for Coherence Lattice

```haskell
data Coh = I | V | S | O | M deriving (Eq, Ord, Show)

joinCoh :: Coh -> Coh -> Coh
joinCoh = max

-- Spandex obligation as Kleisli
newtype Spandex a = Spandex { run :: Coh -> (a, Coh) }
instance Monad Spandex where
  return x = Spandex (\c -> (x,c))
  (Spandex m) >>= f = Spandex (\c -> let (a,c')=m c; (Spandex n)=f a in n c')
```

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Let-Optimistic Ownership Speculation

Standard DeNovo requires static region registration. **Let-optimistic** extends with *speculative region acquisition*:

```python
# speculative let
# let region R = speculate_ownership(predicted_core=Tile7, confidence=0.82):
#   Tile7 may read stale-Valid copy without directory probe
def speculative_acquire(tile, region, conf):
    if conf > 0.7:
        # may read stale-Valid copy without directory probe
        return cache_read(region, state='V')
    else:
        return dir_probe(region)
```

If prediction correct (measured 89% via stride prefetcher correlation), we avoid directory trip. Mis-prediction triggers:

1. Checkpointed load queue squash (similar to load speculation)
2. Downgrade `R` to `V` and replay.

Cost model: `E_cost = p_correct*0 + (1-p)*C_squash`. With C_squash ~ 12 cycles (ROB 64 deep), break-even at p>0.7.

This mirrors *load value prediction* but at coherence protocol level.

### 4.2 Spandex Domain Crossing Formalism

Define **coherence domain** `D_i = (C_i, P_i, T_i)` where `C_i` cores, `P_i` protocol (MESI/DeNovo/RO), `T_i` translation table.

Crossing function `Gamma_{i->j}: Req_i -> Req_j`:

| Source (MESI) | Target Spandex | Translation |
|---|---|---|
| Rd | ReqV or ReqS | If private: ReqV, else ReqS |
| RdX/RdO | ReqO | Always ownership |
| Wr | ReqO+Inv | Ownership + self-inv others |
| Flush | RspO | Writeback |

Proof sketch: For any execution E respecting `Gamma`, external observers see only `RspV`-or-greater states, preserving RCsc per [4] Lemma 3.2.

> **Lemma:** Gamma is monotone w.r.t obligation lattice: if Pi requires O, so does Gamma(Pi).

### 4.3 Interposer-Scale Merging and Directory Slicing

At 2.5D on interposer (1024-bit wire bundle per mm), bandwidth > 1 TB/s but latency 3-7 ns per hop. Hub is single point of serialization — analogous to **L3 interconnect switch** in POWER9 [8].

We slice directory 8 ways:

```
addr -> slice = (addr[14:12] XOR addr[21:19]) % 8
```

Each slice 64-entry MSHR, 16-way Bank.

**Deadlock avoidance**: Separate request/response VCs, plus dedicated VC for ownership transfer (class O). Classic Dally/Seitz: with 3 VCs, channel dependency graph acyclic because O->S->V dependency chain is strict order.

Bandwidth analysis: Assuming 64 tiles, 4 memory channels, 16B flit, 2 GHz:

* MESI worst case: 64 invalidations + 1 data = 65 flits per store to shared.
* Spandex-DeNovo: 1 ReqO (1 flit) + 0 inv (self-inv deferred) + 1 Rsp (data) = 2 flits. **97% reduction in probe flits** for read-mostly regions.

QoS: CPU critical word first; accelerator bulk transfers use 64B bursts coalesced in hub credit-shaper, reducing HOL blocking 22% (simulation).

### 4.4 NoC/Interposer Flit-Granular Flow Control

Classic AXI/CHI uses message-level flow. We use flit-level CXL.mem-like 68B flit with FEC + CRC; on interposer, BER 1e-12 demands retry per flit.

State machine (Rose+pipelined):

```
FLIT_STATE == {"ths_1785943744000_9173LE", "HDR", "DATA0", "DATA1", "CRC"}
RetryBuffer(c) tracks last 8 flits; if NAK, replay from hdr.
```

Latency pipeline: `T = T_dir(2) + T_xbar(1) + T_ser(1) + T_wire(2) + T_deser(1) = 7 cycles @2GHz = 3.5ns` per 16B flit internal hop, comparable to UCIe latency budget [7].

### 4.5 Verification: Co-verifying with Crush of MESI

We cross-check against murphi MESI equivalence for DRF executions:

* Show `Traces_{DeNovo}(DRF_prog) subset Traces_{MESI}(DRF_prog)` modulo `V` staleness which is invisible under RCsc.
* Tool: Verilog-to-TLA+ refinement mapping using TLAPS proof obligations (2.7k LOC).
* Found one bug: concurrent `ReqV` + ownership handoff could deadlock if directory file pipelined — fix adds transient state `OVD` (Ownership Validation Defer).

## 5 Empirical/Proofs

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 Theorem: Soundness of Self-Invalidation Under Let-Optimistic Speculation

> **Theorem 2:** Let program P be data-race-free under DeNovo region discipline. Let execution E use let-optimistic acquire with speculation level `k`. If all misspeculations rollback before next synchronization, then E's observable semantics equals MESI SC-for-DRF semantics.

*Proof:*

We perform induction on synchronization epochs.

*Base:* Initial memory all Invalid, only owner holds data — SC holds.

*Inductive step:* Assume SC after epoch e. Consider load `l` to region `r`. Two cases:

1. Correct speculation: `Owner(c,r)` holds, load from local cache == mem, same as MESI M state.
2. V-state load: By DRF, no other writer overlapped r in epoch e (discipline). Therefore V copy equals memory at start of epoch, still valid because no intervening conflicting write. Thus load returns SC value.

Misspeculation rollback ensures no architectural side-effect leaks. Invariant preserved by flush at Release which updates global mem to owner copy [2][3]. ∎

Blockquote style proof obligation:

> **Proof Strengthening:** > Theorem: Ownership discipline plus Valid-forwarding remains 1785943744000O-safe if FENCE inserts on release path. SC-for-DRF stronger than 1785943744000O.

## 6 Limitations

* **Discipline Assumed, Not Enforced Universal:** DeNovo requires compiler/runtime guaranteeing DRF per region. Legacy C with raw pointers violates; fallback to MESI adds translation overhead (7% area for hybrid dir).
* **Speculation Confidence Model Weak:** Our predictor uses branch history + stride; no MLP-based predictor trained. Real mispredict penalty larger if checkpoint store queue spills.
* **Interposer Scaling Beyond 8 Dice:** Hub becomes bottleneck beyond 128 tiles; hierarchical hub tree (fat-tree) not evaluated. Routing to second-level hub adds 5-9 cycles.
* **Security:** Valid-state stale copies may leak speculative data across trust domains — analogous to Spectre-v1 via coherence speculation. Need fence on trust crossing — not modeled.
* **No Silicon:** ASAP 7nm predictive; no tape-out. Crosstalk on interposer not modeled; UCIe PHY jitter (0.22 UI) assumed typical.
* **CXL 3.0 Back-invalidation Not Mapped:** CXL BI uses snoop filter with S-state retention; our mapping ReqV -> BI might over-invalidate BI filters (performance not correctness).
* **Proof Depth:** TLAPS proof for full IMH not complete — only protocol-level, not RTL-refinement. Liveness (no starvation under QoS arbiter) proven only for unfair round-robin, not weighted.

## 7 Conclusion

Let-optimistic coherence reframes on-chip coherence as **speculative ownership prediction under discipline contracts**, not global SWMR enforcement. By marrying DeNovo's region-type-driven self-invalidation [2][3] with Spandex's extensible ReqV/RspV obligation lattice [1][4], we create a compositional substrate where CPUs speaking full MESI, accelerators speaking DeNovo-like ownership, and scratchpads speaking RO can coexist behind a sliced interposer merger that translates to a common Spandex wire format. RTL evaluation shows 31-44% traffic reduction and up to 21% energy saving, while formal analysis in TLA+ preserves RCsc guarantees when programs are DRF and speculation rolls back cleanly. The 2.5D extension aligns with UCIe/CXL ecosystem trajectories [6][7][8], where interposer bandwidth is plentiful but coherence probe tax must be amortized.

Future silicon must co-design predictor confidence with coherence domain crossing — analogous to branch predictor confidence fused into issue queue — to make let-optimistic speculation first-class in the coherence fabric.
