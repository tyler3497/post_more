# Reconfigurable Photonic Matrix Accelerators: Coherent Mach-Zehnder Meshes, Nonvolatile Phase-Change Attenuators, and Wavelength-Division-Multiplexed Diffractive Deep Networks for Energy-Efficient Inference

## Abstract
Coherent photonic integrated circuits offer *physically intrinsic* linear algebra: propagation through a passive interferometer mesh executes unitary multiplication at the speed of light with $O(1)$ latency independent of matrix dimension. This thesis develops a unified architectural theory of **reconfigurable photonic matrix accelerators** for inference, integrating three dominant paradigms — **Mach-Zehnder interferometer (MZI) meshes** for coherent unitary synthesis, **phase-change material (PCM) nonvolatile attenuators** for zero-static-power weighting, and **wavelength-division-multiplexed (WDM) diffractive networks** for massive spectral parallelism. We formalize the Reck [1994] and Clements [2016] decompositions for $U(N)$ implementation on silicon photonics, analyze phase-shifter control nonidealities, insertion loss accumulation, and calibration stochasticity, and contrast with $Ge_2Sb_2Te_5$ (GST) microheater-programmed GST-on-waveguide memories that store 4-5 bits with $>10^4$ endurance [1][3]. We further construct WDM-aware matrix-vector multiplication using microring resonator (MRR) weight banks and frequency-comb sources enabling $N_λ × N_{spatial}$ parallelism demonstrated at > 1 TOPS/W in 2022-2025 experiments [2][5][6]. Analytical models for **energy per MAC, compute density, and shot-noise-limited precision** show photonic advantage at 6-8 bit equivalent precision and $N>64$, with severe penalties beyond due to **coherent crosstalk, thermal drift, and O/E conversion**. We provide calibration algorithms, Haskell models of unitary learning, and TLA+ specifications of reconfiguration latency hiding.

## 1. Introduction

The von Neumann bottleneck for deep learning is fundamentally energetic: a 32-bit DRAM access costs ~640 pJ, a FP16 multiply ~0.5 pJ, and moving activations across a 2.5D interposer costs 2-5 pJ/bit. Electronic systolic arrays saturate near 1-3 TOPS/mm² at 5 nm with **interconnect-dominated** power [3].

Photonic computing proposes a physical inversion: *encode* information in the **complex amplitude** $E = |E|e^{jφ}$ of guided modes, and perform computation by *passive linear interference*. An ideal $N×N$ MZI mesh performs $y = U x$ where $U ∈ U(N)$ is unitary, with energy dominated by laser wall-plug and transduction rather than the multiplication itself, yielding theoretical sub-fJ/MAC operation [2][3].

Three technology vectors have converged after 2017-2026:

1.  **Coherent MZI meshes:** Shen et al. (2017) and subsequent programmable photonics demonstrations [2][4] showed vowel recognition with 56 MZIs on SOI. Self-learning architectures using *in-situ adjoint backpropagation* now train phase shifters without external models [1].
2.  **Nonvolatile phase-change photonics:** Integration of $Sb_2S_3$, GST, GSST on SiN waveguides achieves nonvolatile amplitude modulation with $Δn > 1.0$ and zero hold power, solving the volatile thermo-optic power dilemma that required ~20 mW per MZI continuously [3][4].
3.  **WDM diffractive and broadcast-and-weight:** Using Kerr combs and laser arrays, a single waveguide carries $λ_1...λ_{64}$ independently weighted by microring weight banks, then summed incoherently at germanium photodiodes, achieving $>10$ TMAC/s in recent JLT results [6].

> **Theorem 1 (Photonic Unitary Universality):** Any $N×N$ unitary matrix can be implemented by a triangular (Reck) or rectangular (Clements) mesh of $N(N-1)/2$ tunable $2×2$ beamsplitters with $2×$ phase shifters, up to a global phase.

*Critical thesis question:* Under realistic **7-15 dB insertion loss, 6-bit phase DAC quantization, and $±0.2 K$ thermal drift**, can reconfigurable photonic matrix accelerators maintain **>6-bit effective number of bits (ENOB)** for ImageNet / Transformer inference, and at what system energy?

We contribute:

- Rigorous derivation of MZI unitary synthesis and error analysis
- PCM multilevel programming and drift endurance model
- WDM broadcast-and-weight protocol and crosstalk budgeting
- System-level benchmark with *loss-aware* simulation in Python/JAX
- Architectural mitigations: hardware-aware training, singular-value rebalancing, and analog error correction

## 2. Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Silicon Photonics Platform

Modern photonic computation relies on **220 nm SOI** or **400 nm SiN** waveguides at 1550 nm C-band ($n_{eff} ≈ 2.44$). Key components:

| Component | Principle | Typical Metric | Limitation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Directional coupler** | Evanescent mode coupling | 50:50 ± 3% | Fabrication variation 5-10 nm |
| **MZI (balanced)** | $2×2$ couplers + $2×$ phase shifters | $V_πL ≈ 1-2 V·cm$ (carrier depletion) vs 10 mW thermal | Loss 0.2-0.8 dB / MZI |
| **MRR weight bank** | Lorentzian resonance $T(λ) = 1 - κ$ | Q ~ 10k-50k, FSR 1-3 THz | Thermal drift 80 pm/K |
| **PCM cell** | Amorphous ↔ crystalline index change | $Δα$ 0-4 dB, 4-bit levels, $10^4×$ cycles | Insertion loss 0.5-2 dB + stochasticity |
| **Ge PD** | Photocurrent $I = R·P$, $R≈0.9 A/W$ | BW >50 GHz | Shot + TIA noise |

*Bold insight:* **Waveguide loss (≈2 dB/cm) and fiber-to-chip coupling (2-4 dB) dominate** for meshes >32×32, forcing *folded* architectures with semiconductor optical amplifiers (SOAs) or electronic regeneration.

### 2.2 Linear Neural Network Mapping

An optical neural network layer $y = f(Wx + b)$ partitions linear $W$ via **singular value decomposition (SVD)**:

$$ W = U Σ V^† $$

where $U, V^† ∈ U(N)$ implemented by MZI meshes, $Σ$ diagonal implemented by Mach-Zehnder modulators (MZM) attenuators or SOA gain [2][4]. For $N=64$, Reck decomposition requires 2016 MZIs per unitary, 4032 + 64 = **4096 phase shifters**.

*Italic nuance:* *Weight-stationary* intuition from electronics maps to *phase-stationary* in photonics — reconfiguration costs µs-ms, so **batch >10k tokens amortizes reprogramming energy**.

### 2.3 Nonvolatile PCM Physics

Phase-change materials, notably $Ge_2Sb_2Te_5$ (GST), $Ge_2Sb_2Te_4$ low-loss variant, and wide-bandgap $Sb_2S_3$ / $Sb_2Se_3$ [3], undergo reversible crystalline ↔ amorphous transition via microheater pulses (10 ns-100 ns, 5-15 pJ). Effective index change $Δn_{eff} ≈ 0.2-0.8$ induces programmable loss/phase. Writable levels demonstrated: 5-bit (32 levels) with iterative write-verify [3].

*PCM advantages:*

- Zero hold power vs thermo-optic 20 mW/MZI
- Multilevel nonvolatile storage $>10^4 s$ @ 85°C
- Wavelength-oblivious attenuation enabling WDM reuse

### 2.4 WDM Broadcast-and-Weight

Introduced by Tait et al. and extended to 2023 JLT work [6], the protocol:

1.  **Comb**: Generate $N_λ = 16-64$ lines from mode-locked laser or Kerr soliton
2.  **Modulate**: Encode input vector $x_i(t)$ onto each λ via IQ modulators
3.  **Weight**: Drop weighted replica via MRR bank: $w_{ij}(λ_k)$
4.  **Summation**: Balanced PD sums incoherently $\sum_λ w_{ij} x_j$

Parallelism $N_λ × N_{spatial}$ yields **linear scaling without extra waveguides** [5][6].

## 3. Methodology

### 3.1 Coherent Mesh Synthesis and Control

We model a $2×2$ ideal MZI transfer:

```
          [ e^{jφ} sin(θ/2) ,  e^{jφ} cos(θ/2) ]
T_MZI =   [ cos(θ/2)       , -sin(θ/2)        ]
```

where $θ$ internal phase sets splitting ratio, $φ$ external phase sets output phase. Reck algorithm factorizes $U$ by nulling elements bottom-up using Givens rotations. Clements (rectangular) nets $O(N)$ depth halved vs Reck $O(2N)$ depth, reducing **accumulated phase error variance from $O(N)$ to $O(N^{0.5})$** [4].

**Calibration flow (Python pseudocode):**

```python
import jax.numpy as jnp
from jax import grad

def mesh_forward(phases, x):
    # phases: [N(N-1)]*2 for theta, phi
    U = eye(N, dtype=complex)
    idx=0
    for layer in clements_layers(N):
        for (i,j) in layer:
            th, ph = phases[idx], phases[idx+1]; idx+=2
            G = givens(th, ph, N, i, j)
            U = G @ U
    return U @ x

def hardware_aware_loss(phases, W_tgt, x_batch):
    # phase quantization noise model
    q_ph = quantize(phases, bits=7) + jnp.normal(0, sigma_phi)
    U = synthesize(q_ph)
    # insertion loss per MZI: exp(-alpha * depth)
    loss_mask = jnp.exp(-alpha_dB/4.34 * depth_count)
    return jnp.mean(jnp.abs(U*loss_mask - W_tgt)**2) + 1e-4*jnp.sum(phases**2)

grad_fn = grad(hardware_aware_loss)
# Use Adam with STE for quantization
```

*Key methodology:* We **co-optimize** with *physics-informed backprop* where forward pass simulates stochastic photonic impairments, backward uses straight-through estimator [1][2].

### 3.2 PCM Programming and Drift Model

Multilevel GST cell programmed via iterative program-read:

```rust
// Rust model for endurance-aware wear-leveling controller
struct PcmCell {
    level: u8,          // 0..31 for 5-bit
    drift_alpha: f32,   // crystallization drift
    cycles: u32,
}

impl PcmCell {
    fn write_level(&mut self, target: u8) -> Result<(), ProgramError> {
        if self.cycles > 10000 { return Err(Wearout); }
        let mut pulses = 0;
        loop {
            let read_trans = self.measure_transmission();
            let err = target as f32 - read_trans * 31.0;
            if err.abs() < 0.5 { break; }
            // adaptive pulse: amorphization 5V/20ns vs crystallization 2V/200ns
            let pulse_energy = if err < 0.0 { 12.0 } else { 3.5 }; // pJ
            self.apply_pulse(pulse_energy, err);
            pulses += 1;
            if pulses > 15 { return Err(Convergence); }
        }
        self.cycles += 1;
        Ok(())
    }
}
```

Empirical: **4-bit PCM achieves <0.8% transmission std dev** after 10k seconds, enabling 8-bit equivalent with 2-cell differential encoding [3][5].

### 3.3 WDM System Architecture

Our reference hybrid architecture:

1. **Frontend:** $λ=1550.12nm + k·0.8nm$, $k=0..31$ Kerr comb, 0 dBm/line
2. **Input E/O:** 32 GS/s, 6-bit DAC → SiPh IQ modulator, $V_π$ 2.5V
3. **Weight core:** Two options
   - *Coherent:* $32×32$ Clements mesh with thermo-optic phase shifters trimmed by PCM phase shifters [4]
   - *Incoherent WDM:* $64 λ × 32$ MRR banks, GST-clad rings for nonvolatile weighting
4. **Summation:** Balanced Ge-PD + TIA, 50 GHz, $I_{dark}=30 nA$
5. **Nonlinearity:** O/E/O with analog ReLU via saturation or electronic FPGA lookup (hybrid approach mandatory because **all-optical ReLU requires >10 mW** [1])

**TLA+ specification of reconfiguration latency hiding:**

```tla
---------------- MODULE PhotonicReconfig ----------------
VARIABLES meshPhase, jobQ, laserOn

Reconfig(mesh, newW) ==
    /\ meshPhase' = ProgramPhases(newW)
    /\ jobQ' = Append(jobQ, "STALL")  \* pipeline bubble
    /\ UNCHANGED laserOn

Compute(job) ==
    /\ jobQ # <<>>
    /\ LET w == Head(jobQ) IN
        w # "STALL" /\ meshPhase stable
    /\ jobQ' = Tail(jobQ)

Spec == [] [Reconfig \/ Compute]_<<meshPhase, jobQ>>
=========================================================
```

This guarantees **no compute during $10 µs$ thermal settling** unless wavefront mux switching.

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Mach-Zehnder Mesh: Reck vs Clements, and Self-Learning Coherence

> **Theorem 2 (Depth-Error Tradeoff):** For unitary construction with independent phase error variance $σ_φ^2$ per shifter, output fidelity $F = |Tr(U^†_{tgt} U_{impl})|^2 / N^2$ obeys $F ≈ exp(-N σ_φ^2·D(N))$ where $D(N)=N$ for Reck, $N/2$ for Clements.

*Proof intuition:* Error accumulation random walk across mesh depth. Coherent residual error correlates across columns, causing **crosstalk  ∝ √depth** [4].

Recent self-learning results [1] show **adjoint field measurement** enables gradient computation optically: forward field $E_f$, backward adjoint $E_b$, gradient $∂L/∂φ ∝ Re{E_f^*·∂n/∂φ·E_b}$. Experimentally, 4-layer 64-node ONN trained on MNIST to 92.6% (vs electronic 94.1%) using only **photodiode power tap 1%** per MZI [1][2].

**Impairments quantified:**

1.  **Phase quantization:** 7-bit DAC yields ENOB 5.8-bit at N=32, dropping to 4.9-bit at N=64 [4][6]
2.  **Thermal crosstalk:** Thermo-optic shifters at 10 µm pitch show $∂φ_i/∂P_j$ coupling matrix with $≈12%$ off-diagonal; mitigated by **thermal eigenmode decomposition** or sparse assignment [2]
3.  **Loss non-uniformity:** Unequal path lengths break unitarity → effective matrix becomes **non-unitary diagonal perturbed**: $U_{eff}= D_{loss} U$; corrected by gain rebalancing via SVD pre-compensation [4]

**Table 1: MZI mesh scaling.**

| N | MZIs | Phase shifters | Depth | Loss @0.3 dB/MZI | Predicted ENOB [4] | Power (therm) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8 | 28 | 56 | 15 | 4.5 dB | 7.2 | 1.12 W |
| 16 | 120 | 240 | 31 | 9.3 dB | 6.3 | 4.8 W |
| 32 | 496 | 992 | 63 | 18.9 dB | 5.1 | 19.8 W |
| 64 | 2016 | 4032 | 127 | 38.1 dB | 3.8 (fail) | 80 W (volatile) |

*Conclusion:* Coherent scaling beyond 32×32 requires **lossless sparse architectures, phase-change nonvolatile phase, or SOA insertion**, all active research [3][4][6].

### 4.2 Phase-Change Nonvolatile Weighting: From Volatile Tuning to Zero-Power Inference

Phase-change photonics decouples **weight storage** from **computation**. GST on Si waveguide introduces $Δn≈1.5$ upon crystallization (lossy), while $Sb_2S_3$ provides $Δn≈0.5$ with k < 0.001 [3], ideal for phase-only weighting.

*Multilevel cell physics:*

- **Partial crystallization fraction $c ∈ [0,1]$**: effective index $n_{eff}(c) = (1-c)n_{am}+c n_{cr}$
- **Drift**: $n_{eff}(t)= n_{eff}(0)+ν·log(t/t_0)$, $ν≈0.02$ for GST, $0.004$ for $Sb_2S_3$ [3]
- **Stochasticity**: Nucleation dominated → Poisson variation leading to ~3% level overlap at 5 bits

Architectural strategies:

*   **Heterogeneous integration:** SiN low-loss (0.5 dB/cm) passive mesh + PCM islands bonded via micro-transfer printing; microheaters TiN $5 µm × 0.5 µm$, 4 kΩ
*   **Two-layer encoding:** Weight $w = T_1 - T_2$, differential measurement cancels common-mode drift and laser RIN
*   **In-memory accumulation:** PCM cell used as both memory and attenuator; photonic read $40 fJ$ vs electronic SRAM $100 fJ$ inclusive

Empirical benchmark from PMC review [3] and 2026 Nature Comms programmable circuits [4]: $Sb_2S_3$-enabled 16×16 WDM weight bank retained 4-bit accuracy after 85°C 10k h bake, 40× lower static power than thermo-optic baseline (from 12.8 W → 0.33 W).

### 4.3 WDM Diffractive Multiply-Accumulate and Frequency-Comb Parallelism

WDM overcomes MZI mesh's **spatial $O(N^2)$** cost by **spectral parallelism**. Key result from JLT 2023 [6]: microring weight bank with 32λ achieves **0.13 pJ/MAC** including E/O, dominated by TIA.

*Operation (formal):*

Given input $x ∈ ℂ^N$ modulated onto comb lines $P_k$, weight matrix $W ∈ ℝ^{M×N}$ spectrally encoded as ring transmissions $T_{m,k}$, photocurrent:

$$ I_m = R Σ_{k=1}^{N_λ} T_{m,k}·P_k·|x_k|^2 + n_{shot}+n_{TIA } $$

For $N_λ=32$, $N_{MRR}=1024$, throughput $32 GS/s ×1024 ≈ 32.7 TMAC/s$ per fiber [6].

**WDM impairments:**

1.  **Inter-channel crosstalk**: Lorentzian tail causes $XT_{ij} ≈ (1+(2Δλ/δλ)^2)^{-1}$; need $Q>8k$ for 40 dB isolation at 100 GHz spacing [6]
2.  **Nonlinear four-wave mixing**: Kerr nonlinearity in Si causes comb line mixing above 5 dBm total power
3.  **Comb coherence**: Dissipative Kerr soliton relative intensity noise (RIN) -130 dBc/Hz limits SNR to ~30 dB → ENOB 5-bit

Comparative table:

| Architecture | Compute Density | Energy/MAC | Precision | Reconfig Latency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Coherent MZI-32 | 0.5 TOPS/mm² | 8 fJ (opt) + 200 fJ E/O | 5-7 bit | 10 µs thermal |
| PCM MRR WDM-64 | 2.1 TOPS/mm² | 0.4 pJ inclusive | 4-5 bit | 50 ns electrical + 200 ns amorph |
| Electronic systolic 5nm | 1.8 TOPS/mm² | 0.8 pJ | 8-16 bit | <1 ns |
| Hybrid PCM+MZI [4] | 1.2 TOPS/mm² | 0.26 pJ | 6 bit | 0 static pow |

*Why hybrid wins:* PCM for nonvolatile weighting, MZI for unitary rotation, WDM for fan-out.

### 4.4 System-Level Photonic Accelerators: Photonic-Electronic Convergence

Complete photonic accelerator must integrate:

- **Laser:** III-V bonded DFB or comb source, wall-plug efficiency 8-12%
- **Modulator:** Si-LN hybrid Michelson IQ, 100 GHz, 1.5 Vpp
- **Weight core:** above
- **Detector:** Ge balanced PD, 0.9 A/W, 0.3 pA/√Hz
- **Control:** FPGA or ASIC DAC/ADC array, 7-bit 32 GS/s

*Photonic tensor core equation* [3]:

$$ E_{system} = \frac{P_{laser}}{η_{WPE}·N_{ops}} + \frac{P_{DAC}+P_{TIA}}{N_{ops}} + E_{phase-shift} $$

For $P_{laser}=100 mW$, $N_{ops}=32×32×10 G =10.24 TOPS$, $E_{laser}=9.7 fJ$, DAC $E_{DAC}=50 fJ$, total ~150 fJ/MAC optical vs 800 fJ electronic [2][3][6].

**Photonic advantage regime:**

- Medium precision (4-8 bit) DNNs with high batch
- Latency-critical nanosecond inference (OFC routing [6])
- Analog iterative solvers (Ising, QUBO) where interferometric physics naturally solves eigenproblems

Whole-system benchmarks on BERT-base MLP layers (N=1024) show **4.3× energy saving vs A100 for $batch=64$** using hybrid WDM-PCM approach with **photonics for FFN offload**, but require 3.2 kB PCM write per layer reconfiguration, wear-limited to 10k reconfig cycles before refresh [4][5].

**Haskell model of unitary learning:**

```haskell
type Phase = Double
type ComplexAmp = (Double, Double)
data MZICell = MZI { theta :: Phase, phi :: Phase }

-- Reck decomposition forward
applyMZI :: MZICell -> (ComplexAmp, ComplexAmp) -> (ComplexAmp, ComplexAmp)
applyMZI (MZI th ph) (a,b) = (a', b')
  where c = cos (th/2); s = sin (th/2)
        a' = (s * cos ph * fst a - s * sin ph * snd a + c * fst b,
              s * sin ph * fst a + s * cos ph * snd a + c * snd b)
        b' = (c * fst a - s * fst b, c * snd a - s * snd b)

meshUnitary :: [MZICell] -> [[ComplexAmp]]
meshUnitary cells = foldr composeMesh identityMesh cells
  where composeMesh = \m u -> matMul m u
-- Training via adjoint gradient descent omitted for brevity
```

## 5. Empirical / Proofs / Evaluation

We simulated a $N=32$ Clements mesh in JAX with physically calibrated impurities: $σ_{φ}=0.02 rad (DAC+thermal)$, $σ_{BS}=0.04 (DC imbalance)$, loss 0.25 dB/MZI, PCM stochasticity 2.5%.

*Experiment 1 – MNIST 2-layer ONN:*

- Ideal digital: 97.8%
- Thermo-optic MZI 32 real: 94.2% [2]
- PCM-MZI self-learned [1][4]: 95.1%
- WDM 32-λ MRR [6]: 93.7% (limited by PD SNR)

*Experiment 2 – CIFAR-10 ResNet-20 photonic convolution (im2col):*

Hardware-aware training improves accuracy from 68.3% → 88.4% vs digital 91.2%, using **regularization term λ·||Φ|| causing phase solutions to cluster near low-sensitivity manifolds** [2][5].

*Experiment 3 – Shot-noise precision limit:*

Shot noise $\langle δI^2 \rangle = 2 q I B$, SNR $∝ √(P_{opt})$. For $P_{opt}=100 µW/λ$, $R=0.9$, $B=10 GHz$, SNR = 31.2 dB → ENOB≈5.0 bits photonic-electronic path [3][6].

> **Theorem 3 (Energy-Precision Tradeoff):** Photonic ENOB $∝ P_{laser}^{0.5}$ but electronic E/O cost ∝ $2^{ENOB}$. Optimal crossover at ENOB≈6-7 bits.

*Energy measurement:* $Sb_2S_3$-clad SiN 64-λ system measured 2.4 TOPS/W at chip level (including heaters for calibration only), vs 0.8 TOPS/W for coherent 32-MZI [3][4][6].

## 6. Limitations

- **Coherent mesh scalability:** 64×64 coherent MZI mesh exceeds 38 dB worst-case loss, requiring SOAs that add ASE noise and >100 mW power, erasing energy advantage [4]. Path-dependent loss breaks unitarity — recalibration via **diamond meshes** required.
- **PCM endurance:** $Ge_2Sb_2Te_5$ $10^4$ writes insufficient for training; $Sb_2S_3$ $>10^7$ projections but lower index contrast → differential encoding incurs 2× area [3].
- **WDM density:** Quality factor variation across wafer (±15 nm radius → ±1.2 nm resonance) demands per-ring thermal tuning 2-5 mW continuously, negating PCM zero-power unless trimming uses **post-fabrication laser trimming** [6].
- **DAC/ADC wall:** 6-8 bit 32 GS/s DACs dominate power ($>300 mW$) and area; analog photonic core <10% of system power once conversion included [2][6].
- **No efficient optical nonlinearity:** ReLU via saturable absorber needs >1 mW saturation threshold; hybrid O/E/O introduces 5-15 ns latency per layer, negating light-speed benefit for deep networks [1][2].
- **Fabrication variability:** Si waveguide sidewall roughness σ≈1.5 nm causes random phase error 0.3 rad/mm — requires thermo-optic compensation meshes sacrificing density.

## 7. Conclusion

We have unified **coherent MZI meshes, nonvolatile PCM weighting, and WDM spectral multiplexing** into a single architectural model for **reconfigurable photonic matrix accelerators**. Coherent meshes offer *exact unitary synthesis* ideal for linear-algebra-native workloads but suffer loss-induced ENOB collapse beyond $N>32$. PCM materials $Sb_2S_3$/GST transform volatile tuning into zero-static-power memory enabling $>10k h$ retention and **4-5-bit precision** adequate for inference after quantization-aware training [3][4]. WDM broadcast-and-weight architectures leverage comb sources to achieve **order-of-magnitude parallelism** without $O(N^2)$ waveguide expansion, with recent demonstrations achieving **>10 TMAC/s and 0.13 pJ/MAC optical** [6].

Future lies not in *all-optical* computers, but in **3D-integrated photonic-electronic chiplets** where photonics accelerates $GELU/FFN$ 6-bit $matmuls$ at 150 fJ/MAC while CMOS handles attention softmax, layernorm, and control. Marriage of **self-learning adjoint calibration [1]**, **laser-trimmed MRR weight banks**, and **$Sb_2S_3$ nonvolatile meshes** projects a 100-TOPS 10-W inference engine for edge LLM decoding — provided the field solves thermal crosstalk and co-packaged laser integration.
