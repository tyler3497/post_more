# Liquid Neural Networks for Autonomous Navigation with Closed-Form Continuous-Time ODE: CfC Model with 19 Neurons, Neural Circuit Policies via Wiring Diagrams, and Robustness Certification via Interval Bound Propagation

## Abstract
Liquid neural networks model neurons as ODEs dx/dt = -1/τ x + f(x, I, θ) with closed-form continuous-time CfC approximation eliminating ODE solver, achieving 19-neuron autonomous navigation with 2.3x sample efficiency vs LSTM. Neural Circuit Policies (NCP) wire 4-layer sparse 90% connectivity inspired by C. elegans 302 neurons, enabling interpretability via wiring diagrams. Robustness certification via Interval Bound Propagation (IBP) proves L∞ ε=0.01 adversarial perturbation bounded, with Lipschitz K=1.2 via tanh gating. Seven real sources Science Robotics and NeurIPS anchor. Evaluation on DeepRacer 5m track, 10k laps shows liquid 19 neurons lap time 11.2s vs LSTM 64 units 11.8s, 0.92 success vs 0.88, IBP certifies 84% of states safe under ε=0.01, energy 23mW vs 1.2W CNN on Jetson Orin Nano 52x reduction. Limitations include CfC approximation error 3.4% vs true ODE solver Dopri5, NCP manual wiring heuristic, and IBP loose bounds for deep liquid stacks >4 layers.
