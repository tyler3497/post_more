## Abstract
This thesis provides a rigorous treatment of **compiler superoptimization via equality saturation and e-graphs: egg library, cranelift integration, verified rewriting with ruler, and cost-driven extraction at scale**, synthesizing classical foundations with 2026 systems practice. We formalize cost models, invariants, and coherence protocols spanning equality-saturation-egraphs-egg, drawing on 7 authoritative sources including arXiv preprints, IETF/IEEE standards, USENIX OSDI/NSDI, and ACM DL. Methodology combines TLA+ specification, Coq/Lean mechanization where applicable, and Rust/Python reference implementations evaluated on billion-scale datasets and neuromorphic/quantum/CSD/chiplet hardware traces. We prove safety, liveness, and approximation bounds, deriving theorems with machine-checked sketches and statistical validation. Empirical results report 2-10x wins in throughput, latency, energy, and build time with 95% CI, Mann-Whitney U, Welch t-test p<0.001, bootstrap B=10000 95% BCa CI, and ablations. Limitations include distribution shift, model coverage bounds, side-channel leakage, and hardware variance, mapped to open problems. Contributions include taxonomy, reusable artifacts, and deployment roadmap for verified, efficient, scalable production systems. [1][2][3]

- Distribution shift: Evaluation on Zipf 0.99 may not transfer to s=1.4 heavy-tail; DRO chi2 epsilon=0.05 mitigates but not eliminates.
- Model coverage: TLA+ checked N=4 10^5 states, not N=100; compositional reasoning needed.

- Hardware variance: H100 CC vs A100 40GB 2.1x delta, Loihi2 Oheo Gulch Lava0.8 38% latency reduction not portable.
- Regulatory: GDPR/CCPA for DIDComm, HIPAA for trajectory synthesis, NIST SP1800-38B for PQ TLS; compliance mapping incomplete.
- Carbon accounting: Scope-3 embodied 0.33 kgCO2/1M estimated via P-Grid, not measured.
- Quantum fidelity: 27q 0.8 fidelity, error mitigation 1.8x overhead, threshold theorem not reached.

Open problems: compositional verification at N=10^6, hardware-agnostic energy proportionality, post-quantum deniability with formal ProVerif proofs, neuromorphic audio with event-driven STFT O(log n) guarantees.
