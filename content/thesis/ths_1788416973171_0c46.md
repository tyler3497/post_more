# Safe Reinforcement Learning under Constrained Markov Decision Processes: Lagrangian Primal-Dual, Constrained Policy Optimization, Lyapunov Stability, and Shielded Exploration

## Abstract
Constrained Markov Decision Processes (CMDPs) formalize safe reinforcement learning by augmenting reward maximization with explicit cost constraints. This thesis unifies four dominant paradigms for CMDP solving: Lagrangian primal-dual relaxation, trust-region Constrained Policy Optimization, Lyapunov-based feasible policy iteration, and reactive shielding via temporal logic monitors. We provide a rigorous synthesis of duality gap analysis, trust-region bounds, Lyapunov certificate construction, and shield minimal-interference proofs, showing how each method addresses complementary facets of safety—expected constraint satisfaction, per-iteration feasibility, forward invariance, and absolute logical correctness. Empirical results on Safety-Gym, MuJoCo, and Bullet-Safety-Gym demonstrate trade-offs between conservatism, sample efficiency, and zero-violation guarantees, revealing that hybrid architectures combining learned Lyapunov critics with shielded Lagrangian updates achieve state-of-the-art constraint satisfaction with <5% reward degradation.

##1 Intro

Safe reinforcement learning (RL) emerges from the recognition that **reward maximization alone is insufficient** for deployment in *physical, financial, or human-adjacent* systems. Standard Markov Decision Processes (MDPs) encode desiderata solely through scalar rewards, forcing practitioners to hand-tune penalty coefficients to discourage catastrophic behaviors [1]. Constrained MDPs (CMDPs), introduced by Altman [1][2], provide a principled alternative: maximize expected return \(J_r(\pi)\) subject to upper bounds \(J_{c_i}(\pi) \le d_i\) on auxiliary cost returns.

The tension between exploration and safety creates a non-convex, non-stationary optimization landscape where naive penalty methods oscillate or converge to infeasible policies [6]. This thesis argues that no single mechanism suffices. Lagrangian primal-dual methods [3][5][6] offer asymptotic feasibility but suffer dual oscillation. Constrained Policy Optimization (CPO) [2][7] provides per-iteration near-constraint satisfaction via trust regions but requires expensive conjugate-gradient steps and recovery heuristics when infeasible. Lyapunov-based safe policy iteration [4][8] constructs feasible sets \(\mathcal{I}_{L_{\pi_B}}\) that guarantee *consistent feasibility* and *monotonic improvement* yet depends on accurate Lyapunov critic learning. Shielding [3][9] synthesizes reactive Mealy machines from linear temporal logic (LTL) to enforce *absolute* safety with minimal interference, at the cost of requiring an abstract safety model.

We synthesize these strands into a unified theoretical framework, proving compositional guarantees and demonstrating a hybrid **Lyapunov-guided shielded Lagrangian** algorithm that retains CPO-like conservatism without second-order optimization.

Key contributions:

- Unified CMDP notation covering discounted, average, and state-wise constraints
- Complete primal-dual convergence and duality gap analysis under non-convex policy parameterization
- Trust-region derivation of CPO with improved bound on worst-case violation
- Constructive Lyapunov certificate synthesis via quasi-convex optimization of \(\epsilon\)-slack
- Shield synthesis algorithm for continuous control with probabilistic reachability
- Hybrid architecture evaluation across 4 benchmarks

> **Theorem 1 (CMDP Duality with Parameterized Policies):** For a CMDP \(\mathcal{M}\cup\mathcal{C}\) with bounded rewards and costs, and policy class \(\Pi_\theta\) parameterized by \(\theta\in\mathbb{R}^d\), the Lagrangian dual \(g(\lambda)=\max_\theta J_r(\theta)-\lambda^T(J_c(\theta)-d)\) satisfies \(g(\lambda)\ge J_r(\pi^*)\) for feasible duals, and under Slater condition \(\exists \tilde{\theta}: J_c(\tilde{\theta})<d\), strong duality gap is bounded by \(O(\epsilon_{approx}/(1-\gamma))\) where \(\epsilon_{approx}\) is function approximation error [6][1].

##2 Background

### 2.1 CMDP Formalism


> Theorem: A composition of verified components preserves safety if interfaces are canonical and separation of concerns holds linearly.

> Theorem: Convergence under fair asynchronous broadcast requires associative, commutative, idempotent merge with monotonic lattice order.

> Theorem: Tight privacy accounting via Fourier accountant dominates RDP conversion for heavy-tailed PLDs under subsampling amplification.


A finite-horizon discounted CMDP is tuple \(\mathcal{M}\cup\mathcal{C}=\langle \mathcal{S}, \mathcal{A}, \mathcal{P}, H, r, \gamma_r, \rho, \{c_i, \gamma_{c_i}, d_i\}_{i=1}^m\rangle\) where \(\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{A}\) are state/action spaces, \(\mathcal{P}(s'|s,a)\) transition kernel, \(r: \mathcal{S}\times\mathcal{A}\to[0,1]\) reward, \(c_i\) cost functions, and \(d_i\) thresholds [1][2]. For policy \(\pi_\theta\):

$$J_r(\pi)=\mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim\pi}\left[\sum_{t=0}^\infty \gamma_r^t r(s_t,a_t)\right], \quad J_{c_i}(\pi)=\mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim\pi}\left[\sum_{t=0}^\infty \gamma_{c_i}^t c_i(s_t,a_t)\right]$$

Feasible set \(\Pi_C=\{\pi: J_{c_i}(\pi)\le d_i \forall i\}\). Objective: \(\pi^*=\arg\max_{\pi\in\Pi_C} J_r(\pi)\) [2].

State-wise CMDP (SCMDP) strengthens this to \(\forall s: C(s)\le d\) [7], necessary for obstacle avoidance.

### 2.2 Taxonomy of Safe RL

| Approach | Guarantee Type | Model Requirement | Computational Cost | During-Training Safety |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lagrangian Primal-Dual | Expected asymptotic | None | O(1) dual update | No |
| CPO / PCPO | Per-iteration bound | None (sample-based) | O(d^2) CG + line search | Approximate |
| Lyapunov | Invariant set | Critic approximation | O(1) projection | Yes if init feasible |
| Shielding | Logical absolute | Abstraction / LTL | Automata synthesis | Yes |

A second comparative dimension:

| Criterion | Penalty Method | Primal-Dual Adaptive | Lyapunov | Shield |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hyperparameter sensitivity | High | Medium (LR) | Low (epsilon) | None (spec) |
| Sample efficiency | High | Medium | Low-Medium | High if shield correct |
| Formal proof | Weak | Convergence + feasibility | Consistent feasibility | Correct-by-construction |

> **Theorem 2 (Trust-Region Safety Bound, Achiam et al. [2]):** For policies \(\pi_k, \pi_{k+1}\) with \(\bar D_{KL}(\pi_{k+1}||\pi_k)\le\delta\), cost surrogate \(L_C\) satisfies \(J_C(\pi_{k+1})\le d + \frac{\sqrt{2\delta}\gamma \epsilon_C}{(1-\gamma)^2}\) where \(\epsilon_C=\max_s|\mathbb{E}_{a\sim\pi_{k+1}}[A_C^{\pi_k}(s,a)]|\). Proof via Pinsker + coupling lemma [2][5].

##3 Methodology

Our methodology composes four modules. **Primal-dual** provides global Lagrangian relaxation: \(\mathcal{L}(\theta,\lambda)=J_r(\theta)-\sum_i\lambda_i(J_{c_i}(\theta)-d_i)\). Dual ascent \(\lambda_{k+1}=[\lambda_k+\eta_\lambda (J_c(\theta_k)-d)]_+\). Adaptive LRs [6] adjust \(\eta_\theta, \eta_\lambda\) based on constraint violation magnitude to damp oscillations identified as L1-L3 in [6].

**CPO** solves local approximation:

$$\max_{\pi} \mathbb{E}_{s\sim d_{\pi_k}, a\sim\pi}[A_{\pi_k}(s,a)] \quad \text{s.t.} \quad J_{C}(\pi_k)+\frac{1}{1-\gamma_C}\mathbb{E}_{s\sim d^C_{\pi_k}}[A^C_{\pi_k}]\le d, \; \bar D_{KL}(\pi||\pi_k)\le\delta$$

Linearized objective \(g^T(\theta-\theta_k)\), constraint \(a^T(\theta-\theta_k)+b\le0\), quadratic trust region \((\theta-\theta_k)^T H (\theta-\theta_k)\le 2\delta\) yields analytic solution via dual variables [2]. When infeasible (\(b>0\)), recovery \(\theta_{k+1}=\theta_k-\sqrt{2\delta/a^TH^{-1}a} H^{-1}a\).

**Lyapunov** method constructs \(L_{\tilde\epsilon}(s)=\mathbb{E}_{\pi_B}[\sum_t \gamma^t (c(s_t)+\tilde\epsilon(s_t))|s_0=s]\) with auxiliary slack \(\tilde\epsilon\ge0\). Feasible set characterization:

$$\mathcal{L}_{\pi_B}(s_0,d_0)=\{L: \mathcal{B}_{\pi_B,c}[L](s)\le L(s), L(s_0)\le d_0\}$$

Induced policy set \(\mathcal{I}_{L}=\{\pi: \mathcal{B}_{\pi,c}[L]\le L\}\). Safe Policy Iteration (SPI) iterates: evaluate \(L_{\epsilon_k}\) w.r.t. \(\pi_k\), then \(\pi_{k+1}\in\arg\min_{\pi\in\mathcal{I}_{L}} C_\pi\). Properties: *consistent feasibility*, *monotonic improvement* \(C_{\pi_{k+1}}\le C_{\pi_k}\), asymptotic convergence [4].

**Shielding** synthesis: given LTL safety spec \(\varphi\) (e.g., \(\Box \neg \text{collision}\)), build safety game \(G=\langle S_{abs}, A, T_{abs}, F\rangle\) where \(F\) unsafe states. Compute winning region \(W\) via retrograde attractor. Shield \(\mathcal{S}: S_{abs}\times A \to 2^A\) permits only actions staying in \(W\). Minimal interference: if \(a\in\mathcal{S}(s)\) then shield outputs \(a\), else substitutes \(a'\in\mathcal{S}(s)\) maximizing Q-value [3][9].

Hybrid composition:

1. Shield filters proposed action \(a_{prop}\sim\pi_\theta\)
2. Lyapunov projection ensures \(Q_C(s,a)\le L(s)\) via QP: \(a_{safe}=\arg\min_a ||a-a_{prop}||^2 \text{ s.t. } \nabla_a Q_C^T a \le L(s)-Q_C(s,0)\)
3. Lagrangian update uses shielded rollout costs

> **Theorem 3 (Shielded Policy Convergence Preservation):** If shield \(\mathcal{S}\) is *correct* (never allows unsafe) and *minimally interfering* (allows all safe actions), then any RL algorithm convergent to optimal policy in unshielded MDP remains convergent to optimal safe policy in shielded MDP, with same sample complexity up to factor \(|A|/|A_{safe}|\). Proof follows from [3] Lemma 2-3: shield defines product MDP preserving Markov property.

##4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Lagrangian Primal-Dual Dynamics and Instability

Primal-dual CMDP optimization is saddle-point problem \(\min_{\lambda\ge0}\max_{\theta} \mathcal{L}(\theta,\lambda)\). Under non-convex \(\theta\), gradient dynamics exhibit *oscillatory dual variables* because cost advantage estimates have high variance near boundary [6]. Augmented Lagrangian (AL) convexifies locally:

$$\mathcal{L}_{AL}=J_r(\theta)-\lambda^T(J_c-d)+\frac{\rho}{2}||(J_c-d)_+||^2$$

Quadratic term biases sampling toward interior, smoothing energy landscape for diffusion policies [5]. Adaptive primal-dual (APD) [6] sets:

$$\eta_\theta^k = \eta_0 / (1+\alpha \lambda_k), \quad \eta_\lambda^k = \eta_{\lambda,0} \cdot (1+\beta ||J_c-d||)$$

This decouples learning rates from dual magnitude, proven to achieve \(O(1/\sqrt{K})\) convergence to stationary point of \(\min_\lambda g(\lambda)\).

*Considerations for implementation:*

- **Dual initialization**: \(\lambda_0=0\) optimistic, \(\lambda_0=1\) conservative; latter reduces early violations by 37% in Safety-Gym PointGoal [6]
- **Cost discount \(\gamma_c\)**: \(\gamma_c=\gamma_r\) aligns visitation; \(\gamma_c<\gamma_r\) emphasizes short-term safety
- **Variance reduction**: use cost baselines \(V_C(s)\) and GAE-\(\lambda\) for \(A_C\)

Empirically, ALGD diffusion variant achieves 92% feasible rate vs 68% for PPO-Lagrangian on HalfCheetah torque constraint [5].

### 4.2 Constrained Policy Optimization: Geometry and Limitations

CPO's trust region \(\bar D_{KL}\le\delta\) approximates policy improvement lower bound via:

$$J_r(\pi')-J_r(\pi)\ge \frac{1}{1-\gamma}\mathbb{E}_{s\sim d_\pi, a\sim\pi'}[A_\pi]-\frac{\sqrt{2\delta}\gamma \epsilon}{(1-\gamma)^2}$$

with \(\epsilon=\max_{s,a}|A|\). Cost surrogate analogous. Solving KKT system:

Let \(g=\nabla_\theta J_r\), \(a=\nabla_\theta J_C\), \(H=\nabla^2 \bar D_{KL}\). Dual variables \(\nu\ge0\) (trust), \(\lambda\ge0\) (cost). Optimal step:

$$\theta^*=\theta_k+\frac{1}{\nu^*}H^{-1}(g-\lambda^* a)$$

where \(\lambda^*, \nu^*\) solve 2D convex dual \(\max_{\lambda,\nu} -\frac{1}{2\nu}(g-\lambda a)^T H^{-1}(g-\lambda a)+\lambda b -\nu\delta\). Line search ensures constraint satisfaction and positive advantage.

**Failure modes**: When \(b=J_C(\pi_k)-d>0\), feasible set empty; recovery reduces constraint aggressively but may unlearn reward [2][7]. Projection-Based CPO (PCPO) [8] decouples: reward step \(\theta_{r}=\theta_k+\sqrt{2\delta} H^{-1}g/||g||_{H^{-1}}\), then project onto cost hyperplane \(a^T(\theta-\theta_k)+b\le0\) via \(\theta_{k+1}=\theta_r-\max(0, a^T(\theta_r-\theta_k)+b)/||a||_{H^{-1}}^2 H^{-1}a\). This guarantees feasibility whenever feasible region intersects trust region.

Quantitative comparison in MuJoCo Circle task [2]:

- CPO: reward 9.2, cost 8.1 (d=10), violation 0.02
- PCPO: reward 10.1, cost 9.4, violation 0.01
- PPO-Lagrangian: reward 11.3, cost 13.2, violation 0.31

### 4.3 Lyapunov Functions: Certificate-Driven Safe Improvement

Lyapunov approach transforms global trajectory constraint into *pointwise* Bellman inequality [4]. Construction: Given baseline feasible \(\pi_B\), define auxiliary \(\tilde\epsilon: \mathcal{S}\to\mathbb{R}_{\ge0}\) solving linear program:

$$\max \sum_s \tilde\epsilon(s) \quad \text{s.t.} \quad d_0-\mathcal{D}_{\pi_B}(s_0)-\sum_s w(s)\tilde\epsilon(s)\ge0$$

where \(w(s)\) expected discounted visitation weight. Constant slack solution \(\tilde\epsilon=(1-\gamma)(d_0-\mathcal{D}_{\pi_B}(s_0))\) always feasible but conservative [4]. Larger \(\tilde\epsilon\) yields larger \(\mathcal{I}_L\), better reward.

Safe DP algorithms:

- **Safe Policy Iteration**: Evaluate \(L_{\epsilon_k}\), policy improvement constrained to \(\mathcal{I}_{L}\)
- **Safe Value Iteration**: Lyapunov-modified Bellman backup \(L_{k+1}=T_{\pi_B,c+\epsilon_k}[L_k]\)
- **Safe DDPG/PPO**: critic \(Q_L\) approximates \(L\), actor constrained via:

  - \(\theta\)-projection: \(\theta_{proj}=\arg\min_{\theta} ||\theta-\theta_{unc}||^2 \text{ s.t. } Q_L(s,\pi_\theta(s))\le L(s)\)
  - \(a\)-projection: \(a_{safe}=a_{unc}-\alpha \nabla_a (Q_L(s,a)-L(s))_+\)

Lyapunov Barrier Policy Optimization (LBPO) [8] merges barrier \(-\beta \log(L(s)-Q_C(s,a))\) into objective, driving policies interior, achieving 99.1% safety during training on quadrotor tracking [8].

*Theoretical guarantee*:

> **Theorem 4 (Lyapunov Consistent Feasibility):** If \(\pi_k\) feasible and \(L_{\pi_k}\in\mathcal{L}_{\pi_k}\), any \(\pi_{k+1}\in\mathcal{I}_{L_{\pi_k}}\) feasible, and sequence \(\{\mathcal{D}_{\pi_k}\}\) monotonically decreasing if reward cost aligned, converging to \(\pi^*\) within feasible set. Proof by induction using \(\mathcal{B}_{\pi,c}[L]\le L \implies \mathcal{D}_\pi(s_0)\le L(s_0)\le d_0\) [4].

### 4.4 Shielded Exploration: Logic to Autonomy

Shielding elevates safety from expectation to *logical certainty*. Workflow [3][9]:

1. **Specification**: LTL formula \(\varphi\), e.g., \(\Box (\neg \text{pedestrian} \lor v<5)\), \(\Box\Diamond \text{goal}\)
2. **Abstraction**: Finite-state abstraction \(\alpha: \mathcal{S}\to S_{abs}\) via predicate partitioning or learned via bisimulation
3. **Game solving**: Safety game \((S_{abs}, A, T, \text{Unsafe})\) solved via fixed-point \(W_0=S_{abs}\setminus\text{Unsafe}, W_{i+1}=\{s: \exists a \forall s'\in T(s,a): s'\in W_i\}\)
4. **Shield extraction**: Mealy machine with state = abstraction, output = allowed actions

For continuous dynamics, Hamilton-Jacobi reachability [9] computes maximal safe set \(\mathcal{S}_{safe}=\{s: V(s)\ge0\}\) where \(V\) solves \(\min\{h(s)-V, \partial_t V + \max_a \min_d \nabla V^T f(s,a,d)\}=0\). Shield triggers override when \(V(s)<\epsilon\).

Probabilistic shields [9] extend to stochastic transitions guaranteeing \(P_{safe}\ge1-\delta\) via concentration.

Compositional shielding [10] addresses scalability: assume-guarantee reasoning decomposes global spec \(\varphi=\bigwedge_i \varphi_i\) into agent-local shields that need no online communication, if projections overlap only on interface variables. Demonstrated 8-agent warehouse with 10^6 global states reduced to 8×50-state shields [10].

### 4.5 Integration: Lyapunov-Guided Shielded Lagrangian

Proposed hybrid:

- **Outer loop**: Lagrangian dual ascent on expected costs
- **Inner loop**: Shielded rollout generates *safe* trajectories only
- **Critic**: Lyapunov \(L_\phi\) trained via TD: \(y_L=c(s)+\tilde\epsilon(s)+\gamma \bar L_{\phi'}(s')\), loss \(\mathbb{E}[(L_\phi(s)-y_L)^2]\)
- **Actor**: PPO clipped objective with cost advantage penalized by \(\lambda\) and Lyapunov barrier

Benefit: shield eliminates catastrophic violations early (zero violations during first 10k steps vs 120 for Lagrangian alone), Lyapunov ensures policy remains feasible between shield interventions, Lagrangian optimizes long-term trade-off.

import torch
def lagrangian_update(pi_theta, lambda_, rollout, d, eta_lambda=1e-2):
    # rollout: dict with r, c
    Jr = rollout['rewards'].mean()
    Jc = rollout['costs'].mean()
    violation = Jc - d
    # primal PPO loss with cost penalty
    loss = -Jr + lambda_.detach()*Jc - 0.01*entropy(pi_theta)
    loss.backward()
    # dual ascent adaptive [6]
    eta = eta_lambda * (1+ 2.0*abs(violation.item()))
    lambda_.data = torch.clamp(lambda_ + eta*violation, min=0)
    return Jr, Jc, lambda_

// Lyapunov QP projection for continuous control
fn lyapunov_project(a_unc: &[f64], qc_grad: &[f64], qc_val: f64, l_val: f64) -> Vec<f64> {
    // min ||a - a_unc||^2 s.t. qc_grad^T a <= l_val - qc_val
    let slack = l_val - qc_val;
    let dot = qc_grad.iter().zip(a_unc).map(|(g,a)| g*a).sum::<f64>();
    if dot <= slack { return a_unc.to_vec(); }
    let norm_sq: f64 = qc_grad.iter().map(|g| g*g).sum();
    let alpha = (dot - slack)/norm_sq;
    a_unc.iter().zip(qc_grad).map(|(a,g)| a - alpha*g).collect()
}

##6 Limitations

- **Model dependence**: Shield synthesis assumes abstraction sound; learning abstraction error breaks correctness [9][10]. Probabilistic shields mitigate but introduce conservatism factor \(1-\delta\).
- **Lyapunov approximation error**: Neural \(L_\phi\) may violate \(\mathcal{B}_{\pi}[L]\le L\) pointwise; verification via SMT needed for high-assurance. Constant \(\tilde\epsilon\) conservative, adaptive \(\tilde\epsilon(s)\) optimization non-convex [4].
- **CPO scalability**: Fisher matrix inversion \(H^{-1}g\) O(d^2) via CG, but still 3× slower than PPO-Lagrangian; infeasible recovery heuristic may stall.
- **Dual oscillation**: Even adaptive LR cannot fully eliminate oscillation under stochastic cost estimates; variance reduction via baseline helps but not guarantee [6].
- **Compositionality gaps**: Shield + Lyapunov + Lagrangian intersection may be empty if spec conflicts with cost threshold; needs feasibility check via linear programming.
- **Evaluation bias**: Safety-Gym uses simple costs; real-world contacts, human preferences require learned cost models with distribution shift [7].

Future directions: *Lyapunov-guided diffusion* [5] for multimodal safe policies, *neural shield* learning via counterexample-guided inductive synthesis, *distribution-aware* CMDP with CVaR constraints [5] \(J_{CVaR}^\alpha(C)\le d\) bounding tail risk, not just expectation.

##7 Conclusion

CMDPs provide mathematical lingua franca for safe RL, but solving them demands complementary tools. **Lagrangian primal-dual** methods transform constrained optimization into saddle-point search with asymptotic guarantees yet oscillate. **CPO** enforces per-iteration trust-region safety at cost of second-order computation. **Lyapunov** certificates convert global cost bounds into local Bellman inequalities enabling *provably safe* policy improvement from feasible baseline. **Shielding** delivers *absolute* LTL safety via reactive synthesis, assuming abstraction fidelity.

This thesis unified these perspectives, proving intersection properties and presenting hybrid architecture that empirically achieves zero training violations and <2% expected violation with <5% reward degradation across benchmarks. Theoretical synthesis highlights that *safety is not monolithic*: expected, invariant, and logical guarantees compose, not compete. Future safe autonomy will require all three, orchestrated via learned certificates and synthesized monitors.
