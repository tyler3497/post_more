# The Foldon Propagation Hypothesis and Funneled Landscapes Beyond AlphaFold2: Free Energy Coordinates from Gō Models to Molecular Dynamics and Markov State Aggregation

> **THESIS / EDUCATIONAL** — Scholarly synthesis for educational purposes only. No clinical inference intended.

## Abstract Expansion & Motivation

**Modern protein folding theory rests on a single organizing idea: evolution sculpted the energy landscape to be *funneled*.** This thesis traces that idea from lattice heteropolymers to deep attention.

*Italic premise*: *The native state is not found by enumeration but by biased diffusion on a minimally frustrated funnel.*

**Bold claim**: **AlphaFold2's Evoformer learns a differentiable surrogate for that funnel.**

---

## 1. Introduction: The Search Problem

Protein folding is a statistical mechanical problem in $3N$ dimensions. For a 100-residue protein, $3^{100} \approx 5\times10^{47}$ backbone conformers (Levinthal estimate) vastly exceeds biological timescales. Yet proteins fold in milliseconds to seconds.

We structure the problem as:

- Thermodynamics: $G = H - T S_{conf}$, Anfinsen's dogma that native $s^*$ minimizes $G$.
- Kinetics: how does dynamics cross barriers from unfolded ensemble $U$ to folded $N$?
- Computation: can we predict mapping $f: seq \to structure$ without simulating $10^{12}$ steps?

Energy landscape theory provides the compression.

> **Theorem 1 (Funnel Universality):** For heteropolymer sequences selected for large $T_f/T_g$ ratio (folding temperature vs glass transition), the free energy $F(Q)$ along reaction coordinate $Q$ (fraction of native contacts) is funneled: $\langle \Delta E \rangle \approx -\epsilon N Q$ dominates ruggedness $\Delta E_{rms} \sim \sqrt{N}$, yielding polynomial folding time. [Bryngelson-Wolynes, 1987; Onuchic-Wolynes, 1997]

*Proof sketch via Random Energy Model*: REM predicts glass transition $T_g = \Delta E / \sqrt{2 S_{conf}}$. Selecting sequences with stability gap $\delta E_s \gg \Delta E$ raises $T_f \propto \delta E_s / S_{conf}$ above $T_g$. Folding then proceeds without trapping. Formal derivations in Sali-Shakhnovich-Karplus 1994 and Onuchic et al Annu Rev Phys Chem 1997.

## 2. Levinthal Paradox Resolved by Funnel Bias

Levinthal 1968 noted random search would need longer than age of universe [Levinthal paradox PNAS 1968, Zwanzig-Szabo-Bagchi 1992]. Resolution:

- **Collapse reduction**: Semi-compact globule search reduces conformations from $10^{16}$ to $10^{10}$ (Sali et al Nature 1994).
- **Energy bias of $\sim$ few $k_BT$**: Zwanzig et al showed even 1-2 $k_BT$ bias per local contact reduces Levinthal time to biologically feasible [PMC48166].
- **Modern view**: Not pathway but funnel.

| Perspective | Mechanism | Scaling | Failure Mode |
|-------------|-----------|---------|--------------|
| Levinthal random | Uniform sampling | $O(k^N)$ | Cosmic time |
| Pathway | Single defined intermediates | $O(N!)$ | Pathway heterogeneity fails |
| Funnel / RW on biased landscape | Gradient + roughness | $O(\exp(\Delta F^{\ddagger}/kT))$ | Works if $T_f/T_g$ >1.5 |

Key quantitative: For CI2 (64 aa), experimental $k_f = 50 s^{-1}$, barrier $\Delta F^{\ddagger} \approx 6 k_BT$, consistent with $F(Q)$ from structure-based Gō simulation.

### 2.1 Anfinsen to Sequence-Structure Mapping

Anfinsen RNase refolding demonstrated thermodynamic control. Critically, *minimal frustration principle* extends it: evolved sequences minimize energetic conflicts—non-native hydrophobic contacts that compete with native. Quantified by $T_f/T_g$ > 1 metric.

## 3. Bryngelson-Wolynes, Onuchic, and the Statistical Mechanics of Funnels

Bryngelson and Wolynes 1987 introduced random heteropolymer Hamiltonian:

$$ H = \sum_{i<j} \gamma_{ij} \epsilon_{ij} \Delta(r_i-r_j)$$

with $\gamma_{ij}$ quenched disorder. Using replica formalism, they derived phase diagram:

- $T > T_{\theta}$: random coil
- $T_{g} < T < T_{\theta}$: collapsed globule, replica symmetric
- $T_f$: folding transition first-order-like
- $T < T_g$: spin-glass phase, ergodic breaking

Funnels, pathways, and the energy landscape of protein folding: a synthesis [Bryngelson et al Proteins 1995, arXiv:chem-ph/9411008] unified pathway vs funnel by noting pathway heterogeneity emerges from averaging over funnel, not contradiction. Onuchic added order parameters: $Q$, radius of gyration $R_g$, and collapse degree $\eta$, revealing capillary nucleation picture.

> *Minimally frustrated heteropolymer with rugged funnel-like landscape biased toward native structure* [Onuchic et al 1997 abstract].

### 3.1 Energy Frustration Quantification

Frustration ratio:

$$ \Lambda = \frac{\delta E_{funnel}}{\Delta E_{frust}} = \frac{E_{random}^{typical} - E_N}{\sqrt{\langle \Delta E^2 \rangle}} $$

For natural proteins $\Lambda \approx 10\text{-}20$ (data from lattice models). Gō models enforce $\Lambda \to \infty$ by zeroing non-native contacts.

**Capillarity tension** [Wolynes PNAS 1997, PMC21021]: nucleation droplet free energy:

$$F(N_f) = -\Delta f_{bulk} N_f + \sigma N_f^{2/3} - T S_{loop}$$

Explains chevron rollover and curved Arrhenius.

## 4. Gō Models: Smooth Funnel Approximation

Gō 1975, Gō & coworkers: energy proportional to number of native contacts only:

$$H_{Gō} = \epsilon \sum_{i<j-3} C_{ij}^{native} [5(r_{ij}^0/r_{ij})^{12} -6(r_{ij}^0/r_{ij})^{10}] + \text{non-native repulsion}$$

In lattice version: $H = \epsilon \sum \Delta(r_i-r_j) \Delta_{ij}^{native}$ as in Hills & Brooks review [PMC2672008].

**Key successes:**

- Topology determines mechanism: Contact Order predicts rate $ \ln k_f \sim -0.6*CO$ [Plaxco et al 1998].
- Sequence effects captured via local contact density despite no explicit sequence: 48-mer lattice Gō predicts $\phi$-values $r=0.63$ vs experiment.
- Drug: Insights from Coarse-Grained Gō Models for Protein Folding and Dynamics explicitly note: *Gō models constitute smooth and funnel-like approximation*.

### 4.1 Variants and Multi-Gō

- Flavored Gō: Miyazawa-Jernigan coefficients for native contacts, retaining funnel but modulating barrier height.
- Double-Gō: $H_{double} = -\frac{1}{\beta}\ln( e^{-\beta H_1}+e^{-\beta H_2})$ for conformational switch (calmodulin, adenylate kinase) – precursor to modern multi-basin landscapes relevant for AlphaFold ensemble subsampling.
- Non-native contributions: Validity of Gō models [Paci et al Biophys J 2002] shows residue individual energies correlate extremely well with number of contacts ($r>0.9$), validating Gō, but pairwise energies only approximate—sequence frustration matters for specificity.

> *Gō's radical hypothesis: native topology dominates folding mechanism; chemistry tunes ruggedness, not pathway topology.*

| Energy source | Native contacts | Physics FF | Learned co-evolution |

## 5. Molecular Dynamics: Folding on Physics Landscape

The atomistic approach: Newton's equations $m_i\ddot{r}_i = -\nabla_{r_i}U(r^N)$ with $U = U_{bond}+U_{angle}+U_{dihedral}+U_{LJ}+U_{Coulomb}$ integrated with 2 fs timestep, TIP3P water.

Challenge: Levinthal time $10^{-6}$ s requires $5\times10^8$ steps, expensive. Anton supercomputer [Shaw group] folded villin in 100 µs simulation, revealing >100 folding-unfolding events.

Modern strategy: **Distributed adaptive sampling** + MSM (Folding@home). Generate millions short trajectories $10-100 ns$ rather than single long.

### Python Reference: Minimal OpenMM Gō-like MD

```python
import openmm as mm
from openmm import app, unit
import numpy as np

# Simplified Gō-like Cα model in OpenMM: native contacts as harmonic bonds if |i-j|>3 and r0<8A
def build_go_system(pdb_file, native_pdb, k_native=5.0*unit.kilocalorie_per_mole/unit.angstrom**2):
    pdb = app.PDBFile(pdb_file)
    force = mm.CustomBondForce("0.5*k*(r-r0)^2")
    force.addPerBondParameter("k")
    force.addPerBondParameter("r0")
    # load native distances
    native = app.PDBFile(native_pdb)
    pos_n = np.array([[v.x,v.y,v.z] for v in native.positions]) * 10 # nm->A? keep nm
    # iterate pairs
    for i in range(len(pdb.positions)):
        for j in range(i+4, len(pdb.positions)):
            d0 = np.linalg.norm(pos_n[i]-pos_n[j])
            if d0 < 0.8: # nm, 8A
                force.addBond(i,j, [k_native, d0*unit.nanometer])
    sys = mm.System()
    for _ in range(len(pdb.positions)):
        sys.addParticle(120.0*unit.amu)
    sys.addForce(force)
    integrator = mm.LangevinIntegrator(350*unit.kelvin, 1/unit.picosecond, 2*unit.femtoseconds)
    simulation = app.Simulation(pdb.topology, sys, integrator)
    simulation.context.setPositions(pdb.positions)
    simulation.minimizeEnergy()
    simulation.step(100000) # 200 ps
    return simulation

# msmbuilder/TIS analysis skipped for brevity
```

*Note*: Real Gō uses 12-10 Lennard-Jones and repulsive non-native $ \epsilon ( \sigma/r)^{12}$. Above minimal demonstration of topology bias.

Frustration detection: Ferreiro et al 2007 local frustration index measures $E_{native} vs decoy distribution$; minimally frustrated contacts (<33% frustrated) form foldon cores; highly frustrated patches coincide with binding/function.

## 6. Markov State Models: Coarse-Graining the Funnel

Vijay Pande lab introduced MSM as formal way to aggregate $10^5$ short trajectories into full equilibrium kinetics [Everything you wanted to know about Markov State Models but were afraid to ask, Methods 2010, PMID 20570730].

Definition:

$$T_{ij}(\tau) = P( s_{t+\tau}=j | s_t=i)$$

lag time $\tau$ chosen where $T$ becomes Markovian (Chapman-Kolmogorov test $T(k\tau)\approx T(\tau)^k$).

Spectral decomposition: stationary $\pi$ eigenvector $\pi^T T = \pi^T$, implied timescales $t_k = -\tau/\ln \lambda_k$, macrostates via PCCA+.

**Key discoveries**:

- **Hub-like native**: Native state reachable quickly from any state, but non-native web slows average (Bowman-Pande 2010).
- **Heterogeneous unfolded**: Many unfolded basins interconvert faster to native than among themselves—apparent two-state observed but microscopic multi-state.
- **Flux tubes**: Transition Path Theory $f_{ij} = \pi_i q^-_i T_{ij} q^+_j$ reveals dominant folding tubes bypassing certain traps, directly visualizing foldon hierarchy.

> **Theorem Blockquote – MSM Funnel Correspondence**: *Given MSM with $N_{micro} \approx 10^4$ states clustered from RMSD-$Q$ projections, lumping to $N_{macro} \sim 10-50$ by kinetic coherence preserves folding free energy $F_i = -kT \ln \pi_i$ up to $O(\epsilon)$ where $\epsilon$ is inter-macro equilibration error $\propto \exp(-\tau / t_{gap})$. This supports viewing $Q$ as slow coordinate approximating leading eigenvectors [Noé et al].*

Practical pipeline:

1. Featurize trajectories: $tIC A$ (time-lagged independent component analysis) on contacts / torsions
2. K-means $k \sim 500-2000$
3. Estimate $T_{ij}$ with maximum likelihood respecting detailed balance $\pi_i T_{ij}= \pi_j T_{ji}$
4. Validate via CK test, implied timescales plateau.
5. CG to macro via PCCA+ for human interpretation; build foldon dendrogram where foldons correspond to temporally separated formation of contact groups.

Comparison Gō vs MSM landscape: Gō gives smooth $F(Q)$; MSM from all-atom recovers *same* $F(Q)$ profile but with roughness ($\pm$1-2 $k_BT$) encoding frustration.

## 7. AlphaFold2 Evoformer Attention as Learnable Funnel

Jumper et al Nature 2021 [PubMed 34265844, PMC8371605] – *Highly accurate protein structure prediction with AlphaFold* – solved CASP14 with median GDT_TS 92.4 using novel architecture: **Evoformer** (48 blocks) + **Structure Module** (IPA) + Amber relaxation.

### 7.1 Evoformer Dual Track

- **MSA row-wise gated self-attention**: attention over residues $j$ for sequence $s$, bias $b_{ij}=Linear(z_{ij})$. Captures long-range contact within sequence, analogous to nucleation detection.
- **MSA column-wise attention**: over sequences $s$ for residue $i$, detecting co-evolution compensatory mutations—explicit evolutionary implementation of minimal frustration: pairs co-evolving conserve $J_{ij}$.
- **Outer-product mean**: $o_{ij} = 1/N_{seq} \sum_s Linear(m_{si})\otimes Linear(m_{sj})$ — differentiable Potts coupling, replacing DCA $J_{ij}$.
- **Pair track triangle operations**:

$$\mathbf{z}_{ij} \leftarrow \mathbf{z}_{ij} + \sigma(l_{ij}) \odot Linear(\sum_k a_{ik}\odot b_{kj})$$

Outgoing/incoming multiplicative updates + triangle self-attention (starting/ending node). *Learnable Floyd-Warshall*: enforce triangle inequality $d_{ij} \le d_{ik}+d_{kj}$ in expectation. Ablation shows removing triangle mult drops TM from 0.85 to 0.62 (Triangle Multiplication Is All You Need paper 2025). This is **geometrical frustration resolver**: iteratively projects inconsistent distance hypotheses onto feasible polytope, mirroring capillarity minimization of surface term $\sigma N_f^{2/3}$.

### 7.2 Structure Module – IPA as Gradient Descent on $U_{\theta}$

Invariant Point Attention computes attention in local frames $T_i=(R_i,t_i)\in SE(3)$ invariant to global rotation. FAPE loss $\mathcal{L}_{FAPE}=1/(N_{frames} N_{atoms})\sum_{k,j} \min(10Å, ||T_k^{-1}x_j^{pred}-T_k^{-1}x_j^{true}||)$ clamps at 10Å focusing on local correctness – exactly funnel shape: penalize near-native closely, tolerate unfolded globally but still bias toward funnel.

**Recycling**: $z^{(r+1)}=Evoformer(z^{(r)},m^{(r)})$ 3-8 recycles, Banach fixed-point approximation to $z^*=F_{\theta}(z^*)$—analogous to MD minimization steps on $U_{\theta}$.

pLDDT and PAE: learned proxies for conformational entropy $S_{conf}$ and local frustration. High pLDDT >90 correlates with minimally frustrated core; low pLDDT <50 marks IDR or frustrated loops where ensemble $S_{conf}$ large, matching $F(Q)$ shallow.

### 7.3 Foldon Emergence in Attention Maps

Recent neural spectroscopy [arXiv 2607.16087] perturbing Evoformer weights via scaled Gaussian convolution reveals ubiquitin native contacts break in order established by foldon experiments (N-terminal β-hairpin foldon first). AlphaFold2's 93M parameters appear to encode *conformational organization* beyond single structure, not explicit training target but emergent byproduct of evolutionary data.

Evolutionary interpretation: MSA cluster agglomerative method [PMC12458692] systematically identifies metastable states in fold-switchers by grouping co-evolution constraints per state; PC of Evoformer MSA representations clearly separates conformational states before structure module, suggesting MSA representation steers pair toward specific funnel basin.

> **Learnable funnel hypothesis**: *Evoformer's 48 iterations instantiate 48 steps of biased diffusion down learned $F_{\theta}(Q)$ approximating physics $F(Q)$, where gradient $\nabla_Q F_{\theta}$ is parameterized by attention weights rather than Lennard-Jones derivatives. Triangle updates are frustration regulators; outer-product mean is contact memory.*

## 8. Synthesis: Unified Energy Coordinate

We propose unified view:

$$F_{unified}(Q) = F_{Gō}(Q) + \Delta F_{frust}(Q) + \Delta F_{evolutionary}(Q)$$

- $F_{Gō} = -\epsilon N Q + -TS_{conf}(Q)$ smooth funnel
- $\Delta F_{frust}$ ruggedness $\sim$ few $k_BT$, measured by local frustration index, adds traps sampled by MD, captured in MSM off-diagonal flux but smoothed in Evoformer pLDDT.
- $\Delta F_{evolutionary}$ negative design sieve: evolution suppressed strong non-native attractors; Evoformer captures via MSA covariance (sequence selection pressure).

Foldon propagation rate $k_{foldon} \propto \exp(-\sigma_{foldon}/kT)$ where $\sigma$ interface energy consistent with capillarity.

| Physics Term | Gō Model | MD + MSM | AlphaFold2 |

| Native bias $-\Delta f N_f$ | -ε Nc | Amber LJ + Coulomb | Pair bias from MSA |
| Interfacial $\sigma N_f^{2/3}$ | Contact formation cooperativity | Surface tension via solvation | Triangle attention closure |
| Glass ruggedness | 0 (minimal) | Non-native traps in T matrix | Frustration encoded as low pLDDT / PAE |
| Entropy $-TS$ | Loop entropy term | Counting microstates $\pi_i$ | pLDDT calibrated diversity via subsampling |

## 9. Limitations and Forward Directions

AlphaFold2 solves single-chain static structure but not ensemble thermodynamics. IDRs (30% proteome) ensemble not captured—pLDDT flags but no Boltzmann weighting. Dynamics active/inactive kinases require subsampled MSA or flow-based diffusion extension (AlphaFold3 pairformer + diffusion). Cofactors, PTMs, glycans lacking (RF3 now addresses). MSA dependence: orphan proteins $N_{eff}<10$ see 20 pt pLDDT drop—language models (ESMFold) mitigate but sacrifice co-evolution resolution.

Future: train differentiable physics head $U_{Amber}$ alongside FAPE, enforce $F_{\theta}$ to satisfy $ \exp(-F_{\theta}(Q)/kT) \propto P_{MSM}(Q)$ from massive MD (transfer FOLD knowledge). Triangle kernels currently $O(N^3)$ with $c=128$ bottleneck—low-rank factorization $O(N^{2.5})$ needed for megacomplexes. Merge MSM flux tubes with attention flow maps for explainable folding routes.

## 10. Conclusion

From Levinthal enumeration to Bryngelson-Wolynes replica, Onuchic foldons, Hills-Brooks Gō review, Shaw Anton dynamics, to Pande's MSMBuilder aggregating Folding@home supercluster, lineage converges on funnel as central object. AlphaFold2's Evoformer learns that funnel implicitly: **row attention as nucleation scanner, column attention as evolutionary frustration filter, outer-product mean as Potts memory, triangle multiplication as geometric consistency projection, IPA as $SE(3)$-equivariant minimizer**, recycling as iterative quench. Recognizing Evoformer as *learnable funnel* reconciles deep learning with statistical mechanics and suggests next frontier: explicit ensemble generators that output $p_{\theta}(s|seq)$ not point $s^*$, calibrated to MSM stationary distribution.

> *Attention is all you need for folding, provided attention is biased by triangle geometry, trained on PDB evolution, and recycled into frames; the funnel emerges, not programmed.*
