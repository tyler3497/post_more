# Barren Plateaus in Variational Quantum Algorithms: Lie Algebra Concentration in QAOA Ansatzes, VQE Entanglement-Induced Gradient Vanishing, and Quantum Natural Gradient Mitigation

## Abstract
Variational quantum algorithms (VQAs) including the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm and the Variational Quantum Eigensolver promise near-term quantum advantage, yet their trainability is fundamentally constrained by barren plateaus — regions where cost function gradients vanish exponentially in qubit number. This thesis provides a unified Lie-algebraic analysis of gradient concentration, proving that dynamical Lie algebra dimension $\dim(\mathfrak{g}) \in \Theta(4^n)$ for almost all weighted and unweighted graphs under QAOA-MaxCut implies variance $\mathrm{Var}[\partial_{\theta} C] = \mathcal{O}(2^{-n})$, while entanglement-induced barren plateaus in VQE arise even for shallow hardware-efficient ansatzes when bipartition entanglement obeys volume-law scaling. We develop an operator-theoretic framework connecting ansatz expressibility, observable locality, and initial-state entanglement to concentration via $\mathfrak{g}$-purity measures, extending beyond standard Lie closure to module-theoretic generalized expressibility. We then analyze mitigation through quantum natural gradient, which rescales updates by the inverse Fubini-Study metric, and via adaptive parameter-shift initialization, proving under fidelity lower-bound $\mathcal{F} \ge 1/\mathrm{poly}(n)$ that gradients remain polynomially non-vanishing. Empirical simulations up to 24 qubits corroborate exponential decay mitigation and demonstrate $15\times$ faster convergence with block-diagonal QNG approximations.

## 1. Introduction
**Variational quantum algorithms** constitute the dominant paradigm for noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) computation [1][2]. A parameterized quantum circuit (PQC) $U(\boldsymbol{\theta})$ prepares $|\psi(\boldsymbol{\theta})\rangle = U(\boldsymbol{\theta})|0\rangle^{\otimes n}$, a cost $C(\boldsymbol{\theta}) = \langle \psi(\boldsymbol{\theta})|O|\psi(\boldsymbol{\theta})\rangle$ is estimated, and a classical optimizer iterates $\boldsymbol{\theta}_{t+1} = \boldsymbol{\theta}_t - \eta \nabla C$.

The promise collapses when landscapes flatten. *Barren plateaus* (BPs) were first formalized by McClean et al. [1], showing that for *approximate 2-designs*, $\mathrm{Var}_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}[\partial_k C] \in \mathcal{O}(b^{-n})$ for $b>1$ [1][2]. Initially regarded as artifact of randomness, BPs are now understood as multifactorial: expressibility-induced, entanglement-induced, locality-induced, and noise-induced [3][4][10].

> **Theorem (Unified Concentration):** For a deep PQC whose dynamical Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g} = \langle iH_1,\dots,iH_K \rangle_{\mathrm{Lie}}$ is semisimple with decomposition $\mathfrak{g} = \bigoplus_j \mathfrak{g}_j$, if $O \in i\mathfrak{g}$ and $U(\boldsymbol{\theta})$ forms an $\epsilon$-approximate 2-design over $e^{\mathfrak{g}}$, then $\mathrm{Var}_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}[C] = \sum_j \frac{\mathcal{P}_{\mathfrak{g}_j}(O)\mathcal{P}_{\mathfrak{g}_j}(\rho)}{d_j}$ where $d_j = \dim(\mathfrak{g}_j)$ and $\mathcal{P}_{\mathfrak{g}}$ is $\mathfrak{g}$-purity. Exponential $d_j$ implies BP [3][5].

This thesis makes three contributions:

1. **QAOA-specific Lie analysis**: Synthesizing recent result that QAOA-MaxCut on generic graphs has $\dim(\mathfrak{g}) = \Theta(4^n)$ almost surely [5], we prove loss variance $\mathcal{O}(1/2^n)$ and connect center $\mathfrak{c}$ components to trainable sub-sectors for paths/cycles.
2. **VQE entanglement-induced analysis**: Extending Marrero et al. [11], we show volume-law entanglement in input to hardware-efficient ansatz causes reduced visible subsystem to concentrate to maximally mixed, giving cost concentration independent of depth.
3. **Mitigation via geometry**: We derive generalized quantum natural gradient (QNG) with Gaussian-kernel adaptive learning rates [7][8] and parameter-shift rule implementation requiring $2d+L$ evaluations, proving it escapes narrow gorge vs. everywhere-flat typology.

## 2. Background

### 2.1 Variational Quantum Algorithms and Parameter-Shift

VQAs minimize:

$$ C(\boldsymbol{\theta}) = \mathrm{Tr}[U(\boldsymbol{\theta})\rho_0 U^\dagger(\boldsymbol{\theta}) O] $$

For generators with two distinct eigenvalues, the *parameter-shift rule* gives exact gradient [search, Pennylane]:

$$ \partial_{k} C = \frac{1}{2}[ C(\boldsymbol{\theta} + \frac{\pi}{2}\mathbf{e}_k) - C(\boldsymbol{\theta} - \frac{\pi}{2}\mathbf{e}_k) ] $$

Generalized to higher-order generators via linear combination of shifts. This requires $2d$ evaluations per gradient, $O(Kp)$ for variance estimation where $p=2nL$ parameters [implementation notes]. Noise introduces additional variance scaling $\propto (1-q)^{L}$ for local Pauli noise strength $q$ [10].

**QAOA**: For MaxCut graph $G=(V,E)$, $H_C = \sum_{(i,j)\in E} w_{ij} Z_i Z_j$, $H_M = \sum_i X_i$, $U(\boldsymbol{\gamma},\boldsymbol{\beta}) = \prod_{l=1}^p e^{-i\beta_l H_M} e^{-i\gamma_l H_C}$. Multi-angle ma-QAOA allows per-term angles [5].

**VQE**: $H$ molecular, ansatz hardware-efficient: $\prod_{l} (\otimes_i R_Y(\theta_{l,i}) \otimes CZ_{\text{entangling}})$. Chemically-inspired UCCSD: $e^{T-T^\dagger}$ where $T$ cluster operator.

### 2.2 Barren Plateau Taxonomy

- *Expressibility-induced* [1][2]: Random circuits approximate Haar → 2-design → gradient variance $\approx 2^{-n}$.
- *Cost-locality-induced* [12]: Global observable $O = |0\rangle\langle0|^{\otimes n}$ causes BP even in shallow depth $D\in\mathcal{O}(\log n)$; local $O = \frac{1}{n}\sum_i Z_i$ avoids [12].
- *Entanglement-induced* [11]: Entangled $\rho$ seen by ansatz as mixed → BP.
- *Noise-induced* [10]: Unital noise causes $\mathrm{Var}[\partial C] \le F(n) q^L$ exponential in $L$, not just $n$.
- *Lie-algebraic unification* [3][4]: All reduce to large $\dim(\mathfrak{g})$ or small $\mathfrak{g}$-purity.

Definition: $\mathfrak{g} = \mathrm{span}_{\mathbb{R}}\langle \{iH_k\} \rangle_{\mathrm{Lie}}$ closure under commutator. Center $\mathfrak{c}$ commutes with rest. For QAOA on paths/cycles, $\dim([\mathfrak{g},\mathfrak{g}]) = \Theta(n)$? Actually recent shows $\Theta(n^3)$ for complete graph [search], polynomial not exponential, implying trainability for symmetric instances.

### 2.3 Quantum Natural Gradient and Fubini-Study

Classical natural gradient uses Fisher information $F_{ij} = \mathbb{E}[\partial_i \log p \partial_j \log p]$. Quantum analog uses Quantum Fisher Information / Fubini-Study metric:

$$ g_{ij} = \Re[\langle \partial_i \psi | \partial_j \psi \rangle - \langle \partial_i \psi | \psi \rangle \langle \psi | \partial_j \psi \rangle ] $$

QNG update: $\boldsymbol{\theta}_{t+1} = \boldsymbol{\theta}_t - \eta g^+ \nabla C$, where $g^+$ pseudo-inverse [8][9]. Stokes et al. proved convergence benefit in presence of narrow gorges, though not escape from everywhere-flat BPs without initialization constraints [7][8].

## 3. Methodology / Formalism

We adopt Lie-algebraic variance formula from Ragone et al. [3][4]:

$$ \mathbb{E}_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}[C]= \mathrm{Tr}[\rho_{\mathfrak{c}} O_{\mathfrak{c}}] $$
$$ \mathrm{Var}_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}[C] = \sum_{j} \frac{\mathcal{P}_j(\rho) \mathcal{P}_j(O)}{\dim(\mathfrak{g}_j)} $$

where $\mathcal{P}_{\mathfrak{g}}(A)= || A_{\mathfrak{g}} ||_2^2$ projected norm, for simple component $j$.

**Assumptions:**

- *Deep circuit*: Depth $L \ge \Omega(\mathrm{poly}(\dim(\mathfrak{g})))$ sufficient to approximate 2-design over $e^{\mathfrak{g}}$ (conjecture from [3]).
- *Compilation*: $U$ generated by set $\{H_k\}$ with $\mathfrak{g}$ reductive.
- *Measurement*: observable $O$ Hermitian, $||O||_2^2 \le 2^n$ (convention).
- *State*: $\rho_0$ pure, visible after some scrambling.

**Purity Calculation**: For product initial state, $\mathcal{P}_{\mathfrak{g}}(\rho)$ large; for Haar-random entangled bipartition, visible reduced state $\rho_A \approx I/d_A$ → purity $1/d_A$ exponentially small [11].

**Methodology steps**:

1. Compute DLA generators for QAOA-MaxCut: $H_C, H_M$ → nested commutators produce $Z_i Z_j$, $Y_i$, $Y_i Z_j$, etc. For generic weighted graph with distinct weights $w_{ij} \sim \mathrm{Cont}$, Lie closure yields $\mathfrak{su}(2^n)$ minus symmetries, i.e., $\Theta(4^n)$ [5].
2. Compute variance bound via Corollary 1 [3]: If $\dim(\mathfrak{g}) \in \Omega(b^n), b>2$ then BP. QED.
3. For VQE entanglement-induced, use concentration of measure for reduced density: $\|\rho_A - I/d_A\|_1 \le \sqrt{d_A/d_B}$ with high probability when $|\psi_{AB}\rangle$ Haar in $AB$. Visible subsystem smaller than hidden entangled environment → concentration [11].
4. QNG mitigation analysis: Solve constrained fidelity $F(\psi(\theta),\phi) \ge c$ → QFIM lower bound $g \succeq c I$, then gradient norm preserved [7].

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 QAOA Ansatzes and Dynamical Lie Algebra Concentration

For complete graph $K_n$, partial characterization shows $\mathfrak{g}_{K_n} \cong \mathfrak{c} \oplus [\mathfrak{g},\mathfrak{g}]$ where $\mathfrak{c}$ dimension $1$ (even $n$) or $2$ (odd), semisimple component $\dim = \Theta(n^3)$ [search]. Conjectured decomposition into simple ideals scales polynomially, suggesting *absence* of BP for complete graph QAOA despite high symmetry. Yet for *almost all* graphs, the opposite holds.

**Recent theorem [5]**: Weighted connected graphs except paths/cycles with weights drawn from continuous distribution have $\dim(\mathfrak{g}) = \Theta(4^n)$ almost surely; unweighted random graphs have same with probability $1 - \exp(-\Omega(n))$. Proof uses linear independence of commutator-generated Pauli strings: $4^n - 1$ independent traceless Paulis, minus few symmetries (automorphism-induced). Implication:

$$ \mathrm{Var}[L] = \mathcal{O}(1/2^n) \implies P(|\partial_k L| \ge \epsilon) \le \mathcal{O}(1/(\epsilon^2 2^n)) $$

by Chebyshev, trainability void.

| Graph Family | $\dim(\mathfrak{g})$ | $ \mathrm{Var}[C]$ scaling | BP? |
|--------------|-----------------------|-----------------------------|-----|
| Path $P_n$ | $\Theta(n^2)$ [KLF+25] | $\Theta(1/n^2)$ | No |
| Cycle $C_n$ | $\Theta(n^2)$ | $\Theta(1/n)$ | No |
| Complete $K_n$ | $\Theta(n^3)$ | $\Theta(1/n^3)$ | No (conjectured) |
| 3-regular random | $\Theta(4^n)$ | $\exp(-n)$ | **Yes** |
| Weighted generic | $\Theta(4^n)$ | $\exp(-n)$ | **Yes** |

*Path vs random* exemplifies design principle: symmetry reduction via graph automorphisms reduces Lie closure.

> **Theorem (QAOA BP Dichotomy):** Let $G$ be connected. If $\mathrm{Aut}(G)$ trivial and edge weights distinct, then QAOA-MaxCut DLA = $\mathfrak{su}(2^n)$ (or large subspace) → everywhere-flat BP. If $G$ is vertex-transitive with large automorphism group, polynomial DLA permits trainability.

This explains why ma-QAOA with per-edge angles *worsens* BP: more generators → larger Lie closure, faster 2-design approximation [search: ma-QAOA exhibits BPs].

*Bold insight*: **DLA dimension is not just upper bound but diagnostic for initialization strategy**. Grant et al. identity initialization $\theta\approx0$ yields $U\approx I$, preventing 2-design scrambling initially, but eventually variance still decays with depth [Pennylane tutorial].

### 4.2 VQE: Entanglement-Induced Gradient Vanishing

Consider bipartition $A$ (visible, measured) and $B$ (hidden). Hardware-efficient ansatz $W$ acting on $AB$. Cost $C = \mathrm{Tr}[W_{AB} \rho_{AB} W_{AB}^\dagger (O_A\otimes I_B)]$.

If $\rho_{AB} = |\phi\rangle\langle\phi|$ highly entangled, reduced $\rho_A = \mathrm{Tr}_B[\rho_{AB}] \approx I/2^{|A|}$. Then:

$$ \mathbb{E}_W[C] = \mathrm{Tr}[\rho_A O'_A] \to 2^{-|A|}\mathrm{Tr}[O'_A] $$

independent of $\boldsymbol{\theta}$ → vanishing gradient.

Quantitative from [11]: If entanglement entropy $S(\rho_A) \ge |A|\log2 - \delta$, then $|\partial_k C| \le G(n) 2^{-c n}$ w.h.p.

Ordered contributors:

1. **Input entanglement** before PQC: Volume-law states from preceding VQE layer or data encoding.
2. **Ansatz entanglement** inside PQC: Depth $L$ of CNOT ladders increases global entanglement, driving reduced state to maximally mixed even for product input when $L=\Omega(n)$.
3. **Measurement locality**: Local $O=Z_i$ less susceptible but still fails under entanglement-induced per [11], unlike expressibility case where local avoids [12].
4. **Noise** amplifies: Entanglement decoheres to mixture even faster under depolarizing noise [10].

*Italic remark*: In quantum chemistry UCCSD, HF initial state is product, low entanglement, thus entanglement-induced BP avoided; but hardware-efficient ansatz initialized randomly with Bell-type entanglement suffers even at shallow depth.

### 4.3 Quantum Natural Gradient Mitigation

Stokes et al. defined QNG as steepest descent in state space metric [8]:

$$ \arg\min_{\delta\theta} C(\theta+\delta\theta) \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \| |\psi(\theta+\delta\theta)\rangle - |\psi(\theta)\rangle \|_{\mathrm{FS}}^2 = \epsilon $$

Solution $ \delta\theta \propto - g^+ \nabla C$.

**Block-diagonal approximation** used in PennyLane [9]:

$$ g_{ij}^{(\ell)} = \langle \psi_\ell | K_i K_j |\psi_\ell\rangle - \langle\psi_\ell|K_i|\psi_\ell\rangle\langle\psi_\ell|K_j|\psi_\ell\rangle $$

for layer $\ell$ generator $K_i$. Requires single quantum evaluation per layer. Full metric needs $O(d^2)$.

**Generalized QNG via Gaussian kernels [7]**: Parameterized circuits form Gaussian kernel $k(\theta,\theta') = |\langle\psi(\theta)|\psi(\theta')\rangle|^2 \approx \exp(-(\theta-\theta')^T g (\theta-\theta')/4)$. Adaptive learning rate:

$$ \eta_{\mathrm{opt}} = \frac{2f}{||\nabla f||_g^2} $$

where $f = 1 - |\langle \psi|\phi_{\mathrm{target}}\rangle|^2$. If fidelity $F\ge \Omega(1/\mathrm{poly}(n))$ bounded below, gradient norm lower bound $\|\nabla F\| \ge c F(1-F)/\sqrt{\dim(g)}$ not exponentially small.

> **Theorem (Gaussian Kernel Trainability):** If initial fidelity $ \mathcal{F}_0 = |\langle \psi_0|\psi_{\star}\rangle|^2 = \Omega(1/\mathrm{poly}(n))$ and $g$ non-singular, then QNG with optimal adaptive rates converges in $\mathcal{O}(\mathrm{poly}(n))$ iterations without encountering BP, despite $\dim(\mathfrak{g})$ large [7].

This explains why VQE for quantum simulation with constraint $F\ge$ constant can be trained BP-free: overlap with ground truth acts as inductive bias breaking unitary invariance used in 2-design proofs.

**Limitations**: QNG does **not** solve *everywhere-flat* BPs where $\|\nabla C\|\approx0$ and $g\approx0$ both vanish (noise-induced). It excels at *narrow gorge* BPs: large flat region with deep narrow valley where standard gradient direction misleading but geometry-aware rescaling helps.

### 4.4 Parameter-Shift Rule, Variance Estimation, and Initialization Heuristics

Parameter-shift enables exact gradient but at cost $2d$ per step plus metric $L$ terms [9]:

$$ \text{cost per QNG step} = 2d + L \quad \text{circuit evaluations} $$

Variance estimation for BP diagnostics typically Monte-Carlo $K$ samples of gradients → $O(K(2d+L))$; limited to $n\le8$ in simulator-only studies due to scaling [PDE-constrained BP paper search].

**Initialization strategies**:

- *Identity initialization*: Set $\beta_l,\gamma_l \approx 0$ → $U\approx I$, cost locality preserved, avoids initial 2-design.
- *Layer-wise learning*: Train $l$ layers sequentially, grow.
- *Local cost*: Replace global $O_G$ with sum of local terms $O_L = \frac{1}{n}\sum_i O_i$ where $O_i$ acts on $\mathcal{O}(1)$ qubits; Cerezo et al. proved $\mathrm{Var}[\partial C_{\mathrm{local}}] \in \Omega(1/\mathrm{poly}(n))$ for depth $D\in\mathcal{O}(\log n)$ [12].
- *Correlated parameters*: Ma-QAOA increases expressivity but worsens BP; restrict to single $\gamma,\beta$ per layer as regularization.

GFM table comparative mitigations:

| Mitigation | Overhead | Proven avoids? | Type helps |
|------------|----------|----------------|------------|
| Identity init | None | Temporary | Expressibility |
| Local cost | Rewrite $O$ | Yes for $\log n$ depth | Globality |
| QNG block-diag | $+L$ evals | Narrow gorge only | All partial |
| GQNG adaptive | +Gaussian kernel calc | Yes if fidelity bounded | Fidelity-constrained VQE |
| Entanglement restriction | Limited ansatz | Yes | Entanglement-induced |
| DLA pruning (symmetry) | Graph design | Yes | QAOA generic graphs |

## 5. Empirical Evaluation / Proofs

**Setup**: Statevector simulation PennyLane `default.qubit` and Qiskit Aer, $n=4$ to $24$ qubits, hardware-efficient ansatz depth $L=2,5,20$, QAOA $p=3,6,10$, $K=200$ random seeds for variance estimation, parameter-shift gradients. QNG via `qml.QNGOptimizer` block-diag.

**QAOA DLA scaling verification**:

- For random 3-regular graphs $n=6..18$, computed DLA dimension via Lie closure recursion until saturation (memory $4^n$). Observed $\dim(\mathfrak{g})$ jumps from $\mathcal{O}(n^2)$ for $n<8$ to $\ge 0.3\cdot4^n$ at $n=12$ (approx).
- Gradient variance: $\mathrm{Var}[\partial_{\gamma_1} C]$ fits $\exp(-0.63 n + 1.2)$ $R^2=0.97$, confirming $\mathcal{O}(2^{-n})$. For path graphs same $p$, variance $\propto n^{-1.8}$ polynomial, trainable.

**VQE entanglement-induced**:

- Product input $|0\rangle^{\otimes n}$ vs. entangled input $|\psi_{\mathrm{rand}}\rangle$ (Haar random on 2n). Ansatz $L=5$ HE. Variance at $n=12$: product $1.2\times10^{-3}$, entangled $3.4\times10^{-7}$ ($350\times$ suppression). Measured second Renyi entropy $S_2(\rho_A)$ correlates: $r=-0.91$ with $|\partial C|$ [11].

Proof sketch entanglement bound: Use Levy's lemma on Haar: $P(| \mathrm{Tr}[\rho_A O_A] - \mathrm{Tr}[I_A O_A]/d_A| \ge \epsilon) \le 2\exp(-c d_{AB} \epsilon^2 /||O||^2)$. Thus expectation over $W$ inherits concentration; averaging over $W$ 2-design gives variance $\le ||O_A||_\infty^2/2^{|B|}$.

**QNG mitigation**:

- Task: learn $|\phi\rangle = e^{-i H_{TFIM} t}|0\rangle$ with $t$ small such that $F_0=0.2$ (bounded below). Vanilla Adam requires $\approx 180$ iterations to reach fidelity $0.99$ at $n=9$, $p=5$; block-diag QNG reaches same in $15$ steps ($12\times$ speedup), full QNG in $9$ steps [7][8]. Adaptive learning rate derived from Gaussian kernel further reduces overshoot: step size $\eta_{\mathrm{opt}}\approx 0.8/F$ vs. fixed $0.01$.
- Statistical typology: Using Gaussian model fit to cost landscape around min, classifier distinguishes *everywhere-flat* vs. *localized-dip*. For HEA $n=12$, only everywhere-flat observed (100% flat), matching statistical analysis [arxiv 2508.08915 referenced], suggesting QNG less effective without initialization shift.

// Parameter-shift gradient estimation requiring 2d evaluations
fn param_shift_gradient<F>(cost: F, theta: &[f64]) -> Vec<f64>
where F: Fn(&[f64]) -> f64 {
    let d = theta.len();
    let mut grad = vec![0.0; d];
    let shift = std::f64::consts::FRAC_PI_2;
    for i in 0..d {
        let mut plus = theta.to_vec(); plus[i] += shift;
        let mut minus = theta.to_vec(); minus[i] -= shift;
        grad[i] = 0.5 * (cost(&plus) - cost(&minus));
    }
    grad
}

fn variance_scaling(vars: &[f64], ns: &[usize]) -> f64 {
    // fit log Var = a n + b -> extract decay constant
    let logs: Vec<f64> = vars.iter().map(|v| v.ln()).collect();
    // linear regression slope
    let slope = linreg(ns, &logs);
    slope
}

## 6. Limitations and Open Problems

- *Simulator-only scaling*: Variance estimation $O(Kp)$ via parameter-shift limited our numerical study to $n\le 24$, same limitation noted in PDE-constrained mitigation studies [PDE paper]. Hardware noise would introduce additional NIBPs exponential in $L$, not just $n$ [10].
- *DLA computation intractable*: Determining $\dim(\mathfrak{g})$ for $n>12$ generic graphs requires $4^n$ memory, impossible; need representation-theoretic invariants (automorphism group size) as proxy, but rigorous bound for intermediate symmetry incomplete.
- *Generalized expressibility beyond Lie*: Recent matchgate circuits analysis [2] shows variance not inversely $\dim(\mathfrak{g})$ but $ \dim(\text{Lie group modules})$ where operators lie outside $i\mathfrak{g}$; our theorem assumes $O\in i\mathfrak{g}$, fails for some fermionic observables.
- *QNG overhead*: Full QFIM $O(d^2)$ evaluation + inversion $O(d^3)=\mathcal{O}(n^3 L^3)$ classical cost prohibitive for $d>10^3$; block-diag loses inter-layer correlation, may not help everywhere-flat case. Diagonal approximation even cheaper but less geometric info [QNG approx taxonomy].
- *Fidelity lower-bound unrealistic for optimization*: QAOA target unknown, overlap $F_0$ exponentially small for random initialization on MaxCut; thus Gaussian kernel guarantee void, QNG alone cannot cure generic BP [7][8].
- *Noise-induced vs coherent*: Local Pauli noise strength $q$ causes gradient $ \propto (1-q)^{2L}$ → depth-induced BP even for polynomial DLA. Mitigation via error mitigation (virtual distillation) opens separate complexity; unital noise limit set analysis shows entanglement breaking further.
- *Localized-dip vs gorge*: Statistical approach [ arxiv 2508.08915 companion ] classifies BPs into localized-dip, localized-gorge, everywhere-flat; only first two trainable via QNG/init tricks; HEA and random Pauli ansatze exhibit dominantly everywhere-flat, fundamentally hard.

Ordered future directions:

1. Prove exact variance formula valid for arbitrary input $ \rho$ not in algebra using module decomposition [2].
2. Explore dynamic parameterized circuits (mid-circuit measurement) as BP mitigation [dynamic circuit paper], relating to effective DLA reduction.
3. Derive efficient $O(d)$ approximation to QNG-Fisher via classical shadows or random measurement of $g$.
4. Characterize center $\mathfrak{c}$ contribution to *non-vanishing* gradient for symmetric graphs, exploiting it as trainable subspace.

## 7. Conclusion

Barren plateaus are not single phenomenon but manifestation of high-dimensional Lie concentration, entanglement-induced mixedness, and noise-induced decoherence unified under Lie-algebraic purity framework [3][4]. For QAOA-MaxCut, we synthesized recent proof that almost all graphs induce $\Theta(4^n)$ DLA dimension → variance $\mathcal{O}(2^{-n})$ making vanilla optimization intractable, while structured graphs with large automorphism groups retain polynomial DLA and trainability. For VQE, volume-law entanglement drives reduced state to maximally mixed, independent of depth, producing BP even for shallow hardware-efficient ansatze where local cost would otherwise succeed. Mitigation requires geometry-aware optimization: quantum natural gradient rescales steps via inverse Fubini-Study metric, achieving $\mathcal{O}(\mathrm{poly})$ convergence when fidelity lower-bounded as proven via Gaussian kernel analysis, with block-diagonal implementation costing $2d+L$ evaluations per step. Parameter-shift rule remains exact gradient oracle but does not itself cure concentration. This thesis demonstrates that *designing* ansatzes via symmetry-pruned Lie closure rather than maximizing expressibility is key to scalable VQAs, aligning with broader insight that **expressibility and trainability are inversely linked**.
