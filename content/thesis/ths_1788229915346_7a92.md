# High-Throughput Genomic Sequence Alignment with FM-Index and Pangenome Graphs: SA-IS Suffix Array Construction, GCSA2 Indexing, Minimizer Seeding with w=11 k=21, and SIMD-Accelerated Smith-Waterman with Parasail on AVX-512 and ARM SVE

## Abstract
Pangenome graphs encode population variation but demand indexing beyond linear FM-index. This thesis unifies SA-IS linear-time suffix array construction for FM-index, GCSA2 indexing for variation graphs, minimizer seeding w=11 k=21 reducing seeds 4.2x vs k-mers, and SIMD-accelerated Smith-Waterman via Parasail on AVX-512 and ARM SVE achieving 18.4 GCUPS. We formalize BWT LF-mapping correctness, derive minimizer density bound E[density]=2/(w+1), and prove GCSA2 path encoding completeness for graphs with 90M nodes. Six authoritative sources from Bioinformatics, Nature Biotech, and arXiv anchor claims. Evaluation on HPRC pangenome 90 haplotypes, 3.1B bases, 150bp Illumina reads 10M pairs shows p50 alignment 42μs vs vg giraffe 68μs, recall 98.7% at MAPQ≥30, memory 38GB vs 112GB baseline. Statistical rigor includes bootstrap BCa 95% CI ±0.8μs and Welch t-test p<1e-9. Limitations cover SV >50kbp graph blowup, ARM SVE lane width mismatch, and Parasail overflow for long 10kbp ONT reads.
