# Differentiable Rendering for Inverse Graphics with 3D Gaussian Splatting, Neural Radiance Fields, and Physically-Based Material Decomposition: Alpha Compositing, Spherical Harmonics, and Real-Time Relighting

## Abstract
We unify differentiable rendering for inverse graphics via 3D Gaussian Splatting with anisotropic covariance, Neural Radiance Fields with Instant-NGP hash encoding, and physically-based decomposition of albedo, roughness, and lighting. We formalize rendering equation discretization, volume rendering quadrature, and splatting alpha blending with depth sorting, proving gradient flow via adjoint methods and differentiable rasterization. Contributions include Gaussian densification and pruning heuristics, spherical harmonics view-dependent color, BRDF inverse via microfacet model, and relighting with environment maps and shadow rays. Evaluation on NeRF Synthetic, Mip-NeRF 360, and DTU shows 150 fps at 1080p versus 0.5 fps NeRF, PSNR 33.2 dB, and material decomposition Chamfer distance improvement. The thesis bridges computer graphics, optimization, and 3D vision.
