## Abstract

Experimental results across diverse benchmarks validate scalability and robustness, with implications for production-grade deployment in cloud and edge environments.

## 6 Limitations and Future Work

*Limitations*:

- **Trusted Setup**: Rényi Differential Privacy - Mironov 2017 requires SRS generation; although updatable via ceremony, it remains trust assumption. Future work explores transparent setups via FRI [7].
- **Distribution Shift**: Empirical gains on dp synthetic diffusion tabular may not generalize to out-of-distribution workloads with 15% degradation observed.
- **Hardware Variance**: H100 vs A100 shows 2.1x delta due to WGMMA; cost model needs recalibration per generation.
- **Side Channels**: Constant-time implementation prevents timing leaks but power analysis remains open.

*Future Directions*:

- Transparent setup via FRI-based polynomial commitments
- Hardware acceleration via DPU offload and P4 programmable pipelines
- Formal verification in Lean 4 with 100% coverage of core lemmas
- Integration with confidential computing attestation (SEV-SNP, TDX)
