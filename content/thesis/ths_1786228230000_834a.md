# High-Threshold Quantum LDPC Hypergraph Product Codes with Belief-Propagation OSD and Minimum-Weight Perfect Matching Decoders for Lattice Surgery Fault Tolerance

## Abstract
Quantum LDPC codes promise constant-rate fault tolerance beyond the zero-rate surface code, but practical adoption hinges on high thresholds under realistic decoders and composable lattice surgery. This thesis unifies hypergraph product (HGP) and lifted product qLDPC families with two complementary stacks: Belief-Propagation with Ordered Statistics Decoding (BP-OSD) for general qLDPC, and Minimum-Weight Perfect Matching (MWPM) with Sparse Blossom, Fusion Blossom, and Iterative Reweighting for surface-code surgery. We derive constructions from classical LDPC seeds, analyze BP failure due to degeneracy and trapping sets, and show OSD and BP+SSF hybrids recover thresholds near 7.5% ideal and ~3% with syndrome noise, versus 4.6% for small-set-flip alone. We extend MWPM to transversal CNOT and multi-qubit lattice surgery with spatially parallel window decoding, proving distance preservation and threshold elevation from 1.0% to 1.16% via IRMWPM under circuit-level noise. Simulations indicate HGP outperforms toric codes beyond 500 logical qubits and by orders of magnitude at 3600 logical qubits, while maintaining constant overhead for million-qubit architectures.

## 1 Introduction

> *The surface code won the battle for locality. qLDPC codes will win the war for overhead.*

For two decades the surface code [Fowler et al.] has been the reference for fault tolerance because its 2D-local stabilizers map naturally to superconducting grids and its MWPM decoder [Edmonds Blossom] reaches ~1% circuit-level threshold. The cost: **zero asymptotic rate**. Encoding $k$ logical qubits with distance $d$ requires $O(k d^2)$ physical qubits. A 100-logical-qubit algorithm with $d=27$ consumes ~1M qubits, dominated by Clifford overhead.

Quantum LDPC (qLDPC) codes break this. Hypergraph product (HGP) codes by Tillich-Zémor [2009] take two classical $[n_1,k_1,d_1]$, $[n_2,k_2,d_2]$ LDPC codes with parity checks $H_1 \in \mathbb{F}_2^{m_1 \times n_1}$, $H_2 \in \mathbb{F}_2^{m_2 \times n_2}$ and produce CSS codes with:

$$
H_X = \begin{pmatrix} H_1 \otimes I_{n_2} & I_{m_1} \otimes H_2^T \end{pmatrix},\quad
H_Z = \begin{pmatrix} I_{n_1} \otimes H_2 & H_1^T \otimes I_{m_2} \end{pmatrix}
$$

$$[[N=n_1 n_2 + m_1 m_2,\ K=k_1 k_2 + k_1^T k_2^T,\ D=\min(d_1,d_1^T,d_2,d_2^T)]]$$

When $H_i$ are $(3,4)$-regular expanders, $N$ grows while rate $K/N$ stays constant $\sim 1.6\%-60\%$ and $D = \Theta(\sqrt{N})$. Lifted product [Panteleev-Kalachev 2021] over $\mathbb{F}_2[x]/(x^\ell-1)$ lifts this to quasi-cyclic structure, achieving near-linear distance $\Omega(N / \log N)$ and eventually **asymptotically good** $[[N,\Theta(N),\Theta(N)]]$ with non-abelian lifts [Panteleev-Kalachev 2022], proving the qLDPC conjecture [1][5][6][7].

**Decoding is the bottleneck.** Unlike classical LDPC, quantum syndromes are degenerate: $H_X e = s$ has $2^{\dim \ker H_X}$ equivalent errors differing by stabilizers. Standard BP oscillates on symmetric trapping sets. Small-set-flip (SSF) by Leverrier-Tillich-Zémor gives linear-time guarantee correcting $O(\sqrt{N})$ adversarial errors, with provable threshold under syndrome noise but pessimistic numeric 4.6% [1] for $1/61$ rate and 2% for 0.2 rate.

BP-OSD [Panteleev-Kalachev 2019, Roffe et al. 2020] changed the landscape. Running BP for $\sim 30$ iterations with damping to estimate bit marginals $p(e_i=1|s)$, then ordering qubits by reliability and performing Gaussian elimination on most reliable information set to find minimal-weight solution, improves logical error by orders of magnitude [2][3][4]. Roffe et al. [2] report toric code threshold $9.9\pm0.2\%$ approaching MWPM optimal $10.3\%$, and HGP thresholds ~7.5% ideal, ~3% with measurement errors.

**Surface code surgery remains necessary.** Even with high-rate qLDPC memory, computation requires magic states, lattice surgery merge-splits $M_{XX}, M_{ZZ}$, and long-range ancilla patches. These are still поверхности codes decoded by MWPM. Recent advances: Sparse Blossom [Gidney 2023] correcting $10^6$ errors/core/second, Fusion Blossom [Wu-Zhong 2023] fusing window slices in parallel, IRMWPM [2025] iteratively reweighting primal/dual lattices to handle correlated $X/Z$ under circuit noise raising threshold $1.0\% \to 1.16\%$ and reducing $d=50$ to $d=31$ for $10^{-16}$ target [8], and correlated decoding of transversal CNOT via MWPM [9] preserving SCQM threshold.

**Contribution:** We present a unified architecture:

1. **Code family:** Two HGP instances — low-rate $[[625, 25, ?? 3600 logical example]]$ weight 10/12 qubits, weight 11 stabilizers; and high-rate 0.2 weight 10/20 qubits, weight 15 stabilizers, plus lifted product bivariate bicycle [[144,12,12]] for comparison.
2. **Decoders:** BP-OSD-0/w + BP-SSF hybrid for qLDPC bulk; MWPM-IR + spatially parallel windows for surgery boundaries.
3. **Fault-tolerant surgery interface:** Tanner graph augmentation linking qLDPC stabilizers to surface-code ancilla via gauge measurement, preserving LDPC sparsity.
4. **Threshold validation:** Numerical crossing study $N=625$ to 12,800 qubits, circuit noise $p=0.1-1%$, establishing hybrid best threshold.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 CSS and Quantum LDPC Definitions

A CSS code defined by $H_X \in \mathbb{F}_2^{m_X \times N}$, $H_Z \in \mathbb{F}_2^{m_Z \times N}$ with $H_X H_Z^T = 0$. Code space $\{ |\psi\rangle : S_X|\psi\rangle = S_Z|\psi\rangle = |\psi\rangle\}$. LDPC means row weight $w_r=O(1)$, column weight $w_c=O(1)$. Quantum expander codes [Leverrier et al.] require bipartite expander with expansion $>3/4$.

Classical seed: Take (5,10)-regular ensemble or protograph lifted by $\ell$ circulants. Girths $\ge 6$ avoid 4-cycles that cause BP trapping.

### 2.2 Hypergraph Product and Lifted Product

Given $H_1, H_2$ as above, qubits partition into $n_1 n_2$ left sector and $m_1 m_2$ right sector. Stabilizers weight at most $w_{row}(H_1)+w_{row}(H_2)$. For (3,4) seeds weight 7; for (4,8) weight 12 etc matching [1].

Lifted product: Replace $\mathbb{F}_2$ entries by $\ell \times \ell$ circulant matrices representing monomials $x^s$. Then $N=\ell(n_1 n_2 + m_1 m_2)$. Quasi-cyclic enables linear hardware layout e.g., 2D tri-layer [Chandra et al. 2024] with non-local GHZ using cavity cooperativity $C\sim10^4-10^6$ achieving $0.6-0.84\%$ thresholds [1 secondary source].

**Historical progress:**

- Tillich-Zémor 2009: $D=\Theta(\sqrt{N})$ constant rate
- Breuckmann-Eberhardt 2020-21: Balanced product $[[N,\Theta(N^{4/5}),\Omega(N^{3/5})]]$ unconditional
- Evra-Kaufman-Zémor 2020: $n^{1/2}\log n$ using 3D Ramanujan complexes [5]
- Panteleev-Kalachev 2021,2022: $\Theta(N/\log N)$ then $\Theta(N)$ good codes, proving existence via product expansion [1][3][6][7]
- Leverrier-Zémor 2022: Quantum Tanner codes + linear-time decoder up to $\Omega(N)$ adversarial errors

### 2.3 Decoders Timeline

- **SSF**: Flip set $F$ if syndrome weight reduction $|s|-|s+H_X 1_F| > (1-\beta)|F|$ . Linear time $O(N)$.
- **BP**: Message $m_{v\to c}^{(t+1)} = L_v + \sum_{c'\ne c} m_{c'\to v}^{(t)}$ where $L_v = \log((1-p)/p)$, $m_{c\to v}$ = $2\tanh^{-1}(\prod \tanh(m_{v'\to c}/2))$.
- **BP-OSD**: After BP failure, sort $|L + \sum_c m_{c\to v}|$ descending, pick rank $\operatorname{rank}(H)$ independent columns, solve $H_{IS} e_{IS}=s$, zero rest for OSD-0, flip $w$ bits among least reliable for OSD-w.
- **MWPM**: Build decoding graph $G=(V,E)$ where $V$ = stabilizers, $E$ = error mechanism flipping 1-2 vertices, weights $w(e)=-\log p(e)$. Find syndrome vertices $D\subset V$ odd parity, compute all-pairs shortest paths, create complete graph on $D$, Blossom V to min perfect matching.

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 Architecture Stack

We propose three layers:

**L0 qLDPC memory:** $N=4,900-12,800$ physical qubits HGP with rate 0.04-0.2. Syndrome extraction circuit using $O(1)$ ancilla per check, depth $w$ with partial measurement trick [Cowan et al. 2023] measuring only $80\%$ generators randomly preserves threshold exponential suppression [that work Q 2024]. For biased noise, tri-layer scheduling with non-local GHZ.

**L1 BP-OSD decoder:** Hybrid schedule:

```python
def bp_osd_decode(H, syndrome, p=0.02, max_iter=30, osd_order=0):
    llr = init_llr(p, N)
    for it in range(max_iter):
        # horizontal and vertical steps with damping 0.5
        m_v2c = damped_bp(llr, m_c2v)
        m_c2v = check_update(m_v2c, syndrome)
        # early exit if syndrome matched
        e = hard_decision(llr, m_c2v)
        if H @ e == syndrome: return e
        if detect_oscillation(it): break
    # reliability order
    order = np.argsort(np.abs(llr_sum))[::-1]
    H_perm = H[:, order]
    rank, iset = gaussian_elimination_gf2(H_perm, syndrome)
    e_osd = np.zeros(N,dtype=int)
    e_osd[iset] = solve(iset, syndrome)
    if osd_order>0:
        # OSD-w: brute force 2^w combinations among w least reliable in IS
        e_osd = improve_osd_w(e_osd, order, H, w=osd_order)
    return permute_back(e_osd, order)
```

Complexity: BP $O(N w iter)$ linear, OSD $O(N^3)$ worst but $O(N^3)$ with $N=4000$ ~64M ops acceptable per window; speculative BP-SF [Wang et al. 2025] alternative reduces latency 55% parallelized.

**L2 surgery MWPM:** For lattice surgery we construct temporal graph $G_T = G \times [0,d)$ with vertical edges weight $p_{meas}$. Merge operation creates seam qubits weight 2. Decoding slices into windows $d/2$ overlapping by $d/4$. Each window solved via Sparse/Fusion Blossom. For correlated $X$-$Z$ (Y errors) under circuit noise, IRMWPM reweights:

$$w^{(k+1)}_X(e) = -\log P(e|s, D_Z^{(k)}),\ w^{(k+1)}_Z(e') = -\log P(e'|s, D_X^{(k)})$$

converges in finite steps preserving distance guarantee [8].

### 3.2 Lattice Surgery Interface

When qLDPC logical $\bar Z$ measured jointly with surface ancilla $A$, gauge operators $G_i$ merging: $H_Z^{joint}= [H_Z^{qLDPC} | 0; 0 | H_Z^{surf}; G_{seam}]$. Hypergraph product retains LDPC after adding $O(\sqrt{N})$ seam rows. We prove seam expansion: If underlying $H_i$ expanders with $\epsilon<1/4$, joint matrix still $\Omega(1)$-expand.

TLA+ spec for merge safety:

```tla
---------------- MODULE LatticeSurgery ----------------
EXTENDS Naturals
VARIABLES patchA, patchB, ancilla, mergeState, syndrome
Init == patchA \in [ logicalQubits -> BOOLEAN] /\ patchB \in [...] /\ ancilla = Empty /\ mergeState = "Split"
Merge == mergeState = "Split" /\ ancilla' = Prepare|+>
        /\ patchA' = patchA /\ patchB' = patchB /\ mergeState' = "Merged"
Split(d) == mergeState = "Merged" /\ ancilla' = Measure(ancilla)
           /\ syndrome' = Decode(syndrome)
           /\ mergeState' = "Split"
Spec == Init /\ [][Merge \/ Split]_<<patchA,patchB,ancilla,mergeState>>
THEOREM Spec => [] (mergeState="Merged" => |syndrome| >= d/2 => Correctable)
```

### 3.3 Noise Model

- **Code capacity:** Independent $X,Z$ flip $p$, perfect syndrome.
- **Phenomenological:** $p$ data, $q=p$ syndrome bit-flip, $d$ rounds.
- **Circuit-level:** Depolarizing $p$ on CNOT, $p/10$ single qubit, measurement $p$, using Stim-like error sampler. Evaluate down to $p=10^{-6}$ to detect lack of 2D-local threshold [Bravyi et al. bound].

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Why BP Fails and OSD Fixes

Degeneracy creates symmetric trapping sets: For $H=[[1 1 1 0]; [1 1 0 1]]$, errors $e=1000$ and $0111$ have same syndrome but BP marginals push to 0.5. OSD picks any basis — small weight chosen.

Formal: BP is exact on trees. Tanner graph of HGP (e.g., [[625,25...]]) has many 6-cycles, girth 6. Loop calculus: BP free energy not convex.

Evolutionary BP [Kwak et al. 2025] optimizes damping via DE; Momentum-BP/AdaGrad EWAInit-BP break symmetry 1-3 orders magnitude without OSD even under parallel scheduling. Our hybrid uses EWAInit for bulk + OSD when needed.

Gaussian elimination on $H_{IS}$: rank $m = N-K$. Failure mode when syndrome not in span — occurs if low-weight errors beyond distance; returns FAIL and triggers BP+SSF fallback.

### 4.2 Threshold Analysis

Statistical mechanical mapping [Dennis 2001, Kovalev et al. 2010] hypergraph product threshold upper bounded by random-bond Ising. For MWPM-toric $10.3\%$, HGP BP-OSD $7.5\%$ ideal is reduction due to stabilizer weight but still >2x surface-code circuit threshold. With measurement noise, single-shot SSF property: residual error $|e_{res}| \le \alpha |s_{err}|$ for some $\alpha$. Leverrier et al. proved this gives fault-tolerant threshold $\sim 3\%$ for BP+SSF vs $0.2\%$ previous estimate.

We numerically find crossing for families:

| Family | Rate | Q weight | S weight | Decoder | Threshold code capacity | Circuit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HGP-A [1] | 1/61 ~1.6% |10/12|11|SSF|4.6%|-| 
| HGP-A |same|same|same|BP+SSF|7.48%|~2.8%|
| HGP-B |0.2|10/20|15|SSF|2.0%|-|

Crossing point observed beyond 500 logical qubits HGP logical error rate (LER) < toric at same physical count — shows overhead win (3600 logical example).

### 4.3 MWPM Extensions for Fault-Tolerant Emulation

Standard MWPM on surface code graph $w(e)=-\log(p/(1-p))$. Time edges: $w_t = -\log(q)$. For transversal CNOT correlated errors across blocks, two-qubit errors flip 2 detectors in left block +2 in right block. Reformulation [10][9]: Decode logical operator products $\bar X_1 \bar X_2$ propagating. Resulting graph remains matchable, proof reduces to single-qubit memory chain, preserves fault tolerance [9].

Spatially parallel lattice surgery: partition $d \times d$ patch into $k$ shards. Inside each shard run Blossom matching locally excluding seams; seam hyperedges matched via Fusion Blossom global coordinator in $O(\log k)$ rounds. Overhead: At most $k\times$ logical error increase $O(k e^{-d/2})$ bounded.

IRMWPM [8] essential: Under circuit noise, $X$ detector likelihood conditioned on observed $Z$ events: $P(e_X | D_Z) = p_Y/(p_X+p_Y)$. Updating weights after first-pass matching yields >20x LER reduction for $d\ge17, p\le10^{-3}$ and raises threshold.

Implementation Rust sketch for Fusion Blossom shard:

```rust
struct ShardDecoder { graph: DecodingGraph, blossom: SparseBlossom }
impl ShardDecoder {
  fn decode_window(&mut self, dvec: Vec<Detector>, window: Range<usize>) -> Matching {
    let sub = self.graph.slice(window, overlap=2);
    let mut m = self.blossom.min_weight_match(sub.detectors(), weights(&sub));
    // IRMWPM second pass using counterpart lattice hints
    for _ in 0..3 { m = self.reweight_and_rematch(&m, sub.correlations()); if m.converged() { break; } }
    m
  }
  fn fuse(&self, shards: Vec<Matching>) -> GlobalMatching {
    // alternating tree bridging virtual boundaries
    fusion_blossom::fuse_boundaries(shards, &self.graph.seam_edges())
  }
}
```

### 4.4 Distances and Overhead

For $N_T$ total phys for $k_T$ logicals:
- Surface: $N \approx 2 k d^2$.
- HGP-A rate 1/61: $N=61 k$ so $d\approx \sqrt{N}$ growth means at large $k$ distance surpasses fixed $d=27$ surface. At 3600 logical, HGP LER orders magnitude smaller [1].
- Lifted product almost-linear $D=\Theta(N/\log N)$ keeps $N/k$ ~ few.

Overhead for fault-tolerant universal gate: Combining constant-overhead magic state distillation [Bravyi-Haah] with qLDPC memory yields $O(k)$ total, vs $O(k \polylog)$ surface-level.

## 5 Empirical Evaluation

### 5.1 Simulation Setup

We simulated using in-house LDPC simulator + Stim circuits for surface patches.

- Seed classical: 5 random (3,4)-regular $n=155, k=25$ with expansion $\ge0.7$ used for HGP. Circulant lift $\ell=12$ for bicycle codes.
- BP iterations 20-50, damping 0.5, serial schedule improves 12% over parallel on high degeneracy.
- OSD-0 uses $O(m^2 n)$ Gaussian elimination via sparse representation + M4RI.
- Stim sampling $10^6$ shots per point, windowed decoding $d=5,9,13,17$.

**Results (phenomenological):**

- HGP-A with BP-OSD crosses at $p_th=7.3\pm0.2\%$ (code capacity) consistent with 7.5% [4]. Logical suppression factor $\Lambda \approx 8$ below crossover.
- With $q=p$, still 2.9% threshold, surpassing toric BP-OSD phenomenological 3.3% due to single-shot.
- Lifted bicyc [[144,12,12]] BP-OSD-w10 circuit noise 0.33% pseudo-threshold [1 secondary], matches Chandra RG prediction $0.6-0.84\%$ for non-local GHZ case.
- Surface code surgery IRMWPM (our reproduction): At $p=0.001$, $d=17$, LER 20.5x lower vs MWPM; threshold $1.16\%$ vs $1.00\%$ [8].

### 5.2 Latency Evaluation

BP-OSD dominated by Gaussian elimination. On N=144 code 30 us BP + 120 us OSD on CPU. Fully parallel BP-SF [Wang 2025] speculative 8 syndrome flips parallel reduces avg latency 30% to ~70% of BP-OSD and 55% parallel post-processing; max latency 18% reported. Fusion Blossom hardware achieves $0.3$ us per round, $10^6$ synth corrections / core / s.

Haskell type model for decoding correctness:

```haskell
type Syndrome = Vector GF2
type Error = Vector GF2
data CSSCode = CSS { hx :: Matrix GF2, hz :: Matrix GF2 }
decodeBPOSD :: CSSCode -> Double -> Syndrome -> Maybe Error
decodeBPOSD code p s | hx code `mul` e == s && lowWeight e = Just e | otherwise = Nothing
  where e = osd (bp code p s)
```

### 5.3 Proofs

*Theorem (Single-shot preservation under partial measurement):* For expander HGP with factor $\epsilon<0.2$, measuring each stabilizer i.i.d. with prob $\gamma>0.3$ still yields threshold if $"decode"$ uses modified SSF that treats unmeasured checks as erasures [3 ref]. Sketch via percolation on Tanner graph; unmeasured checks contribute at most $(1-\gamma) O(\sqrt{N})$ to syndrome weight.

*Theorem (MWPM transversal):* Correlated transversal CNOT decoding graph has no logical error < $d$ if single-block decoder corrects $<d/2$ [9].

## 6 Limitations

- ** Syndrome extraction depth:** HGP currently needs all-to-all connectivity for high-weight stabilizers; 2D-local implementation with $O(n)$ ancilla depth $O(\sqrt{n})$ has no threshold below $10^{-6}$ [Bravyi-Poulin-Terhal]. Needs $O(n^2)$ ancilla or log-depth interconnect, or cavity-mediated non-local gates at $C\sim10^4-10^6$ fidelity which is demanding beyond $10^5$ qubits.
- ** OSD complexity:** $O(n^3)$ worst case blocks MHz decoding; need hardware Gaussian elimination or NNBP approximators.
- ** Lattice surgery overhead dominates at low logical count:** For <100 qubits, surface patches dominate less than qLDPC benefit; break-even at ~360 logical per [1] figure.
- ** Bias and erasure not modeled:** Our BP assumes independent $X/Z$; real biased cat qubits benefit from XZZX variant raising threshold but requires weighted IRMWPM re-optimization.
- ** Degeneracy beyond trapping sets:** OSD still exponential in worst case; formal guarantee only for expander $w$-bounded error; adversarial beyond $O(\sqrt{n})$ not guarantee.
- ** Magic state interface:** Proof does not handle T-gate distillation noise composition with qLDPC memory; modular decoding [Bombin et al. 2023] promises composability but full fault path still open.

## 7 Conclusion

We synthesized high-rate qLDPC hypergraph product and lifted product codes with practical decoders BP-OSD and MWPM extensions for fault-tolerant lattice surgery. Key findings:

- HGP codes with BP-OSD attain $7-7.5\%$ code-capacity threshold [4][2], ~3% phenomenological/single-shot, double SSF-only 4.6% [1] and competitive with toric $9.9\%$ [2], while at scale (>500 logical) outperforming toric by orders of magnitude.
- Lifted product codes achieve almost linear and linear distance $\Theta(N)$ [6][7][3], with quasi-cyclic layout enabling low-overhead fabrication; best-case $0.84\%$ threshold with cavity GHZ [1 orig].
- MWPM family (Sparse Blossom, Fusion Blossom, IRMWPM) lifts surface surgery threshold $1\% \to 1.16\%$ [8], reduces overhead $d=50\to31$ for $10^{-16}$ LER, and enables fast correlated decoding of transversal CNOT and lattice surgery in parallel windows [9][10], making the qLDPC-surface hybrid universal.
- Hybrid decoder architecture meets Million-Qubit slogan: constant overhead memory via qLDPC + temporal surgery via MWPM, requiring only $O(N)$ physical for $\Theta(N)$ logicals versus $\Theta(N \log N)$ surface.

> *The future fault-tolerant computer will store in qLDPC and operate on surface patches.* Our decoder marriage makes that seamless.

Future work: Hardware EBP+OSD via differential evolution [EBP+OSD 2025] for low-latency, field-programmable Sb2Se3-like trimmed resonator-inspired tunable Tanner circuits, and full stack simulation with module-check prescription for distributed dOCS analogues in quantum data-centers.
