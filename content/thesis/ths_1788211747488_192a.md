## 6 Limitations

- **Quantization drift:** INT4 inference drifts 2.1% accuracy after 10k steps; mitigation via QAT with Straight-Through Estimator reduces to 0.4% but increases training time 1.3x.
- **SRAM cap:** Loihi 2 192KB per core limits fanout to 4k; SpiNNaker2 packet drop under 80% utilization requires backpressure, reducing throughput 12% at high load.
- **Proof scalability:** Boogie verification times out beyond 50k LOC; compositional lemmas help but manual annotations still required for 8% of modules.
- **Energy measurement variance:** RAPL PKG domain ±5% noise; we report median over 10 runs with IQR shading.
- **Clock skew:** TrueTime epsilon 7ms in worst-case; HLC tightens to 2ms but sacrifices external consistency under partition.
