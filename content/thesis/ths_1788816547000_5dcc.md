# Guarded Recursion and Step-Indexed Logical Relations: Later Modalities, Clocked Fixpoints, and Semantic Soundness for Higher-Order Store

## Abstract

We present a unified semantic account of two traditions for reasoning about recursion through *higher-order store*: **step-indexed logical relations** and **guarded recursion**. Step-indexing, introduced by Appel and McAllester to model recursive types for foundational proof-carrying code [5], stratifies semantic definitions by a natural-number index counting available computation steps; guarded recursion, introduced by Nakano [1], internalizes the same stratification in the type system via the *later* modality ▷ and the Löb rule *(▷A → A) → A*. We show that every step-indexed logical relation is a guarded-recursive definition in disguise, and that the **topos of trees** provides the canonical categorical home for both. Extending the picture with **clocked** guarded recursion [2,4] recovers genuine coinductive types and finite observations on infinite data, while **Kripke-indexed worlds** resolve the circularity of Landin's knot for mutable references [6,7]. We instantiate the framework on a call-by-value λ-calculus with iso-recursive types and references, prove a fundamental theorem, adequacy, and type safety, and validate the definitions with an executable reference checker — the methodology underlying modern program logics such as **Iris** [8].

## 1 Introduction

Proving *type safety* for a language with mutable references is routine until functions may be stored in the heap. The moment a reference cell can hold a closure that itself closes over references, the meaning of types becomes **circular**: `ref τ` depends on `τ`, which may mention `ref` again — *Landin's knot*, whereby general recursion is encodable through higher-order store alone. Classical logical relations, defined by induction on types, are no longer well-founded.

Two traditions have tamed this circularity, and this thesis argues they are one tradition seen from two sides:

1. **Step-indexed logical relations** [5,6,7]. Predicates are indexed by a natural number *k*, "future computation steps we may still spend." Definitions recurse on *k*, not on types, so circularities dissolve. Appel and McAllester's
   *Vₖ(int) = { n | n integer }*,
   *Vₖ(t→s) = { λx.a | ∀j<k, ∀v ∈ Vⱼ(t), a[x↦v] ∈ Eⱼ(s) }*,
   *Eₖ(t) = { a | ∀j≤k, ∀b, a →ʲ b ∧ b irreducible ⇒ b ∈ V_{k−j}(t) }*
   scale to mutable references and impredicative polymorphism [6].
2. **Guarded recursion** [1,2,3,4]. Nakano's *later* modality ▷ internalizes the index into the *type system*: ▷A is "an A available one step later." Recursion is permitted exactly when guarded by ▷, and the **Löb rule** *(▷A → A) → A* yields unique fixpoints. The **topos of trees** gives these modalities categorical semantics [3,4].

Our claim: step indices *are* later modalities made explicit — the clause "∀j < k" is precisely the semantic content of ▷. Manual index arithmetic can therefore be replaced by **Löb induction**, and ad-hoc well-foundedness arguments by one theorem about contractive functors. **Clocks** [2] turn guarded *recursive* types into genuine *coinductive* types with finite observations, and **Kripke worlds** give the canonical treatment of the store [7,8].

*Contributions.* (i) A unified exposition of step-indexed logical relations, guarded recursion, and clocked fixpoints. (ii) A step-indexed Kripke logical relation for a CBV language with iso-recursive types and references, presented as a *guarded* fixpoint, with fundamental property, adequacy, and type safety. (iii) An executable reference checker validating the definitions. (iv) An account of how this methodology underlies Iris [8].

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Step-indexed models of recursive types

Appel and McAllester [5] introduced *indexed models* for semantic proofs of type safety in foundational proof-carrying code: "expression *a* has type *t*" means "for every step bound *k*, *a* *seems* to have type *t* for *k* steps." Since every use of a value inside *Vₖ* quantifies over *strictly smaller* indices *j < k*, even contravariant recursive equations admit solutions by induction on *k*. Ahmed [6] extended the technique relationally to recursive and quantified types, proved soundness for contextual equivalence of iso-recursive types, and identified the subtle *transitivity* question for the Appel–McAllester PER model — still open. Dreyer, Ahmed, and Birkedal [7] derived **logical**, step-free proof principles from the indexed relation, so contextual (in)equivalence can be proved without mentioning indices; the indices work once, inside the metatheory.

> **Theorem:** (Appel–McAllester [5]) The indexed sets *Vₖ(t)* and *Eₖ(t)* are well-defined by induction on *k*, satisfy downward closure *Vₖ(t) ⊇ Vₖ₊₁(t)*, and validate a fundamental property: every well-typed closed term lies in *Eₖ(t)* for all *k*.

### 2.2 Guarded recursion and the later modality

Nakano [1] proposed a *modality for recursion* inspired by Gödel–Löb provability logic. The **later** modality ▷ provides **next** : *A → ▷A*, applicative application *▷(A→B) → ▷A → ▷B*, and **Löb** : *(▷A → A) → A*. A recursive type *μα.τ* is well-formed when *α* occurs *guarded* in *τ*; streams are *Stream A ≅ A × ▷(Stream A)*, and even *negative* guarded equations like *T ≅ (▷T) → A* admit solutions. Nakano proved normalization via a step-indexed realizability interpretation — already hinting at the connection with step-indexing [9].

### 2.3 The topos of trees

Birkedal and colleagues identified the **topos of trees** 𝓢 — presheaves over *(ω, ≤)*, i.e. chains *X₀ ⇐ X₁ ⇐ X₂ ⇐ ⋯* with restriction maps — as the canonical model [3,4]. The later functor is *(▷X)₀ = 1*, *(▷X)ₙ₊₁ = Xₙ*. Every *locally contractive* endofunctor has a unique fixpoint up to isomorphism; every contractive map *f : ▷X → X* has a unique fixed point. Guarded recursive types are thus not coinductive: *Stream A* at index *n* is simply length-*n* approximations, with restriction dropping the last element [4].

### 2.4 Clocks and the recovery of coinduction

Plain ▷ cannot type `every2nd` (two inputs per output) and admits no *Stream A → A* head function. Atkey and McBride [2] introduced **clock variables** *κ* indexing the modality: *▷^κ*. A *κ*-guarded stream *Str^κ A = fix X. A × ▷^κ X* unfolds at clock *κ*'s rate; quantifying over all clocks, *Stream A ≜ ∀κ. Str^κ A*, yields genuine coinductive types with finite observations. Clouston et al. [3] internalize this as the **box** modality □ (*∀κ. A*, time-independent data); Bizjak and Møgelberg [4] gave the semantic counterpart — a model with **clock synchronisation**, where □ selects the *constant objects* of the topos of trees.

### 2.5 Kripke logical relations and Iris

For higher-order store, relations are refined with **Kripke worlds** *W = (k, Ψ)* pairing a step index with a *heap typing* Ψ, monotone in world extension. The circularity — heap typings mention value relations which mention heap typings — breaks by stratification: Ψ at index *k* constrains values only at indices *< k*. This is the semantic core of **Iris** [8], whose base logic features ▷, **Löb induction**, and a step-indexed model (via OFEs) that users never see: step-indexing surfaces only as ▷ and Löb.

## 3 Methodology

**Object language.** We study *λ^ref_μ*, a call-by-value λ-calculus with:

| Feature | Syntax | Selected typing rule |
|---|---|---|
| Iso-recursive types | `fold v`, `unfold e` | `Γ ⊢ v : τ[μα.τ/α] ⇒ Γ ⊢ fold v : μα.τ` |
| References | `ref e`, `!e`, `e₁ := e₂` | `Γ ⊢ e : τ ⇒ Γ ⊢ ref e : ref τ` |
| General recursion | via Landin's knot | encodable, not primitive |

Types: *τ ::= int \| τ→τ \| ref τ \| α \| μα.τ*. Soundness applies to arbitrary iso-recursive types because the *model* stratifies by steps.

**Semantic method.**

1. **Worlds.** *W = (k, Ψ)* with *k ∈ ℕ* and Ψ a finite map from locations to semantic types. Extension *(k,Ψ) ⊑ (j,Ψ')* requires *j ≤ k* and Ψ' to extend Ψ with *k*-approximations preserved.
2. **Value relation** *V⟦τ⟧_ρ(W)*, defined as the unique fixpoint of a *locally contractive* functor — a **guarded fixpoint** in the topos of trees. Function clauses quantify over strictly future-smaller worlds, mirroring ▷.
3. **Expression relation** *E⟦τ⟧_ρ(W)*: *(e₁,e₂)* are related when, for all *j ≤ k* and related heaps, *j*-step reductions to values yield values and heaps related at the *j*-step-smaller world.
4. **Proofs by Löb induction.** Where classical accounts do index arithmetic ("choose *j < k*, apply the IH at *j*"), we invoke Löb: to prove *P*, assume *▷P* and derive *P*. The bookkeeping is encapsulated once, in Löb's soundness for the model.

**Validation.** We implement *Vₖ/Eₖ* (unary fragment, which already exhibits the full index discipline) as an executable checker and measure membership-check cost versus *k* (§5).

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 The later modality as a step-index transformer

Unfolding the topos-of-trees semantics, *(▷X)ₙ₊₁ = Xₙ*: "▷X at index *n+1*" is "X at index *n*." This is *exactly* the step-index shift — the clause *∀j<k* in *Vₖ(t→s)* says function bodies must be well-behaved at index *j*, i.e. bodies live in *▷(E(t))*. The applicative structure *▷(A→B) → ▷A → ▷B* is the type-theoretic incarnation of the step-indexed application lemma, and **Löb induction**

> **Theorem:** (Löb) *If ▷P ⊢ P then ⊢ P.*

is the incarnation of induction on the step index. Every step-indexed proof that goes "by induction on *k*, applying the IH at *j < k*" is a Löb induction with the plumbing made explicit.

In Agda, ▷ is often *encoded* as a coinductive record for prototyping guarded programs:

```agda
-- Later, encoded as an infinite (coinductive) record
record ▷ (A : Set) : Set where
  coinductive
  field force : A

next : {A : Set} → A → ▷ A
force (next x) = x

-- Guarded streams: the tail is available "later"
record Stream (A : Set) : Set where
  coinductive
  field head : A
        tail : ▷ (Stream A)

-- Productivity is enforced by construction:
nats : Stream ℕ
head nats = 0
force (tail nats) = mapS suc nats
```

The slogan: *where the type theory writes ▷, the semanticist writes "at all j < k."*

### 4.2 Clocked fixpoints and the recovery of coinduction

A single global ▷ is too rigid: it cannot type `every2nd`, and admits no finite observations. **Clocks** relativize time: *▷^κ* ticks only when *κ* ticks, and *∀κ* asserts clock-independence — "available now, regardless of how time flows" [2]. The payoff is a type-theoretic reconstruction of coinduction:

```
Str^κ A  ≜  fix X. A × ▷^κ X      -- κ-guarded streams
Stream A ≜  ∀κ. Str^κ A           -- genuine coinductive streams
head : Stream A → A               -- finite observation, finally possible
```

Here `head s = π₁ (s κ₀)` instantiates the clock quantifier and projects — the ▷^κ guard is discharged by *clock application*, not by waiting. Clocked guarded recursion also explains why step-indexed relations need no clocks: *Eₖ(t)* is already uniform in *k*, and adequacy quantifies over all *k*. The *∀κ* of the type theory and the *∀k ∈ ℕ* of the metatheory play the identical role.

### 4.3 Worlds, heaps, and the stratified store

A world *W = (k, Ψ)* pairs a step budget with a heap typing, but a semantic type is itself a world-indexed relation — Ψ appears on both sides of its own definition. The stratification:

> **Theorem:** (Stratified worlds) A heap typing Ψ is *k*-valid when every Ψ(ℓ) constrains only *strictly fewer* than *k* steps. Then *V⟦τ⟧* is well-defined by guarded recursion on *k*, and world extension is monotone.

The reference clause reads: *((k,Ψ),(ℓ₁,ℓ₂)) ∈ V⟦ref τ⟧* iff *Ψ(ℓ₁) ≈_{<k} V⟦τ⟧* and *Ψ(ℓ₂) ≈_{<k} V⟦τ⟧*, where *≈_{<k}* is *k*-approximation. Storing a function "spends" one index step — operationally, `ref v` takes a reduction step; semantically, the ▷ guard absorbs it. This is Landin's knot, defused: self-reference through the store is permitted, but every cycle passes through ▷, so the fixpoint theorem applies. The slogan — *all recursion in the model goes through later* — is the semantic analogue of Nakano's syntactic guardedness [1,3].

### 4.4 Löb induction versus manual index arithmetic

Consider compatibility for recursive-type unfolding, the historical pain point. Classically [6], one case-splits on *k = 0* vs *k = j+1*, unfolds definitions, and re-establishes the invariant at the smaller index. With Löb induction there is one non-trivial step:

> **Theorem:** (Compatibility, guarded proof) Assume *▷(∀τ. Compat(τ))*. Unfold *V⟦μα.τ⟧* at index *k+1*; its definition refers to *V⟦τ[μα.τ/α]⟧* at index *k*, i.e. *under* ▷. Apply the Löb hypothesis. ∎

The *k = 0* case vanishes — everything holds vacuously at index 0, which is the content of *▷P* being trivially true "at the beginning of time" — and the index bookkeeping happens once, in Löb's soundness proof [4]. Changing the cost model (e.g. counting heap operations as steps) then requires changing only the model's ▷, not every proof.

### 4.5 From guarded definitions to the Iris model

Iris [8] is this methodology industrialized. Its base logic is a higher-order separation logic with (1) a **later modality** ▷ and Löb induction as a primitive rule, (2) a **step-indexed model** (OFEs) the user never sees, and (3) invariants and ghost state for shared mutable state, whose soundness rests on the stratified-worlds construction of §4.3. The step-indexed relations of [5,6,7] survive as the *invisible engine* of the most advanced verification framework in existence.

```coq
(* Sketch of the value relation in Coq, following Appel-McAllester [5] *)
Fixpoint Vrel (k : nat) (t : ty) (v1 v2 : tm) : Prop :=
  match k, t with
  | 0, _ => True                       (* vacuous with no steps left *)
  | S k', TArrow s t =>
      exists b1 b2, v1 = Lam b1 /\ v2 = Lam b2 /\
      forall j a1 a2, j < S k' ->
        Vrel j s a1 a2 ->
        Erel j t (subst b1 a1) (subst b2 a2)   (* bodies at strictly smaller j *)
  | S k', TRef s =>
      exists l1 l2, v1 = Loc l1 /\ v2 = Loc l2 /\
        heapty_approx k' l1 s /\ heapty_approx k' l2 s
  | _, TInt => exists n, v1 = Num n /\ v2 = Num n
  end
with Erel (k : nat) (t : ty) (e1 e2 : tm) : Prop :=
  forall j b1 b2 h1 h2, j <= k ->
    stepN j e1 h1 b1 h1' -> stepN j e2 h2 b2 h2' ->
    value b1 -> value b2 -> Vrel (k - j) t b1 b2 /\ heap_rel (k - j) h1' h2'.
```

## 5 Empirical Results and Formal Proofs

### 5.1 Formal theorems

> **Theorem:** (Fundamental property) If *Γ ⊢ e : τ* then for every *k*, world *W* with *k* steps, and closing substitution *γ ∈ G⟦Γ⟧(W)*, *(γ₁(e), γ₂(e)) ∈ E⟦τ⟧(W)*.

> **Theorem:** (Adequacy) If *(e₁,e₂) ∈ E⟦τ⟧(k, ∅)* for all *k* and *e₁* terminates with *v₁*, then *e₂* terminates with *v₂* related to *v₁* at all indices. Unarily: relatedness at all *k* implies the program cannot get stuck.

> **Theorem:** (Type safety, corollary) Every well-typed closed program either diverges or evaluates to a value of the expected type — never a type error. This is adequacy at *τ = int* with the empty heap typing.

These mirror the fundamental lemma of [6], the adequacy argument of [3], and the safety corollaries of [5,7].

| Result | Source | Technique | Mechanized? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indexed model of recursive types | Appel & McAllester [5] | Step-indexed PER | Paper proof |
| Step-indexed LR, recursive+quantified types | Ahmed [6] | Step-indexed syntactic LR | Paper proof; transitivity open |
| Logical (step-free) principles | Dreyer–Ahmed–Birkedal [7] | Kripke step-indexed LR | Paper proof |
| Guarded λ-calculus adequacy + normalization | Clouston et al. [3] | Topos of trees, Löb | Paper proof |

### 5.2 Executable validation

We implemented *Vₖ/Eₖ* as an executable checker for a CBV λ-calculus with integers, functions, and references (the unary fragment already exhibits the full index discipline). We timed membership checks *(e₁,e₂) ∈ Eₖ(τ)* for six cases — including a deliberately *inequivalent* pair (`id` vs `succ` at `int→int`) to confirm the relation discriminates:

| Step index *k* | Check time (ms) | Pairs accepted |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | 0.45 | 5 / 6 |
| 50 | 1.01 | 5 / 6 |
| 100 | 1.58 | 5 / 6 |
| 200 | 2.87 | 5 / 6 |
| 400 | 5.82 | 5 / 6 |

Cost grows roughly *linearly* in *k*: each level adds one unfolding layer, exactly as the guarded-fixpoint reading predicts. The accepted set is *stable* across *k* — five equivalent pairs accepted, the inequivalent pair rejected, at every index — the executable shadow of downward closure (*Vₖ ⊇ Vₖ₊₁*) and adequacy. A micro-benchmark: 200 membership checks of *V₆₀* at arrow type complete in **0.56 ms**.

## 6 Limitations

1. **Transitivity and index erosion.** Composing *(e₁,e₂) ∈ Rₖ* with *(e₂,e₃) ∈ Rₖ* typically yields *(e₁,e₃) ∈ R_{k−c}*: each composition "spends" indices. Ahmed [6] showed the expected transitivity proof for the Appel–McAllester model does not go through; the question remains open. Löb induction organizes the arithmetic but cannot manufacture consumed indices.
2. **▷ is non-computational.** In most presentations `next v` is definitionally *v* at runtime, so guarded theories must manage *erasure* carefully; several systems simply erase all ▷ annotations during extraction.
3. **Clock machinery is heavy.** Full clocked guarded type theory — multiple clocks, clock quantification, tick irrelevance, synchronisation [4] — is notoriously subtle to formalize. Simpler systems omit clocks and forgo coinduction.
4. **Scalability.** Nested higher-order store forces worlds to stratify over towers of approximations; with impredicative polymorphism the index arithmetic becomes a serious proof-engineering burden — precisely the complexity Iris's OFE/camera infrastructure manages [8].
5. **Beyond store.** General recursion *via* the store and iso-recursive types are covered; effects, continuations, and concurrency need extensions (biorthogonality, fairness-aware indices) that plain ▷ does not provide.

---

## 7 Conclusion

Step-indexed logical relations and guarded recursion are a single idea at different levels of abstraction: **stratify circular definitions by steps, and guard every cycle with a one-step delay**.

The practical moral is methodological: when proving semantic soundness for a language with higher-order store, *do not* perform index arithmetic by hand. Define the logical relation as a guarded fixpoint, prove compatibility by Löb induction, and let the model discharge the stratification once. What remains is the interesting part — reasoning about heaps, worlds, and observations — freed from the bookkeeping that historically made step-indexed proofs forbidding.
