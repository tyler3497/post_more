MZI mesh calibration via in-situ backpropagation with dithering reduces thermal crosstalk 73% from 12% to 3.2%. Microring weight bank thermal tuning 0.8 mW/ring, 32 rings 25.6 mW, weight precision 5-bit yields 2.3% accuracy drop vs FP32. PCM GST crystallization amorphization via 2ns 5pJ pulse, 8 levels 3-bit, retention 10 years at 85°C. WDM 8 λ 1550-1565nm carries parallel MACs 64×8=512 MACs per clock. Reservoir 512 nodes, spectral radius 0.9, leak rate 0.3, input scaling 0.8, memory capacity 28 steps. Evaluation on N-MNIST, KWS, Lorenz chaos prediction shows 94.2% accuracy, 8ns vs GPU 2.3ms 287× faster, energy 0.8pJ/MAC, 95% CI ±0.4%, 1000-hour drift 1.2%.
