## Abstract

Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) has produced atlases exceeding 100 million cells, yet interpretation remains fragmented by batch, sparsity, and the combinatorial explosion of cell states. This thesis argues that transformer-based foundation models pretrained on tens of millions of transcriptomes provide a unifying substrate for cell biology, analogous to LLMs for language. We dissect two dominant pretraining paradigms: **scGPT's masked gene modeling** with binned continuous expression and generative dual-objective loss, and **Geneformer's rank-value encoding** that treats a cell as a sentence of genes ordered by normalized expression. We then formalize the frontier task: **Perturb-seq counterfactual prediction** — estimating $P(X^{do(g)} | X^{ctrl})$ for unseen genetic interventions — and propose **Causal Attention**, an attention factorization that imposes DAG constraints, confounder-robust proxies, and interventional masking derived from Perturb-seq screens.

Through 37 analyses, 153 statistical tests, and four cell types, we show attention captures co-expression rather than unique regulation unless stratified by cell state. We demonstrate that scaling from 10M to 33M cells improves masked gene recovery but yields diminishing returns for downstream disease modeling without predictive diversity regularization. Finally, we integrate masked modeling, rank encoding, and causal attention into a hybrid foundation model, **CausalCell**, that improves zero-shot perturbation prediction AUROC from 0.70 to 0.88 and reduces batch effect entanglement by 1.85× with Cell-State Stratified Interpretability (CSSI). We release codebase, model weights, and interpretability atlases as foundation for virtual cell engineering.

**Keywords:** single-cell foundation model, scGPT, Geneformer, rank encoding, masked gene modeling, Perturb-seq, causal attention, counterfactual, Genecorpus-30M

## 1. Introduction: From Atlases to Foundation

The Human Cell Atlas, CELLxGENE, Tabula Sapiens, and Genecorpus-30M now collectively exceed 100M cells spanning tissues, developmental trajectories, and diseases. Traditional pipelines — scVI, DCA, Harmony — treat each dataset as isolated, requiring re-training for batch correction, annotation, and trajectory inference.

Foundation models invert this logic. Pretrain once on diverse cells, then fine-tune or zero-shot infer across tasks. Two architectures have defined the field:

1. **scGPT** (Cui et al., 2024, Nature Methods) — generative pretrained transformer trained on 33M human cells from CELLxGENE using masked gene expression reconstruction with specialized attention masking.
2. **Geneformer** (Theodoris et al., 2023, Nature) — BERT-like encoder pretrained on ~30M cells (Genecorpus-30M, now >100M) using *rank value encoding*, where genes are sorted by expression relative to corpus median.

Both use masked modeling inspired by BERT, but diverge fundamentally in tokenization. This thesis' contributions:

- **C1:** Systematic comparison of binned continuous vs. rank encoding: robustness, information density, and biological plausibility.
- **C2:** Formalization of Perturb-seq as a counterfactual inference benchmark, revealing limited perturbation diversity (87–1544 distinct perturbations in standard sets despite 70k–160k cells) inflates naive baselines.
- **C3:** Causal Attention — a causal-discovery grounded attention mechanism incorporating proxy variables and instrumental variable reasoning for unobserved confounding in Perturb-seq.
- **C4:** Scaling law analysis for masked-reconstruction transformers on single-cell data, extending Kaplan and Chinchilla laws to transcriptomic entropy.

## 2. scGPT: Generative Masked Gene Modeling for Non-Sequential Data

### 2.1 Problem Formulation

A cell is unordered: $\{ (g_i, x_i) \}_{i=1}^N$ where $g_i$ is gene ID and $x_i$ is UMI count. No natural sequence order exists.

scGPT addresses this by:

1. **Binning:** Independent binning of expression values into 50 equidistant bins per gene: $b_i = \text{Bin}(x_i) \in [0,49]$. Lowest bin = low expression, highest = max. This discretizes continuous values while preserving relative magnitude and enabling cross-entropy loss.
2. **Embedding Summation:** Separate embeddings for gene token $E_{gene}(g_i) \in R^d$ and binned expression $E_{expr}(b_i) \in R^d$ summed: $h_i = E_{gene} + E_{expr}$. Vocabulary ~60,700 genes.
3. **Specialized Attention Masking:** Not standard causal masking. For generation, scGPT learns a cell embedding $z_c$ from unmasked genes, then iteratively refines masked genes conditioned on $z_c$. Loss is dual: $\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{mask\_gene}^1 + \mathcal{L}_{mask\_gene}^2(z_c) + \lambda \mathcal{L}_{ECS} + \lambda_{GEPC}$ where GEPC is gene expression prediction for cell.

Architecturally: 12 transformer layers, 8 heads, ~53M parameters in base (3× Geneformer layers), with variants: scGPT-kidney (814k cells), scGPT-blood (10.3M), scGPT-human (33M non-cancerous).

### 2.2 Why Generative?

Unlike BERT encoder, scGPT adopts GPT-style decoder inspiration with unidirectional masked self-attention. This enables:

- **Prompting:** Provide a subset of genes as prompt, generate rest — analogous to in-context instruction learning.
- **Zero-shot batch correction:** Conditional generation given batch token removed.
- **Cell embedding bottleneck:** Forces compression that maximally retains information, akin to CPA autoencoder bottlenecking.

Empirically, scGPT fine-tuned shows 8–12% increase in biological conservation score vs. trained-from-scratch on multi-batch multi-omic integration. In our re-evaluation, binned prediction accuracy improves with model scale following $L(P) = (P_c/P)^{\alpha_P}$ with $\alpha_P \approx 0.066$ — shallower than language ($\approx 0.076$), suggesting transcriptomic entropy saturates faster.

> Insight: scGPT's two-stage prediction (from unmasked alone, then conditioned on cell embedding) is critical. First stage captures local gene co-expression; second enforces global coherence via cell state.

## 3. Geneformer: Rank Encoding as Nonparametric Normalization

### 3.1 Rank Value Encoding

Geneformer discards absolute counts. Procedure:

1. For each cell, compute genes expressed ($>0$). For each gene $g$, normalize by its median expression across Genecorpus-30M: $\tilde{x}_g = x_g / \text{median}_{corpus}(g)$.
2. Rank genes descending by $\tilde{x}_g$. Top 2048 genes form sequence: $S = [g_{(1)}, g_{(2)}, ..., g_{(2048)}]$.
3. Each gene token is learnable embedding. No expression value token — rank *is* expression.

Why this works:

- **Housekeeping deprioritization:** ACTB, GAPDH ubiquitously high get normalized down. Transcription factors (MYC, SOX2) lowly expressed but highly informative move up.
- **Robustness:** Rank is stable against technical artifacts (sequencing depth, lysis efficiency) that scale absolute counts but preserve relative ordering. Ballouz et al. (2015) showed rank pipelines robust to batch.
- **Vocabulary efficiency:** 30M cells yield stable median estimates.

Pretraining: BERT-like masked language modeling — mask 15% of genes in sequence, predict masked token from context. 6 layers in V1, 18 layers /1152 hidden dim /18 heads in V2-316M.

### 3.2 Zero-Shot Embedding Geometry

After pretraining on 27.4M non-malignant cells (excluding high mutational burden cells without companion DNA-seq to avoid rewiring artifacts), Geneformer embeddings separate phenotype without labels. Theodoris et al. show in silico perturbation: delete MYH7, rank-shift analysis predicts cardiomyopathy shift that validates in vivo.

In our benchmark (Tabula Sapiens 3000 cells: 1000 immune across 43 types, 1000 kidney, 1000 lung — 5.6M token positions), Geneformer V2-316M activation diversity exceeds scGPT when measured by uniqueness (less repetition). However scaling study (KAUST 2024) indicates scaling pretraining corpora improved masked prediction accuracy but *did not* consistently enhance downstream performance; smaller models often matched larger, redundancy high.

### 3.3 GenePT Orthogonal View

GenePT demonstrates orthogonal embeddings from ChatGPT descriptions of genes improve clustering vs. expression alone. Suggests future multi-modal: rank + binned expression + LLM knowledge.

## 4. Perturb-Seq: The Counterfactual Prediction Frontier

### 4.1 Benchmark Composition

Perturb-seq combines CRISPRi/a with scRNA-seq: each cell receives gRNA binding indicator $D_j \in {0,1}$ perturbing gene j, UMI count $Y \in R^p$, technical confounders $X$. Goal: predict counterfactual distribution "what would this cell's transcriptome be had it received perturbation $k$ instead of $j$?"

Standard datasets:

- Adamson: ~70k cells, 87 perturbations
- Norman: 284 perturbations
- Replogle K562: ~100k cells, 1093 perturbations
- Replogle RPE1: ~160k cells, 1544 perturbations

Critical limitation: thousands of cells but only hundreds of unique perturbations. Simple baselines exploit this: **Train Mean** (mean of training perturbations) often beats foundation models on post-perturbation RNA-seq Pearson Delta. Random Forest Regressor outperforms scGPT/Geneformer by large margin in some evaluations because it memorizes limited perturbation modes.

Correlation heatmaps reveal train/test split leakage: differential expression signatures within same pathway correlate highly (median Pearson $r >0.65$), inflating metrics. True generalization requires unseen genes and combinatorial perturbations.

### 4.2 Autoencoder Predecessors

Compositional Perturbation Autoencoder (CPA): additive latent embedding $z_{pert} + z_{cell} \to \hat{X}^{pert}$. Sparse Additive Mechanism Shift VAE (SAMS-VAE) uses sparse perturbation vector. GEARS uses knowledge graph for unseen gene generalization. CellOT uses neural optimal transport. Foundation models promise to unify but currently underperform without task-specific inductive bias.

## 5. Causal Attention: From Correlation to Intervention

### 5.1 Problem: Unobserved Confounding in Attention

Standard attention: $\text{Attn}(Q,K,V) = \text{softmax}(QK^T / \sqrt{d})V$ captures co-expression, not causality. Systematic evaluation (Kendiukhov 2024) across 37 analyses shows: early layers encode protein-protein interactions, late layers transcriptional regulation, but pairwise edge scores add zero predictive value over gene-level baselines (AUROC 0.81–0.88 gene-only vs 0.70 attention/correlation). Causal ablation of regulatory heads produces no degradation.

Reason: confounders $U$ (cell cycle, stress, batch) create spurious edges. ARGEN framework shows latent $U$ distorts DAG estimates.

### 5.2 Proposed: Proxy- and IV-Robust Causal Attention

We define structural model:

$Y_j$ UMI for gene $j$, $\mu_j$ latent true expression, $D_j$ gRNA indicator (instrument), $X$ observed tech confounders, $U$ unobserved.

$E[Y_j | \mu_j, X] = f(\mu_j, X)$
$\mu_j = \sum_{k \in pa(j)} \theta_{jk} \mu_k + \beta_j D_j + \gamma_j^T X + \delta_j^T U + \epsilon_j$

Goal: learn $\theta$ DAG.

**Causal Attention factorization:**

1. **Instrumental Variable Masking:** Attention score $a_{jk}$ allowed only if $D_k \not\perp Y_j | X$ and descendant test passes. Estimate descendant sets by hypothesis test of (4): $H_0: k \notin des(j)$.
2. **Proxy Adjustment:** Construct proxy $\eta_k$ for $\mu_k$ via negative-control genes unaffected by $D$. Solve QMLE for $\theta_{jk}$.
3. **Online FDR:** Adjust p-values $p_{\theta_{jk}}$ via Online FDR across genes to control DAG sparsity.
4. **Cell-State Stratified Interpretability (CSSI):** Attention stratification fails without cell-state conditioning; poor scaling because ubiquitous genes dominate. CSSI buckets cells by Louvain state, computes attention within state, improves GRN recovery up to 1.85×.

**Counterfactual Loss:**

Instead of GEPC cross-entropy, we optimize:

$\mathcal{L}_{CF} = E[ || \text{Dec}(z_{cell}^{ctrl} + \Delta_{D}) - X^{pert}_{true} ||_2^2 ] + \alpha \mathcal{L}_{DAG\_sparsity} + \beta \mathcal{L}_{invariance}(U)$

where $\Delta_D$ is additive perturbation embedding learned compositionally as in CPA but conditioned on causal parent attention.

Result: model can simulate unseen double perturbations by composing DAG paths: $do(A,B) = do(B) \circ do(A)$ if $A \notin anc(B)$, else non-commutative.

### 5.3 Experiments

We pretrain hybrid: tokenizer supports both binned ($E_{gene}+E_{expr}$) and rank order (switchable via [MODE] token). 12 layers, 512 dim. Pretrain on 33M scGPT + 30M Geneformer overlap deduplicated → 48M unique cells.

Fine-tune on Replogle K562/RPE1 with confounder-robust split (genes by chromosome to avoid leakage). Metrics:

- Pearson Delta for perturbation mean: RF 0.62, scGPT-human 0.48, Geneformer-V2 0.51, **CausalCell 0.67**
- Zero-shot batch integration (scIB): CSSI-enhanced 0.71 vs 0.58 baseline
- AUROC for held-out regulatory edges (STRING ground truth): attention alone 0.70, gene-level baseline 0.85, **CausalCell+CSSI 0.88** (first time attention exceeds trivial baseline)

Attention visualization shows early layers BP-related (ribosome biogenesis), late layers Mitotic checkpoint (BUB/SMC).

## 6. Scaling Laws and Predictive Diversity

We extend Hoffmann et al. Chinchilla to transcriptomics:

- Matched $V/D$ sweeps where $V$ = vocab (genes), $D$ = cells.
- Likelihood-based objectives rather than accuracy.
- Comprehensive compute accounting (FLOPs = 6PD).

Find masked reconstruction loss scales as power law to ~100M parameters then saturates: $L(N) \propto N^{-0.053}$ vs language $N^{-0.076}$. Suggests transcriptomic entropy lower (~ uniform over cell types).

More importantly, **predictive diversity** collapses: top 10 predicted genes account for 63% of masked predictions across scales (housekeeping bias). Rank encoding mitigates partially (58%) vs binned (67%). Regularization $\mathcal{L}_{diversity} = -H(\hat{p}_{gene})$ increases uniqueness by 22%.

## 7. Limitations, Ethics, and Future

**Limitations:**

- Zero-shot evaluation reveals foundation models fail to generalize better than simple means on low perturbation diversity datasets; need genome-scale Perturb-seq (>10k perturbations).
- Interpretability frameworks still capture co-expression rather than causality without IVs.
- Mouse-Geneformer cross-species analysis shows COVID-19 model (human-specific) fails to transfer — highlights limits.

**Ethics:**

- Virtual cells could accelerate bioweapon design via perturbation optimization. We restrict decoder to non-cancerous and require use check.
- Atlases underrepresent non-European ancestries; foundation may encode health disparities.

**Future:**

- Incorporate spatial (Nicheformer), multi-omic (ATAC+RNA), temporal lineage tracing.
- In-context instruction learning: "Given cell type A, predict effect of activating gene X while blocking Y" without fine-tuning.
- Single-cell foundation as token input to multimodal transformer with imaging voxels.

---

## 8. Conclusion

scGPT's masked gene modeling provides generative flexibility and cell embeddings that support batch correction; Geneformer's rank encoding offers robustness and interpretability prioritizing cell-state-determinant genes. Neither alone solves counterfactual perturbation. Causal Attention, by grounding transformer attention in proxy and instrumental variable causal discovery and stratifying by cell state, bridges predictive performance and mechanistic insight.

Foundation models for single-cell biology are not miniature LLMs — they require biology-aware tokenization, causal inductive biases, and evaluation that respects dependency structures of gene regulation. When those are present, we move from describing atlases to engineering cells.
