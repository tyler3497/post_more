# The AdS/CFT Correspondence: Entanglement Wedge Reconstruction, the HRT Covariant Proposal, and Holographic Quantum Error-Correcting Codes

## Abstract
The anti-de Sitter / conformal field theory (AdS/CFT) correspondence asserts an exact duality between quantum gravity in asymptotically AdS spacetimes and conformal field theory on the asymptotic boundary. This thesis develops the modern entanglement-based reformulation of the duality. We derive the Ryu–Takayanagi formula for static holographic entanglement entropy and its covariant Hubeny–Rangamani–Takayanagi (HRT) generalization, present the replica-trick derivation via Lewkowycz–Maldacena generalized gravitational entropy, and extend the picture to quantum extremal surfaces, which incorporate bulk entanglement corrections and reproduce the island rule for black hole evaporation. We then analyze subregion duality through the lens of quantum error correction: the Almheiri–Dong–Harlow operator-algebra framework, the HaPPY pentagon tensor-network code, and the Pastawski–Yoshida–Harlow–Preskill toy models. We show how entanglement wedge reconstruction follows from complementary recovery in operator-algebra quantum error correction, discuss the role of relative entropy and the JLMS identity, and close with open problems: covariant islands, backreaction, and the emergence of bulk locality at sub-AdS scales.

##1 Intro
The AdS/CFT correspondence, conjectured by Maldacena in 1997 [1] and sharpened by Gubser, Klebanov, Polyakov [2] and Witten [3], remains the most precise framework for quantum gravity ever formulated. It posits that type IIB string theory on asymptotically AdS(5) × S(5) backgrounds is exactly dual to four-dimensional N = 4 super-Yang–Mills theory on the conformal boundary. The dictionary is *holographic*: a (d + 1)-dimensional gravitational theory is completely encoded in a d-dimensional non-gravitational quantum field theory.

For nearly a decade the dictionary was probed mostly through correlation functions and thermodynamics. The 2006 Ryu–Takayanagi proposal [4] changed the subject: entanglement entropy — a purely quantum-information-theoretic quantity — acquires a *geometric* dual. The entanglement entropy S(A) of a boundary region A equals the area of a bulk minimal surface homologous to A, divided by 4G_N. This is not merely another entry in the dictionary; it suggests that **spacetime geometry itself is built out of entanglement**, a program now known as *entanglement builds geometry* or *it from qubit*.

This thesis pursues three interlocking threads:

1. **Entanglement and geometry**: the Ryu–Takayanagi formula, its covariant HRT extension [5], and the quantum extremal surface prescription [10] that incorporates bulk quantum corrections and yields the island rule for the Page curve.
2. **Proofs**: the Lewkowycz–Maldacena replica derivation [6] of generalized gravitational entropy, and the JLMS relative-entropy identity [11] that implies the entanglement wedge reconstruction theorem.
3. **Error correction**: the Almheiri–Dong–Harlow proposal [7] that bulk reconstruction is quantum error correction, the HaPPY tensor-network realization [8], and the reconciliation of subregion duality with the causal-wedge paradox.

We assume familiarity with quantum field theory, general relativity, and basic quantum information theory (density matrices, von Neumann entropy, completely positive maps).

##2 Background
### The AdS geometry
Anti-de Sitter space AdS(d+1) of radius L is the maximally symmetric Lorentzian manifold of constant negative curvature −1/L². In Poincaré coordinates:

$$ds^2 = \frac{L^2}{z^2} \left( dz^2 + \eta_{\mu\nu} dx^\mu dx^\nu \right), \quad z > 0.$$

The conformal boundary at z → 0 carries a d-dimensional conformal structure; the radial coordinate z is the holographic direction, with small z probing ultraviolet boundary physics and large z probing the deep infrared. The Maldacena decoupling argument [1] considers N coincident D3-branes: at low energies the near-horizon geometry becomes AdS(5) × S(5) while the worldvolume theory flows to N = 4 super-Yang–Mills with gauge group SU(N). The 't Hooft limit — N → ∞ with λ = g_YM²N fixed — corresponds to classical supergravity; λ → ∞ adds quantum-gravity corrections.

### Entanglement entropy
For a bipartite pure state |ψ⟩ on H_A ⊗ H_B, the reduced density matrix ρ_A = Tr_B |ψ⟩⟨ψ| defines the entanglement entropy:

$$S(A) = -\mathrm{Tr} \,\rho_A \log \rho_A.$$

In quantum field theory this quantity is UV-divergent, scaling with the area of the entangling surface ∂A — the *area law*. Strong subadditivity, mutual information monotonicity, and related inequalities constrain any consistent assignment of entropies, and any holographic proposal must reproduce them.

### Early holography of entanglement
Before 2006, entanglement entropy in AdS/CFT was computed for special cases — notably two-dimensional CFTs, where Cardy–Calabrese replica techniques and the AdS(3)/CFT(2) dictionary give S(A) = (c/3) log(ℓ/a) for an interval of length ℓ. The Ryu–Takayanagi formula elevated a coincidence into a universal principle.

> **Theorem (Ryu–Takayanagi, 2006):** Let A be a spatial region of the boundary of asymptotically AdS(d+1) spacetime in a static state. Let γ_A be the codimension-2 bulk minimal surface homologous to A (i.e., there exists a bulk spatial region whose boundary is the union of γ_A and A). Then the entanglement entropy of A is
>
> $$S(A) = \frac{\mathrm{Area}(\gamma_A)}{4 G_N}, \qquad (d+1 \text{-dimensional Newton constant}).$$
>
> All quantities are evaluated on a single static time slice.

The formula generalizes the Bekenstein–Hawking entropy: for A the entire boundary, γ_A wraps the black hole horizon and S(A) = A_horizon/(4G_N).

##3 Methodology
Our approach combines three methods that have structured the literature since 2013:

- **Replica geometry in the bulk**: Following Lewkowycz–Maldacena [6], we compute boundary Rényi entropies S_n = (1/(1−n)) log Tr ρ_A^n by inserting a conical singularity of opening angle 2π/n at the entangling surface, lifting the replica manifold to a bulk solution with a codimension-2 cosmic brane, and analytically continuing n → 1. The minimal-surface condition emerges as the equation of motion of the brane.
- **Operator-algebra quantum error correction**: Following Almheiri–Dong–Harlow [7], we treat the low-energy bulk effective field theory on a fixed background as a *code subspace* of the boundary CFT Hilbert space, and test whether bulk operators are recoverable from boundary subregions using the Knill–Laflamme conditions generalized to operator algebras (Beny–Kempf–Kribs).
- **Tensor-network toy models**: Following HaPPY [8] and subsequent random tensor networks, we discretize the correspondence with perfect-tensor codes on hyperbolic tilings, where RT-like formulas, greedy decoding, and entanglement-wedge reconstruction can be verified by exact combinatorial arguments.

We also make systematic use of the JLMS identity [11]: for bulk and boundary relative entropies within the entanglement wedge, S(ρ||σ) = S_bulk(ρ||σ), which — combined with the Petz recovery map — yields a constructive proof of entanglement wedge reconstruction.

##4 Deep Dive
### The RT formula in AdS(3)/CFT(2)
The simplest nontrivial check is the interval in the vacuum of a two-dimensional CFT. On the boundary, the universal answer is:

$$S_A = \frac{c}{3} \log \frac{\ell}{a},$$

with central charge c and UV cutoff a. In the bulk, the dual state is Poincaré AdS(3). The minimal surface homologous to an interval of length ℓ on the boundary is a spacelike geodesic that dips into the bulk; its regularized length is L_geodesic = 2L log(ℓ/a). Using the Brown–Henneaux relation c = 3L/(2G_N) for AdS(3), the RT formula gives:

$$S_A = \frac{L_\mathrm{geodesic}}{4G_N} = \frac{L}{2G_N} \log \frac{\ell}{a} = \frac{c}{3} \log \frac{\ell}{a},$$

an exact match. The computation generalizes: for a strip or ball in higher dimensions, numerical minimal-surface solutions reproduce field-theoretic expectations, including phase transitions in S(A) as the region size varies (the "entanglement plateaux" of Hubeny).

A compact numerical check of the geodesic-length formula is straightforward:

```python
import numpy as np

def geodesic_length(l, L, a):
    """Regularized length of the RT geodesic in Poincare AdS(3).
    l: boundary interval length, L: AdS radius, a: UV cutoff."""
    return 2.0 * L * np.log(l / a)

def brown_henneaux_c(L, G):
    return 3.0 * L / (2.0 * G)

L, G, a = 1.0, 1.0, 1e-3
for l in (0.5, 1.0, 2.0):
    S_rt = geodesic_length(l, L, a) / (4.0 * G)
    S_cft = brown_henneaux_c(L, G) / 3.0 * np.log(l / a)
    assert abs(S_rt - S_cft) < 1e-9, "RT/CFT mismatch"
print("RT agrees with Cardy-Calabrese for all tested intervals")
```

### The covariant HRT proposal
The RT formula requires a static time slice. Hubeny, Rangamani, and Takayanagi [5] proposed the covariant generalization: replace the minimal surface on a static slice with an *extremal* codimension-2 surface γ_A in the full Lorentzian spacetime, homologous to A, with the additional requirement that γ_A be *causally* related to A appropriately (the homology constraint is refined to a "causal" homology constraint to exclude pathological surfaces). The formula is unchanged in form:

$$S(A) = \frac{\mathrm{Area}(\gamma_A^\mathrm{ext})}{4G_N}.$$

HRT passes decisive tests: it satisfies strong subadditivity in time-dependent backgrounds (checked explicitly in Vaidya collapse geometries modeling quantum quenches), reproduces the growth of entanglement after a global quench, and — in the AdS-Vaidya setting — the extremal surface "reaches" into the black hole interior, probing regions behind the horizon that no static minimal surface could access. The covariant proposal is therefore the first holographic probe of the black hole interior.

### Quantum extremal surfaces and islands
At order G_N^0 the RT/HRT formula is classical. Faulkner, Lewkowycz, and Maldacena [13] computed the leading quantum correction: bulk entanglement across the RT surface contributes S_bulk(Σ_A), where Σ_A is the homology region. Engelhardt and Wall [10] elevated this to a variational principle — the **quantum extremal surface (QES)** prescription:

$$S(A) = \min_{\gamma} \mathrm{ext}_{\gamma} \left[ \frac{\mathrm{Area}(\gamma)}{4G_N} + S_\mathrm{bulk}(\Sigma_\gamma) \right].$$

The minimization is over candidate surfaces; extremization selects them. The QES formula became the key to the black hole information problem: coupled to a bath, an evaporating black hole develops a nontrivial QES — an *island* behind the horizon — whose area term reproduces the Page curve. The radiation entropy first grows (no-island saddle) and then decreases (island saddle), following the minimum. This derivation, developed in 2019 by Penington and by Almheiri, Engelhardt, Marolf, and Maxfield, is the first controlled computation of a unitary Page curve from semiclassical gravity.

### Bulk reconstruction as quantum error correction
The extrapolate dictionary defines boundary operators O from bulk fields φ via lim r^Δ φ = O. Hamilton–Kabat–Lifschytz–Lowe (HKLL) inverted this: a bulk local operator at point x can be written as a smeared integral of boundary operators over the region spacelike-separated from x. But HKLL reconstruction from the *whole* boundary cannot explain a sharper fact: operators deep in the bulk appear reconstructible from boundary *subregions* larger than half the boundary — and the same bulk operator admits inequivalent representations on different subregions.

Almheiri, Dong, and Harlow [7] resolved the tension: bulk reconstruction is **quantum error correction**. The code subspace is the span of states obtained by acting with low-energy bulk operators on the vacuum; the physical Hilbert space is the full CFT. A bulk operator φ(x) is a *logical* operator; its representations on different boundary regions A, B (each containing the entanglement wedge of x) are different *physical* realizations — precisely the redundancy of an erasure-correcting code. The AdS-Rindler reconstruction puzzle — the same bulk operator being reconstructible from either of two overlapping Rindler wedges — is exactly the statement that the code corrects erasure of the complement of each wedge.

The formal condition is operator-algebra quantum error correction. In the exact setting, correctability of erasure of region Ā for the algebra of bulk operators in the entanglement wedge E(A) is equivalent to the RT formula holding on the code subspace with a state-independent area operator — a theorem of Harlow (2017) building on the JLMS identity [11].

### Tensor networks: the HaPPY code
Pastawski, Yoshida, Harlow, and Preskill [8] constructed an explicit toy model: tile the hyperbolic disk with pentagons, place a six-index *perfect tensor* on each tile (five physical legs contracted with neighbors, one logical leg representing the bulk degree of freedom), and leave the dangling boundary legs as the "CFT". Perfect tensors are maximally entangling across every bipartition, which implies:

1. **RT by counting**: the entanglement entropy of a boundary region equals the number of tensor legs cut by the minimal discrete geodesic — an exact combinatorial RT formula.
2. **Greedy reconstruction**: a bulk logical operator can be pushed through the network to any boundary region whose greedy geodesic encloses it — a discrete entanglement wedge.
3. **Complementary recovery**: bulk operators in the wedge of A and in the wedge of its complement are simultaneously recoverable, mirroring the continuum QEC structure.

Random tensor networks (Hayden et al., 2016) later showed these features are generic: random tensors on hyperbolic graphs reproduce the RT formula with high probability, with the bond dimension playing the role of 1/G_N. The network picture makes the slogan literal: *geometry is the entanglement structure of the code*.

A minimal illustration of the greedy reconstruction rule:

```haskell
-- Discrete entanglement wedge via greedy pushing (HaPPY-style)
data Tensor = Tensor { bulk :: Bool, legs :: [Int] } deriving Show

-- A tensor can be "pushed" outward if at least 3 of its 5 legs are known:
-- perfect-tensor property: any 3 legs determine the other 2 + logical leg.
greedyStep :: [Tensor] -> [Int] -> [Int]
greedyStep net known = known ++ newlyReachable
  where
    newlyReachable = [ i | (i, t) <- zip [0..] net
                         , i `notElem` known
                         , length (filter (`elem` known) (legs t)) >= 3 ]

greedyWedge :: [Tensor] -> [Int] -> [Int]
greedyWedge net seed = fixpoint (greedyStep net) seed
  where fixpoint f x = let x' = f x in if x' == x then x else fixpoint f x'
```

### Relative entropy and the JLMS identity
Relative entropy S(ρ||σ) = Tr ρ log ρ − Tr ρ log σ measures distinguishability of states. Jafferis, Lewkowycz, Maldacena, and Suh [11] proved that for states in the code subspace, boundary and bulk relative entropies coincide:

$$S(\rho_A \,\|\, \sigma_A) = S(\rho_{E(A)} \,\|\, \sigma_{E(A)}).$$

Since relative entropy is monotone under quantum channels and equality in monotonicity implies reversibility (Petz), this identity yields a *recovery channel*: there exists a boundary map on region A that reconstructs any bulk operator in the entanglement wedge E(A). This is the modern, information-theoretic proof of entanglement wedge reconstruction — no smearing functions required.

##5 Empirical/Proofs
### The Lewkowycz–Maldacena derivation
The replica trick computes Tr ρ_A^n for integer n by a path integral on an n-sheeted branched cover of the boundary, branched over the entangling surface ∂A. Lewkowycz and Maldacena [6] assumed the dominant bulk saddle preserves the replica Z_n symmetry, so the quotient geometry has a conical singularity of opening angle 2π/n along a codimension-2 surface γ. The bulk action contains the Einstein–Hilbert term plus a cosmic-brane source at γ with tension T_n = (n − 1)/(4nG_N). Varying the brane action gives the minimal-surface equation; differentiating the on-shell action at n = 1 yields:

$$S(A) = -\partial_n \log Z_n \big|_{n=1} = \frac{\mathrm{Area}(\gamma)}{4G_N}.$$

Dong, Lewkowycz, and Rangamani [9] extended the argument to higher-derivative gravity, where the area functional is replaced by the Wald-like entropy functional (the Camps–Dong formula), and to the covariant HRT case by analytic continuation of the replica geometry.

### Consistency checks of RT
Any holographic entropy proposal must satisfy the entropy inequalities. Headrick and Takayanagi proved strong subadditivity from RT by a minimal-surface cut-and-paste argument: for boundary regions A, B, the union and intersection of their minimal surfaces can be rearranged into candidate surfaces for A ∪ B and A ∩ B with no greater total area. Mutual information, Araki–Lieb, and monogamy-type inequalities follow similarly. These proofs use only minimality — which is why the QES prescription, built on extremization plus minimization, inherits them.

### The Page curve from islands
Consider an evaporating black hole in Jackiw–Teitelboim gravity coupled to a non-gravitating bath. The generalized entropy functional for the radiation region R admits two saddles:

| Saddle | Generalized entropy | Dominant epoch |
|---|---|---|
| No-island (Hawking) | S_gen ≈ S_bulk(R), growing | Early times |
| Island | S_gen ≈ Area(QES)/(4G_N) + S_bulk(R ∪ I), decreasing | After the Page time |

The true entropy is the minimum, producing a unitary Page curve [12]. Replica wormholes — Euclidean wormholes connecting the n replicas — supply the gravitational mechanism: they dominate the replica path integral after the Page time and generate the island contribution. This resolves the information paradox at the level of the entropy while leaving the state paradox (the "factorization problem") open.

### Tensor-network verification
In the HaPPY code [8], the RT formula is a theorem, not a conjecture: for a boundary interval, the greedy algorithm finds the minimal cut, and the perfect-tensor property guarantees the entropy equals the cut size times log(bond dimension). Complementary recovery is verified tile by tile. Random tensor networks extend this to approximate isometries, showing the code properties are robust rather than fine-tuned.

A Rust-flavored sketch of the greedy decoder used to verify wedge reconstruction:

```rust
/// Greedy decoding of a holographic tensor network (HaPPY-style).
/// A tensor is "decodable" once 3 of its 5 legs are known (perfect tensor property).
fn greedy_decode(adj: &[Vec<usize>], seed: &[usize]) -> Vec<bool> {
    let n = adj.len();
    let mut known = vec![false; n];
    for &s in seed { known[s] = true; }
    loop {
        let mut changed = false;
        for (i, nbrs) in adj.iter().enumerate() {
            if known[i] { continue; }
            let k = nbrs.iter().filter(|&&j| known[j]).count();
            // perfect 6-index tensor: 3 known legs determine the rest
            if k >= 3 { known[i] = true; changed = true; }
        }
        if !changed { break; }
    }
    known
}
```

##6 Limitations
1. **The code subspace is small.** Operator-algebra QEC theorems apply to the low-energy subspace around a fixed semiclassical background. They say nothing about reconstructing operators that create black holes or change the asymptotic geometry — the "nonlinear" regime where the code itself must change.
2. **State-dependence and the firewall debate.** Reconstructions of the black hole interior (Papadodimas–Raju) are state-dependent, raising questions about whether they define genuine linear operators. The QEC framework accommodates approximate, state-dependent recovery, but a fully satisfactory linear interior operator dictionary remains open.
3. **Covariant islands are poorly understood.** The island rule is best controlled in two-dimensional models (JT gravity) and in static higher-dimensional settings. A general covariant QES prescription for evaporating black holes in higher dimensions, with backreacting quantum fields, does not yet exist.
4. **Derivation assumes the bulk.** The Lewkowycz–Maldacena argument assumes a semiclassical bulk saddle dominates; it derives the RT formula *within* the duality rather than deriving the duality itself. The emergence of the bulk from CFT first principles remains conjectural.
5. **Sub-AdS locality.** Tensor-network models reproduce the RT formula at the AdS scale but do not obviously capture bulk physics at sub-AdS separations; whether MERA-like networks can describe local bulk excitations below the curvature scale is debated.
6. **The factorization problem.** Replica wormholes imply the gravitational path integral computes ensemble-averaged quantities, suggesting the boundary dual of a single evaporating black hole may involve an ensemble of theories — in tension with a single unitary CFT.

##7 Conclusion
The Ryu–Takayanagi formula and its descendants have reorganized our understanding of the AdS/CFT correspondence around a single principle: **entanglement builds geometry, and geometry is protected by quantum error correction**. The RT formula geometrizes entanglement entropy; HRT covariantizes it and probes behind horizons; the quantum extremal surface prescription incorporates bulk quantum effects and derives the Page curve via islands; and the Almheiri–Dong–Harlow framework explains *why* subregion duality is consistent — the boundary theory is a quantum error-correcting code whose logical operators are bulk local fields.

The deepest lesson is the JLMS identity: equality of bulk and boundary relative entropies converts a geometric extremization problem into an information-theoretic recovery theorem. Entanglement wedge reconstruction is not an extra assumption but a consequence of the RT formula itself, applied within the code subspace.

Open frontiers — covariant islands, state-dependent interior reconstruction, sub-AdS locality, and the factorization problem — are not signs of the program's exhaustion but of its youth. Each has already survived contact with exact computation at least once. If the trajectory from RT (2006) through HRT (2007), LM (2013), ADH (2015), and islands (2019) is any guide, the next decade will continue to convert quantum-information theorems into gravitational facts.
