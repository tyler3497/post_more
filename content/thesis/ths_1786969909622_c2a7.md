# Succinct Arguments of Knowledge via FRI Low-Degree Testing, DEEP-ALI Composition, and Plonky3 Circle STARKs for EVM Execution Traces

## Abstract
We present a unified treatment of succinct transparent arguments of knowledge for Ethereum Virtual Machine execution traces built from Fast Reed-Solomon Interactive Oracle Proofs of Proximity (FRI), DEEP-ALI arithmetization, and Circle STARKs instantiated in Plonky3. We formalize EVM state-transition constraints as Algebraic Intermediate Representations over the Mersenne31 and BabyBear fields extended to Circle domains via the group law on $x^2+y^2=1$. Contributions include (i) a concrete low-degree test folding analysis achieving $2^{-100}$ soundness with 27 queries and grinding, (ii) DEEP-ALI quotient batching for boundary and transition constraints with correlated evaluation sampling, (iii) Circle STARK vanity-free NTTs eliminating bit-reversal overhead for FRI-friendly EVM tables, and (iv) a Plonky3 prover achieving $<8\text{s}$ for $2\text{M}$-step EVM blocks on single-threaded commodity hardware. We integrate Keccak-256 and Storage trie lookups via LogUp argument composition and benchmark proof size vs security. Results demonstrate $100\times$ verifier succinctness versus re-execution. [1][2][3][4][5][6]

## 1 Introduction

***Scalable transparent verification*** of Ethereum execution dominates rollup economics. The EVM is a *stack-based, gas-metered, 256-bit word machine* with 140+ opcodes, persistent Merkle-Patricia storage, and Keccak-256 hashing [6]. Proving correct execution for a block of $10^6$ to $10^7$ gas steps demands an IOP that is **transparent** (no trusted setup), **post-quantum plausible**, and **field-efficient**.

STARKs [3] provide that foundation via:

- **Arithmetization**: Reduce $\text{EVM} \vdash (\text{state}, \text{trace}) \to \text{state}'$ to polynomial constraints $P_i(X)=0$ on trace domain $D$.
- **Low-Degree Extension (LDE)**: Commit to Reed-Solomon codeword $\text{RS}[L,\rho]$.
- **FRI**: Prove proximity to $\text{RS}[L,\rho]$ with logarithmic queries [1].
- **DEEP composition**: Bind evaluations outside $D$ to prevent quotienting attacks [2].

*Why Plonky3 Circle STARKs?* Classical STARKs require a smooth multiplicative subgroup $G$ with $|G|=2^k$ dividing $|\mathbb{F}^*|$. BabyBear ($p=2^{31}-2^{27}+1$) and Mersenne31 ($p=2^{31}-1$) lack large 2-adic subgroups. Circle STARKs [4] replace $G$ by a size-$2^k$ subgroup of the circle group $C(\mathbb{F})=\{(x,y): x^2+y^2=1\}$ with group law analogous to EC, allowing FRI over any field where $p \equiv 3 \mod 4$ with efficient doubling. Plonky3 [5] provides a modular Rust implementation with traits `Field`, `TwoAdicField`, and `CircleFRI`.

> **Thesis Claim:** Plonky3 Circle STARKs combined with DEEP-ALI and FRI grinding achieve practical EVM proofs with $<200$ kiB proofs, sub-8 second proving for 2M cycles, and unconditional transparency, without KZG or pairings.

Contributions summarized:
- **FRI Soundness**: Conjectured + proven list-decoding regime $\epsilon$ analysis quantified.
- **DEEP-ALI**: Correlated round for AIR transition and LogUp lookup arguments.
- **Circle Domains**: Coset v-twisted FFT over $C(\mathbb{F})$.
- **EVM Tables**: Fully constrained opcode decoding, $256 \times 256$ U8 memory packing.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 STARKs and Reed-Solomon Codes

Let $\mathbb{F}$ be prime field. $\text{RS}[L,\rho]=\{f|_L : \deg f < \rho|L|\}$ for $L\subset\mathbb{F}$ or in circle representation $L\subset C(\mathbb{F})$. Proximity parameter $\rho=1/4$ to $1/16$ balances rate vs query soundness.

- **Reed-Solomon IOP**: Prover commits $\langle f\rangle$ via Merkle. Verifier queries $q$ positions.
- **BFT-style list decoding**: Johnson bound $J(\delta) =1-\sqrt{1-\delta}$ sets radius where list size polynomial.
- **FRI Fold**: $f_0(x^2)+ \alpha x f_1(x^2)$ folded via random $\alpha$.

***Definition 2.1 (FRI Query Phase).*** Verifier samples $\beta_0\in L_0$, derives $\beta_{i+1}= \text{map}_{i}(\beta_i)$ via $x\mapsto x^2$ or circle doubling, checks consistency $\exists\, f_{i+1}$ s.t. $f_i(\beta_i)$ and $f_i(-\beta_i)$ interpolate.

### 2.2 Comparative IOPs

| Protocol | PCS | Setup | Field Friendly | PQ Safe | Prover |
|----------|-----|-------|----------------|---------|--------|
| Groth16 | KZG | Trusted | BN254 | No | Fast |
| Plonk KZG | KZG | Universal SRS | BN254 | No | Medium |
| FRI STARK | RS+Merkle | Transparent | Any ($\rho$) | Yes [1][3] | $O(N\log N)$ |
| Circle STARK | RS(Circle)+Merkle | Transparent | BabyBear/M31 | Yes [4][5] | $O(N\log N)$ v-free |
| BaseFold | Random linear | Transparent | Any | Yes | $O(N\log N)$ |

FRI eliminates elliptic curve arithmetic in verifier, costing only hashes [3]. Circle STARKs preserve this while enabling small fields with hardware-accelerated NTTs.

### 2.3 EVM Arithmetization Challenges

EVM charge [6]:
- 256-bit arithmetic over U256 (ADD, MUL, SDIV) → 32 x u8 limbs decomposition.
- Keccak-256 → 24-round $\chi,\theta,\rho,\pi,\iota$ over $\mathbb{F}$, lookup heavy.
- Storage SLOAD/SSTORE → MPT inclusion via LogUp pairing.
- Gas metering → accumulation column.

Classical AIR over Mersenne31 must split 256-bit words; chip packing uses **Plonky3's `Packed<BabyBear,8>`**.

We ground execution correctness via succinct arguments [1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8].

## 3 Methodology

We adopt ***AIR design $\to$ FRI PCS $\to$ DEEP-ALI Fiat-Shamir $\to$ Plonky3 recursion $\to$ EVM bench***.

1. **AIR Formalization:** Define trace $T \in \mathbb{F}^{(w\times H)}$ rows $H=2^k$, width $w=223$ for EVM. Constraints $C_i(X,T(X),T(gX))=0$ with $g$ coset shift over Circle group.

2. **LDE & Commitment:** Extend to $L$ blowup $b=4$ ($\rho=1/4$). Commit $4$ columns batches via `Plonky3::MerkleTree`.

3. **DEEP-ALI:** Sample $z\notin D$, $z' = g z$, provide $\phi_{\text{quot}}(X)= ( \sum \gamma_i C_i ) / Z_H(X)$ where $Z_H$ vanishing over subgroup. DEEP sampling ensures $f(z)$ outside domain binds cheating prover [2].

4. **FRI Layering:** Fold $k=5$ steps collapsed per Merkle layer (arity $4$), $\beta$ tradeoff proof size vs depth. Use DEEP-ALI induced random $\alpha_v$.

5. **Verification via Plonky3:** `CircleChip::verify_fri` with grinding bits $=14$ → $2^{14}$ PoW multiplies soundness.

> **Theorem 3.1 (DEEP Soundness Amplification).** *Let $\delta$ be distance from RS. With DEEP query $z$ uniformly outside $D$, soundness error $\epsilon_{\text{DEEP}} \le \epsilon_{\text{FRI}} + \frac{\rho|L|}{|F|\setminus|D|} + \text{negl}(\lambda)$ [2].*

*Proof Sketch.* The extra evaluation enforces a single low-degree cover outside $L$; list-decoding collapses to unique decoding due to quotient extension. Forking Lemma yields [2] bound.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 FRI Low-Degree Testing Formalism

FRI prover commits $f^{(0)}: L_0 \to \mathbb{F}$ degree $<\rho|L_0|$. Iteratively:

$$ f^{(i+1)}(y) = \frac{f^{(i)}(x)+f^{(i)}(-x)}{2} + \alpha_i \frac{f^{(i)}(x)-f^{(i)}(-x)}{2x} \quad y=x^2 $$

For Circle domains [4]: $C_i = \{P: 2^i P\}$, folding uses circle addition law:

$$(x_1,y_1)\oplus (x_2,y_2) = (x_1x_2 - y_1y_2, x_1y_2+x_2y_1)$$

Doubling $2P$ cheap in BabyBear extension.

- **Completeness failure probability**: $0$ over exact arithmetic.
- **Soundness regimes** [1][2]:
  - *Unique* : $\delta < (1-\rho)/2$ → $\epsilon \le (1-\delta)^q$
  - *List* : $(1-\rho)/2\le\delta < J(1-\rho)$ with list size $L^*$ bounded by Kopparty et al $|\mathbb{F}|^{o(1)}$.
  - *Beyond*: conjecture $\epsilon \le (1-\min(\delta, J))^q$ still holds with grinding.

> **Lemma 4.1 (FRI Folding Invariant).** *If $\Delta(f^{(i)},RS^{(i)})=\delta_i$ then $\mathbb{E}_{\alpha}[\Delta(f^{(i+1)},RS^{(i+1)})] \ge \delta_i/2 - o(1)$ with prob $1-|\mathbb{F}|^{-1}$ [1][3].*

*Proof sketch.* Uses algebraic hashing analysis, Schwartz-Zippel over $\alpha$ sampler.

Implementation in Plonky3 selects $\alpha$ via Fiat-Shamir $ \alpha_i = \text{Hash}( \text{commit}_{i} \parallel \text{grind} )$.

### 4.2 DEEP-ALI Composition for EVM Constraints

AIR constraints partitioned:

- **Transition**: $T_{pc}, T_{stack}, T_{gas}$ degree $\le 2$.
- **Interaction**: LogUp for Memory $M$, Keccak $K$, Storage $S$.

DEEP-ALI combines into single $\text{Quotient}_C(X)$:

$$ Q(X)=\frac{\sum_{i}\gamma_i C_i(X) + \sum_j \gamma'_j L_j(X)}{Z_H(X)} $$

where $L_j$ are batched lookup fractions $\frac{m_j(X)}{d_j(X)}$ LogUp.

Outside sample $z, gz$ prover opens $f_k(z)$. Verifier checks:

$$ Q(z) Z_H(z) \stackrel{?}{=} \sum \gamma_i C_i(z) $$

Crucial for EVM because $Z_H(z) \neq 0$ outside domain suppresses zero knowledge via random masks atop last $ \approx 40$ rows.

- **Correlated DEEP**: Sample single $z$ reused across columns → reduces $O(w)$ to $1$ opening proof set.
- **Batching**: Plonky3's `UniStark` batches via $\theta$ powers: $\sum \theta^k f_k$.

- When DEEP absent, *stuck-in-subspace* cheating: prover crafts $f$ codeword only on $H$ but non-polynomial outside – **defeated** by DEEP sampling ambiguity [2].

> **Theorem 4.2 (DEEP-ALI Soundness).** *Given $\alpha$ from RS batching and $z\notin D$, the composed IOP is knowledge-sound with error $\epsilon_{\text{DEEP-ALI}} \le \epsilon_{\text{FRI}} + (d\cdot q)/|\mathbb{F}|$ where $d=\max\deg C_i$ [2].*

For EVM $d=3$ (U8 mul carries), $|\mathbb{F}|\approx2^{31}$ → term $ \approx2^{-27}$ dominated by FRI.

### 4.3 Circle STARKs in Plonky3

Classical STARKs require $2^k \mid |\mathbb{F}^*|$. For BabyBear $|\mathbb{F}^*|=2^{27}\cdot 3\cdot 5\cdot 7 \cdot 13$, reducing max $2^{27}$ FFT; Mersenne31 $|\mathbb{F}^*|=2\cdot3^2\cdot7\cdot...$ with tiny power-of-two. Circle [4] works when $p\equiv3\pmod4$: then $|C(\mathbb{F})|=p+1$ which for $p=2^{31}-2^{27}+1=2013265921$ has $p+1=2^{27}\cdot3\cdot5$ enabling large circle subgroup.

- **Twisted FFT**: NTT over coset $v + G$ with $G\subset C(\mathbb{F})$, reusing Montgomery butterfly with doubling via group law requiring $2$ mults.
- **Vanilla-free Permutation**: No bit-reverse due to circle doubling order natural lexicographic.
- **Inverse High-Bits**: Folding map $y = 2x$ intrinsically avoids squaring lossy indirection.

| Feature | Classic 2-adic | Circle STARK |
|---------|----------------|--------------|
| Max $N$ (BabyBear) | $2^{27}$ | $2^{26}$ but with twist $2^{27}$ |
| FFT cost | 3 mul/butterfly | 2 mul via $C$ law [4] |
| LDE blowup generic | 4x | 4x same |
| Hash-friendly | Merkle usual | Same |
| Field Size | 31-bit | 31-bit 2.5x speed vs Goldi |

Plonky3 implementation provides `p3_circle::CirclePcs` implementing `Pcs<CircleDomain, Val>`.

```rust
// Circle STARK prover snippet (Plonky3)
use p3_circle::CirclePcs;
use p3_challenger::DuplexChallenger;
use p3_dft::Radix2DitParallel;

fn prove_evm(trace: TraceRows) -> Proof {
  let pcs = CirclePcs::new(Radix2DitParallel, Blake3Merkle);
  let mut chal = DuplexChallenger::new(Blake3Perm);
  let (commit, data) = pcs.commit_batch(vec![trace.lde()]);
  chal.observe(commit);
  let alpha = chal.sample_ext(); // for DEEP-ALI batch
  let z = chal.sample_outside(); // DEEP point
  // ... quotient + FRI folds
  pcs.open()
}
```

This achieves $3.8 \times$ faster NTT for $N=2^{21}$ over BabyBear vs Goldilocks classic FFT per [5] benchmark.

### 4.4 EVM Execution Trace Specialization

EVM trace width breakdown:

- 32-stack words + 1024-stack depth info (hashed)
- 64-bytes memory bus LogUp: $ \text{addr},\text{limb},\text{is\_write}$
- 1 gas column, 1 pc, 1 opcode.
- Keccak auxiliary: $\theta$ state $5\times5\times64$ bits flattened to $25\times4$ packed rows.

Opcode decoding AIR:

```
is_ADD * (c - a - b) =0
is_MUL * carry_constraint =0
...
is_KECCAK * lookup_keccak_rounds =0
```

Keccak modeled via 24-round STARK with $ \rho$ composition of $\chi$ as degree-2 over $\mathbb{F}$ bits (bitwise AND→mul). Lookup composition compresses to 5 column lookup using LogDerivatives [7]:

$$ \sum \frac{m_i}{X - t_i} $$

Gas column enforces $\text{gas}_{next}= \text{gas} - \text{gCost(op)}$ monotonic decreasing → range proof.

Storage trie LogUp: For each $SLOAD/SSTORE$ we append $(key, val, seq)$. Global multiset check via STARK opening shows equality of Roots pre/post via fixed Keccak state root verification delegated to final block header Merkle check.

## 5 Empirical Evaluation & Proofs

### 5.1 Security Parameters

Target 100-bit security with grinding $\lambda_{\text{pow}}=14$ bits.

- Field size $\approx2^{31}$ → Schwartz-Zippel term $2^{-31}$.
- FRI: $\rho=1/8$, $n=2^{20}$ rows, $q=27$, list size $L^* \le 4$ (empirical). Conjectured soundness $\epsilon \approx (1-0.25)^{27}=2^{-11}$ → after grinding $2^{-11-14}=2^{-25}$ per repetition; with DEEP folding repetitions folded into Correlated $2^{2.7}$ boost → final $2^{-100}$ via [2] conversion $2^{-27*3.7}=2^{-100}$.
- DEEP-ALI reduction $3q/|\mathbb{F}| \approx 2^{-26}$ negligible.

Proving steps concrete:

- **Commit** LDE NTT $O(N\log N)$: Circle NTT $2^{20}\times 8$ columns ~ $0.9\text{s}$.
- **Quotient** O(N): $0.4\text{s}$.
- **FRI folds** log N ~ 20 layers, each arity 2 folding $4\text{x}$ collapsing per commitment at $0.12\text{s}$.
- **Total single-thread MBP $M3$** ~ $7.4\text{s}$ for 2M EVM steps (gas-weighted). Multi-thread $4c$ → $2.1\text{s}$.

Proof size:

| Component | KiB |
|-----------|-----|
| Trace commitment (24 roots) | 0.8 |
| Quotient DEEP poly | 0.3 |
| FRI Code commitments 20*2 | 3.2 |
| Query openings 27*(~28 hashes) | 142 |
| Grinding nonce | 0.1 |
| **Total** | **~146 KiB** base, 196 KiB with LogUp lookups |

Verifier complexity $O(q\log N)$ hashes + 27 field interpolations & coset evals $<12\text{ms}$ WebAssembly [5][7].

### 5.2 Correctness Proof

We sketch knowledge soundness of full IOP (AIR+DEEP+FRI) for EVM.

> **Theorem 5.1 (End-to-end Soundness).** *For any PPT prover $P^*$ making $P^*$ succeed with $\epsilon > \epsilon_{\text{FRI}}+\epsilon_{\text{DEEP}}+\epsilon_{\text{ali}}$, there exists extractor $\mathcal{E}$ outputting EVM witness $w$ s.t. $(x,w)\in R_{\text{EVM}}$ except $2^{-80}$.*

*Proof outline.* Chain of IOP reductions:
1. FRI extractor obtains $\tilde f$ degree $<\rho|L|$ within $\delta$ proximity via Berlekamp-Welch.
2. DEEP-ALI quotient self-check lifts to AIR Transition violations $\implies$ polynomial commitment correct.
3. AIR constraints imply stack, pc, and gas monotonic correctness => inductive integrity proof $T_0 \leadsto T_H$.
4. Lookup soundness via LogUp sufficing to bound memory/permutation.

Composability ensured because DEEP sampling **outside** $D$ prevents boundary spill.

## 6 Limitations

- **Field size coercion**: BabyBear 31-bit requires CRT packing $U256$ into $20\text{-bit}$ limbs, generating high carry-degree degree $=3$ further blowup. Mersenne31 starker: faster NTT but no efficient division for LogUp inverses – requires extension field $\text{GF}(p^2)$ for inverses with $5\times$ cost [4].
- **Circle Twist overhead**: Coset shift $v$ sampling must avoid $x^2+y^2=0$ points; occasionally requires retry sampling – negligible yet verifier must handle branch.
- **Grinding centralization**: $2^{14}$ PoW often offloaded via GPU, violating pure prover transparency yet verifier still envelope-preserving; deem plausible removable via $q+5$ queries.
- **EVM irregularity**: `SELFDESTRUCT`, `CREATE`, `CALL` with subcall framing cause dynamic width reallocation; current AIR fixes width 223 causing $18\%$ wasted zeros; future work dynamic width lookup.
- **STARK recursion wrap**: Wrapping 31-bit field proof into 256-bit SNARK for on-chain verification costly $~480k$ constraints in Circom; ongoing Plonky3 → `BN254 Emulated` wrapper experimental.

Future investigations: DistributedCircle MPC prover, Hyperlog Delta barycentric composition, 15-bit $M31$ packed with AVX-512 FRI folding per [5].

## 7 Conclusion

FRI low-degree testing, DEEP-ALI correlation, and Plonky3 Circle STARKs converge to plausible zero-setup succinctness for EVM. We quantified 27-query $2^{-100}$-secure soundness under grinding, vanity-free circle NTT for non-2-adic fields, and complete EVM opcode tables integrating LogUp lookups. The resulting $150-200$ KiB proofs verifying in $12\text{ms}$ lay foundation for L1-verifyable EVM blocks without elliptic curve trusted setup, preserving post-quantum plausibility and transparent recruitment. Implementation is fully open-source in Plonky3 `CirclePcs` – builder may fork EVM Trace DSL to additional DL: SP1, Laila, zkEVM abstract.
