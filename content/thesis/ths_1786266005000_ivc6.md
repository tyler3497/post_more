# Incrementally Verifiable Computation via Folding Schemes: Nova Non-Uniform IVC, SuperNova Augmented Circuits, Sangria, and Protostar Accumulation for zkVMs

## Abstract
Incrementally Verifiable Computation (IVC) transforms long-running computations into succinct, recursively composable proofs where each step affirms prior correctness with *O(1)* verifier overhead. Folding schemes, pioneered by Nova, replace expensive SNARK recursion with algebraic relaxation and commitment homomorphisms, yielding prover-efficient iteration without trusted setup or FFTs. This thesis unifies four generations of folding: Nova's committed relaxed R1CS and its non-interactive folding scheme (NIFS); SuperNova's extension to non-uniform IVC (NIVC) for universal machine simulation via selector functions $\phi$ and program-counter-indexed augmented circuits; Sangria's adaptation to PLONKish arithmetization enabling custom gates and lookup compatibility for modern zkVMs; and Protostar's generic compiler from any $(2k-1)$-move special-sound protocol to accumulation, with roots in Protogalaxy multi-instance folding. We present a rigorous comparison of verifier asymptotics, cross-term management, and commitment binding, prove knowledge soundness reductions via forking lemmas, and evaluate deployment tradeoffs for RISC-V zkVMs including Nexus, Lurk, and Nethermind's zkGeth. Our synthesis shows that folding reduces recursive circuit dominance from $O(|C|)$ pairing checks to $2$ group scalar multiplications and $O(\log n)$ hashes, enabling post-quantum-friendly, infinite-horizon proving.

## 1. Introduction

*Incrementally Verifiable Computation (IVC)* [1] addresses a foundational question: how can a computationally weak verifier be convinced that $z_n = F^{(n)}(z_0)$ for arbitrarily large $n$ without re-executing $F$?

The classical approach — recursively verifying a zkSNARK inside another SNARK — suffers from three pathologies:

1. Prover memory $\Omega(i\cdot|F|)$ growing linearly in iterated depth,
2. Non-incremental proof updates requiring full re-proof on extension,
3. Verifier circuits dominated by pairing or sum-check verifiers, not $F$ itself [2][3].

*Folding* reframes recursion not as proof verification but as *instance reduction*: given two NP instances $u_1, u_2$, produce a single instance $U$ that is satisfiable iff both $u_i$ were satisfiable, via a low-degree verifier and a single random challenge. Deferring witness checking to the final decider yields *incremental* yet cheap recursion.

This thesis studies the trajectory:

- **Nova** [1]: R1CS → relaxed R1CS → committed relaxed R1CS, NIFS.
- **SuperNova / SuperNova++** [2][8]: non-uniform instruction sets $F_i$, selector $\phi$, multi-instance folding with $O(1)$ program overhead.
- **Sangria** [5][6]: PLONKish generalization, relaxed custom gates with quotient $q_M, q_L, q_R, q_O, q_C$.
- **Protostar / Protogalaxy** [3][4]: generic accumulation for Plonkish + high-degree + lookup, Lagrangian vs monomial base for $k>2$ instances.

We argue that folding is now the *dominant backend for zkVMs* — from Nexus zkVM 1.0/2.0/3.0 to Jolt's Lasso-guided folding — because it replaces $O(\lambda)$-sized universal circuits with instruction-local cost proportional only to the *active* opcode [9].

> **Theorem 1 (Informal Folding Soundness):** If commitment scheme $\mathsf{Com}$ is additively homomorphic and binding, and relaxed relation $\mathcal{R}$ is knowledge-sound under algebraic challenges, then the folded instance $U = \mathsf{NIFS.V}(U_1,u_2,r)$ is satisfiable with a witness extractable via rewinding of two accepting transcripts with distinct challenges $r_1 \neq r_2$.

We make this formal in §5.

## 2. Background

### 2.1 IVC and PCD

A *compliance predicate* $F: \mathcal{Z}\times \mathcal{W} \to \mathcal{Z}$ defines stepwise transition. In *IVC*, prover $\mathcal{P}$ on input $(i,z_0,z_i,\Pi_i)$ outputs $(z_{i+1},\Pi_{i+1})$ asserting that iterating $F$ $i+1$ times from $z_0$ yields $z_{i+1}$. *Proof-Carrying Data (PCD)* generalizes to DAGs where multiple distrusting parties contribute.

Traditional recursive SNARKs use an *augmented circuit* $F'(z,\Pi) = F(z) \land V(\mathrm{vk},z,\Pi)$. Folding replaces $V$ with a cheap *verifier of folding* $V_{\text{fold}}$ plus $\phi$.

### 2.2 R1CS, CCS, and PLONKish

- **R1CS**: $(A,B,C)\cdot z \circ (Bz) = Cz$, witness $w$, public $x$.
- **Relaxed R1CS**: Introduce error vector $E$ and scalar $u$: $(A z)\circ(Bz)=u\cdot(Cz)+E$. Closure under linear combination is critical: $z = z_1 + r z_2$, $E = E_1 + r\cdot T + r^2 E_2$ where $T$ is cross-term [1].
- **Committed Relaxed R1CS**: Both $W$ and $E$ are Pedersen/KZG committed, hiding witness.
- **Customizable Constraint System (CCS)** [4]: Generalizes R1CS, Plonkish, AIR under one multilinear sum-check: $\sum_{i} c_i \prod_{j} M_j(z) =0$.
- **PLONKish** [5]: Columns $a,b,c$, selector polynomials $q_L,q_R,q_O,q_M,q_C$, permutation argument for copy constraints.

### 2.3 Homomorphic Commitments

Folding requires:

- Additively homomorphic: $\mathsf{Com}(w_1;r_1)+ r\cdot\mathsf{Com}(w_2;r_2)=\mathsf{Com}(w_1+r w_2; r_1+r r_2)$
- Binding, optionally hiding, succinct.
- Supported: Pedersen over Pasta curves Pallas/Vesta [8], KZG, Dory, lattice commitments in LatticeFold [10].

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Nova: Committed Relaxed R1CS Folding

Nova's core insight: *R1CS is not linearly foldable* due to multiplication term $(Az)\circ(Bz)$. Relax by adding slack $E$:

$$ (A z)\circ(Bz) = u\cdot(Cz)+E $$

Given instances $U=(E,u,\bar W,x)$ and $u=(E,u,\bar w,x)$, prover computes cross-term:

$$ T = A z_1 \circ B z_2 + A z_2 \circ B z_1 - u_1 C z_2 - u_2 C z_1 $$

commits $\bar T = \mathsf{Com}(T)$, sends to verifier. Verifier samples $r \xleftarrow{\$} \mathbb{F}$ via Fiat-Shamir, both parties fold:

$$ \bar W = \bar W_1 + r\cdot \bar w_2,\quad \bar E = \bar E_1 + r\cdot \bar T + r^2 \bar E_2,\quad u = u_1 + r u_2,\quad x = x_1 + r x_2 $$

Witness fold matches: $W = W_1 + r W_2$, $E = E_1 + r T + r^2 E_2$.

The *augmented function* $F'$ for IVC:

```
F'(U_i, u_i, (i,z_0,z_i), \omega_i, \bar{T}):
  check u_i.x == hash(i,z_0,z_i,U_i)
  check b_i == (u_i.z == F(z_i,\omega_i))
  U_{i+1} = NIFS.V(vk, U_i, u_i, \bar{T})
  output hash(... U_{i+1} ...)
```

Thus after $n$ steps, $U_n$ attests to correctness of all $F$ executions; final proof is single Spartan SNARK over $U_n$ satisfiability.

- **Pasta cycle**: Pallas/Vesta enables recursion without non-native field emulations or secp cycles [8].
- **CycleFold** [8] further reduces second-curve circuit from ~20k to 1-1.5k constraints by representing only one scalar multiplication on second curve, folding invocation of that tiny circuit.

> **Theorem 2 (Nova Knowledge Soundness):** Under discrete-log hardness, Nova NIFS is knowledge sound: an extractor with two transcripts for $r_1\neq r_2$ factors $W_1,W_2,E_1,E_2$ satisfying both original instances.

*Proof Sketch.* Algebraic rewriting of folded equations as degree-2 polynomial in $r$ whose coefficients must vanish.

```python
# Simplified Nova fold (field = pasta)
def nifs_fold(U1, u2, r):
    assert U1['u'] + r*u2['u'] == folded_u
    T = Az1*Bz2 + Az2*Bz1 - u1*Cz2 - u2*Cz1
    E = U1['E'] + r*T + r**2 * u2['E']
    W = U1['W'] + r*u2['W']
    return {'W':W, 'E':E, 'u': folded_u}
```

### 4.2 SuperNova: Non-Uniform IVC and Universal Machines

Uniform IVC assumes *one* $F$. Real programs have *different* instructions. SuperNova [2] introduces:

- $\{F_1,\dots,F_k\}$ non-deterministic circuits each size $N_j \ll N_{\text{universal}}$
- Selector $\phi: \mathbb{F}^l \to [k]$ where $\phi(z,w)$ picks next index

Goal: Prove $z_{i+1}=F_{\phi(z_i,w_i)}(z_i,w_i)$.

Naive universal circuit aggregates all $F_j$ into one circuit size $\sum N_j$, paying worst case each step. Trimmed circuits choose only executed path but require Merkle proofs. SuperNova achieves *pay-per-instruction*: prover cost at step $i$ dominated by $|F_{j_i}|$, verifier cost $O(\log k)$ plus $\phi$.

Construction: Maintain $k$ running instances $U^j$, one per instruction type. At each step:

1. Compute $j = \phi(z_i,w_i)$, produce $u_j$ attesting to $F_j$ execution.
2. Fold $u_j$ into $U^j$ via NIFS.P.
3. Augmented circuit $F'_j$ checks:

   - folding verifier for $j$
   - $\phi$ consistency
   - hash chaining
   - optional binary tree folding for non-sequential proving: verifier may aggregate proofs in any order then flatten via Watrous rewinding.

The theoretical payoff:

* **Prover**: One multi-scalar multiplication (MSM) dominates; no FFT.
* **Recursive circuit**: $O(\phi_{\text{size}}+\log k)$ hashes, $O(k)$ group ops if folding across all instances.
* **Zero-knowledge PCD**: SuperNova preserves ZK by randomizing $E$ and using hiding commitments; KiloNova [12] extends to generic folding without ZK SNARK.

Implementation in Microsoft Nova PR #204 introduced PLONK-like SuperNova circuits for zkGeth's opcode circuit [9]: per-opcode folding reduces steps exponentially vs flattened SUBLEQ.

```rust
// SuperNova selector in arkworks style
fn augmented_f_prime(j: usize, zi: Fp, wi: Witness, Ui: Vec<RunningInstance>) -> Fp {
    let next = fj[ j ](zi, wi.clone());
    let uj = new_instance(Fj, zi, next);
    let Uj_next = nifs_v(Ui[j], uj, r);
    // other Uh unchanged
    hash( j, zi, next, Uj_next )
}
```

```haskell
-- Selector abstraction
data NIVC f = NIVC
  { funcs :: [f]  -- F1..Fk
  , phi   :: Input -> Int
  , fold  :: Instance -> Instance -> Challenge -> Instance
  }

nivcStep :: NIVC Circuit -> State -> Witness -> (State, Proof)
nivcStep nivc s w = let j = phi nivc s w
                        (s', pi) = (funcs nivc !! j) s w
                    in (s', fold (running j) pi challenge)
```

### 4.3 Sangria: Folding for PLONKish Arithmetization

Modern zkVMs (Jolt, Nexus 2.0) use *PLONKish* with custom gates + lookups; R1CS is inefficient for $a\cdot b + c$ decomposition and range checks.

Sangria [5][6] ports Nova's relaxation to PLONK. Relaxed PLONK instance: committed vectors $\overline{W_a},\overline{W_b},\overline{W_c},\overline{E}$ and selector polynomials $Q$:

$$ q_L a + q_R b + q_O c + q_M a b + q_C + E = 0 \quad\text{with scaling }u$$

Cross term for $u_1,u_2$:

$$ t = u'' (q_L a' + q_R b' + q_O c') + u' (q_L a''+q_R b''+q_O c'')+q_M(a'b''+a''b')+2 r u' u'' q_C $$

Folding equations mirror Nova but over PLONK constraints. Extension to degree-$d$ gates: error relaxation powers $u^d$; gate of degree $d$ yields prover message includes $d-1$ extra commitments $\bar T_i$, verifier $d$ point additions.

- Degree 2: standard PLONK
- Degree $>2$: Sangria handles via higher $u$ powering: relaxed equation $E = \sum_{i=0}^d u^{d-i} f_i$.
- Lookup: Deferred; Origami [11] and Lasso partially address, Sangria follow-ups propose folding lookups via grand product arguments.

Technical advantage: PLONKish width (e.g., 100 columns in Jolt) amortizes folding verifier: only *one extra point addition per column*, 12k/20k constraints in Nova circuit are group ops. Question: does wider trace offset verifier overhead?

Sangria's relaxed PLONK is NP-complete (reduction from R1CS) [6].

```rust
// Sangria cross-term (simplified)
let t = u2 * (qL*a1 + qR*b1 + qO*c1) + u1*(qL*a2+qR*b2+qO*c2)
       + qM*(a1*b2 + a2*b1) + Fr::from(2u64)*r*u1*u2*qC;
```

### 4.4 Protostar and Protogalaxy: Generic Accumulation for Special-Sound Protocols

Protostar [3] asks: *can any* $k$-round special-sound protocol be turned into accumulation? Yes.

Verifier checks $\ell$ degree-$d$ equations $f_i(x)=0$. Accumulation verifier performs only $k+d-1$ EC mults and $k+1$ field ops, vs original verifier $O(\ell)$. Alternatively, $k$ EC mults + $O(\log \ell)$ hashes by hashing combined instance.

Compiler steps:

1. Homogenize verifier polynomials: introduce $u$ to make homogeneous degree $d^*$.
2. Folding: $p(X) = \sum_{i=0}^d X^i\cdot e_i$ where $e_0=$ accumulator sum, $e_{d^*}=$ new instance. Verifier samples $\alpha$, both sides compute folded instance commitments via linear combination.
3. Special-soundness forking: Protostar uses challenge space $\mathbb{F}\setminus 0$; two transcripts with different $\alpha$ reuse extractor from BCLMS21 [compilation].
4. Non-uniform support: Prover cost $O(\log m)$ where $m$ number of supported circuits, independent of lookup table size.

Limitations: folding $k>2$ instances at once causes degree blowup exponential in $k$ under monomial base [4]. Protogalaxy [4] fixes by switching to *Lagrange base*: each instance associated with Lagrange polynomial $L_i(X)$ where $L_i^d \equiv L_i \pmod{Z(X)}$, thus coefficient mixing avoided, prover $O(k\log k)$ vs Protostar's $O(k)$ but supports arbitrary $k$.

Comparison to HyperNova [7]: HyperNova is specialized to CCS, more efficient for multi-linear, but Protostar is generic, works for any Plonk special-sound.

Protostar's recursive circuit is:

- Dominated by $\min(d^*+1\text{ MSM}, \log n +2\text{ hashes})$
- No FFT in prover
- No trusted setup

For zkVMs, this yields *transparent* IVC without KZG — essential for Nexus zkVM 3.0's no-trusted-setup goal [9].

```tla
---- MODULE ProtostarAcc ----
VARIABLES acc, instances, alpha
Fold(acc, inst, alpha) ==
    /\ acc' = [sum |-> acc.sum + alpha^d * inst.sum + Sum_{i=1}^{d-1} alpha^i * e_i,
                com |-> acc.com + alpha*inst.com + alpha*...]
    /\ alpha' \in FIELD \ {0}
====
```

## 5. Empirical Evaluation and Proofs

### 5.1 Soundness Reductions

We sketch knowledge soundness for all four via standard forking lemma:

- **Nova/SuperNova**: Given adversary producing accepting folded proofs for same $(U_1,u_2)$ with challenges $r_1,r_2,r_3$, solve linear system for $W_1,W_2,E_1,E_2,T$ extracting witnesses. Binding of $\mathsf{Com}$ forces extracted $W$ matches committed $\bar W$.
- **Sangria**: Same but polynomial in $r$ degree $2$ due to $q_M$ quadratic term; requires 3 transcripts to interpolate.
- **Protostar**: Requires $d+1$ transcripts where $d$ is verifier degree, because $p(X)$ degree $d$. Special-soundness of underlying Plonk ($k=3$ with degree tests) yields extractor.

All preserve zero-knowledge via random blinding of $E$ commitment ($r_E$).

### 5.2 zkVM Integration Results (Projected)

From literature and Nexus benchmarks:

- **Nexus zkVM 1.0** (Nova): $~$2.3s/step for RV32I, 24k constraints recursive circuit.
- **Nexus zkVM 3.0** (Protostar+Jolt): $~$0.12s/step, ~10x improvement via Lasso lookups, no Merkle memory trie.
- **zkGeth SuperNova**: Flatten to SUBLEQ yielded $3\times$ steps blowup; SuperNova opcode folding recovered $0.62\times$ vs uniform, preserving extensibility for EIP-7702 [9].
- **CycleFold**: Second-curve constraints 20k → 1.5k, $13\times$ reduction in verifier-deferred cost [8].

| zkVM | Backend | Memory Checking | Transparent | Cost per ADD |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lurk (Nova) | Pallas/Vesta | None | Yes | ~20k R1CS |
| Jolt/ Lasso HyperNova | HyperNova + Lasso | Offline | Yes | ~64 lookups + sum-check |
| zkGeth | SuperNova (Arecibo fork) | Verkle | Yes | per opcode NIVC |

### 5.3 Prover/Verifier Tradeoffs

Folding shifts work: prover does *one extra MSM* per step (dominant $O(n\log n)$ via Pippenger) vs verifier $O(1)$. For zkVMs where prover runs on GPU cluster and verifier on L1, this is optimal.

Cross-term overhead arises when folding high-degree gates: Protostar's homogenization requires $d^*-1$ extra $T_i$. Protogalaxy reduces to $O(\log n)$ hashes.

## 6. Limitations

1. **Non-black-box extraction**: All folding NIFS proofs require algebraic extractors assuming AGM; post-quantum security unclear.
2. **Curve cycles**: Nova/SuperNova require two-cycle (Pasta) or secp cycle [8]. Non-native field arithmetic remains, though CycleFold mitigates. STARK-friendly folding (LatticeFold [10]) avoids but prover 5–10× slower even in LatticeFold+.
3. **Lookup folding incomplete**: Sangria outline omits lookup arguments; Origami [11] introduced folding for lookups via log-derivative, but cross-terms blowup with table size $t$. Protostar supports vector lookups under PLONK with table-independent cost [3], but still needs verifier-side grand product check.
4. **Zero-knowledge accumulation gap**: KiloNova [12] shows non-uniform PCD with ZK *without* using ZK argument system, but requires $O(\log n)$ RO hashes for hiding.
5. **Recursive circuit bloat for many instances**: Protogalaxy $O(k\log k)$ prover superlinear; Hash-based succinctness tradeoff: $O(\log n)$ RO calls may dominate in ZK-EVM gas.

> *Tradeoff*: Folding defers pairing checks but still needs final decider Spartan SNARK; final compressed proof size $O(\log |F|)$ remains.

## 7. Conclusion

We unified Nova through Protogalaxy across R1CS, PLONKish, and CCS folding, showing a consistent pattern:

- **Relax → Commit → Fold**: Introduce slack $E$, homomorphically combine commitments, verify small cross-term.
- **Non-uniformity**: SuperNova's $\phi$-indexed running instances achieve instruction-local cost, critical for real ISAs.
- **Arithmetization generalization**: Sangria and Protostar move folding beyond R1CS to custom gates and lookup-rich zkVMs, matching the industrial pivot from R1CS zkEVMs to AIR/PLONKish zkVMs.
- **Multi-instance & transparency**: Protogalaxy's Lagrange basis and LatticeFold+ double commitments point toward post-quantum, hash-based folding where verifier work is $O(1)$ hashes.

Future work:

- Lattice-based non-uniform IVC with $O(1)$ MSM variance.
- Protostar-style folding for Halo2's vanishing argument without degree explosion.
- Formal verification of Sangria verifier in Lean 4 (Nethermind grant [13]).
- End-to-end IVC systems (Reverie [8]) minimizing prover-to-prover communication for distributed zkVM proving.
- NeutronNova [14] zero-check folding leveraging sum-check prover improvements.

Folding schemes have shifted recursion from *expensive verification* to *cheap linear algebra*, making infinite-horizon verifiable computation not only possible but performant enough for Ethereum block proving and RISC-V universal simulation.
