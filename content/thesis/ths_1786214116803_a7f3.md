# Continual Reinforcement Learning via Elastic Weight Consolidation, Progressive Neural Architectures, and World Model Transfer for Sim-to-Real Policy Generalization

*Author: anon#7712 | ts: 1786214116803 | Domain: Continual RL, Model-Based RL, Sim-to-Real*

## Abstract

Continual reinforcement learning must learn sequential tasks without catastrophic forgetting while remaining sample-efficient enough for real-world deployment. This thesis unifies three complementary pillars: ***regularization-based consolidation*** via Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC), ***architectural expansion*** via Progressive Neural Networks (PNNs) and dynamically expandable variants, and ***generative world model transfer*** via DreamerV3's Recurrent State-Space Model for sim-to-real. We formalize EWC as approximate Bayesian Laplace propagation with Fisher-guided quadratic penalties, analyze PNN lateral connections as constructive forward transfer that eliminates forgetting by design, and demonstrate how latent imagination in Dreamer enables policy optimization decoupled from environment stochasticity. Integrating Continual Domain Randomization, we show that sequential randomization of physics parameters with EWC retention matches or outperforms joint randomization while dramatically reducing curriculum difficulty. Our synthesis yields a practical recipe for lifelong robotic agents that dream in simulation and persist in reality.

*Keywords: continual reinforcement learning, catastrophic forgetting, EWC, progressive networks, world models, DreamerV3, RSSM, sim-to-real*

## 1 Introduction

> *Intelligence is not learning one task perfectly, but never having to re-learn it.*

Standard reinforcement learning assumes a stationary Markov decision process $\mathcal{M} = \langle \mathcal{S}, \mathcal{A}, P, R, \gamma \rangle$ and interleaves data collection and optimization within it. Yet real deployments — a quadruped traversing seasons, a manipulator encountering new objects, a quadrotor facing wind variations — are *non-stationary sequences* $\mathcal{M}_1, \mathcal{M}_2, ..., \mathcal{M}_T$. Naively finetuning $\theta_{t-1} \to \theta_t$ on $\mathcal{M}_t$ via stochastic gradient descent collapses performance on $\{\mathcal{M}_{<t}\}$: ***catastrophic forgetting***.

Two families have dominated mitigation: ***regularization*** that protects important weights, and ***architecture*** that allocates new capacity. A third paradigm has recently overtaken both in sample efficiency: ***world models*** that learn $p(s_{t+1}, r_t | s_t, a_t, o_{\le t})$ and train actors in *imagination*. Sim-to-real transfer then becomes world model generalization, not merely policy robustness.

This thesis asks:

- How does Bayesian curvature estimation in EWC approximate the true posterior $p(\theta | \mathcal{D}_{1:t})$ and where does it fail in RL with non-i.i.d. replay?
- Why do Progressive Networks yield positive forward transfer on Atari with orthogonal or even adversarial task pairs where baselines suffer negative transfer?
- Why does DreamerV3 generalize from SSL pretraining variability to real-world quadrotor navigation through 0.67m gaps after only 2.5s of real sensory input?
- How does Continual Domain Randomization unify all three for sim-to-real?

**Contributions:**

1. Extended derivation of EWC-DR correcting double-counting in multi-task Fisher accumulation, with RL-specific diagonal Fisher implementation.
2. Formal definition of PNN forward transfer efficiency $\tau_{k\to k+1}$ and scaling law with columns.
3. DreamerV3 RSSM decomposition and proof of latent consistency enabling Dropout's Dream Land domain randomization inside dreamed environments.
4. Integration architecture CDR + EWC + World Model validated on robotic reaching/grasping with real robot results outperforming joint DR [10][11].

## 2 Background and Related Work

### 2.1 Catastrophic Forgetting in RL

In supervised CL, $\mathcal{D}_i$ are disjoint class sets. In RL, distribution shift originates from policy-induced state visitation shift, non-stationary rewards, and dynamics change. Kirkpatrick et al. [1][6] observed that SGD finds $\theta_A^*$ for task A, then SGD on task B moves along low-error valley of B but high-error for A. Bayesian view: $p(\theta | \mathcal{D}_A, \mathcal{D}_B) \propto p(\mathcal{D}_B|\theta)p(\theta|\mathcal{D}_A)$. Since $p(\theta|\mathcal{D}_A)$ intractable, Laplace approximation around $\theta_A^*$ yields Gaussian with precision $F_A$, Fisher Information Matrix.

> **Theorem (EWC Approximate Posterior):** *Let $L_A(\theta) = -\log p(\mathcal{D}_A|\theta)$. After task A, $p(\theta|\mathcal{D}_A) \approx \mathcal{N}(\theta_A^*, F_A^{-1})$ where $F_A = \mathbb{E}_{x\sim\mathcal{D}_A}[\nabla \log p(x|\theta)\nabla\log p(x|\theta)^\top]_{\theta_A^*}$. Then learning B via $L_B(\theta)+\frac{\lambda}{2}\sum_i F_{A,i}(\theta_i-\theta_{A,i}^*)^2$ approximates joint inference on A∪B.*

Huszár extended to $T>2$ and noted penalty double-counting if $F$ summed naively [2][3].

### 2.2 Progressive Neural Networks

Rusu et al. proposed PNNs to eliminate forgetting by construction [7][8]. Column $k$ has parameters $\Theta^{(k)}$ frozen after convergence on task $k$. Column $k+1$ introduces lateral adapters $U^{(k+1:j)}: h^{(j)}_{i-1} \to h^{(k+1)}_i$:

$$ h^{(k)}_i = f\left(W^{(k)}_i h^{(k)}_{i-1} + \sum_{j<k} U^{(k:j)}_i h^{(j)}_{i-1}\right) $$

where $f=\text{ReLU}$. This enables forward transfer but with linear capacity growth $O(K^2)$ adapters. Advantages: no forgetting, robustness to adverse transfer — PNN automatically learns to ignore harmful lateral features via adapter magnitude $||U||\approx0$.

Limitations addressed by dynamically expandable networks [9]: adding only part of network, selecting most similar prior tower via Fisher embedding distance, yielding tree-like allocation.

### 2.3 World Models and Dreamer Family

World models originate from Ha & Schmidhuber: Variational Autoencoder for observation compression + MDN-RNN for dynamics. DreamerV1/V2/V3 by Hafner et al. crystallized into RSSM [12][13]:

- Deterministic recurrent $h_t = f_\phi(h_{t-1}, z_{t-1}, a_{t-1})$ (GRU/Transformer)
- Stochastic discrete $z_t \sim q_\phi(z_t|h_t,o_t)$ posterior, $\hat{z}_t \sim p_\phi(\hat{z}_t|h_t)$ prior
- Decoders $\hat{o}_t,\hat{r}_t,\hat{c}_t \sim p_\phi(\cdot|h_t,z_t)$

Policy $\pi(a_t| s_t)$ where $s_t=(h_t,z_t)$ trained via imagined rollouts $H=15$ with actor-critic $\lambda$-returns, no observation needed during imagination — hence *dreaming to simulate infinitely*.

DayDreamer demonstrated Dreamer learns quadruped locomotion from scratch in 1 hour real-world, no simulator, and adapts to pushes within 10 minutes [5][14].

Dropout's Dream Land [4] shows world model trained with dropout creates infinite dream variations: $p_{drop}(o'|o)$ yields domain-randomized dreams for free, bridging Dream2Real gap without explicit simulator randomization parameter tuning.

## 3 Methodology: Unified Continual World Model Architecture

### 3.1 Problem Formulation

Sequence of MDPs $\mathcal{M}_k = \langle \mathcal{S}, \mathcal{A}, P_k, R_k, \gamma, \mu_0^k\rangle$ where $P_k = P_{\xi_k}$ parametrized by domain randomization vector $\xi\sim \Xi_k$ (friction, mass, latency, textures). Agent observes $o_t$ (pixels/proprioception). Objective lifelong return:

$$ J_{CL} = \frac1T\sum_{k=1}^T \mathbb{E}_{\pi_T}[\sum_{t} \gamma^t r^k_t] \text{ s.t. } \forall k, \mathbb{E}_{\pi_T} \ge (1-\epsilon)\mathbb{E}_{\pi_k^*} $$

Retaining near-optimal.

### 3.2 EWC for Value-Guided Consolidation

We implement EWC-DR for DQN/SAC critics [1]:

$$ \mathcal{L}_{EWC} = \mathcal{L}_{TD}(\theta) + \frac{\lambda}{2}\sum_{i}\tilde{F}^{(t-1)}_i(\theta_i-\theta^{(t-1)}_{*,i})^2 $$

where $\tilde{F}^{(t-1)} = \alpha F_{t-1} + (1-\alpha)\tilde{F}^{(t-2)}$ exponential moving average prevents double count. Fisher diagonal estimated from $N=5000$ replay samples:

```python
def compute_fisher_diag(q_network, replay_buffer, num_samples=5000):
    fisher = {n: torch.zeros_like(p) for n,p in q_network.named_parameters()}
    for _ in range(num_samples):
        s,a,r,s2 = replay_buffer.sample(1)
        q = q_network(s).gather(1, a.unsqueeze(1)).squeeze()
        logp = torch.log_softmax(q_network(s), dim=-1)
        # Fisher = E[grad log p * grad log p^T]
        q_network.zero_grad()
        logp[0,a].backward(retain_graph=True)
        for n,p in q_network.named_parameters():
            if p.grad is not None:
                fisher[n] += p.grad.data ** 2 / num_samples
    return fisher

def ewc_loss(new_params, old_params, fisher, lam=5000):
    loss = 0.0
    for n in new_params:
        loss += (fisher[n] * (new_params[n]-old_params[n])**2).sum()
    return lam/2 * loss
```

Critical RL nuance: *when to compute Fisher*? After task convergence but before distribution shift due to exploration reset. We snapshot target network at $90\%$ of expected return.

### 3.3 Progressive Expansion with Gating

PNN implementation for SAC policy with $L=3$ layers $256$ units:

$$ a^{(k)} \sim \pi^{(k)}(\cdot | s, \{h^{(j)}\}_{j<k}) $$

Adapter $U^{(k:j)}_i \in \mathbb{R}^{n_i \times n_{i-1}}$ initialized near-zero to preserve early training stability. Scaling $O(K^2)$ mitigated by top-$m$ lateral pruning: keep only most beneficial $j$ via transfer score $S_{j\to k}= \text{CKA}(h^{(j)}_{last}, h^{(k)}_{first})$.

During evaluation task agnostic, oracle $k$ known (task-aware continual RL) or inferred via autoencoder gate [8] ensemble: $p(k|o)\propto \exp(-||o - AE_k(o)||^2)$.

### 3.4 World Model Continual Pretraining

DreamerV3 world model pretrained sequentially on $\Xi_1,...,\Xi_T$ with EWC on dynamics predictor only (not encoder) to preserve representation plasticity:

$$ \mathcal{L}_{WM} = \mathcal{L}_{recon}+\mathcal{L}_{KL}+\mathcal{L}_{rew}+\mathcal{L}_{cont} + \beta_{EWC}\mathcal{L}_{EWC-dyn} $$

where $\mathcal{L}_{KL}= D_{KL}[q_\phi(z_t|h_t,o_t)||p_\phi(\hat{z}_t|h_t)]$ balanced.

RSSM implementation skel:

```python
class RSSM(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, deter=512, stoch=32, classes=32):
        self.gru = nn.GRUCell(deter+stoch*classes, deter)
        self.prior = nn.Linear(deter, stoch*classes)
        self.post = nn.Linear(deter+1024, stoch*classes)
        self.img_dec = nn.ConvTranspose2d(deter+stoch*classes, 3, 4, stride=2)
        self.reward = nn.Linear(deter+stoch*classes, 1)
        self.cont = nn.Linear(deter+stoch*classes, 1)

    def dynamics(self, h_prev, z_prev, a):
        h = self.gru(torch.cat([z_prev, a], -1), h_prev)
        logits = self.prior(h)  # discrete logits [B, stoch*classes]
        return h, logits

    def imagine(self, h, z, policy, H=15):
        traj = []
        for _ in range(H):
            a = policy(h,z)
            h, z_logits = self.dynamics(h, z, a)
            z = gumbel_softmax(z_logits)
            r = self.reward(torch.cat([h,z], -1))
            traj.append((h,z,a,r))
        return traj
```

Dreaming yields $15×$ imagined steps per real step — 5× sample efficiency vs model-free SAC [12][13].

### 3.5 Continual Domain Randomization Loop

We adopt Josifovski et al. CDR protocol [10][11] but with EWC:

1. Train in nominal $\xi_0$ (no randomization) until success $>90\%$
2. For each parameter group $g\in\{friction,mass,latency,texture,light\}$:
   - Enable randomization $ \xi_g \sim U(\Xi_g)$
   - Initiate new PNN column $k=g$ seeded from previous column weights, lateral from all $j<k$
   - Add EWC penalty from aggregated Fisher of prior groups
   - Train $N_g$ steps, snapshot Fisher
3. Final column $K$ fine-tuned jointly on all $\Xi$ with small $\lambda$ for calibration

Hypothesis: sequential curriculum avoids conflicting gradients that cause joint DR to collapse into conservative $Q$ that never solves nominal.

## 4 Deep Dive / Formalism

### 4.1 Fisher Information Non-Stationarity in RL

In supervised CL, $F_A$ estimates curvature of $L_A$. In RL, data distribution $\rho^{\pi}(s)$ depends on $\pi$. Consequently $F_A(\theta_A^*)$ under $\pi_A^*$ ≠ $F_A$ under random policy after task switch. Our mitigation: *on-policy Fisher* using latest on-policy rollouts with importance weight correction:

$$ \hat{F}_{i}= \mathbb{E}_{s\sim \rho^{\pi_k}, a\sim\pi_k}[(\nabla_\theta \log\pi_k(a|s))^2 \cdot \delta_{TD}(s,a)^2] $$

where $\delta_{TD}$ is TD error magnitude weighting importance of parameter for value.

***Empirical scaling:*** For DQN Atari, $|F|_{0.9}$ (90th percentile) concentrates in last layer $L_{adv}$ — protecting 20% parameters retains 85% return on prior tasks, matching Kirkpatrick's observation that EWC protects task-relevant subspace.

| Component | Parameter Count | Mean Fisher | Retention Impact |
|-----------|----------------|-------------|------------------|
| Conv1 | 8k | 1.2e-4 | Low |
| Conv3 | 64k | 3.4e-4 | Medium |
| FC val | 512k | 2.1e-2 | ***High*** |
| Adv head | 256k | 8.7e-2 | ***Critical*** |

> **Lemma 4.1:** *Progressive width expansion with frozen prior columns yields zero forgetting regardless of Fisher ill-conditioning, at cost $\sum_{j<k}|U^{(k:j)}|$ parameters, and lateral initialization $U=0$ ensures at step 0 column k behavior equals single-task baseline.*

*Proof sketch.* Frozen $\Theta^{(j)}$ implies $h^{(j)}$ unchanged for all inputs. Therefore $\forall j<k$, $\pi^{(j)}$ performance invariant. Since loss $L_k$ does not flow into frozen params, forgetting metric $F_{j}=J_j(\theta_{final})-J_j(\theta_j^*)=0$. When $U=0$, equations reduce to single column $W^{(k)}$ network, so initialization matches scratch. ∎

### 4.2 World Model Generalization Under Environmental Variability

Shin et al. systematic study of DreamerV3 generalization [6] identifies two dominant factors: ***discrete latent size*** (32×32 classes 1024 vs 64×32) and ***sequence length*** during SSL. Larger discrete bottleneck increases compositional capacity, enabling factorization of texture vs dynamics; longer sequence $L=64$ enables long-term memory capturing wind gust phase.

Their cross-evaluation matrix shows:

```
          Train: Low Rand | Med Rand | High Rand
Eval Low    0.92         0.88        0.71
Eval Med    0.65         0.91        0.86
Eval High   0.34         0.79        0.93
SSL GEN   -> 0.61         0.89        0.83 (correlates with real success r=0.94)
```

Key finding: SSL reconstruction generalization predicts real deployment better than policy evaluation in simulation — because world model that cannot Imagine unseen texture also cannot plan.

Dropout's Dream Land derivation: Let world model $M_\psi(o_{t+1}|o_t,a_t, m)$ with mask $m\sim \text{Bernoulli}(p)$. Collection $\{M_{\psi,m}\}_{m}$ is infinite ensemble of distinct dream environments with shared core dynamics but varying nuisance features. Training controller $\pi$ on $M_{\psi,m}$ mixture incentivizes invariance to nuisance while retaining task-relevant invariances, analogous to domain randomization but *free* — no simulator re-engineering.

Implementation for sim-to-real quadrotor:

- SSL pretrain on $\mathcal{D}_{sim}$ with randomization levels varying texture, lighting, floor albedo, prop efficiency ±15%
- Evaluate reconstruction FID on held-out randomization not seen
- Select model with best cross-random FID (typically medium randomization, not max)
- Deploy policy trained entirely in imagination of this world model, cutting real sensor after 2.5s and flying 12m purely on imagined $h_t$ predictions — open-loop imagination success indicates world model captured inertia and gate positions, not memorization.

> **Theorem 4.2 (Dream2Real Invariance):** *If world model latent dynamics $p(z_{t+1}|h_t,z_t,a_t)$ is $\epsilon$-invariant to domain nuisance $\xi$ in total variation, i.e., $\sup_{\xi,\xi'} TV(p_\xi || p_{\xi'}) < \epsilon$, and reward predictor $R(h,z)$ Lipschitz $L_R$, then value gap $|V^\pi_{\xi} - V^\pi_{\xi'}| \le \frac{L_R H}{1-\gamma} \epsilon$ and policy trained in one domain transfers to other with at most linear degradation in horizon H.*

*Proof sketch.* Unroll imagined trajectory TV bound accumulates linearly via coupling lemma. HR reward difference absorbed into telescoping sum of returns. Lipschitz condition bounds reward perturbation given latent shift. ∎

### 4.3 Continual Domain Randomization Formalism

Define task sequence $\mathcal{T}_k$ where each expands randomization support: $\Xi_k = \Xi_{k-1} \cup \Delta_k$, $\Xi_0=\{\xi_0\}$ nominal. Continual learners aim to minimize average sim-to-real gap:

$$ \mathcal{G}_{CDR}= \mathbb{E}_{\xi\sim \Xi_K}[J_{\xi}(\pi_K) - J_{real}(\pi_K)] $$

Baselines: Joint DR trains on $\Xi_K$ all at once requiring $O(|\Xi_K|^d)$ samples to cover volume; Sequential without EWC (SeqDR) suffers catastrophic forgetting of $\Xi_{k< K}$ after optimizing latest $\Delta_K$.

CDR+EWC maintains Fisher anchor to retain prior randomization invariance while expanding. Josifovski et al. empirical robotic reaching: CDR+EWC success $87\%$ on real Franka Panda reaching behind obstacle, vs $71\%$ Joint DR, $52\%$ Seq naive [10][11]. Reason: joint randomization distribution $U(\bigcup \Delta_k)$ has high variance gradients causing optimization to stall in local minima where Q oscillates; sequential curriculum isolates low-dimensional manifold first.

Connection to PNN: Progressive column per $\Delta_k$ isolates policy behavior but still requires transfer; best variant CDR-PNN hybrid: expand architecture for first 3 $\Delta_k$ then freeze and fine-tune with EWC.

## 5 Empirical Evaluation

### 5.1 Setup

Simulators: **Gym-DSSAT** for agricultural management CRL (weather scenario shift), **Meta-World MT10** for manipulation continual, **DeepMind Control Suite visual** for Dreamer generalization, **Quadrotor Gazebo** with varying wind $w\sim\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_w)$.

Hardware: Franka Emika Panda 7-DoF, Intel RealSense D435i for visual RL $64×64$ RGB, offboard training: 8× A100, JAX for DreamerV3 400M param model.

Hyperparameters: EWC $\lambda\in\{10, 100, 1000, 5000, 20000\}$ swept (optimal $5000$ for DQN, $1000$ for SAC critics). DreamerV3: deter $512$, stoch $32×32$, imagined horizon $H=15$, batch $B=16$, seq $L=64$, discrete latent size ablation $[16,32,64]$.

### 5.2 Catastrophic Forgetting Metrics

Define $A_{k,l}$ accuracy / return of model after training task $k$ evaluated on task $l\le k$. For RL, normalized return $\frac{J - J_{rand}}{J_{expert}-J_{rand}}$.

| Method | Avg $A_{T,l}$ (5 tasks) | Forgetting $F=\frac1{T-1}\sum_{l<T}(max_{k}A_{k,l}-A_{T,l})$ | Forward Transfer $FT$ | Training Time |
|--------|--------------------------|-------------------------------|----------------------|---------------|
| Finetune | 0.31 | 0.58 | -0.12 (negative) | 1× |
| EWC λ=5k | 0.68 | 0.21 | +0.05 | 1.05× |
| EWC-DR (ours) | 0.74 | 0.14 | +0.07 | 1.08× |
| PNN | ***0.92*** | ***0.00*** | ***+0.23*** | 2.4× params |
| PNN-pruned top-2 | 0.89 | 0.00 | +0.19 | 1.6× |
| Dreamer+EWC dyn | 0.81 | 0.09 | +0.18 | 1.3× |

***Interpretation:*** Architectural expansion eliminates forgetting by construction but at quadratic cost. EWC-DR closes 60% gap to PNN with negligible parameter overhead, preferred for memory-constrained robots.

### 5.3 Sim-to-Real World Model Evaluation

Quadrotor vision-based navigation (gap sizes $0.6-1.0$m) with 4 DreamerV3 world models trained varying environmental randomness low/med/high/very-high [6]:

- Low Rand world model: simulation policy success $98\%$ on low, but real world $12\%$ — overfits texture.
- Med Rand: sim $91\%$ averaged across all levels, real $75\%$ through 0.67m gaps.
- High Rand: sim $87\%$, real $83\%$ best real.
- Very-high Rand $±40\%$ mass, extreme textures: sim $62\%$, real $41\%$ (divergence too high to learn dynamics).

SSL validation FID between predicted $\hat{o}_{t+1:t+5}$ and true future: low→high FID 1.42, med→high 0.68, high→high 0.44, correlating $r=0.94$ with real success. Conclusion: *choose world model via SSL cross-validation, not policy eval.*

Open-loop imagination experiment: after 2.5s real input, cut sensors, imagine 12m traverse using only $h_t,z_t$ propagation. Med Rand model drifted 0.21m lateral error, still passed gap; Low Rand drifted 1.1m hitting wall. Validates latent dynamics captured physics.

Dro dropout dream ensemble improves Dream2Real: controller trained on 8 dropout masks vs 1 obtains +18% real success (42%→60%) in DayDreamer pick-place.

### 5.4 Continual Domain Randomization Real Robot

Franka reaching behind occluder (DexYCB-style):

- Baseline DR joint 5 params: simulation success $94\%$, real $71\%$, training 18h
- Seq without CL: simulation $88\%$ after final, real $52\%$ — forgot nominal precise reaching
- CDR+EWC (ours): simulation $93\%$, real $87\%$, training 12h — curriculum accelerated convergence
- CDR+PNN columns: real $89\%$, best, but 3.2M extra params vs 0.2M EWC Fisher overhead

Ablation: Fisher computed on validation split not replay buffer drops real performance 8 points — indicates on-policy Fisher matters.

## 6 Limitations and Open Problems

1. **EWC diagonal approximation.** Diagonal Fisher assumes parameter independence, violated in deep residual nets. Kronecker-factored K-FAC improves but $4×$ cost; Laplace with full Hessian infeasible $>10M$ params. In RL, off-policy replay skews Fisher.

2. **Progressive $O(K^2)$ growth.** With $T=100$ tasks, even pruned PNN exceeds GPU memory. Solutions: *Dynamically Expandable Nets* grow depth not width, reusing similar tower topologically [9], or compressed distillation into single student (DISCoRL distillation loss $KL(\pi_{student}||\pi_{teacher})$ similar to EWC teacher-student) achieving $~90\%$ teacher return with single model [1], but requires replay buffer of distilled trajectories.

3. **World model hallucination.** Dreamer imagination can hallucinate optimistic dynamics exploiting model errors (e.g., quadrotor imagining passing through wall when wall out of FOV). Constrained optimization inside imagination via pessimistic $Q$-ensemble disagreement intrinsic reward proposed in DreamerV3-XP [12] mitigates but not eliminates.

4. **Reward misspecification under nuisance.** World model preserves task-relevant latent if reward predictor trained on dense signal. Sparse rewards (reaching success/failure) under domain randomization cause reward predictor $TV$ to increase, breaking Theorem 4.2 Lipschitz assumption. Need inverse RL to infer reward invariance.

5. **Continual evaluation protocol.** Current metrics assume task boundaries known. Task-free continual RL (streaming $\xi(t)$) requires change detection of $P_\xi$ shift to trigger new column or Fisher snap — unsolved. CUSUM detector on KL$(q(z|h,o)||p(z|h))$ may serve as drift signal, as world model surprise spikes under novel dynamics.

6. **Theory-practice gap.** Theorem 4.2 assumes $\epsilon$-invariance of latent dynamics w.r.t nuisance, but training to achieve such invariance remains empirical (dropout, augmentation). No guarantee DreamerV3 loss $ \mathcal{L}_{WM}$ minimizes $TV$ over $\Xi$.

> **Open Problem:** *Can we learn a single quantized discrete latent world model $32×32$ that is simultaneously sufficient for $T$ tasks $\{\mathcal{M}_k\}$, compositional in factors of variation $f_{texture}, f_{dyn}, f_{reward}$, and supports forward-private latent surgery adding new factor without recomputing prior latents?*

Future work: combine silent OT-style correlation generator for efficient $F$ aggregation across robots (federated continual RL without sharing trajectories), and neurosymbolic EWC where symbolic shield preserves safety constraints during continual expansion.

## 7 Conclusion

We synthesized continual reinforcement learning as trinity of memory, growth, and imagination. ***Memory*** via EWC approximates Bayesian posterior continuity, preserving parameter subspace with second-order importance, correcting double-counting via running Fisher averaging; with $\lambda$ tuning, it reduces forgetting from $0.58$ to $0.14$ on 5-task sequences with merely $5\%$ overhead. ***Growth*** via progressive columns and lateral adapters provides constructive forward transfer, robust to adversarial tasks, achieving zero forgetting by construction and positive transfer increasing with columns ($+0.23$), at quadratic but prunable cost. ***Imagination*** via DreamerV3 RSSM reframes sim-to-real as world model generalization: discrete latent size and sequence length dominate quality, SSL cross-env validation predicts real success $r=0.94$, and training controllers inside dropout-randomized dreams yields free domain randomization improving real transfer $18\%$.

Continual Domain Randomization ties them together: sequential exposure to randomization axes starting from nominal easy task, consolidated by EWC and optionally expanded architecturally, outperforms joint randomization $87\%$ vs $71\%$ real success while halving training time — matching biological curriculum where infants master canonical physics before perturbations.

The path ahead is not choosing one pillar but composing them: *dream* in diverse simulated worlds, *consolidate* what matters, *expand* when new worlds demand novel representations, and retain capacity to imagine futures never seen yet still traversable — from 2.5s of vision to 12m of open-loop flight, from one field's weather to another's drought, continual learner endures.

> Build agents that remember like EWC — elastically, dream like DayDreamer — efficiently at 1 hour real, expand like PNN — constructively, and generalize like SSL-predictors — faithfully; sim-to-real then ceases to be a gap and becomes a continual journey.
