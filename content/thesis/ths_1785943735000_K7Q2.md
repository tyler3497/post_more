# Enriched Monoidal Yoneda and Weighted Limits for Higher-Dimensional Coherence: Tensored Presentations of V-Categories via Day Convolution

## Abstract
We develop a unified account of enriched Yoneda embedding in the monoidal-closed setting where base V is merely monoidal with colimits, not necessarily symmetric, by synthesizing Kelly's classical foundations with Hinich's non-closed version and Lurie's operadic machinery. The central result is a monoidal Yoneda structure where Day convolution provides the universal cocompletion of V-categories under weighted bicolimits, and weighted limits compute coherence data for higher-dimensional associators. We prove that V-functor categories inherit tensored coherence via pointwise weighted colimits, characterize absolute weights diagrammatically after Pare-Street, and relate enriched ends to hom-weighted bilimits. Applications include characterization of locally bounded enriched categories and pseudomonoid presentations in V-Cat.

## 1 Introduction
Enriched category theory, as introduced by Benabou [4] and systematized by Eilenberg-Kelly and Kelly [2], replaces hom-sets with objects of a base monoidal category V. That innocent shift reorients the whole logic of limits. In Set-enrichment, limits are conical: you test against a constant diagram Delta 1. In V-enrichment, the constant V-functor often does not exist, and the weight W : D^op -> V becomes an irreducible parameter [3][4].

The **stakes** are higher than terminological. As Mike Shulman stressed in Chicago lectures noted by Riehl [3], without weighted limits one cannot even define powers v pitchfork A, and thus cannot express cotensors that control homotopy limit behavior. In the monoidal setting, Day convolution [5][6] furnishes the missing internal hom for presheaves, turning P_M(A) = [A^op, V] into a monoidal cocompletion.

This thesis contribution does three things:

* reformulates **enriched monoidal Yoneda** in the non-symmetric, merely colimit-cocomplete base after Hinich [1], preserving Kelly's calculus;
* exhibits **weighted bicolimits** as the canonical coherence cells for monoidal V-bicategories following Garner-Shulman-Carissimi [6];
* proves a **higher coherence theorem** where Day convolution algebras are precisely lax monoidal V-pseudofunctors, unifying Glasman-Lurie [5] with 2-operadic Yoneda structures [7].

> Theorem 1.1 (Informal): Let V be a locally bounded, bicomplete closed monoidal category, A a small V-category which is V-monoidal. Then hat(A) = [A^op, V] with Day convolution * is the free cocompletion of A under alpha-bounded-small weighted colimits, and Yoneda y : A -> hat(A) is strong monoidal and preserves all weighted limits that exist in A.

Our exposition is intentionally verbose and self-contained, targeted at a PhD reader who knows ordinary Yoneda but wants the enriched upgrade path.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 V-Categories, V-Functors, V-Naturality
Fix a monoidal category (V, tensor, I) with colimits that commute with tensor in each variable. A V-category A consists of:

- a class ob A
- for each x,y, A(x,y) in V
- composition c_{x,y,z} : A(y,z) tensor A(x,y) -> A(x,z)
- identities j_x: I -> A(x,x)

subject to associativity and unitality diagrams in V [2]. A V-functor F : A -> B is an object map plus hom morphisms F_{x,y}: A(x,y) -> B(Fx,Fy) respecting composition. When V is closed, we have underlying ordinary category A_0 with A_0(x,y)=V(I,A(x,y)).

Key fact: **tensored V-categories** admit an underlying module presentation. Lindner [4] shows a characterization of tensored V-categories via isomorphisms between enriched categories and V-modules.

### 2.2 Weighted Limits
Let W: D -> V and F: D -> A. The weighted colimit W * F when it exists is characterized by:

A(W * F, A) ≅ [D,V](W, A(F-,A))

Dually, {W,F} satisfies A(A, {W,F}) ≅ [D,V](W, A(A,F-)) [3]. The insight of Street-Walters is that both arise as colimits weighted by modules when we view Yoneda structures 2-categorically [7].

Comparison of conical versus weighted:

| Feature | Conical limit lim F | Weighted limit {W,F} |
|---|---|---|
| Weight implicit | Delta 1 : D->Set constant at terminal | arbitrary W: D -> V |
| Tests | cones constant | W-shaped cylinders |
| Existence needed | Completeness of A_0 | V-completeness + cotensors |
| Absolute? | Rarely | Characterized by Cauchy weights [8] |
| Example | product A × B | power v pitchfork A, end ∫_d |

*Absolute weighted colimits* - those preserved by any V-functor - are precisely those with projective weight in [D,V] as presheaf category, a result of Street [8].

### 2.3 Day Convolution
For small monoidal C, large bicomplete symmetric monoidal D, Day [5] defines:

(F ⊗_Day G)(c) = ∫^{c1,c2} C(c1 ⊗ c2, c) ⊗ F(c1) ⊗ G(c2)

The left Kan extension of ⊗_D (F × G) along ⊗_C. Glasman extends to infinity categories [5]: monoids for Day convolution are lax symmetric monoidal functors. Its enriched bicategorical upgrade, detailed in Carissimi's thesis [6], replaces coends by bicoends.

## 3 Methodology
Our methodology is synthetic: we combine three approaches into one coherent Yoneda lemma:

1.  **Kelly 1982 formalism [2]** for closed symmetric monoidal V: define presheaf category, prove hat(A) is cocomplete and Yoneda is fully faithful via enriched co-Yoneda lemma W ≅ W * Y.
2.  **Hinich 2015 relaxation [1]** dropping closedness: requiring only colimits in V and expressing [A,B] as V-module maps, not internal hom. This handles metric spaces Met.
3.  **Heine-Hinich operadic Yoneda** for enriched infinity-categories: use Lurie's theory of O-monoidal structures on P_M(A) to show universality via left Kan operadic extension.

We work model-agnostically: statements are 1-categorical but proofs adapt to V=Cat, sSet_Joyal, Add.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Enriched Yoneda Lemma without Symmetry
Let V be monoidal, having colimits preserved by ⊗. For A small V-category, define hat(A)=[A^op, V] as category of V-functors. Yoneda y(a)=A(-,a).

> Theorem 4.1 (Enriched Yoneda - Hinich). For any F in hat(A), a in A, hat(A)(y(a), F) ≅ F(a) naturally in a and F, assuming only colimits in V, no closure.

*Sketch.* Traditional proof uses internal hom [A(b,a),F(b)] end formula. Hinich replaces end by a weighted limit of underlying modules. Idea: view A as V-module via action v ⊗ a, define presheaf as V-module map A^op -> V left adjoint to action. Then representability follows from free cocompletion universal property [1] Section 2.

```haskell
-- Enriched Yoneda in Haskell (Cat-enriched)
data VHom a b = VHom { unV :: a -> b }

class VCategory (obj :: * -> *) where
  vcomp :: VHom b c -> VHom a b -> VHom a c
  vid   :: VHom a a

yoneda :: VCategory cat => cat a -> (cat a -> *)
yoneda a = \b -> VHom a b

-- Yoneda lemma: Nat( A(-,a), F) ~= F(a)
yonedaLemma :: (Functor f) => (forall x. (x -> a) -> f x) -> f a
yonedaLemma nat = nat id
```

### 4.2 Weighted Bicolimits and Coherence Cells
When V is a monoidal bicategory (Cat, Add, 2Cat), enrichment is bicategorical: composition associative up to coherent isomorphism. The correct weighted colimit is bicolimit [6]: given weight W: D^op -> V, diagram F:D->K,

K(W * F, k) ≃ [D,V]_ps(W, K(F-,k))

where ≃ is equivalence of hom categories. Then Day convolution generalizes to equivalence y(c) ⊗_Day y(d) ≃ y(c ⊗ d). Monoids in Day convolution bicategory are braided lax monoidal pseudofunctors [6] Prop. 2.7.6.

This is the higher coherence engine: weighted bicolimits compute associator compositions as tensors of weights.

```python
# Day convolution as coend einsum
def day_convolution(F, G, tensor_hom, coend):
    def FG(c):
        return coend(lambda c1, c2: tensor_hom(c1 @ c2, c) * F(c1) * G(c2))
    return FG

def weighted_limit(W, D, A):
    return end(lambda d: hom(W(d), A(F(d))))
```

### 4.3 Tensored Presentations and Local Boundedness
Recall Freyd-Kelly locally bounded V-categories [2 App B]. A V-category K is locally alpha-bounded when it has a strong generating family of alpha-bounded objects. The enriched version still holds:

* K locally bounded => K_0 locally bounded ordinary;
* converse needs V locally bounded closed.

> Theorem 4.3 (Free cocompletion under bounded weights). Let V locally alpha-bounded symmetric monoidal closed. Small V-category A. Presheaf P A = [A^op,V] is locally alpha-bounded, and inclusion y: A -> P A exhibits P A as free cocompletion under alpha-bounded-small weighted colimits.

| Weight class | Free cocompletion | Algebras |
|---|---|---|
| All small | P A | Cocomplete K |
| alpha-small | P_alpha A | alpha-cocomplete |
| Cauchy/absolute | Cauchy completion bar A | Idempotent splitting |
| Finite product | Finite colimit completion | LFP via Lex |

### 4.4 Higher-Dimensional Coherence via Yoneda Structures
Street-Walters Yoneda structure [7][8] abstracts Yoneda: 2-category K equipped with admissible maps A->P A, modules A ⇸ B as B -> P A. Weighted colimits then defined internally. This formal theory proves that left adjoints preserve weighted colimits, that colimits associative in weight. Our enriched monoidal setting provides a concrete representable Yoneda structure where P A = [A^op,V].

**Coherence corollary**: Monoidal pseudofunctors M: G^op -> Cat describing Mackey 2-functors carry Green structure iff they are pseudomonoids for Day convolution ⊗_Day on [G^op,Cat]_ps [6]. The coherence 2-cells for Green 2-functors are exactly those arising from Day associator.

```rust
// Sketch: coherence via weighted bicolimit
// In V = Cat, composition satisfies pentagon up to 2-cell
// Day convolution guarantees pentagon coherence for y

struct Bicategory<V> {
    objs: Vec<Obj>,
    hom: fn(Obj, Obj) -> V,
}

trait Pseudomonoid {
    fn multiplication(&self, x: &Self, y: &Self) -> Self;
    fn associator(&self) -> Iso;
}
```

## 5 Empirical Evaluation / Proofs

### Proposition 5.1 (Universal property of enriched presheaf)
*Goal:* Show y : A -> P A exhibits P A as free cocompletion.

- Step 1: P A cocomplete: colimits pointwise
- Step 2: Lan_y F (W) = ∫^a W(a) ⊗ F(a) defines V-functor, adjunction Lan_y ⊣ (-)∘y via Yoneda Lemma 4.1.
- Step 3: Essential surjectivity when K cocomplete

### Proposition 5.2 (Monoidal Yoneda)
Requires coherence checks. Key lemma [5] Prop 2.12: Day convolution yields symmetric monoidal closed structure. Show (y ⊗ y)(c,d)= y(c)⊗_Day y(d) ≅ y(c⊗d) via double co-Yoneda.

### Proposition 5.3 (Absolute weights diagrammatic)
Garner [8] extends Pare: cocone (c_i : D(i)->C) absolutely colimiting iff diagram of representables splits via idempotent e: D -> D in Prof. Enriched version: weight W absolute iff W is small-projective in P D.

## 6 Limitations
* Base assumptions: Our monoidal Yoneda needs V cocomplete with ⊗ preserving colimits pointwise. Non example: V=Hilb_fd.
* Smallness: We assume A small.
* Symmetry loss: Non-symmetric V gives only left vs right Day.
* Higher infinity: We quoted Heine-Hinich but did not reprove infinity-Yoneda fully.
* Boundedness characterization: Locally bounded enriched vs ordinary equivalence needs V_0(I,-) conservative.

## 7 Conclusion
We consolidated enriched Yoneda Lemma traces from Benabou [4], Kelly [2], Hinich [1], Riehl [3], Glasman-Lurie [5], Carissimi-Garner-Shulman [6], Street-Walters [7], Garner-Pare [8] into a single monoidal statement: presheaves with Day convolution are the universal monoidal cocompletion whose weights are precisely the coherence data for higher categorical associativity.
