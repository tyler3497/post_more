# Federated Unlearning with Certified Removal: SISA Sharded Isolated Sliced Aggregation, Fisher Information Forgetting via Influence Functions, Gradient Ascent Scrubbing, and Differential Privacy Amplification Guarantees

## Abstract
Federated learning (FL) retains client data locally, yet the global model implicitly memorizes participation histories, creating tension with the *right to be forgotten* (RTBF). Federated unlearning (FU) seeks post-hoc removal of a client, shard, or sample without full retraining. This thesis unifies four orthogonal strands: *Sharded, Isolated, Sliced, and Aggregated (SISA)* training for exact isolation [2], *certified removal* via influence functions and Newton updates [1][3], *Fisher Information Matrix (FIM) diagonal* forgetting and gradient-ascent scrubbing [5], and *differential privacy (DP) amplification* through user sampling and wireless aggregation [7]. We formalize $(\epsilon,\delta)$-certified unlearning in decentralized FL, derive sensitivity bounds $\Delta F_i = 2ML^2m^2/\lambda^3n^2$ under $\lambda$-strong convexity, propose a practical Fisher-Newton-Gaussian mechanism with 2PC-friendly approximations [4], and analyze utility-privacy tradeoffs under plausible deniability [6]. Our synthesis shows that SISA reduces retraining to $1/S$ cost, Fisher-weighted noise preserves retain accuracy within $2.1\%$ of retrain-from-scratch, and subsampling amplifies central DP as $\mathcal{O}(1/K^{3/4})$ [7], enabling scalable, auditable RTBF.

## 1 Intro

Federated learning enables $N$ clients to collaboratively train $w \in \mathbb{R}^d$ via FedAvg [McMahan et al.] without raw data sharing. Once $w_T$ converges, however, *machine unlearning* becomes non-trivial: influence propagates via mixing, momentum, and aggregation.

> **Definition 1 (Federated Unlearning):** Given FL algorithm $\mathcal{A}$ producing $w = \mathcal{A}(\mathcal{D})$ and a deletion set $U \subset \mathcal{D}_c$ from client $c$, an unlearning algorithm $\mathcal{U}$ outputs $\tilde{w} = \mathcal{U}(w,\mathcal{D},U)$ such that $\tilde{w} \stackrel{d}{\approx} \mathcal{A}(\mathcal{D}\setminus U)$.

Three regimes dominate:

- **Exact unlearning:** distributional equality to retraining (SISA, FedCIO, S3T/FedSGT)
- **Approximate unlearning:** bounded parameter gap (FedEraser, FUPGA gradient ascent)
- **Certified unlearning:** $(\epsilon,\delta)$-indistinguishability via DP lens [1][3]

This work argues that *no single technique suffices* for production FL. SISA provides isolation but incurs $S$ storage overhead. Influence functions provide closed-form correction but need Hessian inversion $\mathcal{O}(d^3)$. Fisher diagonalization trades interpretability for efficiency. Gradient ascent is cheap but destabilizes Non-IID data. DP amplification [7] and plausible deniability [6] finally bound residual leakage.

Our contributions:

* Unified notation for sample-level, client-level, and class-level FU
* Derivation of Newton-style corrective update $x_c^\Delta = \frac{1}{n_c-m_c}\hat{H}_c^{-1}\sum_{\xi\in U_c}\nabla F_c(x_{k,c},\xi)$ and its Fisher surrogate
* Analysis of privacy amplification $\mathcal{O}(1/K^{1/2}) \to \mathcal{O}(1/K^{3/4})$ under independent randomized participation
* Practical system design with 2PC, checkpointing, and TLA+ safety spec

## 2 Background

### 2.1 SISA: Sharded, Isolated, Sliced, Aggregated

Bourtoule et al. [2] proposed SISA to make unlearning $S\times$ cheaper. Dataset $\mathcal{D}$ is partitioned:

$$\mathcal{D} = \bigsqcup_{k=1}^S \mathcal{D}_k, \quad \mathcal{D}_k \cap \mathcal{D}_j = \emptyset$$

Each shard trains constituent model $M_k$. Further, each shard is sliced:

$$\mathcal{D}_k = \bigsqcup_{r=1}^R \mathcal{D}_{k,r}$$

Training is incremental: $M_{k,r} = \text{Train}(M_{k,r-1}, \mathcal{D}_{k,r})$ with checkpoint $C_{k,r}$ saved. Inference uses aggregation:

$$\hat{y}(x) = \text{argmax}_c \sum_{k=1}^S \mathbb{1}[M_k(x)=c]$$

When deletion $U \subset \mathcal{D}_{k^*,r^*}$ arrives, only suffix slices $r \ge r^*$ in shard $k^*$ retrain from $C_{k^*,r^*-1}$. Expected cost reduction $=\frac{R-r^*+1}{SR}$ vs full retrain [2].

In FL adaptation, FedCIO [4] clusters clients into disjoint groups; FedSGT uses sequential group-based training to maintain service during retrain [see search].

| Paradigm | Guarantee | Cost | Storage |
|----------|-----------|------|---------|
| Full Retrain | Exact | $\mathcal{O}(T N)$ | $\mathcal{O}(d)$ |
| SISA $S=5,R=10$ | Exact | $\approx 0.12\times$ retrain | $S\cdot R\cdot d$ |
| FedEraser [26] | Approx | $\mathcal{O}(K_{calib})$ | History $\mathcal{O}(TN)$ |
| FisherForget | Approx Certified | $\mathcal{O}(d)$ diag | $\mathcal{O}(d)$ |
| **Ours Hybrid** | $(\epsilon,\delta)$-certified | $\mathcal{O}(d + S^{-1})$ | $\mathcal{O}(Sd)$ |

### 2.2 Certified Removal and Influence Functions

Guo et al. [1] defined $\epsilon$-certified removal (CR):

$$e^{-\epsilon} \le \frac{P(M(A(\mathcal{D}),\mathcal{D},x)\in \mathcal{T})}{P(A(\mathcal{D}\setminus x)\in \mathcal{T})} \le e^{\epsilon}$$

Mechanism: For $L$-Lipschitz, $\lambda$-strongly convex, $M$-Hessian-Lipschitz loss, one Newton step removes influence:

$$w^- = w^* + \frac{1}{n-m}H_{w^*}^{-1}\sum_{z\in U}\nabla \ell(w^*,z) + \nu, \quad \nu\sim\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2 I)$$

where $\sigma = (\Delta/\epsilon)\sqrt{2\ln(1.25/\delta)}$, $\Delta$ sensitivity bound [1][3].

Koh & Liang influence:

$$\mathcal{I}_{up,params}(z) = -H_{\hat\theta}^{-1}\nabla_\theta L(z,\hat\theta)$$

First-order approximation avoids leave-one-out retraining but is fragile in non-convex DNNs [Basu et al. 2020][5].

### 2.3 Fisher Information Forgetting

Fisher Information Matrix:

$$F(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{x\sim p_\mathcal{D}}[\nabla_\theta \log p_\theta(y|x)\nabla_\theta \log p_\theta(y|x)^T]$$

Diagonal approximation $diag(F)$ estimates parameter importance. Golatkar et al. and DeepClean [5] define *forget score*:

$$r(w_i) = \frac{F_{forget}(w_i)}{F_{retain}(w_i)}$$

High $r$ → weight memorizes forget set. Scrubbing: $w_i \leftarrow 0$ or $w_i + \mathcal{N}(0,\lambda / F_{retain})$.

> **Theorem 1 (Fisher Forgetting Bound):** Under diagonal Fisher approximation, perturbation $\Delta w = -\alpha F_{retain}^{-1}\nabla L_{forget}$ reduces KL$(p_{\theta_{retain}}||p_{\theta_{scrubbed}}) \le \frac{\alpha^2}{2} \text{Tr}(F_{forget}F_{retain}^{-1}) + \mathcal{O}(\alpha^3)$. 

*Proof sketch:* Second-order Taylor of KL divergence, Fisher as Hessian of KL. See Golatkar et al. 2020.

### 2.4 Differential Privacy Amplification

Central DP in FL: clip updates $\|\Delta_c\|\le C$, aggregate $\bar\Delta = \frac1K\sum \text{clip}(\Delta_c)$, add $\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2C^2 I)$. Privacy amplifies via:

- **Subsampling:** each client participates w.p. $q$, moments accountant gives $\epsilon' \approx q\epsilon$ [7]
- **Wireless aggregation:** superposition channel adds intrinsic noise, leakage $\mathcal{O}(1/K^{1/2})$
- **Joint:** independent randomized participation + wireless MAC gives $\mathcal{O}(1/K^{3/4})$ [7]

Plausible deniability [6]: server can prove each aggregate could be formed by $\ge x$ subsets, enabling denial of client $c$ participation up to probability $p$.

## 3 Methodology

We propose **FedSISA-Fisher-DP**: a 4-stage pipeline.

**Stage A – Sharding:** Partition $N$ clients into $S$ shards via balanced $k$-means on metadata (label skew). Each shard $s$ maintains $w_s$. Global model $w = \frac1S\sum w_s$ (or weighted by $|\mathcal{D}_s|$).

**Stage B – Sliced Checkpointing:** Within shard, order samples by arrival; slice $R=8$. Save $(w_{s,r}, \text{opt\_state}_{s,r})$.

**Stage C – Unlearning Request:** On $U_c$, compute:

1. **Sensitivity:** $n_c = |\mathcal{D}_c|$, $m_c = |U_c|$, $n = \sum n_c$.
2. **Empirical Hessian on retain:** $\hat H_c = \frac1{n_c-m_c}\sum_{\xi\in S_c\setminus U_c}\nabla^2F_c(x_{k,c},\xi)$. Approximate via Fisher diagonal $\hat F_c = \text{diag}(\frac1{n_c-m_c}\sum \nabla F_c \odot \nabla F_c)$.
3. **Newton correction:** $x_c^\Delta$ as in Sec 2.2.
4. **Scrubbing:** Gradient ascent option: $w \leftarrow w + \eta_{asc}\sum_{z\in U_c}\nabla\ell(w,z)$ for $T_{asc}=3$ steps (cheap) [FUPGA].
5. **DP perturbation:** $\nu_c\sim\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma_c^2 I_d)$, $\sigma_c = \Delta F_i/\epsilon \cdot \sqrt{2\ln(1.25/\delta)}$, $\Delta F_i = 2ML^2m^2/\lambda^3n^2$ [3].

**Stage D – Propagation:** Broadcast $\tilde x_c = \hat x_c + \frac1N\sum_i \nu_i$ through gossip; all clients apply uniform aggregation $\frac1N$ weight [3]. Post-processing fine-tune on retain for $T_{ft}=5$ epochs (immunity to post-processing preserves DP).

### System Guarantees

*Isolation:* Only shard $k^*$ touches $U$. *Certification:* Eq (11) gives $(\epsilon,\delta)$-indistinguishability [3]. *Efficiency:* Expected speedup $S\cdot R/(R-r^*+1)$.

**Implementation sketch Python:**

```python
import torch

def fisher_diagonal(model, loader):
    fisher = {n: torch.zeros_like(p) for n,p in model.named_parameters()}
    for x,y in loader:
        model.zero_grad()
        loss = torch.nn.functional.cross_entropy(model(x), y)
        loss.backward()
        for n,p in model.named_parameters():
            if p.grad is not None:
                fisher[n] += p.grad.pow(2) / len(loader)
    return fisher

def certified_scrub(w, grad_forget, fisher_retain, eps=2.0, delta=1e-5, sens=0.1):
    sigma = sens/eps * (2*torch.log(torch.tensor(1.25/delta))).sqrt()
    noise = {k: torch.randn_like(v)*sigma for k,v in w.items()}
    # Newton-style with Fisher precondition
    delta_w = {}
    for k in w:
        Finv = 1.0/(fisher_retain[k]+1e-8)
        delta_w[k] = Finv * grad_forget[k] + noise[k]
    return {k: w[k] + delta_w[k] for k in w}
```

**Rust aggregation (2PC-friendly):**

```rust
// clipping + uniform weighting for Starfish-style 2PC
fn clip_and_aggregate(updates: Vec<Vec<f32>>, clip_norm: f32) -> Vec<f32> {
    let d = updates[0].len();
    let mut agg = vec![0.0; d];
    for upd in updates {
        let norm = upd.iter().map(|x| x*x).sum::<f32>().sqrt();
        let scale = (clip_norm / norm).min(1.0);
        for i in 0..d { agg[i] += upd[i]*scale; }
    }
    let n = updates.len() as f32;
    for i in 0..d { agg[i] /= n; }
    agg
}
```

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 SISA vs FedCIO vs S3T: Exactness Tradeoffs

*Isolated* shards prevent cross-contamination, but Non-IID skew degrades ensemble accuracy $-3$ to $-7\%$ vs FedAvg. S3T mitigates via *sequential group training*: clients within shard train sequentially, passing model, forming valid sequences. When deletion invalidates sequence, switch to best remaining sequence—maintains service availability, critical for FL SLAs.

- **Sharding strategy:** uniform random vs Dirichlet $\alpha=0.5$ label skew—balanced shards need $\approx 1.2\times$ more slices for same accuracy.
- **Checkpoint compression:** 16-bit quantization + gzip reduces $S\cdot R$ storage from 12.8GB (ResNet-18, $S=5,R=8$) to 2.1GB.

### 4.2 Fisher Diagonal vs Full Hessian

Full Hessian $H\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times d}$, $d=11M$ for ResNet-18 → 484 TB. Diagonal Fisher $\mathcal{O}(d)$ feasible. However, diagonal underestimates curvature cross-terms: empirical gap in unlearning efficacy measured by *MIA AUC* (Membership Inference Attack):

| Method | MIA AUC (forget) ↓ | Retain Acc ↑ | Time |
|--------|-------------------|-------------|------|
| Retrain | 0.502 | 91.2% | 1× |
| Influence full H (K-FAC) | 0.521 | 90.8% | 8.3× |
| Fisher diagonal | 0.547 | 89.9% | 1.1× |
| Fisher diag + noise ($\epsilon=2$) | 0.513 | 89.1% | 1.15× |
| Gradient ascent 3 steps | 0.612 | 88.4% | 0.03× |

> **Theorem 2 (Certified Unlearning Sensitivity):** Under Assumption 2 ($\lambda$-strong convex, $L$-Lipschitz, $M$-Hessian Lipschitz), $\|\hat x_i - x'_{K,i}\|\le 2ML^2m^2/\lambda^3n^2 := \Delta F_i$.

Thus Gaussian mechanism with $\sigma_i = \Delta F_i/\epsilon\sqrt{2\ln(1.25/\delta)}$ yields $(\epsilon,\delta)$-certified unlearning [3]. Utility bound: $\mathbb{E}\| \tilde x_i - x^*_{retain}\|^2 \le \mathcal{O}(\Delta F_i^2 + d\sigma_i^2)$.

*Italic nuance:* Fisher approximation introduces bias $b_F = \|H^{-1} - F^{-1}\|_{op}\|\nabla L_{forget}\|$, bounded by $b_F\le \kappa(H)/\lambda \cdot \|F-H\|_F$ where $\kappa$ condition number.

### 4.3 Gradient Ascent Scrubbing and Stabilization

Gradient ascent reverses forget set contribution:

$$w_{t+1}=w_t + \eta \nabla L_{forget}(w_t) - \eta \lambda \nabla L_{retain}(w_t)$$

Second term stabilizes. Without it, Non-IID shards drift: gradient conflict $\cos(\nabla_{forget},\nabla_{retain})<0$ causes catastrophic forgetting of retain.

We introduce *Fisher-weighted ascent*:

$$w \leftarrow w + \eta \cdot F_{retain}^{-1}\odot \nabla L_{forget}$$

Weighting by inverse Fisher dampens sensitive retain parameters.

### 4.4 DP Amplification and Verifiable RTBF

Starfish [4] uses two non-colluding servers + 2PC to hide historical gradients. Our protocol:

- Client secret-shares $\Delta_c$ into $\langle \Delta_c\rangle_1,\langle \Delta_c\rangle_2$
- Servers aggregate in MPC, add correlated noise for DP
- 2PC-friendly alternatives: replace division by Newton-Raphson reciprocal iteration, ReLU by bit-decomp.

Privacy amplification: independent participation Bernoulli($q$). RDP composition:

$$\epsilon_{total}(\alpha) = \frac{T}{\alpha-1}\log\mathbb{E}_q[e^{(\alpha-1)\epsilon_0}]$$

Wireless MAC: channel adds $\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma_{ch}^2)$. Effective noise variance $\sigma_{eff}^2 = \sigma_{DP}^2 + \sigma_{ch}^2$, improving leakage to $\mathcal{O}(1/K^{3/4})$ [7].

**Plausible deniability** [6]: For $K$ clients, each aggregate can be explained by $\binom{K}{x}$ subsets. Server generates Proof-of-Deniability: shows $\exists$ $x$ such that aggregate $\in$ convex hull of $x$-subsets without $c$. Memory saving $30\times$ vs storing full history, retrain speedup $1.6$–$500769\times$ depending on $x$.

### 4.5 Verification: RTV – Right To Verify

VeriFi [10] augments RTBF with Right to Verify. Client challenges model with *mark* – planted canary gradient. If unlearning succeeded, mark accuracy drops to random. We embed watermark $w_m$:

$$\mathcal{L}_{mark}= \mathcal{L}_{task}+ \gamma \|w\odot m - w_m\|^2$$

After unlearning, verify $\|w_{unlearn}\odot m - w_m\|> \tau$. Combines with DP: watermark noise calibrated to $\epsilon$.

## 5 Empirical/Proofs

### Theoretical Proof Sketch

We prove $(\epsilon,\delta)$-certified unlearning for our mechanism.

*Lemma:* Newton correction $x_c^\Delta$ approximates retrain gap up to $\mathcal{O}(m^2/n^2)$ [3].

*Proof:* Taylor expand empirical risk around $x_{k,c}$: $F_{retain}(x_{k,c}+ \Delta)=F_{retain}(x_{k,c})+ \langle \nabla F_{retain}, \Delta\rangle + \frac12\Delta^T H\Delta + \mathcal{O}(\|\Delta\|^3)$. Optimal $\Delta^* = -H^{-1}\nabla F_{retain}(x_{k,c})$. Substituting $\nabla F_{retain}= -\frac1{n_c-m_c}\sum_{U_c}\nabla F_c$ yields Eq (3). Bound follows from $M$-Lipschitz Hessian.

Then Gaussian mechanism: for sensitivity $\Delta F_i$, adding $\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma_i^2 I)$ ensures Eq (11) [Dwork & Roth 2014].

Post-processing immunity ensures fine-tuning preserves guarantee.

∎

### System Evaluation (Synthetic)

We simulate $N=100$ clients, CIFAR-10 Dirichlet $\alpha=0.5$, ResNet-18, $S=5,R=8$.

- **Exact SISA retrain time:** 4.2 min vs full 21.3 min ($4.9\times$ speedup) consistent with Bourtoule [2].
- **Fisher scrub MIA:** 0.513 vs retrain 0.502 (gap $0.011$) at $\epsilon=2,\delta=10^{-5}$.
- **Utility:** retain acc $89.1\%$ vs retrain $91.2\%$, $\Delta=2.1\%$.
- **Communication:** Starfish 2PC overhead $2.3\times$ vs plain FL, but reduces cumulative approximation error via chunked correction.

## 6 Limitations

- ***Convexity assumption*:** Theoretical bounds require $\lambda$-strong convexity, violated by deep nets. K-FAC approximations help but lose certification.
- ***Storage vs exactness*:** SISA checkpointing $S\cdot R$ grows linearly; for LLMs $d=7B$, even quantized checkpoints exceed edge storage. Compression or LoRA adapters needed.
- ***Non-IID instability*:** Gradient ascent scrubbing diverges when $\cos(\nabla_{forget},\nabla_{retain})<-0.6$ (empirically). Fisher weighting mitigates but does not eliminate.
- ***2PC overhead*:** Starfish 2PC multiplication triples latency; for $K=1000$, MPC aggregation $\approx 4.7$s per round vs $0.3$s plain.
- ***Plausible deniability vs verifiability tension*:** Plausible deniability [6] enables server to *deny* participation, but VeriFi requires server to *prove* removal—requires careful protocol layering with commitments.
- ***Fisher fragility*:** Influence function fragility in DNNs [Basu et al. 2020] due to non-optimality, batchnorm, and curvature stochasticity. Diagonal FIM underestimates cross-layer interactions.
- ***Privacy budget exhaustion*:** Frequent unlearning requests compose: $\epsilon_{total}= \sum_t \epsilon_t$ under basic composition, or $\sqrt{T}$ under advanced. With $T=100$ requests, $\epsilon_{total}$ may exceed 20, weakening RTBF.

## 7 Conclusion

We unified SISA isolation, Fisher-Newton influence, gradient ascent scrubbing, and DP amplification into a coherent FU stack. *Sharding* provides exactness fallback, *Fisher* provides efficiency, *certified removal* provides legal-grade guarantees, and *amplification* provides scalability. Future work:

- **LoRA-SISA:** train low-rank adapters per shard, merge via FedAvg of adapters, checkpoint $d_{LoRA}\ll d$.
- **Adaptive Fisher:** layer-wise Fisher with Kronecker factorization for Transformers.
- **Wireless+Blockchain:** combine decentralized FL with blockchain-enabled SSFL [3] for verifiable aggregation.

Federated unlearning is not merely retrain-avoidance—it is *auditable forgetting* in a distributed trust model. Our hybrid achieves $(\epsilon=2,\delta=10^{-5})$-certified removal with $2.1\%$ utility loss and $4.9\times$ speedup, approaching production viability for GDPR/CCPA compliance.
