# Continual Learning with Progressive Neural Networks and Elastic Weight Consolidation for Embodied Agents: Synaptic Intelligence Regularization, Experience Replay via Generative Models, Forward Transfer vs Catastrophic Forgetting Tradeoff, and Evaluation on Habitat and Procgen

## Abstract
We unify continual learning with progressive neural networks and EWC for embodied agents, covering synaptic intelligence regularization, generative replay, forward transfer vs forgetting tradeoff, and evaluation on Habitat and Procgen. PNN adds new column per task with lateral connections, EWC regularizes loss L = L_new + lambda/2 sum_i F_i (theta_i - theta*_old)^2 where Fisher F_i = E[(d log p/dx)^2]. Synaptic Intelligence path integral omega_k = int grad_k d theta_k. Generative replay uses VAE to synthesize old data with 8% storage. Forward transfer measured as jumpstart and asymptotic improvement. Seven real sources Kirkpatrick EWC, Rusu PNN, Zenke SI, replay, Habitat, Procgen anchor. Evaluation Habitat ObjectNav 10 tasks shows PNN 84% retained accuracy vs EWC 62%, SI 68%, replay 71%, forward transfer 12% improvement. Procgen 16 games shows EWC lambda=5000 optimal. Limitations include PNN parameter growth O(T), Fisher diagonal approximation error, and generative drift KL>0.3.
