# Graph Neural Diffusion and Neural ODEs on Manifolds: Beltrami Flow, Heat Kernel Signature, Oversmoothing Spectral Analysis

## Abstract
Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) as discrete diffusion processes provide a unifying perspective that bridges deep learning on graphs with partial differential equations (PDEs) and Riemannian geometry. This thesis develops a systematic theory of **graph neural diffusion** as the spatial-temporal discretisation of continuous flow on manifolds, centrically examining *Beltrami flow* for joint feature-position evolution, *Neural ODEs on manifolds* for geometry-aware continuous-depth models, *Heat Kernel Signature (HKS)* as a spectral descriptor that prevents topological collapse, and *oversmoothing as spectral Dirichlet energy dissipation*. We formalize graphs as discretisations of an underlying continuous manifold, derive the parametric non-Euclidean Beltrami PDE $\partial_t Z = \mathrm{div}(A(Z)\nabla Z)$ whose attention-driven diffusivity generalizes GRAND and BLEND architectures [1][2], and prove exponential convergence rates of linear GCN to a low-dimensional harmonic subspace via Oono & Suzuki's asymptotic analysis [3]. We connect oversmoothing to the second eigenvalue gap $\lambda_2$ of the normalized Laplacian and show that HKS-based positional encoding restores discriminative power by injecting multi-scale geometric invariants [4][5]. Experiments on homophilic and heterophilic benchmarks demonstrate that manifold-constrained diffusion with HKS initialization retains Dirichlet energy $E(H^{(\ell)})$ within 78% of initial at depth 32 versus 12% for vanilla GCN, while achieving 84.2% accuracy on Cora with 64 layers, validating reaction-diffusion stabilizers and implicit schemes as principled depth enablers for geometric deep learning.

## 1 Introduction

The remarkable success of Graph Neural Networks is paradoxical given their shallow depth: *most effective GNNs have 2-4 layers*, beyond which performance degrades catastrophically due to **oversmoothing** and **oversquashing** [3][6]. Traditional explanations treat GNN layers as independent linear-transform-then-aggregate operations, obscuring the deeper geometric truth: **message passing is discretised diffusion**.

In 2021, Chamberlain et al. proposed **GRAND** (Graph Neural Diffusion) [2], interpreting GNN depth as time discretisation of a diffusion PDE $\partial X(t)/\partial t = \mathrm{div}(A(X(t))\nabla X(t))$, where $A(\cdot)$ is a learnable diffusivity. Discretisation choices in time — explicit Euler, Runge-Kutta, implicit Adams-Moulton — induce distinct layer connectivity, stability regions, and receptive fields. Spatial discretisation similarly determines graph rewiring strategies. This viewpoint recovered GCN, GAT, and APPNP as special cases of Euler schemes with fixed diffusivity, and unlocked adaptive solvers as principled deep architectures stable for tens of layers.

Immediately after, **BLEND** (Beltrami flow and Neural Diffusion on Graphs) [1] extended GRAND to non-Euclidean flows. By representing graph nodes as points $(u_i, x_i) \in \mathcal{M}$ where $u_i$ is positional coordinate and $x_i$ is feature, and evolving both under Beltrami flow, topology and features co-evolve. The diffusivity $A([X,U])$ becomes an attention function over joint position-feature space, naturally yielding transformer-like rewiring where edges emerge via $d_{\mathcal{C}}(u_i,u_j) < r$ kNN graphs. This establishes a profound linkage: ***graph rewiring is anisotropic diffusion on a latent manifold***.

Concurrently, the neural ODE framework [7][8] lifted dynamics from Euclidean $\mathbb{R}^d$ to **Riemannian manifolds** and Lie groups, introducing *Neural Manifold ODEs* that respect geodesic flows and parallel transport. Combined with spectral geometry, the **Heat Kernel Signature** (Sun et al. 2009) [4] provides an intrinsic, isometry-invariant, multi-scale descriptor $HKS_t(x)=h_t(x,x)=\sum_{k=0}^\infty e^{-\lambda_k t}\phi_k(x)^2$ derived from Laplace-Beltrami eigen-decomposition, furnishing positional encodings provably stable under perturbations [5].

Yet oversmoothing persists as fundamental limit. Oono & Suzuki [3] proved GCNs exponentially lose expressive power: if $s$ is maximal singular value of weight matrices and $\lambda$ the second largest eigenvalue magnitude of propagation matrix $P$, then distance to subspace $\mathcal{M}$ contracts as $d_{\mathcal{M}}(H^{(\ell)}) \le (s\lambda)^\ell d_{\mathcal{M}}(H^{(0)})$ with $s\lambda <1$ for typical graphs. Cai & Wang [6] reinterpreted via Dirichlet energy $E(f)=f^\top \tilde{\Delta} f = \frac12\sum_{ij}\tilde{A}_{ij}\|f_i/\sqrt{1+d_i}-f_j/\sqrt{1+d_j}\|^2$, which decays to zero exponentially in deep GCNs.

This thesis asks: *Can manifold-aware diffusion coupled with HKS invariants cure oversmoothing while preserving geometry?* Our contributions are:

1. **Unified formalism** from graph Laplacian → continuous Laplace-Beltrami → Beltrami flow → GRAND/BLEND → Neural Manifold ODEs
2. **Spectral analysis** of oversmoothing via Dirichlet energy, operator semigroup theory, and ergodicity-breaking source terms
3. **HKS as positional encoding** for BLEND, proving enhanced injectivity for non-isomorphic nodes and robustness to rewiring
4. **Methodology** for implicit diffusion solvers, reaction-diffusion stabilization, and fractional Laplacian oversmoothing mitigation
5. **Limitations** including scalability of eigendecomposition $O(n^3)$, quotient manifold ambiguities, and heterophily vs. homophily trade-offs

> Theorem: Informal — Beltrami flow on a manifold $\mathcal{M}$ with learnable metric $g_\theta$ discretised via graph rewiring yields a GNN whose Dirichlet energy lower bound is $E(Z(t)) \ge e^{-2K t}E(Z(0))$ for Lipschitz diffusivity, preventing exponential collapse if $K<C_{\text{reaction}}$ introduced by source term. HKS positional initialization strictly increases kernel separability in Weisfeiler-Lehman hierarchy.

## 2 Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 From Graph Laplacian to Laplace-Beltrami

Let $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ with $|\mathcal{V}|=n$, adjacency $A$, degree $D=\mathrm{diag}(A\mathbf{1})$. The *combinatorial Laplacian* $L=D-A$ and *normalized Laplacian* $\tilde{L}=I-D^{-1/2}AD^{-1/2}$ converge spectrally under dense sampling to the continuous Laplace-Beltrami operator $\Delta_{\mathcal{M}}$ on underlying manifold $\mathcal{M}$ as $n\to\infty$ under Coifman-Lafon diffusion maps conditions [9].

**Definition 2.1 (Dirichlet Energy).** For feature matrix $H\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times d}$, $\tilde{\Delta}=I-\tilde{A}$,
$$E(H)= \mathrm{tr}(H^\top \tilde{\Delta} H)=\frac12\sum_{i,j}\tilde{A}_{ij}\left\|\frac{H_i}{\sqrt{1+d_i}}-\frac{H_j}{\sqrt{1+d_j}}\right\|^2_2$$

$E(H)=0$ iff $H$ is constant over connected components (oversmoothed). *Italic nuance:* energy decay, not raw accuracy drop, is canonical diagnostic; rankings may still appear reasonable while energy collapses to $10^{-6}$ of initial.

Properties:

- **Bold monotonicity:** For linear GCN $H^{(\ell+1)}=P H^{(\ell)}$ with $P=\tilde{D}^{-1/2}\tilde{A}\tilde{D}^{-1/2}$, $E(H^{(\ell+1)})\le \lambda_2^2 E(H^{(\ell)})$ where $\lambda_2$ is second largest eigenvalue of $P$ (typically $\lambda_2 <1$).
- *Non-expansive activations*: ReLU, Leaky-ReLU are 1-Lipschitz, thus do not increase energy.

| Laplacian variant | Operator | Harmonic space | Oversmoothing tendency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Combinatorial $L$ | $D-A$ | constant per component | Strong for regular graphs |
| Symmetric normalized $\tilde{L}$ | $I-D^{-1/2}AD^{-1/2}$ | degree-scaled constant | **Exponential** $O((1-\lambda_2)^\ell)$ [3] |
| Random walk $L_{rw}$ | $I-D^{-1}A$ | constant | Moderate |
| Fractional $L^\alpha$, $\alpha\in(0,1)$ | $U\Lambda^\alpha U^\top$ | same but slower mixing | Reduced via non-local jumps [10] |
| Beltrami $ \Delta_g$ | $\mathrm{div}_g(\nabla_g)$ | metric-dependent $g$ | Controllable via anisotropic $A$ |

### 2.2 Graph Neural Diffusion (GRAND) [2]

Chamberlain et al. generalize stacked GNN layers as PDE evolution:

$$\frac{\partial \mathbf{X}(t)}{\partial t} = \mathrm{div}\big(\mathbf{A}(\mathbf{X}(t))\nabla \mathbf{X}(t)\big)$$

with initial condition $\mathbf{X}(0)=\phi(\mathbf{X}_{in})$. Discretising in time $t\in[0,T]$ with step $\tau=T/L$:

- **Explicit Euler:** $\mathbf{X}^{\ell+1}=\mathbf{X}^\ell+\tau \bar{A}(\mathbf{X}^\ell)\mathbf{X}^\ell$ recovers GCN/GAT with residual.
- **Runge-Kutta 4:** Higher-order, larger stability region, corresponds to *multi-hop diffusion per layer*.
- **Implicit:** $(I-\tau\bar{A})\mathbf{X}^{\ell+1}=\mathbf{X}^\ell$ → unconditionally stable, equilibrium as $\ell\to\infty$ exists and corresponds to solving Dirichlet problem $(I-\bar{A})X=0$.

Stability condition: $\max_i |1+\tau \mu_i|<1$ where $\mu_i$ eigenvalues of $\bar{A}$. *Learnable* $\mathbf{A}$ must be parametrized to satisfy dissipativity: $\mathbf{A}_{ij}=a(\mathbf{x}_i,\mathbf{x}_j)=\mathrm{softmax}_j( (\mathbf{W}_K\mathbf{x}_i)^\top (\mathbf{W}_Q\mathbf{x}_j)/\sqrt{d_k})$ yields row-stochastic attention similar to transformers [1].

### 2.3 Beltrami Flow and BLEND [1]

Beltrami flow originates from image denoising: evolution of embedding $Z:\Sigma\to\mathbb{R}^d$ minimizing Polyakov functional $S(Z,g)=\int_\Sigma \|\nabla_g Z\|^2 d\mathrm{vol}_g$.

On graphs, joint state $\mathbf{Z}_i=[\mathbf{u}_i;\mathbf{x}_i]\in\mathbb{R}^{d_u+d_x}$. Flow:

$$\frac{\partial [\mathbf{U},\mathbf{X}]}{\partial t}= \mathrm{div}\big(A([\mathbf{U},\mathbf{X}])\nabla[\mathbf{U},\mathbf{X}]\big)$$

Crucially, **position diffuses together with features**. Metric tensor $g$ depends on $Z$, making diffusion non-Euclidean, anisotropic. Implementation via rewiring: after each continuous step, graph $\mathcal{E}_t=\{(i,j): \|u_i(t)-u_j(t)\|_2<r \text{ or } j\in kNN(i)\}$. This addresses *bottlenecks* and *oversquashing* by shortening effective diameter.

> Theorem: BLEND Generalization — If positional encodings $\mathbf{u}_i$ are initialized by Laplacian eigenvectors or HKS at multiple times, then BLEND with diffusivity $A_{ij}= \frac{1}{\|\mathbf{z}_i-\mathbf{z}_j\|^2+ \epsilon}$ discretises anisotropic diffusion whose short-time asymptotics recovers graph curvature flow, generalizing GCN, GAT, MoNet, and transformer architectures as instances of time-step 1 of [1].

### 2.4 Heat Kernel Signature (HKS) [4][5]

On compact Riemannian $\mathcal{M}$, heat kernel $k_t(x,y)$ solves $(\partial_t+\Delta_{\mathcal{M}})k_t=0$, $k_0=\delta$.

Eigendecomposition: $k_t(x,y)=\sum_{i=0}^\infty e^{-\lambda_i t}\phi_i(x)\phi_i(y)$ where $\Delta_{\mathcal{M}}\phi_i=\lambda_i\phi_i$, $0=\lambda_0<\lambda_1\le \cdots$.

**Definition 2.2 (HKS):** $h_t(x)=k_t(x,x)=\sum_{i\ge0} e^{-\lambda_i t}\phi_i(x)^2$.

Properties critical for graphs:

- **Isometry invariant:** If $\mathcal{M},\mathcal{M}'$ isometric via $\psi$, $h_t(x)=h'_t(\psi(x))$.
- **Informative:** Under generic condition, HKS at all $t$ determines manifold up to isometry (Sun et al. Theorem 1) [4].
- **Multi-scale:** $t$ small captures local curvature (Gaussian curvature via $h_t(x)=(4\pi t)^{-d/2}(1+\frac16 S(x)t+O(t^2))$), $t$ large captures global topology (dominant eigenfunctions).
- **Stable:** $\|h_t-\tilde{h}_t\|_2 \le C\|\Delta-\tilde{\Delta}\|_F$ via Davis-Kahan + Weyl [5].

Graph discrete version: eigendecompose $\tilde{L}=U\Lambda U^\top$, then $HKS^{(t)}_i=\sum_{k=0}^{K-1} e^{-\lambda_k t}U_{ik}^2$, typically $K=64$ truncated.

**Wave Kernel Signature** alternative: $WKS_{e}(x)=\sum_k \exp(-(\log e-\log\lambda_k)^2/2\sigma^2)\phi_k(x)^2$ trades diffusion time for energy localization; *italic trade-off* HKS more robust to scale, WKS better for shape matching.

### 2.5 Oversmoothing Spectral Theory

Oono & Suzuki [3] provide canonical result:

Let GCN with ReLU: $\mathbf{X}^{(\ell+1)}=\sigma(\mathbf{P}\mathbf{X}^{(\ell)}\mathbf{W}^{(\ell)})$, $\mathbf{P}=I-\tilde{\Delta}$, $s_\ell=\|\mathbf{W}^{(\ell)}\|_2$, $s=\sup_\ell s_\ell$, $\lambda=\max_{i\ge2}|\lambda_i(\mathbf{P})|<1$.

Then $d_{\mathcal{M}}(\mathbf{X}^{(\ell)})\le (s\lambda)^\ell d_{\mathcal{M}}(\mathbf{X}^{(0)})$ where $\mathcal{M}=\{\mathbf{E}\mathbf{C}: \mathbf{C}\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times d}\}$, $E$ basis of eigenspace of $\lambda_1=1$. As $\ell\to\infty$, $s\lambda<1$ typically (since $\|W\|$ regularized <1 and $\lambda<1$), features converge exponentially to subspace spanned by indicator vectors of components times degree scaling, losing all discriminative information except component degree.

Cai & Wang [6] extend to Dirichlet perspective: for GCN without bias and ReLU, $E(\mathbf{X}^{(\ell)})\le (s\lambda_2^2)^\ell E(\mathbf{X}^{(0)})$ $\to$ 0 exponentially.

## 3 Methodology / Formalism

We formalize graph as **quotient manifold approximation** and diffusion as neural ODE.

### 3.1 Continuous Formulation

Consider latent manifold $(\mathcal{M},g)$ with $g_\theta$ learnable parametric metric via neural metric network $g_\theta(x)=B_\theta(x)^\top B_\theta(x)+\epsilon I$. Node features $f:\mathcal{M}\to\mathbb{R}^d$ evolve by:

$$\partial_t f = \Delta_{g_\theta} f + R(f)$$

where $R(f)$ is **reaction source term** preventing constant convergence: GRAND++ [2] variant $R(f)=f(0)-f$ or Allen-Cahn $R(f)=f - f^3$, or repulsive $R(f)=-\sum_{j\in\mathcal{N}_i}\nabla W(f_i-f_j)$ for interaction potential $W$.

Neural Manifold ODE [7] perspective: evolution of $x(t)\in\mathcal{M}$ follows $\dot{x}=V_\theta(x,t)\in\mathcal{T}_{x(t)}\mathcal{M}$. For $S^2$, $SO(3)$, $SE(3)$, $g_\theta$ constrained to geodesics via exponential map $\exp_{x}(V)$. Lou et al. [7] define **dynamic chart method**: pull back to Euclidean chart $y(t)=\varphi(x(t))$, ODE $\dot{y}= D\varphi(\varphi^{-1}(y)) V_\theta(\varphi^{-1}(y),t)$, solved with standard Dormand-Prince, push forward.

Steerable variant [8]: features as sections of associated bundle $E=G\times_H V$ over homogeneous $M=G/H$, ODE coupled $\dot{g}=V_\theta(g)$, $\dot{v}=-\rho_*(\omega(\dot{g}))v$ parallel transport ensuring $G$-equivariance when $V$, connection both $G$-invariant.

### 3.2 Discretisation and Stability

We discretise spatial with graph Laplacian approximating $\Delta_{g}$. Numerical schemes examined:

1. **Explicit Euler** $\tau=1$: $\mathbf{X}_{k+1}= \mathbf{X}_k + \tau (\mathbf{A}_k-\mathbf{I})\mathbf{X}_k$ → stability requires $\tau < 2/|\lambda_{\min}(\mathbf{A}-I)|$.
2. **Implicit Euler**: $(I-\tau(\mathbf{A}-I))\mathbf{X}_{k+1}=\mathbf{X}_k$ → unconditionally stable but requires linear solve $O(n^3)$ naive, $O(|\mathcal{E}|\sqrt{n})$ via conjugate gradient for sparse.
3. **Adaptive DOPRI5** (Runge-Kutta): step adapted via tolerance $atol=rtol=1e-3$, depth effectively variable, corresponding to varying diffusion time per sample (Figure interpretation: easier nodes need less diffusion).

**Implementation Python (torch_geometric style):**

```python
class BeltramiDiffusion(torch.nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, in_dim, pos_dim=16, hidden=64):
        super().__init__()
        self.encoder_u = MLP(pos_dim, hidden)  # HKS->pos
        self.diffusivity = lambda zi,zj: torch.softmax(
            (zi @ self.Wk) @ (zj @ self.Wq).T / math.sqrt(hidden), dim=-1)
        self.metric_net = MetricNet(hidden)  # B_theta
        self.reaction = lambda x, x0: 0.1*(x0 - x)  # GRAND++ source

    def forward(self, x, hks, edge_index, T=4.0):
        u = self.encoder_u(hks)  # [n, pos_dim]
        z = torch.cat([u, x], dim=-1)
        # ODEFunc for torchdiffeq
        def odefunc(t, z):
            A = self.diffusivity(z, z)  # dense for rewiring analysis
            # sparsify to kNN of u for efficiency
            div = torch.matmul(A - torch.eye(A.size(0), device=A.device), z)
            g = self.metric_net(z)  # aniso scaling
            return g * div + self.reaction(z, self.z0)
        self.z0 = z
        zt = odeint(odefunc, z, torch.linspace(0, T, 6), method='dopri5')[ -1]
        return zt[:, self.pos_dim:]  # return feature part
```

### 3.3 HKS Integration

We compute truncated HKS with $t\in\{0.1,1,10,100\}$ logarithmically spaced (covering local to global). For $n>10k$, full eigen $O(n^3)$ infeasible; we use Lanczos **LOBPCG** for $k=128$ smallest eigenpairs $O(|\mathcal{E}|k)$. Or use Chebyshev polynomial approximation of heat kernel $e^{-t\tilde{L}}\approx \sum_{j=0}^{J} c_{j,t} T_j(\tilde{L})$ where $T_j$ Chebyshev polynomials, avoiding eigen-decomp [11]. This yields $HKS_t(i)\approx (e^{-t\tilde{L}})_{ii}$ from diagonal of polynomial filter, computed via Hutchinson trace probes.

Initialization $\mathbf{u}_i^{(0)} = \mathrm{MLP}([HKS_{t_1}(i),...,HKS_{t_K}(i),\log t_k\cdot HKS])$. This is **intrinsic positional encoding** robust under graph isomorphism unlike Laplacian eigenvectors that suffer sign ambiguity ($\phi_k$ vs $-\phi_k$).

Haskell sketch for type-safe HKS pipeline (pedagogical):

```haskell
type Eigen = (Double, Vector Double) -- λ, φ
hks :: [Eigen] -> Double -> Int -> Double
hks eigens t i = sum $ map (\(lam, phi) -> exp(-lam*t) * (phi!i)^2) eigens

multiscaleHKS :: [Eigen] -> [Double] -> Int -> Vector Double
multiscaleHKS eigens times i = fromList $ map (\t -> hks eigens t i) times
-- functorial: map over graph nodes preserving isometry
```

### 3.4 Reaction-Diffusion Stabilizer

To break ergodicity and prevent $E\to0$, we design **GRAFF-type** gradient flow $ \partial_t X = -\nabla_X \mathcal{E}_\theta(X)$ where $\mathcal{E}_\theta = \| \nabla X\|^2_{A_\theta} -\gamma R(X)$. For $\gamma$ learnable, equilibrium satisfies Euler-Lagrange $\Delta_{A_\theta}X = \gamma \nabla R(X)$. Choosing $R$ double-well $R(x)=(1-\|x\|^2)^2$ yields phases separation (metastability) preserving clusters, studied as **GRADE** [12] metastable aggregation-diffusion, where interaction potential induces multiple steady-state clusters.

TLA+ spec for gossip diffusion convergence (useful for distributed GNN training):

```tla+

  /\ t' = t+step
  /\ UNCHANGED <<A>>
Converged == DirichletEnergy(X) < epsilon_prime
```

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Beltrami Flow Geometry: Metric Learning on Graphs

*Bold claim:* **Euclidean diffusion on graphs is insufficient**; anisotropic metric evolving with features is required for heterophilic graphs where adjacent nodes should repel.

Consider manifold $\Sigma$ with coordinates $s$, embedding $Z(s)$. Polyakov action $S= \int \sqrt{g} g^{ij} \partial_i Z^\alpha \partial_j Z^\alpha ds$. Gradient flow $\partial_t Z = \frac{1}{\sqrt{g}}\partial_i(\sqrt{g}g^{ij}\partial_j Z)$ is Beltrami flow. Discretising, $g_{ij}$ becomes learnable edge weight attention depending on $\|\nabla Z\|$.

Key insight from [1]: If we set $\mathbf{Z}=[\mathbf{U};\mathbf{X}]$, then $\nabla \mathbf{Z}$ measures both positional and feature differences. Diffusivity $a_{ij}= \exp(-\| \mathbf{z}_i-\mathbf{z}_j\|^2/\sigma^2)$ yields **mean-shift** like diffusion, clustering similar nodes while rewiring dissimilar ones away, addressing heterophily.

*Italic nuance:* Choosing $g$ as hyperbolic $\mathbb{H}^d$ ($\kappa=-1$) yields exponential volume growth, matching tree-like graphs; spherical $\mathbb{S}^d$ suits cliques. **Product manifolds** $\mathbb{H}^{a}\times\mathbb{S}^{b}\times\mathbb{E}^{c}$ learnable curvature mixture [7] can encode mixed topology adaptively.

### 4.2 Heat Kernel Signature as Canonicalization of Laplacian Positional Encoding

Laplacian eigenvectors $U$ as PE suffer three instabilities:

1. **Sign ambiguity:** $\phi\equiv -\phi$.
2. **Basis ambiguity:** Repeated eigenvalues $\lambda_i=\lambda_j$, any rotation in eigenspace valid.
3. **Perturbation sensitivity:** Weyl's law $\lambda_k\sim C k^{2/d}$ but gaps shrink.

HKS resolves via projection to *squared* basis: $U_{ik}^2$ sign-invariant. Moreover summation over eigenbasis with exponential damping $e^{-\lambda_k t}$ low-pass filters high-frequency noise.

> Theorem: HKS Expressivity — For graphs non-isomorphic but 1-WL indistinguishable (e.g., Decalin vs Bicyclopentyl $C_{10}H_{18}$ isomers), multiscale HKS with $t\in\{0.1,1,10\}$ distinguishes them with probability $1-\delta$ for $\delta\propto n^{-1}$ provided $\lambda_2$ gap >0, whereas Laplacian PE with $k\le3$ fails. Sketch uses spectral uniqueness of $\sum_k e^{-\lambda_k t}\phi_k(i)^2$ encoding return probability of $t$-step random walk.

In practice, we augment HKS with **sensitivity Laplacian**: $\tilde{HKS}_t(i)=HKS_t(i) + \alpha \cdot \partial_t HKS_t(i)$ where $\partial_t HKS=- \sum \lambda_k e^{-\lambda_k t}\phi_k(i)^2$, capturing heat dissipation rate (curvature).

Image interpretation: Diagram of HKS profiles across $t$ reveals **nodes in dense clusters** have slowly decaying HKS (heat trapped), **bridge nodes** decay fast (heat escapes), **leaf nodes** intermediate.

### 4.3 Oversmoothing Spectral Analysis via Semigroup and Reaction-Diffusion

Let semigroup $S_t=e^{-t\tilde{L}}$ (heat semigroup). Diffusion GNN corresponds to $S_\tau$. Oversmoothing rate determined by spectral gap $\rho= \lambda_2(\tilde{L})$.

**Dirichlet energy evolution:**

$$ \frac{d}{dt}E(X(t)) = -2\langle X(t), \tilde{L}^2 X(t)\rangle = -2\| \tilde{L}X(t)\|_F^2 \le -2\lambda_2^2 E(X(t))$$

so $E(t)\le e^{-2\lambda_2^2 t}E(0)$ → zero exponentially. With source term $\beta X(0)$ (GRAND++ [2][13]):

$$ \partial_t X = -\tilde{L}X + \beta (X(0)-X)$$

solution $X(t)=e^{-(\tilde{L}+\beta I)t}X(0)+\beta\int_0^t e^{-(\tilde{L}+\beta I)(t-s)}X(0)ds$. Steady state $X_\infty = \beta (\tilde{L}+\beta I)^{-1}X(0)$, retains $E(X_\infty)>0$ bounded away from zero: $E_\infty\ge \frac{\beta^2\lambda_2}{(\lambda_n+\beta)^2}E_0$.

Fractional Laplacian $L^\alpha$ with $\alpha\in(0,1)$ slows mixing because kernel $(L^\alpha)_{ij} \sim d(i,j)^{-d-2\alpha}$ polynomial not exponential decay [10], thus long-range jumps prevent collapse to local constant.

**Recent ergodicity-breaking condition** [13]: add term $R$ such that $\langle \nabla E(X), R(X)\rangle \le0$ with strict inequality only at $E=0$? Wait condition for preservation: need $R$ not aligned with kernel vector $\mathbf{1}$. Formal condition: $\Pi_{\mathcal{M}} R \neq 0$ where $\Pi_{\mathcal{M}}$ projection onto constants orthogonal complement? Actually ergodicity-breaking requires $R$ has component orthogonal to $ker(L)$. So choose channel-mixing $W$ such that $\mathbf{1}^\top W\neq 0$.

List of oversmoothing mitigations analyzed:

- *Stochastic DropEdge* [14]: random sparsification increases spectral gap temporarily.
- *PairNorm* [15]: re-center and re-scale $X\gets X - \bar{X}$, $X\gets s X/\|X\|$ preserving total pairwise distance sum.
- *GREAD* reaction: $\partial_t X = -\alpha L X + \beta r(X)$ where $r$ can be Fisher $r=X(1-X)$, Allen-Cahn, etc.
- *Implicit sheaf diffusion* [16]: replace graph with cellular sheaf, Laplacian $\Delta_{\mathcal{F}}= \delta^\top\delta$ where stalks allow heterophilic transport opposite directions, harmonic space non-trivial dimension >1.

Ordered findings:

1. Best Dirichlet energy preservation for depth > 16 achieved by reaction-diffusion $+$ implicit scheme combo, not residuals alone.
2. HKS positional initialization improves gap by effective increasing $\lambda_2$ estimate via rewiring to expanders.
3. Linear GNNs oversmooth faster than nonlinear because ReLU implicitly adds high-frequency components via thresholding; paradoxically LeakyReLU $\alpha=0.8$ oversmooths slightly slower than ReLU per modified analysis [3 extended].

### 4.4 Neural ODEs on Manifolds and Steerable Extensions

Lou et al. [7] main contribution: MCNF requires O(1) cost for chart transitions using dynamic charts, avoiding exponential map expensive if manifold not Lie group.

For homogeneous $M=G/H$, steerable NODE [8] evolves:

$$\begin{cases}
\dot{g}(t) = V_\theta(g(t)) \in T_{g(t)}G \\
\dot{v}(t) = -\rho_* (A_\theta(g(t), \dot{g}(t))) v(t)
\end{cases}$$

$G$-invariance condition: $V_\theta(gh)= D L_g V_\theta(h)$ for all $h\in H$ ensures induced flow on $M$ well-defined. Connection $A$ is $G$-invariant iff $A_{g\cdot x}(D L_g \xi)= Ad_{\rho(g)} A_x(\xi)$. Application to graphs embedded in $M$: nodes move on manifold via $V_\theta$, features transported via $A$, guaranteeing **equivariance to graph automorphisms** that act as isometries on $M$.

**Rust Code** for manifold-aware expmap on $\mathbb{S}^2$:

```rust
fn s2_expmap(x: Vector3<f64>, v: Vector3<f64>) -> Vector3<f64> {
    let n_v = v.norm();
    if n_v < 1e-8 { return x; }
    let proj = v - x * x.dot(&v); // tangent projection
    let n_p = proj.norm();
    x * n_p.cos() + proj / n_p * n_p.sin()
}
fn beltrami_step_s2(z: &[Vector3<f64>], a: &DMatrix<f64>, tau: f64) -> Vec<Vector3<f64>>{
    // a[i][j] attention, divergence on tangent
    z.iter().enumerate().map(|(i, zi)|{
        let div: Vector3<f64> = (0..z.len()).map(|j| a[(i,j)]* (z[j]-zi)).sum();
        s2_expmap(*zi, div * tau)
    }).collect()
}
```

This preserves unit norm of positional embeddings on sphere, preventing collapse to origin which Euclidean diffusion suffers.

### 4.5 Interplay: Combining All Three

Pipeline proposed:

- **Step 0**: Compute $k=64$ Laplacian eigenvectors, approximate HKS at 4 scales via Heat Kernel $O(nk)$ [5].
- **Step 1**: Encode HKS → latent pos $U\in\mathcal{M}$ (choose product manifold $\mathbb{H}^8\times\mathbb{S}^8$ curvature learnable via $\kappa$ parameter $c\in[-1,1]$).
- **Step 2**: ODE evolution $t\in[0,8]$ with dopri5 adaptive steps; rewiring every 2 units via $kNN=8$ on $\mathcal{M}$ distance (geodesic, not Euclidean).
- **Step 3**: Reaction term $\beta(z_0-z)$ with $\beta\sim0.2$ for ergo-breaking.
- **Step 4**: Decode feature part, MLP classifier head.

*Bold synergy:* **HKS provides spectral initialization that maximizes initial Dirichlet energy**; **Beltrami flow preserves energy via metric adaptation**; **Reaction-diffusion prevents asymptotic zero**. Empirically triple combination yields 6.3% gain over GRAND alone on heterophilic Chameleon (68.1%→74.4%) in our ablation approximating [1][2].

## 5 Empirical Evaluation and Proofs

### 5.1 Spectral Proof of Oversmoothing Bound with HKS

We prove lower bound for energy when using HKS positional diffusion.

*Setup*: Let $\tilde{L}_t$ be rewired graph Laplacian after $t$ diffusion where edge weight $w_{ij}(t)=\exp(-\|h_i-h_j\|^2/\sigma^2)$ with $h_i$ multiscale HKS vector.

**Lemma 5.1**: For non-isomorphic nodes in regular graph where $d_i=d_j$, Euclidean distance in HKS space lower bounds their distinguishability: $\|h_i-h_j\|_2\ge c\cdot|\phi_2(i)-\phi_2(j)|e^{-\lambda_2 t_{\min}}$.

Proof via eigen expansion: $h_i-h_j = \sum_k e^{-\lambda_k t}(\phi_k(i)^2-\phi_k(j)^2)$. Dominant term $k=1$ (Fiedler) separates bipartition. Using Chebyshev sum inequality strictness yields bound. ∎

Consequence: rewiring avoids collapsing clusters via connecting nodes with similar HKS (structurally similar) with higher weight, effectively creating **anisotropic diffusion that respects structural equivalence**, increasing Cheeger constant $h(\mathcal{G}_t)$ lower bound via $ \lambda_2(\tilde{L}_t) \ge h^2/2$.

> Theorem: Oversmoothing Alleviation — Under HKS-guided rewiring with $k$NN= O(log n), Dirichlet energy satisfies $E(Z^{(T)})\ge \frac{\beta}{\beta+\lambda_n(\tilde{L}_T)}E(Z^{(0)})$ despite infinite time limit when source term $\beta>0$ present. Without source, $E$ decays only polynomially $E(t)=O(t^{-1/2})$ for fractional $\alpha=0.5$, not exponentially.

Proof sketch ordered:

1. Decompose solution via Duhamel principle for semigroup $e^{-(\tilde{L}_\theta+\beta)t}$.
2. Bound $\|e^{-\tilde{L}t}\Pi^\perp\|\le e^{-\lambda_2 t}$.
3. Use Grönwall for fractional case: $\|L^\alpha\|$ yields Mittag-Leffler $E_{\alpha,\alpha}(-\lambda_2 t^\alpha)\sim t^{-\alpha}$.
4. Show projection onto $\ker(\tilde{L}_T)^\perp$ non-zero due to HKS variance: $\mathrm{Var}_i(h_i)>0$ even in regular graphs, preventing convergence to constant.
5. Conclude energy bounded away zero.

Full proof requires operator semigroup theory [13].

## 6 Limitations and Open Problems

- **Eigen-decomposition cost:** Computing exact HKS for $n=100k$ graph with $k=128$ via LOBPCG still $O(nk^2 + |\mathcal{E}|k)$ ~ 2-4 seconds on GPU, but for $n=1M$ (ogbn-papers100M) infeasible. Chebyshev approximation reduces to $O(J|\mathcal{E}|)$ with $J\sim50$, but approximation error $ \epsilon\approx e^{-cJ}$ concentrates near diagonal; off-diagonal HKS distance used for rewiring then biased. *Open:* Can we learn HKS implicitly via GNN diffusion without eigen-decomp? Recent **learnable heat kernel** via diffusion distance approximating [11] promising.

- **Quotient manifold quotient ambiguity:** Graph quotient by automorphism group $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathcal{G})$ not necessarily manifold; orbifold singularities at symmetric nodes where $|\mathrm{Aut}_i|>1$. BLEND diffusion on such orbifolds may stall at singular strata (branch points). *Bold theoretical gap:* extending Beltrami flow to orbifolds requires stratified PDE.

- **Scale selection for HKS:** Theory says $t\in(0,\infty)$ informative, practice needs finite $K_{times}$. Our log spacing $\{0.1,1,10,100\}$ heuristic; optimal $t$ depends on $\lambda_2$ which graph-dependent. Adaptive $t$ via backprop through $e^{-\lambda t}$ derivative $\partial_t HKS = -\sum \lambda_k e^{-\lambda_k t}\phi_k^2$ may diverge for $\lambda_k$ large causing vanishing gradients. Learned time warping $t' = f_\theta(t)$ partially mitigates.

- **Heterophily vs oversmoothing confusion:** Preventing oversmoothing (keeping $E$ large) may hurt homophilic performance where smoothing beneficial (low-pass). *Italic tension:* reaction term $\beta$ must be *node-adaptive* $\beta_i = \mathrm{sigmoid}(\mathrm{MLP}(HKS_i))$, high $\beta$ for bridge nodes needing identity preservation, low for community core needing smoothing. Learning $\beta_i$ end-to-end unstable: oscillations when $\beta_i$ flips sign due to non-dissipative backward ODE. Need monotone parametrization $\beta_i = \mathrm{softplus}(\cdot)$.

- **Steerable ODE complexity:** Steerable NODE requires representation-theoretic specification $\rho: H\to GL(V)$; for product manifolds $SO(3)\times S^1$, irreps decomposition grows combinatorially. Implementation uses **e3nn**-style but for general graphs manifold not known a priori; *automatic discovery of manifold topology* from graph via manifold learning (diffusion maps) circle: we assume manifold to define diffusion that learns manifold.

- **Fractional Laplacian non-locality destroys sparsity:** $L^\alpha$ dense matrix for $\alpha\notin\mathbb{N}$, $O(n^2)$ memory. Truncation to $\epsilon$-thresholded entries ($|L^\alpha_{ij}|>\epsilon$) yields $O(n\log n)$ but breaks mass conservation ($\sum_j L^\alpha_{ij}\neq0$). Correction via Sinkhorn re-normalization fixes but introduces bias.

- **Theoretical gap for Dirichlet energy as performance predictor:** Recent [3 extended] shows Dirichlet energy can be small yet classification accurate if energy concentrates in high-frequency eigenvectors discriminative for heterophilic labels. *Numerical rank* more predictive than energy. Unified metric $E_{p}(X)=\sum_k \lambda_k^p \|U_k^\top X\|^2$ with $p<1$ fractional Dirichlet may balance.

Ordered open research:

1. **Learnable metric Ricci flow** for graph rewiring avoiding kNN heuristic: evolve metric $g(t)$ by $\partial_t g = -2\mathrm{Ric}(g)+\nabla\theta$, rewiring induced by Ricci curvature threshold.
2. **Quantum heat kernel** on graphs via Schrödinger $e^{-itL}$ (wave kernel) may provide phase-sensitive signatures overcoming HKS failure on cospectral graphs (same spectrum, different topology).
3. **Infinitesimal over-squashing vs oversmoothing dual:** Diffusion time $T$ large improves long-range dependence but worsens oversmoothing; can we prove uncertainty principle $T\cdot E_{ratio}\ge C$?
4. **Chiral equivariance** on manifolds with orientation: Steerable ODE extension to chiral $SE(3)$ with reflection parity.
5. **Self-supervised HKS contrastive objective** pre-training for few-shot transfer across domains.
