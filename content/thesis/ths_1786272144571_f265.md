# Neuromorphic Silicon Photonic Tensor Core Calibration for Deep Learning: Clements-Reck Mach-Zehnder Mesh Synthesis, Phase-Change Material Nonvolatile Nonlinearity, Non-Coherent WDM Broadcast-and-Weight Protocols, and Backpropagation Through Analog Photonic Process Variation

## Abstract
Silicon photonic tensor cores promise >100× energy efficiency for deep learning by implementing $O(N^2)$ matrix-vector multiplication in $O(1)$ optical time via coherent Mach-Zehnder interferometer (MZI) meshes. Scaling to $N>64$ exposes three physical bottlenecks: optimal unitary synthesis under fabrication loss, nonvolatile nonlinear activation without O/E conversion, and on-chip gradient transport through analog process variation. This thesis unifies Clements and Reck universal multiport decompositions for calibration using local variance-based $\lvert\sin\theta\rvert$/$\lvert\cos\phi\rvert$ statistics, Ge$_2$Sb$_2$Te$_5$ and Sb$_2$S$_3$ phase-change material (PCM) nonvolatile photonic memories emulating synaptic nonlinearity and self-learning spiking, non-coherent wavelength-division multiplexed (WDM) broadcast-and-weight protocols via microring resonator banks and balanced photodetection, and bidirectional grating-tap in-situ backpropagation that preserves analog gradient fidelity. We demonstrate how integrated Stanford/Bell Labs protocols achieve >90% classification with systematic error tolerance, enabling heterogeneous in-memory training.

## 1 Introduction

Deep neural networks (DNNs) are bottlenecked by von Neumann separation: moving weights dominates FLOPs energy [1]. Photonics offers analog alternative where *linear propagation* itself is computation. Shen et al. 2017 [1] first demonstrated programmable nanophotonic processor with **56 MZIs** in silicon performing vowel recognition at 77% accuracy, projecting $2$ orders speed and $3$ orders power advantage.

> Theorem: Any $N \times N$ unitary $U(N)$ can be decomposed into $N(N-1)/2$ two-mode rotations $T_{m,n}(\theta,\phi)$ implementable by one MZI with two phase shifters. Extension to arbitrary $M \times N$ real $A = U \Sigma V^\dagger$ requires SVD with attenuation.

Photonic scaling stalls on four axes: **Calibration** thermo-optic crosstalk and coupler imbalance, **Nonlinearity & memory** requiring O/E interruption, **Interconnect** phase sensitivity, **Training** digital twin mismatch. We synthesize heterogeneous tensor core: Clements-calibrated coherent front-end, PCM nonvolatile synaptic array, WDM broadcast-and-weight routing, analog backpropagation.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 MZI Unitary Synthesis

A 2×2 MZI: two 50/50 directional couplers, internal $\theta$, external $\phi$:

$$ T_{m,n}(\theta,\phi) = \begin{bmatrix} e^{i\phi}\sin\theta & e^{i\phi}\cos\theta \\ \cos\theta & -\sin\theta \end{bmatrix} $$

Embedding into $N$ dims zeros elsewhere. Any $U \in U(N)$: $U = D \prod T_{m,n}$.

Reck triangular [2] depth $2N-3$ unbalanced loss. Clements rectangular [2] depth $N$, balanced loss, half footprint, robust to imbalanced splitters [3]. Braid architecture [3] further reduces layer count with crossings 0.05 dB.

Miller 2015 self-configuring meshes compensate arbitrary 85:15 splitters via progressive nulling using power sensors.

### 2.2 Process Variation

- Directional coupler imbalance $\kappa=0.5+\delta\kappa$, $\delta\kappa\sim\mathcal{N}(0,0.05)$.
- TiN heaters $P_{\pi}\approx20$ mW, thermal crosstalk $C_{ij}\sim0.1$.
- Balanced MZI dummy bends maintain wavelength independence.

Conventional calibration isolates nodes via reference routes $O(N^3)$. Local variance method [3] avoids isolation: perturb $\theta_i$, measure variance $\sigma^2$ across random background ensemble. Theory $\sigma^2_{MZI}\propto|\sin\theta|$, $\sigma^2_{PS}\propto|\cos\phi|$.

### 2.3 PCM Photonics

GST: $n_{a}=4.38,k=0.09$ vs $n_{c}=5.59,k=0.71$ @1550 nm $\Delta n=1.21$ [4]. $L_{\pi}\sim30$ µm photonic vs 3 µm plasmonic.

Operations:

- Amorphization 5×5 ps 600 pJ melt-quench >600°C.
- Crystallization 200 ns 200 pJ 150°C.
- Retention $>10^5$ s, endurance $10^{12}$ Sb$_2$S$_3$ low-loss [5].
- Multilevel 8 levels via pulse energy staircase.

16×16 matrix memory 256 cells 512 bits 1000×2400 µm² Si₃N₄ [4], two MRR wavelength select. Tensor core convolution via Si₃N₄ soliton comb pumping 40 lines, data on comb, kernels in PCM transmission.

PCM nonlinearity: Feldmann Nature 569 2019 all-optical spiking neurosynaptic self-learning STDP mimics LIF threshold.

### 2.4 Broadcast-and-Weight

Tait 2014 [6][7]:

1. Broadcast $N$ outputs at $\lambda_i$ on single bus.
2. Weight MRR bank tunable $w_i\in[-1,1]$ via balanced detectors $w=(P_{drop}-P_{thru})/(P_{drop}+P_{thru})$.
3. Sum photocurrent $\sum w_i x_i$, drive modulator neuron.

Advantages: intensity $|E|^2$ insensitive phase, massive WDM fan-in >32, modulation-and-weight dual-functional MRR conserves area 50% [7], demonstrated image processing + financial time series.

*Italic*: *non-coherent loses unitary expressivity* compensated via nonlinearity and negative weights.

### 2.5 Backprop Photonics

Digital twin forward $y=f(Wx)$, backward $\partial L/\partial W$. Mismatch accumulates.

Hughes 2018 proposed optical backprop: adjoint field $\delta$ backwards yields $\partial L/\partial\theta\propto Im[E_{fwd}E_{bwd}^*]$ via grating taps [8]. Stanford/Milan 2022-23 6×6 triangular 15 MZIs TiN 3% tap IR camera fiber switch MEMS [8]. Achieved accuracies matching digital.

Bell Labs 2026 [10] end-to-end on-chip gradient-descent with integrated nonlinearity gradient electro-optic photodiode tap.

Hybrid ex-situ differentiable twin + in-situ SPSA fine-tune 20 epochs robust thermal crosstalk 0.43% degradation [2].

### 4.1 Clements-Reck Calibration and Braid Robustness

| Property | Reck Triangular [2] | Clements Rectangular [2] | Braid [3] |
|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Depth | $2N-3$ | $N$ | $\approx N/2+\log N$ |
| Loss balance | Unbalanced | Balanced | Balanced+cross |
| Tolerance [3] | Poor | Good | Best |
| Footprint | $O(N^2)$ | 0.5 $O(N^2)$ | +0.02 dB crossing |
| Ops | $O(N^2)$ isolation | $O(N^2)$ variance | Same |

> Theorem: Self-configuration with embedded photodetectors compensates $\kappa\in[0.15,0.85]$ yielding ideal unitary if Miller progressive nulling. Variance minima valid under random neighbors [3].

Two-stage flow: stage1 randomize $\phi$, sweep $\theta$, fit $\sigma^2=A|\sin\theta|+B$ dip bar $\theta=0$ cross $\pi/2$; stage2 balance $\theta=\pi/4$ sweep $\phi$ $|\cos\phi|$ quadrature.

Python Clements:

```python
import numpy as np
def Clements(U):
    N=U.shape[0]
    thetas=np.zeros((N,N)); phis=np.zeros((N,N))
    for k in range(N):
        for i in range(N-k-1):
            r=np.hypot(abs(U[N-1-k,i]),abs(U[N-1-k,i+1]))
            th=np.arccos(abs(U[N-1-k,i])/r)
            ph=np.angle(U[N-1-k,i])-np.angle(U[N-1-k,i+1])
            T=np.eye(N,dtype=complex)
            T[i,i]= np.exp(1j*ph)*np.sin(th)
            T[i,i+1]= np.exp(1j*ph)*np.cos(th)
            T[i+1,i]= np.cos(th); T[i+1,i+1]= -np.sin(th)
            U=U @ T.conj().T
            thetas[i,k]=th; phis[i,k]=ph
    return thetas,phis

def variance_scan(theta_target,n_ens=100):
    outs=[abs(np.cos(theta_target)-np.sin(theta_target)*np.exp(1j*np.random.rand()*6.28))**2 for _ in range(n_ens)]
    return np.var(outs)
```

### 4.3 Non-Coherent WDM Broadcast-Weight

Comb Si3N4 microresonator 200 mW 40 lines 100 GHz Kerr soliton, DFB 8 λ optional. Modulators carrier-depletion 30 Gbps.

MRR bank cascaded radius tuned $w_i=2t-1$ Lorentzian $T_{drop}=(FWHM/2)^2/(\Delta\lambda^2+(FWHM/2)^2)$. Thermo 2 mW/nm balanced PD signed.

Sum $I=R\sum w_i P_i$ $R=1 A/W$ 20 GHz 10 GS/s cap.

Neuron MRR modulator [Tait 2019] recirculates distinct λ or PCM spiking.

Python:

```python
def mrr_weight(detune_nm,FWHM=0.1):
    T_drop=(FWHM/2)**2/(detune_nm**2+(FWHM/2)**2)
    return 2*T_drop-1
def broadcast_and_weight(x,w_mat):
    return [sum(xi*mrr_weight(wi) for xi,wi in zip(x,bank)) for bank in w_mat]
```

Crosstalk dense WDM <0.8 nm -20 dB weight error 0.05 still 92% vs 94% ideal [7].

### 4.4 Backpropagation Through Analog Variation

Bidirectional protocol [8][10]:

- Forward inject $x$ IQ $E=|x|e^{i\angle}$, $U(\theta)$ mesh, nonlinearity $f$ photodiode→digital early or analog on-chip [10].
- Loss $L=0.5||y-t||^2$.
- Backward inject error $e=(y-t)\odot f'(z)$ backwards $U^T$ via MEMS $2×2$ switch, taps 3% grating IR camera both dirs, gradient $\partial L/\partial\theta=-2 Im[E_{fwd}E_{bwd}^*]$, heater DAC analog update.

TLA+ reciprocal:

```tla
---- MODULE PhotonicBackprop ----
VARIABLES theta, fwd, bwd, grad
Forward == fwd' = MatVec(U(theta), x)
Backward == bwd' = MatVec(Transpose(U(theta)), err)
Grad == grad' = Im[fwd \oast Conj[bwd]]
Update == theta' = theta - eta*grad'
Spec == Forward /\ Backward /\ Grad /\ Update
====
```

Process variation: systematic 0.2 rad error collapses digital twin 96%→45% but in-situ recovers 91% 30 epochs [8]; Bell Labs matches digital >90% without digital computer despite fabrication variation.

Hybrid ex-situ 200 epochs GPU $\theta_0$ + SPSA 20 epochs in-situ fine-tune 0.43% degradation severe coupling [2].

## 6 Limitations & Threats

- **Scalability**: $N=64$ loss 25 dB $0.2 dB$/MZI requires SOA noise; braid crossings 0.02 dB still wall-plug inefficient >256 vs electronics. WDM fan-out $10\log10 N$+demux.
- **PCM**: GST drift 2% $10^4$ s; Sb₂S₃ slower 200 ns bottleneck; void formation $10^7$ plasmonic cycles.
- **Thermal**: 2016 heaters 32×32 40 W; undercut 4 mW/π still 8 W; $C$ T-dependent per-minute recalibration; Sb₂S₃ athermal helps not eliminate Si TO.
- **Backprop**: Tap 3% reduces SNR; IR camera 30 fps bottleneck vs GHz; switch PDL 0.2 dB bias; DAC 8-bit noise; deep $L>5$ backward power decays variance spikes.
- **Task**: MNIST 94% PCNN [2604] vs digital 99%; CIFAR binary >90% not ImageNet ResNet.
- Supply chain custom PDK cost high.

Future: 3D interposer ASIC flip-chip gradient, heterogeneous PCM+Sb₂S₃ memory hierarchy, braid+PCM zero-static, 80-line comb $N=80$ overtaking coherent depth.
