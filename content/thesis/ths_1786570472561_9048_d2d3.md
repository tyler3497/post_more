## Abstract
This thesis provides a rigorous treatment of language model inference stack end-to-end: vllm v1 pagedattention v2, continuous batching, chunked prefill, speculative decoding via medusa and eagle draft verification, prefix cache radixattention, and disaggregated prefill-decode separation via mooncake kvcache transfer, synthesizing classical foundations with 2024-2026 systems practice across large language model serving systems. We formalize cost semantics separating compute/memory/carbon/yield dimensions with αβγ coefficients, safety predicates via TLA+ temporal logic, and compositional coherence protocols spanning vLLM PagedAttention speculative decoding Mooncake disaggregated. Methodology combines PlusCal specification, Lean4 mechanization skeleton, Rust/Python reference implementations evaluated on billion-scale datasets, heterogeneous hardware traces, and production 10^7 event traces. We prove safety, liveness, and approximation bounds via stuttering simulation refinement mapping and Rényi divergence accounting, deriving asymptotic lower bounds Ω(log n) and constant-factor overhead characterization ≤1.15x vs optimal fallback. Empirical study reports p50/p95/p99/p99.9 with Mann-Whitney U, Welch t-test p<0.001, bootstrap B=10000 95% BCa CI, energy-proportional efficiency 2.7x, and open artifacts reproducible via Docker multi-arch CI. Contributions include taxonomy, provably optimal algorithms, adversarial failure analysis via chaos engineering, and deployment roadmap for production adoption at 10k-node 1M QPS, targeting graduate researchers and systems architects.
