# Graph Neural Networks Beyond Locality: Oversmoothing and Over-Squashing as Spectral-Curvature Duality, Ricci-Flow Rewiring, and Equivariant Message Passing for Heterophily and Long-Range Propagation

## Abstract
We unify the two central pathologies of deep graph neural networks — oversmoothing (Dirichlet collapse toward the kernel of the Laplacian) and over-squashing (exponential information compression through topological bottlenecks) — as dual manifestations of the spectral-curvature geometry of the underlying graph. Through Cheeger's inequality linking conductance to the Fiedler value and Ollivier–Ricci and Forman–Ricci analyses of local contraction, we formalize how adding edges to maximize the spectral gap alleviates over-squashing but accelerates mixing to the stationary distribution, thereby sharpening the trade-off identified by Giraldo et al. We review Stochastic Discrete Ricci Flow (SDRF), Batch Ollivier–Ricci Flow (BORF), First-Order Spectral Rewiring (FoSR), and effective-resistance rewiring (GTR), and derive first-order perturbation formulas for λ₂. For heterophily and long-range tasks, we contrast low-pass filtering bias against high-pass, signed and equivariant propagators, showing how Dirichlet energy constraints and rank-collapse diagnostics reveal oversmoothing beyond cosine similarity. Finally we propose an equivariant, curvature-aware relational framework preserving original topology while augmenting expansion.

We unify the two central pathologies of deep graph neural networks — ***oversmoothing*** and ***over-squashing*** — as dual manifestations of spectral-curvature geometry. Oversmoothing corresponds to Dirichlet energy $\mathcal{E}(H)=\text{tr}(H^T \tilde{L} H) \to 0$ under iterated low-pass smoothing, while over-squashing reflects vanishing Jacobian sensitivity $\|\partial h_v^{(L)} / \partial x_u\|_2 \le C \cdot \hat{A}^L_{v,u}$ through bottlenecks of low conductance [1][2][3]. We connect Cheeger's inequality $h_G^2/2 \le \lambda_2 \le 2h_G$ to mixing time, curvature to compression, and equivariant lifting to heterophily.

## 1. Introduction
Message Passing Neural Networks (MPNNs) iterate:

$$ h_v^{(l+1)} = \phi^{(l)}\left( h_v^{(l)}, \bigoplus_{u \in \mathcal{N}(v)\cup\{v\}} \psi^{(l)}(h_u^{(l)}, h_v^{(l)}, e_{uv}) \right) $$

popularized as GCN [7], GIN, GAT. Stacking $L$ layers expands receptive field to $L$-hop ball $|B_L(v)|$, which can grow exponentially in bounded-degree graphs. **Alon & Yahav** [1] observed that fixed-size vectors $h_v^{(L)} \in \mathbb{R}^d$ must compress this exponential growth — *over-squashing*.

Complementary, **Li et al.**, **Oono & Suzuki**, **Cai & Wang** showed that repeated application of normalized adjacency $P=\tilde{D}^{-1/2}\tilde{A}\tilde{D}^{-1/2}$ acts as Laplacian smoothing:

$$ H^{(l+1)} = \sigma(P H^{(l)} W^{(l)}) $$

Under mild conditions $H^{(l)} \to H^{(\infty)} \in \ker(\tilde{L})$, where Dirichlet energy collapses and node features become indistinguishable — *oversmoothing* [4][5].

> **Theorem 1 (Oversmoothing-Oversquashing Duality via Cheeger):** For connected non-bipartite graph $G$, let $P=D^{-1}A$ random walk matrix with eigenvalues $1=\mu_1 > |\mu_2| \ge ...$. Let $h(G)$ conductance. Then oversmoothing rate scales as $|\mu_2|^l = (1-\Theta(h^2))^l$, while over-squashing sensitivity is upper-bounded by $\mathcal{O}((1-h^2)^{d_{\mathcal{G}}(u,v)})$. A larger spectral gap $1-|\mu_2|$ improves long-range flow but accelerates collapse to $\pi$.

This duality was formalized by **Giraldo et al. 2023** and surveyed in **Rubio et al. 2024** [3][8]:

$$ 2h(G) \ge \frac{1}{s} \log \frac{\max_u\sqrt{d_u}}{\varepsilon \min_v\sqrt{d_v}} $$

where $s$ is mixing steps to $\varepsilon$-stationary. Fast mixing $\implies$ less bottleneck but faster oversmoothing. Slow mixing $\implies$ preservation of diversity but severe squashing.

The industry response bifurcated: **spatial rewiring** (SDRF [2], BORF [9], PIORF, GTR [10]), **spectral rewiring** (FoSR [6], Expander propagation, spectral pruning [11]), and **architectural** (GCNII with initial residual $\alpha h^{(0)} + (1-\alpha)P h$, PairNorm, DropEdge, EGNN energy-constrained [4], Framelet EEConv [12], rank-regularized depth [5]).

Heterophily adds twist: most homophilic GNNs assume neighbor similarity $h_{\text{hom}} \approx 1$. Under heterophily ($h_{\text{node}} \ll 0.3$), low-pass filtering destroys information. Long-range dependencies require signed, high-pass, or *equivariant* propagators.

Contributions:

1. Spectral-curvature formalism linking $\lambda_2$, $\kappa_{\text{Ollivier}}(u,v)$, effective resistance $R_{uv}$, Jacobian sensitivity.
2. Derivation of FoSR first-order $\Delta\lambda_2 \approx \frac{2\mu(u)\mu(v)}{(\sqrt{1+d_u})(\sqrt{1+d_v})}$ and Ricci-flow condition $\kappa<0 \implies$ bottleneck.
3. Unification with equivariant GNNs and heterophily via Lovász permutation equivariance and frame averaging.
4. Proposed **Curvature-Aware Relational Equivariant Diffusion (CARED)** architecture.

## 2. Background

### 2.1 Graph Spectral Theory
Let $A \in \{0,1\}^{n\times n}$, $D = \text{diag}(d_i)$, $\mathcal{L} = I - D^{-1/2} A D^{-1/2}$ normalized Laplacian, eigenvalues $0=\lambda_1 \le \lambda_2 \le ... \le \lambda_n \le 2$. Spectral gap $\lambda_2$ = algebraic connectivity. Fiedler vector $f_2$. Propagation matrix $P = \tilde{D}^{-1/2}\tilde{A}\tilde{D}^{-1/2}$ has $\mu_i = 1-\lambda_i$.

Dirichlet energy:

$$ \mathcal{E}(H) = \frac{1}{2} \sum_{(i,j)\in E} A_{\text{norm}}[i,j] \|h_i - h_j\|^2_2 = \text{tr}(H^T \tilde{L} H) $$

Oversmoothing: $\mathcal{E}(H^{(l)}) \to 0$ exponentially if $\|W^{(l)}\|_2 \le 1$ and $\lambda_2$ large.

Cheeger inequality: $$ \frac{\phi(G)^2}{2} \le \lambda_2(\mathcal{L}) \le 2\phi(G) $$ where $\phi(S)=|\partial S|/\min(\text{vol}(S),\text{vol}(V\setminus S))$, $\phi(G)=\min_S \phi(S)$.

### 2.2 Oversmoothing Formalization
**Definition (EGNN, Zhou et al. 2021)**: Let $E(H) = \mathcal{E}(H)$. A GNN oversmooths if $\lim_{l\to\infty} E(H^{(l)}) = 0$ and $\lim_{l\to\infty} \|H^{(l)} - \mathbf{1}c^T\| = 0$.

Oono & Suzuki (2020) prove for GCN with ReLU: $d_{\mathcal{M}}(H^{(l)}) \le s_l \lambda_{\max} d_{\mathcal{M}}(H^{(l-1)})$ with $s_l=\sigma_{\max}(W^{(l)})$, $\lambda_{\max} = \max_{i>1}|\mu_i|$. Therefore product $\prod s_l$ small prevents but also limits expressivity.

**Rank collapse** [5]: Deidda et al. propose effective rank $\text{Rank}_{\varepsilon}(H) = \|H\|_*^2/\|H\|_F^2$. Show rank $\to 1$ earlier than energy $\to 0$, explaining failure at depth 8-10 not 64.

### 2.3 Over-Squashing and Bottleneck
**Alon & Yahav 2020** [1]: sensitivity of output at $v$ to input at $u$ after $r$ layers:

$$ \left\| \frac{\partial h_v^{(r)}}{\partial x_u} \right\| \le (c_\sigma w)^r \underbrace{(\hat{A}^r)_{v,u}}_{\text{walks}} $$

If $d(v,u)=r$ but number of $r$-walks small due to bottleneck, sensitivity decays. With $b$ branching, need width $d = \Omega(c^{L})$.

Topping et al. 2021 [2] tie to *curvature*: edge $e=(u,v)$ with Ollivier-Ricci

$$ \kappa(u,v) = 1 - \frac{W_1(m_u, m_v)}{d(u,v)} $$

where $m_u$ distribution over neighbors (typically uniform $m_u(w)=1/d_u$ if $w\in\mathcal{N}(u)$), $W_1$ Wasserstein-1 optimal transport cost. If $\kappa(u,v) \ll 0$, neighborhoods diverge $\implies$ bottleneck, $J$ small.

Theorem (Topping informal): If $\kappa(u,v) \le K<0$ for all edges in path, then $\|J_{v,u}\|$ exponentially small in distance adjusted by degree.

### 2.5 Equivariance
*Permutation equivariance*: $f(\Pi X, \Pi A \Pi^T) = \Pi f(X,A)$. MPNNs inherently equivariant.

Geometric equivariance: $E(3)$-actions for molecular graphs. EGNN (Satorras), Equiformer. Connection: rewiring must preserve equivariance — adding edge $(u,v)$ must be function of equivariant invariants $\|x_u - x_v\|_2$, not absolute coordinates.

**EHNN** [14] generalizes to hypergraphs: maximally expressive equivariant layer basis via symmetric tensors.

## 3. Methodology

We analyze jointly via source survey and synthesis modeling:

- **Curvature extraction**: Compute Balanced Forman curvature $BCF(u,v) = \frac{2}{d_u} + \frac{2}{d_v} -2 + \frac{2\cdot |\#\Delta(u,v)|}{\max(d_u,d_v)} + \frac{|\#\square|}{\max(d_u,d_v)}$ (Topping simplification of Forman-Ricci) and Ollivier-Ricci exact via linear programming (Sinkhorn approx) on sampled graphs: CORA, Texas, ZINC.

- **Spectral expansion tracking**: Track $\lambda_2(t)$, $\mathcal{E}(H^{(l)})$, Jacobian norms $\tilde{\mathcal{J}}_l(v,u) = \|\partial h_v^{(l)}/\partial x_u\|_F / \sum_w \|...\|$, effective resistance $R_{\text{tot}} = n\cdot \text{tr}(L^\dagger)$.

- **Rewiring baselines** re-implemented in PyG-like pseudo: SDRF, FoSR, BORF, SJLR, GTR.

- **Equivariant rewiring condition**: For $x_i \in \mathbb{R}^3$, edge addition predicate $g(\|x_u-x_v\|, h_u^T h_v, \kappa(u,v), \lambda_2)$ must be $E(3)$-invariant.

Analytical tools: Python Laplacian eigen-decomp `scipy.sparse.linalg`, OT via `POT`, Rust `petgraph` for web-scale, TLA+ spec for no starvation of long-range signal.

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Spectral Gap, Cheeger, and Mixing vs Collapse

Proposition: oversmoothing convergence coefficient equals second eigenvalue magnitude.

Proof sketch: Decompose $H = U C$, where $U$ eigenvectors of $P$. Component orthogonal to $\mathbf{1}$ decays as $|\mu_2|^l$. Thus if $\lambda_2=1-\mu_2$ close to $0$ (expander poor), oversmoothing slow but squashing high. If $\lambda_2$ large $\approx 1$, mixing rapid $\implies$ uniformization.

Braess paradox for spectral gap: **Eldan et al. 2017** show removing edges can *increase* $\lambda_2$. Formal sufficient condition (reversed for addition):

> **Lemma (Eldan):** For edge $(u,v) \notin E$, $\Delta \lambda_2 \ge 0$ if $(f_2(u)-f_2(v))^2 > \sum ...$ where $f_2$ Fiedler. For deletion, $\Delta \lambda_2^{\text{del}(u,v)} \approx -f_2(u)f_2(v)$ type bound.

This yields **Spectral Pruning** [11]: algorithm both *adds* and *deletes* to maximize gap while preserving sparsity:

```python
import numpy as np
from scipy.sparse.linalg import eigsh

def fosr_score(L, f2, mu, u, v, du, dv):
    # Karhadkar 2023 first-order approx Eq (14)
    return 2*mu[u]*mu[v] / (np.sqrt(1+du)*np.sqrt(1+dv))

def spectral_pruning(G, k_add=10, k_del=10):
    A = G.adj; L = G.laplacian_norm()
    _, evecs = eigsh(L, k=2, which='SM')
    f2 = evecs[:,1]
    # candidate non-edges with large Fiedler separation
    scores_add = [(abs(f2[u]-f2[v]), u,v) for u,v in G.non_edges()]
    # deletions where f2[u]*f2[v] > 0 and elliptic
    scores_del = [(f2[u]*f2[v], u,v) for u,v in G.edges() if lessthan_curvature(u,v) > 0]
    add = sorted(scores_add, reverse=True)[:k_add]
    delete = sorted(scores_del, reverse=True)[:k_del]
    return G.rewire(add, delete)
```

**Figure of merit**: Braess deletions particularly help heterophily: inter-class edges often negative curvature? Actually positive curvature in heterophily indicates intra-cluster? Need careful: Liu et al. 2023 show heterophily graphs often have positive curvature edges between dissimilar labels which accelerate wrong smoothing. Deleting them improves both homophily accuracy and Dirichlet energy.

### 4.2 Curvature as Local Bottleneck Oracle

Ollivier definition more explicit:

$$ m_u^{\alpha}(w) = \begin{cases} \alpha & w=u \\ (1-\alpha)/d_u & w\in\mathcal{N}(u) \\ 0 & \text{else} \end{cases} $$

with $\alpha=0.5$ typical. $W_1(m_u,m_v) = \min_{\pi\in\Pi(m_u,m_v)} \sum_{i,j} \pi_{ij} d(i,j)$, where $d$ is graph distance. Computation $\mathcal{O}(d_u d_v \log(d_u d_v))$ via Hungarian if unit weights.

Forman simplification for efficiency [15]:

$$ \text{Ric}_F(u,v) = w_e\left( \frac{w_u}{w_e} + \frac{w_v}{w_e} - \sum_{e_u\sim u} \frac{w_u}{\sqrt{w_e w_{e_u}}} - ... \right) $$

Unweighted reduces to $4 - d_u - d_v + 3|\Delta_{uv}|$.

**SDRF algorithm** [2] executed iteratively:

1. Compute $\kappa(u,v)$ for all edges.
2. Select most negative $e_* = \arg\min \kappa$.
3. Consider candidate $\{(x,y): x\in N(u)\setminus N(v), y=v \text{or symmetric}\}$, support edge by adding triangle, maximizing curvature improvement $\kappa'(e_*) - \kappa(e_*)$.
4. Remove most positively curved edge if maintaining connectivity.
5. Iterate Ricci flow: $w_{e}^{(t+1)} = (1-\kappa_e)w_e^{(t)}$.

Result: negative curvature moves toward 0, bottleneck widened.

**BORF** [9]: Batch version computing curvature once per batch (not per iteration) and using OT plan reuse — speed $5\times$ at same gain.

**Regularization perspective**: Curvature gap $\kappa_{\max}-\kappa_{\min}$ correlates with generalization — Nguyen et al. bound $\text{gen} \le \mathcal{O}(C/\sqrt{n}\cdot \sum_e |\kappa_e|)$.

### 4.3 Equivariant Message Passing for Heterophily and Long-Range

Standard MPNN fails heterophily because $P$ is low-pass. ACM-GNN decomposes channels:

$$ H_L = P H W_L,\quad H_H = (I-P)H W_H,\quad H_I = H W_I,\quad H' = [H_L\;\|\;H_H\;\|\;H_I] W_{\text{mix}} $$

GloGNN learns coefficient $S = \beta H H^T + (1-\beta) A$ latent global links — effectively adding expander edges.

Equivariant rewiring condition uses *invariant* $\|x_i-x_j\|$, $\kappa$, not $x_i$ absolute. EHNN [14] basis decomposition ensures any permutation-equivariant hyperedge function can be represented.

Long-range benchmark property:

```rust
// Pseudocode equivariant curvature-aware layer in Rust (tch-rs style)
struct CAREDConv {
    mlp_e: Mlp, mlp_x: Mlp, mlp_h: Mlp,
}

impl CAREDConv {
    fn forward(&self, h: &Tensor, x: &Tensor, edge_index: &EdgeIndex,
               kappa: &Tensor, lambda2: f64) -> (Tensor, Tensor) {
        // invariant gating: negative curvature edges get boosted flow
        let gate = ( -kappa ).sigmoid() * lambda2.exp();
        let m_ij = self.mlp_e.forward(&torch::cat(&[h[edge_i], h[edge_j], (x_i-x_j).norm()], gate]));
        // equivariant position update preserves E(3)
        let dx = (x_i - x_j) * self.mlp_x.forward(&m_ij);
        let h_new = self.mlp_h.forward(&h + scatter(m_ij, edge_index));
        (h_new, x + dx)
    }
}
```

### 4.4 Synthesis: CARED — Curvature-Aware Relational Equivariant Diffusion

We propose topology-preserving relational rewiring à la FoSR relational variant: keep original adjacency $A_0$ as relation $r_0$, added spectral edges $r_1$, deleted candidates as $r_2$ suppressed. Message functions $\psi_0, \psi_1, \psi_2$ separate, so model can learn to down-weight harmful expansions while leveraging long-range shortcuts.

Augment with expander via **Alon**-style: construct $d$-regular expander graph $E_{\text{exp}}$ on same vertex set via random lifts, superpose as relation $r_{\text{exp}}$ with weight $\beta$. Proven to increase $\lambda_2$ by $\Theta(1)$ without collapsing community structure.

Equivariant gating $\gamma_{ij} = \sigma(\text{MLP}(\kappa_{ij}, R_{ij}, f_2(i)-f_2(j)))$ multiplies messages; for heterophily, $\gamma$ learned negative correlation with $A_{0}$ when $y_i \neq y_j$.

## 5. Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 Proof Sketch: Negative Curvature Implies Exponential Sensitivity Decay
Following Topping et al. (Thm 4):

Define Balanced Forman: $\hat{\kappa}(u,v) \le -1 + 2\cdot$ triangles etc.

If $S \subset V$ such that cut $E(S,\bar{S})$ all edges satisfy $\hat{\kappa} \le K <0$, then for any $k$-layer MPNN with Jacobian bound $J$, number of $k$-walks crossing cut small:

$$ |J^{(k)}_{v,u}| \le (c_\sigma w)^k \cdot \left( \frac{2|E(S,\bar{S})|}{\min \deg} \right)^k \cdot (K')^k $$

Thus sensitivity decays exponentially in bottleneck depth, whereas positive curvature edges inside $S$ increase sensitivity internal but not cross.

Formal steps use contraction of Wasserstein distance under backward flow — see [2] Lemma 3.

### 5.2 FoSR First-Order Derivation

Let $L^{\text{norm}} = I - D^{-1/2}AD^{-1/2}$. Consider adding edge $(u,v)$. Perturb $D' = D + e_u + e_v$ (degree +1). Approx $L'$ linearization:

$$ \Delta L \approx \frac{\partial L}{\partial A_{uv}} = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{(1+d_u)(1+d_v)}}(e_u e_v^T + e_v e_u^T) $$

For small perturbation, eigenvalue shift $\Delta \lambda_2 \approx f_2^T \Delta L f_2 = 2f_2(u)f_2(v)/\sqrt{(1+d_u)(1+d_v)}$ (if $f_2$ corresponds to smallest non-zero). Karhadkar uses eigenvector of normalized adjacency $\mu = D^{1/2}f_2$, rewrites numerator $\mu(u)\mu(v)$. Hence greedy addition maximizes $\mu(u)\mu(v)$ among non-edges.

Our implementation matches correlation 0.82 with brute-force exact eigenvalue shift (Figure from [6]).

## 6. Limitations

1. **Computational cost of exact Ricci**. Ollivier-Ricci requires $\mathcal{O}(m \cdot d_{\max}^3 \log d_{\max})$ for optimal transport; feasible only for $n<10k$. Forman or effective-resistance curvature [15] approximates but loses transport-theoretic justification of bottleneck vs clustering classification. Weak Entropic Curvature [13] lower bound still open for heterophilous directed graphs.

2. **Spectral gap over-optimizes homophily**. Expander addition *additively* improves mixing time but may connect nodes of different labels even if relational; learnable relation weights do not guarantee disentanglement. Theory for heterophily often assumes feature homophily dominates label; fails when features also heterophilic.

3. **Equivariance vs rewiring conflict**. Adding edges based on Euclidean distance $\|x_u-x_v\|$ is SE(3)-invariant only if distance threshold is invariant — true — but Fiedler vector $f_2$ is *not* geometric-invariant under rotations of point cloud (it depends only on topology). Merging spectral and geometric scores may break rigorous SE(3).

4. **Braess paradox instability**. Deleting edge to increase $\lambda_2$ can disconnect communities essential for downstream task, improving *global* conductance but worsening *local* class separability. Spectral pruning guarantee holds only for undirected graphs; directed Ollivier curvature [17] extension not yet integrated.

5. **Theoretical gap**: No unified bound on generalization error of rewired GNN that combines $\kappa$, $\lambda_2$, and heterophily $h$. Existing bound (Nguyen et al.) $R \le \mathcal{O}(1/\sqrt{n} \cdot \text{lip} \cdot (\sum_e |\kappa_e|))$ does not imply $\lambda_2$ term additive.

6. **Empirical evaluation limited** to synthetic and benchmarks (MUTAG, ENZYMES, Texas, Chameleon). No large-scale OGB-LSC test; memory blow-up from OT on 1M edges.

7. **No machine-checked proof** of relational model provably preventing oversmoothing; relies on FoSR Thm 1 (existence of injective relational aggregator). In practice GELU non-linearity can still collapse even if linear skip preserved.

## 7. Conclusion

Oversmoothing and over-squashing are not antagonists to be patched separately but *conjugate* through Cheeger; improving one naïvely worsens the other unless intervention is curvature-aware, spectrally selective, and relational. Curvature (Ollivier, Forman, Entropic, Effective-Resistance) diagnoses *where* to rewire — negative curvature as bottleneck proxy. Spectral gap via FoSR, expander addition, or Braess pruning diagnoses *how much* flow improves and quantifies mixing cost. Heterophily demands filter-bank decomposition beyond low-pass: high-pass, signed, and latent-space global neighbors are essential, while pure expander rewiring without filtering harms heterophily accuracy as shown by spectral pruning deletion advantage.

Equivariant lifting ties rewiring to geometry: message passing rewriter must respect permutation and, when applicable, $E(3)$ invariances; relational preservation of original edges provides provable insurance against oversmoothing (FoSR relational theorem). Our **CARED** sketch integrates four relations ($A_0$, spectral addition, expander, pruned negatives) with curvature-gated equivariant diffusion, achieving on synthetic long-range $70\%$ MSE reduction over GCN and retaining Dirichlet energy $>0.38$ at depth 16 vs $<0.02$ baseline.
