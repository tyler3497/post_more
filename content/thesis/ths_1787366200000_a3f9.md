# Succinct Non-Interactive Arguments via Folding Schemes: Nova, SuperNova, and Customizable Constraint Systems with Cycle of Curves

## Abstract
Folding schemes provide a prover-efficient alternative to monolithic SNARKs for incrementally verifiable computation (IVC) by reducing the task of checking two NP instances to a single instance of the same size. Nova introduced the first efficient folding scheme for relaxed R1CS with prover cost dominated by two multi-scalar multiplications (MSMs). SuperNova extends this to non-uniform IVC where each step may invoke a distinct circuit via a selector function, preserving a la carte prover costs. This thesis formalizes relaxed R1CS, committed relaxed R1CS, and the CCS generalization that subsumes R1CS, Plonkish, and AIR, and analyzes CycleFold for instantiating these schemes over the Pallas/Vesta cycle without expensive non-native field emulation. We prove security under the discrete-log assumption in the random-oracle model and benchmark Spartan compression of IVC proofs.

## 1. Introduction
Incrementally verifiable computation (IVC) allows a prover to demonstrate correctness of a long-running computation $z_0 \to z_1 \to \dots \to z_n$ where $z_{i+1}=F(z_i, w_i)$ for a step function $F$, by producing a proof $\Pi_i$ after each step that attests to all prior steps [1][2]. Classical approaches to IVC invoked a SNARK to prove SNARK verification recursively, incurring heavy prover overhead due to expensive group operations and FFTs inside the verifier circuit [3].

**Nova** [1] departs radically: it replaces recursive SNARK verification with a *folding scheme*, a primitive that takes two NP instances $u_1, u_2$ with witnesses $w_1, w_2$ and folds them into a single instance $u$ with witness $w$ such that $u$ is satisfiable iff $u_1,u_2$ are satisfiable. By iteratively folding the running instance with the new step instance, the prover maintains only one relaxed instance whose size remains $O(|F|)$. Only at the very end (or when compression is desired) does it invoke a succinct argument — typically **Spartan** [4] — to prove knowledge of a satisfying witness for the folded instance.

The implications are profound:

- *Prover efficiency*: No FFTs, no trusted setup; dominant cost is two MSMs per step, linear in circuit size when using Pedersen commitments.
- *Recursion without cycles?* While Nova originally assumed a cycle of elliptic curves to avoid non-native arithmetic, recent work **CycleFold** [2][5] and revisitation by Nguyen et al. [2] formalizes security on Pallas/Vesta (Pasta curves) [6].
- *Non-uniformity*: Real programs are not a single repeated circuit. **SuperNova** [7] generalizes to a family of circuits $F_1,\dots,F_k$ selected by $\phi$, enabling zkVMs and EVM-like execution where each step's cost is proportional only to the invoked instruction [8].
- *Customizability*: **Customizable Constraint Systems (CCS)** [9] and **HyperNova** [10] further generalize from R1CS to simultaneously capture Plonkish and AIR without overhead, via multilinear extensions and sum-check folding.

> Theorem: Nova's IVC from a secure folding scheme for committed relaxed R1CS yields knowledge-sound, succinct IVC under the discrete-log assumption in the ROM.

This thesis provides a self-contained development from relaxed R1CS to CCS, folding, non-uniform folding, and curve-cycle instantiation, with proofs, complexity analysis, and limitations.

## 2. Background

### 2.1 R1CS and Relaxed R1CS
Let $\mathbb{F}$ be a prime field. A Rank-1 Constraint System (R1CS) structure consists of matrices $A,B,C \in \mathbb{F}^{m \times n}$ where $m$ is number of constraints, $n$ is number of variables. An instance $x$ (public IO) and witness $w$ define $z = (w, x, 1)$. The instance is satisfiable if:

$$ (A \cdot z) \circ (B \cdot z) = C \cdot z $$

where $\circ$ denotes Hadamard product.

Relaxed R1CS introduces an error vector $E \in \mathbb{F}^m$ and scalar $u \in \mathbb{F}$:

$$ (A \cdot z) \circ (B \cdot z) = u \cdot (C \cdot z) + E $$

With $u=1, E=0$, this recovers R1CS. Relaxation is *closed under linear combination*, which is crucial for folding. Committed relaxed R1CS adds commitments $\bar{E}, \bar{W}$ to $E$ and $W$.

| System | Witness | Satisfiability | Closure under folding |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1CS | $W$ | $Az \circ Bz = Cz$ | No |
| Relaxed R1CS | $W,E,u$ | $Az \circ Bz = uCz+E$ | Yes |
| Committed Relaxed R1CS | $W,E,u$ + commitments | Same + commitments verify | Yes |

### 2.2 Folding Schemes
A folding scheme for relation $\mathcal{R}$ is a public-coin protocol between prover $P$ holding $(u_1,w_1),(u_2,w_2)$ and verifier $V$ holding $u_1,u_2$, where after interaction both output a folded instance $u$ and $P$ outputs $w$ such that:

- **Completeness**: If $(u_i,w_i)\in\mathcal{R}$, then $(u,w)\in\mathcal{R}$.
- **Knowledge soundness**: An extractor can recover $w_1,w_2$ from a successful prover for $u$.

In Nova, folding is *non-interactive* via Fiat-Shamir: verifier challenge $r \leftarrow \text{RO}(u_1,u_2,\bar{T})$.

### 2.3 IVC and PCD
IVC correctness requires that proof $\Pi_i$ attests to existence of $w_0,\dots,w_{i-1}$ such that $z_{j+1}=F(z_j,w_j)$ for all $j<i$. Proof-carrying data (PCD) generalizes to distributed computations over a graph. Nova constructs IVC; Spartan compresses its proof; SuperNova constructs non-uniform IVC [1][7].

### 2.4 Cycle of Curves
Let $E_1/\mathbb{F}_{q_1}$ have order $q_2$ and $E_2/\mathbb{F}_{q_2}$ have order $q_1$. Then $(E_1,E_2)$ forms a 2-cycle. Pallas and Vesta satisfy $y^2 = x^3+5$ over fields $p=2^{254}+45560315531419706090280762371685220353$ and $q=2^{254}+45560315531506369815346746415080538113$ respectively [6]. This enables efficient verification: operations in $\mathbb{G}_1$ (scalar field $\mathbb{F}_q$) are expressed as circuits over $\mathbb{F}_p$, which is exactly the scalar field of the other curve, avoiding non-native field emulation.

## 3. Methodology

We proceed in three layers:

1. **Algebraic layer**: Define committed relaxed R1CS and its cross-terms. For $z = z_1 + r z_2$, error term $T = A z_1 \circ B z_2 + A z_2 \circ B z_1 - u_1 C z_2 - u_2 C z_1$ must be committed and absorbed.
2. **Protocol layer**: Specify Nova folding, SuperNova multi-folding with selector vector $\vec{s}$, and HyperNova/ CCS folding via sum-check [9][10].
3. **Instantiation layer**: Apply CycleFold transformation [10][2] to realize verifier circuits on a 2-cycle, analyze cost with Pedersen vs KZG commitments.

Methodologically, we rely on discrete-log-based polynomial commitments, random-oracle Fiat-Shamir, and sum-check for CCS. Proofs are conducted via standard forking lemmas for knowledge soundness.

The folding prover computes:

```rust
// Nova prover pseudocode (simplified)
fn fold(U1: RelaxedInstance, W1: Witness, U2: Instance, W2: Witness) -> (U,W) {
    let T = compute_cross_term(A,B,C, z1,z2, u1,u2);
    let com_T = commit(T, r_T);
    let r = ro(U1,U2,com_T); // challenge
    let E = E1 + r*T + r^2*E2; // note E2=0 for fresh R1CS
    let W = W1 + r*W2;
    let u = u1 + r*u2;
    (U_folded(W,E,u), W_folded(W,E))
}
```

And for CCS, we fold via multivariate polynomial IOP:

```python
# CCS folding sketch (Python-style)
def fold_ccs(instances, r):
    # instances: list of (M_j, z_j, c_j)
    # multilinear extension check: sum_{x in {0,1}^l} eq(r,x) * f(x) = 0
    # f encodes constraint polynomial
    coeffs = lagrange_interpolation(r)
    z_fold = sum(c * z_i for c,z_i in zip(coeffs, zs))
    # prover MSM is sum_i coeff_i * commitments
    return z_fold
```

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Nova: Folding Relaxed R1CS
Nova's core insight: fresh R1CS instances have $E=0,u=1$, but folded instances are *relaxed*. Folding preserves $N$-size: two $N$-size instances become one $N$-size instance.

Given $u_1=(\bar{E}_1,u_1,\bar{W}_1,x_1)$, $u_2=(\bar{E}_2,u_2,\bar{W}_2,x_2)$, prover commits to cross-term $T$ defined above. Verifier samples $r$. Folded values:

$$\begin{aligned}
\bar{E} &\leftarrow \bar{E}_1 + r\cdot \bar{T} + r^2\cdot \bar{E}_2 \\
\bar{W} &\leftarrow \bar{W}_1 + r\cdot \bar{W}_2 \\
u &\leftarrow u_1 + r\cdot u_2 \\
x &\leftarrow x_1 + r\cdot x_2
\end{aligned}$$

*Prover cost*: $2$ MSMs of size $m$ (for $E$ and $W$) plus $O(m)$ field ops for $T$. *Verifier cost*: $2$ group scalar multiplications + hash. Knowledge soundness reduces to binding of commitments and forking lemma requiring $3$ transcripts with distinct $r$ to extract $w_1,w_2$ [1].

*IVC construction*: At step $i$, prover folds $U_{i-1}$ (running) with $u_i$ representing $F(z_{i-1},w_{i-1})=z_i$. Augmented circuit $F'$ includes hash of verifier to ensure continuity. The IVC proof is $(U_i,W_i)$. Succinctness achieved by Spartan proving $U_i$ satisfiable.

### 4.2 SuperNova: Non-Uniform IVC and A La Carte Costs
SuperNova [7] addresses programs where $F$ is not uniform. Define circuits $F_1,\dots,F_n$ and selector $\phi(z)=j$. At step $i$, we fold into the $j$-th running instance among $n$:

$$ U^{(j)}_{k+1} = \text{Fold}(U^{(j)}_k, u_i) \quad \text{if } \phi(z_{i-1})=j $$

Crucially, other $U^{(k)}, k\neq j$ remain unchanged. The augmented circuit $F'_j$ must verify only $\phi$ and one folding verifier, not all $n$ circuits, yielding *a la carte* prover cost $O(|F_j|)$, not $O(\sum |F_j|)$. Earlier approaches required universal circuits paying sum cost.

SuperNova maintains list $\vec{U} = (U^{(1)},\dots,U^{(n)})$, each committed relaxed R1CS. Folding scheme generalized to *multi-folding* where $n$ instances fold at once using powers of $r$: $r, r^2, \dots, r^n$. Security proof extends forking to $n+1$ transcripts.

*Implementation insight*: Pedersen commitments over Pallas allow MSM size equal to variables, not constraints, after preprocessing via $\text{Spark}$-like sparse commitment [4][7].

### 4.3 Customizable Constraint Systems and HyperNova
CCS [9] unifies R1CS, Plonkish, AIR. A CCS structure defines $q$ matrices $M_0,\dots,M_{q-1}$, $t$ multisets $S_i \subseteq [q]$, and $t$ constants $c_i$. Instance satisfied if:

$$ \sum_{i=1}^{t} c_i \cdot \prod_{j \in S_i} \left(\sum_{k=0}^{q-1} M_k \cdot z \right)_j = 0 $$

R1CS is CCS with $q=3$, $t=1$, $S_1=\{1,2\}$, $c_1=1$ plus linear term. Plonkish custom gates degree $d$ correspond to multisets size $d$. AIR corresponds to selector polynomials.

**SuperSpartan** [9] proves CCS with linear-time prover without FFTs. **HyperNova** [10] gives folding for CCS: prover cost is single MSM of size $|z|$ (optimal). Zero-knowledge added for free by randomizing commitments, not by masking polynomials with extra degree.

HyperNova's folding verifier avoids non-native ops by using **CycleFold** [10]: it adds an extra circuit that proves correct folding of curve operations, folded itself, decoupling verifier recursion from commitment evaluation.

```haskell
-- CCS as Haskell type
type Matrix f = [[f]]
data CCS f = CCS {
  mCols :: Int,
  matrices :: [Matrix f],
  multisets :: [[Int]],
  coeffs :: [f]
}
-- Satisfiability: sum_i c_i * prod_{j in S_i} (M_j * z) = 0
```

### 4.4 Cycle of Curves, CycleFold, and Instantiation
Original Nova paper left cycle instantiation informal. Nguyen et al. [2] formalize security on a cycle: IVC verifier on curve $E_1$ is expressed as circuit over $\mathbb{F}_{q_1}$, proved on $E_2$, and vice versa. Without cycle, prover would emulate $\mathbb{F}_{q_1}$ arithmetic inside $\mathbb{F}_{q_2}$ circuit at $\sim 10\times$ overhead.

CycleFold [10] improves: instead of emulating commitments inside folding verifier, it creates auxiliary instance tracking verifier's non-native operations and folds it separately. Result: prover's circuit is *native* plus a small CycleFold circuit $O(\log m)$ hashes and $O(1)$ scalar multiplications.

| Curve Pair | Field Bits | Highly 2-adic? | MSM Speed | Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pallas/Vesta | 254 | Yes ($2^{32}$) | Fastest on CPU | Halo2, Nova production |
| BN254/Grumpkin | 254 | Moderate | Pairing-friendly | Need KZG |
| secp/secq | 256 | No | Slower | Bitcoin compatibility |

Pallas/Vesta chosen for transparency (no trusted setup) with Pedersen/Hyrax commitments. BN254/Grumpkin chosen when KZG needed for constant-size proofs.

*Security argument*: Discrete-log over 254-bit prime-order curves with ~128-bit security; random oracle for Fiat-Shamir; forking lemma extracts witnesses if prover succeeds with non-negligible probability [1][2].

## 5. Empirical and Proofs

### 5.1 Security Theorem
> Theorem (Knowledge Soundness of Nova IVC): Let $\Pi_{fold}$ be knowledge-sound folding scheme for committed relaxed R1CS, $\text{RO}$ random oracle, commitment binding. Then Nova IVC is knowledge-sound: for any PPT prover $P^*$ making $Q$ RO queries producing accepting $(U,W)$, there exists extractor $\mathcal{E}$ outputting witnesses for all steps with probability $1-\text{negl}$.

Proof sketch: By induction on IVC steps. Base case trivial relaxed instance $U_\bot$. Inductive step: forking extractor obtains 3 transcripts $(r_1,r_2,r_3)$ for same $U_1,U_2,\bar{T}$ with distinct $r$, solves linear system for $W_1,W_2,E_1,E_2$ due to degree-2 dependence of $E$ on $r$. Commitments binding ensures consistency. Spartan knowledge soundness then extracts final compressed witness [1][4].

### 5.2 Complexity
For $m$ constraints, $n$ variables:

- **Nova folding prover**: $2$ MSMs size $m$ + $O(m)$ field mults. Benchmarks on 2^20 constraints: ~1.2s per fold (Rust, Pedersen, single thread) [1].
- **SuperNova folding**: $(n+1)$ MSMs dominated by one $m$-size, plus $n$ small selector hashes.
- **HyperNova CCS folding**: $1$ MSM size $n$ (optimal) + $O(q \cdot n)$ field ops; verifier $O(\log n)$ hashes.
- **Spartan compression**: Prover $O(m)$ field ops + $O(\sqrt{m})$ group ops (Hyrax), proof size $O(\log m)$ ~10KB for 2^20.
- **CycleFold overhead**: Adds $\approx 2\times$ constraints for curve ops but removes $>10\times$ non-native emulation, net $5\times$ win [10][2].

Empirical from Spartan2 library [4]: Nova over Pallas/Vesta 2^20 R1CS ~ 0.8s folding, 1.5s Spartan prove, 6ms verify. NeutronNova [11] batch-folding 32 instances amortizes to 0.03s per instance.

### 5.3 Comparison
| Scheme | Relation | MSMs per step | Uniform? | Trusted setup |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Halo [3] | IPA accumulation | $O(1)$ | Yes | No |
| Nova [1] | Relaxed R1CS | 2 | Yes | No |
| SuperNova [7] | Relaxed R1CS multi | 1-2 | No (a la carte) | No |
| HyperNova [10] | CCS | 1 (optimal) | No | No |
| NeutronNova [11] | Zero-check | 1 | Batch uniform | No |

HyperNova strictly dominates Nova in prover cost when $q>2$ custom gates, as R1CS encoding of high-degree gate inflates constraints $3\times$ [9].

## 6. Limitations

1. **Verifier succinctness vs prover**: Spartan verifier is $O(\sqrt{m})$ without Spark preprocessing, though Hyrax PCS reduces to $O(\log m)$. Full $O(\log m)$ requires KZG trusted setup, contradicting transparency goal [4].
2. **Cycle requirement**: Security proof in [2] shows standard discrete-log assumption insufficient alone; need *cycle* discrete-log and algebraic group model for KZG variant. Quantum attacks break 254-bit ECDLP in $O(n^3)$ Shor steps.
3. **Non-uniform bookkeeping**: SuperNova list $\vec{U}$ grows with $n$ circuits; for EVM with 100+ opcodes, commitments to $\vec{U}$ dominate verifier circuit ($\approx 20k$ constraints) [7]. Circuit aggregation and decoupling mitigate but not eliminate [8].
4. **Zero-knowledge overhead**: Free ZK claimed in HyperNova holds only with randomized commitments; hiding for public IO still requires extra randomness in $W$, adding 1 MSM.
5. **Lookup arguments**: CCS does not natively support Plookup-style lookups without extra columns; recent Lasso/Jolt RoK to zero-check [11] adds sum-check rounds increasing proof size $1.5\times$.
6. **Recursion depth**: Fiat-Shamir soundness error accumulates $Q/2^{\lambda}$ per fold; for $10^6$ steps, need 256-bit field to keep error $<2^{-80}$.

Future work includes folding for arithmetic with *small* field elements (e.g., 64-bit) avoiding full-field MSMs, and transparent polynomial commitments post-quantum (Lattice-based PCS) to retain cycle benefits.

---

## 7. Conclusion
Nova's folding paradigm reframes recursion from *proving verification* to *folding satisfaction*, achieving prover efficiency unmatched by earlier PCD systems. SuperNova lifts uniformity, enabling true zkVMs with a la carte costs; CCS and HyperNova lift expressiveness, subsuming Plonkish and AIR without overhead; CycleFold and Pasta curves make the theory practical. Together, they form the current frontier for transparent, prover-efficient IVC underpinning production zkVMs like Jolt, SP1, and RISC Zero variants that integrate NeutronNova batching.

The trajectory — from Halo's amortized IPA [3] to Nova's R1CS folding [1] to HyperNova's CCS folding [10] — suggests folding schemes will replace monolithic SNARKs wherever computation is incremental or heterogeneous. Open questions remain around post-quantum folding, lookup-efficient folding, and optimal CycleFold for 2-adic curves, but the foundation is now rigorous and deployed.
