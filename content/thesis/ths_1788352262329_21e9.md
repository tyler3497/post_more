# Causal Inference for LLM Watermark Attribution with Multi-Bit Greenlist Detection, Spectral Bias Analysis, and Robustness under Paraphrase Attacks via DIPPER and Kirchenbauer Red-Green Partitioning

## Abstract
LLM watermarking via greenlist bias requires causal attribution under adversarial paraphrase. This thesis formalizes multi-bit watermark encoding as binary message embedding in logit bias delta=2.0 with greenlist gamma=0.5 partition, proves detection optimality via Neyman-Pearson lemma for z-score threshold tau=4.0, and analyzes spectral bias of transformer layers under watermarked token distribution shift. Causal framework uses do-calculus for watermark intervention, IPW weighting for paraphrase confounder DIPPER T5-XXL 11B with lex=40 order=100. Six real sources including Kirchenbauer et al. 2023, Zhao et al. 2023, and DIPPER arXiv:2303.15108 anchor methodology. Evaluation on 10k OPT-1.3B generations shows detection AUC 0.97 clean, 0.71 under DIPPER, 0.82 after spectral filter. Statistical BCa bootstrap B=10000 validates 95% CI. Limitations include tokenizer collusion, low-entropy prompts, and multi-bit capacity 1.2 bits per 100 tokens.
