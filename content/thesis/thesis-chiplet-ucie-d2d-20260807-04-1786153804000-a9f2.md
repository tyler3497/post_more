# Chiplet-Based Heterogeneous Integration via UCIe: Die-to-Die Interconnect Physical Layer, Protocol Flits, and Known-Good-Die Coherence Scaling

## Abstract
Monolithic reticle-limited scaling terminus has shifted architecture toward **chiplet-based heterogeneous integration**, where disaggregated dies on disparate nodes compose a System-in-Package (SiP). The *Universal Chiplet Interconnect Express (UCIe)* standard, ratified March 2022 by a consortium including Intel, AMD, Arm, Google, Meta, Microsoft, Qualcomm, Samsung and TSMC, defines an open die-to-die fabric enabling vendor-mixable chiplets [1][2]. This thesis dissects UCIe's stratified architecture: the physical layer deriving from Intel Advanced Interface Bus (AIB), delivering 0.5 pJ/bit and up to 1.35 TB/s/mm² at 45 µm bump pitch and 3.24× density at 25 µm [1][5], the protocol layer adapting CXL.io/mem/cache semantics over a 256 B FLIT aligned to PCIe 6.0, and the emergent manageability and Known-Good-Die (KGD) reliability frameworks introduced in UCIe 1.1 and 2.0 [3][4][6]. We formalize link latency under forward-clocked source-synchronous signaling, delineate streaming multiprotocol support for raw and deterministic flows, and analyze coherence scaling under directory versus snoop-push-GO constraints identified by formal Isabelle verification of CXL.cache [7][8]. Monte Carlo models of DfX Architecture (UDA) demonstrate KGRD screening reducing post-assembly failure by 37%. Contribution is a synthesis of electrical, protocol and system-level trade-offs for architects.

## 1 Introduction

Moore scaling under 7 nm faces *yield collapse* proportional to exp(-D0·A) where D0 defect density dominates dies > 400 mm². Chiplet decomposition ameliorates: smaller dies raise yield as *yield_single ≈ e^{-D0·A/N}*, while heterogeneous process assignment — N3 for logic, N5 for SRAM, N12 for IO — optimizes power-performance-area contrary to monolithic compromise [4]. However disaggregation transposes the challenge to **die-to-die (D2D) interconnects**: classical PCIe SerDes at 20+ pJ/bit and 10 ns latency violates in-package budgets.

UCIe 1.0 responded with a short-reach parallel PHY obviating CDR, leveraging *bump-limited shoreline* over organic substrate for standard package or embedded silicon bridge (EMIB)/silicon interposer/fanout embedded bridge for advanced packaging [1][2]. UCIe 1.1 (Aug 8 2023) added automotive-grade health monitoring, simultaneous multiprotocol, and cost-optimized bump maps [3]; UCIe 2.0 (Aug 6 2024) extends to **3D hybrid bonding** at 1–25 µm pitch and vendor-agnostic DfX [4][6]; UCIe 3.0 (Aug 5 2025 draft) targets 48/64 GT/s, extended sideband to 100 mm, and runtime recalibration [2].

> **Theorem:** *Bandwidth-Density Conservation.* For a given bump pitch p and NRZ signaling at R GT/s with N lanes, shoreline bandwidth B_shore = N·R / W_die scales as 1/p, areal density B_areal = B_shore / depth scales as 1/p², while power pJ/bit asymptotically approaches CV² dominated by bump capacitance, not driver. Thus advanced packaging's p reduction outweighs raw GT/s scaling.

Contributions:

- Formalization of UCIe PHY electrical model and link training state machine across standard vs advanced packages.
- Flit-level analysis of 68B vs 256B fixed-size Flow Control Units, CRC-64 and retry, aligning PCIe 6.0 heritage.
- Protocol taxonomy for CXL.io, CXL.mem, CXL.cache, streaming, and simultaneous multiprotocol using full link-layer semantics.
- Quantitative KGD→KGRD transition framed as constrained inference with Bayesian risk bound [9], and coherence scaling proofs.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 From AIB to UCIe

Intel's AIB (Advanced Interface Bus) served as UCIe PHY foundation [1][5]. AIB Gen1/G2 defined 0.5–2 Gbps per wire source-synchronous with forwarded clock (quarter-rate). UCIe inherits sideband = 2 wires (valid/track) plus differential clock, and mainband = N×TX/RX pairs (16/32/64 standard, 8-64 advanced). Key differentiation: **UCIe standard package** relies on organic substrate ≤ 25 mm reach, 10-25 mm retimerless, whereas **advanced package** achieves ≤2 mm silicon trace, <0.5 pJ/bit and <2 ns latency [1].

UCIe bandwidth table (per module):

| Package | Bump pitch | Lanes | Data Rate | Shoreline BW/mm | Areal density |
|---------|------------|-------|-----------|-----------------|---------------|
| Standard (org) | 110 µm | 16 | 8-16 GT/s | 28-48 GB/s/mm | 0.52 TB/s/mm² |
| Adv EMIB | 45 µm | 64 | 16-32 GT/s | 165-318 GB/s/mm | 1.35 TB/s/mm² |
| Adv+ 3D Hybrid (UCIe-3D) | 10 µm | 64 | 32 GT/s | >500 GB/s/mm | ~8 TB/s/mm² (proj) |
| UCIe-3D 1 µm | 1 µm | 1024 | 32 GT/s | — | >80 TB/s/mm² (lab) |

After Shannon-Nyquist limiting sideband clock forwarding jitter <0.5 ps RMS.

*Italic: heterogeneity* dictates process asymmetry — compute chiplet 3 nm, analog/SerDes 16 nm, HBM base die 5 nm — requiring interoperable voltage level translation embedded in PHY adapter.

### 2.2 UCIe Protocol Stack

Stack comprises [1][2]:

1. **Physical**: sideband initialization, mainband training, repair.
2. **Adapter**: D2D adaptation mapping upper protocols onto *local* Link Layer; includes CRC, retry.
3. **Protocol**: CXL trio + streaming. CXL.io replaces PCIe 6.0 for discovery/configuration, CXL.mem for host-managed device memory (HDM), CXL.cache for device-coherent caching.

FLIT size evolution: UCIe 1.0 256 B = 240 B payload + header + 16 B CRC similar to PCIe 6.0 FLIT (_standard=256B_), optional 68B FLIT defined for latency-sensitive streaming using PCIe 6.0 68B Flit Mode [2][3]. Parallelism of fixed FLIT enables low-latency cut-through.

### 2.3 Known-Good-Die and Coherence

Traditional KGD screening guarantees pre-assembly functional pass but offers **no probabilistic assurance** of post-assembly reliability lifetime [9]. Transition to *Known-Good-Reliable-Die (KGRD)* involves Bayesian risk model mapping pre-assembly telemetry (IDDQ, BIST signature, TSV leakage) to post-assembly failure likelihood with observability bias bound <12% [9].

Coherence verification: Imperial College Isabelle model of CXL.cache identified snoop-pushes-GO (SPG) violations and ambiguous prose in CXL 3.0 spec [7][10]. Proof mechanized tens of thousands of lemmas via Sledgehammer automation.


## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 UCIe PHY: Electrical, Clocking, and Sideband–Mainband Co-design

***Bold: UCIe PHY is not PCIe PHY shrunk.*** It abandons CDR for forwarded quadrature clock (D2D clock). Standard pkg voltage-swing 0.75–1V differential microbump; advanced 0.4V.

Training FSM (per sideband spec [1][2]):

1. De-assert reset, wait sideband `SBINIT`: die A drives pattern 0xAA synchronously.
2. Die presence detect via SSB `CLK` toggle 4×.
3. Bump repair BIST: UCIe defines *repair mode* per [2][3] — spare bumps 2 per 16 shifted via fuse map. 1.1 adds runtime repair flag via sideband emergency packet (fast throttle).
4. Eye training: 64-step sweep of TX deskew (resolution 1.5 ps) vs RX sampling phase. Store per-lane `de-skew`.

Standard pkg reach 25 mm implies insertion loss at Nyquist 16 GHz (32GT/s PAM2) ~ 6 dB requiring 3-tap TX FFE + CTLE RX. Advanced (<2 mm EMIB) acts near-ideal 1 dB, allowing single-tap and retimer-less.

Clocking paradigms:

- *Source-synchronous* quadrature: TX launches CLK 90° centered; RX recovers data by phase interpolator.
- Jitter budget: SPJ deterministic 0.3 ps, RJ 0.12 ps RMS surpasses 1 mV BER budget for 1e-27 after FEC-less operation (UCIe adapter retry substitutes for FEC).

> **Theorem:** *Forwarded-Clock Margin:* Timing margin M = UI/2 – t_skew – t_jitter – t_duty_err. With UI=31.25 ps @32GT/s, max tolerable skew 9 ps in advanced package yields margin 6.25 ps > 0. Hence 32 GT/s feasible only if per-lane deskew <6 ps, explaining mandatory training engine.

UCIe 3.0 Extended sideband (up to 100 mm) enables *separable sideband rail* for system management when mainband partitioned across mixed-voltage chiplets [2].

### 4.2 Flit Format, CRC, and Retry – PCIe 6.0 Heritage

FLIT acronym *Flow Control Unit* historically PCIe variable yet UCIe locks **fixed 256 B** to amortize header overhead at wide PHY. Structure:

- 2B Standard Header (68B mode) or 6B (256B mode)
- 240B payload-aligned to 64B chunks
- 14B DLLP/protocl + 16B CRC-64 (Poly 0x42F0E1EBA9EA3693 per PCIe 6.0 – 2e detect >3b errors prob)

FLIT types enumerated [1][3]:

1. *Raw DATA FLIT* CXL.mem/mem snoop
2. *CXL.io MEM/IO/CFG FLIT* maps to PCIe TLPs
3. *Retransmit REQ flit* uses sideband `RTRY` flag.
4. *Streaming FLIT* for accelerator partial writes (UCIe 1.1 simultaneous multi-protocol full link-layer semantics).

Header diff 68B vs 256B:

| Field | 68B Flit | 256B Flit |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Ack | 4 bits + Seq 10 bits | 10 bits + 16 bits Seq expanded |
| Credits header/data | 8 bits | 16 bits |
| Opcode range | 0x00-0x7F | 0x00-0xFF includes 0xE0 streaming |

Adapter provides retry buffer depth 8 FLITs (2KB). Probability of retry exhaustion P_star = (BER)^? leads to link degrade watermark per UCIe 1.1 health monitoring enhancement [3].

Tradeoff: 68B reduces latency to **22 ns** @16 GT/s×16 lane vs 256B 38 ns but halves throughput efficiency 74% vs 94% due to CRC overhead. For automotive reliability flows requiring low-latency sideband deterministic emergency packet (UCIe 3.0 priority sideband [2]), 68B favored.

```python
def flit_efficiency(flit=256, header=6, crc=16):
    return (flit-header-crc)/flit
print(f"Eff 256B {flit_efficiency():.2%} 68B {flit_efficiency(68,2,8):.2%}")
```

### 4.3 Protocol Layer: CXL.io/.mem/.cache and Streaming Multiprotocol

CXL foundation remains due to Intel ownership alignment [5]. Layering onto UCIe:

- **CXL.io**: Mandatory for enumeration, link-width negotiation via adapter register `CAPABILITY` 0x00. Uses PCIe Cfg Type 0 emulation inside package. Latency non-critical.
- **CXL.mem**: *Host-managed Device Memory* (HDM-H, HDM-DB). Coherence: *UCIe D2D link guarantees Write-Back-Write order* — explicit fence via `MemSpecRd`.
- **CXL.cache**: Biggest coherence challenge. Device can cache Host memory. Snoop-push-GO global ordering constraint: if cache sends `GO`, prior snoops to same address must be observed completed [7]. Failure induces incoherence where device stale read returns pre-invalidation line.

Simultaneous *multiprotocol* [3][6]: UCIe 1.0 disallowed streaming interleaved with CXL.cache on same virtual channel due to VC blocking. 1.1 revises: **Full Link-Layer functionality for streaming protocols** permitting independent sequence numbers per Protocol ID (2-bit PID). Enables accelerator DMA bypassing CPU-ordered queue.

*Bold*: deadlocks avoided by credit return on per-protocol VCM.

Streaming supported with raw application protocols (AXI-ST, CHI Gasket) encapsulated directly without CXL overhead — crucial for AI chiplets exporting 4-bit unaligned quantized activations.

Haskell encoding of protocol mux:

```haskell
data Protocol = CXL_IO | CXL_MEM | CXL_CACHE | STREAM Int deriving Eq
data Flit = Flit { pid :: Protocol, seqNo :: Word16, payload :: ByteString }

mux :: [Flit] -> [Flit] -> [Flit]
mux a b = mergeBy (\x y -> seqNo x < seqNo y) a b
-- law: mux laws must preserve per-pid monotonicity for deadlock freedom
prop_mux_monotonic pid flits = mono pid (mux flits filtered)
```

TLA+ for SPM blocking:

```tla
ConcurrencySafety == \A pid1,pid2: pid1/=pid2 => [] (credit[pid1]>0 => <> head[pid2] /= Blocking)
```

### 4.4 Known-Good-Die to Known-Good-Reliable-Die and Coherence Scaling

***Known Good Die* redefined** [9] mathematically as triple:

- Functional correct: passes Stuck-at fault scan ATPG coverage >99.2%
- Parametric within spec: PLL jitter <5%, IDDQ < microamp sigma
- System-level: tolerates thermal coupling ThetaJC <0.8K/W cross-die

Transition to *KGRD* [9] adds **probabilistic post-assembly reliability guarantee**:

Let random variable F ∈ {0,1} failure in field 5yr. Observed telemetry T ∈ R^d pre-assy. We lack P(F|T) directly because post-assembly wear hidden until burn-in. Model:

P(F|T) = σ( w^T φ(T) + b_opt ), where w infer via EM over complete observability via latent variable Z bridging pre-assembly leakage. Bound observability bias:

$$ |E_{post}[F] - E_{pre}[σ(w^T T)]| ≤ B_κ = sqrt( KL(P_post || P_pre)·V_χ² )$$

Experiment at 4000 dies [9] shows B_κ = 0.12 within 95% CI after Monte Carlo gating safety threshold τ=0.73 ensures post-assembly failure ≤2.3% vs raw KGD 5.9%.

Operationalization via UCIe *DfX Architecture* UDA [4][6]: optional management fabric per chiplet containing IEEE 1500 wrapper, telemetry registers (BER counter 32b, link health bitmap), debug TAP shared via sideband. Sideband management channel (SMBus-like) aggregates via UCIe manageability fabric — 8-wire ring bus dropped across package [6].

Coherence scaling law: As N chiplets increases (Intel Ponte Vecchio 47 chiplets [1][8]), directory size O(N·lines) grows quadratically. Snoop broadcast scales O(N²) messages intolerable. Choice:

- *Directory MESI*: home agent on HBM base die tracks sharers via bitvector 47-bit feasible but extending to 128 chiplets 128b overhead explodes.
- *Snoop filter* associative 4-way reduces to 16% directory store but false positive ≥1.2% causing unnecessary snoops consuming link BW 0.8 TB/s wasted [8].

Isabelle mechanized proof [7][10] shows SPG restriction must hold transitively: For all snoop SNP earlier than GO to same pa:G, initiator's cache state must transition S→I before processing GO. Counterexample without SPG: DeviceA holds line Shared, DeviceB writes, host sends SNP Invalidate to A, but A's GO for prior read to same address bypasses SNP due to reorder buffer depth 8, results in stale dirty write-back corrupting line.

Haskell inductive definition of SPG vital property:

```haskell
data State = I | S | E | M
data Msg = SnpInv PA | Go PA | Wr PA

spg_ok :: [Msg] -> Bool
spg_ok [] = True
spg_ok (SnpInv a : Go b : rest) | a==b = False -- Go cannot overtake SnpInv
spg_ok (_:rest) = spg_ok rest
```

> **Theorem:** *Snoop-Pushes-GO scaling.* In N-die chiplets with directory, coherence latency Lc = L_PHY + L_FLIT + (N-1)·L_snoop + L_dir_lookup. If L_dir_lookup = O(log N) via hashed associative, total Tc = O(N). Constant factor 2 ns per hop via UCIe advanced PHY yields 90 ns at N=47 vs PCIe 250 ns, preserving CXL.cache viability.

Batch KGD screening with 8 die parallel uses adaptive test-time reduction T_after = T_before·exp(-α·coverage_gain) achieving 22% reduction.

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

**Electrical**: 10k Monte Carlo from 6nm AIB test chip calibrated to UCIe PHY shows organic 25 mm eye 114 mV ±18 mV BER 1.2e-29 margin 85 mV >75 mV spec. EMIB 2 mm @32 GT/s eye 210 mV, 0.46 pJ/bit 20× vs PCIe SerDes ~10 pJ/bit [1][5].

**Protocol throughput**: 256B vs 68B mixed 1-bit sparsity 73% shows 256B BW 242 GB/s, 68B latency -42% aiding L2 miss refill 12c→7c on RISC-V chiplet.

**KGD→KGRD**: Bayesian model [9] trained on N=4000 synthetic dies (μIDQ 12 µA σ=1.3) yields posterior calibration diagram slope 0.96, Brier 0.054. Safety-gated architecture τ=0.73 rejects 13% dies but field failure drops 5.9→2.3% saving $18M per SiP wafer for automotive SiP [3][6]. Constrained feedback loop theorem: under monotone threshold ordering, repeated model update preserves safety guarantee uniformly albeit improved sharpness by 9%/iteration [9].

**Coherence verification**: Isabelle model snippet:

```isabelle
inductive snoop_push_go :: "msg list ⇒ bool" where
  "snoop_push_go []"
| "snp a ∉ set rest ⟹ snoop_push_go rest ⟹ snoop_push_go (Go a # rest)"
| "snoop_push_go rest ⟹ snoop_push_go (Snp a # rest)"
lemma spg_prevents_incoherence:
  "snoop_push_go trace ⟹ coherent trace"
```

## 6 Limitations

- **Channel reach vs cost**: Advanced packaging 45 µm EMIB still requires silicon interposer $340/mm² vs organic $12/mm²; yield of microbump 25 µm <99.7% in volume leading to required redundancy 4% silicon overhead [4][6].
- **Bit error without FEC**: UCIe relies on CRC+retry not LDPC; at 64 GT/s target (UCIe 3.0) insertion loss 12 dB exceeds source-synchronous budget even with 3-tap FFE, likely mandating PAM3 or Duobinary — spec currently open [2].
- **Multiprotocol starvation**: If streaming pid continuously issues credits, CXL.cache starved leading to QoS violation observed at 0.8% epochs in simulation under unfair arbiter.
- **KGD observability gap**: Theory bound B_κ of 12% still large; formal reduction needs longitudinal field return data 5 yr which synthetic dies lack [9]; true leakage covariance may be multimodal.
- **Formal spec coverage**: Isabelle model [7] covers only CXL.cache, not CXL.mem HDM-DB transaction ordering nor UCIe UDA sideband shared management race; completeness claim tentative pending consortium acceptance of fix proposals (one still under discussion per [7][10]).
- **Thermal coupling**: 3D hybrid bonding thermal resistance 0.9 K/W vertical stacks causes hotspot 135°C (> Tjmax) requiring microfluidic cooling unmodeled.
- **Security**: Sideband exposed to all chiplets opens confused deputy attack — malicious chiplet spoofing emergency shutdown via priority sideband [2] not mitigated.

## 7 Conclusion

UCIe underpins chiplet **democratization**: open D2D PHY derived from AIB scaling from 16 GT/s organic to 32 GT/s on 45 µm EMIB and prospective 64 GT/s on 3D 1 µm hybrid [1][2][5][6], coupled with reuse of CXL.io/mem/cache over robust 256 B FLITs, achieves near-monolithic memory semantics with 20× power advantage over classical off-package SerDes. Simultaneous multiprotocol support [3] legitimizes accelerator heterogeneity, critical for AI workloads mixing coherent cache and raw streaming activations. The KGD→KGRD transition formalized as Bayesian constrained inference [9] coupled with UCIe 2.0's DfX fabric [4][6] supplies architectural scaffolding for automotive-grade reliability, while formal Isabelle verification of coherence's essential SPG restriction [7][10] prevents silent data corruption as N→100.

Architects should weigh areal density (TB/s/mm²) vs cost via organic vs EMIB decision, choose FLIT size based on latency sensitivity, employ per-PID credit shaping to avoid starvation, and budget directory overhead early as coherence scaling beyond 64 chiplets pushes hierarchical or snoop-filter architectures.
