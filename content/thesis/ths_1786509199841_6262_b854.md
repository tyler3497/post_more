## 6 Limitations and Future Work

- **Trusted setup / hardware TCB:** assumes microcode correctly implements IMU and RAPL telemetry calibration; side-channel via power metering not modeled.
- **Scale of verification:** TLC checks to 10^5 states, but production state 10^9; need symmetry reduction and parametric proofs in Coq for full n.
- **Generality of source data:** study trained on domain-specific corpus, generalization to adjacent workloads may see 8-12% MAPE degradation [2] due to privacy-preserving genomics shift.
- **Adversarial adaptivity:** security parameter lambda=128 assumed but quantum adversary with 2^{40} queries not considered [6].
- **Energy proportionality:** rigorous but idle power >30% SoC still dominates when QPS low; for HE private GWAS idle dominates 22% of total.

Future: compositional verification of Wasmtime Cranelift IR -> RVV emission, integration with Hyperlight WASM sandbox for serverless [6], extension to heterogeneous handover.
