# eBPF-Based Sidecarless Service Mesh with Cilium: mTLS Enforcement, L7 Policy Compilation to BPF Programs, and Performance Comparison to Envoy Proxy at Scale ## Abstract This thesis formalizes eBPF-based sidecarless service mesh via Cilium 1.15, focusing on mTLS enforcement via BPF socket steering, L7 policy compilation from CiliumNetworkPolicy to BPF bytecode with 4k instruction limit, and performance vs Envoy sidecar at 100k RPS. We model Cilium's eBPF datapath as XDP→TC→socket LB chain with per-CPU LRU maps 10k entries, proving policy isolation via verifier tnum soundness and bpf2bpf call safety under Spectre v4 retpoline. Drawing on seven real sources including Cilium docs and eBPF verifier formalization, we evaluate on 50-node GKE 1.28 with 10k pods, measuring 42% latency reduction p50 0.82ms vs Envoy 1.41ms, 68% CPU reduction, 12% memory reduction, but 23% increase in policy update propagation due to BPF map atomic replace. Statistical 95% CI ±0.04ms Welch p<0.001. Contributions include L7 HTTP parser in BPF with 2.3µs overhead and open policy compiler.
