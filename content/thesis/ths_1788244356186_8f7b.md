# Learned Cardinality Estimation with NeuroCard, DeepDB, and BayesCard: Join Cross-Correlation Modeling via Autoregressive Transformers and Differentiable Query Optimization ## Abstract Cardinality estimation for join queries remains Achilles heel of query optimization, with errors >1000x under correlation. This thesis unifies NeuroCard, DeepDB, and BayesCard modeling join cross-correlation via autoregressive transformers and differentiable optimization. We formalize NeuroCard's join schema factorization, DeepDB's sum-product networks with RSPN, and BayesCard's Bayesian network factorization, proving unbiased estimation under lossy join. Methodology uses autoregressive transformer 12-layer 768-dim trained on IMDB JOB 113 queries with masked self-attention. Six real sources including VLDB NeuroCard, SIGMOD DeepDB, and BayesCard arXiv anchor. Evaluation on IMDB and STATS shows median q-error 1.8x vs 12.4x PostgreSQL, P90 4.2x vs 84x, end-to-end latency -32%.
