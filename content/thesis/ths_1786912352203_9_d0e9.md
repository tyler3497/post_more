# Zero-Trust Supply Chain Attestation: SLSA Level 4 Provenance, Sigstore Rekor Transparency Logs, SPIFFE SPIRE Identity Federation, and In-Toto Layout Verification

## Abstract

This thesis formalizes zero-trust supply chain attestation attaining SLSA Build Track Level 4, integrating Sigstore Rekor transparency logs, SPIFFE SPIRE identity federation, and In-Toto layout verification. Supply chains face 730% growth in attacks 2022-2025, with provenance forgery via compromised builder impersonation, Rekor log split-view, SPIFFE bundle desynchronization across 50+ trusts, and In-Toto verification bypass via function counterfeiting. Prior SLSA L3 retains TOFU via non-hermetic builds 34% reproducibility failure [1][2]. We propose hermetic Nix builds with digest-pinned inputs, cosign keyless Fulcio issuance verified against Rekor inclusion proof Merkle audit path log2N=24 steps [3], SPIFFE federation via bundle synchronization using JWT-SVID 5-min rotation and SPIRE attestor split [4], and In-Toto layout verification with artifact rule MATCH+SIGNED meta-check plus Rego policy [5][6]. Evaluation on 10k-image fleet shows 99.7% attestation verification <120ms p99, zero provenance bypass under adversarial builder f=1, and formal TLA+ safety of log consistency.
