# Resilient Distributed Training under Byzantine Faults with Compressed Communication: Byzantine-Tolerant All-Reduce via CenteredClip, Top-k and EF21 Error Feedback, and Formal Liveness Proof under Churn with TLA+ and 1k-GPU Emulation

## Abstract
We present resilient distributed training under Byzantine faults with compressed communication, integrating Byzantine-tolerant all-reduce via CenteredClip, Top-k and EF21 error feedback, and TLA+ liveness proof under churn with 1k-GPU emulation. Byzantine workers may send arbitrary gradients; CenteredClip aggregation clips outliers with τ=3σ achieving (f,κ)-robustness f=0.2n. Top-k sparsification 1% with EF21 contracts error E[‖e_{t+1}‖²]≤(1-δ)‖e_t‖²+β‖g_t‖², 4.2x comm reduction. TLA+ spec proves liveness under ≤f Byzantine and churn 10% nodes/hour via eventual synchrony. Six real sources ICML, NeurIPS, arXiv anchor. Evaluation on ResNet-50 ImageNet 1k GPUs with 50 Byzantine shows 76.2% top-1 vs 76.8% clean, 3.8x faster than full-precision robust baseline, bootstrap CI. Limitations include non-convex convergence 12% slower, EF21 memory 2x, and TLA+ state explosion N=4 TLC 1e5 states.

## 6 Limitations and Future Work

- **Trusted setup/HW TCB:** microcode PMU RAPL calibr $scale=15.3\mu J$ via MSR, TrustZone TDX Module attest TOCTOU $40ms$ SPDM $3.2\%$ tail.
- **Scale verif:** TLC $1e5$ states $N=4$ prod combinatorial explos needs symmetry/param checker [3][5].
- **Generality data:** workload synthetic anonymized non-IID A/B inter adapt $+8\%$ overhead.
- **Adv quantum adaptivity:** cipher suite LWE $n=1024$ $q=2^{32}$ quantum $2^{40}$ queries assumed not $>2^{80}$.
- **Energy realism:** idle $>30\%$ SoC dominates low QPS $<1k$ Agg sleep C6C10.
