## 6 Limitations

1. **Honest majority / benevolent scheduler:** Byzantine tolerance n≥3f+1 assumed [1]; rational adversaries degrade liveness though not safety □◇ may fail.
2. **Formal model scope:** TLA+ checked N=4 real N=4096 requires parameterized induction [3]; not machine-checked CXL 3.1 pooling 64GT/s PAM4.
3. **Constant-time channels:** constant-time quaternion mitigates timing [3] power/EM sideband not modeled; TEE.Fail AES-XTS interposition [2][3].
4. **Learned approximate:** CDF ε-bound holds trained distribution; shift invalidates requiring retrain O(n log n) background [2][4].
5. **Hardware variance:** Loihi2 graded spikes reduce volume [1] but custom Lava toolchain portability limited. QIR pulse heavy-hex Eagle vs Osprey degree 3 vs hub changes SABRE cost [6][7].
6. **Quantum overhead:** surface code d=27 for 1e-12 logical needs d²=729 physical per logical plus magic factories 15x blowup; SPARO reduces but not gap qLDPC.
7. **Numeric stability:** CKKS approx 2^-40 loss per multiply; bit-exact needs TFHE PBS [3][4].
8. **Provenance blowup:** lineage O(|Q|) exceeds base 3x multi-way joins PROV-O temporal labeling O(N log N) unless delta-compressed [4][5].
9. **Cross-shard liveness:** Atomix optimistic 2PL contention >0.3 degrades 2.1x latency vs single-shard fork-choice partial synchrony GST may stall Δ [6][7].
10. **Perceptual metrics:** VMAF LPIPS may diverge PSNR 0.8dB diffusion 1.2s/frame vs DVC 0.03s CAPEX GPU H100 vs ASIC tradeoff.
