# Energy-Based Models, Score Matching, and Compositional Generation: Denoising Score Matching, Contrastive Divergence, Langevin Dynamics, and Concept Energy Functions

## Abstract
Energy-based models (EBMs) define unnormalized densities $p_\theta(x)=\exp(-E_\theta(x))/Z_\theta$, offering **flexibility**, *compositionality*, and mode coverage beyond GANs or autoregressive models, yet training is hindered by intractable partition function $Z_\theta$ [1][2]. This thesis unifies the modern EBM training trinity: **score matching**, **contrastive divergence (CD)** with **Langevin dynamics**, and **compositional generation** via concept energy functions. We show how Hyvärinen's score matching [3][8] and its denoising variant [1][2] avoid $Z_\theta$, why multiscale noise is necessary in high dimensions [1][2], and how CD-$k$ and persistent CD approximate MLE using short-run MCMC [4][5][7]. We then formalize Langevin dynamics as gradient-driven sampler converging to $p_\theta$, and prove its annealed version enables high-fidelity synthesis. Finally, we present concept energy composition via *product-of-experts* logic operators AND/OR/NOT enabling zero-shot creation of 1024² images [6]. We provide methodology, experiments, proofs, and limitations outlining path to reliable density estimation and controllable generation. ~172 words.

## 1 Introduction

> *Energy is all you need to define probability; sampling is all you need to make it useful.*

Generative modeling aims to learn $p_{data}(x)$ for $x\in\mathbb{R}^D$. **Energy-based models** parameterize a scalar energy $E_\theta:\mathbb{R}^D\to\mathbb{R}$ [1][8]:

$$
p_\theta(x)=\frac{1}{Z_\theta}\exp(-E_\theta(x)),\quad Z_\theta=\int \exp(-E_\theta(x))dx
$$

Advantages over **GANs**, **VAEs**, flows:

* *No architectural restriction* — any $E_\theta$ yields valid density
* **Explicit (unnormalized) likelihood** enabling outlier detection, inpainting, Bayesian reasoning [1]
* **Compositionality** [6] — sum of energies = product of experts, enabling *zero-shot composition*
* Better mode coverage than adversarial training [5]

The curse: $Z_\theta$ is intractable. Maximum likelihood gradient:

$$
\nabla_\theta \log p_\theta(x) = -\nabla_\theta E_\theta(x) - \nabla_\theta\log Z_\theta = -\nabla_\theta E_\theta(x) + \mathbb{E}_{x'\sim p_\theta}[\nabla_\theta E_\theta(x')]
$$

requires sampling from the model itself. Historically two escapes dominate:

1. **Score matching** [3] — matches $\nabla_x\log p_\theta(x)= -\nabla_x E_\theta(x)$ circumventing $Z_\theta$.
2. **Contrastive Divergence** [4][7] — replaces $\mathbb{E}_{p_\theta}$ by $k$-step MCMC started at data.

This thesis thesis-#5 argues these are not distinct but complementary views of *Fisher divergence minimization*, and that **Langevin dynamics** is the bridge, while **concept energies** show *why we should still care about EBMs in era of diffusion*.

*Contributions:*

- Formal unification of **denoising score matching (DSM)**, **implicit score matching (ISM)**, **sliced score matching (SSM)** scaling
- Geometric explanation why single-noise DSM fails in high-D [1][2] and multiscale remedy
- CD analysis showing CD-$\infty$$\equiv$ MLE but CD-$k$ biases, persistent buffers and KL regularization fix [5][7]
- Compositional calculus: $E_{AND}=E_1+E_2$, $E_{OR}= -\text{logsumexp}(-E_i)$, $E_{NOT}$ via negation [6]
- Implementation blueprint with annealed Langevin [2]

## 2 Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Energy-Based Models and Partition Function

Define EBM family $\mathcal{P}=\{p_\theta: E_\theta\in\mathcal{F}, \theta\in\Theta\}$ where $\mathcal{F}$ deep ConvNets. The **Boltzmann form** links energy to probability monotonic inversely: low energy = high probability.

> **Definition 2.1 (EBM):** $p_\theta(x)=\exp(-E_\theta(x))/Z_\theta$. Score $s_\theta(x):=\nabla_x\log p_\theta(x)=-\nabla_x E_\theta(x)$ independent of $Z_\theta$.

MLE objective $\min_\theta KL(p_{data}\|p_\theta)$ yields gradient as above containing model expectation. MCMC approximations needed.

Standard approach: **Stochastic Gradient Langevin Dynamics (SGLD)**:

$$
x_{t+1}=x_t - \frac{\alpha_t}{2}\nabla_x E_\theta(x_t)+\epsilon_t,\quad \epsilon_t\sim\mathcal{N}(0,\alpha_t)
$$

Under decaying $\alpha_t$, $\{x_t\}$ converges to $p_\theta$ [5][8].

### 2.2 Score Matching Fundamentals

Introduced by Hyvärinen 2005 [8][3], **Fisher divergence**:

$$
J(\theta)=\frac12 \mathbb{E}_{p_{data}}\|s_\theta(x)-\nabla_x\log p_{data}(x)\|^2
$$

Intractable due to unknown data score. Integration by parts yields **implicit score matching (ISM)**:

$$
J(\theta)=\mathbb{E}_{p_{data}}[ \text{tr}(\nabla_x^2 \log p_\theta(x)) + \frac12\|s_\theta(x)\|^2] + const
$$

Requires Hessian trace $O(D)$ backprops [3] — costly for images $D=10^5$.

**Denoising score matching (DSM)** [1][2][4]: corrupt data $\tilde{x}=x+\sigma z$, $z\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I)$. Then minimize:

$$
J_{DSM}= \mathbb{E}_{p_{data}}\mathbb{E}_{q_\sigma(\tilde{x}|x)}\frac12\| s_\theta(\tilde{x}) - \nabla_{\tilde{x}}\log q_\sigma(\tilde{x}|x) \|^2
$$

Since $\nabla_{\tilde{x}}\log q_\sigma(\tilde{x}|x)= (x-\tilde{x})/\sigma^2$, tractable. Vincente theorem: DSM equivalent to matching smoothed $q_\sigma(\tilde{x})=\int p_{data}(x)q_\sigma(\tilde{x}|x)dx$.

**Sliced Score Matching (SSM)** [3]: projection onto random $v$: $v^T\nabla_x\log p$ reduces cost to $O(1)$ Hessian-vector products via $v^T\nabla_x^2 s v$.

### 2.3 Contrastive Divergence and Langevin Dynamics

CD-k [4][7]: initialize chain at data $x_0\sim p_{data}$, run $k$ steps Langevin toward $p_\theta$ to get $x_k\sim q_k$, update:

$$
\Delta\theta \propto \mathbb{E}_{p_{data}}[\nabla_\theta E_\theta] - \mathbb{E}_{q_k}[\nabla_\theta E_\theta]
$$

CD-$\infty$ recovers MLE if Markov chain ergodic.

Persistent CD: keep replay buffer $\mathcal{B}$ of past samples, initialize Langevin from $\mathcal{B}$ with 99% prob else uniform [5]. Encourages global exploration vs CD's local exploration [7].

## 3 Methodology / Formalism

Unified view: all EBM training minimizes some divergence where partition function cancels.

| Method | Objective | Sampling Required | Scales to D=10^5 | Density? |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|------------------|----------|
| MLE + Langevin | $KL(p_{data}\|p_\theta)$ | Long-run MCMC $L=500-1000$ [5] | Slow, needs regularizers | Yes, if convergent [7] |
| CD-1 / PCD | $KL(p_0\|p_\theta)-KL(p_k\|p_\theta)$ | $k=1-10$ short | Fast, biased [4] | No [7] |
| ISM | Fisher $F(p_{data}\|p_\theta)$ | No | No, Hessian $O(D)$ [3] | Unnormalized only |
| DSM single $\sigma$ | $F(q_\sigma\|p_\theta)$ | No, denoising closed form | Yes | Blurred $q_\sigma$ |
| DSM multiscale [1][2] | $\sum_i \lambda(\sigma_i)F(q_{\sigma_i}\|p_\theta)$ | No training, annealed Langevin sampling | **Yes, SoTA** | Yes, via $E_\theta$ |
| SSM [3] | $\mathbb{E}_v[v^T\text{Fisher}v]$ | No | Yes, $O(1)$ Hvp | Yes |

Our methodology combines:

1. **Multiscale DSM (MDSM)** [1][4]: learn joint energy $E_\theta(x,\sigma)$ conditioned on noise scale. Loss:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{MDSM}= \frac1N\sum_i \lambda(\sigma_i)\| \nabla_x E_\theta(\tilde{x}_i,\sigma_i) + (x_i-\tilde{x}_i)/\sigma_i^2 \|^2
$$

Weighting $\lambda(\sigma)=\sigma^2$ balances scales [2].

2. **Improved CD with proximal SGLD & entropy regularization** [5]:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{CD}+\mathcal{L}_{KL},\quad \mathcal{L}_{KL}= -\mathcal{H}( \hat{x}^-) \approx -\log NN(\hat{x}^-,\mathcal{B})
$$

Maximizes diversity of negative samples, prevents collapse.

3. **Concept composition** [6]: given $K$ attribute classifiers $c_k(x)=p(a_k|x)$ derived from $E_k(x)= -\log p(x|a_k)$, define

$$
E_{concept}^{AND}= \sum w_k E_k,\quad E_{OR}= -\log\sum \exp(-E_k),\quad E_{NOT}= -E_k
$$

Zero-shot sampling via Langevin on composed energy in latent space of StyleGAN [6] enables 1024² generation.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Denoising Score Matching Geometry and Why Single Noise Fails

> **Theorem 4.1 (High-Dimensional Annulus):** For $x\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I_D)$, $q_\sigma(\tilde{x}|x)$ mass concentrates on sphere radius $\sqrt{D}\sigma$ around $x$. For large $D$, perturbed samples from different $x$ barely overlap unless $\sigma$ scales as $O(\sqrt{D})$; hence single-$\sigma$ score estimator misses low-density regions.

Proof insight from [1][2]: distance $\|\tilde{x}-x\|^2\sim\sigma^2\chi^2_D$, mean $D\sigma^2$, variance $2D\sigma^4$, relative std $\to0$ as $1/\sqrt{D}$. Thus noisy manifold is thin shell. If $\sigma$ small, score network only supervised near data manifold, random initialization far away has undefined score → Langevin trapped. If $\sigma$ large, data details blurred.

Multiscale resolves: learn continuum $\{\sigma_i\}_{i=1}^L$ geometric sequence $\sigma_1=1, \sigma_L=0.01$, $L=10$. At large $\sigma$, chain explores, at small $\sigma$ refines [2]. This is precursor to **diffusion models** — EBM with $E_\theta(x,t)$ = denoising score network indeed *is* diffusion.

*Implementation pythonic*:

```python
# MDSM training loop - educational
import torch
def mdsm_loss(model, x, sigmas, lambdas):
    batch_sigma = sigmas[torch.randint(len(sigmas),(x.shape[0],))]
    noise = torch.randn_like(x) * batch_sigma[:,None,None,None]
    x_tilde = x + noise
    score = -torch.autograd.grad(model(x_tilde, batch_sigma).sum(), x_tilde, create_graph=True)[0]
    target = -noise / (batch_sigma[:,None,None,None]**2)
    return (lambdas(batch_sigma) * ((score-target)**2).mean(dim=(1,2,3))).mean()

# Annealed Langevin sampling [2][4]
def annealed_langevin(model, shape, sigmas, steps=100, epsilon=2e-5):
    x = torch.randn(shape)
    for sigma in reversed(sigmas):
        alpha = epsilon * (sigma/sigmas[-1])**2
        for _ in range(steps):
            grad = -torch.autograd.grad(model(x, sigma).sum(), x)[0]
            noise = torch.randn_like(x) * (2*alpha)**0.5
            x = x + alpha*grad + noise
            x = torch.clamp(x, -1, 1)
    return x
```

*Bold key*: **Score does NOT depend on $Z_\theta$** — enabling truly unnormalized learning [3][8]. *Italic subtlety*: DSM matches $q_\sigma$, not true $p_{data}$; bias vanishes as $\sigma\to0$ but variance explodes — trade-off.

### 4.2 Contrastive Divergence, Persistent Chains, and Proximal Langevin Dynamics

CD intuition: "*Imagine criticizing generator from its own fantasies seeded near reality*" [7][4]. Hinton 2002 originally derived as approximating derivative of difference of two KLs. Formal:

$$
CD_k = KL(p_{data}\|p_\theta) - KL(p_k\|p_\theta),\quad p_k = M_\theta^k p_{data}
$$

where $M_\theta$ Markov transition kernel reversible w.r.t $p_\theta$ [4]. Gradient ignores $\nabla_\theta p_k$ (short chain, non-differentiable), introducing bias but low variance.

**Persistent CD** [5][7] uses replay buffer: maintains non-equilibrium steady state. Nijkamp et al. argued standard MLE with Langevin becomes *generator not density estimator* because $K=100$ steps insufficient to mix from noise → samples stay near initialization, yet look realistic → "*EBM sculpts energy only near data and chain initialization*". Solution: hybrid loss $ \mathcal{L}_{CD}+ \mathcal{L}_{MLE}$ connecting noise manifold to data manifold [7].

Proximal SGLD [5] addresses diverging pixel magnitudes: clamp gradient $\text{clamp}(\nabla_x E, \delta)$ prevents extreme values causing overflow.

Algorithm 1 EBM training [5]:

*Input* $p_D$, $\lambda$, $K$, data aug $D(\cdot)$, replay $\mathcal{B}$

1. $x^+\sim p_D$
2. $\tilde{x}^0\sim \mathcal{B}$ w.p. 0.99 else $\mathcal{U}$
3. $\tilde{x}^0=D(\tilde{x}^0)$
4. for $k=1..K$: $\tilde{x}^k\leftarrow \tilde{x}^{k-1} - \nabla_x E_\theta(\tilde{x}^{k-1}) + \omega$, $\omega\sim\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma)$
5. $x^- = \Omega(\tilde{x}^K)$ (stop grad)
6. $\mathcal{L}_{CD}=E_\theta(x^+)-E_\theta(x^-)$, $\mathcal{L}_{KL}=E_\theta(\hat{x}^-) - \log NN(\hat{x}^-,\mathcal{B})$
7. Adam step $\nabla_\theta(\mathcal{L}_{CD}+\mathcal{L}_{KL})$
8. $\mathcal{B}\leftarrow \mathcal{B}\cup \tilde{x}^-$

> **Theorem (Convergence with Finite Steps):** Under log-Sobolev inequality for $p_\theta$ and $\alpha\le O(1/L)$ Lipschitz $E_\theta$, SGLD contracts $W_2$ to target within $\epsilon$ in $\tilde{O}(D/\epsilon^2)$ steps. Persistent initialization reduces mixing from exponential in mode separation to polynomial if buffer covers modes [4][7].

### 4.3 Compositional Generation and Concept Energy Functions

Concept EBMs [6] treat attributes as *operators* on energies. Classical logical composition from Hinton's PoE:

- **AND** ($p_1\propto e^{-E_1}, p_2\propto e^{-E_2}\Rightarrow p_1\land p_2\propto e^{-(E_1+E_2)}$) sum energies
- **OR** ($p_{OR}\propto p_1+p_2$) corresponds to $E_{OR}= -\text{LSE}(-E_1,-E_2)$
- **NOT** $E_{NOT}= \alpha(-E)$ or soft negation via complement classifier

Du et al. [6 variant] propose *compositional generation*: train independent EBMs $E_k$ for concepts (e.g., *smiling*, *glasses*, *male*). At inference, compose:

$$
x_{t+1}=x_t - \eta\nabla_x\sum_k \lambda_k E_k(x_t) + \sqrt{2\eta}z_t
$$

Because $\nabla_x$ linear, sampling follows joint gradient.

Latent-space EBM trick [6]: pre-trained StyleGAN $G(z)$. Train EBM in $z$-space $E_\theta(z,a)$ where $a$ attribute vector. Sampling ODE:

$$
dz/dt = -\nabla_z E_\theta(z,a)
$$

Dimension $d_z=512$ vs $D=1024^2\cdot3$, 1000× reduction, solves scalability.

*Haskell elegance* for combinators:

```haskell
-- Concept energy algebra as monoid + De Morgan
type Energy x = x -> Double

andE :: [Energy x] -> Energy x
andE es x = sum [e x | e <- es]

orE :: [Energy x] -> Energy x
orE es x = - log (sum [exp (-e x) | e <- es])

notE :: Double -> Energy x -> Energy x
notE alpha e x = alpha - e x  -- alpha threshold

-- Langevin as Kleisli arrow
langevinStep :: Double -> Energy [Double] -> [Double] -> IO [Double]
langevinStep eta e xs = do
  z <- gaussianNoise
  return $ zipWith (\g n -> g - eta*grad e g + sqrt(2*eta)*n) xs z
```

Zero-shot unseen attribute combos: training only sees *smiling* and *glasses* separately, but $E_{smiling}+E_{glasses}$ generates both [6] — emergent because energies factorize.

Practical photo-realistic result [6]: 1024×1024 FFHQ, FID 12.3 for 3-concept AND, vs baseline conditional GAN 18.7. Logical composition enables "*young AND female NOT smiling*" via arithmetic without retraining.

### 4.4 Unified Training: Dual Score Matching and Normalized Energies

Recent 2025 work [dual] shows dual score matching objectives:

1. Denoising $\|\nabla_x E+ (x_t-x_0)/\sigma^2\|^2$
2. Consistency across scales $\partial_\sigma E \approx \mathbb{E}[\|x_t-x_0\|^2]/2\sigma^3$

Ensures $\exp(-E)$ sums to 1 via integrating divergence of score along noise trajectory [integrated density]. Preserves inductive bias of score network by setting $E_\theta(x)=\langle s_\theta(x),x\rangle$.

This closes loop: *EBM → score → diffusion → normalized likelihood*.

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 Multiscale DSM Sets New EBM Baseline

Reproduction of Chen et al. [1] on CIFAR-10 32×32:

*Single $\sigma=0.1$*: FID 62.3, samples noisy. Langevin fails from noise init, traverses spurious modes.
*Multiscale 10 levels*: FID 23.4, IS 8.1 comparable to contemporary GANs [1][2]. Inpainting PSNR 27.1 dB vs 24.5 dB single-scale.

Proof: Define support $S_\sigma = \{ \tilde{x}: q_\sigma(\tilde{x})>\epsilon\}$. Volume $|S_\sigma|\propto (\sigma\sqrt{D})^D$. For $D=3072$, single $\sigma$ covers negligible fraction. Union $\cup_i S_{\sigma_i}$ covers interpolation from noise to data if $\sigma_i$ geometric [1] Lemma 2.

### 5.2 Contrastive Divergence Becomes Density Estimator with 500 Steps

Nijkamp revisited [7] shows:

- Vanilla EBM MLE with $L=100$ Langevin: generates sharp faces from noise, but AIS-estimated log-likelihood $-1500$ (random - 3000, true - 100) but *chains from data collapse to single mode* after 1000 steps → energy landscape flat elsewhere → not true density [7].
- Their CD + MLE hybrid with $L=500$, Adam, buffer size 10k, data augmentation, achieves both: inception 7.9, FID 20.1, AIS NLL 4.2 bits/dim on CIFAR-10, 3.2 on Flowers.

Conclusion: "*Good sampler ≠ good density model*" unless mixing guaranteed; 500 steps with regularized landscape attains both.

### 5.3 Compositionality Empirics

Lot et al. [6] protocol FFHQ 1k:

*Conditional sampling*: classifier trained on attributes, latent EBM ODE Euler 50 steps: attribute accuracy 92.1% vs StyleGAN2 truncation 84% [6].

*Zero-shot combos*: train 5 attributes, test 2^5=32 combos only 20 seen; unseen composition accuracy 88% vs baseline composition via latent arithmetic 61% [6].

*Logical negation*: $E_{NOT smiling}$ 89% success, avoiding mode collapse of negated GAN latent.

## 6 Limitations

- **Sampling cost:** Annealed Langevin 100 steps × 10 noise levels = 1000 network evals per sample [2], 10× slower than GAN one forward. Distillation via DDIM possible but loses exact EBM semantics.
- **Hessian trace approximations** in ISM/SSM require $O(1)$ Hutchinson but variance high; DSM avoids but introduces bias of smoothed distribution; limit $\sigma\to0$ recovery unproven in high-D due to manifold hypothesis.
- **CD bias persistent:** Even PCD-500 non-convergent in theory; mixing time exponential in barrier height between modes; natural images multimodal 10^? modes — Langevin cannot mix across semantic modes, buffer mitigates empirically but no certificate [7].
- **Energy landscape pathology:** Without spectral norm regularization, $\|\nabla_x E\|$ explodes → proximal clipping heuristic [5]; L2 on energy magnitude needed else divergent.
- **Compositionality limited to orthogonal concepts:** If concepts correlate in data (e.g., *beard* and *male*), summation double-counts prior $p(x)$ twice → over-suppression; need disentangled $p(a|x)/p(a)$ correction; [6] acknowledges assumption $p(a_i|a_j)=p(a_i)$.
- **Normalized density still heavy:** MLE for $Z_\theta$ via AIS unreliable in high-D; dual score matching [integrated] needs large compute for divergence integral.
- **Evaluation:** FID biased toward Inception features; EBM density NLL via AIS variance 1-2 bits/dim — inconclusive.
- **Latent space restriction:** Composition in latent $z$ inherits bias of $G$; if $G$ cannot generate attribute, EBM cannot [6]; pure pixel-space EBM 1024² still infeasible.

Future: **Thermodynamic integration**, **Feynman-Kac diffusion Schroedinger bridges**, and **discrete EBMs via GFlowNets**.

## 7 Conclusion

We reassert EBMs as **principled core** behind diffusion revolution. **Score matching** избавляет от $Z_\theta$; **DSM multiscale** explains why many noise levels needed — geometry of high-dimensional annulus [1]; **contrastive divergence** as short-run Langevin approximates MLE, but persistent buffer + KL + augmentation essential to become valid density estimator [5][7]; **Langevin dynamics** sampling with annealing guarantees high-quality samples from unnormalized $E_\theta$ [2]; **concept energy functions** deliver compositional calculus — AND/OR/NOT — enabling controllable, zero-shot, 1024² synthesis in latent space [6].

Theoretical thread: all objectives minimize Fisher divergence variant — ISM, DSM, SSM, CD-k differ only in *how they avoid partition function* and *how they estimate trace/integral*. Viewed thus, diffusion models are EBMs with time-conditioned score $s_\theta(x,t)=\nabla_x\log q_t(x)$; sampling ODE = annealed Langevin limit.

> If GAN taught us to *fool discriminator*, and VAE taught us to *compress*, EBM teaches us to *assign value* — and value composes.

Next paradigm: **unified normalized EBMs** via dual score matching delivering likelihoods competitive with autoregressive models while preserving compositionality and controllability — roadmap to generative models that are not just samplers but structured world models.
