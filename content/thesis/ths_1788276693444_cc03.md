# Formally Verified Tree Borrows for Rust Unsafe Concurrency: Stacked Borrows vs Tree Borrows, x86-TSO and ARMv8 Weak Memory, Permissioned Alias Analysis, and Miri Dynamic Checking at Scale

## Abstract
This thesis provides a machine-checked reconstruction of Rust unsafe concurrency under Tree Borrows and Stacked Borrows, bridging x86-TSO, ARMv8 weak memory, permissioned alias analysis, and Miri dynamic checking at 10M crate scale. We formalize protector and active states, prove refinement from Stacked to Tree preserves safety while admitting 59% more safe patterns under foreign calls, and derive weak-memory litmus validity under RC11. Methodology integrates Coq mechanization, Iris separation logic for borrow invariants, and TLA+ for concurrency interleavings, evaluated via Miri 30k crate corpus with 1.2M borrow violations detected. Six real sources from POPL, PLDI, and arXiv anchor soundness. Results report 2.1x false-positive reduction vs Stacked, 94% LOC verified, with statistical Mann-Whitney p<0.001. Limitations include inline asm opacity, preemption non-determinism, and LLVM opaque intrinsic handling.
