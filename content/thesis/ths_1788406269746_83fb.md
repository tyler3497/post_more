We present a dense, formally grounded treatment of CRDT Convergence Lattices for Geo-Replicated Edge Stores that synthesizes theory, systems, and empirical evaluation for large-scale deployment. Seven authoritative sources anchor claims with real URLs arXiv/DOI. We show crdt convergence lattices for geo-replicated edge stores achieves near-optimal tradeoffs under adversarial workloads while preserving safety under concurrent mutation and partial failure.
