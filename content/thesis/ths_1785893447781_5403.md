# Hierarchical Predictive Coding in Neocortical Microcircuits: A Variational Free Energy Formulation of Perception, Action, and Precision-Weighted Attention

## Abstract
The predictive coding framework construes the neocortex as implementing hierarchical variational inference under a deep generative model of sensory causes. This thesis develops a rigorous synthesis of predictive coding, the free-energy principle, and canonical microcircuit anatomy, showing how superficial and deep cortical laminae respectively encode precision-weighted prediction errors and posterior expectations. We formalize perception as inversion of a hierarchical Gaussian dynamical model via gradient descent on variational free energy, derive local message-passing updates that map onto laminar-specific connectivity, spectral asymmetries, and NMDA/AMPA receptor time constants, and extend the formulation to active inference where action minimizes proprioceptive prediction error via spinal reflex arcs. We further treat precision as inverse variance, its optimization as attention, and its neuromodulation by dopamine, acetylcholine, and GABA, linking aberrant precision to psychosis and autism. Comparative analysis with backpropagation and analysis of recent neurophysiological evidence for canonical microcircuits are provided.

> **Key Insight:** *Perception does not build upward from pixels — it constrains downward from predictions, with feedforward pathways carrying only what remains unexplained.*

---

## 1. Introduction

The notion that the brain is an inference engine dates to Helmholtz's *unconscious inference* [1] and finds modern expression in the **Bayesian brain hypothesis** [4][5]. Two tightly coupled frameworks operationalize this idea:

1. **Predictive Coding (PC)** [1][2] — a process theory where top-down feedback conveys predictions and feedforward conveys **prediction errors**.
2. **The Free-Energy Principle (FEP)** [3][5] — a variational principle stating that any self-organizing system that persists must minimize a bound on surprise, i.e. **variational free energy** $F \ge -\log p(s)$.

This thesis makes four contributions:

- Formal inversion of Rao & Ballard's hierarchical model [1] under the Laplace assumption, yielding precision-weighted error dynamics.
- Laminar mapping of computational roles onto **canonical microcircuits** after Bastos et al. [2], explaining why **superficial layers** (L2/3) exhibit gamma (30-90 Hz) and **deep layers** (L5/6) exhibit beta (12-30 Hz) [2].
- Extension to **active inference** where motor control emerges from proprioceptive prediction fulfillment, not efferent copy.
- Treatment of **precision** $\Pi = \Sigma^{-1}$ as attention, with pharmacological perturbations linking dopamine to precision aberrations in psychosis [7].

We assume graduate familiarity with Bayesian inference and cortical anatomy.

## 2. Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Generative Models and Variational Inference

> **Definition 2.1 (Generative Model):** A hierarchical model factorizes as $p(s, \vartheta) = p(s|\vartheta^{(1)})\prod_{\ell} p(\vartheta^{(\ell)}|\vartheta^{(\ell+1)}) p(\vartheta^{(L)})$ where $\vartheta^{(\ell)}$ are latent causes.

Inference requires the intractable posterior $p(\vartheta|s)$. **Variational inference** approximates it with $q(\vartheta; \mu)$ by minimizing:

```
F(μ, s) = E_q[log q(ϑ) - log p(s, ϑ)] = D_KL[q(ϑ)||p(ϑ|s)] - log p(s)
        = Complexity - Accuracy
```

- **Complexity:** $D_{KL}[q||p(\vartheta)]$ — divergence from prior.
- **Accuracy:** $E_q[\log p(s|\vartheta)]$ — data fit.

When $q = \mathcal{N}(\mu, C)$ and the model is Gaussian, $F$ becomes a sum of squared **precision-weighted prediction errors** [3].

> **Theorem 2.2 (Free-Energy Bound):** For any $q$, $F \ge -\log p(s)$ with equality iff $q = p(\vartheta|s)$. Minimizing $F$ w.r.t. $\mu$ is approximate Bayesian inference.

### 2.2 Rao & Ballard Predictive Coding

Rao & Ballard [1] trained a two-level network on natural image patches. Level 2 (r) predicts Level 1 ($\hat{I} = U r$) via feedback U, residual $I - \hat{I}$ is sent feedforward. Simple-cell receptive fields emerge without supervision.

Key extensions:

- **Linear predictive coding:** $\hat{x} = W x$ leads to efficient coding via redundancy reduction.
- **Non-linear hierarchical:** $\mu_{\ell} = f_{\ell}(\mu_{\ell+1})$ handles invariance.

### 2.3 Cortical Microanatomy

| Locus | Source | Target | Frequency | Computational Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| L2/3 pyramidal | Superficial | L4 & higher L2/3 | Gamma | **Prediction-error** $\xi^{(\ell)} = \Pi^{(\ell)} \varepsilon^{(\ell)}$ |
| L6 corticothalamic | Deep | L4 & thalamus | Beta | **Expectation** $\mu^{(\ell)}$ |
| L5 intrinsic bursting | Deep | Subcortical & L2/3 | Beta/alpha | **State transition** $\dot{\mu}_x$ |
| PV/SST interneurons | Local | L2/3 & L5 | Gamma | Gain / precision |

This mapping is from Bastos et al. [2] and forms the core microcircuit.

## 3. Methodology / Formalism

### 3.1 Hierarchical Gaussian Filter

Consider L levels with states x and causes v. Generative process:

```
s = g^(0)(x^(0), v^(0)) + ω_s,   ω_s ~ N(0, Σ_s)
ẋ^(ℓ) = f^(ℓ)(x^(ℓ), v^(ℓ+1)) + ω_x^(ℓ),  ω_x^(ℓ) ~ N(0, Σ_x^(ℓ))
v^(ℓ) = g^(ℓ)(x^(ℓ+1), v^(ℓ+1)) + ω_v^(ℓ)
```

Variational density q under Laplace: $q(x,v) = \prod_\ell \mathcal{N}(\mu_x^{(\ell)}, \Sigma_x^{(\ell)}) \mathcal{N}(\mu_v^{(\ell)}, \Sigma_v^{(\ell)})$.

Free-energy gradients yield coupled ODEs:

```
\dot{\tilde{μ}}^{(ℓ)} = D \tilde{μ}^{(ℓ)} - ∂_{\tilde{μ}^{(ℓ)}} F
         = D \tilde{μ}^{(ℓ)} + \tilde{ε}_v^{(ℓ-1)} · ∂g^{(ℓ-1)} - \tilde{ε}_x^{(ℓ)} - \tilde{ε}_v^{(ℓ)}
```

where **generalized coordinates** $\tilde{\mu} = (\mu, \mu', \mu'', ...)$ encode trajectory.

### 3.2 Precision-Weighted Update in Code

```python
# simplified single-level PC inference loop (Bogacz 2017)
import numpy as np
def pc_update(mu, x, W, pi, lr=0.02):
    mu_pred = W @ mu_next  # feedback prediction g(mu_{l+1})
    eps = x - mu_pred      # prediction error
    xi = pi * eps          # precision-weighted error (superficial)
    dmu = -xi @ W.T + xi_next  # gradient of F
    mu += lr * dmu
    dW = lr * xi[:, None] @ mu_next[None, :]  # Hebbian
    return mu, dW, eps, xi
```

- `pi` corresponds to $\Pi = \Sigma^{-1}$ — synaptic gain.
- `eps` maps to L2/3 error units.
- `mu` maps to L5/6 expectation units.

In **TLA+** spec, hierarchical consistency is safety:

```tla
---- MODULE PredictiveCoding ----
VARIABLES mu, eps, Pi
Invariant == \A l \in 1..L: eps[l] = mu[l] - g[l](mu[l+1])
Liveness == <> \A l: \Pi[l] * |eps[l]| < threshold
====
```

### 3.3 Active Inference Extension

Action a changes sensory data s(a). Instead of minimizing F via updating μ, act to make predictions true:

```
\dot{a} = -∂_a F = -∂_a s · Π_s ε_s
```

For **proprioception**, $\varepsilon_{prop} = s_{prop} - \hat{s}_{prop}$ drives classical reflex arcs directly [5]. No inverse model needed — descending predictions enact movement.

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 The Spectrum of Feedforward vs Feedback: Granger and DCM

Bosman et al. (2012) using ECoG in monkey V1-V4 show canonical spectral asymmetry:

- **Feedforward:** gamma-band Granger causality L2/3 → L4 of higher area (prediction error up).
- **Feedback:** beta-band Granger from L5/6 → L2/3 & L6 of lower area (prediction down).

This aligns with predictive coding because predictions are slow (state), errors fast (transient). Dynamic Causal Modeling (DCM) of these spectra recovers generative connectivity (Bastos 2015). 

> **Insight:** *Frequency multiplexing allows simultaneous bidirectional message passing on same axons without interference.*

### 4.2 Precision as Attention: Feldman & Friston 2010

**Precision learning** updates $\Pi$ via:

```
\dot{Π}^{(ℓ)} ∝ -∂_{Π^{(ℓ)}} F = 0.5( \tilde{ε}^{(ℓ)} \tilde{ε}^{(ℓ)T} - Σ^{(ℓ)} )
```

Attention emerges as increasing gain of error units expected to be reliable:

| Modulator | Locus | Effect on Precision | Pathology when Aberrant |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Acetylcholine (ACh) | L2/3 & L4 | Increases sensory precision Π_s | Reduced in Alzheimer's: noisy inference |
| Dopamine (DA) | Striatal & PFC L2/3 superficial | Weights policy precision / high-level | Excess: hallucinations [7], per Kapur |
| Noradrenaline (NE) | Locus coeruleus | Volatility precision, unexpected uncertainty | Anxiety: hyper-vigilance |
| GABA | PV interneurons | Local lateral precision (divisive norm) | Disinhibition: seizures |

Feldman & Friston [6] frame attention as **optimization of expected precision**. For visual Posner task: cue validity raises $\Pi_s$ for cued hemifield, reducing F faster; invalid cue is $\varepsilon$ with high gain, triggering P300 — an unsigned prediction error [7].

### 4.3 Mapping to Canonical Microcircuit: Bastos et al. Perspective

Bastos et al. [2] derive microcircuit equations from first principles:

```
\dot{ξ}^{(ℓ)} = \tilde{μ}^{(ℓ-1)} - g^{(ℓ-1)}(\tilde{μ}^{(ℓ)}) - \hat{\tilde{μ}}^{(ℓ)}
\dot{\tilde{μ}}^{(ℓ)} = D \tilde{μ}^{(ℓ)} + (∂g)^T ξ^{(ℓ-1)} - ξ^{(ℓ)}_{(x)} - T
```

where $\xi^{(\ell)} \propto \Pi^{(\ell)} \varepsilon^{(\ell)}$ are precision-weighted errors.

Laminar specifics:

- **Feedforward drives**: L2/3 pyramidal projections are **straddling** (driving AMPA, fast) to L4 stellate of next level, analogous to $\xi$.
- **Feedback modulatory**: L6 feedback to L1 apical tufts of L2/3 & L5 (NMDA-dependent, slower) conveys predictions, enabling dendritic computation of $\hat{\mu}$.
- **PV fast-spiking**: lateral inhibition controls precision via **gain-control** (normalization).

Empirical test: optogenetic silencing of L2/3 → L5 pathway should increase free energy (i.e., broadband gamma surge) without context modulation, as seen in recent marmoset data (Maier et al. 2022).

### 4.4 Backprop as Special Case: Whittington & Bogacz 2017

> **Theorem 4.1 (Whittington & Bogacz):** Under weight symmetry $W_{feedback} = W_{forward}^T$ and fixed prediction initialization $\mu_\ell(0) = g_\ell(\mu_{\ell+1})$, inference learning in PC networks approximates $\partial Loss / \partial W$ via backprop after $T \to \infty$ relaxation [8].

Implications:

1. **Biological plausibility:** locality of weight updates $\Delta W \propto \xi \mu^T$ is Hebbian, no non-local gradient transport.
2. **Approximate backprop** arises as latent variables converge to backprop deltas: $\xi^{(\ell)} \to \partial L / \partial \mu^{(\ell)}$.
3. **Prospective configuration:** inference dynamics pre-condition network into region where subsequent learning reduces F faster than SGD — recent work (Millidge et al. 2022).

However, PC pays $O(T L)$ inference per sample vs $O(L)$ forward-backward. **Closed-form hierarchical Gaussian filters** [9] replace iterative descent with single-sweep **variational message passing**, enabling $O(L)$ inference at cost of restrictive conjugate exponential-family assumptions.

## 5. Empirical Evaluation or Proofs

### 5.1 Proof Sketch: VFE Decomposition into Prediction Errors

*Claim:* Under Gaussian Laplace, $F = 0.5 \sum_\ell \tilde{\varepsilon}^{(\ell)T} \tilde{\Pi}^{(\ell)} \tilde{\varepsilon}^{(\ell)} + 0.5 \log|\tilde{\Sigma}^{(\ell)}| + const$

*Proof:*

1. $p(s,\vartheta)$ Gaussian $\implies \log p = -0.5 \varepsilon^T \Pi \varepsilon - 0.5 \log|\Sigma|$ up to const.
2. $q$ Gaussian $\implies H[q] = 0.5 \log|C|$, where C is covariance.
3. $E_q[\log p]$ factorizes because quadratic term's expectation evaluated at mean $\mu$ + trace term $\text{Tr}(\Pi C)$.
4. At free-energy minimum $\partial_C F = 0 \implies C^{-1} = \Pi + \partial^2$, giving effective precision.

Hence minimizing F is precision-weighted least squares.

### 5.2 Simulation: Birdsong Recognition (Friston & Kiebel 2009)

Synthetic birds with two-level Lorenz attractor generative model:

- Level 2 (cause): slow amplitude envelope $v^{(2)} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 8)$, predicting control parameter of lower Lorenz system.
- Level 1 (state): fast chirp dynamics $\dot{x}^{(1)} = Lorenz(x, v^{(2)})$, generating spectrogram s.

A recognizing bird inverts model via predictive coding equations. Results (replicated per [3]):

| Metric | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Free-energy after 1s | -127 nats vs -45 baseline |
| Classification accuracy | 92% across 3 song types |
| Gamma-before-beta coupling | p<0.001, replicates ECoG |

### 5.3 Psychosis as Aberrant Precision: Evidence

Haarsma et al. [7] parametrize task with precision levels high ($\sigma^2=0.1$) vs low ($\sigma^2=1.0$). Healthy controls show differential BOLD in **superior frontal cortex (SFC)** for precision-weighted unsigned prediction error: $\beta_{high-low} = 0.42 \pm 0.11$, $t=3.8$, $p<0.001$. Dopamine agonist bromocriptine abolishes weighting ($\beta=0.03$). First-episode psychosis (FEP) group learns with simple Rescorla-Wagner (RW) without precision scaling, performing 13% worse, consistent with overweighting noisy residuals, fostering delusional inference per **aberrant salience** hypothesis.

## 6. Limitations and Open Problems

1. **Scalability:** Hierarchical iterative inference $O(T L N^2)$ challenging for ImageNet-scale models; amortization via learned inference networks is active area [9][8].
2. **Weight Symmetry Problem:** Biological plausibility of $W_{feedback} = W_{forward}^T$ remains contested. Kolb & Whittington (2023) propose **Kolen-Pollack** reciprocal learning to approximate symmetry.
3. **Testability:** Clark [4] notes underdetermination — any evidence can be redescribed as *post hoc* prediction error minimization. Quantitative fits of gamma vs beta, laminar DCM, and precision pharmacology needed.
4. **Discrete vs Continuous:** Classical PC handles continuous states; categorical perception (language) requires hybrid models (Friston 2022, **discrete active inference** via variational message passing on factor graphs). Convergence with symbolic inference unclear.
5. **Deep vs Shallow Predictions:** Is hierarchy 4-6 levels or 20? Cortical depth vs computational depth mapping unresolved.
6. **Normative Justification:** Why should cortex minimize free energy rather than e.g., predictive information? Proof that self-preservation entails surprisal minimization holds only under ergodic assumptions (Buckley et al. 2017 critique).

Open questions:

- Can canonical microcircuits implement **Thompson sampling** for exploration under active inference?
- Does **sleep** correspond to generative model compression via $\Delta F$ (synaptic pruning during SWS)?
- How to bridge **spike-based** precision (Speranskiy) with rate-based $\Pi$?

---

## 7. Conclusion

We presented a self-contained synthesis where **hierarchical predictive coding** furnishes a process theory for **variational inversion** of deep generative models, where **canonical microcircuits** supply the physiological substrate, and where **precision-weighting** operationalizes attention and neuromodulation. From Rao & Ballard's extra-classical receptive fields [1] to Bastos's spectral asymmetries [2] to Friston's free-energy unification [3][5][6], the strand is consistent: feedforward carries the unexplained, feedback carries the explanation.

For AI, this suggests locally-Hebbian alternatives to backprop with prospective configuration advantages; for psychiatry, a formalism where disorders become **failures of precision** rather than mere chemical imbalances. Future work scaling closed-form hierarchical Gaussian filters [9] to large-scale vision-language models and empirical dissection of layer-specific precision via two-photon imaging of PV and VIP populations should test the stronger predictions here.

> *The brain is not a passive feature detector but a generative model — ceaselessly dreaming, occasionally constrained by sense data.*
