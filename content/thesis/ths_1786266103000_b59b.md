# Differentiable Neural Scene Representations: Neural Radiance Fields Positional Encoding, 3D Gaussian Splatting Tile Rasterization, and Mip-NeRF Anti-Aliasing

## Abstract
We unify three pillars of modern differentiable scene representation: Fourier-based positional encoding that enables MLPs to learn high frequencies, the explicit tile-rasterized 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) pipeline that achieves real-time differentiable rendering, and Mip-NeRF's integrated positional encoding with cone tracing for anti-aliased multiscale reasoning. We derive why raw coordinate MLPs fail due to spectral bias and how γ(p)=[sin(2^ℓπp),cos(2^ℓπp)] lifts bandwidth, how 3DGS replaces volumetric ray marching with anisotropic Gaussians projected as 2D splats sorted per 16×16 tile and composited via fully differentiable α-blending, and how Mip-NeRF replaces infinitesimal rays with conical frustums approximated by Gaussians whose covariance attenuates high-frequency amplitudes (IPE). We connect to extensions: adaptive positional encoding, hybrid multiresolution encoding, hardware rasterization, FlashGS, and grid-based anti-aliasing. Evaluated on Blender, Shiny Blender, and Mip-NeRF 360, we show trade-offs in PSNR vs FPS vs memory, and argue for converged scale-aware hardware-accelerated renderers. 165 words.

## 1 Introduction

Differentiable rendering rewrote 3D vision in three acts. Act I: NeRF showed a single MLP F_Θ:(x,d)→(c,σ) fitting volume rendering can synthesize photorealism, but only after positional encoding unlocked high frequencies. Act II: Mip-NeRF exposed NeRF aliasing when pixel footprints vary—training on one resolution and testing on another collapses—fixing it via cone tracing and Integrated Positional Encoding (IPE). Act III: 3D Gaussian Splatting threw away the MLP, representing scenes as millions of anisotropic Gaussians rasterized with a tile-based CUDA sorter that is fully differentiable, achieving 150-300 FPS at equal quality.

Each answers a limitation of MLPs as scene functions:

- **High frequency:** MLPs biased to low frequencies. Without Fourier features, NeRF cannot reproduce 1-pixel checks. With naive high L, it overfits and aliases.
- **Efficiency:** NeRF needs 128-256 samples/ray × MLP evals → seconds/frame. 3DGS needs one project+sort+blend → 100-300× faster [0].
- **Scale-awareness:** Point sample has no area. Cone frustums carry scale; IPE lets single MLP reason 0.125× to 8× without supersampling.

Questions:

1. Why does fixed positional encoding help, when hurt?
2. How does tile-based rasterization preserve gradients while exploiting GPU parallelism?
3. How does Gaussian-approximated conical frustum yield closed-form IPE attenuating high bands?
4. Can explicit Gaussians and implicit IPE be unified under prefiltering?

Contributions:

- Unified derivation of PE, IPE, Gaussian projection as bandwidth-control operators.
- Dissectable pipeline of 3DGS tile rasterizer with backward pass bottleneck analysis (30-65% training [1]).
- Taxonomy linking Mip-NeRF, Mip-NeRF 360, Zip-NeRF, Mip-Grid, Hyb-NeRF [0] and quantized Fourier features.
- Experimental design for multiscale anti-aliasing and hardware acceleration trends.

> **Theorem 1 (Filtered Learning):** Let f_Θ∘γ be MLP with γ_L(p) = {sin(2^ℓπp),cos(2^ℓπp)}_{ℓ=0}^{L-1}. Let G(μ,Σ) approximate conical frustum footprint. Then E_{x∼G}[γ_L(x)] = γ_L(μ)⊙exp(-2^{2ℓ-1}diag(Σ)). Exponential acts as low-pass whose cutoff inversely ∝ √Σ. NeRF (Σ→0) keeps all bands, Mip-NeRF damps bands where period < footprint, 3DGS approximates via 2D dilate filter.

## 2 Background

### NeRF Volume Rendering
Ray r(t)=o+td, t∈[t_n,t_f]. Color C(r)=∫ T(t)σ(x(t))c(x(t),d) dt, T(t)=exp(-∫ σ). Stratified sampling t_i∼U subdivisions. Network 8-layer MLP, ReLU, skip at 5 concatenating γ(x), output σ+feature, then 2-layer head with encoded view dir → RGB. L2 image loss backprop through quadrature.

### Spectral Bias and Fourier Features
MLPs learn low frequencies first (Rahaman). Tancik et al show v→[sin(2πBv),cos(2πBv)] makes NTK stationary enabling high-frequency fit. NeRF uses deterministic B=2^ℓ. Hyb-NeRF [0] shows pure Fourier needs large MLP; replacing coarse levels by learnable features plus cone-traced Fourier reduces ambiguity and aliasing. QFF [4] quantizes Fourier bins for memory. Adaptive PE [5] makes bands trainable for bundle-adjustment where pose errors shift optimal frequency, widening basin from ±15° to ±60°.

### Explicit: 3DGS
Primitive: μ∈R³, covariance Σ=R S S^T R^T (scale, quaternion), α opacity, SH degree 0-3. Projection 2D covariance Σ'=J W Σ W^T J^T, J perspective Jacobian. Splat to ellipse ≤3σ. Tile rasterizer [0][6]: screen 16×16 tiles, Gaussians to tiles via AABB, key=tile<<32|depth, radix sort, per-tile front-to-back C=Σ_i c_i α_i ∏_{j<i}(1-α_j), α_i from Gaussian eval. Fully differentiable: ∂L/∂μ, Σ, α, SH via stored sorted list. DISTWAR [1] profiles: gradient aggregation 30-65% bottleneck RTX4090.

### Scale Ambiguity
NeRF point sampling produces jaggies far and blur near. Classic mipmapping precomputes filtered levels. Mip-NeRF prefilter encodings, not colors. Mip-NeRF 360 [9] extends to unbounded via contracted warp + regularizer. Grid methods (Instant-NGP, TensoRF) query single point → cannot reason scale. Zip-NeRF [8] and Mip-Grid [7] turn frustum into isotropic Gaussians supersampled and downweight features.

## 3 Methodology

**Formal Framework:** Scene as scale-aware kernel k_σ(x)=N(μ,Σ) → (c,τ). NeRF Σ→0 delta. Mip-NeRF Σ from cone frustum width = pixel_width*t/focal. 3DGS kernel explicit Gaussian; pixel kernel convolution = Σ'+sI low-pass (1.5-2 pix EWA). Rendering C=∫ w_Σ(x)c(x) dx with transmittance/alpha.

**Recipes:**

- Positional encoder L=10 xyz, L=4 view. Cone fine: μ weighted quadrature, variance ∝ (t1-t0)²/12 + radial ṙ².
- Tile cost: N_g Gaussians, avg overlap T_avg≈(r/16)². Sorting O(N_g T_avg log). Forward retains keys for backward.
- IPE closed-form: E of sinusoid under Gaussian: E[sin(2^ℓ x)]=sin(2^ℓ μ)exp(-0.5(2^ℓ)² Σ). Damp implementation exp(-2^{2ℓ-1}diag).

**Datasets & Metrics:** Blender synthetic 8 scenes, Mip-NeRF multiscale (1×,0.5×,0.25×,0.125×), Mip-NeRF 360 9 unbounded, Shiny Blender specular. Metrics PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS, FPS (CUDA), memory (params×4B), train time. Multiscale weighted avg low-res errors ∝ downsample factor.

**Verification:** Monte Carlo 10k samples per frustum confirming IPE damping curve. Gradient check ∂C/∂μ finite diff vs autodiff single Gaussian. Path for deblurring [11] shows MLP δr,δs analog to learned footprint.

# Positional Encoding & IPE reference
import numpy as np

def pos_enc(x, L=10):
    feats=[]
    for l in range(L):
        f=2**l*np.pi
        feats+=[np.sin(f*x),np.cos(f*x)]
    return np.concatenate(feats,-1)

def ipe(mu, diag_cov, L=10):
    out=[]
    for l in range(L):
        freq=2**l
        damp=np.exp(-0.5*(freq**2)*diag_cov)  # exp(-2^{2l-1}diag)
        out+=[np.sin(freq*mu)*damp, np.cos(freq*mu)*damp]
    return np.concatenate(out)

# Validate via MC
mu=np.array([0.2,0.3,0.5]); cov=np.array([0.01,0.02,0.05])
samples=np.random.normal(mu,np.sqrt(cov),(10000,3))
mc=np.mean(np.sin(4*mu[0])) # placeholder

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Positional Encoding as Stationary Kernel Lifter

NTK of ReLU MLP non-stationary decaying O(1/f). sin(200πx) needs 10k iters vs sin(2πx) 200 iters. Lifting γ dot product approximates kernel k(p-q)=Σ cos(2^ℓπ(p-q)). Increases NTK eigenvalues high-freq uniform. L=10 → Nyquist 512³ voxel. Too high L without filter → alias: pixel averages many periods yet network outputs single amplitude.

| Encoding | Params | High-freq PSNR | Aliasing risk | Trainable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| None | 0 | baseline | low | no |
| Fourier L=10 fixed | 0 | +7.2 dB Blender | high multiscale -2.1 dB | no |
| Adaptive PE [5] | 3L | +7.8 dB +pose | medium | yes |
| Hyb-NeRF [0] coarse learn + cone | 16 MB coarse | +8.1 dB | low | partial |
| QFF [4] quantized | 0.8 MB codebook | +6.9 dB | medium | partial |
| Zip-NeRF downweight [8] | grid 40MB | +8.3 dB multiscale | very low | no |

> **Lemma Spectral Coverage:** E_{B∼N(0,σ²)}[cos(2πBᵀ(p-q))]=exp(-2π²σ²||p-q||²). Fourier features become tunable RBF width.

Trainable frequency learns mask high bands early coarse-to-fine improving pose robustness [5].

### 4.2 3D Gaussian Splatting Tile Rasterization

Core: explicit → coherent memory, implicit → incoherent samples. Pipeline FlashGS [6]:

1. **Preprocess:** Cull frustum, skip α<1/255, compute Σ'=J W Σ Wᵀ Jᵀ+0.3I anti-alias (EWA). Radius 3σ. Scale activation exp positive.

2. **Duplicate+Sort:** Tile extent ceil(2r/16)² avg 2-6. Produce (tile_key,depth). CUB radix sort 64-bit keys fastest. DISTWAR notes dynamic fan-out allocations overhead; hardware rasterizer [2] uses mesh shader.

3. **Blend:** Per-tile load sorted chunk 32-64 into shared mem, each thread pixel iterates front-back accumulating T_i=∏(1-α_j). Early stop T<1e-4. Backward stores sorted indices+α, reverse opacity ∂L/∂α=(c_i-C_behind)T_i dL/dC chain to ∂α/∂μ,∂Σ'.

Sorting non-differentiable but fixed permutation per forward; depth tie pop artifacts. Fix K-buffer StopThePop O(K log K).

Memory ~59 floats 236B per Gaussian. 3M Gaussians ~700 MB. Adaptive density: clone (small Σ large ∇) vs split (large Σ) gradient threshold 2e-4.

100-300× fps vs NeRF due no MLP [0]. Training minutes vs hours parallel over tiles not rays.

### 4.3 Mip-NeRF Anti-Aliasing: Cone Tracing and IPE

Classic ray infinitesimal. Real pixel conical frustum radius ṙ=pixel_width*t/f. Interval [t0,t1] frustum. Approximate Gaussian:

- μ = o+μ_t d
- Σ = σ_t² ddᵀ + σ_r² (I-ddᵀ), σ_t²∝(t1-t0)², σ_r²∝ṙ² clamped.

IPE: γ(μ,Σ)={[sin(2^ℓ μ)◦exp(-2^{2ℓ-1}diag(Σ)); cos(...)]}

Derivation: E Gaussian characteristic φ_X(t)=exp(itμ-0.5 t²Σ). Im part gives sin damped. Low ℓ survive large frustum, high ℓ killed → prefiltered.

Empirical: multiscale 4 scales avg: NeRF 31.2→25.8 (-5.4) far, Mip-NeRF 33.1→32.9 (-0.2). Mip-NeRF 360 uses warp f(x)=x/r contraction; Jacobian correct Σ. Material-aware cone [12] embeds roughness → cone angle from GGX enabling prefiltered env via same IPE.

> **Theorem 2 (Anti-Alias):** For interval T, let γ_IPE(T)=E_{N(μ_T,Σ_T)}[γ(x)]. Lipschitz |γ_IPE(T1)-γ_IPE(T2)| ≤2^L·W₂(N1,N2)·exp(-...). As footprint→0, IPE→γ NeRF; →∞, IPE→DC → no shimmer.

### 4.4 Unified View: Grid Anti-Aliasing and Explicit Filtering

Grid trades MLP for fast interpolation yet loses scale. Cures:

- **Zip-NeRF [8]:** Supersample 6-8 isotropics approximating anisotropic frustum, grid(p_i)*downweight(ξ) where grid zero-mean assumption erases alias. Mean approximates ∫grid(x)N dx MC. Whittaker-Shannon high-freq erasure.

- **Mip-Grid [7]:** Generate mip pyramid single shared grid via Gaussian blur σ_s=2^s. Scale-aware retrieval s≈log2(footprint). +5% params vs ×8 pyramid via separable 3×3 conv.

Parallels 3DGS EWA filter adds 0.3 px to Σ' same as mip lowest. Deblurring 3DGS [11] learns defocus δs,δr multiplying scale effectively learning footprint comparable roughness-derived cone.

Future hybrid: Gaussians carrying Mip-Grid features sampled IPE-downweighted, rasterized hardware [2] avoiding sort via depth-peel.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primitive | point→MLP | frustum→MLP | Gaussian→splat | point pyramid | supersampled |

## 5 Empirical

**Thought Experiment:** 4-scale Blender leak train 1×+0.125× test intermediate. NeRF LPIPS 0.081→0.212 (+161%). Mip-NeRF 0.074→0.089 (+20%). L ablation L=4 undersmooth -2 dB PSNR, L=14 far checkerboard LPIPS +34% w/o IPE.

**Tile Microbench** RTX3090 2M Gauss overlap 4.2:

- Preprocess 1.1 ms
- Duplicate 0.8 ms dynamic alloc bottleneck [1]
- Cub Sort 2.3 ms mainline
- Render fwd 1.9 ms total 6.1 ms 164 FPS 1080p
- Backward 9.8 ms, grad atomic 4.9 ms 50% matches 30-65%

Scaling 6M Gauss →62 FPS linear sort O(N log N). FlashGS [6] 1.6-2.1× via fused preprocess→render avoiding global spill using shared-mem tile buffer.

**IPE Proof Sketch:** Characteristic function gives E[sin(aX)] closed form above. Diagonal only approx axis-aligned after rotation negligible oblique case overblur slight [9].

**Gradient Preservation:** Compute ∂L/∂α_i=(c_i-C_behind)T_i dL/dC. T monotonic opaque saturation early termination bias <1e-4. Ties depth<1e-3 max color swing bounded O(α1α2||c1-c2||)<0.01 PSNR.

**Multiscale Trade:** Zip-NeRF vs Mip 360: +0.8 dB PSNR 360 dataset thanks prefiltered grid removing z-alias popping. Training 6× longer 6 supersamples. Single-scale shared grid conv [7] 95% benefit 1 sample 5 levels.

## 6 Limitations

- PE curse: Fourier inflates dim 3→60 → first layer 15K params plus aliasing unless filtered. Hash grids reduce MLP but collision artifacts; Hyb-NeRF coarse 16 MB still relies Fourier fine.

- 3DGS memory: city scale 30-50M Gaussians 7-12 GB sorting impossible real-time. Proposal sampler analogy for 3DGS LoD hierarchies H3DGS not standardized. Custom CUDA breaks Web/Mobile. Hardware rasterizer mesh-shader promises but depth still software [2].

- IPE approx error: frustum indicator uniform vs Gaussian moment underestimates corners overblurs slanted edges. Cov diagonal ignores cross-axis correlation oblique views slight over-antialias [9].

- Evaluation bias: multiscale downsampled GT box filter not sensor ISP. No Pareto FPS vs quality standard; papers cherry-pick.

- Scale supervision sparsity: Rare continuous scale training; 8× zoom unseen damping unseen color drift. Zip multisample partial mitigation.

- Differentiability leak: sorting non-differentiable w.r.t depth; co-planar transparent Gaussians unstable vs volume stable but slow.

## 7 Conclusion

Sinusoids to splats to frustums matured from "can we memorize scene in MLP?" to "can we rasterize scale-aware primitives at 300 FPS?" Positional encoding overcame spectral bias; Hyb-NeRF, QFF, Adaptive PE refine memory-efficient pose-robust. Mip-NeRF lifted rays to cones replacing point integration with Gaussian expectation IPE damping high frequencies proportional pixel footprint—progeny 360, Mip-Grid, Zip-NeRF port to contracted/unbounded/grid. 3DGS swapped implicit for explicit anisotropic Gaussians tile rasterizer sorting splat-tile pairs α-blending; DISTWAR, FlashGS, hardware rasterizers expose bottlenecks accelerating to real-time. Unified: all control bandwidth. Fourier sets bands, covariance sets surviving bands (EWA 2D or 3D frustum). Future hybrids—Gaussians carrying learned grids IPE-downweighted, hardware raster LoD, proposal samplers for unbounded—convergent renderer neither pure MLP nor splat but differentiable scale-aware prefilter on GPU. Use L≈10 bounded synthetic, enable IPE/supersample multiscale deploy, adopt 3DGS+EWA real-time budget memory densification sorting-limited scaling.
