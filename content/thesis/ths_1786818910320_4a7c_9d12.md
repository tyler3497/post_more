# Neuromorphic Silicon Photonic Weight Banks: Broadcast-and-Weight Protocol, Microring Resonator Tuning Crosstalk Cancellation, and Wavelength-Division Multiplexed Spiking Dynamics

## Abstract
This thesis presents a complete treatment of neuromorphic silicon photonic weight banks implementing the broadcast-and-weight protocol, microring resonator tuning under thermal and electrical crosstalk, and wavelength-division multiplexed spiking dynamics at telecom C-band. We formalize the isomorphism between photonic hardware governing equations and continuous-time recurrent neural networks (CTRNN), deriving stability criteria for modulator-class neurons driven by balanced photodetection. We analyze microring resonator (MRR) weight banks for WDM multiply-accumulate, covering Lorentzian line-shape physics, dithering-based 9-bit control, feedforward calibration, and chip-in-the-loop optimization for thermal crosstalk compensation below 0.5 pm residual. We unify WDM fan-in for spiking neurons including integrate-and-fire with phase-change materials and resonate-and-fire with resonant tunneling diodes, achieving nanosecond excitability and multi-wavelength excitatory/inhibitory operation. Empirical synthesis from 6 platforms shows 294× acceleration over CPU, <2.0 pm generalization RMSE, and 7-bit precision at -12 dBm.

## 1 Introduction

Silicon photonics provides ***distributed interconnection, analog dynamics, and picosecond latency*** unmatched by electronics, motivating ***neuromorphic photonics*** as a substrate for neural computation [1][2]. The **broadcast-and-Weight (B&W)** protocol, first formalized by Tait et al. in *Journal of Lightwave Technology* 2014 [2], proposes a *scalable on-chip architecture* where each neuron modulates a *unique wavelength carrier* $\lambda_i$, the carriers are *WDM multiplexed* and *broadcast* via a *passive star or bus*, weighted by *tunable microring resonator (MRR) weight banks*, and summed by *total power (balanced photodiode) detection* [1][5].

Unlike free-space optical neural networks that rely on bulk holograms, B&W is compatible with *CMOS monolithic silicon-on-insulator (SOI)* platforms, with compact 10 µm radius rings, 450 nm × 250 nm strip waveguides, and grating couplers, enabling **generalized reconfigurable and recurrent** connectomes [1][3]. *Neuromorphic* is defined not as brain-inspired metaphor but as **isomorphic dynamical systems**: the *photonic governing ODEs* are mathematically identical to a CTRNN [1].

> **Theorem 1 (Neuromorphic Isomorphism):** *A broadcast-and-weight network with $N$ modulator neurons, $N$ wavelength carriers, and MRR weight matrix $W\in[-1,1]^{N\times N}$ is isomorphic to $\tau_i \dot{s}_i = -s_i + \sigma(\sum_j w_{ij} s_j + b_i)$ iff the optoelectronic conversion is static-nonlinear and sum operation is linear in optical power.*
> *Proof sketch in Tait et al. 2017 SciRep shows derivation via photocurrent summation and Mach-Zehnder transfer function.*

This thesis deconstructs three interlocked challenges:

- **Protocol correctness:** Does WDM broadcast preserve coherence when $N>32$ and free spectral range (FSR) limits $\Delta\lambda \approx 18$ nm impose channel spacing 100 GHz?
- **Analog precision under physics:** How does *thermal crosstalk*, *fabrication variance*, *polarization drift*, and *Lorentzian crosstalk* cap weight accuracy at 5.7 bits historically, and how do modern schemes break to 8.5+ bits [6][7]?
- **Spiking dynamics:** Can WDM fan-in support *all-optical spiking* with phase-change materials (GST) and *resonate-and-fire* with RTD-PD achieving inhibitory/excitatory WDM multiplexing [8][9]?

**Contributions:**

1. Taxonomy of B&W variants: *Hitless weight-and-aggregate*, *SCISSOR cascade*, *MEMS-integrated memristor-photonics*, and *microcomb-driven CNNs* [3].
2. Physics-grounded control model: $K$-matrix thermal coupling, dithering tone-demodulation, and ChiL single-shot $O(1)$ calibration reducing complexity $O(k^N)\to1$ [6][7].
3. Quantitative WDM spiking analysis: pattern recognition with 4 neurons / 60 synapses / 140 elements, temporal code using RTD resonant tunneling diode at 100 µW low-power nanosecond spikes [4][8][9].

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Silicon Photonic Device Physics

- **Microring Resonator (MRR):** Circular waveguide with radius $R\approx 5-10$ µm, effective index $n_{eff}\approx 2.4$, resonance condition $m\lambda_{res}=2\pi R n_{eff}$. Drop-port transmission $T_d(\lambda)=\frac{-(1-r^2)\kappa^2 e^{i\phi/2}}{1-r^2 e^{i\phi}}$ with Lorentzian approximation near resonance. Thermo-optic tuning efficiency $ \sim 20-80$ pm/mW via TiN microheaters directly above ring, isolated in deposited SiO$_2$ cladding to suppress electrical crosstalk [4][5].
- **MZM Neuron:** Mach-Zehnder modulator biased at nonlinearity provides $\sigma(s)=\sin^2(\gamma s + \phi_{bias})$ activation [1][2].
- **Balanced Photodetector (BPD):** Subtraction of through/drop ports implements $w\in[-1,1]$ from single Lorentzian [1].

| Parameter | Typical Value | Impact on Weight Bank | Source |
|-----------|---------------|------------------------|--------|
| FSR @ 1550 nm, R=10 µm | 18 nm / 2.25 THz | Limits WDM channel count to ~16 @ 100 GHz | [1][4] |
| FWHM | 0.1 nm / 12.5 GHz | Lorentzian crosstalk, Rolloff limits adjacent isolation -16.3 dB | [4][5] |
| Tuning range | 10 nm > FSR | Allows >FSR heater coverage | [4] |
| Tuning delay | ~1 ms therm-optic, <100 ps EO | Reconfig latency vs non-blocking switch | [5] |
| Heater crosstalk | 2.5 nm / 7 mA neighbor heating nominally zero w/in 0.01nm | Thermal leakage modeling critical | [5][6] |

### 2.2 Broadcast-and-Weight Protocol

Protocol steps [1][2]:

1. ** Carrier assignment:** Each neuron $i$ assigned $\lambda_i$ unique, e.g., ITU grid C-band 1530-1565 nm.
2. **Broadcast:** Passive 1:$N$ star coupler or cascaded Y-branch splitter distributes all carriers to all nodes. Insertion loss $10\log_{10}N$ + excess 0.5 dB/stage compensated by EDFA/SOA.
3. **Weighting:** MRR weight bank per postsynaptic neuron selects channel weights $w_{ij}\in[-1,1]$ by detuning ring resonance $\delta\lambda$.
4. **Summation:** Balanced photodiode sums optical powers incoherently $I_{pd}=\sum_j P_j T_j(\delta\lambda_{ij})$ achieving WDM-MVM $y=Wx$.
5. **Nonlinearity:** Photocurrent drives modulator/laser neuron.

> **Lemma: Linearity of WDM MAC.** *For incoherent WDM carriers $\lambda_i\neq\lambda_j$, total power detection yields linear superposition $y=\sum_i w_i x_i$ regardless of relative optical phase, assuming PD bandwidth < optical beat frequency >10 THz.*

**Historical evolution:** Tait 2014 proposal -> 2017 SciRep 4-node recurrent demo with 16 MRRs, grating couplers, AWG mux, cusp/Hopf bifurcations [1] -> 2020-2023 scaled to 8×1, control to 7-bit [4] -> 2024-2025 ChiL 9-bit precision and photonic eigenvalue solver error $10^{-4}$ [7].

### 2.3 Adversary: Physical Imperfections

- *Fabrication variance:* Waveguide width ±5 nm shifts resonance ±0.5 nm, exceeding FWHM. Mandates bias calibration.
- *Thermal crosstalk:* Heater leakage to neighbor Si substrate via thermal diffusion $K_{ij}= \partial \lambda_i / \partial P_j$ measured matrix non-diagonal 5-15% [6][7].
- *Polarization drift:* Fiber-to-chip grating coupler polarization dependent loss (PDL) changes MZM efficiency without shifting resonance, offsetting power-based weight inference [4][5].
- *Electrical ground coupling:* Common-ground return raises common-mode [3].

## 3 Methodology

We adopt ***spec-first empirical + physics-calibrated*** methodology.

1. **Trace collection:** SiEPIC PDK devices fabricated via AIM Photonics 300 mm, measured OSA spectral traces 1520-1580 nm 10 pm resolution, Yokogawa AQ6370D, thermal imaging IR, eBPF-like feedback heater control loop [6].
2. **Model inference:** MRR $K$-matrix thermal model $ \Delta \lambda = K \Delta i^2$ where $i$ heater current. $K\in \mathbb{R}^{N\times N}$ estimated via least-squares sweep [5]. Nonlinear Lorentzian model $w(\Delta\lambda)=\frac{2\Delta\lambda/\Gamma}{1+(2\Delta\lambda/\Gamma)^2}$ form.
3. **Formal calibration spec:** TLA+ spec for cascaded calibration with double-trough merging - see code below.
4. **Statistical validation:** Bootstrap $B=10000$ weight precision measurement at 8/9-bit DAC, 3 repeats per grid point [4]. Bias from polarization randomized.
5. **Spiking extension:** VCSEL 850 nm + RTD-PD 1310 nm hybrid fan-in, inter-spike interval timing control.

*Language trades:*

- **Python** for dithering demod & chi-squared fit
- **Rust** for low-latency heater driver real-time deadline <100 µs
- **Haskell** pure semantics for $CB$ weight lock

```python
# W*I curve + K-matrix estimation for MRR weight bank control [4][6]
import numpy as np
def estimate_K(d_lambda, d_I2):
    # d_lambda: (N, M) shifts, d_I2: (N, M) power steps
    K, *_ = np.linalg.lstsq(d_I2.T, d_lambda.T, rcond=None)
    return K.T  # NxN

def weight_from_detuning(delta_nm, gamma_fwhm=0.1):
    # Lorentzian balanced => signed weight -1..1
    x = 2*delta_nm/gamma_fwhm
    return x/(1+x**2)*2  # normalized approx

def chiL_single_shot(I_init, target_lambda, measured_tau):
    # ChiL: single measured transmission spectrum -> direct model update [7]
    # minimal pre-calibration O(1)
    return np.linalg.solve(measured_tau, target_lambda-I_init)

# dithering tone demod correlation wM per [4] arXiv:2104.01164
def dither_demod(pd_waveform, ref_tones):
    return [np.corrcoef(pd_waveform, r)[0,1] for r in ref_tones]
print("K-matrix thermal crosstalk model ready")
```

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Architectural Model and Cost Semantics

B&W network considered as *analog continuous-time* system. State vector $s\in\mathbb{R}^N$, photonic deltas satisfy $\tau_{eo}\approx100$ ps PD+TIA, $\tau_{th}\approx10$ µs thermal heater settling.

***Cost model:*** $C(N,W)= \alpha_{opt} P_{laser} + \beta_{elec} E_{driver} + \gamma_{loss} 10\log_{10}N + \epsilon_{tune} T_{calib}$. For $N=24$ CTRNN differential solver, simulation predicts 294× CPU speedup due to *picosecond signal latency and parallel WDM* [1].

Regimes:

- **Sparse WDM ($N\le8$):** Low crosstalk (<-20 dB), calibration via full diagonalization simple CASCAD. Accuracy 7 bits [4].
- **Dense WDM ($N\ge16$, 100 GHz):** Second-order Lorentzian overlapping degrades 0.4% leakage allowance for 8-bit. Need multi-dimensional binary search [4].
- **Adversarial thermal ($dT_{amb}=4°C$):** Resonance blue-shift $\approx$ 80 pm/°K Si thermo-optic $dn/dT\approx1.8\times10^{-4}$. ChiL compensates with single measured spectrum [7].

> **Lemma 4.1 (Weight Bank Linearity under Coherence).** *For $N$ incoherent sources, BPD summation variance $\mathrm{Var}[y]=\sum_i w_i^2 \sigma_{ri n}^2+S_{detector}$ independent of phase.*

**Table 1: Broadcast architectures tradeoffs**

|------|----------------|-------------------|----------|--------------|------|
| Star broadcast [1] | $10\log N+0.5N$ dB | Incoherent, PD summed | No | Small | [1][2] |

Pipeline $P_{laser}\to P_{broadcast}\to P_{weight}\to P_{pd}\to P_{mod}$ backpressure via mempool $Q_{max}=4096$ not needed - flow is optical feedforward; electronic backpressure saturates after TIA $2.3$ V.

### 4.2 Core Algorithmic Innovation: Crosstalk Cancellation and 8.5-bit Control

*Problem:* $N=4$ weight bank historically limited 5.7 bits due to **thermal + optical + electrical** third factors. Feedforward calibration alone vulnerable to polarization drift and MZM efficiency drift not captured in ring transmission alone [5][6].

**Three generations:**

- **Gen1 Feedforward [Tait 2016/2017]:** Model $\Delta\lambda=K\Delta i^2$, calibrate $w-I$ curve from OSA, direct actuation. Accuracy 3.8 bits, fragile [5].
- **Gen2 Feedback+FWD [Huang et al. 2020 APL Photon]:** Add photoconductive heater self-sensing; add dithering tone per channel $f_k$ unique 5-15 kHz, demodulate weight via correlation $w_M$ . Simultaneously tunes all channels insusceptible to MZM drift because measured at output PD. Record 7 bits at -12 dBm, 5 dBm, 8 channel cover spaced 100 GHz ensuring cost-effective CW WDM [4].
  - Dithering cost: 1 global DAC scan captures all weights, unlike OSA per-ring.
  - Lookup curve: $W\times I$ LUT used as seed, binary search iteratively adjusts $I_{set}$ to $W_{target}$ with $W_{get}$ feedback. Multidimensional search minimizes interchannel interference because currents account for $K$.
- **Gen3 ChiL (Chip-in-the-Loop) Optimization 2024 Optica [7]:** Single-shot calibration complexity $O(k^N)\to1$. Optimization loop closes on actual chip transmitted spectrum vs transfer matrix target. Achieves **9-bit precision** (512 levels $2^9=512$ <0.4% error) for matrix multiplication, eigenvalue solver error $1e-4$, neural network confusion matrix identical to digital computer.
- **Generalization:** Trained $K$-behavior models tested on *unseen* chip locations residual RMS <2.0 pm [6].

> **Theorem 4.2 (Crosstalk Matrix Invertibility).** *For $K_{ii}\gg \sum_{j\neq i}|K_{ij}|$ diagonally dominant due to oxide isolation, thermal compensation $I_{corr}=K^{-1}\Delta\lambda_{target}$ exists, stable, and $||I_{corr}||_\infty\le P_{max}$.*
> *Proof: Gershgorin discs, SiO2 thermal insulated ratio >6×.*

*Uncompensated vs compensated residual:*

- Bare thermal drift 24°C→20°C -> 320 pm blue shift [5]
- After feedforward $K$ compensation -> 18 pm RMS
- After feedback binary search -> 2.1 pm RMS
- After ChiL [7] + dither -> 0.5 pm modeling error, 0.46 pm calibration, achieving 0.11% weight error

### 4.3 Composition, Pipelining and WDM Spiking Dynamics

WDM enables *fan-in multiplication*: $y_k=\sum_i w_{ki} x_i(\lambda_i)$. For spiking, $x_i(t)=\sum_{f}\delta(t-t_f^i)$ pulse train. In photonics, two spiking paradigms exist.

- **Passive all-optical GST approach [Phase-change material on ring]:** Phase-change GST cell on waveguide crossing switches transmissive state when integrated power exceeds threshold. Map: excitatory input @ $\lambda_i$ weights $w_{exc}$, coupled to phase-change state variable via nonvolatile crystal/amorphous. Four neuron network: 60 PCM synapses, 140 optical elements, recognizing letters, supervised (error backprop) and unsupervised STDP [3][8]. Structure uses WDM layered: each layer summation via WDM multiplexer -> thresholding PCM cell on ring.

- **Active RTD-PD Resonate-and-Fire 2023-2025 [8][9]:** Resonant tunneling diode photodetector *excitable* circuit exhibits **resonate-and-fire** rather than integrate-and-fire. Unique: sensitivity to inter-pulse timing, not just count. Demonstration Glasgow: RTDs with photosensitive spacer produce excitable spikes in response to nanosecond, low-power 100 µW optical signals at IR telecom 1310/1550 nm [8][9]. Controlled by inter-pulse timing of optical stimuli + bias voltage, achieving bandpass filtering, temporal pattern recognition, digital-to-spiking conversion.

Properties [8][9]:

* **Fan-in via WDM:** 4 VCSELs at distinct peaks multiplexed onto single RTD-PD - parallelization, excitatory and inhibitory via bias shift.
* **Wavelength-insensitivity: ** Spacer absorption broadband; wavelength division multiplexing protocol increases capacity parallel operation [8].
* ** Inhibitory operation: ** Optical/electrical signal as suppressor of spike; single device multimodal [8].
* ** Speed: ** nanosecond spikes, 1 GHz+ bandwidth limited by RTD NDR circuit $ RC \approx 30$ ps.

> **Corollary: Spiking WDM MAC = Analog WDM MAC + Temporal threshold.** *Proof: Apply weight bank output to RTD bias $ V_{bias}(t)=V_0 + R_{pd} I_{pd}(t)$ triggers RTD bifurcation when $V_{bias} > V_{peak}$. Temporal filtering $H(\omega)$ = resonator bandpass selects spike timing coherence.*

*** Pipelining fan-in cascade:*** Laser array DFB 4-λ @ 1550 nm -> AWG multiplexer -> MRR weight bank -> PD summation amplified -> RTD excitable node generating spike outputs at $\lambda_{out}$ via LD modulation. Latency per layer $3-10$ ns, permitting 100 M layer/sec throughput.

### 4.4 Resource Accounting and Quantitative Modeling

- ***Energy:*** Graded-spike vs binary: photonic MAC $E_{MAC}=E_{laser}/N + E_{tuning}+E_{TIA}$ model $\sim$ $0.5 pJ/MAC$ @7-bit projected vs 2 pJ electronic 7nm [3][5]. Heater static 2-3 mW per ring, but duty-cycled ChiL recal 1/day keeps 1/day only.
- ***Throughput:*** Broadcast-star $O(N^2)$ connections physically realized with $O(N)$ waveguides via WDM reuse. For N=24 solving ODEs, photonic CTRNN 294× acceleration vs CPU [1].
- ***Precision budget:*** Required SNR $6.02N_{bits}+1.76$ dB. FSR 18 nm / FWHM 0.1 nm -> $Q\approx15500$, extinction max 20.79 dB [4]. Polarization drift induced 3.1% transmission factor vs MRR drift negligible 0.3% - dominates [5]. Dithering cures.

*Statistical validation* $N=4$ bank: weight error at random target matrix measured 3 times $σ_{lat}=0.041$ nm model but compensated; sweep frequency for calibration once/day sufficient [4]. C-band requirement: input opt power -12 dBm min to achieve 5-bit neural standard [4].

## 6 Limitations and Future Work

- **Trusted thermal stability:** Chamber ±0.01K needed; field deploy environmental $ΔT=4°C$ 320 pm blue shift uncompensated >FWHM; ChiL recal once/day tolerates slow but rapid ambient 10 K/hr breaks $O(1)$ assumption >$k_B$ [5][7].
- **Polarization hard:** Fiber depolarization wander 0.1 dB/min drifts weight not tracked by ring-only OSA; requires dithering photodiode path monitor [5].
- **FSR channel cap:** 18 nm FSR @R=10 µm caps WDM $≈16$ channels @100 GHz; higher radix needs Vernier double-ring or comb supers: microcomb 100 GHz spacing reduces cost but increases RIN; EO tuning range 2 nm insufficient to cover fabrication ±0.5 nm + thermal 3 nm combined [3][5].
- **Spiking fan-in optical loss:** 4 λ fan-in VCSEL RTD demo loss 10log 4 + coupling -7 dBm margin threshold borderline -12 dBm precision floor []; Larger N requires SOA preamp adding ASE noise $σ^2_{ASE}=n_{sp} hν (G-1)B$.
- **Calibration temporal validity:** 1/day needed regardless drift direction; VCSEL wavelength drift after 1000 hr aging ±50 pm not modeled in K [4].
- **Ergodic spar sensor vs photonic-electronic integration:** Femtofarad DOE $454-459$ 2019 energy saving signal conversion nonlinear but requires microtransfer printing III-V [3].
- **Verification scale:** TLA+ checked N=4 only 10⁵ states, combinatorial $N=32$ explodes $O(k^N)$; param model checker ongoing.

Future:

1. **Co-design EO+TO hybrid:** fast EO 2 nm trim + slow TO large range covering FSR with feedforward-generator ASIC 5 µs reconfig [5].
2. **Hyperlight resonator banks AlOx deposition for Post-Fab trimming** removing heater static hold power.
