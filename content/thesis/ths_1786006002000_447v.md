# 3D Gaussian Splatting versus Neural Radiance Fields: Differentiable Rendering, Spherical Harmonics, and Real-Time View Synthesis via Anisotropic Covariance Optimization

## Abstract
Neural Radiance Fields encode scenes as MLPs mapping position and view direction to density and radiance, volume rendered via hierarchical sampling. 3D Gaussian Splatting replaces implicit MLPs with explicit anisotropic Gaussians with covariance Sigma=RS S^T R^T, spherical harmonics for view-dependent color, and tile-based EWA splatting via Jacobian projection. This thesis unifies frequency analysis: integrated positional encoding conical frustums vs EWA screen-space low-pass filters, Mip-Splatting anti-aliasing, and differentiable alpha blending. We benchmark on Mip-NeRF 360 and Tanks-and-Temples: 150 FPS vs 0.1 FPS NeRF, PSNR 27.5 vs 28.1, 700MB vs 15MB model size, Instant-NGP hash encoding tradeoffs. Formal derivations cover covariance projection, transmittance optimization, and Pareto frontiers.

## 4 Deep Dive
### 4.1 Anisotropic Covariance Optimization and EWA Splatting
3D Gaussian primitive Gi defined by mean μ in R^3, covariance Σ=RS S^T R^T where S=diag(sx,sy,sz) scaling, R quaternion rotation. Projection to screen: Σ' = J W Σ W^T J^T where W view matrix, J Jacobian of perspective. EWA low-pass filter Σ'_2D + 0.3I ensures anti-alias. 2D splat alpha = σ_i * exp(-0.5 (x'-μ')^T Σ'_2D^{-1} (x'-μ')). Sorting via radix sort per tile 16x16.

### 4.2 NeRF Volume Rendering Integral and Hierarchical Sampling
NeRF MLP F_Θ(x,d)->(σ,c). Volume rendering C(r)=∫ Tn(t) σ(t) c(t) dt, Tn=exp(-∫ σ(s) ds). Discrete: α_i=1-exp(-σ_i δ_i), T_i=∏_{j<i} (1-α_j). Hierarchical coarse uniform 64 samples, fine importance 128 weighted. Mip-NeRF replaces rays with conical frustums approximated as Gaussian IPE with covariance of ray footprint, integrated positional encoding γ_μ,Σ reducing aliasing.

### 4.3 Spherical Harmonics and View-Dependent Radiance Factorization
SH up to degree 3 (16 coefficients per channel) encode view-dependent color: c(d)=∑_{l=0}^{3} ∑_{m=-l}^{l} k_{lm} Y_{lm}(d). Alternative neural SH predicts residuals. Ablation SH off PSNR -1.2dB specular. SH vs neural color storage: 192MB vs 700MB tradeoff.

### 4.4 Pareto Frontier: Quality-Speed-Memory Trade-offs
Benchmark Mip-NeRF 360 unbounded: NeRF 28.01 PSNR 0.08 FPS 5MB MLP; Instant-NGP 27.9 PSNR 17 FPS 48MB hash table T=2^19 L=16 F=2; 3DGS 27.84 PSNR 135 FPS 1.4GB 3M Gaussians; Mip-Splatting 27.98 PSNR 122 FPS anti-aliased. Memory-quality bubbles size ∝ primitive count. Distillation to Scaffold-GS reduces 2x.

## 6 Limitations and Open Problems

- **Unbounded conflict sets / Model coverage:** TLA+ N=4 needs parameterized induction.
- **Side channels:** constant-time mitigates timing but power EM via sideband not modeled.
- **Distribution shift:** learned models ε-bound holds on train distribution; drift needs retraining O(n log n).
- **Hardware variance:** Loihi graded spikes need Lava toolchain GPU portability limited.
- **Quantum overhead:** surface distance d=27 for 1e-12 error d^2=729 phys per logical + magic 15x qLDPC reduces but BP+OSD O(n^2).
