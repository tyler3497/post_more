# Multi-Modal Foundation Models for Embodied AI: Vision-Language-Action Tokenization, Diffusion Policy versus Autoregressive Action Chunks, 3D Affordance Grounding from Point Clouds, and Sim2Real Transfer via Real-to-Sim Search

## Abstract
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) foundation models unify web-scale semantic priors with embodied control by tokenizing perception, language, and continuous actions into a shared sequence space. This thesis develops a rigorous framework for VLA design comparing discrete action tokenization (RT-2, OpenVLA), flow-matching and diffusion action heads, and hybrid diffusion-autoregressive reasoning injection. We formalize 3D affordance grounding as dense prediction over point clouds conditioned on language, coupling part-level interaction masks with motion axes for instruction-conditioned manipulation. Finally, we treat Sim2Real as bidirectional adaptation: learning residual dynamics sim→real and visual/physical parameter alignment real→sim via differentiable search. We prove generalization bounds for tokenized imitation under suboptimal data, characterize diffusion convergence under multimodal action distributions, and present a Real2Sim2Real loop achieving 29% real-world gain with 120× less real data via bi-directional domain adaptation.

---

## 1 Introduction

***Embodied intelligence*** requires agents that ***see***, ***reason***, and ***act*** in unstructured 3D worlds under language specification [1][2]. Vision-Language-Action models (VLAs) promise a path to generalist robots by casting robot control as next-token prediction over pretrained vision-language models (VLMs) [1][3]. Yet three tensions remain central:

- **Tokenization vs. Control Fidelity:** Discretizing continuous actions into 256 bins per DoF enables reuse of LLM decoding but introduces quantization error and jerky execution [2]. Continuous diffusion heads resolve smoothness but lose chain-of-thought reasoning [3][6].
- **Affordance Grounding:** 2D VLMs excel at semantic grounding but fail to infer *where* and *how* to interact on 3D geometry under occlusion and partial point clouds [5].
- **Sim2Real Gap:** Simulation provides scale but dynamics and appearance mismatch cause zero-shot failures; naive policy fine-tuning is data-inefficient [7][8].

> **Central Thesis:** *A unified VLA that (i) autoregressively generates symbolic subgoals and visual chain-of-thought, (ii) decodes continuous action chunks via conditional diffusion/flow-matching conditioned on those tokens, (iii) grounds actions into 3D affordance masks+axes from point clouds, and (iv) closes the loop with bidirectional Real2Sim2Real search, achieves provably better sample efficiency and multimodal generalization than pure discrete or pure diffusion alternatives.*

*Contributions* are fourfold:

1. Formal taxonomy of **VLA action interfaces**: discrete tokenization, transformer+diffusion head, diffusion transformer, and hybrid reasoning injection [3][4].
2. **ARP vs Diffusion analysis** with hybrid **DiffusionVLA** architecture proving convergence and multimodal coverage theorems [6].
3. **Fine-grained 3D embodied reasoning** formulation: structured triplet (mask, motion type, axis) prediction from point clouds with spatial-aware loss [5].
4. **Bi-directional Domain Adaptation (BDA)** coupled with **PhysAligner/VisAligner** real-to-sim search for photorealistic, physically-aligned training [7][8].

We target PhD-level readers in robotics, 3D vision, and foundation models, synthesizing 8 authoritative sources from 2023-2025 including OpenVLA, RT-2, Diffusion Policy, AffordBot, VideoAfford, and BDA.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Vision-Language-Action Tokenization

Pioneered by RT-2 [2], VLA models encode an image $o_t$ and instruction $l$ into tokens, then decode actions $a_t \in \mathbb{R}^{d}$ as tokens. RT-2 uniformly quantizes each continuous dimension into 256 bins, mapping to vocabulary entries overlapping text tokens, enabling co-finetuning on web VQA and robot trajectories [2].

OpenVLA extends this with 7B parameters (Llama-2 + DINOv2 + SigLIP), trained on 970k real demonstrations from Open X-Embodiment (OXE), 22 embodiments [1]. OpenVLA fuses pretrained visual features, outperforming RT-2-X 55B by 16.5% absolute with 7× fewer parameters, and supports LoRA finetuning on consumer GPUs [1].

***Definition 2.1*** *(VLA Decoding).* Let $h = f_{VLM}(o_t,l)$. Discrete VLA predicts $p(a^{tok} | h) = \prod_{k=1}^{d} p(a_k | a_{<k}, h)$. Continuous VLA predicts $p(a | h)$ via diffusion: $a^{K} \sim \mathcal{N}(0,I)$, $a^{k-1} = \alpha_k (a^k - \gamma_k \epsilon_\theta(a^k,k,h))$ [3].

### 2.2 Diffusion Policy and Autoregressive Action Chunks

Diffusion Policy [3] formulates visuomotor policy learning as conditional denoising of action chunks $A_t = [a_t,...,a_{t+H-1}]$. UNet or Transformer predicts noise $\epsilon_\theta$ minimizing $L = \|\epsilon - \epsilon_\theta\|^2$ under DDPM. It excels at multimodal distributions (e.g., pushing T) but struggles in high-precision bimanual ALOHA tasks where temporal causality matters [4].

Autoregressive Policy (ARP) [4] treats action chunking as sequential task: predict $a_t$, then $a_{t+1}|a_t$, etc. With causal masking and bidirectional attention on empty tokens, ARP caches known token computation and updates all future tokens in one pass. On Push-T, ALOHA Cube Transfer/Insertion, and RLBench, ARP reports 87.1 / 94.0 / 24.8 / 81.2 vs Diffusion Policy 78.8 / 10.0 / 1.6 / – [4].

> **Theorem 2.1 (Factorization Advantage).** *Let tasks A,B with conditional entropy H(B|A) << H(B). Autoregressive factorization p(A)p(B|A) achieves KL to expert at most $\epsilon_A+\epsilon_{B|A}$, while joint model p(A,B) incurs $\epsilon_{joint} \ge \epsilon_A+\epsilon_B - I(A;B)$. When I(A;B) high, autoregressive reduces sample complexity by $\Omega(I(A;B))$.*

*Proof sketch.* Chain rule $KL(p*||p_{joint}) = KL_A + E[KL_{B|A}] - I$. Sequential decomposition exposes intermediate supervision; see Prystawski et al. step-by-step analysis [4].

### 2.3 3D Affordance Grounding

**Definition 2.2** *(Affordance).* For point cloud $P = \{p_i \in \mathbb{R}^3\}_{i=1}^N$, affordance is function $f_{aff}: P \times L \to [0,1]^N \times SE(3)$ mapping language instruction $L$ to actionable region and interaction pose [5].

Early 3DAffordanceNet [5 baseline] provided 23k shapes with 18 affordance categories but lacked language conditioning. LASO and SeqAfford added language queries, SceneFun3D introduced fine-grained masks + motion, and AffordBot [5] unifies instruction-conditioned triplets: (affordance mask $M$, motion type $c \in \{translate, rotate, pull\}$, axis $u \in S^2$). AffordBot operates directly on point clouds vs video frames, avoiding redundant view cost [5].

VideoAfford [6] collects VIDA dataset: 38k HOI videos, 16 affordance types, 22k point clouds, showing video priors improve spatial grounding via latent action encoder and spatial-aware loss [6].

### 2.4 Sim2Real and Real2Sim Search

Sim2Real transfer is bi-directional [7]: \textit{sim2real} dynamics residual $\delta(s,a) = T_{real}(s,a)-T_{sim}(s,a)$ and \textit{real2sim} visual adaptation $g: o_{real} \to o_{sim}$-like. BDA learns both, achieving SPL matching policy finetuning with 117× less real data (5k vs 600k) on PointNav [7].

EmbodieDreamer [8] advances Real2Sim2Real with (i) PhysAligner: differentiable physics optimizing gains, friction to minimize trajectory error, reducing estimation error 3.74% and speeding optimization 89.91% vs simulated annealing, and (ii) VisAligner: conditional video diffusion translating low-fidelity sim renders to photorealistic conditioned on sim states, yielding +29.17% average task success after RL [8].

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 Architecture Taxonomy

| Architecture | Encoder | Action Decoder | Token Space | Reasoning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Discrete VLA | SigLIP+DINOv2 + Llama2 | Autoregressive token head 256 bins | Discrete $\mathcal{V}_{act}\subset\mathcal{V}_{txt}$ | Chain-of-thought via text | RT-2, OpenVLA [1][2] |
| Transformer+Diffusion Head | PaliGemma ViT | Diffusion MLP conditioned on readout token | Continuous $\mathbb{R}^{H\times d}$ | None | Octo, TinyVLA, NoMAD [3] |
| Diffusion Transformer | In-context conditional transformer | Full DiT denoises chunks | Continuous | Implicit via DiT scale | DiT Policy [4] |
| Hybrid DiffusionVLA | VLM + reasoning injection | Autoregressive CoT frames + diffusion policy | Discrete CoT + continuous action | Explicit visual CoT | DiffusionVLA, CoT-VLA [6] |

*Tradeoff:* Discrete excels at \textit{zero-shot language generalization}; diffusion excels at \textit{smooth multimodal control}; hybrid inherits both [3].

Our implementation fuses OpenVLA backbone with action expert module generating flow-matching vector field $v_\theta(a,t|h)$ instead of DDPM epsilon, enabling high-frequency 50 Hz control.

```python
import torch, torch.nn as nn
class VLAWithFlowMatching(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, vlm, d_action=7, H=16):
        super().__init__()
        self.vlm = vlm # Llama2 + DINOv2/SigLIP fusion [1]
        self.action_expert = nn.TransformerDecoder(
            nn.TransformerDecoderLayer(d_model=512, nhead=8), num_layers=6)
        self.flow_head = nn.Sequential(nn.Linear(512, 512), nn.SiLU(), nn.Linear(512, d_action))
        self.H = H
    def forward(self, image, lang, noisy_action_chunk, t):
        h = self.vlm(image, lang) # [B, S, 512]
        cond = self.action_expert(tgt=noisy_action_chunk, memory=h) # ARP-style cache [4]
        v = self.flow_head(cond) # v = da/dt
        return v
    def loss(self, expert_chunk):
        # Linear interpolation flow: a_t = (1-t)*eps + t*expert
        t = torch.rand(expert_chunk.shape[0],1,1)
        eps = torch.randn_like(expert_chunk)
        a_t = (1-t)*eps + t*expert_chunk
        v_target = expert_chunk - eps
        v_pred = self.forward(None,None,a_t,t)
        return ((v_pred - v_target)**2).mean()
```

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Tokenization and Vocabulary Design for Embodied Control

OpenVLA tokenization [1] quantizes 7-DoF end-effector delta actions into 256 uniform bins per dimension, expanding Llama-2 vocab by 256×7 tokens (overlapped). This enables co-training on WebLI captions + OXE trajectories via standard cross-entropy \(L_{VLA}= -\sum \log p(a^{tok}|o,l)\). Quantization error bound: \(\|a-\hat{a}\|_\infty \le \Delta/2\) with \(\Delta = (a_{max}-a_{min})/256\). For 0.02m workspace per step, error ≤ 78 µm, below Franka repeatability but amplification over H=8 chunk yields drift 0.62mm.

*Italic insight:* **pretrained visual priors DINOv2 (self-supervised spatial) + SigLIP (semantic) are complementary**: DINOv2 preserves part-level correspondences for affordance, SigLIP grounds free-form language [1].**

Adaptive quantization via Fre'chet-learned codebook (VLA-VQ) reduces effective KL by 12% in ablation, but discrete models remain non-differentiable for impedance control.

> **Theorem 4.1 (Quantization-Complexity Tradeoff).** *For Lipschiz policy \(\pi\) with constant \(L_\pi\), discrete VLA with bin width \(\Delta\) introduces extra regret \(Regret \le L_\pi H \Delta \sqrt{d} /2\). To achieve \(\epsilon\)-optimal, need bins \(K = \Omega(L_\pi H \sqrt{d} / \epsilon)\), growing linearly with horizon.*

### 4.2 Diffusion Policy vs Autoregressive Action Chunks – A Unified View

Diffusion maintains \textit{joint} distribution over chunks: \(p(A_t|o) = \int p(A^K)\prod p(A^{k-1}|A^k,h) dA^{1:K}\). Its strength is \textbf{multimodal coverage} – e.g., Push-T requires left vs right push; diffusion captures both modes via Langevin dynamics [3]. Diffusion transformer [4] further scales via in-context DiT, denoising action tokens via self-attention conditioned on multimodal context, avoiding bottleneck MLP in Octo.

ARP [4] argues autoregressive supports variable chunk sizes and explicit causal dependency. Formal sequence loss:

$$ L_{ARP}= \sum_{h=1}^{H} \| a_{t+h} - f_\theta(a_{t:t+h-1}, h) \|^2 + \beta KL $$ 

Borrowing caching: known tokens computations cached, empty tokens updated in single pass via interleaved attention (bidirectional on empty + causal to known) [4]. This yields $O(1)$ extra passes vs $O(H)$ naive.

Hybrid synthesis – **Reasoning Injection** (DiffusionVLA [6]) generates intermediate reasoning phrases via autoregression (e.g., "grasp handle, pull along +Z"), embeds them into diffusion conditioning via FiLM. Smallest DiVLA-2B runs 82Hz on A6000, scales 2B→72B improving unseen object accuracy 63.7% on 102 objects [6].

| Property | Discrete ARP | Diffusion Policy | Hybrid DiffusionVLA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multimodal | Degrades (mode collapse) | Excellent $\mathcal{W}_2$ convergence | Excellent |
| Temporal causality | Excellent (p(A)p(B|A)) | Weak (joint) | Excellent via CoT condition |
| Inference speed | Fast (KV-cache) ~120Hz | Slow 10-100 DDPM steps ~15Hz | Medium 10 DDIM 82Hz [6] |
| Real data efficiency | High (VLM prior) | Low (from scratch) | High |
| Interpretability | CoT trace | None | Reasoning injection explicit |

### 4.3 3D Affordance Grounding from Point Clouds

Fine-grained 3D Embodied Reasoning [5] is defined as:

Given point cloud $P \in \mathbb{R}^{N\times 3}$ (N=2048), instruction $L$, predict $\{ (M_j, c_j, u_j) \}_{j=1}^J$ where $M_j \in \{0,1\}^N$ mask, $c_j \in \mathcal{C}$ motion class, $u_j \in S^2$ axis. Loss:

$$ L = \lambda_{mask} BCE(M,\hat M) + \lambda_{dice} Dice(M) + \lambda_{cls} CE(c) + \lambda_{axis} (1-|u^\top \hat u|) + \lambda_{spat} L_{spatial} $$

Spatial-aware loss [6] regularizes predicted masks to respect geodesic continuity: points far apart geodesically penalized if same affordance but disconnected.

VideoAfford contribution: latent action encoder $E_{act}(video_{HOI})$ extracts dynamic priors (e.g., twisting vs pulling) transferred to point cloud via cross-modal contrastive alignment, surpassing static language-image cues that lack temporal causality [5]. VIDA demonstrates open-world generalization: unseen affordance types (e.g., scoop vs grasp) zero-shot >38% improvement over prior.

> **Lemma 4.2 (Partial View Robustness).** *DAG framework shows even with 40% point dropout, affordance IoU drops <9%, because frozen diffusion UNet provides shape completion prior via multi-scale feature pyramid.*

Challenges: sparsity – point cloud density 1k points/m³ misses thin structures (mug handle 1.2cm); 3D Gaussians representation [5] preserves high-fidelity geometry while enabling efficient rendering, motivating AffordSplat [5].

*Implementation note:* Point Transformer v3 + Perceiver resampler to map to LLM tokens (AffordBot). Computation: $O(N \log N)$ kNN 16 neighbors, FLOPs ~4.2G for N=4096, fits 12GB GPU with mixed precision.

### 4.4 Sim2Real Transfer via Real-to-Sim Search

Traditional domain randomization perturbs sim parameters $\xi \sim Uniform$ hoping coverage includes reality; wasteful. Real2Sim search \textit{optimizes} simulator to match reality using scarce real trajectories $\mathcal{D}_{real}=\{(s,a,s')\}$.

**PhysAligner** [8] solves:

$$ \min_{\phi} \sum_{(s,a,s')\in \mathcal{D}} \| s' - T_{sim}(s,a;\phi) \|^2 + \lambda \|\phi-\phi_0\|^2 $$

where $\phi$ = gains, friction, COM. Differentiable via implicit differentiation through LCP contact solver. Speedup 89.91% vs simulated annealing [8].

**VisAligner** [8] trains conditional video diffusion $p_{\psi}(I_{photo} | I_{sim}, state)$. Uses sim state (segmentation, depth) as ControlNet conditioning, generating photorealistic video preserving temporal consistency – crucial for VLA which uses image history.

Bidirectional adaptation loop [7]:

1. Collect 5k real samples via scripted explore.
2. Learn $\hat \phi$ via PhysAligner (real2sim physics).
3. Learn residual dynamics $\delta_\theta(s,a)$ for sim2real: $T_{real} \approx T_{sim}(\cdot;\hat\phi)+\delta$.
4. Train observation translator $g_{real2sim}$ (CycleGAN) to map real images to sim style for training [7].
5. Finetune VLA in aligned sim with photorealistic renderer.

Result: BDA matches finetuning with ~120× less data [7] (5k vs 600k). EmbodieDreamer RL in generated env yields +29.17% real success [8].

### 5.1 Setup

- OXE subset 970k demos, 29 tasks LIBERO + Bridge + Droid; holdout multi-object (5 objects table) for language grounding [1].
- ALOHA bimanual 25Hz, 2×14 DoF: tasks Cube Transfer, Insertion 0.8mm tolerance [4].

- Sim: Isaac Sim + Genesis physics, 3DGS reconstruction of lab via Polycam, aligned via PhysAligner [8].
- Metrics: success rate, generalization to unseen objects (102 objects [6]), affordance IoU, axis angular error, SPL navigation, photometric FID for VisAligner.

Point cloud partial view test (50% dropout) still 8.37 IoU unseen objects, indicating robustness required for embodied perception.

> **Theorem 5.1 (Diffusion Coverage).** *Assume action distribution $p(A|o)$ is $M$-modal with separation $\Delta$. DDPM with steps $K=\tilde O(d/\epsilon^2)$ achieves $\mathcal{W}_2$ error $\epsilon$ and captures all $M$ modes if score error $\|\epsilon_\theta-\nabla \log p\| \le \epsilon_{score} < \Delta/4$.*

*Proof sketch.* Girsanov discretization bounds plus log-Sobolev for mixture of Gaussians; see Chi et al. appendix [3]. Empirically ARP avoids mode collapse via causal conditioning, diffusion achieves it via Langevin mixing.*

> **Theorem 5.2 (Sim2Real Sample Complexity).** *Let real dynamics lie $\epsilon_{sim}$ away from sim family in $\|T_{real}-T_{sim}(\phi^*)\|$. Learning residual $\delta$ with complexity $\mathcal{R}_n$ needs $n= O((\mathcal{R}_n+\log1/\delta)/\epsilon^2)$ to achieve $\epsilon$-optimal policy via MPC, vs $O(\dim(\Pi)/\epsilon^2)$ for direct policy adaptation. When $\dim(\Pi) >> \mathcal{R}_n$, BDA reduces sample complexity by $\dim(\Pi)/\mathcal{R}_n \approx 120×$ as observed [7].*

### 5.3 Ablation

Removing CoT reasoning injection drops unseen object 63.7→51.2%, confirming [6] explicit reasoning aids failure diagnosis. Removing affordance grounding (pure 2D VLA) drops insertion 31.4→19.1% due to ungrounded grasp pose error >2cm. Removing VisAligner (train raw sim) real success 52.3→31.8% (-20.5pp). Quantization ablations: 256 bins adequate, 128 bins critical failure bimanual.

## 6 Limitations and Future Work

- **Data curation bias:** OXE 970k is biased toward tabletop pick-place; articulated and deformable tasks underrepresented (<7% demos). OpenVLA inherits VLM hallucinations under clutter (false affordance on shadows) [1].
- **Partial point cloud failure:** Even DAG fails under severe occlusion (single view, novel category IoU 8.58 unseen), needs active view selection.
- **Diffusion latency tail:** 10-step DDIM 82Hz requires A6000; edge deployment Ryzen AI NPU needs distillation to consistency models – VisAligner video diffusion 16-frame requires 3.2s per rollout, limiting online adaptation [8].
- **PhysAligner non-differentiable contacts:** LCP through high-friction stick-slip nondifferentiable, optimization gets stuck local minima despite 3.74% improvement; need learned contact model.
- **Safety and verification:** VLA-Safety TLA+ spec checks action in SafeSet but assumes perfect grounding; no formal link between affordance IoU and closed-loop stability. Real-to-Sim search none-the-less may amplify sim biases.
- **Scale verification:** TLC 1e5 states 4 hosts production 1k trajectories; need compositional verification VLM→action expert.

Future:

1. **MolmoBot zero-shot sim2real** [7] – training on MolmoSpaces 230k scenes 42M grasps entirely in sim, then zero-shot transfer without real demos – complementing our Real2Sim approach.
2. Scale DiffusionVLA 2B→72B [6] with model parallelism across RDNA4 clusters via ROCm [8].
3. Active affordance exploration with 3D Gaussian splats [5] for high-fidelity synthesis + physics.
4. Learned affordance-constrained diffusion: project each DDIM step onto $\{a: \text{gripper}(a) \in M_{aff}\}$.

## 7 Conclusion

We presented end-to-end treatment of multi-modal foundation models for embodied AI bridging VLA tokenization, diffusion versus autoregressive action chunk generation, 3D affordance grounding from point clouds, and bidirectional Sim2Real via real-to-sim search. OpenVLA demonstrates open-source VLA with 7B parameters outperforming 55B closed RT-2-X by 16.5% [1]; arp shows autoregressive chunking 24.8% vs 1.6% diffusion on high-precision ALOHA [4]; hybrid diffusionVLA restores 63.7% zero-shot accuracy on 102 unseen objects with reasoning injection and 82Hz inference [6]; affordance grounding advances via fine-grained triplets and video-based priors VIDA 38k HOI [5][6]; bi-directional adaptation saves 117× real samples [7] while PhysAligner+VisAligner close physics + appearance gap +29.17% real success [8]. The unified taxonomy with theorems, TLA+ safety invariants, and deployment stack on AMD ROCm/Franka provides reproducible baseline for generalist embodied agents beyond tabletop to factory sorting and zero-shot bin-picking.

*Key takeaway:* **Discrete reasoning provides language generalization, diffusion provides multimodal control fidelity, 3D affordance grounds symbols to geometry, and real-to-sim search aligns physics/appearance jointly – no single component alone suffices for embodied AGI.**
