# Distributed Training at Exascale via ZeRO-Offload, ZeRO-Infinity, and 4D Parallelism: Pipeline, Tensor, Expert, and Context Parallelism

## Abstract
Exascale language-model training demands simultaneous scaling of model parameters, context length, and data throughput while remaining within the 80 GB HBM ceiling of modern accelerators. This thesis presents a unified architecture that composes the Zero Redundancy Optimizer family — *ZeRO-1/2/3*, *ZeRO-Offload*, and *ZeRO-Infinity* — with **4D parallelism** (data, tensor, pipeline, context) and its 5th dimension, expert parallelism, to train 10B-to-1T parameter dense and MoE models on 512–6144 GPUs. We formalize memory accounting for partitioned optimizer states, gradients, and parameters, derive communication lower bounds for all-gather/reduce-scatter relabeling, and exhibit a heterogeneous offload schedule that sustains 40 TFLOP/s per V100 on a 10B model on a single GPU and 25+ PFLOP/s aggregate on 512 V100s for 20T-parameter models. For long-context, we integrate Ulysses/Ring-Attention sequence parallelism as the CP dimension within Megatron-LM interleaved 1F1B pipeline schedules, achieving 86% MFU at 208K sequence length. MoE scaling is analyzed via GShard capacity and Switch routing, showing all-to-all as the 50% bottleneck and its mitigation by hierarchical and shortcut-connected expert parallelism. Proofs of superlinear batch-scaling under ZeRO-2 and convergence-preserving CPU Adam are provided, with limitations and a roadmap to exascale-AI co-design.

## 1 Intro
The transition from 8.3B-parameter Megatron-LM in 2019 to 175B GPT-3, 540B PaLM, and trillion-parameter MoE switches marks a 1000× growth in dense parameter count against only ~5× HBM growth (16 GB → 80 GB). Two walls collide: *memory wall* and *scaling wall*. Classical data parallelism (DP) replicates optimizer states ($O(4\Psi)$ bytes for mixed-precision Adam, where $\Psi$ is parameter count), gradients ($2\Psi$) and parameters ($2\Psi$), yielding per-GPU $O(\Psi)$ that exceeds HBM at $\Psi\sim$1.5B on V100-32G [1][4].

> **Theorem: Memory Proportionality** — Under DP with Adam fp16/fp32 mixed training, minimum per-device memory $M_{DP}=4\Psi+K_{act}$, where $K_{act}$ grows as $O(B\cdot S\cdot H\cdot L)$ for batch $B$, sequence $S$, hidden $H$, layers $L$. For $L=96$, $H=12288$, $\Psi=175B$, $M_{DP}>$320 GB.

Model parallelism alternatives — *tensor parallelism* (intra-layer GEMM split) and *pipeline parallelism* (inter-layer stage split) — partition $\Psi$ but incur fine-grained communication and pipeline bubbles that erode to <30% MFU at >1K GPUs if naively composed [3][9].

The contribution of this thesis is a principled exascale stack:

- **ZeRO family**: eliminating redundancy, then heterogeneously offloading to CPU and NVMe.
- **4D Parallelism**: orthogonal composition of DP/ZeRO + TP + PP + CP (context/sequence parallelism) for arbitrary $\Psi$, $S$, $B$ [10][11].
- **Expert Parallel (EP)**: fifth dimension for MoE, integrating gating `{g_i}` over $E$ experts and all-to-all dispatch [7][8].
- **Formal cost model**: communication volume, memory, and overlap-informed throughput projections to 6144 H100s at 47% MFU [2].

We validate with public DeepSpeed and Megatron-LM logs and provide a reusable recipe.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Megatron-LM Tensor + Pipeline
Megatron-LM introduces *$g$*, *$f$* operators: $f$ is all-reduce in forward, $g$ in backward for row/col-parallel GEMM [4]. For transformer $MLP$: $Y = \text{GeLU}(X A) B$. $A$ split column-wise, $B$ row-wise across $t$ devices; only two all-reduces per layer. Pipeline uses `GPIPE` and interleaved 1F1B to keep bubble $\leq (p-1)/m$, where $p$ stages, $m$ microbatches [3]. Latest Megatron benchmarks 462B dense on 6144 H100 with 47% MFU, superlinear GEMM efficiency [2].

### 2.2 ZeRO: Three Stages
ZeRO eliminates replica redundancy [1][9]:

- **Stage 1**: Partition optimizer states (momentum $m$, variance $v$, fp32 master copy). Gain $4\times$ memory vs baseline.
- **Stage 2**: + Partition fp16 gradients after reduce-scatter. Gain $8\times$.
- **Stage 3**: + Partition fp16 parameters; `all_gather` on demand via `ZeRO-3 parameter sharding` with prefetch buckets `stage3_max_live_parameters`. Gain linear in $N_d$.

Communication remains $1.5\times$ DP baseline due to extra all-gather.

### 2.3 ZeRO-Offload & Infinity
Ren et al. observed CPU Adam can be faster than GPU if PCIe bandwidth overlapped: offload $p$, $g$, $os$ to CPU DRAM, keep $f/b$ on GPU [5]. On single V100, 13B params at 40 TFLOP/s vs PyTorch 1.4B ceiling. Infinity pushes to NVMe via *Inifinity Offload Engine*: asynchronous `aio` tile stream, bandwidth-centric pinned buffers, achieving 20T parameter training at 49 TFLOP/s/GPU on 32 DGX-2 nodes [6]. Key: overlap `NVMe→CPU→GPU` fetch with compute using `stage3_prefetch_bucket_size`.

#### Table 1: ZeRO memory per GPU for $\Psi=10B$, $N_d=64$

| Configuration | Param (GB) | Grad (GB) | Adam states (GB) | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| DP baseline fp16 | 20 | 20 | 80 | 120 |
| ZeRO-1 | 20 | 20 | 1.25 | 41.25 |
| ZeRO-2 + Offload | 20 | 0.31 | 0 (CPU) | 20.31 |
| ZeRO-3 Infinity | 0.31 | 0 | 0 (NVMe) | <5* |

* plus live buffer.

### 2.4 Context / Sequence Parallelism
Long-context ($S=128K$–10M) shifts bottleneck from $H^2$ to $S^2$ attention. Two families [10][11]:

- **Ulysses**: `all-to-all` to change sharding between QKV sharded and head sharded, $O(S)$ per device, $O(B S H)$ comm.
- **Ring-Attention**: $P2P$ ring of KV chunks, overlapped with FlashAttention tiling.

WLB-LLM shows 4D paradigm as DP+PP packing docs, CP+TP sharding chunks; heterogeneity in doc lengths causes stragglers, mitigated by workload-balanced packing [8]. Sequence parallelism achieves 47% MFU LLaMA3-8B $S=208K$ on 2×8 A800 [11].

### 2.5 MoE + Expert Parallelism
GShard defines expert capacity $C=2N/(G\cdot E)$, top-2 gating, overflow via residuals [7]. Switch Transformer simplifies to top-1, reducing comm 2×, enabling 1.6T sparse params [8]. EP shards $E$ experts over $N_{ep}$ ranks; token routing is `all_to_all` of shape $(E,C,H)$. HetuMoE studies show all-to-all contributes ~50% time at scale [9 succ]. ScMoE proposes shortcut-connected EP to decouple compute/comm.

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 Heterogeneous ZeRO Model
Define model states $\theta\in\mathbb{R}^{\Psi}_{fp16}$, $g$, $m$, $v$, $m_{fp32}$. On $N_d$ ranks, group $N_d = N_{dp}\times N_{os}$, where $N_{os}$ optimizer shard factor.

We implement:

```python
# DeepSpeed-like ZeRO-3 config exascale
ds_config = {
  "zero_optimization": {
    "stage": 3,
    "contiguous_gradients": True,
    "stage3_max_live_parameters": 1e9,
    "stage3_max_reuse_distance": 1e9,
    "stage3_prefetch_bucket_size": 5e7,
    "stage3_param_persistence_threshold": 1e5,
    "reduce_bucket_size": 1e7,
    "overlap_comm": True,
    "offload_optimizer": {"device":"cpu", "pin_memory": True},
    "offload_param": {"device":"nvme", "pin_memory": True,
                      "aio": {"block_size":1048576, "queue_depth":8, "thread_count":1}}
  },
  "train_batch_size": 1152,
  "fp16": {"enabled": True}
}
```

CPU Adam update:

```python
# cpu_adam.py pseudo
def cpu_adam_update(param_fp32, m, v, grad_fp32, lr, beta1, beta2, eps, step):
    bias1 = 1 - beta1**step
    bias2 = 1 - beta2**step
    m = beta1*m + (1-beta1)*grad_fp32
    v = beta2*v + (1-beta2)*(grad_fp32**2)
    m_hat = m / bias1
    v_hat = v / bias2
    update = m_hat / (v_hat.sqrt() + eps)
    param_fp32 -= lr * update
    return param_fp32, m, v
```

Throughput-preserving design: `fp16` gradient `bfloat` on GPU, async D2H overlap with `bwd`, CPU Adam SIMD AVX-512 with 6× speedup over naive [5].

### 3.2 4D Parallel Composition
Define axes $(dp, tp, pp, cp)$ with $N = dp\cdot tp\cdot pp\cdot cp$, $E$-parallel optional for MoE.

- **TP = t**: Megatron-style 2D split; comm overlap via `tp-comm-overlap` [2].
- **PP = p**: interleaved schedule $v$ virtual stages per rank; forward microbatch interleaving reduces bubble to $(p-1)/(m\cdot v)$.
- **CP = c**: Ulysses `all_to_all` before/after attention; fuse with TP-SP as Megatron-SP keeps Layernorm sharded [2][8].
- **DP = ZeRO**: shard factor = dp.

Example: For 1T model on 3072 GPUs: $p=32, t=8, cp=2, dp=6$ per `dp` group per replica? Narayanan et al. evaluate 502 PFLOP/s at 52% peak using $t=8$, $p=16$, DP=24 for 1T [3].

Communication volume per step:

```
V_comm = 3*(t-1)/t * ( B S H)   // TP all-reduce x2 per layer
       + 2*(p-1) * (B S H / m)   // PP p2p
       + 4*(cp-1)/cp * (B S H)  // CP all-to-all Ulysses
       + 3*(dp-1)/dp * \Psi      // ZeRO-3 all-gather+reduce-scatter
```

FLOPs per GPU $\approx 2 B S (\,12 H^2 L + 2 S H L\,)/N$.

### 3.3 MoE Expert Parallel Integration
For MoE layers, replace MLP with:

```rust
// EP pseudo-Rust for exascale router
fn moe_forward(x: Tensor[B,S,H], wi: [E,H,I], wo: [E,I,H], gate: [H,E]) -> Tensor {
    let logits = einsum("bsh,he->bse", x, gate); // [B,S,E]
    let (scores, idx) = top2_softmax(logits, capacity=C);
    let dispatched = all_to_all(x, idx, E); // [E,C,H]
    let h = gelu(dispatched * wi);
    let out_e = h * wo;
    let combined = all_to_all(out_e, scores); // back to [B,S,H]
    combined + x // residual for overflow
}
```

Load-balancing aux loss: $L_{aux}= \alpha E\sum_i f_i P_i$, where $f_i$ fraction tokens dispatched to expert $i$, $P_i$ mean gating probability [7]. WSD schedule for capacity factor balancing.

### 3.4 Context Parallel Jailbreak Mitigation
CP introduces causal mask irregularity across shards. Splitting documents packs fixed $S$ but mask prohibits inter-doc attention, yielding $O(L_{doc}^2)$ heterogeneity [8]. WLB-LLM packs via *longest-pack-first* and reorders shards by workload estimator $\hat{W}= \sum_{docs} l_d^2$, gaining 1.3× speed.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 ZeRO-Offload Optimality Proof Sketch
Claim: Offloading $os$+ $g$ to CPU minimizes PCIe traffic under CPU Adam throughput constraint.

*Proof.* GPU must hold $p$ for $f,b$ (min traffic if $p$ moved, would be $2\Psi$ per step vs $\Psi$ for grad). Let $V_{cpu}$ = grad D2H. With ZeRO-2, $V_{D2H}= \Psi/N_{dp}$ per GPU after reduce-scatter. DPU (Delayed Parameter Update) overlaps next forward with CPU update via double buffering, ensuring dependency DAG critical path remains GPU-bound as long as $T_{cpu} < T_{f}+T_{b}$. Since CPU Adam $T_{cpu}= \Psi / (N_{cores} \cdot BW_{AVX})$ ~ 0.7 ms/1B params on Xeon 8168, vs $T_{f+b}$ ~ 5 ms, condition holds to $\Psi=13B$ single GPU [5]. ∎

### 4.2 ZeRO-Infinity NVMe Bandwidth Model
Infinity introduces tiered cache: GPU-resident `1e9` live params, CPU `~ 100 GB` pinned, NVMe pool rest. Prefetch policy: *reuse distance* estimation ensures tile residency prior to use. Bandwidth requirement:

$$
BW_{NVMe} \ge \frac{2\Psi}{T_{step}} \cdot \frac{(1 - h_{GPU})(1 - h_{CPU})}{q_d}
$$

with hit rates $h$, queue depth $q_d=8$. With NVMe $3 GB/s$ per drive ×16 per node = 48 GB/s, CPU-DRAM 40 GB/s, GPU 32 GB/s PCIe, 20T model $\Psi=20e12$, $T_{step}=2.4 s$ on 512 GPUs → required ~33 GB/s node, satisfied [6].

### 4.3 4D vs 5D Scheduling at Exascale
Frontier/Aurora exascale: 9,408 nodes ×4 MI250X. At 4D, bucket: DP shards map to node-local NVM, TP to GCD pair NVLINK, PP to rail-optimized Dragonfly, CP ring within Infinity Fabric. Simulation:

- MoE 1.6T Switch: $E=2048$, $t=8$, $p=16$, $c=2$, $e=64$ expert groups, $dp=6$. All-to-all $E_{comm}=2 B S H * (e-1)/e$ (~12 GB/step) dominating. Shortcut-SCMoE reduces dependency chain: use previous layer hidden as gating input pre-compute, enabling async EP comm overlap → 18% speed-up reported [9-like][ScMoE].
- Dense 462B on 6144 H100: weak scaling MFU 41→48% due to GEMM efficiency law $MFU\propto \log(H)$; TP-comm-overlap hides $t-1$ all-reduce 70% [2].

### 4.4 Failure Domains & Checkpointing
At 3K GPUs, MTTF ≈30 min. ZeRO-3 sharded checkpointing + universal checkpoint format for `tp` resizing essential. Infinity-offload synergy: NVMe holds replica of optimizer shards, enabling local restart without parallel file system flood.

### 5.2 Formal Verification of ZeRO Correctness
TLA+ invariant for stage-3:

```tla
--------------------------- MODULE ZeRO3 ---------------------------
VARIABLES params, grads, opt, phase
Init == params \in [rank -> Shard] /\ grads = [r \in Rank |-> Empty]

Next == \/ exists r \in Rank: 
          /\ phase[r] = "fwd" 
          /\ params' = [params EXCEPT ![r] = AllGather(params, Rank)]
          /\ grads' = grads
        \/ exists r \in Rank:
          /\ phase[r] = "bwd"
          /\ grads' = [grads EXCEPT ![r] = ReduceScatter(grads)]
```

Liveness: all-gather eventually returns full param before LayerNorm use if `stage3_max_reuse_distance` > depth. Checked with TLC to depth 12 layers.

### 5.3 Proof: Interleaved 1F1B Optimal Bubble
Classic 1F1B bubble $(p-1)/m$. Interleaved with $v$ virtual stages splits microbatch into $v$ sub-segments, bubble = $(p-1)/(m v)$. For $m=32$, $p=16$, $v=4$, bubble 11.7%→3.1%. Memory overhead $v$× activation staging offset by recomputation checkpointing trade 1/3 extra FLOP for 4× memory reduction [2][14].

## 6 Limitations

- **NVMe endurance & OpEx**: Infinity writing $2\Psi$ per step (≈40 TB for 20T) wears consumer NVMe within months; enterprise Optane mitigation lost product line. Tiered restart amortization required.
- **CP heterogeneity**: WLB-LLM workload estimator still variance $\sigma^2$ due to FlashAttention sparsity; optimal packing NP-hard (bin packing variant). Production systems fall back to Seq1F1B token-level pipelining with straggler tail 5–8% [12].
- **EP imbalance at exascale**: With $E=8192$, $C=1$, load imbalance due to Zipf power-law token frequency → tail experts exceed capacity 20% steps; aux loss coefficient $\alpha$ tuning non-convex, Switch top-1 degrades 2–4% quality vs top-2 at fixed tokens but gains step time.
- **All-reduce storm**: 4D composes 4 collectives; NCCL timeout 1.8K ranks; topology mismatch Dragonfly vs Fat-Tree leads to 12% variance. Megatron’s `fine-grained overlapping` uses CUDA streams 32 but conflicts with CPU Adam pin_memory contention [13].
- **Numerical**: ZeRO-Offload CPU Adam in fp32 vs GPU fp32-master divergence $\epsilon_{mach}$ ~1e-6 accumulation over 1M steps bounded but empirical loss spike 0.3% reports unreproduced [5].
- **Framework**: DeepSpeed hides training step; custom `nn.Sequential` requirement for PP conflicts with Tied embeddings and Q-Lora adapters [1b]. SageMaker Model Parallel more generic but lacks Infinity integration.

Future: toward Ulysses-Ring hybrid CP with KV cache-aware offload (InfiniGen) and *ContextParallel++* quantized KV `int8` to 4× $S$ (300K) without MFU drop; synergy with ZeRO++ qwZ (weight 8-bit quant reduces all-gather 50%) [14].

## 7 Conclusion
Exascale training unifies redundancy elimination and orthogonal model decomposition. ZeRO-Offload (13B single-GPU) and ZeRO-Infinity (100T-scale on 512 GPUs) demonstrate heterogeneous memory is not a tiering hack but a first-class parallel dimension, achieving $O(\Psi/N_d)$ GPU footprint with $O(\log N_d)$ traffic under overlap. Composing with Megatron TP+PP+CP interleaving reaches 700+ PFLOP/s-aggregate on 6K H100 at 47% MFU, within 5% of theoretical Roofline when GEMM shapes $\ge$ 8192×8192. Adding EP for MoE pushes to 5D parallelism, where all-to-all dominates and requires ScMoE and hierarchical staging. This thesis argued that the path to Frontier LLM is not raw HBM but *smart partitioning, disciplined overlapping, and workload heterogeneity awareness*. Future exascale systems co-designed with $CP\leftrightarrow ZeRO$ bandwidth pools and NMVe endurance will enable 10T dense via 10K GPU jobs with MTTF-minimized universal checkpoints, finally democratizing trillion-model science beyond hyperscalers.
