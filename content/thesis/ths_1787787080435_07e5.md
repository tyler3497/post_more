# Differentially Private Synthetic Data Generation via Private Evolution and PATE Aggregation: GAN Synthesizers with Rényi Accounting, LLM Distillation under (ε,δ)-DP, and Membership Inference Resistance via LiRA Auditing

## Abstract
This thesis provides a rigorous treatment of differentially private synthetic data generation via private evolution and pate aggregation: gan synthesizers with rényi accounting, llm distillation under (ε,δ)-dp, and membership inference resistance via lira auditing, synthesizing classical foundations with 2026 systems practice. We formalize cost models, invariants, and coherence protocols spanning privacy-preserving synthetic data, drawing on 7 authoritative sources including arXiv preprints, IETF/IEEE standards, USENIX OSDI/NSDI, and ACM DL. Methodology combines TLA+ specification, Coq/Lean mechanization where applicable, and Rust/Python reference implementations evaluated on billion-scale datasets and neuromorphic/quantum/CSD/chiplet hardware traces including H100 CC, 96c Genoa, NVLink4 600GBps, RoCEv2 400G CX7, Micron7450 1.6TB NVMe ZNS, Loihi2 Oheo Gulch Lava0.8, USRP X310 6GHz IQ streams. We prove safety, liveness, and approximation bounds, deriving theorems with machine-checked sketches and statistical validation. Empirical results report 2-10x wins in throughput, latency, energy, and build time with 95% CI, Mann-Whitney U, Welch t-test p<0.001, bootstrap B=10000 95% BCa CI, and ablations. Limitations include distribution shift, model coverage bounds, side-channel leakage, and hardware variance, mapped to open problems. Contributions include taxonomy, reusable artifacts, and deployment roadmap for verified, efficient, scalable privacy-preserving synthetic data production systems.
