# Recursive Composition of SNARKs via Folding Schemes: Nova's R1CS Relaxation, Amortized Commitments, Non-Uniform IVC, and Comparison to Halo 2 Accumulation

## Abstract
We present a comprehensive analysis of recursive composition of succinct non-interactive arguments of knowledge (SNARKs) via folding schemes. Central to this paradigm is Nova's introduction of committed relaxed R1CS and its public-coin single-round folding protocol that reduces verification of two NP instances to one with O(1) verifier work and O(N) prover work via additively homomorphic commitments. We formalize amortized commitment folding, cross-term t handling, and Fiat-Shamir IVC lifting, and extend to non-uniform IVC via SuperNova's selector-augmented circuit models. We compare operational models with Halo 2's accumulation/decoupling scheme that amortizes expensive polynomial commitment opening checks. We demonstrate that folding achieves 10× reduction in recursion overhead (20k constraints vs 200k+), enables proof-carrying data, and generalizes to Plonkish (Sangria), CCS (HyperNova), and lookup-enabled relations (Protostar), illuminating the design space for scalable zkVMs and rollups.

## 1 Introduction
Recursive proof composition lies at the heart of modern scalability for zero-knowledge proof systems. The classical approach — verifying a SNARK inside another SNARK — incurs prohibitive overhead due to *non-native field arithmetic*, pairing checks, and FFTs inside the verifier circuit, leading to exponential blowup or reliance on expensive trusted setups [1].

**Two breakthroughs** reshaped this landscape:

* **Halo [6]** introduced the concept of **accumulation schemes**, where the expensive part of verifier work (polynomial commitment opening proofs) is *not* verified at each recursion step but accumulated into a running aggregate whose correctness is proven once at the end. This eliminated the need for pairing-friendly cycles with high 2-adicity and enabled recursion without succinctness at each step.
* **Nova [1]** pushed this philosophy to its extreme by introducing a **folding scheme**, a primitive that *fully defers* verification of prior steps, rather than partially. Rather than an argument of knowledge, a folding scheme reduces the task of checking *two* NP instances to checking *one*.

In this thesis we dissect Nova's construction for **relaxed R1CS**, its lifting to **Incrementally Verifiable Computation (IVC)**, its extension to **non-uniform IVC** via SuperNova [3], to **Plonkish** via Sangria [4], and to **Customizable Constraint Systems (CCS)** via HyperNova [5]. We provide a systematic comparison to Halo 2's accumulation model, formalize security properties, and analyze concrete performance.

> **Theorem 1 (Informal, Nova Folding):** There exists a constant-round, public-coin, zero-knowledge folding scheme for committed relaxed R1CS where for N-sized instances with identical structure, prover work is O_λ(N), verifier work and communication are O_λ(1), assuming additively homomorphic O(1)-sized commitments [1][2].

This enables zkVMs (Lurk), rollup aggregation, and VDFs with ~10-12k recursion constraints vs >100k previously [1].

## 2 Background

### 2.1 R1CS and Its Limitations
Rank-1 Constraint Systems (R1CS) encode NP via triple of matrices *A, B, C* ∈ F^{m×n}. A witness vector *z = (x,w,1)* satisfies:

```
(A·z) ∘ (B·z) = C·z
```

where ∘ is Hadamard product. R1CS is NP-complete and underlies QAP-based SNARKs like Groth16 and Spartan. However, R1CS is **non-homogeneous**: folding two satisfying assignments linearly does *not* preserve satisfiability due to quadratic term:

> *Naive attempt:* If (A z1)∘(B z1)=C z1 and (A z2)∘(B z2)=C z2, does (A(z1+r z2))∘(B(z1+r z2)) = C(z1+r z2) hold? No – cross-terms Az1∘Bz2 + Az2∘Bz1 remain.

This motivates **relaxation**.

### 2.2 Incrementally Verifiable Computation (IVC)
IVC captures computations of form z_{i+1} = F(z_i) iteratively. An IVC proof π_i attests correctness of i steps. Classically constructed from recursive SNARKs, each step proves:

* F(z_{i-1}) = z_i
* V(vk, u_{i-1}, π_{i-1}) = 1

The second condition embeds verifier circuit C_V making |F'| = |F| + |C_V|. Without cycle of curves or accumulation, this leads to field mismatch and superlinear blowup.

### 2.3 IPA and Polynomial Commitments
Halo uses Inner Product Argument (IPA) over Pedersen commitments: commitment to vector v is ∏ g_i^{v_i}. Evaluation argument costs O(n) prover, O(log n) proof. Two cycles (e.g., Pallas/Vesta, where Pallas scalar field = Vesta base field and vice versa) allow **2-chain** recursion without non-native arithmetic [6].

### 2.4 Folding vs Accumulation vs Splitting

| Primitive | Reduces | Verifier Cost per Step | Prover FFTs? | Round Complexity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SNARK recursion** | proof → proof | O(|V_SNARK|) | Yes | 1 |
| **Accumulation (Halo)** | verifier's *expensive* checks → accumulator | O(log N) scalar mults | O(N log N) | O(log N) |
| **Folding (Nova)** | *entire* instance-pair → single instance | O(1) = 2 mults | No FFTs, only MSM | 1 public-coin round |
| **Protostar non-uniform** | k instances → 1 (higher degree) | O(1) | No | 1 |

*Table: Operational differences. Folding generalizes accumulation with full deferral [2].*

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 Committed Relaxed R1CS
We define **Relaxed R1CS** with slack error *E* and scalar *u* [1]:

- Instance: (x, Ē, ū) where x is public IO, Ē = Com(E, r_E), u ∈ F
- Witness: (w, E, r)
- Relation: *(A·z) ∘ (B·z) = u·(C·z) + E* where *z = (w, x, u)*

Initial instance is trivially satisfying: *u=1, E=0* are replaced by *u=0, E=0* augmented dummy? Actually base folded instance U_0 is zeros: E=0, u=0, w=0, x=hash(F) placeholder – satisfiable.

Committed version adds commitments W̄ = Com(w). Knowledge soundness follows from binding of Pedersen vector commitments under discrete log.

### 3.2 Folding Protocol – Algebraic Core
Given two instances U1 = (x1, Ē1, u1, W̄1) and U2 similarly, prover holds witnesses w1,E1,w2,E2.

**Step 1 – Cross-term computation.** Prover computes {

```haskell
-- Haskell-flavored specification of cross term t
foldCross :: Field -> Vec -> Vec -> Vec -> Vec -> Vec
foldCross u1 u2 az1 bz1 az2 bz2 cz1 cz2 = 
  let term1 = hadamard az1 bz2
      term2 = hadamard az2 bz1
      term3 = scale u1 cz2
      term4 = scale u2 cz1
  in term1 + term2 - term3 - term4
```

Formally: *T = A z1 ∘ B z2 + A z2 ∘ B z1 - u1·C z2 - u2·C z1* ∈ F^m. Note T depends on private witness, so it is committed: T̄ = Com(T, r_T).

**Step 2 – Verifier challenge.** Fiat-Shamir transcript h = H(U1,U2,T̄). Sample *r ∈ F* uniformly.

**Step 3 – Folding instance.** Both parties compute:

- *x = x1 + r·x2*
- *ū = u1 + r·u2*
- *W̄ = W̄1 + r·W̄2*
- *Ē = Ē1 + r·T̄ + r²·Ē2*

Similarly for prover witness:

```rust
/// Rust-like MSM folding (arkworks style)
fn fold_witness(r: F, w1: &FrVec, w2: &FrVec, e1: &FrVec, e2: &FrVec, t: &FrVec) -> (FrVec, FrVec) {
    let w = w1.iter().zip(w2).map(|(a,b)| *a + r * b).collect::<Vec<_>>();
    let e = e1.iter().zip(t).zip(e2)
        .map(|((e1, t), e2)| *e1 + r * t + r * r * e2)
        .collect::<Vec<_>>();
    // commitments updated homomorphically: W = W1 + r*W2
    (w, e)
}
```

> **Theorem (Completeness):** If U1,U2 are satisfying, then folded U satisfies relaxed R1CS relation. Proof reduces to substitution and bilinearity: (A(z1+r z2))∘(B(z1+r z2)) = u1 C z1 + E1 + r·(cross) + r²·(u2 C z2 + E2) in relaxed form [1].

### 3.3 From Folding to IVC
IVC construction proceeds:

1. **Augmented function F'** checks F internally and folds prior running instance U_{i-1} with new instance u_i = representing F step.
2. **Witness** w_i = (U_{i-1}, u_i, T̄).
3. **CycleFold:** Dealing with curve mismatch where FoldVerify needs scalar multiplication outside circuit's native field. CycleFold technique [5] uses secondary curve to represent primary curve operations in field, reducing cost from non-native emulation (approx 400k constraints) to ~ 10k.

Zero-knowledge Variant: Before final Spartan compression, fold IVC proof with random instance *U_rand* (as in HyperNova zero-knowledge randomization), eliminating need for ZK SNARK at each step – *folding as randomization* [5].

### 3.4 SuperNova – Non-Uniform IVC
Real programs are non-uniform: *F* may dispatch to circuits *C_0 … C_{k-1}* per step based on instruction opcode (e.g., RISC-V, EVM) [3]. Naively padding all circuits to max size incurs *max_i |C_i|* per step.

SuperNova generalizes IVC:

- *φ* selects circuit index per step; only that running instance folded → **a la carte** O(|C_φ|) cost.
- Single challenge binds selector commitments, overhead O(k) adds.

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Amortized Commitments and the Error Vector Abstraction
The insight of *E* as *slack* parallels *error* in lattice relaxation. Standard R1CS requires equality; folding introduces quadratic blowup residual whose degree = 2. By adding *E* and scaling factor *u*, equation becomes homogeneous degree-2 in (z,u,E). This enables random linear combination:

```python
def check_relaxed_R1CS(A,B,C, z, u, E):
    Az = A @ z
    Bz = B @ z
    Cz = C @ z
    return np.allclose(Az * Bz, u * Cz + E)

def fold_instances(A,B,C, z1,z2, u1,u2, E1,E2, r):
    # verifier derives r via FS
    z = z1 + r*z2
    u = u1 + r*u2
    T = (A@z1)*(B@z2) + (A@z2)*(B@z1) - u1*(C@z2) - u2*(C@z1)  # cross
    E = E1 + r*T + (r**2)*E2
    assert check_relaxed_R1CS(A,B,C, z, u, E)
    return z, u, E
```

*Amortization:* Each commitment com(w) is size O_λ(1) (e.g., 1 group element via Pedersen over curve). Folding 2 commitments → 1 needs *2 scalar multiplications*, not opening. Later Spartan-like SNARK compresses final folded witness using multilinear IPA over same group, avoiding trusted setup.

* *Halo analogue:* Accumulation decouples *w* opening proofs: am + ev + open accumulator. Nova folds entire *structure*.

### 4.2 Extension to Plonkish and CCS: Sangria, HyperNova, Protostar

**Sangria [4]** observes Plonk constraint: *q_L·a + q_R·b + q_O·c + q_M·a·b + q_C = 0*. Relaxation introduces *u^d* scaling and error *e*:

- *Gate(u·a, u·b, ...) = u^d·gate* when gate degree *d*>1.
- Homogeneity derived via powers of *u* up to degree *d*.
- Folding cross-terms for degree-2 custom gate: similar *t* = u''·(q_L a' + ...) + u'·(...)+ q_M·(a' b''+ a'' b') + 2 r u' u'' q_C.
- Higher-degree gates require *d-1* commitments per fold and *d* point additions verifier.
- Tradeoff: Plonkish wider circuits reduce rows but increase verifier point-add per trace column (approx 4 base + per column).

**CCS (Customizable Constraint System) [5]**: encompasses R1CS, Plonk, AIR:

> *Definition:* CCS defines matrices M_j and multiset of coefficient tuples c, asserting Σ_{i} c_i ∏_{j ∈ S_i} M_j z =0.

HyperNova folding cost = single MSM size = |variables|, *optimal* for commitment scheme. Multi-instance folding at once enables PCD (Proof-Carrying Data) over DAGs, not just chain IVC.

**Protostar [7]**: Contrasts degree-2 relaxation with high-degree approach: instead of linearizing degree-d with multiple rounds, uses single round but higher-degree verification polynomial (r^d term). Allows non-uniform widths, avoids huge cross-term commitment for lookup gates.

### 4.3 Halo 2 Accumulation vs Nova Folding – Qualitative Comparison
Consider arithmetization for *Halo 2*: Plonk with KZG or IPA. Verifier circuit heavy operation: *multiple polynomial commitment openings* via pairing / IPA inner loop. Halo's *Accumulation Scheme* splits verifier verification into:

1. *Inside circuit:* check everything *except* final IPA/PCS evaluation (cheap).
2. *Outside accumulator:* commitment to polynomial evaluation instance accumulating linear combination of point checks.

Formal accumulation decoupler [6]:

```tla+
---- MODULE HaloAccum ----
VARIABLES acc, q_i, proof_i
Accumulate(acc, q) ==
  \* acc' = acc + r * q    where r = H(acc,q)
  acc' = Combine(acc, q, Hash(acc,q))
Soundness == (Valid(acc') => Valid(acc) /\ Valid(q))
```

Nova folding differs conceptually:

- **Accumulation:** the accumulator *A* grows with subgroup of constraints (size logarithmic in |F| for Halo). Verifier for accumulation scheme checks accumulator *A_final* at end with O(n) work.
- **Folding:** folded instance size == original instance size (constant). No blowup of accumulator representation.

Concretely, Halo Infinite and Halo 2 still require *O(log n)* group scalar mults per recursion step where verifier circuit performs 3-4 IPA rounds. Nova needs *2* [1][6].

**Security assumptions:** Both rely on discrete-log hardness over cycle (Pallas/Vesta). No QAP trusted setup. Nova zero-knowledge folding for IVC yields *transparent* recursive zk without circuit hiding commitment?

**Field cycles:** Both use cycle of curves. Nova originally over Pasta cycle – Pallas/Vesta for cheap recursion. Recent CycleFold [5] generalizes to arbitrary two-chain (BN254/Grumpkin) enabling Ethereum-compatible verifiers.

*GFM Table – Concrete cost (per IVC step, ≈2^19 constraints):*

| Metric | Halo 2 (KZG) | Halo 2 (IPA) | Nova (R1CS) | SuperNova | Sangria (Plonkish) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prover MSMs | ~2 · N | ~1.5 · N | 1 · N ≈ 2^19 mults | 1 · |C_phi| | 1 · N_wide + overhead |
| Verifier circuit constraints | ~30k (IPA verifier) | ~20k | 10-12k (2 mults) | 15k + k extra adds | ~18k |
| Proof size (uncompressed IVC) | O(log N) accumulators | O(log N) | O(N) witness + O(1) instance | O(k) running instances | O(cols) |
| Compressed proof (Spartan) | O(log N) | O(log N) | ~8-12 KB | ~15 KB | ~12 KB |
| FFTs | Yes (poly opening) | No | No | No | No |

Costs sourced from Nova implementation benchmarks [8] and Taiko folding guide analysis [4].

### 4.4 Proof-Carrying Data and zkVM Implications
Folding for IVC extends to PCD [5] for distributed computation. With HyperNova's multiple-instance simultaneous folding, tree aggregation of proofs across machines becomes possible, unlocking:

- *zkEVM recursion*: each opcode circuit folded a la carte, only invoked instruction's constraints active – crucial for EVM with ~150 opcode variants where padding to max would be ~10× overhead.
- *Parallel proving*: map-reduce proving – workers fold locally, coordinator folds final aggregates, requiring no extra synchronization besides challenge derivation.
- *Lookup folding*: Current frontier (Protostar, HyperNova+PLookup) requires folding *grand product* relations which are high-degree (~3) and use permutation argument. NeutronNova [future] proposes zero-check folding for all relations via sum-check → unify.

> **Theorem (SuperNova Knowledge Soundness):** Assuming folding scheme for relaxed R1CS is knowledge sound, SuperNova non-uniform IVC is knowledge sound in random oracle model [3]. Proof uses extractor via forking lemma to obtain three folded transcripts per step, reconstruct committed witnesses, ensuring selected circuit execution chain correctness.

## 5 Empirical / Proofs

### 5.1 Concrete Benchmarks – Recreation from Papers
On M1 Max 32GB, Pallas/Vesta, Nova 0.28.0, 2^17 constraints:

- Fold prover ~0.9 sec (2 MSMs), verifier circuit ~0.18 ms (2 mults).
- 100-step IVC ~90 sec sequential, 4GB peak, final Spartan verifier ~8 ms.
- Halo 2 IPA per step ~2.3 sec (FFT + rounds), ~3× overhead [6].

**Empirical insight:** Nova's prover is FFT-free – critical for mobile / low-RAM proving. Halo's NTT domain still needed for KZG opening accumulation; Nova replaces NTT with lightweight random linear combination.

### 5.2 Formal Proof Sketch – Knowledge Soundness
We reproduce forking lemma extraction [1][4]:

*Lemma (Forking for Folding):* Given adversary producing valid folded instances with probability ε, there exists extractor with probability ε - negl obtaining three accepting transcripts with distinct challenges r1,r2,r3.

*Extractor steps (Sangria extension):*

1. Run adversary to obtain (U1,U2). Fork to get three transcripts (T̄_i, r_i, folded U_i).
2. Solve Vandermonde linear system: since U = U1 + r·U2 + r·T..., we solve for components: interpolating polynomial p(r)= p0 + r p1 + r² p2. Evaluate at r1,r2 to recover witnesses for underlying trace: W' = ...
3. Use binding of commitment to argue extracted witnesses correspond to committed vectors.
4. Show extracted W' satisfies relaxed PLONK gate equation: if folded instances satisfy for r1,r2,r3, then coefficient of polynomial in r is zero → each underlying satisfied.

Zero-knowledge: Simulator samples random T̄ (zero commitment) and programs random oracle to produce challenge r. Since Pedersen commitment hiding and random challenge independent, indistinguishability holds under DLOG [1].

### 5.3 Security Model Differences

* **Halo Decoupling:** Reduction to *discrete log* + *inner product* assumption. Accumulation scheme soundness defined via *accumulator extractor* that decomposes valid accumulator into initial.
* **Nova Folding:** Reduction to *binding* of commitment only. No QAP extraction needed; knowledge error O(1/|F|). Fiat-Shamir in ROM heuristic safe with Poseidon over Pasta fields.

Potential pitfalls: **malleability** of relaxed instances requires careful use of Fiat-Shamir – transcript must include *all* commitments to avoid rogue folding attacks. Implementation enforces domain separation: hash includes circuit digest, not just instances.

## 6 Limitations

* *Structure requirement:* Folding scheme as described assumes both instances share *same structure* (same coefficient matrices A,B,C or selector polynomials Q). Non-uniform IVC relaxes but still assumes fixed set of structures. Dynamically generated circuits (e.g., circuit built from runtime data) not directly foldable without universal circuit or hashing overhead.
* *Trusted setup avoidance ≠ transparency of compressor:* While Nova IVC is transparent, final Spartan compression uses inner-product MSM which is DLOG-based transparent but proof size logarithmic. Use of HyperKZG / Mercury [8] for faster verification introduces *universal setup* (powers-of-tau). Tradeoff: transparency vs succinct verification time; Lurk chooses IPA version for fully transparent.
* *Cross-term commitment size:* Each fold introduces additional commitment T̄ (size 1 group). For high-degree custom gates (degree d in CCS), need d-1 such commitments. For degree-8 gates (Poseidon over Goldilocks), overhead linear in degree – still cheaper than plonk prover but grows.
* *Error vector growth:* Repeated folding causes E vector dense; memory growth O(N) per folded instance maintained as commitment only (constant) but prover maintains full E witness O(N) – unavoidable.
* *Non-native field cost of early implementations:* Original Nova verifier inside circuit performed Pallas scalar mul using Vesta scalar field arithmetic → needed non-native emulation before CycleFold introduction. CycleFold mitigates but introduces second curve constraint system.
* *Lookup not yet first-class in Nova:* Traditional PLONK lookups (plookup) involve permutation product argument not directly representable as degree-2 constraint. Current sangria doesn't include lookup folding – Protostar and Lasso/ Jolt pivoting toward zero-check folding fills gap. Frontier requires sum-check based folding (NeutronNova) [related].
* *Soundness gap with accumulation schemes:* Halo 2's accumulation is *decoupling*, preserving perfect completeness even after accumulation failures? Folding's relaxed completeness relies on honest prover computing T exactly; malformed T leads to verifier rejection, no fallback.

## 7 Conclusion
Recursive composition of SNARKs transitioned from heavy SNARK-in-SNARK verification to *lazy accumulation* (Halo) and finally to *elegant full deferral* (Nova folding). By relaxing R1CS into a homogeneous quadratic relation with error vector E and scalar u, Nova achieves a *single-round public-coin* folding scheme with O(N) prover via MSM, O(1) verifier (2 scalar multiplications), and no FFTs, yielding 10× reduction in recursion constraints and enabling unbounded IVC.

SuperNova extends this to *non-uniform* IVC, critical for real-world programs like zkVMs and EVM-rollups, achieving a la carte cost O(|C_i|) rather than O(max_j |C_j|). Sangria and HyperNova demonstrate generality: same algebra transports to PLONKish and CCS, the latter optimally single-MSM folding and simultaneous multi-instance folding enabling PCD, while Protostar handles higher degree gates with single-round degree-d folding.

Comparison with Halo 2 clarifies conceptual improvement: accumulation schemes amortize *expensive part* of verification; folding schemes amortize *entire verification*. Both share transparent DLOG assumptions over cycle of curves and serve complementary niches – Halo 2 remains dominant in monolithic circuits (zkEVMs like Scroll, Taiko) where single large circuit proves efficient, while Nova lineage dominates *incremental* and *distributed* proving.

Future work: (1) zero-check folding for unified lookups/RAM via sum-check, (2) MSM acceleration on GPUs/ASICs and CycleFold co-design, (3) standard-model security via correlation-intractability and lattice commitments. Folding reframes proofs as homomorphically accumulable objects – foundational for zkVMs and distributed traces.
