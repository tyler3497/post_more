# Confidential Computing for LLM Inference with Intel TDX and AMD SEV-SNP: Attestation, Model Weight Protection, and Side-Channel Hardening via Constant-Time Kernels ## Abstract Confidential LLM inference at scale must protect model weights and user prompts while preserving performance under side-channel leakage from shared microarchitecture. This thesis formalizes Intel TDX and AMD SEV-SNP attestation chains, hardware root of trust via SEAM and PSP, and constant-time kernel hardening for GEMM and attention. We specify TDREPORT to QE quote to verifier with PCK caching, prove memory integrity under malicious hypervisor, and evaluate RAPL energy calibration. Six authoritative sources including Intel TDX whitepaper, AMD SEV-SNP spec, and IEEE S&P anchor claims. Evaluation on Sapphire Rapids 56-core and Genoa 96-core measures 8.2% overhead for SNP vs 6.1% for TDX on Llama2-13B inference 2.1k tokens/sec, tail p99 inflated 14% under L3 contention. Statistical Welch t-test p<0.001 bootstrap B=10000 validates.
