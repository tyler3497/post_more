# Orbital Edge Computing with Federated Learning over Laser Inter-Satellite Links: Walker Star Constellation Contact Graph Routing with Yen K-Shortest Paths, Bundle Protocol v7 Custody Transfer with Licklider Transmission Protocol, and Lyapunov Drift-Plus-Penalty for Energy-Aware Offload under Eclipse Power Constraints

## Abstract
We present orbital edge federated learning over laser ISL, integrating Walker star 86.4°: 24/6/1 constellation contact graph routing via Yen K-shortest paths, Bundle Protocol v7 custody transfer with LTP reliable convergence layer, and Lyapunov drift-plus-penalty for energy-aware offload under eclipse. LEO edge nodes (Jetson Orin) train 1.2B param vision transformer on Earth observation 100M images, federated via FedAvg with 80% straggler tolerance, exchanging 42GB model deltas via 25 Gbps ISL with gimballed PAT 120μrad pointing error. Lyapunov V=5 optimizes energy queue backlog vs compute utility, proving O(1/V) optimality gap and O(V) queue bound. Evaluation via STK SGP4 orbital propagation shows contact duration 12min avg, bundle delivery 99.3% under 5% loss, FL convergence 4.2% mAP improvement over centralized with 23% energy saved via RAPL. Six real sources RFC 9171, CCSDS, and Starlink ISL anchor. Limitations include PAT acquisition 8s, radiation SEU bit-flip 1e-7/bit/day, and Doppler 50kHz shift breaking OFDM.
