# Orbital-Free Density Functional Theory via Machine-Learned Kinetic Energy Functionals: SE(3)-Equivariant Graph Neural Networks with Ewald Long-Range Correction, Physics-Informed Pauli Deconvexification, and Differentiable KS-Implicit Training on Materials Project at Scale

## Abstract

Orbital-free density functional theory (OF-DFT) promises \(O(N)\) and \(O(N\log N)\) electronic structure without Kohn-Sham orbitals, but accuracy is limited by approximate kinetic energy functionals \(T_s[n]\). We develop a machine-learned Pauli kinetic functional \(T_\theta[n]=T_{vW}[n]+T_{Pauli}^\theta[n]\) parameterized by \(SE(3)\)-equivariant message passing with spherical tensor products, augmented by differentiable Ewald long-range electrostatics and physics-informed constraints (non-negativity, Lieb-Thirring lower bound, uniform scaling, free-electron limit). Training uses differentiable Kohn-Sham implicit supervision: densities obtained by minimizing \(E_v[n]=T_\theta[n]+E_H[n]+E_{xc}[n]+\int v n\) are match-constrained to Kohn-Sham densities from 132k Materials Project PBE calculations. On held-out solids, molecules, and defect super-cells, learned OF-DFT achieves 3.2 meV/atom total energy vs KS-PBE, 0.041 e/atom density \(L_1\), and stable BFGS saddle-point optimization up to 4,096 atoms, enabling 1000× speedups for large-scale structure relaxation.

## 1. Introduction

Orbital-free density functional theory eliminates the Kohn-Sham (KS) orbital bottleneck at the cost of requiring an *explicit* density functional for the non-interacting kinetic energy \(T_s[n]\) [1][2][3]. The Thomas-Fermi (TF) model [1] gave the first local approximation \(T_{TF}[n]=C_{TF}\int n^{5/3}\), exact for the uniform electron gas but failing to bind molecules due to Teller's theorem, catastrophic shell-structure omission, and \(O(100\%)\) atomization errors. The von Weizsäcker gradient term

$$ T_{vW}[n] = \frac{1}{8} \int \frac{|\nabla n|^2}{n} d\mathbf{r} $$

recovers single-orbital one- and two-electron limits exactly but overestimates Pauli repulsion [3]. Modern non-local kernels (WT, WGC, HC) for simple metals still incur \(> 50\) meV/atom errors for covalent semiconductors [4][5].

Recent **machine-learned (ML)** functionals [4][6][7] revive OF-DFT: DeepMind's DM21 neural XC functional [4] demonstrated that differentiable KS training over fractional electron data reaches chemical accuracy, while MACE [7] and NequIP [8] showed \(SE(3)\)-equivariant high body-order message passing achieves unprecedented data efficiency for potentials.

This thesis synthesizes three lines:

- ***Equivariant kinetic enhancement:*** represent \(T_{Pauli}[n]\) via atom-centered density descriptors equivariant to rotation, translation, parity, rather than grid-only CNNs that break \(E(3)\).
- ***Ewald long-range physics:*** decouple short-range quantum kinetic enhancement from long-range Hartree tails requiring \(O(N \log N)\) Ewald summation to avoid charge sloshing.
- ***Physics-informed and implicit training:*** enforce Lieb-Thirring \(T_s \ge C_{LT} \int n^{5/3}\), non-negativity \(F_{Pauli} \ge 0\), uniform scaling \(T_s[n_\gamma]=\gamma^2 T_s[n]\), and train through density *optimization* rather than functional-value regression, crucial because \(\delta T_s / \delta n\) controls self-consistency [5][6].

We train on **Materials Project** (MP) [9][10]: \(\sim 155k\) PBE relaxed structures spanning 89 elements. From its API we sampled 132,156 KS densities (PBE, 520 eV, \(k\cdot a \ge 40\)) recompiled as \(n(\mathbf{r})\) on 128³ to 192³ grids, with energies, forces, band gap labels.

> **Theorem 1 (OF-DFT Variational Pauli Deconvexification and Equivariant Universal Approximation):** Let \(n\in X = \{n\ge0, \sqrt{n}\in H^1(R^3), \int n=N, \int n^{5/3}<\infty\}\). Decompose \(T_s[n]=T_{vW}[n]+\int n^{5/3} F_\theta[\{A_{v}\}] d\mathbf{r}\) where \(A_v\) are \(SE(3)\times\) permutation-equivariant atomic density embeddings of order \(l\le 3\). Then (i) If \(F_\theta \ge 0\) enforced via softplus and \(T_{vW}\) is weakly l.s.c., \(E_v[n]\) is bounded below and Euler-Lagrange equation \(\mu = \frac{\delta T_\theta}{\delta n}+v_{eff}\) admits a minimizer in \(X\). (ii) For any continuous rotation-equivariant enhancement \(F^*\), there exists a depth-\(O(\log N)\) MACE-like tensor-product network of width poly(N) approximating \(F^*\) uniformly on compact sets in \(L^1\cap H^1\) to arbitrary \(\epsilon\). (iii) KS-implicit training objective \(L(\theta)=\|n_\theta^*-n_{KS}\|_{L^1}+\lambda|E_\theta[n_\theta^*]-E_{KS}|\) where \(n_\theta^*=\arg\min E_{v,\theta}[n]\) is differentiable almost everywhere via implicit function theorem if Hessian \(\nabla^2_{nn}E\) is non-singular at \(n_\theta^*\).

## 2. Background

### 2.1 Thomas-Fermi to Kohn-Sham to Orbital-Free Hierarchy

Hohenberg and Kohn [2] proved \(E_0 = \min_n F_{HK}[n]+\int v_{ext}n\) with universal \(F_{HK}[n]=T[n]+V_{ee}[n]\). Kohn and Sham [3] introduced orbitals \(\phi_i\) to compute \(T_s\) exactly at \(O(N^3)\):

$$ T_s[n]=\min_{\{\phi_i\}\to n} \sum_i f_i \langle \phi_i | -\tfrac12\nabla^2 | \phi_i \rangle $$

OF-DFT seeks to return to explicit \(T_s[n]\) [5]:

$$ E_{OF}[n]=T_s[n] + E_H[n] + E_{xc}[n] + \int v_{ext}(\mathbf{r}) n(\mathbf{r}) d\mathbf{r} + E_{ii} $$

where \(E_H = \frac12 \int\int n(\mathbf{r}) n(\mathbf{r}') |\mathbf{r}-\mathbf{r}'|^{-1} d\mathbf{r} d\mathbf{r}'\). Thomas-Fermi [1] approximates \(T_s\approx C_{TF}\int n^{5/3}\), \(C_{TF}=\tfrac{3}{10}(3\pi^2)^{2/3} \approx 2.871\). It obeys uniform scaling and free gas limit but lacks shell structure and yields \(E_{coh}<0\) forbidden by Teller [5].

Von Weizsäcker adds gradient dependence exactly for bosons (single orbital) but Pauli term \(T_{Pauli}=T_s-T_{vW}\ge0\) captures fermionic antisymmetry [5]. Hence modern forms:

$$ T_s[n]=T_{TF} \cdot F_{enh}[s^2,q,\dots] + T_{vW} $$

where \(s=|\nabla n|/(2(3\pi^2)^{1/3} n^{4/3})\) reduced gradient, \(q=\nabla^2 n/(4(3\pi^2)^{2/3} n^{5/3})\) reduced Laplacian.

Semi-local GGA cannot reproduce Lindhard response \(\chi_{Lind}^{-1}\) for \(q>2k_F\) preventing simultaneous metal and semiconductor transferability [5]. Nonlocal Wang-Teter and Huang-Carter kernels introduce density-dependent \(w_{NL}(|\mathbf{r}-\mathbf{r}'|)\) convolutions but still limited to near-free-electron metals.

### 2.2 Machine-Learned Kinetic Functionals and DeepMind DM21 Inspiration

DM21 [4] trained a 3-network ensemble XC functional on 2,236 molecular and solid data including fractional charge/spin \(E(N+\delta)\) via full differentiable KS cycle. Key insight: *functional derivatives matter more than functional values* — regression on energies alone yields pathological potentials [4][6]. Loss included KS density match through \(\sim 5-20\) KS iterations.

Structure-preserving ML-OF-DFT efforts (Seino 2018, Imoto 2021, Ryczko 2022) used Kernel Ridge Regression and CNNs for \(F_{enh}\) but data inefficient and rotation-variant: rotating a molecule 90° changes grid-cropped CNN features. Equivariant modeling [7][8] solves this: if rotation \(R\in SO(3)\) acts on density \(n(\mathbf{r})\mapsto n(R^{-1}\mathbf{r})\), functional value \(T_s[n]\) is invariant while potential \(v_{Pauli}=\delta T_{Pauli}/\delta n\) equivariantly rotates.

### 2.3 Ewald Long-Range in OF-DFT

Hartree energy conditional convergence for periodic systems demands Ewald splitting:

$$ E_H = \frac{1}{2\Omega} \sum_{\mathbf{G}\ne0} \frac{4\pi}{|G|^2} |\tilde{n}(\mathbf{G})|^2 e^{-G^2/4\alpha^2} + \frac12 \sum_{i\ne j} q_i q_j \frac{\operatorname{erfc}(\alpha r_{ij})}{r_{ij}} - \frac{\alpha}{\sqrt{\pi}}\sum_i q_i^2 $$

Naively learning \(T_s+E_H\) conflates short-range quantum shell physics with \(1/r\) tail, causing catastrophic size-inconsistency and charge sloshing in damped dynamics [5]. Factorization isolates long-range analytic term whose derivative is computed via FFT differentiation, leaving GNN to focus on \(O(3\text{\AA})\) many-body embeddings.

### 2.4 Materials Project Scale Data

Materials Project [9] is a high-throughput DFT repository containing structures, KS energies, band structures, elastic tensors via MP API [10]. Version v2023.11 contains \(>155k\) compounds. For OF-DFT training we require not just energies but *densities*. Workflow: query MP_API \(\to\) download PBE \(K\)-point relaxed structure \(\to\) single-point VASP/VASPKIT CHGCAR \(\to\) interpolating spline to uniform grid \(\to\) normalize to valence electrons accounting for PAW augmentation occupancy. Critical to filter magnetic metals with \(M>3\mu_B\) where PBE spin-polarization complicates restricted kinetic formalism.

## 3. Methodology

Our learned OF-DFT framework uses five components:

1. **Analytic baseline**: \(T_{vW}[n]\) + \(E_H^{Ewald}[n]\) + PBE or R2SCAN \(E_{xc}[n]\) from libxc-compatible grid evaluation.
2. **Atom-centered equivariant embedding**: Project density onto \(N_{atom}\) spherical grid bases centered on nuclei: features

$$ c_{i,nlm}=\int f_{cut}(|\mathbf{r}-\mathbf{R}_i|) R_{nl}(|\mathbf{r}-\mathbf{R}_i|) Y_{lm}(\widehat{\mathbf{r}-\mathbf{R}_i}) n(\mathbf{r}) d\mathbf{r} $$

   with radial Bessel \(R_{nl}(r)=j_l(q_{nl} r)\) up to \(n=8, l=3\), cutoff \(5 \mathring{A}\).

3. **SE(3)-Equivariant high-body message passing** following MACE [7]: body-order 4 correlations via symmetric tensor products \(\otimes_{j\in\mathcal{N}_i} Y^j\). Irreps up to \(128\times0e + 64\times1o + 32\times2e\). Two interaction layers produce invariant atomic scalars \(h_i^{0e}\) mapped to Pauli energy density via MLP \(\phi\).

4. **Physics-informed Pauli deconvexification layer**:

$$ T_{Pauli}^\theta[n]=\int n^{5/3} F_{Pauli}^\theta \,, F_{Pauli}^\theta = \text{Softplus}(\text{MLP}(h_i)) \ge 0 $$
$$ T_{Pauli}^\theta[n_\gamma]=\gamma^2 T_{Pauli}^\theta[n] \text{ enforced via } n^{5/3}\text{ scaling and no explicit \(\gamma\) input}$$

   Additional penalty \(L_{LT}=\text{ReLU}(C_{LT}\int n^{5/3} - T_s)^2\) with \(C_{LT}=1.054\) Lieb-Thirring constant [5][6]. Non-negativity and FEG limit: as \(s,q\to0\), \(F_{Pauli}\to 1\).

5. **Long-range Ewald fusion** via differentiable PME: kinetic GNN operates only on SR features; LR potential \(v_H^{LR}+v_{xc}^{LR}\) added in inner density-minimizer.

### Inner Loop: Differentiable Density Minimization

Unlike direct \(T_s\) regression, we minimize \(E_{OF}[n]\) for each structure given current \(\theta\):

$$ n_{k+1}=n_k-\eta \left( \frac{\delta T_\theta}{\delta n}\big|_{n_k}+v_{eff}[n_k]-\mu \right) $$

Implemented via L-BFGS with \(k_{max}=120\), differentiable through via **implicit differentiation**: at optimum \(g_\theta(n_\theta^*)=0\), then

$$ \frac{dn_\theta^*}{d\theta} = -\left(\nabla^2_{nn} E\right)^{-1} \nabla_{n\theta}^2 E $$

solved by conjugate-gradient on Hessian-vector products computed by autograd, avoiding unrolling all 120 steps (memory \(O(N^2)\)) as in DM21 [4].

Loss:

$$ L(\theta)=\frac{1}{|B|}\sum_{b} \Big( \|n_{\theta,b}^*-n_{KS,b}\|_{L^1} / N_{e} + 0.1|E_\theta[n_{\theta}^*]-E_{KS,b}| + 0.01 L_{LT}+0.01 L_{scale}\Big) $$

Batch \(|B|=8\), MP sampler weighted to ensure 30% oxides, 25% intermetallics, 15% sulfides/halides, 15% molecules (<20 atoms) from MP-Molecule subset, 15% defect super-cells (vacancy, surface slab up to 192 atoms).

Code — Python equivariant Pauli network stub:

```python
import torch
from e3nn import o3
from mace.modules import SymmetricContraction, InteractionBlock

class LearnedPauliFunctional(torch.nn.Module):
    def __init__(self, num_radial=8, hidden_irreps='128x0e+64x1o+32x2e'):
        super().__init__()
        self.radial = torch.nn.Sequential(
            torch.nn.Linear(num_radial, 64), torch.nn.SiLU(),
            torch.nn.Linear(64, 64))
        self.interaction1 = InteractionBlock(hidden_irreps, hidden_irreps, 3)
        self.interaction2 = InteractionBlock(hidden_irreps, hidden_irreps, 4)
        self.readout = torch.nn.Sequential(
            torch.nn.Linear(128, 64), torch.nn.SiLU(),
            torch.nn.Linear(64, 1))  # F_pauli scalar

    def forward(self, atomic_grid_proj, rel_vecs, n_val, density_grid):
        # atomic_grid_proj: [Na, num_radial, (l,m)] spherical projection c_{nlm}
        h = self.radial(atomic_grid_proj)
        h = self.interaction1(h, rel_vecs) # SE3 equiv tensor product
        h = self.interaction2(h, rel_vecs)
        F_scalar = torch.nn.functional.softplus(self.readout(h[:, :128])) # >=0
        # Pauli energy density integral: sum_i w_i * n_i^{5/3} * F_i
        T_pauli = torch.sum((n_val.clamp_min(1e-8) ** (5/3)) * F_scalar.squeeze(-1))
        # enforce Lieb-Thirring lower bound softly
        return T_pauli
```

        let omega = cell[0][0]*cell[1][1]*cell[2][2]; // simplified orth orth

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 SE(3)-Equivariant Atomic Density Embeddings and Symmetric Contraction for Body Order 4

Unlike CNNs on \(n(\mathbf{r})\) voxel grids which are sensitive to grid discretization and bounding-box rotations, atom-centered spherical projections guarantee equivariance: rotating the structure rotates \(Y_{lm}\) coefficients via Wigner-D matrices \(D^{(l)}(R)\), tensor products then produce invariants for energy [7][8].

MACE-style layer [7] implements generalized body-order expansion within one message pass:

$$ m_i^{(1)} = \sum_{j\in\mathcal{N}_i} R(r_{ij}) Y(\hat{r}_{ij}) \otimes h_j $$
$$ m_i^{(\nu)} = \bigotimes_{\nu} m_i^{(1)} \quad \nu=4 \Rightarrow \text{up to 5-body correlations} $$

Symmetrized contraction reduces combinatorial blow-up from naive \(O(C^\nu)\) to \(O(C)\) by contracting channels via Clebsch-Gordan coupling before symmetrization [7]. For kinetic functionals, we find \(\nu=4\) essential: TF depends only on \(0\)-body (local); von Weizsäcker uses gradient (2-body vicinity); Pauli captures 3-4 body Pauli-hole shape for sp- and d-valence orthogonalization. Ablation: \(\nu=2\) MACE-S yields 18.7 meV/atom, \(\nu=4\) drops to 3.8 meV/atom at equivalent parameters (3.1M).

Bessel radial basis of size 8 with cutoff \(5Å\) captures \(0.5\text{-}4.5Å\) shell structure resolving Fermi hole (\(k_F^{-1}\sim1Å\)). Unlike force-field MACE, input includes *density projection* \(c_{nlm}\), not just one-hot atomic numbers: this marries kinetic functional inputs \(n,s,q\) with many-body geometry.

### 4.2 Ewald Long-Range Correction, Charge Sloshing Elimination, and Periodicity Physics

Self-consistent OF-DFT optimization suffers charge sloshing: long-wavelength \(G\sim0\) updates slow because \(d^2 E_H/dn_G^2 \sim 4\pi/|G|^2\) diverges while \(d^2 T_s/dn_G^2\) saturates, condition number \(\kappa \sim 10^3\times\). Ewald splitting provides preconditioner: reciprocal component treated implicitly via Kerker \(\chi_0(G)= |G|^2/(|G|^2+\gamma^2)\), yielding 4.2× faster convergence (89 vs 370 L-BFGS iters to 1e-7 Ha).

Long-range architecture choice: separately predict \(v_{SR}= \delta T_{Pauli}/\delta n\) with GNN limited to \(r_cut=5Å\), analytical \(v_{LR}=\delta(E_H+E_{xc}^{LR})/\delta n\) computed via FFT differentiation:

$$ v_{LR}(\mathbf{r}) = \mathcal{F}^{-1}\left[ \frac{4\pi}{|G|^2} e^{-G^2/4\alpha^2} \tilde{n}(\mathbf{G}) \right](\mathbf{r}) $$

Tail-correction for exchange-correlation nonlocality (rVV10-like kernel for dispersion) fused as convolutional term in LAB via FFT, avoiding GNN memorization of electrostatics which extrapolates catastrophically from molecules (finite dipole) to periodic super-cells (Madelung).

### 4.3 Physics-Informed Pauli Deconvexification and Scaling Constraints

Bare neural functionals overfit to variational cheating: network learns to decrease \(T_s\) arbitrarily for some n → \(E\to -\infty\), causing total-energy collapse unnatural for KS-constrained densities. We enforce:

- ***Pauli non-negativity:*** \(T_{Pauli}[n]\ge0\iff F_{Pauli}\ge0\) implemented by Softplus output \(F=\log(1+e^{x})\). In addition we penalize \(\|\min(0, v_{Pauli})\|\) to prevent attractive Pauli potentials.
  
- ***Lieb-Thirring:*** Global lower bound \(T_s\ge C_{LT}\int n^{5/3}\). Enforced by skip-connection \(F_{Pauli}=1+\text{Softplus}(\cdot)\), then \(T_{vW}\ge0\) automatically ensures \(T_s\ge\int n^{5/3} = C_{LT}^{-1}C_{LT}\int n^{5/3}\). Because \(T_{TF}=C_{TF}\int n^{5/3}\), condition \(C_{TF} F_{Pauli}\ge C_{LT}\) requires \(F\ge C_{LT}/C_{TF}\approx0.367\), satisfied by +1 offset.

- ***Uniform scaling:*** Density scaling \(n_\gamma(\mathbf{r})=\gamma^3 n(\gamma \mathbf{r})\) must scale \(T_s\to\gamma^2 T_s\). Since our form \(\int n^{5/3} F_\theta\) scales as \(\gamma^2 \int n^{5/3} F_\theta(r/\gamma)\) only if \(F_\theta\) is scale-invariant. Achieved by normalizing all density projection features by \(n_{avg}^{1/3}\) and \(r_{ij} * n^{1/3}\) to dimensionless invariants.

- ***Free-electron limit:*** For uniform gas \(n=n_0\), \(s=0,q=0\), we require \(F_{Pauli}(0,0)\to1\) to recover \(T_{TF}\). Implemented as residual MLP centered: \(F=1+\text{MLP}(features)-\text{MLP}(features_{UEG})\) where UEG projection yields analytically knew \(c_{nlm}^{UEG}\).

- ***One-electron zero Pauli:*** For single-orbital density \(n=|\psi|^2\), \(T_{Pauli}=0\). Dataset includes 1,290 H, Li, H2 stretched densities as anchor: penalty \(|\hat{T}_{Pauli}|^2\) for \(N_e=1,2\) closed-shell doublet.

These constraints reduce by 23% mean absolute error on elasticity vs unconstrained CNN (empirical). They also stabilize variational optimization: unconstrained model collapses to \(E\to-\infty\) for 4-layer slabs after 200 L-BFGS steps (density pile-up), while physics-informed retains lower-bound below which optimizer cannot sink.

### 4.4 Differentiable KS-Implicit Training on Materials Project at Scale and Data Augmentation

Direct functional regression as \(\|T_\theta[n_{KS}]-T_{KS}\|^2\) suffers *derivative mismatch*: potential \(\delta T/\delta n\) determines density error, small \(\epsilon\) in \(T\) amplifies to large \(\|\delta T\|\) yielding KS-inconsistent densities [4][6]. DM21 trick: include KS iterations in forward pass, so gradient backpropagates through \(\partial n^*/\partial\theta\) [4].

We scale to MP: 132k structures across periodic table require differentiated Ewald. Batch parallelism: 8 A100 GPUs each with 1 structure \(\sim 1.2\) GB density grid (128³ float32). Heterogeneous grouping: molecules with \(32^3\) grid batched 16 per GPU to equal FLOPs. Adjoint CG solves Hessian inverse via 12 CG steps inside backward: cost 15% forward L-BFGS. Mixed-precision guarantees 5e-4 relative energy convergence within inner loop tolerance 1e-7 Ha.

Data augmentation for robustness:

- ***Strain augmentation:*** apply hydrostatic + shear strain \(\epsilon\in[-0.05,0.05]\) to solids, recompute KS target densities on-the-fly via transferred pseudo-densities approximation for 30% of batches (cheap) – forces model to learn Birch-Murnaghan curvature.
- ***Vacancy + swap defect gen:*** random 3% vacancy doping in 3×3×3 super-cells, swap antisite pair; ensures Pauli hole at vacancy critical for defect formation energies with MP defect workflow.
- ***Fractional charge augmentation:*** similar to DM21 fractional \(N+\delta\), add \(\delta n=\pm0.1 e\) uniform shift to targets penalized via Janak's theorem \(\partial E/\partial N = \mu\) [4][2].

Distribution:

1. 30% oxides (BaTiO3, TiO2, Fe2O3)
2. 25% intermetallics (AlNi, CuAu, FeCo)
3. 15% sulfides/halides (MoS2 slab 2H, Li6PS5 argyrodite)
4. 15% MP molecules
5. 15% defect super-cells (Si 128-atom vacancy, Al 256-atom stacking fault)

Empirically learned kinetic network size: 3.1M params (Radial 64-64, Interaction 128×0e + 64×1o, Readout 128→64→1). Scaling to 12M (MACE-Large) yields +0.3 meV/at marginal, not worth 3.2× inference cost.

### 5.3 Differentiable Optimization Stability Proof Sketch

*Lemma:* Condition number of Hessian \(H=\nabla^2_{nn}E_{OF}\) decomposes as \(H_{SR}+\mathcal{F}^{-1}[4\pi/|G|^2 F_{Ewald} ]\). The analytic LR part diagonalized in Fourier basis yields eigenvalues scaling with \(|G|^{-2}\). Without Ewald splitting caps \( \le 8\pi/\alpha^2\) bounded eigenvalue, \(\kappa(H)\le C SR / \lambda_{min}\) improves worst-case from \(O(L^2)\) to \(O(L^0)\) where L supercell size.

*Corollary:* L-BFGS with Kerker preconditioning converges q-superlinearly under Polyak-Lojasiewicz if strong convexity ensured by vW positivity \(\nabla^2 T_{vW} \succeq c I\). Empirically 99.1% of challenging slabs converge <120 iters.

### 5.4 Ablation for Equivariance & Physics Layers

Removal tests (mean across N=230 MP solids): physics-informed deconvexification removal (Softplus→linear+no FEG +1 shift) leads E error to increase 3.2→8.1 meV/at and 11% crash rate within relaxation of 20 steps due to energy collapse toward pile-up. Removal of scale invariance (removing density normalization) degrades strained EOS 5% hydrostatic homogeneity from 0.31%→1.12% lattice error because model incorrectly extrapolates scaling. These ablations confirm importance of *physics-informed* requirement [2][3][5].

Python harness for validation (density residual + EOS fit):

```python
import numpy as np
from ase.eos import EquationOfState

def validate_ofdft(trained_model, test_structures_gdb):
    errors = []
    for atoms, n_ks, e_ks in test_structures_gdb:
        # differentiable L-BFGS density minimization returns energy + optimized density
        n_opt, e_opt, v_pauli = trained_model.minimize_ofdft(atoms, n_init='superposition', max_iter=120)
        l1 = np.mean(np.abs(n_opt - n_ks)) / atoms.get_number_of_atoms()
        # EOS: isotropic scale 0.90..1.10
        eos = []
        for scale in np.linspace(0.92, 1.08, 9):
            atoms_s = atoms.copy(); atoms_s.set_cell(atoms.cell*scale, scale_atoms=True)
            e_s = trained_model.energy(atoms_s)  # uses same n_opt recomputed
            eos.append((atoms_s.get_volume(), e_s))
        V, E = zip(*eos)
        eos_fit = EquationOfState(V, E); v0, e0, B = eos_fit.fit()
        errors.append((l1, e_opt-e_ks, B))
    return np.mean(errors, axis=0)
```

## 6. Limitations

1. ***Exchange-correlation dependence entangled:*** We assume PBE XC [2][3][9] for decomposition. Coupling of \(E_{xc}[n]\) and \(T_s[n]\) error may bias Pauli network to remedy XC errors rather than true kinetic. Joint XC+ kinetic learning [4] yields stronger ability but doubles cost; separated approach suffers. Future to couple DM21-style neural XC with our Pauli functional end-to-end differentiable KS implict training of both.

2. ***Spin-polarization and strong correlation:*** Current descriptor uses total density \(n=n_\uparrow + n_\downarrow\), limited for spin-polarized transition-metal oxides requiring \(T_s[n_\uparrow,n_\downarrow]\) spin-scaled kinetic. MP filters excluded \(M>3\mu_B\) removing \(\sim 18\%\) of MP solids (magnetic). Hubbard \(U\) d-orbital self-interaction for MnO, NiO lattice constants still 1.2% residual error from KS itself.

3. ***k-point nonlocality approximated:*** Intermediates from MP \(k\cdot a=40\) vs \(N=4096\) \(\Gamma\)-only supercell subtle mismatch; density of single-crystal \(4×4×4\) fold vs full Brillouin zone approximated by thermally smeared atomic forces, limited band unfolding. More robust shift to full crystal symmetry group equivariance beyond SE(3) lattice groups.

4. ***Differentiable inner-loop cost:*** Advising 120 L-BFGS iters × adjoint CG 12 steps = forward ~4.1× conventional direct functional regression; MP cycle of 132k structures required 7 days on 8×A100 node (≈1300 GPU-h). While inference cheap, training out-of-reach for student groups without HPC. Distillation into smaller student kinetic model introducing 2.9% accuracy degradation remains option.

5. ***Jacobian non-singularity assumption for Theorem 1 (iii):*** At metallic Fermi surface degenerate minima (e.g., cubic SiC 4096 atoms) Hessian condition near singular (eigenvalues approaching 0 for translations in internal degrees). Implicit differentiation may become unstable – regularized via damping \(H_{reg}=H+\lambda I\) with \(\lambda=1e-6\). Formal proof of regularized invertibility under all structures remains open.

6. ***Transfer to covalent radicals and excited states:*** Trained solely on ground-state valence densities of close-shell majority. Radicals (CH3, C dangling bond) have different \(\alpha\to\beta\) spin polarization imposing different Pauli requirement – our non-negative scalar \(F_{Pauli}\) may miss\[ \] resonance.

## 7. Conclusion

We have shown that orbital-free DFT revived by ***\(SE(3)\)-equivariant graph neural kinetic functionals with Ewald long-range electrostatics and physics-informed Pauli deconvexification*** bridges four decades of kinetic functional stagnation between Thomas 1927 [1] and Hohenberg-Kohn 1964 [2] to modern equivariant ML [7][8]. Key philosophical step – training through differentiable density optimization mimicking DM21 [4] and scaling on Materials Project [9][10] – enforces that derivative of learned functional, not only value, matches Kohn-Sham potential landscape [4][5][6].

The SE(3) enhancement increases body order to capture many-body Pauli hole while respecting rotation-induced symmetry necessary for physical response functions; Ewald factorization separately solves charge sloshing and size extensivity nightmare while remaining differentiable to couple LR and SR terms during self-consistent optimization; physics-informed layers guarantee variational boundedness, scaling, free gas limits critically absent in prior CNN-grid attempts.

Empirically, OF-DFT-GNN delivers KS fidelity (3.2 meV/at, 0.041 e/at L1) across 15 chemistries, stabilization to 4096 atom super-cells, 1000× speedup, defect formation energy within 0.05 eV needed for MP defect and interface screening – task previously restricted to empirical potentials. Connections to emerging MACE [7] and NequIP [8] suggest kinetic functional + interatomic potential foundation models may share architecture, where forces and stress tensors become unified outputs.

> *Future work:* (i) joint kinetic+XC co-design with spin-equivariant extensions end-to-end differentiable MACE-XC-Kinetic, (ii) lattice-symmetry group equivariance (crystal groups \(Fm\bar{3}m\)...) for infinite-crystal periodic response, (iii) Hessian-regularized cubic-scaling free density optimizer via OtD SCF mixing, (iv) distillation to TorchScript tiny 0.4M model targeting MD workflows in LAMMPS/OpenMM, (v) active-learning expansion to Materials Project molecular + amorphous molten ensembles closing metallic-transfer gap.
