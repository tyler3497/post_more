# On the Evoformer as a Learnable Funnel: Triangular Attention, Invariant Point Reasoning, and the Energy Landscape Reconciliation in AlphaFold2 Protein Structure Prediction

## Abstract

AlphaFold2 solved single-chain protein structure prediction to near-experimental accuracy by replacing physics-based energy minimization with a learnable attentional system that implicitly encodes the funneled energy landscape hypothesized by Anfinsen and formalized by Bryngelson and Wolynes. This thesis dissects the Evoformer’s dual-track architecture—MSA row/column attention and pair representation triangular multiplication and self-attention—and its coupling to the Invariant Point Attention (IPA) structure module. We formalize how triangle updates enforce geometric consistency analogous to the triangle inequality in distance geometry, how outer-product mean and recycling implement iterative refinement resembling energy minimization, and how pLDDT and PAE emerge as learned proxies for conformational entropy. Spanning Levinthal’s paradox, funnel theory, and DeepMind’s 2021 Nature implementation [1], we provide full mathematical formalism, reference implementations, and critical limits for orphan proteins, conformational ensembles, and intrinsically disordered regions, arguing that attention is not merely correlation but a differentiable surrogate for Boltzmann-weighted ensemble averaging.

## 1. Introduction

> **Key Insight:** *AlphaFold2 does not fold proteins by simulating physics; it learns to approximate the mapping from co-evolutionary constraints to a distribution over frames whose gradient flow mimics funnel dynamics, implemented via attention.*

Protein folding has been approached as both a thermodynamic and computational problem. Anfinsen’s dogma [1973] postulated that the native state minimizes Gibbs free energy under physiological conditions [6]. Levinthal [1969] observed the paradox: random sampling of $\phi/\psi$ angles ($\sim 10^{100}$ conformers for a 100-residue chain) cannot be traversed in milliseconds [7][0]. Resolution required **energy landscape theory**: a minimally frustrated, funneled landscape biases search toward $H(s) = E(s) - T S_{conf}(s)$ [1][4].

Rosetta/I-TASSER reached $\sim 4$Å at CASP12; AF2 reached median GDT_TS 92.4 on hardest FM at CASP14 [2][3].

**Contributions:**

1. **Mechanistic Formalization** — We recast Evoformer operations as differentiable message passing over $SE(3)$-invariant pair embeddings, revealing tight isomorphism to distance geometry and belief propagation on Markov Random Fields.
2. **Funnel Correspondence** — We map recycling iterations $z^{(r+1)} = \text{Evoformer}(z^{(r)}, m^{(r)})$ to gradient descent on a learned potential $U_\theta(z)$, and show Invariant Point Attention solves a Procrustes-aligned coordinate regression akin to AlphaFold’s FrameAligned Point Error (FAPE).
3. **Empirical Criticality** — We synthesize subsampling results [6], open-source re-implementations [ColabFold, OpenFold], and limitations for dynamics, complexes with DNA/RNA, and ligand binding, post-AlphaFold3 / RoseTTAFoldNA developments [5].

We target graduate-level readers: assumed background includes transformer attention [Vaswani 2017], $SE(3)$ equivariance, and basic statistical mechanics of polymers.

## 2. Background / Preliminaries

### 2.1 Thermodynamic Foundations

> **Definition 2.1 (Anfinsen Hypothesis):** The native conformation $s^*$ of protein $P$ under conditions $(T,pH,[\text{salt}])$ satisfies $G(s^*) = \min_{s \in \Omega} G(s)$, where $G = H - T S$, and refolding can occur *in vitro* without chaperone assistance for small globular proteins [e.g., RNase A].

> **Definition 2.2 (Energy Funnel):** A landscape $F(Q) = -kT \ln P(Q)$ parameterized by reaction coordinate $Q$ (fraction native contacts) is funneled if $\langle \Delta F \rangle$ decreases monotonically with $Q$ and roughness $\Delta F_{rms} \ll \delta E \cdot Q$ where $\delta E$ is stability gap, per random-energy-model analysis of Bryngelson-Wolynes [1].

**Levinthal estimate $3^{100}\approx5\times10^{47}$ vs $10^{-3}$s folding requires hierarchy reducing $O(e^n)$ to $O(n^2)$ [7].

**Table 1: Classical Taxonomy of Folding Models vs AlphaFold2**

| Model | Search Signal | Complexity | pLDDT Proxy | Failure Mode |
|-------|--------------|------------|-------------|--------------|
| Levinthal random | none | $O(3^n)$ | — | Never folds |
| Framework | Secondary first | $O(n \log n)$ | Helix propensity | Misses knotted proteins |
| Nucleation-Condensation | Contact order bias | $O(n^2)$ | Contact prediction | Over-favors local |
| Funnel/R rugged | Biased Monte Carlo | $O(e^{\Delta^{\ddagger}/kT})$ | $F = -kT \ln P$ | Trapped in intermediates |
| AlphaFold2 | Learned $P_{evol}(z|MSA)$ + geometry | $O(N^2 c_z + N_{seq} N_{res} c_m)$ per block | pLDDT $= E[d_{LDDT}]$ | Overconfident on disorder |

### 2.2 Evolutionary Coupling Precursor

Before AF2, methods like DCA, EVfold, and trRosetta used Potts models to infer contacts from Multiple Sequence Alignment (MSA) covariation: strong mutual information $MI(i,j)$ suggested spatial proximity [Castro et al., 3]. However, sparsity of MSAs and chaining effects limited precision.

AlphaFold2’s innovation: treat MSA not as static covariance matrix, but as *attentional memory* refined jointly with pair representation.

> **Theorem 2.3 (Triangle Inequality as Inductive Bias):** For any embedding $z_{ij}$ representing $P(d_{ij}|MSA)$, consistency requires $d_{ij} \le d_{ik}+ d_{kj}$ for all $k$. Triangle multiplicative updates and triangle self-attention are sufficient to enforce this in expectation.

*Proof Sketch.* Consider pair representation $z_{ij} \in \mathbb{R}^{c_z}$. Outgoing layer: $z_{ij} \leftarrow z_{ij} + \sigma(g_{ij}) \odot \sum_k (a_{ik} \odot b_{kj})$ where $a_{ik}=W_a z_{ik}$, $b_{kj}=W_b z_{kj}$. This is exactly a learnable relaxation of the min-plus matrix product enforcing transitivity. In limit of large gating, fixed point satisfies $z_{ij} = \text{AGG}_k f(z_{ik}, z_{kj})$, approximating closure under triangle inequality. Self-attention over $k$ with bias $b_{ij}$ yields soft max implementation of same constraint, with learned weighting. Formal proof in Jumper et al Suppl. Sec 7 [1][2] and referenced analysis of Pairformer ablations [0][6].

## 3. Methodology / Formalism

We adopt notation from Jumper et al 2021 [1] and OpenFold reimplementation.

### 3.1 Input Featurization

Given sequence $s \in \{20\}^N$, AF2 builds MSA $M \in \{21\}^{N_{seq} \times N_{res}}$ ($N_{seq} \sim 512$ clusters) using MMseqs2 / HHblits against UniRef90, BFD, MGnify [1][2]. Templates $T$ from PDB70 provide distances $d_{temp}$.

Featurized:

- MSA representation: $m_{si} \in \mathbb{R}^{c_m}, c_m=256$
- Pair representation: $z_{ij} \in \mathbb{R}^{c_z}, c_z=128$, initialized via outer product of $m$ plus template + relative positional encoding $r_{ij}= \text{clip}(i-j)$.

$$
 z_{ij}^{0} = \text{Linear}( [ m^{1}_{i} \otimes m^{1}_{j} ; r_{ij}; t_{ij} ] )
$$

### 3.2 Evoformer Block – 48 Stacked Iterations

Each block computes:

#### 3.2.1 MSA Stack

- **Row-wise gated self-attention:** attention over residues $j$ for fixed sequence $s$, biased by pair $z$.
  $$
  \text{Attn}_{row}(m_{s,i}) = \sum_j \text{softmax}_j\left( \frac{q_{si}^T k_{sj}}{\sqrt{d}} + b_{ij}\right) v_{sj}, \quad b_{ij}= \text{Linear}(z_{ij})
  $$
  Interpretation: *within-sequence long-range dependency*, analogous to contact enhancement.

- **Column-wise gated self-attention:** attention over sequences $s$ for fixed residue $i$.
  Captures **co-evolution**: residues that co-vary across homologs signal contact.

- Transition MLP: $m \leftarrow m + \text{MLP}(\text{LN}(m))$ with $c_m$ expansion $4\times$.

#### 3.2.2 Communication: Outer Product Mean

Translate MSA evolution to pair:
$$
 o_{ij} = \frac{1}{N_{seq}} \sum_s \text{Linear}(m_{si}) \otimes \text{Linear}(m_{sj}) \in \mathbb{R}^{c_z}
$$
This is $O(N^2 c^2)$ but captures mean co-evolutionary coupling, replacing explicit Potts $J_{ij}$.

#### 3.2.3 Pair Stack – The Geometric Reasoner

This is core to our thesis: pair track is $SE(3)$-invariant latent graph.

1. **Triangle Multiplicative Update (outgoing):**
$$
z_{ij} \leftarrow z_{ij} + \sigma(l_{ij}) \odot \text{Linear}\left( \sum_k a_{ik} \odot b_{kj} \right), \quad a_{ik}=\sigma(g^L) \odot W_L z_{ik}$$
Complexity $O(N^3 c)$ but implemented with $c_{inter}=128$ intermediate bottleneck and chunking; open source analysis shows $N=384$ max on 16GB V100 [analysis in 0][5].

2. **Triangle Multiplicative Update (incoming):** symmetric $z_{ij} \leftarrow f(z_{ki}, z_{kj})$.

3. **Triangle Self-Attention:** Starting node vs ending node alternation. Attention logit $a_{ik} = q_{ij}^T k_{kj} /\sqrt{d} + b'_{kj}$ where $b'$ from $z$. Enforces that if $i-k$ and $k-j$ close, $i-j$ should be informed. Formally equivalent to attention over 3-cliques, approximating path-consistency in constraint satisfaction.

> **Theorem 3.1 (Pairformer Ablation):** Removing triangle attention degrades TM-score from 0.85 to 0.78 on CASP14; removing triangle multiplication collapses to 0.62, while removing both MSAs collapses to single-sequence language-model baseline 0.45 [reanalysis in [0] and Ruff et al].

LayerNorm and dropout ($p=0.1$ train, 0 at inference) stabilize 48-block depth; residual scaling $\sim 1/\sqrt{L}$ critical.

### 3.3 Structure Module – IPA and FAPE

Single representation $s_i = \text{Linear}(m_{1,i})$ (first row = query sequence). Structure module predicts $N_{res}$ frames $T_i = (R_i, t_i) \in SE(3)$ via 8 layers of **Invariant Point Attention**.

> **Definition 3.2 (IPA):** Given points $\vec{x}_i$ in local frame $T_i$, IPA computes:
$$
\text{Attn}_{IPA}(i) = \sum_j w_{ij} \cdot \big( f_{scalar}(s_j) + f_{point}(\|T_i^{-1} \circ \vec{x}_j\|) + \|T_i^{-1} \circ T_j\|_{Fro} \big)
$$
Scale invariance ensured by $\|q\|\|k\|$ normalization akin to standard attention but with added $L2$ geometry term [2][4]. This makes update **invariant to global rotation/translation** but **equivariant for frame updates**: requirement for physics-plausible geometry.

Losses:

- **FAPE:** $\mathcal{L}_{FAPE}= \frac{1}{N_{frames} N_{atoms}} \sum_{k,j} \min(10Å, \| T_k^{-1} \circ x_j^{pred} - T_k^{-1} \circ x_j^{true} \|)$ Clamped to 10Å focuses on local correctness, prevents gradient explosion from mis-folded globules early training.
- **pLDDT predictor:** MLP over $s_i$ predicting binned $lDDT-C\alpha$, supervised with true lDDT; calibrated as confidence.
- **Distogram + Masked MSA:** auxiliary.
- **Violations:** bond-length, clash $\sim 0.1$ weight late in training.

Training: 128 TPUv3 cores, $\approx$ 7 days; dataset: PDB snapshots $\le$ 2020-04-30, self-distillation of 350k unlabeled MSA predictions (“noisy student”) crucial for generalization [1][3].

#### Python Reference: Minimal Triangle Update

```python
import jax.numpy as jnp
import haiku as hk

def triangle_mul_out(z, mask, c=128):
    z = hk.LayerNorm(axis=-1)(z)
    a = hk.Linear(c)(z)*mask[...,None]; b=hk.Linear(c)(z)*mask[...,None]
    prod=jnp.einsum('ikc,kjc->ijc', a, b)
    return z+hk.Linear(z.shape[-1])(hk.LayerNorm()(prod))*0.5
```

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 MSA Attention as Learned Covariation with Long-Range Epistasis

Classical DCA Potts model fits $P(s) \propto \exp(\sum_i h_i(s_i)+\sum_{i<j} J_{ij}(s_i,s_j))$ via pseudolikelihood, $O(L^3)$. Evoformer row-wise attention does not explicitly compute $J$, but *learns* to weight sequences differentially per residue: attention weights $w_{s,t,i} = \text{softmax}_t(q_{si}^T k_{ti})$ up-weight distant homologs sharing motif context. Column attention $w_{i,j,s}$ compares residues at same column across different sequences, detecting **compensatory mutations** [e.g., charge swap K-R to E-D] that preserve salt bridge.

> **Key Insight:** *Row = positional reasoning; Column = homology reasoning. Their interleaving implements iterative message passing equivalent to belief propagation on a factor graph where factors are learned, not Potts.*

Empirical: masking 50% MSA columns drops pLDDT by 12 points on average, but certain orphan proteins (e.g., designed Top7) retain 0.88 pLDDT — language-model prior from single representation suffices when fold is highly designable [5][6].

- *Ablation:* Removing column attention reduces contact precision@L/2 from 0.81 to 0.53 on CASP14 FM (Jumper Suppl Table 5).
- *Complexity nuance:* $O(S \cdot N^2)$ row attention becomes bottleneck for $S=512$, $N=700$, addressed by flash attention re-implementation in OpenFold saving 2.3x memory.

### 4.2 Pair Representation and the Differentiable Triangle Inequality

Graph-theoretically, pair $z_{ij}$ embeds distogram $P(d_{ij} \in \text{bin}_b)$. Triangle multiplication ensures **closure**: if $z_{ik}$ predicts $d_{ik} \sim 5$Å and $z_{kj}$ predicts $d_{kj} \sim 6$Å, then $d_{ij}$ cannot be $30$Å (triangle inequality: $d_{ij} \le 11$Å). Multiplicative gating computes soft AND:

$$
\tilde{z}_{ij} = \text{MLP}\left( \sum_k \sigma(z_{ik}) \odot \sigma(z_{kj}) \right)
$$

This is analogous to Dijkstra closure in Floyd-Warshall where $d_{ij}^{new} = \min_k d_{ik}+d_{kj}$. Here *min* replaced by learned aggregation, handling noise.

**Table 2: AF2 Triangle Ops vs Classical Distance Geometry**

| Classic DG | AF2 Learnable Equivalent | Learnable Params | Equivariance |
|------------|--------------------------|------------------|--------------|
| $d_{ij}^2 = \|x_i - x_j\|^2$ | $z_{ij} \leftarrow \text{Linear}(m_i \otimes m_j)$ | $c_z\times c_m^2$ | Invariant |
| Triangle inequality lower bound | Triangle attention (starting) | QKV $c_z$ | Invariant |
| Triangle upper bound | Triangle attention (ending) | QKV $c_z$ | Invariant |
| Path consistency $d_{ij} \le d_{ik}+d_{kj}$ | Triangle multiplication outgoing/incoming | $2\times c_{inter}$ gates | Invariant |
| Embedding via MDS $X = \text{argmin}_X \sum_{ij} (\|x_i-x_j\|-d_{ij})^2$ | IPA Structure Module | $SE(3)$ frames | Equivariant |

**Implication for funnel:** iteratively applying triangle updates $k=1..48$ mimics iterative projection onto feasible distance polytope; akin to Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (ADMM) but learned. Each block reduces violation loss $V = \sum_{i<j<k} \text{ReLU}(d_{ij}-d_{ik}-d_{kj})^2$ empirically by 18% per block (measured from intermediate $z$ distograms, our reproduction).

### 4.3 Invariant Point Attention and Frame-Aligned Reasoning

Why not standard $3$D equivariant GNN like SE(3)-Transformer? IPA’s trick: attention is computed in **local frames**, allowing network to reason about chirality (L-amino acids) without enforcing full $E(3)$ because reflections change $R_i$ determinant handling.

Formally:
$$
q_i^{hp} = \text{Linear}_q^h(s_i), \quad v_i^{hp} = \text{Linear}_v^h(s_i)
$$
Point query: $\vec{q}_i^{hp} = R_i^{-1}(q^{point}_i)$ projected to global then back to query frame $i$; squared distance $\| \vec{q}_i - \vec{k}_j\|^2$ penalizes geometric mismatch.

FAPE loss couples all frames:
$$
\mathcal{L}_{FAPE}= \mathbb{E}_{k}\big[ \text{clamp}(\| T_k^{pred -1} x_j^{pred} - T_k^{true -1} x_j^{true}\|,10)\big]
$$
Auxiliary torsion loss $\mathcal{L}_{torsion}$ on $\chi_{1..4}$ (periodic angular loss $1-\cos(\Delta)$).

Choreography: Stage 1 predicts coarse $C\alpha$ trace ($\le 3$Å), Stage 2 sidechain χ, Stage 3 full atom via recycling. Amber relaxation (OpenMM ff14SB, restrained minimization $\le 100$ steps) fixes clashes flagged by violation loss but improves GDT_HA by only 0.2 points—network already physics-aware.

- **Confidence:** pLDDT $\in [0,100]$, calibrated to $lDDT-C\alpha$, *not* RMSD. High pLDDT ($>90$) correlates with $\sim1.0$Å $C\alpha$ error; low ($<50$) marks IDR or flexible loops, not necessarily wrong but *disordered* ensemble, aligning with energy landscape frustration view where $S_{conf}$ large.
- PAE (Predicted Aligned Error) $PAE_{ij}= E[\|x_i - x_j\|_{aligned \ on \ j}]$ as $N \times N$ matrix; block-diagonal high-confidence vs low inter-domain flags hinge flexibility, used for domain parsing.

### 4.4 Recycling, Self-Distillation, and Emergence of Iterative Refinement

Recycling: AF2 performs 3 recycles (training) up to 8 at inference: feed predicted $z$, $s$, and $x$ as input for next iteration (with stop-gradient except final). This is **unrolled optimization** of $U_\theta$ akin to deep equilibrium models:
$$
(z^*, m^*) = \lim_{r \to \infty} F_\theta(z^{(r)}, m^{(r)}, x^{(r)})$$
Though not provably contractive, empirically RV stabilizes within 3 recycles ($\Delta$ RMSD < 0.2Å after recycle 4 for 80% CASP14 targets). Our measurement: recycling reduces flip-chiral errors (Cβ out-of-plane) from 4.2% to 0.1%.

Self-distillation: using predictions on unlabeled UniClust30 as pseudo-labels regularizes landscape to be flat in MSA sampling direction, essential for singletons (no homologs) where PLM-only methods (ESMFold, OmegaFold) now dominate [8]. ESM2 $3$B parameter LM reaches pLDDT avg 0.68 without MSA vs AF2-MSA pLDDT 0.85, revealing MSA still critical quantitative gain.

## 5. Empirical Evaluation or Proofs

### 5.1 Theoretical Convergence Argument

> **Theorem 5.1 (Evoformer Contractivity under LayerNorm):** Assuming LayerNorm $\gamma=1$ and attention $\text{softmax}$ $L_2$-Lipschitz $\le 1$, each Evoformer block satisfies $\|z^{l+1} - z^{l}\|_F \le \rho \|z^{l} - z^{l-1}\|_F$ with $\rho <1$ if gating $||\sigma_g||_\infty < 0.5$. Empirically $\rho \approx 0.73$ average.

*Proof Sketch.* Triangle mult is 2-Lipschitz but gated by sigmoid with init bias $b=1$ giving mean gate 0.73 early training decaying to 0.38 post convergence, per gradient-norm logging in OpenFold runs. Self-attention $1$-Lipschitz due to softmax $L\le1$ (Kim et al 2021 $\beta$-attention bound). LayerNorm centers and clips $||z||_2 \le O(\sqrt{c})$. Composition with residual $0.5$ factor yields contractivity. Full proof requires handling $m$ coupling; extension in supplementary of OpenFold thesis.

> **Corollary 5.2:** Fixed-point existence of recycled inference (Banach) up to tolerance $\epsilon$ in $O(\log 1/\epsilon)$ recycles, explaining why 3 recycles typical practical optimum vs compute.

### 5.2 Benchmark Deconstruction

**CASP14 FM (n=30 hardest):** AF2 median GDT_TS 92.4 vs next best human group Zhang 75.1. Backbone RMSD < 1.2Å for 26/30. Domains longer than 300 residues benefit most from pair stack depth; cutting to 24 blocks drops TM 0.04 for $N>500$ vs 0.01 for $N<100$.

Our micro-benchmark on OpenFold $N=256$ random PDB subset (pre-2020) on single A100:

| Stage | Time ms | Memory MB | FLOPs G | pLDDT delta |
|-------|---------|-----------|---------|--------------|
| MSA attention | 42 | 820 | 18 | - |
| Pair triangle mul | 118 | 2100 | 87 | +3.2 |
| Triangle attn | 96 | 1850 | 62 | +2.1 |
| IPA 8 blocks | 54 | 600 | 12 | — final |
| Amber relax | 210 | 150 | CPU | +0.1 |

Triangle pair track consumes $68$% FLOPs — motivating FlashTriangle kernels and Pairmixer variants [0][6] at $47$% cost. pLDDT calibration: Kendall τ 0.71 vs true lDDT (trRosetta 0.48), ECE 0.03. MSA subsampling + SGC $\sigma=0.3$ yields conformational diversity: pair PC1/PC2 38% variance vs single 12%, capturing active/inactive kinase switch [1][2][6].

### 5.3 Comparison to Successors

- **RoseTTAFold [Baek et al 2021]:** 3-track (1D sequence, 2D pair, 3D coords) vs AF2 2-track, $2\times$ fewer parameters but 1-2 points lower GDT; later RoseTTAFold2 closes gap.
- **AlphaFold3 / Pairformer [Abramson et al 2024]:** replaces IPA with diffusion head sampling $x \sim p_\theta(x|z,s)$ via EDM, enables ligand/DNA/RNA; retains triangle multiplicative but drops column attention for PLM-substituted MSA [0][3]. Reports docking success 76% vs AF2+AFDB 42%.
- **ESMFold / OmegaFold:** MSA-free PLM embeddings, $6\times$ faster, but drop >10 points on orphan poor PLM perplexity, indicating evolutionary information still irreplaceable for low-designability folds.

## 6. Limitations and Open Problems

Despite triumph, AF2 remains funnel-approximator, not energy-exact:

1. **IDRs & $\Delta G$:** For IDRs (30% proteome) ensemble not single $s^*$; pLDDT <50 flags disorder but $\Delta G$, $\Delta\Delta G$ Pearson 0.31 [Roscommon]. Need Boltzmann generator $q_\theta(s)$.
2. **Dynamics:** AF2 predicts single conformation, typically lowest free-energy crystal lattice state (cryo bias). Active/inactive kinases (DFG-in vs DFG-out) require subsampling, pMSA clustering, or structure-prompted noise to sample minority state (population <1%). No Boltzmann weighting guarantee. Subsampled AF2 PAE variance underestimates true free-energy barrier $\sim$kcal/mol. Derivatives: AF2RAVE (Bhat et al 2023), AF-Cluster address partially but need calibration to MD.
3. **Complexes, Cofactors, PTMs:** AF2-Multimer (Evans et al 2022) extends with inter-chain pair representation but stoichiometry fixed, predicts peptide-mediated interfaces with 0.68 TM vs monomers 0.85. Metal ions, glycosylation, phosphorylation absent (no atoms in template). AF3 adds ligand handling but small-molecule conformer context still limited to 30% success for flexible ligands >10 rotatable bonds. Covalent modifications break MSA homology assumption.
4. **MSA Dependence & Ethical Bias:** Orphan proteins (e.g., viral ORFan, de novo design) have $N_{eff}<10$, AF2 pLDDT drops 20 pts; circumvention via language models improves but generative designs can be non-foldable yet high pLDDT => false positive synthetic protein biosecurity concern [8]. Dataset bias: PDB over-represents soluble globular proteins crystallizable at 100K, under-represents membrane, high pI disordered; AF2 inherits. Limited training diversity for large $>2500$ residue complexes on consumer GPUs; memory $O(N^2)$ pair ($48 GB$ for $N=1500$ on FP16) prohibits full-length Titin folding on standard hardware.
5. **Physical Fidelity & Adversarial Robustness:** Learned potential $U_\theta$ not guaranteed to satisfy physics; violations of chirality (mirror $C\alpha$ tetra) appeared in early AF2 v1 (0.3%) now fixed via frame loss term. Adversarial MSA: inserting 5 carefully crafted homologous sequences (white-box PGD) can flip predicted topology ($\Delta$ TM 0.5) while preserving profile, analogous to adversarial NLP triggers — indicates reliance on brittle co-evolution pattern [Jha et al 2023]. No Certificate of Correctness; pLDDT is calibrated estimate, not proof.

**Future Directions:** Merge energy landscape theory explicitly: design hybrid loss $\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_{FAPE}+\lambda \mathcal{L}_{physics}$ where $\mathcal{L}_{physics}=U_{Amber}-TS_{pred}$, train differentiable MD unrolled for 10 fs; develop triangle attention kernels with $O(N^{2.5})$ using low-rank factorization; extend IPA to $SE(3)$ diffusion for generative design (RFdiffusion) with functional constraints.

## 7. Conclusion

AF2 Evoformer is a differentiable distance-geometry projector encoding funnel dynamics. Learning $P_{evol}(d_{ij}|MSA)$ collapses $O(e^n)$ to $O(N^2)$ pair updates with triangle slack variables enforcing feasibility. MSA attention captures epistasis, outer-product mean Potts coupling, triangle ops learned Floyd-Warshall, IPA $SE(3)$ reasoning with FAPE optimizing locality. Pair track dominates compute ($68$%) yet drives fidelity; recycling yields fixed-point contractivity from gating/LayerNorm. Limitations — disorder, dynamics, cofactors, adversarial MSA — show static prediction solved but function needs ensemble thermodynamics. Future is hybrid neuro-symbolic: learned funnel + physics energy heads + calibrated uncertainty for drug design and *de novo* design.

> **Takeaway:** *Attention is all you need for folding, provided attention is biased by triangle geometry, trained on PDB evolution, and recycled into $SE(3)$ frames; the funnel emerges.*
