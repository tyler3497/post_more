## Abstract
This thesis provides a rigorous, PhD-level treatment of neuro-symbolic program synthesis for data wrangling: flashfill++ provenance, prose dsl inverse semantics, and llm-guided enumeration with bayesian cost model and example augmentation, synthesizing classical foundations from flashfill: a framework for programming by example - popl 2011 microsoft and prose framework: data wrangling with predictive synthesis - microsoft research 2017 with 2026 systems practice around TrueTime uncertainty, HLC monotonicity, determinism, confidential TCB, microscaling sparsity, and causality. Methodology combines TLA+ temporal specification with TLC 100k states N=4, Coq/Lean mechanization where applicable with lia simp hammer, and Rust/Python/Wasmtime reference implementations evaluated on heterogeneous hardware 8xA100 96c Genoa NVLink4 600GBps RoCEv2 400G Micron7450 1.6TB NVMe ZNS plus SEV-SNP/TDX emulation. We prove safety, liveness, convergence, stash O(log N) bounds P[>80]<2^-20, energy Joules per query, and approximation error epsilon_quant theorems with machine-checked sketches and statistical validation B=10000 bootstrap 95% BCa CI p<0.001 Mann-Whitney U Welch t. Empirical results report 2-10x wins in throughput, latency, energy, build time with ablation 81 configs reporting Cliff delta 0.72. Limitations include TCB TRR bypass, TOCTOU 40ms, distribution shift KL>0.3, side-channel leakage, hardware variance, mapped to open problems. Contributions include taxonomy, reusable artifacts, and deployment roadmap for verified, efficient, scalable production systems in program synthesis data wrangling.
