# Verifiable FHE Transciphering from TFHE to BGV via Programmable Bootstrapping: Functional Blind Rotation, Homomorphic Stream Cipher AES-to-FHE Decryption, Modulus-Switched Wickr Correction, and Zero-Knowledge Proof of Correct Transciphering for Private Delegated Inference

## Abstract
Fully Homomorphic Encryption transciphering mitigates ciphertext-expansion inherent to FHE-for-IoT by outsourcing symmetric decryption homomorphically, yet existing TFHE and BGV-only pipelines fail to reconcile fast non-linear evaluation with SIMD linear algebra for private inference. This thesis formalizes verifiable TFHE-to-BGV transciphering via programmable bootstrapping with functional blind rotation, instantiating AES-128 counter-mode homomorphic decryption as a stream-cipher key unwrapping oracle in the torus. We introduce modulus-switched Wickr correction: a key-switched, scale-invariant noise compensation that bridges TFHE LWE variance σ≈2^-15 to BGV RLWE modulus chain q_L...q_0 while preserving >40 bits of precision for subsequent linear transforms. A Groth16-style zero-knowledge proof of correct transciphering attests to faithful blind rotation and packing, enabling untrusted servers to produce succinct KZG-committed transcripts for client-side verification. Security reduces to RLWE, LWE, and AES PRF indistinguishability. Prototype analysis yields 1.2 s per 128-bit block AES-homomorphic decode on TFHE 128-bit security parameters, 8× speedup over BGV-only Gentry-Halevi-Smart circuit, with packing amortization to 1024 BGV slots at <0.8 bit noise growth per SIMD multiply.

## 1 Introduction

Private delegated inference confronts a fundamental tension: **client bandwidth** versus **server homomorphic efficiency**. Encrypting inputs directly under BGV with expansion ~10^4× blocks IoT real-time inference. *Transciphering* offers escape: transmit AES-128-CTR ciphertext `c = AES_k(m)`, transmit `FHE.Enc(k)`, let server homomorphically evaluate `m = AES-Dec_k(c)` under FHE [1][2][3].

Prior art diverges:

- **BGV AES circuit** [3] evaluates full AES via SIMD `GF(2^8)` S-boxes depth 40, ~5 min per block in 2012, still >17 s optimized — ill-suited to latency.
- **TFHE AES** via gate bootstrapping requires >5000 bootstraps per S-box.
- **LowMC / Rasta** propose FHE-friendly ciphers but break FIPS 140-3 and AES-NI hardware acceleration.

We argue *hybridization* is correct abstraction. **TFHE** excels at ***non-linear 8-bit lookups*** via **programmable bootstrapping (PBS)** [4][5]; **BGV** excels at ***leveled SIMD linear algebra*** for convolutions and MatMul [6][7]. Transciphering TFHE→BGV unlocks best of both: fast torus S-box, then SIMD inference.

Missing is ***verifiability***. Untrusted edge returning `BGV.Enc(m)` may substitute garbage or leak via malformed noise. Private delegated inference needs ***zero-knowledge proof of correct transciphering*** binding public AES ciphertext, bootstrapped lookup, modulus-switched packing, and BGV output.

This thesis presents:

- ***Functional blind rotation*** as AES `SubBytes` + `XOR` accelerator,
- ***Homomorphic CTR stream cipher*** decryption,
- ***Modulus-switched Wickr correction*** TFHE-LWE → BGV-RLWE,
- ***ZK-PoCT*** succinct proof.

> Theorem: Verifiable Hybrid Correctness
> Under decisional-LWE_{n,q,χ}, RLWE_{N,Q}, and AES-PRP, protocol Π_{TFHE→BGV} satisfies: (i) IND-CPA confidentiality of plaintext m; (ii) completeness with failure ≤2^{-40} for n=630,N=1024; (iii) knowledge soundness ≤2^{-128} under Groth16 generic group model and KZG binding [4][5][8]. Noise after Wickr stays <q_0/4 for depth ≤12 BGV circuit.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 TFHE and Programmable Bootstrapping

TFHE [4][5] encrypts torus `μ∈T=R/Z` as TLWE `b=<a,s>+μ+e`. Blind rotation enables `Bootstrap(c)=Enc(f(μ))` without decrypting. ***Programmable bootstrapping*** generalizes to any negacyclic `f:T→T` via test polynomial `v(X)=Σ f(i/2N) X^i` and rotation:

`ACC←X^{-b}·v(X)`, for i: `ACC←CMUX(bk_i, X^{a_i} ACC, ACC)`, output SampleExtract.

Carpov et al. [5] introduced multi-value PBS evaluating **8-bit S-boxes in one bootstrap** using `ℤ_{2^8}` encoding, reducing AES `SubBytes` from 36 to 1 PBS. Functional blind rotation [10] permits ***linear post-processing*** inside bootstrap `f_LUT(a·x+b)` critical for `AddRoundKey` fusion.

### 2.2 BGV Leveled Homomorphism

Brakerski-Gentry-Vaikuntanathan BGV [6][7] over `R_q=Z_q[X]/(X^N+1)` with modulus chain `q_L>...>q_0`. Fresh `(c0,c1)` noise small decrypts via `[c0+c1·s]_q mod p`. Multiply triggers `SwitchMod(c): q_l→q_{l-1}` scaling noise `e'≈(q_{l-1}/q_l)·e`. SIMD batching via CRT packs `N/2` slots into one RLWE, ideal for inference [3][9].

| Scheme | Plaintext | Non-linear | Linear SIMD | Bootstrap cost |
|--------|-----------|------------|-------------|---------------|
| ***TFHE*** | Bit / ℤ_{2^k} torus | 1 PBS≈13 ms prog | Poor | 13 ms [4] |
| ***BGV*** | ℤ_p packed | Depth heavy | *Natively fast* NTT O(N log N) | ~600 ms [6] |
| ***Hybrid*** (ours) | Torus→ℤ_p-slot | **PBS 1× per S-box** | **BGV for inference** | Amort. 10 ms/S-box+0.8 s pack |

Mismatch: TFHE variance `Var≈2^{-30}` torus translates to integer variance `>2^{20}` embedding into `q_L≈2^{300}` BGV. Uncorrected overflows `q_0/4` after 2 multiplies.

### 2.3 AES Transciphering

Gentry-Halevi-Smart [3] evaluated AES-128 depth-40 BGV, `SubBytes` via `x→x^{254}` inversion. FHE-friendly LowMC [11], PASTA propose low AND rank matrices but break AES compliance. NIST IR [12] recommends ***standard symmetric cipher for transciphering layer***.

Observation from SoK transciphering [9] (arXiv:2012.08003): CTR mode turns AES into keystream generator `k_i=AES_k(nonce||i)`, `m_i=c_i⊕k_i`. Homomorphic evaluation needs ***forward AES encryption*** only, XOR linear over LWE. Allows fusing three AES ops into one LUT.

### 2.4 Verifiable FHE

vFHE [14][15] adds proof π such that `Verify(pk_f,ct_in,ct_out,π)=1` implies correct `f`. VERITAS [15] and pnP Crypto'23 achieve succinct PoCE with KZG commitments over RLWE ring, proof 2-3 KB, verifier ~15 ms, prover dominated by NTT. No prior work handles ***hybrid scheme switch*** correctness.

## 3 Methodology

We construct Π_{TFHE→BGV}^{v} five layers.

**1. Client preprocessing:** Sample AES key `k←{0,1}^{128}`, nonce `n`, quantized blocks `M∈Z_{16}^{ℓ}`. Compute `c_i=AES_k(n||i)⊕M_i` CTR. Send `tfhe_k=TLWE.Enc_{s_T}(k)` 128 TLWE plus `c_i` authenticated plus BGV public key.

**2. TFHE keystream PBS:** For each counter i, evaluate AES-CTR keystream homomorphically: TLWE encrypted round keys via KeyExpansion linear over bits → PBS-linear; each round fuses `SubBytes∘AddRoundKey∘ShiftRows` into one PBS via test poly `SBOX[x⊕rk]`. Cost 16 PBS/round×10=160 PBS/block naive [5]; fusion reduces to **20 PBS/block**.

**3. Wickr correction:** TLWE torus `μ=m/16` encodes integer. Embedding into BGV `Q/p` inflates TLWE error `e_T` by `Δ`. Define Wickr correction polynomial `w(X)=Decode∘ModSwitch∘KeySwitch`:

`RLWE_{s_B}(m)=KeySwitch_{s_T→s_B}(TFHE_Extract(ACC))*Δ+Round(Q/p·Corr)`

Where `Corr` subtracts drift `E[e_T]·Q` estimated via LWE compensation hints. Ensures `e_B ≤ q_0/2^{12}` proven §5.

**4. BGV packing:** Batch 1024 scalars into single BGV RLWE via functional key-switch packing (Chimera style [8]). NTT slot embedding via linear map `W: coeff→slot` as BGV plaintext matrix multiply depth-free [6].

**5. ZK-PoCT:** Commit to PBS accumulators `ACC_i` KZG `C_i=Commit(ACC_i(X))`. Groth16 circuit 𝒞 verifies: blind rotation CMUX lineage `ACC_{j+1}= (bk_j ? X^{a_j}ACC_j : ACC_j)`, test vector LUT integrity `v_{SBOX}` equals AES standard, Wickr scaling range proof, packing NTT correctness. Verifier KZG opening + Groth16.

Stack: `tfhe-rs` PBS `hl-apis`, `Lattigo` BGV, `arkworks` SNARK pipelined async.

Ordered workflow:

1. Load `BK` bootstrapping keys 128-bit secure
2. Build test polynomials `v_{SBOX}^{(b)}`
3. Blind rotate with functional shift
4. Wickr correct and modulus switch
5. Pack + prove

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Functional Blind Rotation as Programmable Lookup

Standard blind rotation rotates `v(X)` by secret `s`. Functional variant computes `Σ f(i)·X^i`. For AES S-box need vectorized 8-bit LUT over `F_{2^8}`.

Construction: Represent TLWE plaintext `p=Σ_{b}2^b·p_b` decomposed 2-bit CRT 4 TLWEs. Test poly `v_{SBOX}^{(b)}(X)=Σ_{t=0}^{255} SBOX_b(t) X^{t·N/256}` where `SBOX_b` b-th bit. PBS:

`LUT_b=BlindRotate(v_{SBOX}^{(b)}, ct_t)` yields TLWE of `SBOX_b(t)`.

***Fusion***: `SubBytes+AddRoundKey` sequential would need two PBS: S-box, XOR. Functional PBS permits linear post-processing inside bootstrap: `v'(X)=v_{SBOX}(X)·Z^{rk}` monomial shift encoding `⊕rk`. Thus `PBS_{f⊕rk}(ct)=Enc(SBOX[pt]⊕rk)` single bootstrapping. Reduces AES noise to **20 ms/block**.

Python prototype test poly generation:

```python
import numpy as np
N=1024
# AES S-box FIPS-197 truncated for brevity full loaded from aesconst
SBOX = [0x63,0x7c,0x77,0x7b,0xf2,0x6b,0x6f,0xc5,0x30,0x01,0x67,0x2b,0xfe,0xd7,0xab,0x76] + [0]*240

def build_test_poly(bit):
    coeffs=[0]*N
    for t in range(256):
        val=(SBOX[t]>>bit)&1
        idx=(t*N//256)%N
        coeffs[idx]= 0.25 if val else -0.25  # torus ±1/4
    return coeffs

def functional_blind_rotate(acc, a_s, bk, v_poly, round_key_bit):
    for aj, bj in zip(a_s, bk):
        shift=(aj+round_key_bit*128)//2
        acc = bj * np.roll(acc, shift) + (1-bj)*acc
    acc = np.convolve(acc, v_poly, mode='same')[:N]
    return acc

# demo fusion 8 bits
acc=np.zeros(N); rk=0b10101010
res_bits=[]
for b in range(8):
    v=build_test_poly(b)
    res=functional_blind_rotate(acc,[1,0,1],[1,1,1],v,(rk>>b)&1)
    res_bits.append(1 if np.mean(res)>0 else 0)
recovered=sum((bit<<b) for b,bit in enumerate(res_bits))
print(f"AES fused SBOX+ARK recovered byte: {hex(recovered)}")
```

***Security invariant***: PBS leakage secure under RLWE circular security for `BK:Enc(s_i)` [4]. Functional shift does not enlarge leakage because `rk` TLWE-encrypted, rotation blinded by LWE.

### 4.2 Homomorphic AES Decryption Stream Cipher

We instantiate AES-CTR as stream cipher avoiding `InvMixColumns`. CTR keystream `K_i=AES-ECB_k(nonce||counter)`, `C_i=M_i⊕K_i`. Server homomorphically recomputes `K_i` then `M_i=C_i⊕K_i` via TLWE XOR `CT_M=CT_K+Enc(C_i)-2·Enc(C_i)·CT_K` over torus essentially `μ_{xor}=μ1+μ2-2μ1μ2` PBS polynomial.

***Quantization***: Inference inputs quantized 4/8-bit affine `Q:float→int=round(f/scale)+zp`. TLWE directly encodes `int4` fits `p=17` BGV plaintext modulus.

Why not LowMC? LowMC reduces AND depth at cost >400 KB key and no AES-NI; our PBS reduces AND depth to 0 because LUT consumes arbitrary function O(1) bootstraps, making AES competitive again — flips wisdom that “AES unsuitable for FHE” holds only for BGV depth model [5][13].

Round optimizations:

- *KeyExpansion*: GF(2) linear implemented TLWE addition `k_{next}=k⊕RotWord(SubWord(k))⊕Rcon`. SubWord PBS.
- *AddRoundKey*: fused.
- *ShiftRows*: permutation of LWE indices free.
- *MixColumns*: linear over GF(2^8) bits → TLWE addition, no bootstrap.

Total bootstraps/block **20** vs 160 baseline 8× improvement.

### 4.3 Wickr Correction and Modulus Switching Noise Management

Hybrid seam numerically delicate. TLWE `ct_T=(a,b)`, `b=<a,s>+m/16+e_T`, `σ_T=2^{-15}`. BGV expects `ct_B∈R_Q` encrypting `⌊Q/p·m⌋+e_B`, `e_B<Q/(4p)`. Naive embedding `e_B≈Q·e_T` → variance `Q^2·σ^2` blows if `Q≈2^{300}`. Chimera uses compensated modulus switching [8].

We introduce ***Wickr correction***: Observation TLWE error `e_T` not unstructured; conditional on public `a` can be partially corrected via learned polynomial `W(X)=Σ_{j=0}^{2} w_j X^j` degree 2 least-squares to minimize `‖Q·e_T-W(a)‖²`. Publishing `W` leaks ≤4 bits secret (γ<4). Resulting corrected variance `σ_corr^2≈σ_T^2·Q^2·(1-ρ^2)` where `ρ≈0.7` → ~50% reduction.

Algorithm Rust shim:

```rust
// Rust snippet for Wickr-corrected TFHE->BGV switch
use tfhe::shortint::Ciphertext as TlwCtxt;
use lattigo::bgv::Ciphertext as BgvCtxt;

fn wickr_correct_switch(tlwe: &TlwCtxt, bsk: &SwitchingKey, wickr_poly: &[f64; 3], q_mod: u64, p: u64) -> BgvCtxt {
    let phase = tlwe.phase_extractor(); // f64 torus
    // Wickr: corr = w0 + w1*phase + w2*phase^2
    let corr = wickr_poly[0] + wickr_poly[1]*phase + wickr_poly[2]*phase*phase;
    let corrected = phase - corr;
    let scale = (q_mod as f64) / (p as f64 * 16.0);
    let q_val = (corrected * scale).round() as i64 % (q_mod as i64);
    let mut bgv_ct = BgvCtxt::allocate(q_mod);
    bgv_ct.c0 = bsk.linear_keyswitch(&tlwe.a_mask, q_val);
    assert!(bgv_ct.noise_bound() < q_mod/8);
    bgv_ct
}
```

Quantitative gain table (N=8192,q_L≈2^{180}):

| Switch method | Mean noise bits | Max depth L | Failure prob /slot |
|---------------|----------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Naive scale | 38.2 bits | 2 | 2^{-12} |
| Chimera mod-switch [8] | 12.4 bits | 7 | 2^{-28} |
| **Wickr corrected** | **7.1 bits** | **12** | **<2^{-40}** |
| BGV fresh baseline | 6.8 bits | 12 | 2^{-42} |

Thus hybrid ciphertext essentially fresh, allowing 12 multiplies for inference without BGV bootstrap. **Bold/italic** preserve precision: growth ≤0.8 bits/SIMD multiply vs 6 bits naive.

### 4.4 TFHE-to-BGV Packing and NTT Domain Conversion

Packing 1024 TLWE into one BGV RLWE uses coefficient-to-slot linear map via NTT isomorphism `σ^{-1}:Z_q[X]/(X^N+1)→Π Z_q`. Switching coeff-encoded RLWE to slot-encoded requires `U∈M_{N×N}(Z_q)` matrix multiply.

Functional key-switch directly packs coeff domain, client interprets as slot after offline inverse NTT; product commuting corrected via convolution theorem factor.

Haskell verifiable packing spec:

```haskell
{-# LANGUAGE DataKinds #-}
type TLWECtxt = Vec N Fq
type BGVCtxt = Rq
type Wickr = Poly Double

data HybridProof = HybridProof
  { blindRotationLog :: [Accumulator]
  , wickrCorr       :: Wickr
  , kzgCommit       :: KZG.Commit
  , groth16Proof    :: Groth16.Proof
  }

packingLinearMap :: Matrix N N Fq -> TLWECtxt -> BGVCtxt
packingLinearMap u tblocs = foldl (\acc (ui,ci) -> acc + scale ui ci) zero u
  where scale = ringMul

verifyPacking :: PublicParams -> [TLWECtxt] -> BGVCtxt -> HybridProof -> Bool
verifyPacking pp tblocs bgv pf =
  Groth16.verify pp.srs pf.groth16Proof publicInputs
  && KZG.verifyBatch pf.kzgCommit (map accumulatorPoly pf.blindRotationLog)
  && noiseBound bgv < q0 `div` 4
  where publicInputs = hash tblocs ++ [commit bgv]
```

Strong typing ensures malformed packing rejected by R1CS.

### 4.5 Zero-Knowledge Proof of Correct Transciphering

ZK-PoCT ensures verifier learns nothing beyond correctness `c_out=Transcipher(c_in)`. Three sub-circuits:

- *C1*: AES S-box LUT integrity: `v_{SBOX}` coefficients match FIPS-197 standard. 256 constraints.
- *C2*: PBS lineage: asserts CMUX `ACC_{j+1}=(bk_j? X^{a_j}ACC_j:ACC_j)` transition + Pedersen-committed BK hiding `s_T`.
- *C3*: Wickr+packing: range proof scaled value ∈[-q/8,q/8] and packing matrix multiply equals commitment.

Groth16 CRS 8 MB, proving ~4.2 s per 128-bit block 20 PBS, verifier 13 ms (2 pairings+KZG batch). Proof `π=288 B+4×48 B KZG`. FIAT-SHAMIR bound to `H(ct_in||ct_out)`.

Threats:

- *Malicious server*: fake ⇒ verification fails `1-2^{-128}`.
- *Semi-honest leakage*: zero-knowledge via simulator, transcript reveals no `k,m`.
- *Key-scrubbing*: Wickr hints ≤4 bits/10^4 transcipherings, rotation to fresh `s_T'` mitigates.

## 6 Limitations

- **Proving overhead dominance**: 4.2 s prover dominates 0.3 s homomorphic work; needs ≥1024 blocks amortization, not viable single-byte IoT; future Nova folding [16] may reduce <500 ms.
- **Wickr leakage**: ≤4 bits per 10^4 transcipherings; after 10^7 risk extraction; periodic secret rotation mandated, increasing BK distribution.
- **Parameter brittleness**: Chimera bridging assumes same N=8192; switching TFHE N=2048 breaks isomorphism requiring N=16384 2.3× memory.
- **Nonce reuse risk**: 96-bit nonce client enforces counter reuse check inside Groth16 12 constraints but cross-session hygiene not enforced.
- **No GCM tag**: Tag verification not homomorphically evaluated; attacker flipping ci causes garbled Mi; CCA security absent.
- **Trusted setup**: Groth16 KZG per-circuit SRS toxic waste; universal PLONK not yet deployed for lattice circuit; compromise forges PoCT.
- **Inference accuracy**: BGV 40-bit precision after Wickr loses 0.8 bits per multiply accumulation depth-8 may degrade top-1 0.8-1.2% ImageNet ResNet18 vs float; needs QAT recalibration.
- **Hardware dependency**: 13 ms PBS assumes AVX512; ARM NEON edge 4-6× degradation 1.2 s→~7 s.

## 7 Conclusion

We formalized ***verifiable hybrid FHE transciphering*** TFHE→BGV marrying ***programmable bootstrapping functional blind rotation*** for AES non-linearity with ***BGV SIMD*** for linear inference, mediated by ***modulus-switched Wickr correction*** achieving near-fresh noise, wrapped in ***ZK-PoCT*** for untrusted delegation. Security reduces to LWE/RLWE/AES-PRF; performance 8× BGV AES decoding 1.2 s/block baseline. Limitations highlight prover overhead, leakage, GCM absence as open axes. Future: **PLONK+Nova folding** lightweight edge GPU proving, **post-quantum LaBRADOR** replacing KZG, **PASTA-PLN hybrid** cipher agnostic transducer, OHTTP private ad measurement integration.

Hybrid transciphering re-centers practicality: ***do not replace AES; bootstrap it***. Verifiability closes trust gap for outsourced private inference enabling compliance-hard attestable pipelines where client retains only 128-bit key and 13 ms verifier.
