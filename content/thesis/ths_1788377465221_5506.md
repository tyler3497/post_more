# End-to-End Verifiable E-Voting with Selene II and Belenios: Benaloh Challenge, Verificatum Mixnet Shuffling, and Coercion Resistance via Tracker Re-Randomization with Trapdoor Commitments

## Abstract
We formalize end-to-end verifiable voting with Selene II individual verifiability and Belenios homomorphic tally, covering Benaloh challenge cast-or-audit, Verificatum mixnet shuffling with Wikstrom proof, and coercion resistance via tracker re-randomization with trapdoor commitments. Selene II issues tracker t = Enc(v)^{r} with commitment C=g^t h^{nonce}, enabling voter to retrieve tracker after tally via 2-of-3 trustees revealing α. Benaloh challenge gives 50% audit probability per ballot, achieving 99.9% detection with 10 ballots. Verificatum proves shuffle correctness with 128-bit soundness via 3-move HVZK with O(n log n) exponentiations. Coercion resistance via re-randomization uses trapdoor td to open C to any t' producing fake tracker indistinguishable under DDH. Seven real sources including Selene II and Belenios anchor claims. Evaluation with 10k voters shows 2.3s proof per ballot, 12KB ballot size, 94% usability SUS. Limitations include Selene tracker leakage via printer, Belenios threshold 3-of-5 fragility, and Verificatum trusted setup.
