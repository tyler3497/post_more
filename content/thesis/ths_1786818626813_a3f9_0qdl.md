# Quantum LDPC Codes vs Surface Codes: Threshold Scaling, Decoder 3D Matching, and Fault-Tolerant Magic State Distillation

## Abstract
This thesis examines high-rate quantum LDPC codes against surface codes under realistic fault-tolerance constraints: geometrical locality, syndrome extraction depth, decoder latency, and magic-state overhead. We synthesize pivotal results from Bravyi et al. Nature 2024 on bivariate bicycle (BB) codes [[144,12,12]], Panteleev-Kalachev asymptotically good families [1], Fowler's surface code threshold analysis [3][4], Stephens' circuit-level thresholds 0.502-1.14% [2], Roffe et al. BP-OSD decoding [5], and Bravyi-Kitaev distillation [6]. Abstract claim: qLDPC codes reduce qubit overhead by ~10× at comparable pseudo-threshold but impose weight-6 long-range checks and OSD cubic post-processing unless mitigated by BP-SF clustering or neural decoding. Decoder 3D matching for surface codes remains linearizable to O(n^2) average and O(1) parallel with local communication, while qLDPC Tanner graphs necessarily contain 4-cycles forcing message-passing oscillation and requiring ordered-statistics or localized statistics decoding. Magic-state distillation scaling exponent γ→1 for multilevel protocols asymptotically dominates factory footprint when k>10. Empirically, [[144,12,12]] BB code achieves LER ~1e-5 at physical 1e-3 with BP-OSD depth 10k iterations vs surface distance-11 requiring 241 qubits per logical vs 12 logicals per 144. We provide formal lemmas, GFT tables, and TLA+ spec sketch for pipelined extraction.

## 1 Introduction

***The central tension in fault-tolerant quantum computation is locality vs rate.*** Surface codes exploit 2D nearest-neighbor Clifford closure and transversal logical Pauli to achieve threshold ***approaching 1%*** with cheap MWPM decoding [3]. Their fatal flaw: ***rate k/n → 0***, distance scaling d~√n, overhead O(d^2) per logical - a 100-logical machine at d=27 demands ~1M physical qubits [6]. Quantum LDPC promises to break this: constant k/n, linear d=Θ(n), bounded-weight checks.

Recent breakthroughs made promise concrete:

- **Asymptotic goodness proved.** Panteleev-Kalachev lifted product over non-abelian groups yields [[n, Θ(n), Θ(n)]] - qLDPC conjecture settled [1]. Leverrier-Zémor quantum Tanner codes provide linear-time decoder [2].
- **Practical mid-scale codes discovered.** Bravyi et al. 2024 introduced bivariate bicycle codes: $\mathbb{F}_2[x,y]/(x^\ell-1, y^m-1)$, $A(x,y)=x^{a1}+y^{a2}+y^{a3}$, $B(x,y)=y^{b1}+x^{b2}+x^{b3}$, giving $H_X=[A|B], H_Z=[B^T|A^T]$, weight-6 (3+3) CSS orthogonal $H_X H_Z^T=0$ by construction [8]. Canonical $[[144,12,12]]$ *gross code* reduces overhead 10× vs 12 surface patches at similar performance on superconducting layout with tunable couplers.
- **Decoder revolution.** BP-OSD improves LER 2-3 orders over BP [5]. Fully parallel BP-SF generates syndrome-flipped candidates via oscillation detection, achieving 55% latency reduction post-parallelized and 18% worst-case tail [7]. Hypergraph neural decoders (HyperNQ) show 84% improvement over BP, 50% over GNN by modeling hyperedge stabilizer correlation [4].

> **Theorem 1 (Informal rate-distance tradeoff):** *No topological 2D local code can have k d^2 = ω(n); achieving k=Θ(n), d=Θ(n) requires non-local checks or expansion.* Proof sketch via Bravyi-Poulin-Terhal bound and cleaning lemma. Thus qLDPC must incur long-range connectivity.

Contributions:

1. Taxonomy across hypergraph-product, lifted-product, 2BGA, BB, quantum Tanner.
2. Cost model $C(N)=\alpha T_{dec}(N)+\beta E_{anc}(N)+\gamma S_{overhead}(N)$ quantifying threshold vs overhead Pareto.
3. Specification for modular distributed syndrome extraction (star network, Bell-pair mediated non-local CNOT).
4. Integration analysis of magic-state factories (15-to-1, 10-to-2, level-r multilevel) with high-rate memories.

**Roadmap:** Sec2 background, Sec3 methodology, Sec4 deep dive 4×, Sec5 empirical/proofs, Sec6 limitations, Sec7 conclusion.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Definitions

Let quantum CSS code $\mathcal{C}=[[n,k,d]]$ defined by $H_X \in \mathbb{F}_2^{m_x\times n}$, $H_Z \in \mathbb{F}_2^{m_z\times n}$ s.t. $H_X H_Z^T=0$. Stabilizer weight $w$ = max row weight. LDPC iff $w=O(1)$, column weight $j=O(1)$. BB subclass: $n=2\ell m$, $H_X=[A|B]$, $H_Z=[B^T|A^T]$ where $A,B \in \mathbb{F}_2^{\ell m \times \ell m}$ circulant from $A(x,y),B(x,y)$. For example, IBM gross code $\ell=12, m=12, n=144, A=x^3+y+y^2, B=y^3+x+x^2$ yields $[[144,12,12]]$.

***Def 2.1 (Threshold).*** Family $\{\mathcal{C}_n\}$ has threshold $p_{th}$ under noise model $\mathcal{N}(p)$ and decoder $D$ iff $\lim_{n\to\infty} P_{logical}(p)=0$ for $p<p_{th}$ and $\to 1$ for $p>p_{th}$. Circuit-level: each gate/measurement fails i.i.d. with $p$, including ancilla prep and measurement flips. Code-capacity simplified: only data error, perfect syndrome.

***Def 2.2 (Single-shot).*** Code is single-shot iff one noisy syndrome measurement suffices to correct up to $O(d)$ errors after $O(1)$ rounds, via redundancy $M H =0$ metachecks. qLDPC Tanner expanders provide this; surface code requires $d$ rounds building 3D space-time graph.

### 2.2 Surface Code Baseline

Surface code $d\times d$ patch: $n=d^2+(d^2-1)$, $k=1$, $d_{code}=d$, checks weight 4 (or 2 at boundary). Fowler et al. [3] simplified scheme: 2D NN lattice, Clifford via lattice surgery, logical overhead $O(d^3)$ for non-Clifford gate. MWPM decoder on graph $G=(V,E)$ where $V$ syndrome defects (including temporal boundaries), edge weight $w_e=-\log(p/(1-p))$, finds minimum-weight pairing via Edmonds' blossom $O(|V|^3)$, optimized to $O(n^2)$ average via local union-find or sparse blossom, $O(1)$ parallel with constant compute per unit area [Fowler 2012].

Stephens [2] showed thresholds under balanced Pauli noise:

| Noise model | Decoding | Threshold (% per gate) |
|-------------|----------|------------------------|
| Circuit depolarizing + measurement | MWPM | 0.502(1)%-0.672% |
| Phenomenological (perfect gates, noisy syndrome) | MWPM | 0.92-1.14% |
| Code-capacity (perfect syndrome) | Max-likelihood | ~1% approaching 10.9% bias-tailored |

Bias-tailored XZZX variant raises infinite-bias threshold to ~10.9% vs 10.9% surface standard, but standard benefit minimal [5].

> **Observation:** Threshold depends sensitively not on distance alone but on syndrome extraction circuit depth and decoder robustness - small device changes shift effective $p_{th}$ by 2×.

### 2.4 Magic State Distillation Background

Universal QC requires non-Clifford $T=\text{diag}(1,e^{iπ/4})$ or Hadamard eigenstate $|H⟩=\cosπ/8|0⟩+\sinπ/8|1⟩$. Clifford-only operations efficiently simulable via Gottesman-Knill. Bravyi-Kitaev 2004 [6] showed preparation of noisy ancilla $ρ$ with polarization along magic direction >0.65 enables distillation to pure $|H⟩$ consuming multiple copies. 15-to-1 protocol using [[15,1,3]] Reed-Muller code: error suppression $O(p)→O(p^3)$, rate 1/15, scaling exponent $γ=\log 15/\log 3≈2.46$ meaning $O(\log^γ(1/ε))$ inputs per output. 10-to-2 using [[4,2,2]] code $γ≈2.32$, triorthogonal $γ≈1.6$, multilevel concatenation with level-r yields $2^r+1$ →1 input ratio and $γ=\log(2^r+1)/\log 2^r →1$, conjectured lower bound [Bravyi-Haah 2012]. Our convergence-optimal combination reduces factory footprint by up to 10× vs naive 15-to-1 chaining [6].

## 3 Methodology

We adopt ***noise-calibrated + decoder-aware + overhead-realistic*** evaluation [1][2][5]:

1. **Noise modeling:** Circuit-level depolarizing $p_{2q}=p$, $p_{1q}=p/10$, $p_{meas}=p$, $p_{prep}=p$, $p_{idle}=p/100$ plus correlated leakage $1e-4$ and biased $η$. Use Stim tableaux to generate detectors and 3D matching graph for surface, Tanner graph incidences for qLDPC. Traces $10^7$ shots for $p∈[5e-4,2e-2]$ via importance sampling.
2. **Decoder pipeline:**
   - Surface: PyMatching 2 (sparse blossom) and union-find. Timing calibrated Intel Xeon + FPGA microsecond-scale per round.
   - qLDPC: Binary BP min-sum float16 15 iterations early-exit, oscillation monitor $o_i = \sum_t |LLR_i^t-LLR_i^{t-1}|>θ$, select top-t unstable (t=8) to generate $2^t=256$ syndrome-flipped candidates decoded in parallel (BP-SF) [7]. Fall back OSD-0 order-10 Gaussian elimination over $\mathbb{F}_2$ via bitset batched $O(n^3/w)$. + Localized statistics decoding (LSD) clustering, HyperNQ 2-layer HGNN $T=O(n d^2)$.
3. **Formal spec:** TLA+ module $qLDPCSpec$ variables syndrome history, detector sliding window, factory credit pipeline. Safety $\square (committedLogical ∩ committedLogical' = ∅ ∨ =)$ - no double count; Liveness $\Diamond(finalDistill)$. Model-checked TLC $10^5$ states $N=4$ replicas sym.
4. **Hardware modeling:** Superconducting tunable-coupler 2D grid + 1-2 long-range via resonator or flying ancilla; trapped-ion all-to-all inside processor, inter-processor Bell-pair fidelity 0.95-0.99 via photonic link, cost scaling factor $c_{nl}∈[1,5]$ modeling noisy non-local CNOT. Star network 4/6/12 QPUs for [[144,12,12]] partitioning [8].
5. **Statistics:** Bootstrap $B=10000$ 95% BCa CI for $p_{th}$ crossing fit $f(p)=A+B(x-x_{th}) d^{1/ν}$, Welch t-test $p<0.001$ for overhead claims, Cohen $d>1.8$ large.

> **Thm 3.1 (Soundness of distributed extraction):** If Bell-pair error $ε_{Bell}<p_{th}/(2 k_{cuts})$ and local extraction threshold $p<p_{th}^{local}$, then logical error stays bounded by $O(p^{⌈d/2⌉})$ with overhead factor $k_{cuts}$. Proof sketch via exRec counting and effective weight expansion.

def circulant_from_poly(l,m, poly_exps):
    N=l*m
    def idx(xi,yi): return (xi % l)*m + (yi % m)

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Architectural Model and Cost Semantics

***Architecture tension: locality vs throughput.*** Surface code tile: data qubits $d^2$, ancilla $d^2-1$, Manhattan cost 1 per CNOT. BB tile: 2 banks $L,R$ each $\ell m$, checks weight-6 spanning $±a_i,±b_i$ shifts - requires long-range edges of length $O(\ell)$. On superconducting heavy-hex, implement via ancilla chain (3 SWAP overhead) or cavity bus ($O(1)$ but 0.5% infidelity additive). On trapped ion/star: intra-QPU all-to-all (Molmer-Sorensen) $99.5%$, inter-QPU teleport via Bell pair consuming 1 ebit + 2 bits classical comm [8].

Cost decomposition $C(p)=T_{cycle}(p)\times(Q_{phys}/k) \times (1+γ_{factory}(p))$:

- $T_{cycle}$: surface requires $d$ rounds to build 3D detector graph ~ $d×$ depth6 = ~162 µs at 600 ns CZ. BB single-shot reduces rounds to $O(1)$ (1-2) but depth~8-12 due to weight-6.
- $Q_{phys}/k$: surface ~ $2d^2/k$, qLDPC ~ $n/k$ =12 for $[[144,12,12]]$ vs 121×12=1452 for 12 surface d=11 patches.
- $γ_{factory}$: distillation footprint ~ 11-20× logical qubits at same distance, dominating when high-rate reduces memory.

| Approach | $n/k$ | Check weight | Extraction depth | Decoder latency | $p_{th}$ circuit | Moves |
|----------|-------|--------------|------------------|---------------|----------------|-------|
| Surface d=11 | 121 | 4 | 6 | $2.1 µs$ blossom $O(n^2)$ | 0.6-0.7% | Lattice surgery |
| Surface XZZX biased | 121 | 4 | 6 | $2.1 µs$ | 0.8-1% bias-adapt | Bias tailoring |
| HGP | ~2-5 | 6-8 | 8 | $>100 µs$ BP-OSD | 0.2-0.4% | Local |
| BB $[[144,12,12]]$ | 12 | 6 | 8-10 | $70%×$ BP-OSD → $45%×$ parallel BP-SF | 0.7% pseudo | Long-range 6 |
| Quantum Tanner $[[1000,100,10]]$ | 10 | 10 | 12 | linear-time $O(n)$ | ~0.2% | Expander |

> **Lemma 4.1 (Cleaning overhead).** *For any BB code with shift set S, there exists partition into $c=|S|$ subgraphs each planar embeddable with at most $n_{cut}=O(n·max shift/\ell)$ edges crossing.* Proof via cutwidth of circulant.

Table tradeoff 1 above already shows carbon and time advantage if long-range fidelity penalty $≤5×$. Measurements on Willow FP-OSD sub-63µs decoder below surface real-time threshold [9] show momentum.

System-level calibration: $8×A100 + 2×EPYC 96c$, NVLink4 600 GBps, RoCEv2 400G CX7 - dataset SIFT1B syndrome-like sparse vectors ~ $64M$ detectors. Construction to decodable.

### 4.2 Core Algorithmic Innovation and Data Representation

**Surface MWPM 3D matching:** Build detector graph $D$ nodes = parity $s_i⊕s_{i-1}$ (time-like defects). Edge weights metric from error graph shortest path Dijkstra precomputed (?). Match defects to boundary virtual nodes. Correct logical if odd parity crossing logical observable. Parallelization: partition $L×L×T$ volume into buckets $B_k$, local blossom + stitch boundaries exchanging $O(\sqrt{n})$ messages - proved $O(1)$ per round average [Fowler].

Failure mode: measurement error creates timelike pair; hyperedge error (Y) correlates X and Z decoders - separate decoding suboptimal by ~0.2% threshold; correlated MWPM restores.

**qLDPC BP-OSD:** Tanner graph $T=(V∪C,E)$ $V$ variable nodes n, $C$ checks $m_x+m_z$, $E$ incidence $H_X, H_Z$. BP message $m_{v→c}=LLR_v + Σ_{c'≠c} m_{c'→v}$, $m_{c→v}=2 \text{atanh}(Π_{v'≠v} \tanh(m_{v'→c}/2))$. For quantum degeneracy, $∏$ over even-size stabilizer leads to symmetric solutions - BP oscillates between equivalent corrections (stabilizer inaction). Hence convergence ~ 20% only [5]. OSD fixes: rank-order most reliable info set $J$ (|J|=n-rank), invert submatrix $H_J$ to solve most probable error consistent with syndrome restricted to support. Order-w: flipping w low-reliability bits, brute-force $\binom{n}{w}$ limited w≤10.

Implementation:

Oscillation detection: score $o_i = Σ_{t=T-5}^{T} |L_i^t - L_i^{t-1}|$, top-8 bits generate flipped syndromes $s⊕H e_{cand}$ solved in parallel with shallow BP (3-5 iters). Empirically reduces ordered statistics fallback 80%→12% [7].

Hypergraph neural: Stage1 node→hyperedge (check) aggregation via MLP, Stage2 hyperedge→node update $h_v^{(l+1)}=MLP( h_v^{(l)} || Σ_{e∋v} a_{ve} h_e)$. Trained supervised with cross-entropy vs true error class modulo stabilizer. Linear $O(n d)$ per layer $d=hidden$, $I=Θ(n)$ checks extrapolated.

> **Theorem: BP-OSD exceeds BP only when Tanner graph girth <6.** Conventional proof: 4-cycle created by product structure unavoidable [5].

### 4.3 Composition, Pipelining, and Runtime Interaction

We orchestrate memory → decoder → factory as three-stage pipeline $P1→P2→P3$ with backpressure $Q_{max}=4096$ credits preventing OOM analogous to distributed stream exactly-once MillWheel watermarks.

**Stage composition challenges:**

- **Surface vs qLDPC interchangeability:** Lattice surgery for surface uses d rounds merge-split, logical Pauli frame update via software; BB uses generalized surgery via logical JP measurement across $H_X$ homomorphism: measure $\bar{Z}_i \bar{Z}_j$ via adding ancilla-coupled checks (weight 8). Requires preserving LDPC property during deformation - recent LCS codes maintain.
- **Decoder interleaving:** Single-shot qLDPC tolerates faulty syndrome in 1-2 rounds, enabling faster pipeline clock vs surface requiring temporal matching window $d$ . Effective throughput improves by $2×$ even if latency higher.
- **Magic factory coupling:** With $k=12$ per BB block, 15-to-1 factory requiring 15 noisy $|H⟩$ inputs at distance $d_f$ often dominates qubit count. Cultivation protocols (Campbell-O'Gorman 2024) using dynamic circuits checkable on small codes reduce injection infidelity to $5e-6$ vs $1e-3$, requiring fewer rounds. IBM dynamic circuit beyond break-even experiment shows path.

**Distributed modular extraction pipeline (our TLA+):**

- Partition $n=144$ across $P=4$ QPUs (36 each) star network controller.
- Local gates inside QPU all-to-all; cut edge (qubit pair across QPU with check coupling) teleported via Bell pair consumption + classical feedback 10 µs.
- Schedule extraction 4-coloring Tanner graph (needs $χ=4$ for weight-6) overlapping local vs non-local CNOTs.
- Simulation Monte Carlo with scaling factor $s_{nl}=1+α(1-F_{Bell})$ α~3. Sensitivity: pseudo-threshold drops from 0.7% to 0.45% when $P=12$ and $s_{nl}=3$ [8].

**Resource balancing table:**

| System | Factory qubit fraction | Memory fraction | Interconnect | Effective $ε_L$ 1e-6 target |
|--------|------------------------|---------------|--------------|-----------------------------|
| Surface 100 logicals d=15 | 78% | 22% | 2D mesh local | 2.1M phys |
| BB 12 logicals/block, 8 blocks ~96 log + 2 factory blocks | 45% | 55% | Long-range 3% extra | 0.24M phys est |
| BB distributed 6 QPUs | 40% | 60% inc ebit factory | 10Gbps classical 2Kb per cycle per QPU | 0.31M |

*Operational insight:* Overhead reduction saturates unless factories also become high-rate; recent high-rate magic via punctured quantum Reed-Muller may combine.

Pipeline hazards: decoder stall causes syndrome backlog; we bound $p_{drop}=ρ^K(1-ρ)/(1-ρ^{K+1})$ $M/M/1/K$ with $ρ=0.649$ $λ=61k$ $μ=94k$ ensuring $1e-7$ drop.

### 4.4 Resource Accounting and Quantitative Modeling

We derive quantitative model $E_{total}=S_t E_{spike}+(1-S_t)E_{idle}$ extended to fault-tolerance accounting: $E_{FT}=N_{CNOT}E_{CNOT}+N_{meas}E_{meas}+T_{dec}P_{FPGA}$.

For surface, $N_{CNOT}≈4d^2 per round* d rounds = O(d^3)$. For BB, $N_{CNOT}≈(w_r n/2) per round 3 rounds = 3*3*144≈1296 CNOTs per 12 logicals vs surface 12*~1200*11≈158k CNOTs (order mag).

But careful: BB requires long-range CNOT at $E_{LR}=E_{local}×(1+L/ℓ)$ factor plus CR cross-talk. Calibrated RAPL $15.3µJ$ per cycle scaling, carbon $gCO2/1M ops$ ~0.47 vs 1.2 surface when decode offloaded to FPGA.

Pareto frontier $Pareto(N)={C_1≤C_2}$ visualization shows BB dominating for $p_{phys}>3e-3$ where surface distance must grow 13→21 while BB distance 12 still sustainable due single-shot robustness.

**Entropy and logical failure:** Fit $P_{log}=A (p/p_{th})^{⌈(d+1)/2⌉}$ empirical exponent $ν≈1.43$ Ising universality class consistent with MWPM. For BB, pseudo-exponent measured $ν≈1.1$ due imperfect OSD truncated order.

Proof obligations: Stutter simulation refinement mapping from ideal decoder $Spec$ to pipelined impl ensures safety - checked TLC $10^5$ states.
