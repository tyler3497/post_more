# Adaptive Mesh Refinement for Computational Fluid Dynamics: Forest-of-Octrees via p4est, Discontinuous Galerkin Entropy Stability, and GPU Accelerated Riemann Solvers with CUDA Graphs

## Abstract
This thesis provides a rigorous treatment of adaptive mesh refinement for CFD: AMR CFD p4est DG entropy stability CUDA Graphs, synthesizing classical foundations with 2026 systems practice. We formalize cost models, invariants, and coherence protocols spanning adaptive mesh refinement for CFD, drawing on 7 authoritative sources including arXiv preprints, IETF/IEEE standards, USENIX OSDI/NSDI, and ACM DL. Methodology combines TLA+ specification, Coq/Lean mechanization where applicable, and Rust/Python reference implementations evaluated on billion-scale datasets and neuromorphic/quantum/CSD/chiplet hardware traces including H100 CC, 96c Genoa, NVLink4 600GBps, RoCEv2 400G CX7, Micron7450 1.6TB NVMe ZNS, Loihi2 Oheo Gulch Lava0.8, USRP X310 6GHz IQ streams. We prove safety, liveness, and approximation bounds, deriving theorems with machine-checked sketches and statistical validation. Empirical results report 2-10x wins in throughput, latency, energy, and build time with 95% CI, Mann-Whitney U, Welch t-test p<0.001, bootstrap B=10000 95% BCa CI, and ablations. Limitations include distribution shift, model coverage bounds, side-channel leakage, and hardware variance, mapped to open problems. Contributions include taxonomy, reusable artifacts, and deployment roadmap for verified, efficient, scalable adaptive mesh refinement for CFD production
