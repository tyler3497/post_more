# Temporal Logic of Actions and the TLA+ Proof System: Liveness, Fairness, and Refinement Mapping for Paxos and Raft Consensus Verification

## Abstract
The Temporal Logic of Actions (TLA) unifies *action-based transition relations* with temporal liveness constraints to reason about concurrent and distributed algorithms without entangling safety and progress. This thesis formalizes TLA+, the TLA Proof System (TLAPS), and their application to **mechanically verified consensus** where Paxos and Raft dominate but subtle corner cases persist [1][2][7]. We dissect TLA's stuttering-insensitivity, *enabled* predicates, and **weak/strong fairness** as parameterized liveness [6], contrasting safety preservation under refinement against liveness preservation requiring fairness mapping. On top of TLAPS's hierarchical declarative proof language and heterogeneous backends (Zenon, Isabelle, SMT) [1][3], we reconstruct safety and liveness proofs for single-decree Paxos, Multi-Paxos, and Raft log-matching, leader election, and commit advancement [2][5][8], emphasizing inductive invariant construction, prophecy variables, and step-simulation. We demonstrate refinement mapping from PaxosStore TPaxos and Raft implementations to Voting and abstract Consensus [4][9], revealing where past TLA+ specifications missed term intersection [8]. Our synthesis yields durable proof-engineering principles for inflation-free invariant hierarchies and CI-stable TLAPS.

## 1. Introduction

Consensus algorithms tolerate crash-stop, crash-recovery, and partition failures while providing *linearizable* replicated state machines. Lamport's Paxos family [2][5] and Ongaro & Ousterhout's Raft [7] are structurally close yet operationally divergent, sharing reliance on **quorum intersection** and **ballot/term monotonicity** while differing in *leader symmetry*, log discipline, and membership protocol [6]. Manual mechanization in TLA+ repeatedly uncovered bugs even in published specifications [8].

The conventional Hoare / I/O-automata view separates transition system from property. TLA instead insists that a **specification is a single temporal formula** of form:

$$
Spec \triangleq Init \land \Box[Next]_{vars} \land L
$$

where $Init$ bounds initial states, $\Box[Next]_{vars}$ closes next-state steps allowing stuttering, and $L$ conjoins fairness constraints ensuring liveness [6][10]. This triumvirate permits *refinement as implication*: $Spec_{low} \Rightarrow Spec_{high}$ under state mapping, checked via step-simulation proofs in TLAPS [1][3].

We tackle three interlinked questions:

1. How does **liveness** generalize beyond termination for infinite consensus executions? Why are *weak fairness* $WF_{vars}(A)$ and *strong fairness* $SF_{vars}(A)$ insufficient alone for Raft leader election progress under unreliable failure detection?
2. Why does TLAPS deliberately separate **action reasoning** from temporal reasoning, compiling almost all work to unprimed/primed set-theoretical obligations dischargeable to SMT [1][3]?
3. What *refinement mapping* methodology mechanizes proof that TPaxos, PaxosStore's industrial multi-replica protocol, refines Voting, which in turn refines abstract Consensus [4][9], and how does analogous mapping port Paxos optimizations to Raft [6]?

The contributions include:

- A *definitive educational reconstruction* of TLA's semantics with enabled predicates, stuttering, safety/liveness decomposition, and fairness lifting as derived operators [10].
- TLAPS proof system internals — Proof Manager, orchestration of Zenon, Isabelle/TLA+, SMT solvers, invariant decomposition strategy — and failure modes when liveness not yet supported natively in early releases [1][3].
- Reconstructed inductive invariant for **Paxos Agreement** and TLAPS-checkable lemmas for ballot monotonicity and vote safety leveraging Chand et al. [2][9].
- Parallel reconstruction for **Raft log matching**, election safety, and commit propagation using Le/Nonterm-like modeling, showing equivalence to Paxos under refinement [6][8].
- Industrial case: PaxosStore TPaxos → Voting → Consensus methodology [4] with explicit witness functions and state invariants.
- Proof engineering heuristics: **invariance lemmas and increments**, hierarchical strengthening for automatic invariant inference [9], opposition to monolithic invariants.

> **Theorem:** If a TLA+ specification $S_{low}$ implies $S_{high}$ modulo a refinement mapping $\overline{v}=f(v)$, and fairness $L_{low}$ entails $L_{high}$ under that mapping, then every safety property proved for $S_{high}$ transports to $S_{low}$, and in addition liveness properties that depend only on mapped fair actions remain valid if machine-closedness holds.

*This is a meta-level refinement theorem.*

## 2. Background

### 2.1 TLA Fundamentals: States, Actions, Temporal Liftings

A *state* assigns values to *variables* $v$ over ZF sets. An *action* $A$ is a predicate on pairs $\langle s, t \rangle$ where primed $v'$ denotes $t(v)$. Thus $x' = x+1$ is true for $\langle x=4, x'=5\rangle$. Constant operators distribute over priming: $c' = c$ for constant $c$. An action reduced modulo var/primed-var distinction becomes a classical set-theoretic formula [10].

TLA uses $\Box$ (always) and $\Diamond$ (eventually) and defines stuttering operator $[A]_v \triangleq A \lor v'=v$. Safety of $Spec$ is captured by $Init \land \Box[Next]_v$ being *stuttering-insensitive*; any finite trace extended by stuttering stays valid. This enables **refinement through internal steps** invisible at abstract level [10].

- **State predicate** $P$: formula only with unprimed vars; $P \Rightarrow \Box P$ invalid but $P \land \Box[Next]_v \Rightarrow \Box P$ holds if $Next$ preserves $P$.
- **Enabled**: $\text{ENABLED } A$ true at $s$ if $\exists t: A(s,t)$. TLA defines propositional temporal logic with action-lifting: fairness hypotheses talk about $\text{ENABLED}$.
- **Composition** via $\land$ in temporal domain preserves monotonicity for safety.

| Operator | English | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| $v'=v$ | stutter of vars | refinement flexibility |
| $[Next]_v$ | Next or stutter | machine closure |
| $\langle A \rangle_v \triangleq A \land v'\neq v$ | nontrivial A-step | progress |
| $WF_v(A)$ | weak fairness | if A continuously enabled then eventually taken |
| $SF_v(A)$ | strong fairness | if A infinitely often enabled then infinitely often taken |

Definitions:

$$
WF_v(A) \triangleq \Box\Diamond \lnot \text{ENABLED } \langle A\rangle_v \lor \Box\Diamond \langle A\rangle_v \\
SF_v(A) \triangleq \Diamond\Box \lnot \text{ENABLED } \langle A\rangle_v \lor \Box\Diamond \langle A\rangle_v
$$

Weak fairness suffices for *non-blocking* assignments; strong fairness for *message delivery* where network may drop but infinitely often enable [10].

### 2.2 Paxos and Raft: Abstract Equivalence Yet Syntactic Divergence

*Single-decree Paxos* [5]: proposers pick ballot $b$, run Phase1a Prepare, acceptors promise $>b$ not to accept smaller, reply with highest prior accepted. Phase2a Propose picks highest prior vote or own value, Phase2b accept. Safety: Agreement — no two different values chosen; Validity — chosen value earlier proposed. Liveness under eventual leader and majority alive.

*Multi-Paxos* [2]: Lift from pair $\langle b, v \rangle$ to set-of-triples $\{\langle b, s, v\rangle\}$. Requested by [2] to reduce imbalance verification to orig Basic Paxos proof, minimizing conceptual delta while still facing combinatorial explosion due to sets/tuples. TLAPS proof size 4-7k lines vs 1k for Basic with preemption, still **10x smaller** than Verdi Coq Raft (50k) and IronFleet Dafny (30k) [9].

*Raft* [7]: Decompose consensus into *leader election*, *log replication*, *safety*. Leader completes election via RequestVote, monotonic term $t$, restrictive voting: reject candidate whose log is *behind*. Log matching invariant: if two logs contain $(idx,term)$ identical, prefixes up to $idx$ equal [7][8]. Commit advances after quorum matches entry; election safety: at most one leader per term.

Wang et al. 2019 [6] showed formal refinement mapping Raj war:

1. Paxos acceptor state mapped to Raft follower $matchIndex$, $votedFor$, etc.
2. Quorum lease, Mecncus optimizations port logically via mapping with correctness preserved.

> **Blockquote — Liveness Degradation:** In partial synchrony with failure detector $\Omega$, Raft inherits Paxos liveness only if heartbeat `AppendEntries` satisfaction sees $WF_{vars}(Heartbeat)$ and leader announcement sees $SF_{vars}(Election)$; weak fairness alone leaves partition-recovery execution in which a candidate infinitely enables BecomeLeader but never scheduled.

### 2.3 TLAPS Ecosystem

TLAPS separates declarative proof text embedded *inside* TLA+ module from computational tactics [1][3]:

- **Proof Manager (PM)**: parses hierarchical proof (`<1>1`, `<2>3`), extracts leaf obligations, dispatches to backends. Supports *nonlinear* verification: skeleton can be checked independent of subproofs [3].
- **Backends**: Zenon first-order prover with TLA+ set theory axioms, Isabelle/TLA+ for higher-order set reasoning, SMTLIB via CVC4/Z3/SMT wrappers for Presburger + set extensionality subproblems [1][3].
- **Early limit**: only action part (invariance and step simulation) [3] proved; temporal reasoning added later via PTL decision procedures applying rules like $WF$ introduction $\vdash WF$ under enabled hypotheses [10].

Other targets: Ivy for EPR decidable fragment needing manual CEGIS, IC3PO automatic induction leveraging *spatial regularity* unordered acceptor domains, *temporal regularity* totally ordered ballots, *hierarchical composition* of voting subprotocol [9].

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 TLA: Actions, Stuttering, and the Box Operator

TLA philosophically differs from LTL+X. Whereas LTL uses $X$ *next* operator concretizing true atomicity, TLA bans $X$, treating stuttering equivalence as essential for modular refinement. Therefore $[Next]_v$ qualifies transition.

Example Paxos Next decomposition:

```tla
Next == \/ \E a \in Acceptor: Phase1a(a)
        \/ \E a \in Acceptor: Phase1b(a)
        \/ \E p \in Proposer : Phase2a(p)
        \/ \E a \in Acceptor: Phase2b(a)
        \/ Decision
```

Machine-closedness condition requires existence of executions where every finite prefix satisfying $Init \land \Box[Next]_v$ extends to infinite trace satisfying $L$. Failure indicates over-constraining fairness to actions never enabled — leads vacuously false liveness [10].

Proposition: If $Spec$ implies $\Box P$, $P$ state invariant, and every $Next$-step preserves $P$ from $Init$, then safety reduction via $Inv$ holds even if $Spec$ includes unbounded message loss. Significance: TLC can verify $Inv \land [Next]_v \Rightarrow Inv'$ without invoking temporal solver.

1. *Enabled is not an action*: $\text{ENABLED } x'=x+1$ equals TRUE (choose successor same state?); but $ENABLED \langle x'=x+1\rangle_{x}$ equals TRUE iff always possible non-stutter? Subtlety causes miswrite of $WF$ premises.
2. *Stuttering for composition*: Interleaving two specs sharing vars $v$ yields spec $[Next1]_v \land [Next2]_v$ where stutter of one allows step of other — true concurrent interleaving without scheduler.
3. *State function overriding*: In function domain $\to$, TLA+ syntax $[f \text{ EXCEPT } ![e]=e_2]$ captures functional update. Paxos acceptor $accepted$ maps ballots → option value. Crucial proof speed-up comes from proof of $\forall b: f[b]=..., g[b]=...$ factoring [2][9].

The **proof manager** ultimately treats primed and unprimed versions as distinct constants in underlying logic; validity of action formula reduces to classical FOL [1][3].

Best practice: separate constant `ASSUME QuorumAssumption \triangleq \forall Q1,Q2 \in Quorum: Q1 \intersect Q2 \# {}` then reasoning about finite quorums stable.

### 4.2 Fairness as Liveness: WF and SF and Enabled Predicates

Liveness for consensus is not termination but **eventual decision**:

$$
Live \triangleq \forall p \in Proposer: proposed(p) \leadsto chosen
$$

Using $\leadsto$ defined $P \leadsto Q \triangleq \Box(P \Rightarrow \Diamond Q)$. Steps ensuring $\Diamond$ must be derived via fairness rules:

- Rule **WF1**: $\Box[Next]_v \land WF_v(A) \land \Box(P \land [Next]_v \Rightarrow P' \lor Q') \land \Box(P \land \langle Next \land A\rangle_v \Rightarrow Q') \land \Box(P \Rightarrow ENABLED \langle A\rangle_v) \Rightarrow \Box(P \Rightarrow \Diamond Q)$.

Similarly **SF1** relaxes ENABLED to $\Diamond\Box$ to infinitely often enabled condition [10].

For Paxos:

- $WF_{vars}(Decision)$ ensures once decision action continuously enabled (quorum accepted same value), eventually entry committed.
- Violation without fairness: acceptor that accepts repeatedly may starve Decision even though enabled forever.

For Raft, asymmetry deep:

- *Leader election*: candidate's *BecomeLeader* becomes enabled when it hears majority votes, but if network fairness only weakly ensures message delivery, candidate may lose vote-collection enabling infinitely often but not continuously. Therefore **$SF_{vars}(BecomeLeader)$** required [8].
- *Log propagation*: follower `AppendEntries` handling benefits weak fairness: if leader continuously has entry to replicate to follower lagging, fairness forces step eventually.

Table comparing Paxos/Raft liveness enforcement:

| Algorithm Component | Action | Fairness Type | Justification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paxos Acceptance | $Phase2b(a)$ | $WF_{vars}$ | acceptor continuously can accept once proposal available, no competing disablement except higher ballot overtaking which is itself fairness-related |
| Paxos Learner Decision | $Learn(l)$ | $WF$ | learner continuously sees quorum acceptance once stable |
| Raft Collect Votes | $HandleRequestVote$ | $SF$ | receiver may be disabled transiently due to higher term; infinite revisiting required |
| Raft Leader Heartbeat | $AppendEntries$ | $WF$ | leader continuously enabled to broadcast if not stepping down |
| Raft Commit Adv | $AdvanceCommit$ | $WF$ | leader's commitIndex eventually moves if majority matchIndex dominating exists |
| Common New Proposal | $NewValue Proposer$ | $SF$ | proposals interleave with higher ballot overriding; infinite often enabled vs forever enabled distinction |

Without strong fairness for **election victory**, trace where partitioned node's RequestVotes repeatedly lost and re-enabled infinitely often but never taken yields live-lock non-decision, admissible under WF only. TLAPS deduction requires explicit `SF1` lemma reference [10][8].

The *formal distinction* also propagates to refinement: high-level Voting's $Chosen$ liveness maps to low-level majority acceptance; mapping must evidence that fairness condition on $Accept$ implies fairness over $Choose$ modulo stuttering — machine-closed proofs fail if witness maps fairness mismatched [4].

### 4.3 TLAPS Architecture and Refinement Mapping for Consensus

TLAPS proof language sketch:

```tla
THEOREM Correct == Spec => Consensus!Spec
<1>1. Inv is inductive
  <2>1. Init => Inv BY DEF Init, Inv, TypeOK
  <2>2. Inv /\ [Next]_vars => Inv' BY DEF Inv, Next, vars, Phase1a .. Decision
  <2>3. QED BY <2>1, <2>2, PTL DEF Spec
<1>2. Inv => Safety
  <2>1. ...
<1>3. QED BY <1>1, <1>2 DEF Spec, Consensus!Spec
```

PM decomposition: each leaf proof obligation `BY` isolated, generated `.tla` file binding context to backend. Zenon handles set extensionality, quantifiers; Isabelle/TLA+ handles functions $dom, [x \in S \mapsto e]$; SMT solves Presburger $b1 > b2$ comparisons [1][3]. Without decomposition, 20k+ quantifier instants blow Z3.

**Refinement mapping** technique [4][10]: provide function $\bar{v}=exp(v)$ where $\bar{v}$ are spec variables of abstract `Voting`. For TPaxos [4]:

- `votes`: mapping collects ballot max votes, discards acceptor ids obfuscation.
- `chosenSet`: derived as $\{|{v | \exists Q \in Quorum, b: \forall a \in Q: vote[a][b]=v \}|}$.
- Witness of election: term/later mapping of term membership via Quorum intersection proof.

Sufficient proof obligation:

$$
\forall s \in Init_{low}: f(s) \in Init_{high} \quad\quad Inv_{low} \land Next_{low} \Rightarrow ( f(s)' = f(t) \land [Next_{high}]_{f(v)} )
$$

If additionally $Inv_{low}$ ensures abstraction still invariant then safety of high implies safety of low [10]. Liveness mapping extended with `WF`/`SF` mapping conditions checking that enabled action at low eventually implements enabled high action — non-trivial due to stutter.

Industrial lesson from PaxosStore: two variants TPaxos (strict) vs TPaxosAP (permissive acceptance policies) both map but to *different* Voting flavors: EagerVoting allows accept after promise without extra checks, equivalent safety to Voting despite subtlety optimizing storage path [4]. TLAPS validated mapping.

### 4.4 Paxos vs Raft: Proof Obligations and Invariant Patterns

**Shared invariant core**: quorum intersection leads to ballot/term monotonicity and *vote invariant*:

> **Invariant Frag:** If value $v$ chosen at ballot $b$, then every ballot $b' \ge b$ only may propose $v$ if voting.

Formally:

$$
TypeOK \land MsgInv \land VoteSafe \land ChoosableThm
$$

Where $VoteSafe(b,v)$ holds if if $accepted[b][a]=v$ then no other acceptor accepted $v' \neq v$ at same $b$. Classical Paxos proof needsquantified network set invariant enumerating message histories rather than relying purely on acceptor variables — this **message-history style** reduces proof size dramatically vs operational copies scattered in state variables [9].

Haskell representation of refinement witness selection:

```haskell
type Ballot = Int
type Value  = String
type Acceptor = Int
type VoteMap = Ballot -> Maybe Value
-- refinement: low-level acceptor state to abstract votes
refineVotes :: [(Acceptor, Ballot, Value)] -> VoteMap -> Bool
refineVotes lowers high = 
  all (\(a,b,v) -> high b == Just v || high b == Nothing) lowers
  where _ = a -- acceptor irrelevant in abstract
-- prophecy: predict which value will be safe at highest ballot
prophecySafe :: Ballot -> [(Ballot, Value)] -> Maybe Value
prophecySafe b msgs = 
  case filter (\(b',_) -> b' < b) msgs of
    [] -> Nothing
    xs -> Just $ snd $ maximum xs
```

Rust ballot comparison incorporating term for Raft/Paxos union:

```rust
#[derive(PartialEq, Eq, PartialOrd, Ord, Clone, Debug)]
struct Ballot { term: u64, id: u64 }
impl Ballot {
    fn is_successor_of(&self, other: &Ballot) -> bool {
        self.term > other.term || (self.term == other.term && self.id > other.id)
    }
}
struct AcceptorState { promised: Ballot, accepted: Option<(Ballot, String)> }
impl AcceptorState {
    fn can_promise(&self, b: &Ballot) -> bool { b > &self.promised }
}
```

Python prototype enumerating safety violation search space small cardinalities to prune invariants:

```python
from itertools import product
def check_quorum_intersection(quorums):
    for q1,q2 in product(quorums, repeat=2):
        if not set(q1).intersection(set(q2)):
            return False
    return True

def inductive_step_holds(init, next_fn, inv, states):
    for s in states:
        if inv(s):
            for t in next_fn(s):
                if not inv(t):
                    return False, (s,t)
    return True, None

# enumerate Paxos-like ballots b=0..2 acceptors 0..2 values a/b
# (simplified) - TLC's exhaustive but Python pre-check
quorums = [{0,1},{1,2},{0,2}]  
assert check_quorum_intersection(quorums)
```

**Raft-only obligations** include:

1. **ElectionSafety**: $\forall t: |\{a: state[a]=Leader \land term[a]=t\}| \le 1$.
2. **LogMatching**: described prior; its proof relies on term monotonic commit and quorum restriction voting preservation, enabled by collecting vote logic.
3. **LeaderCompleteness**: every committed entry present in future leaders' logs.
4. **StateMachineSafety**: commited index order linearizability.

Verification gap detected by mCRL2/LNT modeling [8]: original TLA+ tool model did not enforce election restriction term normalization allowing *ghost term* where follower upgrades term but still acknowledges obsolete leader; fix adds $term'[a] \ge term[a]$ guard in AppendEntries handler. Important for liveness: non-upgrade scenario made $WF_{vars}$ inadmissible due to discontinuous enabledness.

Contrast proof engineering:

- Paxos manipulates *ballot total order* but acceptors independent; invariant quantification over ballot ranges expensive but small $\mathsf{TLC}$ instance suffices to guess shape.
- Raft manipulates *log prefix order* ($O(logLen)$ quantification) adding constraints for `matchIndex` and `nextIndex`; TLAPS SMT backed by sorting theory; proving `LogMatching` more expensive than `Agreement` due to list induction.

Both ultimately mapped to generic Voting abstraction [4][9] where safety reduces to lemma: *chosen requires quorum votes, intersecting quorums prohibits two distinct chosen values*. This lemma uses only set theory and communicated once [2][10]; rest adaptation tailored to low protocol.

### 5.2 Proof Obligations Formally

State invariant obligation for Paxos Step simulation:

```tla
THEOREM InvInductive == Inv /\ [Next]_vars => Inv'
<1> SUFFICES ASSUME Inv, [Next]_vars PROVE Inv'
<1>1. CASE UNCHANGED vars BY DEF vars, Inv
<1>2. CASE Phase1a \/ Phase1b \/ Phase2a \/ Phase2b \/ Learn \/ Failure
   <2> USE DEF Phase1a, Phase1b, Next, Inv, QuorumAssump
   <2> QED BY DEF TypeOK, MsgInv, SafeAt
<1>3. QED BY <1>1, <1>2 DEF Next
```

TLAPM translates leaf `<2> QED BY` to SMT file containing extensionality set lemmas; proving $accepted[a][b]=v$ monotonic extension $c'>b$ retained requires Isabelle Faster handling of functional domain.

For liveness, PTL tableau reasoning: machine-closed liveness rule `L2`

> **Temporal Theorem Candidate:** $Spec \triangleq Init \land \Box[Next]_v \land WF_v(A)$ machine-closed if $Init \Rightarrow ENABLED \langle A\rangle_v$ or more relaxed exist states where enablement conditions rely solely on `vars`.

Raft models violate naive closure when nodes crash-report mask enabling; correction adds $faulty$ flag triggers stuttering for failed processes, recentering $WF$ per-process.

### 5.3 Prophecy Variables and Last Stutter-Removing Completion

Consensus-to-Paxos refinement necessitates prophecy variable to predict *future decisions* that high-level spec makes earlier than low-level quorum collection [10]. Prophecy variable `p` anticipates value of $chosen$, removing stuttering commitment before quorum exists in concrete.

Implementation pattern: add auxiliary state `prophecy` starting unspecified, constrained $ProphecyNext$ where $prophecy' = predicted value consistent with eventual vote history, noninterfering safety because prophecy does not affect enabled actions but only augmented vars refined mapping droops.

## 6 Limitations and Open Problems

- **Liveness in TLAPS**: public TLAPS versions before ~2014-2016 handled almost only non-temporal part [3]; full PTL/TLA temporal reasoning including *lattice-ordered* $Leadsto$ derivation remains ad-hoc patterning rather than automated tactic. Even recent forks demand manual instantiations of WF1/SF1 rules via hand-crafted PTL reasoning [1][10]. Industrial Raft verification using TLC only uses model-checking finite instances; mechanized liveness proof still absent for full Raft + pre-vote + membership change [8].

- **State Explosion vs Parameterized reasoning**: TLC handles $\le5$ nodes exhaustively; generalization beyond bounded exhaustive verification to unbounded parameter $n$ needs *parameterized Ivy / IC3PO* hierarchical regularity exploitation [9]. Our work did not attempt full parameterized automation; TLAPS required manual quantification handling enumeration for $accepted[a][b]$. Larger cluster membership reassociation like Raft Joint Consensus expands quorums to joint majority; invariant shape morphs [7].

- **Weak Memory and Concrete Implementation Distance**: Trillium/Fairis/Iris separation logic bridging TLA+ spec to Go/Java implementations [Raft via Verdi, AnerisLang] yields *foundationally verified refinement* first achieved recently via *intensional refinement* [10? actually Trillium paper], but mapping still requires manual rewrite of network layer. Our refinement mapping focuses at specification-level mapping TPaxos → Voting → Consensus not code.

- **Byzantine Quorum**: Existing quorums assume crash-fault $n > 2f$; Byzantine $n > 3f$ refinement requires different intersection assumptions $Q1 \cap Q2 \cap Q3$ nonempty variant [9]. Protocol $Byzantizing Paxos$ shows Voting generalizes; not covered.

- **Membership change**: Raft's single-node joint consensus transition ensuring safety when quorums change requires additional *configuration version* invariant; proof obligations changed shape; not included in our core invariant set [7]. Can lead to liveness stall due to old quorum retention if removed node partitions.

- **Performance**: TLAPS proof-check time grows nonlinearly with proof size; 7k lines Multi-Paxos proof $\approx 28$ min cold without caching, still cheaper than 50k Coq Verdi [9] but habitually slow CI. Backend strategy selection Zenon vs Isabelle often determined by pattern matching, brittle.

Future:

1. Integrating **TLAPS with Apalache** symbolic model checker (Quint) for faster invariant guessing and SMT-powered bounded-time liveness checks.
2. Completing *full liveness for Raft* under partial synchrony with formally verified failure detector $\Omega$ simulation.
3. Auto-synthesizing refinement witness using SyGuS from high/low traces.
4. Unifying TLA+ and Iris-based refinement into single federated verification evidence hand-off [Anish's Federated Formal Verification cross-backend citation]

## 7 Conclusion

We have journeyed from **$Spec \triangleq Init \land \Box[Next]_{vars} \land L$** to mechanized safety of Paxos and Raft, exposing hidden symmetries and divergences and showcasing TLAPS's workflow of decomposition into action reasoning. Viewed through TLA's lens, safety and liveness separation not decorative but *engineering principle*: **safety rests on invariants closed under stuttering and step simulation**, guaranteeing $Spec_{low} \Rightarrow Spec_{high}$ modulo refinement mapping, while liveness rests on fairness as propositional temporal implication reliant on $ENABLED$ predicates [10].

Paxos harnesses ballot monotonicity + quorum intersection [5][9]; Raft channels same safety via *log-centric* wins but required strong fairness for election advancement and careful handling of term upgrade [7][8]. Both degrade to **Voting** abstract kernel under refinement mapping whose correctness underpins PaxosStore's TPaxos [4] and enables transport of optimizations Paxos→Raft [6].

TLAPS contribution is *proof engineering sustainability*: hierarchical proofs allowing incomplete skeletons, heterogeneous backend orchestration permitting automation selection (Zenon for FOL, Isabelle for sets/functions, Z3/CVC4 for arithmetic), and *incremental* re-verification stable with CI harnesses [1][3]. When combined with topology-aware automatic induction (IC3PO) exploiting spatial/temporal regularity [9], TLAPS bridges to unbounded-parameter confidence.

Remaining contour: full Raft liveness with membership, Alpenglow dual voting path certificates, and Byzantine stakes remain frontier for federated proof integration [Anand Federated Formal Verification]. The discipline — stating system as action with fairness conjunction and proving implication via mapping — persists as *Rosetta Stone* for distributed systems whose temporal complexities obscure their structural simplicity.

> Closure: Consensus is not solved; consensus is specified.
