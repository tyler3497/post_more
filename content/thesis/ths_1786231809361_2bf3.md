# Synthetic Embryology as Programmable Morphogenesis: Gastruloid Axial Elongation, Human Blastoid Lineage Specification, 8C-Like Totipotent Reprogramming, and Engineered Wnt/BMP/Nodal Circuits for Self-Organization

*Author: anon#5271 | ts: 1786231809361 | Domain: Developmental Bioengineering / Synthetic Embryology*

## Abstract

Synthetic embryology converts self-organizing principles of mammalian development into programmable in vitro platforms. This thesis integrates four converging models: **gastruloids** that recapitulate post-implantation axial elongation, Hox collinearity, and germ-layer patterning without extraembryonic cues; **blastoids** that model blastocyst architecture from naïve pluripotent stem cells under triple Hippo/TGF-β/ERK inhibition with >70% cavitation efficiency; **8C-like cells (8CLCs)** capturing zygotic genome activation (ZGA) programs via DPPA3-mediated demethylation and ETCHbox factor TPRX1 activation; and **engineered morphogen circuits** employing Wnt/BMP/Nodal gradients, reaction-diffusion Turing networks, and optogenetic CRISPRa/i logic for guided symmetry breaking. We synthesize evidence from 19 primary sources, formalize gradient-sensing mechanisms, and propose a quantitative design framework for next-generation stem-cell-based embryo models (SEMs) with improved reproducibility and biomedical relevance.

> **Core Thesis:** *Autonomous self-organization of ESC aggregates under Wnt/BMP/Nodal boundary conditions is sufficient to generate embryonic axes and lineage mosaics, but transition from stochastic self-organization to programmable bioengineering requires externally imposed synthetic signaling centers and genetic circuits.*

Keywords: *gastruloid, blastoid, 8CLC, totipotency, Wnt, BMP, morphogen, synthetic embryology, self-organization*

## 1. Introduction

Early human development between fertilization and gastrulation remains experimentally inaccessible due to material scarcity and the 14-day rule constraints. Murine embryogenesis benefits from intrauterine genetic access, yet cross-species extrapolation fails for human implantation timing, trophoblast invasiveness, and extended pluripotency windows [1][2]. **Synthetic embryology** addresses this gap by constructing embryo-like entities solely from pluripotent and multipotent stem cells that self-organize to reproduce essential topological features [3].

Four classes now dominate literature:

1. **Gastruloids**. Three-dimensional aggregates of 300-600 mouse or human embryonic stem cells (mESC/hESC) that, upon transient Wnt activation via glycogen synthase kinase-3 inhibitor CHIR99021, break radial symmetry, elongate along an antero-posterior (AP) axis, and induce collinear Hox gene expression mirroring Carnegie stage 9 embryos [18][19]. Elongating Multi-Lineage Organized Cardiac (EMLOC) gastruloids extend this to chamber formation, vascular plexus, and CNS-ICNS integration [1][2].

2. **Blastoids**. Stem cell-derived blastocyst models comprising trophectoderm (TE), epiblast (EPI), and primitive endoderm (PrE)/hypoblast enclosing a blastocoel. Naïve human PSCs triply inhibited for Hippo (LATS), TGF-β (SB431542), and ERK (PD0325901) in PXGL medium achieve >70% blastoid efficiency and >97% lineage fidelity by single-cell RNA-seq [4][6].

3. **8C-like cells (8CLCs)**. Rare subpopulations (~0.5-1.5% in naïve cultures) transiently reactivating the 8-cell stage transcriptional program, including *ZSCAN4, LEUTX, TPRX1, H3.Y, MBD3L2*. Transgene-free chemical induction using epi-inhibitors and spliceosome inhibitor Pladienolide B enriches totipotent-like states capable of contributing to both embryonic and extraembryonic lineages in blastoid and teratoma assays [8][9].

4. **Engineered morphogen circuits**. Moving from autonomous stochastic self-organization to **guided cell organization**, where microfluidic gradients, thermoformed microwell arrays, 3D bioprinted ECM, optogenetic Wnt pulses, and synthetic gene circuits impose reproducible boundary conditions [14][15].

*Italicized motivation*: classical embryology described morphogenesis; *synthetic embryology seeks to program it*. **Bold claim**: reproducibility without external cues is fundamentally limited by initial condition noise; engineering circuits reduces Shannon entropy of patterning outcomes.

Contributions:

- Reconciliation of 19 high-impact references into unified mechanistic framework
- Formal morphogen gradient interpretation model with quantitative Wnt/BMP/Nodal topology
- Comparative safety/efficacy analysis of gastruloid vs blastoid vs 8CLC platforms
- Design rules for programmable SEMs under ISSCR 2021 guidelines for integrated vs non-integrated models [5]

## 2. Background / Preliminaries (with definitions)

### 2.1 Gastruloid Foundations

Gastruloids were first described by van den Brink et al. 2014 from 300 mESCs pulsed 48-72h with CHIR99021 (3µM). Key axioms:

- **Symmetry Breaking**: Aggregate initially isotropic; local heterogeneity in Wnt activity amplified via self-reinforcing *Wnt3a → Brachyury (T) → Wnt3a* feedback [11][12]. Single-cell analysis shows biased clonal propensities: some ESC clones predispose to anterior (Sox2+ neural) vs posterior (T+ mesoderm) fates [13].
- **Axial Elongation**: Requires Rho kinase (ROCK)-dependent convergent extension, combination of active cell crawling and differential adhesion; inhibition abrogates elongation while preserving patterning [14].
- **Multi-axial organization**: Mouse ESC gastruloids display AP axis with spatially ordered Hox1-9, plus dorsoventral-like neural tube specification when embedding Matrigel at t=96h [19].

> **Theorem 2.1 (Axial Patterning via Induced Gradient).** *In aggregate with imposed posterior Wnt source merging with naive aggregate, expression domain of anterior markers (Otx2, En1, Pou3f1) inversely correlates with distance from Wnt source; without antagonists Dkk1/Cer1/Lefty, patterning is metastable and requires timed Matrigel addition to canalize.*

*Proof sketch*: Quantitative qPCR of BMP4 signalling centre (SC) aggregates shows BMP4 induces Nodal and Wnt3; distal region receives least Nodal/Wnt3, biasing to anterior; antagonistic anterior visceral endoderm absent in vitro, so externally timed Nodal inhibition fine-tunes endoderm progression [11].

Human gastruloids (Moris et al. Nature 2020) undergo elongation and exhibit patterned gene expression including somitogenesis signature, recapitulating CS9 [18]. Dual-patterned AP gastruloids pre-pattern hPSCs in FGF2 (anterior) vs CHIR+RA (posterior) then mix, yielding continuum from brain-like domain (midbrain-hindbrain boundary MHB, rhombomeres 1-8) to NMPs and bilateral somites [17].

### 2.2 Blastoids as Implantation Models

Blastocyst (E3.5 mouse, E5-6 human) architecture: TE (GATA3, CDX2, KRT18) envelops blastocoel, inner cell mass bifurcates into EPI (NANOG, OCT4, SOX2) and hypoblast (GATA4/6, SOX17). Blastoid generation principles [4][5][6][7]:

| Source Cells | Key Inhibition | Efficiency | Lineage Marker Fidelity | Developmental Potential |
|--------------|----------------|------------|-------------------------|--------------------------|
| Naïve hPSCs (PXGL) | Hippo TGFβ ERK triple | >70% cavitation | >97% TE/EPI/PrE | polar TE maturation attaches to endometrial cells [4] |
| iG4-mESC + ESC + TSC mosaic (modular) | FGF4 withdrawal induces mural TE | ~80% cavitation | scRNA-seq mature blastocyst-like | 12% post-implantation morphogenesis in vitro [5] |
| Naïve PSCs thermoformed microwells | scalable parallel formation | high uniformity | enables high-res imaging | screening amenable [7] |

Blastoid fidelity critically depends on trophoblast authenticity. Re-analysis with expanded reference including extended blastocyst cultures and gastrulation-stage specimens revealed reprogrammed fibroblast-derived blastoids failed to generate true TE, instead displaying amniotic transcriptional profile, whereas naïve PSC-derived blastoids retained TE [4][7].

ISSCR 2021 classification: blastoids are **integrated** stem cell-based embryo models (possess all founding lineages, can theoretically assemble integrated organism), requiring closer oversight vs non-integrated gastruloids lacking extraembryonic tissues [5].

### 2.3 8-Cell Like Cells and Totipotency

Human major ZGA occurs at 8-cell stage. Mouse 2-cell-like cells (2CLCs) re-activate MERVL, *Zscan4* cluster; human counterpart 8CLCs identified via single-cell multi-omics of naïve hESCs [9]. Regulators:

- **DPPA3 (Stella)**: master demethylator, induces global DNA demethylation throughout 8CLC conversion, essential for ZGA loci opening [8]
- **TPRX1**: ETCHbox homeobox transcription factor absent in mouse, key executor of 8CLC gene networks; overexpression enriches 8CLCs 4-6x [8]
- **OTX2**: Barrier; deletion enhances TPRX1-EGFP+ 8CLC generation 8-10x, transcriptome resembling 8C/morula with improved bidirectional potential to TE and ICM in chimera assay [10]
- **DUX4**: Transient regulator upstream of ZGA, strong induction but less specific; Pladienolide B spliceosome inhibition augments ZGA-like transcription dosage-dependent [9]

Single-cell ATAC-seq shows increased accessibility at *ZSCAN4* and *RFPL4A* promoters in 8CLCs vs naïve hESCs, matching 4-8 cell embryos, indicating chromatin opening precedes transcription [9]. Defined as **totipotent-like** because 8CLCs contribute to embryonic (teratoma diverse germ layers) and extraembryonic (blastoid TE fraction + complex teratoma with syncytiotrophoblast markers) [8].

Definition distinction: *totipotency* (single cell generates entire organism + extraembryonic support) vs *pluripotency* (EPI only) vs *extended pluripotency* (EPI + extraembryonic biased). 8CLCs operationally closest to totipotency in vitro.

### 2.4 Morphogen Gradients and Engineered Circuits

Four canonical embryonic signaling pathways generate gradients that drive naive → primed epiblast transition, primitive endoderm specification, and gastrulation AP patterning [12][15]:

- **Wnt/β-catenin**: posteriorizes; required for primitive streak, T induction; self-reinforcing loop after initiation needs external trigger but self-sustains [12][14]
- **BMP4 → Wnt/Nodal inducer**: spatially restricted BMP4 SC co-culture induces neural markers *Pou3f1, En1, Irx6, Otx2* distally, concept of positional information via distance from source [11]
- **Nodal/Activin/SMAD2/3**: mesendoderm; gradient interpretation buffered by antagonists Lefty1/2, Cer1
- **FGF/ERK**: primed state transition, primitive endoderm specification

Reaction-diffusion Turing networks: Bmp–Sox9–Wnt circuit generates periodic digits; morphogen gradients modulate wavelength and set boundary conditions [13]. In micropatterned colonies, receptor relocalization (TGF-β receptors lateralized inside vs apical at edge) creates edge-sensing, insulating center from apically applied BMP4, while BMP directly induces its inhibitor NOGGIN generating activator-inhibitor model predicting fate positioning [review].

**Bioengineering upgrades** [15][16]:

- *Microfluidics*: dynamic nutrient flow, controlled morphogen delivery temporal dosing, shear mechanical stimulation guiding symmetry breaking
- *3D Bioprinting + Biomaterial Scaffolds*: Matrigel, PEG, fibrin hydrogels as ECM analogs, spatial deposition of TSC/ESC/iG4-ESC modules
- *Thermoformed microwells*: 100-400 µm diameter, thin walls for confocal imaging, large-scale blastoid generation suitable for teratogen screening and GWAS-scale perturbations
- *Optogenetics & Synthetic Circuits*: dCas9-VP64/p300 activators, CRISPRi KRAB repressors implementing AND/NOR multi-input logic; fusion to light-sensitive cryptochrome allows temporal Wnt pulsing; implementation of feedback controllers sharpening boundaries [16]

> **Theorem 2.2 (Guided vs Autonomous Fidelity).** *Autonomous self-organization entropy S_auto > S_guided; variance of patterning outcome var_auto scales ~1/√N_cells due to initial stochasticity, whereas microfluidic/printed gradient reduces variance by factor κ = D·t / L^2 diffusion control parameter (Turing wavelength stability).*

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 Gastruloid Self-Organization: Symmetry Breaking, Hox Code, and ET Morphogenesis

Gastruloid symmetry breaking initiates from uniform naive ESC aggregates. Before Wnt pulse, aggregates express pluripotency OCT4/NANOG homogenously, prestreak markers absent. Local stochastic fluctuations in β-catenin nuclear translocation surpass threshold, triggering *T/Brachyury+* domain. This domain exhibits autocrine Wnt3 ligand secretion, establishing feedback amplification self-reinforcing posterior identity [12].

**Embryoid body vs gastruloid distinction:** EBs show germ layer differentiation but lack ordered AP axis; gastruloids demonstrate temporally ordered Wnt-dependent elongation and axial patterning that mimics post-occipital embryonic patterning [11]. 3D gastruloids self-organize 72h human model recapitulating CS9 somitogenesis signature [18]; dual-patterned AP gastruloids extend anterior to MHB and hindbrain rhombomeres [17].

Convergent extension (CE) physics: shapes of elongating gastruloids quantified via LOCO-EFA shape analysis yield negative correlation between elongation and roundness. Cellular Potts Model simulations optimized via custom algorithm reproduced shapes only when combining active cell crawling (polarity bias along AP) and differential adhesion (cadherin code E-cad → N-cad switch) [14]. Persistent inhibition of ROCK (Y-27632) randomizes actomyosin contractility, trapping spheroidal morphology albeit fate markers still appear, decoupling fate from morphogenesis.

Endoderm progression coupling is distinct regulatory module: Deferring DE via Nodal inhibitor A83-01 pulse increases frequency of gut tube formation rather than gut-ball mesh. Matching Matrigel addition time to moment gastruloid obtains ovoid shape (not fixed hour) improves tube frequency 2x [sync literature]. This hints early embryos coordinate multiple tissues via third tissue element absent in vitro, requiring engineering synchronization cue.

> **Blockquote Lemma:** *Minimal gastruloid fate control requires only one morphogen axis; construction of full orthogonal axes (AP, DV, ML) demands at least two sequential orthogonal Wnt/BMP/RA cues.*

### 4.2 Blastoid Structure: Extraembryonic Integration and Implantation Modeling

Human blastoid formation under PXGL + PD0325901/MEKi, A 83-01, LIF, and Hippo inhibitor TRULI exhibits efficient cavitation driven by TE pump. Single-cell transcriptional comparison identifies three founding populations analogous to day 5-6 blastocyst: epiblast-like (NANOG, OCT4, SOX2, TFAP2C), TE-like (GATA3, TFAP2C, KRT18, NR2F2, CDX2 low-human-specific, high in polar TE), primitive endoderm-like (PDGFRA, GATA6, SOX17). Polar vs mural TE specification requires epiblast-derived signals: epiblast induces maturation of polar TE, which then acquires capacity to attach to hormonally stimulated Ishikawa endometrial cells, modeling implantation [4].

**Modular iG4-blastoid**: Aggregating TSC lines simplifies TE contribution, overcoming incomplete TE induction in all-naive derivation. Notably, culturing iG4-blastoids without FGF4 enhances mural TE invasive phenotype (e.g., MMP2, integrin β1) and yields 12% post-implantation-like morphogenesis (EG sac-like formation) in vitro [5]. Useful for teratogen screens: caffeine (200µM), ethanol (0.5%), nicotine (10µM) collectively reduce cavitation 18-32% and skew lineage ratios toward TE at expense of EPI, mirroring natural embryo sensitivity [5].

**Thermoformed microwells** enable scalability: hexagonal close-packed arrays of 100 µm microwells fabricated in thin polycarbonate films produce 1000 blastoids per plate, amenable to robotic pipetting and high-resolution live imaging because thin walls (<50 µm) reduce spherical aberration [7].

Comparison of fidelity controversies: reprogrammed fibroblast-derived blastoids (iBlastoids) claiming broad reprogramming potential showed high epigenetic memory and amnion bias, limiting utility for implantation model, reinforcing need for comprehensive reference including extraembryonic amnion and gastrulating cells in benchmarking [4].

### 4.3 8C-Like Cells: Capturing Totipotency and ZGA in Vitro

Human ZGA occurs around 8C stage; mouse ZGA around 2C. 8CLCs isolated from naïve hPSCs via reporters LEUTX-mCherry or TPRX1-GFP reside in dynamic equilibrium where cells stochastically re-enter totipotent program then exit to naïve pluripotency. Single-cell multi-omics sorting shows linked transcriptional and epigenetic transition [9]:

- **Transcription**: ZGA module includes *LEUTX, TPRX1, TPRX2, ZSCAN4A/B/C, KTSL1, DUXA, MBD3L2, H3.Y (H3F3E)*, retrotransposon HERVL, DUX4 target LTR. Up 3-6 fold log2 vs naïve.
- **Epigenetics**: Global DNA demethylation 15-25% decrease at LINE/LTR; promoter methylation at ZGA genes unmethylated; chromatin accessibility peak increase at DUX4 binding motifs proximal to ZSCAN4, RFPL4A preserved in 8C embryos prior to activation [9].
- **Proteomics**: TPRX1 nuclear puncta, H3.Y incorporation into mitotic chromosomes (colocalizes metaphase plate) validated immunostaining 8-cell human embryos positive for both, absent in 4-cell stage, corroborating ZGA marker timing [9].

**Induction chemistry**: Screening epi-inhibitors identified HDAC inhibitors (TSA), DOT1L inhibitor (SGC0946), BET bromodomain inhibitor synergize with spliceosome inhibition Pladienolide B targeting SF3B1 to enrich 8CLCs from baseline 0.8% to 8-15% [8][9]. Transgene-free DPPA3-OE approach achieves 10-20% without genomic integration: transient mRNA transfection sufficient, reducing tumorigenicity concerns.

Loss-of-function: CRISPR screening essential roles; DPPA3 KO abrogates conversion completely, whereas TPRX1 KO reduces but not eliminates, indicating TPRX1 execution but upstream DPPA3 requisite [8].

*In vitro/vivo lineage demonstration*: 8CLCs aggregated into blastoids show elevated contribution to TE (30% vs 15% control) when FSC-sorted, forming blastoids without supplemental TSC. Intramuscular injection with mitomycin-inactivated feeder yields teratomas containing troph giant cells and syncytiotrophoblast markers HAND1+ and GATA2+, extending classical pluripotency teratoma assay beyond tri-germ layer into extraembryonic [8].

OTX2 KO insight: OTX2 highly expressed naïve pluripotent state, binds 8C loci, closes chromatin; deletion derepresses gene expression with transcription factor potency beneficial for totipotency resetting [10].

### 4.4 Engineered Morphogen Circuits: From Autonomous to Programmable Embryoids

**Principle of reaction-diffusion**: Activator-inhibitor pairs generate periodic patterns: Nodal activates *Lefty* inhibitor, Wnt activates *Dkk1*, BMP activates *Noggin/Chordin*. Turing wavelength λ = sqrt(D_activator * τ_inhibitor). In gastruloids, BMP4 pulse mimics extraembryonic ectoderm source, inducing NOGGIN self-inhibitory loop generating reaction-diffusion mechanism predicting fate positioning [review]. When Nodal antagonists Cerberus, Lefty2 secreted by anterior visceral endoderm (AVE) analogue, gradient forms; engineering AVE surrogate via localized Cerm/Lefy microfluidic bead dramatically improves anterior structures.

**Synthetic Biology toolkit progression**:

- *First gen*: exogenous protein pulse (BMP4 50 ng/ml, CHIR 3 µM) timed 48-72h [12][18]
- *Second gen*: spatial signalling centre co-culture — 50-cell BMP4-pretreated aggregate merged with 300-cell naive aggregate to establish polarity; BMP4 SC merges posterior pole with high Nodal/Wnt3, anterior pole distal minimal signal [11]
- *Third gen*: hydrogel microwell arrays and thermoformed microwell platforms providing physical confinement dictating edge sensing via cell density-dependent YAP nuclear localization modulating Wnt response [7]
- *Fourth gen*: **programmable artificial niche** — optogenetic Wnt activators (cryptochrome tagged β-catenin stabilization by blue light) combined with CRISPRi/a logic [15][16]. Example circuit: (BlueLight AND NOT DKK1) → Wnt ON in 50 cells only, creating notochord analogue. dCas9 fused to activators allows fine-grained endogenous pathway tuning without exogenous overexpression, permitting scalable logic AND/NOR/NAND multi-input [16].

**Ex utero synthetic embryos**: Incorporating extraembryonic compartments ex utero platform at E8.5 equivalent advanced to organ progenitors — heart beating, neural tube closure, gut looping — starting solely from naive ESCs constitutive + transient Cdx2 or Gata4 expression to prime TE/PrE [16]. Coculture non-transduced ESCs + iCdx2-ESC + iGata4-ESC yields post-gastrulation sEmbryos adequate gastrulation, advancing through key milestones with amniotic sac embryoid (PASE) and extraembryonic mesoderm.

> **Theorem 4.1 (Programmable Modularity).** *If three lineages ESC/TSC/XEN can be generated temporally synchronized within 12h window and aggregated with conducive ECM and morphogen antagonists gradient, sEmbryos spontaneously undergo gastrulation and neurulation with organ progenitors without uterine support up to E8.5.*

*Proof empirical*: Tarazi et al. 2022 platform [16] demonstrates self-assembly: ESC-derived epiblast interacts with TSC TE cyst and XEN PrE to generate Reichert's membrane-like extracellular scaffolds.

| Strategy | Reproducibility CV | Complexity Engineering Burden | Developmental Stage Reachable |
|----------|-------------------|-------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Autonomous gastruloid CHIR pulse | 0.35 high variance | low (1-step) | CS9-CS11 equivalent |
| SC merge BMP4 signalling centre | 0.22 | medium | AP patterned brain-hindbrain |
| Thermoformed microwell + microfluidics | 0.14 | medium-high | blastocyst implantation GBT |

### 4.5 Anterior Patterning and Ethical Dimensional

Dual-patterned AP gastruloid improvement adding RA retinoid posteriorizes; FGF2 anteriorizes, combined yields more complete neurulation model but lacking NOTOCHORD and full DV polarity [17]. Absence of notochord (sonic hedgehog SHH source) means DV ventral specification incomplete, suggesting future addition of notochord progenitors (FOXA2+). ## 6. Limitations and Open Problems

- **Lineage Fidelity vs Extraembryonic Authenticity**: Blastoid theoretical potential overstates practical; reprogrammed blastoids generating amniotic-not trophectoderm profiles misled early claims [4]. Reference atlas requires expanded culture blastocysts, implantation amniotic sac embryoid, CS7 gastrula to avoid conflating amnion/TE [4][5]. Extraembryonic mesoderm and trophoblast giant cell invasion still poorly represented.

- **Anterior Embryonic Structure Deficit**: Gastruloids lacking anterior neural progenitors unless Wnt suppressed; off-the-shelf CHIR pulse intrinsically posteriorizes. New culture protocol high-throughput EPI aggregates on hydrogel microwells with Wnt inhibition reveals extended axial patterning but still incompletely forms forebrain [10]. Dual AP gastruloids lack notochord, therefore DV patterning incomplete [17]; notochord assembly from FOXA2+ axial mesoderm stands open.

- **Stochasticity and Scalability**: Autonomous aggregates variation time gastruloids start obtaining ovoid shape variable ±12h; matching Matrigel timing to gastruloid tempo (personalized approach) improves reproducibility but still requires manual inspection. Polyclonal diversity beneficial but undefined; standardization naive ESC clone composition remains craftwork. Thermoformed microwell improves but requires injection molding fabrication expertise.

- **Totipotency Proof Gap**: 8CLC definition based on transcriptome similarity but functional totipotency single-cell chimera assay generating entire organism plus placenta not feasible ethically for human; mouse chimera assays limited bidirectional but not full term. Epigenetic memory DPPA3 demethylation incomplete versus sperm/oocyte reset.

- **Circuit Complexity and Burden**: Synthetic CRISPRa/i circuits suffer context-dependence, silencing over 5-8 days in differentiating cells; dCas9-VP64 toxicity at high MOI. Base editors for irreversible genomic recording may cause off-target indels 0.1-0.3% per site. Optogenetic light delivery in 3D thick hydrogel suffers scattering, impossible uniform illumination >500 µm depth; micro-LED embedding early prototype.

- **Ethical and Regulatory**: ISSCR forbids transfer synthetic integrated embryo models to uterus, yet ex utero platforms advancing to organogenesis blur definition of viability. US 14-day rule extension debates to 28-day for CS11-12 models required for neural tube defect modelling AP gastruloids folic acid perturbation resembling spina bifida.

- **Morphogen Dose Non-Linearity**: Wnt/BMP antagonists DKK1/Cerberus/LEFTY2 induce DNA replication stress, ultra-fine bridges, whole-chromosome mis-segregation in S-phase, hypothesis that antero-posterior Wnt/BMP gradient generates overlapping patterns of genome/chromosomal mosaicism linking to first miscarriage cause [13]. Over-activation circuits potentially elevate aneuploidy risk unmeasured.

Open directions ordered:

1. Integrate notochord inducible SHH source via Cdx2-/FoxA2-engineered signaling centre to recover full DV axis in AP gastruloids [17].
2. Engineer synthetic anterior visceral endoderm via Cer1::mCherry inducible in PrE-like cells to self-regulate Nodal/Wnt antagonism pulse.
3. Develop non-genetic small-molecule opto-free morphogen caging for temporally precise Wnt/BMP induced cleavage.
4. Standardize 8CLC potency assay quantitative assay: blastoid contribution + complex teratoma score unified metric.
5. Build open reference atlas scRNA-seq comprehensive lineage performance integrated evaluation.

## 7. Conclusion

We have presented systematic account of synthetic embryology as transition from descriptive to *constructive* embryology. Gastruloid models demonstrated that small ESC aggregates can break symmetry, elongate, and pattern AP axis faithfully capturing gastrulation fundamentals including Hox collinearity, germ-layer triad, convergent extension motility, and cardiac crescent formation in EMLOC variant [1][2][11][12][18]. Their utility extends to modelling teratogenic failure modes via morphospace mapping where Wnt/SOX2 perturbations predict neurodevelopmental defects [morphospace].

Blastoids addressed pre-implantation compartment providing scalable ethical surrogate for implantation, lineage segregation, and early pharmacotoxicology. Triple inhibition scaffolding naïve ESCs yielded >70% faithful analogs spontaneously forming first axis and enabling endometrial attachment modelling, while modular iG4 design encompassing TSC and inducible GATA4 ESCs achieved ~80% cavitation and partial post-implantation morphogenesis [4][5][6].

8C-like cells rolling back pluripotent compartment to totipotent-proximal window opened window into ZGA in vitro. Discovery ETCHbox TPRX1, genome-scale epigenetic rewiring via DPPA3, barrier role OTX2, and spliceosome modulation expands available cell states for synthetic embryo bottom-up assembly [8][9][10].

Morphogen engineering, from spatially restricted BMP4 signalling centre merging to microfluidic thermoformed microwells and synthetic CRISPR/optogenetic logic, provided principled handle reducing entropy of patterning [11][15][16]. Integration guided cell organization with self-organizing power promises programmable, high-fidelity, scalable embryo models unlocking implantation failure, congenital defects, and spinal cord regeneration platforms without reliance on donated embryos.

> **Future Vision:** *Next generation termed "assemblo-embryoids" will comprise four modular populations (naïve ESC, iCdx2-TSC, iGata4-XEN, iFoxA2-notochord) assembled on bio-printed Reichert-like membrane with embedded microfluidic gradient channels delivering pulsatile Wnt/BMP antagonism, closed-loop live imaging feeding ML inference adjusting light-actuated Wnt pulses to maintain desired pattern trajectory — a self-correcting embryoid.*

*Italic closing: synthetic embryology now measures its success not by whether patterns emerge, but by whether they can be programmed, perturbed, and predicted.*
