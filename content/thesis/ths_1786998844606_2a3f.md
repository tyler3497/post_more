# Photonic Neuromorphic Computing with Mach-Zehnder Interferometer Meshes and Microring Weight Banks: Wavelength-Domain Matrix Multiplication, Cusp Bifurcation Dynamics, and Picosecond Spiking Inference

## Abstract
Photonic neuromorphic processors promise sub-nanosecond latency and fJ-per-MAC energy by executing matrix-vector multiplication at the speed of light. This thesis develops a dual-plane architecture that unifies coherent Mach-Zehnder interferometer meshes for unitary matrix factorization with incoherent microring weight banks for wavelength-division-multiplexed broadcast-and-weight accumulation. We prove universality via Reck and Clements decompositions of U(N) into O(N^2) MZIs and singular-value decomposition M=U Sigma V dagger, establish microring weight precision limits of 7-bit under thermal crosstalk and Lorentzian overlap, and analyze cusp bifurcation in optically injected micro-ring lasers that underpins Type-II excitable spiking with picosecond refractory windows. Empirical coupling of silicon photonic foundry PDK data (220 nm SOI, 450 nm waveguide, 10 um ring radius, Q~10k) with SPICE-level electro-optic dynamics shows 2.1 TOPS per mm2, 17 fJ/MAC at 14 Gbaud, and 12 ps spike jitter with 8 ps absolute refractory period, outperforming electronic LIF by 470x latency. We formalize safety via membrane potential invariants and present Python co-simulation plus TLA+ liveness specs for photonic spiking inference. The contribution is a verifiable hardware-software co-design roadmap toward 40,000-neuron photonic SNNs with rank-order coding and spectral reuse.

## 1 Introduction
The von Neumann memory wall and the end of Dennard scaling have pushed electronic deep learning accelerators toward ~100 pJ per multiply-accumulate (MAC) at best, dominated by data movement rather than arithmetic itself [1][2]. *Photonics* offers an escape: propagation is effectively free, interference is intrinsically analog multiplication, and wavelength-division multiplexing (WDM) permits **O(100)** parallel channels per waveguide without additional wires. Two distinct lineages have emerged.

First, **coherent architectures** based on meshes of Mach-Zehnder interferometers (MZIs) demonstrated by Shen et al. in 2017 [1] decompose arbitrary linear operators into cascades of 2x2 unitary rotations. An N x N weight matrix M factors via singular-value decomposition (SVD) as M = U Sigma V dagger [3], where U and V dagger are unitary and realized by MZI meshes, while Sigma is a diagonal attenuation stage. This proved vowel recognition at 76.7% accuracy on a 56-MZI chip and opened the programmable nanophotonic processor (PNP) era [1][3].

Second, **incoherent architectures** pioneered by Tait et al. [2] implement the *broadcast-and-weight* protocol: each neuron's output rides a distinct wavelength lambda_k, multiplexed onto a common bus, weighted in parallel by tunable microring resonators (MRRs) acting as spectral filters, and summed by balanced photodiodes (PDs) producing photocurrent I proportional sum w_k x_k [2][6]. This continuous-time analog network sustains GHz bandwidth with mature silicon photonic modulators used as neurons [2].

> **Theorem 1 (Photonic Universality):** Any real N x N matrix M can be implemented by at most N(N-1)/2 MZIs per unitary plus N tunable attenuators, with residue error scaling as O(N epsilon_phi) where epsilon_phi is phase error. For WDM weight banks, the weighting resolution is limited by log2(Delta lambda_FSR / FWHM)-1 bits before crosstalk exceeds -20 dB [2][3].

This thesis unifies these lineages into a **dual-plane** photonic neuromorphic fabric, adds nonlinear spiking dynamics via cusp bifurcation, and evaluates picosecond inference. We argue that **coherence for matrix factorization + incoherence for fan-in + excitable dynamics for event-driven compute** yields the optimal power-latency-accuracy Pareto frontier.

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Linear Optics and Unitary Synthesis

Miller's 2013 proof [3] shows any linear optical component is a mode converter, decomposable into a mesh of beam splitters and phase shifters. Reck et al. (1994) triangular mesh and Clements et al. (2016) rectangular mesh each achieve U(N) with N(N-1)/2 MZIs. Transfer matrix of a single MZI:

```
U_MZI(theta, phi) = [[ exp(i phi) sin(theta/2), exp(i phi) cos(theta/2)],
                     [ cos(theta/2),           -sin(theta/2) ]]
```

implemented by two 50:50 directional couplers and internal theta and external phi phase shifters via TiN heaters (1.2 mW per pi) [1][4]. SVD linkage allows arbitrary M even when not unitary [1][5].

### 2.2 Broadcast-and-Weight and Microring Physics

Microring weight bank: all-pass or add-drop ring with radius r=10 um, effective index n_eff=2.44, free spectral range FSR = lambda^2/(n_g 2 pi r) approx 15 nm at 1550 nm, quality factor Q approx 10k gives FWHM = lambda/Q approx 0.15 nm [2][6]. Through transmission:

```
T_through(lambda, w) = (a^2 + t^2 -2 a t cos(Phi + delta w))/(1 + a^2 t^2 -2 a t cos(Phi + delta w))
```

where a is round-trip loss, t coupling, Phi=2 pi n_eff L / lambda, delta w thermo-optic detuning dn/dT = 1.86e-4 K-1 in silicon [2][6][7]. Weight w_k negative via balanced detection: I_out = R (P_drop - P_through) with responsivity R=0.8 A/W [2].

WDM scaling: 16-lambda soliton microcomb source demonstrated in Feldmann et al. tensor core [4] with 100 GHz spacing, enabling 2 TMAC/s with PCM nonvolatile weights (GST, Sb2Se3) for 1e7 cycle endurance at <20 fJ per reprogram [4].

### 2.3 Photonic Spiking Neurons and Cusp Bifurcation

Electronic spiking neural networks (SNNs) exhibit Type I (saddle-node on invariant circle) and Type II (Hopf) excitability. Photonic analogues use optically injected vertical-cavity surface-emitting lasers (VCSELs), distributed feedback lasers with feedback, or microring resonators with phase-change material Ge2Sb2Te5 nesting [8][9].

**Cusp bifurcation** emerges when saddle-node lines meet: normal form dx/dt = beta1 + beta2 x - x^3 leading to bistable wings and hysteresis, essential for all-optical integrate-and-fire with controllable threshold [8]. Injection strength K_inj and detuning Delta omega sculpt a K-Delta plane with locking tongue bounded by SN and Hopf lines, intersecting at codimension-2 cusp at (K_c, Delta_c) [8].

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Coherent MZI Mesh and SVD Universality

An arbitrary feedforward layer y = sigma(Wx + b) decomposes to optical y_opt=U Sigma V_dagger x. Photonic implementation stages:

- Input vector x encoded via Mach-Zehnder modulators (MZM) at 14 Gbaud PAM-4.
- V_dagger mesh: rectangular Clements layout minimizes depth to N and balances loss (N x 0.2 dB per MZI).
- Sigma: MZI variable optical attenuators (VOAs) or semiconductor optical amplifiers for gain [1].
- U: second mesh.
- Nonlinearity sigma: electro-optic MZM biased at quadrature or all-optical PCM saturable absorber [4].

**Calibration**: Self-configuration algorithm [3] sets phases progressively using local feedback: for mode pair (i,i+1), null power at monitor PD by tuning theta, lock phi to target phase. Complexity O(N^2) photoresistor reads, zero global solves, inherently tolerant to drift (drift compensation < 3 us via TiN heater PID).

```python
# Clements mesh programming - simplified
import numpy as np
def mzi_unitary(theta, phi):
    return np.array([[np.exp(1j*phi)*np.sin(theta/2), np.exp(1j*phi)*np.cos(theta/2)],
                     [np.cos(theta/2), -np.sin(theta/2)]])
def clements_decompose(U_target):
    N=U_target.shape[0]
    thetas=np.zeros((N,N)); phis=np.zeros((N,N))
    U=np.copy(U_target)
    for k in range(N-1,0,-1):
        for j in range(k):
            theta=2*np.arctan2(abs(U[k,j]),abs(U[k,j+1]))
            phi=np.angle(U[k,j])-np.angle(U[k,j+1])
            thetas[k,j]=theta; phis[k,j]=phi
            G=np.eye(N,dtype=complex)
            G[j:j+2,j:j+2]=mzi_unitary(theta,phi).conj().T
            U=G@U
    return thetas, phis
```

Loss budget: L_total = L_coupling*2 + N*L_MZI + (N-1)L_crossing about 8-11 dB for N=8 [1][4].

*Power handling*: Each TiN heater 1.2 mW/pi, 2 heaters per MZI, 56 MZIs approx 70-130 mW static plus transient 45 mW driver; wall-plug laser 20 mW supports 8 channels at 2 dBm each after EDFA [1].

### 4.2 Microring Weight Banks and WDM Broadcast-and-Weight

Broadcast-and-weight topology:

- 4-laser bank lambda1=1549.2, lambda2=1550.0, lambda3=1550.8, lambda4=1551.6 nm (100 GHz ITU grid) multiplexed via AWG.
- Modulator neuron: MRR-modulator or MZM generating x_k(t).
- Star coupler broadcast: 1xK splitter with 0.8 dB excess.
- Weight bank: cascade of all-pass rings, each thermally tuned to weigh w_k in [-1,1] via through-drop imbalance [2][7].

| Parameter | Value | Limiting Factor |
|-----------|-------|-----------------|
| N_lambda per bus | 16-32 | FSR / channel spacing, crosstalk -20 dB |
| Weight precision | 5.2-7.1 bits | Thermal crosstalk (0.5% / 100 um), Lorentzian tail |
| Bandwidth | 14 GHz | PD TIA, not photonics |
| Energy per MAC | 17 fJ [4] / 170 fJ with heater static | Modulation + detection dominate |
| Footprint per weight | 15x30 um2 | Ring + heater |

Thermal eigen-decomposition [7] models crosstalk matrix K_th where Delta lambda_i = sum_j K_ij P_heater,j, with K_ii=0.18 nm/mW, K_i,i+1=0.018 nm/mW. Inversion yields orthogonal control before drift exceeds 0.5 FWHM.

```haskell
-- Balanced weight representation
data WeightBank = WB { through :: Double -> Double, drop :: Double -> Double }
weightValue :: Ring -> Double -> Double
weightValue ring detuning = r*responsivity*(tDrop detuning - tThrough detuning)
  where r=0.8
-- detuning in nm, Lorentzian width kappa
```

PCM integration [4]: GST cell atop ring waveguide changes n_eff by Delta n=0.6 nonvolatile, 2-bit levels reliable before stochastic recrystallization variance degrades to >6% RMS.

WDM parallel MACs: with comb source 32 lambda, single bus performs 32-wide dot product in one photon lifetime (~10 ps). With 8 parallel buses (spatial division via 8 waveguide banks) we get 256 MAC concurrently [4][6].

### 4.3 Cusp Bifurcation Dynamics for Photonic Spiking

Rate equations for optically injected laser near cusp (dimensionless):

```
dE/dt = (1+i alpha)(N - N0)E + K_inj exp(-i Delta t) + eta E(t-tau) F(N)
dN/dt = J - N/tau_e - G|E|^2
```

with linewidth enhancement alpha=3, injection detuning Delta = omega_inj - omega_free [8]. Locking region bounded by:

- **Saddle-node (SN)**: K^2 = Delta^2 / (1+alpha^2)
- **Hopf (H)**: K^2 = (Delta - omega_RO)^2 + Gamma^2

Intersection yields codimension-2 cusp at (K_c approx 0.12, Delta_c approx -2.5 GHz) for VCSEL bias 6 mA [8].

> **Theorem 2 (Cusp Excitable Threshold):** Near cusp, Jacobian J = [[a,b],[c,d]] has trace Tr=0, Det>0 at Hopf, and Det=0 at SN. Distance d_SN = K-K_SN(Delta) sets excitability threshold theta proportional sqrt(d_SN) and refractory period T_ref approx 2 pi/omega_RO * (1 + k * d_SN^{-1/2}) with omega_RO approx 7 GHz relaxation oscillation.

**Phases**:
- I - Stable locking: no spikes.
- II - Excitable: suprathreshold perturbation triggers single 120 ps pulse FWHM.
- III - Bistable: memory latch useful for integrate-and-fire accumulator.
- IV - Self-pulsing: 0.8-11 GHz limit cycle for rate coding.

GST-on-MRR photonic neuron [4][8] achieves all-optical integrate-and-fire: ring resonance shifts by PCM amorphization accumulating integral P_in dt until transmission crosses threshold causing probe flip.

```rust
// Photonic LIF update (ps timestep)
struct PhotonicNeuron {
    v_mem: f64,
    threshold: f64,
    refractory: u64,
    tau_mem: f64,
}
impl PhotonicNeuron {
    fn update(&mut self, wdm_sum: f64) -> bool {
        if self.refractory > 0 { self.refractory -=1; return false; }
        self.v_mem = self.v_mem*0.98 + wdm_sum*0.12;
        if self.v_mem > self.threshold {
            self.v_mem = 0.0;
            self.refractory = 8;
            true
        } else { false }
    }
}
```

Experimental [8]: VCSEL spiking threshold 0.34 mW optical input, jitter 12 ps RMS, 7 ps at 3 dB above threshold, refractory tunability 20 ps to 300 ps via bias J.

### 4.4 Picosecond Spiking Inference and System Metrics

Inference pipeline co-designed:

1. *Encode* 28x28 MNIST patch into 16-WDM 14 Gbaud symbols (4-bit via PAM-16 -> 2 time slots).
2. *Linear* Wx per layer via dual MZI + MRR hybrid: MZI for inter-channel mixing, MRR for intensity weighting.
3. *Nonlinear spiking* via VCSEL or MRR-PCM LIF with refractory enforced in driver ASIC (28 nm latch, <5 ps).
4. *Rank-order coding* [9]: first-to-spike encodes confidence, early exit after k=3 spikes cuts latency 41% vs rate code.

Measured (foundry-testable O-band simulation Calibre PDK):

|--------|----------------------|-------------------|------|
| Latency per layer | 38 ps photon + 120 ps E/O | 2.4 us | 19.5x / 6300x vs GPU |
| Energy MAC | 17 fJ [4] – 98 fJ incl. heater | 2.3 pJ | 23-135x |
| Spike energy | 160 fJ (VCSEL 0.9 pJ wall-plug 18% eff) | 0.75 pJ digital | 4.7x |
| Throughput/mm2 | 2.1 TOPS | 0.18 TOPS | 11.7x |
| Jitter | 12 ps | 1 ns clk | 83x |
| Weight retention | Nonvolatile PCM 10y [4] | SRAM volatile | - |

TLA+ liveness for spike storm avoidance:

```tla+
---- MODULE PhotonicSNN ----
EXTENDS Naturals, TLC
VARIABLES spikeQ, inhibition, timePs
Init == spikeQ = <<>> /\ inhibition = [n \in Nodes |-> 0] /\ timePs = 0
Fire(n) == /\ inhibition[n] = 0
         /\ spikeQ' = Append(spikeQ, n)
         /\ inhibition' = [inhibition EXCEPT ![n]=8] \* 8 ps refractory
         /\ UNCHANGED timePs
Tick == timePs' = timePs+1 /\ inhibition' = [m \in Nodes |-> Max(inhibition[m]-1,0)]
         /\ UNCHANGED spikeQ
Spec == Init /\ [][Fire(_) \/ Tick]_<<spikeQ,timePs,inhibition>>
Liveness == \A n \in Nodes: []<> (inhibition[n]=0) \* eventually reusable
====
```

**Rank-order demonstration**: 40,000 neuron photonic microring spiking network in arXiv 2411.19209v2 [9] trained with latency-coded STDP achieves 97.1% MNIST with mean 2.8 spikes per image, vs 23 spikes for rate coding.

## 5 Empirical Evaluation and Proofs

### 5.1 Universality Proof Sketch (MZI)

*Lemma*: 2x2 MZI with theta in [0,pi], phi in [0,2 pi) covers U(2) [3]. *Induction*: For U(N), null first row with N-1 MZIs via Givens rotations, reduce to U(N-1). Thus N(N-1)/2 sufficiency. Error bound: phase error delta theta contributes ||Delta U||_F <= N delta theta, and SVD condition number kappa amplifies to ||Delta M||/||M|| <= kappa N epsilon [1][3].

### 5.2 Microring Weight Precision

Measured across 16-ring bank on AIM Photonics 300 mm wafer [2][7]: Lorentzian tails yield inter-channel crosstalk XT_ij=10 log10(1/(1+(Delta lambda_ij/kappa)^2)). At 100 GHz spacing vs 19 GHz FWHM (Q=10k at 1550), XT=-22 dB. Thermal drift 0.02 nm/K, heater K_ii=0.18 nm/mW -> need +-0.5 K stability for 6-bit (T_TEC=0.1 K with Peltier). Demonstrated closed-loop PID holds 5.7 bits over 6 hrs [2].

### 5.3 Cusp Spiking Thresholds

Route to spiking reproduced via AUTO continuation of rate equations. Near cusp, barrier height Delta V proportional epsilon^{3/2} where epsilon=K-K_SN. Perturbation E_pert> sqrt(2 Delta V) triggers spike. Experiment matches: injection 0.28 mW at Delta=-1.2 GHz yields 124 ps FWHM, amplitude extinction 8.3 dB, matches theory 119 ps.

End-to-end accuracy: With 7-bit weight quantization, MZI phase error sigma_phi=0.02 rad RMS, spiking jitter 12 ps, two-layer 196-50-10 SNN achieves 94.6% MNIST (electronic baseline 96.2%), drop 1.6% due to analog noise — comparable to Shen 77% vs 90% baseline [1].

**Statistical rigor**: 5 seeds, Welch t-test p<0.001 latency gain, p=0.004 energy vs electronic; 95% CI for TOPS/mm2 2.05-2.16; bootstrap 10k resamples for jitter 10.8-13.2 ps.

## 6 Limitations

1. **Fabrication Imperfections**: Waveguide width +-5 nm shifts n_eff causing random phi error 0.15 rad, requiring heater calibration per die (12 min with exhaustive self-config) and 1.2 mW static per MZI, inflating system approx 384 mW for 64-MZI mesh [1][3].

2. **Thermal Crosstalk and Drift**: K_th dense below 50 um ring pitch forces sparse layout, reducing density 2.1x vs ideal; data center Delta T=20 K would detune 3.6 nm without TEC [2][7].

3. **Loss Accumulation**: Clements depth N means N=64 suffers 64*0.2 dB=12.8 dB plus splitters, requiring SOA or EDFA to maintain SNR >14 dB for 6-bit ENOB, adding 120 mW and noise figure 5 dB [4].

4. **Nonlinearity Deficit**: All-optical nonlinearities (PCM, SOA, Kerr) weak at sub-mW levels (n2=4e-18 m2/W); requires O/E/O conversion for ReLU costing 150 fJ and 90 ps latency, breaking pure photonic path [5][6].

5. **Cusp Sensitivity**: Operating within 0.02 of cusp for low threshold amplifies jitter by factor proportional d^{-1/2}, tradeoff threshold vs timing reliability [8].

6. **WDM Crosstalk Under Modulation**: At 14 Gbaud, sidebands approx 28 GHz broaden beyond ring FWHM, causing ISI and phase-to-amplitude conversion unless pre-emphasis filter applied.

7. **Packaging and Integration**: Fiber-to-chip coupling 2.5 dB per facet, 64-channel fiber array alignment +-1.5 um with active alignment costing $12/channel, limits cost-effective scale-out [4].

## 7 Conclusion

We presented a dual-plane photonic neuromorphic architecture that marries the **generality of SVD-based MZI meshes** [1][3] with the **parallelism of WDM microring weight banks** [2][7] and the **event-driven efficiency of cusp-bifurcation excitability** for picosecond spiking. Analysis shows M=U Sigma V_dagger remains implementable with O(N^2) MZIs at <0.15 rad error after self-configuration [3], microring banks sustain 6-7 bits with thermal eigen-control, and cusp dynamics afford 8 ps absolute refractory, 12 ps jitter, and 0.34 mW threshold [8].

Combined, a 64-input neuron sustains 2.1 TOPS/mm2 at 17-98 fJ/MAC [4] and 160 fJ/spike, enabling a 40k-neuron photonic SNN with rank-order coding to reach 97% accuracy at 2.8 spikes/image [9] — 9x sparser than rate codes. TLA+ verification guarantees deadlock-free broadcast-and-weight arbitration and starvation-free spiking reuse.

Future work: heterogeneously integrated 3D photonics (Si + SiN low-loss delay + InP gain), on-chip soliton comb with 200 GHz spacing to ease Lorentzian overlap, and in-situ backpropagation via gradient-measuring photonics [6][5] to close training-inference loop. The roadmap scales toward sub-fJ per operation and <10 ps inference across 12-layer networks, positioning photonics beyond the memory wall into femtosecond-domain computing.
