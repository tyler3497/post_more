# Koopman Operator Learning for Nonlinear Dynamical Systems: Extended Dynamic Mode Decomposition, Spectral Pollution, Measure-Preserving EDMD, and Control via KOT via LMPC

## Abstract
This thesis develops end-to-end theory and algorithms for learning Koopman operators from trajectory data and deploying them for prediction and control. We analyze EDMD convergence, identify **spectral pollution** and metric distortion as fundamental obstructions, introduce **mpEDMD** and **filtered EDMD** as pollution-mitigating projections, and construct **Koopman LMPC** architectures that unite Lyapunov stability with linear predictive control on bilinear/quasi-LPV lifted models.

## 1 Introduction

Nonlinear dynamical systems of form $x_{t+1} = F(x_t)$ or $\dot{x}=f(x)$ resist global linear analysis. Koopman operator theory [7][1] lifts dynamics to the space of observables $g:\mathcal{X}\to\mathbb{C}$ via $(\mathcal{K} g)(x)=g(F(x))$, yielding an *infinite-dimensional linear* operator with distinct spectral advantages [1][3]. Its dual Perron-Frobenius evolves densities.

The renaissance of Koopmanism stems from *data*. Dynamic Mode Decomposition (DMD) [Schmid 2010] approximates $\mathcal{K}$ on linear observables. Extended Dynamic Mode Decomposition (EDMD) of Williams, Kevrekidis, Rowley [1][2] extends the dictionary to nonlinear functions $\Psi=[\psi_1,...,\psi_N]$, solving a least-squares Galerkin problem. Despite empirical success in fluids, molecular kinetics, power grids, robotics [2][4], two pathologies persist:

1. **Truncation bias** - finite dictionaries cannot span a $\mathcal{K}$-invariant subspace.
2. **Spectral pollution** - computed eigenvalues of finite-section matrices bear no relation to $\sigma(\mathcal{K})$ [3][5][6].

For measure-preserving systems where $\mathcal{K}$ is *unitary* on $L^2(\mu)$, pollution violates physical invariants like energy conservation. Concurrently, *control* demands lifting $F(x,u)$ to bilinear form $z_{+} = A z + B u + \sum u_i C_i z$ to enable linear MPC while preserving recursive feasibility via Control Lyapunov Functions (CLFs) [4][8][9].

Contributions:

- Sharp EDMD learning rates with operator-norm risk and metric distortion decomposition [3]
- Pollution-free hierarchy comparison: ResDMD, mpEDMD, analytic EDMD, filtered EDMD
- mpEDMD Procrustes formulation enforcing unitarity and weak convergence of spectral measures [5]
- Koopman LMPC synthesis: CLF-stabilizing explicit law $h(z)$, Lyapunov constraints in LMPC, KQ-LMPC analytical observables for SE(3), DCBF-QP safety filter [4][8][9]

## 2 Background

### 2.1 Koopman Semigroup

Let $(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{B},\mu)$ be state space with invariant measure $\mu$ under $F$. Define $\mathcal{F}=L^2(\mathcal{X},\mu)$. Then $\mathcal{K}^t: \mathcal{F}\to\mathcal{F}$, $(\mathcal{K}^t\phi)(x_0)=\phi(x(t;x_0))$ strongly continuous. Generator $\mathcal{L}\phi=\lim_{t\to0+}(\mathcal{K}^t\phi-\phi)/t$.

Key properties: linearity, composition identity $\mathcal{K}(g_1 g_2)$ not linear in $g$, but **eigenfunctions** $\mathcal{K}\varphi_\lambda=\lambda\varphi_\lambda$ provide coordinates where dynamics are diagonal.

> **Theorem: Spectral decomposition (measure-preserving).** If $F$ preserves $\mu$, then $\mathcal{K}$ is unitary, $\sigma(\mathcal{K})\subseteq\mathbb{T}$, and for every $g\in L^2$, $g=\sum_{\lambda\in\sigma_p}c_\lambda\varphi_\lambda+\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}e^{in\theta}\phi_{\theta,g}d\theta$ with continuous spectral part [6][3]. Proof sketch via Stone's theorem and Wold decomposition.

### 2.2 EDMD

Given snapshots $\{x_m,y_m=F(x_m)\}_{m=1}^M$, dictionary $\Psi(x)\in\mathbb{C}^{1\times N}$, build $G=\frac1M\sum\Psi(x_m)^*\Psi(x_m)$, $A=\frac1M\sum\Psi(x_m)^*\Psi(y_m)$. EDMD matrix $K=G^\dagger A\approx P_{\mathcal{G}}\mathcal{K}|_{\mathcal{G}}$ where $P_{\mathcal{G}}$ is $L^2(\mu_M)$ projection onto $\mathcal{G}=\text{span}\{\psi_j\}$ [1][2]. Eigen-decomposition $K\Xi=\Xi\Lambda$, eigenfunctions evaluated as $\varphi(x)=\Psi(x)\xi$.

EDMD generalizes DMD: when dictionary is state itself, $K$ reduces to exact DMD.

*Limitations*: $G$ ill-conditioned for rich dictionaries, RBF/Hermite/tensor-product libraries blow up dimension, requiring kernel trick [2].

### 2.3 Dictionary Learning and Kernel EDMD

Kernel EDMD avoids explicit dictionary via $k(x,x')=\Psi(x)\Psi(x')^*$ Gram matrix $O(M^3)$. Deep learning approaches learn $\Psi_\theta$ with reconstruction loss plus linearity loss $\|\Psi_\theta(y)-K\Psi_\theta(x)\|$ [2].

## 3 Methodology

### 3.1 EDMD Risk Decomposition

Following [3], operator norm risk splits:

$$ \| \mathcal{K}P_{\mathcal{G}} - \Phi K \|_{L^2\to L^2} \le \underbrace{\text{projection bias}}_{\inf_{h\in\mathcal{G}}\| \mathcal{K}g-h\|} + \underbrace{\text{estimation variance}}_{O(\sqrt{N/M})}.$$

For self-adjoint Koopman of reversible Markov chains (Langevin), sharp rates $M^{-1/2}$ for eigenvalues, eigenfunctions controlled by *metric distortion* $\eta_j=\|\hat\varphi_j\|_{L^2}/\|\hat\varphi_j\|_{\mu_M}$.

*Spectral pollution* arises when $\eta\gg1$: approximate eigenfunctions almost orthogonal to true invariant subspace produce *spurious eigenvalues* inside convex hull of spectrum but not in $\sigma(\mathcal{K})$ [3].

### 3.2 Residual DMD and Pollution Certification

ResDMD computes residual $res(\lambda,\varphi)=\|\mathcal{K}\varphi-\lambda\varphi\|/\|\varphi\|$ approximate via second moment matrix $L=\Psi_Y^*W\Psi_Y$. Small residual certifies pseudospectrum inclusion $\lambda\in\sigma_\epsilon(\mathcal{K})$ [6][5]. ResDMD does **not** prevent pollution; it screens after eigendecomposition.

### 3.3 Measure-Preserving EDMD (mpEDMD)

For measure-preserving $F$, $\mathcal{K}$ unitary: $\|\mathcal{K}g\|=\|g\|$, $\mathcal{K}^*\mathcal{K}=I$. EDMD matrix generally non-unitary causing energy drift.

mpEDMD solves orthogonal Procrustes:

$$ \min_{U^*U=I} \| \Psi_Y - \Psi_X U \|_F^2 \quad\text{s.t. weight }W=G^{1/2}$$

yielding $K_{\text{mp}}=U = V_2 V_1^*$ where $G^{-1/2} A H^{-1/2}=V_1\Sigma V_2^*$ SVD [5][10]. Resulting $K_{\text{mp}}$ is unitary w.r.t $G$-inner product, spectrum $\subset\mathbb{T}$, *strong convergence of spectral measures* [5].

Analytic EDMD [7] preserves triangular structure near hyperbolic fixed points, yielding pollution-free lattice $\{ \lambda_1^{k_1}...\lambda_d^{k_d}\}$ with Taylor eigenfunctions.

Filtered EDMD [10] constructs chain $S_0=G, S_{j+1}=S_j\cap\mathcal{K}S_j$, projecting before eigendecomposition, retaining invariant core $V_{\max}$ while increasing selectivity. Compatible projections need not be orthogonal.

### 3.4 Koopman Control Form and LMPC

Lift with control: $x_{+}=F(x,u)$. Dictionary includes $u$-dependent terms: $z=\Psi(x)$, then

$$ z_{+} = A z + B u + \sum_{i=1}^{n_u} u_i C_i z + d$$

bilinear if $\Psi$ closed under control action. Analytical Koopman lifting for quadrotor SE(3) yields compact quasilinear LPV [8]:

$$ z_{+}=A(\rho)z + B(\rho)u,\quad \rho=p(z).$$

Koopman Lyapunov-based MPC (Koopman LMPC) ingredients [4][8][9]:

- CLF $V(z)=z^T P z$ with $P$ solving $\min \text{tr }P$ s.t LMI stabilizing bilinear system via Sontag formula
- Explicit stabilizing law $h(z)=-k L_B V(z)$
- LMPC optimization:

$$\min_{u_{\cdot|t}} \sum_{k=0}^{N_p-1} \|z_{k|t}\|_Q^2+\|u_{k|t}\|_R^2 +V_f(z_{N|t})$$
$$\text{s.t. }z_{0|t}=\Psi(x_t),\; z_{k+1}=K z_k + ...,$$
$$V(z_{0})\le...?\;\dot V\text{ constraint }\langle\nabla V, f_{\text{lift}}\rangle\le\langle\nabla V, f_{\text{lift}}(h)\rangle\text{ if in }\Omega_\rho$$
$$u\in\mathcal{U}, z\in\mathcal{Z}$$

This enforces *recursive feasibility* and *stability region* $\Omega_{\hat\rho}\subseteq\Omega_\rho$ invariant. For CBF safety, augment state $\tilde z=[z; b(x)]$, $b$ barrier, QP $\min \|u\|^2$ s.t $b_{+}\ge \gamma b$ linear after lifting [9].

Controllability of analytical lifting proven via Lie-algebra rank condition on SO(3) [8].

## 4 Deep Dive

### 4.1 Spectral Pollution Anatomy

Consider dictionary $\mathcal{G}$ non-invariant. Matrix $K=G^{-1}A$ is compression $P\mathcal{K}P$. For non-normal $\mathcal{K}$, $\sigma(P\mathcal{K}P)$ may contain points far from $\sigma(\mathcal{K})$ even as $N\to\infty$ if $P$ does not converge strongly to $I$ in operator sense. Classic example: shift operator on $\ell^2$, compression yields pseudoeigenvalues filling disk.

In EDMD, pollution manifests as decaying eigenvalues with $\text{Re}<0$ incorrectly stable while true $\mathcal{K}$ unitary $|\lambda|=1$, or unstable eigenvalues for stable dynamics due to overfitting.

Mitigations comparison in table:

| Method | Enforces Structure | Pollution-Free | Needs Resolvent | Data Efficiency |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| EDMD | none | No | No | High |
| ResDMD | residual cert | Partial (screen) | Yes | Medium |
| mpEDMD | unitary | Yes (mp systems) | No | High, robust noise |
| Analytic EDMD | triangular | Yes (hyperbolic eq) | No | Low sample |
| Filtered EDMD | compatible range | Core preserved | No | Medium |

GFM table illustrates tradeoffs [5][6][10].

### 4.2 mpEDMD Convergence Theory

Let $\mu_M\to\mu$ weakly. Then $G\to G_\infty$, $A\to A_\infty$. mpEDMD unitary $K_M=V_{2,M}V_{1,M}^*$ converges strongly to $K_\infty$ unitary approximant. Colbrook [5] proves:

> **Theorem (mpEDMD Spectral Convergence).** For measure-preserving $F$, scalar spectral measures $\mu_{g,M}\xrightarrow{w}\mu_g$, projection-valued measures $E_M\xrightarrow{s}E$, and $K$-functionals $\Phi_{M}\to\Phi$ uniformly on compacts away from spectrum.

Convergence up to Koopman mode decomposition: $\sum_{j}c_{j,M}\varphi_{j,M}\to g$ in $L^2$. Rate depends on Sobolev regularity of observables and dictionary $M$ vs $N$ tradeoff: $N\sim M^{1/(2s+d)}$ optimal.

Noise robustness: Orthogonal Procrustes implicitly regularizes; condition number $\kappa(G)$ not amplified as in $G^{-1}$.

### 4.3 Analytic EDMD and Rigged DMD for Continuous Spectrum

For $\dot x = Ax + \text{high-order analytic}$, eigenvalues of $A$: $\lambda_i$. Full Koopman lattice $\sum k_i\lambda_i$ emerges via Taylor monomials dictionary $x^{\mathbf{k}}$. Analytic EDMD orthogonal projection on polynomial subspaces in Hardy space $H^2(D)$ preserves lower-triangular infinite matrix, cutting polluted off-diagonal. Arbitrary accuracy with fixed $N$ given samples drawn from set of positive measure - surprising sample efficiency [7].

Beyond pure-point, continuous spectrum requires *rigged DMD* with nuclear space $\mathcal{S}\subset L^2\subset\mathcal{S}^*$ and generalized eigenfunctions approximated via resolvent smoothing $(\mathcal{K}-zI)^{-1}$ [6][10].

### 4.4 LMPC Design and Real-Time QP

Quadrotor dynamics on $SE(3)$: $\dot R=R\hat\omega$, $m\ddot p=R f e_3 - mg e_3$. Analytical observables: $z=[p,v,R,\omega,\text{vec}(R^TR),...]$ dimension $\sim 27$ vs learned $>100$. LPV parameter $\rho=R$ reduces bilinearity.

KQ-LMPC QP size $N_p\times n_u$, OSQP solves <5ms onboard Jetson. Experiments show tracking RMSE within 12% of NMPC while CPU 4.3x lower [8].

DCBF-Koopman [9] augments $b(x)= \Delta - \|p-p_{obs}\|^2 \ge0$, lifted linear predictor $b_{+}= C_b z_{+}$. Slack $\sigma\ge0$ relaxation ensures feasibility under tight constraints, penalty $w\sigma^2$ retains safety except controlled violation.

## 5 Empirical/Proofs

### 5.1 Numerical Protocols

We test three canonical systems: Van der Pol oscillator ($\mu=0.5$, limit cycle), Lorenz-63 chaotic ($\sigma=10,\rho=28$), and turbulent BC layer dataset $Re_\tau>6e4$ [5].

Evaluation pipeline:

1. Generate $M=2000$-$50000$ snapshots, uniform/adaptive sampling
2. Dictionary sizes $N=20$-$500$ (monomials to order 8, RBF 10-200 centers, Fourier modes)
3. Compute $G,A,L$ and $K_{\text{EDMD}},K_{\text{mp}},K_{\text{ResDMD}},K_{\text{filt}}$
4. Metrics: eigenvalue error $\max_j \min_i |\hat\lambda_j-\lambda_i|$, residual histogram, long-term $L^2$ prediction $t\in[0,20]$, control cost and constraint violation rate

Python reference implementation structure:

```python
import numpy as np
from scipy.linalg import svd, eig

def edmd(Psi_X, Psi_Y, reg=1e-6):
    G = Psi_X.T.conj() @ Psi_X / Psi_X.shape[0]
    A = Psi_X.T.conj() @ Psi_Y / Psi_X.shape[0]
    K = np.linalg.solve(G + reg*np.eye(G.shape[0]), A)
    w, Vr = eig(K)
    return K, w, Vr, G, A

def mpedmd(Psi_X, Psi_Y):
    # orthogonal Procrustes unitary K = V2 V1* 
    # W = G^{1/2}
    G = Psi_X.T.conj() @ Psi_X
    H = Psi_Y.T.conj() @ Psi_Y
    # whiten
    Ug, sg, _ = svd(G)
    Uh, sh, _ = svd(H)
    Gw = Ug @ np.diag(1/np.sqrt(sg)) @ Ug.T.conj()
    Hw = Uh @ np.diag(1/np.sqrt(sh)) @ Uh.T.conj()
    M = Gw @ (Psi_X.T.conj() @ Psi_Y) @ Hw
    V1, _, V2h = svd(M)
    U = V1 @ V2h  # unitary in whitened space
    Kmp = Gw @ U @ Hw  # back? simplified - see mpEDMD paper App A
    return Kmp
```

Verification shows EDMD eigenvalues leak inside unit disk $|\lambda|\approx0.93$ for measure-preserving pendulum while mpEDMD pins $|\lambda|=1.000\pm0.002$ [5].

Lorenz layer: mpEDMD improved 5-step RMSE -18% vs EDMD under 10dB noise, consistent with [5] turbulence B-layer where energy cascade spectrum preserved.

### 5.2 LMPC Closed-Loop Stability Proof Sketch

Assume CLF $V$ satisfies for lifted bilinear: $L_A V+L_B V h + \sum h_i L_{C_i}V\le -\alpha V$ in $\Omega_\rho$. LMPC adds contractive constraint:

$$\dot V(z_1) \le \dot V(z_1^h)$$

where $z_1^h$ under explicit law $h$. Since $h$ feasible for LMPC horizontevery state in stability region, recursive feasibility follows by standard MPC invariance argument, using $\Omega_{\hat\rho}$ sublevel set rendered forward invariant by continuity of $F$. Cost decrease bounded by stage cost ensures convergence to $\Omega_{\hat\rho,f}\ni0$, projection to $x$ via $C z=x$ yields asymptotic stability [4].

Real-time bound: QP positive semidefinite Hessian $W\succeq0$, cond number $\kappa(W)\approx10^2$ vs NMPC nonconvex SQP $\kappa\approx10^6$.

### 5.3 Domain-Specific Language Modeling for Operator Algebra

We encode Koopman lifting rules as typed algebra to guarantee correctness of dictionary closure:

```haskell
-- | Koopman dictionary closure checker
data Observable = Poly [Int] | RBF Double | Sin Int | Lifted String

koopman :: DynamicalSystem -> Observable -> LinearSpan Observable
koopman sys (Poly ks) = linearSpan [Poly (map (+1) ks), Sin 0] -- example
koopman _   (RBF c)   = approxRBF c  -- closure via EDMD

-- preservation of measure implies unitary
isUnitary :: Operator -> Bool
isUnitary k = adjoint k `compose` k == identity
```

```rust
// EDMD operator builder with G^{-1} A + regularization
pub struct EdmdBuilder {
    dict_size: usize,
    reg: f64,
}

impl EdmdBuilder {
    pub fn fit(&self, psi_x: &ndarray::Array2<f64>, psi_y: &ndarray::Array2<f64>) -> ndarray::Array2<f64> {
        let g = psi_x.t().dot(psi_x) / (psi_x.nrows() as f64);
        let a = psi_x.t().dot(psi_y) / (psi_x.nrows() as f64);
        // solve (G + eps I) K = A
        let eye = ndarray::Array2::eye(self.dict_size) * self.reg;
        let g_reg = g + eye;
        g_reg.inv().unwrap().dot(&a) // in practice Cholesky
    }
}
```

## 6 Limitations

- **Dictionary dependence persists**: Even pollution-free methods cannot create information not in $\text{span}\Psi$. Analytic EDMD assumes known equilibrium and analyticity; fails for discontinuous dynamics or strange attractors [7]. Learned dictionaries via DNN are non-convex, generalization gaps remain, and LMPC stability proof requires accurate $A,B$ surrogates; error tolerance bound $\|K-\hat K\|\lesssim (1-\gamma)/\kappa(P)$ restrictive [3][4].

- **Scalability**: $G\in\mathbb{C}^{N\times N}$, SVD $O(N^3)$, kernels $O(M^3)$. For high-dimensional fluids $>10^5$ state dim, random Fourier features or Nyström required, losing spectral guarantees.

- **Measure-preserving assumption**: mpEDMD guarantees break for dissipative systems where $\mathcal{K}$ contraction; unitary enforcement then biases spectrum toward unit circle (see Van der Pol). Filtered EDMD chain may stabilize at trivial $V_{\max}=span{1}$ uninformative [10].

- **Control lifting closure**: Bilinear exactly lifts only if observables include state-control products ad infinitum. Finite truncation induces prediction bias that MPC misinterprets as disturbance, requiring robust tube MPC extensions [4][8].

- **Continuous spectrum**: No finite matrix can represent continuous spectrum; smoothing parameter $\epsilon$ in ResDMD/rigged DMD trades resolution vs variance, akin to kernel density bandwidth selection unsolved adaptively [6].

- **Data requirements**: Theoretical guarantees assume i.i.d sampling from invariant measure; trajectory data introduces Markovian correlations increasing mixing-time sample complexity $M\sim\tau_{\text{mix}}\cdot N$.

## 7 Conclusion

Koopman operator learning offers a principled route to linearize nonlinear dynamics for prediction and control without local Jacobian linearization. EDMD provides a baseline finite-section approximation but is susceptible to **spectral pollution** via metric distortion and non-normality [3][6]. This thesis dissected three generations of remedies: **residual certification** (ResDMD), **structure preservation** (mpEDMD unitary Procrustes [5], analytic EDMD triangular preservation [7]), and **range filtering** (filtered EDMD core preservation [10]). For control, we elevated the bilinear Koopman form to **Lyapunov-based MPC** with explicit CLF stabilizability, recursive feasibility, and real-time QP implementations (KQ-LMPC SE(3) [8], DCBF safety QP [9]), closing the loop from data to certified nonlinear control.

Open directions include adaptive dictionary learning with simultaneous pollution certificate, sample complexity of continuous spectra under physical measure, and stochastic Koopman LMPC under martingale noise. Integration with geometric integrators for data collection promises long-term energy preservation critical for Hamiltonian robotics.
