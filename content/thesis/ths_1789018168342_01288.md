# From Coevolution to Creation: The Evoformer Architecture, Equivariant Structure Prediction, and Diffusion-Based Generative Design of Protein Structures

## Abstract

The prediction of protein tertiary structure from amino acid sequence, and its inverse — the design of sequences that fold into specified structures — are the central forward and inverse problems of computational structural biology. This thesis synthesizes the architectural lineage that transformed both. We dissect the **Evoformer** of AlphaFold2 [1], which couples multiple sequence alignment (MSA) and pairwise residue representations through axial gated self-attention and triangle-consistency updates; the **invariant point attention** (IPA) structure module that renders SE(3)-equivariant coordinate refinement in a globally consistent frame set; the three-track design of RoseTTAFold [2]; and the inversion of these predictive networks into generative engines — **RFdiffusion** [3] for backbone design and **ProteinMPNN** [4] for inverse folding — complemented by the MSA-free paradigm of ESMFold [5]. We formalize the attention mechanisms, the equivariance constraints, and the diffusion objectives; derive the evaluation metrics *lDDT* and *TM-score*; and weigh empirical evidence from CASP14 and experimental design campaigns. The arc from the Evoformer's geometric reasoning to diffusion-based *de novo* design marks a methodological unification: structure prediction networks, run in reverse, become protein design tools.

---

## 1. Introduction

For half a century, the mapping from a protein's one-dimensional amino acid sequence to its three-dimensional functional form stood as the defining unsolved problem of structural biology. Anfinsen's thermodynamic hypothesis — that the native conformation is determined by the totality of interatomic interactions and hence by the sequence itself — promised that a computable mapping existed, yet the combinatorial vastness of conformational space, dramatized by Levinthal's paradox, made brute-force search inconceivable [7]. The biennial Critical Assessment of Structure Prediction (CASP) tracked incremental progress for twenty-five years, with template-based modeling improving only slowly and *ab initio* folding of small proteins remaining the province of heroic physics-based simulation.

The decisive rupture arrived at CASP14 in December 2020, when DeepMind's AlphaFold2 achieved a median backbone accuracy of 0.96 Å and a median GDT_TS of 92.4 — performance competitive with experimental structure determination for nearly two-thirds of targets [1]. Within months, the Baker laboratory's RoseTTAFold showed the essential ideas — joint processing of 1D sequence, 2D coevolutionary, and 3D coordinate information — were independently reproducible [2]. Within two more years, the same machinery was turned upside down: RFdiffusion repurposed the structure-prediction network as the denoiser of a diffusion generative model, producing *de novo* binders, symmetric assemblies, and enzyme scaffolds at experimental success rates two orders of magnitude above prior art [3]; ProteinMPNN solved the inverse-folding problem with 52.4% native sequence recovery, rescuing designs that physics-based methods had failed to produce [4]; and ESMFold showed that a protein language model scaled to fifteen billion parameters could replace the multiple sequence alignment entirely [5].

This thesis reconstructs that arc as a single intellectual development. Section 2 reviews the pre-2020 landscape: fragment assembly, direct-coupling analysis, and contact prediction. Section 3 describes the methodology of the AlphaFold2 system end to end. Section 4 provides a deep technical exposition of five pillars: the Evoformer block, invariant point attention, RFdiffusion, ProteinMPNN, and ESMFold. Section 5 presents empirical results and formal statements about the metrics and invariants involved. Section 6 confronts the remaining limitations, and Section 7 concludes with the emerging research frontier.

## 2. Background

### 2.1 The folding problem and its classical decompositions

Anfinsen's 1973 Nobel work established that ribonuclease refolds spontaneously after denaturation, implying that the native structure is the global free-energy minimum accessible to the chain [7]. Levinthal observed that a 100-residue chain possesses on the order of $3^{100}$ rotameric states, so folding cannot proceed by random search; the energy landscape must be funneled. Classical approaches therefore decomposed the problem into *search* and *scoring*. Rosetta's fragment assembly — the dominant 2000s paradigm — sampled 3- and 9-residue fragments from the PDB and recombined them under a physical all-atom energy function. It succeeded on small proteins but plateaued near 60 GDT_TS on free-modeling CASP targets.

### 2.2 Coevolution as a structural signal

The crucial statistical insight was that *evolution had already performed the experiment*: contacting residues show correlated substitutions across homologs. Early mutual-information methods confounded direct and indirect couplings, but **direct-coupling analysis** (DCA) — inferring a Potts model via mean-field or pseudo-likelihood methods — disentangled direct interactions, yielding contact maps accurate enough to fold small proteins. Deep residual networks (RaptorX, AlphaFold1) then predicted distograms from MSAs with superhuman accuracy, though converting distances to coordinates still required a separate, non-differentiable optimization step.

### 2.3 CASP and the metrics of success

CASP evaluates blind predictions against withheld experimental structures using two families of metrics that this thesis will formalize in Section 5: **GDT_TS**, the average fraction of Cα atoms superimposable within 1, 2, 4, and 8 Å cutoffs, and **lDDT**, a superposition-free score comparing all interatomic distance differences. The former rewards global topology; the latter rewards local chemistry. AlphaFold2 was the first system to excel at both simultaneously, and its per-residue confidence estimate **pLDDT** proved so well calibrated that it became a design criterion in its own right.

## 3. Methodology

### 3.1 The AlphaFold2 pipeline

AlphaFold2 accepts a query amino acid sequence and proceeds through four stages [1]:

1. **MSA construction.** The sequence is searched against genomic databases (BFD, MGnify, UniRef90) to build a multiple sequence alignment of up to several thousand homologs. A template search against the PDB provides structural priors.
2. **The Evoformer stack.** Forty-eight Evoformer blocks jointly refine two representations: the MSA representation $m \in \mathbb{R}^{S \times N \times c_m}$ ($S$ sequences, $N$ residues) and the pair representation $z \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times N \times c_z}$ encoding residue–residue relationships. Information flows within and *between* the tracks.
3. **The structure module.** Eight layers of invariant point attention consume the single-sequence and pair representations and directly predict all-atom coordinates, placing each residue as a rigid backbone frame and refining it iteratively.
4. **Recycling.** The network is applied up to four times with previous outputs fed back as inputs — unrolled iterative refinement, fully learned.

### 3.2 Training objectives

The network is trained end to end with a composite loss dominated by the **frame-aligned point error (FAPE)**, which measures the deviation of predicted atom positions after aligning each residue's local frame, clamped to 10 Å to emphasize local correctness. Auxiliary losses predict the distogram, masked MSA tokens (a BERT-like objective that forces the MSA track to model evolutionary statistics), per-residue confidence (pLDDT), and the predicted TM-score (pTM). Training proceeds in stages of increasing crop size (256, then 384 residues), with fine-tuning under a violation loss penalizing steric clashes and chain breaks.

### 3.3 RoseTTAFold and architectural convergence

Independently, Baek *et al.* introduced RoseTTAFold, a **three-track** network jointly processing the 1D sequence, 2D distance/orientation map, and 3D coordinates [2]. Its strong performance confirmed the core thesis: *the winning inductive bias is bidirectional exchange between evolutionary, pairwise, and geometric representations* — licensing the next step of treating the prediction network as a differentiable simulator invertible for design.

## 4. Deep Dive

### 4.1 The Evoformer: attention over evolutionary and pairwise space

The Evoformer block is the engine room of AlphaFold2, and its design reflects a single conviction: long-range dependencies in proteins are mediated simultaneously by *evolution* (which residues coevolve) and *geometry* (which residues are near in space), and the network must let each representation interrogate the other.

**MSA track.** For each column, *row-wise gated self-attention* lets sequences attend to one another with the pair representation injected as an additive bias on the attention logits:

$$\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d}} + b(z)\right)V \odot g.$$

*Column-wise gated self-attention* then propagates coevolutionary couplings along the chain within each sequence. The asymmetry is deliberate: rows model homology, columns model epistasis.

**Pair track.** *Triangle multiplicative updates* route information for each pair $(i, j)$ through every third residue $k$ — naive $O(N^3)$, implemented in a memory-efficient fused kernel — encouraging the triangle inequality on implied distances. *Triangle self-attention* (starting- and ending-node variants) refines edges via attention over the shared node, followed by a two-layer MLP *transition* block.

> **Theorem (Triangle consistency):** *If $z_{ij}$ encodes a consistent Cβ–Cβ distance estimate $d_{ij}$, the triangle multiplicative update preserves the metric constraints $d_{ij} \le d_{ik} + d_{kj}$, since it aggregates only products of edge features sharing a common node — path-relaxation of the all-pairs shortest-path polytope in the distance-embedding limit.*

The outer-product mean collapses the MSA track into the pair track, so coevolutionary evidence continuously rewrites the geometric hypotheses. After forty-eight blocks, the pair representation is so informative that the structure module's task is, in a precise sense, merely to *read out* coordinates.

### 4.2 Invariant point attention and SE(3)-equivariant folding

Predicting coordinates raises a symmetry problem: structural biology is invariant under global rotations and translations, so the network's output must transform equivariantly. AlphaFold2 never predicts absolute coordinates. Instead, each residue $i$ gets a rigid **backbone frame** $T_i = (R_i, t_i) \in SE(3)$ built from its N, Cα, and C atoms via Gram–Schmidt orthonormalization, and the network predicts *updates to these frames*.

**Invariant point attention (IPA)** is the mechanism. Each residue generates query, key, and value points in its *local* frame; attention weights derive from invariant scalar features, while aggregated values — expressed in local coordinates — are mapped back through the current frames. Because every geometric quantity is expressed relative to $T_i$, the operation commutes with global rigid motions:

> **Theorem (SE(3)-invariance of IPA):** *Let $T_g \in SE(3)$ be a global rigid transformation applied to all frames, $T_i \mapsto T_g \circ T_i$. Then the IPA attention weights $a_{ij}$ are unchanged, and the updated frames satisfy $T_i' \mapsto T_g \circ T_i'$. Consequently the predicted backbone is equivariant and the FAPE loss is invariant.*

*Proof sketch.* Query/key/value points are linear projections of single representations; only their coordinates are interpreted in local frames. Weights derive from invariant products and squared distances $\|T_i^{-1} \circ T_j \circ \vec{q}_j - \vec{k}_i\|^2$, unchanged under left-multiplication by $T_g$. The aggregated update transforms as $T_i^{-1} \circ T_g^{-1} \circ T_g \circ (\cdot)$, and the frame update applies the same correction locally, yielding global equivariance. ∎

The structure module stacks eight IPA layers with transition blocks, then refines side-chain $\chi$ angles and backbone frames. The elegance of the design is that *no energy function appears anywhere*: the manifold of plausible protein geometry is learned purely from data, with equivariance enforced architecturally rather than by augmentation.

### 4.3 Generative inversion: RFdiffusion

If a prediction network has internalized the distribution of plausible backbones, running it *backwards* — from noise to structure — should sample new proteins. RFdiffusion [3] makes this precise with **denoising diffusion** on the manifold $SE(3)^N$ of residue frames.

**Forward process.** From a native backbone $x_0$, Gaussian noise is added to translations and Brownian motion to rotations, ending in an isotropic soup of random frames $x_T$. **Reverse process.** A network initialized from RoseTTAFold weights predicts the clean structure $\hat{x}_0$ from each noisy $x_t$. Two innovations proved decisive: *self-conditioning* on the model's own previous $\hat{x}_0$ (mirroring AlphaFold2's recycling), and a mean-squared-error loss on denoised frames that, by breaking global-frame invariance, promotes continuity between timesteps.

**Conditioning.** Because the denoiser accepts auxiliary inputs, RFdiffusion supports an extraordinary range of design tasks within one framework:

- **Motif scaffolding:** functional residues (e.g., an active site) are held fixed while the scaffold is diffused;
- **Binder design:** a target surface conditions generation of a complementary binder via a hotspot-focused interface loss;
- **Symmetric oligomers:** cyclic, dihedral, and cubic symmetries imposed by averaging updates over symmetry mates;
- **Partial diffusion:** brief noising and re-denoising of a native backbone for controlled diversification.

Every generated backbone passes through the standard validation funnel: ProteinMPNN designs sequences (§4.4), AlphaFold2 refolds them *in silico*, and designs matching the backbone (self-consistency RMSD < 2 Å, pLDDT > 80) advance to experiment. Results were unprecedented: 19% *de novo* binder success across five targets — roughly a hundred-fold improvement over Rosetta pipelines — with a cryo-EM structure of the designed influenza binder HA_20 matching the model at 0.63 Å backbone RMSD [3], and symmetric assemblies confirmed by electron microscopy.

### 4.4 Inverse folding: ProteinMPNN

Given a backbone, the *inverse-folding* problem asks for sequences that fold into it. ProteinMPNN [4] uses a message-passing encoder over the backbone graph — nodes featurized by dihedrals and relative frame orientations, edges by distances and orientations — and an **order-agnostic autoregressive decoder**: residues are decoded in random permutation order, learning $p(s_i \mid s_{\setminus i}, x)$ for arbitrary conditioning sets rather than a fixed N-to-C factorization. Gaussian coordinate noise during training proved essential for robustness to imperfect designed backbones.

The numbers redefined expectations: **52.4%** native sequence recovery versus 32.9% for Rosetta, about one second per protein (200× faster than the previous best learned method), and no expert tuning [4]. Experimentally, it rescued previously failed Rosetta and AlphaFold-hallucinated designs — monomers, cyclic homo-oligomers, tetrahedral nanoparticles, target-binding proteins — raising median soluble yield from 9 to 247 mg/L. A schematic of the order-agnostic decoding loop:

```python
import torch, random

def design_sequence(encoder, decoder, backbone_graph, temperature=0.1, n_samples=8):
    """Order-agnostic autoregressive sequence design (schematic)."""
    h = encoder(backbone_graph)          # invariant node embeddings
    L = backbone_graph.num_nodes
    designs = []
    for _ in range(n_samples):
        seq = torch.full((L,), -1)       # -1 = masked
        order = torch.randperm(L)        # random decode order
        for i in order:
            logits = decoder(h, seq, position=i) / temperature
            seq[i] = torch.multinomial(logits.softmax(-1), 1)
        designs.append(seq)
    return designs
```

Permutation-invariant training lets the decoder redesign arbitrary residue subsets — loops, interfaces, whole chains — conditioned on the rest, making it the universal sequence-design backend for RFdiffusion backbones and beyond.

### 4.5 Beyond MSAs: ESMFold and the language-model frontier

Everything above leans on MSAs, whose construction dominates runtime and fails for orphans, designed sequences, and viral antigens with shallow homology. Lin *et al.* asked whether evolutionary information could be *internalized* at training time rather than supplied at inference [5]. Their ESM-2 transformer — masked-language modeling on hundreds of millions of sequences, scaled to **15 billion parameters** — develops representations from which atomic structure decodes directly. ESMFold attaches a folding trunk (a simplified Evoformer over the language model's embeddings) to the AlphaFold2 structure module, predicting structure from a *single sequence*.

The trade is explicit: roughly an order of magnitude faster than AlphaFold2, matching it on low-perplexity sequences within the language model's training distribution, but lagging where a deep MSA would supply decisive coevolutionary evidence [5]. Its triumph is scale: the **ESM Metagenomic Atlas** — over 617 million metagenomic sequences, 225 million at high confidence — a structure census of microbial dark matter no MSA-based pipeline could complete on practical timescales.

### 5.2 The metrics, formalized

**lDDT** (local Distance Difference Test) avoids structural superposition entirely. For a reference structure, consider all atom pairs within 15 Å (excluding pairs from the same residue); a pair is *preserved* if the corresponding distance in the model differs by less than a threshold $t \in \{0.5, 1, 2, 4\}$ Å. The lDDT is the mean preserved fraction over the four thresholds:

$$\text{lDDT} = \frac{1}{4}\sum_{t \in \{0.5,1,2,4\}} \frac{1}{|P|}\sum_{(i,j)\in P} \mathbb{1}\big[\,|d_{ij}^{\text{model}} - d_{ij}^{\text{ref}}| < t\,\big].$$

> **Theorem (Superposition-freedom of lDDT):** *lDDT is invariant under any rigid transformation of either structure and, unlike RMSD or GDT_TS, requires no optimal alignment. It therefore scores multi-domain proteins fairly even when inter-domain orientations are wrong but each domain is correct — precisely the failure mode it was designed to diagnose.*

**TM-score** addresses the complementary question — global topological correctness — with a length-normalized, superposition-based formula:

$$\text{TM} = \max_{\text{align}} \frac{1}{L}\sum_{i=1}^{L_{\text{aligned}}} \frac{1}{1 + (d_i/d_0)^2}, \qquad d_0 = 1.24\,(L-15)^{1/3} - 1.8,$$

where $d_i$ is the distance between aligned residue $i$ after optimal superposition. A TM-score above 0.5 indicates the same fold with high statistical significance, independent of protein length — the property that makes it the standard for fold-level claims. A reference implementation of the core computation:

```python
import numpy as np

def tm_score(dists_aligned, L_target):
    """dists_aligned: per-residue distances after optimal superposition."""
    d0 = 1.24 * (L_target - 15) ** (1/3) - 1.8
    d0 = max(d0, 0.5)
    return float(np.mean([1.0 / (1.0 + (d / d0) ** 2) for d in dists_aligned]))

def lddt(dist_ref, dist_model, thresholds=(0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 4.0)):
    """dist_ref, dist_model: dicts mapping (i,j) -> distance, same pair set."""
    diffs = np.abs(np.array([dist_model[p] - dist_ref[p] for p in dist_ref]))
    return float(np.mean([np.mean(diffs < t) for t in thresholds]))
```

### 5.3 What the evidence establishes

Three claims rest on convergent evidence. **First**, geometric deep learning with the right inductive biases — axial attention over evolutionary data, triangle-consistent pair updates, SE(3)-equivariant refinement — predicts single-chain structure at experimental accuracy for most natural sequences [1][2][7]. **Second**, the learned distribution is *generative*: diffusion models on prediction backbones design functional proteins with success rates that moved *de novo* design from demonstration to pipeline [3]. **Third**, the sequence–structure mapping is bidirectionally tractable: ProteinMPNN inverts backbones to sequences at superhuman recovery, and composed with RFdiffusion and AlphaFold2 validation forms a closed, experimentally validated design loop [4].

## 6. Limitations

The revolution is real, and it is incomplete. **MSA dependence** remains the sharpest limitation of the Evoformer paradigm: orphan proteins, designed sequences, and rapidly evolving viral proteins with shallow alignments are predicted markedly worse, and ESMFold's single-sequence alternative trades peak accuracy for speed [5]. **Dynamics are absent**: AlphaFold2 predicts a single static conformation (modulo recycling stochasticity), yet function lives in ensembles — allosteric transitions, cryptic pockets, and intrinsically disordered regions are poorly served, and pLDDT, while calibrated as a local-confidence measure, is not a Boltzmann weight. **Chemical context is thin**: ligands, cofactors, post-translational modifications, and membrane environments are modeled crudely or not at all, limiting direct application to drug discovery without downstream physics-based refinement.

On the design side, bottlenecks have moved from computation to the wet lab: diffusion models propose thousands of backbones per hour, but each validation cycle takes weeks, and *developability* — expression, solubility, immunogenicity, aggregation — is only weakly constrained by current objectives. Sampling efficiency also lags theory: RFdiffusion typically needs tens to hundreds of denoising steps per backbone, and principled acceleration via flow matching or consistency distillation is active research. Finally, the field's reliance on AlphaFold2-based *self-consistency* as an *in silico* filter risks a subtle circularity — designs are selected for being predictable by the same family of models that generated them — making orthogonal experimental validation, as practiced in [3] and [4], not a luxury but a methodological requirement.

## 7. Conclusion

The Evoformer showed that *evolutionary information, properly attended to, is geometric information*: axial attention over MSAs and triangle-consistent pair updates distill coevolution into explicit residue–residue geometry [1][2]. Invariant point attention showed that coordinates can be predicted directly, without energy functions or fragment libraries, provided the network respects the SE(3) symmetry of physical space. RFdiffusion then inverted the entire apparatus — the predictor became the denoiser, and the learned manifold of protein geometry became a sampler over new proteins [3] — while ProteinMPNN closed the loop from backbone to sequence [4] and ESMFold removed the alignment altogether [5].

The emerging frontier is **co-design**: joint generative modeling of sequence and structure (via flow matching and discrete diffusion), all-atom generation including ligands and cofactors, and the prediction of conformational ensembles rather than single states. The lesson is that the right inductive bias — attention over the right representation, equivariance to the right symmetry, diffusion over the right manifold — can collapse a fifty-year problem into a training run. The next problems are harder, but the method for attacking them is now clear: learn the geometry, respect the symmetry, and generate.
