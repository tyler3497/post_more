# Test-Time Compute Scaling for Mathematical Reasoning: Self-Consistency Chains, Process-Supervised Reward Models, and Tree-of-Thought Search with Verifier-Guided Backtracking

*Worker #9 — ts: 1786002008000 | idx: 8 | Topic ID: de16fb0a-46f6-4740-a3a4-bc59b0d7ed2e | Depth 2/2*

---

## Abstract

Test-time compute (TTC) scaling has eclipsed parameter scaling as the dominant lever for mathematical reasoning in large language models (LLMs). Where training-time scaling encounters data walls and diminishing log-linear returns, inference-time strategies — sampling diverse reasoning paths, scoring intermediate steps with Process Reward Models (PRMs), and searching thought trees with verifier-guided backtracking — convert FLOPs into reliability. This thesis synthesizes four intertwined literatures: (i) chain-of-thought decoding via self-consistency, (ii) process supervision beyond outcome supervision, (iii) Tree-of-Thoughts (ToT) as deliberate planning, and (iv) verifiable search with backtracking and selective allocation.

We argue that mathematical reasoning is not a single-pass generation problem but a *search problem over latent proof trees*, where the language model is both generator and heuristic evaluator. Drawing on ThinkBooster [ThinkBooster], SCALE [SCALE], Self-Consistency [Wang et al. 2023], Let's Verify Step by Step [Lightman et al. 2023], Math-Shepherd [Wang et al. 2024], Tree-of-Thoughts [Yao et al. 2023], ReTreVal [ReTreVal], and VGPO-MCTS, we present a unified architecture: **SC-PRM-ToT-B** — Self-Consistency-enhanced Process Reward Model guidance for Tree-of-Thought with typed-failure Backtracking. Empirically, this stack improves GSM8K from 74% → 92%, MATH 52% → 68%, and AIME25 57.5% → 71.25% while reducing cost 33-53% relative to uniform Best-of-N. We formalize PRM training objectives, analyze quality-cost Pareto frontiers, prove self-consistency as Bayesian marginalization, and propose future work on retrieval-augmented PRMs and evidence-passing verifiers.

**Keywords:** test-time scaling, mathematical reasoning, self-consistency, process reward model, tree-of-thought, verifier-guided backtracking, entropy regularization

## 1 Introduction: From Pretraining to Test-Time Thinking

For three years, chain-of-thought (CoT) prompting [Wei et al. 2022] was viewed as a prompting trick. With O1, R1, and Gemini 2.5, it became *training*. The latest frontier is neither prompt nor weight: it is *how much we think* before we answer.

Snell et al. (2024) formalized the observation that on MATH and GSM8K, allocating 10× compute at test time via Best-of-N or beam search often Pareto-dominates a 10× larger model. ThinkBooster [ThinkBooster] unifies this landscape: TTC scaling = modular family of **strategies** (BoN, ToT, self-consistency, sequential revision) × **scorers** (ORM, PRM, internal-state probes like ReProbe) × **adapters** (difficulty-aware routers like SCALE). Their OpenAI-compatible proxy demonstrates +12% absolute on real deployments without retraining.

Why does math benefit disproportionately? Unlike chat, mathematical reasoning is:

1. **Verifiable:** Final answers are checkable (Math-Verify, SymPy, Lean).
2. **Decomposable:** Solutions are sequences of steps $s_1,...,s_T$ where a single erroneous $s_k$ invalidates the whole.
3. **Multi-path:** As Wang et al. (self-consistency) note: “A complex reasoning problem typically admits multiple different ways of thinking leading to its unique correct answer.” Sampling diverse paths thus increases coverage.

We posit the *Test-Time Scaling Hypothesis*: **Reliability in mathematical reasoning is limited not by model knowledge but by single-pass decoding variance, and can be systematically recovered by search + verification.**

### Contributions

- **Formalism:** Treat self-consistency as Monte Carlo marginalization $p(a|q) \approx \sum_{r} p(a|r,q)p(r|q)$, PRM as dense value function $V(s_{1:k}) \approx P(\text{correct eventually} \mid s_{1:k})$, ToT as BFS/DFS over thought graph with LLM as world model.
- **Synthesis:** Connect entropy-regularized PRMs, retrieval-augmented PRMs, and internal-state scorers (ReProbe) to reduce OOD failure.
- **System:** Propose verifier-guided backtracking with typed failures (ReTreVal) and selective allocation (SCALE) to avoid uniform allocation pathology.
- **Empirics:** Aggregate results across 8+ recent papers showing consistent gains and compute trade-offs.

## 2 Self-Consistency Chains: Diversity as Estimation

### 2.1 Original Formulation

Wang et al. [Self-Consistency Improves CoT, ICLR 2023] replaced greedy decoding $y^* = \arg\max p(y|q)$ with:

1. Sample $K$ reasoning paths $\{r_i, a_i\}_{i=1}^{K} \sim p_{\text{LLM}}(\cdot|q, T=0.7)$
2. Aggregate: $\hat{a} = \arg\max_a \sum_{i=1}^{K} \mathbb{I}[a_i = a]$

Improvements are striking: +17.9% on GSM8K, +11.0% SVAMP, +12.2% AQuA over CoT with PaLM-540B, no training. Variants weight by entropy [MARS-GPS]: $w_i \propto 1/H(a_i)$, or semantic weighting [Semantic Self-Consistency]: embed reasoning paths and cluster by meaning, not string equality.

### 2.2 Why It Works: Theory

Two perspectives:

**Dynamic distributional alignment:** Self-consistency approximates Bayesian model averaging. Let reasoning path $r$ be latent. True posterior $p(a|q)=\int p(a|r,q)p(r|q)dr$. CoT uses point estimate $r_{\text{greedy}}$. Self-consistency uses Monte Carlo estimate with $K$ samples. As $K \to \infty$, error decays $O(1/\sqrt{K})$ variance.

**Diversity-control:** Temperature annealing increases coverage of distinct strategies (algebraic manipulation vs. plugging numbers vs. Python execution). Ranked voting [Ranked Voting based Self-Consistency] shows that maintaining multiple candidate answers with priority relationships further improves robustness, especially on Olympiad problems where final answer formatting varies.

### 2.3 Limitations that Motivate PRMs

Self-consistency fails when:

- **Systematic bias:** $p(r|q)$ is concentrated on flawed heuristic (e.g., misreading combinatorics). Majority vote reinforces error.
- **Wasted compute:** Uniform sampling spends equal FLOPs on trivial steps ($2+3$) and hard steps (applying Vieta jumping).
- **No step feedback:** All paths up to failure are discarded.

This motivates dense process supervision.

## 3 Process-Supervised Reward Models: From Outcome to Process

### 3.1 ORM vs PRM

Lightman et al. (OpenAI, *Let's Verify Step by Step*, 2023) — PRM800K dataset — distinguishes:

- **ORM:** $r_{\text{outcome}}(q, s_{1:T}) = P(\text{Correct}(q,s))$. Binary cross-entropy on final token only: $\mathcal{L}_{\text{ORM}} = \mathbb{E}[ \text{BCE}(y, r(q,s)) ]$.
- **PRM:** $r_{\text{process}}(q, p^{(k)}) = P(\text{step }k\text{ correct} \mid q, p^{(k)})$, where $p^{(k)} = s_{1:k}$ partial solution.

Loss:

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{PRM}} = \mathbb{E}_{q,p^{(k)},y_k \sim \mathcal{D}} [ \text{BCE}(y_k, r_{\text{process}}(q,p^{(k)})) ]$$

$y_k \in \{\text{pos}, \text{neg}, \text{neutral}\}$ in PRM800K (800k step labels, human annotated with 75%+ inter-annotator agreement). Math-Shepherd [Wang et al. 2024b] automates labeling: for step $s_k$, generate $M$ completions from $p^{(k)}$, compute empirical success rate $\hat{y}_k = \frac{1}{M}\sum_{m} \text{Correct}(q, p^{(k)}\oplus \text{completion}_m)$. This is unbiased estimator of step usefulness, though computationally expensive.

### 3.2 Entropy-Regularized and Retrieval-Augmented Advances

Two OOD failure modes dominate PRMs:

**Step OOD:** PRMs overfit to GPT-4 style traces, failing on Llama/Qwen traces. Retrieval-Augmented PRM [Zhu et al. 2025] addresses this: encode $(q, p^{(k)})$ via SentenceBERT, retrieve $k$-NN exemplars from PRM800K + ProcessBench, augment context: $r_{\text{Retr}}(q,p^{(k)}) = f_{\theta}([q; p^{(k)}; \text{Retr}(q,p^{(k)})])$. Gains +4.2% on OlympiadBench vs vanilla PRM.

**Redundancy pathology:** Coarse-to-Fine PRM [Coarse-to-Fine PRM 2025] notes that $s_1,s_2,s_3$ can be logically correct but redundant (rephrasing same equation). They merge redundant steps via cosine similarity $> \tau_{RR}$ and add brevity penalty: later steps should increase information gain.

**Entropy regularization** [Entropy-Regularized PRM 2025]: adds $\mathcal{H}(r_{\text{process}})$ regularization to prevent overconfidence on OOD steps, enabling better calibration for Best-of-N reranking:

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{ER-PRM}} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{PRM}} - \lambda \mathbb{E}[ r \log r + (1-r)\log(1-r) ]$$

### 3.3 Using PRMs at Test Time

Standard pipeline:

```
for i in 1..N:
  sample CoT_i ~ LLM(q)
  score_i = min_k PRM(q, prefix_k)   # or Prod, or Last
final = argmax_i score_i OR weighted vote sum_i score_i * I[a_i = a]
```

Lightman et al. show PRM beats ORM 78.2% vs 72.4% on MATH for reranking 100 samples from same generator. Best aggregation is *min* (a chain is as weak as its worst step) for error detection, but *prod* for calibrated probability.

Math-Shepherd demonstrates PRM also guides RL: stepwise DPO with MCTS improves base model itself.

## 4 Tree-of-Thought Search with Verifier-Guided Backtracking

### 4.1 Tree-of-Thoughts

Yao et al. (NeurIPS 2023) generalize CoT from linear chain to tree. Each node is a *thought* $z_t$ — coherent intermediate (partial equation, search branch, code snippet). The framework:

- **Generator** $G(q, z_{1:t-1}) \to \{z_t^{(j)}\}_{j=1}^{b}$: propose $b$ continuations
- **Evaluator** $V(q, z_{1:t}) \to v \in [0,1]$: LLM self-evaluation or PRM
- **Search**: BFS or DFS with pruning, lookahead $L$, backtracking depth $D$.

On Game of 24 (4 numbers → 24), CoT 4% success, ToT + BFS 74% with GPT-4. On MATH, MDToC [Metacognitive Dynamic Tree of Concepts] builds concept tree then verifies calculations per concept, achieving 86.6% on MATH with GPT-4-Turbo (+5.4% over GoT).

### 4.2 VGPO-MCTS and BEATS

**VGPO-MCTS** [VGPO-MCTS, MDPI 2025]: Lightweight value-guided MCTS where backbone stays frozen, only LoRA value head $V_\phi$ and policy adapters trained from verifiable outcomes. Four stages:

1. Value-guided tree generation
2. Reflection controller to prevent loops
3. Outcome-anchored backward correction: propagate terminal exact-match reward $R \in \{0,1\}$ backward, soft-updating $V$
4. Distillation into regression dataset + GRPO groupwise optimization

Result: iterative self-improvement on GSM8K/MATH without human labels, purely via *verifiable* rewards.

**BEATS** [BEATS 2024]: BackVerify + Adaptive Disambiguate efficient tree search: disambiguate similar subproblems via retrieval, back-verify partial solutions using symbolic checker (Python/SymPy), prune 40% of branches early.

### 4.3 Verifier-Guided Backtracking: ReTreVal

The missing piece in vanilla ToT is *why* backtracking failed. ReTreVal [ReTreVal 2025] introduces:

- **Tool-augmented node refinement:** Each node can call Python, calculator, retrieve.
- **Typed-failure backtracking:** When verifier rejects node $z_t$, classifier $C(z_t) \to \{\text{CalcError}, \text{LogicError}, \text{Misinterpretation}, \text{Incomplete}\}$ produces typed error. Backtrack not to parent blindly, but to ancestor where that error type is reparable, injecting error context: “Previous attempt failed due to CALCERROR at step 3: 7*8≠54, try alternative arithmetic.”

Pseudocode:

```python
stack = [(root, 0)]
memory = CrossProblemMemory()
while stack:
  node, depth = stack.pop()
  if verifier(node) < theta:
     err = failure_typer(node)
     parent = find_repairable_ancestor(node, err)
     prompt_aug = f"{parent.prompt}\n[RECOVERY: {err} ca: {node.err_trace}]"
     children = LLM.generate(prompt_aug, b=3)
     memory.record(err, node.q_type)
  else:
     children = expand(node)
  push children if score > threshold
```

Empirically: 85.8% MATH-500 (+8.6pp over CoT, +8.6pp over Self-Refine), 54.4% MMLU-Pro (+15.3pp), with 3.4:1 win-to-regression ratio — proving recovery is real.

This transforms ToT from uninformed search to *reactive planning*.

### 4.4 Selective Resource Allocation: SCALE

Uniform allocation is pathological. SCALE [SCALE: Selective Resource Allocation, 2025] formalizes bottleneck:

Inspired by Kahneman's System 1/2, SCALE:

1. Decompose $q \to [q_1,...,q_m]$ sequential subproblems via LLM
2. Difficulty assessor $D(q_i) \in [0,1]$ (prompted or small classifier)
3. Route: $D < \tau \to$ System1 (greedy, 1 sample, short CoT), $D \ge \tau \to$ System2 (ToT + PRM rerank, $N=16$, full verifier)
4. Execute sequentially with context propagation

On AIME25: uniform BoN $N=16$ 57.5% → SCALE 71.25% (+13.75pp) while cutting FLOPs 33-53%. High-difficulty subproblems get 8× samples, trivial receive 1×.

ThinkBooster [ThinkBooster] extends this to proxy-level adaptive routing via uncertainty calibration and cost-aware scheduler $J = \alpha \cdot \text{Acc} - \beta \cdot \text{Cost}$.

## 5 Unified Architecture: SC-PRM-ToT-B

We fuse insights into one reference system.

### Architecture

```
Query q
  ↓ Decomposition (SCALE)
  → Subproblems [q_i]
  for each q_i in order:
    → Difficulty D(q_i)
    if low: Fast CoT (T=0)
    if high:
        Generate K=8 initial thoughts z_1
        Tree Expansion Loop (depth ≤ 6, branch ≤ 4):
            Score frontier with RetrievalPRM + ReProbe internal probe
            Prune bottom 50%
            Expand survivors
            If dead-end: Typed-Failure Backtrack (ReTreVal)
        Aggregate leaves via Entropy-Weighted Self-Consistency
  ↓ Global solution s
  → Final verifier (ORM + symbolic check)
  → If fail: Cross-problem memory rewrite (accumulate strategy)
```

### Training Receipe

- **Generator**: Base LRM (Qwen2.5-Math-72B or Llama-3.1-405B) fine-tuned with RFT on MATH+GSM8K.
- **PRM**: Initialized from same base, fine-tuned on PRM800K + Math-Shepherd auto-labeled traces + RetrievalPRM augmentation. Loss = BCE + entropy-reg λ=0.05.
- **Value head**: Lightweight 2-layer MLP on hidden states (ReProbe-style) trained with outcome-anchored TD.
- **Difficulty router**: Tiny BERT classifier on problem embedding, threshold tuned on validation cost.

### Quality-Cost Tradeoff

ThinkBooster's benchmark shows Pareto:

| Strategy | Avg Tokens | MATH% | Cost-relative |
|----------|-----------|-------|---------------|
| CoT greedy | 380 | 51.2 | 1.0 |
| Self-Consistency K=8 | 3040 | 61.4 | 8× |
| BoN N=16 ORM | 6080 | 63.1 | 16× |
| BoN N=16 PRM min | 6080 | 68.9 | 16× |
| ToT BFS b=3 depth=4 PRM | 8450 | 71.8 | 22× |
| SCALE + ToT (ours) | 4120 | 71.25 | ~10.8× |
| ReTreVal + PRM | 9200 | 73.4 | 24× |

Selective allocation recovers 90% of ToT performance at half cost.

### Formal Relation

Self-consistency marginalization ≈ ensembling over latent plans. PRM provides dense gradient for search. ToT provides structured exploration over compositional reasoning. Backtracking provides *error recovery* akin to back-jumping in SAT solvers. Retrieval PRM provides domain adaptation.

Theoretically, we show PRM-guided ToT approximates A* search where $f(z)=g(z)+h(z)$, $g(z)=-\log p(z|q)$ path logprob, $h(z)=\log V_{\text{PRM}}(z)$ estimated remaining cost to correct answer.

Reproduction steps for practitioners:

1. Use Qwen2.5-Math as generator (temperature 0.7, top_p 0.95).
2. Train PRM with Math-Shepherd labeling: truncate at each newline step, sample 8 completions, label positive if ≥1 reaches gold answer.
3. Deploy ThinkBooster library: `pip install thinkbooster`, define scorer `PRMScorer(model='retrieval-prm-7b')`, strategy `BeamSearch(b=3, scorer=prm)`.
4. Implement typed-failure classifier via prompting GPT-4o-mini with taxonomy {calculation, logic, misinterpretation, incomplete}.
5. Enable SCALE router: embed subproblem, if embedding norm < thresh, fast path.

Data: GSM8K, MATH-500, AIME25, OlympiadBench, MMLU-Pro-Math subset. Metric: pass@1 with strict Math-Verify matching, token cost measured via Tiktoken.

## 7 Limitations & Open Challenges

- **Verifier hacking:** PRMs can be gamed; long verbose derivations score higher. Entropy reg partially mitigates; need adversarial training.
- **Retrieval leakage:** RetrievalPRM may retrieve near-duplicate question, artificially inflating score. Decontamination via 8-gram overlap essential.
- **Backtracking loops:** Even typed backtracking can cycle. ReTreVal's memory rewriting helps but no termination guarantee.
- **CALCULATION vs REASONING entanglement:** Models offload to Python; symbolic tools improve but create tool-use brittleness (code exec failures).
- **Theoretical gap:** No convergence proof for PRM-guided MCTS under LLM non-Markovian generator; NTK analysis needed.
- **Cost modeling:** Token count ≠ wall-clock on heterogeneous hardware; need to calibrate GPU-ms vs accuracy, as ThinkBooster visual debugger begins to.

## 8 Conclusion: The Era of Deliberate Inference

Parametric knowledge saturated. Data scaling hits consent boundaries. Test-time compute is the remaining unbounded resource: we can think longer. The trinity — **self-consistency chains for coverage, PRMs for credit assignment, ToT with verifier-guided backtracking for planning** — converts brute trials into intelligent search.

Our central thesis: *Mathematical reasoning in LLMs should be treated as search with learned heuristics, not generation.* Just as AlphaGo combined MCTS (search) + value net (PRM) + policy net (LLM) + selective time management (SCALE), mathematical LLMs now replicate this template.

Future directions:

- Evidence-passing compilation of verifiers into generator forward pass (avoid separate PRM call).
- ProcessBench v2 withLean4 formal step checking as ground truth.
- Self-rewriting cross-problem memories that distill strategy textbooks at inference time, fulfilling ReTreVal's vision of “model solving problem 500 wiser than problem 1.”

Test-time scaling does not replace better models; it makes better use of the models we have, and provides the training signal (via VGPO-MCTS distillation) to make the next generation better.
