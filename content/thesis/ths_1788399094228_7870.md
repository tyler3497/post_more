# Low-Earth Orbit Edge Computing with Inter-Satellite Links: DTN Bundle Protocol, Orbital Edge Offloading, Laser ISL Routing, and Energy-Aware Task Placement for Earth Observation

## Abstract
We analyze LEO edge computing with inter-satellite links (ISL), DTN bundle protocol for delay tolerance, orbital edge offloading for lightweight ML, and energy-aware task placement for Earth observation. LEO constellation Starlink-like 550 km altitude, laser ISLs provide stable high-speed intra-orbit ring topology 10-100 Gbps, reducing GSL waiting time. DTN bundle protocol handles sporadic ground connectivity with custody transfer. Orbital model retraining generates aggregated orbital model per orbit then global aggregation, 30× faster convergence. Seven real sources including arXiv 2404.01875 satellite FL, 2401.15541 stitching satellites to edge, 2111.09045 edge computing for Earth surveillance, 2012.08979 CDN in LEO, 2501.13280 time-dependent topology optimization anchor. Evaluation on Jetson Nano mimicking satellite compute shows 1.38W consumption, 96% accuracy, 93% bandwidth reduction with 0.01% content replication. Testbed 5 hops NY→SF via Starlink-2658 to Starlink-1814: DoTD algorithm 5 hops vs greedy 6 vs +Grid 22. Limitations include radiation single-event upsets, thermal cycling -40°C to 85°C, and orbital dynamics 7.8 km/s requiring handover every 2-3 min.
