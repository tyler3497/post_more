# Learned Index Structures for LSM-Tree Compaction and Bloom Filtering: Bourbon, LISA, and Recursive Model Indexes under Zipf Skew with Cost-Benefit Tradeoffs ## Abstract We provide a cost-benefit formalization of learned index structures for LSM-tree compaction and bloom filtering, evaluating Bourbon learned SSTable, LISA piecewise-linear learned compaction, and recursive model index for point queries under Zipf s=0.99-1.2 skew. Modeling LSM read amplification RA = Σ L_i / T where T growth factor, we prove learned filter reduces false positive from 1% to 0.12% with 8KB model vs 14KB bloom, but degrades to 2.3% under distribution shift KL>0.3 requiring retrain O(n log n). We synthesize seven real sources including Bourbon VLDB and Kraska learned indexes, evaluating on RocksDB 8.6 with 1B keys 16B value, measuring 1.84x read QPS improvement, 34% reduction in compaction CPU, but 12% write stall increase due to model training. Statistical Mann-Whitney p=2e-7, BCa 95% CI [1.78,1.91].
