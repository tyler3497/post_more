## 6 Limitations

1. **Honest majority / scheduler benevolent:** Byzantine n>=3f+1 assumed [1] rational adversaries degrade liveness not safety.
2. **Formal model scope:** TLA+ checked N=4 real N=4096 requires parameterized induction [3] not yet machine-checked.
3. **Constant-time side channels:** constant-time quaternion mitigates timing [3] power EM sideband health monitoring [6] not modeled.
4. **Approximate learned models:** CDF eps-bound trained distribution drift invalidates bound requiring retraining O(n log n) [2][4].
5. **Hardware variance:** Loihi graded spikes reduce comm [1] but Lava toolchain required portability GPU limited.
6. **Quantum overhead:** surface code distance d=27 1e-12 logical error demands d^2=729 physical per logical plus magic factories 15x blowup SPARO reduces but not eliminates qLDPC gap [5][7]
7. **Numeric stability:** CKKS approx homomorphism 2^-40 loss per multiply bit-exact needs TFHE PBS switching [3][4].
8. **PIR rate optimality:** 2-server PIR rate <=0.5 proven upper bound [1] construction achieves 0.48 4pct gap correction-word overhead.
