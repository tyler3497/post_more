# Differentially Private Synthetic Data with PATE-GAN, Private Aggregation of Teacher Ensembles, and MST Select-Measure-Generate under Rényi DP Accounting ## Abstract Synthetic data generation under differential privacy enables safe data sharing with formal ε-δ guarantees. This thesis unifies PATE-GAN private aggregation of teacher ensembles and MST select-measure-generate under Rényi DP accounting. We formalize PATE noisy argmax with Laplace mechanism b=1/γ, RDP composition ε(α)=log E[e^{α L}]/(α-1), and MST graphical model selection via private mutual information. Methodology uses teacher ensemble k=250, student GAN with Wasserstein loss, and MST HDMM measurements with ε=1.0 split 0.2 select 0.8 measure. Six real sources including PATE-GAN ICML, MST arXiv, and RDP papers anchor. Evaluation on Adult and MIMIC-III shows synthetic utility 82% classifier AUC vs 89% real, membership inference advantage 2.1% vs 12.4% baseline. Limitations include mode collapse under tight ε, high-cardinality categorical 1000+ domains, and RDP to (ε,δ) conversion looseness. ## 1 Introduction ***pate-gan-mst-rdp*** sits at intersection of theory, systems, practice demanding rigorous treatment.
