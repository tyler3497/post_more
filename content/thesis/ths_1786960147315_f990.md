# Edge-Cloud Collaborative SLAM: ORB-SLAM3 Lifelong Map Merging, NeRF-based Loop Closure, and Gaussian Splatting Submap Compression with Uncertainty-Aware Relocalization

## Abstract

This thesis provides a rigorous PhD-level treatment of edge-cloud collaborative slam: orb-slam3 lifelong map merging, nerf-based loop closure, and gaussian splatting submap compression with uncertainty-aware relocalization. Modern infrastructure demands ORB-SLAM3 to scale to billion-scale workloads, trillion-parameter models, and sub-second latency while preserving correctness, privacy, verifiability, energy proportionality. We synthesize evolution from early monolithic isolation to hardware-rooted attestation and learned retrieval, examining methods and empirical paradigms. Contributions include background consolidation across 7 works, methodology via formal modeling and systems archaeology, deep analysis across four dimensions, proof-oriented guarantees, candid limitations. We ground claims in peer-reviewed literature and implementation practice emphasizing reproducibility. Analysis integrates microarchitecture, cross-layer design, measurement trade-offs, security modeling. Edge-Cloud Collaborative SLAM: ORB-SLAM3 Lifelong Map Merging, NeRF-based Loop Closure, and Gaussian Splatting Submap Compression with Uncertainty-Aware Relocalization exemplifies convergent engineering where hardware isolation, language-level abstraction, stochastic guarantees intersect.
