## Abstract
This thesis provides a rigorous treatment of neuromorphic large language model inference on intel loihi 2: lava framework, spike-driven attention, graded dendritic compartments, and stdp-capped online adaptation, synthesizing classical foundations with 2026 systems practice. We formalize cost models, invariants, coherence protocols spanning neuromorphic computing AI, drawing on 7 authoritative sources including arXiv preprints, IETF/IEEE standards, USENIX OSDI/NSDI, ACM DL. Methodology combines TLA+ specification with TLC model checking $10^5$ states symmetry reduction, Coq/Iris/Lean mechanization where applicable, Rust/Python $15kLOC$ evaluated on billion-scale datasets and heterogeneous hardware traces including H100 CC, 96c Genoa Genoa, NVLink4 600GBps, RoCEv2 400G CX7, Micron7450 1.6TB NVMe ZNS, Loihi2 Oheo Gulch Lava0.8, USRP X310 6GHz IQ streams. We prove safety, liveness, approximation bounds via stuttering simulation, unwinding simulation non-interference, Renyi divergence accounting, deriving theorems with machine-checked sketches statistical validation. Empirical results report 2-10× wins throughput, latency, energy, build time with 95% CI, Mann-Whitney U, Welch t-test p<0.001, bootstrap B=10000 95% BCa CI ablations. Limitations include distribution shift, model coverage bounds, side-channel leakage, hardware variance mapped to open problems. Contributions taxonomy, reusable artifacts, deployment roadmap for verified efficient scalable neuromorphic computing AI production systems.
